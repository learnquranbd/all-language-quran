# SOURCES ledger — 18:99

## Tafsir fetched (all from PREP.md, already on disk; none re-fetched)

- **t15 at-Tabari (ar)** — NAMED. "yaqūlu: yakhtaliṭ jinnuhum bi-insihim" (the all-creation reading: their jinn mix with their mankind on the day the promise comes and the mountains are levelled). Also his gloss on the Ṣūr as "qarnun yunfakhu fīh" and on "fa-jamaʿnāhum jamʿan" = "jamaʿnā jamīʿa al-khalq … li-mawqifi al-ḥisāb."
- **t14 Ibn Kathir (ar)** — NAMED. "ay al-nās yawmaʾidhin … yamūjūna fī al-nās wa-yufsidūna" (Gog/Magog surge over mankind at emergence, citing as-Suddi); "hādhā awwalu yawmi al-qiyāma" (Ibn Zayd); "yakhtaliṭ al-ins wa'l-jinn" and Ibn Abbas "al-jinn wa'l-ins yamūju baʿḍuhum fī baʿḍ"; Ṣūr = "qarn yunfakhu fīh," blower Israfil; "aḥḍarnā al-jamīʿ li'l-ḥisāb."
- **t169 Ibn Kathir (en, abridged)** — NAMED. English confirmation of the Gog/Magog-over-mankind reading, the As-Suddi citation, "before the Day of Resurrection and after the Dajjal," and the ṣūr/Israfil gloss. Source of the Zaynab bint Jahsh hadith with Ibn Kathir's own grading (see below).
- **t16 al-Muyassar (ar)** — NAMED. "wa-taraknā Yaʾjūja wa-Maʾjūja … yamūju baʿḍuhum fī baʿḍ mukhtaliṭīn li-kathratihim; wa-nufikha fi'l-qarn li'l-baʿth" (explicit Gog/Magog reading; Horn blown "for the resurrection").
- **t90 al-Qurtubi (ar)** — NAMED. Three views laid out; his ranking "aẓharuhā awsaṭuhā (Gog/Magog at the wall's completion), wa-ḥasuna al-awwal (jinn+mankind on the Day, because the Resurrection was just mentioned), wa-abʿaduhā ākhiruhā"; and the mawj metaphor = "al-ḥayra wa-taraddud baʿḍihim fī baʿḍ … shabbahahum bi-mawji al-baḥr."
- **t91 as-Sa'di (ar)** — NAMED. "yaḥtamil anna al-ḍamīr yaʿūd ilā Yaʾjūja wa-Maʾjūja … wa-yaḥtamil … ilā al-khalāʾiq yawma al-qiyāma … min al-ahwāl wa'l-zalāzil al-ʿiẓām"; both held without deciding; the following verses read as evidence for the second; Israfil blows, souls returned to bodies, first and last / kāfir and muʾmin gathered.
- **t94 al-Baghawi (ar)** — NAMED. "kamawji al-māʾ … yakhtaliṭ baʿḍuhum bi-baʿḍ li-kathratihim"; and "khurūj Yaʾjūja wa-Maʾjūja min ʿalāmāti qurbi al-sāʿa" (emergence a sign of the Hour); "fī ṣaʿīdin wāḥid" for the gathering.
- **t168 Ma'arif al-Qur'an (en)** — NAMED. Pronoun of baʿḍahum "obviously reverts to Ya'juj Ma'juj … their state when the passage lies open and they hasten down from the hilly heights"; fa-jamaʿnāhum reverts to "the creation at large, human beings and the Jinn … mukallaf … gathered on the plains of al-Hashr."

## Hadith

- **Zaynab bint Jahsh (RA)** — USED (§4). Sahih al-Bukhari 3346.
  - URL fetched: https://quranx.com/hadith/Bukhari/DarusSalam/Hadith-3346
  - First ten words (EN, as quoted): "None has the right to be worshipped but Allah. Woe…"
  - Grading: **Sahih.** Ibn Kathir (t169) states it verbatim and adds "This is a Sahih Hadith, both Al-Bukhari and Muslim recorded it." Not upgraded; reported as Ibn Kathir gives it. Directly on the barrier-breach that produces this verse's surging (Ibn Kathir's own commentary covers 18:97-99).

## Dropped (could not confirm grading, per SOURCE-GATE §2)

- The trumpet narration "How can I relax while the owner of the Horn has put the Horn in his mouth…" (Atiyah al-Awfi, in both t15 at-Tabari and t14/t169 Ibn Kathir). Cited by the mufassirūn but no grading confirmed on this verse; Atiyah is commonly weak. Kept the *sense* (Ṣūr = a horn, blower Israfil) attributed to the mufassirūn; dropped the quoted wording. Noted in §5 that further narrations are set aside for lack of a confirmable grading.
- At-Tabari's Abu Hurayra narration on the three blasts (fazaʿ / ṣaʿq / qiyām) and Israfil holding the Ṣūr — no grading confirmed; dropped.
- Ibn Kathir's two Gog/Magog-lineage narrations (Tabarani: "min wuldi Ādam…", graded by Ibn Kathir himself "gharīb bal munkar ḍaʿīf"; and the Nasai report) — weak / off-topic; dropped.
- The Iblis-on-that-day narration (shaykh min Banī Fazāra, in t14 and t15) — a report, not marfūʿ, no grading; off the verse's core; dropped.

## Asbab

- None. No asbab an-nuzul applies; the verse's placement (closing the Dhul-Qarnayn section, opening onto the Hour) is used instead, per SOURCE-GATE §2.
