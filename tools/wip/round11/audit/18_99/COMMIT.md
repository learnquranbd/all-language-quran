Closes the Dhul-Qarnayn section and turns to the Hour: the barrier is a
worldly mercy on a timer (18:98), and this verse steps from it to the
universal gathering.

Card: a wall trusted for safety is on loan and will fall; live now for
the gathering where only what was sent ahead stands with you. Themes
hereafter + accountability.

Article, 8 sections. Sources kept the two readings of "yamūju fī baʿḍ"
as a genuine disagreement, not forced:
- Gog/Magog surging at emergence — al-Muyassar, Ma'arif (the "obvious"
  sense), Ibn Kathir via as-Suddi, and as-Sa'di's first option.
- All creation surging on the Day — at-Tabari (jinn mix with mankind),
  Ibn Kathir via Ibn Abbas and Ibn Zayd, as-Sa'di's second option.
- al-Qurtubi's three-way ranking reported faithfully (middle most
  apparent, all-creation "good", "loose in the world" most far-fetched).
Mawj = bewilderment / a churning sea (al-Qurtubi, al-Baghawi).
Horn: ṣūr a horn, blower Israfil (at-Tabari, Ibn Kathir); blown "for
the resurrection" (al-Muyassar); souls returned to bodies (as-Sa'di).
Gathering: whole creation for the Reckoning (at-Tabari, Ma'arif), sealed
with 56:49-50 and 18:47 (Ibn Kathir).

Hadith: Zaynab bint Jahsh (RA), Sahih al-Bukhari 3346, on the hole in
the barrier — graded Sahih (agreed upon) by Ibn Kathir, not upgraded.

Not a condemned-group verse: no licensing sentence; a section states the
tafsir names Gog/Magog as a reality but licenses no identification of any
living nation with them.

Dropped for unconfirmable grading: the "owner of the Horn" trumpet
narration (Atiyah), the three-blasts report, Ibn Kathir's Gog/Magog
lineage narrations (he graded one munkar), and the Iblis-on-that-day
report. Listed in LEDGER.
