Replaces the 639-word, 5-section article with a 1,761-word, 8-section deep article, built only on tafsir fetched for 85:14 (shipped card kept).

Sources: at-Tabari, Ibn Kathir (ar; en abridged for 85:11-20), al-Qurtubi,
as-Sa'di, al-Baghawi, al-Muyassar (85:12-16), Ma'arif al-Qur'an (85:10 group).

Placement: Muyassar's one-unit paraphrase of 85:12-16 and Ibn Kathir's reading
of the grip (on enemies who rejected the messengers) beside forgiveness for the
repenter; al-Majid's two readings (Ibn Kathir). 11:90 as the only other wadud,
verified by search of the text.
al-Ghafur: by repentance (at-Tabari, Ibn Kathir, as-Sa'di, Muyassar) and by
believers / covering, as-Sattur (al-Qurtubi, al-Baghawi). At-Tabari's "dhu
al-mahabba lahu" binds both names to the repenter.
al-Wadud, none chosen: al-Habib (Ibn Abbas in at-Tabari, Ibn Kathir); al-Muhibb
(al-Qurtubi, al-Baghawi); Mujahid's fa'ul bi-ma'na fa'il; mawdud like rakub and
halub ("qila" in al-Qurtubi, al-Baghawi); as-Sa'di holds both with 5:54. Ibn
Zayd ar-Rahim; ad-Dahhak's brother simile; al-mutawaddid bi-l-maghfira; "loves
to forgive" (al-Baghawi); Isma'il al-Qadi's "no child" reading via al-Mubarrad.
as-Sa'di: love of Allah as root of servanthood; the pairing shows repenters are
forgiven and loved, against unnamed "ba'd al-ghalitin".
Hadith: Muslim 2747a (Anas, the lost mount), quoted whole, the narration
as-Sa'di brings unsourced; marked as not mentioning the verse.
Asbab: none. People of the Ditch: licensing sentence in both languages; 85:10
link via al-Hasan in Ma'arif.
Dropped from the old article (unsourced): "fa'ul, the intensive form", Makkan
revelation, the Suhayb boy-and-king narration, the "both occurrences attach it
to turning back" thesis.
