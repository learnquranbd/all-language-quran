# SOURCES ledger — 30:51

## Tafsir fetched (from PREP, not re-fetched) and named

- **at-Tabari (15)** — NAMED. Rests on: "ولئن أرسلنا ريحا مفسدة ما أنبته الغيث ... فصار من بعد خضرته مصفرا، لظلوا من بعد استبشارهم، وفرحتهم به يكفرون بربهم." Used for: the wind spoils what the rain grew; green→yellow; and the turn comes *after* their rejoicing/joy — they disbelieve in their Lord (S1P2, S5P2).
- **al-Muyassar (16)** — NAMED. Rests on: "ريحًا مفسدة ... فصار من بعد خضرته مصفرًا، لمكثوا من بعد رؤيتهم له يكفرون بالله ويجحدون نعمه." Used for: corrupting wind; and both senses of yakfurūn side by side — disbelieve in Allah AND deny His blessings; the gloss "لمكثوا" (they would remain) for the staying/persistence sense (S2P1, S3P1, S4P2). (Muyassar not in sweep NAME-PARITY stem list; rendered মুয়াসসার.)
- **al-Qurtubi (90)** — NAMED. Rests on: "وقيل: فرأوا السحاب. وقال ابن عباس: الزرع، وهو الأثر ... واصفرار الزرع بعد اخضراره يدل على يبسه، وكذا السحاب ... والريح على أنها لا تلقح"; and "لظلوا ... أي ليظلن ... لما في الكلام من معنى المجازاة ... قاله الخليل وغيره." Used for: the dispute over the referent of "hu" (cloud vs crop/Ibn Abbas's trace) and the three signs; and the past-for-future grammar of ẓallū on al-Khalīl's authority (S2P2, S4P1).
- **as-Sa'di (91)** — NAMED. Rests on: "ريحا مضرة متلفة أو منقصة ... قد تداعى إلى التلف ... فينسون النعم الماضية ويبادرون إلى الكفر." Used for: wind that harms/destroys/diminishes; the crop tumbled toward ruin; forgetting past blessings and rushing into kufr; the speed of the turn (S1P3, S2P1, S4P3, S8P1).
- **Ibn Kathir — ar (14) and en abridged (169)** — NAMED. Rests on: "ريحا يابسة على الزرع ... فرأوه مصفرا، أي قد اصفر وشرع في الفساد ... يكفرون، أي يجحدون ما تقدم إليهم من النعم"; cross-ref الواقعة 63-67. En file: "a wind which dries up the crops which they have tended ... they would become ungrateful, i.e., deny the previous blessings ... This is like the Ayah, 56:63-67." Used for: dry wind on matured crop; yakfurūn = denying prior blessings; reading 30:48–50 as one scene; the Wāqiʿa cross-reference (S1P1, S2P1, S3P2, S4P2, S5P3).
- **al-Baghawi (94)** — NAMED. Rests on: "ريحا باردة مضرة فأفسدت الزرع ... رأوا النبت والزرع مصفرا بعد الخضرة ... لظلوا، لصاروا ... يكفرون، يجحدون ما سلف من النعمة ... يفرحون عند الخصب، ولو أرسلت عذابا على زرعهم جحدوا سالف نعمتي." Used for: cold/harmful wind; yellowed-after-green; la-ẓallū = la-ṣārū (they would become); ingratitude for the earlier favour; the conditional "rejoice at abundance" heart (S2P1, S3P2, S4P1, S4P2, S5P1).
- **Ma'arif al-Qur'an — en (168)** — READ, NOT NAMED. The grouped text covers 30:47 only (vengeance on the guilty / help for the believers); it carries no comment bearing on 30:51, so nothing was attributed to it.

## Dropped (could not confirm / chose to omit)

- **The "eight winds" report** (Ibn Kathir 14, from Ibn Abi Hatim, ʿAbdullah b. ʿAmr: four mercy — nāshirāt, mubashshirāt, mursalāt, dhāriyāt; four punishment — ʿaqīm, ṣarṣar, ʿāṣif, qāṣif): DROPPED. It is a companion's statement (mawqūf), not a comment on this verse's wording, and adding it buys number-parity burden for no interpretive gain.
- **The marfūʿ hadith on the wind sent against ʿĀd** (Ibn Kathir 14, same chain): DROPPED. Ibn Kathir himself calls it gharīb and its rafʿ munkar ("هذا حديث غريب، ورفعه منكر"), judging it more likely the words of ʿAbdullah b. ʿAmr. A rejected marfūʿ is not written.
