# LEDGER 37:63

## SOURCES

- **at-Tabari (ar, tafsir 15)** — fetched (t15_63.txt). Named: glosses lil-ẓālimīn as li-hāʾulāʾi al-mushrikīn ("these very polytheists who said what they said"). Used in §1 (referent of the pronoun) and §8 (identity of the wrongdoers). Arabic clause: "يعني لهؤلاء المشركين الذين قالوا في ذلك ما قالوا".
- **al-Qurtubi (ar, tafsir 90)** — fetched (t90_63.txt). Named extensively: §1 (al-mushrikīn gloss), §2 (the Makkans' own objection, quoted "كيف تكون في النار شجرة وهي تحرق الشجر"), §4 (fitnah = al-ikhtibār, cross-ref to 74:31's "وما جعلنا عدتهم إلا فتنة للذين كفروا"), §5 (fitnah = al-ʿuqūbah, cross-ref to 51:14's "ذوقوا فتنتكم"), §6 (jahl verdict; the nineteen-guardians parallel at 74:30; "ليس بمستحيل في العقل" that Allah create a fire-proof tree-kind, parallel to chains/shackles/snakes/scorpions/keepers), §7 (the istibʿād recurring in people who reinterpret Paradise/Fire/Scale/Bridge/Pen — kept general, no group named, per watch-point).
- **al-Muyassar (ar, tafsir 16)** — fetched (t16_63.txt). Named in §2 (confirms the same "fire burns trees" objection) and §5 (zalimin = those who wronged themselves through kufr and sins — anfusahum framing).
- **as-Sa'di (ar, tafsir 91)** — fetched (t91_63.txt). Named in §5 and §8: glosses fitnah as "ʿadhāban wa nakālan" (punishment/exemplary chastisement) and lil-ẓālimīn as "anfusahum bi'l-kufri wa'l-maʿāṣī" (themselves, through disbelief and sins).
- **al-Baghawi (ar, tafsir 94)** — fetched (t94_63.txt). Named in §2 (same objection, "al-kāfirīn" gloss) and §3 (the Ibn al-Zibaʿrā / Abu Jahl "dates and butter" anecdote — servant-girl scene, "zaqqimīnā"/"tazaqqamū" quoted from the fetched text).
- **Ma'arif al-Qur'an (en, tafsir 168)** — fetched (t168_63.txt). Named in §3: the parallel Abu Jahl anecdote sourced by Ma'arif to ad-Durr al-Manthur — used to show the same episode reaches us through a second chain, not to duplicate the Baghawi version's wording.
- **Ibn Kathir (ar, tafsir 14)** — fetched (t14_63.txt). Named in §3 (Mujahid's report of Abu Jahl's own words, "إنما الزقوم التمر والزبد أتزقمه") and §4 (Qatadah's framing of fitnah as ikhtibār, and Ibn Kathir's own gloss tying this verse to 17:60's ruʾyā-fitnah).
- **Ibn Kathir (en, abridged, tafsir 169)** — fetched (t169_63.txt), grouped file covering 37:62-70. Used only for 37:63's portion: Qatadah's and Mujahid's reports (cross-checked against the Arabic in t14_63.txt) and the explicit 17:60 cross-reference quoted in English. The rest of the grouped file (37:64-70 details) was read for context but not used — future ground for those verses' own articles.

## Asbab an-nuzul
Not used as formal asbab. The Abu Jahl/Ibn al-Zibaʿrā anecdote functions as the occasion the mufassirūn record for why fitnah was the word chosen, but it is reported as tafsir narrative (Baghawi, Ma'arif/ad-Durr al-Manthur, Mujahid via Ibn Kathir), not confirmed on a quranx.com/Tafsirs al-Wahidi asbab page, so it is presented in the article as a tafsir report, not asserted as the formal occasion of revelation.
