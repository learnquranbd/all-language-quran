## Watch-points
- Which blast: Muyassar (second, of resurrection), al-Qurtubi (the last, for the raising), al-Baghawi (blowing of resurrection). Tabari and Ibn Kathir refer back without a number; Sa'di silent. No fetched text says first blast, so there is no disagreement to keep; the article says so explicitly (§3.2) and invents none.
- Wa'id: Tabari / Qurtubi / Baghawi (threat made to the disbelievers, carried out that day); Muqatil via Baghawi and Muyassar (the punishment itself / day of its occurrence); Sa'di (threat to the wrongdoers AND promise to the believers). Reported as a difference of emphasis, no position taken.
- Unseen: Horn described only by Muyassar's gloss "al-qarn" and the hadith wording. No angel named (Israfil not mentioned; the hadith says sahib al-qarn, rendered "the bearer of the Horn"). Ibn Kathir's faza'/sa'q/ba'th reported as his words, no elaboration. No Companion/Tabi'i report on the Horn used other than Muqatil's gloss on wa'id (attributed as his).
- 50:19 used as context only (§1); 50:21-22 not pulled forward (§8.1 says the following verses are their own subject). Headings checked against PREP (50:16, 50:37): no overlap.
- Licensing: the verse names no group, but the tafsirs name the disbelievers/wrongdoers and §6 cites 50:14 (destroyed peoples). Licensing sentence in EN and BN at §5.2 and §6.2.
- Surah-internal cross-refs 50:14, 50:28, 50:45 (wa'id recurrences): verified against data/quran-json/50.json (only these four verses carry وعيد in the surah). Labelled in the article as the article's own observation, not a commentator's reading.

## Hadith
- Jami' at-Tirmidhi 3243. URL https://quranx.com/hadith/Tirmidhi/Hadith-3243 (saved hadith_Tirmidhi_3243.txt). Abu Sa'id al-Khudri. First ten words (Arabic matn): "كَيْفَ أَنْعَمُ وَقَدِ الْتَقَمَ صَاحِبُ الْقَرْنِ الْقَرْنَ وَحَنَى جَبْهَتَهُ وَأَصْغَى". Grading: at-Tirmidhi "هذا حديث حسن" (hasan). Matn quoted whole in my rendering from the Arabic (quranx English omits "وأصغى سمعه", inclined his ear, which is kept). Sufyan's narrator note ("perhaps he said 'ala Allahi tawakkalna") omitted as a transmission remark, not matn. Chosen because its wording (حنى جبهته) matches Ibn Kathir's.
- Jami' at-Tirmidhi 2431. URL https://quranx.com/hadith/Tirmidhi/Hadith-2431 (saved). Same Companion, different chain (Khalid Abu l-'Ala' from 'Atiyya). First ten words: "كَيْفَ أَنْعَمُ وَصَاحِبُ الْقَرْنِ قَدِ الْتَقَمَ الْقَرْنَ وَاسْتَمَعَ الإِذْنَ مَتَى يُؤْمَرُ". Grading: "هذا حديث حسن". Mentioned only as a second record with the "weighed heavily on the Companions" line (§7.3); not quoted as wording.
- Attached to the verse by Ibn Kathir (ar and en). No hybrid: §7.2 quotes 3243 only; §7.1 paraphrases Ibn Kathir's own wording as his.
