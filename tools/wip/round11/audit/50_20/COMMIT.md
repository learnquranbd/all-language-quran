Card and 8-section bilingual article for 50:20: wa-nufikha fi s-sur
after the stupor of death in 50:19, which blowing is meant, and
dhalika yawmu l-wa'id. 50:21-22 left to their own entries.

Sources: Tabari, Ibn Kathir (ar; en abridged for 50:19 and the hadith
citation), Muyassar, Qurtubi, Sa'di, Baghawi. Ma'arif read, covers
only 50:19, not named.
- Sur: Muyassar's gloss al-qarn; Tabari, Qurtubi and Ibn Kathir refer
  back to earlier discussions, which are not supplied from memory.
- Which blowing: Muyassar (second, of resurrection), Qurtubi (the last,
  for the raising), Baghawi (of resurrection). No fetched text says
  first; no dispute recorded or invented.
- Wa'id: Tabari, Qurtubi, Baghawi (threat made to the disbelievers);
  Muqatil via Baghawi and Muyassar (the punishment, its occurrence);
  Sa'di (threat to the wrongdoers and promise to the believers on one
  day). Kept as a difference of emphasis, no position.
- Wa'id across Qaf (50:14, 50:28, 50:45) marked as own observation.
Hadith: Tirmidhi 3243, Abu Sa'id al-Khudri, "how can I be at ease"
with hasbuna Allahu wa ni'ma l-wakil, attached by Ibn Kathir; quoted
whole, graded hasan by at-Tirmidhi; 2431 noted, also hasan.
No asbab. Licensing sentence in both languages (§5, §6).
Dropped: Tirmidhi 2430 (not attached), the bearer's name, the earlier
Sur discussions, 50:19 and 50:21 material from the grouped texts.
