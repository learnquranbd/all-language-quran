Card and 8-section article on fa-l-muriyati qadha, two words, the
second oath of al-'Adiyat; 100:1 and 100:3 named as neighbours, 100:6
as the answer (Ibn Kathir en). Kanud left to the shipped 100:6 entry.

Sources: at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi, al-Baghawi, Ibn Kathir (ar + en
abridged), as-Sa'di, Muyassar. Slot check clean; at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi
and Ibn Kathir 100:1 slots fetched and saved for the oath-group dispute.
al-Qurtubi on qadh as istikhraj, miqdahah, qaddahah, wara az-zand.

Kept as disagreements, attributed, none chosen:
- hooves on rock ('Ikrimah, Qatadah, al-Kalbi, 'Ata', ad-Dahhak,
  Muqatil) vs camels' feet on pebbles (Ibn Mas'ud) vs stirring war
  (Qatadah) vs scheming (Ibn 'Abbas, Mujahid, Zayd b. Aslam) vs tongues
  ('Ikrimah) vs gathered fire (Muhammad b. Ka'b).
- horses (Ibn 'Abbas, Mujahid et al.; at-Tabari's choice on 100:1) vs
  camels of Hajj ('Ali, Ibn Mas'ud, Ibrahim, 'Ubayd b. 'Umayr et al.).
- at-Tabari 100:2: every striker of fire included; al-Qurtubi: literal
  horses, the rest majaz; Ibn Kathir reports Ibn Jarir as choosing the
  first, differing from the fetched at-Tabari text. Both shown.
Zamzam report ('Ali and Ibn 'Abbas) with at-Tabari's chain, no grading
in the texts; Ibn Kathir's short version and al-Qurtubi's ash-Sha'bi
version noted.

Hadith: none; no fetched tafsir attaches one to 100:2 (stated).
Dropped: Qurtubi's ungraded sariyyah asbab, unsourced khabar on the
raider's horse, likely misprint in Ibn Kathir ar (bi-l-Muzdalifah).
Licensing sentence in both languages; war readings framed as reports.
