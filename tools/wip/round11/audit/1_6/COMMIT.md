Replaces the 740-word, 6-section article with a 1,777-word, 9-section
one, sourced from at-Tabari, Ibn Kathir, al-Qurtubi, al-Baghawi, as-Sa'di,
al-Muyassar and Ma'arif on 1:6 (plus Ibn Kathir, as-Sa'di and al-Muyassar
on 1:7 for the context section). The shipped card is kept.

Why the guided still ask: Ibn Kathir (firmness, rootedness, insight,
increase, continuance; 4:136, 3:8, Abu Bakr in Maghrib), al-Baghawi (Ali
and Ubayy: "make us firm"; firmness and more), as-Sa'di (guidance to the
path and within it). Disagreement kept: at-Tabari reads ihdina as tawfiq
for steadfastness in the years ahead and folds "increase" back into it.
The sirat: Islam, the Book of Allah, the truth, the Prophet and his two
companions, the road to Paradise, the Sunnah, Hajj (al-Qurtubi: too
narrow), each with its source; Ibn Kathir's and at-Tabari's own
reconciliations. 1:7 kept to one section with the licensing sentence.
Hadith: Tirmidhi 2859 (an-Nawwas, parable of the path), graded hasan
gharib; Tirmidhi 2954 ('Adi ibn Hatim), hasan gharib per 2953. Ibn
Mas'ud's drawn line could not be confirmed and is dropped. No asbab.

Corrected from the old article: as-Sa'di's "knowing and acting on the
truth" defines the sirat, not hidayah; "guidance in each particular
matter" is as-Sa'di's, not Ibn Kathir's. Dropped as unsourced: sirat as
a broad made road, the definite-article and plural-byways points.
