Card and deep bilingual article on the epistemic indictment of shirk:
worship resting on neither a warrant sent down (sultan) nor knowledge (ilm).

Sources gave a rare full agreement. Ibn Kathir, al-Qurtubi and al-Baghawi
gloss sultan as hujjah wa burhan, a decisive proof; at-Tabari sharpens it to a
proof sent down from heaven in a revealed Book to the messengers, naming the
object as a god fit for worship. On ilm, Ibn Kathir and al-Baghawi read plain
ignorance, not knowledge; as-Sa'di reads taqlid from straying fathers and adds
the distinctive second edge: Allah sent down no warrant FOR their worship and
sent down the decisive proofs AGAINST it, making their state the ugliest.
al-Muyassar frames the whole line as Quraysh persisting despite plain falsity.
Ibn Kathir and al-Qurtubi cross-refer (23:117; Al Imran).

No disagreement to keep — the article notes the commentators' unusual unanimity.

Hadith: no tafsir attaches one to the verse, stated plainly. Sahih al-Bukhari
2856 (Mu'adh, Allah's right is that they worship Him and associate nothing)
used as a GENERAL tawhid narration, explicitly not the occasion. PREP's
suggested Bukhari 660 (the seven shaded) fetched and rejected as off-topic.

Differentiated from shipped 22:73: no fly parable, no powerlessness-of-idols
angle; spine is authority-plus-knowledge only.

Dropped: Ma'arif (grouped excerpt did not reach a 22:71 comment); the
