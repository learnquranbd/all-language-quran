Replaces the 744-word, 5-section article with a 1,678-word, 9-section
one, sourced from at-Tabari, Ibn Kathir, al-Qurtubi, al-Baghawi, as-Sa'di,
al-Muyassar and Ma'arif on 2:155, 2:156 and 2:157. The shipped card is kept.

The trials: the sworn verb (al-Baghawi), the qiblah test (at-Tabari),
why it is told in advance (al-Qurtubi, as-Sa'di, Ma'arif), what testing
sifts, shay' as "something of each" (at-Tabari's grammar, ad-Dahhak's
plural reading) and the five trials glossed source by source.
Disagreement kept: ash-Shafi'i's inward reading (fasting, zakat, illness,
children as the fruit of the heart; al-Baghawi, al-Qurtubi) against Ibn
'Abbas, with Ibn Kathir's «وفي هذا نظر». Salawat kept as four readings:
forgiveness (at-Tabari), praise (Ibn Kathir, al-Muyassar, as-Sa'di),
mercy (al-Baghawi), all together (al-Qurtubi). 'Umar's two loads and the
extra attributed to the three tafsirs that carry it.
Hadith: Muslim 918 (Umm Salamah, 918b quoted whole; 918a reported apart);
Bukhari 1283 (patience at the first blow); Tirmidhi 1021 (House of
Praise), graded hasan gharib; Bukhari 1497 as at-Tabari's evidence.
Dropped: at-Tabari 2329 (weak per its footnote), Ibn Majah's renewed
istirja' (no grading), 'Ikrimah's lamp report, Sa'id ibn Jubayr on
Ya'qub (AS), al-Baghawi's general trial narrations. No asbab.

Corrected from the old article: "agreed-upon" (only al-Bukhari
confirmed); Umm Salamah's report was a blend of two Muslim wordings;
"patience called guidance" is as-Sa'di's alone. Dropped as unsourced:
the Madinan-setting sentence, "sized to capacity", "earnings", bushra as
victory vocabulary, the 39:10 and 64:11 claims.
