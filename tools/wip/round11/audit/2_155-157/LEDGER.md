## Disagreements kept (both names, no position)
- The five trials: Ibn Abbas (enemy, famine, killing/death in jihad, scant growth) vs the reading ash-Shafi'i (al-Baghawi «حكي عن», al-Qurtubi «قال»), which Ibn Kathir reports from "some commentators" and calls «وفي هذا نظر». Fruits = children is attributed only to that reading.
- Salawat: forgiveness (at-Tabari) / praise (Ibn Kathir, al-Muyassar, as-Sa'di) / mercy (al-Baghawi) / pardon, mercy, blessing and honour together (al-Qurtubi).
- Muhtadun: al-Baghawi's three readings set beside at-Tabari's and as-Sa'di's.
- ad-Dahhak's reading «بأشياء» vs the majority's singular (al-Qurtubi).

- **Tirmidhi 1021**, https://quranx.com/hadith/Tirmidhi/Hadith-1021, saved. Abu Musa via Abu Sinan and Abu Talhah al-Khawlani. First words: «إذا مات ولد العبد قال الله لملائكته قبضتم ولد». ## Dropped (unconfirmed, weak, or cut)
- At-Tabari 2329, "whoever says the istirja' at a calamity, Allah mends his calamity ...": Shakir's footnote quotes al-Haythami («وفيه علي بن أبي طلحة، وهو ضعيف») and notes the chain is broken. Not used.
- Ibn Majah / Ahmad, al-Husayn: renewed istirja' for an old calamity earns its reward (al-Qurtubi, Ibn Kathir): not fetched, no collector's grading. Not used.
- 'Ikrimah's lamp report ("everything that harms the believer is a calamity") and al-Qurtubi's Muslim parallel (Abu Sa'id/Abu Hurayrah): not confirmed on a fetched page. Only al-Qurtubi's definition of musibah is used.
- 'Ata' (via Abu 'Umar): "remember your calamity in me": not used (unconnected chain as al-Qurtubi gives it).
- Sa'id ibn Jubayr on Ya'qub (AS) and «يا أسفى على يوسف» (at-Tabari, al-Baghawi, al-Qurtubi): cut; it reads too easily as a fault in a prophet and is not needed.
- Sa'id ibn Jubayr "safety from punishment" (Ibn Kathir), az-Zajjaj (al-Qurtubi), "mercy = removal of distress" (al-Qurtubi), Ibn Abbas's "three qualities" (at-Tabari 2329's first half), Ibn Kathir's 16:112 «لباس الجوع والخوف», Ma'arif on why war leads the passage and on reward varying with the quality of patience, Sahl at-Tustari/Ruwaym/Dhun-Nun definitions: confirmed, cut for length.
- Al-Baghawi's string of hadith on 2:157 (Abu Hurayrah «من يرد الله به خيرا يصب منه», Sa'd «أشد الناس بلاء», Anas «إذا أحب قوما ابتلاهم», the plant and cedar parable, «عجب للمؤمن»): not used. They are general narrations not checked here; the "trial means love" line also runs against the brief's rule not to read affliction or comfort as a sign of Allah's attitude; «عجب للمؤمن» belongs to 2:153.
- Ibn Kathir «ومن قنط أحل [الله] به عقابه» and as-Sa'di's "whoever is not patient gets the opposite: blame, punishment, misguidance": not used (kept to what the verse says of the patient).
