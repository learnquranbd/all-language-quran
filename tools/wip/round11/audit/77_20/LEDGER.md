Differences kept as differences (no position taken):
- mahin: weak only (Tabari, Ibn 'Abbas) / weak and lowly (Qurtubi, Muyassar, Ibn Kathir with "in relation to the Creator's power") / utmost lowliness only (Sa'di) / no gloss (Baghawi). Translations: PREP EN "disdained", Ibn Kathir en "despised", PREP BN "নগণ্য".
- Addressee: O people (Tabari), O children of Adam (Sa'di), O company of disbelievers (Muyassar).
- 77:22-23 (context only): six to nine months / measure (Ibn Kathir en) vs time known to Allah / power (Muyassar).
- Qurtubi's man's-water-alone report vs Ibn Kathir en's "fluid of the man and the woman" on 77:21: both reported as theirs; explicit disclaimer that the article makes no embryology claim.
