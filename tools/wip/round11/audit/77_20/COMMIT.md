Card and 8-section bilingual article for 77:20, alam nakhluqkum min ma'in
mahin: the turn from destroyed peoples (77:16-18) to the listener's own
making; 77:21-23 as context only, as the texts frame them.

Sources: Tabari, Ibn Kathir (ar; en abridged grouped 77:16-25), Qurtubi,
Sa'di, Muyassar, Baghawi. Ma'arif (grouped 77:17-24) has nothing on 77:20.
- mahin kept as the commentators split it: weak only (Tabari, with Ibn
  'Abbas's chain), weak and lowly (Qurtubi, Muyassar), utmost lowliness
  (Sa'di), weak and lowly "in relation to the Creator's power" (Ibn
  Kathir); Baghawi only names the nutfa. Translations disdained/despised/
  নগণ্য set against these.
- Purpose only as Ibn Kathir states it: favour (imtinan) and argument for
  the return from the beginning; Sa'di's 86:7 wording noted, no comment.
- Addressee kept as a difference: O people (Tabari), O children of Adam
  (Sa'di), O disbelievers (Muyassar). Licensing sentence in EN and BN.
- 77:22-23 context: six to nine months / measure (Ibn Kathir en) vs known
  to Allah / power (Muyassar), unsettled. Qurtubi's man's-water-alone
  report and Ibn Kathir en's man-and-woman line both attributed; article
  makes no embryology claim.
Hadith: Ibn Majah 2707 (Busr bin Jahhash, palm and throat), attached by
Ibn Kathir, quoted whole from quranx; no grading by Ibn Majah or on the
page, none added.
Asbab: none in the sources.
Dropped: taqrir label (no source uses it), 17:70 cross-reference, tajwid
note on nakhluqkum.
