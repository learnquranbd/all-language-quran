Card + 8-section bilingual article (1,711 EN words) for 37:19, "it will be
only one zajra, and at once they will be looking."

Sources: al-Qurtubi, as-Sa'di, al-Baghawi, Ibn Kathir (ar + en),
al-Muyassar, Ma'arif al-Qur'an, each quoted from the text fetched for 37:19.
at-Tabari not used (fetch failed).
- Placement as the "how" of 37:18's yes to 37:16-17 (Ma'arif); referent of
  hiya = the raising or the Resurrection (Baghawi).
- Zajra as a driving/herding shout, "ka-zajri al-ibil wa'l-khayl 'inda
  al-sawq" (Qurtubi); cattle made to rise and move (Ma'arif). Named apart
  from sayha (36:29, 36:49, 36:53) and nufikha fi al-sur (36:51), vocabulary
  only; 36:51's content not rebuilt.
- Second blowing via al-Hasan (Qurtubi), nafkhat al-ba'th (Baghawi), Israfil
  in the Horn (Sa'di, Ma'arif); Ibn Kathir's "single command"; Ma'arif citing
  Tafsir Kabir on the Horn blown for awe.
- Yanzurun kept as a named range, no position: look at each other / wait to
  see what is done with them / the staring of 21:97 (Qurtubi quotes it
  unreferenced; verse located in repo text) / see the raising they denied;
  plus Ibn Kathir and Muyassar (horrors), Ma'arif (sight as in the world),
  Baghawi (alive).
- As-Sa'di: raised as first created, hufatan 'uratan ghurlan, remorse.
- Hadith: Bukhari 6527 ('Aisha) quoted whole, matching Sa'di's three words;
  Muslim 2859 noted; Bukhari 3349 (Ibn 'Abbas, 21:104) noted. All flagged
  general, not attached to 37:19. Stock Bukhari 660 not used.
- Asbab: none. Licensing guard EN+BN on the deniers.
- 37:20-21 left as future ground.
