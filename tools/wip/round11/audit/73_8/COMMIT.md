Replaces the 725-word, 5-section article with a 1,758-word, 8-section deep article, built only on tafsir fetched for 73:8 (shipped card kept).

Sources: at-Tabari, Ibn Kathir (ar; en abridged for 73:1-9), al-Qurtubi,
as-Sa'di, al-Baghawi, al-Muyassar (73:8-9), Ma'arif al-Qur'an.

Remember the name: call upon Him by it (at-Tabari, Muyassar), by tawhid and
glorification (al-Baghawi), every kind of dhikr (as-Sa'di), much dhikr (Ibn
Kathir); al-Qurtubi's beautiful names, sincere aim, Sahl's basmalah, promise and
threat, and al-Kalbi's "pray by day", which he approves via 25:62. Ma'arif on
constancy (tongue, heart, body) and its name-repetition view, with the dissent
it reports; no side taken.

Tabattul: root b-t-l as cutting (al-Qurtubi, al-Baghawi), al-Batul, mutabattil,
Ibn Arafah's "singling out" vs al-Qurtubi's preference. Readings held by name,
none ranked: sincerity (Ibn Abbas, Mujahid and others; Mujahid's asking and du'a;
Qatadah), striving (al-Hasan), free time with 94:7-8 (Ibn Zayd, Ibn Kathir),
reliance (Sufyan), rejecting the world (Zayd ibn Aslam / Ibn Zayd via Ma'arif).
Tabtilan not tabattulan: al-Qurtubi (meaning plus rhyme), al-Baghawi (taf'il).
Commanded vs forbidden tabattul: Ibn al-Arabi via al-Qurtubi (98:5, 5:87),
Ma'arif (57:27). 73:7 and 73:9 from Ibn Kathir and Muyassar.

Hadith: Bukhari 5073 ('Uthman ibn Maz'un), to which at-Tabari, Ibn Kathir and
al-Qurtubi allude without a source; Muslim 373 ('A'ishah), quoted by Ma'arif.
Asbab: none.

Fixed from the old article: sabh as "a long swim" (sources: free time, sleep);
"the verbal form doubles the emphasis" (al-Qurtubi gives meaning and rhyme);
Bukhari 5073 called agreed upon (only Bukhari confirmed). Dropped: 76:25, the
"most constant deeds" hadith, armies and screens. Dropped from Ma'arif: the
unconfirmed "no monasticism in Islam" narration.
