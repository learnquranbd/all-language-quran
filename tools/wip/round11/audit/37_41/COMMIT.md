Card + 8-section bilingual article (1,639 EN words) on 37:41, the first
word on the reward of the sincere servants excepted in 37:40: "those
will have a known provision".

Sources (all fetched for 37:41): at-Tabari (own gloss: the fruits Allah
created for them in Paradise; Qatada and as-Suddi: "in Paradise"), Ibn
Kathir ar + abridged en (Qatada/as-Suddi: "means Paradise"; group note
on 37:40 — no reckoning, evil deeds overlooked), al-Qurtubi ("a known
gift that does not cease"; Muqatil "when they desire it"; Ibn al-Sa'ib
"morning and evening" with the bukratan wa 'ashiyya verse), al-Muyassar
(does not cease), as-Sa'di (not unknown, yet its depth unreachable),
al-Baghawi (morning and evening, Maryam 62), Ma'arif al-Qur'an (three
readings; Thanawi's choice of the "already described" reading).

Range kept open: content (Paradise / its provision / its fruits),
permanence, availability on desire, morning-and-evening timing, and
as-Sa'di's known-yet-beyond-grasp — no position taken. The "in Paradise"
vs "means Paradise" difference between at-Tabari's and Ibn Kathir's
transmission of Qatada/as-Suddi noted.

Cross-reference 19:62 verified against data/quran-tokens.json (wording
match), and named by al-Baghawi and Ma'arif; al-Qurtubi gives no number.

Hadith: none attached by any fetched tafsir; said once. No stock 660.

Humility: article stresses ula'ika points to a described group and the
reader is to ask to be among them, not assume it.

Kept out: 37:42-47 content (named as next verses only). No asbab.
Defect fixed in draft: Ibn al-Sa'ib's ghadāh rendered "morning", not "meal".
