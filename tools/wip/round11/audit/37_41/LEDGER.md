# SOURCES ledger — 37:41 (ulā'ika lahum rizqun ma'lūm)

## Tafsir fetched (all on 37:41; t169 grouped 37:38-47) — all named
- t15 at-Tabari — own gloss: "وذلك الرزق المعلوم: هو الفواكه التي خلقها الله لهم في الجنة";
  Qatada (via Bishr < Yazid < Sa'id): "في الجنة"; as-Suddi (via ... Asbat): "قال: في الجنة". §3.1, §7.1
- t14 Ibn Kathir (ar) — "قال قتادة ، والسدي : يعني الجنة". §3.2 (noted as differing in form from Tabari's "fi al-janna")
- t169 Ibn Kathir (en, abridged, grouped 37:38-47) — "Qatadah and As-Suddi said, 'This means Paradise.' It is
  explained further in the next Ayah: (Fruits) meaning, of different kinds"; on 37:40 (group): "they will not taste
  the painful torment nor will they be brought to account. Their evil acts, if there are any, will be overlooked";
  comparison with 103:1-3, 95:4-6. §1.2, §3.2, §8.1 (group named in §1.2)
- t90 al-Qurtubi — "أي : لهم عطية معلومة لا تنقطع" (§4.1); "قال قتادة : يعني الجنة . وقال غيره : يعني رزق الجنة .
  وقيل : هي الفواكه التي ذكر" (§3.3); "قال مقاتل : حين يشتهونه" (§5.1); "وقال ابن السائب : إنه بمقدار الغداة
  والعشي ، قال الله تعالى : ولهم رزقهم فيها بكرة وعشيا" (§5.2). Qurtubi gives NO verse number.
- t16 al-Muyassar — "أولئك المخلصون لهم في الجنة رزق معلوم لا ينقطع". §4.1
- t91 as-Sa'di — "أي: غير مجهول، وإنما هو رزق عظيم جليل، لا يجهل أمره، ولا يبلغ كنهه". §6.1, §6.3
- t94 al-Baghawi — "يعني: بكرة وعشياً [كما قال: ولهم رزقهم فيها بكرةً وعشياً (مريم-62)]". §5.2
- t168 Ma'arif (en) — three readings: (1) detailed descriptions across surahs, "Maulana Ashraf Thanavi has elected
  to go by this very Tafsir" (§6.2); (2) timings determined, morning and evening, Maryam 19:62 (§5.3);
  (3) certain and everlasting vs this world's uncertainty, "(Qurtubi, and others)" (§4.2, §8.2). Also
  "first ten verses describe the comforts... then an event relating to a particular inmate of Jannah" (§1.2).

## Cross-reference verification
- 19:62: Qurtubi's quoted wording "ولهم رزقهم فيها بكرة وعشيا" checked against data/quran-tokens.json
  (diacritic-stripped search for بكرة + عشيا → only 19:11 and 19:62; 19:62 tokens end
  "ولهم رزقهم فيها بكرة وعشيا"). Number also given independently by al-Baghawi (Maryam-62) and Ma'arif (19:62).
  Article says Qurtubi cites the wording; the number is attributed to the Qur'an text match and to al-Baghawi.

## Dropped / not used
- mukhlaṣīn vs mukhliṣīn qira'at and its meaning (from memory, not in the 37:41 texts) — dropped.
- Any claim about the rhetorical effect of fronting "lahum" (not sourced) — not made; §2.1 only describes the words.
- Ibn al-Sa'ib's "al-ghadāh wa al-'ashiyy" first drafted as "morning meal and evening meal" — corrected to
  "morning and evening" (ghadāh = morning, not ghadā' = meal).
- Ibn Kathir's (group) "good deeds multiplied ten to seven hundred" — not needed, omitted.
- 37:42-47 content (fruits, honoured, couches, cup, spring, no ghawl) and Ibn Kathir's material on it — kept
  as future ground; 37:42 named only as the next verse / that it opens with fawākih.
- Thanawi's full name: source says "Maulana Ashraf Thanavi"; Bengali mirrors it (no added "Ali").
