Replaces the 731-word, 5-section article with a 9-section, 1,785-word
source-gated article (shipped card kept).

Sources: at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi, as-Sa'di, Ibn Kathir (ar; en abridged,
grouped 59:18-20), al-Baghawi, Muyassar, Ma'arif. The turn to the
believers via Ma'arif; taqwa glosses (Ibn Kathir, at-Tabari, as-Sa'di).
Ghad as the Day of Resurrection (al-Qurtubi; Qatada, ad-Dahhak, Ibn Zayd
via at-Tabari, Ibn Zayd with 10:24); al-Qurtubi's two reasons and the
poet Qurar ibn Ajda'; Ma'arif's three points and two phases of the Hour.
Singular nafs (at-Tabari, al-Baghawi, Muyassar); good and evil sent
ahead; Ma'arif's bank image. "Reckon yourselves before you are
reckoned" as Ibn Kathir's own gloss; as-Sa'di's asl of muhasaba, his
three steps and haya'. 59:19 glosses (at-Tabari, Sufyan, Muyassar,
al-Baghawi, Ibn Kathir's recompense-of-the-kind, as-Sa'di); fasiq as
going out, with 63:9 (Ibn Kathir). Licensing sentence in both languages.
Disagreements kept: repeated taqwa as emphasis (Ibn Kathir, al-Qurtubi)
vs distinct objects (al-Qurtubi's qila, Thanawi via Ma'arif, Ma'arif's
counterfeit deeds); ansahum as the self's share, by punishment (Ibn
'Isa) or at repentance (Sahl); fasiqun as disobedient or liars.

Hadith: Muslim 1017a (Jarir, the Mudar sermon reciting 59:18, "even if
it be half a date"), attached by Ibn Kathir, quoted whole.

Dropped: 'Umar attribution of the muhasaba saying (no fetched text names
him), Ma'arif's unsourced traditions, Abu Bakr's sermon (length); from
the old article 9:67, the nightly-review and two-ledger claims. Fixed
its attributions to "the commentators" of the despair/self-congratulation
reading of the repeated taqwa and of 59:19's converse (no source has
them).
