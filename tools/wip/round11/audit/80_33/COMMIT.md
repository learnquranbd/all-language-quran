Card and 8-section article on fa-idha ja'ati as-sakhkha (3 Arabic words),
the turn from the provision list of 80:24-32 to the Day.

Sources: at-Tabari, Ibn Kathir, al-Qurtubi, as-Sa'di, al-Baghawi, the
Muyassar and Ma'arif al-Qur'an, all fetched for 80:33. Ibn Kathir abridged
(en) used only for its passage heading over 80:33-42.

Root sad-kha-kha checked in morphology data: only here in the Qur'an;
sakhra/sakhr (18:63, 31:16, 89:9) named as a different root.
Glosses kept side by side: a name of the Day (Ibn Abbas via at-Tabari and
Ibn Kathir); perhaps the Trumpet blowing (at-Tabari); the blowing
(Ma'arif); the second blowing (al-Qurtubi); the cry of the Resurrection
(al-Baghawi, Muyassar, as-Sa'di). None chosen.
Derivation disagreement kept: deafen vs listen intently, with al-Qurtubi's
reported verdict for the first; mismatch between at-Tabari's own text and
al-Qurtubi's quote of him recorded, not resolved.
al-Qurtubi's order (livelihood, then the return, to provision with deeds
and spending) and Ibn al-Arabi's deafens-yet-makes-hear used.
idha: no source names the answering clause; Muyassar's run-on into
80:34-37 reported. 80:34-37 named only. 79:34 parallel opening noted
from the text.

Hadith: none attached by any fetched tafsir. Bukhari 4935 and Tirmidhi
2457 cross-referenced to the 78:18 and 79:7 articles by number. al-Qurtubi's
Friday narration (Abu Dawud 1046, ungraded on page, spelled with sin)
mentioned as lexical only, not quoted. Asbab: none; read by placement.
No group addressed, so no licensing sentence.
