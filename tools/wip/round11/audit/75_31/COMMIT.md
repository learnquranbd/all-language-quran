Card and 8-section article for 75:31: fa-la saddaqa wa la salla, the
record read after the deathbed of 75:26-30 (Ma'arif; IK's kalla).

Sources: Tabari, Ibn Kathir (ar + en abridged), Muyassar, Qurtubi, Sa'di,
Baghawi, Ma'arif (grouped 75:26-33).
- Grammar of la + past kept open: al-Kisa'i (must be paired) vs
  al-Akhfash (no condition; 90:11, la dhahaba, Zuhayr), via Qurtubi.
- Saddaqa: affirm the Message (Ibn Abbas via Qurtubi), the Book of Allah
  (Tabari; Qatadah via Qurtubi), the Qur'an (Baghawi), Messenger and
  Qur'an (Muyassar), the six articles (Sa'di). Qurtubi's unnamed "qila"
  reading (gave nothing of his wealth, sadaqa) kept as a minority view,
  nothing built on it.
- Heart and frame: IK's qalb/qalib, "no good in him inwardly or
  outwardly"; Qurtubi's heart/body view; Tabari carried into 75:32.
- Salla: Tabari, Qatadah, Muyassar (fara'id), Qurtubi's wider gloss.
- Identity kept open: Abu Jahl (Ibn Abbas via Qurtubi; Baghawi) vs
  al-insan as a generic noun (Qurtubi); others general. No chain in the
  fetched text, so a reported view, not asbab. IK's Abu Jahl reports
  (Nasa'i via Sa'id b. Jubayr; Qatadah via Ibn Abi Hatim, "they claim")
  placed on 75:34-35, no grading in the text. Licensing sentence in both
  languages.
Hadith: no tafsir attaches one (said in one sentence). Review fix: cut
Muslim 82 (abandoning salat), unattached and takfir-adjacent here.
Dropped: Tabari's truncated list of interpreters, a 75:5 link of my own,
death-angel imagery, IK's al-Bara' narration on 75:30.
