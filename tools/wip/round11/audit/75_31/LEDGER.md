# SOURCES ledger — 75:31

## Tafsir fetched (all from PREP.md; none re-fetched)

## Attributions and the clause each rests on
- Ma'arif (§1.2), deathbed: "At this juncture neither is his repentance acceptable nor a righteous deed. Therefore, it is necessary for a wise person to make amends before this moment arrives."
- al-Qurtubi, al-Kisa'i (§2.1): "قال الكسائي : لا بمعنى لم ولكنه يقرن بغيره ; تقول العرب : لا عبد الله خارج ولا فلان ، ولا تقول : مررت برجل لا محسن حتى يقال ولا مجمل"
- al-Qurtubi, 90:11 set apart (§2.2; worded as "the passage, just after al-Kisa'i's words", since it is unclear whether the sentence is al-Kisa'i's or al-Qurtubi's): "وقوله تعالى : فلا اقتحم العقبة ليس من هذا القبيل ; لأن معناه أفلا اقتحم ; أي فهلا اقتحم ، فحذف ألف الاستفهام"
- al-Qurtubi, al-Akhfash (§2.2): "وقال الأخفش : فلا صدق أي لم يصدق ; كقوله : فلا اقتحم أي لم يقتحم ، ولم يشترط أن يعقبه بشيء آخر ، والعرب تقول : لا ذهب ، أي لم يذهب ، فحرف النفي ينفي الماضي كما ينفي المستقبل ; ومنه قول زهير"
- at-Tabari, object of saddaqa / salla (§3.1, §5.1): "فلم يصدّق بكتاب الله، ولم يصلّ له صلاة"
- at-Tabari, carried into 75:32 (§4.2): "ولكنه كذّب بكتاب الله، وتولى فأدبر عن طاعة الله"
- al-Baghawi (§3.1, §6.1): "يعني: أبا جهل، لم يصدق بالقرآن ولا صلى لله"
- al-Qurtubi, Ibn Abbas line (§3.1, §5.1, §6.1): "أي لم يصدق أبو جهل ولم يصل . ... والأول قول ابن عباس . أي لم يصدق بالرسالة ولا صلى ودعا لربه ، وصلى على رسوله"
- al-Qurtubi, Qatadah (§3.1, §5.1): "وقال قتادة : فلا صدق بكتاب الله ، ولا صلى لله"
- al-Qurtubi, charity reading, unnamed (§3.2, §5.1): "وقيل : ولا صدق بمال له ، ذخرا له عند الله ، ولا صلى الصلوات التي أمره الله بها"
- al-Qurtubi, heart/body (§4.1): "وقيل : فلا آمن بقلبه ولا عمل ببدنه"
- al-Qurtubi, generic reading (§6.1): "وقيل : يرجع هذا إلى الإنسان في أول السورة ، وهو اسم جنس"
- al-Muyassar (§3.1, §5.1, §6.2): "فلا آمن الكافر بالرسول والقرآن، ولا أدَّى لله تعالى فرائض الصلاة"
- as-Sa'di, six articles (§3.1): "لا آمن بالله وملائكته وكتبه ورسله واليوم الآخر والقدر خيره وشره"
- Ibn Kathir (ar), heart/frame (§4.1, §4.2, §6.2, §8.2): "هذا إخبار عن الكافر الذي كان في الدار الدنيا مكذبا للحق بقلبه متوليا عن العمل بقالبه فلا خير فيه باطنا ولا ظاهرا"
- Ibn Kathir (en), opening prayer and kalla-as-rebuke on 75:26 (§7.1): "May Allah make us firm at that time with the Firm Statement." / "If we make the word 'Kalla' negative ... `O son of Adam! You are not able to deny that which I informed you of at that time (death). This will become something witnessed by you with your own eyes.'"
- Ibn Kathir (en), Abu Jahl reports on 75:34-35 (§6.2): "Abu `Abdur-Rahman An-Nasa'i recorded from Sa`id bin Jubayr ... `The Messenger of Allah said this to Abu Jahl, then Allah, the Mighty and Sublime, revealed this Ayah.'" and "Ibn Abi Hatim recorded from Qatadah ... They claim that the Prophet of Allah grabbed the clothes of the enemy of Allah, Abu Jahl." No grading given in the fetched text for either; the article says so and places both on 75:34-35, not on 75:31.

## Disagreements kept (no position taken)
- al-Kisa'i (la must be paired) vs al-Akhfash (no pairing required); 90:11 treated as a case apart.
- Object of saddaqa: Message (Ibn Abbas via Qurtubi), Book of Allah (Tabari; Qatadah via Qurtubi), Qur'an (Baghawi), Messenger and Qur'an (Muyassar), six articles (Sa'di). Charity reading (Qurtubi, "qila", unnamed) kept as a recorded minority view; no other fetched mufassir adopts it.
- Identity: Abu Jahl (Ibn Abbas via Qurtubi; Baghawi) vs al-insan as generic noun (Qurtubi, "qila"); Tabari, Ibn Kathir, Muyassar keep it general. No chain for the identification in the fetched text; stated as a reported view, not established asbab.

## Hadith
- Sahih Muslim 82 (82b wording), https://quranx.com/hadith/Muslim/Hadith-82 (saved: hadith_Muslim_82.txt).
  First ten words used: "Between man and polytheism and unbelief is the abandonment of salat." (whole matn, 82b English).
  Grading: in Sahih Muslim; no upgrade. Marked in the article as a general narration NOT attached to the verse, with a sentence that it rules on no person.
- One sentence states that no fetched tafsir attaches a hadith to 75:31.
- Abu Jahl reports (an-Nasa'i via Sa'id b. Jubayr; Qatadah via Ibn Abi Hatim) used only as reported by Ibn Kathir on 75:34-35; no number, no grading claimed; Nasa'i number not fetched.

## Asbab
- None established for 75:31. Abu Jahl identification given as a reported view (Qurtubi: "qawl Ibn Abbas"; Baghawi), explicitly not as an established occasion of revelation. Licensing sentence in EN and BN (§6.3).
