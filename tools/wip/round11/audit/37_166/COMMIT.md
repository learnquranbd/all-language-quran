Card and 8-section bilingual article for 37:166, the third sentence of the angels'
speech in 37:164-166 and their answer to the "daughters of Allah" claim.

Sources: Tabari, Qurtubi, Ibn Kathir (ar/en), Baghawi, Sa'di, Muyassar (Ma'arif fetched,
nothing on 37:166).
- Speakers: Ibn Abbas and Mujahid (Ibn Kathir, Tabari), Qatada, Muyassar: the angels;
  Baghawi: Jibril telling the Prophet that they worship and are not worshipped.
- al-musabbihun kept as a disagreement: al-musallun (Qatada, Tabari, Qurtubi's first) vs
  al-munazzihun (Qurtubi's wa-qila, Sa'di, Muyassar); Baghawi joins both, Qurtubi's summary
  names both. No side taken.
- Qurtubi: they are not worshipped and not daughters of Allah; Sa'di: how could they be
  partners? Tied to 37:150, 37:153, 37:158 by number only.
- Ibn Kathir: servants, in need of Him, humbled; his parallel 21:26-29 (text checked).
Hadith: Sahih Muslim 522 (Hudhayfah, rows like the angels' rows), quoted whole, marked as
belonging to 37:165's lining up. Tabari's 'A'ishah report via ad-Dahhak/Masruq given
ungraded with its chain, not found in the six collections; Ibn Mas'ud marked as a
Companion's statement; Umar's iqamah exhortation via Abu Nadrah, ungraded. No asbab.
Dropped: Muslim 430 (not attached, not fetched); Ibn Jubayr's 37:1 link (belongs to 37:165);
grammar claims on the emphasis.
Fixed: brief listed Baghawi as al-musallun only; his text joins both readings.
Themes: tawheed, remembrance.
