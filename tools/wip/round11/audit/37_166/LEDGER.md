Disagreement kept: al-musabbihun = al-musallun (Qatada; at-Tabari; al-Qurtubi's first) vs al-munazzihun (al-Qurtubi's wa-qila; as-Sa'di; Muyassar). Noted, without taking a side, that al-Baghawi joins both and al-Qurtubi's summary names both. Speaker framing: angels speak (Ibn 'Abbas, Mujahid, Qatada, Muyassar) vs Jibril relating it (al-Baghawi); both say the "we" are angels.
Watch-point correction: the brief listed al-Baghawi under "al-musallun"; his text is "al-musallun al-munazzihun", both together, and the article says so.

## Hadith
1. Sahih Muslim 522 (522a), Hudhayfah. URL: https://quranx.com/hadith/Muslim/Hadith-522 (saved hadith_Muslim_522.txt). First ten words used: "We have been made to excel (other) people in three". Quoted whole in quranx English, including "And he mentioned another characteristic too". Grading: in Muslim's Sahih; no other grade claimed. Marked in the article as belonging to the lining up of 37:165, used only to show the angels' ranks as the pattern.
2. 'A'ishah via Masruq: at-Tabari's transmission only (chain Muhammad b. 'Ali b. al-Hasan b. Shaqiq al-Marwazi > Abu Mu'adh al-Fadl b. Khalid > 'Ubayd b. Sulayman > ad-Dahhak b. Muzahim: "Masruq used to narrate from 'A'ishah"). Not confirmed in any of the six collections; presented ungraded, with the chain as given; the article notes ad-Dahhak does not say he heard it from Masruq.
3. Ibn Mas'ud (mawquf) via Masruq: at-Tabari, marked as a Companion's statement; ungraded.
4. 'Umar's iqamah exhortation: at-Tabari from Abu Nadrah; Ibn Kathir (en) says Ibn Abi Hatim and Ibn Jarir recorded it; no grading in either text, none given.
Stock Bukhari 660 not used.
