Card and 8-section bilingual article for 38:85: la-amla'anna jahannama
minka wa-mimman tabi'aka minhum ajma'in, read as what Allah's oath in 38:84
swears to, answering Iblis's vow (38:82) and its exception (38:83).

Sources: Tabari, Qurtubi (38:84 and 38:85), Baghawi (38:84; verse text only
on 38:85), Sa'di (38:84 only; 38:85 fetch failed with 503), Muyassar
(grouped 84-85), Ibn Kathir (ar). Ma'arif and Ibn Kathir en: wrong group.
- Oath: Mujahid's raf' (I am the Truth / the truth is from Me); as-Suddi:
  an oath Allah swore; Qurtubi/Baghawi: by al-Haqq, who is Allah; Sa'di:
  truth My attribute, truth My word; Tabari: both readings sound, and
  la-amla'anna as the thing sworn (Qurtubi).
- minka: Tabari keeps it to Iblis; Qurtubi and Muyassar add his offspring.
- minhum: Tabari, Qurtubi, Muyassar all gloss "of the children of Adam"
  with the qualifier "who follow you"; no fetched text reads it as all of
  mankind. ajma'in echoes the vow in 38:82.
- Ibn Kathir's parallels: 32:13 and 17:63.
Disagreement kept, no position: width of minka (Iblis vs Iblis and offspring).
Hadith: none attached by any fetched source; said in one sentence.
Bukhari 4850 dropped (only partial extracts possible; the full text's sifat
clause is unexplained in the fetched sources). "No one wronged" now rests
on 14:22 and 18:49, verified in data/quran-json.
Asbab: none. Framing: licensing sentence in both languages; no individual
assigned a fate. Cross-ref 38:78 (curse) not rebuilt; 38:86-88 untouched.
Dropped: Iblis as a jinn (unsourced); Sa'di on 38:85 (unavailable).
Themes: hereafter, character.
