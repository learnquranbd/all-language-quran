## Disagreements kept (both sides named, no position)
- Object of the patience: harm in Allah's cause (at-Tabari, Muyassar), calling to Allah (as-Sa'di) vs pardoning instead of retaliating for the mutilation (al-Qurtubi, Ma'arif).
- "them" in la tahzan 'alayhim: the opponents (at-Tabari, al-Baghawi, Ibn Kathir, Muyassar, as-Sa'di) vs the slain of Uhud (al-Qurtubi).
- Abrogation: Ibn Zayd (abrogated by fighting) vs the majority (muhkam), per al-Qurtubi.
- dayq/diq: fatha (Iraqi readers, preferred by at-Tabari; majority per al-Qurtubi) vs kasra (Ibn Kathir the reader; some Madinan readers); report from Nafi' called an error by al-Qurtubi. Lexical: Abu 'Amr's distinction vs Ibn as-Sikkit's equivalence; Ibn Qutayba's lightened dayyiq.
- Licensing sentence (§8.3) in both languages.
