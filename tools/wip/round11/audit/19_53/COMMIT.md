Aaron given to Moses as a prophet, out of mercy, in answer to his prayer.

The card and a deep bilingual article for 19:53, the gift of a brother-prophet
to Moses in Surah Maryam's procession of prophets.

Sources (all fetched, none from memory):
- Ma'arif al-Qur'an (via Mazhari): wahabna is from hiba, a gift; Aaron is called
  hibatullah, the Gift of Allah, for that reason.
- al-Qurtubi and al-Baghawi: the gift answers Moses's own prayer, "appoint for me a
  minister from my family, Aaron my brother" (Ta-Ha 20:29-30).
- Ibn Kathir: gathers the parallel grantings — al-Qasas 28:34, Ta-Ha 20:36,
  ash-Shu'ara 26:13-14 — and the Salaf saying that no intercession in this world was
  greater than Moses's plea for Aaron to be made a prophet.
- as-Sa'di: reads it as Moses's virtue and sincere counsel to his brother; Aaron's
  prophethood is subordinate to (tabi'a) Moses's, following and supporting it.
- at-Tabari and al-Muyassar: the gift's purpose is ta'yid / mu'azara — to strengthen
  and back Moses on a hard mission.

Older brother, second in order: at-Tabari and Ibn Kathir carry the Ibn Abbas athar
that Aaron was older than Moses; reported as a companion statement, not marfu', with
Ibn Kathir's note that Ibn Abi Hatim gave it mu'allaq. Grading not upgraded.

Dropped/omitted: no sound hadith attaches to the verse in any tafsir consulted (said
plainly, slot left empty); no established asbab (placement used instead); no age-gap
claim beyond "older"; watch-point's 20:31-32 not cited since fetched text quotes only
20:29-30 and 20:36. No licensing sentence (not a condemned-group verse). 19:54-55 left
for its own turn.
