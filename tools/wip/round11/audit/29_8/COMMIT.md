Goodness to parents is enjoined (husnan), but obedience stops where
they strive to compel shirk. The card and article hold the whole
tension: obedience withheld is not kindness withheld.

Sources (all from PREP, not re-fetched):
- Ibn Kathir: placement after tawhid, parents as cause of existence
  (father by spending, mother by compassion), 17:23 and 17:24; and
  the gathering with the righteous, not the parents' company — one is
  raised with those he loves for God's sake (then 29:9).
- at-Tabari and al-Qurtubi: husn grammar; "no knowledge I have a
  partner"; Qurtubi reads "to Me is your return" as a warning.
- as-Sa'di: husn = birr/ihsan in word and deed; shirk magnified;
  precede parents' obedience except over obedience to Allah.
- al-Baghawi, al-Muyassar, Ma'arif: the governing principle, "no
  obedience to a creature in disobedience of the Creator."

Sabab, confirmed on quranx: Sa'd ibn Abi Waqqas and his mother's
hunger strike. Quoted whole from Sahih Muslim 1748 (its presence in
Muslim is the grading; Tirmidhi's wording graded hasan sahih — not
upgraded). The Muslim hadith itself pairs 29:8 with 31:15, used as
the balance: kindness (ma'ruf) continues when obedience must stop.
Cross-light to 31:14 and 46:15 (Baghawi: all revealed about Sa'd).

Disagreements kept: Tabari vs Sa'di on "ma laysa laka bihi 'ilm";
Tabari vs Qurtubi on "ilayya marji'ukum."

Dropped: Ibn Abbas variants ('Ayyash; whole-ummah); fixed length of
the fast; the three off-verse revelations in the same Muslim hadith.
Licensing sentence included: no warrant to mistreat or cut off a
non-Muslim parent — the line is around an act, not a person.
