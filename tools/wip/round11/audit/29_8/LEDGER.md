# SOURCES ledger — 29:8

## Tafsir fetched (all from PREP.md, not re-fetched)
- t14 Ibn Kathir (ar), t169 Ibn Kathir abridged (en) — NAMED. Rests on:
  "آمرا عباده بالإحسان إلى الوالدين بعد الحث على التمسك بتوحيده ... فالوالد بالإنفاق والوالدة بالإشفاق"
  (placement after tawhid; father by spending, mother by compassion; cites 17:23-24);
  "وإن حرصا عليك أن تتابعهما على دينهما إذا كانا مشركين ... لا تطعهما في ذلك"
  (if idolaters striving to draw you into their religion, do not obey);
  "أحشرك مع الصالحين لا في زمرة والديك ... يحشر يوم القيامة مع من أحب حبا دينيا"
  (gathered with the righteous, not the parents' company; raised with those one loves). Hadith chain + grading below.
- t15 at-Tabari — NAMED. "وصّينا الإنسان بوالديه أن يفعل بهما حُسنا"; grammar of husnan
  (Basran تكرير reading); "ما ليس لك به علم أنه ليس لي شريك" (no knowledge that I have a
  partner); "إليّ معادكم ومصيركم يوم القيامة ... فأخبركم". Sabab via Qatada (Sa'd's hijrah form) noted.
- t90 al-Qurtubi — NAMED. Sabab (Tirmidhi) + "قال أبو عيسى: هذا حديث حسن صحيح";
  the "mائة نفس" reply; "شجروا فاها"; grammar; "إلي مرجعكم وعيد في طاعة الوالدين في معنى الكفر"
  (the return read as a wa'id / warning). Also records Ibn Abbas variants (see dropped).
- t91 as-Sa'di — NAMED. "ببرهما والإحسان إليهما بالقول والعمل ... ولا يعقهما"; "وليس لأحد علم
  بصحة الشرك بالله، وهذا تعظيم لأمر الشرك"; "فبروا والديكم وقدموا طاعتهما إلا على طاعة الله ورسوله".
- t94 al-Baghawi — NAMED. Sabab (Hamnah bint Abi Sufyan; a day+night then another day; the
  "مائة نفس" reply); "نزلت هذه الآية والتي في سورة لقمان والأحقاف في سعد" (this verse + 31:15 +
  46:15 about Sa'd); hadith "لا طاعة لمخلوق في معصية الله".
- t16 al-Muyassar — NAMED. "يبرهما ويحسن إليهما بالقول والعمل"; "ويلحق بطلب الإشراك بالله سائر
  المعاصي، فلا طاعة لمخلوق كائنا من كان في معصية الله ... كما ثبت ذلك عن رسول الله".
- t168 Ma'arif (en) — NAMED. Wasiyyah per Mazhari = call to action from counsel/well-wishing;
  husn = infinitive; "limit of kind treatment ... must not go beyond ... injunctions of Allah";
  hadith "لا طاعة لمخلوق في معصية الخالق" "reported by Imam Ahmad and Hakim ... held it as Sahih";
  Hamnah bint Abi Sufyan, al-'Ashrah al-mubashsharah; Baghawi's day/night (some: three days).

## Hadith (sabab) — CONFIRMED on fetched page
- Collection: Sahih Muslim, no. 1748 (variant 1748c is the mother narration).
- URL fetched: https://quranx.com/hadith/Muslim/Hadith-1748 (via tools/wip/round11/hadith.js Muslim 1748)
- First ten words of wording used (English, quranx): "Mus'ab b. Sa'd reported on the authority of his father"
  — relevant segment: "His mother Umm Sa'd had taken oath that she would never talk with him
  until he abandoned his faith and she neither ate nor drank ... She passed three days in this
  state until she fainted because of extreme hunger ... Allah revealed these verses: 'And We have
  enjoined upon a person goodness to his parents ...' (29:8); 'Treat them with customary good in
  this world' (31:15)."
- Grading: in Sahih Muslim (sahih by Muslim's conditions) — NOT upgraded. Cross-note reported as
  fetched: Ibn Kathir (t169) lists Ahmad, Muslim, Abu Dawud, Nasa'i; at-Tirmidhi "Hasan Sahih"
  (al-Qurtubi t90: "هذا حديث حسن صحيح"). Only Muslim's wording is quoted, whole, not blended.
- Note: the Muslim narration itself pairs 29:8 with 31:15 — used as the balance/licensing pairing.

## Second hadith named (principle)
- "No obedience to a created being in disobedience of the Creator." Attributed per Ma'arif (t168)
  to Ahmad and al-Hakim, al-Hakim grading it sound; al-Baghawi (t94) and al-Muyassar (t16) also
  cite it as from the Messenger. Reported exactly as the tafsirs give it; grading not upgraded.

## Asbab
- CONFIRMED and used: Sa'd ibn Abi Waqqas and his mother's hunger strike, via the Muslim hadith
  above and al-Baghawi/al-Qurtubi/Ibn Kathir/Ma'arif. Name Hamnah bint Abi Sufyan from Baghawi+Ma'arif.

## Disagreements kept
- "ma laysa laka bihi 'ilm": at-Tabari ("no knowledge that I have a partner") vs as-Sa'di
  ("no one has knowledge that shirk is valid; wording magnifies the sin") — both given, no ruling.
- "ilayya marji'ukum": at-Tabari (return + recompense) vs al-Qurtubi (a wa'id / warning against
  obeying parents in what amounts to kufr) — both given.

## Dropped (could not confirm / out of scope)
- al-Qurtubi's Ibn Abbas variants — that it was revealed about 'Ayyash ibn Abi Rabi'a, and that
  it was "revealed about the whole ummah" — DROPPED to keep the sabab clean and uncontested; the
  Sa'd occasion is the one carried by the sound hadith.
- Exact length of the fast (one day/night vs three days/nights) — sources differ, so written as
  "a day and a night, then a second day ... stretched longer still" rather than a fixed count;
  the Muslim quote's own "three days" is kept inside the quotation.
- The other three revelations listed in the same Muslim hadith (spoils of war 8:1; the one-third
  bequest; wine 5:90) — DROPPED as off-verse; only the parents segment is quoted.
