Card and 8-section bilingual article for 78:23, labithina fiha ahqaba
(3 words). Completes 78:21-22; 78:24-26 named only as the next verses.
No shipped neighbour in range (78:21-22, 78:24-26 not shipped).

Sources: Tabari, Ibn Kathir (ar), Qurtubi, Baghawi, Sa'di, Muyassar
(grouped 78:21-26), Ma'arif (grouped 78:23-25), Ibn Kathir en (one gloss).
- Qira'at lābithīn/labithīn: Tabari's reader groups and preference;
  Baghawi names Hamza and Ya'qub, Qurtubi Hamza and al-Kisa'i.
- huqb/hiqba lexis (Tabari, 18:60; Qurtubi, Qutrub); Ma'arif's hiqbah.
- Figures listed, none chosen: 80 (Ali/Hilal, Abu Hurayra, Ibn Abbas,
  Sa'id b. Jubayr, Qatada), 300 (Bashir b. Ka'b), 70 (al-Hasan, as-Suddi
  in IK), 40 (Abdullah b. Amr in IK; Ibn Umar in Qurtubi), 17,000
  (Muqatil in Baghawi), 43 ahqab (Mujahid in Baghawi; al-Qurazi in
  Qurtubi), 700 ahqab (as-Suddi). Mismatches noted: al-Hasan 70,000 in
  Tabari vs 70 in IK; Ma'arif's "seventy" list vs IK's Arabic.
- Without end: Qatada, ar-Rabi', al-Hasan, Muyassar, Qurtubi (Ibn
  Kaysan; "five or ten" argument; figures not established from the
  Prophet), Ma'arif (al-Baydawi, consensus claim).
- Kept as disagreements: Tabari's one-kind-of-punishment reading (38:55-58)
  vs IK's "sound view"; Muqatil/Ibn Zayd abrogation vs Tabari and Qurtubi
  (khabar not abrogated); Khalid b. Ma'dan's ahl at-tawhid reading,
  Qurtubi/Mazhari vs Abu Hayyan (78:27-28).
Hadith: none quoted. Marfu' reports in the tafsirs (Abu Umama, called
munkar jiddan by IK; al-Bazzar; ath-Tha'labi) could not be confirmed on
a collection page; said so in one paragraph. Asbab: none.
Licensing sentence in both languages; no group named.
Dropped: all figures as fact, marfu' wordings, 78:24-26 content.
