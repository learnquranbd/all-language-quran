# SOURCES ledger — 17:90

## Tafsir fetched (all pre-fetched in audit/17_90/)
- **at-Tabari (15)** — USED. yanbu' is yaf'ul from "naba'a al-ma'" (water appears/gushes);
  clause: "يفعول من قول القائل: نبع الماء إذا ظهر وفار". Qatadah + Mujahid: yanbu' = 'uyun
  (springs) "أي حتى تفجر لنا من الأرض عيونا". Qira'at: light "تفجر" first / heavy
  "فتفجر" second — "التخفيف في الأولى والتشديد في الثانية ... أعجب إليّ"; grants heavy
  first also sound "وإن لم تكن الأولى مدفوعة صحتها".
- **al-Qurtubi (90)** — USED. Verse "نزلت في رؤساء قريش" (names 'Utba, Shayba, Abu Sufyan,
  al-Nadr b. al-Harith, Abu Jahl, 'Abdullah b. Abi Umayya, Umayya b. Khalaf, Abu al-Bakhtari,
  al-Walid b. al-Mughira). Occasion via Ibn Ishaq behind the Ka'ba after sunset. al-Wahidi
  from 'Ikrima from Ibn 'Abbas for the revelation. yanbu' = al-'uyun (Mujahid), yaf'ul from
  naba'a; qira'at 'Asim/Hamza/al-Kisa'i light, Abu Hatim preferred (yanbu' singular);
  "الينبوع عين الماء والجمع الينابيع"; singular intends plural (Mujahid).
- **as-Sa'di (91)** — USED. yanbu' = "أنهارًا جارية" (flowing rivers); the Messenger brought
  the Qur'an "المشتمل على كل برهان وآية".
- **al-Muyassar (16)** — USED. "ولما أعجز القرآن المشركين وغلبهم أخذوا يطلبون معجزات وَفْق
  أهوائهم" — once the Qur'an overwhelmed them they demanded wonders per their whims; a
  flowing spring from the land of Makkah.
- **al-Baghawi (94)** — USED. Qira'at (Kufa + Ya'qub light; rest heavy). Occasion via
  'Ikrima from Ibn 'Abbas (same gathering). yanbu' = 'uyun in "أرض مكة" (land of Makkah).
- **Ibn Kathir (14 ar / 169 en)** — USED. The theological core: had they asked seeking
  guidance they would have been answered "لو علم الله منهم أنهم يسألون ذلك استرشادا لأجيبوا
  إليه، ولكن علم أنهم إنما يطلبون ذلك كفرا وعنادا"; the choice offered (grant + unmatched
  punishment on disbelief, OR the gate of tawba/rahma) and the Prophet chose "بل تفتح عليهم
  باب التوبة والرحمة". Cross-refs 17:59, 25:7-11, 10:96-97, 6:111. yanbu' = spring of
  flowing water, springs in the Hijaz. Some of the gathering later became sincere Muslims,
  incl. 'Abdullah b. Abi Umayyah (from the en abridged text). House of zukhruf = gold
  (Ibn 'Abbas, Mujahid, Qatada) — NOT USED (17:93 is future ground).
- **Ma'arif al-Qur'an (168, en)** — USED. Demands = conditions heard as mockery; the taught
  answer gives no rebuke/duel, just plain truth; the mistaken notion that a messenger must
  possess all divine powers; a rasul only conveys, miracles are by Allah's power/decision
  alone; model for leaders/reformers.

## Occasion report grading
The gathering-behind-the-Ka'ba narrative is carried by al-Qurtubi, al-Baghawi and Ibn
Kathir via Ibn Ishaq (Ibn Kathir's isnad: Ibn Jarir > Abu Kurayb > Yunus b. Bukayr > Ibn
Ishaq > "شيخ من أهل مصر" [an UNNAMED shaykh of Egypt] > 'Ikrima > Ibn 'Abbas; a second route
via al-Bakka'i > Ibn Ishaq > "بعض أهل العلم" [unnamed people of knowledge]). Both chains
carry an unnamed transmitter (mubham), so the isnad is NOT established as sound. No tafsir
grades it sahih. Reported in the article as revelation background, explicitly not as proof.
Named individuals given only as the tafsirs name them.
