Tadabbur 17:90 — we will not believe until you make a spring gush

Card plus deep bilingual article on the disbelievers' demand for a
yanbu' (spring) from the ground, the opening of the list running through
17:93. Spine: miracles demanded as transactional bargaining, not sincere
seeking — the tell is lan ("never") placed before the condition.

Sources (all confirmed against fetched tafsir):
- at-Tabari: yanbu' = yaf'ul from naba'a (to gush); Qatadah/Mujahid gloss
  'uyun (springs); the tafjura light/heavy qira'at split.
- al-Qurtubi + al-Baghawi: springs in the land of Makkah; qira'at detail;
  the named Quraysh chiefs; al-Wahidi's "then Allah revealed" note.
- as-Sa'di: flowing rivers. al-Muyassar: demand made per their whims once
  the Qur'an had overwhelmed them.
- Ibn Kathir: had they sought guidance they'd be answered; they asked from
  kufr and 'inad; the choice of the gate of repentance over a sign that
  would seal their ruin; 'Abdullah b. Abi Umayya later embraced Islam.
- Ma'arif: a messenger conveys, miracles are Allah's alone; the answer's
  restraint as a model for leaders.

Occasion (gathering behind the Ka'ba, via Ibn Ishaq): chain carries an
unnamed transmitter, so relayed as background only, explicitly not graded
sound and not used as proof.

No sound hadith attaches directly to the verse; slot left empty per gate.
Bukhari 660 fetched and dropped as off-topic. Licensing sentence included:
the verse condemns the closed-hearted stance, not any living person.
Full exegesis of 17:91-93 held back as future ground.
