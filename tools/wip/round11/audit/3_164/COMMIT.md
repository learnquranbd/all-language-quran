Key 3:164 alone: the favour named as a favour, a Messenger from among themselves,
and the plain error before him. New card and a 1,749-word, 8-section article.
3:161-163 (ghulul) and 3:165 left free; 3:165 cited only as text.

Sources: at-Tabari (with Qatadah and Ibn Ishaq), Ibn Kathir (ar, en abr.),
al-Qurtubi, as-Sa'di, al-Baghawi, al-Muyassar and Ma'arif on 3:164, plus
Ahsanul Bayaan (the app's Bengali tafsir) for the human-not-angel point, the
separate mention of recitation, hikmah as hadith "to most mufassirun" and in as inna.
- Manna: at-Tabari's tatawwala, Qatadah's "unasked", as-Sa'di's "greatest
  blessing and its root"; Ma'arif: the favour line is what 3:164 adds to 2:129.
- Min anfusihim: Ibn Kathir (of their kind; 30:21, 18:110, 25:20, 6:130),
  at-Tabari (their tongue), as-Sa'di (lineage, state, speech), al-Qurtubi's
  views incl. the battle duty and the shadhdh anfasihim reading.
- Hikmah as the Sunnah attributed to at-Tabari, Qatadah, Ibn Kathir,
  al-Muyassar, as-Sa'di (with his second sense and al-kitab as writing).
Kept as differences, no position: Arabs only (Aishah report in al-Qurtubi,
ungraded; al-Muyassar; 62:2) vs all believers (al-Baghawi, al-Qurtubi; 9:128);
emphatic in vs in = ma (al-Qurtubi, the Kufan grammarians).
Hadith: none attached to the verse by any fetched tafsir, said once; Bukhari
4330 (Hunayn, the Ansar's "Allah and His Messenger are more gracious") marked
as not tied to the verse, one contiguous segment, root link amann/manna.
Asbab: none; placement and al-Qurtubi's reported battle link only.
Licensing line on the Jahiliyyah description (§7.3); Qatadah's target group unnamed.
Dropped: al-Qurtubi's ethnic ranking in the shadhdh note, the named excepted clan
(al-Baghawi and al-Qurtubi differ), Ma'arif's ruh excursus and "stations of
the Companions" line, 3:165 / 4:78-79 discussion.
