Card and 8-section bilingual article for 77:1, wa-l-mursalati 'urfa: the
unnamed sent ones, who they are, what 'urfan means, and where the five
oaths land (77:2-7 as context; 77:8 and 77:13 not developed).

Sources: Tabari, Qurtubi, Sa'di, Baghawi, Ibn Kathir (ar 77:1; en grouped
77:1-10), Muyassar, Ma'arif (en grouped 77:1-5). All named.
- mursalat kept as a three-way disagreement: winds (Ibn Mas'ud via Abu
  al-'Ubaydayn, Ibn Abbas, Abu Salih, Mujahid, Qatada; Qurtubi's jumhur;
  Baghawi; Muyassar) vs angels (Masruq from Ibn Mas'ud, Muqatil, Abu
  Hurayrah and others in IK; Sa'di) vs messengers/prophets (Abu Salih's
  al-rusul, filed by Tabari under angels; Ibn Abbas in Qurtubi).
- Tabari's verdict quoted: no evidence for one party, the oath covers
  angel, wind and human messenger. Ma'arif's "safer to keep silent"
  summary reported as Ma'arif's, with the difference noted.
- IK abridgement and Ma'arif: winds in 77:1-3, angels in 77:4-5.
- 'urfan: in succession like a horse's mane (Tabari, Salih ibn Buraydah,
  Qurtubi, Baghawi; "many" per Baghawi) vs with al-ma'ruf / Allah's
  command and prohibition (Tabari's angels group, Qurtubi's grammar, Sa'di).
Hadith: Bukhari 4934 (cave, snake; "at Mina" from 'Umar ibn Hafs's note,
as carried by Ibn Kathir) and Bukhari 763 (Umm al-Fadl, Maghrib), both in
al-Bukhari's Sahih, marked as about the surah, not the verse's meaning. No
asbab.
Dropped: Qurtubi's "laylat al-jinn" variant (no collection), the Makki
exception note, IK-en's 77:3 remark on Tabari.
