Card and bilingual article (8 sections, 1,638 EN words) for "We made its
highest its lowest and rained upon them stones of sijjil", read as the
second telling of 11:82 and built on what differs, not what repeats.

Overlap: 11:82 shipped the sijjil readings, mandud, the Jibril lifting
narrative and al-Qurtubi's legal note; one pointer to it, none restated.
Ibn Kathir (ar and abridged) himself defers sijjil to Hud.

Counts (quran-json): 11:82 has 12 words, 15:74 has 8; 6 identical,
ja'alna/fa-ja'alna, 'alayha/'alayhim; missing fa-lamma ja'a amruna and
mandud. 15:73 is also 3 words (noted, nothing built on it). Mushriqin
twice (15:73, 26:60); musbihin five times (incl. 15:66, 15:83).

Sunrise vs dawn, from 15:73 tafsirs fetched for this key: al-Baghawi,
punishment began at morning and was completed at sunrise; al-Qurtubi,
hal = time of sunrise (intended), or the dawn's rising, or begun at
subh and completed at sunrise, wa-llahu a'lam; at-Tabari and Ibn
Jurayj, when the sun rose, mushriqin and musbihin both hal; as-Sa'di,
sunrise when the punishment was hardest; Ibn Kathir, a crashing sound
at sunrise.

On 15:74: at-Tabari "their land", Muyassar "their towns", as-Sa'di
"their city" and the stones pursuing stragglers (now on the rain, with
no mandud). 15:75-77 only via Ibn Kathir abridged (grouped 15:73-77):
mutawassimin glosses of Mujahid, Ibn Abbas/ad-Dahhak, Qatadah; sabil
muqim with 37:137-138. Ma'arif grouped 15:72-74, all on la'amruka; unused.

Hadith: none; no fetched tafsir attaches one to 15:74.

Dropped: the Dead Sea/Sodom identification in Ibn Kathir abridged.
Fresh licensing sentence in both languages; reflection on why the
Qur'an retells a story, marked as reflection.
