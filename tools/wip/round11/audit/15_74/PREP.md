   15:80 [5 words] وَلَقَدْ كَذَّبَ أَصْحَٰبُ ٱلْحِجْرِ ٱلْمُرْسَلِينَ
   EN: And certainly did the companions of al-Ḥijr1 [i.e., the Thamūd] deny the messengers.
