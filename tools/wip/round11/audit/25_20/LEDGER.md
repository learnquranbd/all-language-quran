## Hadith
- SLOT (§7): Sahih Muslim 2963a, Abu Hurayra. URL fetched:
  https://quranx.com/hadith/Muslim/Hadith-2963 (via hadith.js, saved audit/25_20).
  First ten words (AR): "إذا نظر أحدكم إلى من فضل عليه في المال والخلق".
  Wording quoted whole (my EN of the 2963a Arabic): "When one of you looks at
  someone favoured over him in wealth and bodily form, let him look at one who is
  below him, among those over whom he himself has been favoured." Grading: recorded
  in Sahih Muslim (Sahih); same in Bukhari and cited by Ma'arif and al-Baghawi (whose
  chain Sufyan b. Uyayna→Abu az-Zinad→al-A'raj→Abu Hurayra matches Muslim's 2963a
  chain). Not upgraded; no hybrid — Muslim's wording only.
- SUPPORTING (§7, not the slot, attributed as Ibn Kathir's citation): Sahih Muslim,
  Iyad b. Himar, "إني مبتليك ومبتل بك" ("I am testing you and testing others through
  you"), explicitly marked as one clause within a longer Prophetic address (Muslim
  2865). Cited to show the fitna clause in the first person, not quoted as the slot
  hadith.

## Asbab an-nuzul
- Reported, USED as placement (§1, §5): al-Baghawi (ad-Dahhak from Ibn Abbas) and
  al-Qurtubi (Ibn Abbas) — verse answers the taunt of 25:7 ("mā li hādhā ar-rasūl
  ya'kulu aṭ-ṭa'ām..."). Presented as the mufassirūn's placement/report, not as a
  graded hadith. Narrower occasions (Muqatil: named Quraysh chiefs mocking the
  low-born believers; al-Kalbi: a noble delaying Islam; a third: poor believers
  tested by scoffers) kept side-by-side with the general reading, no side chosen.

## Protective sentence (§4)
Wealth/health are no proof of God's favour nor poverty/illness of His displeasure;
the verse licenses no contempt for the poor, the sick or the low-born. Grounded in
al-Qurtubi (rich must console not mock; poor must not envy) + Ma'arif.

## Dropped / not used

  5:75 parallel — dropped as tangential.
- The elided-word grammar dispute and al-Qurtubi's "taking means" passage — written
  then trimmed to meet the 1,800-word gate (draft was 1,995). Not doubted; length only.
- Abu ad-Darda' "woe to the scholar from the ignorant..." (Qurtubi via ath-Tha'labi)
  — dropped: ath-Tha'labi's isnad not independently graded here.
- Aisha "mountains of gold" (Mazhari) — dropped in favour of the Abu Umamah report,
  which Ma'arif gives with named collections (Musnad Ahmad, Tirmidhi).
