Card and deep bilingual article on 25:20 — the two spine teachings kept
together: the full humanity of every messenger (they ate, they walked the
markets), and "We made some of you a trial for others — will you be patient?"

Sources (all fetched for this verse; none from memory):
- at-Tabari: the verse as God's argument against the taunt of 25:7; the elided-
  word grammar (Farra'/Kisa'i "man" vs Zajjaj "rusulan" vs ahl al-ma'ani "qīla",
  41:43); the wisdom of withholding the world so following is not bought.
- Ibn Kathir: messengers ate for nourishment, walked markets to earn/trade,
  rank untouched; 12:109, 21:8; fitna to know obedient from defiant; 6:124.
- Ma'arif: God could have made them kings (Dawud, Sulayman) but kept them level;
  Abu Umamah (Musnad Ahmad, Tirmidhi) — Makkah-as-gold declined; economic
  difference as wisdom, rich/healthy tested for shukr, poor/sick for sabr.
- as-Sa'di: not bodies-that-don't-eat, not angels, "so you have a pattern (uswa)";
  fitna running every direction; basīr knows states and chooses for the message.
- al-Qurtubi: taking means as an asl; the general mutual trial with each side's
  duty (rich console/not mock, poor not envy); atasbirūn as question with elided
  answer + minority imperative reading; al-Muzani hearing the verse in the market.
- al-Baghawi + Muyassar: the pairs; the named occasions (Muqatil, al-Kalbi).

Disagreements kept, not resolved: atasbirūn as question vs command (al-Qurtubi's
minority imperative reading); general mutual-trial reading kept beside the narrower asbab
(named Quraysh chiefs / a noble delaying Islam / poor believers tested by
scoffers) — no side chosen, none makes birth or wealth a measure of a soul.

Hadith: Sahih Muslim (no. 2963), Abu Hurayra, "look to one below you," quoted
whole in Muslim's wording (also in Bukhari; cited by Ma'arif and al-Baghawi).
Iyad b. Himar "I am testing you and testing others through you" carried only as
Ibn Kathir's citation, marked as one clause within a longer address.

Asbab used as placement (Ibn Abbas via al-Baghawi/al-Qurtubi): answers 25:7.
Protective line added: wealth/health prove no favour, poverty/illness no
displeasure; the verse licenses no contempt for the poor, sick or low-born.

Dropped (in ledger): al-Qurtubi's market lore and market du'a (Tirmidhi gharīb),
the yamshūn qira'at, the kināya reading, Abu ad-Darda' via ath-Tha'labi, the
Aisha "mountains of gold" report, and — trimmed for length — the elided-word
grammar dispute (Farra'/Zajjaj/ahl al-ma'ani) and al-Qurtubi's "taking means"
passage (its earning point stays in §2). For focus, not doubt. Differentiated from
shipped 25:8 (the deniers' demand) by staying on God's answer + the mutual trial.
