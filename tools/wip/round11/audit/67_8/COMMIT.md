Card and 8-section bilingual article (1,659 EN words) on 67:8: placement
after 67:5-7 (Ma'arif's turn to punishment and reward), takadu tamayyazu,
min al-ghayz, kullama and fawj, the keepers, the question as rebuke,
Ibn Kathir's justice reading with 17:15, an explicit no-licence section.
67:9 "bala" pointer only.

Sources: at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi, as-Sa'di, Ibn Kathir (ar; en group
67:6-11 for 67:7-8), al-Baghawi, Muyassar, Ma'arif (en group 67:5-14).
- Tamayyazu: Tabari (tafarruq/taqatu', Ibn Abbas by two chains,
  ad-Dahhak), Qurtubi (Sa'id ibn Jubayr; asl tatamayyaz), Baghawi with
  Ibn Qutayba, Muyassar, Sa'di's 'ala ijtima'iha, Ibn Kathir.
- Min al-ghayz: rage with an object (all); Ibn Zayd's "ghadaban lillah";
  Qurtubi's unnamed "qila" reading = boiling. Kept as a disagreement.
- Fawj = jama'a (Tabari, Baghawi, Qurtubi, Muyassar); keepers only as
  "al-muwakkalun bi-amriha" (Muyassar), nothing added.
- Question as tawbikh (Qurtubi, Baghawi, Muyassar); nadhir = messenger;
  Sa'di's "as though you were never told of it".
- 39:71 and 17:15 as cited by Ibn Kathir (en); verified in quran-json.
Explicit paragraph in both languages: the verse licenses nothing against
any living person or community and gives no test for sorting people.
Hadith: none; no fetched tafsir attaches one, said in one sentence.
Dropped: keepers' identity (angels, Malik, 74:30), 25:12, 39:73,
40:49-50 (not cited); IK's Ahmad narration (under 67:11, not checkable);
67:9-11 content beyond the pointer.
- Review fix: dropped an unsourced 'same seven verses' phrase (not in Ma'arif); now 'a few verses on'.
