Replaces the 881-word, 6-section article with a 1,752-word, 8-section deep article built only on the tafsir fetched for 64:15 (shipped card kept).

Sources: Ibn Kathir (ar; en abridged, grouped 64:14-18), at-Tabari, al-Muyassar,
al-Qurtubi, al-Baghawi, Ma'arif. as-Sa'di fetch failed, not used.
- Innama: no commentary discusses the particle; at-Tabari and the Muyassar
  carry it as "nothing but". 'Indahu = Day of Resurrection (Ibn Kathir).
- Fitnah glossed by name: test and trial so He knows who obeys (Ibn Kathir),
  bala' (at-Tabari, Qatada, Muyassar), preoccupation from the Hereafter leading
  to sin (al-Baghawi), a pull to unlawful earning (al-Qurtubi, Ma'arif),
  ighram (al-Qutabi via al-Qurtubi), mihnah ("it is said").
- Partitive min in 64:14 vs none in 64:15 (al-Hasan via al-Qurtubi; "some"
  via al-Baghawi); Ibn Mas'ud's "fitnahs that lead astray", both wordings.
- 64:14 via Ibn Kathir en: 63:9, Mujahid, Ibn Zayd; balance from al-Qurtubi
  (do not obey them in disobedience), at-Tabari, Muyassar, Ma'arif (love
  within limits). Reward = Paradise (Qatada, at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi); 3:14-15.
Hadith: Tirmidhi 3774 (Buraydah, al-Hasan and al-Husayn at the minbar),
hasan gharib; Tirmidhi 3317 (Ibn Abbas, the families who held back hijrah),
hasan sahih; Bukhari 6549 (His good pleasure), marked as not about the verse.
Dropped: Ahmad's and al-Bazzar's "cowardice and miserliness" reports,
at-Tabarani's report, "his family ate his good deeds" (unconfirmable); 8:28,
18:46, 25:74 (no fetched tafsir cites them); 'Awf ibn Malik's report (not in
the fetched texts).
Defect fixed: the old article said the commentators leave ajr 'azim
undescribed (they name Paradise) and claimed a majority view on 64:14 that no
fetched text gives; also dropped its unsourced gold-assaying etymology.
