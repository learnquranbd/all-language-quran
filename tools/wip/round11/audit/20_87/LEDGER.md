# SOURCES ledger — 20:87

## Tafsir fetched (all pre-fetched in audit/20_87/)
- **at-Tabari (15)** — fetched. Covers mostly 20:86 (ghadbān asifan = grieving/angry). Not
  named in the article; nothing here rests on it.
- **al-Qurtubi (90)** — fetched, NAMED. Rests on:
  - qirāʾāt of *bi-malkinā*: fatḥa = "bi-ṭāqatinā" (Mujāhid, as-Suddī); Ibn Zayd "lam
    namlik anfusanā ... kunnā muḍṭarrīn" (compulsion); ḍamma = "bi-sulṭāninā".
  - kasra reading as maṣdar, object dropped: "kaʾannahu qāla bi-milkinā al-ṣawāb bal
    akhṭaʾnā, fahuwa iʿtirāf minhum bi-l-khaṭaʾ" → the "confession of error" reading.
  - *ḥamalnā* (light) chosen "li-annahum ḥamalū ḥaliyy al-qawm maʿahum wa mā ḥamalūhu
    karhan" → the willing-carrying counter (§4).
  - *awzāran ... zīnat al-qawm* = "athqālan min zīnat al-qawm ... min ḥuliyyihim";
    borrowed ("istaʿārūhu"), pretext of a feast; "sammiyat awzāran bi-sabab annahā kānat
    āthāman ... lam taḥill lahum al-ghanāʾim"; sea-cast alternative "qīl huwa mā akhadhūhu
    min āl firʿawn lammā qadhafahum al-baḥr".
  - *fa-qadhafnāhā* = weighed on us, cast into the fire to melt / threw it to Sāmirī.
- **as-Sa'di (91)** — fetched, NAMED. "mā faʿalnā ... ʿan taʿammud minnā"; "taʾaththamnā min
  zīnat al-qawm ... istaʿārū ḥuliyyan kathīran min al-qibṭ"; Sāmirī "baṣura yawm al-gharaq
  bi-athar al-rasūl, fa-akhadha qabḍa min atharihi ... fatnatan wa-mtiḥānan". The calf
  moving/lowing and "dhahaba yaṭlub rabbahu" in his text belong to 20:88 — RESERVED, not used.
- **Ibn Kathir ar (14)** — fetched, NAMED. "sharaʿū yaʿtadhirūn bi-l-ʿudhr al-bārid" (§7);
  ḥadīth al-futūn: Hārūn ordered the ḥuliyy thrown into a pit with fire; Sāmirī cast "al-qabḍa
  allatī akhadhahā min athar al-rasūl". Result (calf with khuwār) → RESERVED for 20:88.
- **al-Baghawi (94)** — fetched, NAMED. qirāʾāt of malk ("wa-naḥnu namliku amranā" /
  "bi-ikhtiyārinā" / "bi-qudratinā wa sulṭāninā ... idhā waqaʿa fī al-baliyya lam yamlik
  nafsahu"); *ḥummilnā* = "juʿilūnā naḥmiluhā wa-kullifnā ḥamlahā"; ornaments = "ḥuliyy qawm
  firʿawn", "istaʿārū ... sammāhā awzāran"; sea-cast alt = ghanīma not lawful; as-Suddī: Hārūn
  "iḥfurū ḥafīra fa-alqūhā fīhā ḥattā yarjiʿ Mūsā"; Saʿīd b. Jubayr ← Ibn ʿAbbās: Hārūn lit
  the fire; Sāmirī cast "turāb ḥāfir faras Jibrīl"; Qatādah: a handful in his turban.
- **al-Muyassar (16)** — fetched, NAMED. "ḥummilnā athqālan min ḥuliyy qawm firʿawn,
  fa-alqaynāhā fī ḥufra fīhā nār bi-amr al-Sāmirī ... turāb ḥāfir faras Jibrīl."
- **Ma'arif en (168)** — fetched, NAMED. awzār = pl. of wizr (burden = sin carried on the
  Day of Judgment); ornaments borrowed from Egyptians on festival pretext, not returned;
  pit/casting from ḥadīth al-futūn (Hārūn's order) with variant that Sāmirī urged it, "both
  may be true"; explicit reserve: "These traditions cannot be wholly relied upon, yet ...
  we do not possess sufficient ground to disprove them either." Used for §5 source-note.
- **Ibn Kathir en abridged (169)** — fetched (grouped 20:83–89). Corroborates "lame excuses",
  "bi-malkinā = our power and our choice", and the "rid themselves of something detestable
  ... wound up doing something worse" point (§7). Later-verse material (calf lowing, hādhā
  ilāhukum, Baḥmūt name, Ibn ʿAbbās on the wind, aḥadith on al-Husayn) → RESERVED / dropped.

## Hadith
- **None cited.** No rigorously authenticated hadith in the primary collections attaches to
  20:87. The pit/fire/dust scene rests on **ḥadīth al-futūn** (long narration attributed to
  Ibn ʿAbbās), which the mufassirūn relate but Ma'arif itself flags as not wholly reliable.
  Stated as such in §5 in one sentence, per the gate. No hadith number claimed.

## Asbab an-nuzul
- Not used. Verse sits in continuous narrative (Mūsā's return, 20:83–98); no separate sabab
  needed or claimed.

## Disagreements kept (as disagreements, no position taken)
- Who ordered the casting: Hārūn (AS) (Baghawi/Suddī, Ibn Kathir, Ma'arif via ḥadīth al-futūn)
  vs the Sāmirī urging it; Ma'arif: both may be true. Kept open.
- How the ornaments were obtained: borrowed-and-unreturned vs sea-cast spoil. Kept open.
- What the Sāmirī threw: dust from Jibrīl's horse's hoofprint (Muyassar, Baghawi) / a handful
  from "the track of the messenger" (Sa'di, Ibn Kathir). Reported side by side.

## Dropped / not written
- Al-Qurtubi's numbers (12,000 who stayed firm of 600,000 Israelites; "qālū" ʿāmm/khāṣṣ) —
  dropped to avoid over-precision and numeric-parity risk; the "speakers were the ones who
  fell" idea was not asserted.
- Ibn Kathir en's Ibn ʿUmar athar (mosquito blood / killing of al-Husayn) — dropped: a general
  illustrative narration not attached to this verse, and off-topic/sensitive.
- The calf coming alive, its khuwār, and "hādhā ilāhukum wa-ilāhu Mūsā ... fa-nasiya" — RESERVED
  for 20:88, not preempted.
