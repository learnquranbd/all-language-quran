## Attributions and the Arabic/clause each rests on
- **Reading naṣb vs rafʿ** (§1.1): at-Tabari — "اختلفت القراء... (وَلِسُلَيْمَانَ الرِّيحَ) بنصب الريح... وقرأ ذلك عاصم: (الرِّيحُ) رفعًا... والصواب... النصب لإجماع الحجة من القراء." al-Qurtubi confirms rafʿ reading (Abū Bakr ʿan ʿĀṣim) and that meaning is unchanged "لأنه قد علم أنه لم يسخرها أحد إلا الله."
- **ghuduww/rawāḥ = a month each; two months in a day** (§1.2): at-Tabari "غدوها إلى انتصاف النهار مسيرة شهر، ورواحها... مسيرة شهر"; Qatadah "مسيرة شهرين في يوم"; al-Muyassar "من أول النهار إلى انتصافه مسيرة شهر..."; as-Sa'di "فتسير في اليوم مسيرة شهرين."
- **al-Hasan al-Basri route** (§1.3): Ibn Kathir (t14/t169) "كان يغدو... من دمشق فينزل بإصطخر... ويبيت بكابل"; al-Qurtubi has the Bayt al-Maqdis variant "فيغدو من بيت المقدس إلى إصطخر."
- **ʿayn al-qiṭr = molten copper (nuḥās)** (§2.1): at-Tabari "وأذبنا له عين النحاس، وأجريناها له"; Ibn Kathir names Ibn ʿAbbās, Mujāhid, ʿIkrimah, Qatādah, as-Suddī et al. "القطر: النحاس"; al-Khalīl in al-Qurtubi "القطر: النحاس المذاب."
- **Yemen / three-day dispute** (§2.2): Qatadah (Tabari) "عين النحاس كانت بأرض اليمن، وإنما ينتفع اليوم بما أخرج الله لسليمان"; as-Suddi/al-Baghawi three days "أسيلت له ثلاثة أيام"; al-Qushayri's doubt in al-Qurtubi "وتخصيص الإسالة بثلاثة أيام لا يدرى ما حده، ولعله وهم من الناقل" + Mujāhid report about place (Ṣanʿāʾ) not duration. Kept as a disagreement, no position taken.
- **Jinn subjected by leave** (§3.1): Ibn Kathir "وسخرنا له الجن يعملون بين يديه بإذن الله، أي: بقدره"; al-Baghawi/Ibn ʿAbbās "سخر الله الجن لسليمان وأمرهم بطاعته"; as-Sa'di "لا يقدرون أن يستعصوا عن أمره."
- **bayna yadayhi / not sorcery** (§3.2): Ma'arif — "before him... worked before him like vassals," distinct from the harnessing of sun and moon; subjection by Allah's command. (Ma'arif's long ʿamils/incantation discussion noted but not elaborated — see Dropped.)
- **man yazigh → the Blaze; dunyā vs ākhira** (§4.1–4.2): at-Tabari "يعدل من الجن عن أمرنا الذي أمرناه من طاعة سليمان... نُذِقْهُ... في الآخرة"; al-Qurtubi "في الآخرة... قاله أكثر المفسرين. وقيل ذلك في الدنيا"; al-Baghawi "وقال بعضهم: في الدنيا... وكل بهم ملكا بيده سوط من نار." Kept as disagreement.
- **jinn of fire yet burned** (§4.3): Ma'arif — "the Jinns are made of fire in the same sense as man is made of dust... pure and potent fire would burn them too."
- **Power all leased; kingship under God** (§6): as-Sa'di "سخر له الريح... وسهلنا له الأسباب... وسخر الله له... الشياطين والجن." Synthesis of the bi-idhni rabbih clause across all mufassirūn.
- **Isrā'īliyyāt restraint** (§7): throne/ship/inscription reports are in at-Tabari (Ibn Zayd's great vessel; Wahb b. Munabbih's Tigris inscription) and al-Qurtubi (poetry, inscription) — relayed there with chains as reports. Flagged as lore, not endorsed.

## Dropped (could not confirm, or deliberately excluded)
- **Horse-sacrifice recompense** (al-Hasan, in al-Qurtubi/Ma'arif: wind given after the ʿAṣr/horses episode of Sūrah Ṣād 38): dropped — belongs to 38:30–40, extra-Qur'anic in detail here, and the watch-point warns against importing it.
- **Ibn Zayd's 1,000-chamber flying vessel; Wahb's Tigris inscription; Ibn ʿAbbās's 400,000 thrones; the poetry (an-Nābigha)**: not asserted; referenced only generically in §7 as lore the mufassirūn relay without endorsing.
- **Ma'arif's extended ruling on ʿamils / jinn-subjugation by incantation (Akām al-Marjān material)**: out of scope for the verse; only the core "subjection by Allah's leave, not sorcery" point retained.
- **Ibn Kathir's gharīb reports on the "three kinds of jinn"** (Abū Thaʿlaba etc.): Ibn Kathir himself grades them gharīb jiddan; dropped.
- **"death-on-the-staff" / Temple-building**: belong to 34:13–14; excluded per watch-point.
