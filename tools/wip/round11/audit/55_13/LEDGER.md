## Disagreements kept
- Ala': favours (al-Qurtubi: all commentators; at-Tabari's reports) vs power, al-qudra (Ibn Zayd in at-Tabari and al-Qurtubi; al-Kalbi; chosen by Muhammad ibn Ali at-Tirmidhi). Both stated.
- Dual: jinn and mankind (majority; at-Tabari's answer, Ibn Kathir/Mujahid, al-Qurtubi's "sahih") vs one addressed in the dual by Arab habit (at-Tabari "qad qila", al-Baghawi, al-Qurtubi "qila"). Al-Qurtubi's preference reported as his; the article picks none.

## Hadith
- Jami' at-Tirmidhi 3291, https://quranx.com/hadith/Tirmidhi/Hadith-3291 (saved hadith_Tirmidhi_3291.txt). Attached to the verse by al-Qurtubi (names at-Tirmidhi) and al-Baghawi. First ten words used (translated from the Arabic): "The Messenger of Allah ﷺ came out to his Companions and recited". Quoted whole. Grading: at-Tirmidhi "hadith gharib", known only via al-Walid ibn Muslim from Zuhayr ibn Muhammad, with Ahmad's and al-Bukhari's remarks on Zuhayr's Syrian narrators (both reported).
- At-Tabari's Ibn Umar report: mentioned as his, with chain and no grading; not quoted, not checked in a collection.
- Ibn Abbas "la bi-ayyiha": mawquf athar, as at-Tabari and Ibn Kathir carry it.
