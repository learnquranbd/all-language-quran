Replaces the 730-word, 6-section article with a 1,736-word, 8-section one,
from at-Tabari, Ibn Kathir, al-Qurtubi, al-Baghawi, as-Sa'di, the Muyassar
and Ma'arif al-Qur'an on 55:13. Slots checked; the abridged English Ibn
Kathir (wrong group) not used. The shipped card is kept.
Refrain count verified in the Arabic text: 31 times, 55:13 to 55:77.
Placement after 55:1-12 via al-Qurtubi quoting Muhammad ibn Ali
at-Tirmidhi; pointers to 55:1, 55:9, 55:14-16, 55:19, 55:26-27, 55:46, 55:60.
Ala' kept as a difference: favours (al-Qurtubi "all the commentators",
at-Tabari's reports, religion and world per Muyassar and as-Sa'di) vs
power (Ibn Zayd, al-Kalbi, chosen by Muhammad ibn Ali at-Tirmidhi).
Dual kept as a difference, none chosen: jinn and mankind (majority;
at-Tabari's answer, Ibn Kathir after Mujahid, al-Qurtubi with 55:10, 55:31,
55:33 and al-Jurjani on 38:32) vs one addressed in the dual by Arab habit
(at-Tabari, al-Baghawi with 50:24, al-Qurtubi with Imru' al-Qays).
Takdhib: assigning partners (Muhammad ibn Ali at-Tirmidhi) and
ingratitude (al-Baghawi's analogy, Ibn Kathir, as-Sa'di). Licensing
sentence in both languages.
Repetition: al-Baghawi, al-Qutabi, al-Husayn ibn al-Fadl.
Hadith: Tirmidhi 3291 (Jabir, the jinn's reply), attached by al-Qurtubi
and al-Baghawi, quoted whole with at-Tirmidhi's "gharib" and his remarks
on Zuhayr ibn Muhammad. At-Tabari's Ibn Umar report and Ibn Abbas's "la
bi-ayyiha" mentioned as theirs.
Dropped: Ibn Kathir's Asma' report from Ahmad (not checkable), the
shipped article's luck/cleverness list, "warning as favour", the 16:18 link
and the Qur'an-before-man remark (no source for this verse).
