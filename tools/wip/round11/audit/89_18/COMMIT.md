Card and 9-section bilingual article for 89:18: wa la tahaddun 'ala
ta'am al-miskin, five words. 89:15-17 named as context; 89:19-20 named
and left to their entries; 107:3 cross-referenced via Ibn Kathir.

Sources: Tabari, Qurtubi, Baghawi, Sa'di, Ibn Kathir (ar + en abridged),
Muyassar, Ma'arif. All slots open on 89:18. Ibn Kathir on 107:3 fetched
to confirm his 89:17-18 parallel and miskin definition.
- tahaddun: urge each other (Kufans/Abu Ja'far, Qurtubi's tatahaddun
  origin, Abu 'Ubayd's choice); tahuddun, you do not command (Tabari);
  yahuddun, Basran third person (Tabari); tuhaddun (Tabari, la
  tuhafizun; Qurtubi's chain). Tabari: all three readings sound.
- ta'am al-miskin: it'am, feeding (five commentaries); ihsan, kindness
  (Ibn Kathir). Qurtubi's households and the poor man at the door; Sa'di's
  cause, shuhh and love of the world; Ma'arif's duty of the affluent and
  the poor's duty to encourage. Each attributed, none chosen.
107:3 vs 89:18 wording (4 of 5 words shared; verb form I singular vs
form VI plural) from quran-json and morphology, labelled as observation.
Licensing sentence in both languages; reader invitation labelled as the
article's own reading.
Hadith: none attached. Ibn Kathir's orphan narration belongs to 89:17;
named, not quoted. Asbab: none.
Dropped: 69:34, 74:44 (no fetched tafsir cites them); "food as the right
of the poor" and a ta'adun reading (not in fetched text); hunger hadith.
