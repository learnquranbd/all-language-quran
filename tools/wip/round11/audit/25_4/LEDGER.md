# SOURCES ledger — 25:4

## Tafsir fetched (all from PREP, not re-fetched)
- **at-Tabari (15)** — USED. "Others" = the Jews, via Ibn Abī Najīḥ from Mujāhid:
  «وأعانه عليه قوم آخرون … يعلِّم محمدا هذا الذي يجيئنا به اليهود … قال مجاهد: يهود».
  ẓulm/zūr gloss: «معنى الظلم: وضع الشيء في غير موضعه … والزور: أصله تحسين الباطل»;
  Mujāhid on the clause: «فقد جاءوا ظلما وزورا … قال: كذبًا».
- **al-Qurtubi (90)** — USED. Speaker = an-Naḍr b. al-Ḥārith per Ibn ʿAbbās, «وكذا كل ما
  في القرآن فيه ذكر الأساطير»; Ibn Isḥāq: «وكان مؤذيا للنبي ﷺ». Helpers = the Jews
  (Mujāhid); Ibn ʿAbbās names «أبو فكيهة مولى بني الحضرمي وعداس وجبر … من أهل الكتاب»,
  with «وقد مضى في (النحل) ذكرهم» — the an-Naḥl / 16:103 tie.
- **al-Baghawi (94)** — USED. Speaker = «النضر بن الحارث وأصحابه»; Mujāhid: «اليهود»;
  al-Ḥasan: «عبيد بن الخضر الحبشي الكاهن»; also «جبر ويسار وعداس بن عبيد … بمكة من أهل
  الكتاب». ẓulm/zūr = «شركا وكذبا بنسبتهم كلام الله … إلى الإفك والافتراء».
- **al-Muyassar (16)** — USED. Inimitability line: «فالقرآن ليس مما يمكن لبشر أن يختلقه».
- **as-Sa'di (91)** — USED. «مكابرة منهم … وهم أشد الناس معرفة بحالة الرسول ﷺ وكمال صدقه
  وأمانته … لا يمكنه، لا هو ولا سائر الخلق أن يأتوا بهذا القرآن … ولم يجتمع بأحد يعينه».
- **Ibn Kathir ar (14)** — USED (overlaps 169). «واستعان على جمعه بقوم آخرين … افتروا
  قولا باطلا هم يعلمون أنه باطل».
- **Ibn Kathir en abridged (169)** — USED. ummi/al-Amīn: "Muhammad ﷺ never learned to
  read or write … grew up among them ~40 years … used to call him Al-Amin"; shifting
  charge: "sorcerer … poet … crazy … liar" + cites 17:48; al-Ḥasan al-Baṣrī: "They
  killed His friends and He is calling them to repentance and mercy"; 25:6 as invitation.
- **Ma'arif en (168)** — NOT USED. Fetched text covers 25:2 taqdīr / the honour of ʿabd,
  nothing on 25:4's accusation clauses; dropped to avoid off-verse citation.

## Hadith
- **Sahih al-Bukhari, Hadith 3** (ʿĀ'isha), URL
  https://quranx.com/hadith/Bukhari/DarusSalam/Hadith-3 . First ten words: "The
  commencement of the Divine Inspiration to Allah's Messenger was". Wording used:
  angel said "Read", Prophet ﷺ answered "I do not know how to read" (mā anā bi-qāri')
  three times, then Sūrat al-ʿAlaq. Grading: Sahih (Bukhari's collection). **Marked in
  the article as a narration on the onset of revelation, NOT an occasion (sabab) of
  25:4** — used only to ground the ummī/illiteracy answer to the borrowing charge.

## Asbab an-nuzul
- Not used as a hard sabab. The speaker is reported (al-Qurtubi via Ibn ʿAbbās;
  al-Baghawi) as an-Naḍr b. al-Ḥārith; presented as an attributed report, not asserted.

## Cross-reference
- 16:103 (Sūrat an-Naḥl) — brought as the passage al-Qurtubi explicitly points back to
  ("وقد مضى في النحل"); its content (foreign tongue vs clear Arabic) given as the Qur'an's
  own answer to the borrowing charge, grounded on al-Qurtubi's cross-reference.
- 17:48 — quoted by Ibn Kathir (169) on this verse; used in §6.
- 25:1, 25:3, 25:5, 25:6, 25:7, 25:8 — context verses from PREP, quoted in meaning.

## Dropped / not asserted
- The individual informant names (Abū Fukayha, ʿAddās, Jabr, Yasār, ʿUbayd al-Ḥabashī)
  are kept explicitly as differing, non-matching **reports**, not as settled fact — the
  commentaries themselves disagree.
- No claim in the article's own voice that any named group taught the Prophet ﷺ; every
  such statement is attributed to the disbelievers' claim or to a named narrator.
