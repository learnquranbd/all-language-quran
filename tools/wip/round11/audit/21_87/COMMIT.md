Replaces the 870-word, 6-section article with a 1,756-word, 8-section
deep article on Dhun-Nun's call from the darknesses.

Sources: at-Tabari, Ibn Kathir (ar + en), al-Qurtubi, as-Sa'di,
al-Baghawi, al-Muyassar, Ma'arif al-Qur'an, all on 21:87.
- Placement between Ayyub (21:83-84) and Zakariyya (21:89); the title
  (Tabari, Qurtubi); Sa'di on praise, 10:98 and 37:147.
- Mughadiban kept as a dispute: his people (Ibn 'Abbas/al-Awfi,
  ad-Dahhak, Ma'arif) or in relation to his Lord (Sa'id ibn Jubayr,
  ash-Sha'bi, 'Urwa); an-Nahhas/Qurtubi "for Allah, not at Him",
  Baghawi's qualifier. Preferences attributed: Tabari, Qurtubi, Baghawi.
  Sa'di links 37:142 and declines to name the matter.
- Naqdira: straiten (Ibn Kathir, Tabari's choice, 'Ata', most per
  Ma'arif) vs decree (Mujahid, Qatada, al-Farra', Tha'lab); readings
  nuqaddira and yuqdara; overpower rejected by Qurtubi, Tabari, Ma'arif;
  Ibn Zayd's question; Sa'di's two senses with his caveat.
- Zulumat: night/sea/belly vs fish-in-fish; Tabari leaves the third open.
- The words: Sa'di's three movements, Qurtubi's readings of
  "I was of the wrongdoers", al-Wasiti with 7:23, tamhis not punishment.
- 21:88 via Ibn Kathir; al-Hasan's stored-worship report via Tabari.
Hadith: Tirmidhi 3505 (Sa'd), quoted whole; no grading in the fetched
text, which the article states. Al-Hakim's version not used.
Dropped: Abu Hurayra and Anas marfu' reports (unconfirmed routes), duration
reports, 37:145-148 plant summary.
Fixed: the old article merged Tabari and Ibn Kathir into a "decree"
reading; both choose straitening. Own-voice "slip" line removed.
