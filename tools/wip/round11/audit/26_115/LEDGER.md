# SOURCES ledger — 26:115 (إن أنا إلا نذير مبين — "I am only a clear warner")

## Tafsir fetched (all from PREP.md, not re-fetched)

- **14 Ibn Kathir (ar)** — USED. Clause: «إنما بعثت نذيرا، فمن أطاعني واتبعني
  وصدقني كان مني وكنت منه، سواء كان شريفا أو وضيعا، أو جليلا أو حقيرا». Grounds §5
  (noble-or-lowly bond runs through faith) and §2 (sent only as a warner).
- **15 at-Tabari (ar)** — USED. Clause: «ما أنا إلا نذير لكم من عند ربكم أنذركم
  بأسه وسطوته على كفركم به … نذير قد أبان لكم إنذاره ولم يكتمكم نصيحته». Grounds §3
  (mubīn = warning made plain, no counsel withheld; warns of Allah's baʾs/saṭwa).
- **16 al-Muyassar (ar, covers 114+115)** — USED. Clause: «وما أنا بطارد الذين
  يؤمنون بدعوتي مهما تكن حالهم؛ تلبية لرغبتكم كي تؤمنوا بي. ما أنا إلا نذير بيِّن
  الإنذار». Grounds §4 (motive behind the demand, re 26:114) and §3 (nadhīr bayyinu
  al-indhār).
- **90 al-Qurtubi (ar)** — USED. Clause: «إن الله ما أرسلني أخص ذوي الغنى دون
  الفقراء، إنما أنا رسول أبلغكم ما أرسلت به، فمن أطاعني فذلك السعيد عند الله وإن كان
  فقيرا». Grounds §5 (not sent for the rich; the poor obedient one is the saʿīd).
- **91 as-Sa'di (ar)** — USED. Clause: «ما أنا إلا منذر ومبلغ عن الله ومجتهد في
  نصح العباد وليس لي من الأمر شيء إن الأمر إلا لله». Grounds §2 (warner's limited
  mandate; the command is Allah's alone).
- **94 al-Baghawi (ar)** — NOT USED. File carries only the verse text (24 chars), no
  reading to attribute.
- **168 Ma'arif al-Qur'an (en, grouped 111–120)** — USED. "Nobility of a person
  depends on deeds and moral qualities and not on family or status"; and, citing
  Qurtubi, "Unless one knows fully well about the morals and deeds of others, it is
  not right to pass any judgment about them." Grounds §6 (deeds not birth; judgment
  caution).
- **169 Ibn Kathir (en, abridged, grouped 111–115)** — USED. "It seems that they
  asked him to drive these people away, then they would follow him, but he refused";
  "whatever is in their hearts is for Allah to know"; "whether he is noble or common,
  upper-class or lower-class." Grounds §4 (the demand and refusal) and §2 (hearts are
  Allah's to know).

Disagreements: none material on this verse. All mufassirūn converge on two axes — the
warner's limited, defined brief (as-Saʿdi, at-Tabari) and the rejection of class
exclusion (Qurtubi, both Ibn Kathirs, Ma'arif). Reported as agreement, not forced into
a dispute.

## Hadith (§7)

- Collection: **Sahih Muslim**, number **2564** (variant 2564c on the fetched page).
  URL fetched: https://quranx.com/hadith/Muslim/Hadith-2564
  First ten words (wording used): "Verily Allah does not look to your faces and your"
  Arabic quoted: «إن الله لا ينظر إلى صوركم وأموالكم ولكن ينظر إلى قلوبكم وأعمالكم».
  Grading: sound by Muslim's placement in his Ṣaḥīḥ (collector's own grading; not
  upgraded). Chosen over Bukhari 660 per watch-point (stock/repeated nearby); this one
  sits exactly on the class-contempt axis (faces/wealth vs hearts/deeds). One variant's
  wording quoted whole, not hybridised with the 2564a/2564b "bodies/hearts" variants.
