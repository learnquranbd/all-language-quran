Nūḥ answers the chiefs with the limit of his office: "I am only a
clear warner." The card and 8-section bilingual article read the
five-word restriction (in … illā) as the whole claim, not a modest
add-on, and tie it to its immediate context (26:113 account, 26:114
refusal to expel the believers).

Sources (all fetched for 26:115, none from memory):
- as-Saʿdi (91): warner only warns and conveys; "I have no part in the
  command; the command is Allah's alone" — the mandate's limit.
- at-Tabari (15): mubīn = the warning made plain, no counsel withheld;
  warns of Allah's baʾs/saṭwa for kufr.
- al-Muyassar (16): will not expel the believers to satisfy the elite
  (re 26:114); nadhīr bayyinu al-indhār.
- al-Qurtubi (90): not sent to favour the rich over the poor; the poor
  obedient one is the saʿīd.
- Ibn Kathir ar (14) + en (169): bond runs through faith, noble or
  lowly; they demanded the poor be driven off, he refused; hearts are
  Allah's to know.
- Ma'arif (168): nobility is by deeds and morals, not family or status;
  do not judge those whose deeds you do not know.

Hadith: Sahih Muslim 2564 — "Allah does not look to your faces and your
wealth but to your hearts and your deeds" (sound by Muslim's placement;
chosen over the stock Bukhari 660 and sitting on the class-contempt axis).

Carries the "licenses nothing against any living person or community"
sentence in both languages; qawm Nūḥ kept as condemned deniers; no
prophet said to err in the article's voice; warner's role kept precise
(warns and conveys, guidance and reckoning are Allah's).

Dropped: Nūḥ's preaching-years (29:14, not fetched) and a 26:121
reference (would preempt the flood of 26:116–120). All gates clean:
1750 EN words, 8 sections, paragraphs 55–110, headings unique.
