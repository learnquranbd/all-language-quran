Replaces the 724-word, 5-section article with a 1,770-word, 8-section one,
from at-Tabari, Ibn Kathir (ar and en), al-Qurtubi, al-Baghawi, as-Sa'di and
the Muyassar on 15:54-56. Slots checked, none shifted; Ma'arif's 15:56 slot
holds only 15:48 and is not used. Shipped card kept.
15:54: Mujahid via at-Tabari (wonder at his age and his wife's), the 'ala
note with 7:105, al-Qurtubi's two views (wonder / real question), as-Sa'di's
"means ceased", Ibn Kathir's wonder and seeking confirmation.
15:55: bil-haqq glosses from all six; as-Sa'di's "mercy and blessings on
this house"; at-Tabari's "accept the good news".
Sensitive line kept attributed: al-Qurtubi and the Muyassar read al-qanitin
as despair of a child, al-Qurtubi and Ibn Kathir state he did not despair of
Allah's mercy. No doubt or despair asserted in the article's voice.
Qira'at: yaqnatu / yaqnitu (at-Tabari, al-Baghawi), yaqnutu reported
(al-Qurtubi); at-Tabari's preference for yaqnitu (42:28, throat letters)
kept against al-Baghawi and al-Qurtubi's "two dialects". No position taken.
Ad-dallun: at-Tabari, Muyassar, al-Qurtubi, al-Baghawi (major sin, his own
ruling), as-Sa'di (no knowledge of their Lord). Licensing line in both
languages.
Hadith: none attached by any fetched tafsir; said in one sentence. No asbab.
Dropped from the shipped article: "seven words" (it is eight), "he does not
say he was not despairing" (Ibn Kathir: he answered that he was not),
33:35 and witr qunut,
12:87 and 39:53 (no fetched tafsir cites them), the errand to Lut's people.
