"من جميع المخلوقات ناطقها وجامدها" + cites 17:44 "وإن من شيء إلا يسبح بحمده"; al-Malik "مالك السماوات والأرض المتصرف فيهما بحكمه"; al-Quddus "المنزه عن النقائص، الموصوف بصفات الكمال"; al-'Aziz al-Hakim "تقدم تفسيره غير مرة"; surah "مدنية"; hadith "كان يقرأ في صلاة الجمعة بسورة الجمعة والمنافقين. رواه مسلم في صحيحه" from Ibn Abbas and Abu Hurayrah.

- t94 al-Baghawi: named only for "مدنية" (file has nothing else).
- t168 Ma'arif (en): named. Musabbihat definition; symbolic "circumstantial language" vs its own view of real tasbih ("Allah has bestowed some sort of sense and perception to every particle ... even to stones and trees"); cites 17:44 "but you do not understand their extolling"; "Only at the beginning of Surahs Al-Jumu'ah and At-Taghabun the present form yusabbihu is used ... The past tense connotes certitude ... The present form connotes continuity".
- Disagreement/range kept: al-'Aziz glossed three ways (Tabari retribution / Muyassar never overcome / Sa'di mastery over all), stated as different emphases, no position. Tasbih: Ma'arif's symbolic vs real is reported as Ma'arif's own position.
- Own-text observations (no attribution): received reading has the four names in the genitive like "lillahi"; Muyassar/Ibn Kathir/Sa'di share the word munazzah (root n-z-h) for al-Quddus, Muyassar uses yunazzih for the verb.
