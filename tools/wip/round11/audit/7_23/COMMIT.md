Replaces the 773-word, 5-section article with a 1,760-word, 8-section
one, from at-Tabari, Ibn Kathir (ar and en), al-Qurtubi, as-Sa'di and
the Muyassar on 7:23, with their 2:37 entries for the kalimat and
al-Qurtubi on 20:121 for how the act is spoken of. Slots checked; the
shipped card is kept.
Words: al-Qurtubi's vocative note (dropped ya, veneration); at-Tabari's
paraphrase (command opposed, enemy obeyed) and his glosses of maghfirah
as veiling, rahmah, khasirin as halikin; as-Sa'di and the Muyassar
(wasted share) set beside him; no imperative, against 23:109.
The act: only the commentators' terms; Ibn al-'Arabi's caution at
20:121; al-Qushayri's report, the minor / better-left / pre-prophethood
view al-Qurtubi calls good, Ibn Furak, and the later scholars' mistake
and forgetting, kept side by side with no preference.
Iblis: at-Tabari's khilaf jawab Iblis, Qatadah's report (repentance vs
respite), as-Sa'di's resemblance lesson; licensing line in both
languages.
Kalimat: ad-Dahhak on 7:23 (at-Tabari, Ibn Kathir), Ibn Kathir's and
al-Qurtubi's name lists at 2:37; the other identifications (Mujahid's
threefold prayer, Ibn 'Abbas via as-Suddi, 'Ubayd ibn 'Umayr,
al-Baghawi's three things); at-Tabari's preference and his reason.
Hadith: none quoted; no 7:23 tafsir attaches one, and the Adam-Musa
argument sits under 20:121 only.
Dropped: the Throne-inscription view, al-Baghawi's years of weeping,
Ubayy's marfu' report (Ibn Kathir: gharib, broken chain).
Corrected from the shipped article: the kalimat credited to Ibn Abbas
via at-Tabari and Ibn Kathir (both cite ad-Dahhak on 7:23), and the
unsourced zulm and "commercial word" glosses.
