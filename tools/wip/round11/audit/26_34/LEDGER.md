# SOURCES ledger — 26:34

قَالَ لِلْمَلَإِ حَوْلَهُۥٓ إِنَّ هَٰذَا لَسَٰحِرٌ عَلِيمٌۭ
"[Pharaoh] said to the eminent ones around him: Indeed, this is a learned magician."

## Tafsir fetched (per PREP.md; not re-fetched)

- **15 at-Tabari (ar)** — NAMED. Used for: (a) "the chiefs around him" = the
  nobles of his people around him — الآية: «(لِلْمَلإ حَوْلَهُ) يعني لأشراف قومه
  الذين كانوا حوله»; (b) the charge relabels the sign as a trick worked on the
  eyes — «إن موسى سحر عصاه حتى أراكموها ثعبانا»; (c) ʿalīm = one with knowledge
  of magic and discernment in it — «(عَلِيمٌ) يقول: ذو علم بالسحر وبصر به».
- **14 Ibn Kathir (ar)** — NAMED. Used for: sāḥir ʿalīm = «فاضل بارع في السحر»
  (accomplished, outstanding in the craft); the strategy — «فروج عليهم فرعون أن
  هذا من قبيل السحر لا من قبيل المعجزة» (promoted it as magic, not miracle);
  then «هيجهم وحرضهم على مخالفته والكفر به» (stirred/incited them against him).
- **169 Ibn Kathir (en, abridged)** — NAMED (grouped 26:29-37; the 26:34 portion
  only). Used for: proof established "clearly and rationally," then force;
  serpent "huge body… big mouth, terrifying," hand "shining like a piece of the
  moon"; "since Fir'awn was already doomed, he hastened to stubborn denial";
  "well-versed sorcerer… one who knows a great deal of magic"; "trying to
  convince them that this was sorcery, not a miracle"; "then he provoked them."
- **16 al-Muyassar (ar)** — NAMED (grouped 26:34-35). Used for: he spoke to the
  nobles «خشية أن يؤمنوا» (out of fear that they would believe); «لَساحر ماهر»
  (a skilled magician). The 26:35 "drive you out of your land" clause is cited
  as 26:35, not as 26:34.
- **91 as-Sa'di (ar)** — NAMED. Used for: he said it «معارضا للحق, ومن جاء به»
  (opposing the truth and the one who brought it).
- **90 al-Qurtubi (ar)** — FETCHED, NOT NAMED. File reads «لا يوجد تفسير لهذه
  الأية» (no commentary for this verse). Nothing attributed to al-Qurtubi.
- **94 al-Baghawi (ar)** — FETCHED, NOT NAMED. Only restates the verse words with
  the speaker named (فرعون); adds no reading. Nothing attributed to al-Baghawi.
- **168 Ma'arif (en)** — NOT USED. Flagged WRONG GROUP in PREP (covers 26:17-26).

## Hadith

- **Sahih Muslim 91a** — https://quranx.com/hadith/Muslim/Hadith-91 (fetched via
  hadith.js, saved to this dir). First ten words of the wording used: "He who
  has in his heart the weight of a…". Clause quoted: "Pride is disdaining the
  truth… and contempt for the people" (الكبر بطر الحق وغمط الناس), from Ibn
  Mas'ud (RA). Grading: recorded in Sahih Muslim (sound by inclusion; no separate
  grade given). **Marked in the article as a general narration NOT attached to
  26:34** — used only to name the disposition (pride = rejecting truth) behind
  Pharaoh's deflection. No tafsir fetched attaches any hadith to this verse; the
  article states this explicitly.

## Asbab an-nuzul

- Not used. No occasion of revelation established for this verse in the fetched
  sources; the article works from placement in the Mūsā–Pharaoh sequence
  (26:29-35) instead.

## Dropped / not asserted

- "Irony" (a true miracle called magic) is drawn as the article's OWN reflection
  grounded in Ibn Kathir's sihr/muʿjiza distinction — NOT attributed to any
  mufassir as "irony."
- Did NOT pre-empt 26:36 (the chiefs' advice / gathering magicians) or 26:44
  (the contest). 26:35 ("drive you out") is touched only as the immediate next
  clause of the same speech, and cited as 26:35.
- al-Qurtubi and al-Baghawi carried no usable reading and are named nowhere.
- Nothing taken from memory; every attribution rests on a clause in a fetched file.
