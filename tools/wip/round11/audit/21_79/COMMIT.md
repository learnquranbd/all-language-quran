Card and article for Anbiya 21:79: We gave Solomon understanding of
the case, and to each We gave judgement and knowledge; and We subjected
the mountains and birds to glorify along with David.

Sources: at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi, Ibn Kathir (ar + en), al-Baghawi,
as-Sa'di, al-Muyassar, Ma'arif al-Qur'an.

The case: at-Tabari (Ibn Mas'ud, Zuhri, Qatadah) and Ma'arif (via
Baghawi) — David gave the flock to the vine-owner; Solomon proposed
the tend-and-return compromise; nafash is straying by night.

Both prophets honoured, neither faulted in our voice: as-Sa'di reads
fafahhamnaha as understanding of THIS case only, not a verdict on
David; at-Tabari reads hukm+ilm as prophethood and knowledge given to
each; al-Hasan al-Basri (Baghawi + Ibn Kathir) — God praised Solomon
and did not blame David.

Disagreement kept as disagreement: ijtihad vs revelation, and whether
every mujtahid is right (Mujahid: both bona fide, Solomon's a sulh,
4:128; the naskh view) — no position taken.

Hadith: Sahih al-Bukhari 7352 (Amr ibn al-As), the two-rewards/one-reward
ijtihad hadith, quoted whole, graded sahih, guarded by Baghawi (reward
is for the effort, not the error) and Ibn Kathir (prophets are ma'sum).

Tasbih: as-Sa'di and Ibn Kathir on David's voice and the mountains/birds
answering; Qatadah's yusabbihna = they pray; Qurtubi on tasbih from
sibaha and the mountains not reasoning, so their glorifying is a sign;
Ma'arif that the joining was a miracle.

Dropped: the two-women/wolf hadith (parallel, not this verse); the Abu
Musa voice narration (unfetched); Ibn Asakir's isra'ili tale; Ma'arif's
animal-damage fiqh and its science aside. No asbab. Did not preempt
21:80-82.
