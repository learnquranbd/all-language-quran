# SOURCES ledger — 18:77 (the wall incident and Mūsā's objection)

## Tafsir fetched (from PREP, not re-fetched)
- 14 Ibn Kathir (ar) — used. Clauses: "إسناد الإرادة هاهنا إلى الجدار على سبيل الاستعارة" (intent to wall = metaphor); "فأقامه أي فرده إلى حالة الاستقامة… رده بيديه" (set it upright, with his hands); "لأجل أنهم لم يضيفونا كان ينبغي ألا تعمل لهم مجانا" (Mūsā's logic: since they did not host us, you should not have worked free); "قرية لئاما أي بخلاء" (mean/miserly town).
- 15 at-Tabari (ar) — used. Clauses: "حائطا يريد أن يسقط ويقع… ومنه انقضاض الكوكب" (wants to fall; same root as a star's plunge); the two readings of ajr — "حتى يقروْنا فإنهم قد أبوا أن يضيفونا" (so they feed us) vs "العِوَض والجزاء على إقامته الحائط" (compensation for the labor). Town named الأيلة.
- 16 al-Muyassar (ar) — used (plain sense): "فطلبا منهم طعامًا على سبيل الضيافة فامتنع… فعدَّل الخَضِر مَيْلَه." Not name-checked by gate.
- 90 al-Qurtubi (ar) — used heavily. Clauses: Ṣaḥīḥ Muslim / Ubayy "لئامًا" + "مائل" + "فأقامه الخضر بيده"; majāz "فكان فيه دليل على وجود المجاز في القرآن وهو مذهب الجمهور"; the deniers "أبو إسحاق الإسفراييني وأبو بكر محمد بن داود الأصبهاني"; ijāra "فيه دليل على صحة جواز الإجارة وهي سنة الأنبياء والأولياء"; Mūsā's word "على جهة العرض لا الاعتراض"; Ibn ʿAbbās "كان قول موسى في السفينة والغلام لله وكان قوله في الجدار لنفسه لطلب شيء من الدنيا فكان سبب الفراق"; Qatāda "شر القرى التي لا تضيف الضيف"; Wahb b. Munabbih "جداراً طوله في السماء مائة ذراع."
- 91 as-Sa'di (ar) — used: "استضافاهم فلم يضيفوهما"; "فأقامه… بناه وأعاده جديدا" (rebuilt it new); "أهل هذه القرية لم يضيفونا مع وجوب ذلك عليهم."
- 94 al-Baghawi (ar) — used. Town names (Antioch/Ibn ʿAbbās, al-Ubulla/Ibn Sīrīn, al-Andalus/Abū Hurayra); Qatāda "شر القرى"; repair variants — Saʿīd b. Jubayr "مسح الجدار بيده فاستقام", Ibn ʿAbbās "هدمه ثم قعد يبنيه", as-Suddī "بل طينا وجعل يبني الحائط"; Mūsā's logic "إنك قد علمت أننا جياع… فلو أخذت على عملك أجرا."
- 168 Ma'arif (en) — used for the town-name reports (Antakiah/Aikah/al-Andalus, "Allah knows best").
- 169 Ibn Kathir (en, abridged) — cross-check for fa-aqāmahu ("fixed it so it was standing upright"), the "mean/miserly" note, and Mūsā's objection. No unique claim rests on it alone.

## Hadith
- Ṣaḥīḥ al-Bukhārī 3401 (Ubayy b. Kaʿb, via Ibn ʿAbbās). URL fetched via hadith.js: https://quranx.com/hadith/Bukhari/DarusSalam/Hadith-3401
  First ten words (EN, quoted portion): "Then both of them went on till they came…"
  Grading: Ṣaḥīḥ al-Bukhārī — sound by the collector's inclusion; not upgraded. Only the wall/wages portion + the Prophet's ﷺ closing wish were used. The reveal portion (orphans/treasure) exists in the same hadith but was deliberately NOT used (belongs to 18:82).
- Note: al-Qurtubi cites the Ubayy narration as from Ṣaḥīḥ Muslim; the article attributes the quoted wording to Ṣaḥīḥ al-Bukhārī 3401, which is what was fetched and verified.

## Asbab
- None. This is narrative; no sabab an-nuzūl claimed. Placement given instead (final incident of the Mūsā–al-Khiḍr journey, before the parting at 18:78).

## Kept as disagreement (no position taken)
- Town identity: al-Ubulla / Antioch / al-Andalus / al-Ayla — left open.
- Existence of majāz in the Qur'an: majority (per al-Qurtubi) vs al-Isfarāyīnī & Ibn Dāwūd al-Asbahānī — both stated, no ruling.
- How the wall was repaired: wiped by hand / demolished-then-rebuilt / clay-laid / rebuilt new — reported as variant wordings of one deed.

## Dropped / not used (could not confirm or out of scope)
- The reveal of 18:82 (orphans, buried treasure, righteous father) — out of this drafter's beat; explicitly withheld.
- Abū Hurayra report (in al-Baghawi) that a Berber woman fed them after the men refused, and that the Prophet supplicated for the women and cursed the men — dropped as a single-chain narrative detail not needed for the beat and not independently graded.
- Wahb b. Munabbih's "hundred cubits" — included but flagged in-text as a detail al-Qurtubi records "without leaning on it."
- Qirā'āt dispute (lattakhadhta / latakhidhta) in at-Tabari/al-Baghawi — dropped as a recitation-variant point not serving the reflection.
