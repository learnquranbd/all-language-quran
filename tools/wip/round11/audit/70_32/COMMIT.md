Card and 8-section bilingual article for 70:32: wa-lladhina hum
li-amanatihim wa 'ahdihim ra'un. 70:19-22 as the frame (Tabari reads
it inside the exception); 70:31 and 70:33-35 only as the Muyassar
glosses them. Same wording as 23:8, noted as a textual fact.

Sources: Tabari, Sa'di, Ibn Kathir (ar), Muyassar (grouped 70:31-35),
Ma'arif (condensed from Mazhari); Qurtubi and Baghawi named for their
one line each. Ibn Kathir (en) skipped: wrong group (70:19-28).
- Amanat: Allah's trusts = His obligations (Tabari), the secret duties
  only Allah sees (Sa'di), salah/siyam/hajj/zakah (Ma'arif); people's
  trusts (Tabari, Muyassar), wealth and secrets (Sa'di), Allah-imposed
  rights and contracts (Ma'arif). Ma'arif's plural note with 4:58.
- 'Ahd: singular in the verse, plural in Tabari/Muyassar, kept general
  by Sa'di; with Allah and with people; Sa'di's "the servant will be
  asked". No position taken.
- Ra'un: Tabari's chain of verbs, Sa'di's gloss, Muyassar's hafizun,
  Ibn Kathir's negative framing; set side by side.
Hadith: Sahih al-Bukhari 33 (Abu Hurayrah), quoted whole, attached by
Ibn Kathir; Bukhari 34 ('Abdullah ibn 'Amr) described in full, since
Ibn Kathir's riwayah gives three of its four clauses. Licensing
sentence on "hypocrite" in both languages.
Asbab: none.
Dropped: the singular reading (not in fetched texts), lexical root of
ra'un, 8:27, 33:72, 17:34 (not cited by any source).
