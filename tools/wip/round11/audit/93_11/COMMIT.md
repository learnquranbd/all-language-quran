Replaces the 778-word, 6-section article with a 1,761-word, 8-section article.

Sources: Ibn Kathir (ar) on 93:11 plus his 93:6, 93:7, 93:9 and 93:10 slots
(fetched this run for the correspondence), at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi, al-Baghawi,
as-Sa'di, al-Muyassar (93:9-11 group), Ma'arif al-Qur'an.
- Ibn Kathir pairs the three commands with the three favours, verse by verse;
  Muqatil (in al-Baghawi) draws the same line in one sentence.
- 93:6-7 as Ibn Kathir gives them: the orphan years; three readings of dallan
  (42:52, lost in the passes of Makkah, lost on the Syria road), none picked.
- Disagreement kept: ni'mah = prophethood / the Qur'an (Mujahid's two reports,
  al-Zajjaj, al-Kalbi; haddith = convey, recite) vs every favour, religious and
  worldly (Muqatil, al-Qurtubi, as-Sa'di, Muyassar; haddith = speak in thanks).
- Ibn Ishaq: the first telling was secret, to trusted family.
- Telling one's own good: al-Hasan b. Ali, Abu Firas (al-Qurtubi).
- Hadith: Tirmidhi 1954 (hasan sahih; also Abu Dawud 4811), Abu Dawud 4814,
  an-Nasa'i 5224, all quoted whole with no upgraded grading. The Nu'man b.
  Bashir report is named and set aside: Ibn Kathir calls its chain weak.
- Takbir from ad-Duha onward per al-Baghawi and al-Qurtubi (not Qur'an; a
  sunnah by solitary reports, recommended not required).
Dropped from the old article (unsourced): fronting-as-emphasis, the 99:4
verb count, the "not with pride" condition, Qarun 28:78, "Allah loves His
favours spoken of". Corrected: Tirmidhi's own grading is hasan sahih.
