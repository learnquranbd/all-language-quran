Card and 8-section bilingual article for 70:13: wa-fasilatihi allati
tu'wihi. 70:10-14 as the frame (the unasking friend, the ransom list,
kalla); 70:15-18 pointed to only as Ibn Kathir explains them.

Sources: Tabari, Qurtubi, Ibn Kathir (ar; en grouped 70:8-17), Sa'di,
Baghawi, Muyassar (grouped 70:11-14), Ma'arif (grouped 70:10-14, text
on 70:10-11). All named.
- Fasila kept as a range, none picked: tribe (Mujahid), tribe and clan
  (Mujahid, Suddi), clan (Ibn Zayd), fakhidh (Ikrima), relatives (Sa'di),
  nearest relatives (Baghawi's others), the clan he was separated out
  from (Baghawi), smaller than the tribe (Abu 'Ubayda), nearest
  forefathers (Tha'lab), a piece of the body / 'itra (Mubarrad), mother
  (Malik via Ashhab; Qurtubi via al-Mawardi).
- Tu'wihi: supports him (Qurtubi, Mujahid/Ibn Zayd), gathers him in and
  makes him safe from fear (Qurtubi), refuge (Baghawi), belonging by
  kinship (Muyassar), mutual support in this world (Sa'di).
- Qatada's order of the list (Tabari); Qurtubi's waqf/bequest question
  (general = clan, specific = forefathers; first more common).
- Ibn Kathir's 80:34-37, 31:33, 35:18, 23:101 framed as cited on 70:10-11.
Grammar checked against data/irab; no qira'at in the sources.
Licensing sentence in both languages: the verse licenses nothing against
any living person or community; the relatives are not framed as sinners
and family ties are not condemned.
Hadith: none attached to 70:13; Ibn Kathir's 70:18 narration left there.
Asbab: none.
Dropped: 3:91, 5:36, 13:18, 39:47 (cited by no fetched tafsir), the
Hujurat cross-reference, Ibn Kathir's 70:17 description of the Fire.
