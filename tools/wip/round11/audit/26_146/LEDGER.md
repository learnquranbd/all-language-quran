# SOURCES — 26:146 (أَتُتْرَكُونَ فِى مَا هَٰهُنَآ ءَامِنِينَ)

## Tafsir fetched (all from PREP, none re-fetched)

- **at-Tabari (15)** — NAMED. Clause: «أيترككم يا قوم ربكم في هذه الدنيا
  آمنين, لا تخافون شيئا؟» → §1 (reads the āya as Ṣāliḥ reporting God's
  question to Thamūd), §3 ("secure, fearing nothing").
- **al-Baghawi (94)** — NAMED. Clause: «(في ما هاهنا) أي: في الدنيا (آمنين)
  من العذاب» → §2 (here = in the world), §3 (secure from the punishment).
- **al-Muyassar (16)** — NAMED. Clause: «أيترككم ربكم فيما أنتم فيه من النعيم
  مستقرين في هذه الدنيا آمنين من العذاب والزوال والموت ... أَشِرين بَطِرين» →
  §2 (the comfort you are in, settled in this world), §3 (secure from
  punishment, ruin and death), §6 (ashirīn baṭirīn = insolent / swollen with
  it, the attitude — stated of the people, not the wealth).
- **al-Qurtubi (90)** — NAMED. Clause: «يعني في الدنيا آمنين من الموت والعذاب.
  قال ابن عباس: كانوا معمرين لا يبقى البنيان مع أعمارهم. ودل على هذا قوله:
  "واستعمركم فيها" [هود:61] فقرعهم صالح ووبخهم وقال: أتظنون أنكم باقون في
  الدنيا بلا موت» → §3 (secure from death and punishment), §5 (Ibn ʿAbbas:
  they were long-lived, buildings would not outlast their lifespans; support
  from 11:61; Ṣāliḥ rebuked/reproved them: do you think you remain without
  death?).
- **Ibn Kathir, ar (14)** — NAMED. Clause: «يقول لهم واعظا لهم ومحذرا إياهم نقم
  الله أن تحل بهم، ومذكرا بأنعم الله عليهم فيما رزقهم من الأرزاق الدارة،
  وجعلهم في أمن من المحذورات» → §4 (admonishing and warning them of
  punishment, while reminding them of God's favours in abundant provision and
  in making them secure from the things to be feared).
- **Ibn Kathir, en abridged (169)** — NAMED. Covers 26:146–152 (grouped).
  Used only 146–149 material: "reminding them of the blessings ... gardens and
  flowing springs ... date palms with soft clusters"; Ibn ʿAbbas on 26:148
  "ripe and rich" / "growing luxuriantly" / "ripe and soft" → §4. Mountain
  homes 26:149: "with great skill" (Ibn ʿAbbas) vs "greedy and extravagant"
  (Mujahid), "no contradiction ... as a form of extravagant play, with no need
  for them as dwelling places" → §6. Deliberately did NOT use the she-camel /
  hamstringing verses quoted at the tail of this file (26:150–157 — separate
  targets).
- **as-Sa'di (91)** — FETCHED, NOT NAMED. The fetched text held only the verse
  words («أَتُتْرَكُونَ فِي مَا هَاهُنَا آمِنِينَ») with no commentary; nothing
  to attribute.
- **Ma'arif al-Qur'an (168)** — NOT USED. Marked WRONG GROUP in PREP (covered
  26:128–137).

No genuine disagreement among the sources on this āya; they converge (world /
secure from death and punishment). The one spread of views — skill vs
extravagance on 26:149 — is Ibn Kathir's own, and he reconciles it; reported as
his reconciliation, no position taken in the article's voice.

## Hadith

- **Sahih al-Bukhari 6416.** URL fetched:
  https://quranx.com/hadith/Bukhari/DarusSalam/Hadith-6416
  First ten words (EN): "Narrated Mujahid: `Abdullah bin `Umar said, Allah's
  Messenger took hold". Marfūʿ wording quoted whole in §7: "Be in this world as
  if you were a stranger or a wayfarer." Ibn ʿUmar's following lines (if you
  reach the evening ... from your life for your death) are his own words
  (mawqūf) and are attributed to him as such, not to the Prophet ﷺ.
  Grading: sound by inclusion in Bukhari's Ṣaḥīḥ (no separate grade given;
  not upgraded). Chosen to avoid the nearby stock hadith (Bukhari 660, Bukhari
  3443 used at 26:123), per the orchestrator's watch-point.
