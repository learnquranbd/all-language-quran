# SOURCES ledger — 19:19

Verse: قَالَ إِنَّمَآ أَنَا۠ رَسُولُ رَبِّكِ لِأَهَبَ لَكِ غُلَٰمًۭا زَكِيًّۭا
"He said, I am only the messenger of your Lord, to give you a pure boy."

## Tafsir fetched (all provided in PREP, none re-fetched)

- **at-Tabari (15)** — USED. Qirāʾa: general reciters of Hijaz and Iraq except Abu Amr
  read `لأهَبَ لكِ` (that I may give, ʿalā l-ḥikāya); Abu Amr ibn al-ʿAlāʾ read
  `ليهب لك غلامًا زكيا` (that Allah may give). Abu Jaʿfar's preference: `لأهَبَ`
  with alif, "لأن ذلك كذلك في مصاحف المسلمين... وغير جائز خلافهم فيما أجمعوا عليه."
  Gloss of zakiyy: "الطاهر من الذنوب... تقول العرب: غلام زاكٍ وزكيّ." All in §3, §5.
- **Ibn Kathir ar (14)** — USED. "هكذا قرأ أبو عمرو بن العلاء... وقرأ الآخرون (لأهب لك)...
  وكلا القراءتين له وجه حسن، ومعنى صحيح، وكل تستلزم الأخرى." → §3 (both sound, each entails the other).
- **Ibn Kathir en abridged (169)** — USED (grouped 19:16-21; only 19:19-relevant clauses used).
  "the angel said to her, in order to remove the fear she felt within herself... I am not
  what you think, but I am the messenger of your Lord... Allah has sent me to you." → §1.
  "It is said that when she mentioned Ar-Rahman, Jibril fell apart and returned to his true
  form" — marked as *reported* ("it is said") in §2. Framing of ʿĪsā as "His servant and
  Messenger... a child without a father" → §6.
- **al-Baghawi (94)** — USED. "قرأ نافع وأهل البصرة ليهب لك بالياء، أي ليهب لك ربك، وقرأ
  الآخرون لأهب لك أسند الفعل إلى الرسول، وإن كانت الهبة من الله تعالى، لأنه أرسل به." → §3, §4.
  "ولدا صالحا طاهرا من الذنوب" → §5.
- **al-Qurtubi (90)** — USED. "جعل الهبة من قبله لما كان الإعلام بها من قبله. وقرأ ورش عن
  نافع (ليهب لك) على معنى أرسلني الله ليهب لك." → §3, §4.
- **as-Sa'di (91)** — USED. "إنما وظيفتي وشغلي، تنفيذ رسالة ربي فيك" → §1. "بشارة عظيمة
  بالولد وزكائه، فإن الزكاء يستلزم تطهيره من الخصال الذميمة، واتصافه بالخصال الحميدة" → §5.
- **al-Muyassar (16)** — USED. "إنما أنا رسول ربك بعثني إليك؛ لأهب لك غلامًا طاهرًا من الذنوب." → §5.
- **Ma'arif al-Qur'an en (168)** — USED (grouped; 19:19 clause only). "Jibrāʾīl had attributed
  to himself the gift of the child because Allah... had sent him in order that he should
  breathe into her collar, and this breath would be the means of her conceiving... whereas
  in reality this gift was by the act of Allah." → §4.

## Hadith

- **Sahih al-Bukhari 660** (the seven whom Allah shades). URL fetched:
  https://quranx.com/hadith/Bukhari/DarusSalam/Hadith-660
  First ten words: "Narrated Abu Huraira: The Prophet said, Allah will give shade..."
  Grading: Sahih (Bukhari's own collection). USED in §7 **only as a marked general
  narration** — the clause "a youth who grew up in the worship of his Lord" — explicitly
  stated NOT to be transmitted on this verse. No tafsir attaches a verse-specific sound
  hadith to 19:19; §7 says so plainly.

## Asbab an-nuzul

- Not used. No asbāb established/confirmed for 19:19; the verse sits within the continuous
  Maryam narrative (19:16-21), so §1 gives its placement instead.

## Dropped / not asserted

- Did not preempt 19:20-21 (Maryam's "how, when no man has touched me" and "it is easy for
  Me; a sign and a mercy"). §6 gestures that the question and answer follow without stating
  their content, per the watch-point.
- Did not quote the later sonship-refutation verse (19:35) verbatim; §6 refers to the
  claim "Allah has taken a son" as one the surah "names later" without citing its number,
  to avoid asserting content outside the ±6 window.
- "One of the five Great Messengers" (Ibn Kathir en) — the number dropped; rendered as
  "servant and prophet" to avoid an unneeded count.
- 19:13 parallel (Yaḥyā given "with purity", wa-zakātan) used in §5 as a plain textual
  observation from the surah itself, not attributed to a mufassir.
