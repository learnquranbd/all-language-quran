Allah as the sufficient Witness when human testimony is refused, and the
true loss of investing belief in bāṭil — to "believe," but in nothing.

Reads 29:52 as the answer to the sign-demand of 29:50-51: they would not
take the Book as proof, so the verse calls a Witness instead of a new
miracle (at-Tabari, Ibn Kathir en).

Sources:
- at-Tabari: witness "for me and against me" (lī wa ʿalayya); bāṭil = shirk
  (also Qatada); khāsirūn = al-maghbūnūn fī ṣafqatihim, cheated in the bargain.
- Ibn Kathir / as-Saʿdi: the testimony clears him — had he forged, He would
  have struck (69:44-47); continued support is the verdict. He knows all,
  so His witness binds (al-Qurtubi: they already concede His knowledge).
- al-Muyassar, al-Baghawi: witness to his truthfulness and to their denial.

Disagreement kept open on the referent of al-bāṭil: shirk (Tabari/Qatada),
Iblīs (Yaḥyā ibn Sallām), idols/images (Ibn Shajara), "other than Allah"
(Ibn Abbas), worship of Shayṭān (Muqātil).

Hadith: Sahih al-Bukhari 4981 (Abu Hurayrah) — "what I have been given is
Divine Inspiration" — the Qur'an as the standing miracle; Ibn Kathir places
it on this passage. Grading Sahih, not upgraded.

Condemnation note: the verse describes a spiritual loss on the Day of
Resurrection and licenses nothing against any living person.

Dropped: Ma'arif al-Qur'an (168) — its text is on 29:48's ummī point, off
this verse. No sabab asserted; wrote the placement instead.
