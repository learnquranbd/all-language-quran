# SOURCES — 26:106 (إذ قال لهم أخوهم نوح ألا تتقون)

## Tafsir fetched (from PREP.md, not re-fetched)
- **15 at-Tabari (ar)** — USED. Clause: «أَلا تَتَّقُونَ فتحذروا عقابه على كفركم به، وتكذيبكم رسله». → §5.1: taqwā = guarding against His punishment for disbelief in Him and denial of His messengers.
- **90 al-Qurtubi (ar)** — USED. Clauses: «أي ابن أبيهم وهي أخوة نسب لا أخوة دين» (§2.1 kinship not faith); «وقيل هي أخوة المجانسة» + «من قول العرب يا أخا بني تميم ... يريدون يا واحدا منهم» + Zamakhshari's Hamāsa line (§2.2 alternative readings); «وما أرسلنا من رسول إلا بلسان قومه وقد مضى هذا في الأعراف» → §3.2 (cited as 14:4, with his al-A'rāf back-reference); «ألا تتقون الله في عبادة الأصنام» (§5, fear re idol-worship).
- **91 as-Sa'di (ar)** — USED. Clauses: «أخوهم في النسب» (§2.1); «إنما ابتعث الله الرسل من نسب من أرسل إليهم، لئلا يشمئزوا من الانقياد له، ولأنهم يعرفون حقيقته، فلا يحتاجون أن يبحثوا عنه» (§3.1 wisdom of a brother-messenger); «مخاطبا بألطف خطاب كما هي طريقة الرسل» (§4.1 gentlest address); «أَلا تَتَّقُونَ الله ... فتتركون ... عبادة الأوثان، وتخلصون العبادة لله وحده» (§5.2 taqwā = leave idols, sincere worship for Allah alone).
- **14 Ibn Kathir (ar)** — USED. Clause: «أي ألا تخافون الله في عبادتكم غيره؟» → §5.2.
- **94 al-Baghawi (ar)** — USED. Clause: «في النسب لا في الدين» → §2.1 (kinship not faith, plainest statement).
- **16 al-Muyassar (ar, grouped 106–110)** — USED, 106 clause only. «ألا تخشون الله بترك عبادة غيره؟» (§5.2); «كَذَّبت قوم نوح ... فكانوا بهذا مكذبين لجميع الرسل؛ لأن كل رسول يأمر بتصديق جميع الرسل» (§6.2, denying one = denying all). Did NOT use its 107–110 content (amīn, obey, payment) — future verses.
- **169 Ibn Kathir (en, abridged, grouped 105–110)** — USED, 105/106 only. "Nuh was the first Messenger sent by Allah to the people of earth after they started to worship idols ... their disbelieving in him was akin to disbelieving in all the Messengers" → §1.1 and §6.2. Did NOT use its 107–110 lines.
- **168 Ma'arif (en)** — NOT USED. Marked WRONG GROUP in PREP (covers 26:88–97).

## Hadith
- **Jami' at-Tirmidhi 2004** — USED in §7. URL fetched: https://quranx.com/hadith/Tirmidhi/Hadith-2004
  First ten words (EN): "Abu Hurairah narrated that the Messenger of Allah was asked".
  Wording quoted whole (taqwā clause): "Taqwa of Allah, and good character" / Fire: "The mouth and the private parts."
  Grading: collector's own — Abū ʿĪsā (at-Tirmidhi): «هذا حديث صحيح غريب» (ṣaḥīḥ gharīb). Reported as his grade, not upgraded.
  Marked explicitly in §7.1 as a general narration NOT attached by any commentator to this verse; brought for theme (taqwā).
- **Sahih al-Bukhari 660** (seven shaded) — CONSIDERED, REJECTED. Already used twice within surah 26 (js/tadabbur-articles/26.js at ~26:19 and 26:95) and in surah 21 / islam-mustahabb.js — overused nearby.
- **Sahih al-Bukhari 3443** (prophets paternal brothers, one religion) — CONSIDERED, REJECTED. Thematically ideal but already used in tadabbur-articles 2,3,4,5,21.
- **Jami' at-Tirmidhi 2616** (Muʿādh, head/pillar/hump) — fetched, not used; off-theme (tongue/jihad) and would touch future obey-ground.

## Asbāb an-nuzūl
- NOT USED. No established sabab for 26:106; it is the opening of Nūḥ's narrative in the surah's refrain-series. §1/§6 give the verse's placement (first messenger after idolatry; same opening call across messengers) instead.

## Dropped / not asserted
- Did not unpack 26:107 (amīn), 26:108 & 110 (fear-and-obey refrain), 26:109 (no payment — next target), 26:111 (the "lowest" followers). Al-Muyassar and Ibn Kathir (en) both carry this material in the grouped text; deliberately left for those verses.
- Did not treat the flood or the drowned son (shipped live at 23:27); kept this verse on the opening taqwā-call.
- Condemned-people caveat ("licenses nothing against any living person or community") carried in both languages, §8.1.
- No claim in the article's voice that any prophet erred (none arises here).
