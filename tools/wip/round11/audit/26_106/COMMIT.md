Nūḥ's opening call to his people: a brother by blood, and the
one question that begins every messenger's mission — will you not
fear Allah?

Card plus 8-section bilingual article (1,738 EN words).

Sources (all confirmed against fetched text, SOURCE-GATE):
- al-Baghawi, al-Qurtubi, as-Sa'di: أخوهم is brotherhood of
  lineage, not of faith — they are his deniers. al-Qurtubi's
  alternative readings kept (akhuwwat al-mujānasa; the Arab idiom
  yā akhā banī Tamīm; Zamakhshari's Hamāsa line).
- as-Sa'di on the wisdom of a messenger from their own lineage,
  and his "gentlest address" (bi-alṭaf khiṭāb).
- al-Qurtubi cites 14:4 (each messenger in his people's tongue).
- at-Tabari / Ibn Kathir / al-Muyassar / as-Sa'di on what the
  taqwā is: guard against punishment for shirk; leave idols for
  sincere worship of Allah alone — taqwā as the message's gateway.
- al-Muyassar + Ibn Kathir (en): denying one messenger = denying
  all, explaining the plural in 26:105.

Hadith: Tirmidhi 2004 (taqwā of Allah and good character most
admits to Paradise), collector's grade ṣaḥīḥ gharīb, kept as his;
marked as a general narration not attached by any commentator to
the verse. Rejected Bukhari 660 (overused twice in surah 26) and
Bukhari 3443 (used in 4 other articles) to stay distinctive.

Held back for their own verses: 26:107–111 (amīn, obey-refrain,
no-payment, the "lowest" followers). Kept off the flood.
Condemned-people caveat carried in both languages; no claim that
any prophet erred.
