Card and 9-section bilingual article for 40:78-79: wa-laqad arsalna
rusulan min qablik ... wa-khasira hunalika l-mubtilun, then Allahu
lladhi ja'ala lakumu l-an'am, placed after the command to be patient
in 40:77 (one line).

Sources: Tabari, Ibn Kathir (ar + en abridged), Muyassar, Qurtubi,
Sa'di, Baghawi. Ma'arif fetched (grouped text covers 40:77 only), not named.
- Consolation (IK musalliyan, Qurtubi 'azzahu). Told/untold: Baghawi,
  Qurtubi, IK; IK's Surat an-Nisa' pointer matched to 4:164 (verified).
- Aya: IK miracle (khariq lil-'adat) as proof; Qurtubi min qibali nafsih;
  Sa'di sam'iyya/'aqliyya; Muyassar hissiyya/'aqliyya; Tabari and Sa'di
  on the demand for signs.
- Amr Allah kept open: punishment (IK, Muyassar), appointed time and
  delay, qila Badr (Qurtubi), fasl and fath (Sa'di), judgment between
  prophets and nations (Baghawi). No fetched text names the Day of
  Resurrection; stated as such.
- Mubtilun glosses (Qurtubi, Tabari, Muyassar, Sa'di) and Sa'di's warning;
  licensing sentence in both languages.
- 40:79: no fetched tafsir links it to 40:78; said so, no bridge invented.
  Animals kept as a disagreement: Tabari (ride horses/donkeys, eat
  camels/cattle/sheep), IK (camels, cattle, sheep; 36:72; camel both),
  az-Zajjaj via Qurtubi (camels). Qurtubi's horse-meat argument and 16:8
  reported, ruling left to an-Nahl.
Hadith: none attached by any fetched tafsir; said in one sentence.
Asbab: none; placement given.
Dropped: Tabari's numbered narrations (Anas, Salman) and the 'Ali report
(no grading confirmable); no prophet count given. Abu Dharr report not
cited by any fetched tafsir, not used. 40:80-81 not pulled forward.
