# SOURCES — 18:50

## Tafsir fetched (all from PREP, read; none re-fetched)
- **at-Tabari (15)** — used. Clauses rested on:
  - "kāna min al-jinn": records the whole dispute — «واختلف أهل التأويل في معنى قوله كان من الجنّ»; Ibn ʿAbbās "clan of angels called al-jinn, from nār al-samūm"; the istijnān/concealment gloss and the khāzin-of-the-Garden gloss; Ibn ʿAbbās «لو لم يكن من الملائكة لم يؤمر بالسجود»; al-Hasan «ما كان إبليس من الملائكة طرفة عين قط وإنه لأصل الجنّ كما أن آدم أصل الإنس».
  - fasaqa: «فخرج عن أمر ربه وعدل عنه ومال»; the ruṭaba/mouse usage; Mujāhid «في السجود لآدم».
  - dhurriyya: Mujāhid «ذرّيته هم الشياطين»; Qatāda «يتوالدون كما تتوالد بنو آدم»; Ibn Zayd «هو أبو الجنّ كما آدم أبو الإنس … فليس من ولد آدم أحد إلا له شيطان قد قرن به».
  - rebuke: «أفتوالون يا بني آدم من استكبر على أبيكم وحسده…».
  - badalan: Qatāda «بئسما استبدلوا بعبادة ربهم إذ أطاعوا إبليس».
- **Ibn Kathir (14 ar / 169 en)** — used. "خانه أصله؛ فإنه خلق من مارج من نار"; ʿĀʾisha Muslim hadith cited here; al-Hasan al-Basrī «رواه ابن جرير بإسناد صحيح»; the Isrāʾīliyyāt caution «وغالبها من الإسرائيليات… وفي القرآن غنية»; ʿAzāzīl / "noble angel" reports flagged as such; fisq = khurūj; rebuke like Yā Sīn 36:59-62 (cross-ref not cited in article to limit ref load).
- **al-Qurtubi (90)** — used. dhurriyya debate «واختلف هل لإبليس ذرية من صلبه»; Ibn ʿAtiyya «ظاهر اللفظ يقتضي الموسوسين من الشياطين»; al-Qushayrī «لا يثبت عندنا كيفية… فيتوقف الأمر فيه على نقل صحيح»; the sound Salmān bazaar hadith «باض الشيطان وفرخ»; Ibn ʿAtiyya «هذا وما جانسه مما لم يأت به سند صحيح» except Muslim's Khinzab and Tirmidhī's al-Walahān; tawbīkh of the disbelievers; «بئس عبادة الشيطان بدلا عن عبادة الله».
- **as-Sa'di (91)** — used. dhurriyya = «الشياطين»; Iblīs's own words «أأسجد لمن خلقت طينا» / «أنا خير منه»; two wilāyāt (Shayṭān who commands only faḥshāʾ/munkar vs ar-Raḥmān); «وأي ظلم أعظم من ظلم من اتخذ عدوه الحقيقي وليا»; quotes 2:257.
- **al-Baghawi (94)** — used. Ibn ʿAbbās "clan of angels called jinn from nār al-samūm" vs al-Hasan "of the jinn, origin of the jinn as Adam of men"; fasaqa = kharaja; Qatāda "yatawāladūn"; Qatāda on badalan.
- **al-Muyassar (16)** — used. "kharaja ʿan ṭāʿat rabbihi wa-lam yasjud kibran wa-ḥasadan".
- **Ma'arif al-Qur'an (168, en)** — used. dhurriyya shows offspring/followers; "some say progeny = accomplices, loins not necessary"; the sound Salmān bazaar hadith via Humaidī's al-Jamʿ bayn aṣ-Ṣaḥīḥayn; al-Qurtubi: armies established by conclusive proof, offspring by a sound hadith.

## Disagreements kept (no position taken)
1. Was Iblīs originally of the jinn or an angel? — al-Hasan al-Basrī (never an angel; father of the jinn as Adam of men; Ibn Jarīr sound chain) vs Ibn ʿAbbās (a clan of angels called "the jinn," from the samūm-fire; "min al-jinn" by his keeping of the Garden / concealment). Both named, Tabari's neutrality noted; Ibn Kathir's Isrāʾīliyyāt caution noted.
2. Did Iblīs have literal offspring? — literal progeny (Qatāda, Ibn Zayd) vs "his offspring" = his devil-helpers (Ibn ʿAtiyya; and per al-Qushayrī the manner of reproduction is undetermined, pending a sound report).

## Hadith
- **Sahih Muslim 2996** — quoted whole (section 4). URL fetched: https://quranx.com/hadith/Muslim/Hadith-2996 (also Book 42, Hadith 7134 USC-MSA). First ten words (EN): "The Angels were born out of light and the Jinns…". Arabic: «خُلِقَتِ الْمَلاَئِكَةُ مِنْ نُورٍ وَخُلِقَ الْجَانُّ مِنْ مَارِجٍ مِنْ نَارٍ…». Grading: in Ṣaḥīḥ Muslim = ṣaḥīḥ. Directly on "kāna min al-jinn" and cited there by Ibn Kathir.
- **Salmān bazaar hadith** ("do not be first into the market…, there the devil lays his eggs and hatches"): reported only as the tafsir deploys it on dhurriyya (Ma'arif via Humaidī's al-Jamʿ bayn aṣ-Ṣaḥīḥayn; al-Qurtubi grades it ṣaḥīḥ). Not independently re-fetched; presented as the mufassirūn's evidence, grading reported as al-Qurtubi's, not upgraded.
- **Khinzab (Muslim) / al-Walahān (Tirmidhī)**: named only as al-Qurtubi/Ibn ʿAtiyya report — Ibn ʿAtiyya says the wider named-devil lists lack sound chains; article states exactly this (Muslim records Khinzab; Tirmidhī mentions al-Walahān), no grade upgraded.
