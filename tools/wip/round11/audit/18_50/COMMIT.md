Card and deep bilingual article for al-Kahf 18:50: the angels
prostrate to Adam, Iblis of the jinn refuses, and God turns to ask
the living listener whether he will take Satan and his offspring as
allies in place of his Lord.

Sources (all fetched for this verse, none from memory):
- The "kāna min al-jinn" dispute kept open with both sides named:
  al-Hasan al-Basri (Iblis never an angel; origin of the jinn as Adam
  of men; Ibn Jarir sound chain, via Ibn Kathir) vs Ibn Abbas (a clan
  of angels called "the jinn," from the samum-fire; named for his
  keeping the Garden / for concealment — at-Tabari, al-Baghawi).
  at-Tabari's neutrality and Ibn Kathir's Israiliyyat caution noted.
- fafasaqa: departure/khuruj from the command (at-Tabari, Ibn Kathir),
  its root arrogance and envy (at-Tabari, al-Muyassar), pinned to the
  prostration by Mujahid; as-Sa'di lets Iblis condemn himself.
- dhurriyya kept as a disagreement: literal offspring (Qatada, Ibn
  Zayd) vs his devil-helpers (Ibn Atiyya; al-Qushayri: manner of
  reproduction undetermined). Ma'arif and al-Qurtubi.
- The rebuke and "bi'sa lil-zalimin badalan": two guardianships
  (as-Sa'di, quoting 2:257), tawbikh (al-Qurtubi), Qatada on the
  wretched exchange. Standing licensing sentence included in both
  languages (condemns a choice, not any living person/community).
- Held to al-Kahf's angle (Satan as protector instead of Allah); did
  not preempt 18:51.

Hadith: Sahih Muslim 2996 (Aisha — angels from light, jinn from
smokeless fire, Adam as described), quoted whole, on "kāna min
al-jinn" as Ibn Kathir cites it. The Salman bazaar hadith and
Khinzab/al-Walahan reported only as the mufassirun deploy them, with
al-Qurtubi's grading, not upgraded.

Dropped: named-devil lists and egg-procreation reports (Ibn Atiyya:
no sound chains), Azazil details (Israiliyyat), the fasaqa grammar
dispute, Ya Sin cross-ref. All logged in the ledger.
