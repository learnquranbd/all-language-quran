# SOURCES ledger — 26:133 (أمدكم بأنعام وبنين)

## Tafsir fetched (all from PREP.md; none re-fetched)

- **t15 at-Tabari (ar)** — USED. Clause: `وأعانكم به من بين المواشي والبنين`.
  Rests the reading of *amadda* as "aided / supplied you" and the gloss of
  *anʿām* as al-mawāshī (herds) and *banīn* as al-banīn (the sons). §1.2.

- **t16 al-Muyassar (ar, grouped 131–134)** — USED. Clause:
  `أعطاكم الأنعام: من الإبل والبقر والغنم، وأعطاكم الأولاد`. Rests *anʿām* =
  camels, cattle and sheep, and *banīn* = children. §2.1. (Its gardens/springs
  clause belongs to 26:134 and was not developed — watch-point.)

- **t91 as-Saʿdi (ar)** — USED. Clause:
  `وَبَنِينَ أي: وكثرة نسل، كثر أموالكم, وكثر أولادكم, خصوصا الذكور, أفضل القسمين`.
  Rests *banīn* = a plenty of offspring, wealth-and-children multiplying together
  (§2.2), and the note that it points above all to the males, "the more prized of
  the two kinds," reported as that society's valuation, not a ranking before God
  (§3.1).

- **t90 al-Qurtubi (ar)** — USED. Clause:
  `أي سخر ذلك لكم وتفضل بها عليكم , فهو الذي يجب أن يعبد ويشكر ولا يكفر`.
  Rests the pivot: God made this subservient (*sakhkhara*) to you and favoured
  you with it, so He must be worshipped and thanked, not denied. §4.1–4.2.

- **t14 Ibn Kathir (ar)** — USED. Clause:
  `أي إن كذبتم وخالفتم فدعاهم إلى الله بالترغيب والترهيب فما نفع فيهم`.
  Rests the targhīb/tarhīb (encouragement-and-warning) reading, the conditional
  sense of the warning ("if you deny and oppose"), and "it did not benefit them."
  §5.1–5.2.

- **t94 al-Baghawi (ar)** — READ, NOT NAMED. Its clause
  `( أمدكم بأنعام وبنين وجنات وعيون ) أي : بساتين وأنهار` glosses only the
  *jannāt wa ʿuyūn* of 26:134 (gardens and rivers). Dropped to avoid preempting
  26:134.

- **t168 Maʿarif al-Qurʾan (en, grouped 128–137)** — READ, NOT USED. Its
  substance is on 26:128–130 (the palaces/high buildings and the Tirmidhi
  construction report `النفقة كلها في سبيل الله إلا البناء`). That hadith is
  attached to the building verses, not to 26:133, so it was deliberately not used
  here.

- **t169 Ibn Kathir abridged (en)** — WRONG GROUP (covers 26:123–132), not read.

## Hadith

- **Sahih Muslim 2963** (variant 2963c / Book 42 Hadith 7070). URL fetched:
  `https://quranx.com/hadith/Muslim/Hadith-2963` (saved in this folder).
  First ten words of the wording used: "Look at those who stand at a lower level".
  Arabic: `انظروا إلى من أسفل منكم ولا تنظروا إلى من هو فوقكم فهو أجدر أن لا تزدروا نعمة الله`.
  Grading: sound by Muslim's own standard (in his Ṣaḥīḥ); grading reported, not
  upgraded. **Marked in-text as a general narration on gratitude, NOT attached to
  26:133 by any mufassir** (§6.1). Chosen to avoid the nearby stock hadith
  (Bukhari 660; Bukhari 3443 used at 26:123).

## Asbab an-nuzul

- None. 26:133 is part of Hud's address to ʿĀd; no sabab claimed. Verse placement
  used instead (within the gratitude-argument 26:132–135).

## Dropped / not asserted

- The intensive/continuous morphology of *amadda* ("supply that keeps coming"):
  cut, because the fetched at-Tabari gloss only says "aided you" — not sourced, so
  the article keeps to "aid/supply" without a lexical claim.
- al-Baghawi's gardens/rivers (26:134) and al-Muyassar's springs clause: left for
  26:134 per watch-point; the list is only named as continuing "into the next
  verse."
- The Maʿarif/Tirmidhi construction hadith: belongs to 26:128–129, not used.
- The "left secure in comfort" development (26:146): kept at the level of the
  brief's own theme ("not a licence for heedless security") without citing or
  developing 26:146.

## Fairness

- §7.1–7.3 state plainly, in both languages, that ʿĀd were a condemned, destroyed
  people (26:139) but that this verse is Hud listing God's gifts while they still
  lived, is a lesson in gratitude, and licenses nothing against any living person
  or community, of any people, faith or age.
