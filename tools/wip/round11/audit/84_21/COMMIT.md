Card and 8-section bilingual article for 84:21: wa-idha quri'a
'alayhimu al-Qur'anu la yasjudun, six Arabic words continuing the
question of 84:20; sajdah sign in the data. 84:22-25 left for their own entries.

Sources: Tabari, Ibn Kathir (ar; en abridged), Muyassar, Qurtubi,
Sa'di, Baghawi, Ma'arif. Slot check clean; no re-fetch.
- La yasjudun: not humbling/submitting (Tabari, Sa'di), prostration
  of reverence (Ibn Kathir), prostrate + accept (Muyassar), do not
  pray (Qurtubi; Kalbi and Muqatil via Baghawi), do not yield/obey
  (Qurtubi, reason for Malik's view). None chosen.
- Ma'arif's scope argument (not the technical sense, else prostration
  at every verse) reported as Ma'arif's, consensus claim attributed.
- Quri'a/al-Qur'an: ayat and speech of Allah (Ibn Kathir), Book of
  their Lord (Tabari); some Hanafis' al- = this verse (Ma'arif: far-fetched).
Fiqh as sources state: Malik not among 'aza'im; Ibn al-'Arabi it is,
Madinans' report from Malik (Qurtubi); Abu Hanifah obligatory as at
other mufassal verses (Ma'arif). No own-voice ruling.
Ibn al-'Arabi avoiding the surah as imam (Qurtubi; Ma'arif's reading).
Licensing sentence in both languages; no verdict on anyone's prayer.
Hadith: Bukhari 1078 (Abu Rafi', chain matches Baghawi) and Muslim 578
('Ata' b. Mina; this surah and al-'Alaq) quoted whole; Tirmidhi 573-574
hasan sahih. Qurtubi's "in the Sahih" form = Muslim 578a. No asbab.
Dropped: Tirmidhi's practice remark, Qurtubi's ma'ruf/munkar comment,
Aisha/Ka'ba hadith and Turtushi anecdote; 96:19, 53:62, 25:60,
17:107-109 (no fetched tafsir cites them). Abu Rafi' left without honorific.
Fix: Bukhari 1078 and Muslim 578c now quote the page's English (were own renderings from the Arabic).
