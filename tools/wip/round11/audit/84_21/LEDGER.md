## Tafsirs fetched (all in audit/84_21/, none re-fetched) and named
- t15 at-Tabari: "لا يخضعون ولا يستكينون" + "وقد بيَّنا معنى السجود قبلُ بشواهده"; "قُرئ عليهم كتاب ربهم". NAMED.
- t91 as-Sa'di: "لا يخضعون للقرآن، ولا ينقادون لأوامره ونواهيه". NAMED.
- t14 Ibn Kathir (ar): "وما لهم إذا قرئت عليهم آيات الله وكلامه وهو هذا القرآن لا يسجدون إعظاما وإكراما واحتراما". NAMED (one-sentence reading of 84:20-21; glosses of quri'a and la yasjudun).
- t169 Ibn Kathir (en): "what prevents them from believing... they do not prostrate due to awe, respect and reverence" (consistent with ar).
- t16 Muyassar: "وما لهم إذا قرئ عليهم القرآن لا يسجدون لله، ولا يسلِّمُون بما جاء فيه"; "بعد ما وُضِّحت لهم الآيات" (on 84:20). NAMED.
- t90 al-Qurtubi: "أي لا يصلون"; "وفي الصحيح : أن أبا هريرة قرأ إذا السماء انشقت فسجد فيها ، فلما انصرف أخبرهم أن رسول الله ﷺ سجد فيها"; "وقد قال مالك : إنها ليست من عزائم السجود ; لأن المعنى لا يذعنون ولا يطيعون في العمل بواجباته"; "ابن العربي : والصحيح أنها منه ، وهي رواية المدنيين عنه ، وقد اعتضد فيها القرآن والسنة"; "لما أممت بالناس تركت قراءتها ; لأني إن سجدت أنكروه ، وإن تركتها كان تقصيرا مني ، فاجتنبتها إلا إذا صليت وحدي". NAMED. Note: whether "لأن المعنى..." is Malik's own reason or al-Qurtubi's gloss of it is ambiguous; the article says al-Qurtubi gives it "as the reason for a view of Malik's", not as Malik's words.
- t94 al-Baghawi: "قال الكلبي ومقاتل : لا يصلون"; Tirmidhi chain to Abu Hurayra "سجدنا مع رسول الله ﷺ في اقرأ باسم ربك وإذا السماء انشقت"; Bukhari chain (Musaddad, Mu'tamir) to Abu Rafi'. NAMED.
- t168 Ma'arif: sajdah literally "to bow", connotes obedience, not technical sense here; argument from scope (would require prostration at every verse; "by unanimous agreement of the Ummah is not the case. Neither salaf nor khalaf"); some Hanafi jurists' definite-article (previous knowledge) argument, called "merely a possibility" and "far-fetched"; narrations differ, jurists differ; "Imam Abu Hanifah ... obligatory duty as is obligatory at other verses of mufassal", adduces Bukhari (Abu Rafi') and Muslim (Abu Hurayra, this surah and al-'Alaq); relays Qurtubi/Ibn al-'Arabi as "obligatory duty"; Ibn al-'Arabi avoiding the surah as imam, "should not unnecessarily split the community". NAMED.

Glosses kept side by side, none chosen: la yasjudun = not humbling/submitting (Tabari, Sa'di), prostration of reverence (Ibn Kathir), prostrate + accept (Muyassar), do not pray (Qurtubi; al-Kalbi and Muqatil via Baghawi), do not yield/obey (Qurtubi, reason for Malik's view), bowing in submission, not the technical sense (Ma'arif, argued). quri'a: ayat of Allah and His speech (Ibn Kathir), Book of their Lord (Tabari), Qur'an replete with clear guidelines (Ma'arif); al- = this verse (some Hanafi jurists via Ma'arif).

## Fiqh (reported only as sources state)
Malik: not among 'aza'im (Qurtubi). Ibn al-'Arabi: sound view it is; Madinans' report from Malik; Qur'an and Sunnah concur (Qurtubi); rendered as "obligatory" by Ma'arif. Abu Hanifah: obligatory as at other mufassal verses (Ma'arif). No own-voice ruling.
