## Asbab
Used: the guest of the Ansari (Bukhari 4889), attached by Ibn Kathir, at-Tabari, al-Baghawi, al-Qurtubi, as-Sa'di. Alternatives reported as al-Qurtubi's list, with his verdict والأول أصح.
The Banu an-Nadir distribution: reported as at-Tabari's (Ibn Ishaq) and al-Qurtubi's (no isnad) accounts, attributed; Ibn 'Abbas's offer report appears in al-Baghawi with "ruwiya" and in al-Qurtubi — mentioned only as one of al-Qurtubi's listed alternatives.

## Disagreements kept
- 59:9 joined to 59:8 as a fay' share (Isma'il b. Ishaq; Qatada in at-Tabari) vs fresh sentence of praise (al-Qurtubi).
- min qablihim: believed before many emigrants (Ibn Kathir) vs before the Prophet's hijrah (al-Qurtubi).
- Ansar who received a share: three (al-Qurtubi) vs two named (at-Tabari).
- mimma utu: fay' (at-Tabari, al-Baghawi), fay' and other (Muyassar), rank and precedence (Ibn Kathir).
- khasasah: gap/window (at-Tabari) vs ikhtisas, being alone in need (al-Qurtubi).
- shuhh vs bukhl: same (al-Qurtubi), stronger (some lexicographers / Sihah), distinct (Ibn Mas'ud in at-Tabari; Tawus in al-Qurtubi; Ibn 'Umar in al-Baghawi).
