Card and 8-section bilingual article for 46:24: fa-lamma ra'awhu
'aridan mustaqbila awdiyatihim qalu hadha 'aridun mumtiruna, bal huwa
ma-sta'jaltum bihi, rihun fiha 'adhabun alim. Placed after the dare of
46:22-23; the wind's work left to 46:25.

Sources: Tabari, Qurtubi, Sa'di, Ibn Kathir (ar; en on 46:22 and the
'A'ishah report only), Baghawi, Muyassar, Ma'arif (one line).
- Ra'awhu: al-Mubarrad's unnamed referent vs "what you promise us"
  (Qurtubi); the punishment itself (Tabari, Ibn Kathir, Baghawi, Sa'di,
  Muyassar).
- 'Arid: Tabari's usage note with al-A'sha; Qurtubi and al-Jawhari;
  Baghawi; Sa'di; Ibn Abbas "wind that stirred up clouds" (Tabari).
- Awdiya and drought: Sa'di, Ibn Kathir, Qurtubi, Qatada; valley of
  customary rain (Ibn Abbas via Qurtubi); al-Mughith (Ibn Ishaq, Baghawi).
- Mumtiruna grammar: al-Jawhari vs Qurtubi, kept.
- Speaker of bal huwa kept as a disagreement: Hud (Muyassar, Tabari with
  Qatada, 'Amr ibn Maymun, Ibn Abbas; Qurtubi with qala Hudun) vs Allah
  (Ibn Kathir, Sa'di, Baghawi, Ibn Ishaq); qul bal reading. None chosen.
- Ma-sta'jaltum tied to 46:22 (Tabari, Ibn Kathir, Sa'di); 42:18 from
  Ibn Kathir; rih as restatement (Tabari); wind from that cloud and Hud's
  departure (Qurtubi); tent and litter image only.
Hadith: Bukhari 4828 ('A'ishah, the cloud and the face), attached by
Ibn Kathir, quoted whole from the Arabic; in al-Bukhari's Sahih, no
grading added. Muslim 899 cited as the same exchange, not quoted.
Asbab: none in the sources. Licensing sentence in both languages; no
modern identification of 'Ad.
Dropped: Muslim 899b and Ahmad's cloud/wind supplications (not quoted
whole), Qayl ibn 'Anz, 46:25 aftermath reports, 51:42, 'Ad geography.
- Draft: closing paragraph reworded so it no longer echoes an unquoted
  supplication.
