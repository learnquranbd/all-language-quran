Card and 8-section bilingual article for 71:16: wa-ja'ala al-qamara
fihinna nuran wa-ja'ala ash-shamsa sirajan. 71:13-15 and 71:17 as the
frame only; the subject is the two lights.

Sources: Tabari, Qurtubi, Ibn Kathir (ar), Sa'di, Baghawi, Muyassar
(grouped 71:12-16), Ma'arif (grouped 71:15-22). All named. Ibn Kathir
(en) was the wrong group and was not used.
- Fihinna kept as unranked views: plain "in the seven heavens"
  (Tabari); a figure of speech, like "I came to Banu Tamim" (Basran
  grammarians, al-Akhfash, al-Hasan: the lowest heaven); "in one is in
  them" (Ibn Kaysan via an-Nahhas); fi = ma'a, "with" (Qutrub, al-Kalbi).
- The faces-toward-heaven reports kept with their differences:
  'Abd Allah ibn 'Amr by Qatada's two chains (Tabari, Baghawi, also from
  Ibn 'Abbas); Ibn 'Abbas/Ibn 'Umar on the moon's face and back, and
  al-Qushayri's report on the sun, "the reverse has also been said"
  (Qurtubi).
- Light for whom: the people of the earth (Sa'di, Suddi), heaven and
  earth ('Ata'), al-Mawardi's two views on the sun.
- Nur/siraj only as far as Ibn Kathir goes (they differ in brightness;
  day and night versus months and years; 10:5), plus Ma'arif's 25:61.
  The bracketed "reflected"/"burning" are flagged as the translator's
  words. No astronomy in the article's own voice.
- Sa'di's conclusion (magnify, love, worship, fear, hope) tied to 71:13
  as the article's own reading, not his.
Licensing sentence in both languages (Nuh's people, 71:25).
Hadith: none attached to 71:16. Asbab: none.
Dropped: Ibn 'Umar's summer/winter heaven report (no chain, not about
the wording); ad-Dahhak's gloss and Ma'arif's "body of the heavens"
remark, for space.
