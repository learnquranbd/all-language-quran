Replaces the 731-word, 5-section article with a 1,755-word, 9-section one,
built only from the eight tafsir files fetched for 62:9 (shipped card kept).

Sources: at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi, as-Sa'di, Ibn Kathir (ar; en abridged,
grouped 62:9-10), al-Baghawi, Muyassar, Ma'arif. 62:1 and 62:8 one line
each. Al-Qurtubi on the address and 5:58; min as fi (46:4). The name from
jam' and the sixth day (Ibn Kathir); Ka'b ibn Lu'ayy vs the Ansar with
As'ad ibn Zurarah (Abu Salamah, Ibn Sirin via al-Qurtubi and al-Baghawi).
The call: at the imam's sitting (at-Tabari, al-Baghawi, Ibn Kathir),
'Uthman's added call, Makhul, Ibn Zayd's two calls, Ma'arif moving the
trade ban to the first adhan; al-Qurtubi on "third" counting the iqamah.
Fas'aw: Ibn Kathir (17:19), at-Tabari's action, Shurahbil, as-Sa'di;
'Umar's and Ibn Mas'ud's famdu with al-Qurtubi/al-Anbari's verdict
(tafsir, not recitation; Ibn Mas'ud chain broken). Disagreement kept:
al-Hasan's intention vs Ibn Zayd's hurry, with Qatada joining them.
Dhikr as khutbah and prayer (at-Tabari, al-Baghawi, al-Qurtubi with Ibn
al-'Arabi, Muyassar, Ma'arif). Sale: who is obliged (Ibn Kathir,
al-Qurtubi), validity two views (Ibn Kathir; Malik vs ash-Shafi'i).
62:10 and 62:11 briefly; no caravan account (not in fetched tafsir).

Hadith: Bukhari 876 (last and first), 636 (iqamah, walk with calm),
912 (as-Sa'ib on the adhan), each attached by Ibn Kathir, quoted whole.

Dropped from the old article: 24:37, the Jabir caravan report and
"twelve remained", "best day" attributed to Muslim (only in al-Baghawi
via Malik), "surah named for this passage", own-voice market lines.
Not used: Abu Malik's Baqi' az-Zubayr occasion report (single report).
