## Disagreements kept (no position)
- Why the five are named: ulu-l-'azm (IK, Muyassar "on the well-known view", Sa'di) vs bearers of law-codes (Qurtubi).
- Scope of "do not divide": Sa'di (agreement in usul and furu'; target is hostility over masa'il) vs Ma'arif (prohibition is at the common core; juristic differences in ancillaries not covered).
