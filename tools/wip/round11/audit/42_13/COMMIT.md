Card and 8-section bilingual article for 42:13: shara'a lakum mina-d-din,
the five named, an aqimu-d-dina wa la tatafarraqu fih, kabura 'ala-l-
mushrikin, and yajtabi / yahdi man yunib. 42:14-15 left to their own
verses; 42:5 and 42:7 not drawn on.

Sources: Tabari, Ibn Kathir (ar; en abridged as check), Muyassar,
Qurtubi (with Ibn al-'Arabi), Sa'di, Baghawi, Ma'arif.
- Shara'a: Qurtubi's road senses (nahaja, ash-shari'); Baghawi "made
  clear and set down"; inner blessing (Ma'arif), greatest favour (Sa'di).
- The five: IK first/last/ulu-l-'azm with 33:7; Muyassar "on the
  well-known view"; Qurtubi "bearers of law-codes"; Ma'arif on the order.
  Nuh not Adam: Ibn al-'Arabi (intercession hadith confirmed, Bukhari
  4712) and Ma'arif.
- Shared din: worship of God alone, 21:25, laws differ, 5:48 (IK,
  Qurtubi, Ma'arif all cite it); Ibn al-'Arabi's list; Mujahid, Suddi,
  Qatada, Ibn 'Abbas "enough for you is what you have been told".
- Aqimu: Tabari's three parsings and one bequest, Qurtubi's fourth;
  act on it (Tabari, Suddi); permanent and settled (Ibn al-'Arabi, 48:10).
- Division: Tabari/Qatada, IK, Baghawi. Kept as a difference: Sa'di
  (usul and furu', hostility over questions) vs Ma'arif (common core;
  juristic differences not covered). No group named.
- Kabura: Tabari, Qatada, Sa'di (39:45, 38:5), Ma'arif. Licensing
  sentence in both languages.
- Yajtabi/yunib: Qurtubi, Tabari, Mujahid, Sa'di (5:16); qadar only in
  IK's words; Ma'arif's two ways (38:46).
Hadith: Bukhari 3443 quoted whole (prophets are paternal brothers, one
religion), with 3442 quoted whole beside it; Sahih, no grading added.
Dropped: Ma'arif's jama'a hadiths and "first in creation" report
(unconfirmed), Sa'di's Companions-as-proof inference, own-voice qadar.
