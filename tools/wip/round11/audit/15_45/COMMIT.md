Card and bilingual article (8 sections, 1,454 EN words) for "the muttaqin
will be among gardens and springs", read as the turn that follows Iblis's
vow and the Fire's seven gates in 15:39-44.

Sources: Ibn Kathir (ar) and as-Sa'di frame the placement (after the
people of the Fire / what was prepared for the followers of Iblis, what
was prepared for the awliya'); at-Tabari and the Muyassar define taqwa as
obeying and avoiding; al-Qurtubi (guarded against fawahish and shirk) and
as-Sa'di (guarded against obeying Satan and all sins) kept as a difference
of scope, no position. Jannat = basatin (al-Qurtubi, al-Baghawi, Muyassar);
as-Sa'di's trees and ripe fruit in every season, nothing added. 'Uyun
glossed as rivers (al-Baghawi), flowing rivers (Muyassar), the four
rivers of water, wine, milk and honey (al-Qurtubi); 47:15 named as a
pointer in the article's voice (al-Qurtubi gives no ref; 47:15 shipped).
Al-Qurtubi's named springs (76:5, 76:17-18, 83:27) and his damma/kasra
reading of 'uyun. Muyassar (15:45-48), Ma'arif (15:45-47) and Ibn Kathir
abridged (15:45-50) named as grouped; 15:46-48 pointers only.

Hadith: none; no fetched tafsir attaches one to 15:45 (the group's
narrations sit on 15:47-48 and are deferred).

Dropped: any link of the muttaqin to al-mukhlasin of 15:40 (unsourced);
Ma'arif's and Ibn Kathir's 15:47 reports. Plain statement that 15:43-44
license nothing against any living person or community.
