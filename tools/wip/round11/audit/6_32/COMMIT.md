Replaces the 806-word, 5-section article with a 1,756-word, 7-section
one, from at-Tabari, Ibn Kathir (ar and en), al-Qurtubi, as-Sa'di,
al-Baghawi, al-Muyassar and Ma'arif on 6:32. All slots checked. The
shipped card is kept.

Placement: the verse as Allah's refutation of 6:29 (at-Tabari,
al-Qurtubi); licensing lines in both languages; one line to 6:33.
Glosses: at-Tabari's "the seeker of its pleasures is at play",
al-Qurtubi's three (short span; its enjoyment; falsehood and delusion),
al-Baghawi, as-Sa'di's bodies and hearts; lahw's root with al-Mahdawi's
objection.
Scope kept as reported: Ibn Abbas "the life of the disbeliever" (via
al-Qurtubi, no chain), "most of it" (Ibn Kathir, Muyassar), al-Qurtubi's
exclusion of Hereafter deeds, Ma'arif on heedless time.
Readings: Ibn 'Amir's wa la-daru al-akhirati (al-Baghawi, al-Qurtubi),
with their two grammatical accounts; ta/ya in afala ta'qilun with
al-Baghawi's qurra' list (Hafs: ta).
Disagreements kept: what is better (deeds, at-Tabari and Muyassar; the
home itself, al-Qurtubi and as-Sa'di); yattaqun as shirk (al-Baghawi,
al-Qurtubi) or obedience (at-Tabari, as-Sa'di).
Hadith: Tirmidhi 2322 (hasan gharib) and 2320 (sahih gharib from this
route), both via al-Qurtubi, quoted whole, marked as general. 'Ali's
saying via al-Qurtubi, no chain. One line to 47:36, 29:64, 57:20.
Dropped: the prison-of-the-believer and finger-in-the-sea hadiths (no
fetched tafsir cites them on 6:32), Ma'arif's al-kayyis wording.
Corrected from the shipped article: the lahw-root claim, the Muslim
prison hadith, unsourced 6:70, 28:77, 7:32, 18:46 arguments.
