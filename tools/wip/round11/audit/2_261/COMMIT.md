Replaces the 853-word, 5-section article with a 1,762-word, 8-section
one, from at-Tabari, Ibn Kathir, al-Qurtubi, as-Sa'di, al-Baghawi,
al-Muyassar and Ma'arif on 2:261. All slots checked. The shipped card is kept.

Setting: the verse returns to 2:245 after the interposed stories, with
2:244 as their spur (at-Tabari; as-Sa'di). Occasion: 'Uthman and 'Abd
ar-Rahman ibn 'Awf at Tabuk, al-Qurtubi only, "it is related", no chain.
Fi sabil Allah kept as a disagreement: jihad (Makhul, at-Tabari with Ibn
Zayd and ar-Rabi'), obedience (Sa'id ibn Jubayr), jihad and Hajj (Ibn
'Abbas via 'Ikrimah), every good with jihad first (al-Baghawi's qila,
as-Sa'di, al-Qurtubi), widest list (Ma'arif). No position.
Parable: elided noun and sower (al-Qurtubi, al-Baghawi), wheat or barley
(at-Tabari), seven branches (al-Muyassar), picture over number (Ibn
Kathir, as-Sa'di), 10 vs 700 (al-Qurtubi). Does such an ear exist: only
as at-Tabari, ad-Dahhak, al-Baghawi, al-Qurtubi and Ibn 'Atiyyah say.
Conditions: sower/seed/soil (al-Qurtubi, Ma'arif), as-Sa'di, Ibn Kathir.
Beyond 700 kept as a disagreement: ad-Dahhak (non-spenders) vs beyond
(at-Tabari's preference, al-Qurtubi's "sounder", al-Baghawi's two qilas);
Ibn 'Umar report ungraded by both; two million unestablished (at-Tabari,
Ibn 'Atiyyah); 'Imran ibn Husayn report gharib (Ibn Kathir).
Hadith: Muslim 1892 (haltered she-camel, Abu Mas'ud al-Ansari; Ibn
Kathir's Arabic says Ibn Mas'ud) and Muslim 1151 (ten to 700, Waki'
wording, whole), both cited by Ibn Kathir. One bridging line to 2:262-264.
Dropped: Ibn Kathir's Ahmad/Abu Dawud narrations, Bukhari 42 (uncited),
qira'at, Ma'arif's Musnad attribution, editor's footnote.
Corrected: "700 is a floor, not a ceiling" stated as settled.
