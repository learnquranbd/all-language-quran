Card and 8-section bilingual article (1,699 EN words) on 63:3: dhalika
bi-annahum amanu thumma kafaru fa-tubi'a 'ala qulubihim fa-hum la yafqahun.
63:4 not touched.

Sources: at-Tabari (with Qatada), al-Qurtubi, Ibn Kathir (ar; en group
63:1-4), as-Sa'di, al-Baghawi, al-Muyassar (group 63:2-3), Ma'arif (group).
- dhalika: 63:2's conduct (at-Tabari, Muyassar); hypocrisy decreed (Ibn
  Kathir); what adorned hypocrisy for them (as-Sa'di). All three kept.
- amanu thumma kafaru: tongue only, hearts denying (al-Qurtubi's first
  gloss, Muyassar, Qatada); before the believers vs alone with the
  idolaters (al-Baghawi); a faith entered then left (al-Qurtubi's qila,
  Ibn Kathir, as-Sa'di, at-Tabari's shakk). No position taken.
- fa-tubi'a: khatm bil-kufr (at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi, al-Baghawi); "because
  of their disbelief" (Muyassar); no guidance or good reaches (Ibn Kathir,
  as-Sa'di); at-Tabari's deferral to his fuller treatment noted, not
  reconstructed; Zayd ibn 'Ali's fa-taba'a Allahu (al-Qurtubi).
- la yafqahun: truth from falsehood (at-Tabari), faith/good (al-Qurtubi,
  al-Baghawi), their own welfare (Muyassar, as-Sa'di).
Hadith: none attached by any fetched tafsir; said in one sentence.
Licensing sentence in both languages: describes 63:1-2 only, licenses
nothing against anyone living, no test for judging hearts.
Dropped: Ma'arif's Banu al-Mustaliq background (belongs to 63:7-8), Ibn
Kathir's Ahmad narration (on 63:4), lexical gloss of fiqh, 2:7 and 83:14
(no fetched tafsir cites them).
