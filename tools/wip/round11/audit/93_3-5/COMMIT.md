Replaces the 780-word, 6-section article with a 1,784-word, 9-section deep article (shipped card kept).

Sources: at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi, al-Baghawi, Ibn Kathir (ar; en abridged grouped
93:1-10 to confirm), as-Sa'di, Muyassar and Ma'arif al-Qur'an, all on 93:3-5.
Covers the occasion, the length of the pause (12/15/25/40 days via al-Qurtubi),
wadda'aka (tawdi' vs the light reading of Ibn Abbas and Ibn az-Zubayr), qala,
as-Sa'di's "negating the opposite affirms it", the 93:4 readings, Ibn Kathir's
abstinence portrait, the 93:5 glosses, intercession, and 93:6-8/93:11 in one line.

Disagreements kept, no position: 93:4 Hereafter vs this world (Ibn Kathir,
at-Tabari, Muyassar, Ibn Ishaq) or each later state better (as-Sa'di), Ma'arif
gives both; the unsaid object of qala (hearer's understanding, at-Tabari; verse-end,
al-Qurtubi); who taunted (Bukhari: a woman; Umm Jamil via ath-Tha'labi and Ma'arif;
two at-Tabari chains name Khadija); a thousand vs a thousand thousand palaces;
the most-hopeful-verse saying from 'Ali (al-Qurtubi) or Abu Ja'far (al-Baghawi).

Hadith: Bukhari 4950 (Jundub, quoted whole from the Arabic; al-Qurtubi calls it
the soundest report) and Muslim 202 ("my ummah, my ummah", quoted whole, marked as
not naming the verse). Puppy, Cave-questions and 19:64 reports given as reports.
Licensing sentence in both languages for the named taunter and the idolaters.

Dropped: Ibn Kathir's gharib on the puppy report (not in the fetched text); the
old article's unsourced "seven heavens", "tenderness" of the omitted object and
biography-stage reading. Defect fixed: the old article gave Bukhari's report as
"a night or two"; Bukhari 4950 says two or three nights.
