## Watch-point 1: glosses kept side by side, none chosen
- sabbih: magnify (Tabari 1; Ibn 'Abbas and as-Suddi per Qurtubi); keep the name from others / idols (Tabari 2 and his choice); declare Allah free of what idolaters/deviators say (Tabari 3; Baghawi; Qurtubi "qila"); keep your naming humble (Tabari 4; Baghawi; Qurtubi); pray with His remembrance (Tabari 5); say subhana rabbiya al-a'la (Baghawi, a group of Companions and Successors); pray by the command of your Lord (Ibn 'Abbas per Baghawi, and per Qurtubi via Abu Salih + "that is saying subhana..."); pray to your Lord (al-Hasan per Qurtubi); pray with Allah's names, not whistling and clapping; raise your voice (Qurtubi "qila", Jarir); broad tasbih of dhikr, worship, humility (Sa'di); tanzih from partner and deficiency (Muyassar); honour the name (Ma'arif).
- isma: connective (sila) (Ibn 'Abbas/as-Suddi per Qurtubi, Labid; Baghawi's "qawm"; Tabari 3 "laysa al-ism ma'niyyan"); naming (tasmiya, ism in place of masdar: Tabari 4, Baghawi, Qurtubi); ism = musamma (Qurtubi's own "al-awla"; Baghawi: used as evidence by those who hold them one); Dhat (Ma'arif citing Qurtubi); a name to be guarded (Tabari's choice). Construction: bi- in 56:74/96 vs none in 87:1 stated as a text fact from quran-json, no conclusion drawn.
- al-a'la: Tabari "no lord higher or greater"; Sa'di "names high above every name"; Muyassar "befits His greatness"; Ma'arif "most exalted"; Ibn 'Umar per Qurtubi "the name of Allah is al-A'la". Article states the texts go no further and adds nothing (watch-point 2).
