Card and 8-section bilingual article for 87:1: sabbih isma rabbika
al-a'la, four words. 87:2-5 left to their entries; 87:14-17 (Eid and
Friday recitation) and 6:108 cross-referenced by number.

Sources: Tabari, Qurtubi, Baghawi, Sa'di, Ibn Kathir (ar + en
abridged), Muyassar, Ma'arif. All slots open on 87:1.
- sabbih: Tabari's five readings (magnify; keep the name from idols,
  his choice; declare Allah free of idolaters' words, via 6:108; name
  Him only in humility; pray with His remembrance); say subhana rabbiya
  al-a'la (Baghawi); pray by His command (Ibn 'Abbas per Baghawi and
  Qurtubi); al-Hasan, Sa'di, Muyassar, Ma'arif. None chosen.
- isma: connective (Ibn 'Abbas/as-Suddi per Qurtubi; Baghawi), naming
  (Tabari, Baghawi, Qurtubi), the name is the Named (Qurtubi's own
  preference), the Being (Ma'arif). Kept as a difference.
- al-a'la: only Tabari, Sa'di, Muyassar, Ma'arif and Ibn 'Umar (per
  Qurtubi), quoted; no theology added, said so.
- Companions' spoken reply ('Ali, Ibn 'Abbas, Ibn 'Umar) and Qurtubi's
  line that it is not part of the Qur'an; Ma'arif: outside prayer.
Hadith: Abu Dawud 869 ('Uqba, bowing/prostration) quoted whole; Ibn
Majah 887 named; no grading on either page, none given. Ibn 'Abbas's
report given via Ibn Kathir with Abu Dawud's mawquf remark; Abu Dawud
883's page English mistranslates the phrase, so not quoted.
Asbab: none. Dropped: Qurtubi's angel and reward narratives (no
checkable source), Ahmad-only reports, 69:52 and 17:44 (no tafsir).
