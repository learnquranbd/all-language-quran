# SOURCES — 26:183

Verse: وَلَا تَبْخَسُوا۟ ٱلنَّاسَ أَشْيَآءَهُمْ وَلَا تَعْثَوْا۟ فِى ٱلْأَرْضِ مُفْسِدِينَ
"And do not deprive people of their due, nor spread corruption in the land." (Shuʿayb to his people)

## Tafsir fetched (from PREP, not re-fetched)

- **t15 at-Tabari (ar)** — USED. بخس = "ولا تنقصوا الناس حقوقهم في الكيل والوزن" (do not reduce people their rights in measure and weight). تعثوا...مفسدين = "ولا تكثروا في الأرض الفساد" (do not multiply corruption in the land) — the BROAD reading. Named in §2 and §4 (the general-corruption side of the disagreement).
- **t14 Ibn Kathir (ar)** — USED. بخس = "أي لا تنقصوهم أموالهم" (do not diminish them their wealth). تعثوا = "يعني قطع الطريق" (highway robbery/banditry), with the parallel "ولا تقعدوا بكل صراط توعدون" (7:86). Named in §2 (wealth gloss) and §4 (the banditry side of the disagreement) and §5 (the "give as you take" symmetry, from t169).
- **t169 Ibn Kathir (en, abridged)** — USED. Supplies the English "do not shortchange them… by engaging in banditry," the 7:86 parallel, and for §5 the symmetry on 181–182: "when you give to people, give them full measure… Give as you take, and take as you give." (Attributed to Ibn Kathir — same author as t14, English abridgement.)
- **t16 al-Muyassar (ar, covers 182–183)** — USED. breadth clause: "ولا تنقصوا الناس شيئًا من حقوقهم في كيل أو وزن أو غير ذلك" (…or ANYTHING ELSE) — the off-the-scales breadth in §3. fasād enumerated: "بالشرك والقتل والنهب وتخويف الناس وارتكاب المعاصي" (shirk, killing, plunder, terrorising people, committing sins) — §4 ¶3.
- **t168 Ma'arif al-Qur'an (en, covers 183–188)** — USED (only its 26:183 portion). breadth: "unlawful to reduce any quantity… whether measurable or otherwise"; the servant/labourer who does not work full time "is included in this warning" — §3 ¶2. The ʿUmar (RA) report Imam Malik places in his Muwatta (ṭaffafta — "you have reduced in measuring" — said of a missed ʿasr prayer), and the conclusion that any obligation done below due falls under the prohibition, linked to Sūrat al-Muṭaffifīn — §3 ¶3. NOTE: Ma'arif's text prints "Surah At-tatfif 84"; the surah number is garbled (al-Muṭaffifīn is 83), so I named the surah WITHOUT a number to avoid propagating the error.
- **t90 al-Qurtubi (ar)** — DROPPED. Text returned "لا يوجد تفسير لهذه الأية" (no tafsir for this verse). Not cited.
- **t94 al-Baghawi (ar)** — DROPPED. File contained only the verse text, no commentary. Not cited.
- **t91 as-Sa'di** — NOT USED. PREP marks FETCH FAILED / WRONG GROUP.

## Hadith (article §6)

- **Sahih Muslim 2581** — the muflis (bankrupt) hadith, Abu Hurayra.
  - URL fetched: https://quranx.com/hadith/Muslim/Hadith-2581 (via tools/wip/round11/hadith.js, saved to this audit dir)
  - First ten words (EN used): "Abu Huraira reported Allah's Messenger (ﷺ) as saying: Do you…"
  - Grading: in Ṣaḥīḥ Muslim; Muslim's collection grading = sahih. No per-hadith grade upgraded.
  - Marked in the article as a GENERAL narration offered for its theme, NOT a cause of revelation (no tafsir fetched attaches a hadith to this verse). Fits both prohibitions: "consumed the wealth" = bakhs; "shed the blood… struck" = fasād/corruption.
  - Quoted whole from the one collection (Muslim); not hybridised. The English in the article is condensed for the 110-word paragraph cap but keeps Muslim's wording and sense; the fuller wording is preserved in the Bengali.

## Asbab an-nuzul

- None used. No established, confirmable sabab for 26:183. Wrote the verse's placement within Shuʿayb's address and its pairing with 26:181–182 instead.

## Dropped / not asserted

- al-Qurtubi and al-Baghawi: no commentary in the fetched files — dropped, not softened.
- Did NOT identify the people of al-Aykah with Madyan as one and the same (a disputed point not in the fetched text); referred to them only as "the people of al-Aykah" / the people Shuʿayb addressed.
- Did not preempt 26:184 (fear the One who created you and the former generations) or 26:185+ (their denial, the Day of the Shadow) beyond stating, generally, that the Qur'an records them as destroyed for rejecting Shuʿayb — with the explicit "licenses nothing against any living person or community" statement in both languages (§7).
