## Hadith

1. **Sahih al-Bukhari 4477** — https://quranx.com/hadith/Bukhari/DarusSalam/Hadith-4477 (saved hadith_Bukhari_4477.txt). From 'Abdullah (Ibn Mas'ud). First ten words (Arabic matn): "سَأَلْتُ النَّبِيَّ صلى الله عليه وسلم أَىُّ الذَّنْبِ أَعْظَمُ عِنْدَ اللَّهِ". Quoted whole (all three questions). Rendered from the Arabic: "وَهْوَ خَلَقَكَ" as "when He created you" (the page's English adds "Alone", not in the Arabic). Grading: in al-Bukhari's Sahih; no other grading added. Attached to the verse by al-Baghawi (Bukhari's own chain) and Ibn Kathir ("في الصحيحين").
2. **Sahih Muslim 86** — https://quranx.com/hadith/Muslim/Hadith-86 (saved). Confirms the same report; named only ("Muslim (86) records it as well"), wording not mixed.
3. **Sunan Ibn Majah 2117** — https://quranx.com/hadith/IbnMajah/Hadith-2117 (saved). From Ibn Abbas. First ten words: "إِذَا حَلَفَ أَحَدُكُمْ فَلاَ يَقُلْ مَا شَاءَ اللَّهُ وَشِئْتَ". Quoted whole in the page's English. Ibn Majah gives no grading of his own; the page shows none; none added. Chain (Isa b. Yunus, al-Ajlah, Yazid b. al-Asamm, Ibn Abbas) is the one Ibn Kathir names for Ibn Majah, but the wording differs from Ibn Kathir's Ibn Mardawayh text (see dropped).
4. Checked, not used: Ibn Majah 2118 (Hudhayfa; Tufayl chain "bi-nahwihi") and Abu Dawud 4980 (Hudhayfa, "ما شاء الله ثم شاء فلان"). One report on the phrase was enough.

## Watch-points
- Firash / bina' given as fetched. Earth's shape appears only as Ma'arif's explanation (round globe looks flat) and the Suddi report's dome image, both attributed; no cosmology in the article's voice.
- "Wa antum ta'lamun": addressees and object of knowledge given per Tabari, Qurtubi (with glosses named); "rivals cannot help or harm / own nothing" via Sa'di.
- 2:21: one section (§1). 2:23-24: one sentence (§8.2).
- Provision: §4.3 says in own voice that the verse makes no claim about favour on one people.
- Licensing: §8.2 says the verse names a fault to look for in oneself and licenses no verdict on anyone else (both languages).
