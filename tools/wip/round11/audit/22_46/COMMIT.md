Replaces the 875-word, 6-section article with a 1,743-word, 9-section
deep article on travelling the land and the blindness of hearts in the breasts.

Sources: at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi, Ibn Kathir (ar + en), as-Sa'di,
al-Baghawi, al-Muyassar, Ma'arif al-Qur'an, all on 22:46 (t169 grouped 22:42-46).
- Placement: 22:42-44 roll call; 22:45 via Ibn Kathir (ad-Dahhak, Ikrimah,
  "close in meaning", 4:78); 22:47-48 in one line.
- Who travels and what they would see: Tabari (Ad, Thamud, Lut, Shu'ayb,
  sunnat Allah), Qurtubi/Baghawi (Makka), Muyassar (Quraysh); hear: the
  stories (Baghawi, Sa'di, Muyassar); Tabari's ears that tell truth from falsehood.
- Body and thought: Ibn Kathir ("that is enough"), Sa'di; Ibn Abi ad-Dunya's
  wise saying; Malik ibn Dinar's Musa report marked as his, with Ma'arif's caution.
- Heart as seat of reason: al-Qurtubi; the brain view he records from Abu
  Hanifa and his doubt of that ascription, kept without a position.
- Fa-innaha: ha' 'imad (Tabari, Farra via Qurtubi, Baghawi); Ibn Mas'ud's
  fa-innahu; Qurtubi's absar/qissa. Fis-sudur as emphasis: Tabari (3:167),
  Baghawi (6:38).
- Blindness: Ibn Kathir (basira), Sa'di, Qatada, Mujahid's four eyes,
  Muyassar's "ruinous blindness", Ibn Sara's two guides.
Hadith: none on 22:46. Bukhari 4686 (Abu Musa, respite to the oppressor,
11:102) quoted whole as Ibn Kathir's attachment to 22:44; Muslim 2583 noted.
Asbab: al-Qurtubi's Ibn Umm Maktum report relayed as chainless, not used.
Licensing sentence in both languages.
Dropped from the old article: 7:179, 47:24, 37:137-138, 6:11, 83:14,
38:29, 57:16, Muslim's "taqwa is here" (none attached by fetched tafsir),
the own-voice aql/qalb claim, the trade-route, media and tourism passages.
