source-gated article (shipped card kept).

Sources: at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi, as-Sa'di, Ibn Kathir (ar, en abridged),
al-Baghawi, Muyassar, Ma'arif, all on 50:16 itself. Covers the placement
after 50:15 (Ma'arif's linkage); al-insan as people or Adam (al-Qurtubi)
and the whole human kind (as-Sa'di); the whisper as the soul's hidden talk
(at-Tabari, al-Baghawi, al-Qurtubi with al-A'sha, as-Sa'di, Ibn Kathir's
good and evil); habl al-warid located by at-Tabari, al-Baghawi, Ibn 'Abbas,
Mujahid, al-Qurtubi, al-Hasan's al-watin and the Muyassar, with the
self-annexation answer; nearness of knowledge and power (al-Baghawi,
al-Qurtubi's tamthil and Muqatil, at-Tabari's two sayings, Ma'arif's
consensus claim and its Sufi/Mazhari report with 96:19); Ibn Kathir's
reading that the angels are meant (nahnu vs ana, 56:85, 15:9), kept as a
disagreement with no position; 50:17-18 per Ibn Kathir (al-Hasan and
Qatada, 82:10-12) and as-Sa'di's muraqabah. 50:19, 50:20, 50:23 one line.

Hadith: Muslim 127a (Allah has forgiven my ummah what arises in their
hearts until spoken or done), attached by Ibn Kathir, quoted whole, no
grading beyond the Sahih; Bukhari 5269 mentioned. Bilal b. al-Harith
report noted, not quoted.

Dropped: the shipped article's "above His Throne" line (not in sources),
57:4 and 2:186 (no tafsir cites them), "the record is kept for our sake"
and "the vein that feeds the brain" (unsourced), and its homiletic
closing section. Ibn Kathir's unattributed "devil runs like blood" report
and Ma'arif's uncollected traditions not used.
