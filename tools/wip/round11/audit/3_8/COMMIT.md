Replaces the 848-word, 6-section article with a 1,781-word, 8-section
one, from at-Tabari, Ibn Kathir, al-Qurtubi, as-Sa'di, al-Baghawi,
al-Muyassar and Ma'arif on 3:8. All slots checked. The shipped card is kept.

Context: 3:7 in one section (muhkam/mutashabih per Ibn Kathir; the pause
on "illa Allah" named as he gives it, no position; licenses nothing
against any living person or community, in both languages).
Speaker: continuation of ar-rasikhun (at-Tabari, al-Baghawi, as-Sa'di,
Ibn Kathir, al-Muyassar, Ma'arif) beside al-Qurtubi's "say, O Muhammad"
and cut-off readings, kept as readings.
Swerving: glosses per source; al-Qurtubi's question with 4:66 and Ibn
Kaysan's 61:5; Ibn Ishaq's "even if we incline in our deeds" with the
editor's manuscript fix; as-Sa'di; at-Tabari (41:46) and al-Qurtubi
against the Qadariyya/Mu'tazila, attributed; Abu Waqid's reading.
Mercy and al-Wahhab: at-Tabari, al-Baghawi with ad-Dahhak, Ibn Kathir,
as-Sa'di, al-Muyassar, al-Qurtubi ("a favour, not for any cause from us").
Hadith: Tirmidhi 3522 (Umm Salamah, hasan; the verse recited by Mu'adh,
a narrator) and Muslim 2655 ('Abdullah ibn 'Amr), quoted whole, no
comment on the attribute. The route with the Prophet ﷺ reciting the verse
and Ibn Kathir's gharib 'A'ishah route reported with their gradings.
Practice: Abu Bakr's third rak'ah of Maghrib (al-Qurtubi from al-Muwatta')
and 'Umar ibn 'Abd al-'Aziz (Ibn Kathir). One bridging line to 3:9.
Dropped: Abu Dawud 5061 (no grading on the page), Tirmidhi 2140 (confirmed,
unused), 18:10, 8:24, 14:27, 3:191-194, 2:250.
Corrected: as-Sa'di's "continuing mercy renewed moment by moment" (not in
his text); the qalb/taqallub root and "a request strengthened by its
matching name" (unsourced); the Bukhari oath "by the Turner of hearts"
(not confirmed here); the speaker given as settled.
