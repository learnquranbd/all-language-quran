Card and 8-section bilingual article for 74:11. This key owns the
identification of the man and the sense of wahidan; 74:12-15 (wealth,
sons, wanting more) and 74:18-25 (deliberation, "magic") are named only
as context for the parallel 74:15 and 74:23 drafts.

Sources: Tabari, Qurtubi, Ibn Kathir (ar; en grouped 74:11-20), Sa'di,
Baghawi, Muyassar, Ma'arif (grouped 74:7-11). All named.
- Identification: al-Walid ibn al-Mughira (al-Makhzumi in Tabari,
  Qurtubi, Baghawi, Ibn Kathir en) in every fetched source; Qurtubi
  calls it the commentators' position. No differing name exists to keep.
- dharni: threat (Qurtubi "wa'id wa tahdid", Ibn Kathir) vs handing the
  matter to Allah, addressed to the Prophet (Tabari "kil ya Muhammad",
  Muyassar); kept side by side, no ranking.
- wahidan: alone in the womb without wealth or child (Mujahid, Qatada,
  Tabari, Ibn Kathir, Baghawi, Muyassar, Sa'di); referring to Allah, in
  two senses (a group in Qurtubi); raised alone as created alone (qila);
  his nickname al-Wahid, by his own claim, not confirmed (Ibn 'Abbas in
  Qurtubi; Baghawi).
- Review fix: cut Qurtubi's qila "father unknown" sense (tied to 68:13);
  lineage-sensitive and weakly marked; merged the leftover sentence.
- Mujahid's "and so are all creatures" (Tabari) carries the general lesson.
Asbab: Tabari's Ibn 'Abbas chain (Sa'id ibn Jubayr or 'Ikrima doubt as
written) and Ibn Kathir's al-'Awfi report, both stated as ungraded.
Hadith: none attached in any fetched tafsir; said in one sentence.
Licensing sentence in EN and BN; Ibn Kathir en's curse not reproduced.
Dropped: chain gradings (none in the texts), unsourced data/nuzul summary.
