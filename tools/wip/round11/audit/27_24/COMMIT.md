The hoopoe reports Sheba prostrating to the sun, and names the
mechanism of their misguidance: Satan adorned their deeds, so he
barred them from the way, so they are not guided.

Sources (all fetched for 27:24):
- at-Tabari: zayyana unpacked as ḥassana + ḥabbaba — Iblis beautified
  the sun-worship and made it beloved; by that adorning he blocked the
  straight road (the dīn Allah sent prophets with); they keep wavering
  (yataraddadūn) in their misguidance.
- al-Muyassar: he beautified the evil deeds they were already doing —
  the act relabelled, not the knowledge changed.
- as-Saʿdi: the hinge — they SAW what they were upon as the truth; no
  prospect of guidance until the belief itself changes.
- al-Qurtubi: sabīl = the way of tawḥīd; what is not that path is no
  beneficial path at all.
- Ibn Kathir: Sheba = Himyar in Yemen; the sun-oriented palace; the
  41:37 cross-reference; the hoopoe, calling to good, protected from
  killing.
- Maʿarif: star-worshippers; some say Zoroastrians.

Disagreement kept (unresolved): the people's exact creed — sun-
worshippers/heretics vs Magians/light-worshippers (al-Qurtubi) vs
Zoroastrians (Maʿarif).

Hadith: Sunan Abu Dawud 5267 (Ibn Abbas) — the Prophet forbade killing
four creatures: ants, bees, hoopoes, sparrow-hawks. No grading on the
fetched page, so none asserted; chose it over the stock Bukhari 660 /
3443 as it is directly about the hudhud.

Licenses-nothing sentence carried in both languages: the verse condemns
the shirk, not a people; this same people is later guided, the door is
not sealed.

Dropped: al-Baghawi (verse text only, no commentary); al-Qurtubi's
27:23 waqf grammar (off-verse); Ibn Kathir's "360 windows" count.
Asbab: none established; verse placement used instead.
