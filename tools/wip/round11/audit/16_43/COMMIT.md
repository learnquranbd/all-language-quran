Card and bilingual article (8 sections, 1,784 EN words) for "So ask the
people of the Reminder if you do not know". The first half nearly repeats
12:109, whose article already covers angels, messengers who ate food,
women prophets and the people of the towns; one pointer, nothing repeated.

Sources: at-Tabari (men not angels; Quraysh addressed; ahl adh-dhikr =
readers of Torah and Injil; Mujahid, al-A'mash, Ibn Abbas "Muhammad is in
the Torah and Injil"; Ibn Zayd and Abu Ja'far under "others said");
Ibn Kathir (ad-Dahhak < Ibn Abbas logic; Ibn Zayd true but not meant here;
al-Baqir = this ummah); al-Qurtubi (Makkan occasion; Sufyan; wa qila People
of the Book; Ibn Abbas ahl al-Qur'an; wa qila ahl al-'ilm); al-Baghawi;
as-Sa'di (generality: praise of scholars, their tazkiya and duty); the
Muyassar (general in every religious question); Ma'arif (dhikr = knowledge;
Rummani, Zajjaj, Azhari; Ruh al-Ma'ani envoys; its taqlid section,
attributed and bounded). Ibn Kathir abridged grouped 16:43-44; said so,
16:44 left as a pointer.

Disagreements kept: three identities of ahl adh-dhikr with names; Ibn
Kathir vs as-Sa'di on the Qur'an reading; what was to be asked. No
position. Taqlid/ijtihad reported only as Ma'arif states it, with the
other sources' silence noted (al-Qurtubi's fetched entry has none); no
ruling in the article's voice.

Hadith: none attached by any fetched tafsir; said so. Abu Dawud 336
(cure for not knowing is asking) fetched, ungraded on page, general;
dropped. No asbab beyond the commentators' own "nazalat fi mushriki Makka".
Licensing sentence in both languages (no verdict on any living community).
