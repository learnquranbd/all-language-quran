## Asbab an-nuzul
No established asbab narration used. Al-Qurtubi and al-Baghawi state "nazalat fi mushriki Makka" without isnad; reported as their statement (§1.2). The ad-Dahhak < Ibn Abbas report (Ibn Kathir, at-Tabari) is given as a report explaining the logic, not as a graded sabab. Ma'arif's Ruh al-Ma'ani envoys report is attributed to Ma'arif citing Ruh al-Ma'ani; timing ("after the revelation of the first verse cited above") dropped as ambiguous.

## Disagreements kept, no position
- Identity of ahl adh-dhikr: earlier scriptures' people (at-Tabari, Mujahid, al-A'mash, Ibn Abbas via Mujahid/ad-Dahhak, Ibn Kathir) / the believers among them only (Sufyan, al-A'mash, al-Baghawi) vs the People of the Book generally (al-Qurtubi's "wa qila") / the Qur'an's people (Ibn Zayd, Ibn Abbas per al-Qurtubi, Abu Ja'far) / people of knowledge (al-Qurtubi "wa qila"; Ma'arif via Rummani, Zajjaj, Azhari). Article: "this article does not choose among them" (§4.2).
- Ibn Kathir (Qur'an reading true but not meant here) vs as-Sa'di (people of the Qur'an are the best of ahl adh-dhikr in the wider sense).
- What is asked: messengers' humanity (most) vs the Prophet's mention in Torah/Injil (Ibn Abbas via Mujahid in at-Tabari).
- General principle / taqlid: Muyassar, as-Sa'di, Ma'arif attributed; al-Qurtubi's fetched entry silent (said so). No ruling in the article's voice.

## Licensing
§5.2, both languages: the address is to Makkan deniers in one argument; the verse passes no judgment on any living religious community and licenses nothing against any person or group.
