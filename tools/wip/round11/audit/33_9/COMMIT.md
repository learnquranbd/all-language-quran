Card and deep bilingual article on the Battle of the Trench verse,
built only from the tafsir fetched for 33:9.

Sources gave: at-Tabari names the confederate armies (Quraysh, Ghatafān,
Jews of Banū an-Naḍīr) and the wind as rīḥ aṣ-ṣabā, the east wind, that
overturned the besiegers' pots and tore up their tents (Mujāhid, ʿIkrimah).
al-Muyassar and al-Qurtubi echo the uprooted tents and flung pots; Qurtubi
adds that the believers lay only a trench's width from the storm yet were
kept safe and unaware of it. as-Sa'di reads the āyah as a call to gratitude
(shukr) — the spine of the lesson. "junūdan lam tarawhā" = the angels, agreed
by Mujāhid, Qatādah and Yazīd b. Rūmān, who "did not fight that day"; al-Baghawi
adds their multiplying takbīr. at-Tabari's close reads Baṣīr as Allah seeing the
believers' patience and steadfastness.

Disagreement kept: date of al-Khandaq — Shawwāl 5 AH (Ibn Kathir, well-known)
vs 4 AH (Mūsā b. ʿUqbah).

Hadith: Sahih Muslim 1788 (Ḥudhayfah's night scouting in the cold), quoted in
Muslim's wording alone, graded sound by its place in the Ṣaḥīḥ, not upgraded.

Dropped: a Bukhari number for "nuṣirtu biṣ-ṣabā" (hadith.js timed out on Bukhari,
so the saba-victory wording is kept only as the mufassirūn's report from Ibn
ʿAbbās, no number asserted); Ma'arif al-Qur'an (its content is on 33:7-8, the
prophets' covenant, not 33:9); any count or name of the angels.

Lesson held on gratitude for the unseen help when human means were spent; the
fear of 33:10-11 kept as context, not retold as the point. Not a condemnation
verse — no licensing line needed.
