Card and 8-section bilingual article for 44:51: inna al-muttaqina fi
maqamin amin. Five words; the turn from 44:47-50 named, 44:43 in one
contrasting line, 44:52-57 placed in one line.

Sources: Tabari (with Qatada), Qurtubi, Baghawi, Ibn Kathir (ar + en),
Muyassar, Sa'di, Ma'arif (44:51 sentence only).
- The turn: Ibn Kathir (wretched -> blessed, hence mathani); Qurtubi
  (abode of the disbelievers / lodging of the believers); Sa'di (the
  reward of the muttaqin).
- Al-muttaqin as the texts define them: Tabari, Muyassar (in this
  world, the station in the Hereafter), Ibn Kathir, Sa'di (displeasure
  removed, pleasure established).
- Readings: muqam (damma) / maqam (fatha). Tabari: Madinah vs Kufah
  and Basrah, both widespread and sound, no preference. Qurtubi: Nafi'
  and Ibn 'Amir damma, the rest fatha. Baghawi: Madinah and al-Sham
  damma (verbal noun, residing), the rest fatha (secure sitting-place).
  Qurtubi's al-Kisa'i and al-Jawhari on place vs residing reported.
- Amin kept as differences: harms (Qurtubi); harms and griefs
  (Muyassar); the world's aches, illness, weariness, grief (Tabari);
  the Shaytan (Qatada); change: death and leaving (Baghawi); all of
  these (Ibn Kathir); free from fear (Ma'arif).
- Licensing sentence EN+BN: no verdict on any living person, and no
  self-awarded title of muttaqin.
Hadith: none attached by any tafsir; said in one sentence.
Asbab: none in the sources; not used.
Dropped: Tabari's "secure from departure" clause (editor marks it not
in the original); 15:45 and other parallels (uncited by the sources);
Paradise detail of 44:52-57; the poet of al-Kisa'i's verse.
