## Hadith
- Sahih al-Bukhari 5976 (Abu Bakrah). URL https://quranx.com/hadith/Bukhari/DarusSalam/Hadith-5976 (saved: hadith_Bukhari_5976.txt). First ten words of the wording used: "Shall I not tell you of the greatest of the major". Translated whole from the Arabic on the page; it carries "ألا وقول الزور وشهادة الزور" twice, matching Ibn Kathir's quotation. Attached by Ibn Kathir (ar + en) to the testimony reading, "in the two Sahihs". Grading: its place in al-Bukhari's Sahih; nothing added. (Bukhari 2654 was also fetched; its wording has only "قول الزور", so 5976 was used. The 2654 file was deleted.)
- Ibrahim ibn Maysarah report on Ibn Mas'ud: given only as the tafsirs give it (Ibn Abi Hatim via Ibn Kathir, two chains, the second "بلغني"; at-Tabari; al-Qurtubi "روي"). No grading stated by any source; the article says so and calls it an illustration. Not treated as a hadith from a collection.
- Sentence that no further hadith is attached to the second half: present (8.2).
