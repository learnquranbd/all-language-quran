Card and 8-section bilingual article for 43:11-12: rain sent down
bi-qadar, the dead land revived (ansharna), kadhalika tukhrajun, al-
azwaj kullaha, and ships and livestock ma tarkabun. 43:9-10 as lead-in;
43:13-14 context only (riding words not quoted, no riding hadith).

Sources: Tabari, Ibn Kathir (ar; en abridged for 43:9 only), Muyassar,
Qurtubi, Sa'di, Baghawi, Ma'arif.
- Bi-qadar: measure of need (Tabari, IK, Sa'di); not the flood of
  Nuh's people (Tabari, Qurtubi from Ibn 'Abbas, Baghawi; Muyassar
  without naming them). Licensing sentence in both languages.
- Ansharna = ahyayna (Tabari, Qurtubi, Sa'di); nasharat al-ard and
  al-A'sha's line (Tabari); land described in 22:5's words (IK).
- Tukhrajun: IK, Tabari with Qatada, Muyassar and Baghawi "from your
  graves", Qurtubi "whoever can do this can do that", Sa'di barzakh and
  recompense; Qurtubi's active/passive qira'a report.
- Azwaj kept as a disagreement: pairing (Tabari, Ibn 'Isa), kinds
  (Sa'id ibn Jubayr, Baghawi, Muyassar, IK; plants with 50:7, 26:7),
  opposites and human states (al-Hasan, qila; Qurtubi prefers the
  last); Sa'di in 36:36's words. No biology claims.
- Ships/cattle: Tabari sea/land and four animals, IK meat/milk/riding,
  Sa'di sail and fire ships, Ma'arif man-made vehicles to spacecraft.
Hadith: none attached by any tafsir; said in one sentence. No asbab.
Dropped: 35:9 and 30:19 (no tafsir cites them), ma tarkabun gender
note (not fetched), Qurtubi's al-A'raf pointer (trimmed).
