Card and 8-section bilingual article for 40:15: rafi' ad-darajat dhu
al-'arsh, yulqi ar-ruha min amrihi 'ala man yasha'u min 'ibadihi,
li-yundhira yawm at-talaq.

Sources: Ibn Kathir (ar + en abridged, 40:15 portion only), Tabari,
Qurtubi, Sa'di, Baghawi, Muyassar, Ma'arif. All named.
- Placement after 40:14: Tabari (nominative, accusative also correct),
  Qurtubi (implied subject, Akhfash's accusative of praise), Sa'di.
- Rafi' ad-darajat, both readings kept, no position: exalted in
  attributes/standing (Qurtubi via Halimi, Muyassar, Sa'di; seven heavens
  from Ibn Abbas, Kalbi, Sa'id b. Jubayr) vs Raiser of ranks (Yahya b.
  Sallam via Qurtubi, Baghawi; Ma'arif gives both, 6:83, 3:163).
- Dhu al-'Arsh in the sources' words only: Tabari, Qurtubi (not in need
  of it; thulla 'arshuhu = permanence of dominion), Baghawi, Muyassar,
  Sa'di, Ibn Kathir (70:3, 70:4).
- Ar-ruh, each gloss attributed, none chosen: revelation (Qatada), Book
  (Dahhak), Qur'an (Ibn Zayd, 42:52, 26:193), prophethood (Suddi), Jibril
  (Qurtubi qila, 16:102); Tabari: close in meaning. Why "ruh": Qurtubi,
  Baghawi, Sa'di. Min amrihi: decree/word/by His command.
- Li-yundhira subject (Qurtubi, Tabari, Baghawi); li-tundhira reading
  (Qurtubi: Ibn Abbas, Hasan, Ibn as-Samayfa'; Baghawi: Ya'qub).
- Yawm at-talaq, every meeting attributed: heaven/earth, Creator/created,
  Adam and his last descendant, servants, first and last, wrongdoer and
  wronged (+ disputants), worshippers/worshipped, person and deed.
Hadith: none attached by any fetched tafsir; said in one sentence.
Dropped: Ibn Kathir's red-ruby Throne report and hadith al-aw'al; the
grouped 40:16-17 material (Ibn 'Umar, as-Sur, Abu Dharr). Asbab: none.
40:16 not pulled forward; 40:8 not used.
Themes: majesty, hereafter.
