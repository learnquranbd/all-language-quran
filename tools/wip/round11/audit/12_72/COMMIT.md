Card and 8-section article (1,738 EN words, 19 paragraphs, 55-110 each).
12:72 is 12 Arabic words, per PREP.md.

Sources: all eight tafsir IDs; 169 is grouped 12:70-72, stated, and only
its 12:72 portion used. Readings of suwa' (Abu Hurayra, Abu Raja', Yahya
ibn Ya'mar) with at-Tabari accepting only suwa'. Vessel material kept as
reported: silver (Ibn Abbas via Sa'id, Ikrima; "majority" in Ibn Kathir
en), gold ("some"), copper (one Ibn Abbas chain), Persian makkuk (Sa'id).
Ba'ir: camel (most, per al-Qurtubi) vs donkey (Mujahid, a dialect use).
Za'im = kafil (Ibn Abbas, Sa'id, Qatada, ad-Dahhak, Mujahid); the "I" is
the caller (Mujahid, as-Sa'di, al-Baghawi, Ibn Ishaq's "messenger");
al-Qurtubi: the guarantor was other than Yusuf. No source makes it Yusuf.
Fiqh reported, none issued: Ibn Kathir's ju'ala / daman-kafala labels;
al-Qurtubi's two proofs, ju'ala vs ijara, al-Shafi'i's dissent on the
customary wage; kafala disagreements (Kufans and Tahawi vs Malik, al-Layth,
al-Awza'i; claim-from-either vs Malik's later view; Ibn Abi Layla); no
kafala in hudud with Abu Yusuf and Muhammad dissenting. Al-Qurtubi's
"perhaps valid in their law" given; the wider shar' man qablana question
stated as not taken up. Rulings referred to qualified scholars.

No fetched tafsir attaches a sound hadith. Bukhari 2289 (Abu Qatada's
guarantee, summarised, not quoted) and Bukhari 33 (signs of a hypocrite,
quoted whole minus the translator's list numerals), both confirmed on
quranx DarusSalam, marked not attached.

the verse licenses nothing against any living person or community.
