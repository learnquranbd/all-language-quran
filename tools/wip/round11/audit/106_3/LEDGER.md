  إما لا فليعبدوه لإيلافهم؛ على معنى أن نعم الله تعالى عليهم لا تحصى، فإن لم يعبدوه لسائر

  فذكر لهم ذلك، تذكيرا لنعمته". "أمرهم الله تعالى بعبادته وتوحيده، لأجل إيلافهم رحلتين".

  ألفوا رحلة إلى بصرى ورحلة إلى اليمن، فقيل لهم: فليعبدوا رب هذا البيت أي يقيموا بمكة".

## Glosses kept side by side (none chosen)
- fa: conditional sense, "if nothing else, this" (al-Qurtubi); gratitude for the favour
  (Ibn Kathir, as-Sa'di, Muyassar); the fa as extra, governing li-ilafi (al-Khalil in
  al-Qurtubi); Kufan reading with this verse as evidence (at-Tabari).
- li-ilafi: attached to fa-l-ya'budu (al-Qurtubi's "qila", al-Khalil); attached to 105:5 /
  surah connected (Basran in at-Tabari; al-Farra', Ubayy in al-Qurtubi; Ibn Ishaq, Ibn Zayd
  in Ibn Kathir en); lam of wonder (at-Tabari's own choice; al-Kisa'i, al-Akhfash in
  al-Qurtubi); sense of ila (al-Qurtubi's "qila").
- rabb hadha al-bayt: idols distinguished vs reminder of honour (al-Qurtubi's two aspects);
  pride/honour (Muyassar); source of blessings (Ma'arif); safe sanctuary + 27:91 (Ibn Kathir).
- the command: tawhid/ikhlas (Ibn Kathir, as-Sa'di, Muyassar, al-Qurtubi); stay in Makkah
  (at-Tabari; Ikrimah in al-Qurtubi); become accustomed to worship (Ibn Abbas via Ikrimah in
  at-Tabari; "qila" in al-Qurtubi).
