Card and 8-section bilingual article for 106:3: fa-l-ya'budu rabba
hadha al-bayt, four words. 106:1-2 named as what precedes and 106:4 as
what follows; neither is treated.

Sources: Tabari, Qurtubi, Ibn Kathir (ar + en abridged), Sa'di,
Muyassar, Baghawi, Ma'arif on 106:3; Tabari and Qurtubi on 106:1 for
the grammar only. Slot check: all Arabic slots discuss 106:3.
- fa: conditional "if nothing else, this" (Qurtubi) beside thanks for
  the favour (Ibn Kathir, Sa'di, Muyassar). None chosen.
- li-ilafi: tied to this verse with an extra fa (al-Khalil in Qurtubi),
  to 105:5 (Basran in Tabari; al-Farra', Ubayy in Qurtubi; Ibn Ishaq,
  Ibn Zayd in Ibn Kathir), lam of wonder (Tabari's choice; Kisa'i,
  Akhfash). Tabari's consensus argument for separation kept as his.
- The House is the Ka'ba (Tabari, Ibn Abbas, Baghawi, Qurtubi,
  Muyassar); Umar pointing to it, from Tabari, ungraded, not a hadith.
- Why "Lord of this House": Qurtubi's two aspects, Muyassar, Ma'arif,
  Ibn Kathir with 27:91 and 29:67.
- Command: tawhid (most), stay in Makkah (Tabari; Ikrimah), make worship
  a habit (Ibn Abbas via Ikrimah). Three readings, none chosen.
- Ibn Kathir's consequence with 16:112 and 16:113; township unnamed.
Licensing sentence in both languages. No hadith attached to the verse;
no asbab. No shipped overlap.
Dropped: al-Farra' trade narrative, journey seasons, qira'at, Sufyan's
imam report, any history from outside the texts.
