## Found wrong in the shipped article
- §4 said "The commentators do not narrow dhikr Allah ... to words on the tongue; they read it as the whole of what a person owes Allah". That presents al-Hasan's view as the commentators' consensus; in the fetched texts al-Baghawi attributes the five-prayers reading to "the commentators" (qala al-mufassirun), at-Tabari reports only the five-prayers reading, and al-Qurtubi lists five readings. The new §4 reports them as a disagreement.
