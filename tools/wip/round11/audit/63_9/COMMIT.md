Sources: at-Tabari (with his editor's footnote on lahw), Ibn Kathir (ar; en
abridged as cross-check), al-Qurtubi, as-Sa'di, al-Baghawi, al-Muyassar,
Ma'arif, all fetched for 63:9.
- The turn from the hypocrites: Ma'arif's two sections; al-Qurtubi's warning
  against the hypocrites' character and his link to 63:7.
- Kept as a disagreement: addressees are the sincere believers (Ma'arif) vs
  the hypocrites, "believe with the heart" (al-Qurtubi's qila).
- tulhikum: at-Tabari's paraphrase, verb family and Imru' al-Qays witness;
  la tashghalkum (al-Baghawi, Muyassar); Ma'arif's dividing line.
- Why wealth and children (Ma'arif, Ibn Kathir); the command to remember much
  (Ibn Kathir, as-Sa'di); as-Sa'di's innate love and 64:15 in one line.
- dhikr Allah, no position taken: five prayers (ad-Dahhak; "the commentators"
  per al-Baghawi), Hajj and zakah, Qur'an, continual remembrance, all
  obligations (al-Hasan; Ma'arif calls it most comprehensive).
- 24:37 as al-Baghawi's counterpart verse.
- al-khasirun: maghbun of their shares (at-Tabari, Muyassar); sold the eternal
  for the perishable (Ma'arif, as-Sa'di); lose selves and families (Ibn Kathir).
- No hadith or asbab attached by any fetched tafsir, said in one sentence.
  Licensing sentence on the hypocrites in both languages. 63:10 in one line.
Dropped: "Medinan", 102:1, 24:36, 103:2, the khusr-as-capital gloss and the
giving-reverses-diversion claim (none in the fetched texts).
Defect fixed: the old article presented al-Hasan's all-obedience reading as
the commentators' consensus; al-Baghawi gives the five prayers to "the
