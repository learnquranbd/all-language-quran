Every human's ta'ir is fastened to his neck: his own deeds,
not a bird's flight. Card plus deep bilingual article, 8 sections.

Sources gave:
- at-Tabari and al-Baghawi: the word ta'ir is repurposed from the
  Arabs' bird-omens (sawanih/bawarih); the Qur'an keeps the word and
  relocates the source to a person's own decreed deed.
- Deed reading from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah (Tabari, Ibn Kathir);
  wider readings kept as a live disagreement: rizq (Mujahid), the
  wretched/blessed slip, shaqawah/sa'adah (Hasan), and the "share/lot"
  reading of Abu 'Ubaydah and al-Qutaybi. at-Tabari prefers the
  interpreters' fate-by-deed reading but grants the share reading a face.
- fi 'unuqihi: al-Zajjaj (luzum, like a necklace); Tabari (neck as site
  of collars); Ibn Kathir (a part with no counterpart, no escape).
- The record spread open: Ibn Kathir (right/left hand, legible), Qurtubi
  (manshuran hastens news and reproach), Ma'arif via Qatadah (even the
  illiterate will read it); the sheet's lifetime from al-Baghawi's
  athar, Abu Sawwar al-'Adawi, and al-Hasan al-Basri.

Hadith: Sahih al-Bukhari 5757 (no 'adwa, no tiyarah…), quoted whole,
sound by its place in the collection, marked as NOT verse-attached but
clearing the omen-ground the word grew in. Verse-attached Jabir
narrations (Tabari; Ahmad via Ibn Lahi'ah) reported with caveat: no
grade in the fetched text, Ibn Lahi'ah weak, gloss called gharib jiddan.

Kept universal (every human, no condemned-group verdict). Points toward
17:14 ("Read your record") without spending its payoff. Dropped, per
ledger: the al-Isbahani/Abu Umamah backbiting report (ungraded), the
taklif reading, and the qira'at variants.
