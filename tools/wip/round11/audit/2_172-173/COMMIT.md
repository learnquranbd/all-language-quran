Assigned 2:173, widened to 2:172-173 (eat of the good things and give thanks,
then the four forbidden and the necessity clause), inside the free window
2:170-176; 2:168-169 and 2:177 already ship. New card and a 1,778-word,
9-section article: gratitude for the tayyibat, how far innama reaches, the
four named, and the mercy in necessity.

Sources: at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi, Ibn Kathir (ar), as-Sa'di, al-Baghawi,
al-Muyassar, Ma'arif al-Qur'an on 2:172 and 2:173 (all slots on target;
Ibn Kathir en read as a check). No occasion of revelation in any (said once).
- Shukr as praise (at-Tabari), heart/tongue/limbs (al-Muyassar), righteous
  action and the dropped "halal" (as-Sa'di, with 23:51).
- Ihlal as the raised voice (at-Tabari); the named intention as the cause
  (al-Qurtubi). One line: no ruling on any present-day food.
Disagreements kept, no position: innama total (Ibn al-'Arabi via al-Qurtubi,
at-Tabari) vs relative (Ma'arif) vs kinds of the foul (as-Sa'di); uhilla as
slaughtered-for vs name-spoken; idtirar as hunger vs coercion (Mujahid);
ghayra baghin as the person (Mujahid, Sa'id b. Jubayr, Ibn Abbas, ash-Shafi'i
per al-Baghawi) vs the eating (Qatadah, al-Hasan, ar-Rabi', Ibn Zayd,
as-Suddi), at-Tabari's preference reported as his; the sinful traveller per
al-Qurtubi (ash-Shafi'i's two views, Abu Hanifa, Malik's reports differ);
survival vs full meal; "no sin" (Ma'arif) vs "made lawful" (Sa'id b. Jubayr);
four readings of ghafur rahim.
Hadith: Ibn Majah 3314 (no grading by him, said), at-Tirmidhi 69 (hasan
sahih), Ibn Majah 2298 (Ibn Kathir: chain sound); Muslim 1015 as a pointer
to 2:168-169. Modern questions (medicines) left to a qualified scholar.
Dropped: Salman's "what He was silent about" (Tirmidhi 1726: gharib, likely
mawquf), festival-meat reports, the 'anbar and locust hadith (not fetched).
