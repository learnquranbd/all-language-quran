Replaces the 878-word, 6-section article with an 8-section, 1,771-word
source-gated article (shipped card kept).

Sources: at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi (with an-Nahhas), as-Sa'di, Ibn Kathir (ar
and en abridged), al-Baghawi, Muyassar, Ma'arif, all on 39:53 itself.
Covers the address before the record (as-Sa'di's qul for every caller),
israf (Muyassar, as-Sa'di, Mujahid, al-Baghawi), la taqnatu (as-Sa'di's
despair that keeps sinning; Ibn Mas'ud and the storyteller; Ibn 'Abbas:
whoever makes people despair has denied the Book), jami'an with the
repentance condition (Ibn Kathir, at-Tabari on shirk and Ibn Mas'ud's
"for whom He wills", an-Nahhas with 20:82, as-Sa'di's one cause), and
39:54-56 via Ibn Kathir.

Disagreements kept: the asbab dispute, none chosen: polytheists who asked
for expiation (Ibn 'Abbas, as-Suddi, Qatadah, Ibn Zayd), Wahshi (RA)
('Ata' via al-Baghawi, al-Qurtubi, at-Tabari; ungraded by all), 'Umar's
report of Hisham (al-Qurtubi/Ibn Kathir) vs 'Ayyash and al-Walid (at-Tabari),
Ibn 'Umar on major sinners; at-Tabari's general reading as his own.
Ibn 'Umar vs Ibn 'Abbas (13:6) on the most hopeful verse.

Hadith: Bukhari 4810 quoted whole (also Muslim 122); Bukhari 3470, the man
who killed a hundred, quoted whole, with Muslim 2766's added detail;
Tirmidhi 3237 (Asma' bint Yazid, "and He does not mind"), graded hasan
gharib by at-Tirmidhi.

Dropped: Ahmad-only narrations in Ibn Kathir, al-Baghawi's unconfirmed
Bani Isra'il hadiths, and the old article's unsourced material (tawbah
conditions list, consequences paragraph, "near-unanimous", the Tirmidhi
hadith qudsi that no fetched tafsir attaches to the verse).
