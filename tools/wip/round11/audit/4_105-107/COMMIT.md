Assigned 4:107, widened to 4:105-107 (judge by what Allah has shown you and do not
be a pleader for the traitors; seek Allah's forgiveness; do not argue for those who
betray themselves). 4:108 used for one paragraph; 4:104 and 4:108 left free.
New card and a 1,754-word, 8-section article.

Sources: at-Tabari, Ibn Kathir (ar), al-Qurtubi, as-Sa'di, al-Baghawi, al-Muyassar
and Ma'arif on 4:105, 4:106 and 4:107, all slots on target (at-Tabari's 4:107 slot
also carries 4:108; his narrations sit under 4:106).
Occasion kept short (it ships with 4:110): at-Tirmidhi 3036, graded gharib by him;
Mujahid, Ikrimah, Ibn Zayd (at-Tabari) and al-Baghawi, where the blame falls on a
Jew. Stated plainly: the Qur'an cleared an innocent man of another faith against
men who counted themselves Muslims. Licensing in both languages for Jews, the
Ansar, the Banu Zafar and the Banu Ubayriq.
Centre: khasim and jidal (al-Qurtubi's form and roots), yakhtanuna anfusahum,
khawwan athim, as-Sa'di and al-Qurtubi on pleading for a wrongdoer, al-Qurtubi's
rule against shielding one's own, a disbeliever's property protected like a
Muslim's, the "wicked Jew" plea answered by the facts (Ibn Zayd).
Kept as differences, no position:
- the istighfar: at-Tabari, the narration's gloss, Ibn 'Atiyyah via al-Qurtubi
  (no sin), tasbih, al-Baghawi's three senses, as-Sa'di, Ma'arif; the article
  adopts none and says nothing in its own voice about a fault.
- representation: as-Sa'di (known or thought wrong) vs al-Qurtubi (only when
  known to be right).
Hadith: Bukhari 2458 (Umm Salamah, "more eloquent than another"), quoted whole.
Dropped: Ibn Zayd's "leaned against him" and Qatadah's "excused him" lines,
the Ibn Mardawayh telling, ad-Dahhak's report, Abu Dawud's addition (unfetched);
al-Qurtubi's "hasan gharib" for Tirmidhi 3036 (the page reads gharib).
