## Glosses of da'b, each attributed
- Ibn 'Abbas (in Tabari): state/condition (hal). Ibn Zayd (in Tabari): what befell them.
- at-Tabari: Allah's sunna and His dealing with them.
- al-Muyassar: their custom ('ada) in kufr and denial. al-Baghawi: their custom of persisting in denial until the punishment came.
- as-Sa'di: both their custom and Allah's custom of punishment hastened in this world before the Hereafter.
Kept as differing emphases, no position taken (§3.1).

## Those after them
Named only by at-Tabari (people of Ibrahim, people of Lut, also of the ahzab); Qatada: "they are the ahzab". Ibn Kathir: "of the denying nations"; Muyassar: "who came after them in kufr and denial". No other nation named (Madyan etc. NOT mentioned; not in any fetched text).
