Card and 8-section bilingual article for 40:31: mithla da'bi qawmi Nuhin
wa 'Adin wa Thamuda wa-lladhina min ba'dihim, wa ma-llahu yuridu zulman
lil-'ibad, the believer of Fir'awn's family continuing from 40:30.

Sources: Tabari, Muyassar, Sa'di, Baghawi, Ibn Kathir (ar + en abridged,
grouped 40:30-35); Qurtubi (no comment on this verse, said so); Ma'arif
(grouped 40:28-31, covers only the concealed faith of 40:28).
- Frame: speech of the believer to Fir'awn and his council (Tabari);
  Baghawi quotes 40:30-31 as one phrase. Shipped 40:24/27/44 one line.
- da'b, five glosses kept as differing emphases: Ibn 'Abbas "hal" and
  Ibn Zayd "ma asabahum" (both via Tabari); Tabari Allah's sunna and
  dealing; Muyassar/Baghawi the people's 'ada of denial (Baghawi: persisting
  until the punishment came); Sa'di both customs, punishment hastened in
  this world before the Hereafter.
- min ba'dihim: Tabari names the people of Ibrahim and of Lut, also of the
  ahzab; Qatada "they are the ahzab"; IK "the denying nations"; Muyassar
  by conduct. No other nation added.
- zulm: no punishment without sin (Tabari, Muyassar, Sa'di, IK); Baghawi:
  no destruction before the proof is established. Both reported.
- No destruction details for Nuh, 'Ad, Thamud supplied from outside.
- Licensing sentence in both languages.
Hadith: none attached by any fetched tafsir; stated in one sentence.
Asbab: none; placement in the believer's speech given instead.
Dropped: Tabari's earlier da'b discussion (not located), outside
cross-references, 40:32-33 material.
