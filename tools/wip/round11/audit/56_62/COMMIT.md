Card and 8-section bilingual article (1,614 EN words) on 56:62: wa-laqad
'alimtumu an-nash'ata al-ula fa-lawla tadhakkarun. Framed as the close of
56:57's argument (fa-lawla tusaddiqun / tadhakkarun) without repeating the
unit outline shipped at 56:60.

Sources: at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi, Ibn Kathir (ar; en grouped 56:57-62),
as-Sa'di, al-Baghawi, al-Muyassar, Ma'arif (grouped 56:57-62).
- an-nash'a al-ula, three glosses kept unranked: from nothing (Mujahid via
  at-Tabari; al-Baghawi, al-Muyassar); nutfa/'alaqa/mudgha (al-Qurtubi,
  from Mujahid and others); creation of Adam (Qatada, Abu 'Imran al-Jawni
  via at-Tabari; Qatada and ad-Dahhak via al-Qurtubi).
- fa-lawla = halla (at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi, al-Muyassar, Ibn Kathir); each
  source's wording of the inference reported; as-Sa'di names the method.
- Ibn Kathir's a fortiori (bi-tariq al-awla) with his parallels 30:27,
  19:67, 36:77-79, 75:36-40, each verified in quran-json.
- Address: at-Tabari general (ayyuha an-nas); Ibn Kathir (en) and Ma'arif
  tie the passage to the 56:47 deniers. Both reported.
- Qira'at: an-nash'a short vs long, from al-Qurtubi.
- al-Qurtubi's "fi al-khabar" saying quoted, labelled as unattributed and
  not a hadith.
- Licensing sentence in both languages.
Hadith: none attached in any fetched note; stated in one sentence.
Asbab: none.
Dropped: 17:51 (no source cites it), qari identification, wa-laqad grammar,
a separate "first life vs afterlife" gloss; 56:63-67 left alone.
Gate fix: EN-only "one"/numbers in s1.1, s3.1, s4.2, s4.3, s5.1, s8.1, s8.2.
- Review fix: Qurtubi's unattributed 'fi al-khabar' saying now says he names no collection (not 'could not be traced'); kept as his quotation, not a hadith.
