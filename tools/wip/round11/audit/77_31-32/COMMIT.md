Card and 9-section bilingual article for 77:31-32, la zalilin wa la
yughni mina l-lahab / innaha tarmi bi-shararin ka-l-qasr, read with
77:29-30 (the shadow of three branches) and 77:33 (jimalatun sufr) as
context. 77:8 and 77:13 angles not repeated.

Sources: Tabari, Qurtubi, Ibn Kathir (ar; en grouped 77:29-38), Baghawi,
Sa'di, Muyassar (grouped 77:30-33), Ma'arif (77:32-34).
- The shadow as smoke of Jahannam (Muyassar, Ibn Kathir); "no shade"
  per Tabari, Qurtubi (lahab defined), Baghawi (al-Kalbi), Sa'di (no rest,
  flame on every side; 39:16, 7:41).
- Qurtubi's five reported views on the three branches kept unranked.
- Tabari's grammar note on singular qasr (54:45, 33:19).
- Kept as a disagreement: al-qasr as palace/fort (Ibn Abbas via Ali ibn
  Abi Talhah, al-Qurazi, Ibn Mas'ud; Tabari's preference, al-Akhtal) vs
  cut logs/tree trunks (Ibn Abbas via Abd al-Rahman ibn Abis, Mujahid,
  Qatadah, ad-Dahhak, Zayd ibn Aslam, Sa'id ibn Jubayr; Qurtubi "the
  soundest"). Ibn Abbas noted on both sides in Tabari's chains.
- Readings: qasar (Ibn Abbas, Ali, Mujahid, Humayd, as-Sulami; palm or
  camel necks), qisar (Sa'id ibn Jubayr); Tabari prefers the still sad.
- 77:33: black-yellow camels vs ship ropes; Hafs/Hamzah/Kisa'i jimalah.
- Qurtubi's ruling on storing firewood, attributed to him.
Hadith: Bukhari 4932 (Ibn Abbas, logs of three cubits), quoted whole from
the Arabic, marked as a Companion's explanation; attached by Qurtubi and
Ibn Kathir. 4933 (ship ropes) not quoted.
Licensing sentence in EN and BN on the deniers.
Asbab: none in the sources.
Dropped: Qurtubi's unsourced "sun draws near" narration; at-Tirmidhi's
objection on sufr; jabal and copper glosses.
Defect noted: quranx English of Bukhari 4932 glosses al-qasr as "Forts";
the article follows the Arabic.
