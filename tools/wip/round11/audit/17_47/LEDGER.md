# SOURCES ledger — 17:47

## Tafsir fetched (from PREP.md, not re-fetched)
- t14 Ibn Kathir (ar), t15 at-Tabari, t16 al-Muyassar, t90 al-Qurtubi,
  t91 as-Sa'di, t94 al-Baghawi, t168 Ma'arif (en, grouped 47-51),
  t169 Ibn Kathir abridged (en, grouped 47-48).

## Named in the article, with the Arabic/English clause each rests on
- **al-Baghawi (94)** — "بما يستمعون به قيل: به صلة أي يطلبون سماعه" → the bi / "they seek
  to hear it." matbub for masḥūr: "مطبوبا". lists majnun/kahin/sahir/sha'ir.
  masruf ʿan al-haqq: "مصروفا عن الحق". Abu ʿUbayda lung reading: "له سحر، والسحر: الرئة".
- **al-Qurtubi (90)** — "الباء زائدة في قوله به أي يستمعونه" (the bi is extra). Qatada:
  najwa = majnun/sahir/asatir al-awwalin. two occasion reports (ʿUtba's meal; ʿAli's meal),
  each "وقيل" = qila. az-Zajjaj: "النجوى اسم للمصدر… أي سرار". az-zalimun = "أبو جهل والوليد
  بن المغيرة وأمثالهما". masḥūr = "مطبوبا قد خبله السحر… يقولون ذلك لينفروا عنه الناس".
  Mujahid = "مخدوعا". Abu ʿUbayda lung + Labid verse.
- **as-Sa'di (91)** — "نعلم أن مقاصدهم سيئة… ليس استماعهم لأجل الاسترشاد وقبول الحق";
  built on masḥūr so "جازمون أنهم غير معتبرين لما قال، وأنه يهذي لا يدري ما يقول".
- **al-Muyassar (16)** — "نحن أعلم بالذي يستمعه رؤساء قريش… ومقاصدهم سيئة"; masḥūr =
  "رجلا أصابه السحر فاختلط عقله".
- **at-Tabari (15)** — najwa = Dar an-Nadwa consultation, via Mujahid ("مثل قيل الوليد بن
  المغيرة ومن معه في دار الندوة"); Qatada content. lung reading via Basran grammarians +
  Labid; at-Tabari's own verdict "غير بعيد من الصواب" (not far from correct).
- **Ibn Kathir (14 + 169 en)** — leaders of Quraysh listening secretly; masḥūr "على المشهور"
  = affected by magic, or "من السحر وهو الرئة" (a lung / mortal); his objection "وفيه نظر",
  holding they meant a spell giving him a familiar. Sira narrative of Abu Sufyan, Abu Jahl,
  al-Akhnas listening three nights, via Ibn Ishaq from az-Zuhri (169) — **no isnad grading
  given; reported in the article as a Sira report, ungraded.**
- **Ma'arif al-Qur'an (168 en)** — "Can Magic affect a Prophet?"; cites Bayan al-Qur'an and
  Muhammad Taqi Usmani; magic touches the body/physical illness, never prophetic function;
  disbelievers meant "insane," which the Qur'an refutes.

## Disagreement kept (no position taken)
- masḥūr as "a man with lungs / mere mortal": at-Tabari judges it "not far from correct";
  Ibn Kathir records it but finds it questionable ("fihi nazar"). Both names, no verdict.

## Hadith
- **None used.** No fetched tafsir attaches a sound, verse-specific marfūʿ hadith to 17:47.
  hadith.js NOT run. The article states this plainly (§8.2).
- Ma'arif's reference to a hadith that the Prophet ﷺ was once affected by magic is reported
  only as Ma'arif reports it (body, not prophethood); not independently quoted, no number,
  because it is not on this verse. al-Qurtubi's ʿAʾisha shahid ("بين سحري ونحري") is a
  lexical witness for sahr = lung only — dropped, not cited.

## Asbab an-nuzul
- The ʿUtba-meal and ʿAli-meal occasion reports (al-Qurtubi) and the Dar an-Nadwa link
  (at-Tabari via Mujahid) are all marked "qila" / not established; reported as uncertain,
  none adopted as the sabab. Verse placement (after the veil verses 17:45-46) used instead.

## Standing licensing sentence
- §7.2 states in EN and BN that the verse describes the slander of a specific group of
  Makkan chiefs in the Prophet's lifetime, licenses nothing against any living person or
  community, and passes no verdict on anyone alive today.

## Dropped / not preempted
- 17:48 (the comparisons they coined) and 17:49 (denial of resurrection) only gestured at,
  not developed.
- Ma'arif's long "hidden-from-enemy-sight" excursus (18:57, 16:108, 45:23, 36:1-9, and the
  Abu Lahab's-wife report) belongs to 17:45-46, not this verse — dropped.
