Allah knows not what they say but how they listen. Card and deep
bilingual article for the mocking listening and secret slander of Quraysh.

Sources gave:
- al-Baghawi and al-Qurtubi on the particle bihi (they seek to hear it /
  the bi is extra); the listening itself is what is weighed.
- as-Sa'di and al-Muyassar: their aims were corrupt, not to be guided but
  to catch the smallest thing to attack — why listening profited them nothing.
- Ibn Kathir's Sira scene (via Ibn Ishaq from az-Zuhri): Abu Sufyan, Abu Jahl
  and al-Akhnas listening three nights, then confessing they would not believe.
  Reported as a Sira narration, ungraded.
- najwa content via Qatada (madman, magician, tales of the ancients); al-Baghawi's
  list; occasion reports (Dar an-Nadwa; Utba's meal; Ali's meal) all marked qila.
- masḥūr readings: bewitched / mind deranged (al-mashhur — Ibn Kathir, al-Muyassar,
  al-Qurtubi's matbub, al-Baghawi); "a man with lungs," a mortal who eats and drinks
  (Abu Ubayda, Labid's verse); Mujahid's "deceived"; al-Baghawi's "turned from truth."
- az-zalimun named (Abu Jahl, al-Walid ibn al-Mughira) and the slander filed under zulm.
- Ma'arif on whether magic can touch a prophet (body, never prophethood).

Disagreement kept: at-Tabari deems the "lungs / mortal" reading not far from correct;
Ibn Kathir records it but finds it questionable. Both names, no verdict.

Hadith: none — no fetched tafsir attaches a sound verse-specific hadith to 17:47; said so.

Dropped: al-Qurtubi's Aisha lexical shahid; Ma'arif's hidden-from-sight excursus (belongs
to 17:45-46); 17:48-49 only gestured at, not developed.

Standing sentence: the verse licenses nothing against any living person or community and
gives no verdict on anyone alive today.
