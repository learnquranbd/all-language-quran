## Disagreements kept
- dha al-qurba: a person's own kin (al-Hasan, 'Ikrimah, Ibn 'Abbas via at-Tabari; al-Baghawi "the majority"; Muyassar) vs the Prophet's kin ('Ali b. al-Husayn, via at-Tabari, al-Baghawi, al-Qurtubi). At-Tabari's tarjih reported as his; article takes no side.
- ibn as-sabil's right: three days' hospitality (qila) vs any right (at-Tabari's preference).
- tabdhir: where the money goes (Ibn Mas'ud, Mujahid, al-Shafi'i "no tabdhir in good"; Malik via Ashhab = israf, haram) vs also how much, even in giving (as-Sa'di; Ibn Kathir's moderation, 25:67). Both kept.

## Orchestrator watch-points

- "Madani in a Makkan surah" and "Shi'i-leaning narrators": in no fetched file. Not used. Ibn Kathir's objection is the Makkan date vs Fadak in 7 AH.
