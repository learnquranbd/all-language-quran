## Asbab
Jabir's account (Bukhari 4926 / Muslim per Ibn Kathir) used as the occasion: confirmed on fetched quranx page, called al-mahfuz by Ibn Kathir. Other occasions (at-Tabarani/Ibn Abbas al-Walid meal; Qushayri's "qila"; 'Uqba bin Rabi'a report) reported as reports only, with Ibn al-Arabi's "batil" and the note that no grading is given; not adopted.

## Disagreements kept (no position)
- First revelation: Jabir (al-Muddaththir first) vs al-jumhur per Ibn Kathir, az-Zuhri per Tabari, Ma'arif ("well-known authentic ahadith": Iqra'). Reconciliation (first after the fatra) attributed to Ibn Kathir and Ma'arif; article notes Jabir himself named it first outright.
- Gloss: literal (Ibn Abbas "sleeper", Qatada "wrapped in clothes", Tabari's own opening gloss, Qurtubi, Muyassar, Ma'arif) vs Ikrima (wrapped in prophethood and its burdens; Tabari, Qurtubi), with Ibn al-Arabi's objection.

## Watch-points
- 'A'isha NOT named: no fetched text on 74:1 names her; the Iqra' side is named as the fetched texts give it (jumhur, az-Zuhri, Ma'arif). 96:1 article referenced for the cave night.
