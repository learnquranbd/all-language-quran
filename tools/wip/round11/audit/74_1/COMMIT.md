Card and 8-section bilingual article for 74:1, ya ayyuha al-muddaththir:
the word, its glosses, the occasion, the first-revelation question and
the gentleness of the address. 74:2-7 only as the call that follows.

Sources: Tabari, Ibn Kathir (ar; en abridged cross-check), Qurtubi,
Sa'di, Muyassar, Ma'arif; Baghawi read (hadith only, not named).
- Word: dithar (Ma'arif); al-mutadaththir, ta assimilated, Ubayy's
  reading (Qurtubi); qatifa (Tabari, Ibrahim); Muyassar.
- Glosses: Ibn Abbas "sleeper", Qatada "wrapped in clothes" vs Ikrima
  "wrapped in prophethood and its burdens" (Tabari, Qurtubi) with Ibn
  al-Arabi's "far-fetched figure" objection. Unranked.
- Occasion: Jabir's account, al-mahfuz per Ibn Kathir; Tirmidhi hasan
  sahih per Qurtubi. Tabarani/Ibn Abbas al-Walid report, Qushayri's
  qila and the 'Uqba report (Ibn al-Arabi: batil) kept as reports only.
- Address as mulatafa (Qurtubi), with qum Aba Turab / qum ya Nauman.
- 73:1 link via Sa'di (one meaning) and Ma'arif (near-synonyms, order
  unclear; personal purification vs preaching).
Disagreements kept: first revelation, Jabir vs al-jumhur (Ibn Kathir),
az-Zuhri (Tabari) and Ma'arif; reconciliation "first after the fatra"
attributed to Ibn Kathir and Ma'arif, noting Jabir named it first
outright. 'A'isha not named (no fetched text on 74:1 names her).
Hadith: Bukhari 4926 quoted whole (Darussalam English), Bukhari 4922
paraphrased for the Yahya/Abu Salama/Jabir exchange.
Prophet's fear presented as the human response, never as a fault.
Dropped: az-Zuhri's mountain-top balagh, Ibn al-Arabi's unclear second
clause, Muslim's prayer-obligation note (74:2-5), Bukhari 4925 (fetch
failed).

Review fix: removed al-Qurtubi's secondhand report that at-Tirmidhi graded the Jabir hadith hasan sahih (EN + BN); not confirmed on a fetched Tirmidhi page.
