Card and 9-section bilingual article for 48:17-18: laysa 'ala l-a'ma
harajun ... and laqad radiya llahu 'ani l-mu'minina idh yubayi'unaka
tahta sh-shajara. 48:11-16 used as context only, with the licensing line.

Sources: Tabari, Qurtubi, Ibn Kathir (ar; en cross-check), Sa'di,
Baghawi, Muyassar, Ma'arif.
- The excused: staying behind from jihad (Tabari, Qatada, ad-Dahhak,
  Muyassar); IK's lasting vs passing excuse; Muqatil via Qurtubi; Ibn
  'Abbas report via Qurtubi marked as given without a chain. No own fiqh.
- Turning away: Tabari (staying behind, Jahannam), IK (livelihood,
  humiliation and Fire), Sa'di (all obedience).
- Qira'at as fetched: Qurtubi (Nafi', Ibn 'Amir nudkhilhu), Baghawi
  (Madina and Sham, nun in both verbs).
- Occasion via Ibn Ishaq in Tabari: Khirash, 'Umar, 'Uthman, the rumour;
  Qurtubi's hand for 'Uthman.
- Kept as disagreements: death vs not fleeing (Jabir, Ma'qil / Salama /
  Bukayr); numbers 1,400 / 1,500 / 1,525 / 1,300 with holders, none
  picked; tree's fate (forgotten, Bukhari; gone, Tabari/Baghawi; felled,
  Ma'arif); hearts (good vs a weight: Muqatil, qila, Sa'di both); fath
  qarib (Khaybar vs Makka vs IK's treaty-and-after, Ma'arif "consensus").
Hadith: Muslim 1856, Bukhari 4163, Bukhari 4154, Muslim 2496, each
attached by a fetched tafsir and quoted whole with its collection.
Dropped: Ma'arif/Mazhari sectarian passage and Badr comparison, al-Jadd
ibn Qays, first-to-pledge difference, treaty narrative (48:1/48:4
ground), wa-atahum reading, 48:10 and 48:19-20 beyond pointers.
Muslim 2496: narrator Umm Mubashshir per Muslim's page (Ma'arif says Umm Bishr, not used); her reply 'bala' rendered 'Yes indeed' / Bengali 'অবশ্যই করবে'; Ma'arif sect passage dropped.
