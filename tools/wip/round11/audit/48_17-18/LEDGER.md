### Hadith

1. **Sahih Muslim 1856 (1856a)**: https://quranx.com/hadith/Muslim/Hadith-1856.
   First words: "We were 1,400 on the day of al-Hudaybiya. We pledged..."
   Attached by: al-Qurtubi (وفي صحيح مسلم عن أبي الزبير عن جابر), at-Tabari (own chain via
   al-Layth / Abu az-Zubayr), al-Baghawi (via Muslim's chain, 1856c wording). Quoted whole
   (1856a matn). Grading: in Muslim's Sahih; no further grade.
2. **Sahih al-Bukhari 4163** (Dar-us-Salam numbering): https://quranx.com/hadith/Bukhari/DarusSalam/Hadith-4163.
   First words: "Tariq ibn 'Abd ar-Rahman said: I set out for Hajj..."
   Attached by: Ibn Kathir (قال البخاري : حدثنا محمود ...), Ma'arif ("Bukhari and Muslim").
   Quoted whole. Grading: in al-Bukhari's Sahih.
3. **Sahih al-Bukhari 4154**: https://quranx.com/hadith/Bukhari/DarusSalam/Hadith-4154.
   First words: "The Messenger of Allah ﷺ said to us on the day..."
   Attached by: al-Baghawi (through al-Bukhari's chain: محمد بن إسماعيل، حدثنا علي بن عبد الله،
   حدثنا سفيان), Ma'arif ("Bukhari and Muslim"). Quoted whole (the matn; the trailing
   تابعه الأعمش corroboration note is isnad-level and left out). Grading: in al-Bukhari's Sahih.
4. **Sahih Muslim 2496**: https://quranx.com/hadith/Muslim/Hadith-2496.
   First words: "None of the people of the tree, those who pledged..."
   Attached by: Ma'arif (cites Muslim via Mazhari), al-Baghawi (from Jabir by a Tha'labi
   chain, short wording), al-Qurtubi (states the sense, no wording). Quoted whole from Muslim.
   Grading: in Muslim's Sahih. NOTE: Ma'arif names the narrator "Umm Bishr"; the fetched
   Muslim page names Umm Mubashshir. The article uses Muslim's name. Translation of
   قالت بلى kept as "She said: Yes indeed" (EN); BN renders the sense "অবশ্যই করবে".
   Orchestrator may want to verify the BN rendering.
5. Bukhari 4162 fetched (al-Musayyib: "I saw the tree ... then could not recognise it"): not used;
   4163 is the version Ibn Kathir quotes.

## Dropped / not used

- Ma'arif's Mazhari passage on "vilification of the Companions" and its named sectarian
  group: dropped entirely (no polemic, no ruling on any later group).
- Ma'arif's "participants are like the people of Badr" and "forgiveness of their slips":
  Mazhari's extension; dropped.
- Ma'arif's link "verse 18 reinforces verse 10": not used (48:10 is another ground).
- Al-Jadd ibn Qays hiding under his camel (Tabari, Qurtubi, Baghawi, Muslim 1856c):
  dropped by choice; naming an individual who held back adds nothing to these verses.
- First to pledge: Abu Sinan ibn Wahb (Tabari, Baghawi via ash-Sha'bi) vs "Abu Sufyan
  al-Asadi" (Qurtubi via ash-Sha'bi): a real difference, dropped for budget.
- Aban ibn Sa'id's protection of 'Uthman: dropped for budget.
- At-Tabari's "the tree was in a pass towards Makka" (inside the Bukayr report): not used,
  per the watch-point on not placing the tree.
- Sa'id ibn al-Musayyib's grandfather Hazn version (Tabari): not used; Bukhari 4163 used.
- Qurtubi's long Hudaybiya narrative (treaty terms, Abu Jandal, Suhayl, 'Umar's "are we not
  in the right"): treaty ground (48:1, 48:4 territory); not used.
- Qurtubi's variant reading "wa-atahum" for wa-athabahum: dropped for budget.
- Qurtubi's note on 'araj (one leg vs both) and his pointer to Surat Bara'a: dropped for budget.
- Ibn Kathir (en) 48:16 list of the "people of great might": 48:16 ground, not used.
- 48:19-20 spoils: pointer only.
- Ma'qil ibn Yasar "the tree was a samura" (watch-point example): not in any fetched tafsir
  for this verse (Baghawi has Ma'qil only on "not death"); not used.
- No fiqh ruling given in the article's own voice; §1 says so.
