Key 5:12 (Allah took the covenant of the Children of Israel and raised twelve chiefs;
"I am with you" if you establish prayer, give zakah, believe in and support My messengers
and lend Allah a goodly loan; whoever disbelieves after that has strayed). Not widened:
5:9-11 used for placement only; 5:13 shipped, referred to once, not repeated.
New card and a 1,738-word, 9-section article.

Sources: at-Tabari, Ibn Kathir (ar), al-Qurtubi, as-Sa'di, al-Baghawi, al-Muyassar and
Ma'arif on 5:12, all slots on target. Ibn Kathir (en) read to confirm, not cited alone.
Centre: the covenant's terms and "I am with you", not its breaking.
Naqib from the texts: guarantor (at-Tabari), witness (Qatada), trustees (ar-Rabi'),
senior who searches into his people's affairs (al-Qurtubi), overseer (as-Sa'di).
'Aqabah's twelve naqibs (Ibn Kathir, al-Qurtubi), no names.
"I am with you" as conditional companionship: at-Tabari's "helper ... if you keep My
covenant" and his rule that Allah supports whoever obeys Him; as-Sa'di's help in the
measure of the burden; Ma'arif's two senses; al-Qurtubi's view tying the condition to both.
Application: 73:20 (prayer, zakah, goodly loan), 2:285, 16:128, 57:4, 64:17.
Licensing line in both languages; the article's voice generalises about no people.
Kept as differences, no position:
- addressee: the chiefs (ar-Rabi' ibn Anas, as-Sa'di) vs all Israel (at-Tabari).
- 'azzartumuhum: support (at-Tabari via 48:9, Mujahid, Ibn Kathir) vs honour (as-Sa'di,
  al-Qurtubi, al-Baghawi's second view).
- the goodly loan: spending in His way / sadaqat / zakah or family (al-Baghawi's "it is
  said") / charity beyond zakah (Ma'arif).
Hadith: Bukhari 7176 (the 'urafa' of Hawazin's captives), which al-Qurtubi cites as a
parallel; quoted from Bukhari's wording, marked as not about the verse.
Asbab: none used.
Dropped: the chiefs' names, the spy and giant stories (one attributed sentence says they
are not relied on), ar-Rabi's scouting narration, Ibn Kathir's twelve-caliphs narrations
and his sectarian discussion, al-Qurtubi's ruling on spies.
