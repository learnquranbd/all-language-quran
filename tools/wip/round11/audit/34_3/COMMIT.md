Card plus deep bilingual article on the commanded oath affirming the Hour,
grounded in Allah's exhaustive knowledge (the atom's weight) and the clear
Record (al-Lawḥ al-Maḥfūẓ).

Sources gave:
- at-Tabari: deniers of the power to restore creation; three qira'at of
  ʿālim/ʿālimi/ʿallām (he prefers ʿallām); lā yaʿzub = does not vanish from
  Him; dharra = a speck's weight up and down the scale; the Record notes,
  counts and knows each thing.
- al-Qurtubi: the deniers as Meccans; the self-contradiction of granting the
  first creation while denying its repetition; dharra = a small ant's weight.
- al-Muyassar and as-Sa'di: the clear Record = al-Lawḥ al-Maḥfūẓ. as-Sa'di's
  a-fortiori: He who misses no speck's weight and knows what the earth takes
  of the dead is all the more able to raise them.
- Ibn Kathir: this is one of three verses (no fourth) where the Prophet is
  commanded to swear by his Lord on the resurrection (10:53, 34:3, 64:7);
  bones scatter yet He restores them; ties 34:4-34:5 as reward and justice.
- Ma'arif al-Qur'an: the name "Knower of the unseen" is chosen because the
  subject is resurrection-deniers, who measured Allah's knowledge by their own.

Disagreement kept: the three sound qira'at of ʿālim al-ghayb, with Tabari's
stated preference and no position forced.

Hadith: Sahih Muslim 2653 (ʿAbdullah ibn ʿAmr) — "Allah ordained the measures
of creation fifty thousand years before He created the heavens and the earth,
His Throne upon the water" — used as a general narration explicitly NOT tied by
the commentators to this verse, to light up the clear Record.

Dropped: Bukhari 660 (not apt); al-Baghawi (no distinct reading); any
modern-physics "atom" reading of dharra; the Abu Sufyan/Muqatil report kept
only as a relayed tafsir attribution, not as an established sabab.

Keeps the oath + atom's-weight register distinct from 31:34's five keys. States
in both languages that the verse's condemnation of the mockers licenses nothing
against any living person.
