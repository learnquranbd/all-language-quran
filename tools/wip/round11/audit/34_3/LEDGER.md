# SOURCES ledger — 34:3

## Tafsir fetched (all from PREP.md, pre-fetched; none marked WRONG GROUP)

- **at-Tabari (15)** — NAMED. Rests on: "ويستعجلك يا محمد الذين جحدوا قدرة الله على إعادة خلقه" (deniers of the power to restore creation, mocking); the three-qira'at discussion ʿālim/ʿālimi/ʿallām with his preference for ʿallām "أبلغ في المدح"; "عَلام ما يغيب عن أبصار الخلق" (unseen = hidden from creation's sight); "لا يَعْزُبُ عَنْهُ … لا يغيب عنه ولكنه ظاهر له" (with Ibn ʿAbbas/Mujahid/Qatadah chains); "زنة ذرة … فما فوقها فما دونها"; "هو مثبت في كتاب يبين للناظر فيه أن الله … أثبته وأحصاه وعلمه."
- **al-Qurtubi (90)** — NAMED. Rests on: "قيل: المراد أهل مكة. قال مقاتل: قال أبو سفيان … واللات والعزى لا تأتينا الساعة أبدا ولا نبعث" (Meccan deniers; Muqatil/Abu Sufyan report — relayed as "qīla/Muqatil", NOT asserted as established sabab); "فهؤلاء الكفار مقرون بالابتداء منكرون الإعادة" (admit the first creation, deny repetition = self-contradiction); "مثقال ذرة أي قدر نملة صغيرة" (weight of a small ant).
- **al-Muyassar (16)** — NAMED. Rests on: "لا يغيب عنه وزن نملة صغيرة" (gloss of dharra as small ant's weight) and "في كتاب واضح، وهو اللوح المحفوظ" (clear Record = al-Lawḥ al-Maḥfūẓ).
- **as-Sa'di (91)** — NAMED. Rests on: "{لَا تَأْتِينَا السَّاعَةُ} أي: ما هي إلا هذه الحياة الدنيا, نموت ونحيا" (their claim = only this life); "أمر اللّه رسوله أن يرد قولهم ويبطله ويقسم على البعث"; "{عَالِمِ الْغَيْبِ} … فكيف بالشهادة؟" (knows unseen, so all the more the seen); "أحاط به علمه, وجرى به قلمه, وتضمنه الكتاب المبين, الذي هو اللوح المحفوظ"; the a-fortiori: "قادر على بعثهم من باب أولى, وليس بعثهم بأعجب من هذا العلم المحيط."
- **Ibn Kathir ar (14) + en abridged (169)** — NAMED. Rests on: "هذه إحدى الآيات الثلاث التي لا رابع لهن" + the three verses named (10:53, 34:3, 64:7); "قال مجاهد وقتادة: (لا يعزب عنه) لا يغيب عنه"; "فالعظام وإن تلاشت وتفرقت وتمزقت، فهو عالم أين ذهبت … ثم يعيدها كما بدأها"; (169) ties 34:4-34:5 as reward/punishment the record makes possible ("favour upon the blessed … punish the doomed").
- **al-Baghawi (94)** — FETCHED, not named. Only duplicates the qira'at list and "لا يعزب = لا يغيب" already carried by Tabari/Qurtubi; added nothing distinct, so left uncited to avoid padding attributions.
- **Ma'arif al-Qur'an en (168)** — NAMED. Rests on: ʿālim al-ghayb chosen "because the text is dealing with the … deniers of … Resurrection"; the deniers' objection (dust scattered, particles unsortable) and that they "had … taken the knowledge and power of Allah … on the analogy of their own"; "No created entity, whether an angel or prophet, can ever have such … knowledge" (used for the "none shares — not angel nor prophet" line).

Disagreement kept as disagreement: the qira'at of ʿālim al-ghayb — stated as three sound readings (ʿālim nominative / ʿālimi genitive / ʿallām intensive) with at-Tabari's stated preference for ʿallām, no position forced. (Attributions of specific readings to specific reciters were left general per Tabari/Qurtubi to avoid a reciter-by-reciter claim.)

## Hadith

- **USED — Sahih Muslim 2653 (ʿAbdullah ibn ʿAmr ibn al-ʿAs wording).**
  - URL fetched: https://quranx.com/hadith/Muslim/Hadith-2653 (via tools/wip/round11/hadith.js, saved in this dir).
  - First ten words (EN): "Allah ordained the measures of creation fifty thousand years before…"
  - AR opening: "كَتَبَ اللَّهُ مَقَادِيرَ الْخَلاَئِقِ قَبْلَ أَنْ يَخْلُقَ السَّمَوَاتِ وَالأَرْضَ بِخَمْسِينَ أَلْفَ سَنَةٍ … وَعَرْشُهُ عَلَى الْمَاءِ."
  - Grading: Sahih Muslim (sound by Muslim's criterion); not upgraded.
  - **Marked in the article as a GENERAL narration NOT tied by the commentators to 34:3** (§5 p2: "though the commentators do not tie it to this verse"). Used to illuminate kitāb mubīn / al-Lawḥ al-Maḥfūẓ as a writing that precedes creation.
- **DROPPED — Sahih Bukhari 660** (the seven whom Allah shades). Fetched (saved in this dir) per PREP's suggestion, but it concerns the Day's shade, not the oath on the Hour or the atom's-weight record; not apt, so not used.
- No tafsir fetched attached a sound hadith directly to 34:3.

## Asbab an-nuzul

- NOT used as an established sabab. al-Qurtubi relays from Muqatil that Abu Sufyan swore by al-Lat and al-Uzza that the Hour would never come. This is relayed in the article strictly as al-Qurtubi's report from Muqatil ("relays from Muqatil a report that…"), not asserted as a confirmed occasion of revelation. The verse's placement (Meccan deniers of resurrection, ch. 34) carries the point.

## Dropped / not asserted

- Abu Sufyan/Muqatil report: kept only as a relayed tafsir attribution, not as sabab (above).
- al-Baghawi: fetched, not cited (no distinct reading).
- "dharra = atom" in the modern-physics sense: deliberately NOT asserted. Rendered throughout as "the weight of a small ant / the tiniest thing that carries any weight," per at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi and al-Muyassar.
- Reciter-by-reciter attribution of the three qira'at (Nafiʿ/Ibn Kathir rafʿ, etc. in Qurtubi/Baghawi): dropped to keep the point on meaning, not to risk a mis-assigned reciter.
