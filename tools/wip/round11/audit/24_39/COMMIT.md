Replaces the 751-word, 6-section article with a 1,786-word, 9-section one built only
on the eight tafsir files fetched for 24:39.

Sources: al-Qurtubi places the verse as the disbeliever's parable after the believer's;
Ma'arif reads 24:35-38 as the believers' light and 24:39-40 as its loss. Sarab vs al
(at-Tabari, Ibn Kathir), al-Qurtubi's yasrubu etymology, al-Baghawi's shimmer that
scatters up close; as-Sa'di's thirsty man who imagines what others do not; at-Tabari's
question on the pronoun in ja'ahu (mist seen from afar, or the mirage's place).
Why the deeds fail: built on kufr (at-Tabari), reward voided (al-Qurtubi), lack of
sincerity or of following the Shari'ah with 25:23 (Ibn Kathir), the barren qi'ah as
the heart (as-Sa'di). Swift reckoning: no finger-counting (at-Tabari).
Disagreements kept: qi'ah plural of qa' vs singular (Abu 'Ubaydah via al-Qurtubi);
arrival at death (Mujahid, Qatadah, Ibn 'Abbas, al-Baghawi) vs on the Day (Ubayy,
Ibn Kathir, as-Sa'di, Muyassar), combined by at-Tabari; 'indahu as bil-mirsad, at his
deed, or Allah's promise/command (al-Qurtubi: meanings close); the 24:40 pairing as
leaders vs followers (Ibn Kathir, abridged en), Hereafter-believers vs deniers
(Ma'arif), or simply both parables of kufr's deeds (as-Sa'di).
Asbab: al-Qurtubi's three unchained reports (Muqatil: Shaybah ibn Rabi'ah; Abu Sahl:
People of the Book; ad-Dahhak: kinship and neighbourly deeds), marked as his.
Hadith: Bukhari 4581 (Abu Sa'id), the passage Ibn Kathir attaches (the Fire shown as a
mirage to the thirsty), quoted verbatim as an excerpt of the long hadith.
Licensing sentence added; application kept to examining one's own deeds.
Dropped from the old article: 18:104-105, 14:18 and the "occurs only here" corpus
claims (no fetched source). Defect fixed: old text said Ibn Kathir calls the mirage
group's ignorance "compound"; the fetched text says "deep and advanced".
