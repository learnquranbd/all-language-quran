## Disagreements kept (no position taken)
- qi'a: plural of qa' (at-Tabari, Ibn Kathir, al-Harawi in al-Qurtubi) vs a singular synonym (Abu 'Ubaydah via an-Nahhas in al-Qurtubi).
- When he "reaches" the mirage: at death (Mujahid, Qatadah, Ibn 'Abbas in at-Tabari; al-Baghawi) vs the Day of Resurrection/Recompense (Ubayy in at-Tabari; Ibn Kathir, as-Sa'di, Muyassar; al-Qurtubi "coming before Allah"); at-Tabari combines both.
- 'indahu: bil-mirsad (at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi, al-Baghawi, Muyassar); "at his deed" / "came before Allah" (al-Baghawi); Allah's promise / Allah's command at the gathering (al-Qurtubi's qila, "meanings close").
- Pairing with 24:40: leaders/compound vs followers/simple ignorance (Ibn Kathir, en abridged); believers in the Hereafter vs deniers (Ma'arif); both simply parables of disbelievers' deeds (as-Sa'di).
