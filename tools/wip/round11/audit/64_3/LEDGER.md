## Watch-points
- bi-l-haqq: five glosses named and unranked: IK (justice and wisdom; en equity and wisdom), Tabari (justice and fairness), Muyassar (consummate wisdom), Sa'di (wisdom and intended end), Qurtubi (truly/certainly; qila ba = lam, "for the truth" = recompense). Baghawi and Ma'arif silent on it, said so.
- Forming / ahsana: only the sources' glosses (takhtit/tashkil, maththalakum, ashkal, ahsan surah, al-Musawwir; upright not bent per Qurtubi; best of creatures per Qurtubi/Sa'di/Ma'arif). No anatomy or biology from memory. Ma'arif's square-inch figure not reproduced (only "how small the human face is"). Explicit section paragraph (§6.3) that the verse ranks no face and makes no claim about any person's looks; comparison is human vs non-human per Ma'arif, Qurtubi, Sa'di.
- al-masir: IK, Tabari, Muyassar, Qurtubi, Sa'di. No fetched tafsir links it to 64:7-10 or 64:9 by number; article says no such link is drawn. 64:4-5 not discussed (one sentence leaves them to their own verses).
- Shipped neighbours 64:11, 64:15: no overlap. Shipped 40:64 article already covers this clause at length (Tabari, IK, Sa'di 95:4, Muyassar, Qurtubi's siwar reading, Baghawi/Ibn 'Abbas upright with hands, Ma'arif hands/feet, Sa'di limb-by-limb): article points to it and does not repeat those points. 95:4 and 82:6 articles not repeated; card lesson differs from 40:64 and 95:4 lessons.
