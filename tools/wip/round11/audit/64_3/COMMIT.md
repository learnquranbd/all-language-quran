Card and 8-section bilingual article for 64:3: bi-l-haqq, wa sawwarakum
fa-ahsana suwarakum and wa ilayhi al-masir. 40:64's treatment of the
same clause pointed to, not repeated; 64:4-5 left to their own verses.

Sources: Ibn Kathir (ar; en abridged for one gloss), Tabari, Muyassar,
Qurtubi, Sa'di, Ma'arif; Baghawi noted as giving the verse only.
- Bi-l-haqq, five glosses unranked: justice and wisdom (IK), justice
  and fairness (Tabari), consummate wisdom (Muyassar), wisdom and an
  intended end (Sa'di), truly/certainly or "for the truth" = recompense
  (Qurtubi, qila).
- Taswir: takhtit and tashkil (Qurtubi), maththalakum (Tabari), ashkal
  (IK), ahsan surah (Muyassar); al-Musawwir and the blessing (Ma'arif).
- Ahsana: best of living creatures, upright not bent, 95:4 (Qurtubi);
  95:4 (Sa'di); 82:6-8 and 40:64 (IK); faces unique, human shape most
  beautiful relative to non-human creatures (Ma'arif).
- Al-masir: marji' wa ma'ab (IK), all of you (Tabari), recompense
  (Muyassar, Qurtubi), questioning about thanks for blessings (Sa'di).
Disagreement kept: sawwarakum = Adam created with His hand (Muqatil,
Ibn 'Abbas via Tabari) vs all creatures (Qurtubi's second view).
Explicit paragraph: the verse ranks no face and makes no claim about
anyone's looks. No licensing sentence needed (no group addressed).
Hadith: none attached by any tafsir; said in one sentence. No asbab.
No 64:9 link drawn: no fetched tafsir makes it.
Dropped: 53:31 echo (uncited by Qurtubi), Ma'arif's face-size figures,
grading of Tabari's chain.
