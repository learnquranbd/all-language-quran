Card and 8-section bilingual article (1,757 EN words) on the answer to the
oath of 56:75-76: innahu la-qur'anun karim.

Sources: all eight fetched tafsirs named. Ma'arif sets 75-76 as the oath and
77 onward as its jawab; at-Tabari and al-Baghawi take the pronoun as the
Qur'an; Ibn Kathir links the greatness of the oath to the greatness of what
is sworn and reads fala as a denial of the magic/sorcery charge; al-Qurtubi
and Ma'arif name the charges answered (magic, soothsaying, fabrication,
human forgery, devil-inspired speech), Ibn Kathir adds poetry at 56:80.

Karim glosses laid out, none chosen: rank (Ibn Kathir kitab 'azim,
al-Baghawi 'aziz mukram, Ma'arif, al-Qurtubi mahmud and mu'jiza) and giving
(as-Sa'di and the Muyassar kathir al-khayr ghazir al-'ilm, al-Baghawi's ahl
al-ma'ani, al-Qurtubi karim al-akhlaq); al-Qurtubi's honoured among believers
and the people of heaven, and his qila reports, ghayr makhluq not developed.

Disagreement kept: Ibn Kathir vs some commentators on whether the la of
fala uqsimu is redundant.

Hadith: none attaches to 56:77; Ibn Kathir's Zayd ibn Khalid narration
belongs to 56:82 and is said so. No asbab in the sources.

Dropped: the 56:79 al-mutahharun dispute (only noted as that verse's
matter), mawaqi' glosses, 56:81-82 glosses, uncited parallels.
Licensing sentence included, since the article names the deniers' charges.
