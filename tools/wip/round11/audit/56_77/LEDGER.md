# SOURCES ledger, 56:77

## Tafsir fetched (all from PREP.md, none re-fetched)
14 Ibn Kathir (ar), 15 at-Tabari, 16 al-Muyassar (group 77-79), 90 al-Qurtubi, 91 as-Sa'di,
94 al-Baghawi, 168 Ma'arif (en), 169 Ibn Kathir (en, group 75-82). All eight named.

## Attributions and the clause each rests on
- Ma'arif (§1.1, 3.2, 4.1, 7.1): "Verses [75-76] constituted oath and the current set of verses is
  the subject of the oath jawab-ul-qasam"; "The Qur'an is a noble and glorious Book"; "refutes the
  assumption of the pagans that this Book has been forged by a human being or ... speech inspired by
  the devil"; "a protected book [i.e. the Preserved Tablet]".
- at-Tabari (§1.2): فلا أقسم بمواقع النجوم أن هذا القرآن لقرآن كريم، والهاء في قوله: ( إنه ) من ذكر القرآن
- al-Baghawi (§1.2, 4.1, 5.2): (إنه) يعني هذا الكتاب وهو موضع القسم / عزيز مكرم لأنه كلام الله /
  قال بعض أهل المعاني: الكريم الذي من شأنه أن يعطي الخير الكثير
- as-Sa'di (§1.2, 5.1, 8.2): فهو إثبات القرآن، وأنه حق لا ريب فيه، ولا شك يعتريه / كثير الخير، غزير العلم،
  فكل خير وعلم، فإنما يستفاد من كتاب الله ويستنبط منه
- Ibn Kathir ar (§2.2, 4.1): إن هذا القرآن الذي نزل على محمد لكتاب عظيم
- Ibn Kathir en, group file (§2.1, 3.1, 3.2, 4.1): "if you knew the greatness of this vow, you will know
  the greatness of the subject of the vow"; La "is not an extra character without meaning, as some of
  the scholars of Tafsir say ... The matter is not as you people claim - about the Qur'an - that it is
  a result of magic or sorcery, rather it is an Honorable Qur'an"; Ibn Jarir: "Some of the scholars of
  the Arabic language said ... `The matter is not as you people have claimed.' Then He renews the
  oath"; "a Glorious Book"; on 56:80 "not as they say that it is magic, sorcery or poetry".
- al-Qurtubi (§2.2, 3.2, 4.2, 5.2, 6.1, 6.2): قيل: إن الهاء تعود على القرآن، أي: إن القرآن لقسم عظيم؛ قاله
  ابن عباس وغيره. وقيل: ما أقسم الله به عظيم / إنه لقرآن كريم ذكر المقسم عليه / ليس بسحر ولا كهانة، وليس
  بمفترى، بل هو قرآن كريم محمود، جعله الله تعالى معجزة لنبيه / كريم على المؤمنين، لأنه كلام ربهم، وشفاء
  صدورهم، كريم على أهل السماء، لأنه تنزيل ربهم ووحيه / وقيل: كريم أي: غير مخلوق / وقيل: كريم لما فيه من
  كريم الأخلاق ومعاني الأمور / وقيل: لأنه يكرم حافظه، ويعظم قارئه
  (The Ibn 'Abbas remark is about the "mighty oath" wording of 56:76; the article presents it as a
  remark on that oath, not as a reading of 56:77's pronoun.)
- al-Muyassar (§5.1, 7.1): لقرآن عظيم المنافع، كثير الخير، غزير العلم، في كتاب مَصُون مستور عن أعين الخلق،
  وهو الكتاب الذي بأيدي الملائكة

## Glosses of karim, laid out, none chosen (§4-6)
Rank: Ibn Kathir, al-Baghawi (first gloss), Ma'arif, al-Qurtubi (mahmud, mu'jiza).
Giving: as-Sa'di, Muyassar, al-Baghawi's ahl al-ma'ani, al-Qurtubi (karim al-akhlaq).
Al-Qurtubi's "karim to whom" (believers / people of heaven) and his qila reports.
"Ghayr makhluq" reported in one clause, explicitly not developed.

## Disagreement kept
Ibn Kathir vs "some scholars of Tafsir" on whether the la of fala uqsimu is redundant; Ibn Kathir's own
statement of it, no position taken. Al-Qurtubi's two remarks on the mighty oath left side by side.

## Article's own textual observations (no attribution)
56:76 and 56:77 share the frame innahu + la- + closing adjective; Ibn Kathir's 'azim for the Book
matches the adjective 56:76 gives the oath.

## Hadith
None used. No fetched tafsir attaches a hadith to 56:77. The Zayd ibn Khalid narration (rain at
al-Hudaybiyah) in tafsir 169 belongs to 56:82; 'A'ishah's "La, by Allah" is given as an Arabic usage
example for la. Both mentioned only to say they do not bear on 56:77. hadith.js not run. The letter of
'Amr ibn Hazm not mentioned (not in any fetched text for 56:77).

## Asbab an-nuzul
None given in the sources; the article says so and gives the verse's placement.

## Licensing sentence
Included (§3.3), because §3 names the charges of those who rejected the Qur'an at revelation.
