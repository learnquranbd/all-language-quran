Card and 8-section bilingual article for 43:47: fa-lamma ja'ahum
bi-ayatina idha hum minha yadhakun. 43:46 used as the frame; 43:48-50 one
line (the lamma ... idha hum frame of 43:50); 43:51-53 not covered.

Sources: Tabari, Muyassar, Baghawi, Qurtubi, Sa'di, Ibn Kathir (ar; en
abridged), Ma'arif.
- Signs as proofs: Tabari (hujaj, adilla; call to tawhid and disowning
  other gods, tied to 43:45), Muyassar (bayyinat wadihat). Signs named
  only as IK (en) names them, with 7:133-135; qummal left untranslated.
- idha: no fetched tafsir glosses it; the article says so and stays with
  the text (same frame at 43:50 and 7:135).
- Object of the laughter kept as a difference of emphasis: the signs
  (Tabari, Muyassar, Baghawi) vs also the bearer (IK ar and en).
- Motives only as attributed: Qurtubi (followers made to think it was
  sorcery), Sa'di (zulm, 'uluww; no shortfall or unclarity in the signs),
  IK (istakbaru). No psychology in the article's own voice.
- Tabari's tasliya for the Prophet ﷺ (four points) and Muyassar's "as We
  sent you"; Ma'arif on the "not wealthy" objection and the drowning.
- Laughter elsewhere: 53:43, 11:71, 23:110, 83:29, 83:34 (quran-json).
- Licensing sentence in both languages; Musa (AS)'s message not framed
  as weak or failed.
Hadith: none; no fetched tafsir attaches one to 43:47 (stated in text).
Dropped: grammatical glosses of idha and yadhakun, any gloss of qummal,
43:49 "O sorcerer" and 43:51 palaces/rivers material.
