## Watch-points
- Signs named only as IK (en) names them (hand, staff, flood, locusts, qummal,
  frogs, blood, loss of crops and lives). Qummal left untranslated in both
  languages, as IK leaves it.
- idha (surprise): no fetched tafsir glosses the particle. Article says so and
  stops at the text (translation's "at once"; same frame in 43:50 and 7:135).
  The term fuja'iyya is not used.
- Laughter: motives only as attributed (Qurtubi: making followers think the
  signs were sorcery; Sa'di: zulm and 'uluww; IK: istakbaru). Article states it
  adds no motive of its own.
- Object of laughter kept as a difference of emphasis, no position: the signs
  (verse's minha; Tabari, Muyassar, Baghawi) vs also the bearer (IK ar and en).
- Musa (AS): article's own voice says he delivered the message; Sa'di's
  "no shortfall or unclarity in the signs" carries the point.
- Licensing sentence in both languages (§7.1): licenses nothing against any
  living person or community; no people of today equated with Pharaoh's people.
