Card and deep bilingual article on 29:47: the Book sent down as
earlier Scriptures were, and the response that sorts its hearers.

Sources gave a steady core: wa-kadhalika ties this revelation to the
line of sending before it (Ibn Jarir via Ibn Kathir, at-Tabari,
Muyassar, Baghawi, Sa'di). "Those given the Scripture [who] believe"
= the sincere People of the Book, named as ʿAbdullah ibn Salam and
his like (Ibn Kathir, at-Tabari, Baghawi, Muyassar). "Among these" =
the Makkans/Quraysh who believed (all four, plus at-Tabari's "those
among you today"). jahd = denial of what one inwardly knows, "ʿala
ʿilm ... ʿinadan" (at-Tabari), the man veiling the sun (Ibn Kathir);
Qatada: "al-juhud innama yakunu baʿda al-maʿrifa" (at-Tabari,
Baghawi). Sa'di's hasr: rejection cannot come from a sincere seeker.

Disagreement kept: most mufassirun name specific believers (historical
reading); as-Sa'di reads the clause as a disposition (anyone given
Scripture who meets it without envy) — both held, no position taken.

Hadith: Sahih al-Bukhari 7274 (Abu Hurayra), whole wording, the
revealed Book as the Prophet's standing sign; cited by Ibn Kathir on
this verse. Dropped the ʿIyad b. Himar hadith as off-verse (29:48–49).

Balance enforced: the verse affirms believers among ahl al-kitab and
licenses nothing against any living Jewish or Christian person or
community. al-Baghawi's bare "al-yahud knew and rejected" narrowed to
"some among the People of the Book" to avoid a blanket reading.

al-Qurtubi has no tafsir for the verse; Ma'arif text is on 29:46 and
was not attributed. Asbab: none used; verse placement given instead.
