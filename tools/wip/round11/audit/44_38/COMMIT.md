Replaces the 756-word, 6-section article with a 1,777-word, 8-section depth article (shipped card kept).

Sources: at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi, as-Sa'di, Ibn Kathir (ar, en abridged), al-Baghawi,
al-Muyassar, Ma'arif al-Qur'an on 44:38, plus all seven on 44:39, fetched for the
completing verse (Ibn Kathir en grouped 44:38-42, Ma'arif grouped 44:37-38 and 44:39-42).
- Placement: the reply to the deniers of 44:34-36; at-Tabari's tanbih on the soundness
  of the resurrection and requital; Tubba' in one line from Ma'arif (royal title of
  Himyar, "the people of Tubba'", not Tubba' himself).
- La'ibin kept as differing glosses: ghafilin (Muqatil) vs lahin (al-Kalbi) via
  al-Qurtubi; as-Sa'di's la'ib, lahw, suda without benefit; Ibn Kathir's justice
  reading with 38:27 and 23:115-116 (one paragraph, not rebuilding 23:115's article).
- Bil-haqq in one section: by the true command (Muqatil), for the truth (al-Kalbi,
  al-Hasan), to establish tawhid and obedience; al-Baghawi's reward and punishment;
  at-Tabari's sound governance; al-Muyassar's sunna of Allah; as-Sa'di's purpose;
  al-Qurtubi's back-reference to al-Anbiya (21:16).
- At-Tabari's play-versus-test paraphrase with 53:31; Ma'arif's four realities.
- Most of them: these polytheists (at-Tabari, al-Muyassar) vs most people
  (al-Qurtubi); no reflection (al-Muyassar, as-Sa'di). 44:40-42 in one line via Ibn Kathir.
- Hadith: none attached to 44:38; Ma'arif's Tubba' narration (Ahmad et al.) could not
  be confirmed via quranx, so it is not quoted. Licensing sentence in both languages.
- Dropped from the old article (unsourced): la'ibin's three occurrences, haqq as
  true-and-due, 21:17, the 6:32 / 57:20 play-is-not-forbidden section.
