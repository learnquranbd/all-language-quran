Condemns the splitting of the one dīn into self-satisfied sects, every
faction smug in its own portion, as the mufassirūn read it — in the
abstract, naming no living group and licensing nothing against any
living person or community, and not as a tool of takfīr.

Sources (all pre-fetched, none from memory):
- Qirāʾāt kept as a dispute, no position: farraqū "altered it, believed
  in part, disbelieved in part" (Ibn Kathir, at-Tabari) vs fāraqū "left
  it behind their backs / parted from tawhid" (Hamza, al-Kisā'ī, 'Ali —
  al-Qurtubi; Ibn Kathir "some read").
- Who is meant kept as a range: the mushrikīn (as-Sa'di, al-Muyassar,
  Ibn Kathir); the Jews and Christians (Qatada, Ibn Zayd, al-Baghawi,
  ar-Rabi' b. Anas, Ma'mar); the people of the qibla / ahl al-bidaʿ
  (Abu Hurayra, 'A'isha, Abu Umama — al-Qurtubi; al-Baghawi 2nd view).
- shiyaʿan: shīʿah = followers of a leader (Ma'arif); firaq (al-Kalbī),
  adyān (Muqatil). fariḥūn = masrūrūn muʿjabūn, each sure the truth is
  with it alone (al-Qurtubi, at-Tabari, as-Sa'di, Ma'arif).
- Grammar noted lightly: connected to "do not be of the mushrikīn" vs
  istiʾnāf (al-Farrā', an-Naḥḥās).
- as-Sa'di's turn made the spine: a warning to the Muslims against their
  own tafriqa over hidden / branch issues, among Shaytan's greatest
  provocations; uniting the word among the best jihad.

Hadith: at-Tirmidhi 2641 ('Abdullah ibn 'Amr), quoted whole, graded by
at-Tirmidhi himself ḥasan gharīb (mufassar) — reported as given, not
upgraded. The saved group named by a description (what the Prophet ﷺ and
his Companions were upon), not a roster for excommunication; al-Hakim's
Mustadrak version named only as corroboration, not quoted.
