Card and deep bilingual article for 30:9 — travel the earth and
see the end of those before: nations stronger and better built,
yet ruined once they denied clear proof. Not God's injustice;
they wronged themselves.

Sources read (all from PREP, none re-fetched): at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi,
as-Sa'di, al-Baghawi, al-Muyassar, Ibn Kathir (ar + en abridged),
Ma'arif al-Qur'an (en, grouped 30:9-14; cited only from its 30:9 part).

Kept the athārū al-arḍ dispute as a dispute, named, no position:
plough/till for cultivation (Mujāhid via Tabari, al-Qurtubi, al-Baghawi,
al-Muyassar) vs excavate/extract — mining and drawing water (Tabari's
istakhrajū, Ma'arif) vs owned the land (Ibn ʿAbbās via Tabari).
ʿamarūhā = built up and settled (as-Sa'di: palaces, workshops, trees,
crops, rivers). al-Baghawi's distinct gloss of the acquittal clause
(no shortchanging of their due) kept beside al-Qurtubi's (no ruin
without sin, messenger, proof).

Hadith: Ṣaḥīḥ al-Bukhārī 3381 (Ibn ʿUmar), the al-Ḥijr warning not
to enter the dwellings of "those who wronged themselves" except
weeping — quotes the verse's own closing phrase. Verified on quranx;
graded only as al-Bukhārī's inclusion, not upgraded.

Distinct from 29:20 (shipped this session): that verse reads
resurrection off creation's beginning; this one reads the moral from
nations' endings. Pairing named in §2, angle not reused.

Licensing sentence included in both languages (§8): the verse records
a closed, just decree upon named dead peoples and licenses nothing
against any living person or community.

No asbāb asserted (none established in the fetched sources); Qatāda's
"āthār" cross-link dropped as off-focus. 8 sections.
