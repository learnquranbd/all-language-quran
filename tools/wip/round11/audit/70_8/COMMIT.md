Card and 8-section bilingual article for 70:8: yawma takunu al-sama'u
ka-l-muhl. 70:1-7 as the frame (Ma'arif on 70:6-7: far/near as
possibility); 70:9 only as Ibn Kathir and the Muyassar tie it.

Sources: Tabari, Qurtubi, Ibn Kathir (ar; en grouped 70:8-17), Sa'di,
Baghawi, Muyassar (grouped 70:8-9), Ma'arif (grouped 70:6-9). All named.
- Governing word of yawma (Qurtubi): waqi' of 70:1 as his own; narahu,
  yubassarunahum and badal from qariban as wa-qila. No position.
- al-muhl kept as a disagreement, none picked: residue of oil (Ibn
  Abbas, Mujahid, Ata, Sa'id ibn Jubayr, Ikrima, Suddi via IK; Ibn Abbas
  via Qurtubi; Baghawi; Muyassar "flowing"); lead, copper and silver
  (Ibn Mas'ud via Qurtubi); melted silver (al-Hasan via Baghawi); molten
  lead from splitting and terror (Sa'di); pus and blood (Mujahid via
  Qurtubi); "the thing melted" (Tabari, who defers to his earlier
  discussion). Mujahid's two reports in IK and Qurtubi both kept.
- 18:29 and 44:45, the only other occurrences of al-muhl, used because
  Qurtubi points to al-Kahf and ad-Dukhan; his comments there not
  imported. 101:5 via IK for 70:9.
- en/bn translations' differing choices (murky oil; molten silver;
  molten copper at 44:45) shown as translators' single picks.
Hadith: none attached by any source; said in one sentence. Ibn Mas'ud's
gloss described as a Companion's word-explanation, chainless here.
Asbab: none. No astronomy. No licensing sentence (no group addressed).
Dropped: 55:37 (no source cites it), 70:10-14 content, 70:4 content.
