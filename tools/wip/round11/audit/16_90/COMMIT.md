Replaces the 724-word, 5-section article with a 1,782-word, 8-section one.

Sources (all fetched for 16:90): at-Tabari reads 'adl as insaf beginning with
thanks to the Giver, hence tawhid (Ibn 'Abbas: the shahada), ihsan as steady
obedience = performing the obligations, kin as the right of the womb, fahsha'
as zina, baghy as arrogance and oppression, ya'izukum as yusikum. Al-Qurtubi
sets out the disagreement (fard/nafila; 'Ali: insaf/tafaddul; Ibn 'Atiyya),
Ibn al-'Arabi's three justices, ihsan's two senses (the caged bird and cat),
al-Bukhari's chapter with Ibn Battal, and al-Mansur's governor "just, not
muhsin". Ibn Kathir: qist and muwazana, ihsan recommended (16:126, 42:40,
5:45), munkar as the open, 7:33. As-Sa'di: the verse as a rule for every
particular. Al-Baghawi (Muqatil, ikhlas), Muyassar, Ma'arif (17:26 object).

Disagreements kept: al-Qurtubi questions Ibn 'Abbas's "performing the
obligations" via the Jibril hadith; at-Tabari takes it without objection.
Glosses of fahsha'/munkar/baghy given per mufassir. Ibn 'Uyayna's inner/outer
reading attributed, noting al-Qurtubi's copy lacks the fahsha'/munkar part.

Hadith: Tirmidhi 2511 (hasan sahih) quoted whole, marked as not mentioning
the verse; Bukhari 50 (ihsan exchange) and Bukhari 6063 (closing words), each
marked as part of a longer hadith. 'Uthman ibn Maz'un from Ahmad given as
Ibn Kathir's citation with his grading; Musnad not checked.

Dropped: 'Umar ibn 'Abd al-'Aziz khutba report (in no fetched file), Aktham,
al-Walid, khutba practice (space). Old article's unsourced "list moves
outward", vice/command pairing and 5:8/4:135 framing removed.
