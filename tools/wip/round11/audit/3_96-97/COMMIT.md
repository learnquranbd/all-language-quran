Key 3:96-97 as given: the first House at Bakkah, the station of Ibrahim, safety,
and Hajj owed by the able. New card and a 1,780-word, 8-section article.

Sources: at-Tabari, Ibn Kathir (ar), al-Qurtubi, al-Baghawi, al-Muyassar and
Ma'arif on 3:96 and 3:97; as-Sa'di on 3:96 only (the API has no 3:97 text for him).
- "First": first for worship (Ali, al-Hasan) vs first house of all (Abdullah ibn
  Amr, Mujahid, as-Suddi, Qatadah); at-Tabari's and Ibn Kathir's preferences
  given as theirs; Ibn Kathir's weak grading of al-Bayhaqi's Adam report.
- Bakkah: same as Makkah (ad-Dahhak, Mujahid, Ma'arif) vs the House or mosque
  only (Abu Malik, an-Nakha'i, Malik, Ibn Abbas's bounds); Ibn az-Zubayr two ways.
Kept as differences, no position: report vs command in "whoever enters it is
safe", and the refuge-seeker (Qatadah, al-Hasan, Ibn Abbas with Abu Hanifah,
Mujahid, Ibn al-Arabi via al-Qurtubi, at-Tabari); provision-and-mount vs
capacity (at-Tabari, Malik, Ikrimah); man kafara as denial vs neglect (Ibn
Abbas, Mujahid, as-Suddi, Ibn Zayd, al-Hasan reported both ways, al-Qurtubi's
taghlidh, Ma'arif).
Asbab: the Muslim-Jewish boast (Mujahid in al-Qurtubi, al-Baghawi) reported
as unchained; placement after 3:95 kept as the surer ground.
Hadith: Muslim 520 (Abu Dharr, first mosque, the earth a mosque) quoted whole;
Tirmidhi 813 (provision and a mount) with at-Tirmidhi's "hasan" and his note
on al-Khuzi, beside at-Tabari's rejection of those chains.
Licensing stated twice (the boast; Ikrimah's report on the Jews).
Dropped: the "die a Jew or Christian" reports (Ali via al-Harith, Abu Umamah,
'Umar), Muslim 1337 (confirmed; budget), the 100,000-prayers and Jamarat
narrations, Ka'bah rebuild history, and all Hajj fiqh (left to the Hajj module).
