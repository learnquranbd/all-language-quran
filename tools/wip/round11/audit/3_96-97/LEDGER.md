## Watch-points
- **"First":** both readings kept with names (worship: Ali, al-Hasan; absolute: Abdullah ibn Amr, Mujahid, as-Suddi, Qatadah). At-Tabari's and Ibn Kathir's preferences are given as theirs. 22:26 already covers who built it, so the builder question is not repeated.
- **Abu Dharr hadith:** confirmed by hadith.js. Quoted whole in Muslim's wording (520b, the Ali ibn Mushir route with Ibrahim at-Taymi's prostration-in-the-road frame). 22:26 ships Bukhari 3366, so a different collection's wording is used here, with no hybrid.
- **Bakkah vs Makkah:** both views given with names (same place: ad-Dahhak, Mujahid in al-Qurtubi, Ma'arif; narrower place: Abu Malik al-Ghifari, Ibrahim an-Nakha'i, Malik, Ibn Abbas's boundaries). At-Tabari's own conclusion is attributed to him. Ibn az-Zubayr is reported two ways, and both are stated. The article takes no position.
- **Report or command, and the refuge-seeker:** report reading (Qatadah, al-Hasan, al-Qurtubi's preference); command reading (al-Qurtubi's "some people of meanings", al-Baghawi's qila); Ibn Abbas's no-dealings view with Abu Hanifah; Mujahid's take-him-out; Ibn al-Arabi's majority claim with Ibn Khatal; at-Tabari's safe-while-inside reading; the agreement on crimes committed inside. No winner chosen.
- **Istita'ah:** Tirmidhi 813 is given with at-Tirmidhi's own grading ("hasan") and his own note on al-Khuzi's memory. At-Tabari's judgment that the marfu' reports are unfit as proof is given beside it. Both are kept, with no position.
- **Man kafara:** denial (Ibn Abbas, al-Hasan in at-Tabari, Ata', ad-Dahhak); no-birr/no-sin (Mujahid, Ibn Abbas); disbelief in Allah (Mujahid); Ikrimah's report about the Jews; neglect is kufr (as-Suddi; al-Qurtubi's report from al-Hasan); Ibn Zayd against it; at-Tabari's preference; al-Qurtubi's taghlidh; Ma'arif's warning. Al-Hasan is reported both ways, and the article says so.
- **Licensing:** stated in both languages, at §1.2 (the boast report) and §7.1 (Ikrimah's report).
- **No Hajj manual:** the article points to the app's Hajj module (§8.1). The fiqh detail (mahram, safe route, ma'dub, fawr/tarakhi) is left out.
- **Overlap:** the maqam stone and where it stood are left to 2:125 (one pointer in §4.2). No hadith from 2:125-126, 2:196 or 22:26 is reused.

## Asbab an-nuzul
- The Muslim-Jewish boast over which House is greater: from Mujahid in al-Qurtubi (no isnad) and from al-Baghawi (no source). The article reports it as attributed, says neither carries a chain, and treats the placement after 3:93-95 as the surer ground. It is not presented as established.
- On "man kafara": Ikrimah's report (Ibn Kathir, via Sa'id ibn Mansur) is used as a mufassir's reading. Sa'id ibn al-Musayyib's version in al-Baghawi and ad-Dahhak's "six religions" report in at-Tabari (5936) are not used.

## Dropped
- Ali via al-Harith, "may die a Jew or a Christian" (at-Tirmidhi: gharib, isnad has issues, Hilal unknown, al-Harith weak; al-Bukhari: Hilal munkar al-hadith), with its Abu Umamah (al-Baghawi) and Abd Khayr (al-Qurtubi) versions. Left out under the watch-point, because the collector's grading is weak.
- 'Umar: "whoever could perform Hajj and did not, it is the same whether he dies a Jew or a Christian" (al-Isma'ili, which Ibn Kathir calls sahih to 'Umar), and the jizyah statement (Sa'id ibn Mansur, via al-Hasan). Both are harsh, and neither collector's grading can be confirmed on a fetched page.
- Al-Bayhaqi's marfu' report of Adam building the House: it appears only as Ibn Kathir's judgment (Ibn Lahi'ah, weak) and is not quoted. Ma'arif cites the same report without a grading; it is not used.
- Al-Bayhaqi's report "whoever enters the House enters into good" (Ibn Kathir: Abdullah ibn al-Mu'ammal alone, not strong). Not used.
- Ma'arif's 100,000-prayers narration (Ibn Majah/Tahawi), al-Baghawi's "obedience and charity multiplied 100,000", and al-Baghawi's Abu Hurayrah hadith on prayer in the Prophet's mosque: none fetched on quranx, so not used.
- Ma'arif's report that angels lift the Jamarat pebbles, attributed to the Prophet ﷺ: not confirmed, dropped.
- The Ka'bah's rebuilding history in Ma'arif (Jurhum, Amalekites, Quraysh, Ibn az-Zubayr, al-Hajjaj, Malik's fatwa) and the stone that rose with the walls: out of scope, and not confirmed.
- Al-Qurtubi's problem of al-Aqsa being built by Sulayman a thousand years later, and its resolution: dropped for budget.
- An-Nahhas's list of signs in al-Qurtubi (birds not flying over the House, rain on the corners), al-Baghawi's similar list, and Ibn Abbas's "the whole Haram is the maqam": dropped for budget.
- At-Tabari's unattributed "blessed because tawaf around it is forgiveness of sins" (the editor says it is al-Farra's): dropped.
- Fiqh detail: Hajj immediate vs deferred (al-Qurtubi §2), ma'dub and ability through another (al-Qurtubi §7, al-Baghawi, al-Khath'amiyyah), mahram and a safe route (Ma'arif). Left to the Hajj module; the article mentions none of it.

- Az-Zuhri's "Bakkah is the House and the mosque, Makkah the whole sanctuary" (at-Tabari 7444, al-Qurtubi): cut to keep to the word budget.
