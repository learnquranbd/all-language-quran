# SOURCES ledger — 26:123 (كذبت عاد المرسلين)

## Tafsir fetched (files in this dir)
- **t15 at-Tabari (ar)** — USED. Clause: «يقول تعالى ذكره: (كَذَّبَتْ عَادٌ) رسل الله إليهم.»
  → glossed as "ʿĀd denied the messengers of God sent to them" (§1.1).
- **t16 al-Muyassar (ar)** — USED. Clause: «كذَّبت قبيلة عاد رسولهم هودًا … فكانوا بهذا مكذِّبين
  لجميع الرسل؛ لاتحاد دعوتهم في أصولها وغايتها.» → ʿĀd denied their one messenger Hūd and so
  denied all the messengers, because the call is one in foundations and goal (§2.1).
  (Muyassar is not in the sweep STEM list, so no bn name-parity requirement; rendered মুয়াসসার.)
- **t91 as-Sa'di (ar)** — USED. Clause: «كذبت القبيلة المسماة عادا رسولهم هودا، وتكذيبهم له
  تكذيب لغيره, لاتفاق الدعوة.» → denial of him is denial of the others, the call being in
  agreement (§2.1). bn সাদী.
- **t90 al-Qurtubi (ar)** — USED. Clause: «التأنيث بمعنى القبيلة والجماعة. وتكذيبهم المرسلين
  كما تقدم.» → the feminine verb is for al-qabīla / al-jamāʿa (§3.1); "as has already come
  before" = the repeating frame, back to the Nūḥ opening (§5.1). bn কুরতুবী.
- **t14 Ibn Kathir (ar)** + **t169 Ibn Kathir (en, abridged)** — USED for 26:123 framing only.
  Clauses used: dwelt in al-Aḥqāf (sand-hills near Ḥaḍramawt bordering Yemen); came after the
  people of Nūḥ (cites al-Aʿrāf 7:69, "successors … increased you in stature"); great strength,
  tall stature, flowing provisions, wealth, gardens and springs, children, crops and fruits;
  "and with all that, they worshipped other than Allah." (§4.1–4.2). bn কাসীর.
- **t94 al-Baghawi (ar)** — NOT USED (only re-quotes the verse, no content).
- **t168 Ma'arif al-Qur'an (en)** — NOT USED: PREP marks it WRONG GROUP (covers 26:111–120).

## Hadith (article §6)
- **Sahih al-Bukhari 3443**, from Abu Hurayrah (RA).
  URL fetched: https://quranx.com/hadith/Bukhari/DarusSalam/Hadith-3443
  First ten words (EN used): "Both in this world and in the Hereafter, I am…"
  Arabic opening: «أَنَا أَوْلَى النَّاسِ بِعِيسَى ابْنِ مَرْيَمَ …، وَالأَنْبِيَاءُ إِخْوَةٌ لِعَلاَّتٍ، أُمَّهَاتُهُمْ شَتَّى، وَدِينُهُمْ وَاحِدٌ»
  Grading: contained in al-Bukhari's Ṣaḥīḥ → authentic by its collector; not upgraded further.
  Relevance: "their religion is one" (dīnuhum wāḥid) grounds the deny-one = deny-all idiom.
  Quoted whole, one collection's wording, not blended with any variant.
