# SOURCES ledger — 27:54

Sensitive verse (people of Lūṭ). Register kept sober; the condemned object is a
CONDUCT (the fāḥisha) as the Qur'an frames it. Licensing section (§6) is prominent
in both languages: licenses nothing against any living person or community;
judgement belongs to Allah and lawful authority under due process, never private
hands; no slur, no caricature, no vigilantism; human dignity untouched.

## Tafsir fetched (PREP.md, not re-fetched)
- t14 Ibn Kathir (ar) — USED. Arabic clauses rested on:
  - "إتيان الذكور دون الإناث ... فاحشة عظيمة" → the fāḥisha named as approaching males
    instead of females, which none of mankind did before them (§1, §3).
  - "أي: يرى بعضكم بعضا، وتأتون في ناديكم المنكر" → the open/brazen reading; the word
    nādī (assembly) (§4).
- t15 at-Tabari — USED. "أَتَأْتُونَ الْفَاحِشَةَ وَأَنْتُمْ تُبْصِرُونَ أنها فاحشة؛ لعلمكم بأنه لم
  يسبقكم ... أحد" → tubṣirūn = you perceive it is a fāḥisha, grounded in knowing none
  preceded them (§2, §3). Also the opening "وأرسلنا لوطا إلى قومه ... يا قوم" (§1).
- t16 al-Muyassar (covers 54,55) — USED for 54 only. "أتأتون الفعلة المتناهية في القبح،
  وأنتم تعلمون قبحها" → tubṣirūn = while you know its ugliness; and "خالفتم ... أمره،
  وعصيتم رسوله" → opposed His command, disobeyed His messenger (§2, §3). 27:55 half not used.
- t90 al-Qurtubi — USED. "وهم أهل سدوم" → people of Sodom (§1). "وأنتم تبصرون أنها فاحشة،
  وذلك أعظم لذنوبكم" → knowing-reading, sin is greater for it (§3). "وقيل: يأتي بعضكم بعضا
  وأنتم تنظرون إليه. وكانوا لا يستترون عتوا منهم وتمردا" → the open reading; no concealment
  out of insolence and rebellion (§4).
- t91 as-Sa'di — USED. "الفعلة الشنعاء التي تستفحشها العقول والفطر وتستقبحها الشرائع" →
  minds, fiṭra and the revealed laws judge it ugly. "وأنتم تبصرون ذلك وتعلمون قبحه
  فعاندتم وارتكبتم ذلك ظلما منكم وجرأة على الله" → saw/knew, opposed (ʿinād), out of ẓulm
  and jurʾa ʿalā Allāh (§3, §7).
- t94 al-Baghawi — USED. "وأنتم تبصرون أي: تعلمون أنها فاحشة" (knowing-reading, §3).
  "وقيل: ... يرى بعضكم بعضا وكانوا لا يستترون عتوا منهم" (open reading, §4).
- t169 Ibn Kathir (en abridged, covers 54–58) — USED for 54 only: "while you see one
  another, and you practice every kind of evil in your meetings" (§4). 55–58 are future
  ground — NOT used; the identity gloss "(i.e., homosexuality)" in this source was NOT
  adopted (brief bars clinical/identity vocabulary; conduct named as the Arabic names it).
- t168 Ma'arif (en, covers 49–58) — NOT USED. The fetched text comments on 27:49 (the
  Thamūd/Ṣāliḥ night-plot), not on the Lūṭ verse; nothing in it attaches to 27:54.

## The named difference kept (both senses, no position taken)
(a) inward: tubṣirūn = you perceive/know it is a fāḥisha → Tabari, Baghawi, Muyassar,
    Qurtubi (first), Saʿdi. (b) outward: you see one another, openly, no concealment out
    of insolence → Qurtubi (wa-qīl), Baghawi (wa-qīl), Ibn Kathir. Verse holds both.

## Hadith
- Ṣaḥīḥ al-Bukhārī, Dar-us-Salam 6120 (Kitāb al-Adab).
  URL fetched: https://quranx.com/hadith/Bukhari/DarusSalam/Hadith-6120
  First ten words used: "Among the words people received from the earliest prophethood
  is" (Arabic: "إن مما أدرك الناس من كلام النبوة الأولى إذا لم تستحي فاصنع ما شئت").
  Narrator: Abū Masʿūd. Grading: Bukhārī gives none per hadith; soundness rests on its
  inclusion in his Ṣaḥīḥ — reported so, NOT upgraded.
  Marked in-article (§5) as a GENERAL principle on ḥayāʾ, NOT a narration about the
  people of Lūṭ. Bukhari 3484 is the same report (shorter wording); 6120 chosen.
- Stated plainly in §5: none of the commentaries fetched attaches a hadith to this verse.
