Replaces the 834-word, 6-section article with a 1,757-word, 8-section one,
built only from the eight tafsirs fetched for 4:82.

Who is asked: at-Tabari reads the question as put to the night plotters of
4:81 and al-Qurtubi as faulting the hypocrites; Ibn Kathir, as-Sa'di and
Ma'arif read a command to all. Both kept, with a licensing line in both
languages.

ikhtilaf: Ibn Kathir (disturbance, contradiction), al-Qurtubi and al-Baghawi
(unevenness and contradiction, from Ibn Abbas; al-Qurtubi adds Qatada and
Ibn Zayd and excludes variant readings and surah lengths), at-Tabari (rulings
differing, meanings colliding), and the qila glosses on reports of the unseen
and falsehood. "kathira" answered as Ma'arif (via Bayan al-Qur'an) and
as-Sa'di answer it: no contradiction at all. Apparent clashes as Ibn Kathir
(3:7, clear and ambiguous) and Ibn Zayd treat them.

Hadith: Muslim 2666, cited by Ibn Kathir, quoted whole from the Arabic
(quranx's English misnames the narrator Abdullah b. Umar; the Arabic has
Abdullah b. Amr). Ibn Majah 85 paraphrased, no grading of his own.
