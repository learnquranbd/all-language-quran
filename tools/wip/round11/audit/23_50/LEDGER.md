## Hadith
- NONE used as the sound narration. The only report tying a location to the verse
  is the Murrah al-Bahzi marfu' ("you will die at the rabwah"): Ibn Kathir grades
  it غريب جدا (extremely strange); at-Tabari's chain has Kurayb say he does not
  know what Murrah related (broken/uncertain). Stated as weak in §5.3; claimed as
  no one's binding proof. No sound hadith attaches to the verse — reported as such.

## Asbab an-nuzul
- Not used as a formal sabab. The Bayan-ul-Qur'an background (flight from a tyrant)
  is reported via Ma'arif as context/placement, attributed, not as an established
  occasion of revelation.
