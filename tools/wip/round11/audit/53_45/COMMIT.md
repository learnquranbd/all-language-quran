Card and 8-section bilingual article for 53:45, wa-annahu khalaqa
az-zawjayni adh-dhakara wa-l-untha, in the an/anna chain after 53:36-38.

Sources: Tabari, Qurtubi, Sa'di, Baghawi, Muyassar, Ibn Kathir (ar; en
checked), Ma'arif (silent on this verse; speaks of 53:43 only).
- Sa'di: the verse explains az-zawjayn by male and female; ism jins.
- Tabari: ibtada'a insha'; each is the zawj of the other.
- Scope kept as a disagreement: Qurtubi (children of Adam, not Adam
  and Hawwa from a drop) vs Sa'di, Baghawi, Muyassar (all creatures,
  humans and animals). Tabari and Ibn Kathir silent on scope.
- Grouping with 53:46 per Tabari, Ibn Kathir, Muyassar; drop left to
  its own entry; only Muyassar's "poured into the womb" reported.
- Ibn Kathir's sole parallel 75:36-40, ending on raising the dead;
  53:47 pointed to only.
Text observations checked in quran-json: the full phrase occurs only
at 53:45 and 75:39; huwa absent here but present in 53:43/44/48/49
(noted, not explained).
Hadith: none attached; said so. Asbab: none.
Dropped: 4:1, 35:11, 49:13, 51:49, 53:21, 92:3 (not tafsir-cited); any
embryology or biology; modern gender commentary; Ma'arif's 53:43 notes.
