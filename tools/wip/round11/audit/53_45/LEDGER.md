# SOURCES ledger, 53:45

## Tafsir fetched (all from PREP; none re-fetched) and named
- 15 at-Tabari: NAMED. Quotes 53:45-46 together: "وقوله (وَأَنَّهُ خَلَقَ الزَّوْجَيْنِ الذَّكَرَ وَالأُنْثَى * مِنْ نُطْفَةٍ إِذَا تُمْنَى)".
  Origination: "وأنه ابتدع إنشاء الزوجين الذكر والأنثى, وجعلهما زوجين". Reciprocity (§3):
  "لأن الذكر زوج الأنثى, والأنثى له زوج فهما زوجان, يكون كلّ واحد منهما زوجا للآخر". He names no kind
  (human/animal), stated in §4.3 as "does not take up the question directly".
- 90 al-Qurtubi: NAMED (narrow side of the disagreement). "أي من أولاد آدم ولم يرد آدم وحواء بأنهما
  خلقا من نطفة" rendered as "of the children of Adam ... Allah did not mean by it that Adam (AS) and Hawwa
  were created from a drop". Its dependence on 53:46 is the article's observation from the wording.
- 91 as-Sa'di: NAMED. "فسر الزوجين بقوله: { الذَّكَرَ وَالْأُنْثَى } وهذا اسم جنس شامل لجميع الحيوانات،
  ناطقها وبهيمها، فهو المنفرد بخلقها."
- 94 al-Baghawi: NAMED. "من كل حيوان" (three words after the verse).
- 16 al-Muyassar: NAMED. Grouped 53:45-46: "الذكر والأنثى من الإنسان والحيوان، من نطفة تُصَبُّ في الرحم."
  "poured into the womb" is the only description of the drop used; nothing added.
- 14 Ibn Kathir (ar): NAMED. Quotes 53:45-46 as one unit, then "كقوله" + 75:36-40 in full.
- 169 Ibn Kathir (en, abridged): NAMED. Same parallel (75:36-40); English renderings of 75:36-40 in §6
  follow its translation in substance. Mu'adh ibn Jabal report is on 53:42 (return to Allah), mentioned
  only to say it is not on this verse; not quoted.
- 168 Ma'arif (en): fetched, grouped 53:43-47; text speaks only of 53:43 (laughter/weeping). NAMED only
  to say it has nothing on this verse.

## Disagreement kept (no position taken)
- Scope of az-zawjayn / adh-dhakar wa-l-untha: al-Qurtubi = children of Adam (excluding Adam and Hawwa
  from the drop); as-Sa'di (all living creatures, speaking and dumb), al-Baghawi (every animal),
  al-Muyassar (humans and animals) = wider. Tabari and Ibn Kathir do not address scope.
