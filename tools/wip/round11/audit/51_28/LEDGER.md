## Attributions and the clause each rests on
- Al-Muyassar, not eating -> fear (§1): فلما رآهم لا يأكلون أحسَّ في نفسه خوفًا منهم
- As-Sa'di, hands not reaching (§1): حين رأى أيديهم لا تصل إليه
- At-Tabari, fear concealed (§2): فأوجس في نفسه إبراهيم من ضيفه خيفة وأضمرها
- Al-Qurtubi, sensed within (§2): أي أحس منهم في نفسه خوفا
- Al-Qurtubi, they saw the fear, said la takhaf, disclosed they were angels (§2, §5):
  ولما رأوا ما بإبراهيم من الخوف قالوا " لا تخف " وأعلموه أنهم ملائكة الله ورسله
- Al-Qurtubi, qila: no bond through food; custom (§3):
  وقيل : أضمر لما لم يتحرموا بطعامه . ومن أخلاق الناس أن من تحرم بطعام إنسان أمنه
- Ma'arif (§3): "custom of the noble people ... even the rogues and wrongdoers ... would not
  cause any harm to anyone whose food they had eaten. Hence refusing to eat was a sign of
  hostility or danger."
- Ibn Kathir, refer to Hud 11:70-71, 11:72-73 (§4): هذا محال على ما تقدم في القصة في السورة
  الأخرى ... [هود : 70 ، 71] ... [هود 72 ، 73]
- Ibn Kathir, tidings to him are tidings to her (§4): فالبشارة له هي بشارة لها ; لأن الولد
  منهما ، فكل منهما بشر به
- Al-Qurtubi refers back to Hud (§4): وقد تقدم هذا في " هود "
- Muyassar, "we are messengers of Allah" (§5): لا تَخَفْ إنا رسل الله
- As-Sa'di, told him their errand (§5): وأخبروه بما جاؤوا له
- Ibn Kathir (en) three angels Jibril, Mika'il, Israfil as handsome young men; reason for
  "a people unknown" (§5): "The three angels; Jibril, Mika'il and Israfil came ... This is why
  Ibrahim said ..."
- Ibn Kathir (en) hospitality manners and "would you be kind" simile (§5, §8):
  "this Ayah indicates proper manners for honoring guests ..."
- 'Amr ibn Dinar report via al-Qurtubi (§5): وقال عمرو بن دينار : قالت الملائكة : لا نأكل إلا
  بالثمن ... لهذا اتخذك الله خليلا. Marked in the article as a report, no chain to the
  Prophet or a Companion in the fetched text, not a hadith.
- At-Tabari, 'alim = knowing when grown; al-Farra' note (§6): عليم بمعنى عالم إذا كبر، وذكر
  الفراء ... قد قاله الله في عليم وحكيم وميت
- Al-Qurtubi, knowledge of Allah and His religion after maturity (§6): ومعنى " عليم " أي يكون
  بعد بلوغه من أولي العلم بالله وبدينه
- Al-Muyassar, same (§6, §7): سيكون من أهل العلم بالله وبدينه، وهو إسحاق عليه السلام
- At-Tabari, Ishaq; Mujahid said Isma'il (two chains to Ibn Abi Najih); reason Sarah/Hajar
  (§7): يعني: بإسحاق ... عن ابن أبي نجيح, عن مجاهد ... قال: إسماعيل. وإنما قلت: عنى به
  إسحاق, لأن البشارة كانت بالولد من سارّة, وإسماعيل لهاجَر لا لسارّة
- Al-Qurtubi, jumhur Ishaq; Mujahid alone; "laysa bi-shay'"; وبشرناه بإسحاق نص (§7)
- As-Sa'di, Ishaq (§7): وهو: إسحاق عليه السلام

## Dropped
- Ibn Kathir's gloss on the wife's laughter in 11:71 (rejoicing at the destruction of the
  people of Lut): would bring a condemned people into the article; not needed for 51:28.
- Al-Qurtubi's qila that Ibrahim did not believe the angels until they prayed and the calf
  was revived, and 'Awn ibn Abi Shaddad's report that Jibril wiped the calf with his wing:
  unsourced reports, and the first frames a prophet as disbelieving his visitors.
- 15:51-53 (al-Hijr, where 15:53 has the same بغلام عليم): Ibn Kathir (en) names al-Hijr
  only by surah, with no verse key, so no key or wording from al-Hijr is cited.
- Any reading of 'alim as prophethood: no fetched tafsir glosses it so.
- "Mujahid was an early authority" framing: not in the fetched text; removed.
- Al-Baghawi: text is the verse only, nothing to attribute.
