Card and 8-section bilingual article on fa-awjasa minhum khifa ... wa
bashsharuhu bi-ghulamin 'alim. 51:25-27 as context; 51:29-31 pointers only.

Sources: at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi, Ibn Kathir (ar + en abridged, grouped
51:24-30), al-Muyassar, as-Sa'di, Ma'arif al-Qur'an (al-Baghawi verse only).
- Fear kept inward: Tabari (admaraha), Qurtubi and Muyassar (ahassa fi
  nafsihi); Qurtubi has the guests see it and answer.
- Cause: not eating (Muyassar, Sa'di); Qurtubi's qila on the food bond
  (lam yatahharramu bi-ta'amihi) and Ma'arif's custom note.
- Ibn Kathir reads it through 11:70-73; tidings to him are tidings to her.
- La takhaf joined to disclosure (Muyassar, Qurtubi, Sa'di); Ibn Kathir
  (en) names Jibril, Mika'il, Israfil; his hospitality manners from 51:26-27.
- 'Alim: Tabari (when grown) with al-Farra's note; Qurtubi and Muyassar
  (knowledge of Allah and His religion). Not glossed as prophethood; 37:112
  noted as the Qur'an's own statement only.
- Disagreement kept: Ishaq (Tabari, Qurtubi's jumhur, Muyassar, Sa'di, Ibn
  Kathir via Hud) vs Isma'il (Mujahid, via Tabari's chains); both rebuttals
  reported with their reasons, no position taken.
- 'Amr ibn Dinar's price-of-the-meal report given as Qurtubi's relay, not
  a hadith.
Hadith: none attached by any fetched tafsir; stated in the article.
Asbab: none used.
Dropped: Ibn Kathir's gloss on the wife's laughter (people of Lut); Qurtubi's
revived-calf reports and his qila that Ibrahim disbelieved the angels;
15:51-53 (al-Hijr named by Ibn Kathir only by surah).
