## Hadith
- Sahih al-Bukhari 3682 (Darussalam), https://quranx.com/hadith/Bukhari/DarusSalam/Hadith-3682 (saved hadith_Bukhari_3682.txt). First ten words of EN used: "In a dream I saw myself drawing water from". Quoted whole (Darussalam EN). Collection is Sahih; no separate grading added. Marked as NOT attached to 55:76; usage witness for 'abqari, cited by Ibn Kathir, al-Baghawi, al-Qurtubi via al-Khalil. The page's Arabic also carries "قال ابن جبير العبقري عتاق الزرابي. وقال يحيى الزرابي الطنافس لها خمل رقيق" (used).
- No sound hadith attached to the verse: stated in one sentence. Tabari's prophetic reading report given with his own verdict (not preserved, not sound in chain); Qurtubi's Abu Bakr/Tha'labi report given with "no grading attached".
