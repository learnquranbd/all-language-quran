Card and 8-section bilingual article (1,690 EN words) on 55:76:
muttaki'ina 'ala rafrafin khudrin wa-'abqariyyin hisan. 55:62-75 as
frame only; one pointer each to 55:54 (posture, linings) and 55:77.

Sources: at-Tabari, Ibn Kathir (ar), al-Qurtubi, al-Baghawi, as-Sa'di,
al-Muyassar, Ma'arif (group 55:76-77, 55:76 only). Ibn Kathir en
(group 55:62-71) wrong group, unused.
- rafraf kept as a disagreement, as at-Tabari sets it out with no stated
  preference: riyad al-jannah (Sa'id ibn Jubayr; from Ibn Abbas per
  al-Baghawi); mahabis / their fudul (Ibn Abbas, Qatadah, ad-Dahhak, Ibn
  Zayd, Ghazwan, Marwan); spreads (al-Hasan, Muqatil, al-Qurazi); marafiq
  (al-Hasan, Ibn Kaysan); pillows (al-Jahdari); zarabi (Ibn 'Uyaynah);
  garment border, green spread cloth, broad cloth (al-Qurtubi's lexicon),
  "views close to one another"; root raffa, bird's fluttering, as-Sihah.
- 'abqari glosses attributed: zarabi, finest zarabi, dibaj, tanafis,
  thick tanafis (at-Tabari's text, al-Farra'); 'Abqar (Yemen village;
  Ibn al-Anbari, al-Jawhari "taz'umu") reported as the Arabs' manner of
  speech, explicitly not as fact about jinn; "Allah addressed them with
  what they knew" (al-Qurtubi).
- Readings rafarif/'abaqiri: at-Tabari (prophetic report "not preserved,
  not sound in chain"; Zuhayr al-Furqubi; grammarians), al-Qurtubi
  (Uthman, al-Jahdari, al-Hasan; Qutrub; Tha'labi report, ungraded).
- Comparison of the two pairs only as Ibn Kathir and as-Sa'di draw it.
Hadith: Bukhari 3682 (quranx), quoted whole, marked as a usage witness
for 'abqari, not attached to the verse. No sound hadith on 55:76. Asbab: none.
Dropped: al-Hakim at-Tirmidhi's flying rafraf and its unchained mi'raj
report (via al-Qurtubi); two unidentified narrations in al-Qurtubi.
