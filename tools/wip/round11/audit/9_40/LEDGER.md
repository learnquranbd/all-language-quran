## Disagreements kept (no position)
- Sakinah upon the Prophet ﷺ (at-Tabari; Ibn Kathir "better known of two"; al-Muyassar; Baghawi's qila) vs upon Abu Bakr (Ibn 'Abbas and others via Ibn Kathir and al-Baghawi; Ibn al-'Arabi's scholars "stronger" via al-Qurtubi).
- Kalimat Allah: la ilaha illa Allah (Ibn 'Abbas) vs the promise of help (al-Baghawi, al-Qurtubi); their word: shirk vs their plot (al-Baghawi).
- Junud: three accounts in al-Baghawi; Ma'arif's wider reading.
- Readings kalimatu (majority) / kalimata (al-A'mash, Ya'qub) with al-Farra', Abu Hatim, an-Nahhas.

All left out per watch-point: no sectarian polemic, no comparison of Companions.
