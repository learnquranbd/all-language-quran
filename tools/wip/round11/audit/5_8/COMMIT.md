Replaces the 827-word, 6-section article with a 1,771-word, 9-section one,
built only from the eight tafsirs fetched for 5:8.

Glosses: qawwamina lillah as for Allah and not for people or reputation (Ibn
Kathir), for Allah alone (as-Sa'di), for His reward (al-Qurtubi); at-Tabari's
two failures, overstepping with enemies and falling short with allies.
la yajrimannakum as la yahmilannakum; shana'an as enmity (at-Tabari) or hatred
(Ibn Kathir, al-Baghawi, Muyassar); az-Zajjaj and al-Kisa'i via al-Qurtubi.
As-Sa'di: testify for the enemy and against the ally, accept truth from a
kafir or mubtadi'. Al-Qurtubi: an enemy's testimony stands; no mutilation.
huwa aqrabu: the pronoun for the deed (at-Tabari, Ibn Kathir) and the elative
with nothing on the other side (Ibn Kathir, 25:24).

Occasion: at-Tabari's qila report via Ibn Jurayj from 'Abdullah ibn Kathir
(Jews of Khaybar, the blood-money visit), no grading added; Ibn Kathir ties a
similar plot to 5:11 (Banu an-Nadir). Licensing line in both languages.

Hadith: an-Nu'man ibn Bashir, cited by Ibn Kathir, quoted whole from Bukhari
2587; Bukhari 2650 paraphrased with the narrator's "I think he said".
4:135 order swap as Ma'arif gives it from Abu Hayyan.

Dropped from the old article: the unsourced grammar claims, the 5:1 and 5:2
framing, 60:8 and 16:90, and the Anas hadith no 5:8 tafsir cites.
One bridging line to 5:9-10.
