Card and 8-section bilingual article for 44:3: inna anzalnahu fi laylatin
mubarakatin inna kunna mundhirin. 44:2 as the oath it answers; 44:4 named
as the continuation only; 97:3 a one-line pointer.

Sources: Tabari, Qurtubi, Ibn Kathir (ar), Muyassar, Sa'di, Baghawi,
Ma'arif (44:3 part of a grouped entry). IK (en) duplicates IK (ar).
- Mubarakah glossed as fetched: Muyassar and Sa'di (abundant good),
  Qurtubi (blessings, good and reward sent down in it); Sa'di's best
  speech / best night / best of creation line.
- Dispute kept as a dispute. Night of Decree: Qatada and Ibn Zayd (Tabari,
  Baghawi), IK, Muyassar, Sa'di, Ma'arif's "majority", resting on 97:1 and
  2:185 as IK/Qurtubi/Ma'arif cite them. Mid-Sha'ban: Ikrimah (IK,
  Qurtubi, Ma'arif), unnamed "others" (Tabari, Baghawi). Preferences given
  as each one's own: Tabari's sawab, Qurtubi's asahh, IK's "abʿada
  al-nujʿa", Ma'arif's objection; Ma'arif's Ruh al-Ma'ani reconciliation
  ("if confirmed") and its separate note on mid-Sha'ban's merit.
- Anzalnahu: Qurtubi's three qila readings, no preference; Qatada and
  Ibn Zayd's descent to Bayt al-Izzah, 23 years (Qurtubi) vs 20 years
  (Baghawi), discrepancy reported; Ma'arif's whole-descent reading.
- Mundhirin: IK, Muyassar, Tabari (punishment on the disbeliever), Sa'di
  (rescue of a people covered by ignorance); licensing sentence EN+BN.
Hadith: none sound attached to 44:3, said in one sentence. IK's Sha'ban
report given only with IK's own grading (mursal). Ibn Majah 1390 checked
on quranx, different chain/wording, no collector grading; not used.
Asbab: none in the sources; not used.
Dropped: Baghawi's own-chain mid-Sha'ban report (unconfirmable); Wathila
marfu' date list (Qatada's own statement via Tabari used, labelled);
Tabari's garbled proof-verse clause; 44:4 yearly-decree detail.
