## Dropped
- al-Baghawi's own-chain report (القاسم بن محمد عن أبيه أو عمه عن جده: ينزل الله ... ليلة النصف من شعبان ... إلا إنسانا في قلبه شحناء أو مشركا): could not be confirmed in a collection in this wording; dropped entirely.
- Wathila marfu' list of scripture dates (Qurtubi; Ma'arif citing Qurtubi): not in a hadith.js collection, unconfirmable; named only as what Qurtubi and Ma'arif give, explicitly not relied on. Qatada's own statement from at-Tabari used instead, labelled as Qatada's.
- Ruh al-Ma'ani's Ibn Abbas report: reported only as Ma'arif reports it (no chain, "if confirmed").
- At-Tabari's justification in the fetched text reads "لقوله تعالى (إنا كنا منذرين)" right after "أخبر أن ذلك كذلك", which looks like a dropped phrase; the article gives only "because Allah has informed that it is so", no proof verse attributed to him.
- At-Tabari's mundhirin gloss: fetched vowelling "خلَقْنا" ambiguous; "Our creatures" left out, only the punishment clause used.
- At-Tabari's footnote (Shawkani: Qatada, Bayt al-Izza, 23 years): footnote, not Tabari; not used.
- Ma'arif's yearly-decree detail (Ibn Abbas on 44:4, Mahdawi, angelic scribes) and Muyassar/IK on 44:4: left to 44:4.
- Ibn Kathir (en) opening (Ibn Sayyad, Bazzar): about the surah, not 44:3; not used.
- Discrepancy kept, not resolved: Qurtubi (Qatada and Ibn Zayd) 23 years vs Baghawi (same names) 20 years.
- Sa'di's "قوما عمتهم الجهالة" names no community; licensing sentence included anyway (sec 7, EN+BN) as a precaution.
