Replaces the 907-word, 6-section article with a 9-section, 1,744-word
source-gated article (shipped card kept).

Sources: at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi, as-Sa'di, Ibn Kathir (ar, en abridged),
al-Baghawi, Muyassar, Ma'arif, all on 53:39-42 (en Ibn Kathir grouped
53:33-41 and 53:42-51). Sa'y = 'amal with 92:4 (al-Baghawi, Ibn Zayd).
"Only what he strove for" kept as a dispute, no position and no ruling:
abrogated by 52:21 (Ibn Abbas via at-Tabari, al-Baghawi, al-Qurtubi);
muhkam (most mufassirun per al-Qurtubi, with Malik and ash-Shafi'i as
he relays them); for the people of Ibrahim and Musa only (Ikrimah via
al-Baghawi); meaning the disbeliever (ar-Rabi' ibn Anas); the lam of
due vs Allah's favour (al-Qurtubi's qila and his own comment, evil
deeds only, al-Warraq's "intended"); as-Sa'di's gift analogy; Ibn
Kathir on ash-Shafi'i and recitation, du'a and charity agreed, the
three as the person's own striving; Ma'arif's obligation/voluntary line.
53:40-41 via all eight; at-Tabari's gloss on whom the passage answers.
53:42: return (at-Tabari, Ibn Kathir with Mu'adh, as-Sa'di); Ubayy's
"no thought about the Lord" only as al-Baghawi and al-Qurtubi carry it,
with Ibn Kathir's "not preserved in this wording".

Hadith: Muslim 1336 and Bukhari 1388 quoted whole (attached by
al-Baghawi); Muslim 1631 in one line; Muslim 2674 referenced.

Dropped: al-Qurtubi's hadith qudsi and Sa'd/Aisha reports, al-Baghawi's
shirt report; from the old article Safa-Marwah, 2:281, 99:7-8, 76:22,
17:19 and the "mashkur twice" claim. Fixed: the old article had Ibn
Kathir noting scholars who allowed recitation gifts; his text does not.
