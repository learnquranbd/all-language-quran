## Disagreements kept (no position taken)
- Addressee: believers (Ibn Kathir, Muyassar, as-Sa'di; al-Qurtubi's first gloss,
  spoils of war) vs the Makkan idolaters during the famine (al-Qurtubi "qila"; at-Tabari
  "some", which he himself rejects). Tabari's own reading: "O people", livestock.
- Halal tayyib: Tabari (slaughtered + not forbidden) vs Muyassar (lawful + pleasant)
  vs as-Sa'di (not forbidden + not usurped; in measure).
- The condition: obedience (Tabari), submission + tawhid (Muyassar), exclusive thanks
  (as-Sa'di), the Giver alone deserves worship (Ibn Kathir).
