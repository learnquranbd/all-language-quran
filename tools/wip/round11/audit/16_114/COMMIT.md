Replaces the 808-word, 5-section article with a 1,736-word, 8-section one.

Sources (all fetched for 16:114): at-Tabari reads the address as "O people",
the food as livestock Allah made lawful, halal tayyib as slaughtered and not
forbidden, and the verse as voiding the bahira/sa'iba/wasila bans; he reports
and rejects the famine reading because 16:115 follows. Al-Qurtubi gives the
spoils-of-war gloss, then the "qila" report of the Makkan seven-year hunger,
Abu Sufyan's appeal and the food sent while they were still idolaters.
As-Sa'di: halal tayyib as a hal excluding the forbidden and the usurped,
enjoyment without excess, thanks by heart, praise and use, and the condition
as thanking none but Him. Muyassar (mustatab; acknowledge and spend in
obedience; worship Him alone), Ibn Kathir (the Giver alone deserves worship),
Ma'arif on the sequence from the ungrateful town.

Disagreements kept: who is addressed (believers vs the idolaters in the
famine); the three glosses of halal tayyib; the readings of the condition.
