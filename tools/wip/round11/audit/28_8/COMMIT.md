Pharaoh's household lifts the infant Mūsā from the Nile "to become for
them an enemy and a grief" — the lām of outcome, and the decree no
caution outruns.

Card plus eight-section bilingual article, source-gated.

The verse's key, named as the fetched tafsir gives it: the lām in
l/iyakūna is lām al-ʿāqiba / lām al-ṣayrūra, the lām of outcome, not of
purpose (al-Qurtubi, al-Baghawi, and Ibn Kathir citing Ibn Isḥāq): they
did not take him intending an enemy; the result of taking him was that
he became one. Kept Ibn Kathir's second reading as a genuine second
layer — that on the sense of the context the lām stands for taʿlīl of
God's purpose, since He appointed them to pick the child up precisely to
make him their enemy, the more completely to defeat their own wariness.

Spine from as-Sa'di: al-ḥadhar lā yanfaʿu min al-qadar — caution does
not avail against the decree. The tyrant who slaughters Israel's boys is
made to raise the deliverer under his own hand, watch and care. Ibn
Kathir's note that ʿUmar ibn ʿAbd al-ʿAzīz cited this verse against the
Qadariyya carries the point.

Disagreement kept with no position: at-Tabari's three reports on who
"the family of Pharaoh" were (the wife's maidservants / his daughter /
his retainers), with his own refusal to rank them, resting on the
Qur'an's word āl Firʿawn.

khāṭiʾīn rendered "deliberate sinners," glossed from the tafsir (Tabari:
sinning against their Lord; Qurtubi: disobedient idolaters; Baghawi:
disobedient and sinful; Muyassar: sinful idolaters) — not from any
morphological claim. Carried the plain sentence that the verse condemns
the named regime and licenses nothing against any living person or
community.

Hadith: Sahih Muslim 2656 ("Everything is by measure, even incapacity
and capability"), in Muslim's Book of the Decree, used as a general
qadar narration and marked as not attached by any mufassir to the verse;
stated that no fetched tafsir attaches a Prophetic hadith to 28:8.

Dropped: the qirāʾāt note (ḥazanan/ḥuznan, no meaning change) and the
daughter's leprosy-cure report. Held 28:9–13 as future ground.
