# LEDGER 37:75

## SOURCES — tafsir fetched (all 8 listed in PREP.md), named vs not

- **14 — Ibn Kathir (ar)**, fetched, named. Clause rested on: "فدعى ربه أني مغلوب فانتصر، فغضب الله لغضبه عليهم؛ ولهذا قال: (ولقد نادانا نوح فلنعم المجيبون) أي: فلنعم المجيبون له." Used for: the anger mechanism ("Allah became angry because Nuh was angry with them") — §6.1; the ~1000-year stay — §1.2.
- **15 — at-Tabari**, fetched, named. Clause: "لقد نادانا نوح بمسألته إيانا هلاك قومه، فقال: رب إني دعوت قومي ليلا ونهارا * فلم يزدهم دعائي إلا فرارا ... إلى قوله رب لا تذر على الأرض من الكافرين ديارا." Used for: tying the call to 71:5-6 and 71:26 in full — §4.1.
- **16 — al-Muyassar**, fetched, named only implicitly (not quoted directly in final draft; confirms the plain "called for help against his people" reading, consistent with al-Qurtubi's istighatha and with the Ibn Kathir/Baghawi reading — used as a cross-check, not cited by name).
- **90 — al-Qurtubi**, fetched, named. Clause: "ولقد نادانا نوح من النداء الذي هو الاستغاثة ... قال الكسائي: أي فلنعم المجيبون له كنا." Used for: nida'=istighatha — §2.1-2.2; al-Kisa'i's grammatical gloss on "fa-la-ni'ma al-mujibun" — §7.1.
- **91 — as-Sa'di**, fetched, named. Clause: "نوح عليه السلام، أول الرسل ... تلك المدة الطويلة فلم يزدهم دعاؤه إلا فرارا، أنه نادى ربه فقال: ربِّ لا تذر على الأرض من الكافرين ديارا." Used for: "awwal ar-rusul" description — §3.1; the long-calling-then-cry sequence and 71:6 — §3.2; agreement with at-Tabari on 71:5-6/71:26 — §4.2.
- **94 — al-Baghawi**, fetched, named. Clause: "دعا ربه على قومه فقال: إني مغلوب فانتصر (القمر 10) ... فلنعم المجيبون نحن، يعني: أجبنا دعاءه وأهلكنا قومه." Used for: the 54:10 reading, named alongside Ibn Kathir — §5.2.
- **168 — Ma'arif al-Qur'an (en)**, fetched, named. Used for: the explicit statement that "most commentators" split between 71:26 and 54:10 as the referent — §5.1; the detail that Nuh's people had "crossed all limits... conspiring to kill him" — §5.1 (added in expansion pass).
- **169 — Ibn Kathir (en, abridged)**, fetched, used to corroborate the Arabic Ibn Kathir (14) wording in English ("I have been overcome, so help (me)!"; "Allah became angry because Nuh was angry with them") — §6.1, and to confirm the surah-75-82 block is future ground (76-81 content listed in §8.1 is drawn from this same grouped fetch, used only to say what is NOT yet claimed by 37:75).

## Disagreement kept open (per SOURCE-GATE §1, "state as a difference")

Which prayer "Noah called Us" refers to: at-Tabari and as-Sa'di both quote 71:5-6 → 71:26 (Surah Nuh); Ibn Kathir and al-Baghawi both quote 54:10 (Surah al-Qamar, "I have been overcome, so help"). Ma'arif al-Qur'an states explicitly that commentators split over this. Kept as a genuine named disagreement, no position taken (§5, "Two Surahs, One Verdict").
