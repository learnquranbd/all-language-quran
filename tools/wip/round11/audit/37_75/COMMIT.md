Card + 8-section bilingual article (1,663 EN words) on 37:75, "Noah had
certainly called Us, and [We are] the best of responders," the hinge between
the warned-nations passage (37:69-74) and Noah's rescue (37:76-81).

Sources (all 8 fetched for 37:75 via PREP.md, none re-fetched): al-Qurtubi
(nida' here as al-istighatha, crying out for rescue, not an ordinary summons;
al-Kisa'i's grammatical gloss on "fa-la-ni'ma al-mujibun" as "fa-la-ni'ma
al-mujibuna lahu kunna," how excellent the responders We were to him, the
majestic plural referring to Allah alone), as-Sa'di (naming Nuh "His servant
and messenger... the first of the messengers"; the long calling period and
71:6's "increased them not except in flight"), at-Tabari (quoting 71:5-6 and
71:26 in full as the call named in this verse), Ibn Kathir ar/en and
al-Baghawi (the rival reading: the call as 54:10's "I have been overcome, so
help," and the mechanism "Allah became angry because Nuh was angry with
them"), Ma'arif al-Qur'an (stating plainly that commentators split between
the two prayers, and the circumstance of conspiracy against Nuh behind
either one), al-Muyassar (read as a silent cross-check, not quoted by name).

Which prayer "Noah called Us" names — Surah Nuh's 71:5-6/71:26 (at-Tabari,
as-Sa'di) versus Surah al-Qamar's 54:10 (Ibn Kathir, al-Baghawi) — kept as a
genuine disagreement, both names, no position taken, per Ma'arif al-Qur'an's
own framing of the split.

Cross-referenced 71:5, 71:6 and 71:26 against data/quran-json/71.json before
quoting, per the brief's watch-point; EN/BN wording for all three taken from
the site's own translations/en.json and bn.json rather than invented.

No hadith attaches to this verse in any of the 8 sources fetched; §8 says so
in one sentence rather than reaching for the stock Bukhari 660 fallback. No
asbab in any fetched source.

Licensing sentence carried in both languages (§6): the destruction of Nuh's
people is what the text states about that people in their own time, and
licenses nothing against any community or person living today.

37:76-81 (the rescue, descendants, "peace upon Noah," reward, believing
servants) explicitly kept as future ground, listed by number only in the
closing section, not drafted here.

Dropped: the precise "1,000 years less 50" figure, softened to "nearly a
thousand years" to avoid re-litigating the whole-life-vs-calling-span
dispute already fully handled in the shipped 29:14 article.
