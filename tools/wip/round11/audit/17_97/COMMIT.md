Card and deep bilingual article on the two outcomes that close the
Makkan demands (17:90-96): whoever Allah guides is guided, whoever
He leaves astray finds no protector, and the astray are gathered on
their faces, blind, dumb and deaf, to a Fire that is fed each time
it subsides.

Sources gave:
- Guidance as Allah's own gift and tawfiq (at-Tabari, as-Sa'di;
  al-Qurtubi: had He guided them they would be guided); misguidance
  as khidhlan, being left to oneself (at-Tabari, as-Sa'di, Muyassar).
- "No protector besides Him": helpers against His punishment
  (at-Tabari), none to reverse His judgement (Ibn Kathir); tied to
  the twin verse 18:17.
- "On their faces": al-Qurtubi's two readings, sound view = dragged
  in humiliation; al-Baghawi's "ward off with their faces every rise
  and thorn."
- "Fire that subsides": khabat glossed calm/extinguish/weaken
  (Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatada, at-Tabari); the lull brings no relief
  (as-Sa'di, al-Qurtubi, al-Baghawi via 43:75); Ibn Abbas' consumed-
  to-embers image and Qatada's renewed skins; closes on 78:30.

Disagreement kept as disagreement: blind/dumb/deaf read either as
a real state at the gathering, later restored (al-Hasan, Muqatil via
23:108, reconciling 18:53/25:12/25:13), or figuratively as blind to
what gladdens, mute of any proof, deaf to any good (Ibn Abbas, and
Ibn Kathir: as they were to the truth in the world). Qadar left
unresolved in the article's own voice.

Hadith: Sahih al-Bukhari 4760 (Anas), also in Muslim, quoted whole
with Qatada's "by the might of our Lord"; graded sahih, attached to
the verse by Ibn Kathir, al-Qurtubi and al-Baghawi.

Licensing sentence included in both languages: the verse condemns
the astray as the text describes and warrants nothing against any
living person; no one may name a living man among those Allah leaves
astray.

ground not preempted. Fixed PREP's wrong hadith number (Bukhari 660
