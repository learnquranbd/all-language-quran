## Disagreements kept (no position taken)
- **Why she withdrew:** purification — as-Suddi (via at-Tabari, Ibn Kathir), al-Qurtubi, al-Baghawi (already purified, went to bathe); vs. worship — al-Qurtubi's "غيره" reading, which he calls hasan and Ma'arif notes he preferred, and Nawf al-Bikali (dwelling to worship); vs. drawing water — Muhammad ibn Ishaq. Held as differing reports (§4.1-§4.3, §8.1).
- **Where "eastward" points:** east of the mihrab (as-Suddi); toward sunrise not sunset (at-Tabari); eastern side / far and remote (Qatadah); toward the east from them (as-Sa'di); a spot in the house facing east (al-Baghawi) (§5.1).
- **Maryam's rank:** prophetess vs. a supremely truthful/favoured woman — al-Qurtubi records both and marks the first "أظهر"; kept as a disagreement, both named, no verdict, honour affirmed on either (§7.2). Phrased as rank only; the angel/annunciation mechanism NOT narrated (watch-point 3).

## Hadith
None used. No fetched tafsir attaches a sound Prophetic hadith to 19:16's account of her withdrawal; the material is Companion/Successor athar (Ibn Abbas, Qatadah, as-Suddi, Nawf al-Bikali, Muhammad ibn Ishaq). The article says so plainly in §7.1. hadith.js not run. The sound reports on Maryam's general excellence (e.g. "the best of the women of the world") are NOT on this verse, so deliberately omitted rather than misattached.

## Asbab an-nuzul
Not used; none in any fetched source. The verse's placement (the surah turning from Zakariyya/Yaḥyā to Maryam, the parallel births by Allah's power) used instead (§1).

## Cross-references used
- Āl ʿImrān 3:37, quoted by Ibn Kathir on this verse ("فتقبلها ربها بقبول حسن وأنبتها نباتا حسنا"), cited in §2.2. Watch-point named 3:42-43; those were NOT in the fetched files, so 3:37 (which Ibn Kathir actually quotes here) is used and 3:42-43 is not cited.
- Surah Al ʿImran and Surah al-Anbiya named (not numbered) per Ibn Kathir's statement of the three-place pairing (§1.2).

## Dropped
- al-Baghawi's "تفلي رأسها" (sat combing/delousing her hair): too incidental, would clutter; the cold-winter-day sunlit-spot detail kept as his contribution (§4.3).
- Ibn Kathir en (169) content on 19:17-21 (the screen, Jibril as a man, "How can I have a boy", the sign to mankind, four types of creation): withheld to avoid pre-empting 19:17 onward (watch-point 3).
- Any claim of Maryam's prophethood in our own voice: kept strictly as al-Qurtubi's reported dispute.
- No number "five" (five resolute messengers, Ibn Kathir) or "four" (four modes of creation, Ibn Kathir en) carried in, to keep bilingual number parity clean and stay on this verse.
