Replaces the 744-word, 6-section article with an 8-section, 1,774-word
source-gated article (shipped card kept).

Sources: at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi, as-Sa'di, Ibn Kathir (ar, en abridged),
al-Baghawi, Muyassar, Ma'arif, all on 42:19 itself. Covers placement after
42:17-18 and before 42:20 (Ibn Kathir en on the two harvests), the early
glosses of Latif (Ibn 'Abbas, 'Ikrimah, as-Suddi, Muqatil via al-Baghawi
and al-Qurtubi, with 2:212), as-Sa'di's definition and his three forms of
lutf (unlooked-for guidance, communal worship, wealth withheld), Ibn
Kathir on provision for all with 11:6, Ma'arif/Mazhari on why "whom He
wills", Ja'far's two sides of lutf in provision, al-Qurtubi on unequal
means (43:32, 25:20) and his long list of glosses (al-Qurazi, al-Husayn
b. al-Fadl, al-Junayd, al-Kattani), and al-Qawiyy al-'Aziz (at-Tabari,
Muyassar, Ibn Kathir, as-Sa'di). 42:27 quoted as the surah's own echo,
marked as not cited by the tafsirs.

Disagreements kept: scope of "man yasha'" (every creature fed vs who is
given more or less); Ja'far's saying in al-Qurtubi vs Ma'arif. Licensing
sentence on the hasteners of 42:18 in both languages.

Hadith: none. al-Qurtubi's two unsourced Prophetic sayings could not be
confirmed and are not reproduced (stated).

Dropped: Ma'arif's 70-recitations practice, al-Qurtubi's likely-misprinted
"obedience above capacity" gloss, and the old article's unsourced claims
(indefinite-Latif grammar point, 12:100, 31:16, 35:2, own-voice reading
of the Strong/Mighty pairing, an overstated as-Sa'di causal link).
