Dhul-Qarnayn at the west sets his policy: punish the one who
persists in wrong, then he is returned to his Lord for worse.

Sources (all pre-fetched, none re-fetched):
- man zalama read as the persistent disbeliever by every commentator
  fetched — Ibn Kathir (kufr + shirk), at-Tabari/al-Baghawi (kufr),
  al-Qurtubi (stays upon kufr), as-Sa'di (by kufr), al-Muyassar
  (wrongs himself by rejecting his Lord).
- Worldly punishment: Qatadah "by killing" (at-Tabari, Ibn Kathir);
  al-Muyassar "in this world." as-Suddi (molten copper) and Wahb
  (darkness) kept as named reports with Ibn Kathir's "Allah knows
  best," not as fact.
- 'adhaban nukran: at-Tabari/al-Qurtubi/al-Muyassar = Hell; al-Baghawi
  "the Fire is more nukr than the killing"; as-Sa'di two penalties
  (dunya + akhira); Ibn Kathir affirms the Return and recompense.
- Setting-sun image handled per Ibn Kathir (reaching the sky is
  impossible; storytellers' tales untrue, mostly People-of-Book myths)
  and Ma'arif/as-Sa'di (onlooker's perception; hami'ah = dark mud).

Disagreements kept: Dhul-Qarnayn's prophethood left open (Ma'arif's
full discussion, Abu Hayyan in al-Bahr al-Muhit; as-Sa'di reads him a
righteous just king). No identification with Alexander — not in the
fetched tafsir, caution stated in-article.

Licensing: siyasa shar'iyya (as-Sa'di) framed as lawful God-given
authority; no warrant for private punishment; no verdict on anyone
living. No sound hadith on the verse; Bukhari 660 (just ruler among
the seven shaded, Sahih) used only as a general narration, marked as
not attached to 18:87.
