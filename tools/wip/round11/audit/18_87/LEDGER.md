# SOURCES ledger — 18:87

## Tafsir fetched (all pre-fetched in audit dir; none re-fetched)

- **Ibn Kathir (ar), id 14** — USED.
  - "man zalama" = persistent disbeliever: «من استمر على كفره وشركه بربه».
  - Worldly punishment reports: Qatadah «بالقتل»; as-Suddi «كان يحمي لهم بقر النحاس ويضعهم فيها حتى يذوبوا»; Wahb b. Munabbih «كان يسلط الظلمة... تدخل أفواههم وبيوتهم» — Ibn Kathir seals with «والله أعلم» (marked as reports, not certainties).
  - "'adhaban nukran" = «شديدا بليغا وجيعا أليما»; «وفيه إثبات المعاد والجزاء».
- **at-Tabari, id 15** — USED. «أما من كفر فسوف نقتله»; Qatadah «هو القتل»; nukr = «عذابا عظيما... وذلك عذاب جهنم».
- **al-Muyassar, id 16** — USED. «من ظلم نفسه منهم فكفر بربه، فسوف نعذبه في الدنيا... عذابًا عظيمًا في نار جهنم» (ground for "wronged himself" + worldly/Hereafter split).
- **al-Qurtubi, id 90** — USED. «من أقام على الكفر منكم»; «بالقتل»; «يوم القيامة»; «شديدا في جهنم».
- **as-Sa'di, id 91** — USED. Silence on the asbab / ignore Isra'iliyyat «لا يسعنا غير السكوت عنها... النقلة للإسرائيليات»; army & reach; onlooker sees sun set in water; ground for the choice «لأن الظاهر أنهم كفار أو فساق... لو كانوا مؤمنين... لم يرخص في تعذيبهم»; zulm «بالكفر»; two penalties «العقوبتان، عقوبة الدنيا، وعقوبة الآخرة»; siyasa shar'iyya «فكان عند ذي القرنين من السياسة الشرعية ما استحق به المدح والثناء»; righteous just king «من الملوك الصالحين الأولياء، العادلين العالمين».
- **al-Baghawi, id 94** — USED. zulm = «كفر»; «نقتله»; nukr = «منكرا يعني بالنار، والنار أنكر من القتل» (the Fire more nukr than the killing).
- **Ma'arif al-Qur'an (en), id 168** (covers 86-88) — USED. Onlooker perception & hami'ah = dark mud; he chose the second option (counsel first, then punish/reward); the prophethood question — if prophet, address by wahy; if not, through a prophet of his time (al-Khidr reportedly with him) or wahy in literal sense (mother of Musa); Abu Hayyan in al-Bahr al-Muhit: command to punish/kill needs revelation to a prophet, so either he is a prophet or a prophet was present; "And Allah knows best."
- **Ibn Kathir (en, abridged), id 169** (covers 85-88) — USED. Following routes/landmarks (Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah); reaching the sky's setting-place impossible, storytellers' tales untrue, mostly People-of-the-Book myths; sun as if setting in the ocean; Allah gave him power + choice (kill/capture or free with/without ransom); "His justice and faith became apparent"; zulm = persist in kufr/shirk; punish = Qatadah "by killing"; nukr = severe/painful; "confirmation of the Hereafter and the reward and punishment."

## Asbab an-nuzul
- None used. 18:87 is a narrative verse within the Dhul-Qarnayn account (18:83-88); no asbab claimed. Verse placement described instead.

## Disagreements kept (as disagreements, no side taken)
- Dhul-Qarnayn's prophethood: prophet vs. non-prophet (Ma'arif lays out both; Abu Hayyan's argument reported; as-Sa'di reads him as a righteous just king without asserting prophethood). No position taken.
- Manner of the worldly punishment: Qatadah (killing) vs. as-Suddi (molten copper) vs. Wahb (darkness) — reported as differing transmitted reports, verse commits to none.

## Dropped / not asserted
- Any identification with Alexander or any named conqueror — NOT in the fetched tafsir; the fetched commentators name no conqueror; dropped and the caution stated in-article.
- The specific nature of the "means" (asbab) of 18:84 — as-Sa'di forbids speculation; left as "strong and many," per his instruction.
- as-Suddi / Wahb manner-of-punishment details — kept only as named reports with Ibn Kathir's "and Allah knows best," not stated as fact.
