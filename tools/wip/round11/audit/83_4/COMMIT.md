Card and 8-section bilingual article for 83:4: a-la yazunnu ula'ika
annahum mab'uthun, five Arabic words questioning the mutaffifin.
83:1-3 as context only; 83:5-6 named by number; 83:7-9 untouched.

Sources: Tabari, Ibn Kathir (ar; en abridged), Muyassar, Qurtubi,
Sa'di, Baghawi, Ma'arif. No re-fetch.
- Yazunnu: certainty (Baghawi yastayqin; Qurtubi zann = yaqin),
  wavering (Qurtubi's wa-qila: then why not at least suppose it and
  take the cautious course), fear (Ibn Kathir), firm belief
  (Muyassar ya'taqidu). Tabari and Sa'di give no gloss. None chosen.
- Ula'ika: Tabari, Muyassar, Baghawi, Sa'di, Qurtubi all keep it on
  the mutaffifin. Mab'uthun: from graves (Tabari), raised and
  reckoned (Muyassar), raised then questioned (Qurtubi).
- Sa'di: lack of iman in the Last Day emboldened them; Muyassar's
  a fortiori on outright theft; Ma'arif's wider tatfif.
- IK en cross-refs 17:35, 6:152, 55:9 and Shu'ayb's people.
Licensing sentence in both languages; no living trade or group named.
Hadith: Ibn Majah 2223 (Ibn 'Abbas, Madinah) quoted whole, no
collector grading on the page; Ma'arif's "sound chain" attributed.
Marked as on the surah, not 83:4; no hadith attaches to 83:4.
Makki/Madani dispute kept (Ibn Mas'ud vs Ibn 'Abbas and others).
Dropped: five-for-five narrations (unconfirmable), 'Umar's
"taffafta" athar, Ma'arif's present-day remarks, 83:5-6 sweat
hadiths (left for those entries).
