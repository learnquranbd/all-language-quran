Card and 8-section bilingual article for 86:6: khuliqa min ma'in
dafiq, four words, the answer to 86:5's question. 86:7-8 named only;
86:5's register, 32:8/77:20 parallels and the return argument
cross-referenced by number, not re-argued.

Sources: Tabari, Qurtubi, Baghawi, Sa'di, Ibn Kathir (ar + en
abridged), Muyassar, Ma'arif (rendering only). All slots open on 86:6.
- What: al-mani (Sa'di, Qurtubi, Baghawi, Ibn Kathir); poured into
  the womb (Baghawi; Farra' and Akhfash per Qurtubi; Muyassar).
- Whose: both parents (Ibn Kathir, "by the permission of Allah";
  Qurtubi and Baghawi: two waters named as one because they mix).
  Man's-only reading absent from every fetched text; said so.
- Grammar kept as a difference: fa'il in the sense of maf'ul, poured
  (Tabari, Hijazi usage; Baghawi via 69:21; Qurtubi, sirr katim) vs
  possessed of gushing, like dari'/faris/nabil (az-Zajjaj, Sibawayh's
  school, per Qurtubi). None chosen.
- lazij, from 'Ikrima from Ibn 'Abbas per Qurtubi, reported as given.
- No science claim: none in the 86:6 texts, none added. Ma'arif's
  embryology concerns 86:7 and is left there.
Hadith: none attached to 86:6 (said in the article); Ibn Kathir's
grouped narrations concern the surah, tariq and 86:9.
Asbab: none. Dropped: all 86:7-8 material; Qurtubi's unvocalised
"la yuqal" clause; a gloss for hamm nasib.
