## Disagreements kept (no position)
- Promise: worldly victory/establishment (at-Tabari, al-Baghawi, al-Qurtubi) vs this world and the Hereafter (Ibn Kathir) / including thawab (Muyassar); as-Sa'di gives no content, reads it as aid to patience.
- Object of patience: others' harm and opposition (at-Tabari, Ibn Kathir, Muyassar, al-Qurtubi) vs the command and the da'wah (as-Sa'di); at-Tabari's "and convey" holds both.
- yastakhiffannaka: make light of hilm and ra'y (at-Tabari; al-Baghawi's qila) / carry into ignorance and error (al-Baghawi, al-Qurtubi's usage) / yastafizzannaka 'an dinika (al-Qurtubi, Muyassar).
