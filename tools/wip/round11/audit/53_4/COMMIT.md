Card and 8-section bilingual article on in huwa illa wahyun yuha.
53:1-3 as context; 53:5 (taught by one intense in strength) a pointer only.

Sources: at-Tabari, al-Baghawi, al-Muyassar (grouped 53:1-4), as-Sa'di,
al-Qurtubi, Ibn Kathir (ar; en abridged checked), Ma'arif al-Qur'an.
- Chain Allah > Jibril > Muhammad ﷺ (Tabari); conveyed "without addition
  or reduction" (Ibn Kathir, Ma'arif); following revealed guidance in
  himself and others (Sa'di).
- Reach of "it": his speech in religion vs the Qur'an (both al-Baghawi,
  the latter as an unnamed wa-qila); Qur'an and Sunnah (Muyassar); Sunnah
  is wahy, with 4:113 (Sa'di); Sunnah like revealed wahy in practice
  (Qurtubi); two kinds of wahy (Ma'arif). Article picks none and states it
  gives no ruling on the standing of hadith.
- Ijtihad: verse cited by those denying it to the Prophet ﷺ (reported by
  Qurtubi) vs ijtihad within revealed principles (Ma'arif, attributed).
- Grammar: as-Sijistani's badal reading vs Ibn al-Anbari's objection.
Hadith: Abu Dawud 3646 ('Abdullah ibn 'Amr, "Write ... only right comes
out from it"), attached by Ibn Kathir, quoted whole; no grading on page.
Asbab: none. Cross-refs: 4:113 only (Sa'di-cited, verified).
Dropped: Ahmad/al-Bazzar narrations in Ibn Kathir (not checkable);
al-Qurtubi's Miqdam pointer (his introduction not fetched; Abu Dawud
4604 fetched but not quoted); 16:44, 4:80, 59:7, 10:15, 69:44-47 (no
fetched tafsir cites them).
Abu Dawud 3646 quoted whole, no grading on the page so none claimed; source typo 'to him mouth' shown as 'to [his] mouth'. 4604 not used (Qurtubi's reference unconfirmed).
