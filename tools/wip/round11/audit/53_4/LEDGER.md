## Disagreements kept (no position taken)
- Reach of huwa/wahy: his speech in religion (al-Baghawi, 1st) / the Qur'an (al-Baghawi's wa-qila, holder unnamed) / Qur'an and Sunnah (al-Muyassar) / Sunnah is wahy (as-Sa'di, with 4:113) / Sunnah like revealed wahy "in practice" (al-Qurtubi) / two kinds of wahy (Ma'arif).
- Ijtihad: verse cited by those denying the Prophet ijtihad (reported by al-Qurtubi) vs ijtihad within revealed principles (Ma'arif).
- Grammar: as-Sijistani's badal vs Ibn al-Anbari's objection (al-Qurtubi gives the objection last).
- Article states explicitly it gives no ruling on the standing of hadith (§6).

## Hadith
- Abu Dawud 3646 (Dar-us-Salam ref; Ahmad Hasan tr. 3639). URL: https://quranx.com/hadith/AbuDawud/DarusSalam/Hadith-3646 (saved: hadith_AbuDawud_3646.txt). First ten words: "I used to write everything which I heard from the". Quoted whole from that page's English; one typo bracketed ("to him mouth" -> "to [his] mouth"). Grading: none shown on the page; none claimed. Attached by Ibn Kathir (14/169), who names Abu Dawud's chain (Musaddad, Abu Bakr ibn Abi Shaybah < Yahya), matching the page's isnad.
- Abu Dawud 4604 (al-Miqdam, "I have been given the Qur'an and something like it"): fetched (hadith_AbuDawud_4604.txt) but NOT quoted. Al-Qurtubi on 53:4 only points to "the hadith of al-Miqdam" given in his introduction, which was not fetched, so which version he meant is unconfirmed; the full Abu Dawud wording (donkeys, fanged beasts, lost property, hospitality) would also need quoting whole. Article says the introduction was not read and the hadith is not quoted.
