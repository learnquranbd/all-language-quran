Card and eight-section bilingual article for 13:38: messengers given wives
and offspring, no sign except by Allah's permission, li-kulli ajalin kitab.

Sources: all eight fetched tafsirs named (Tabari, Qurtubi, Ibn Kathir ar/en,
Baghawi, Sa'di, Muyassar, Ma'arif). Ibn Kathir en (169) is grouped 13:38-39;
only its 13:38 part is used, and all of it is also in the Arabic (14).

Asbab: the objection to the Prophet's marriages is given as the texts word it:
Baghawi "it is reported" the Jews, "it is said" the idolaters; Qurtubi "it is
said" the Jews, with Dawud and Sulayman recalled; Ma'arif the idolaters; the
Muyassar names no one. No grading added. The article states that the verse
licenses nothing against any community living today.

Hadith: Bukhari 5063 (Anas, the three men), attached to the verse by
al-Qurtubi; Ibn Kathir gives a short form "in the two Sahihs". The Prophet's
words are quoted whole in Darussalam's English, confirmed via hadith.js.

Disagreement kept, with names: every term has a writing (Tabari, al-Hasan in
Qurtubi, Ibn Kathir) against the inverted every writing has a term
(ad-Dahhak in Tabari and Ibn Kathir; al-Farra' and ad-Dahhak in Qurtubi;
Baghawi "it is said"). Tabari's reconciliation through 50:19 is reported as his.
Signs: Tabari's list (mountains, land, the dead) ties back to 13:31; Baghawi
names Abdullah ibn Abi Umayya; Qurtubi on prohibition form, negation sense.

Dropped: Abu Ayyub "four sunan of the messengers" (weak chain, no grading
confirmed); Qurtubi's other marriage narrations (unconfirmed); all 13:39
material beyond one pointer.
