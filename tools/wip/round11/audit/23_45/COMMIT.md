Adds the Tadabbur card and bilingual article for 23:45, the sending of
Musa and his brother Harun to Pharaoh's court with Our signs and a clear
authority — the prophetic pattern of the destroyed nations renewed at
full strength on the largest stage.

Sources gave:
- at-Tabari places the sending after the messengers already described and
  glosses "with Our signs" as "with Our proofs" (bi-hujajina).
- al-Muyassar names the nine signs and lists them (staff, hand, locusts,
  lice, frogs, blood, flood, years of drought, failing crops); al-Baghawi
  anchors the proof in the hand and the staff.
- Ibn Kathir (ar) reads them as signs plus crushing arguments and
  conclusive demonstrations.
- as-Sa'di supplies the spine: Harun given as an answered request
  (20:32; 28:34), sultan mubin as an overpowering proof (17:101), and
  Pharaoh recognising the truth yet refusing it (27:14; 17:102).

Disagreement kept open: sultan/ayat as the concrete miracles (al-Baghawi,
al-Muyassar) versus overwhelming proof as such (at-Tabari, Ibn Kathir,
as-Sa'di) — given as emphasis, not contradiction.

Hadith: Sahih al-Bukhari 4416 (hadith al-manzila), used thematically for
Harun's rank as deputy, explicitly flagged as not attached to the verse
by any tafsir.

Turn kept: both as-Sa'di and Ibn Kathir (en) read this sending as the
point after which whole-nation destruction ceased and jihad was
legislated (28:43); §7 states plainly, in both languages, that the verse
licenses nothing against any living person or community.

Dropped: al-Qurtubi (no tafsir for the verse); Ma'arif (en) grouped file
commented on 23:50, off-verse. 23:46-48 kept light.
