## Hadith
- Bukhari 4563 (Dar-us-Salam), https://quranx.com/hadith/Bukhari/DarusSalam/Hadith-4563. Arabic first words: حسبنا الله ونعم الوكيل قالها إبراهيم عليه السلام حين ألقي في النار. Quoted whole, translated from the Arabic (the quranx English adds "(i.e. hypocrites)" and "a great army"; not used). Grading: in al-Bukhari's Sahih. Ibn Kathir's notes on an-Nasa'i and al-Hakim reported as his. Attached to the verse by its own wording.
- Bukhari 4564, https://quranx.com/hadith/Bukhari/DarusSalam/Hadith-4564. First words: كان آخر قول إبراهيم حين ألقي في النار حسبي الله ونعم الوكيل. Arabic on the fetched page is singular (hasbiya); Ibn Kathir's quotation of it reads hasbuna, and the quranx English says "for us". Article follows the Bukhari Arabic. In al-Bukhari's Sahih.

## Dropped
- Abu Dawud 3627 (Awf ibn Malik, "Allah blames for incapacity ... hasbiya Allah"), cited by Ibn Kathir and Ma'arif: fetched; the page gives no grading, Abu Dawud gives none, and Ibn Kathir notes the narrator Sayf is unidentified by lineage. Not used; Ma'arif's means point kept without it.
- Ibn Mardawayh reports in Ibn Kathir: Anas ("said to him on the day of Uhud"), Abu Rafi' (Ali sent after Abu Sufyan, a Khuza'i bedouin), Abu Hurayra "say it in a great matter" (Ibn Kathir: gharib). Ungraded or weak.
- Ahmad's Ibn Abbas report on the horn-blower (Ibn Kathir: jayyid) and the A'isha / Zaynab exchange: general, off the verse's line.
- at-Tabari 8246 (Ibn Abbas via the al-Awfi chain, a caravan bribed by Abu Sufyan) and as-Suddi's Hamra' report 8245: cut for length; Abd Allah ibn Amr "word of Ibrahim" report (at-Tabari, Ibn Kathir): redundant with Bukhari.
- as-Suddi's hypocrites speech before Badr as-Sughra (al-Qurtubi): only the view "the people were the hypocrites" kept.
- 8:2, 9:124, 48:4, 33:22 parallels and 21:69 (written, cut for length).
