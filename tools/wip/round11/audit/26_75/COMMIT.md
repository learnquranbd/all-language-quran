Card and deep bilingual article on 26:75, Ibrāhīm's pivot: "then have
you considered what you have been worshipping?" — the call to examine
inherited worship instead of following the fathers blindly.

Sources gave:
- at-Tabari and al-Qurtubi gloss أفرأيتم as "have you considered what
  you worship of these idols" (ما = the idols).
- al-Muyassar sharpens it: "أفأبصرتم بتدبر" — observe with reflection —
  the idols "that neither hear nor benefit nor harm."
- Ibn Kathir: they knew the idols were powerless yet followed their
  fathers' footsteps (taqlīd); and the daring bara'a — if they have any
  power, let them harm me. Cross-ref 43:27, with Nūḥ and Hūd's like stand.
- as-Sa'di (group head 26:69) frames the whole episode as Ibrāhīm's
  muḥājja (reasoned argument) and ibṭāl (dismantling) of their falsehood.

Hadith: Sahih al-Bukhari 1385 (Abu Huraira), the fitra hadith, quoted
whole in Dar-us-Salam wording — fitra vs the parents' religion mirrors
26:74's "we found our fathers doing thus." Graded as in Bukhari, not
upgraded.

Bridges: light to 26:76 (the ancient forefathers, antiquity is not
truth) and, in a clause, to 26:77 (enemy except the Lord of the worlds);
stays on 26:75's challenge to look, does not pre-empt 26:78-82.

Licensing sentence in both languages: describes how false worship is
transmitted; licenses nothing against any living person or community.

Dropped: al-Baghawi (fetched text had no commentary); as-Sa'di's
verse-specific 26:75 line (fetch held only the lemma). Both noted in
the ledger.
