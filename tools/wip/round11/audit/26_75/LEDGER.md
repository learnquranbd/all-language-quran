# SOURCES ledger — 26:75 (قَالَ أَفَرَءَيْتُم مَّا كُنتُمْ تَعْبُدُونَ)

## Tafsirs fetched (per PREP.md) and how used

- **at-Tabari (15)** — USED. Arabic clause: "قال إبراهيم لقومه: أفرأيتم أيها القوم
  ما كنتم تعبدون من هذه الأصنام". Rests §2.1 on: أفرأيتم = "have you considered, O
  people," and ما = "these idols."
- **al-Qurtubi (90)** — USED. Arabic clause: "قال إبراهيم أفرأيتم ما كنتم تعبدون
  من هذه الأصنام". Rests §2.1 on: the same plain gloss, ما points at the idols.
- **al-Muyassar (16, grouped 26:75-82)** — USED. Arabic clause: "أفأبصرتم بتدبر ما
  كنتم تعبدون من الأصنام التي لا تسمع ولا تنفع ولا تضر". Rests §2.2 on: "بتدبر"
  (with reflection) and the idols "that neither hear, benefit, nor harm."
- **Ibn Kathir (14 ar / 169 en abridged, en grouped 26:69-77)** — USED. Clauses:
  (a) "إن كانت هذه الأصنام شيئا ولها تأثير، فلتخلص إلي بالمساءة، فإني عدو لها لا
  أباليها" → §7.1 (the daring challenge). (b) en: "They knew that their idols could
  not do anything, but they had seen their fathers doing this, so they made haste
  to follow in their footsteps" → §3.1 (taqlīd). (c) cross-refs he adduces: Nūḥ
  (10:71) and Hūd (11:54-56) telling their people to plot; and Ibrāhīm 43:26-28
  "إلا الذي فطرني فإنه سيهدين" → §7.2 (I cite 43:27 only, the "except Him who
  created me" clause, to avoid a range ref).
- **as-Sa'di (91)** — USED, from the GROUP HEAD 26:69 (the 26:75 fetch returned only
  the verse text, no commentary — noted as re-fetch of the group). Arabic clause:
  "من أعجب أنبائه وأفضلها هذا النبأ المتضمن لرسالته ودعوته قومه ومحاجته إياهم
  وإبطاله ما هم عليه". Rests §4.1 on: the episode as message + call + muḥājja
  (reasoned argument) + ibṭāl (dismantling) of the falsehood. Named as as-Sa'di on
  the passage (group at 26:69).
- **al-Baghawi (94)** — fetched, NOT named. The fetched text was only the verse
  lemma ("قال أفرأيتم ما كنتم تعبدون")—no commentary to attribute. Dropped.
- **Ma'arif al-Qur'an (168 en)** — NOT used. Flagged WRONG GROUP (covers 26:62-71).
  Not opened.

## Hadith (§5)

- **Sahih al-Bukhari 1385**, narrated Abu Huraira (RA).
  URL fetched: https://quranx.com/hadith/Bukhari/DarusSalam/Hadith-1385
  First ten words (Dar-us-Salam EN): "Every child is born with a true faith of
  Islam..." Arabic: "كل مولود يولد على الفطرة، فأبواه يهودانه أو ينصرانه أو
  يمجسانه...". Grading: in Sahih al-Bukhari (authentic); no separate grade given,
  none upgraded. Quoted whole in §5.2 in the Dar-us-Salam wording. Chosen because
  the fitra-vs-parents image maps exactly onto 26:74's "we found our fathers doing
  thus" and the inherited-worship theme of 26:75.

## Asbāb an-nuzūl

- Not used. None established for 26:75; the article uses the verse's placement in
  Ibrāhīm's reckoning with his people (26:69-82) instead.

## Licensing sentence

- Present in both languages (§5.3): the verse and hadith describe how a false
  worship is transmitted and license nothing against any living person or
  community. The Jew/Christian/Magian wording in the hadith is framed as
  transmission, not a verdict against communities.
