  - "ويوم ينادي الله هؤلاء المشركين به في الدنيا الأوثان والأصنام: أين شركائي الذين كنتم تشركونهم في عبادتكم إياي"

## Disagreements kept (no position)
- Speaker of "qalu adhannaka": the idolaters (Tabari, IK, Baghawi, Sa'di, Muyassar) vs Qurtubi's three: the idols / (qila) the idolaters / possibly both worshipper and worshipped.
- Gloss of adhannaka: a'lamnaka (Ibn 'Abbas via Tabari; IK, Baghawi, Sa'di, Muyassar) ; asma'nak wa a'lamnak (Qurtubi) ; as-Suddi "ata'nak" with editor's suggested "atla'nak".
- "No witness": all = none of us testifies You have a partner; Sa'di's fuller confession (bear witness against us; we disown them).

## Hadith
- Sahih al-Bukhari 50 (Dar-us-Salam), https://quranx.com/hadith/Bukhari/DarusSalam/Hadith-50 (via hadith.js; saved hadith_Bukhari_50.txt). Abu Hurayra. Attached to the verse by Ibn Kathir (he quotes "ما المسئول عنها بأعلم من السائل" without naming a collection).
  - First ten words of the quoted passage: "When will the Hour be established?" Allah's Messenger (ﷺ) replied, "The..."
  - Quoted: the Hour passage whole, one wording (USC-MSA English, with its 1./2. numbering), from "When will the Hour be established?" to "...nobody knows except Allah." The faith/Islam/ihsan part and the ending (recitation of 31:34's opening, "That was Gabriel who came to teach the people their religion") summarised, not quoted. Bengali rendered from the Arabic of the same hadith.
  - Grading: none beyond inclusion in his Sahih; stated so, not upgraded.
  - 31:34 appears only because the hadith itself says the Prophet ﷺ recited it; no fetched tafsir cites 31:34. 33:63 not used.
- Muslim's 'Umar version and the "keys of the unseen are five" hadith: not fetched, not used (not attached by any fetched tafsir).
