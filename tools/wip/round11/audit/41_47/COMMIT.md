Card and 8-section bilingual article for 41:47: ilayhi yuraddu 'ilmu
as-sa'ah, fruit from its akmam, every conception and birth within His
knowledge, and the Day of "where are My partners?". 41:46 for placement
only; 41:48 left to its own verse; shipped neighbours not drawn on.

Sources: Tabari, Ibn Kathir (ar; en abridged as check), Muyassar,
Qurtubi, Sa'di, Baghawi. Ma'arif's grouped text covers only 41:44.
- The Hour: Tabari, Baghawi, Muyassar; Sa'di's all creation, messengers
  and angels included, refers it back; IK with 79:44 and 7:187.
- Akmam: Tabari (container; Mujahid "when it emerges", Suddi "its
  spathe"; kumm = husk of the spathe; both readings thamarat/thamara);
  Ibn 'Abbas via Qurtubi and Baghawi (spathe before it splits); Sa'di.
- Wombs: Tabari, Sa'di (humans and animals), Baghawi (by His permission),
  IK with 6:59, 13:8, 35:11 (same clause verbatim, checked in
  quran-json); Qurtubi/Baghawi's Hour-as-fruit-and-offspring link.
- Adhannaka: a'lamnaka (Ibn 'Abbas via Tabari, IK, Baghawi, Sa'di,
  Muyassar), Qurtubi's asma'naka with al-Harith b. Hilliza; Suddi's
  ata'naka with the editor's atla'naka note. No witness: none testifies
  You have a partner; Sa'di's fuller confession and disowning.
Disagreement kept: the speakers are the idolaters (five) vs Qurtubi's
idols / idolaters / both.
Hadith: Bukhari 50 (Abu Hurayra), the Hour passage quoted whole, attached
by IK; no grading beyond his Sahih. Licensing sentence in both languages.
Asbab: Qurtubi's chainless report not used.
Dropped: "none of us sees them" gloss (not in fetched text), any
embryology or botany, 31:34/33:63 as cross-refs (31:34 only as recited
in the hadith), 41:48 content.
Fix: added one sentence (EN/BN) after the Bukhari 50 quote saying the portents belong to the hadith's own subject, not this verse, and no fetched tafsir on 41:47 explains them (none mentions them), so the article does not interpret them.
