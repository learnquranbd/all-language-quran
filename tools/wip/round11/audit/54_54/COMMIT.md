Card and 8-section bilingual article on inna l-muttaqina fi jannatin wa
nahar. 54:55 (seat of truth near a Sovereign) a pointer only.

Sources: at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi, al-Baghawi, as-Sa'di, al-Muyassar, Ibn
Kathir (ar; en abridged for the contrast and 54:55). Ma'arif has nothing
on this verse.
- Placement after the records of 54:52-53; al-Qurtubi: having described
  the disbelievers, He described the believers.
- 54:47 / 54:54 same sentence frame; Ibn Kathir's reversal of 54:48.
  Licensing sentence in both languages.
- muttaqin defined by at-Tabari (obedience, fara'id, avoiding sin) and
  as-Sa'di (shirk, major and minor sins).
- nahar singular: meaning plural (Tabari, parallel ad-dubur 54:45) and
  verse-end (Baghawi, Qurtubi). Rivers of water, wine, milk, honey
  (Qurtubi via Ibn Jurayj; Baghawi).
- Light and spaciousness (Tabari "qad qila", al-Farra' line; Qurtubi;
  Baghawi from ad-Dahhak); nuhur "no night for them" reading (Qurtubi's
  five names; Baghawi: al-A'raj). Kept side by side, none picked.
- as-Sa'di's list ending in the King's pleasure and nearness; Ibn
  Kathir on 54:55 echoing 54:49.
Hadith: none attached to 54:54; Ibn Kathir's narration sits under 54:55
and is left there.
Asbab: none.
Dropped: as-Sa'di's "no eye has seen" phrase (unattributed narration),
any status label for nuhur, cross-refs no tafsir cites (47:15, 52:17,
51:15, 15:45, 44:51-52).
