No disagreement adjudicated: rivers (Tabari main gloss, Qurtubi via Ibn Jurayj, Baghawi, Muyassar, Sa'di) vs light and spaciousness (Tabari "qad qila", Qurtubi "qila", Baghawi from ad-Dahhak) reported side by side, order of presentation stated as fact, none picked. Reason for singular: meaning plural (Tabari) and verse-end (Baghawi, Qurtubi), both reported.

Own observations (not attributed): the 54:47 / 54:54 parallel frame (inna + group + fi + two nouns joined by wa), from the Arabic in data/quran-json/54.json; the verse-end sounds 54:47-55 (short vowel + r); 54:54 is the surah's second-to-last verse; link from Sa'di's "minor sins" to 54:53 (flagged in text as the reader's observation); Ibn Kathir's "measured its destiny" echoing 54:49.

## Hadith
None used. No fetched tafsir attaches a hadith to 54:54. Ibn Kathir (169) places one narration (Muslim, "al-muqsitun 'inda Allah 'ala manabir min nur") under 54:55; left for that verse, stated in §7. Not fetched via hadith.js, since not used.

## Asbab an-nuzul
None for 54:54. The Quraysh/qadar narrations in 169 and 168 concern 54:48-49; not used.

## Cross-references
54:45 (cited by at-Tabari), 54:47, 54:48, 54:49, 54:52, 54:53, 54:55 (same passage, PREP/Ibn Kathir). 47:15, 44:51-52, 52:17, 51:15 and 15:45 are not cited by any fetched tafsir on this verse: not used.

## Dropped
- as-Sa'di's phrase "ما لا عين رأت، ولا أذن سمعت، ولا خطر على قلب بشر": it is the wording of a well-known narration that he does not attribute here; quoting it would need the hadith confirmed and marked as not attached. Omitted rather than half-cited.
- Any status label (shadhdh, mutawatir) for the nuhur reading: no fetched source gives one. Article says only that the app's text reads nahar and nuhur is reported as the reading of those named.
- Any description of Paradise beyond the sources' own lists.
- Ma'arif's qadar material (54:49) and the Magians-of-the-ummah report: off-verse.
