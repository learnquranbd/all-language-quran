# SOURCES ledger — 19:49

## Tafsir fetched (all pre-fetched in PREP; none re-fetched)

- **at-Tabari (15)** — USED. Arabic clause: «فلما اعتزل إبراهيم قومه وعبادة ما كانوا
  يعبدون من دون الله من الأوثان آنسنا وحشته من فراقهم، وأبدلناه منهم بمن هو خير منهم
  وأكرم على الله». Rests: withdrawal from people + idol-worship (§2); "consoled his
  loneliness / replaced with better and nobler" (§4); the grammar note «فوحد ولم يقل
  أنبياء لتوحيد لفظ كلّ» → nabiyyan singular because kull is singular (§7).
- **al-Muyassar (16)** — USED. Clause: «فلما فارقهم وآلهتهم التي يعبدونها من دون الله».
  Rests: the double object of the withdrawal, them and their gods (§2).
- **al-Qurtubi (90)** — USED. Clause: «أي آنسنا وحشته بولد؛ عن ابن عباس وغيره». Rests:
  "consoled his loneliness with a child," from Ibn Abbas and others (§4). The file's
  «عسى» discussion belongs to 19:48 and was not used.
- **as-Sa'di (91)** — USED. Clauses: «مفارقة الإنسان لوطنه ومألفه وأهله وقومه من أشق
  شيء على النفس … انفراده عمن يتعزز بهم ويتكثر» (§3 hardship of leaving); «من ترك شيئا
  لله عوضه الله خيرا منه» (§3 the rule); «هؤلاء الصالحين المرسلين … خصهم الله بوحيه
  واختارهم لرسالته واصطفاهم من العالمين» (§7 honour of the line).
- **al-Baghawi (94)** — USED. Clauses: «فذهب مهاجرا»; «وهبنا له بعد الهجرة إسحاق
  ويعقوب»; «آنسنا وحشته وأقررنا عينه بأولاد كرام». Rests: the gift came after the
  hijra (§5); consoled loneliness / gladdened his eye (§4).
- **Ibn Kathir ar (14) + en abridged (169)** — USED. Ishaq = son, Yaʿqub = grandson,
  with 21:72 (نافلة) and 11:71 (§6); no dispute Ishaq is father of Yaʿqub, per 2:133
  (§6); Allah named only Ishaq and Yaʿqub to show prophets from his line in his
  lifetime, else Yusuf would be named (§5, §8); the lineage hadith cited muttafaq
  ʿalayh (§8).
- **Ma'arif al-Qur'an en (168)** — USED. 19:48 was a prayer against loneliness; this
  verse describes how it was granted; a son to compensate, foretold to live long and
  father Yaʿqub; a better family of prophets and pious people (§1, §3). Note: file
  covers 19:49–50 grouped; the 19:50 content was NOT used (future ground, watch-pt 5).

## Hadith

- **Sahih al-Bukhari 3353** (Dar-us-Salam), fetched
  https://quranx.com/hadith/Bukhari/DarusSalam/Hadith-3353 . First ten words (EN
  used): "Narrated Abu Huraira: The people said, O Allah's Messenger…". Wording quoted
  whole from Bukhari only (not hybridised with Muslim). Grading: sahih (in Sahih
  al-Bukhari); Ibn Kathir cites it as muttafaq ʿalayh. Directly on this verse: it is
  the Ibrahim→Ishaq→Yaʿqub→Yusuf lineage Ibn Kathir adduces to prove Yaʿqub's
  prophethood in Ibrahim's lifetime.

## Asbab an-nuzul

- Not used. No asbab claimed; the verse is placed by its context (the reply to the
  father's threat, 19:46–48), which is what the article states.

## Dropped / not overclaimed

- The popular explanation that Ismail is unnamed **because he was in Makkah while
  Ishaq/Yaʿqub settled in Shām** is NOT in any fetched tafsir. Dropped from the
  article's own voice; §5 states plainly that the fetched commentaries rest the naming
  only on the migration (Baghawi) and on the lifetime-lineage point (Ibn Kathir), and
  do not give the Makkah reason. Ismail's separate honour at 19:54 is a Qur'anic fact
  (PREP), stated as such.
- 19:50 material (mercy + lisan sidq / lofty renown), present in the grouped files
  168 and 169, deliberately left for the next ayah (watch-point 5).
- No condemned/destroyed-people licensing sentence: this is not that kind of verse
  (watch-point 4).
