- WRONG/unattached: the Tirmidhi narration to Ibn 'Abbas ("the pens have been lifted"), cited without number or grading and attached by no fetched tafsir.
- Unsourced: "heat and scarcity" of Tabuk; "The commentators read the guided heart as ..." (64:11 material, no 9:51 source); the 57:22-23 and 64:11 paragraphs (now pointer only); "Islam does not oppose precaution; the same surah's campaigns involved provisions, mounts and planning"; the Mawla "who claims you and whom you claim" reading; "decree, rightly believed, is fuel for risk-taking"; the diagnosis/interview application paragraph; "double burden" line.
