Card and 8-section bilingual article for 2:120-121: the approval that
asks for one's millah, hudallahi huwal-huda, and tilawah as following.
Target 2:120 widened to 2:121; 2:118-119 left free beside 2:116-117.

Sources: Tabari, Qurtubi, Ibn Kathir (ar + en abridged), Sa'di, Baghawi,
Muyassar, Ma'arif on 2:120 and 2:121; al-Wahidi from quranx. Slots on target.
- Millah as din (Tabari), the way (Baghawi), = shari'ah, din apart
  (Qurtubi); ahwa' plural because the desires differ (Qurtubi).
- Tabari: no way to please both parties at once; hold to the guidance
  that gathers people; huda as arbitration by the Torah.
- The conditional: address to the Prophet ﷺ, warning for the ummah
  (Qurtubi's two views, Baghawi with 39:65, Ibn Kathir, Muyassar, Sa'di);
  Ma'arif's supposition, impossible for him. No implied compliance.
- Haqqa tilawatihi = following (Ibn Abbas with 91:2, Tabari by consensus,
  Sa'di); Ibn Mas'ud's oath; Qurtubi on "recite properly"; Umar's asking.
Disagreements kept: who was given the Book (Qatadah: Companions; Ibn Zayd,
preferred by Tabari: believers of Bani Isra'il; Baghawi's further reports;
Qurtubi: general; Ma'arif: forty from Abyssinia).
Asbab: Wahidi's truce and qiblah reports, Tabari's qila, kept as unchained
reports; placement after 2:111-118 used instead.
Hadith: Muslim 772 (Hudhayfah), marked general. Ibn Umar marfu' reported
with al-Khatib's unknown-narrator note; no sound hadith attaches.
Licensing sentence in both languages: an ideological demand, no licence
for hostility or suspicion toward any living person or neighbour.
Dropped: Qatadah's mursal, the millah inheritance fiqh (length), Qurtubi's
jihad clause and Ahmad's fa'idah, Ma'arif's translator note.
