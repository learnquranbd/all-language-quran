Replaces the 865-word, 6-section article with a 1,770-word, 9-section one,
from at-Tabari, Ibn Kathir (ar and en), al-Qurtubi, al-Baghawi, as-Sa'di,
the Muyassar and Ma'arif on 14:7. Slots checked, none shifted.
The shipped card is kept.
Ta'adhdhana: at-Tabari's tafa''ala/af'ala note with Ibn Hilliza, al-Qurtubi's
adhan link from al-Hasan, the Muyassar's emphatic informing, as-Sa'di's
informed and promised, Ibn Kathir's announced, or possibly swore (7:167);
Ibn Mas'ud's "wa idh qala", with the editor's warning on the chain.
Who speaks kept as a disagreement: al-Qurtubi's two views (Musa, or Allah
to the Prophet ﷺ); the others read it as Musa's speech. Licensing line
on the Children of Israel in both languages.
Increase: blessing (most), reward (Ibn Abbas, al-Baghawi), amount or
continuity (Ma'arif). At-Tabari rejects "from My obedience" (al-Hasan,
Sufyan, 'Ali b. Salih) except in one narrow sense; al-Qurtubi calls the
readings close and the verse an explicit text that thanks causes increase.
Shukr: as-Sa'di's heart, praise and use; al-Qurtubi's definition with the
Dawud report (no chain) and the righteous man's answer; al-Baghawi's
fetter and snare. Kufr: Ibn Kathir's cover and deny, al-Qurtubi's "My
right" against "My blessings"; the punishment as loss of the gift.
Ma'arif's asymmetry kept against the Muyassar's and at-Tabari's direct
pledge. Hadith: Ibn Majah 4022 (Thawban), the one Ibn Kathir cites, quoted
whole, no grading claimed; a no-verdict line on others' hardship.
One line on 2:152, one bridge to 14:8.
Dropped from the shipped article as unsourced: the "unlimited object" claim,
the feet-swelling hadith, the Mu'adh du'a graded "sound", 14:34, the
knee-and-income advice. Also dropped: Ibn Kathir's Musnad date report.
