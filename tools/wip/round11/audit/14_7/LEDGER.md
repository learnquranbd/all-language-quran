## Hadith
- Sunan Ibn Majah 4022, https://quranx.com/hadith/IbnMajah/Hadith-4022 (saved hadith_IbnMajah_4022.txt). Thawban. First ten words used: "Nothing increases one's life span except righteousness and nothing repels". Quoted whole. Arabic ends بالذنب يصيبه, the wording Ibn Kathir cites (he has العبد where Ibn Majah has الرجل). Ibn Kathir names no collection. Grading: none on the fetched page; none claimed in the article.
- Ibn Majah 90 also fetched (same chain, ends للخطيئة يعملها); not used, since 4022 matches Ibn Kathir's wording.

## Asbab
None. No fetched source gives an occasion of revelation. Placement given instead (inside Musa's speech, 14:5-8).

## Dropped
- Ibn Kathir's Musnad Ahmad report (the beggar who refused a date, forty dirhams): Ibn Kathir himself records criticism of 'Umara b. Zadhan. Mentioned only as not used.
- Ma'arif's Ibn Marduwayh report from Ibn Abbas ("shall never be deprived of barakah"): no chain or grading I could confirm.
- Al-Qurtubi's al-Hadi verses and the choking anecdote: unclear whether al-Hadi recited the lines or heard them (أنشد / أُنشِد), so dropped.
- Ja'far as-Sadiq's line in al-Qurtubi: the text looks corrupt (إذا سمعت النعمة نعمة الشكر).
- Al-Qurtubi's point that the fa' is dropped before inna: was in the draft, cut for length.
- Ibn Kathir's (en) Muslim hadith of Abu Dharr: it belongs to 14:8 (future ground).

## Points from the shipped 865-word, 6-section article
Kept, because a fetched file confirms them: ta'adhdhana as public announcement, linked to adhan (al-Qurtubi); context of 14:5-6; the asymmetry of the warning (Ma'arif); k-f-r as covering (Ibn Kathir سترتموها); the 14:8 bridge.
Dropped or corrected:
- "The commentators dwell on this omission: an unnamed object is an unlimited one ... knowledge, ease of worship, contentment": no fetched commentator says this. They name blessing, bounty, reward, obedience, or amount and continuity (Ma'arif).
- The emphatic-lam / "sworn undertaking" grammar point, written in the article's own voice: replaced by Ibn Kathir's attributed "swore" gloss.
- "Shukr ... by the tongue, the heart and the use made of the gift": now credited to as-Sa'di, whose text has it.
- Bukhari/Muslim "shall I not be a grateful servant" (night prayer until his feet swelled): no fetched tafsir on 14:7 cites it.
- Mu'adh du'a "Allahumma a'inni ..." called "graded sound": no fetched tafsir cites it, and the grading was never confirmed.
- 14:34 "you could not enumerate it": not in the sources for this verse.
- "Kufr can mean both disbelief and thanklessness — the same act on different objects": rewritten as al-Qurtubi's two named glosses.
- "Nobody thanks Allah for a working knee", "a name that is not feared", "wealth is thanked by spending ...": own-voice advice with no source; replaced by practice drawn from as-Sa'di, al-Qurtubi and al-Baghawi.
- The lesson's "thank Allah aloud" stays in the card, which is unchanged.
