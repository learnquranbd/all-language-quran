## Hadith
1. Sahih Muslim 2742. URL https://quranx.com/hadith/Muslim/Hadith-2742 (saved hadith_Muslim_2742.txt). Attached to 18:7 by Ibn Kathir (same chain, Abu Maslamah-Abu Nadrah-Abu Sa'id), at-Tabari (via Qatadah) and al-Qurtubi. First ten words (Arabic matn): "إن الدنيا حلوة خضرة وإن الله مستخلفكم فيها فينظر كيف". Translated from Muslim's Arabic (quranx English drops "فاتقوا الدنيا"). Grading: in Sahih Muslim; no separate grading given by Muslim. The Ibn Bashshar variant "لينظر" not quoted.
2. Sahih Muslim 38. URL https://quranx.com/hadith/Muslim/Hadith-38 (saved hadith_Muslim_38.txt). Cited by al-Qurtubi as the Prophet's summary of Ibn Atiyyah's father's definition; marked in the article as general and not attached to the verse. First ten words: "قلت يا رسول الله قل لي في الإسلام قولا لا". Quoted whole with the Abu Usamah variant. Muslim's wording is "فاستقم" (al-Qurtubi quotes "ثم استقم"); Muslim's wording used.
Not used: al-Qurtubi's "إن أخوف ما أخاف عليكم... زهرة الدنيا" and "فمن أخذه بطيب نفس بورك له" (not confirmed via hadith.js); "قد أفلح من أسلم ورزق كفافا" (al-Qurtubi gives it unmarked; not used, to avoid misattribution). Umar's prayer is reported as al-Qurtubi's citation of al-Bukhari, not independently confirmed.
The orchestrator's watch-point "the most abstinent of you from the world, the most intelligent in understanding" as a Prophet-attributed explanation: it does not appear in any of the eight fetched files. Not used.
