Card and 8-section bilingual article for 41:22: wa ma kuntum
tastatiruna an yashhada 'alaykum sam'ukum wa la absarukum wa la
juludukum, wa lakin zanantum anna-llaha la ya'lamu kathiran mimma
ta'malun, with 41:19-21 as one line of context.

Sources: Tabari, Qurtubi, Sa'di, Ibn Kathir (ar; en abridged read),
Baghawi, Muyassar, Ma'arif.
- Speaker kept as a disagreement: the limbs (IK) vs limbs, Allah or the
  angels, undecided (Qurtubi).
- Tastatirun glosses side by side: hiding (Suddi; Tabari's preference
  with his reason; "most scholars" per Qurtubi, Baghawi; Muyassar),
  guarding (Mujahid), expecting (Qatada); Sa'di joins hiding and
  wariness. Tabari's and Qurtubi's "hiding = leaving the sin"; Ma'arif's
  "hide from others, not from your limbs".
- Zanantum ... kathiran: Tabari (hasibtum), IK (not all deeds),
  Muyassar/Sa'di, Qurtubi (so you disputed), Ma'arif.
- Qatada's exhortation (Tabari) and his hearing/eyes testimony (Qurtubi).
Hadith/asbab: Ibn Mas'ud's report quoted whole from Bukhari 4817
(quranx, Dar-us-Salam), graded only as in his Sahih; Bukhari 4816 and
the Ka'ba-coverings chains not blended.
Licensing sentences in both languages: a'da' Allah (41:19) and the
three men's tribes.
Dropped: Bahz b. Hakim, Ma'qil b. Yasar, Anas/Abu Musa (unconfirmed or
41:20-21 ground); the three men's names; Tirmidhi's grading (not
fetched); all 41:23 material.
