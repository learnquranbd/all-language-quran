Card and 8-section bilingual article for 40:64: Allahu lladhi ja'ala
lakumu l-arda qararan wa-s-sama'a bina'an wa-sawwarakum fa-ahsana
suwarakum wa-razaqakum mina t-tayyibat ... fa-tabaraka llahu rabbu
l-'alamin.

Sources: Tabari, Ibn Kathir (ar; en abridged grouped 40:61-65 as check),
Muyassar, Qurtubi, Sa'di, Baghawi, Ma'arif (grouped 40:64-71).
- Frame: same opening as 40:61; Qurtubi "zada fi ta'kid at-ta'rif
  wa-d-dalil". Shipped 40:60 one line; 40:65 and 40:67 not drawn on.
- qarar: Tabari, IK (mustaqarr, bisat, mihad, anchored by mountains),
  Sa'di (till, plant, build, travel), Muyassar, Baghawi firash, Qurtubi
  "in your lives and after death". Stated as glosses, not science.
- bina': IK guarded roof; Baghawi dome-like roof; Tabari raised without
  visible pillars until your terms; Sa'di/Muyassar lights and guiding
  signs. IK's parallel with 2:22 (firash).
- Best form: Tabari/Muqatil; IK ahsan taqwim; Sa'di cites 95:4, limb by
  limb, hearts' inclination, intellect/faith/love/ma'rifa; Ibn 'Abbas via
  Baghawi upright, eats with hand; Ma'arif reason, hands. Qurtubi's
  siwarakum reading (Abu Razin, al-Ashhab al-'Uqayli; al-Jawhari).
- Multiple readings kept on tayyibat: lawful + pleasant (Tabari,
  Muyassar), food and drink (IK), every good vs khaba'ith (Sa'di), other
  than animals' provision (Baghawi, qila). No position taken.
- IK's triad: abode, inhabitants, provisions. Tabari on dhalikum.
- tabaraka: IK exalted/sanctified; Sa'di magnified, abundant good;
  Muyassar both. Rabb al-'alamin: Tabari owner, Sa'di nurturer.
Hadith: none attached by any fetched tafsir; said in one sentence.
Asbab: none; placement given instead.
Dropped: 82:7-8 (not cited by any fetched tafsir); Ma'arif's "Best
Creator" rendering (23:14 wording); IK-en Quraysh/idols note on 40:63.
