## Disagreements / multiple readings kept
- tayyibat: lawful + pleasant (Tabari, Muyassar), food and drink (Ibn Kathir), every good incl. marriage/clothing/sight/sound (Sa'di), other than animals' provision (Baghawi, qila). No position taken.
- tabaraka: exaltation line (Ibn Kathir) vs abundance-of-good line (Sa'di), both (Muyassar).
