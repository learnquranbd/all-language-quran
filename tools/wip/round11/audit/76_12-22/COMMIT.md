Replaces the 776-word, 6-section article with a 1,766-word, 8-section deep article, built only on tafsir fetched for 76:12-22 (shipped card kept).

Sources: at-Tabari (his texts sit one slot late from 76:16 on; all present in
the range, none re-fetched), Ibn Kathir (ar; en abridged as cross-check),
al-Qurtubi, as-Sa'di, al-Baghawi; al-Muyassar and Ma'arif read, not named.

Spine 76:12: bima sabaru as cause (Ibn Kathir, at-Tabari); the patience glossed
as obedience / away from sin (Qatadah, al-Baghawi) and under painful decree
(as-Sa'di); poverty, fasting, hunger, three days of the vow (ad-Dahhak,
al-Qurazi, 'Ata'); ad-Darani's leaving desires (Ibn Kathir via Ibn 'Asakir).
al-Qurtubi's masdari ma for all the righteous; his unchained 'Ali-Fatimah
report marked as a report, nothing built on it. Spine 76:22: at-Tabari and
Qatadah, al-Qurtubi's definition of Allah's shukr (acceptance, praise, reward)
with Mujahid's maqbul, Ibn Kathir's little-for-much with 69:24 and 7:43.
Disagreements kept: zamharir as bitter cold vs the moon (Tha'lab, al-Qurtubi);
Salsabil as a name ('Ikrimah) vs a description (at-Tabari, al-Baghawi);
mukhalladun as undying, bracelets, earrings or fixed youth (at-Tabari, Ibn
Kathir), none chosen. qawarira min fiddah from at-Tabari, Ibn Kathir, as-Sa'di.
Hadith: al-Bukhari 3260 (the Fire's two breaths, zamharir) quoted whole,
attached by at-Tabari and al-Qurtubi. Bukhari 6571 confirmed, not used.
The Habashi report weighed by Ibn Kathir as very strange (gharib jiddan).
Dropped from the old article: the unsourced "no servant carries this one"
line and the single-gloss "boys of unchanging youth".
