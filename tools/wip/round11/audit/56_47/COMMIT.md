Card and 8-section bilingual article (1,655 EN words) on 56:47: wa-kanu
yaquluna a-idha mitna wa-kunna turaban wa-'izaman a-inna la-mab'uthun.
56:45-46 frame only (Ibn Kathir en; 56:46 idolatry gloss, Ibn Abbas, one
sentence); 56:48 and 56:49-50 frame only; 56:57-62 framed with a pointer
to the shipped 56:60 article; one pointer back to 56:41 (v770).

Sources: Ibn Kathir (ar, en), at-Tabari, al-Muyassar, al-Qurtubi,
as-Sa'di, al-Baghawi, all on 56:47 itself; Ma'arif's group text covers
56:39-40 only (stated, its hadith not used).
- Sense: denial and istib'ad, deeming the raising far-off: Ibn Kathir
  (mukadhdhibina ... mustab'idina), al-Qurtubi and Muyassar (same
  sentence, noted), as-Sa'di (yunkiruna ... istib'adan), at-Tabari
  (kufr, inkar; "in our graves", "crumbling bones", "alive as we were").
- as-Sa'di's paraphrase with kayfa, how; his bracketed "hadha min
  al-muhal" reported as bracketed, without ascribing the brackets.
- Readings, kept unranked as al-Baghawi gives them: question in a-idha
  only (Abu Ja'far, Nafi', al-Kisa'i, Ya'qub) vs both (the others); the
  site's printed text matches the second; no meaning drawn.
- Licensing sentence in both languages; no living group named.
Hadith: none attached in any fetched note; stated in one sentence.
Asbab: none.
Dropped: parallels 23:82, 37:16, 17:49, 36:78-79 (no fetched note cites
them for 56:47); other 56:46 glosses; any naming of the printed reading.
