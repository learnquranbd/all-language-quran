# SOURCES ledger — 56:47

Tafsir fetched (by prep.js, files in this folder): 14 Ibn Kathir (ar), 15 at-Tabari, 16 al-Muyassar,
90 al-Qurtubi, 91 as-Sa'di, 94 al-Baghawi (all single-verse 56:47); 168 Ma'arif (en, group 56:39-48),
169 Ibn Kathir (en abridged, group 56:41-50). No re-fetch; no quranx page fetched.

## Named, with the clause each attribution rests on
- Ibn Kathir (ar, t14): «يعني أنهم يقولون ذلك مكذبين به مستبعدين لوقوعه» (s1.3, s4.2).
- Ibn Kathir (en, t169), used only as frame for neighbours: 56:45 "Then, Allah the Exalted stated that
  they deserve this end ... enjoying life's pleasures and satisfying their lusts, all the while ignoring
  what the Messengers brought to them" (s1.2); 56:46 "It means idolatry, according to Ibn `Abbas ...
  Mujahid, `Ikrimah ... and others" (s1.2, one sentence, left to 56:46); on 56:47-48 "They said this while
  denying and rejecting the idea that resurrection will ever occur" (s1.3); quotes 56:47-48 as one
  sentence (s5.2); 56:49-50 "Say, O Muhammad, that the earlier and latter generations of the Children of
  Adam will be gathered ... none of them will be left out ... will not come late, early" (s6.1, frame).
- at-Tabari (t15): «كفرا منهم بالبعث، وإنكارًا لإحياء الله خلقه من بعد مماتهم» (s2.1, s4.2);
  «أئذا كنا ترابا في قبورنا من بعد مماتنا، وعظاما نخرة، أئنا لمبعوثون منها أحياء كما كنا قبل الممات» (s2.2, s8.2).
- al-Muyassar (t16): «أنُبعث إذا متنا وصرنا ترابًا وعظامًا بالية؟» (s2.3); «وهذا استبعاد منهم لأمر البعث وتكذيب له» (s4.1).
- al-Qurtubi (t90): whole note «هذا استبعاد منهم لأمر البعث وتكذيب له» (s4.1; identical to Muyassar's last sentence, stated).
- as-Sa'di (t91): «وكانوا ينكرون البعث، فيقولون استبعادا لوقوعه» (s4.2); «كيف نبعث بعد موتنا وقد بلينا، فكنا ترابا وعظاما؟»
  (s2.3, s5.1); «[هذا من المحال]» reported as bracketed, attribution of brackets not stated (s5.1);
  quotes 56:47 with 56:48 and repeats «أئنا لمبعوثون أوآباؤنا الأولون» (s5.2).
- al-Baghawi (t94): «قرأ أبو جعفر، ونافع والكسائي ويعقوب: "أئذا" مستفهما "إنا" بتركه، وقرأ الآخرون بالاستفهام فيهما» (s3).
  Readers named exactly as he names them; no ranking, no meaning-difference (he gives none).
- Ma'arif (t168): grouped text speaks only to 56:39-40 (two views on earlier/later; Mu'awiya hadith via
  Bukhari and Muslim on a group of the Ummah on the truth). Nothing on 56:47; s6.3 says so; hadith not used.

## Disagreements kept
- Readings (al-Baghawi): question in a-idha only (Abu Ja'far, Nafi', al-Kisa'i, Ya'qub) vs question in both
  (the others). Unranked. Site's printed text carries both, noted as matching his second group (observation
  from the printed Arabic; no reader of the printed text named).
- No disagreement on the sense: all six single-verse notes read it as denial/istib'ad, none as inquiry.
