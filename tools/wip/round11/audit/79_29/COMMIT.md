Card and 8-section bilingual article for 79:29, wa aghtasha laylaha wa
akhraja duhaha (4 words). 79:27-28 used as context; 79:30 named only.

Sources: Tabari, Ibn Kathir (ar), Qurtubi, Baghawi, Sa'di, all on 79:29;
Muyassar (grouped 79:28-33), Ibn Kathir en (grouped 79:27-33), Ma'arif
(grouped 79:25-34, group argument only).
- -ha read as the heaven (Tabari, Qurtubi, Baghawi). Reason kept as each
  gives it: sun sets and rises in it, nujum al-layl (Tabari, Qurtubi);
  darkness and light both descend from it (Baghawi).
- aghtasha = azlama: Ibn Abbas (2 chains), Mujahid, Qatada (2 chains),
  ad-Dahhak, 'Ikrima, Ibn Zayd (the darkness) in Tabari; IK adds Sa'id b.
  Jubayr and a large group. No disagreement.
- Lexicon from Qurtubi: ghatash/ghabash (also Baghawi), rajul aghtash =
  blind, falat ghatsha; two al-A'sha lines.
- duha glosses side by side: its light / its day made to appear (Tabari),
  lit it (Mujahid), lit its light (Qatada), its day (Dahhak), daylight
  (Ibn Zayd), lit its day (IK), day, light and sun (Qurtubi), day and
  light (Baghawi), day by sunrise (Muyassar), great light when He brought
  the sun (Sa'di). None ranked.
- Resurrection argument: Muyassar's bracket, IK en with 40:57 and 36:81,
  Ma'arif on 79:10-11.
Hadith: none attached by any fetched tafsir; said so. Asbab: none.
Dropped: IK en's Fussilat sequence note (79:30 ground), owls for fiyad,
"guests" in al-A'sha, any cosmology; 91:1-4, 93:1-2, 92:1-2 (not cited).
