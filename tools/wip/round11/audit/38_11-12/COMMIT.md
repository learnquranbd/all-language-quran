Card and 8-section bilingual article for 38:11-12: the Makkan host named "defeated, of the
confederates", and the roll call of Nuh's people, 'Ad and Pharaoh dhu l-awtad.

Sources: Tabari, Qurtubi, Ibn Kathir (ar/en), Baghawi, Sa'di, Muyassar (38:11-14), Ma'arif (38:12).
- Grammar: ma = sila (Tabari, Qurtubi, Baghawi); Qurtubi's omitted "hum"; Tabari's word order.
- Mahzum: Qurtubi (subdued, argument cut off; the cracked waterskin), Baghawi maghlub;
  al-Farra' and al-Qutabi (via Qurtubi) tie it to 38:9-10; Sa'di reads 38:10-11 together.
- Hunalika: Badr (Tabari; Qatada via Tabari, Qurtubi, Baghawi; Ibn Kathir with 54:44 to 54:46);
  Qurtubi's qila readings (Madinah confederates; past generations, with 2:249) kept beside it.
- Al-ahzab: Tabari (parties of Iblis), Mujahid, Baghawi, Muyassar; 38:13's own gloss.
- 38:12: Tabari names Quraysh as "them"; Sa'di 'Ad = people of Hud; Qurtubi on the feminine
  verb (two grammarians' views) and the list as consolation; Ibn Kathir (38:13) on strength.
- Dhu l-awtad: every view listed with its names (games, torture, buildings, strength,
  soldiers and tents, stable rule); Ibn Qutayba / al-Qutaybi on the tent idiom; Ma'arif
  closes "Allah knows best". No position taken.
- Aykah (38:13): Muyassar's identification only; Madyan dispute cross-referenced to 26:178.
Hadith: none attached by any fetched tafsir; stated in one sentence.
Asbab: not used; Qatada's Makkah-to-Badr note kept as timing only.
Framing: licensing sentence in both languages; torture reports summarised, not dwelt on.
Dropped: Badr hadiths (not attached), graphic torture detail, watad morphology, 38:15-16.
Arabic counts: 38:11 six words, 38:12 eight. Themes: trust.
