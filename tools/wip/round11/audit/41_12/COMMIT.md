Card and 8-section bilingual article for 41:12: fa-qadahunna sab'a
samawatin fi yawmayn ... wa-hifzan, dhalika taqdiru l-'Azizi l-'Alim,
with 41:9-11 as context only.

Sources: Tabari, Qurtubi, Sa'di, Ibn Kathir (ar + en abridged),
Baghawi, Muyassar. Ma'arif fetched (grouped text covers 41:11), not named.
- Qada: completed/finished (Qurtubi, Baghawi, Tabari); qila ahkamahunna
  with Abu Dhu'ayb's line (Qurtubi).
- Day count: four including the two (IK Sun-Wed, heavens Thu-Fri);
  Qurtubi "besides the four", six as in 7:54; 'Abdullah ibn Salam;
  Suddi's one heaven split into seven and the name al-Jumu'ah; Mujahid's
  thousand years. Muyassar/Sa'di: able in a moment, created in measure
  (Sa'di: hakim rafiq).
- Awha glosses kept side by side: command/will (Mujahid, Muqatil, Sa'di),
  contents (Suddi, 'Ata'-Ibn 'Abbas, Qatadah, IK, Tabari), amr takwin
  with 99:5 and 5:111 (Qurtubi); Ibn 'Abbas's house in each heaven.
- Lamps: stars (Tabari, IK, Sa'di); Qurtubi's qila/qila on stars in every
  heaven. Hifz from eavesdropping devils (IK, Muyassar, Qurtubi, Sa'di).
  Hifzan grammar kept as a disagreement: Basran vs Kufan, Tabari prefers
  Kufan; Baghawi and Qurtubi take the masdar reading.
- Sequence vs 79:27/79:30 raised by Qurtubi and Sa'di; dahw not khalq
  (Ibn 'Abbas via Qurtubi; many salaf via Sa'di); IK's foundation/roof,
  2:29 and Ibn 'Abbas's answer (per IK, in al-Bukhari), marked as a
  Companion's explanation. No harmonisation added.
Hadith: Muslim 2789 described, with IK's gharib/Bukhari ta'lil and
Qurtubi's note; Jews' report given only with IK's "fihi gharabah".
No narration settles the count; said in one sentence.
Asbab: none. Licensing sentence in both languages (Sa'di on rivals).
Dropped: 37:6-10 and 67:5 (no fetched tafsir cites them); 41:13 material.
