Replaces the 699-word, 6-section article with a 1,748-word, 8-section
bilingual article on waylun li-kulli humazatin lumazah; shipped card kept.

Sources: at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi, al-Baghawi, Ibn Kathir (ar + en abridged,
grouped 104:1-9), as-Sa'di, Muyassar, Ma'arif. Slot check clean.

Wayl: disgrace/punishment/destruction (al-Qurtubi), valley in Jahannam
(al-Qurtubi as qila; at-Tabari as his own gloss), threat (as-Sa'di).
Hamz root: breaking, biting with force; or pushing and striking
(al-Qurtubi, al-Baghawi); intensive form like sukhara/duhaka.
Kept side by side, none chosen:
- face vs behind (Abu al-'Aliya, ar-Rabi', al-Hasan, Mujahid, 'Ata;
  an-Nahhas with 9:58) and the reverse (Muqatil; Ma'arif "most")
- hand/eye vs tongue (Mujahid, Ibn Zayd, as-Sa'di) and the reverse
  (Ibn Kathir's own line, Sufyan ath-Thawri, Ibn Kaysan)
- backbiter vs attacker (at-Tabari; Mujahid's three narrations and
  at-Tabari's note on them) and synonyms (Ibn Abbas, Murra)
Asbab as reports: al-Akhnas, Umayya/Ubayy ibn Khalaf, al-Walid ibn
al-Mughira, Jamil ibn 'Amir; Mujahid "general", al-Qurtubi "majority",
at-Tabari's verdict on kull. None picked; licensing sentence both langs.
104:2-7 one section via Ibn Kathir en; 104:8 one line.

Hadith: none. The "worst servants of Allah" saying (al-Qurtubi, Ma'arif,
no collection; at-Tabari as Ibn Abbas's words) not confirmed, not quoted.
Dropped from the old article: "habit not incident" claim on the form,
unsourced readings on ranking and the heart, the 49:11 section (now one line).
