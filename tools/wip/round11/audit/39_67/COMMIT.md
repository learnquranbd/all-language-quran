Card and 8-section bilingual article for 39:67: wa-ma qadaru-llaha
haqqa qadrihi, wal-ardu jami'an qabdatuhu yawma-l-qiyamah,
was-samawatu matwiyyatun bi-yaminih.

Sources: Tabari, Qurtubi, Sa'di, Baghawi, Ibn Kathir (ar + en abridged),
Muyassar, Ma'arif.
- qadr: Tabari (mushrikun calling him to idols), Qurtubi via al-Mubarrad
  and an-Nahhas; glosses of Suddi, Muhammad b. Ka'b, Ibn 'Abbas (via Ibn
  Kathir, Tabari); Sa'di and Muyassar (equating the helpless with Him).
- Grammar and scale: Tabari's two clauses; Qurtubi's seven earths;
  Sa'di; Tabari's reports from al-Hasan and Ibn 'Abbas as their words.
- Sifat kept as a disagreement, no position: Ibn Kathir (imrar without
  takyif or tahrif), Muyassar (ithbat without takyif or tashbih), Tabari
  (rejects the Basran "in His power" reading), Qurtubi (power and
  possession, 69:45; tanzih line attributed to his an-Nahhas passage),
  Ma'arif (salaf real sense + mutashabihat vs later metaphor).
- Why the Day: Qurtubi, claims cut off (82:19, 1:4 verified).
Hadith: Bukhari 4811 (Ibn Mas'ud, the rabbi) and 4812 (Abu Hurayrah,
"where are the kings of the earth"), each whole in Bukhari's wording,
in his Sahih, no grading added; "finger" left as the narration's word.
Asbab: reports differ (Mujahid, Tabari, Ahmad "anzala" vs Bukhari
"qara'a"); read by placement after 39:64-66.
Licensing sentence in EN and BN; no living group named.
Dropped: 6:91 and 22:74 (no tafsir cites them here); Tabari's right/left
reports; Ibn Umar minbar, 'A'isha sirat and Tirmidhi gradings (not
confirmed on quranx); weak Tabarani and Ibn Humayd reports.
39:68 and 39:73 not pulled forward. Themes: majesty, tawheed.
