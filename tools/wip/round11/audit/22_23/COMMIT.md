Add Tadabbur 22:23 — the reward that answers the fire; gold, pearl, silk

The mercy panel that replies to 22:19-22 (garments of fire, boiling
water). Weight kept on the reward side; the punishment sits in a single
bridge, with a plain note that the verse licenses nothing against any
living person or community.

Sources (all from PREP, none re-fetched):
- Ibn Kathir: reward set against the fire; rivers coursing beneath trees
  and palaces, turned where the dwellers wish; bracelets on the arms;
  sundus/istabraq vs garments of fire (76:21-22).
- at-Tabari: belief bound to commanded righteous deeds; libas = what
  touches the skin; the "wa-lu'lu'a" qira'at (nasb/khafd), both sound,
  one meaning.
- as-Sa'di: true of none but those who believed in all Books and
  Messengers; rivers of water, milk, honey, wine.
- al-Qurtubi: three bracelets (gold, silver, pearl); rejects the
  gold-for-women/silver-for-men split; all clothing/bedding/hangings silk.
- al-Muyassar: unending bliss; adornment and silk for men and women.
- al-Baghawi: silk (ibrisam) forbidden to men in the dunya.
- Ma'arif: the "royal privilege" reading; Suraqah ibn Malik and the
  emperor of Persia's bracelets, delivered under Umar; Paradise silk vs
  world silk share only the name; Qurtubi's ranks/contentment answer to
  the deprivation puzzle.

Hadith: Bukhari 5426 (Hudhayfah, via Abd al-Rahman b. Abi Layla), quoted
whole — silk and gold/silver vessels are "for the disbelievers in this
world and for you in the Hereafter." Grading reported as its inclusion in
Sahih al-Bukhari; not upgraded. Muslim 2067 fetched only to confirm.

Dropped: Ka'b al-Ahbar's jewellery-angel report; the silk-from-a-fruit
reports (Ibn Umar/Jabir); no numbered standalone for the Abu Sa'id
al-Khudri narration (attributed to Baghawi/Qurtubi who carry it). No
sabab; verse placement given instead.
