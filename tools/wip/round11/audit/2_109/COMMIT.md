Card and 8-section bilingual article for 2:109: envy from within the
envier after the truth was clear, met with pardon; 2:110 as its remedy.
Key kept at 2:109; 2:104-105 named only for the frame and the verb.

Sources: Tabari, Ibn Kathir (ar + en grouped 2:109-110), Qurtubi,
Baghawi, Muyassar, Ma'arif. Sa'di empty for 2:109 (neighbour slots hold
his 2:106-107 naskh text); not named. Slots checked, all on target.
- Kathir = many in number: Tabari's objection to the Ka'b ibn al-Ashraf
  reading; licensing sentence in both languages (many, not all).
- Min 'indi anfusihim: Tabari, ar-Rabi', Baghawi, Qurtubi (emphasis,
  3:167, 2:79, 6:38), Ma'arif (envy not caused by the envied).
- After the truth was clear: Tabari, Abu al-'Aliya, ar-Rabi', ad-Dahhak.
- Qurtubi's two kinds of hasad; 'afw vs safh (Qurtubi, Baghawi, Tabari).
Kept open, no winner: abrogation of the pardon (Tabari's own 9:29 view
reported as his; Ibn Abbas 9:5 / 9:29 by source; Qatada both; ar-Rabi',
Suddi 9:29); what "His command" is (Ibn Abbas, Qatada, Ibn Kaysan);
Makkan or Madinan (Abu 'Ubayda vs Ibn 'Atiyya, Qurtubi's view as his).
Hadith: Muslim 815a quoted whole (cited by Qurtubi), marked general;
Bukhari 4566 (Usama: Sa'd's "pardon him and overlook him", the Prophet's
pardon until permission), summarised, Sa'd's words quoted.
Asbab: reports naming Ka'b, Huyayy and Abu Yasir given as reports, none
settled. Dropped: Baghawi's unchained Hudhayfa/'Ammar account and its
Prophetic words; Ibn Abi Hatim's chain note; 3:186 parallel (length).
