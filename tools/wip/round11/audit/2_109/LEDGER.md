## Disagreements kept open, both sides named, no position in the article's voice
- Abrogation of fa'fu wa-sfahu (§7): at-Tabari's own statement (abrogated by the obligation to fight, 9:29) is reported as his. Which verse: Ibn Abbas via 'Ali ibn Abi Talha 9:5 (at-Tabari), 9:5 and 9:29 (Ibn Kathir), 9:29 (al-Qurtubi); ar-Rabi', as-Suddi 9:29; Qatada both, by chain. Ibn Kathir's list for "the verse of the sword". Al-Baghawi only "before the verse of fighting"; Muyassar "it came". The article says it reports and rules between none. None of the fetched texts carries a view that the verse was never abrogated, so none is invented.
- What "His command" is (§7.3): punishment of Qurayza and an-Nadir (Ibn Abbas per al-Baghawi; al-Qurtubi names the same two events); the command to fight in 9:29 (Qatada); His knowledge and judgement, Islam for some (Ibn Kaysan).
- Makkan or Madinan (§7.2): Abu 'Ubayda vs Ibn 'Atiyya; al-Qurtubi's "وهو الصحيح" reported as his.
- Whom the verse names (§2): az-Zuhri (Ka'b ibn al-Ashraf) vs the Ibn Ishaq report (Huyayy and Abu Yasir); at-Tabari's objection to the single-man reading reported as his.

## Own observations (article's voice, not attributed)
- 2:105 and 2:109 use the same verb (yawaddu / wadda): visible in the Arabic in PREP.
- 2:109 "min 'indi anfusihim" and 2:110 "li-anfusikum ... 'inda Allah" share words: visible in the Arabic; stated as being about the Arabic in both languages.
- The verse's wish matches al-Qurtubi's blameworthy kind (loss for the other, not gain for oneself).
- Ibn Ubayy "was not of the People of the Book": the narration places him before his Islam and among "المشركين وعبدة الأوثان" with him. "What Sa'd describes has the verse's shape" is my reading; Sa'd does not use the word hasad (the page's English has "grieved with jealousy").
