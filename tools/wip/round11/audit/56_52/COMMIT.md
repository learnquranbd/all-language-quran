Card and 9-section bilingual article (1,735 EN words) on 56:52: la-akiluna
min shajarin min zaqqum. 56:51 read as its subject (one sentence);
56:47 and 56:49-50 frame the inna/lam echo; 56:53-56 framed in one
paragraph only; pointers to shipped 37:63 and 44:43 for the tree itself.

Sources: at-Tabari (56:51, 56:52), al-Muyassar (group 56:52-55),
al-Qurtubi, as-Sa'di, al-Baghawi (verse text only, stated), Ibn Kathir
(ar; en from the quranx group 56:41-56), al-Jalalayn, Tanwir al-Miqbas,
Maududi (pointer to al-Saffat).
- Two min: first as "from" (at-Tabari's sheep comparison), second as
  explication of shajar (al-Jalalayn); no other comment in the sources.
- Reading kept unranked, as at-Tabari gives it: shajara in the reading
  of Abdullah (RA); he says shajar and shajara mean one thing.
- Pronoun gender in 56:53/54: grammarians differed (at-Tabari); only
  his first view reported, the second is truncated in the fetched text.
- Hunger as the reason for filling bellies: al-Muyassar, stated as his.
- inna/lam echo across 56:47, 56:50, 56:52 offered as own reading,
  labelled as no mufassir's view.
- Licensing sentence in both languages; no living group named.
Hadith: none attached in any fetched note; stated in one sentence;
one-drop narration left to 44:43.
Asbab: none.
Dropped: al-him/hamim glosses, Ibn Kathir's "yusjarun", the truncated
Kufan view, any tree description beyond the 56:52 notes.
