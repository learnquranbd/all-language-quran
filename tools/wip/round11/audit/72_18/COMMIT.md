Replaces the 641-word, 6-section article with a 1,767-word, 9-section deep article, built only on tafsir fetched for 72:18 (shipped card kept).

Sources: at-Tabari, Ibn Kathir (ar; en abridged for 72:19-20), al-Qurtubi,
as-Sa'di, al-Baghawi, al-Muyassar, Ma'arif al-Qur'an, all fetched for 72:18.

Placement: at-Tabari joins the verse to qul uhiya ilayya (72:1); al-Qurtubi
gives that and al-Khalil's "because the masajid are Allah's". Readings of
al-masajid held by name, none chosen: buildings (Ikrima, al-Baghawi, al-Qurtubi,
who calls it the most apparent, from Ibn Abbas); all the earth (al-Hasan);
Makkah as qibla (Ibn Abbas via al-Qurtubi); limbs of prostration (Sa'id ibn
Jubayr in al-Baghawi and Ibn Kathir; Ibn al-Musayyib, Talq and Ata in
al-Qurtubi); the prayers (al-Hasan via al-Qurtubi; Ma'arif's masdar mimi).
masjid/masjad grammar from al-Baghawi and al-Qurtubi. 22:26 via al-Qurtubi.

Hadith: Bukhari 812 (seven bones, same Wuhayb-Ibn Tawus route the tafsirs give),
quoted whole; Bukhari 335 clause on the earth as a mosque, as al-Qurtubi cites
it. Both marked as not mentioning the verse.
Asbab: Qatada (Mujahid in al-Qurtubi) on churches and synagogues, with the
licensing sentence; Sa'id ibn Jubayr / al-A'mash jinn question; both as the
commentators' reports. Ibn Abi Hatim's Ibn Abbas report flagged for its unnamed
narrator. Du'a of worship and of asking (as-Sa'di); 72:19-20 via Ibn Kathir.

Dropped: al-Abbas "seven limbs" report, lost-animal, three-mosques and
entering du'a narrations (unconfirmed). Fixed from the old article: "not
ranked" (al-Qurtubi ranks the buildings reading first), "narrows it to
al-Masjid al-Haram" (source says Makkah as qibla), the six-occurrences count,
the ahadan grammar note and the mosque-wall claim (unsourced).
