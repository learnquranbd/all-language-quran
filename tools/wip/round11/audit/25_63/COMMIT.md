Replaces the 856-word, 6-section article with a 1,735-word, 9-section
deep article on the servants of the Most Merciful, walking hawnan and
answering the ignorant with salam.

Sources: at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi, Ibn Kathir (ar + en), as-Sa'di,
al-Baghawi, al-Muyassar, Ma'arif al-Qur'an, all on 25:63 (t169 grouped 25:63-67).
- 'Ibad ar-Rahman: al-Qurtubi (placement, honour, 17:1, 7:179), as-Sa'di's
  two servanthoods (19:93), al-Baghawi; al-Akhfash vs az-Zajjaj (25:75).
- yamshuna as a whole life (al-Qurtubi); hawn glossed per Ibn Kathir (17:37),
  al-Qurtubi (31:18), al-Baghawi; at-Tabari's three groups with holders.
- Not an affected slow walk: Ibn Kathir, 'Umar's report, Ma'arif; Ibn
  'Atiyya's grey wolf, az-Zuhri and the middle way, all via al-Qurtubi.
- Al-Hasan's portrait (35:34): at-Tabari vs Ibn Kathir on its clause.
- al-jahilun glossed as each tafsir gives it; licensing sentence in both languages.
- salama kept as five readings with holders (28:55); an-Nahhas, Sibawayh,
  al-Khalil's anecdote.
- Abrogation kept as a dispute: al-Kalbi, Abu l-'Aliya, Sibawayh vs Ibn
  al-'Arabi and al-Qurtubi; no position.
Hadith: Tirmidhi 3648 (gharib, quoted whole), Tirmidhi 3637 (hasan sahih,
walking clause, marked as part of a longer text), Bukhari 908 (Ibn Kathir's
definition of hawn), marked general; no occasion of revelation attached.
Dropped: Ahmad's an-Nu'man report (unconfirmable), unconfirmed gait
narrations in al-Qurtubi; from the old article the Anas, 'A'isha and
"strong man" hadiths, 25:41-42, 4:135, the "majority" claim and the
modern-application editorial.
