Replaces the 751-word, 5-section article with a 1,775-word, 8-section article built only from the fetched tafsirs on 40:44. The shipped card is kept.

Sources: at-Tabari, Ibn Kathir (ar, en abridged grouped 40:41-46), al-Qurtubi,
as-Sa'di, al-Baghawi, al-Muyassar, Ma'arif al-Qur'an.
- Placement: close of the speech begun at 40:28 (Ma'arif); 40:41-43 recap
  via Ibn Kathir (salvation, la jarama, as-Suddi, 46:5-6 and 35:14).
- Speaker kept as al-Qurtubi gives it: "it was said Musa", his preference the
  believer of Pharaoh's family (Ibn 'Abbas). No name supplied: none in the texts.
- Fa-sa-tadhkuruna: at the sight of the punishment (at-Tabari, al-Baghawi);
  Ibn Zayd "in the Hereafter"; tahdid wa wa'id and ma relative/masdariyya
  (al-Qurtubi, none chosen); regret too late (Ibn Kathir, Muyassar); lost
  reward (as-Sa'di).
- Ufawwidu: surrender + rely (at-Tabari, as-Suddi, al-Qurtubi); seek help and
  cut off from them (Ibn Kathir); refuge, all affairs, ward off harm (as-Sa'di,
  Muyassar). All gloss it with tawakkul; none distinguishes the two.
- Threat context (al-Baghawi; al-Qurtubi's "it was said" they meant to kill
  him; Ma'arif); Muqatil's flight to the mountain (al-Qurtubi, Ma'arif).
- Basir bil-'ibad: at-Tabari, al-Baghawi, Muyassar, Ibn Kathir, Ma'arif;
  as-Sa'di's two-sided reading, including "if He gives you power over me".
- 40:45 per Ibn Kathir (saved with Musa, Paradise; drowning then Hell); 40:46
  one line. Licensing sentence for Pharaoh's people in both languages.
No hadith: none attached to 40:44; Ibn Kathir's narrations in the group are on 40:46.
Dropped from the old article: the "root used only here" claim, the tafwid vs
tawakkul distinction (contradicted by every gloss), unsourced grammar readings,
40:19-20 and 3:159 links (no fetched tafsir cites them).
