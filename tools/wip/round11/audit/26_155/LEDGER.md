# SOURCES ledger — 26:155

## Tafsir fetched (all in audit/26_155/)
IDs fetched: 14, 15, 16, 90, 91, 94, 168, 169. Named in the article: 14, 15, 16, 90, 91, 94, 169. Fetched but NOT named: 168.

### at-Tabari (15) — named
- she-camel brought out of stone, place left open: "فأتاهم بناقة أخرجها من صخرة أو هضبة" → §2 "from a rock or from a hilltop (min ṣakhrah aw haḍbah); he settles it neither way".
- shirb = share of water: "ويعني بالشرب: الحظّ والنصيب من الماء" → §3 "the lot and the allotment of the water".
- boundary runs both ways: "ليس لكم في يوم وردها أن تشربوا من شربها شيئا, ولا لها أن تشرب في يومكم مما لكم شيئا" → §4 "you have no right to drink any of her share; and on your day she has no right to drink any of yours".
- three vocalizations: "والشُّرْب والشَّرْب والشِّرْب مصادر كلها بالضم والفتح والكسر" → §3 "shirb, sharb and shurb ... all verbal nouns of the same root".

### al-Muyassar (16) — named
- Allah brought her from the rock; "she-camel of Allah": "أتاهم بناقة أخرجها الله له من الصخرة ... هذه ناقة الله لها نصيب من الماء في يوم معلوم، ولكم نصيب منه في يوم آخر" → §2 "Allah brought her out of the rock ... calls her the she-camel of Allah (nāqat Allāh)"; §4 "hers a share of the water on a known day, and yours a share on another day".
- NOT used from (16): the harm prohibition and killing/punishment ("ولا تنالوها بشيء مما يسوءها كضَرْبٍ أو قتل ... فيهلككم الله بعذابِ يومٍ") — reserved for 26:156/26:158.

### al-Qurtubi (90) — named
- Ibn ʿAbbās demand: red she-camel, ʿushrāʾ (ten-months pregnant), out of the mountain, delivering as they watch, giving milk in return: "فادع الله يخرج لنا من هذا الجبل ناقة حمراء عشراء فتضع ونحن ننظر ... وتغدو علينا بمثله لبنا" → §1/§2 "a red she-camel, ten months pregnant (ʿushrāʾ) ... bring forth her young while they looked on"; §5 the milk exchange.
- a day of hers: "فكانت إذا كان يوم شربها شربت ماءهم كله أول النهار وتسقيهم اللبن آخر النهار، وإذا كان يوم شربهم كان لأنفسهم ومواشيهم وأرضهم" → §4 "she drank the whole of their water at the start of the day; and on their own day the water was for themselves, their livestock and their fields"; §5 "gave them milk to drink at the close of that same day".
- al-Farrāʾ: shirb = share of water: "قال الفراء: الشرب الحظ من الماء" → §3.
- an-Naḥḥās on vocalizations; Abū ʿAmr & al-Kisāʾī prefer fatḥa → §3.

### as-Sa'di (91) — named
- emerges from a solid smooth rock, publicly witnessed: "تخرج من صخرة صماء ملساء ترونها وتشاهدونها بأجمعكم" → §2 "a solid, smooth rock (ṣakhrah ṣammāʾ malsāʾ) that you all see and witness together".
- milk exchange: "تشرب ماء البئر يوما وأنتم تشربون لبنها ثم تصدر عنكم اليوم الآخر وتشربون أنتم ماء البئر" → §5.

### al-Baghawi (94) — named
- shirb = share and portion of water: "( قال هذه ناقة لها شرب ) حظ ونصيب من الماء" → §3 "a share and a portion of the water (ḥaẓẓ wa-naṣīb min al-māʾ)".

### Ibn Kathir ar (14) — named

### Ibn Kathir en, abridged (169) — named
- demand/promise/rock-split/exactness: "A crowd ... demanded that he immediately bring forth from the rock a she-camel that was ten months pregnant ... Salih made them promise that if he responded ... they would believe ... the rock ... split open, revealing a she-camel ... excactly as they had requested. So some of them believed, but most of them disbelieved." → §1, §2, §7.
- alternation: "she will drink from your water one day, and on the next day you will drink from it" → §4.
- milk while she lived among them: "they benefitted from her milk which they took in sufficient quantities for every one to drink his fill" → §5.
- NOT used from (169): the conspiracy to kill her, the hamstringing, the earthquake/Sayhah and destruction — reserved for 26:156/26:157/26:158.

- No miraculous-origin detail beyond what the fetched tafsir gives (rock or hilltop; red; ten-months pregnant; public emergence). No isrāʾīliyyāt, nothing from memory. §2 states explicitly that the account stops where the commentaries stop.
