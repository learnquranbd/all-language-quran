Card and deep bilingual article for an-Nahl 16:72: the household
niʿmah of spouse, sons and grandchildren, and the good provision,
closing on the rebuke to those who trust falsehood while denying
Allah's favour.

Sources (all confirmed against fetched tafsir, nothing from memory):
- Ibn Kathir, at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi, al-Baghawi, as-Saʿdi, al-Muyassar,
  Ma'arif al-Qur'an.
- min anfusikum = mates of your own kind (Ibn Kathir, Baghawi, Ma'arif);
  tranquillity in the spouse (as-Saʿdi).

Disagreement kept as disagreement — ḥafadah pulled four ways, each with
its own names:
- grandchildren (Ibn ʿAbbās, ʿIkrimah, al-Hasan, ad-Dahhāk, Ibn Zayd);
- servants/helpers (Mujāhid, Qatādah, Abū Mālik, al-Hasan al-Basrī);
- sons-in-law / in-laws (Ibn Masʿūd, Abū ad-Duhā, Ibrāhīm, Saʿīd b.
  Jubayr; the Zirr/Ibn Masʿūd correction);
- a wife's children from a former marriage (al-ʿAwfī from Ibn ʿAbbās).
at-Tabari's synthesis (ḥafd = swiftness in service, cf. the qunūt) and
Qurtubi/Ibn al-ʿArabī's grandchildren reading both given, no winner forced.

Hadith: none attached. The only sound report (Ibn Kathir's Day-of-
Resurrection reminder) is general and carries no collection/number, so
it is placed as an echo, not a proof-text.

Dropped: Qurtubi's household-service fiqh and its Bukhari/ʿĀʾishah hadith
(kept off marriage-law ground); Ibn Kathir's Abū Dāwūd "al-walad ʿabd
lak" (off-topic). No asbab established — verse placement given instead.

Standing sentence included: the closing rebuke targets deniers and
licenses nothing against any living person or community.
