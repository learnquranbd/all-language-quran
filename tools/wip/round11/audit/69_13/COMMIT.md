Card and 8-section bilingual article for 69:13: fa-idha nufikha fi
al-suri nafkhatun wahida. 69:12 as context; 69:14-15 only as the
tafsirs tie them; 69:16-18 left to their own verses.

Sources: Tabari, Baghawi, Muyassar, Qurtubi, Sa'di, Ibn Kathir (ar;
en abridged only to confirm reading order), Ma'arif (grouped 69:13-16).
- Sur = al-qarn (Muyassar, Ma'arif); blower unnamed in the verse,
  al-Zajjaj via Qurtubi; Israfil (Tabari, Sa'di), "the angel" (Muyassar).
- Which blast, kept as a disagreement: first/death (Tabari, Baghawi,
  Muyassar, Ibn Abbas via Qurtubi, Ma'arif) vs last/raising (Sa'di,
  Qurtubi's qila, al-Rabi' via IK, IK's sentence in its written order).
- Count: Ma'arif two blasts with 39:68 (verified), terror as a stage of
  the first via Mazhari; IK names three in sequence.
- Wahida: IK (command not opposed, needs no repeating); Qurtubi la
  tuthanna; Qurtubi grammar (masculine verb, al-Akhfash, Abu al-Sammal's
  nafkhatan, darabtu darban).
Hadith: Tirmidhi 2430 (Abdullah ibn 'Amr, sur is a horn blown into),
attached by Ma'arif; at-Tirmidhi: hasan (Arabic; page's English says
hasan sahih, not upgraded). Ma'arif's "Ibn Umar" vs the chain's
Abdullah ibn 'Amr noted in both languages.
No condemned group addressed; no licensing sentence.
Dropped: owner-of-the-Horn narration (not attached by any fetched
tafsir), 36:51/20:102/6:73 (not cited), 69:16-18 material, the ship
as the referent of 69:12.
- Tirmidhi 2430: Arabic page gives hasan; the quranx English says 'Hasan Sahih'. Article uses hasan (the lower), no upgrade.
