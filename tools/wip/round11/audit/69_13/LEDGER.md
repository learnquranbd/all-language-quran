## Disagreement kept (which blast), no side picked
- First blast / death: at-Tabari, al-Baghawi, al-Muyassar, Ibn Abbas (via al-Qurtubi), Ma'arif.
- Last blast / raising: as-Sa'di, al-Qurtubi's unnamed "qila", al-Rabi' (via Ibn Kathir), Ibn Kathir's own sentence as ordered in the fetched text.
- **Orchestrator to verify:** Ibn Kathir's `وهي هذه النفخة` follows `نفخة القيام` in the fetched Arabic and the abridged English reads it the same way; but he then quotes al-Rabi' ("the last blast") and adds `والظاهر ما قلناه`, which reads oddly if both say the same. The article reports the words in order ("in the order it is written, places it there as well") and does not claim he agrees or disagrees with al-Rabi'. If the full Ibn Kathir places `وهي هذه النفخة` after `نفخة الفزع`, §4.2 and §4.3 need adjusting.
- Count of blasts: Ma'arif two (terror as a stage of the first, via Mazhari) vs Ibn Kathir's three in sequence; kept as a difference in description.

## Hadith
- Tirmidhi 2430, https://quranx.com/hadith/Tirmidhi/Hadith-2430 (saved hadith_Tirmidhi_2430.txt). Attached to this verse by Ma'arif. First ten words (EN): "'Abdullah bin 'Amr bin Al-'As said: "A Bedouin came to the Prophet". Quoted whole (the matn). Grading: the Arabic `قَالَ أَبُو عِيسَى هَذَا حَدِيثٌ حَسَنٌ`; the page's English says "Hasan Sahih". Article reports the lower, Arabic **hasan**; not upgraded.
- Discrepancy flagged in the article: Ma'arif names the companion as Abdullah Ibn Umar; the collection's chain names Abdullah ibn 'Amr ibn al-'As.
- Tirmidhi 2431 and 3244 fetch attempts timed out (network); not needed, since no fetched tafsir attaches them.
- "How can I be at ease while the owner of the Horn has put it to his mouth": no fetched tafsir on 69:13 attaches it; not used, not described. The article says in one sentence that no other narration is attached.
- Abu Musa "three presentations" (Ibn Kathir en): on 69:18, future ground; not used.
