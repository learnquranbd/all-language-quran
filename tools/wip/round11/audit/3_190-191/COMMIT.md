Replaces the 784-word, 5-section article with a 1,756-word, 8-section
one, from at-Tabari, Ibn Kathir (ar and en), al-Qurtubi, as-Sa'di,
al-Baghawi, al-Muyassar and Ma'arif on 3:190 and 3:191. All slots
checked. The shipped card is kept.

Placement: al-Qurtubi's closing command to look and reason (certainty,
not taqlid); at-Tabari's argument against those who called Allah poor,
with one line back to 3:189. Asbab: at-Tabarani's as-Safa report via
Ibn Kathir, with his own "mushkil" (Madinan verse, Makkan request), and
Ma'arif's use of it as background. Not taken as the occasion.
Signs and ulu al-albab: Ibn Kathir, as-Sa'di, at-Tabari, Ma'arif, the
Muyassar; one line to 2:164; 12:105 and 12:106 with a licensing line.
Postures kept as a disagreement: prayer of the sick ('Ali, Ibn 'Abbas,
an-Nakha'i, Qatadah via al-Baghawi; al-Hasan via al-Qurtubi) vs dhikr
in every state (al-Baghawi's "rest of the commentators", al-Qurtubi,
Ibn Kathir); at-Tabari spans both, as-Sa'di holds both; the Qatadah
mismatch is noted. 4:103.
Tafakkur: al-Qurtubi and as-Sa'di (an act of worship); Ma'arif's limit
(the signs, not the Essence); al-Hasan and ad-Darani via Ibn Kathir,
marked as sayings, not hadith. Reflection vs prayer: Sufis vs jurists
via Ibn al-'Arabi in al-Qurtubi.
Batil: at-Tabari, al-Baghawi, al-Qurtubi (Labid); hadha kept as a
disagreement: this creation (Ibn Kathir, al-Baghawi, Muyassar) vs the
commanded and forbidden (at-Tabari). Subhanaka and fa-qina from Ibn
Kathir and as-Sa'di. 3:192 to 3:194 get one paragraph, checked against
the Arabic.
Hadith: Bukhari 4569 (Ibn 'Abbas at Maymunah's, sat up, looked at the
sky, recited) quoted whole; Bukhari 1117 ('Imran); Muslim 373 ('A'ishah).
The 'A'ishah/Bilal weeping report is given as the tafsirs give it (Ibn
Marduyah, Ibn Hibban and Ibn 'Asakir, "ruwiya"), ungraded, not on quranx.
Dropped: al-Qurtubi's unsourced fikr hadiths, Ma'arif's science excursus.
Corrected: the shipped Bukhari attribution (blended "wiped the sleep"
wording, "deliberately watching", recitation "lying on his bedding";
4569 says he sat up), the kernel-and-husk gloss, Najran and 41:53.
