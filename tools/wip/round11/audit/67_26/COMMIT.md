Card and 8-section bilingual article (1,640 EN words) on 67:26: the reply
to 67:25's "when is this promise", qul and the two innama clauses, al-'ilm
as the knowledge of the Hour's time, the 7:187 counterpart, truth without
a timetable, nadhir mubin, "unknown when, certain that". 67:27 pointer only.

Sources: at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi, as-Sa'di, Ibn Kathir (ar; en group
67:20-27 for the 67:25-26 part), Muyassar. Baghawi (verse text only) and
Ma'arif (nothing on 67:26) fetched, not named.
- The promise named from four angles, each attributed: Hashr (Muyassar),
  gathering after separation (IK), punishment and the Hour (Tabari),
  recompense (Sa'di). Muyassar's reading of kuntum as aimed at the
  believers kept as his.
- Al-'ilm = time of the Hour: Tabari, Qurtubi, Muyassar; IK's 'ala
  at-ta'yin qualifier and his "commanded me to tell you it will happen,
  so beware of it".
- Qurtubi's naziruhu: 7:187 (wording matches 'inda rabbi, not 33:63).
- Sa'di: no necessary link between a report's truth and telling its
  time; truth known by its proofs (echo of 50:37 noted as the article's).
- Nadhir mubin: Tabari, Muyassar, Qurtubi's mukhawwif wa mu'allim, IK's
  "upon me is only the conveying, and I have conveyed it".
Explicit paragraph in both languages: the verse licenses nothing against
any living person or community, and no label for a sincere questioner.
Hadith: none; no fetched tafsir attaches one, said in one sentence.
Dropped: Hadith of Jibril / five unseen things, 31:34, 33:63 (not cited
in the fetched texts); Ahsanul Bayaan notes (sanity check only).
- 50:37 pointer is the drafter's own identification of as-Sa'di's quoted words; worded so.
