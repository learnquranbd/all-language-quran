Disagreement kept as a disagreement (no position taken): the ruling on taking wages for teaching religion — righteous early generations held it impermissible; later generations permitted it under pressing need (Ma'arif, §6.2).

## Hadith

- Sunan Abī Dāwūd 3664 (Abū Hurayrah). URL fetched: https://quranx.com/hadith/AbuDawud/DarusSalam/Hadith-3664 (via hadith.js; saved hadith_AbuDawud_3664.txt).
- First ten words of the wording used (English): "If anyone acquires knowledge that should be sought seeking the Face…"
- Arabic opening: "مَنْ تَعَلَّمَ عِلْمًا مِمَّا يُبْتَغَى بِهِ وَجْهُ اللَّهِ…"
- Grading: the fetched page (hadith.js output) did NOT expose a collector's grading, and Abū Dāwūd gives none in the text to hand. No grading is claimed; the article states it is "reported just as he reports it, no stronger." Not upgraded.
- Fit: direct — sacred knowledge pursued for worldly advantage is the inverse of Nūḥ's "no wage." Deliberately NOT the stock niyyāt (Bukhari 1) nor the seven-shaded hadith (Bukhari 660), which the 26:95 neighbour used ("Loyalty That Earns Shade").
