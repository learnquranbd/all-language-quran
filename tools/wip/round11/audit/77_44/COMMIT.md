Card and 9-section bilingual article for 77:44, inna kadhalika najzi
al-muhsinin: what kadhalika points back to (77:41-43 as context, not
developed), who the muhsinin are, and the 77:45 refrain as context only.

Sources: Tabari (77:44, 77:43), Muyassar (grouped 77:41-45), Qurtubi
(77:44, 77:41), Ibn Kathir (ar 77:41, 77:43, 77:44; en grouped 77:41-50
for 77:46), Sa'di (77:41, 77:43, 77:44), Baghawi (77:43). Context-verse
entries fetched and saved as t<id>_41/43.txt. Ma'arif grouped text covers
77:35-36 only; not named.
- kadhalika: Tabari "as We rewarded these muttaqin ... so We reward ahl
  al-ihsan"; Muyassar "bi-mithli dhalika al-jaza' al-'azim"; Ibn Kathir
  "khabar musta'naf: this is Our reward for whoever does deeds well".
- al-muhsinin, five glosses named: obedience and worship (Tabari,
  Muyassar), affirming Muhammad ﷺ and deeds (Qurtubi), doing deeds well
  (Ibn Kathir), ihsan in worship of Allah and to His servants (Sa'di, who
  already calls the muttaqin of 77:41 muhsinin). Stated as emphases, not
  a dispute.
- Deeds and mercy: Sa'di "deeds are the cause", Tabari "reward not lost",
  Ibn Kathir "said to them out of ihsan to them"; article notes the texts
  do not take up the larger question and takes no position.
- 77:45-46: Sa'di's "missing this bliss is loss enough"; Ibn Kathir (en)
  "command of threat". Licensing sentence in EN and BN.
Hadith: none attached by any fetched tafsir; said in one sentence. Jibril
hadith not used.
Sisters verified in data/quran-json: 37:80/121/131, 37:105, 12:22, 28:14,
55:60, 9:120, 18:30, 52:19.
Du'a: composed in the verse's vocabulary, marked as not narrated.
Asbab: none in the sources.
Dropped: Raghib's senses of ihsan (memory), Makki claim, occurrence
count, Qurtubi's zilal/zulal variant reading.
