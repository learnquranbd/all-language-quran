Sources: at-Tabari, Ibn Kathir (ar + en abridged), al-Qurtubi, as-Sa'di,
al-Baghawi, al-Muyassar, Ma'arif al-Qur'an, all on 70:22-23.

- 70:19-21 portrait: Ma'arif's limits (impatience beyond the Shari'ah,
  withholding owed dues); human nature (Ibn Kathir, Muyassar, Ma'arif) vs
  about the disbelievers (al-Qurtubi, at-Tabari) kept as a disagreement,
  with a sentence that neither labels anyone living; al-Baghawi's grammar,
  103:2-3 as cited by al-Qurtubi and al-Baghawi.
- Who al-musallin are: at-Tabari's gloss and his two further views,
  an-Nakha'i and Ibn Zayd; al-Qurtubi's range incl. trust and certainty.
- da'imun, three readings held open: times and obligations (Ibn Mas'ud,
  Masruq, an-Nakha'i; as-Sa'di's three contrasts); stillness ('Uqbah ibn
  'Amir via Abu al-Khayr, al-ma' ad-da'im, 23:1-2); a deed kept up once
  begun (Ibn Kathir's third, Ibn Jurayj and al-Hasan on voluntary prayer).
- Hadith: Bukhari 6465 quoted whole ("the most constant of them, even if
  few"), attached by Ibn Kathir from the Sahih; at-Tabari's Abu Salamah
  report ending with the verse.
- 70:23 vs 70:34 per Ma'arif; opening and closing on prayer, 23:10-11 vs
  70:35 per Ibn Kathir (abridged); 70:24-33 in one attributed section.
- Dropped: Qatada's Daniel report, 107:4-5, the Makkan/zakat dispute and
  51:19, the "Muslim" attribution, the active-participle claim.
- Fixed: the old article put inner attention under 70:34; Ma'arif places
  khushu' in 70:23 and times/etiquette in 70:34.
