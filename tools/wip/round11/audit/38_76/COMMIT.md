Card and 8-section bilingual article for 38:76: ana khayrun minhu, khalaqtani
min narin wa-khalaqtahu min tin, Iblis's reply to the question of 38:75, read
after 38:71-75 and with the same reply at 7:12.

Sources: Tabari, Qurtubi, Sa'di, Ibn Kathir (ar + en abridged), Muyassar,
Baghawi (verse text only). Ma'arif (grouped, on 38:75's "hands") not used.
- Tabari: the reply declines both options of 38:75 (not arrogance, not the
  'alin, "because I am nobler"), against 38:74's istakbara; fire eats clay as
  Iblis's reasoning; the scene as rebuke to the deniers of 38:8 and 21:3.
- Qurtubi: al-Farra' on khayr as a shortened akhyar; "jahl", substances are of
  one kind, "he made an analogy and erred".
- Sa'di: qiyas fasid; fire vs clay properties; the elder and his pupils.
- Ibn Kathir: "in his claim"; he erred, opposed the command, disbelieved; the
  name Iblis from ablasa.
Disagreement kept, no position: Qurtubi denies any ranking of substances;
Sa'di reverses the ranking in clay's favour.
Iblis's argument kept as his claim throughout, never endorsed.
Hadith: none attached by any fetched source; said in one sentence, with a
pointer to 15:33 and 18:50. Asbab: none.
Overlap: 15:33 (shipped) read in full; its motive, fire-eats-clay, Muslim 91
and daily-life material not repeated; 15:33 deferred the rebuttal, given here.
Framing: licensing sentence in both languages (Tabari's deniers).
Dropped: Qurtubi's al-A'raf discussion (not fetched), Ibn Kathir's "nature
betrayed him" (used at 18:50), an unsourced bashar link to 21:3.
Arabic count: 38:76 ten words (3 + 3 + 3 after qala). Theme: character.
