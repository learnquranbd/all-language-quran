# SOURCES ledger — 20:47

## Tafsir fetched (all from PREP.md, not re-fetched) and where named

- **Ibn Kathir (ar, 14)** — NAMED. "قد جئناك بآية من ربك ... أي بدلالة ومعجزة من ربك" → sign = evidence/miracle. "والسلام على من اتبع الهدى أي والسلام عليك إن اتبعت الهدى" → the conditional reading (peace be upon you IF you follow guidance). Heraclius-letter passage ("سلام على من اتبع الهدى ... فأسلم تسلم يؤتك الله أجرك مرتين"). Also the Ibn Ishaq report ("وقفا بباب فرعون ... إنا رسل رب العالمين ... سنتين يغدوان ويروحان ... بطال") and the as-Suddi night report ("فضرب موسى باب القصر بعصاه ... ليلا"), relayed without tawthiq.
- **at-Tabari (15)** — NOT named. Fetched text on 20:47 is thin and mostly carries over to 20:46; nothing verse-specific worth attributing, so dropped to avoid over-claiming.
- **al-Muyassar (16)** — NAMED. "أن أطلق بني إسرائيل، ولا تكلفهم ما لا يطيقون من الأعمال" (release + labour beyond capacity); "والسلامة من عذاب الله تعالى لمن اتبع هداه" (safety from punishment for who follows guidance).
- **al-Qurtubi (90)** — NAMED. Ellipsis "في الكلام حذف ... فأتياه فقالا له ذلك"; "ولا تعذبهم أي بالسخرة والتعب"; the severity "يذبح أبناءهم ويستحيي نساءهم ويكلفهم من العمل في الطين واللبن وبناء المدائن ما لا يطيقونه"; Ibn Abbas "يريد العصا واليد"; the white hand "بيضاء لها شعاع مثل شعاع الشمس غلب نورها على نور الشمس"; az-Zajjāj "سلم من سخط الله وعذابه ... وليس بتحية ... ليس بابتداء لقاء ولا خطاب"; al-Farrā' "السلام على من اتبع الهدى ولمن اتبع الهدى سواء".
- **as-Sa'di (91)** — NAMED. Two matters "دعوته إلى الإسلام، وتخليص هذا الشعب الشريف بني إسرائيل من قيده"; staff→serpent + white hand cast in order; "حصلت له السلامة في الدنيا والآخرة".
- **al-Baghawi (94)** — NAMED. "خل عنهم وأطلقهم من أعمالك ... لا تتعبهم في العمل"; white hand "لها شعاع كشعاع الشمس"; "ليس المراد منه التحية إنما معناه سلم من عذاب الله من أسلم".
- **Ma'arif al-Qur'an (en, 168)** — NAMED. "the prophets have the duty of guiding mankind towards their salvation as well as to liberate their people from worldly and economic bondages." (dual duty).
- **Ibn Kathir abridged (en, 169)** — used to corroborate 14: "peace be upon you if you follow the guidance," Heraclius letter wording in English. (Not separately named in article; folded under Ibn Kathir.)

## Hadith used

- **Sahih al-Bukhari — Dar-us-Salam Hadith 7** (USC-MSA Vol.1 Bk.1 H.6). URL fetched: https://quranx.com/hadith/Bukhari/DarusSalam/Hadith-7 (via tools/wip/round11/hadith.js Bukhari 7).
  First ten words (EN): "Narrated 'Abdullah bin 'Abbas: Abu Sufyan bin Harb informed me that..."
  Letter clause used: "...Peace be upon him, who follows the right path. Furthermore I invite you to Islam, and if you become a Muslim you will be safe, and Allah will double your reward..." (Arabic: "سلام على من اتبع الهدى ... أسلم تسلم يؤتك الله أجرك مرتين").
  Grading: Sahih (in Sahih al-Bukhari; no upgrade — reported as the collection's own status). Directly on the verse: the Prophet ﷺ used this verse's exact formula toward a non-Muslim king, confirming the "conditional, not greeting" reading.

## Dropped / not asserted

- **at-Tabari** not attributed on 20:47 (thin verse-specific text; see above).
- **Ibn Ishaq (two years at the gate) and as-Suddi (striking the gate at night)** — kept only in §6 explicitly flagged as narrative material the commentators relay without vouching (isrā'īliyyāt/qasas), doctrine not built on them. Not stated as fact.
- **"hadith al-Futūn"** (Ibn Abbas long narration mentioned by Ibn Kathir) — dropped; disputed authenticity, not fetched/confirmed as sound, so not cited.
- **"yawm al-zīna" (day of adornment)** detail in al-Qurtubi (staff not shown until then) — dropped to avoid preempting later verses.
- No modern-application reading offered; §7 states the verse passes no verdict on anyone living today.
- Did not preempt 20:48 (punishment on who denies/turns away) or 20:49 (Pharaoh's "who is your Lord"); gestured forward only, without stating their content.
