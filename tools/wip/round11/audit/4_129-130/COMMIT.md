Assigned 4:129, widened to 4:129-130 (you will never manage equality between wives
however much you want it, so do not lean wholly and leave her hanging; if they part,
Allah enriches each from His plenty). 4:128 shipped and not redone; 4:131-134 left free.
New card and a 1,759-word, 8-section article.

Sources: at-Tabari, Ibn Kathir (ar/en), al-Qurtubi, as-Sa'di, al-Baghawi, al-Muyassar
and Ma'arif on 4:129 and 4:130, all slots on target.
Centre: what is excused (love, intimacy, the heart: at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi, Ubaydah, Ibn
Abbas, al-Hasan, Ibn Zayd, as-Sa'di) and what is not (division, maintenance, decent
companionship: at-Tabari, Mujahid via al-Qurtubi, as-Suddi, al-Baghawi, as-Sa'di).
Ma'arif reads 4:129 as explaining the justice of 4:3; its anti-polygamy-argument
section left out, no polemic either way.
Mu'allaqah with the wife's rights in view: at-Tabari's list, Ibn Zayd, as-Sa'di,
Qatadah's "imprisoned", Ubayy's reading, al-Qurtubi's image and Umm Zar' (Bukhari 5189).
4:130 as consolation for both: al-Qurtubi's husn az-zann, as-Sa'di's "no harm in parting";
24:32 as the Qur'an's own parallel. No fiqh of divorce.
Kept as differences, no position:
- the enrichment: Ibn Kathir (a better spouse) vs at-Tabari (or provision, protection,
  chastity) vs as-Sa'di (provision guaranteed, perhaps a husband).
- wa in tuslihu: household (at-Tabari, Ibn Kathir) vs widened to all reconciliation (as-Sa'di).
Hadith: Abu Dawud 2134 (the prayer), quoted whole with his "the heart"; at-Tirmidhi 1140's
remark that the mursal is more correct reported beside Ibn Kathir's "sound chain", not
upgraded. Tirmidhi 1141 (two wives, one side drooping) with his note on Hammam.
Bukhari 2587 (be just to your children) marked as not attached to the verse.
Asbab: Ibn Abi Mulaykah's "revealed about A'ishah" not used (a Successor's statement).
Dropped: Umar's saying (Qatadah's "it was mentioned to us"), al-Qurtubi's Ja'far story,
al-Baghawi's and as-Sa'di's fiqh of division and separation.
