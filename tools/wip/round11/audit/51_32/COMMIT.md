Card and 8-section bilingual article on qalu inna ursilna ila qawmin
mujrimin, the messengers' reply to Ibrahim (AS); 51:33-37 as pointers
only, 51:28's arrival scene not retold.

Sources: at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi, Ibn Kathir (ar + en abridged),
al-Baghawi, al-Muyassar, as-Sa'di, Ma'arif al-Qur'an.
- Identification as the people of Lut: Ibn Kathir, al-Qurtubi,
  al-Baghawi, as-Sa'di, Ma'arif, Ibn Kathir (en).
- Mujrimin glossed only in the sources' words: Tabari and Muyassar
  (crimes through kufr), Sa'di (shirk, denying their messenger, the
  unprecedented fahisha, not described further), Ma'arif (unspeakable
  crime); kept as a difference of breadth, no position taken.
- Passive ursilna; Muyassar names the sender (inna Allaha arsalana).
- 15:57-58 identical to 51:31-32 (text check); 15:59 names Lut's family.
- Mujrimin / musrifin (51:34, Muyassar gloss) / zalimin (29:31).
- 11:74-76 and 29:32 only as Ibn Kathir (en) places them; keys checked.
Licensing paragraph in both languages: describes a past people,
licenses nothing against any living person or community.
Hadith: none attached by any fetched tafsir; said in one sentence.
Asbab: none used.
Dropped: 169's names-on-stones and dead-sea details (later verses),
Ma'arif's stone size, any j-r-m root gloss, naming the town.
