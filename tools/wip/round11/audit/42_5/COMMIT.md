Card and 8-section bilingual article for 42:5: takadu as-samawatu
yatafattarna min fawqihinna, the angels' tasbih and istighfar for those
on earth, and ala inna-llaha huwa-l-ghafuru-r-rahim. 42:4 for placement;
40:8 one clause; 42:6 left to its own verse.

Sources: Tabari, Ibn Kathir (ar; en abridged as rendering), Muyassar,
Qurtubi, Sa'di, Baghawi, Ma'arif (grouped 42:5-6).
- Cause: awe of the greatness (Tabari with Ibn 'Abbas, Qatadah, Dahhak;
  IK's five; Muyassar; Qurtubi via Dahhak/Suddi) vs the polytheists'
  "Allah has taken a son" (Baghawi with 19:88-90; Qurtubi from Ibn
  'Abbas). Ibn 'Abbas reported both ways; kept, no choice.
- min fawqihinna: each heaven above the next / above the earths / tied
  to His majesty; Qurtubi's qira'at (yanfatirna, 82:1); Sa'di, Ma'arif.
- Tasbih: Tabari, Muyassar, Sa'di; Qurtubi's tanzih, wonder ('Ali),
  submission (Ibn 'Abbas), Suddi's "by command".
- Istighfar: believers (Tabari, Suddi, Muyassar, Baghawi, Dahhak; IK and
  Qurtubi with 40:7, Throne-bearers) vs all angels (Kalbi); Wahb's
  abrogation vs Mahdawi and Ibn al-Hassar; Mawardi's sins vs provision,
  Qurtubi preferring provision, then Salman's mawquf report narrowing it;
  Zamakhshari's forbearance with 35:41 and 13:6; Ma'arif's worldly respite.
- Closing names: IK, Tabari, Muyassar, Sa'di, Ma'arif; Qurtubi's "awe at
  the start, good news at the end".
Licensing sentence in both languages. Cross-refs checked in quran-json.
Hadith: none quoted; Tirmidhi 2312 (Hasan Gharib) checked and dropped
(not attached by any fetched tafsir; clauses unexplained by them).
Asbab: Kalbi's Harut-and-Marut occasion not used.
Dropped: Ka'b al-Ahbar's report in Tabari, any own-voice gloss on sifat.
