## Disagreements kept (no position)
- Cause of near-splitting: awe of the greatness/majesty (Tabari with Ibn 'Abbas, Qatadah, ad-Dahhak; IK's five; Muyassar; Qurtubi via ad-Dahhak/as-Suddi) vs the polytheists' saying "Allah has taken a son" (Baghawi with 19:88-90; Qurtubi from Ibn 'Abbas). Ibn 'Abbas is reported both ways; stated as such.
- min fawqihinna: each heaven above the next (Muyassar, Baghawi, Ibn 'Abbas via Qurtubi) / above the earths (Tabari; Qurtubi qila, with khashyah "if they could reason") / tied to Allah's greatness and majesty "fawqahunna" (ad-Dahhak, as-Suddi via Qurtubi).
- Tasbih: prayer in obedience and thanks (Tabari), tanzih (Muyassar, Qurtubi), wonder (qila; 'Ali), submission (Ibn 'Abbas via Qurtubi); bi-hamdi = by command (as-Suddi).
- li-man fi-l-ard: believers (Tabari, as-Suddi, Muyassar, Baghawi, ad-Dahhak; IK with 40:7; Qurtubi: Throne-bearers on this reading) vs all angels of heaven (al-Kalbi); abrogation (Wahb) vs not abrogated (al-Mahdawi; Ibn al-Hassar: other angels ask for all on earth); content: sins (Muqatil) vs provision (al-Kalbi, preferred by Qurtubi) vs forbearance/respite, general (Zamakhshari via Qurtubi; Ma'arif close); Salman report narrowing to rememberers. Al-Qurtubi's own two leanings reported in sequence, not reconciled.
