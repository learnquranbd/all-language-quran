## Disagreements kept (both sides named, no position)
- Which garden: heaven, held by most (Ibn Kathir) vs on earth (reported by al-Qurtubi from the Mu'tazila and Qadariyya); al-Qurtubi's answers and Ibn Kathir's note that the Iblis-entry question is the earth view's argument both given; no verdict in the article's voice.
- The tree: six sayings vs at-Tabari's "unknown, and not needed" (Ibn Kathir: correct; as-Sa'di same restraint).
- Readings fa-azallahuma vs fa-azalahuma (Hamza): at-Tabari prefers the first; al-Qurtubi: close in meaning.
- How Iblis reached them: face to face (Ibn Mas'ud, Ibn Abbas, most, via al-Qurtubi) vs whispering without re-entry (a group).
- Minor sins of prophets: at-Tabari and others allow vs a majority of Maliki/Hanafi/Shafi'i jurists deny (al-Qurtubi); how the eating happened: the specific tree vs forgetting (al-Qurtubi calls forgetting correct, the first also good); Ma'arif: a lapse from deception, not deliberate.
- Penalty or purpose: at-Tabari (penalty for the khati'a Iblis caused) vs al-Qurtubi (not a punishment; after repentance; wisdom of populating the earth) vs Ma'arif (authority then wisdom).
- Repeated ihbitu: emphasis / separate rulings / two stages (al-Qurtubi); Ibn Kathir picks separate rulings.
- The words of 2:37: 7:23 (Ibn Kathir's list; at-Tabari: what the Book indicates) vs other formulas (not rejected, unproven).

## Prophets and Hawwa
- No sentence in the article's voice says Adam (AS) sinned or erred. The verse's own verb (azallahuma, "caused them to slip") is used; at-Tabari's word khati'a appears once, attributed. 'Isma: one paragraph, every position named.
- Hawwa not blamed: the article notes the dual, the named cause (Shaytan), 7:20 (whisper to both) and 20:120 (to Adam), and al-Hasan (via al-Qurtubi) that the repentance includes her.
- Licence sentence for 2:39 in both languages, grounded in at-Tabari's "if they die on disbelief before repenting".
