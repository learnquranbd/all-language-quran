## Watch-point vs source (orchestrator please note)
The watch-point listed Ibn 'Abbas/Qatada with the conquest reading and Mujahid/al-Hasan with the heavens-and-body reading.
The fetched texts say otherwise, and the article follows the texts:
- Conquests / Makkah: al-Minhal and as-Suddi (at-Tabari); Mujahid, al-Hasan, as-Suddi (al-Baghawi); Mujahid, with al-Minhal and as-Suddi (al-Qurtubi); Ibn Kathir's own lead gloss, and his quote of Mujahid, al-Hasan, as-Suddi for anfusihim = Badr and the conquest of Makkah.
- Past nations: Ibn 'Abbas (al-Baghawi: dwellings of past nations; trials and illness); al-Qurtubi's unattributed opening gloss; Qatada (al-Baghawi) and Qatada with ad-Dahhak (al-Qurtubi): Allah's dealings with nations; anfusihim = Badr.
- Heavens/earth/body: "others" and Ibn Zayd (at-Tabari); 'Ata' and Ibn Zayd (al-Qurtubi, al-Baghawi).
At-Tabari's preference for as-Suddi's reading is reported as his, with his reason; the article adopts none.
