Replaces the 836-word, 6-section article with a 9-section, 1,732-word
source-gated article (shipped card kept).

Sources: at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi, as-Sa'di, Ibn Kathir (ar, en abridged),
al-Baghawi, Muyassar, Ma'arif, all on 41:53 itself. Covers 41:52 and
41:54 (Ibn Kathir en) with one line each on 41:33, 41:34, 41:37, 41:47;
who is shown (at-Tabari, Muyassar, Ibn Kathir, as-Sa'di); ayat and afaq
(al-Qurtubi, al-Jawhari's Sihah); three lines on the horizons and the
self: conquests and Makkah (al-Minhal, as-Suddi, Mujahid, al-Hasan),
past nations and Badr (Ibn 'Abbas, Qatada, ad-Dahhak), heavens, earth
and body ('Ata', Ibn Zayd, al-Qurtubi's list); Ibn Kathir's anatomy
possibility and the Ibn Abi ad-Dunya verse; the four referents of
annahu al-haqq (al-Qurtubi, al-Baghawi); the closing question
(at-Tabari, Ibn Kathir with 4:166, Muyassar, as-Sa'di, Muqatil,
az-Zajjaj, al-Qurtubi on shahid).

Disagreements kept: what the horizons and the self name, three lines,
with at-Tabari's preference for as-Suddi's reading reported as his;
what al-haqq refers to. Ma'arif's "covers all new discoveries of
science" attributed to Ma'arif and set against the older tafsirs.
Licensing sentence in both languages.

Hadith: none attached by any fetched tafsir (stated).

Dropped: the old article's two-readings framing (the sources give
three), its unsourced cross-references (2:164, 3:190-191, 10:101,
51:20-21) and its "two books" line; al-Qurtubi's minor qila glosses
and the annahu grammar, for length.
