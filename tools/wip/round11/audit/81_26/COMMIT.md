Replaces the 639-word, 6-section article with a 1,761-word, 8-section one.

Sources: at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi, Ibn Kathir, al-Baghawi, as-Sa'di, the Muyassar
on 81:26; Ma'arif al-Qur'an for 81:19-25; the same five on 81:27-29 (slots
fetched and checked for this pass).
- Wording: fa as the consequence of 81:19-25; at-Tabari and al-Qurtubi (from
  al-Farra') on ayna without ila, "dhahabtu ash-Sham", Banu 'Uqayl verse.
- Glosses: Qatada (via at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi, Ibn Kathir) "from My Book and
  My obedience"; al-Baghawi "healing and clarity"; az-Zajjaj's "which road is
  plainer"; al-Junayd's link to 15:21 as al-Qurtubi gives it, unexplained.
- Ibn Kathir and the Muyassar read it as the mind gone astray; Abu Bakr's
  remark to Banu Hanifa on Musaylima, flagged as a Companion's saying with no
  chain; as-Sa'di's "inversion of realities".
- 81:27-29 in one section: al-Qurtubi's two readings of huwa (kept as two);
  at-Tabari's lam of substitution; Mujahid; the Abu Jahl report on 81:29 via
  Sulayman ibn Musa (at-Tabari, Ibn Kathir; al-Qurtubi adds Abu Hurayra),
  ungraded; the will in Ibn Kathir's, al-Baghawi's, al-Qurtubi's and
  as-Sa'di's own words.
- Licensing sentence for the deniers in both languages.
Hadith: none attached by any fetched tafsir; said in one sentence.
Dropped: the old article's unsourced "said to a traveller off the road",
plural-vs-singular and "stopping device" reflections; al-Qurtubi's Qadariyya
aside on Abu Jahl; Wahb's "87 books" saying.
