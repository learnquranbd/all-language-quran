Card and deep bilingual article on 17:1, the Isrāʾ (night journey
from al-Masjid al-Ḥarām to al-Masjid al-Aqṣā).

Sources gave: the opening tasbīḥ Subḥān as God glorifying His own
unmatched power (Ibn Kathir, Muyassar); asrā + indefinite lailan =
part of the night, a vast distance covered and returned in it
(Ma'arif, Ibn Kathir, as-Saʿdī); bi-ʿabdihi as the summit-title of
servitude — as-Saʿdī's three stations (Isrāʾ, revelation, the
challenge), Ma'arif's unique bond of love and the ʿabd that guards
against divinity (cf. 25:63); the two named mosques and the blessing
around al-Aqṣā (trees, rivers, fertility; Syria/Palestine, religious
and worldly — as-Saʿdī, Ibn Kathir, Ma'arif); closing on as-Samīʿ
al-Baṣīr (Muyassar).

Disagreement kept, no verdict: the soul/true-vision view (Muʿāwiyah,
ʿĀʾishah; also al-Ḥasan, Ibn Isḥāq — Qurtubi, Tabari) vs the
bodily-and-awake view argued from bi-ʿabdihi and Subḥān (Qurtubi,
the majority per Ma'arif); Tabari leaves it with God.

Hadith: Abū Dharr on the two mosques and the forty years between
them, quoted whole from Ṣaḥīḥ al-Bukhārī 3366 (also Muslim 520);
its closing line that the whole earth is a place of prayer.

Dropped: all Miʿrāj/ascension detail (kept to the Isrāʾ per scope),
the 25-Companion count, the disputed date, and 17:60/37:102 as
proof-texts. Ibn ʿAbbās left out of the vision-view names since the
clause I can cite names only Muʿāwiyah and ʿĀʾishah.

Scope guarded: nothing preempts 17:2 onward (Mūsā, Banī Isrāʾīl).
Gate clean; 1492 EN words, 8 sections.
