# SOURCES ledger — 17:1 (al-Isrāʾ)

## Tafsir fetched (all pre-fetched in audit/17_1/)
- **Ibn Kathir (14 ar / 169 en)** — NAMED. Clauses relied on (en file):
  - "Allah glorifies Himself, for His ability to do that which none but He can do" → Subḥān / §1.
  - "لَيْلاً (by Night) means, in the depths of the night" → §2.
  - "بَارَكْنَا حَوْلَهُ … its agricultural produce and fruits are blessed" → §4.
  - "لِنُرِيَهُ … great signs" → §7.
  - "The truth is that the Prophet was taken on the Night Journey when he was awake, not in a dream" → background for the bodily-awake side (§6).
- **at-Tabari (15)** — NAMED. Ar clauses: report 16628 "أن معاوية بن أبي سفيان … كانت رؤيا من الله صادقة"; 16629 "أن عائشة كانت تقول: ما فقد جسد رسول الله … ولكن الله أسرى بروحه"; al-Ḥasan / Ibn Isḥāq did not reject it (citing 17:60, 37:102, "تنام عيني وقلبي يقظان"); Tabari's own close "فالله أعلم أي ذلك كان". Used in §6 (vision-view names + Tabari leaves it to God).
- **al-Qurṭubī (90)** — NAMED. Ar clause on the dispute: "اختلف … السلف والخلف، فذهبت طائفة إلى أنه إسراء بالروح، ولم يفارق شخصه مضجعه، وأنها كانت رؤيا … ذهب إلى هذا معاوية وعائشة، وحكي عن الحسن وابن إسحاق. وقالت طائفة: كان الإسراء بالجسد يقظة …"; his argument "ولو كان مناما لقال بروح عبده ولم يقل بعبده"; Subḥān + "ولا فضل أبو بكر بالتصديق"; Umm Hāniʾ "لا تحدث الناس فيكذبوك". Used §6 (both sides + linguistic argument).
- **as-Saʿdī (91)** — NAMED. Ar clauses: "أَسْرَى بِعَبْدِهِ … من المسجد الحرام … أجل المساجد على الإطلاق … إلى المسجد الأقصى … محل الأنبياء"; "أسري به في ليلة واحدة إلى مسافة بعيدة جدا ورجع في ليلته"; "ذكره هنا وفي مقام الإنزال للقرآن ومقام التحدي بصفة العبودية لأنه نال هذه المقامات الكبار بتكميله لعبودية ربه" (the three stations, §3); "بَارَكْنَا حَوْلَهُ أي: بكثرة الأشجار والأنهار والخصب الدائم" + preference over other mosques + reward of travelling to it (§4); "أراه الله من آياته ما ازداد به هدى وبصيرة وثباتا وفرقانا" (§7). Used §2,§3,§4,§7,§8.
- **al-Muyassar (16)** — NAMED. Ar: "يمجِّد الله نفسه ويعظم شأنه، لقدرته على ما لا يقدر عليه أحد سواه" (§1); "السميع لجميع الأصوات، البصير بكل مُبْصَر، فيعطي كُلا ما يستحقه في الدنيا والآخرة" (§7). Also affirms body+soul, awake (bi-jasadihi wa rūḥihi yaqẓatan).
- **Ma'arif al-Qur'an (168, en)** — NAMED. Clauses: Isrāʾ (this verse) vs Miʿrāj (an-Najm + mutawātir hadith) distinction; "lailan … a part of the night, not the whole night"; "bi'abdihi … the highest honour any man could ever have," unique bond of love, guards against divinity (cf. 25:63 ʿibād ar-Raḥmān), ʿabd = body+spirit; "the position of the majority" that it was physical; ḥawl = whole land of Syria/Palestine, religious + worldly blessings. Used §1,§2,§3,§4,§6.
- **al-Baghawi (94 ar)** — fetched, NOT named (its material duplicated what Ibn Kathir/Qurtubi already carried).

## Asbāb an-nuzūl
- Not used. The Isrāʾ has no discrete "cause of revelation"; its placement (the sūrah opening on the night journey) is what the tafsir gives. Ma'arif and Qurtubi discuss the *date* of the event (disputed: Musā b. ʿUqbah 6 months before Hijrah; al-Ḥarbī 27 Rabīʿ ath-Thānī; etc., "not followed by any decisive statement") — dropped as disputed, not written into the article.

## Dropped (could not confirm cleanly / out of scope)
- **Miʿrāj / ascension detail** (Buraq into the heavens, the prophets on each heaven, the fifty→five prayers, Sidrat al-Muntahā): present in Ma'arif's and Ibn Kathir's discussion and in Bukhari 3887, but the watch-point bounds this verse to the Isrāʾ; deliberately not narrated.
- **Ibn Kathir's count of 25 Companions / Naqqāsh's 20** who narrate the Isrāʾ: true in Ma'arif but a number-parity risk and not load-bearing; dropped.
- **Ibn ʿAbbās** as a holder of the "dream" view (Ma'arif) — Ma'arif pairs him with ʿĀʾishah, but the Arabic tafsirs I could point a clause at name Muʿāwiyah + ʿĀʾishah (Tabari/Qurtubi), so the article names only those two plus al-Ḥasan/Ibn Isḥāq, which I can cite verbatim.
- **17:60 (ar-ruʾyā) and 37:102** as the vision-party's proof-texts (in Tabari): dropped from the article to keep ref-parity lean; the position is still attributed correctly to its holders.
