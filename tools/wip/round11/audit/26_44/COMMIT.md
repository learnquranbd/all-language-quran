Card and deep bilingual article on 26:44, the magicians' oath
"by the might of Pharaoh, indeed we are the victors" — confidence
built on the wrong foundation, sworn in the name of a mortal tyrant
moments before it is overturned.

Sources (all confirmed against fetched tafsir):
- at-Tabari: bi-'izzati Fir'awn = an oath by Pharaoh's strength, the
  severity of his authority, the invincibility of his kingdom; the
  unspoken object of al-ghalibun is "over Musa."
- as-Sa'di (spine): they sought aid in the might of a weak, powerless
  servant who had only made himself a tyrant with the outward form of
  kingship; the pomp deluded them and their insight never reached the
  reality; or the words are simply an oath, the thing sworn their victory.
- Ma'arif al-Qur'an (with Ruh): swearing by other than Allah is not
  permitted; a true oath by a creature is as sinful as a false oath in
  Allah's name. Everyday parallels (by the king / your head / your father's grave).
- Ibn Kathir: the "ignorant masses" reflex ("by the virtue of so-and-so");
  the illusion of moving snakes (7:116, 20:69); truth against falsehood
  (21:18, 17:81); the 26:45 staff that swallowed what they faked.
- al-Muyassar: the staffs made to appear as snakes darting.

Hadith: Sahih al-Bukhari 6646 (Ibn Umar) — "Allah forbids you to swear
by your fathers; whoever must take an oath, let him swear by Allah or
keep silent." Quoted whole; sahih, not upgraded. Tirmidhi 1535 (hasan)
fetched but not used.

Dropped: al-Qurtubi (90) — no tafsir on this verse ("لا يوجد تفسير
لهذه الأية"), despite the watch-point naming it; al-Baghawi (94) — verse
text only, no commentary. Substituted with as-Sa'di / Ma'arif / al-Muyassar.
No verse number asserted for "'izzah belongs to Allah" (not fetched);
contrast grounded in as-Sa'di alone. No asbab; placement used instead.

Standing rule carried in both languages: the verse describes a tyrant's
court and licenses nothing against any living person or community. The
magicians' later belief is not narrated; only the 26:45 reversal is bridged.
