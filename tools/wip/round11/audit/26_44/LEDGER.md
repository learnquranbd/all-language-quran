# SOURCES ledger — 26:44

فَأَلْقَوْا۟ حِبَالَهُمْ وَعِصِيَّهُمْ وَقَالُوا۟ بِعِزَّةِ فِرْعَوْنَ إِنَّا لَنَحْنُ ٱلْغَٰلِبُونَ

## Tafsir fetched (all in audit/26_44/, from PREP.md — not re-fetched)

- **at-Tabari (15)** — NAMED. Rests on: "( وَقَالُوا بِعِزَّةِ فِرْعَوْنَ ) يقول: أقسموا بقوّة
  فرعون وشدّة سلطانه, ومنعة مملكته" → they swore by Pharaoh's strength, the severity
  of his authority, the invincibility of his kingdom. And "( إِنَّا لَنَحْنُ الْغَالِبُونَ ) موسى"
  → victors *over Musa* (the unspoken object). Used in §1.
- **as-Sa'di (91)** — NAMED (spine). Rests on: "فاستعانوا بعزة عبد ضعيف, عاجز من كل
  وجه, إلا أنه قد تجبر, وحصل له صورة ملك وجنود، فغرتهم تلك الأبهة, ولم تنفذ بصائرهم
  إلى حقيقة الأمر، أو أن هذا قسم منهم بعزة فرعون والمقسم عليه, أنهم غالبون." → sought
  aid in the might of a weak, powerless servant who had only made himself a tyrant and
  gained the outward form of kingship/soldiers; the pomp deluded them; their insight did
  not reach the reality; OR it is simply an oath, the thing sworn being their victory.
  Also "فإذا هي حيات تسعى, وسحروا بذلك أعين الناس" (snakes; bewitched eyes). Used §2, §6, §8.
- **al-Muyassar (16)** — NAMED (minor). Rests on: "وخُيِّل للناس أنها حيَّات تسعى" → made
  to appear to people as snakes darting. Used §6.
- **Ma'arif al-Qur'an (168, en)** — NAMED. Rests on: "This was an expression of pledge…
  It is not permitted to make an oath of this nature under the Islamic law (because
  swearing in the name of someone other than Allah is a sin)… swearing a true oath in
  this way is as much sinful as swearing a false oath in the name of Allah. (Ruh)."
  Used §3 (the everyday-oaths list) and §4 (the ruling; Ruh cited as Ma'arif cites it).
- **Ibn Kathir (14 ar / 169 en)** — NAMED. Rests on: (ar) "وهذا كما تقول الجهلة من العوام
  إذا فعلوا شيئا هذا بثواب فلان" → the ignorant masses' "by the virtue of so-and-so";
  cross-refs 7:116 (سحروا أعين الناس واسترهبوهم), Ta-Ha 20:69 (يخيل إليه… أنها تسعى /
  ولا يفلح الساحر). (en 169) the crowd "followed the religion of their king"; number
  "known to Allah Alone"; truth-vs-falsehood via 21:18 and 17:81; 26:45 staff "swallowed
  up all that they falsely showed." Used §1, §3, §6.

## Hadith

- **Sahih al-Bukhari 6646** — USED (§5). URL fetched:
  https://quranx.com/hadith/Bukhari/DarusSalam/Hadith-6646 (via tools/wip/round11/hadith.js).
  First ten words (EN): "Allah's Messenger met Umar bin Al-Khattab while the latter was".
  Wording quoted whole from this one collection. Grading: in Sahih al-Bukhari (sahih);
  no separate grade given, none upgraded. Chosen because it is the sound, on-point
  parallel to swearing "by the might of Pharaoh" — an oath by a creature turned back to God.
- **Jami' at-Tirmidhi 1535** — fetched, NOT used. URL: https://quranx.com/hadith/Tirmidhi/Hadith-1535.
  "Whoever swears by other than Allah has committed disbelief or shirk." Graded *hasan*
  by Abu Isa (at-Tirmidhi) in the text. Left out only to keep one collection's wording;
  the Bukhari report is stronger and covers the point.

## Standing-rule note

§7 carries, in both languages, that the verse describes what its words describe (magicians
in a tyrant's court on the losing side) and licenses nothing against any living person or
community. The magicians' later belief (26:46-51) is NOT narrated; §6 bridges only to the
26:45 reversal per watch-point #3.
