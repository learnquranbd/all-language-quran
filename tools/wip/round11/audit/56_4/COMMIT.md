Card and 9-section bilingual article (1,661 EN words) on 56:4: idha
rujjati al-ardu rajja. 56:1-3 as frame; 56:5-6 and 56:7 pointed to only.
No shipped articles in surah 56 to overlap.

Sources: at-Tabari, Ibn Kathir (ar), al-Qurtubi, al-Baghawi, as-Sa'di,
al-Muyassar (group 56:4-6, 56:4 clause only), Ibn Kathir en (group
56:1-10, 56:4 passage only). Ma'arif (group 56:3-6) speaks to 56:3 only,
unused.
- rajj: root = tahrik (al-Baghawi); rajjahu yarujjuhu, naqa rajja'
  (al-Qurtubi, no link drawn); the arrow yartajju in the target
  (at-Tabari); hurrikat wa-dtarabat (as-Sa'di).
- Glosses with chains per at-Tabari: Ibn Abbas (zalzalaha), Mujahid
  (zulzilat), Qatada (two transmissions); Ibn Kathir's group line with
  bracketed shadidan; Ibn Abbas "violent motion with a sound" (al-Qurtubi).
- Images: length and breadth (Ibn Kathir); sieve (ar-Rabi' ibn Anas);
  cradle, "the commentators said" (al-Qurtubi, al-Baghawi, one-word
  difference noted); al-Kalbi: earth convulses in fear when Allah
  reveals to it.
- Syntax of idha kept as al-Qurtubi lists it, no position: badal of
  56:1; governed by khafidatun rafi'ah; "befalls when" (az-Zajjaj,
  al-Jurjani); implied "remember". No other source addresses it.
- rajjan: masdar indicating repeated quaking (al-Qurtubi); not called
  emphasis.
- Parallels 99:1 and 22:1 only, as Ibn Kathir cites them.
Hadith: none attaches to 56:4. al-Qurtubi's sea-travel narration
(lexical) and Ibn Kathir's surah-level report noted as not attached,
not quoted. Asbab: none.
Dropped: emphasis reading of the masdar; 73:14/79:6-7/89:21/69:15;
earthquake science and sign-of-the-times readings (disclaimer in §9).
