Card + 8-section bilingual article (1,714 EN words) for 36:47, the
disbelievers' reply "shall we feed one whom Allah, had He willed, would
have fed?"

Sources: at-Tabari, Ibn Kathir, al-Baghawi, al-Qurtubi, as-Sa'di,
al-Muyassar, Ma'arif al-Qur'an, each quoted from the text fetched for 36:47.
- Frame: majority (Tabari, Baghawi, Ma'arif) = Makkan mushrikin; Qurtubi's
  minority readings named as such: Jews (al-Hasan), zanadiqa of Makkah
  (Ibn Abbas), zanadiqa (al-Qushayri, al-Mawardi).
- Core: Qurtubi "true in the words, lying in the argument" with his 6:148
  and 63:1 parallels; Sa'di "the divine will is never a proof for the
  disobedient"; Qurtubi's "as if He took that portion back" rebuttal;
  Baghawi on misers and wealth/poverty as trial; Ma'arif on provision
  delivered through people.
- Disagreement kept, named: speaker of "in antum illa fi dalalin mubin":
  Tabari two wujuh (disbelievers, preferred / Allah); Ibn Kathir finds the
  second open to question; Qurtubi three voices (disbelievers per Muqatil,
  the Companions, Allah). The Companions option is Qurtubi's, not Tabari's.
- Qurtubi's chainless Abu Bakr / Abu Jahl report used as a tafsir
  narrative only, not hadith, not established asbab.
- Hadith: Sahih al-Bukhari 1442 (two angels: replacement for the spender,
  loss for the withholder), flagged as general, not attached to the verse.
  Stock Bukhari 660 deliberately not used.
- Licensing: verse describes one condemned posture and licenses nothing
  against any living person or community (both languages).
- Dropped: Qurtubi's 92:5 add-on to the Abu Bakr report; 36:49-53 kept
  for later.
