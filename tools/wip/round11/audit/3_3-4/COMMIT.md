Assigned 3:3, widened to 3:3-4 (the Book sent down with the truth,
confirming what came before; the Torah and the Injil; al-Furqan; the
warning). 3:1-2 and 3:5 left free. New card and a 1,745-word, 8-section
article centred on the Book as continuity and as criterion.

Sources: at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi, Ibn Kathir (ar + en abridged), as-Sa'di,
al-Baghawi, al-Muyassar, Ma'arif on 3:3 and 3:4 (all slots on target).
- 3:2's Names kept to one paragraph (2:255 ships them); as-Sa'di's link
  from al-Qayyum to the sending of the Book.
- Najran given only as the abridged English Ibn Kathir and at-Tabari
  report it, called a backdrop; licensing sentence in both languages.
- nazzala/anzala (al-Qurtubi, al-Baghawi); bil-haqq glosses; musaddiq as
  two-way confirmation (Ibn Kathir), one Sender (at-Tabari, 4:82), fields
  of agreement incl. "some of the laws" (al-Baghawi), muzakki (as-Sa'di),
  original books vs present Testaments (Ma'arif).
Disagreements kept: al-Furqan as the Qur'an (Qatada, ar-Rabi',
al-Qurtubi), the separating proof (Muhammad ibn Ja'far ibn az-Zubayr,
preferred by at-Tabari) or the Torah (Abu Salih, weak per Ibn Kathir);
hudan lin-nas in their time (Ibn Kathir), all three Books (as-Sa'di),
as-Suddi's reordering, the God-fearing (Ibn Furak); Mujahid vs Qatada on
what is confirmed; Injil etymologies.
Hadith: none attached to the verses, said so. Muslim 223 (Abu Malik, "the
Qur'an is a proof for you or against you"), closing lines of a longer
hadith, marked general.
Dropped: Greatest Name hadith (3:1-2's, no wording given), al-Qurtubi's
unsourced munajat report, Ibn Kathir (en)'s "632 CE", at-Tabari's
emended "na'tika" clause, Bukhari 4485 (not fetched).
