## Disagreements kept (no position taken)
- al-Furqan: the Qur'an (Qatada, ar-Rabi' via at-Tabari and Ibn Kathir; al-Qurtubi) / the separating proof in the dispute (Muhammad ibn Ja'far ibn az-Zubayr, preferred by at-Tabari, with his reason) / the Torah (Abu Salih via Ibn Abi Hatim, called weak by Ibn Kathir, with his reason). Shared root sense from al-Baghawi, Muyassar, Ibn Kathir, as-Sa'di.
- hudan lin-nas: in their time (Ibn Kathir) / all three Books incl. the Qur'an (as-Sa'di) / reordered to include al-Furqan (as-Suddi via al-Baghawi) / the God-fearing (Ibn Furak via al-Qurtubi).
- musaddiq: book or messenger (Mujahid; ar-Rabi' "book and messenger") vs books (Qatada); fixed vs shifting hal (al-Qurtubi: majority vs some).
- Injil etymology: origin (al-Qurtubi's first) vs bringing out effaced truth (al-Baghawi; also in al-Qurtubi); non-Arabic view (both).
