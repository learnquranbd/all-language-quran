Card and 8-section bilingual article on fa-ummuhu hawiya, two words
(fa + umm + hu, then hawiya); 101:8 named as the verse it answers,
101:10-11 as its explanation. 101:5 and 101:6-8 pointed to, not treated.

Sources: at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi, al-Baghawi, Ibn Kathir (ar + en
abridged), as-Sa'di, Muyassar. Slot check clean; Ibn Kathir 101:10,
al-Qurtubi 101:11 and at-Tabari 101:10-11 slots fetched and saved for
the al-Ash'ath report and the context verses.

Kept as disagreements, attributed, none chosen:
- ummuhu as shelter/abode (Ibn Zayd, Ibn 'Abbas via at-Tabari, Ibn
  Jarir, al-Baghawi, as-Sa'di with 25:65, Qatada, Muyassar) vs umm
  ra'sihi, falling headlong (Ibn 'Abbas, 'Ikrimah, Abu Salih, Qatadah
  per Ibn Kathir; al-Baghawi, as-Sa'di as qila). Names on both sides
  noted; al-Akhfash "his settling place, the meaning is close".
- hawat ummuhu as an Arab saying (Qatadah; al-Qurtubi: thakila, with
  Ka'b b. Sa'd al-Ghanawi); Umayya b. Abi as-Salt line for "mother".
Hawiya: a name of the Fire, the drop whose bottom is not reached;
al-Qurtubi's unsourced "lowest gate" reported as such.

Hadith: none; no fetched tafsir attaches one with collection and
grading (stated). Al-Ash'ath's report given as his own words; Ibn
Kathir's Ibn Marduyah/Anas note and al-Qurtubi's unsourced Abu Hurayrah
khabar mentioned, not quoted. Dropped: Nasa'i 1833 (not cited by a
tafsir, long, garbled English), seventy-parts hadith (101:11), Ma'arif
two-weighings view and Abu Bakr on the scales (101:6-8).
Licensing sentence in both languages; no verdict on anyone's fate; the
"mother" figure reported only as the tafsirs explain it.
