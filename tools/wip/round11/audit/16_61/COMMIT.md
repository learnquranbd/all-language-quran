Card and bilingual article (9 sections, 1,782 EN words) for "if Allah
took people for their wrongdoing He would not leave a single creature on
it, but He defers them to a named term". One pointer to 15:4; 7:34, 10:49
and 35:45 not repeated.

Sources: at-Tabari (disobedient of Bani Adam; bi-hilmihi; term = time set
for their destruction; Ibn Mas'ud dung-beetle reports by three chains;
Abu Hurayra's bustard with chain via Abu Salama; az-Zuhri on terms);
Ibn Kathir (hilm and satr; animals destroyed tab'an after Bani Adam);
al-Qurtubi ('alayha = earth by allusion; three readings of dabba incl.
al-Hasan's general reading; Ibn Mas'ud with withheld rain and 42:30;
sa'a the smallest time-name; the slain dies at his term vs the
Mu'tazila, qisas for the transgression; the blameless compensated in the
Hereafter); al-Baghawi (Qatada on Nuh's flood; lineage reading; Abu
Hurayra "it is narrated"); as-Sa'di (placement after the fabrications;
"without increase or decrease"; shu'm of sins ruins tillage and offspring;
term = Day of Resurrection; beware within the respite); the Muyassar.
Ibn Kathir abridged grouped 16:61-62 and Ma'arif 16:60-65; said so, and
Ma'arif's passage has nothing on 16:61.

Disagreements kept: named term as end of lifespans vs Day of Resurrection
(al-Qurtubi gives both); three readings of dabba. No position.

Hadith: Muslim 2879 (Ibn Umar: punishment reaches all, raised by their
deeds), quoted whole, marked general and not attached. No sound hadith
attached to the verse; said so. Abu ad-Darda marfu' (Ibn Kathir, ungraded)
and Umm Salama report dropped. Companion sayings reported without grading.
No asbab. Licensing sentence in both languages; no modern events named.
