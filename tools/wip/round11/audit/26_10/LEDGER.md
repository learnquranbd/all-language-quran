# SOURCES ledger — 26:10

## Tafsir fetched (PREP.md, not re-fetched)

- **at-Tabari (15)** — USED. Arabic clause: «يعني الكافرين قوم فرعون, ونصب القوم
  الثاني ترجمة عن القوم الأوّل» — grounds §3.1: the wrongdoing people = the
  disbelievers, the people of Pharaoh; and the grammar note that "qawm Firʿawn"
  (26:11) is accusative as an explication (tarjama) of "al-qawm al-ẓālimīn"
  (26:10). Also «واذكر يا محمد إذ نادى ربك موسى» supports the "remember, O
  Muhammad / recited to the Prophet" framing.
- **al-Qurtubi (90)** — USED. Clauses: «"إذ" في موضع نصب ; المعنى: واتل عليهم إذ
  نادى ربك موسى ... ذكره النحاس» (recite-to-them reading via an-Nahhas, §2.1) and
  «وقيل: المعنى: واذكر إذ نادى كما صرح به في قوله: واذكر أخا عاد ... واذكر في
  الكتاب مريم» (the "remember" reading, §2.2); «والنداء: الدعاء بـ"يا فلان" أي قال
  ربك يا موسى» (nidāʾ = address by name, §1.1 and §8.1). The 26:69 cross-ref
  «واتل عليهم نبأ إبراهيم» is quoted in his text.
- **as-Saʿdi (91)** — USED. Clauses: «أعاد الباري ... قصة موسى وثناها في القرآن,
  ما لم يثن غيرها, لكونها مشتملة على حكم عظيمة, وعبر ... وهو صاحب الشريعة الكبرى,
  وصاحب التوراة أفضل الكتب بعد القرآن» (§5.1); «الذين تكبروا في الأرض, وعلوا على
  أهلها وادعى كبيرهم الربوبية» (§3.3, the arrogance and Pharaoh's claim to
  lordship).
- **al-Baghawi (94)** — USED. Clauses: «حين رأى الشجرة والنار» (the tree and the
  fire, §1.2); «الذين ظلموا أنفسهم بالكفر والمعصية، وظلموا بني إسرائيل باستعبادهم
  وسومهم سوء العذاب» (the two-fold ẓulm — against themselves by kufr, against
  Israel by enslavement, §3.2).
- **Ibn Kathir (14, ar)** — USED. Clause: «حين ناداه من جانب الطور الأيمن ، وكلمه
  وناجاه ، وأرسله واصطفاه ، وأمره بالذهاب إلى فرعون وملئه» — grounds §1.1 (called
  from the right side of the Ṭūr, spoke with and conversed privately, chose, sent).
- **Ibn Kathir (169, en abridged)** — READ, corroborates §1.1 / §5.2 wording
  ("called him from the right side of the mountain… chose him, sent him, and
  commanded him to go to Firʿawn and his people"). Its material on 26:12-19 (his
  fear, Harun, the killing, "Kalla") was deliberately NOT used — the brief targets
  26:12 and 26:22 separately and says not to pre-empt them. §5.2 references the
  following verses' reassurance only in passing, without detailing his fear.
- **al-Muyassar (16)** — USED (covers 26:10-11). Clause: «أن ائت القوم الظالمين،
  قوم فرعون، وقل لهم: ألا يخافون عقاب الله تعالى، ويتركون ما هم عليه من الكفر
  والضلال؟» — grounds §3.3 (identification) and §6.1 (the mission announced as the
  question "will they not fear God's punishment and leave their kufr/ḍalāl").
- **Maʿarif al-Qurʾan (168, en)** — NOT USED. The fetched text (covers 26:7-11)
  discusses only zawj karīm at 26:7; it says nothing about 26:10. Dropped.

## Hadith

- **Jāmiʿ at-Tirmidhī 2174** — USED in §7.1, wording quoted whole:
  "Indeed, among the greatest types of jihad is a just statement before a
  tyrannical ruler." (Abū Saʿīd al-Khudrī.)
  URL fetched: https://quranx.com/hadith/Tirmidhi/Hadith-2174
  First ten words (EN): "Indeed, among the greatest types of jihad is a just".
  Grading: at-Tirmidhī's own words «هَذَا حَدِيثٌ حَسَنٌ غَرِيبٌ مِنْ هَذَا
  الْوَجْهِ» → reported as ḥasan gharīb, not upgraded.
  Abu Dawud 4344 (https://quranx.com/hadith/AbuDawud/DarusSalam/Hadith-4344) was
  also fetched for the same report ("The best fighting… a word of justice to an
  oppressive ruler"); the Tirmidhī wording was chosen because it carries the
  collector's explicit grading. Only one collection's wording is quoted.
  **Attachment:** marked in-text (§7.1) as NOT attached to 26:10 by any mufassir
  consulted — a general narration brought for thematic kinship, not as tafsir.

## Asbab an-nuzul

- Not used. No sabab is established for 26:10 in the fetched tafsir; the verse's
  placement (the opening of the Mūsā narrative of ash-Shuʿarāʾ) is given instead
  via §2 and §5.

## Dropped / not written

- Maʿarif al-Qurʾan (168): nothing on 26:10 in fetched text — dropped.
- Ibn Kathir's 26:12-19 material (fear, Harun, the slain Egyptian, "Kalla"):
  withheld to avoid pre-empting the 26:12 and 26:22 targets.
- Exact verse numbers for al-Qurtubi's other "wa-dhkur" cross-refs (akhā ʿĀd,
  ʿibādanā Ibrāhīm, fī l-kitāb Maryam): named descriptively without numbers to
  avoid citing numbers not present in the fetched text; only 26:69, which he
  quotes as his evidence, is given a reference.

## Licenses-nothing sentence

Carried in §4.1 both languages: the verse describes Pharaoh's court and the wrong
recorded against it and "licenses nothing against any living person or
community"; §4.2 reinforces that even this regime is addressed as capable of
taqwā.
