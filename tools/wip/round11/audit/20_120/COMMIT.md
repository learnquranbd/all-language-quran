Card and bilingual article on Iblis's whisper to Adam.

The verse: Satan's waswasa baits Adam with two lies — shajarat al-khuld,
the tree of eternity, and mulk la yabla, a kingdom that never decays.

Sources (all fetched for this verse):
- at-Tabari: the whisper cast in and spoken (alqa ... wa haddathahu);
  eat and "you will not die, and hold a dominion that does not run out";
  as-Suddi's harder reading, "a king like God, or among the deathless".
- al-Muyassar, al-Baghawi, as-Sa'di: gloss khuld (deathless) and la yabla
  (does not perish/end); as-Sa'di gives the disguise — Satan came "in the
  guise of a sincere adviser", gentle in speech, so Adam was deceived.
- al-Qurtubi: the wording shows mushafaha, direct face-to-face address.
- Ibn Kathir (ar+en): cross-refs al-A'raf 7:21-22 (the oath of goodwill,
  the deception); Ibn Abbas on insan/nasiya, Mujahid & al-Hasan on "forgot".
- Ma'arif al-Qur'an: the honour of Adam — prophets' 'isma, the act "did not
  constitute a sin", Ibn al-'Arabi's ruling that we may not use the word of
  Adam except as scripture does, and ghawa read as "deprived" (Qushairi,
  Qurtubi). The article never calls Adam a sinner in its own voice.

Hadith: Sahih al-Bukhari 3409 (Abu Hurayra), the Adam-Moses dispute,
quoted whole; graded Sahih, not upgraded.

Dropped: the Tayalisi/Ahmad "tree of eternity" narration (ungraded, and a
different tree) and the Ubayy b. Ka'b report (Ibn Kathir flags a broken
chain). Frames the slip as forgetting (20:115), points toward the mercy of
20:121-122 without expounding it. Not a condemned-group verse; no licence line.
