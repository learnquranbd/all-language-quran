- t91 as-Sa'di — used. Clauses: نهاه ... وَلَا تَقْرَبَا هَذِهِ الشَّجَرَةَ; شجرة الخلد أي: التي من أكل منها خلد في الجنة; وَمُلْكٍ لَا يَبْلَى أي: لا ينقطع; فأتاه بصورة ناصح، وتلطف له في الكلام، فاغتر به آدم. (§2, §3, §4)
- t94 al-Baghawi — used. Clauses: إن أكلت منها بقيت مخلدا; وملك لا يبلى: لا يبيد ولا يفنى. (§2, §3)
- t168 Ma'arif al-Qur'an (en, covers 120-122) — used, the core of §6. Prophets protected from all sin; Adam's act "did not constitute a sin" under divine law but the minor lapse named 'iṣyān; Ibn al-'Arabi (Ahkam al-Qur'an) ruling that we may not use the word of Adam except where it occurs in the verse or a hadith; ghawā = "to err" or "to be deprived", al-Qushairi & al-Qurtubi taking the second (deprived of the Garden's ease). (§5, §6)
- t169 Ibn Kathir (en abridged, covers 115-122) — used. Ibn Abbas: man = insan because given a covenant then forgot (nasiya); Mujahid & al-Hasan: forgot = abandoned. Also carried the Musa-Adam hadith and the Ubayy b. Ka'b narration (see below). (§5, §7)

## Hadith
- USED: Sahih al-Bukhari, Dar-us-Salam Hadith 3409 (Abu Hurayra), the Adam–Moses dispute.
  URL: https://quranx.com/hadith/Bukhari/DarusSalam/Hadith-3409 (fetched via tools/wip/round11/hadith.js; saved in this dir).
  First ten words (EN used): "Adam and Moses argued with each other. Moses said to Adam".
  Grading: Sahih (Sahih al-Bukhari). Ibn Kathir (t169) notes it has several routes in the two Sahihs. Not upgraded. One collection's wording quoted whole (Bukhari), Muslim 2652 variants not blended in.
- DROPPED (noted, not used as an article proof): the "Tree of Eternity" narration Ibn Kathir cites on this verse (Abu Dawud at-Tayalisi / Ahmad, from Abu Hurayra: a tree a rider travels under 100 years, "it is the tree of eternity"). Ibn Kathir gives no grading; it describes a tree in Paradise, not the forbidden tree. Left out to avoid an ungraded citation.
- DROPPED: the Ubayy b. Ka'b narration in t169 (Adam's hair caught, "are you fleeing from Me"). Ibn Kathir himself flags a break in the chain (al-Hasan did not hear from Ubayy) and questions its attribution. Not used.
