Card and 8-section bilingual article for 45:12: Allahu alladhi
sakhkhara lakumu l-bahra li-tajriya l-fulku fihi bi-amrihi wa-li-tabtaghu
min fadlihi wa-la'allakum tashkurun. Placed after the warnings of
45:7-11; 45:13 gets one pointer only.

Sources: Tabari, Qurtubi, Sa'di, Ibn Kathir (ar + en heading only),
Muyassar, Ma'arif (45:12 paragraph only). Baghawi: verse quoted, no
comment, said so.
- Opening: Tabari's "Allah, to whom alone godhood is fitting".
- Bi-amrihi: Ibn Kathir "He commanded the sea to carry them"; Sa'di
  "by His command and His taysir".
- Fadl framing: Sa'di (fadl, ihsan) and Qurtubi's one sentence
  (power, favour, created for their benefit).
- Seeking: trades and earnings (Ibn Kathir, Muyassar, Sa'di);
  livelihoods and moving through the lands (Tabari).
- Ma'arif's three senses kept unranked; its science remark reported
  as its own, not asserted.
- Gratitude: goods from far regions (Ibn Kathir); worship and obedience
  (Tabari, Muyassar's "alone"); increase and reward (Sa'di). Kept side
  by side, no position.
Hadith: none attached by any tafsir; said in one sentence.
Asbab: none in the sources; not used.
Licensing sentence for the 45:7-9 figure in both languages.
Dropped: parallels (none cited by any tafsir), fishing/pearls/storms,
general sakhkhara (45:13), wind and la'alla analysis (30:46), Ma'arif's
kept-command mercy (22:65).
