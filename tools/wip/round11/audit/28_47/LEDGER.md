## Arabic/English clause each attribution rests on

- **at-Tabari (15)** — muṣība = punishment: "والمصيبة في هذا الموضع: العذاب والنقمة". "bimā qaddamat aydīhim" = what they earned: "بما اكتسبوا". Closes with the excuse-removal principle: "لئلا يكون للناس على الله حجة بعد الرسل". Paraphrase of the whole hanging sentence ("ولولا أن يقول هؤلاء... ربنا هلا أرسلت... لعاجلناهم العقوبة").
- **al-Qurtubi (90)** — apodosis elided: "وجواب ( لولا ) محذوف"; supplied answer "وقيل: لعاجلناهم بالعقوبة". Hands singled out: "وخص الأيدي بالذكر؛ لأن الغالب من الكسب إنما يقع بها". Referent "them": "يريد قريشا. وقيل: اليهود". fanattabiʿa subjunctive: "فنتبع آياتك نصب على جواب التحضيض". Messengers = removal of the excuse: "وبعث الرسل إزاحة لعذر الكفار". The ʿaql argument (reported, not settled): "وقد احتج بهذه الآية من قال: إن العقل يوجب الإيمان والشكر". al-Qushayri's reading: "قال القشيري... ولكن تطاول العهد... ولكن أكملنا إزاحة العذر، وأكملنا البيان فبعثناك يا محمد إليهم وقد حكم الله بأنه لا يعاقب عبدا إلا بعد إكمال البيان والحجة وبعثة الرسل".
- **al-Baghawi (94)** — muṣība = "عقوبة ونقمة"; apodosis elided + supplied: "وجواب لولا محذوف، أي: لعاجلناهم بالعقوبة... لولا أنهم يحتجون بترك الإرسال إليهم لعاجلناهم".
- **as-Sa'di (91)** — the purpose of sending: "فأرسلناك يا محمد، لدفع حجتهم، وقطع مقالتهم".
- **Ibn Kathir (14 ar / 169 en)** — establish proof / cut off excuse: "وأرسلناك إليهم لتقيم عليهم الحجة ولتقطع عذرهم" / "to establish proof against them, and to give them no excuse." Cross-verses quoted in his text: 4:165 (النساء:165, "لئلا يكون للناس على الله حجة بعد الرسل"), 6:156-157 (الأنعام, "أن تقولوا إنما أنزل الكتاب على طائفتين..."), 5:19 (المائدة:19, "أن تقولوا ما جاءنا من بشير ولا نذير"). The en (169) adds the gloss "the people of Makkah."

al-Qushayri is named only because his position is quoted verbatim inside al-Qurtubi's fetched text (SOURCE-GATE allows a source confirmed on a page actually fetched).

## Hadith

- **Sahih al-Bukhari 7416** (Kitab at-Tawhid). Narrator: al-Mughira ibn Shu'ba (RA).
- URL fetched: https://quranx.com/hadith/Bukhari/DarusSalam/Hadith-7416 (saved: hadith_Bukhari_7416.txt)
- First ten words of the wording used (DarusSalam, the clause quoted whole): "there is none who likes that the people should repent..." (full: "...to Him and beg His pardon than Allah, and for this reason He sent the warners and the givers of good news"). The Arabic term the phrase renders is al-ʿudhr (the excuse): "ولا أحد أحب إليه العذر من الله، ومن أجل ذلك بعث المبشرين والمنذرين."
- Grading: in Sahih al-Bukhari (sahih); no separate grade given, none upgraded. The jealousy backstory (Saʿd ibn ʿUbada) is paraphrased, not quoted, so no hybridising of variants; only the excuse clause is quoted verbatim.
- Bukhari 660 (the PREP default suggestion) was fetched and rejected: it is the "seven whom Allah shades" hadith, unrelated to 28:47. Saved as hadith_Bukhari_660.txt.

- **al-Qurtubi's cross-reference pointers** to the end of Ṭā-Hā (20:134) and al-Isrā/"Subḥān" (17:15): his text names them ("كما تقدم في سبحان وآخر طه") but does not quote them. Their verse wording was NOT in the fetched text, so those verses were left out rather than quoted from memory. Ibn Kathir's three quoted cross-verses (4:165, 6:156-157, 5:19) were used in their place.
- **al-Muyassar (16)** and **Ma'arif (168)**: fetched, corroborated the reading, but not cited — Ma'arif's content sits on 28:46, not 28:47.
- The "them = Quraysh / the Jews" split (al-Qurtubi) was kept as a reported historical-referent disagreement, with both positions and no ruling, and paired with the §8 disclaimer that the verse licenses nothing against any living person or community.
- The future-ground angle (28:59 "umm al-qurā" / the wrongdoers-condition, and the shipped 28:43-45 material) was deliberately avoided per the orchestrator's watch-points.
