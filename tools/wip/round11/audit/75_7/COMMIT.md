Card and 8-section bilingual article for 75:7, fa-idha bariqa al-basar:
the reply to "when is the Day?" (75:6) given as a scene of the asker's own
eyes; 75:8-9 as context only.

Sources: Tabari, Qurtubi, Sa'di, Baghawi, Ibn Kathir (ar 75:7; en grouped
75:1-10), Muyassar (grouped 75:7-10), Ma'arif (en grouped 75:7-12). All named.
- fa as answer: Tabari (asked while deferring repentance, answered by
  75:7-9); Qurtubi (ma'na al-jawab); Ibn Kathir en (a question of denial).
- Readings: baraqa (fatha) Nafi' and Aban from Asim per Qurtubi, the people
  of Madinah per Baghawi; bariqa (kasra) the rest. Two dialects (Baghawi;
  Qurtubi with qil); Ibn Kathir: close in meaning.
- bariqa = bewildered, not blinking (Abu Amr, Zajjaj in Qurtubi; Abu Amr in
  Ibn Kathir); Farra and Khalil: alarmed, stunned; Dhu al-Rumma and a Farra
  verse; Ibn Kathir's 14:43 parallel; Muyassar.
- baraqa = glinting from a fixed stare (Qurtubi); eyes split wide (Abu
  Ubayda, al-Kilabi verse); from al-bariq, glitter (Baghawi); Qatada and
  Muqatil: staring at wonders once denied; Sa'di with 14:42 and 14:43.
- When, kept as a disagreement with no position: at death (Mujahid and
  others in Qurtubi; qil in Baghawi) vs Day of Resurrection (al-Hasan in
  Qurtubi; Sa'di, Ibn Kathir, Muyassar, Ma'arif); Kalbi: disbelievers'
  eyes on seeing Hell.
Hadith: none attached by any fetched tafsir; stated in one sentence. Umar's
"let him recite this surah" (Tabari, report via Qatada on 75:6) given as an
attributed Companion's saying, ungraded, not a hadith.
Licensing paragraph in EN and BN.
Dropped: Ibn Kathir en's "diminished" for Abu Amr's hara (Arabic used);
Ma'arif's sun-and-moon narratives (75:9); 75:8-9 detail; 75:2/14/20 untouched.
