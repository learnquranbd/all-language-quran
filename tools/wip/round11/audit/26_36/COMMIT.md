Card and deep bilingual article for 26:36, the chiefs' counsel to
Pharaoh: put Mūsā and his brother off, and send musterers through the
cities to collect every skilled magician for a public contest.

Spine: the machinery of a threatened establishment. Delay the accused,
marshal every specialist, and stage a spectacle to bury an inconvenient
truth under a crowd — and how that very effort became the stage of its
own defeat.

Sources (all confirmed against fetched text):
- at-Tabari: the chiefs answered Pharaoh; arjih glossed akhkhir wa-anẓir
  (delay and grant respite).
- Ibn Kathir (ar + en abridged): gather sorcerers from every city and
  region; Pharaoh did as advised, which was Allah's decree, so all the
  people gathered in one place and the signs were made manifest in one
  day.
- as-Sa'di: the cities as "the seat of learning and the mine of magic".
- al-Muyassar: troops mustering the sorcerers; every man skilled and
  surpassing in magic (covers 26:36-37).

Dropped: al-Qurtubi (no tafsir for the verse) and al-Baghawi (verse text
only) carry nothing to cite. No qirāʾa on أرجه raised in any fetched
text, so none asserted.

Hadith: Ṣaḥīḥ al-Bukhārī 660 (seven whom Allah shades; the just ruler),
used explicitly as a general narration NOT attached to the verse — the
just-ruler counterpoint to the tyrant's court. Grading sound, unchanged.

Safety: states plainly in both languages that the verse licenses nothing
against any living person or community. Did not pre-empt 26:44; 26:46
touched only as the decreed outcome. No shipped-neighbour overlap.
