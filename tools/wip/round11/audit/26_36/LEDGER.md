# SOURCES ledger — 26:36

Verse: قَالُوٓا۟ أَرْجِهْ وَأَخَاهُ وَٱبْعَثْ فِى ٱلْمَدَآئِنِ حَٰشِرِينَ
"They said: put him and his brother off, and send musterers into the cities."

## Tafsir fetched (in PREP, not re-fetched)
- t14 Ibn Kathir (ar), covers 26:36 — USED. Clause rested on:
  "أخّره وأخاه حتى تجمع له من مدائن مملكتك وأقاليم دولتك" → §3.1 (delay him and
  his brother until men are gathered from the cities of the kingdom and regions
  of the realm).
- t15 at-Tabari (ar), covers 26:36 — USED. Clauses:
  "فأجاب فرعون الملأ حوله بأن قالوا له" → §1.2 (the chiefs answered Pharaoh);
  "أخِّر موسى وأخاه وأنظره" → §2.1 (arjih glossed as akhkhir wa-anẓir: delay and
  grant respite).
- t16 al-Muyassar (ar), covers 26:36-37 — USED. Clause:
  "أخِّر أمر موسى وهارون، وأرسِلْ في المدائن جندًا جامعين للسحرة، يأتوك بكلِّ مَن
  أجاد السحر، وتفوَّق في معرفته" → §2.1 (defer the matter of Moses and Aaron)
  and §3.3 (troops mustering the sorcerers; every man skilled and surpassing).
- t91 as-Sa'di (ar), covers 26:36 — USED. Clauses:
  "أرجه وأخاه أي: أخرهما" → §2.1 (postpone the two of them);
  "ابعث في جميع مدنك, التي هي مقر العلم, ومعدن السحر" → §3.2 (all your cities,
  the seat of learning and the mine of magic).
- t169 Ibn Kathir (en, abridged), covers 26:29-37 — USED. Clauses:
  "When proof had been established against Fir`awn, clearly and rationally, he
  resorted to using force" → §1.1; "delay him and his brother until you gather
  together all the sorcerers from every city and region of your kingdom so that
  they may confront him and produce something like he produces" → §3.1; "So
  Fir`awn did as they suggested, which is what Allah decreed would happen to
  them, so that all the people would gather in one place and the signs and proof
  of Allah would be made manifest before them all in one day" → §6.1.
