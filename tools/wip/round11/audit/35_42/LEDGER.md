# SOURCES ledger — 35:42

## Tafsir fetched (all pre-fetched in audit/, per PREP.md)

- **t15 at-Tabari (ar)** — NAMED. "jahda aymanihim" = *ashadd al-ayman … fa-balaghu fiha* (the
  strongest oaths, they went to the utmost). "ahda min ihda al-umam" = *aslak li-tariq al-haqq
  wa ashadd qabulan lima ya'tihim bihi al-nadhir*. "nufuran" glossed *nufuran wa haraban*
  (aversion and fleeing) — basis for the "aversion and flight together" reading. Warner = Muhammad
  ﷺ (via Qatada). Clauses used in §1, §4.
- **t91 as-Sa'di (ar)** — NAMED. *qasaman ijtahadu fihi bil-ayman al-ghaliza* (a solemn oath they
  strove in); *fa-lam yafu bi-tilka al-iqsamat* (they did not fulfil the oaths); nufur = *ziyadat
  dalal wa baghy wa 'inad* (increase in error, insolence, obstinacy). §1, §4.
- **t90 al-Qurtubi (ar)** — NAMED. They are Quraysh; swore *qabla an yab'ath Allah rasulahu* when
  word reached them that *ahl al-kitab kadhdhabu rusulahum*; *kanat al-'arab tatamanna an yakuna
  minhum rasul kama kanat al-rusul min bani isra'il*; when the wished-for warner came *nafaru 'anhu
  wa lam yu'minu bihi*. §2.
- **t94 al-Baghawi (ar)** — NAMED. *kuffar makka* said *la'ana Allah al-yahud wa al-nasara atathum
  al-rusul fa-kadhdhabuhum*; *law atana rasul la-nakunanna ahda dinan minhum*; nufuran = *tabā'udan
  'an al-huda* (going far from guidance). §2, §4.
- **t16 al-Muyassar (ar)** — NAMED. "ahda min ihda al-umam" = *akthar istiqamatan wa ittiba'an
  lil-haqq min al-yahud wa al-nasara wa ghayrihim*; nufur = *bu'dan 'an al-haqq wa nufuran*. §3.
- **t14 Ibn Kathir (ar)** + **t169 Ibn Kathir abridged (en)** — NAMED. "ihda al-umam" = all nations
  to whom messengers were sent, *qalahu al-Dahhak wa ghayruhu*; cross-refs 6:156-157 and 37:167-170;
  warner = Muhammad ﷺ with *al-qur'an al-mubin*; nufuran = *ma izdadu illa kufran*. For §8 (35:43):
  *istikbaran* = too proud to follow Allah's signs; *wa la yahiqu al-makr al-sayyi' illa bi-ahlih*
  = the evil plot recoils on its own author. §3, §4, §5, §8.
- **t168 Ma'arif (en)** — NOT USED. Its text covers 35:41 (the "holding" of the heavens), not 35:42;
  excluded to avoid mis-attribution.

## Hadith

- **Sahih al-Bukhari, no. 1** (hadith of intentions), narrated 'Umar ibn al-Khattab. URL fetched:
  https://quranx.com/hadith/Bukhari/DarusSalam/Hadith-1 (saved hadith_Bukhari_1.txt). First ten
  words used: "The reward of deeds depends upon the intentions, and". Grading: in Sahih al-Bukhari
  (sahih). **Marked in the article (§6) as a GENERAL principle, not a narration attached to this
  verse** — SOURCE-GATE §2 satisfied. Paired with 35:38 ("Knower of what is in the breasts").
- **Sahih al-Bukhari, no. 660** (the seven whom Allah shades) — FETCHED (PREP suggestion), saved
  hadith_Bukhari_660.txt, then DROPPED: its subject (secret devotion, shade on Judgment Day) does
  not fit this verse's theme of a vowed intention that collapsed under testing.

## Asbab an-nuzul

- Not fetched separately (al-Wahidi not pulled). The occasion-of-revelation setting (Quraysh heard
  the People of the Book had belied their messengers, cursed them, and vowed to do better) is used
  only as reported inside al-Qurtubi and al-Baghawi, and attributed to them by name — not presented
  as an independent graded asbab report.

## Disagreement kept

- Scope of "ihda al-umam": the Jews and Christians specifically (al-Muyassar, as-Sa'di, al-Baghawi,
  al-Qurtubi) vs. all nations ever sent a messenger (Ibn Kathir, via ad-Dahhak). Stated in §3 as a
  difference of scope, with no position taken.

## Dropped / not asserted

- The zoological etymology of nufur ("bolting like a startled animal") is not asserted from memory;
  the "bolting / flight" sense is carried only where at-Tabari's gloss (*haraban*) supports it.
- 35:43's "sunnat al-awwalin / la tabdila li-sunnat Allah" referenced lightly in §8 as a paraphrase
  without a standalone verse ref, to keep it context rather than a second target.
