Card and 8-section bilingual article for 53:7, wa huwa bi-l-ufuqi
l-a'la, the third of the four three-word verses 53:5-8.

Sources: Tabari, Muyassar, Baghawi, Qurtubi, Sa'di, Ibn Kathir (ar
and en), Ma'arif; all named.
- Huwa kept as a disagreement, no position: Jibril (Ibn Kathir from
  'Ikrimah; Muyassar; Ma'arif; Qurtubi "sahih") vs Jibril and Muhammad
  together on the Night Journey (Tabari, Baghawi first, via al-Farra'
  and 27:67). Qurtubi's "weak" and Ibn Kathir's "grammar yes, meaning
  no" reported as theirs.
- Horizon = where morning/sun/day comes ('Ikrimah, Mujahid, Qatadah,
  Ibn Zayd, Sufyan); Baghawi and Qurtubi: the east. Sa'di: beyond the
  devils' reach; Ma'arif: so he could be seen clearly.
- Sighting given only as Qurtubi and Baghawi tell it (Hira, the faint,
  53:8 as the embrace); Ibn Abi Hatim report kept with its narrator's
  "I think ... from Ibn Mas'ud", tying the verse to the ascent.
Hadith: Muslim 177d (Masruq/Aisha on 53:8-10: Jibril, horizon blocked)
and Muslim 176b (Ibn Abbas: saw him twice with his heart), both
attached by Ibn Kathir, quoted whole, graded only as in Muslim's
Sahih. Split placed on 53:11-13; article takes no side, consistent
with the 6:103 article.
Asbab: none. Pointers: 81:23, 81:19-20, 26:193-194, 2:97, 17:1.
Dropped: wing/jewel descriptions (Ahmad), Abu Dharr "a light",
Bukhari 3234/4855 (fetched, other chains), Tabari on 53:8, "Makkan".
