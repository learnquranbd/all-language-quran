## Asbab
Used, as the tafsirs' report: polytheists asking the Lord's lineage (at-Tabari, Ibn Kathir, al-Qurtubi, al-Baghawi, Ma'arif) and Jews asking "who created Allah" (at-Tabari), 'Amir b. at-Tufayl / rabbis (al-Baghawi). The Ubayy report quoted whole from Tirmidhi 3364 with at-Tirmidhi's own preference for the mursal (3365). Not presented as an established sound occasion.

- Wrong or loose: (1) "Ahad, Not Merely One" asserted one side of a disagreement as fact; al-Baghawi says there is no difference between wahid and ahad. (2) The thirds explanation "who Allah is, what He commands, what He has done and will do" — al-Qurtubi's text is rulings / promise and threat / names and attributes. (3) Ibn Abbas "perfect in every attribute of majesty" — the text lists specific attributes (lordship, nobility, greatness, forbearance, knowledge, wisdom...). (4) 'Aishah's bedtime report had the order recite-then-blow; Bukhari 5017 has gather palms, blow, recite into them. (5) Asbab given as plain fact with no grading; Tirmidhi prefers the mursal. (6) 19:88-92 quoted; Ibn Kathir cites 19:88-95.
