Card and 8-section bilingual article for 49:3: inna lladhina yaghudduna
aswatahum 'inda rasuli-llah. 49:1-2 as context only; 49:4 a pointer.

Sources: Tabari, Ibn Kathir (ar; en abridged), Muyassar, Qurtubi,
Sa'di, Baghawi, Ma'arif.
- Turn from prohibition to praise: IK (nadaba, hatha, arshada,
  raghghaba), Sa'di (thumma madaha).
- Ghadd: Tabari (al-kaff fi lin, ghadd al-basar, Jarir); Muyassar and
  Baghawi (yakhfidun, ijlalan lahu); Qurtubi's two cases (speaking to
  him, or to others before him). 24:30 and 31:19 noted as Qur'an usage.
- Reach after his lifetime: Ma'arif only (Ibn al-'Arabi; resting place;
  hadith recitation; scholars' gatherings citing Qurtubi), reported,
  no ruling.
- Imtahana glosses side by side: tested then purified like gold (Tabari,
  Baghawi), Mujahid, Qatada, Muyassar, IK (ahlan wa mahallan), Farra',
  Akhfash (ikhtassaha), Qurtubi's hide/silver roots, Ibn Abbas, Sa'di
  (command, prohibition, hardship). None picked.
- Disagreement kept: 'Umar "removed desires" (Qurtubi) vs 'Umar's letter
  praising the one who desires and refrains (IK from Ahmad's Zuhd).
- Abu Bakr's ka-akhi s-sirar oath given as Qurtubi/Baghawi/Ma'arif
  reports, marked as not checked in a collection.
Hadith: Bukhari 4845 quoted whole (attached by IK); 4847 noted as naming
49:1. That 49:3 came down about 'Umar is Qurtubi's and Baghawi's report
from Ibn az-Zubayr, kept separate from the hadith.
Dropped: Thabit ibn Qays (4846 confirmed, left for 49:2), Baghawi's
Yamama/dream report, Ma'arif's Thabit weeping, Tabari footnote narration.
Bukhari 4845 final clause rendered literally from the Arabic ('from his father, meaning Abu Bakr'); quranx English says grandfather, the Arabic does not.
