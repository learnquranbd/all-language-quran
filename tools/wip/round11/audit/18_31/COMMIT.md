Card and deep bilingual article for the believers' reward: gardens of
Eden, gold bracelets, green silk, canopied couches.

Sources: at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi, Ibn Kathir (ar + en), as-Sa'di, al-Baghawi,
al-Muyassar, Ma'arif al-Qur'an. jannat adn as lasting residence and the heart
of Paradise (Qurtubi); rivers beneath the chambers, set against Pharaoh's
boast in 43:51 (Ibn Kathir). asawir min dhahab with the Qur'an's own spread of
gold / gold+pearl / silver across 22:23, 35:33, 76:21 (Qurtubi); Saeed ibn
Jubayr's three bracelets kept as a scholar's reading, not the verse's words.

sundus = fine/thin silk, istabraq = thick/heavy brocade, confirmed from four
Arabic sources plus Ibn Kathir en; al-Baghawi's note that thickness in Paradise
means firmness; Qurtubi on why green alone is named. araik as canopied couches
(Qatada, Ibn Abbas) and muttakiin as settled rest (as-Sa'di).

Hadith: Sahih Muslim 250, "the adornment reaches where the ablution reaches,"
cited by al-Qurtubi under yuhallawna; graded sound by inclusion, not upgraded;
tied to the general-to-both-sexes reading (as-Sa'di) and the men's-ornament
point (Ma'arif).

Brings out the murtafaqan hinge: nima al-thawab wa hasunat murtafaqa answers
18:29's bisa al-sharab wa saat murtafaqa word for word (Ibn Kathir), with the
Furqan parallel (25:66, 25:76) and Tabari's grammar of nima/hasunat. Reward
kept distinct from the dunya adornment of 18:45-46; 18:32 left for its own turn.

Dropped for want of grading: the Nasai "garments split from the fruit" report,
Qurtubi's pearl-house narration, al-Jawni's gold-woven sundus, and as-Sa'di's
reversed sundus/istabraq wording. No asbab; verse placement used instead.
