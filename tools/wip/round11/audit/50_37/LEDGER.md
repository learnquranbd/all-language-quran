## Disagreements kept (no position taken)
- Reach of dhalika: at-Tabari/Muyassar (50:36's destroyed generations) vs al-Qurtubi (the whole surah); al-Baghawi joins both.
- qalb: intellect (Mujahid, Ibn Abbas, at-Tabari, al-Farra') vs life/living self (Qatada report, al-Qurtubi's "qila", Ma'arif's "some scholars").
- shahid: present in heart (Mujahid, ad-Dahhak, ath-Thawri, az-Zajjaj, at-Tabari, Muyassar, al-Baghawi, as-Sa'di, Ma'arif) vs testimony (at-Tabari's "others": Qatada on the People of the Book; Abu Salih on the believer).
- Addressees: People of the Book (Mujahid, Qatada per al-Qurtubi); Jews and Christians specifically (al-Hasan per al-Qurtubi); people of the Qur'an (Muhammad ibn Ka'b, Abu Salih per al-Qurtubi); al-Hasan per at-Tabari via Ma'mar: a hypocrite who listened without benefit. The two al-Hasan reports differ and both are stated. Article adds: none is a label for any listener today.
