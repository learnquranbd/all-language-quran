Card plus 8-section bilingual article on Aaron's reply under Moses's hands.

Sources (all fetched for 20:94, none from memory): Ibn Kathir (ar+en),
at-Tabari, al-Muyassar, al-Qurtubi, as-Sa'di, al-Baghawi, Ma'arif al-Qur'an.

- Qurtubi's Ibn Abbas report frames the grip as ghayra fillah, zeal for Allah,
  not contempt or punishment; Ma'arif reads it as anger only. Neither prophet
  faulted in the article's voice.
- yabnaʾumma read with Ibn Kathir and as-Sa'di: appeal to the shared mother as
  araqq wa ablagh, a softening, though the two were full brothers.
- Harun's fear of causing division (Baghawi, Qurtubi, as-Sa'di): staying held
  the people together; leaving or forcing would have split them, risking blood.
- Ma'arif's ijtihad reconciliation carried whole: two prophets, one aim, method
  differing; both views have merit, no final judgment.
- wa lam tarqub qawlī tied to 7:142; two readings kept (Muqatil: heed the trust;
  Abu Ubayda: await my return).
- 7:150 plea and Ibn Abbas's athar on Harun's obedience; close on 7:151.

No sound hadith attaches to this exchange in the fetched tafsirs; the mu'ayana
report Ibn Kathir cites belongs to the A'raf parallel with no grading given, so
it was dropped and the absence stated. Ibn Abbas line marked as athar, not marfu'.
No licensing sentence (not a condemned-group verse). 20:90 (sibling) and 20:95–97
(the Samiri, future ground) kept out.
