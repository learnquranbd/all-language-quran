Card and 8-section bilingual article for 44:25: kam taraku min jannatin
wa 'uyun. 44:24 (the army to be drowned) as the lead-in; 44:26-27 named
in one line as the list continuing; 44:28 named only as where the
inheritance question sits, no side taken; 44:29 not touched.

Sources: Tabari, Qurtubi, Sa'di, Ibn Kathir (ar + en), Muyassar, Baghawi.
All six Arabic entries are 55-242 characters; the article stays at the
low end of the band (1,416 EN words) and says plainly what they omit.
Ma'arif's grouped entry covers only 44:24 (stillness of the sea).
- Order of events: Baghawi ("after the drowning"), Tabari, Muyassar.
- kam: Qurtubi only (li-t-taktheer); no figure in any source.
- jannat / 'uyun: Tabari (orchards and trees; sources bursting in their
  gardens), Muyassar (verdant gardens, running water), Ibn Kathir
  (orchards; rivers and wells). Kept side by side as glosses, not a
  disagreement; no source sets them against each other.
- 26:57 cited only through Qurtubi's pointer to al-Shu'ara (no verse
  number in his text, stated); wording checked in verse-base.json. Only
  the We-removed / they-left contrast used; no shipped 26:57 material.
- Sa'di: "they left what they had been given to enjoy of this world".
- Licensing sentence EN+BN. No present-day people named.
Hadith: none attached to 44:25 in any fetched tafsir; said in one sentence.
Asbab: none in the sources; not used.
Dropped: Tabari's "min al-Qibt" (deliberate); Sa'di's naming of the heirs
(44:28); any geography (no 44:25 text names a place); Ibn Kathir (en)
material on 44:26-29.
