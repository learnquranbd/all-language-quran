## Glosses kept side by side (none chosen)
- al-'ihn: wool (Ibn Kathir + 8 Salaf; al-Baghawi; Qatada in at-Tabari) vs coloured
  wool (at-Tabari's own, + "ahl at-ta'wil"), many-coloured (Muyassar), dyed wool
  (al-Qurtubi from the lexicographers).
- al-manfush: teased by hand (al-Qurtubi, Muyassar); carded (al-Baghawi, Ibn Kathir EN);
  begun to go and tear (Ibn Kathir); very weak, slightest wind (as-Sa'di).
- Relation to 101:4: one Day (at-Tabari's paraphrase); contrast of people with minds and
  hard mountains (as-Sa'di); both lead to the weighing (as-Sa'di, Ibn Kathir).
