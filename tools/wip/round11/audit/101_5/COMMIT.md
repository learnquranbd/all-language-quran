Card and 8-section bilingual article for 101:5: wa-takunu al-jibalu
ka-l-'ihni al-manfush, four words. 101:4 named as the preceding picture
and 101:6-7 as what follows; the weighing is not treated.

Sources: Tabari, Qurtubi, Baghawi, Sa'di (101:5, plus 101:4 for his
contrast), Ibn Kathir (ar + en abridged), Muyassar, Ma'arif. Slot check:
all Arabic slots discuss 101:5; no neighbour fetch needed.
- al-'ihn: wool (Ibn Kathir with eight early authorities; Baghawi;
  Qatada in Tabari) beside coloured wool (Tabari, Muyassar) and dyed
  wool (Qurtubi from the lexicographers). Kept side by side, none chosen.
- al-manfush: teased by hand (Qurtubi, Muyassar); carded (Baghawi);
  begun to go and tear (Ibn Kathir); weak before the slightest wind (Sa'di).
- Next stage: haba' and vanishing (Qurtubi with 56:6, Muyassar); Sa'di's
  sequence with 27:88; Tabari's unattributed dhukira report kept as such.
- Link to 101:4: one Day (Tabari); people with minds vs hard mountains,
  then the scales (Sa'di); deeds next (Ibn Kathir).
- 70:9 identified from Qurtubi's back-reference; three words vs four.
Hadith: none attached to the verse; Ibn Kathir's narrations belong to
101:11 and are not quoted. No asbab.
