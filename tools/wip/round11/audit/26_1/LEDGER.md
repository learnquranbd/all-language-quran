# SOURCES ledger — 26:1 (طسٓمٓ, muqaṭṭaʿāt)

## Primary stance
The article LEADS with and is framed by: the safest, most-reported position that the
true meaning of the disconnected letters is known to Allah alone (among the
mutashābihāt). No single decoding is asserted as the meaning; every offered reading is
reported as a view (ijtihād), not a verdict.

## Tafsir fetched (all from PREP; none re-fetched)
- t91 as-Saʿdi (ar) — USED, anchors §2. Clause: "الحروف المقطعة في أوائل السور، الأسلم
  فيها السكوت عن التعرض لمعناها من غير مستند شرعي، مع الجزم بأن الله تعالى لم ينزلها عبثا
  بل لحكمة لا نعلمها." → §2.1 (al-aslam = refrain without legal proof; not in vain; a
  wisdom we do not know).
- t14 Ibn Kathir (ar) — USED heavily. Clauses relied on:
  · "منهم من قال هي مما استأثر الله بعلمه فردوا علمها إلى الله... حكاه القرطبي عن أبي بكر
    وعمر وعثمان وعلي وابن مسعود... وقاله عامر الشعبي وسفيان الثوري والربيع بن خيثم" → §2.2
    (referred to Allah's knowledge by the four caliphs + Ibn Masʿūd, ash-Shaʿbī,
    ath-Thawrī, ar-Rabīʿ ibn Khuthaym).
  · "فإن صح لنا فيها عن المعصوم شيء قلنا به وإلا وقفنا حيث وقفنا وقلنا آمنا به كل من عند
    ربنا" → §2.3 (mutashābih posture; the āmannā bihi of 3:7).
  · views as names of sūrah / Qurʾan / Allah's name, az-Zamakhshari "عليه إطباق الأكثر"
    + Sibawayh; single letter → name only by transmission → §3.
  · the taḥaddī reading (strongest per Ibn Kathir): "بيانا لإعجاز القرآن وأن الخلق
    عاجزون عن معارضته بمثله... مركب من هذه الحروف المقطعة التي يتخاطبون بها"; attributed to
    al-Mubarrad + muḥaqqiqīn, al-Farrāʾ, Quṭrub, az-Zamakhshari (نصره أتم نصر), Ibn
    Taymiyya; the two weaker wisdom-views (marking sūrah-starts; catching mushriks'
    ears) both weakened; every lettered sūrah then vindicates the Qur'an, known across
    all 29 → §4.
  · the ḥurūf-numerology caution: "وأما من زعم أنها دالة على معرفة المدد... فقد ادعى ما
    ليس له وطار في غير مطاره وقد ورد في ذلك حديث ضعيف... مداره على محمد بن السائب الكلبي
    وهو ممن لا يحتج بما انفرد به" → §6 (sound caution; the Ḥuyayy b. Akhṭab report weak,
    isnād on al-Kalbī, tells against the method). This is the fetched sound caution the
    brief asked for.
- t15 at-Tabari (ar) — USED. Clauses: Ibn ʿAbbās "قسم أقسمه الله وهو من أسماء الله";
  Qatāda "اسم من أسماء القرآن" → §3.1.
- t90 al-Qurtubi (ar) — USED. Clauses: "ابن عباس: طسم قسم... والمقسم عليه: إن نشأ ننزل
  عليهم من السماء آية" (26:4 as thing sworn) → §3.2; "الربيع: حساب مدة قوم. وقيل: قارعة
  تحل بقوم" → §3.2; "الطواسين/الطواسيم" group name → §3.3; the far-end letter-decodings
  (Ṭūr Sīnāʾ/Iskandariyya/Makka; Ṭūbā/Sidrat/Muḥammad; place-names & lofty words) noted
  as "sayings, not knowledge" → §6.2. NOTE: the fanciful decodings deliberately NOT
  spelled out as meanings — flagged only as why the humble stance is safest.
- t94 al-Baghawī (ar) — USED. Clauses: "عن عكرمة عن ابن عباس: طسم عجزت العلماء عن
  تفسيرها" → §2.2; oath/name-of-Allah (Ibn ʿAbbās via ʿAli b. Abi Ṭalḥa), Qatāda
  (name of Qurʾan), Mujāhid (name of sūrah) → §3.1; "الطواسين" → §3.3.
- t16 al-Muyassar (ar) — USED (light). "سبق الكلام على الحروف المقطعة في أول سورة
  البقرة" → §1.2 (its restraint as a lesson).
- t169 Ibn Kathir abridged (en) — USED for 26:2-4 context: "clear and unambiguous Book
  which distinguishes between truth and falsehood"; 26:3 grief; 26:4 could compel belief
  but wills belief by choice → §7.
- t168 Maʿārif al-Qurʾan (en) — NOT USED. Fetched text (covers 25:77,26:1,26:2)
  actually only comments on 25:77 and closes al-Furqān ("The Commentary on Surah
  Al-Furqan Ends here"); it gives nothing on 26:1, so nothing was attributed to it.

## Hadith
- USED, §5: Jāmiʿ at-Tirmidhī, Hadith 2910 (Dar-us-Salam Vol 5, Book 42).
  URL fetched: https://quranx.com/hadith/Tirmidhi/Hadith-2910
  First ten words (EN used): "Whoever recites a letter from the Book of Allah, he..."
  Grading: at-Tirmidhī — ḥasan ṣaḥīḥ gharīb (reported as ḥasan ṣaḥīḥ; not upgraded).
  Relevance: names Alif-Lām-Mīm as the very disconnected letters; reward per letter.
  Marked in-text as a general virtue, NOT a decoding of the muqaṭṭaʿāt's meaning.

## Dropped (could not confirm / deliberately excluded)
- The "given Ṭā-Hā and the Ṭawāsīn from the Tablets of Moses / Preserved Tablet"
  report (al-Qurtubi via Ibn ʿAbbās; al-Baghawī) — grading not established in the
  fetched pages, so NOT leaned on; only the group-name "Ṭawāsīn" (a naming fact) was
  used from those passages.
- al-Barrāʾ b. ʿĀzib "seven long / Ṭawāsīn in place of the Zabūr" (al-Qurtubi) —
  dropped, grading unconfirmed.
- The Abū Hurayra Ṣaḥīḥayn Fajr-Friday recitation of "Alif Lām Mīm as-Sajda" (cited by
  Ibn Kathir as support for the name-of-sūrah view) — NOT quoted; the article keeps a
  single quoted hadith (Tirmidhi) per the source gate. Its content (letters serving as
  a sūrah's name) is carried only through Ibn Kathir's argument, not as a hadith quote.
- The specific single-letter symbolic decodings (Ṭ=Ṭāhir/Quddūs/Salām, etc.) — listed
  in al-Qurtubi; deliberately not reproduced as meanings, per the primary-stance rule.
