Muqaṭṭaʿāt verse (طسٓمٓ, Ṭā-Sīn-Mīm). Handled among the mutashābihāt:
the article leads with and is framed by the safest, most-reported stance —
the true meaning is known to Allah alone (Allāhu aʿlam bi-murādihi). No
single decoding asserted as the meaning; every reading kept as ijtihād.

Primary stance anchored on as-Saʿdi (al-aslam = refrain without a legal
proof, certain it is not in vain but for a wisdom we do not know) and Ibn
Kathir relaying al-Qurtubi (the four caliphs + Ibn Masʿūd, ash-Shaʿbī,
ath-Thawrī, ar-Rabīʿ b. Khuthaym referred it to Allah's knowledge), plus
al-Baghawī (ʿIkrima ← Ibn ʿAbbās: the scholars were unable to interpret
it). Posture tied to 3:7 (āmannā bihi, all from our Lord).

Offered views reported as views: an oath / a name of Allah (at-Tabari,
al-Baghawī ← Ibn ʿAbbās); a name of the Qurʾan (Qatāda); a name of the
sūrah (Mujāhid; az-Zamakhshari "most agree," w/ Sibawayh); al-Qurtubi's
range (thing-sworn = 26:4; ar-Rabīʿ's "term of a people"; a calamity) —
kept as disagreement, no position taken. Group-name "Ṭawāsīn" noted.

Spine = taḥaddī + tawḥīd of the Book: Ibn Kathir's strongest reading —
iʿjāz from the ordinary alphabet the Arabs speak (al-Mubarrad, al-Farrāʾ,
Quṭrub, az-Zamakhshari, Ibn Taymiyya); every lettered sūrah then vindicates
the Qurʾan (across all 29); 26:2-4 context (clear Book; belief by choice,
not force) via Ibn Kathir abridged (en). Turned to humility before
revelation and trust in what is withheld.

Hadith: Tirmidhī 2910 (ḥasan ṣaḥīḥ), "Alif is a letter, Lām a letter, Mīm
a letter" — the very letters, reward per letter; marked a general virtue,
not a decoding.

Sound caution included (Ibn Kathir): ḥurūf-numerology that forecasts
durations/events "claimed what is not his"; its supporting report weak
(isnād on al-Kalbī), telling against the method.

Dropped: "Ṭawāsīn from the Tablets of Moses" and al-Barrāʾ reports
(grading unconfirmed); symbolic single-letter decodings (kept out on
purpose); Maʿārif fetch only covered 25:77, gave nothing on 26:1.
No asbāb (none sound); verse placement used instead.
