Card and 8-section bilingual article for 38:78: wa-inna 'alayka la'nati ila
yawmi d-din, the curse on Iblis that follows the expulsion of 38:77, read after
his claim at 38:76 and before the reprieve of 38:79-81.

Sources: Tabari, Qurtubi, Sa'di, Muyassar, Ibn Kathir (ar + en abridged),
Baghawi (verse text only). Ma'arif (grouped, on 38:75's "hands") not used.
- Tabari: la'na as "My driving you out of the Garden"; the Day as the day of
  the servants' recompense and reckoning.
- Qurtubi: "driving away and distancing from My mercy"; "until the Day" as
  notice of his persistence in kufr, the cursing ending then and its reality
  appearing on his entry into the Fire.
- Sa'di: tard and ib'ad; "until the Day" means always and forever.
- Muyassar: rajim as "stoned with words"; tard and ib'ad until the Resurrection.
- Ibn Kathir: driven from the door of mercy, intimacy, holiness and heaven;
  the name Iblis from ablasa; the Forbearing who does not hasten; 17:62, 17:65.
Disagreements kept, no position: Garden (Tabari) vs mercy (Qurtubi) as what
the curse distances from; Sa'di's "forever" vs Qurtubi's pronounced-then-
fulfilled reading of "until".
Textual note in own voice: la'nati here vs al-la'nata at 15:35; no source
gives a reason, and none is offered.
Hadith: none attached by any fetched source; said in one sentence. Asbab: none.
Overlap: 38:76's fire-and-clay rebuttal and 15:36's reprieve not repeated.
Framing: licensing sentence in both languages (curse on one named being).
Dropped: an extrapolated gloss of Sa'di's "forever"; any reason for la'nati.
Orchestrator's watch-points described 38:67-68 (naba' 'azim); the article
follows 38:78 as fetched. Arabic count: six words. Theme: character.
