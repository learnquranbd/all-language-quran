tafsir file. The article follows the actual verse. Watch-points applied where they
still bind: no hadith (none attached), overlap with 38:76 checked, licensing sentence

## Disagreements kept (no position taken)
- What la'na is a distance from: at-Tabari, the Garden (place); al-Qurtubi, My mercy (relation). Stated as compatible but distinct.
- "Until the Day": as-Sa'di, always and forever; al-Qurtubi, a real limit to the cursing, after which entry into the Fire makes the curse's reality plain. Neither reads release. Article does not choose.
