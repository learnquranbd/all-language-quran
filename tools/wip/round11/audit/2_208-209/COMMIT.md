Assigned 2:208, widened to 2:208-209 (enter wholly, then "if you slip after
the clear proofs"); 2:204-207 and 2:210 left free. New card and a 1,761-word,
8-section article: the believers called in, silm and kaffah, the Sabbath
reports, footsteps that look like devotion, the foot that slips, and one
person in every room of life.

Sources: at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi, Ibn Kathir (ar + en abridged), as-Sa'di,
al-Baghawi, Muyassar, Ma'arif on 2:208 and 2:209. Slots on target.
- Occasion as reports only: Ikrimah via at-Tabari, al-Baghawi's version,
  with Ibn Kathir's "za'ama" and his doubt about Abdullah ibn Salam (RA).
  Licensing sentence in both languages.
- Angle kept apart from 2:84-86 (which commands) and 2:168-169 (one more
  step): as-Sa'di's hawa test and the step/slip pairing (al-Qurtubi on zalal).
Disagreements kept: silm as Islam / obedience / muwada'ah (named lists,
at-Tabari's preference as his); fatha vs kasra and Abu Amr vs al-Mubarrad;
kaffah of the entrants vs of Islam (Ibn Kathir, at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi,
Ma'arif); addressees (Ikrimah's sense, Ibn Abbas and ad-Dahhak, at-Tabari
general, Ibn Abi Hatim report).
Hadith: Muslim 153 named under al-Qurtubi's People-of-the-Book reading, not
built on; al-Baghawi's Jabir narration left out (grading not confirmable).
Hudhayfah's "eight shares" marked as a Companion's saying without a chain.
Dropped: 2:210 (only Ma'arif covers it, as mutashabihat); Ma'arif's
"consensus" on silm; Muqatil's report; Mutarrif's saying; several
secondary glosses cut for length (listed in LEDGER).
