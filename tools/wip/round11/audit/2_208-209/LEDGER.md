# SOURCES ledger: 2:208-209

## Key
Assigned 2:208; drafted as **2:208-209**. 2:209 ("if you slip after the clear proofs") is the
direct consequence clause of 2:208 and carries the foot image (khutuwat, then zalal).
- 2:204-207 left out (separate passage, per watch-point).
- **2:210 left out.** The only fetched commentary that covers 2:210 here is Ma'arif (t168 group
  208-210), which frames the "coming in shadows of clouds" as mutashabihat with a "standard
  policy" on how-questions. Saying that briefly without a kalam framing was not safe, so the
  verse stays free for its own key.
- `node prep.js 2:208-209` built this folder. The orchestrator's `audit/2_208/` folder (PREP +
  2:208 tafsir only) is still on disk and is now redundant.

## Slot check (BRIEF §6)
Every t*_208 opens on ادخلوا في السلم كافة; every t*_209 opens on فإن زللتم. t168 is the
208-210 group, t169 the 208-209 group (same text under both slots). No neighbour fetch needed.

## Overlap check
- 2:84-86 (shipped): "which commands do you keep" list exercise. This article points to it once
  (§8) and asks a different question: what decides where a command may enter (as-Sa'di's hawa test).
- 2:168-169 (shipped): footsteps as "one more step" towards evil (food setting). This article's
  footsteps are the commentators' religious-looking step (Sabbath, camel meat: at-Tabari,
  al-Baghawi, Ma'arif) and the step-then-slip pairing with 2:209 (al-Qurtubi's zalal-in-the-foot).
  The "first small step / refuse the next" practice is not repeated.

## Disagreements kept (names on both sides, no position of the article's own)
- silm: Islam (Mujahid, Qatadah, Ibn Abbas, as-Suddi, Ibn Zayd, ad-Dahhak; Tawus, Ikrimah via Ibn Kathir) vs obedience (ar-Rabi'; Abu al-Aliyah via Ibn Kathir) vs Qatadah's second gloss muwada'ah. At-Tabari's preference reported as his.
- salm/silm reading: Hijaz fatha vs Kufa kasra (at-Tabari); Abu Amr's distinction vs al-Mubarrad's rejection (al-Qurtubi).
- kaffah: of the entrants (unnamed commentators via Ibn Kathir) vs of silm (Ibn Kathir "correct"; at-Tabari); al-Qurtubi both; Ma'arif's two dimensions.
- addressees: believers in Muhammad ﷺ (Ikrimah's sense) vs People of the Book (Ibn Abbas, ad-Dahhak via at-Tabari); at-Tabari: general; Ibn Kathir's Ibn Abi Hatim report (believers of the People of the Book).
- occasion: Ikrimah's report (at-Tabari), al-Baghawi's version, vs Ibn Kathir's doubt on Abdullah ibn Salam.
