Card and 8-section bilingual article (1,766 EN words) on 58:22: la tajidu
qawman ... ula'ika hizbu Allah. Read after 58:21's kataba (the verb repeats);
58:14 and 58:19-20 touched as context only; nothing developed beyond the verse.

Sources: at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi, as-Sa'di, Ibn Kathir (ar, en group 58:20-22),
al-Baghawi, al-Muyassar, Ma'arif.
- Muwaddah glossed as love and alliance (al-Qurtubi, Muyassar); hadda via
  at-Tabari and Qatada; as-Sa'di's "the two do not meet".
- Occasion reports attributed one by one: Sa'id ibn 'Abd al-'Aziz (Ibn Kathir),
  Ibn Mas'ud via Muqatil ibn Hayyan (al-Baghawi), al-Suddi and Ibn Jurayj
  (al-Qurtubi), Hatib as a "qila" alternative (al-Baghawi, al-Qurtubi).
- Disputes kept: Badr vs Uhud, al-Waqidi's "father died before Islam";
  Abu Bakr's son 'Abd al-Rahman vs 'Abdullah; Mus'ab at Badr vs Uhud.
- The Prophet's restraining words in the reports: "be gentle with him and
  treat him well", "do not go back to it", "let us enjoy your company".
- Scope: Malik on the Qadariyya, al-Thawri/Sufyan on company of rulers,
  Ma'arif's muwalah vs muwasah, mu'amalat, mudarah.
- Kataba glosses and al-Qurtubi's preferred reading; ruh glosses (proof,
  help, revelation, faith, Qur'an with 42:52, mercy, Jibril), none chosen.
- Rida (at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi, as-Sa'di, Ibn Kathir's "beautiful secret");
  hizb Allah glosses with a licensing sentence.
- Licensing paragraph in both languages: no harm, no shunning, no present-day
  group named; no ruling on relatives of another faith. 60:8 and 31:15 not
  cited (no fetched tafsir links them).
Hadith: none attached to 58:22 with a grading; stated in one sentence.
Asbab: reported only as each tafsir's attribution, not as established.
Dropped: at-Tabari's naming of the 58:14 allies; Ibn Kathir's gapped Ibn Abi
Hatim narration and the Badr-captives consultation; minor kataba/ruh glosses.
