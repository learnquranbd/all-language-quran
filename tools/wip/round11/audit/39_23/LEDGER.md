## Hadith
None used. al-Baghawi (chain via ath-Tha'labi) and al-Qurtubi (no chain) cite al-'Abbas: "إذا اقشعر جلد العبد من خشية الله تحاتت عنه ذنوبه كما يتحات عن الشجرة اليابسة ورقها" (Baghawi; Qurtubi has "البالية"), and a second wording "حرمه الله على النار" (Baghawi; Qurtubi and Ma'arif give it from Ibn 'Abbas). No fetched tafsir gives a six-book reference or any grading, so hadith.js had nothing to confirm; not run. Article says so in one sentence and does not quote the wording.
Also not used: Zayd ibn Aslam's report that the Prophet ﷺ said "اغتنموا الدعاء عند الرقة" when Ubayy recited (al-Qurtubi; mursal, ungraded).
