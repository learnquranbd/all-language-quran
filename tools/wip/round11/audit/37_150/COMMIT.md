Card plus 8-section bilingual article on the question "or did We create
the angels female while they were witnesses?" (wa hum shahidun).

Sources gave:
- al-Qurtubi, al-Baghawi, al-Muyassar: shahidun = hadirun, present at
  the angels' creation; al-Baghawi recasts am as a-khalaqna.
- at-Tabari: full paraphrase; the claim itself a shahada given without
  the witnessing behind it.
- as-Sa'di: "laysa al-amr kadhalik"; said bila 'ilm, iftira' 'ala Allah.
- Ibn Kathir: "kayfa hakamu ... wa ma shahadu khalqahum"; 43:19, they
  will be asked on the Day of Resurrection.
- Ma'arif (group 37:149-157): observation / report / reason / book
  scheme, 37:150 = observation; ilzami answer, which concedes nothing and
  would not make "sons" correct either; al-Wahidi via Tafsir Kabir on the
  tribes.
- Ibn Kathir en (group): 16:58, 53:21-22, 17:40 by number.

No disagreement among the six Arabic tafsirs; stated so, none invented.
43:19 used as the Qur'an's own counterpart (al-Baghawi: nazir), Arabic
checked against data/quran-json; its "'ibad ar-Rahman" is the only claim
made about the angels' nature. They are not called male.

Licensing guard: one invented claim answered; licenses nothing against
any living person or community, nor against women or daughters.

Hadith: none attaches in any fetched tafsir; said so, no stock fallback.
Asbab: none; the Wahidi tribes note is marked as not an occasion.

Dropped: Mujahid's Abu Bakr report (belongs to 37:158), any grammar of
am beyond al-Baghawi, 37:153's qira'at (its own target).
