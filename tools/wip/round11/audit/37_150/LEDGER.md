### Clauses each attribution rests on

- **al-Baghawi** (§1.1 "opening particle recast as direct question"): «معناه : أخلقنا الملائكة إناثا»
- **al-Baghawi** (§2.1 gloss): «( وهم شاهدون ) حاضرون خلقنا إياهم»
- **al-Baghawi** (§4.1 nazir of 43:19): «نظيره قوله : " أشهدوا خلقهم " ( الزخرف - 19 )»
- **al-Muyassar** (§1.2, §2.1): «واسألهم أخَلَقْنا الملائكة إناثًا، وهم حاضرون؟»
- **al-Qurtubi** (§2.1): «أي حاضرون لخلقنا إياهم إناثا»; (§4.1 cites 43:19) «وهذا كما قال الله - عز وجل - : وجعلوا الملائكة الذين هم عباد الرحمن إناثا أشهدوا خلقهم»
- **at-Tabari** (§2.2 full paraphrase, claim as shahada): «أم شهد هؤلاء القائلون من المشركين: الملائكة بنات الله خلقي الملائكة وأنا أخلقهم إناثا، فشهدوا هذه الشهادة، ووصفوا الملائكة بأنها إناث»
- **as-Sa'di** (§3.1, §8.2): «قال تعالى في بيان كذبهم» … «أي: ليس الأمر كذلك، فإنهم ما شهدوا خلقهم، فدل على أنهم قالوا هذا القول، بلا علم، بل افتراء على اللّه»
- **Ibn Kathir (ar)** (§3.2 hakama; §8.2): «أي : كيف حكموا على الملائكة أنهم إناث وما شاهدوا خلقهم ؟»; (§4.1/§4.2) «كقوله : ( وجعلوا الملائكة الذين هم عباد الرحمن إناثا أشهدوا خلقهم ستكتب شهادتهم ويسألون ) [ الزخرف : 19 ] أي : يسألون عن ذلك يوم القيامة»
- **Ma'arif (en, grouped 37:149-157)**:
  - §5.1 three proofs + 37:150 = observation: "There could be three kinds of arguments to prove a claim: (1) Observation (2) Report ... and (3) Reason. ... you have just not seen Allah Ta'ala creating angels that could have given you the knowledge of the gender of angels ... When it was said: [37:150] ... this is what it means."
  - §5.2 report = 151-152, reason = 153, book = 156-157: the three "When it was said ... this is what it means" passages.
  - §6.2 ilzami: "An ilzami response means that their claim should be refuted through the analogy of some other view of things they themselves have ... it is never necessary to concede ... It is obvious that it does not mean that it is so with Allah Ta'ala as well, nor does it mean that, had they called angels 'sons of God' rather than 'daughters of God,' it would have been correct."
  - §6.3 real answer: "Allah Ta'ala is independent and need-free and neither does He need any children, nor does it befit His Most Exalted State of Being."
  - §6.3 tribes: "According to `Allamah Whidi, this belief was not restricted to the Quraish alone. It also prevailed among the tribes of Juhainah, Banu Salamah, Banu Khuza'ah and Banu Malih (Tafsir Kabir, p. 112, v. 7)". The jinn-mothers clause is in Ma'arif's own voice ("the daughters of the chieftains of Jinns were the mothers of angels"), and the article attributes it that way, not to al-Wahidi.
  - §7.1 "sons would not be correct": same ilzami passage as above.
- **Ibn Kathir abridged (en, grouped 37:149-158)**:
  - §6.1: 16:58 quoted ("his face becomes dark ... filled with inward grief"); "Then how can they attribute to Allah the share that they would not choose for themselves"; 53:21-22 ("a division most unfair"); 17:40 quoted on 37:153.
  - §5.3: "Allah mentions three of the things they said about the angels ... daughters of Allah and that Allah had offspring ... Then they made these offspring female, then they worshipped them instead of Allah ... any of which on its own would be sufficient to condemn them to spend eternity in Hell." (The article softens "eternity in Hell" to "enough to condemn them". This is a shortening, not a change of claim.)

## Disagreements
None. All six Arabic tafsirs gloss shahidun as presence (hadirun) at the angels' creation and read the question as a denial. Stated as such in §3.3; no dispute manufactured.

## Hadith
None used. None of the eight fetched tafsir texts attaches a hadith to 37:150. hadith.js was not run, since no candidate number came from the sources, and per the watch-point I did not fall back on the stock Bukhari 660. The article says in one passage (§7.3) that no hadith attaches.
