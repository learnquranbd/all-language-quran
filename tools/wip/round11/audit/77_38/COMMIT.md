Card and 8-section bilingual article for 77:38, hadha yawmu al-fasl,
jama'nakum wa-l-awwalin: who "you" and "the former peoples" are, and the
link to the dare of 77:39. The name al-fasl is left to the 77:13 article.

Sources: Tabari, Qurtubi, Ibn Kathir (ar; en grouped 77:29-38), Baghawi,
Sa'di, Muyassar (grouped 77:38-39), Ma'arif (grouped 77:35-44); extra
by_ayah fetches for 77:39 (Tabari, Qurtubi, Sa'di, Baghawi) and 77:35
(Tabari, Qurtubi).
- "You": deniers of resurrection (Tabari), disbelievers / deniers of this
  umma (Muyassar, Baghawi), those who denied Muhammad (Ibn 'Abbas via
  ad-Dahhak, Qurtubi). Ibn Kathir's wider framing (address to His
  servants, all gathered on one plain) kept as a difference.
- "Former peoples": earlier deniers / destroyed nations, tied to 77:16.
- Tabari's "fa-qad waffayna lakum": the promise reported as kept.
- 77:35-37 context only: Tabari's "some conditions, not others" with his
  linguistic proof (23:107, 40:11), Qurtubi's stations and Ibn al-Azraq
  report (20:108, 22:47), al-Hasan; Ibn Kathir and Ma'arif on stages.
- 77:39: Tabari, Muyassar, Muqatil (Baghawi), Sa'di (55:33); Qurtubi's
  readings kept unranked (stratagem; Ibn 'Abbas "wage war"; disobedience;
  the Prophet's words like Hud's 11:55); Ibn Kathir's threat with 11:57.
Hadith: Bukhari 4712 (intercession, "first and last on one plain"),
general, not attached to the verse, marked so; Muslim 2577 (Abu Dharr,
"you will never reach harming Me"), cited by Ibn Kathir on 77:39, Muslim's
wording. No gradings added. Licensing sentence in EN and BN.
Asbab: none in the sources.
Dropped: Quraysh/Makka labels (not in texts), third verse of the Ibn
al-Azraq question (no ref given), Qurtubi's other 77:35 views.
