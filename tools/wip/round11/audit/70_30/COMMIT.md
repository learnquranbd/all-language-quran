Card and 8-section bilingual article for 70:30: illa 'ala azwajihim aw
ma malakat aymanuhum fa-innahum ghayru malumin. 70:29 as the clause it
completes; 70:31 quoted and placed only; 70:32 named as a neighbour.

Sources: Tabari, Qurtubi, Sa'di, Ibn Kathir (ar), Baghawi, Ma'arif
(grouped 70:24-30; its note is on 70:24 only, said so). Muyassar and
Ibn Kathir (en) skipped as wrong group (70:19-28).
- Tabari: exception read through the blame clause ("not blamed in
  leaving off its guarding"); ma malakat aymanuhum = their bondwomen;
  his grammar point on illa with no preceding negation and his
  "do what you like, except..." illustration.
- Ibn Kathir: from the bondwomen. Sa'di: their concubines, with his
  limit "in the place of tillage". Baghawi recites without comment.
- Qurtubi points back to Surah al-Mu'minun; 23:6-7 (identical wording,
  verified) cited by number only, shipped 23:6 content not repeated.
- ghayru malumin: Tabari's and Sa'di's glosses side by side, none picked.
No dispute, qira'at or asbab recorded in the fetched texts; stated so.
Hadith: none attached by any fetched tafsir; stated in one sentence.
Licensing sentence in both languages: glosses recorded as the
commentators' words, no ruling on slavery, concubinage or any person's
status, nothing licensed against any living person or community, no
permission to harm or own any person.
Dropped: Qurtubi's al-Mu'minun discussion (not fetched; shipped at
23:6); 2:223, 4:3, 24:33 (not cited by any fetched source).
- Review fix: Tabari's grammar note opens with 'it was said' (wa-qila); article now says so.
