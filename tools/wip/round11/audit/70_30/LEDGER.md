### Attributions and the clause each rests on
- Tabari, exception read through the blame clause, "not blamed in leaving off its guarding": t15 «(إلا) أنهم غير ملومين في ترك حفظها، (على أزواجهم أو ما ملكت أيمانهم)».
- Tabari, ma malakat aymanuhum = from among their bondwomen: «من إمائهم».
- Tabari, grammar (illa with no preceding negation; the blame clause shows a sense of negation) and his illustration: «وقيل: (لفروجهم حافظون * إلا على أزواجهم) ولم يتقدم ذلك جحد لدلالة قوله: (فإنهم غير ملومين)، على أن في الكلام معنى جحد، وذلك كقول القائل: اعمل ما بدا لك إلا على ارتكاب المعصية، فإنك معاقب عليه، ومعناه: اعمل ما بدا لك إلا أنك معاقب على ارتكاب المعصية.» Note: the clause opens with «وقيل»; the article presents the grammar point as Tabari's discussion ("takes up a point of grammar", "he notes", "he explains") without claiming it as a view he attributes to others or as his own preference. Orchestrator may prefer "it has been said" framing; the substance is unchanged either way. The article's sentence "an exception of this kind would normally follow a negative statement" is the plain implication of «ولم يتقدم ذلك جحد», not a separate claim.
- Ibn Kathir (ar), = from the bondwomen, close recited without comment: t14 «أي : من الإماء ، ( فإنهم غير ملومين)».
- Sa'di, = their concubines: t91 «أي: سرياتهم»; limit on the blame clause: «في وطئهن في المحل الذي هو محل الحرث».
- Baghawi, verse recited with no comment: t94 is the verse text only.
- Qurtubi, refers to Surah al-Mu'minun: t90 «تقدم القول فيه في سورة " قد أفلح المؤمنون "». The article identifies the place as 23:6 (same wording, verified in data/quran-json/23.json) and says only that he points back; nothing from his 23:6 discussion is reported (not fetched this session; the shipped 23:6 article covers it).
- Ma'arif, grouped note concerns 70:24 (zakah quantities) only: t168 "Rates of Zakah are Specified by Allah ... This verse shows that the quantifications of Zakah have been fixed by Allah."
