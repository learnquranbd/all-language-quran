Replaces the 722-word, 5-section article with an 8-section, 1,649-word
source-gated article (shipped card kept).

Sources: at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi, as-Sa'di, Ibn Kathir (ar, en abridged),
al-Baghawi, Muyassar, Ma'arif, all on 51:22 (en Ibn Kathir grouped
51:15-23). Rizq kept as a dispute: rain (Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Muqatil,
ad-Dahhak; at-Tabari's own rain-and-snow gloss; Sa'id b. Jubayr on snow
and springs; al-Hasan's "kept from it by your sins"; ahl al-ma'ani's
sama' = rain with the poet line) vs the decree above (at-Tabari's
"others" with Wasil al-Ahdab; Ibn Kaysan with 11:6, Sufyan ath-Thawri,
the unnamed Umm al-Kitab view in al-Qurtubi; Ma'arif's Preserved
Tablet as the plain reading; Muyassar). As-Sa'di combines both.
Ibn Muhaysin and Mujahid's raziqukum / ar-raziq (51:58) via al-Qurtubi.
Wasil al-Ahdab's ruin report as the three tafsirs give it, marked as
theirs. Ma tu'adun: Paradise, Paradise and Fire, good or evil, reward
and punishment, the Hour, recompense in both worlds; at-Tabari's
preference for Mujahid's general reading reported as his. 51:23 via
al-Baghawi and Ibn Kathir (with Mu'adh's saying); 51:20-21, 51:19,
51:12, 51:15, 51:24, 51:28, 51:47-49, 51:55-56 in one line each.

Hadith: none attached by the tafsirs; the article says so.

Dropped: the she-camel Companion report (not in fetched texts),
at-Tabari's hedged Isma'il b. Umayya report, Ma'arif's uncollected Abu
Sa'id narration; from the old article the fronting-as-emphasis claim,
Ahsanul Bayaan, 45:5, 2:22, 15:21, 51:5-6, 51:57 (no fetched source)
and the "readings are not rivals" framing.
