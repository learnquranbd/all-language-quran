## Watch-points
- qalilan ma: three glosses, each attributed, none picked: Tabari (believe in it a little), Qurtubi (ma za'ida; the little = saying Allah created them), Baghawi (negation altogether, qallama ta'tina idiom).
- shair: Tabari (Muhammad did not compose poetry well) + Qatada; Qurtubi (unlike every kind of poetry); Sa'di (poet or sorcerer charge cleared); Muyassar (as you claim).
- Qira'at: only as fetched. Qurtubi lists Ibn Muhaysin + Ibn Kathir + Ibn 'Amir + Ya'qub for ya; Baghawi omits Ibn Muhaysin. Difference stated. Reader Ibn Kathir disambiguated from the mufassir. Vocalisation of the 69:42 ya-verb not given (not in fetched text).
- Noble Messenger (69:40): Jibril (al-Hasan, al-Kalbi, Muqatil via Qurtubi) vs Muhammad ﷺ (al-Kalbi also, al-Qutabi via Qurtubi, reason = 69:41; Tabari, Baghawi, IK). Ibn Kathir's reconciliation reported as his. No position taken.
- Licensing sentence in both languages (§8.1); "you" = polytheists of Quraysh per Tabari.
- 69:42 soothsayer not developed (one sentence says it is left to its verse); 69:43 quoted only as the Muyassar/PREP wording; 69:44-52 untouched.
