Card and 8-section bilingual article for 69:41: wa ma huwa bi-qawli
sha'ir; qalilan ma tu'minun. The oath of 69:38-39 and 69:40 as setting;
69:42 left to its own verse; 69:44-52 not touched.

Sources: Tabari, Ibn Kathir (ar; en abridged for 69:38-43), Muyassar,
Qurtubi, Sa'di, Baghawi, Ma'arif (69:38-39 only). 69:40 fetched for
five and 69:42 for Qurtubi and Baghawi, where they treat this clause.
- Poet: Tabari (he did not compose poetry well) with Qatada; Qurtubi
  (unlike every kind of poetry); Sa'di (poet or sorcerer charge
  cleared); Muyassar (as you claim).
- Qalilan ma: Tabari (believe in it a little, addressed to Quraysh);
  Qurtubi (ma za'ida, the little = saying Allah created them);
  Baghawi (negation altogether, the qallama ta'tina idiom).
- Qira'at: ya for Ibn Muhaysin, Ibn Kathir, Ibn 'Amir, Ya'qub
  (Qurtubi; Baghawi omits Ibn Muhaysin), ta for the rest.
- 81:19-25 parallel per Ibn Kathir, verified in quran-json.
Disagreements kept: noble Messenger of 69:40 (Jibril: al-Hasan,
al-Kalbi, Muqatil; Muhammad: al-Kalbi, al-Qutabi on the strength of
69:41, Tabari, Baghawi, IK) with IK's both-convey line; qalil as a
small belief (Qurtubi) or none (Baghawi).
Hadith: none attached by any tafsir; said in one sentence. No asbab.
Licensing sentence in both languages; "you" = Quraysh then (Tabari).
Dropped: 36:69, 52:30, 21:5, 37:36 (no fetched tafsir cites them).
- 69:41's own files for Qurtubi/Baghawi were near-empty; their treatment of this clause comes from their 69:40 and 69:42 texts, marked as such in the article.
