# SOURCES ledger — 31:34 (the last verse of Luqmān; the five keys of the unseen)

## Tafsir fetched (from PREP; not re-fetched)
- t15 at-Tabari (ar) — USED. Plain gloss: `إنَّ اللهَ عِنْدَهُ عِلْمُ السَّاعَةِ ... لا يعلم ذلك أحد غيره` / `وينـزلُ الغيْثَ ... لا يقدر على ذلك أحد غيره` / `وَما تَدْرِي نَفْسٌ ... بأيّ أرض تكون منيتها`. Links 31:33→34 (`هو آتيكم؛ علم إتيانه ... عند ربكم ... لا يأتيكم إلا بغتة`). Mujahid: `هنّ مفاتح الغيب التي قال الله وَعِنْدَهُ مَفَاتِحُ الْغَيْبِ` (6:59). Qatādah: `أشياء من الغيب، استأثر الله بهنّ، فلم يطلع عليهنّ ملَكا مقرّبا، ولا نبيا مرسلا` + year/month/night/day detail. ʿĀisha: `من حدّثك أنه يعلم ما في غد فقد كذب` reciting `وَما تَدْرِي نَفْسٌ ماذَا تكْسبُ غَدًا`. Ibn Masʿūd: `كلّ شيء أوتيه نبيكم ... إلا علم الغيب الخمس`.
- t169 Ibn Kathir (en, abridged) — USED. Womb list: "male or female, red or black, or what it is"; "when He decrees whether male/female, blessed/doomed, the angels entrusted know." Place of death: "on the land or in the sea, on a plain or in the mountains"; report "If Allah wants to take a person's soul in a particular land, He will give him a reason to go there." Jibrīl hadith (Abū Hurayrah): "The one asked about it does not know more than the one asking" — "recorded by Al-Bukhari in the Book of Faith, and by Muslim." Qatādah echoed.
- t91 as-Saʿdi (ar) — USED. `طوى علمها عن جميع المخلوقات، فلا يعلمها نبي مرسل، ولا ملك مقرب`; the womb angel `يسأل الملك الموكل بالأرحام ربه: هل هو ذكر أم أنثى؟ فيقضي اللّه ما يشاء`; wisdom of hiding: `ومن حكمته التامة، أن أخفى علم هذه الخمسة عن العباد، لأن في ذلك من المصالح`.
- t90 al-Qurtubi (ar) — USED. Ibn ʿAbbās: `هذه الخمسة لا يعلمها إلا الله ... ولا نبي مرسل؛ فمن ادعى أنه يعلم شيئا من هذه فقد كفر بالقرآن` + prophets know much of ghayb by Allah's informing + target = `الكهنة والمنجمين ومن يستسقي بالأنواء`. KEY womb/experience clause: `وقد يعرف بطول التجارب أشياء من ذكورة الحمل وأنوثته ... وقد تختلف التجربة وتنكسر العادة ويبقى العلم لله تعالى وحده`. Place-of-death report via Ibn Mājah (`وخرجه ابن ماجه من حديث ابن مسعود بمعناه`).
- t168 Ma'arif (en, grouped 31:34/32:1/32:2) — USED (31:34 portion only). Five = keys of 6:59; miftāḥ = key opening a lock; the five are what people most seek and what diviners claim. ʿilm al-ghayb (absolute, mediumless) vs anbāʾ al-ghayb (news via waḥy/ilhām). "total knowledge which comprehends everything created as well as every relative condition" is what is withheld; partial via physical cause is not ʿilm al-ghayb. Astrologers "hardly come up to ... a single truth out of ten lies." Place-vs-time-of-death argument; nominal (Hour) vs verbal (rain, wombs) construction; why first three positive, last two negative.
- t16 al-Muyassar (ar) — read, not named (plain, duplicative).
- t14 Ibn Kathir (ar, full) — not separately read; English abridged (t169) used instead for the same content.
- t94 al-Baghawi (ar) — read; NOT named in article. Carries the asbāb (al-Wārith b. ʿAmr b. Ḥāritha, a bedouin, asked about the Hour, the rain, his pregnant wife, where he would die) + Ibn ʿUmar hadith `مفاتيح الغيب خمس` via a Bukhārī chain (Muḥammad b. Ismāʿīl).

## Hadith
- Ṣaḥīḥ al-Bukhārī 1039 (Ibn ʿUmar) — QUOTED WHOLE in §5.1. URL fetched: https://quranx.com/hadith/Bukhari/DarusSalam/Hadith-1039 (Dar-us-Salam 1039; USC-MSA Vol 2, Book 17, Hadith 149). First ten words of wording used: "Keys of the unseen are five which nobody knows but...". Grading: in Ṣaḥīḥ al-Bukhārī → sahih; reported as such, NOT upgraded. This is the "five keys of the unseen" hadith of Ibn ʿUmar named in the watch-point.
- Jibrīl hadith tail — "The one asked about it is no more knowing than the one asking" — §5.2, a short attested phrase taken from the Ibn Kathir (en) source fetched here, which attributes it to al-Bukhārī (Book of Faith) and Muslim (Abū Hurayrah). Not quoted whole; used as a one-line reference, attributed to Ibn Kathir's citation of the two Ṣaḥīḥs. No grading upgraded.
- Place-of-death report ("when Allah wills to take a soul in a land, He gives it a need to go there") — §6.2, cited as a report carried by Ibn Kathir (Usāmah b. Zayd, Ṭabarānī) and al-Qurtubi (via Ibn Mājah). Presented as "a report," NOT given a grading (sources gave none clearly); attached to the verse by the mufassirūn who recite it with the āyah.

## Asbāb an-nuzūl
- Confirmed in al-Baghawi (t94), al-Qurtubi (t90, via Muqātil, "ذكره القشيري والماوردي"), and at-Tabari (t15, via Mujāhid, "أحسبه أنا"): a bedouin (named al-Wārith b. ʿAmr) asked the Prophet ﷺ about the Hour, the rain over his barren land, his pregnant wife, and where/when he would die. NOT USED in the article — the piece writes the verse's placement (seal of Luqmān, after 31:33) instead, to keep focus on the theology. Noted here as available.

## Dropped / not asserted
- The womb's full ordainment as "sex, lifespan, provision, deeds, wretched-or-blessed" (the famous Ibn Masʿūd ḥadīth of creation in the womb) — the quadruple (lifespan/provision/deeds) was NOT in any text fetched for this verse; I kept only what Ibn Kathir (en) states here ("male/female, wretched/blessed, colour, its whole making") and as-Saʿdi's angel asking male/female + "Allah decrees what He wills." Did not import the uncut ḥadīth.
- al-Baghawi's Ibn ʿUmar chain / variant wording — not quoted; Bukhārī 1039 (fetched page) used as the single whole quotation to avoid a hybrid.
- Did not claim the Qurʾān "predicted ultrasound" and did not concede the objection; §4 states the scholars' measured response (Qurtubi on experience failing; Ma'arif on total vs partial/caused knowledge).
