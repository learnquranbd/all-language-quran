Card plus deep bilingual article on the last verse of Luqmān, the
five keys of the unseen (mafātīḥ al-ghayb): the Hour, the rain, the
womb, tomorrow's earning, and the land of one's death.

What the sources gave:
- at-Tabari: plain gloss keeping the exclusivity (ʿinda-hu / none but
  He); Mujāhid ties the five to 6:59; Qatādah (kept to Himself, no near
  angel or sent prophet); ʿĀisha and Ibn Masʿūd narrations.
- Ibn Kathir (en): the womb as male/female, blessed/doomed, colour,
  whole making; the Jibrīl hadith tail; the land-of-death report.
- as-Saʿdi: folded from every creature; the womb angel; the wisdom of
  hiding the five.
- al-Qurtubi: Ibn ʿAbbās (claiming any of the five denies the Qurʾān;
  target is the diviner/astrologer); the clause that long experience may
  read some of the womb's sex yet experience breaks and the knowledge
  stays Allah's alone.
- Ma'arif: ʿilm al-ghayb (absolute, mediumless) vs anbāʾ al-ghayb;
  total vs caused/partial knowledge; place-vs-time of death.

Hadith: Ṣaḥīḥ al-Bukhārī 1039 (Ibn ʿUmar), quoted whole, graded sahih,
not upgraded. Jibrīl line used as a short attested reference (Bukhārī
and Muslim, per Ibn Kathir).

Sensitive modern claim handled with balance: the ultrasound objection
answered as the scholars' response (Qurtubi + Ma'arif) — a scan reads
present form, not the decree; no claim that the verse "predicted"
ultrasound, and the objection not conceded.

No disagreement of substance among the sources to keep open.

Dropped: the womb "lifespan/provision/deeds" quadruple (not in the
texts fetched for this verse); al-Baghawi's Ibn ʿUmar variant (avoided
hybrid quotation). Asbāb (al-Wārith b. ʿAmr) confirmed but not used;
surah placement written instead. Not a condemnation verse — no
licensing sentence.
