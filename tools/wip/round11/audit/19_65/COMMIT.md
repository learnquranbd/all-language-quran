Replaces the 791-word, 5-section article with a 1,709-word, 8-section article
built only from the tafsir fetched for 19:65.

- Placement: 19:64 occasion from Bukhari 4731 (attached by Ibn Kathir),
  quoted whole; al-'Awfi's fuller report kept as Ibn Kathir's, ungraded.
- Speaker: no fetched text names who speaks 19:65. At-Tabari reads it as one
  sentence with 19:64 (rabb nominative, back to rabbuka); as-Sa'di as the
  reason for His never forgetting; Ibn Kathir en prefaces it "Allah said".
- Rabb: Ibn Kathir, Muyassar, al-Qurtubi (times and things); al-Qurtubi's
  "owner" point on acquired acts, attributed.
- Wastabir: al-Qurtubi's ta-to-ta' sound change only; Ma'arif's sense of
  strain; glosses from at-Tabari, al-Baghawi, as-Sa'di, al-Qurtubi (do not
  grieve at the delay), Muyassar (and those who follow you).
- As-Sa'di: worship as consolation, citing 20:131-132 (same verb).
- Samiyy kept as a dispute, none chosen: namesake (Ibn 'Abbas via 'Ikrimah,
  an-Nahhas on the chain; Qatadah and al-Kalbi; Ma'arif); like or equal
  (Ibn 'Abbas via 'Ali ibn Abi Talhah, Mujahid, Qatadah, Ibn Jurayj,
  Ibn al-Musayyab, as-Sa'di, at-Tabari's own); deserving/rival (al-Qurtubi's
  Ibn 'Abbas, Mujahid's musamah, at-Tabari's framing through worship).
- Hal = la (al-Qurtubi); negation known by reason (as-Sa'di). Muyassar's
  wording traced to 42:11; Qatadah's 43:87 from at-Tabari. 19:66-67 in a line.
- Dropped from the old article: angels-as-speaker as fact, the eighth-form
  count and 54:27, the 'A'ishah hadith, 19:7, ar-Rahman frequency, 19:88-90,
  112:4.
