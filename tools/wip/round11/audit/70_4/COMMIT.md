Card and 9-section bilingual article for 70:4: ta'ruju al-mala'ikatu
wa-r-ruhu ilayhi fi yawmin kana miqdaruhu khamsina alfa sanah. 70:1-3
as the frame; 70:5 named only as Ibn Kathir explains it.

Sources: Tabari, Qurtubi, Ibn Kathir (ar; en grouped 70:1-7), Sa'di,
Baghawi, Muyassar (grouped 70:1-4), Ma'arif (grouped 70:4-5). All named.
- Ruh: Jibril (Tabari, Baghawi, Ibn Abbas via Qurtubi with 26:193,
  Ma'arif) beside another angel, Abu Salih, Qabisa, IK's two
  possibilities and Sa'di's generic souls. ilayhi kept in the sources'
  words (Tabari, Baghawi, Qurtubi's three glosses with 37:99, Sa'di).
- The day kept as a disagreement along IK's four views: distance of the
  ascent (Tabari, Mujahid, Baghawi, Ibn Ishaq, Wahb, Kalbi, Sa'di's
  first possibility); the world's age (Mujahid, Ikrima, al-Hakam); day
  of separation (Muhammad ibn Ka'b, "very strange"); Day of Resurrection
  (Ikrima/Ibn Abbas, chain sahih per IK; Qatada, Dahhak, Ibn Zayd;
  al-Hasan, Yaman, Ata/Muqatil, Kalbi; Qurtubi's tamthil and preference).
  Muhammad ibn Ka'b is placed under different views by IK and Qurtubi;
  both kept.
- Grammar: Tabari ties the day to the ascent; Baghawi/Qurtubi taqdim wa
  ta'khir to 70:2-3; Ma'arif's understood "will occur". No position.
- 32:5: Mujahid's reconciliation (Tabari, Qurtubi, IK); Ibn Abbas's
  refusal via Ibn Abi Mulayka; Ma'arif's relativity.
Hadith: Sahih Muslim 987 (Suhayl route), treasure-owner unit quoted
whole; attached by IK, and by an-Nahhas via Qurtubi. Abu Sa'id "lighter
than a prescribed prayer" given as the tafsirs' report with IK's verdict
(Darraj and his shaykh weak); Ibrahim at-Taymi via Qurtubi.
Asbab: none. Licensing sentence on the asker of 70:1 in both languages.
Dropped: Ibn Abbas's heaven/earth thickness figures and the Throne
description (IK), Mu'adh via al-Mawardi, Ma'arif's Abu Hurayrah report
(English and Arabic disagree on the prayer times) and its "five
thousand" misprint, 22:47 (not cited by any source).
- Review fixes: Muslim 987 described not partly quoted; 'sound' dropped; card no longer leans on the unconfirmed Abu Sa'id report (weak per Ibn Kathir).
