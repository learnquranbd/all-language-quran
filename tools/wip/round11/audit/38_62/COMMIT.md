Card and 8-section bilingual article for 38:62: the people of the Fire, after the
leaders-and-followers quarrel of 38:59-61, asking why they do not see men they used
to count among the worst.

Sources: Tabari, Qurtubi, Baghawi, Sa'di, Ibn Kathir (ar/en), Muyassar (38:62-63).
- Speakers: at-taghun, "fima dhukira" Abu Jahl and al-Walid (Tabari); akabir
  al-mushrikin (Qurtubi); sanadid Quraysh (Baghawi); unnamed (Sa'di, Muyassar,
  Ibn Kathir's general opening).
- The men: every source makes them believers; none names another group. Lists
  vary: Suhayb/Khabbab/Bilal/Salman (Tabari, Mujahid x2 via Layth), companions
  per Ibn Abbas with Bilal/Suhayb/Ammar (Qurtubi), "poor believers" five names
  (Baghawi), "the believers" (Sa'di).
- Disagreement kept, no position: particular Quraysh exchange (Tabari, Qurtubi,
  Baghawi) vs "mathal duriba", all disbelievers (Ibn Kathir; Sa'di general).
- Counting: Tabari "min ashrarina"; Sa'di "naz'umu ... mustahiqqin li-'adhab
  an-nar"; Muyassar adds al-ashqiya'.
- 38:63 via Muyassar's grouped reading and Baghawi's bridge; Ibn Kathir (en)
  wishful thinking and 7:44. Qurtubi's remark on Abu Jahl's household accepting
  Islam and his line of verse, attributed.
Hadith: none attached by any fetched tafsir; stated in one sentence. Asbab: none.
Cross-refs: 38:59-61 (not shipped, summarised from text), 38:63, 38:64, 38:65, 7:44.
Framing: licensing sentence in both languages; reading the verse as a verdict on
any present group named as repeating the speakers' own error.
Dropped: Ma'arif (wrong group), wider 7:44-49 range, companion biography not in
sources, ambiguous Ibn Kathir clause. Orchestrator's believers-vs-others premise
not found in sources; actual split reported instead.
Arabic count: 38:62 ten words. Themes: hereafter, character.
