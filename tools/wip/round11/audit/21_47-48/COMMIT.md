A two-verse entry, two distinct topics held in balance.

21:47 — the scales of justice set for the Day. al-qist glossed as pure justice
(at-Tabari, al-Baghawi), no shortchanging as in worldly weighing (al-Qurtubi),
no soul wronged in the least. The plural mawazin worked through: a minority read
it as many real scales (per person, or per class of deed), the majority as one
Balance named in the plural for the multitude of deeds (Ma'arif, Ibn Kathir);
and Qurtubi's three possibilities laid side by side. The deeper split kept as a
split: Mujahid/Qatadah/ad-Dahhak take the mizan as a figure for justice itself
(at-Tabari from Mujahid: "innama huwa mathal"), while the reports and the great
majority hold a literal balance with pans and a tongue (Qurtubi, al-Baghawi) —
no verdict imposed. The mustard seed as the smallest measure of a deed (as-Sa'di),
tied to Zalzalah 99:7-8 and to 18:49, 4:40, 31:16 (Ibn Kathir); at-Tabari's
reading atayna / aatayna (bring forth / recompense) and Ibn Zayd's "written and
counted." "Sufficient as reckoner" unpacked by as-Sa'di; hasibin glossed by
as-Suddi and Ibn 'Abbas in al-Baghawi.

Hadith: Sahih al-Bukhari 6406 (Abu Hurayrah), the two words light on the tongue
and heavy in the balance — quoted whole, sound, and cited by Ibn Kathir on this
verse. The man-and-his-slaves narration presented as the mufassirun attach it
(Ibn Kathir via Ahmad, Ma'arif via Qurtubi/Tirmidhi), no grade upgraded.

21:48 — the Furqan given to Moses and Aaron, with a genuine disagreement kept:
the Criterion as the Torah that parts truth from falsehood (Mujahid, Qatadah,
as-Sa'di, az-Zajjaj) versus the victory over enemies from 8:41's day of Badr
(Ibn Zayd), at-Tabari and al-Baghawi leaning to the latter on the waw of
wa-diya'; Ibn Kathir reconciling both. diya' as guiding light and dhikr for the
God-fearing (as-Sa'di, at-Tabari), closing on the pairing the surah draws
without preempting 21:49.

Dropped: al-Baghawi's Dawud-and-the-scale story and Ma'arif's Mustadrak/Lalkai/
Hakim mizan narrations — not confirmed here; listed in the ledger.
