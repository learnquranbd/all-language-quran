# SOURCES ledger — 21:47-48

## 21:47 (the scales of justice, the mustard seed)

Tafsir fetched: 14 (Ibn Kathir ar), 15 (at-Tabari), 16 (Muyassar), 90 (al-Qurtubi),
91 (as-Sa'di), 94 (al-Baghawi), 168 (Ma'arif en), 169 (Ibn Kathir en abridged).
Named in the article: at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi, al-Baghawi, as-Sa'di, Ibn Kathir, Ma'arif.

- at-Tabari (t15_47): al-qist = al-'adl; "wa ja'ala al-qist ... min na't al-mawazin,
  wa huwa jam'"; "li-yawm al-qiyamah ... li-ahli yawm al-qiyamah" + the grammarians'
  "fi yawm al-qiyamah"; "fala yazlimu Allah nafsan ... bi-an yu'aqibahu bi-dhanbin lam
  ya'malhu aw yabkhasahu thawab 'amal 'amilahu." Mustard seed: "ja'na biha
  fa-ahdarnaha"; Ibn Zayd "katabnaha wa ahsaynaha lahu wa 'alayhi ... thumma ya'fu in
  sha'a aw ya'khudh"; Mujahid read "aatayna" = "jazayna biha." hasibin: "la ahada
  a'lam bi-a'malihim ... minna."
- al-Qurtubi (t90_47): "al-mawazin jam' mizan"; three readings (a mizan per mukallaf;
  several mawazin per person each a sinf of deeds, with the verse of poetry; one mizan
  in the plural). Mujahid/Qatadah/ad-Dahhak: "dhikr al-mizan mathal ... innama huwa
  al-'adl"; "wa-lladhi waradat bihi al-akhbar wa 'alayhi as-sawad al-a'zam al-qawl
  al-awwal"; al-Tadhkirah + al-A'raf + al-Kahf cross-refs; "al-qist al-'adl ay laysa
  fiha bakhsun wala zulmun kama yakunu fi wazn ad-dunya"; "li-l-mizan kaffatan wa
  khuyut wa lisan wa-sh-shahin." Slaves hadith via Tirmidhi/'A'isha ("hadith gharib").
- al-Baghawi (t94_47): "al-qist al-'adl"; "la yunqasu min thawab hasanatihi wala yuzadu
  'ala sayyi'atihi"; "al-mizan lahu lisan wa kaffatan." hasibin: as-Suddi "muhsin,"
  al-hasab = al-'add, Ibn 'Abbas "'alimin hafizin." (Dawud-and-the-scale story in this
  text NOT used — a narration I could not confirm; dropped.)
- as-Sa'di (t91_47): khardal "asghar al-ashya' wa ahqaruha, min khayr aw sharr";
  "atayna biha wa ahdarnaha li-yujaza biha sahibuha"; quotes Zalzalah 99:7-8; hasibin
  = "nafsahu al-karimah ... 'aliman bi-a'mal al-'ibad, hafizan laha, muthbitan laha fi
  al-kitab ... musilan li-l-'ummal jaza'aha."
- Ibn Kathir en (t169_47): majority = one Balance, plural for the number of deeds;
  parallels 18:49, 4:40, 31:16; the two-word hadith (Bukhari/Muslim) + the slaves
  narration (Imam Ahmad).
- Ma'arif en (t168_47): mawazin plural of mizan; minority = many scales (per individual
  / per type of deed); majority = one balance, plural for the multitude of deeds of all
  Adam's children. qist = justice. (Mustadrak/Lalkai/etc. narrations in this text NOT
  quoted — used only Bukhari 6406, quoted whole; dropped the rest.)

Hadith used: Sahih al-Bukhari 6406, from Abu Hurayrah — "Kalimatani khafifatani 'ala
al-lisan, thaqilatani fi al-mizan, habibatani ila ar-Rahman: Subhan Allah al-'Azim,
Subhan Allah wa bihamdihi." URL fetched:
https://quranx.com/hadith/Bukhari/DarusSalam/Hadith-6406 (Dar-us-Salam 6406; USC Vol 8
Book 75 Hadith 415). First ten words (EN): "There are two expressions which are very
easy for". Grading: Sahih (al-Bukhari). Directly on the verse's mizan; Ibn Kathir cites
it under 21:47. Quoted whole, one collection's wording.

Second narration (man and his slaves): reported by Ibn Kathir (via Imam Ahmad) and by
Ma'arif (via Qurtubi/Tirmidhi) UNDER this verse — the Prophet recites 21:47 in it, so
it is verse-attached. Presented as the mufassirun report it, with no grade asserted
beyond theirs (Qurtubi: "hadith gharib"); not quoted as the primary sound hadith.

Asbab an-nuzul: none established for 21:47; not used. Placement: within the passage
answering the deniers (21:41-46) with the certainty of the final reckoning.

## 21:48 (the Criterion given to Moses and Aaron)

Tafsir fetched: 14 (Ibn Kathir ar), 15 (at-Tabari), 16 (Muyassar), 90 (al-Qurtubi),
91 (as-Sa'di), 94 (al-Baghawi), 168 (Ma'arif en), 169 (Ibn Kathir en abridged).
Named: at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi, al-Baghawi, as-Sa'di, Ibn Kathir, Ma'arif; Mujahid,
Qatadah, Abu Salih, Ibn Zayd, az-Zajjaj, al-Farra', ath-Tha'labi via them.

Genuine disagreement kept, both positions, no verdict:
- al-Furqan = the Torah / the Scripture that parts truth from falsehood: at-Tabari
  ("al-kitab alladhi yafruqu bayna al-haqq wa-l-batil ... wa dhalika huwa at-tawrah"),
  Mujahid ("al-kitab"), Qatadah ("at-tawrah, halaluha wa haramuha"), Abu Salih
  ("at-tawrah" in Ibn Kathir ar t14_48), as-Sa'di ("hiya at-tawrah al-fariqa bayna
  al-haqq wa-l-batil"), az-Zajjaj (in Qurtubi).
- al-Furqan = an-nasr (victory over enemies): Ibn Zayd, citing 8:41 "yawm al-furqan" =
  Badr (at-Tabari t15_48, al-Baghawi t94_48, al-Qurtubi t90_48). at-Tabari and
  al-Baghawi (via ath-Tha'labi) lean this way "li-dukhul al-waw fi ad-diya'."
- The waw argument and its rebuttal: at-Tabari — had al-Furqan been the Torah (the
  diya'), it would read "al-furqan diya'" without waw. al-Qurtubi — az-Zajjaj rebuts
  al-Farra' ("al-waw taji' li-ma'nan fala tuzad"); Ibn 'Abbas/'Ikrimah recite
  "al-furqan diya'" without waw (noted, not built on). Ibn Kathir reconciles: the
  heavenly Books all carry the furqan and a light in the hearts.
- diya' = light (nur) to guide, to know rulings, to part halal from haram (as-Sa'di);
  Ma'arif: light giving manifestation of truth to hearts.
- dhikr li-l-muttaqin: as-Sa'di ("yatadhakkarun bihi ma yanfa'uhum wa ma yadurruhum ...
  khassa al-muttaqin li-annahum al-muntafi'un"); at-Tabari ("tadhkiran li-man ittaqa
  Allah bi-ta'atihi wa ada' fara'idihi wa-jtinab ma'asihi").

Asbab: none for 21:48; not used. Placement: paired with 21:47 in the entry; the surah
sets the criterion beside the final scale. Did NOT preempt 21:49 (those who fear their
Lord unseen).
