Sources: at-Tabari, Ibn Kathir (ar, en cross-check), al-Qurtubi, as-Sa'di,
al-Baghawi, al-Muyassar, Ma'arif al-Qur'an.
- Placement after 35:28 (Ma'arif: the heart's awe, then its outward acts);
  Mutarrif's "this is the verse of the reciters" (at-Tabari's three chains,
  Ibn Kathir, Ma'arif); al-Qurtubi's "reciters who act and who know".
- Yatluna kept as a disagreement: read (at-Tabari, al-Baghawi); recite, believe,
  act (Ibn Kathir, Muyassar); follow first (as-Sa'di); constant recitation
  weightier, "follow" held by others (Ma'arif).
- Sirran wa 'alaniyatan: zakah plus voluntary charity (at-Tabari, Muyassar);
  obligatory openly, voluntary secretly (Ma'arif's jurists); all times
  (as-Sa'di); night and day (Ibn Kathir).
- Lan tabur from barat as-suq (at-Tabari); the trade = promised reward, the
  Lord's pleasure (al-Baghawi, Ibn Kathir, as-Sa'di, Muyassar); 61:10-11 (Ma'arif).
- Yarjuna as predicate (Tha'lab via al-Qurtubi, al-Farra' via al-Baghawi;
  at-Tabari names tijaratan); hope not certainty (Ma'arif from Ruh al-Ma'ani).
- Yazidahum min fadlih: multiplication, intercession, the vision, "what no eye
  has seen" (Ibn 'Abbas via al-Baghawi), at-Tabari's open reading; none chosen.
  24:38 and 4:173 via al-Qurtubi.
- Ghafur Shakur: Qatada, Ibn 'Abbas, Ibn Kathir, al-Qurtubi, as-Sa'di.
