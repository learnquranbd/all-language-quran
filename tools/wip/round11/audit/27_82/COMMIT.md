Card plus an 8-section bilingual article on the Beast of the earth (Dābbat al-Arḍ),
one of the signs of the Hour. Source-gated to the fetched tafsir; nothing from memory.

What the sources gave:
- "the word befalls them" read as the decree/punishment falling due (Tabari: Mujahid,
  Qatada, Ibn Jurayj; Qurtubi the same), with its timing variously placed at the end of
  commanding good and forbidding wrong (Ibn ʿUmar, Abu Saʿid), the loss of knowledge
  (Ibn Masʿud), and the close of the age of accepted faith (Abu al-ʿAliya / an-Nahhas,
  via Qurtubi who folds them into one meaning).
- the beast "brought out" of the earth, not born (as-Sa'di), in a corrupted last age
  (al-Muyassar, Ibn Kathir); place reported as Makkah / al-Ṣafā (Tabari, Ibn ʿUmar) but
  marked as report, not the verse's words.
- as-Sa'di's and Ma'arif's explicit caution that no proof fixes its form, and that most
  of the appearance-reports are untrustworthy — foregrounded as its own section.

Disagreements kept (both sides, no position):
- qirāʾa تُكَلِّمُهم "speaks to them" (qirāʾat al-ʿāmma; Tabari permits no other) vs
  تَكْلِمُهم "marks/wounds them" (Baghawi, Tabari, Qurtubi, ʿIkrima), reconciled by
  Ibn ʿAbbas — speaks to the believer, marks the disbeliever.
- qirāʾa أنَّ (content of the speech) vs إنَّ (new sentence); Tabari rules both sound.
- Tabari chose ʿAtaʾ al-Khurasani's view that the beast utters the clause; Ibn Kathir
  recorded a naẓar in it. Kept as a disagreement.
- Qurtubi's record of, and rejection of, the "beast is a speaking human" reading.

Hadith: Sahih Muslim 158 (Abu Hurayra) quoted whole — three signs after which faith no
longer benefits; collector stated as Muslim's own authentication, grade not upgraded.
Muslim 2947 confirmed but set aside to avoid stacking Hour-sign narrations.

No asbāb (eschatological verse); surah placement (27:80–86) used instead. No licensing
sentence needed. Dropped all physical descriptions, staff/ring details, the unsound
ʿIsā-timing (Ibn Kathir: isnād lā yaṣiḥḥ) and Wahb's isrāʾīliyyāt — see LEDGER.
