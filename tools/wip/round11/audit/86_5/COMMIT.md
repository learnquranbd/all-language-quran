Replaces the 795-word, 6-section article with a 1,771-word, 8-section one.

Sources: al-Qurtubi, at-Tabari, al-Baghawi, as-Sa'di, Ibn Kathir (ar and en),
al-Muyassar and Ma'arif al-Qur'an, all fetched on 86:5 (Ma'arif's 86:6-7 text
from the 86:6 folder, not re-fetched).
- Qurtubi: mimma khuliq is an interrogative; al-insan = ibn Adam; the fa joins
  86:4: look at your beginning, work for the Day, "dictate to your guardian only
  what will gladden you at the end".
- Baghawi: nazar al-mutafakkir; Sa'di: let him ponder his make and beginning.
- Tabari and Muyassar aim the verse at the denier of the resurrection; Qurtubi
  reads son of Adam with no qualifier. Kept as two emphases, none chosen.
- Ibn Kathir: alert to the weakness of origin + guidance to the return,
  "bi-tariq al-awla", with 30:27; Muyassar: re-creation no harder than creation.
- 86:7 in one section: man's backbone and woman's chest (Muyassar; Ibn Kathir
  with Ibn 'Abbas via 'Ikrimah; Ma'arif's "general body of commentators") vs
  Ma'arif's whole-body-of-both reading. Embryology named, not reported.
- 86:4 and 86:8-10 one line each, attributed.
No hadith: none attached to 86:5 by any fetched tafsir; no asbab.
Dropped from the old article: lam-of-command and mimma/78:1 grammar notes,
32:8, 77:20, 36:77-79, 75:37-40 (no fetched tafsir cites them).
