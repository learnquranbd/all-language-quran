Card and 8-section bilingual article for 54:34: inna arsalna 'alayhim
hasiban illa ala Lutin najjaynahum bi-sahar. 54:31-33 as frame; 54:35,
54:36-39 pointers only; no retelling of shipped Lut articles.

Sources: Tabari, Ibn Kathir (ar; en abridged for one section), Muyassar,
Qurtubi, Sa'di, Baghawi. Ma'arif skipped (wrong group).
- Hasib: stones (Tabari, IK, Muyassar) vs wind pelting with gravel
  (Qurtubi, Baghawi; an-Nadr, Abu Ubaydah, al-Sihah, Labid, Farazdaq);
  ad-Dahhak small pebbles; hasib as thrower (Baghawi); Sa'di's sijjil
  stones. Kept side by side.
- Al Lut: those who believed and followed (Tabari, Qurtubi); two
  daughters (Qurtubi, Baghawi) vs daughters unnumbered (IK). Wife not
  saved per IK only; no 66:10 pointer.
- Sahar: Qurtubi's definition and al-Akhfash/az-Zajjaj on tanwin, with
  2:61 and 12:99; IK and Muyassar: end of the night.
- Rescue as ni'ma and karama (Tabari), reward for belief and thanks
  (Muyassar).
- Named deeds: only IK (en) sentence, quoted whole and attributed.
Hadith: none attached in any fetched tafsir; stated. No asbab.
Licensing sentence in both languages.
Dropped: IK (en) guests narrative (54:37), 15:71 and 11:79 refs, sijjil
meanings (shipped at 11:82).
Bengali 'পায়ুকাম' appears once, inside a sentence attributed to the abridged Ibn Kathir (en); 54:34 names no deed in the Arabic tafsirs.
