# SOURCES — 26:57 (فَأَخْرَجْنَٰهُم مِّن جَنَّٰتٍۢ وَعُيُونٍۢ)

## Tafsir fetched (all in audit/26_57/)
- **at-Tabari (15)** t15_57.txt — NAMED. Clause: «فأخرجنا فرعون وقومه من بساتين وعيون ماء» → plain gloss: driven out of orchards and springs of water; "them" = Pharaoh and his people. Used in §1.
- **al-Qurtubi (90)** t90_57.txt — NAMED. Clauses: «يعني من أرض مصر» (= the land of Egypt, §1/§2); Abdullah ibn ʿAmr report «كانت الجنات بحافتي النيل ... من أسوان إلى رشيد ، وبين الجنات زروع» (gardens both banks of Nile, Aswan→Rashid, crops between, §2); «والجمهور على أن المراد بالعيون عيون الماء . وقال سعيد بن جبير : المراد عيون الذهب» (majority = springs of water; Saʿid ibn Jubayr = springs of gold — the kept disagreement, §3); cites the Muslim four-rivers hadith «سيحان وجيحان والنيل والفرات ... لفظ مسلم» (§4).
- **al-Baghawi (94)** t94_57.txt — NAMED. Clauses: «البساتين كانت ممتدة على حافتي النيل» (orchards along both Nile banks, §2); «(وعيون) أنهار جارية» (springs = flowing streams — agrees with majority water reading, §3).
- **as-Saʿdi (91)** t91_57.txt — NAMED. Clause: «بساتين مصر وجناتها الفائقة، وعيونها المتدفقة، وزروع قد ملأت أراضيهم، وعمرت بها حاضرتهم وبواديهم» (surpassing gardens, gushing springs, crops filling the land, town and countryside flourishing, §2).
- **al-Muyassar (16)** t16_57.txt — NAMED. Clause: «فأخرج الله فرعون وقومه من أرض مصر ذات البساتين وعيون الماء وخزائن المال والمنازل الحسان. وكما أخرجناهم، جعلنا هذه الديار من بعدهم لبني إسرائيل» (Egypt's orchards/springs/treasuries/dwellings, and the dwellings given to the Children of Israel after them, §6).
- **Ibn Kathir ar (14)** t14_57.txt — NAMED. Clause: «فخرجوا من هذا النعيم إلى الجحيم، وتركوا تلك المنازل العالية والبساتين والأنهار والأموال والأرزاق والملك والجاه الوافر في الدنيا» (from bliss to Hellfire, left behind dwellings/gardens/rivers/wealth/provision/dominion/standing, §5).
- **Ibn Kathir en abridged (169)** t169_57.txt — NAMED. Clauses: "thrown out of those blessings and into Hell ... gardens and rivers, wealth, provision, position and power" (§5); "he and his troops were punished with the very things he sought to inflict upon the Children of Israel" (§5); "We caused the Children of Israel to inherit them" + parallel to 7:137 / 28:5 on the weak made heirs (§6).
- **Ma'arif en (168)** t168_57.txt — FETCHED, NOT USED. Grouped 26:50–58 but the fetched text is about 26:50 (the magicians' faith), gives nothing verse-specific to 26:57. Dropped to avoid misattribution.

## Hadith
- Sahih Muslim 2839 — URL https://quranx.com/hadith/Muslim/Hadith-2839 (fetched via hadith.js). First ten words (EN): "Abu Huraira reported Allah's Messenger (ﷺ) as saying: Saihan, Jaihan...". Wording used whole: "Sayhan, Jayhan, the Euphrates and the Nile are all among the rivers of Paradise." Grading: in Sahih Muslim; Muslim gives no separate grade (reported as such). Attached to the verse via al-Qurtubi, who cites it under 26:57 for the Nile's blessedness. Marked in §4 as describing the rivers' rank, not a ruling. Note: Qurtubi's citation orders it Nile before Euphrates ("والنيل والفرات"); the article uses Muslim's own fetched order (Euphrates then Nile).

## Asbab an-nuzul
- None specific to 26:57. Verse is narrative sequence. Placement given instead (§1): follows Pharaoh mustering his cities (26:53–56); the verdict on what was left behind runs 26:57–59, closing with the Children of Israel inheriting (26:59).

## Kept disagreement
- ʿuyun: springs of water (jumhūr, per Qurtubi; Baghawi "flowing streams") vs. springs of gold (Saʿid ibn Jubayr, per Qurtubi). Stated as a difference, no side taken (§3).

## Dropped / not asserted
- Ma'arif (168): no 26:57 content in fetched text — dropped.
- The long Qurtubi excursus on Nile canal counts, ʿUmar's letter to the Nile, Kaʿb al-Ahbar's four-rivers-of-Paradise saying, and Saʿid ibn Jubayr aside on Dukhan 44:25 — read but not used; peripheral to the spine. Only the confirmed Muslim hadith and the water/gold reading were carried.
- Nothing asserted from memory.
