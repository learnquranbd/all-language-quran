Card and deep bilingual article for 26:57, the verdict mid-chase:
Pharaoh's people driven from the gardens and springs of Egypt as
they rode out to run down the people they had enslaved.

Sources (all fetched for this verse):
- at-Tabari, Muyassar: plain gloss, "them" = Pharaoh and his people,
  driven from orchards and springs of water.
- al-Qurtubi + al-Baghawi: gardens along both Nile banks, Aswan to
  Rashid; Egypt's whole green ribbon fed by the one river.
- as-Sa'di: the surpassing gardens, gushing springs, crops that
  filled the land — the wealth named so its removal registers.
- Ibn Kathir (ar + en): from bliss into the Fire; punished with the
  very thing Pharaoh sought to inflict on the Children of Israel.
- Muyassar + Ibn Kathir on 26:59 (one clause): the enslaved inherit
  the gardens — the reversal the account moves toward.

Disagreement kept: 'uyun = springs of water (jumhur, per Qurtubi;
Baghawi "flowing streams") vs. springs of gold (Sa'id ibn Jubayr,
per Qurtubi). Stated as a difference, no side taken.

Hadith: Sahih Muslim 2839 (four rivers of Paradise incl. the Nile),
cited by Qurtubi under the verse for the water's blessedness; carried
as rank, not a ruling; Muslim's grade not upgraded.

Asbab: none specific; verse placement given (follows 26:53-56).
Carries the "licenses nothing against any living person or community"
note in both languages for the drowned tyrants.

Dropped: Ma'arif (168) — grouped 26:50-58 but fetched text is 26:50
(magicians), nothing verse-specific; Qurtubi's Nile-canal excursus
and Ka'b al-Ahbar saying left out as peripheral.
