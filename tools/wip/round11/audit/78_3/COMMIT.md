Card and 8-section bilingual article for 78:3, alladhi hum fihi
mukhtalifun. Placed as the description that identifies 78:2's great
news; 78:1 and 78:4-5 as context only (Ibn Kathir: severe threat,
78:6ff as proof of power over the Hereafter). No overlap with 78:9.

Sources: Ibn Kathir (ar 14 on 78:2 and 78:3, en 169 grouped 78:1-10),
Tabari (78:2 and 78:3), Qurtubi, Sa'di, Baghawi (78:2 and 78:3),
Muyassar (group 78:2-3), Ma'arif (group 78:2-3).
- The great news: Qur'an (Mujahid via Tabari/IK/Baghawi; Ibn Abbas via
  Abu Salih in Qurtubi, proof 38:67; Baghawi: "and most") vs the
  resurrection (Qatada, Ibn Zayd) vs the matter of the Prophet (Qurtubi,
  qila). Muyassar joins Qur'an and resurrection. No preference
  attributed to Tabari or Ibn Kathir; none stated in the text.
- mukhtalifun: believers vs deniers (Tabari, Qatada, IK, Baghawi,
  Qurtubi) vs differing among non-believers (Ibn Zayd; Sa'di takdhib
  wa istib'ad; Muyassar doubt and denial; Ma'arif half mockery, half
  doubt). Kept as two claims.
- Qatada: all acknowledged death, having seen it; they differed over
  the rising. Carried into the card and application.
Hadith: none attached, said in one sentence. Tirmidhi 3297 (grey hair,
Hud ... 'Amma yatasa'alun) as a general surah narration, graded hasan
gharib as Tirmidhi gives it, marked not attached. Du'a: Muslim 770
(night-prayer opening, ihdini lima ukhtulifa fihi), marked not attached.
Asbab: none; Ma'arif's chainless Ibn Abbas report kept as setting only.
Licensing sentence on the Quraysh/Makkah identification in EN and BN.
Dropped: Ibn Kathir's preference line (not in fetched text), any
unsourced rhetorical claim on word order.
