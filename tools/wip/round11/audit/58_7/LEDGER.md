## Sensitive clause (huwa rabi'uhum / ma'ahum)
Reported only in the commentators' words, each attributed (Ibn Kathir's reported ijma' plus his
hearing/sight addition; at-Tabari and ad-Dahhak; as-Sa'di; Muyassar; al-Baghawi; al-Qurtubi's
opening/closing evidence and his qila "without departing or moving"). One sentence states the
article adds nothing of its own. No kalam terms beyond what the texts carry.
