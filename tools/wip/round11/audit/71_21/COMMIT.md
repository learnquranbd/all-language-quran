Card and 8-section bilingual article for 71:21: qala Nuh rabbi innahum
'asawni wa-ttaba'u man lam yazidhu maluhu wa waladuhu illa khasara.
71:5-20 as the frame (the appeal ends at 71:20, the complaint resumes);
71:22-23 pointed to only, left for their own targets.

Sources: Tabari, Qurtubi, Ibn Kathir (ar; en grouped 71:21-24), Sa'di,
Baghawi, Muyassar. Ma'arif (grouped 71:15-22) read, not named.
- The complaint: shakwa in Qurtubi and Sa'di; Ibn Kathir's framing of
  reporting to the All-Knowing after a varied call. Never framed as a
  fault; 12:86 (Ya'qub) as the Qur'an's own parallel.
- The followed, per source: those who called them, many in wealth and
  children (Tabari); great ones and rich (Qurtubi); mala' and ashraf
  (Sa'di); abna' al-dunya (Ibn Kathir). The followers: lowly and poor
  (Baghawi), the weak (Muyassar). Sa'di's closing question.
- Khasar: distance from Allah (Tabari); misguidance here, punishment or
  destruction hereafter (Qurtubi, Baghawi, Muyassar); ruin and lost
  profits (Sa'di); istidraj not honour (Ibn Kathir). 71:12's promise of
  amwal wa banin set against the verse (article's own observation).
- Waladuhu / wulduhu kept unranked: Tabari (Madinah, Kufa, Abu 'Amr;
  all correct), Qurtubi (Madinah, Sham, 'Asim vs the rest; dialect form
  or plural), Ibn Kathir (both, close).
- Qurtubi: 950 years; Ibn 'Abbas on seven generations and sixty years
  after the flood; al-Hasan via al-Mawardi on two sowings a month.
Licensing sentence in both languages; no moral drawn against wealthy
people, families or leaders today.
Hadith: none attached to 71:21; the idols report left for 71:23.
Asbab: none.
Dropped: 71:22 plot and 34:33, idol names and origins, Ma'arif's
"hooligans", 10:88 and 14:35-36 (belong to 71:24).
