Card and 8-section bilingual article for 44:58: fa-innama yassarnahu
bi-lisanika la'allahum yatadhakkarun. Five words; the turn from the
Paradise of 44:51-57 to the Book named, 44:59 in one placing line.

Sources: Tabari (with Qatada and Ibn Zayd), Qurtubi, Baghawi, Sa'di,
Ibn Kathir (ar + en), Muyassar (44:58 sentence only). Ma'arif fetched:
silent on 44:58, said so.
- Unnamed pronoun: all texts supply the Qur'an; Baghawi (kinaya 'an
  ghayri madhkur) and Qurtubi (though not mentioned) name the feature.
- What was eased, kept as differences: recitation (Tabari); Ibn Zayd
  "He set his tongue free with it"; wording and meaning (Muyassar,
  Sa'di); for you and whoever reads (Qurtubi); easy, plain, clear
  (Ibn Kathir).
- Bi-lisanika: on your tongue (Baghawi) vs in your language
  (Muyassar, Qurtubi); most eloquent of languages (Ibn Kathir, Sa'di).
- Yatadhakkarun: admonished (Baghawi); admonished and held back
  (Qurtubi, Muyassar); understand and act (Ibn Kathir ar) vs
  understand and know (Ibn Kathir en abridgement), reported; do what
  benefits, leave what harms (Sa'di); Tabari's five-step chain.
- Qurtubi's closing-to-opening frame (44:3, 97:1) and his parallel
  54:17 attributed; 54:17 material not repeated.
- "They": Tabari's mushrikun, Ibn Kathir's deniers; licensing
  sentence EN+BN.
Hadith: none attached by any tafsir; said in one sentence.
Asbab: none in the sources; not used.
Dropped: 19:97 (uncited by the sources); "Arabic" (no text says it);
la'alla as hope; any claim on learning or translation; Ibn Kathir's
44:56-57 narrations.
