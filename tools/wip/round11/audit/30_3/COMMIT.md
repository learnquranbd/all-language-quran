Card and deep bilingual article on the Rum prophecy: the Byzantines
defeated "in the nearest land," and after their defeat they will overcome.

Sources gave:
- as-Sa'di and al-Baghawi: the two empires, Persia (fire-worshippers) vs
  Rome (People of the Book), and the Makkan taunt thrown at the believers.
- adna al-ard, glossed aqrab (nearest) by al-Qurtubi; located variously at
  Adhri'at ('Ikrima), al-Jazira (Mujahid), Jordan and Palestine (Muqatil);
  at-Tabari reads Syria toward Persia; Ibn 'Atiyya reconciles by vantage point.
- The major qira'at, both named without taking a side on recitation: dominant
  ghulibat / sayaghlibun (passive then active, about the Romans; an-Nahhas:
  reading of most people; Ibn 'Atiyya: more sound) vs minority ghalabat /
  sayughlabun (Abu Sa'id, 'Ali, Mu'awiya ibn Qurra; Ibn 'Umar), which Ibn
  'Atiyya says inverts the attested meaning; an-Nahhas spells out how the
  i'rab shifts the referent to Persia.

Hadith: the Abu Bakr bet quoted whole from Jami' at-Tirmidhi 3194 (Niyar ibn
Mukram), graded on the fetched page sahih hasan gharib — reported as given,
not upgraded. Variant stake/term figures (Ubayy, camels) kept out of the quote.

Disagreements kept: the two readings (no position); the location of adna;
Badr vs al-Hudaybiya as the day the victory landed.

Added plainly, both languages: the verse foretells a 7th-century war and
licenses nothing against Persians, Iranians or any living people; the
believers' preference for Rome was relative (ahl kitab "though they distorted
it," per al-Muyassar), never an endorsement of their creed.

Dropped (see ledger): "lowest land" as a mufassir's gloss (all gloss aqrab;
dual sense of d-n-w noted as linguistic/geographic, not attributed); the
Shahrbaraz intrigue and Ibn Kathir's Rum ethnography as off-target; 30:4–5
not exhausted (30:9 is next).
