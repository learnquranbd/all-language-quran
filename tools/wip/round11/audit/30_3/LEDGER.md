# SOURCES ledger — 30:3

## Tafsir fetched (from PREP.md, all read)

## Tafsir NAMED in the article, and the Arabic/text clause each rests on
- **as-Sa'di (91):** Persia and Rome among the strongest states; Persians fire-worshippers,
  Romans ahl kitab nearer to Muslims; Muslims loved Rome's victory, idolaters Persia's.
  Clause: "كانت الفرس والروم في ذلك الوقت من أقوى دول الأرض… فكان المؤمنون يحبون غلبتهم… وكان المشركون… يحبون ظهور الفرس على الروم."
- **at-Tabari (15):** adna = from the land of Syria toward the land of Persia; "the Romans,
  after Persia defeated them, will overcome Persia."
  Clause: "( فِي أَدْنَى الأرْضِ ) من أرض الشام إلى أرض فارس … والروم من بعد غلبة فارس إياهم سيغلبون فارس."
- **al-Baghawi (94):** the Makkan taunt (you are ahl kitab like the Christians, we are unlettered
  like Persia, our side beat yours, we will beat you); adna = aqrab ard al-Sham ila ard Faris;
  'Ikrima Adhri'at/Kaskar, Mujahid al-Jazira, Muqatil al-Urdun+Filastin; al-ghalab wa al-ghalaba
  lughatan. Clause: "إنكم أهل كتاب… ونحن أميون وقد ظهر إخواننا… ( في أدنى الأرض ) أي أقرب أرض الشام إلى أرض فارس، قال عكرمة: هي أذرعات وكسكر، وقال مجاهد: أرض الجزيرة. وقال مقاتل: الأردن وفلسطين."
- **al-Qurtubi (90):** adna ma'nahu aqrab; places (arD al-Sham; 'Ikrima Adhri'at between Arab
  lands and Sham; Mujahid al-Jazira between Iraq and Sham; Muqatil al-Urdun+Filastin); Ibn
  'Atiyya's vantage-point reconciliation; **qira'at**: an-Nahhas "qira'at akthar al-nas ghulibat
  bi-Damm al-ghayn wa kasr al-lam"; Ibn 'Atiyya "al-qira'a bi-Damm al-ghayn aSaHH… ajma'a al-nas
  'ala sayaghlibun annahu bi-fatH al-ya' yurad bihi al-Rum"; minority ghalabat read by Abu Sa'id
  al-Khudri, 'Ali, Mu'awiya ibn Qurra (Abu Hatim); Ibn 'Umar read sayughlabun (bi-Damm al-ya');
  Ibn 'Atiyya "fi hadhihi al-qira'a qalb lil-ma'na alladhi taZaharat al-riwayat bihi"; an-Nahhas
  "wa man qara'a sayughlabun fa-al-ma'na 'indahu wa Faris min ba'd ghalabihim… sayughlabun";
  reasons for believers' joy (ahl kitab kinship; an-Nahhas: fulfilment of the promise = dalil on
  nubuwwa — attributed to an-Nahhas in article; Ibn 'Atiyya: prefer the smaller enemy); victory
  fell on day of Badr (Abu Sa'id/Sufyan) or al-Hudaybiya ('Ikrima/Qatada); charity of the stake.
- **al-Muyassar (16):** Persia defeated Rome in adna ard al-Sham ila Faris; Rome will beat Persia
  in 3–10 years; "fariH al-muslimun… li-kawn al-Rum ahl kitab wa in Harrafuhu" (People of the
  Book though they distorted it). Used for the "relative, not endorsement" point.
- **Ma'arif al-Qur'an (168, en):** improbability of the forecast; Gibbon quote ("no prophecy could
  be more distant from its accomplishment"; Heraclius' early years announced dissolution); Taqi
  Usmani: the fulfilled forecast is a solid proof of prophethood; also source for the bet frame.

## Hadith (article §4, quoted whole)
- **Jami' at-Tirmidhi 3194**, Niyar ibn Mukram al-Aslami.
  URL fetched: https://quranx.com/hadith/Tirmidhi/Hadith-3194 (via tools/wip/round11/hadith.js).
  First ten words (EN used): "When (the following) was revealed: Alif Lam Mim. The Romans…"
  Grading on the fetched page: **"هذا حديث صحيح حسن غريب"** → reported in article verbatim as
  "sahih hasan gharib". NOT upgraded. (al-Qurtubi also cites Abu 'Isa "hasan sahih gharib" for
  this and the Abu Sa'id report; I report the grading as the fetched Tirmidhi page states it.)
- Note: the bet is narrated across Tirmidhi/Ibn Jarir with variant stake/term details (10 vs 100
  camels; 3/6/7/9 years; opponent Ubayy ibn Khalaf per Qurtubi/Baghawi vs unnamed Quraysh per the
  Tirmidhi 3194 wording). Article quotes ONLY the Tirmidhi 3194 wording whole; the variant stake
  figures (Ubayy, camels) are NOT asserted inside the quote.

## Asbab
- The bet/battle is the established occasion, confirmed on the fetched Tirmidhi page and in
  al-Baghawi/al-Qurtubi; treated as the verse's setting, not quoted as a separate al-Wahidi asbab.

## Dropped / not asserted
- **"lowest land" as a mufassir's gloss:** every fetched mufassir glosses adna as *aqrab*
  (nearest). The article presents the "lowest land / Jordan–Dead Sea basin" sense as the dual
  meaning of the root d-n-w plus a geographic fact (Muqatil's Jordan/Palestine is the lowest
  terrain), explicitly NOT attributed to any mufassir's reading. Watch-point honoured without a
  false attribution.
- **Shahrbaraz/Farrukhan court-intrigue narrative** (al-Baghawi/al-Qurtubi, cause of Rome's
  comeback): dropped as off-target colour; not needed for the prophecy/readings/bet focus.
- **Ibn Kathir's long Rum ethnography** (Bani al-Asfar, Constantine, councils): dropped as
  off-target.
- **30:4–5 content** (bid' sinin detail, "the believers will rejoice", lillahi al-amr): touched
  only as the frame of 30:3; not exhausted (30:9 is the next target). "lillahi al-amr min qablu
  wa min ba'd" used once in §7 as the verse's own frame, not expounded.
