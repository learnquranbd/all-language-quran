Card and deep bilingual article on the vision and the accursed tree as a
trial for the people.

Sources: at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi, as-Sa'di, Ibn Kathir (ar) and al-Baghawi,
with Ma'arif al-Qur'an. The majority (Ibn Abbas and those after him) read
"the sight We showed you" as the waking eye-vision of the Night Journey,
not a dream; al-Qurtubi and Ma'arif refute the dream reading because a dream
carries no fitnah. The accursed tree is the tree of Zaqqum at the root of
the Fire (Zaqqum by ijma per at-Tabari and Ibn Kathir); the Qur'an curses
the disbelievers who eat it, not the tree as such (al-Qurtubi), and the
Arabs called any repugnant food "accursed."

Hadith: Sahih al-Bukhari 4716 (Ibn Abbas), quoted whole, fixing both halves
of the verse; at-Tirmidhi graded the same report sound. Grading not upgraded.

Disagreements kept: the several readings of "your Lord has encompassed the
people" (power / knowledge / protection / Mecca's destruction); the Hudaybiyya
vision reading, marked weak (Meccan surah).

The Banu Umayya / pulpit-vision reading of the tree is carried only as an
attributed, graded-weak view (Ibn Kathir: gharib, chain very weak; al-Qurtubi:
weak and newly-introduced; Ibn Atiyyah: questionable) and never asserted. A
standing sentence states in both languages that the verse licenses nothing
against any living person, family or community; no living lineage is named in
the article's own voice.

Dropped: the long Ibn Ishaq Isra narration (belongs to the shipped 17:1
article; only Abu Bakr's affirmation kept, for the trial-of-belief angle),
Aisha's rebuke of Marwan, and the two-mi'raj minority — all listed in the
ledger.
