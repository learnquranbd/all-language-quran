Replaces the 763-word, 6-section article with a 1,788-word, 9-section article
built only from the eight tafsir files fetched for 29:60. The shipped card is kept.

Sources: placement after 29:56-59 from Ibn Kathir's abridged English (command
to migrate, Ethiopia and the Negus, death wherever you are, lofty chambers,
the patient who rely), and his point that the emigrants' provision grew.
Occasion kept as the tafsirs give it: the migration report (al-Baghawi
unattributed, al-Qurtubi from Ibn Abbas, at-Tabari's framing) and the
Ibn Umar orchard report (Ibn Kathir: gharib, Abu al-Atuf weak; al-Qurtubi:
weak on content, preferring the migration report). Ka-ayyin per al-Qurtubi
with al-Khalil and Sibawayh; dabbah per Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, as-Sa'di;
an-Naqqash's Prophet reading and al-Qurtubi's rejection. "La tahmilu":
at-Tabari, Abu Mijlaz, Ali ibn al-Aqmar, al-Hasan, Ibn Kathir, Muyassar;
the man/ant/mouse exception (Ibn Abbas via al-Qurtubi, Sufyan via al-Baghawi),
Ma'arif on ants and the crow. Ibn Kathir and as-Sa'di with 11:6; Ibn
Kathir's raven-chick report as "they mentioned". "Wa iyyakum": as-Sa'di,
al-Baghawi, al-Qurtubi's equal-in-provision line. Sami'/'Alim in each
tafsir's own words. 29:61-64, 29:45, 29:52 from their text only.

Hadith: Tirmidhi 2344 (Umar, the bird), attached by al-Qurtubi and
al-Baghawi, quoted whole; at-Tirmidhi: hasan sahih, known only by this
route. Bukhari 5357 confirms al-Qurtubi's year-of-food objection.

Dropped: 17:31 and 29:17 parallels, the 11:6 contrast and the asked/unasked
reading of the names (no fetched tafsir); Ibn Kathir's travel narrations and
al-Baghawi's Ibn Mas'ud and Anas reports (ungraded). Fixed: the hadith,
paraphrased without grading, now quoted whole with at-Tirmidhi's grade.
