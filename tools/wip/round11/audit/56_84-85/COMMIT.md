Card and 8-section bilingual article (1,660 EN words) on 56:84-85: wa
antum hina'idhin tanzurun, wa nahnu aqrabu ilayhi minkum wa lakin la
tubsirun. 56:83 framed as the sentence's opening; 75:26-30 parallel via
Ibn Kathir; 56:86-87 left as a pointer only.

Sources: Ibn Kathir (ar 84, 85; en group 56:83-87), at-Tabari (84, 85),
al-Muyassar (group 56:83-85), al-Qurtubi (84, 85), as-Sa'di, al-Baghawi
(84, 85), Ma'arif al-Qur'an (group 56:83-87).
- Nearer one kept as a three-way difference, none picked: power, knowledge
  and sight (al-Qurtubi, al-Baghawi first; Ma'arif only); the angels who
  take the soul (at-Tabari, Ibn Kathir, al-Muyassar; qila in al-Qurtubi
  and al-Baghawi); both (as-Sa'di). EN "[i.e., Our angels]" and BN
  "(অর্থাৎ প্রাণের)" brackets named as translators' choices.
- tanzurun: looking at the dying man's agonies; waiting for the soul to
  leave (al-Baghawi; al-Qurtubi via Ibn Abbas); "My command and
  authority" (qila); address to the dying man (al-Qurtubi, qila).
- at-Tabari's general address meant for those present, with his Arab
  usage example.
- al-Qurtubi's rebuttal readings tied to 3:156 and 45:24 (refs mine,
  verified); 6:61-62 via Ibn Kathir.
- nazar/absara contrast offered as own reading, labelled.
- al-Baghawi's "alladhina hadaruhu" reported with its ambiguity unresolved.
- Licensing sentence in both languages; no reading of anyone's deathbed.
Hadith: none attached in any fetched note; stated in one sentence. The
'Amir ibn 'Abd Qays saying (al-Qurtubi) marked as not a hadith.
Asbab: none.
Dropped: 50:16 and 32:11 (no fetched tafsir cites them), 56:86-87
glosses (future ground), Ma'arif's mudhinun note.
