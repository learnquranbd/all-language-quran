## Disagreement kept (no position taken)
- Who is "We"/the nearer: (a) Allah by power/knowledge/sight: al-Qurtubi (first), al-Baghawi (first), Ma'arif (only); (b) the angels who take the soul: at-Tabari (only), Ibn Kathir ar+en, al-Muyassar, al-Qurtubi and al-Baghawi (qila); (c) both, as-Sa'di. English edition bracket "[i.e., Our angels]" and Bengali bracket "(অর্থাৎ প্রাণের)" named as translators' choices, not sources.
- tanzurun: looking at the dying/his agonies (Ibn Kathir, as-Sa'di, al-Muyassar); waiting for the soul to leave (al-Baghawi; al-Qurtubi via Ibn Abbas); looking at "My command and authority" (al-Baghawi and al-Qurtubi, qila); address to the dying man himself (al-Qurtubi, qila).
- al-Baghawi's «الذين حضروه» on la tubsirun: ambiguous (subject vs object); reported literally, ambiguity stated, not resolved.
