## Attributions and the Arabic/text clause each rests on
- **al-Qurtubi**, verse joined to 16:89 & meaning is "when you intend to recite":
  "هذه الآية متصلة بقوله: ونزلنا عليك الكتاب تبيانا لكل شيء فإذا أخذت في قراءته
  فاستعذ… وليس يريد استعذ بعد القراءة؛ بل هو كقولك: إذا أكلت فقل بسم الله؛ أي إذا
  أردت أن تأكل." Analogies: "إذا قلت فاصدق، وإذا أحرمت فاغتسل؛ يعني قبل الإحرام."
  §3.1 "after" report of some salaf via al-Kiyā: "ونقل عن بعض السلف التعوذ بعد
  القراءة… احتجاجا بقوله… ولا شك أن ظاهر ذلك يقتضي أن تكون الاستعاذة بعد القراءة
  كقوله: فإذا قضيتم الصلاة فاذكروا الله." (I relayed this as al-Qurtubi's record,
  NOT under the name al-Kiyā aṭ-Ṭabarī, to avoid a false "Tabari" attribution and
  name-parity collision; kept it a genuine dispute.)
- **at-Tabari**: reads "when you are reciting"; grammarians' inverted-order reading
  and his rejection: "وكان بعض أهل العربية يزعم أنه من المؤخر الذي معناه التقديم…
  ولا وجه لما قال." Not binding: "وليس قوله… بالأمر اللازم، وإنما هو إعلام وندب."
  Consensus that omitting it (before/after) neglects no farḍ: "لا خلاف بين الجميع
  أن من قرأ… ولم يستعذ… أنه لم يضيع فرضا واجبا."
- **al-Baghawi**: "أي: أردت قراءة القرآن"; analogy "كقوله: إذا قمتم إلى الصلاة
  فاغسلوا" (5:6); "والاستعاذة سنة… وأكثر العلماء على أن الاستعاذة قبل القراءة. وقال
  أبو هريرة: بعدها." istiʿādhah = holding fast: "والاستعاذة بالله هي الاعتصام به."
- **as-Sa'di**: "فإذا أردت القراءة… فإن الشيطان أحرص ما يكون على العبد عند شروعه في
  الأمور الفاضلة، فيسعى في صرفه عن مقاصدها ومعانيها… متدبرا لمعناها، معتمدا بقلبه
  على الله… التحلي بحلية الإيمان والتوكل."
- **Ibn Kathir (ar)**: "إذا أرادوا قراءة القرآن"; "وهو أمر ندب ليس بواجب، حكى
  الإجماع… أبو جعفر بن جرير." Purpose: "لئلا يلبس على القارئ قراءته… ويمنعه من
  التدبر." Dispute: jumhūr before; "وحكي عن حمزة وأبي حاتم السجستاني: أنها تكون بعد
  التلاوة… ونقل النووي… عن أبي هريرة أيضا، ومحمد بن سيرين، وإبراهيم النخعي،
  والصحيح الأول."
- **al-Muyassar** (ar-rajīm): "مِن شرِّ الشيطان المطرود من رحمة الله" — the sense
  used: driven away / expelled from Allah's mercy. (SI EN "the expelled [from His
  mercy]"; shipped Bengali "অভিশপ্ত" noted as a neighbouring rendering, not claimed
  as al-Muyassar's gloss.)
- **Ma'arif al-Qur'an (en)**: sequence after covenant/righteous-action verses;
  "needed in every good deed"; "recitation is so unique that the Satan himself runs
  away from it"; two-enemies note from Ibn Kathir's preface (human enemy met by a
  visible response, the unseen jinn met only by seeking Allah's protection);
  rulings — majority hold it Sunnah not wājib, "a position on which Ibn Jarir
  al-Tabari has reported a consensus (ijmāʿ)"; occasional omission "stands proved
  from authentic (sahih) ahadith"; one taʿawwudh suffices for a sitting, re-say it
  with bismillah on resuming after a break; only for the Qur'an (bismillah for
  other books); Abu Hanifa: first rakʿah only, Shafiʿi: every rakʿah mustaḥabb.

## Watch-points
1. Timing — named the readings actually found: literal "when you have recited"
   (grammarians, rejected by at-Tabari) vs jumhūr "when you intend to recite"
   (Tabari, Baghawi, Muyassar, Sa'di, Ibn Kathir). Did not force a winner beyond
   what the texts say.
2. Obligatory vs recommended — kept as attested: nadb/Sunnah with ijmāʿ reported by
   Ibn Jarir at-Tabari (Ibn Kathir, Baghawi, Ma'arif); at-Tabari "iʿlām wa nadb."
   **The ʿAṭāʾ / ẓāhirī wājib view named in the brief's watch-point is NOT present
   in the fetched al-Qurtubi text (t90), which treats only the timing masʾalah and
   names no one for a wājib position. Per the source-gate it is DROPPED, not
   softened.** Ma'arif's "majority have not classed it obligatory" is the only hint
   that a minority did; I reported that phrasing without inventing a name.
3. ar-rajīm — gave only the sense the text supports (driven from / expelled from
   Allah's mercy, al-Muyassar). "Stoned"/"cursed" lexical senses are NOT in the
   fetched texts and were NOT asserted.
4. Future ground 16:99–100 — not preempted; the "two enemies" material is about the
   command to seek refuge, not Satan's authority.
5. Hadith — see below.

## Hadith
- **Abu Sa'id al-Khudri istiftāḥ** (Prophet ﷺ sought refuge before reciting) — the
  narration al-Qurtubi/al-Baghawi point toward.
  - Sunan Abu Dawud, Hadith 775, https://quranx.com/hadith/AbuDawud/DarusSalam/Hadith-775
    first ten words: "When the Messenger of Allah (ﷺ) got up to". Abu Dawud's own
    note: chain narrated "from al-Hasan omitting the Companion… misunderstanding on
    the part of Ja'far" (a defect / waham).
  - Jami' at-Tirmidhi, Hadith 242, https://quranx.com/hadith/Tirmidhi/Hadith-242
    at-Tirmidhi: "قد تُكلِّم في إسناد حديث أبي سعيد… وقال أحمد لا يصح هذا الحديث"
    — Ahmad said it is NOT authentic; ʿAlī ibn ʿAlī ar-Rifāʿī criticised.
  - **DROPPED as a proof** (graded not-sound by the collectors; grading reported,
    not upgraded). Article §4 states plainly no sound hadith is rested on; the
    command stands on the verse.
- **Bukhari 660** (PREP default) fetched and REJECTED: it is the "seven whom Allah
  will shade" hadith — off-topic, nothing to do with istiʿādhah or recitation.
- Jubayr ibn Muṭ'im opening-supplication (al-Qurtubi, al-Baghawi) — about the
  prayer's opening takbir, not the istiʿādhah for reciting; not used.
