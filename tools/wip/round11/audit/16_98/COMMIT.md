The istiʿādhah verse: seek refuge before reciting the Qur'an.

Card plus 8-section bilingual article (1438 EN words), source-gated.

Sources gave:
- al-Qurtubi joins the verse to 16:89 (the Book sent down as a clarification of
  everything); Ma'arif places it after the covenant/righteous-action verses (16:97),
  refuge being needed at the start of every good deed.
- Timing: the words read literally "when you have recited," and at-Tabari reports
  grammarians who inverted the order; he rejects it. Tabari, al-Baghawi (analogy to
  5:6), al-Muyassar, as-Sa'di and Ibn Kathir all gloss it "when you intend to recite."
- Ruling: nadb / Sunnah, not wājib — Ibn Kathir and Ma'arif both cite an ijmāʿ
  reported by Ibn Jarir at-Tabari; at-Tabari calls it "instruction and urging" and
  notes no farḍ is neglected by omitting it.
- ar-rajīm: al-Muyassar glosses it "driven from Allah's mercy" (SI: "the expelled").
- as-Sa'di on why Satan is keenest at the start of a good act; al-Baghawi that
  istiʿādhah is "holding fast to Allah"; Ma'arif's practical rulings (one taʿawwudh
  per sitting; re-say on resuming; only for the Qur'an; Abu Hanifa vs ash-Shafiʿi
  in the prayer).

Disagreements kept as disagreements:
- Before vs after reciting — jumhūr/al-Baghawi "before" against Ḥamza, Abū Ḥātim
  as-Sijistānī, and (via an-Nawawī) Abū Hurayra, Ibn Sīrīn, an-Nakhaʿī "after";
  Ibn Kathir judges "before" the sound view. No position forced beyond the texts.

Hadith: none used. The Abū Saʿīd al-Khudrī istiftāḥ narration (Abu Dawud 775,
Tirmidhi 242) that the mufassirūn point toward is graded not-authentic (Aḥmad:
"lā yaṣiḥḥu"; isnad criticised); reported and dropped. Bukhari 660 from PREP was
off-topic ("seven whom Allah shades") and rejected. Article says plainly the
command rests on the verse.

Dropped: the ʿAṭāʾ/ẓāhirī wājib view — not present in the fetched al-Qurtubi text,
so not asserted. "Stoned/cursed" senses of rajīm — not in the fetched texts. §6
states in both languages that the verse licenses nothing against any living person
or community; the enemy named is the unseen whisperer.
