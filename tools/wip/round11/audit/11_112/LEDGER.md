## Hadith
1. **Muslim 38** — https://quranx.com/hadith/Muslim/Hadith-38 (saved hadith_Muslim_38.txt). Cited at this
   verse by al-Qurtubi (names Sahih Muslim) and al-Baghawi (own chain via Abu Usamah). First ten words used
   (EN): "I said: O Messenger of Allah, tell me a saying about". Translated from Muslim's Arabic, quoted whole
   including the Abu Usamah variant note. Muslim's Arabic reads قل آمنت بالله فاستقم (the tafsirs quote ثم استقم);
   the article's "then stand straight" renders the fa of sequence. Grading: in Sahih Muslim; no upgrade.
2. **Bukhari 39** (Darussalam) — https://quranx.com/hadith/Bukhari/DarusSalam/Hadith-39 (saved). Attached to
   the verse by al-Baghawi, who narrates it through Muhammad ibn Isma'il (al-Bukhari) from 'Abd as-Salam ibn
   Mutahhar, the same chain as the page. First ten words: "The religion is ease, and no one contends with the".
   Translated from the Arabic, whole. Grading: in Sahih al-Bukhari.
3. **Tirmidhi 3297** — https://quranx.com/hadith/Tirmidhi/Hadith-3297 (saved). Not named by any fetched tafsir;
   used as "a related report" because the tafsirs relay Ibn 'Abbas's "Hud and its sisters" remark. Quoted whole
   (Abu Bakr's question and the five surahs). First ten words: "Abu Bakr (RA) said, O Messenger of Allah, you have".
   Grading: at-Tirmidhi's own, hasan gharib, plus his note that he knows it from Ibn 'Abbas only by this route.
   The article states it names five surahs and no verse; the link to 11:112 is the tafsirs' Ibn 'Abbas remark.
   Orchestrator may prefer to drop this paragraph if the watch-point is read strictly ("only if a fetched tafsir
   cites it"): the tafsirs cite the remark but no collection.
- Ibn 'Abbas "no verse harder" remark: kept with al-Qurtubi / al-Baghawi / Ma'arif attribution, no grading
  (none stated by them).
- Ad-Darimi report (Ibn 'Abbas's counsel): kept with al-Qurtubi's attribution; not checkable via hadith.js; no grading.
