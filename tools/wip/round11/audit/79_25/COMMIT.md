Card and 8-section bilingual article for 79:25, fa-akhadhahu llahu nakala
al-akhirati wa-l-ula (5 words). 79:15-24 used as the story frame; 79:26 named only.

Sources: Tabari, Ibn Kathir (ar), Qurtubi, Baghawi, Sa'di, all on 79:25;
Muyassar (grouped 79:23-26, 79:25 clause only), Ma'arif (grouped 79:25-34,
79:25 paragraph only). Ibn Kathir en skipped (wrong group).
- Four glosses of al-akhira wa-l-ula kept side by side, none chosen:
  this world and the next, drowning and Fire (al-Hasan, Qatada, Ibn Zayd's
  "others", Baghawi, Muyassar, Sa'di, Ma'arif); his two sayings, 28:38 then
  79:24, forty years apart (Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, ash-Sha'bi, ad-Dahhak,
  'Ikrima, Khaythama); denial first, the boast last (Abu Razin, Qatada in
  Qurtubi); first and last of his deeds (Mujahid via Mansur; Qurtubi has
  "his life").
- Disagreement kept: Tabari leads with the two sayings; Ibn Kathir calls
  this world and the next "correct, without doubt".
- Nakal: Qurtubi's definition and n-k-l lexicon, three i'rab accounts
  (az-Zajjaj, dropped preposition, al-Farra'); root itself left to 2:66.
- Ibn Kathir's 11:99 and 28:41 quoted; 28:41-42, 10:92, 26:66 pointed to
  by number. Licensing sentence in both languages.
Hadith: none attached by any fetched tafsir; said so. Asbab: none.
Dropped: unvocalised chain names, the "'Amir" report, al-Kalbi's terse
gloss, Qurtubi's al-Muzzammil pointer; 10:90-92 and 40:46 (not cited).
