## Glosses of "nakal al-akhira wa-l-ula", all reported with holders, none chosen
1. This world and the next (drowning / Fire): al-Hasan, Qatada (Tabari, Baghawi, Qurtubi), Ibn Zayd's "others", Ibn Kathir (declared correct), Muyassar, Sa'di, Ma'arif.
2. His two sayings (28:38 first, 79:24 last), forty years apart: Tabari's own lead, Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, ash-Sha'bi, ad-Dahhak, 'Ikrimah (Qurtubi), Khaythamah, Mujahid and a group (Baghawi).
3. First = denial and disobedience, last = "ana rabbukum": Abu Razin (Tabari); Qatada also (Qurtubi, "denial of Musa"); Ibn Kathir's qila "kufruhu wa 'isyanuhu".
4. First and last of his deeds: Mujahid via Mansur (Tabari); Qurtubi's text has "awwal 'umrihi wa akhirihi" (life, not deeds) — reported as each text gives it.
