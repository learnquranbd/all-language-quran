Card and bilingual article (8 sections, 1,758 EN words) for Iblis's plea,
"My Lord, then grant me respite until the Day they are raised", read as
the request that follows the curse in 15:34-35. 15:33 is pointed to, not
repeated.

Sources: at-Tabari gives the fa its cause (since You have put me out and
cursed me, postpone me); the Muyassar adds "in the world" and names the
day as the Resurrection; Ibn Kathir (ar) names the motive as the fullness
of his envy of Adam and his offspring, once the wrath was final;
al-Baghawi: he wanted not to die; as-Sa'di (on 36-38) glosses anzirni as
amhilni and says the answer was no honour but a test for him and for the
servants, hence the warning. Ibn Kathir abridged (grouped 15:34-38) and
Ma'arif (grouped 15:31-40) named as grouped only; neither speaks to the
request. Al-Qurtubi's returned text is off-verse and set aside.
Qur'an parallels 7:14-15, 38:79-81 and 17:62 compared on wording only.

Hadith: Bukhari 4686 (Allah gives respite to the oppressor), sahih, and
Tirmidhi 3537 (repentance accepted before the death rattle), graded hasan
gharib by at-Tirmidhi; both marked general, not attached to the verse.

Dropped: the identity of the known time and whether the full request was
granted (no fetched text says; deferred to 15:38); any comment on "Rabbi";
Sa'id b. Jubayr's report on the curse. Plain statement that the verse
licenses nothing against any living person or community.
