Replaces the 905-word, 6-section article with an 8-section, 1,769-word article
built only from the eight tafsir files fetched for 29:69.

Placement: the surah's last verse (at-Tabari, Ibn Kathir and as-Sa'di all close
the surah here); 29:67-68 via Ibn Kathir (en); at-Tabari reads the strivers as
those who fought the fabricators of 29:68, with a licensing sentence in both
languages.
Who strives: Ibn Kathir (the Prophet, Companions, followers), as-Sa'di (migrants,
fighters, effort in His pleasure, guided because muhsinun), Muyassar (enemies,
self, Shaytan, patience).
Disagreement kept: fighting (at-Tabari, Ibn Zayd, al-Baghawi) vs a general
striving revealed before fighting was prescribed (as-Suddi, Ibn Atiyyah in
al-Qurtubi); Ma'arif holds both. No position.
Other readings with holders: ad-Darani's greater jihad, al-Hasan, Ibn Abbas
(obedience), ad-Dahhak (hijra), Sahl (Sunnah), acting on knowledge (Abbas
al-Hamdani, Ibn Abbas, Ibrahim ibn Adham, Abu ad-Darda'), Umar ibn Abd al-Aziz
with 2:282, Fudayl, as-Sa'di on knowledge as jihad, ad-Darani's caution.
Fina and subulana glosses attributed; 19:76 via al-Baghawi; Sufyan ibn Uyaynah.
Ma'a al-muhsinin only in the tafsirs' words, incl. al-Qurtubi's two withnesses.
Hadith: Bukhari 50 (ihsan exchange), attached by Ibn Kathir, marked general.
The "whoever acts on what he knows" report is cited by al-Qurtubi without chain
or source; the article says so and does not treat it as confirmed.
Isa (AS) saying via ash-Sha'bi given as a report, not a hadith.
Dropped from the old article: fi-vs-ilayna argument, oath-form and tense
sequencing claims, 6:153/1:6 comparisons, "paths of earning/service" attributed
to commentators, Meccan-pressure framing, the unsourced practical section.
