Card and eight-section bilingual article for 14:49 (1,739 EN words, 20
paragraphs): the criminals seen that Day bound together in fetters.

Sources: Ibn Kathir ar, Qurtubi, Baghawi, Sa'di, Muyassar named on the
verse; Ma'arif (168, grouped 14:48-15:2) named with its group stated.
Tabari's fetched text is his 14:48 comment only; named once for his
restraint on the changed earth (silver, fire or bread all "possible", no
binding report). 14:48, 14:50 and 14:51 are single context pointers.

"Muqarranin": readings kept side by side, no position. Bound to one
another (Baghawi); each sort with its sort (Ibn Kathir, with 37:22, 81:7,
25:13, 38:37-38); each disbeliever with his devil, via 37:22 qurana'
(Qurtubi, Baghawi, both "qila"); gathered in fetters as they gathered on
sins (Qurtubi, "qila"); hands and feet to necks (Baghawi "qila",
Muyassar); each group of one deed in chains of fire (Sa'di), echoed by
Ma'arif. "Asfad": quyud per Ibn Kathir with Ibn 'Abbas, Sa'id ibn Jubayr,
al-A'mash, Ibn Zayd; aghlal wa-quyud per Qurtubi, with his lexical note
that safad is also a gift "because it binds", flagged as a note on the
word, not his reading of the verse.

Hadith: none attached to 14:49 in any fetched tafsir; said once. The
Muslim narration in Ibn Kathir (169) belongs to 14:50 and is not used.

The article states in both languages that the verse describes the
Hereafter, names no living person and licenses no contempt; its warning
turns on the reader's own companions and deeds.

Dropped: Tabari's and Ma'arif's 14:48 reports; the 169 gloss of 81:7;
unsourced labels for 'Amr ibn Kulthum.
