at-Tabari and al-'Awfi in Ibn Kathir; Mujahid, Abu Malik, as-Suddi, ar-Rabi',
ad-Dahhak, Ibn Zayd), adab of the Qur'an ('Atiyya, 'Ali, al-Hasan), Qatada's
command-and-prohibition gloss; at-Tabari's own view fuses adab and religion.
'Ala as elevation from as-Sa'di only.

Kept as a disagreement: al-Mawardi (apparent sense is a noble nature) vs
al-Qurtubi ('A'isha's report the soundest; khuluq lexically acquired, khim
inborn); Ibn Kathir holds both. Why 'azim: al-Junayd, gathered virtues, 7:199
(al-Qurtubi, al-Baghawi); as-Sa'di's 7:199, 3:159, 9:128 and his portrait.

Hadith: Muslim 746 (opening portion of the long Sa'd ibn Hisham hadith,
marked), Bukhari 6038 (Anas, ten years), Tirmidhi 2016 and 2002 (both hasan
sahih per at-Tirmidhi). "Sent to perfect character" only as Ibn Kathir's
citation from Ahmad. Asbab: none in the fetched texts.
