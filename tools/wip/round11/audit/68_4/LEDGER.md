## Disagreement kept (no position taken)
Al-Mawardi (via al-Qurtubi): the apparent meaning is a noble nature (tab' karim). Al-Qurtubi: 'A'isha's report in Sahih Muslim is the soundest view, and lexically khuluq is acquired (al-tab' al-mutakallaf). Ibn Kathir holds both (Qur'an became his disposition, together with innate character Allah created in him). Religion vs adab readings: at-Tabari fuses them; Ma'arif says almost the same.
