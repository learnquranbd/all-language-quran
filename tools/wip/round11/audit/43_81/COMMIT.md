Card and 8-section bilingual article for 43:81: qul in kana li-r-Rahmani
waladun fa-ana awwalu al-'abidin. 43:79-80 as placement; 43:82 one line
as continuation; 43:59 a one-line pointer on the particle in only.

Sources: Tabari, Muyassar, Ibn Kathir (ar; en abridged), Qurtubi, Sa'di,
Baghawi, Ma'arif (grouped file, 43:81 portion only).
- Dispute (a), in conditional vs negative (= ma), kept open: negative
  from Ibn 'Abbas, Qatada, Ibn Zayd (with 14:46), Zayd ibn Aslam;
  conditional from as-Suddi. Qurtubi groups as-Suddi with the negative
  too; the conflict is stated. Tabari's preference for the conditional
  and his reasons, al-Mahdawi's (via Qurtubi) and Ibn Kathir's weighing
  reported as theirs; the article picks no side.
- Conditional argument framed as the tafsirs frame it: IK (condition
  implies neither occurrence nor possibility, 39:4), Qurtubi (debater's
  "if proved, I am first to believe"), Sa'di (messengers first to every
  good). Tabari's "not by way of doubt but gentle address" with 34:24,
  Qurtubi's tarqiq, Ma'arif's debate etiquette.
- Dispute (b), al-'abidin worshippers vs disdaining/denying, kept open:
  Tabari's "others", Thawri and Bukhari's gloss via IK, Qurtubi's
  lexicographers; objections from Qurtubi (Ibn 'Arafa, the alif-less
  reading), Baghawi and IK's fihi nazar.
- Addressees named only as Tabari and Muyassar name them; licensing
  sentence in sec 8, EN and BN.
Hadith: none attached to the verse in any source; said in one sentence.
The 'Uthman-era report is mentioned only as Tabari's lexical witness.
Asbab: none in the sources; not used.
Dropped: Qurtubi's Kufan reading wuld (length), poetry lines.
