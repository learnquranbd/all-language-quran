## Disputes kept open (article takes no side in its own voice)
(a) in = conditional vs negative (= ma). Negative: Ibn 'Abbas (Tabari via 'Ali ibn Abi Talha; Baghawi), Qatada, Ibn Zayd, Zayd ibn Aslam (Tabari); Qurtubi groups Ibn 'Abbas, al-Hasan, as-Suddi. Conditional: as-Suddi (Tabari, Baghawi; Qurtubi "ayDan"). Preferences reported as theirs: Tabari (conditional, with reasons), al-Mahdawi via Qurtubi (ajwad), Ibn Kathir (aqrab). The as-Suddi attribution conflict between Tabari/Baghawi and Qurtubi is stated in the article.
(b) al-'abidin = worshippers vs disdaining/denying (anifin/jahidin). Disdain/deny: Tabari's "others", Sufyan ath-Thawri and al-Bukhari's gloss (both via Ibn Kathir), Abu Zayd, Abu 'Amr (via al-Jawhari), al-Kisa'i and al-Qutabi (via al-Mawardi), Ibn al-A'rabi, Abu 'Ubayda (all via Qurtubi), Baghawi (qila). Objections: "some scholars" + Ibn 'Arafa (Qurtubi), Baghawi's qawm, Ibn Kathir's fihi nazar. Ibn Kathir's "two forms in use" also reported.

## Hadith
- None used. No fetched tafsir attaches a prophetic hadith to 43:81; the article says so in one sentence (sec 7).
- The 'Uthman/'Ali report (Tabari: Yunus < Ibn Wahb < Ibn Abi Dhi'b < Abu Qusayt < Ba'ja ibn Zayd al-Juhani; also IK, Qurtubi "ruwiya") is cited by the mufassirun only as a lexical witness for 'abida = istankafa. Article mentions it only as that, explicitly "not as an explanation of the verse from the Prophet"; no grading given by Tabari, none claimed; case details (six-month birth, stoning order) not narrated.
- Al-Bukhari's gloss is reported only as Ibn Kathir reports it (a lexical note, not a hadith); no number cited.

## Context limits kept
- 43:82: one sentence as continuation (sec 1); 43:83-85 not mentioned.
- 43:59: one-line pointer only, on the particle in (sec 2); its servanthood/favour content not repeated.
