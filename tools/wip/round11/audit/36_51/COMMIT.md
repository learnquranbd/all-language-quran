The resurrection blowing: out of the graves and hurrying to their
Lord, with both senses of yansilūn kept.

Sources gave:
- at-Tabari: nafkhat al-baʿth; ajdāth = graves, jadath/jadaf as
  two dialects (al-ʿĀliya / as-Sāfila); Ibn ʿAbbās and Qatāda:
  yansilūn = they come out; nasalān = haste in walking.
- al-Qurtubi: the second blowing, "two, not three"; Qatāda's ṣūr =
  forms vs an-Naḥḥās's ṣūr = the horn; ajdāf reading (az-Zamakhsharī);
  yansilūn = come out (nasl, offspring) and = hasten (wolf's gait),
  joined as "they come out hastening"; 31:28, 54:7, 70:43.
- as-Sa'di: first blast of terror and death, this of raising; no
  power to linger; deniers' grief and remorse.
- al-Baghawi (last blowing; nasl), al-Muyassar (second; souls
  returned), Ibn Kathir (third; walking quickly, 70:43), Ma'arif
  (39:68 harmonised; disbelievers driven by compulsion).

Disagreement kept: second (al-Qurtubi) vs third (Ibn Kathir) blast,
both named, no position; Qatāda vs an-Naḥḥās on aṣ-ṣūr.

Hadith: Sahih al-Bukhari 4935 (Abu Hurayra, "between the two blowings
forty", unit withheld; coccyx bone), quoted whole, sound by its place
in the Sahih, marked not attached to the verse. Fresh, not a stock repeat.

Qurtubi's al-Mubārak ibn Faḍāla / al-Ḥasan "forty years" report:
attributed to his tafsir as a report, no Companion in the chain as
given, not called a hadith, no grading, not presented as chronology.

Dropped: Qurtubi's chainless "ʿalaykum bil-nasl" khabar; 36:52
content left for its own verse. No asbab.
