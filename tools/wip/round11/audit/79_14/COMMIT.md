Card and 8-section bilingual article for 79:14, fa-idha hum bis-sahira
(3 words). 79:10-13 used as context only (the deniers' words, reported
as theirs); 79:15-26 named only as the next story.

Sources: Tabari, Ibn Kathir (ar), Qurtubi, Baghawi, Sa'di, Muyassar
(grouped 79:13-14), Ma'arif (grouped 79:5-14). Ibn Kathir en wrong group.
- Referent of hum kept as a disagreement: the deniers (Tabari) vs all
  creatures (Qurtubi, Sa'di).
- Sahira glosses listed, none chosen: whole earth (Ibn Abbas et al. in IK),
  face/back of the earth (Tabari, 'Ikrima, al-Hasan, ad-Dahhak, Ibn Zayd),
  level place (Mujahid), al-Sham (Sufyan), tract between Jabal Hassan and
  Jabal Ariha / Bayt al-Maqdis ('Uthman b. Abi al-'Atika), mountain by
  Bayt al-Maqdis (Wahb), Jahannam (Qatada), white / renewed / seventh /
  silver earth (Qurtubi). IK's "all gharib" verdict reported as his.
- Etymology from wakefulness (Tabari, al-Farra' in Qurtubi, Baghawi);
  'ayn sahira vs na'ima (Qurtubi); Umayya and Dhu Qar verses.
- Cross-refs from IK's ar-Rabi' passage: 14:48, 20:105-107, 18:47 (IK's
  bracket says 105-106; quoted words reach 107).
Hadith: none attached; Sahl's white earth reported as his words via
Ibn Abi Hatim. Asbab: none.
Licensing sentence in both languages; no group named.
Dropped: Bukhari 6521 (no fetched tafsir cites it), "earth once asleep" gloss (not in sources), Ariha=Jericho,
al-Qurtubi's lexical tangents, Ma'arif's Musa-story link.
