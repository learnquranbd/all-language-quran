Range kept, no choice made (§4.1): argument + end (al-Baghawi), outcome (Ibn Kathir), argument + strength for the messengers and the end for the host (al-Muyassar), a mighty help establishing the religion (as-Sa'di), success and falah (at-Tabari).
Grammar (§2.2): al-Qurtubi's 'ala al-ma'na and ash-Shaybani's ra's ayah both recorded, no side taken.
Licensing sentence in both languages (§4.2): "licenses nothing against any living person or community".

Watch-point precision notes:
- Ibn Kathir's 58:21 and 40:51 appear in the abridged English under 37:171 (group), not in his Arabic on 37:173, which says only «تكون لهم العاقبة». The article attributes them to the abridged English and names the group.
- In al-Muyassar «بالحجة والقوة» qualifies the messengers' nusra (37:171-172); the host's prevailing in 37:173 is «باعتبار العاقبة والمآل». The article keeps that split.
