Card and 8-section bilingual article for 37:173, the close of the promise in 37:171-173,
turning from the angels' answer (37:164-166) to the messengers and Allah's host.

Sources: Tabari, Qurtubi, Ibn Kathir (ar/en), Baghawi, Sa'di, Muyassar, Ma'arif (group 37:171-179).
- Who the jund are: Tabari, hizb Allah and ahl wilayatihi, theirs zafar and falah over those
  who disbelieve; Baghawi, hizb Allah, ghalaba by hujja and nusra in the 'aqiba; Ibn Kathir,
  the 'aqiba is theirs (en: 58:21, 40:51, text checked); Sa'di, a mighty help to establish
  the religion, his "fights those he was commanded to fight" reported in his wording, not
  as a general ruling; Muyassar, al-mujahidun fi sabilina, prevailing by 'aqiba and ma'al.
- Range kept as the commentators' (argument / outcome / strength); no gloss chosen.
- Grammar: Qurtubi's plural 'ala al-ma'na (cf. 38:11) and ash-Shaybani's ra's ayah, both kept.
- Ma'arif's reconciliation (most prophets saved, the few kept ascendancy in argument, trace
  deferred; Thanavi's highway robber) and its obedience condition for "Our army", attributed.
- Licensing sentence in both languages; no prophet called defeated in the article's voice.
Hadith: none attached by any fetched tafsir, said plainly. Khaybar left for 37:177. No asbab.
Dropped: Ma'arif's second-hand Ibn Abbas quote; 58:22; lexicon claims on j-n-d / gh-l-b.
Precision: Ibn Kathir's 58:21/40:51 sit in the abridged English under 37:171, not his Arabic;
Muyassar's bil-hujja wal-quwwa qualifies the messengers' help, the host's victory is by 'aqiba.
Themes: trust, patience.
Fixed at merge: "no commentary turns it into permission for violence" overreached (Sa'di, Muyassar mention fighting/mujahidun); now "none draws a ruling from this verse; a promise, not an order", EN and BN.
