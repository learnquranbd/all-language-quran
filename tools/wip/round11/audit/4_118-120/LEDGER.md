# SOURCES LEDGER: 4:118-120 (assigned 4:119, widened)

Key widened from 4:119 to 4:118-120. The vow starts in 4:118 (la-attakhidhanna ...
nasiban mafrudan), runs through 4:119, and 4:120 is Allah's verdict on it.
`prep.js 4:118-120` was run. 4:117 and 4:121-124 stay free. 4:123 and 4:122 are cited
once each as sister verses in §7. The older `audit/4_119/` folder came from an earlier prep
and was not touched.

## Tafsir fetched (IDs 14, 15, 16, 90, 91, 94, 168, 169 on 4:118, 4:119 and 4:120)

Named in the article: at-Tabari (15), Ibn Kathir ar (14), al-Qurtubi (90), as-Sa'di (91),
al-Baghawi (94), al-Muyassar (16).
Not named: Ma'arif (168). Its grouped text for 4:116-122 discusses only 4:116 (shirk, kinds
of zulm) and has nothing on 4:118-120. Ibn Kathir en (169) was only a cross-check. It
mistranslates "أزين لهم ترك التوبة" as "tempting them to feign repentance", so the article
follows the Arabic (t14_119).

§4
- at-Tabari: "البتك القطع، وهو في هذا الموضع: قطع أذن البحيرة ليعلم أنها بحيرة". Qatadah, 10445: "كانوا يبتكون آذانها لطواغيتهم". 'Ikrimah, 10447: "دين شرعه لهم إبليس، كهيئة البحائر والسيب". at-Tabari: "بعبادة غيرك من الأوثان والأنداد حتى ينسكوا له، ويحرموا ويحللوا له، ويشرعوا غير الذي شرعته لهم" (t15_118).
- as-Sa'di: "وهذا نوع من الإضلال يقتضي تحريم ما أحل الله أو تحليل ما حرم الله" (t91_119). 5:103 is the Qur'an itself; as-Sa'di names the four animals listed there.
- Castration view (at-Tabari, t15_118): Ibn 'Abbas 10448 ("كره الإخصاء وقال: فيه نزلت"), Anas 10449, 10450 and 10459, 'Ikrimah 10454-10457. Ibn Kathir (t14_119) and al-Qurtubi (t90_119) give the same three names.
- Religion view (at-Tabari, t15_118): Ibn 'Abbas via 'Ali 10463; Ibrahim 10464 (Ibn Kathir and al-Qurtubi call him an-Nakha'i); Mujahid 10470-10474; Qatadah 10475-10476; as-Suddi 10478; ad-Dahhak 10479, who cites 30:30. Mujahid 10471 recites "ذلك الدين القيم". Mujahid 10472: "الفطرة دين الله". Mujahid 10473: "الفطرة، الدين".
- al-Hasan: tattooing in 10483-10485; religion in 10475 ("أي: دين الله، في قول الحسن وقتادة"). Ibn Kathir lists him under both.
- at-Tabari's tarjih: "وأولى الأقوال بالصواب ... دين الله. وذلك لدلالة الآية الأخرى ... فطرة الله ... دخل في ذلك فعل كل ما نهى الله عنه: من خصاء ما لا يجوز خصاؤه، ووشم ما نهى عن وشمه ووشره ... ودخل فيه ترك كل ما أمر الله به". His language argument: "لأن تبتيك آذان الأنعام من تغيير خلق الله الذي هو أجسام ... إذ كان الفصيح في كلام العرب أن يترجم عن المجمل من الكلام بالمفسر، وبالخاص عن العام، دون الترجمة عن المفسر بالمجمل".
- as-Sa'di holds both: "وهذا يتناول تغيير الخلقة الظاهرة بالوشم ... ويتناول أيضا تغيير الخلقة الباطنة، فإن الله تعالى خلق عباده حنفاء مفطورين على قبول الحق وإيثاره" (t91_119).
- al-Muyassar: "ولأدعونهم إلى تغيير خلق الله في الفطرة، وهيئة ما عليه الخلق" (t16_119).
- The article reports at-Tabari's preference as his own and declares no winner.

## Asbab an-nuzul
Not used. No fetched source gives an established occasion for 4:118-120. The statements
"فيه نزلت" attributed to Ibn 'Abbas and Anas (at-Tabari 10448-10449) are interpretive
remarks about castration, not occasion reports. Ma'arif's sequence note (the thief of 4:105ff
apostatised) is from Bayan al-Qur'an and concerns 4:116, so it was not used.

## Dropped, and why
- Muqatil ibn Hayyan's "999 of every 1,000 to the Fire" (Ibn Kathir, t14_118), al-Qurtubi's khabar "من كل ألف واحد لله", and his link to Muslim's ba'th an-nar: not needed, and they invite despair.
- al-Qurtubi on cursing Iblis by name and on cursing the living; the custom of a nail for the newborn: off topic.
- The Mujahid-'Ikrimah exchange in at-Tabari 10455 and 10469 (Mujahid's harsh words about 'Ikrimah): not edifying, and not needed.
- Footnote 48 of at-Tabari's editor (Shakir) on the women of his own day: an editor's modern polemic, never used.
- al-Qurtubi's hadith of Abu al-Ahwas's father via al-Qadi Isma'il (splitting ears, "Allah's razor is sharper"): not verified, so dropped.
- al-Qurtubi's fiqh on sacrificial animals with cut ears, branding, human castration and hair extensions: out of scope, and close to a modern fatwa.
- Tawus's refusal to attend mixed-colour marriages, and Ibn al-'Arabi's correction from the Prophet's practice (al-Qurtubi): a good caution, cut for length.
- The sun, moon and stones reading (az-Zajjaj; Mujahid, ad-Dahhak, Sa'id ibn Jubayr, Qatadah, in al-Qurtubi and al-Baghawi): cut for length.
- as-Sa'di's citation of 2:111 ("tilka amaniyyuhum", naming two religious communities) and of 57:14 (the hypocrites): avoided under the no-targeting rule. His 18:103-104 was used instead.
- Ibn Kathir and at-Tabari on "khusranan mubinan" (losing both worlds; هلك هلاكا): not used beyond the §8 line.
