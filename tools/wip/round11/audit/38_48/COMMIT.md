Card and 8-section bilingual article for 38:48: Isma'il, al-Yasa' and Dhul-Kifl,
eight words and no story, read after the patriarchs of 38:45-47.

Sources: Tabari, Qurtubi, Ibn Kathir (ar, en), Sa'di, Muyassar, Ma'arif on 38:48;
Ibn Kathir, Tabari, Qurtubi, Sa'di on 21:85 and Tabari on 6:86, where the 38:48
commentaries send the reader.
- Purpose: Tabari, remember them and follow their patience and delivery (to the
  Prophet ﷺ); Muyassar/Sa'di, finest mention and praise.
- Isma'il = son of Ibrahim (Ibn Kathir, Tabari "sadiq al-wa'd", Sa'di); his
  separation from 38:45 noted with no reason given, since no source gives one.
- Al-Yasa': Ma'arif (named only at 6:86 and here; chronicles make him Ilyas's
  cousin and deputy; Kings refs); Tabari's lineage and al-Yasa'/al-Laysa' reading.
- Dhul-Kifl: Tabari's al-kifl = hazz/jadd; the pledge story (Mujahid via Tabari and
  Ibn Kathir; Ibn Abbas's dying judge).
- Disagreement kept, no position: prophet (Ibn Kathir's reading of context, al-Hasan,
  Sa'di, Qurtubi's 38:48 gloss) vs righteous man (Mujahid, Abu Musa with Ibn Kathir's
  munqati' note, Qurtubi's jumhur); Ibn Jarir suspended; Zakariya (qila).
- Al-akhyar (Muyassar, Sa'di) tied to 38:47; 21:85 "sabirin" with Sa'di's three
  kinds of patience.
Hadith: Tirmidhi 2496 (Ibn 'Umar, al-Kifl), attached by Qurtubi at 21:85, summarised
not quoted; Tirmidhi's own "hasan" kept; Ibn Kathir's gharib and "perhaps another
man" kept; stated that no hadith attaches to 38:48 itself. Licensing sentence in
both languages.
Cross-refs: 6:84-87, 4:163, 37:123 (not repeated).
Dropped: Qurtubi's raised form of Abu Musa's statement, Ka'b's king story, minor
etymologies, Abdullah ibn al-Harith version. Defect fixed: unsourced "Shaphat"
replaced by Ma'arif's "Safit". Asbab: none.
Arabic count: 38:48 eight words. Theme: character.
