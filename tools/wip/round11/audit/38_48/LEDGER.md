## Dropped
- Qurtubi's "وقال أبو موسى عن النبي صلى الله عليه وسلم : إن ذا الكفل لم يكن نبيا" — Tabari and Ibn Kathir carry it as Abu Musa's own statement (and Ibn Kathir calls one route munqati'); the raised form is ungraded and unconfirmed in any collection, so the article reports only Abu Musa's statement.
- Ka'b's story of the unbelieving king (Qurtubi); the "chaste man who stood surety for anyone in trial" and "Allah guaranteed him double the work of others" etymologies (Qurtubi qila) — not needed; dropped for length.
- Tabari's 'Abdullah ibn al-Harith version (unnamed prophet and a young man) — dropped for length.
- Any reason for Isma'il's separation from 38:45 — no fetched source gives one; the article says so.
- Bible form "Shaphat" (from memory) replaced with Ma'arif's "Safit".
- Watch-point wording "Ismail (al-Yasa')": the verse names three distinct figures; sources identify Isma'il as son of Ibrahim and al-Yasa' as Elisha.
