Tadabbur 33:6 — The Prophet is closer to the believers than their own
selves, and his wives are their mothers

Card plus an 8-section bilingual article (1758 EN words), source-gated to the
eight tafsirs pre-fetched for the verse.

What the sources gave:
- awlā = precedence of the Prophet's command and love over the self, not
  ownership (al-Baghawi: Ibn ʿAbbās/ʿAṭāʾ, Ibn Zayd's master's-decree image;
  as-Saʿdī; al-Qurtubi: more worthy to rule; Ibn Kathir ties to 4:65).
- al-Qurtubi's reason via Ibn ʿAṭiyya — the self calls to ruin, he calls to
  safety — anchored by the Ṣaḥīḥ Muslim "waistbands/moths" parable (Abū
  Hurayra; Jābir's "slipping out of my hands").
- "his wives are their mothers" kept to the mainstream fiqh: mothers in honour
  and in the permanent marriage-prohibition (33:53), not in khalwa, maḥram
  status, inheritance or hijab (Ibn Kathir, al-Baghawi/Shāfiʿī, Maʿarif,
  al-Muyassar, as-Saʿdī). Honour of the Mothers stated as creed, carrying
  nothing against anyone.
- inheritance clause: abrogates inheriting by the Muhājir–Anṣār brotherhood/
  hijra pact, restoring blood-kinship (Zubayr's account, Ibn Kathir & al-Qurtubi);
  maʿrūf to allies = a permissible bequest up to a third (al-Baghawi) / support
  (Mujāhid).

Disagreements kept with both holders, no verdict: whether the Mothers are also
mothers of believing women (ʿĀʾisha/Ibn al-ʿArabi restrict; al-Qurtubi general);
the non-canonical "and he is a father to them" reading (Ubayy/Ibn ʿAbbās) and
the ʿUmar–Ubayy exchange, with 33:40; and whether maʿrūf covers a bequest to
non-Muslim kin (Ibn al-Hanafiyya/Qatāda vs aṭ-Ṭabari).

Hadith: the Abū Hurayra "I am the closest of all people to him... recite 33:6..."
report quoted whole as Ṣaḥīḥ al-Bukhārī (Ibn Kathir: Bukhari alone, Kitāb
al-Istiqrāḍ; al-Qurtubi: the two Ṣaḥīḥs), plus the "dearer than his own self"
report and the ʿUmar exchange from the Ṣaḥīḥ.
