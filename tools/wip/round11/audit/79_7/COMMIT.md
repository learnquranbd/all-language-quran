Card and 8-section article on tatba'uha ar-radifa, the follower of 79:6.

Sources: at-Tabari, Ibn Kathir, al-Baghawi, al-Qurtubi, as-Sa'di and the
Muyassar, all fetched for 79:7. Ma'arif (grouped 79:5-14) says nothing on
the verse and is not named.

Glosses kept side by side: the second blast (Ibn Abbas, al-Hasan, Qatada,
ad-Dahhak; at-Tabari's own gloss; Muyassar: blast of bringing to life);
Mujahid's sky splitting and the land crushed (84:1, 69:14), filed by
at-Tabari under "others said" but by Ibn Kathir among the two-blasts view;
Ibn Zayd: the Hour; 'Ata': the raising; al-Qurtubi's qila: a quake that
ends the earths. No ranking given.

Hadith: Ubayy ibn Ka'b's night call, at-Tirmidhi 2457, quoted whole from
quranx, graded hasan by at-Tirmidhi; attached to the verse by at-Tabari.
The hour varies (page Arabic two thirds, page English a third, al-Qurtubi
and al-Baghawi a quarter) and the article says so.

The forty (Bukhari 4935) is cross-referenced to 78:18, not retold.
Dropped: at-Tabari's three-blowings report (two unnamed narrators),
Qatada's "it was mentioned to us" rain report, Ahmad's variant ending.
39:68 cited because al-Hasan recites it in at-Tabari.
