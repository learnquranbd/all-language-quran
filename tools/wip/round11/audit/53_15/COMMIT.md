Card and 8-section bilingual article on 'indaha jannatu l-ma'wa.
53:13-14 and 53:16 as context only; the Tree and its covering left to
their own verses.

Sources: at-Tabari, al-Muyassar, al-Qurtubi, as-Sa'di, al-Baghawi,
Ma'arif al-Qur'an. Ibn Kathir (ar) unfetchable (503); en wrong group.
- "Near it" = near Sidrat al-Muntaha (Tabari, Qurtubi, Sa'di, Muyassar).
- Ma'wa: abode / original home of Adam and Hawwa' (Ma'arif); refuge of
  believers' souls, or of Jibril and Mika'il (Qurtubi); where wishes
  reach their end (Sa'di). 79:41 as a Qur'anic pointer only.
- Holders kept side by side: martyrs (Tabari; Ibn 'Abbas; Qatadah;
  Muqatil and al-Kalbi per Baghawi), the God-fearing (al-Hasan;
  Muyassar), 32:19 pointer (Ibn 'Abbas via Abu al-'Aliyah), all
  believers' souls, Adam, Jibril and the angels ('Ata' from Ibn 'Abbas).
  Ibn 'Abbas reported three ways; not reconciled.
- Location left unreconciled: above the 7th heaven (Sa'di), on it under
  the Throne (Ma'arif), beneath / right of the Throne (Qurtubi, Tabari).
- Variant reading jannahu (Qurtubi: 'Ali, Abu Hurayrah, Anas et al.;
  Mujahid, al-Akhfash) recorded without judgement.
Hadith: none attached to 53:15 by any fetched tafsir; stated in one
paragraph, no Mi'raj narration quoted. Asbab: none.
Dropped: Qurtubi's unclear "jannat al-mabit" gloss; Ma'arif's 52:6
Hell deduction and excavation aside.
