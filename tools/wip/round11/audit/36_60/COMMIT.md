Card + 8-section bilingual article (1,631 EN words) for 36:60, Allah's
question to the separated criminals: "did I not charge you, O children of
Adam, not to worship Satan? He is to you a clear enemy."

Sources: at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi, as-Sa'di, Ibn Kathir, al-Baghawi,
al-Muyassar, Ma'arif al-Qur'an, each quoted from the text fetched for 36:60.
- Setting: Tabari's elided "then it will be said", following straight on
  36:59; Ibn Kathir "taqriʿ" and Muyassar "rebuke and reminder".
- ʿAhd = wasiyya conveyed "on the tongues of the messengers" (Qurtubi,
  Sa'di, Muyassar), "in this world" (Tabari): revelation-mediated, not
  the primordial covenant (7:172 deliberately not brought in).
- Worship glossed as obedience in disobedience to Allah (Tabari, Qurtubi,
  Baghawi, Sa'di, Muyassar); Ma'arif's objection-and-answer that no one
  needed to bow to Satan. Kisaʾi's "la of prohibition" via Qurtubi.
- Framing difference kept, both named: Ibn Kathir names the addressees
  (the disbelievers); Sa'di widens what is rebuked to every kufr and sin.
- "Clear enemy": Tabari's brief backstory (refused prostration out of envy,
  deceived Adam until he and his wife left the Garden), Sa'di's "utmost
  warning", Ibn Kathir's obeyed-the-enemy / disobeyed-ar-Rahman contrast.
- Hadith: Sahih al-Bukhari 2886 (slave of the dinar), via Ma'arif's use on
  this verse; flagged general, Bukhari's Israʾil note reported; Ma'arif's
  "servant of the spouse" phrase not attributed to Bukhari. Stock Bukhari
  660 not used.
- Licensing: verse addresses humanity and licenses nothing against any
  living person or community (both languages).
- Dropped: Ma'arif's "(even to the Jinn)"; 36:61-62 content left for later.
