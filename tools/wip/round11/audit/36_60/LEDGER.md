Difference of framing kept (not a contradiction, both named, no position): Ibn Kathir names the
addressees as al-kafara among Bani Adam; as-Sa'di widens what is rebuked to all kinds of kufr and maʿasi.
The article states explicitly that as-Sa'di does not place every sinner among the criminals of 36:59
(this is a reading of his sentence, not an added claim).

## Hadith

- Sahih al-Bukhari 2886 (Dar-us-Salam), Abu Hurayra. URL fetched via hadith.js:
  https://quranx.com/hadith/Bukhari/DarusSalam/Hadith-2886 (saved in this folder).
  First ten words (EN): "Let the slave of Dinar and Dirham of Quantify and Khamisa"
  Arabic: «تَعِسَ عَبْدُ الدِّينَارِ وَالدِّرْهَمِ وَالْقَطِيفَةِ وَالْخَمِيصَةِ، إِنْ أُعْطِيَ رَضِيَ، وَإِنْ لَمْ يُعْطَ لَمْ يَرْضَ»
  Grading: in al-Bukhari's Sahih; no upgrade. Al-Bukhari's own note after it, «لَمْ يَرْفَعْهُ إِسْرَائِيلُ عَنْ أَبِي حَصِينٍ», reported in the article.
  The page's English "Quantify" is an obvious transliteration slip for al-qatifa (Arabic confirms); article writes "qatifa".
  Attachment: brought in by Ma'arif (t168) on this verse to explain worship = obedience; marked in the article
  as a general narration, not attached to the verse as occasion.
