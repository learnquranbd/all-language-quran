Replaces the 739-word, 6-section article with a 1,769-word, 8-section
deep article on the heavens and earth joined and parted, and life from water.

Sources: at-Tabari, Ibn Kathir (ar + en), al-Qurtubi, as-Sa'di,
al-Baghawi, al-Muyassar, Ma'arif al-Qur'an, all on 21:30.
- Placement after 21:24-29; Ibn Kathir's framing (creation proves sole
  worship); one line each on 21:16, 21:19, 21:31-33, 21:35.
- A-wa-lam yara as knowing: Tabari's eyes of the heart, Muyassar,
  Baghawi, Qurtubi, Sa'di, Ma'arif. Qira'a without the waw (Ibn Kathir
  the reciter, Meccan mushaf) from Baghawi and Qurtubi.
- Ratq/fatq lexicon (Tabari, Baghawi, Ma'arif) and grammar: masdar,
  dual kanata (Tabari, al-Akhfash with 35:41, Abu Ishaq).
- Dispute kept with no position: one mass parted (Ibn 'Abbas, al-Hasan,
  Qatadah, Sa'id ibn Jubayr), one layer into seven (Mujahid, as-Suddi,
  Abu Salih), rain and plants ('Ikrimah with 86:11-12, 'Atiyyah, Ibn
  Zayd), night before day. Ibn 'Umar's "ask that old man" report.
  Preferences attributed: Tabari and Ma'arif for rain, Ibn Kathir blends.
  Taqi Usmani's Big Bang note attributed with his own caveat.
- Water: Qurtubi's three ta'wilat, Abu al-'Aliyah, Tabari on plants,
  Baghawi with 24:45, Qurtubi on general "every" (27:23, 46:25).
Hadith: "everything was created from water" (Abu Hurayrah) as Ibn Kathir
carries it from Ibn Abi Hatim and Ahmad, with his chain remark; Qurtubi
cites Ibn Hibban. Not checked in either collection; the article says so.
Dropped from the old article: 21:22, 25:54, 3:191 (no tafsir cites them).
Also dropped: Ka'b's wind report, Qurtubi's seven-earths narrative.
Fixed: the old article's two water glosses; Qurtubi gives three.
