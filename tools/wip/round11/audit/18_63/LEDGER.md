# SOURCES ledger — 18:63

Beat: the fish sign and the forgetting (Mūsā–al-Khiḍr, 18:60–82). Not the boat/boy/wall
(future ground), not the patience exchange of 18:67–68 (sibling draft covers 18:68), not
the retracing/finding of 18:64–65, not the prophet-vs-walī question.

## Tafsir fetched (all from PREP; none re-fetched)
- t14 Ibn Kathir (ar) — read; used for the grammar note only (brief).
- t15 at-Tabari (ar) — USED. Arabic clauses relied on:
  - Speaker = servant: «قال فتى موسى لموسى حين قال له آتنا غداءنا … أرأيت إذا أوينا إلى الصخرة».
  - Grammar of «أن أذكره»: «فأن في موضع نصب ردّا على الحوت … وما أنساني أن أذكر الحوت إلا الشيطان».
  - Ibn Masʿūd variant: «وقد ذكر أن ذلك في مصحف عبد الله: وما أنسانيه أن أذكره إلا الشيطان».
  - Rock location: «الصخرة التي … دون نهر الذئب على الطريق» (footnote: نهر الزيت).
  - ʿajaban readings: Mujāhid «موسى يعجب من أثر الحوت في البحر ودوراته التي غاب فيها»;
    Ibn ʿAbbās (Saʿīd b. Jubayr) «كان سرب الحوت في البحر لموسى عجبا»; Ibn Zayd «عجب … حوت
    كان يوكل منه أدهرا … ثم صار حيا».
- t16 al-Muyassar (ar) — USED. «فإن الحوت الميت دبَّتْ فيه الحياة، وقفز في البحر، واتخذ له
  فيه طريقًا، وكان أمره مما يُعْجَبُ منه»; and the servant forgot to *tell* Mūsā «نسيت أن
  أخبرك ما كان من الحوت».
- t90 al-Qurtubi (ar) — USED. Clauses:
  - Grammar «بدل اشتمال … أي وما أنساني ذكره إلا الشيطان»; Ibn Masʿūd variant.
  - Names the servant + apology frame: «وهذا إنما ذكره يوشع في معرض الاعتذار».
  - Three ʿajaban readings: servant's speech (wonder to people) / end-of-report + fresh
    «عجبا لهذا الأمر» / God's report about Mūsā or the fish. Wonder = «حوت قد مات فأكل شقه
    الأيسر ثم حيي».
  - Spring: Bukhārī via Ibn ʿAbbās «حيي لأنه مسه ماء عين هناك تدعى عين الحياة»; Tirmidhī /
    Sufyān «يزعم ناس أن تلك الصخرة عندها عين الحياة»; wudū-drop account; closes «والله أعلم».
- t91 as-Saʿdī (ar) — USED. «الصخرة المعروفة»; «لأنه السبب في ذلك» (Satan the cause);
  «كان ذلك المسلك للحوت سربا، ولموسى وفتاه عجبا»; the promise «إذا فقد الحوت وجد الخضر»
  (used only for the sign = loss marks the place; did NOT narrate the meeting).
- t94 al-Baghawi (ar) — USED. Rock «دون نهر الزيت» (Maʿqil b. Ziyād); the servant rose to
  tell Mūsā then forgot «قام ليدرك موسى فيخبره فنسي أن يخبره فمكثا … حتى صليا الظهر من الغد»;
  ellipsis «نسيت أن أذكر لك أمر الحوت»; ʿajaban as servant's OR Mūsā's amazement; report
  «كان للحوت سربا ولموسى وفتاه عجبا»; Ibn Zayd.
- t168 Maʿārif (en, grouped 61–64) — USED. sarab = tunnel; water did not obstruct;
  forgetting attributed to both like 55:22 though the servant forgot; second mention ʿajaban
  when Yūshaʿ related it; "no contradiction" between saraban and ʿajaban. NOT used: the
  Mūsā-over-Khiḍr precedence / Khiḍr-prophethood discussion (would preempt watch-point 5).
- t169 Ibn Kathir (en, grouped 60–65) — USED. Spring ʿAyn al-Hayāt, fish revived, jumped,
  sarab; "forgetfulness attributed to them both even though it was actually Yushaʿ who
  forgot" + 55:22; second Bukhārī narration: spring at base of rock. Also the full Ubayy
  hadith text (cross-check for the hadith fetched below).

## Hadith
- PREP's number was WRONG: `hadith.js Bukhari 660` returns the "seven under the shade"
  hadith (Abū Hurayra), unrelated. Not used.
- USED: **Ṣaḥīḥ al-Bukhārī 3401** (Dar-us-Salam; USC-MSA Vol 4, Book 55, Hadith 613).
  - URL: https://quranx.com/hadith/Bukhari/DarusSalam/Hadith-3401 (saved via hadith.js).
  - First ten words (EN): "Narrated Saʿid bin Jubair: I said to Ibn ʿAbbas, Nauf…".
  - Grading: recorded by al-Bukhārī in his Ṣaḥīḥ (ṣaḥīḥ by the collection); not upgraded,
    no other grade asserted.
  - Only the fish-sign portion quoted/paraphrased: the command to carry a fish in a basket,
    "where you lose the fish, there he is"; Yūshaʿ ibn Nūn as companion; sleep at the rock;
    the fish stirring, leaving the basket, falling into the sea, taking its way as a tunnel;
    Allah holding back the water so it stood "like an arch"; the servant's later report
    ("none but Satan made me forget"). Boat/sparrow/boy/wall deliberately not used.

## Asbāb
- None used. This is narrative sequence within the sūrah, not an occasion-of-revelation
  verse; no asbāb was sought or asserted.

## Dropped / not asserted
- Ibn ʿAṭiyyah's and Abū Shujāʿ's personal-sighting reports of the surviving half-fish
  (in Qurtubī / Maʿārif): dropped as extraneous curiosities, not needed for the beat.
- The precise identity of the "named river" (Nahr al-Dhiʾb vs Nahr al-Zayt): sources
  disagree; kept vague ("a named river") rather than pick one.
- Khiḍr's prophethood / Mūsā's precedence over him (Maʿārif): withheld per watch-point 5.
- Did NOT state in the article's own voice that any prophet erred; the plural "they forgot"
  (18:61) is presented as idiom + the tafsir's clarification that the servant forgot.
