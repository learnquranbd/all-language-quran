Card and 9-section bilingual article for 41:37: wa min ayatihi
al-laylu wan-nahar wash-shamsu wal-qamar, la tasjudu lish-shams wa la
lil-qamar. 41:33, 41:34 and 41:36 one line each; 41:38 only for the
sajda place; 41:39 and 41:53 not drawn on.

Sources: Ibn Kathir (ar; en abridged as check), Tabari, Muyassar,
Qurtubi, Sa'di, Baghawi, Ma'arif. All named except IK-en.
- Signs: Qurtubi, Tabari, Sa'di glosses; night/day alternation (IK,
  Tabari with 36:40, Sa'di's light-for-work, dark-for-rest).
- Sun/moon: IK on lunar stations and reckoning of rights, worship and
  dealings; IK "two servants among His servants"; Sa'di on livelihoods.
- Prohibition: Tabari (they run only as Allah runs them; He could efface
  their light); Qurtubi (no merit in themselves); Sa'di and Muyassar
  (governed, subjected, created).
- khalaqahunna: Tabari, Qurtubi (three referents), Baghawi; brief.
- in kuntum iyyahu ta'budun: Tabari, Muyassar, Sa'di; IK with 4:48.
- Ma'arif on prostration only to Allah, worship vs respect, abrogation;
  attributed throughout.
Disagreement kept: place of the sajda, via Qurtubi: 41:37 ta'budun
(Malik; 'Ali, Ibn Mas'ud) vs 41:38 yas'amun (Ibn Wahb, ash-Shafi'i, Abu
Hanifa; Ibn 'Abbas; Ibn 'Umar and others); Ibn al-'Arabi "the matter is
close". No position taken.
Hadith: Bukhari 1041 (Abu Mas'ud), eclipse, quoted whole, no grading
beyond his Sahih; marked as general, not attached to the verse; link via
Qurtubi's Ibn Khuwayz Mindad note.
No fetched tafsir names who prostrated to sun or moon; none named;
licensing sentence in both languages.
Dropped: fronting-of-iyyahu rhetoric, named sun-worshippers, eclipse
prayer details (not in fetched text).
