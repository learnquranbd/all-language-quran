Card and 8-section bilingual article for 85:17: hal ataka hadithu
al-junud, four Arabic words after the names of 85:12-16. 85:18
(Fir'awn and Thamud) named only as the next verse; 85:18-22 left for
their own entries. 85:14 cross-referenced by number.

Sources: Tabari, Ibn Kathir (ar; en abridged as check), Muyassar,
Qurtubi, Sa'di, Baghawi. Ma'arif's grouped text stops at 85:10; said so.
Slot check clean for all six Arabic files. No re-fetch.
- hal ataka: affirmation "qad ataka" (Tabari, Qurtubi, Baghawi) vs
  question kept "hal balaghaka" (Ibn Kathir, Muyassar "O Messenger").
  Kept side by side, none chosen.
- al-junud: massed against Allah and His Messenger with harm (Tabari);
  massed against the prophets (Baghawi); multitudes who denied their
  prophets (Qurtubi, Muyassar); deniers made among the destroyed (Sa'di).
- hadith: khabar (Qurtubi, Baghawi, Muyassar); the might and retribution
  none turned back (Ibn Kathir); punishment and nakal (Muyassar).
- Function: consolation "yu'nisuhu wa yusallihi" (Qurtubi); be patient,
  keep conveying (Tabari); taqrir of 85:12 (Ibn Kathir); proof of the
  messengers' truth (Sa'di).
Licensing sentence in both languages; no living group named.
Hadith: none from the six attached. Ibn Kathir's Ibn Abi Hatim report
(Amr ibn Maymun, ungraded) not confirmable; left aside, said so.
No asbab.
Dropped: Muyassar's 85:19-22 clauses (future ground); Ma'arif's 85:10
material; 20:9, 51:24, 79:15, 88:1, 38:21 (no fetched tafsir cites them).
