## Watch-point glosses (all attributed, none chosen)
- al-junud: those who massed against Allah and His Messenger with harm and hostility (Tabari); disbelieving multitudes who massed against the prophets (Baghawi); disbelieving multitudes who denied their prophets (Qurtubi, Muyassar); described by their end, deniers made among the destroyed (Sa'di).
- hadith: khabar (Qurtubi, Baghawi, Muyassar); kept as hadith (Tabari); content = the might and retribution Allah brought down, which none turned back (Ibn Kathir), punishment and exemplary penalty (Muyassar).
- hal ataka: affirmation «قد أتاك» (Tabari, Qurtubi, Baghawi) vs question kept «هل بلغك» (Ibn Kathir, Muyassar «أيها الرسول»). Function: consolation (Qurtubi «يؤنسه ويسليه»; Tabari «فاصبر»); taqrir of 85:12 (Ibn Kathir); proof of the messengers' truth (Sa'di). Kept side by side.
