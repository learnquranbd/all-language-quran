# SOURCES ledger — 34:34

## Tafsir fetched (all from PREP, not re-fetched)
- **t15 at-Tabari (ar)** — NAMED. Rests on: "وما بعثنا إلى أهل قرية نذيرًا ... إلا قال كبراؤها ورؤساؤها في الضلالة" (warner = one who warns of Allah's punishment for persisting in disobedience; speakers of refusal = the town's great ones and chiefs in misguidance). Also Qatadah via Tabari: "هم رءوسهم وقادتهم في الشر."
- **t90 al-Qurtubi (ar)** — NAMED. Rests on Qatadah: "أغنياؤها ورؤساؤها وجبابرتها وقادة الشر" (its rich, chiefs, tyrants, leaders of evil) → used as "its chiefs and its rich" + Qatadah's "tyrants, leaders, heads in evil."
- **t91 as-Sa'di (ar)** — NAMED (the article's core mechanism). Rests on: "كفر به مترفوها, وأبطرتهم نعمتهم وفخروا بها" (its mutrafūn disbelieved; their comfort made them insolent and they boasted of it) → the bounty→insolence→boasting arc.
- **t14 Ibn Kathir (ar)** + **t169 Ibn Kathir (en abridged)** — NAMED. Rests on: "وهم أولو النعمة والحشمة والثروة والرياسة" (people of comfort, pomp, wealth, leadership); consoling the Prophet / follow the example of earlier messengers; the cross-light list (26:111 الشعراء; 11:27 هود; 7:75-76 الأعراف; 6:123 الأنعام; 17:16 الإسراء); and the Heraclius parallel "وهكذا قال هرقل لأبي سفيان ... وهم أتباع الرسل."
- **t94 al-Baghawi (ar)** — NAMED. Rests on: "رؤساؤها وأغنياؤها" (its chiefs and its rich).
- **t16 al-Muyassar (ar)** — NAMED. Rests on: "المنغمسون في اللذات والشهوات" (those immersed in pleasures and desires).
- **t168 Ma'arif al-Qur'an (en)** — NAMED. Rests on: mutraf from taraf = "a life abounding in material assets, ease and comfort"; mutrafīn = "rich people of a community (who are arrogant and careless about what is right or wrong)"; relays the two-partners occasion "(Ibn Kathir and Mazhari)."

## Cross-light verses cited (all confirmed in the fetched Ibn Kathir text)
- 26:111 (Nuh's people), 7:75 and 7:76 (Salih's arrogant chiefs — the echoed wording), 17:16 (mutrafīn commanded before a town's ruin), 6:123 (great ones of its wicked plotting — appears in article §4 before trim; dropped from final to keep cross-light lean, still in ledger as confirmed).
- 34:31 and 34:32 (same-surah close-up of arrogant vs. oppressed), 34:35 (the wealth/children boast, referenced lightly), 34:37 (wealth/children do not bring nearness — the licensing/balance verse). All from PREP verse block.

Note: the brief's example 23:64 was NOT fetched for this verse and is NOT cited. 17:16 serves as the confirmed mutraf cross-light instead.

## Hadith
- **Sahih al-Bukhari, no. 7** (Dar-us-Salam). URL: https://quranx.com/hadith/Bukhari/DarusSalam/Hadith-7 (fetched via hadith.js, saved in this folder). First ten words (EN): "Narrated 'Abdullah bin 'Abbas: Abu Sufyan bin Harb informed me". Grading: in Sahih al-Bukhari (sahih by the collection's standard; not upgraded). Used: the Heraclius–Abu Sufyan exchange "Do the nobles or the poor follow him?" → "the poor" → "all the Apostles have been followed by this very class of people." Ibn Kathir attaches this scene to the verse.
- **"Allah does not look at your forms or your wealth, but at your hearts and deeds"** — cited in article §7 strictly AS reported by Ibn Kathir from Muslim's collection (t169: "Muslim and Ibn Majah also recorded this"). Not independently fetched from Muslim; no number given. Framed as Ibn Kathir's citation, not as a direct verification. It sits on 34:37 in the tafsir, used only to light the balance point.

## Asbab an-nuzul
- Two-partners narration: reported by Ibn Kathir on the authority of Ibn Abi Hatim (chain: ... Sufyan ← Asim ← Abu Razin), and by Ma'arif from Ibn Kathir and Mazhari. Abu Razin is a Successor (tābiʿī), so the report is **mursal**, not fully connected. Used in §6 and explicitly flagged as mursal / a commentary narration, not a ruling. Not asserted as an established sabab.
