New key 2:78-79 (extended from 2:78: the ummiyyun and the writers are one unit).
Card plus a 1,745-word, 8-section article built from the fetched tafsir only.

Sources: at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi, Ibn Kathir, al-Baghawi, as-Sa'di, Muyassar,
Ma'arif al-Qur'an on 2:78 and 2:79 (all slots checked; no neighbour-slot issue).
No asbab used; placement after 2:75-77 and before 2:80 given instead.
Disagreements kept: amani as bare recitation (as-Sa'di, al-Baghawi, al-Qurtubi
via 22:52) vs lies and wishful hopes (Mujahid, Ibn Abbas via ad-Dahhak, Qatadah,
Abu al-Aliyah), with at-Tabari's argument from yazunnun for the lies reading;
ummiyyun as unlettered (at-Tabari, an-Nakha'i) vs Ibn Abbas via ad-Dahhak
(deniers of the books), rejected by at-Tabari and doubted in chain by Ibn
Kathir; zann as doubt (at-Tabari) vs lying (al-Qurtubi).
Hadith: Bukhari 1913 (unlettered nation) quoted whole, marked as not about the
verse. Tirmidhi 3164 (wayl a valley) given with at-Tirmidhi's own "gharib" and
Ibn Kathir's "munkar"; nothing rests on it.
Licensing sentence in both languages; no claim about any scripture or community
today. Inward turn through as-Sa'di's Shaykh al-Islam quote and al-Qurtubi:
the Quran known only as recitation, and opinion labelled as the religion.
Sister verses as cited: 22:52, 4:157, 2:80, 2:111, 29:48, 62:2, 2:46, 12:42;
4:123, 2:82, 16:116 as Quranic text only.
Dropped: Ibn Abbas's "do not ask the People of the Book" (al-Bukhari via Ibn
Kathir), the 'Uthman "mountain" report, the 73-sects hadith, the sect names
in the Shaykh al-Islam quote, the unsourced Torah-description reports.
