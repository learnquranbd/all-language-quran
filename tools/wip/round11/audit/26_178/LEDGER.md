# SOURCES ledger — 26:178 (إِنِّى لَكُمْ رَسُولٌ أَمِينٌ — Shuʿayb to Aṣḥāb al-Aykah)

## Tafsir fetched (from PREP.md, not re-fetched)

- **at-Tabari (15)** on 26:178 — NAMED. Arabic clause:
  `( إِنِّي لَكُمْ مِنَ اللَّهِ رَسُولٌ أَمِينٌ ) على وحيه.`
  Rests the attribution "trustworthy over the revelation he carries (amīn ʿalā waḥyih)"
  on `أمين ... على وحيه`. Used in §2.1.
- **al-Qurtubi (90)** on 26:178 — NAMED. Arabic clause:
  `أي صادق فيما أبلغكم عن الله تعالى.`
  Rests "ṣādiq, truthful in what he conveys to them from Allah" on this clause. Used in
  §2.1, §2.2 (the "only question left is whether they listen" is my gloss on ṣādiq, not
  a separate claim), and §8.1 ("as al-Qurtubi said of the messenger's conveying").
- **as-Sa'di (91)** on 26:178 — NAMED. Arabic clause:
  `إِنِّي لَكُمْ رَسُولٌ أَمِينٌ يترتب على ذلك, أن تتقوا الله وتطيعون.`
  Rests "from the credential there follows that they fear Allah and heed him; the order is
  the argument" on `يترتب على ذلك`. Used in §5.1. Deliberately NOT developing 26:179
  (fear/obey) content — only the structural point that the credential grounds the demand.
- **al-Muyassar (16)** on 26:176-180 (grouped) — NAMED. Arabic clauses:
  `كذَّب أصحاب الأرض ذات الشجر الملتف رسولهم شعيبًا ... فكانوا بهذا مكذِّبين لجميع الرسالات`
  → §1.1 (al-Aykah = land of tangled/interwoven trees; denying one messenger = denying all)
  and §7.2. And `حفيظ على ما أوحى الله به إليَّ من الرسالة` → §2.1, §8.1 (amīn = a guardian
  over the message who neither adds nor lets any fall).
- **Ibn Kathir (169, en abridged)** on 26:176-180 (grouped) — NAMED. Rests on:
  - akhūhum omission: "it does not say here, their brother Shuʿayb ... because they called
    themselves by a name denoting their deification of Al-Aykah, which was a tree they used
    to worship; it was said that it was a group of trees which were tangled, like a thicket."
    → §1.2, §3.1.
  - "Some people did not notice this point, so they thought the dwellers of Al-Aykah were
    different ... claimed Shuʿayb was sent to two nations; some said that he was sent to
    three." → §3.2, §4.1.
  - Named sides: "This was the view of Ishaq bin Bishr"; "Someone besides Juwaybir said,
    'The dwellers of Al-Aykah and the people of Madyan are one and the same.'" → §4.1.
  - Conclusion: "the correct view is that they were one nation, but ... described differently
    ... Shu`ayb preached ... to be fair in their weights and measures ... which also
    indicates that they were the same nation," and "And Allah knows best." → §4.2.
    (Weights/measures named ONLY as Ibn Kathir's evidence for identity; the ethic itself is
    left for 26:181-182.)

## Hadith

- **Sahih al-Bukhari 4770** — USED in §6.1-6.2.
  - URL fetched: https://quranx.com/hadith/Bukhari/DarusSalam/Hadith-4770
  - Refs shown on page: Dar-us-Salam Hadith 4770; USC-MSA Vol 6, Book 60, Hadith 293.
  - First ten words (English wording used): "Narrated Ibn `Abbas: When the Verse: 'And warn
    your tribe...'" — key line quoted whole: "Suppose I told you that there is an (enemy)
    cavalry in the valley intending to attack you, would you believe me? They said, Yes, for
    we have not found you telling anything other than the truth."
  - Grading: Sahih al-Bukhari (sound by al-Bukhari's collection; not upgraded).
  - NOTE marked in the article: this report is tied to a LATER verse (the warning of near
    kin / Surah 111 context), NOT a commentary on 26:178. Offered to illustrate the
    principle (established trustworthiness grounds the call), explicitly flagged as "a report
    tied to a later verse, not to this one." One collection's wording, quoted whole, no hybrid.
- Bukhari 33 (signs of a hypocrite) was also fetched/confirmed as a backup but NOT used.
- Avoided stock hadith repeated nearby (Bukhari 660; Bukhari 3443 at 26:123) — not used.
