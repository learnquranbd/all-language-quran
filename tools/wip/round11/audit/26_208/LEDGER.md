# SOURCES — 26:208 (وما أهلكنا من قرية إلا لها منذرون)

## Tafsir fetched (all from PREP, not re-fetched)
- **14 Ibn Kathir (ar)** — USED. Clause: "مخبرا عن عدله في خلقه: أنه ما أهلك أمة من الأمم إلا بعد الإعذار إليهم، والإنذار لهم وبعثة الرسل إليهم وقيام الحجج عليهم." Also his two cross-refs 17:15 (وما كنا معذبين حتى نبعث رسولا) and 28:59. Carries §1–2.
- **169 Ibn Kathir (en, abridged)** — USED as the English confirmation of the same reading ("His justice towards His creation... does not destroy any nation until after He has left them with no excuse, by warning them, sending Messengers... establishing proof"), same two cross-refs. §2.
- **15 at-Tabari** — USED. Clause: "(من هذه القرى التي وصفت في هذه السور) ... إلا بعد إرسالنا إليهم رسلا ينذرونهم بأسنا على كفرهم." And on ذكرى: "تذكرة لهم وتنبيها لهم على ما فيه النجاة لهم من عذابنا," plus the two iʿrāb readings (naṣb as maṣdar / rafʿ as mubtadaʾ). Carries §1 ("these very towns"), §3.2.
- **90 al-Qurtubi** — USED. Clause: "( من ) صلة ... وما أهلكنا قرية. إلا لها منذرون أي رسل." Carries §2.1 (min is extra; munḏirūn = rusul).
- **91 as-Sa'di** — USED. Clause: "عن كمال عدله... ما أوقع بقرية هلاكا وعذابا إلا بعد أن يعذر... ويبعث فيهم النذر بالآيات البينات، ويدعونهم إلى الهدى، وينهونهم عن الردى، ويذكرونهم بآيات الله، وينبهونهم على أيامه في نعمه ونقمه." Carries §3.1.
- **94 al-Baghawi** — USED. Clause: "رسل ينذرونهم" (messengers who warn them). §2.1.
- **16 al-Muyassar (covers 208–209)** — USED. Clause: "إلا بعد أن نرسل إليهم رسلا ينذرونهم، تذكرة لهم... وما كنا ظالمين فنعذب أمة قبل أن نرسل إليها رسولا." Carries §3.3 and the reading of 26:209's ẓālimīn as "punish before warning." §5.2.
- **168 Ma'arif (en, covers 205–213)** — USED for §8.1 only, and only for 26:205 (the prior verse): long life as God's grace wasted by those who take respite for safety; the report (via Imam Zuhri) that Umar ibn Abd al-Aziz recited the verse each morning holding his beard. Marked as prior-verse context, not a 26:208 claim.

No tafsir marked WRONG GROUP. No disagreement of meaning among the mufassirūn — full consensus on the ʿadl reading; the only divergence is at-Tabari's two grammatical parsings of ذكرى, kept as a grammatical note, not a dispute of sense.

## Hadith
- **Sahih al-Bukhari 7283** (Abu Musa). URL fetched: https://quranx.com/hadith/Bukhari/DarusSalam/Hadith-7283 (also USC-MSA Vol 9, Book 92, Hadith 387). First ten words (EN): "My example and the example of what I have…". Arabic opens: "إنما مثلي ومثل ما بعثني الله به كمثل رجل أتى قوماً…" (al-nadhīr al-ʿuryān; the deniers destroyed — fa-ahlakahum, the verse's own verb). Grading: in al-Bukhari's Ṣaḥīḥ, ṣaḥīḥ by his own criterion (collector's own; not upgraded). Deliberately NOT one of the stock-nearby hadiths (Bukhari 660, Bukhari 3443).

## Asbab an-nuzul
Not used. No asbāb attaches to this verse; it is a concluding maxim after the surah's seven narratives. Placement written instead (§1: capstone/seal after the seven peoples; at-Tabari's "these very towns").

## Cross-references used (all from Ibn Kathir's own text)
17:15, 28:59 (§2.2); 26:205 prior-verse context and 26:209 completing clause (§3.3, §5.2, §8.1).

## Dropped (could not confirm or out of scope)
- The authentic hadith in Ibn Kathir (en) about the disbeliever dipped once in the Fire is attached to 26:207 ("shall not avail them"), not 26:208 — dropped to keep the hadith slot on the verse's own theme (warning).
- Ibn Ashur / ar-Razi not in-API and not fetched — not named.
- No Arabic poem or extended Umar ibn Abd al-Aziz narration beyond the single act Ma'arif reports; kept to the one confirmed detail.
