# SOURCES ledger — 25:25

Verse: وَيَوْمَ تَشَقَّقُ ٱلسَّمَآءُ بِٱلْغَمَٰمِ وَنُزِّلَ ٱلْمَلَٰٓئِكَةُ تَنزِيلًا
"And [mention] the Day when the heaven will split open with clouds, and the angels
will be sent down in a [great] descent."

## Tafsir fetched (all from PREP.md; none re-fetched)

- t15 at-Tabari (ar) — NAMED. Arabic clauses relied on:
  - qirāʾāt: "قرأته عامَّة قرّاء الحجاز (وَيَوْمَ تَشَّقَّقُ) بتشديد الشين … وقرأ ذلك عامة
    قرّاء أهل الكوفة (وَيَوْمَ تَشَقَّقُ) بتخفيف الشين … أنهما قراءتان مستفيضتان … فبأيتهما
    قرأ القارئ فمصيب." → §2 (Hijaz doubled shīn / Kufa lighter; both sound, meaning one).
  - bāʾ = ʿan: "تأويل الكلام: ويوم تُشقق السماء عن الغمام … وجعلت الباء … مكان عن كما تقول

  - Mujahid link to 2:210: "عن مجاهد … هو الذي قال: فِي ظُلَلٍ مِنَ الْغَمَامِ الذي يأتي الله
    فيه يوم القيامة, ولم يكن في تلك قطّ إلا لبني إسرائيل." → §3.
  - descent to earth: "وَنـزلَ الْمَلائِكَةُ تَنـزيلا … ونـزل الملائكة إلى الأرض تنـزيلا." → §4.
- t90 al-Qurtubi (ar) — NAMED. Clauses:

## Hadith

- USED — Sahih Muslim 2788 (2788a wording). URL fetched:
  https://quranx.com/hadith/Muslim/Hadith-2788
  First ten words (EN): "Abdullah b. 'Umar reported Allah's Messenger saying: Allah, the…"
  Wording quoted whole from 2788a only (folds heavens → right hand → "I am the
  Sovereign; where are the tyrants, where are the proud?" → folds earths → left hand →
  same). NOT hybridised with Ibn Kathir's slightly fuller phrasing ("أنا الديّان، أين
  ملوك الأرض") — that variant was left out.
  Grading: recorded by Muslim in his Ṣaḥīḥ; stated as "his own grading of soundness,"
  not upgraded further. Attached to the passage via Ibn Kathir's Sahih citation on
  25:25–26 (t169) and to 25:26 by wording.

- CONSIDERED, NOT USED — Sahih Bukhari 660 (the seven whom Allah shades on the Day of no
  shade but His). Fetched https://quranx.com/hadith/Bukhari/DarusSalam/Hadith-660 .
  Dropped: no tafsir on 25:25 attaches it; it is a general Day-of-Judgment narration, and
  the sovereignty hadith (Muslim 2788) is genuinely tied to the passage by Ibn Kathir.

## Asbāb an-nuzūl

Not used. No occasion of revelation for 25:25 in the fetched tafsirs; the verse's placement
(right after 25:24, the companions of Paradise; before 25:26, sovereignty to ar-Raḥmān) is
used instead. §1 and §6 carry the placement.

## Kept as a live disagreement (no position taken)

- The nature/mechanism of al-ghamām: (a) a thin white cloud like the one that shaded Bani
  Israʾīl, the sky splitting to reveal it (Qurtubi, Baghawi; Mujahid's 2:210 link via
  Tabari; Ibn Kathir's "canopies of light"); vs (b) the cloud lying between sky and people,
  its rending rending the sky, the sky then rolled up and angels sent to a place in its
  stead (Qurtubi's second view). Both set side by side in §3; verse's manner left open.
- The descent verb's qirāʾāt (Ibn Kathir's active nunazzil vs the passive nuzzila) reported
  as a reading difference with one settled sense (§4).

## Dropped (could not confirm / out of scope)

- The full Ibn ʿAbbās catalogue (angels' vast measurements, the eight Throne-bearers'
  dimensions "500 years," the "yawm at-talāq" dialogue) — chain graded weak by Ibn Kathir
  (Ali b. Zayd b. Judʿān); only the skeleton (heaven-by-heaven descent, Throne-bearers
  last) kept, explicitly flagged as weak in §4. Numbers and measurements dropped.
- "Karūbiyyūn / cherubim have horns like spear-tips," and the water-in-darkness report
  (mawqūf on ʿAbdullah b. ʿAmr, "لعله من الزاملتين") — dropped as unconfirmable/isrāʾīliyyāt.
- 25:27–29 (the wrongdoer biting his hands, "so-and-so as a friend") — deliberately not
  pre-empted; those verses are un-shipped.
