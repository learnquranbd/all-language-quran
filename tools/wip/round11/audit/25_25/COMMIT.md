Card and deep bilingual article on the terror and majesty of the Day of
Judgment: the sky torn open with the white cloud, the angels sent down rank
upon rank.

Sources (all fetched for 25:25, confirmed clause by clause):
- at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi, al-Baghawi: the bāʾ in bi-l-ghamām stands for ʿan (the
  sky splits away from the cloud); the qirāʾāt split tashshaqqaqu (Hijaz) vs
  tashaqqaqu (Kufa), both sound, one meaning.
- al-ghamām: thin white cloud like the shade given only to Bani Israʾīl
  (Qurtubi, Baghawi); Mujahid via at-Tabari ties it to 2:210; Ibn Kathir's
  "canopies of tremendous light"; Ma'arif/Bayan al-Qurʾan's canopy that is an
  opening, not the Trumpet's shattering. Mechanism kept open as a disagreement.
- the emphatic tanzīlan and the descent-verb qirāʾāt (Ibn Kathir's nunazzil vs
  the passive nuzzila) — al-Qurtubi; the angels rank upon rank, submissive,
  none speaking but by leave (as-Sa'di); descent for the reckoning of the two
  weighty kinds (al-Qurtubi).
- God's coming for the decisive judgment "befitting His majesty" (al-Muyassar),
  with al-Qurtubi's tanzīh guard against creaturely movement.
- as-Sa'di on the Day hard for disbelievers, light for believers, and mercy in
  the name ar-Raḥmān; bridges to 25:26 (sovereignty to the Most Merciful).

Hadith: Sahih Muslim 2788 (God folds the heavens, "I am the Sovereign; where
are the tyrants, where are the proud?"), quoted whole from 2788a, attached to
the passage by Ibn Kathir's Sahih citation; grading not upgraded.

Kept the ghamām mechanism and the descent-verb readings as open differences.
Dropped the weak Ibn ʿAbbās measurements (Ali b. Zayd chain, graded weak by
Ibn Kathir) — only the heaven-by-heaven skeleton kept and flagged weak; dropped
the cherubim/water-in-darkness isrāʾīliyyāt. Did not pre-empt 25:27–29.
