Card and deep bilingual article on the denial of purposeless
creation: the heaven, earth and all between were not made in play.

Sources gave a clean convergence. al-Baghawi, al-Qurtubi, at-Tabari
and al-Muyassar all gloss lāʿibīn as ʿabath wa bāṭil (futility and
falsehood); at-Tabari carries Qatadah, "not in vain nor in falsehood."
Ibn Kathir and as-Sa'di affirm the positive: creation bi'l-ḥaqq — with
justice — cross-read with 53:31 and 38:27. at-Tabari and al-Muyassar
turn it into creation as a standing proof (ḥujja) and a summons to
tawheed. al-Qurtubi supplies the hinge: the play denied of the Wise
has wisdom (ḥikma) as its opposite, and a purposeful world implies a
reckoning; as-Sa'di adds that the power to create guarantees the power
to resurrect and requite. Ma'arif frames the disbelieving mind that
treats creation as amusement, and Raghib's laʿib = "no useful aim."

Kept on 21:16 only. Did NOT preempt 21:17–18: the lahw argument, the
Mujahid "from Ourself" report, and "We hurl truth against falsehood"
were all held back, though they sit in the grouped Ibn Kathir file.

No hadith: no tafsir attaches one to this verse. Bukhari 660 (the
seven whom Allah shades — sahih) was checked and dropped as not
directly on the ayah; the article says so in one sentence.
