# SOURCES ledger — 21:16

Verse: وَمَا خَلَقْنَا ٱلسَّمَآءَ وَٱلْأَرْضَ وَمَا بَيْنَهُمَا لَٰعِبِينَ
"And We did not create the heaven and the earth and what is between them in play."

## Tafsir fetched (all pre-fetched per PREP.md; none re-fetched)

- **14 Ibn Kathir (ar)** — USED. Arabic clause: "خلق السماوات والأرض بالحق، أي: بالعدل والقسط ... وأنه لم يخلق ذلك عبثا ولا لعبا". Rests §3 (Made in Truth): created bi'l-ḥaqq = with justice/equity (bi'l-ʿadl wa'l-qisṭ); did not create it in ʿabath or laʿib. His cross-refs 53:31 and 38:27 are cited (both appear in his ar text; 38:27 quoted in full there).
- **169 Ibn Kathir (abridged, en)** — USED for the 21:16 portion only ("Creation was made with Justice and Wisdom ... created the heavens and the earth in truth, i.e. with justice ... did not create all that in vain or for mere play", + 38:27). This file is GROUPED (covers 21:16–20); the 21:17–20 material (lahw / Mujahid "from Ourself" / hurling truth against falsehood / angels' worship) was DELIBERATELY NOT USED — orchestrator watch-point 2 forbids preempting 21:17–18.
- **15 at-Tabari** — USED. Arabic clause: "إلا حجة عليكم أيها الناس، ولتعتبروا بذلك كله، فتعلموا أن الذي دبره وخلقه لا يشبهه شيء ... ولم يَخْلق ذلك عبثا ولعبا"; plus the Qatadah report "ما خلقناهما عَبَثا ولا باطلا". Rests §2 (Qatadah witness) and §4 (creation as a proof/ḥujja and iʿtibār → tawheed).
- **16 al-Muyassar** — USED. Arabic clause: "عبثًا وباطلا بل لإقامة الحجة عليكم - أيها الناس - ولتعتبروا بذلك كله ... ولا تصلح العبادة إلا له". Rests §2 (ʿabath/bāṭil gloss) and §4 (proof + worship His alone).
- **90 al-Qurtubi** — USED. Arabic clause: "أي عبثا وباطلا؛ بل للتنبيه على أن لها خالقا قادرا يجب امتثال أمره، وأنه يجازي المسيء والمحسن ... ما خلقنا السماء والأرض ليظلم بعض الناس بعضا ... ثم يموتوا ولا يجازوا ... وهذا اللعب المنفي عن الحكيم ضده الحكمة". Rests §5 (play's opposite is ḥikma; design→designer→accountability) and §6 (the requital reasoning).
- **91 as-Sa'di** — USED. Arabic clause: "ما خلق السماوات والأرض عبثا، ولا لعبا من غير فائدة، بل خلقها بالحق وللحق، ليستدل بها العباد على أنه الخالق العظيم، المدبر الحكيم ... قادر على إعادة الأجساد بعد موتها، ليجازي المحسن بإحسانه، والمسيء بإساءته". Rests §3 (bi'l-ḥaqq wa li'l-ḥaqq; standing evidence) and §6 (power to create ⇒ power to resurrect ⇒ requital).
- **94 al-Baghawi** — USED. Arabic clause (whole entry): "أي عبثا وباطلا". Rests §2 (briefest gloss of lāʿibīn).
- **168 Ma'arif al-Qur'an (en)** — USED. Clauses: "not created ... for mere amusement"; the preceding annihilation of settlements followed "a set design ... a definite purpose"; Raghib's distinction — لِعَب (play) = "having no useful objective"; disbelievers "have a notion that all this creation is meant for amusement and pastime", which the verse rejects. Rests §1 (link to the destroyed towns), §2 (Raghib on laʿib), §7 (the disbelieving mind). NOTE: I used only the laʿib gloss from Raghib and did NOT develop Ma'arif's لَهْو/pastime contrast, since لَهْو is the vocabulary of 21:17 (watch-point 2).

## Disagreements
None material. All commentators converge (lāʿibīn = ʿabath/bāṭil; creation bi'l-ḥaqq with a purpose implying requital). Presented as convergence, no position invented.
