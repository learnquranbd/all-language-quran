Assigned 3:183, widened to 3:183-184: the demanded fire-offering and its
answer, then the consolation that answers the same denial. 3:181-182 left
free (bound only grammatically; their occasions already ship in 3:186 and
3:189). New card and a 1,719-word, 9-section article.

Sources: at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi, Ibn Kathir (ar + en abridged), as-Sa'di,
al-Baghawi, al-Muyassar, Ma'arif on 3:183 and 3:184 (all slots on target).
- Licensing sentence in both languages, up front (§1) and in its own
  section (§6): the verses report what some people said and did then and
  license nothing against any Jew or anyone living; al-Qurtubi and
  al-Baghawi tie the charge to approval, not descent.
- Qurban and the fire as at-Tabari gives it (a sign of acceptance in that
  age), with Ibn 'Abbas and ad-Dahhak; the white smokeless fire in one
  attributed sentence (al-Baghawi, al-Qurtubi's qila).
- as-Sa'di: the sign of messengers narrowed to their own choice, refusal
  called obedience; al-Qurtubi's two qila (hidden exception / abrogation).
- 3:184 as ta'ziya (at-Tabari, ad-Dahhak, Ibn Jurayj), Ibn Kathir's uswa,
  as-Sa'di's "habit of wrongdoers"; zubur, munir; 35:25 parallel.
Readings kept as reported: wa-bi-z-zubur in the Sham copies (at-Tabari),
Ibn 'Amir with bi on both words (al-Qurtubi).
Hadith: none attached by any fetched tafsir; said once.
Asbab: al-Kalbi's report (al-Baghawi, al-Qurtubi) as a report, no chain or
grading; the 3:181 Ibn 'Abbas report not used.
Dropped: al-Qurtubi's "about seven hundred years" (written as centuries),
the fuller fire lore, at-Tabari's lines on altering the Torah and Injil,
named killings beyond Zakariyya, Yahya and Sha'ya.
