Card and 8-section bilingual article for 51:12: yas'aluna ayyana yawmu
d-din. 51:6 and 51:8-11 as context; 51:13 one line only, not developed.

Sources: Tabari, Ibn Kathir (ar), Muyassar, Qurtubi, Sa'di, Baghawi,
Ma'arif (grouped 51:10-16). Ibn Kathir en skipped (wrong group).
- Askers = the kharrasun of 51:10-11 (Tabari); deniers of requital or
  raising (Ibn Zayd via Tabari); "these liars" (Muyassar); addressed
  to the Prophet ﷺ, "ya Muhammad" (Baghawi). Kharrasun as conjecturers,
  readable as kadhdhabun (Ma'arif); touched lightly only.
- Motive, five tafsirs: takdhib/'inad/shakk/istib'ad (IK, "innama"),
  istihza' and doubt (Qurtubi), takdhib and istihza' (Baghawi), shakk,
  takdhib, istib'ad (Sa'di), su'al istib'ad wa-takdhib (Muyassar).
- Yawm ad-din: mujazat and hisab, yadinu (Tabari); hisab and jaza'
  (Muyassar); hisab (Qurtubi); jaza' = qiyama (Baghawi); Sa'di reads
  the question as "when will they be raised".
- Mujahid via Tabari: "when is it, or will it be" kept as the when/
  whether reading.
Hadith: none attached by any fetched tafsir; stated in one sentence.
Licensing sentence in both languages; questions about the Last Day not
condemned as such (IK's innama puts the fault in the motive).
Dropped: Jibril's hadith (not attached); 7:187, 79:42-44, 33:63 (not
cited); 51:14 link; Mazhari as a named source (tag ambiguous in Ma'arif).
