# SOURCES ledger, 51:12

## Tafsir fetched (all from PREP; none re-fetched) and named
- 14 Ibn Kathir (ar): NAMED. "وإنما يقولون هذا تكذيبا وعنادا وشكا واستبعادا" (the four motives; the
  restrictive إنما is the basis for "only" and for §7.2 placing the fault in the motive).
- 15 at-Tabari: NAMED. "يسأل هؤلاء الخرّاصون الذين وصف صفتهم متى يوم المجازاة والحساب, ويوم يُدينُ الله
  العباد بأعمالهم" (ayyana = mata; link to 51:10-11; yawm ad-din gloss; verb yadinu).
  Ibn Zayd (via Yunus ← Ibn Wahb): "الذين كانوا يجحدون أنهم يُدانون, أو يُبعثون".
  Mujahid (via Ibn Abi Najih): "يقولون: متى يوم الدين, أو يكون يوم الدين" — rendered "or, will the Day of
  Judgement come about?" (reads أو يكون as a second question of whether). Chains reported as given, no grading.
- 16 al-Muyassar: NAMED. "يسأل هؤلاء الكذابون سؤال استبعاد وتكذيب: متى يوم الحساب والجزاء؟"
- 90 al-Qurtubi: NAMED. "أي متى يوم الحساب ; يقولون ذلك استهزاء وشكا في القيامة".
- 91 as-Sa'di: NAMED. "على وجه الشك والتكذيب أيان يبعثون أي: متى يبعثون، مستبعدين لذلك، فلا تسأل عن حالهم
  وسوء مآلهم" (article says he paraphrases the question as "when will they be raised").
- 94 al-Baghawi: NAMED. "يقولون : يا محمد متى يوم الجزاء ، يعني : يوم القيامة تكذيبا واستهزاء".
- 168 Ma'arif (en, grouped 51:10-16): NAMED. kharrasun "plural of kharras which means one who estimates or
  say things by conjecture"; "stubborn and obstinate infidels who, without any reason or evidence, say
  discordant things about the Messenger ﷺ"; "it would not be out of turn to interpret kharrasun as
  kadhdhabun"; "After the mention of infidels, several verses that follow describe the qualities of the
  righteous people and the pleasant consequences of their righteousness."
- 169 Ibn Kathir (en abridged): NOT USED — marked WRONG GROUP (covers 51:1-10).

## Dropped / not used
- Ma'arif's "(Mazhari)" tag: it sits after "condemned, denounced, cursed and imprecated", so it is unclear
  whether the kadhdhabun reading itself is Mazhari's. Mazhari not named; the reading is attributed to Ma'arif.
- al-kharrasun: touched lightly only (§2), flagged in the article as deserving its own study.
- 51:14 ("this is what you used to hasten"): tempting link to the "when" question, but not drawn by any
  fetched tafsir on 51:12 → not used.
- §3.3 grouping of the motives into "heart" (doubt, istib'ad) and "tongue" (mockery, obstinacy) is the
  article's own synthesis, not attributed to any commentator.
- Licensing sentence present in both languages (§7.1): describes what the text describes; licenses nothing
  against any living person or community.
