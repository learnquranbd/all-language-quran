Card and bilingual article (8 sections, 1,771 EN words) for "It is said to
the mindful, what has your Lord sent down? They say: good", read as the
answer that mirrors 16:24.

Sources: Ibn Kathir (fortunate vs wretched; khayran = mercy, blessing,
good; 16:97, both worlds; 28:80, 3:198, 87:17, 93:4); at-Tabari (Kufan
grammarians on asatiru vs khayran; who did good; karama; honour hastened
vs prepared; Qatada); al-Qurtubi (madha one noun; ath-Tha'labi; season
visitor report; Day of Resurrection view; al-Hasan vs the majority on the
excellent home); al-Baghawi (tribal envoys at the season; Ibn Abbas,
ad-Dahhak, Mujahid on hasana); as-Sa'di (ihsan in worship and to people;
reception of the blessing); the Muyassar. Ma'arif grouped 16:24-33 and
Ibn Kathir abridged 16:30-32, said so; only 16:30 content used.

Disagreements kept: whether "for those who did good..." continues the
believers' answer (Ibn Kathir) or is Allah's new statement (at-Tabari,
al-Baghawi), al-Qurtubi giving both; hasana as Paradise vs worldly good;
the excellent home as this world (al-Hasan) vs the Hereafter (majority).

Who asked: reports only, as al-Qurtubi and al-Baghawi carry them, no
narrator named and no grading added; Ma'arif's three views.

Hadith: none attached to 16:30 by any fetched tafsir; said so.

Dropped: grammar labels for fi hadhihi ad-dunya; 169's "reluctantly";
16:31-32 beyond pointers. Licensing sentence in both languages.
