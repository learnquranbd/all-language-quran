Card and 8-section bilingual article for 47:4: fa-idha laqitumu
lladhina kafaru fa-darba r-riqab ... fa-imma mannan ba'du wa imma
fida'an hatta tada'a l-harbu awzaraha. Placed after 47:1-3.

Sources: Tabari, Qurtubi, Sa'di, Ibn Kathir (ar; en 47:4 lines only),
Baghawi, Muyassar, Ma'arif.
- Setting: battlefield per Tabari (ahl al-harb), Sa'di, Muyassar;
  choice with the Messenger and those in charge after him (Tabari),
  the imam (Ibn Kathir, Baghawi); Sa'di: no war, no killing or capture.
- Glosses: darb ar-riqab (Zajjaj, Qurtubi), athkhantum (Tabari,
  Qurtubi, Baghawi, Muyassar), wathaq (Sa'di, Qurtubi, Tabari).
- Mann and fida per source; Ibn Kathir "after Badr" (8:67, 8:68);
  Ma'arif/Mazhari order; Ibn Abbas via Baghawi.
- Abrogation kept as a disagreement: 9:5 / 8:57 camp (Ibn Jurayj,
  Suddi, Dahhak, Qatada, Ibn Abbas via 'Awfi, Mujahid, Abu Hanifa's
  well-known view); reverse (Dahhak, Ata', al-Hasan); muhkam with
  choice (Malik, Shafi'i, Thawri, Abu 'Ubayd; Qurtubi's and Tabari's
  preference). Awza'i placed in opposite camps by Baghawi and Qurtubi.
  Ibn Umar's refusal and Ibn al-'Arabi's answer. No pick.
- Awzar: weapons/truce, Kalbi, Farra', Sa'di; Mujahid ('Isa), Qatada
  (no shirk, 2:193), Tabari's sins reading.
- Test: Tabari, Sa'di (faith not built on following the winner),
  Ma'arif. Qira'at qatalu/qutilu/quttilu per Tabari, Baghawi; Qatada's
  Uhud report as "it was mentioned to us".
Hadith: Bukhari 4372 (Thumama) quoted whole from the Arabic, attached
by Baghawi through al-Bukhari's own chain.
Licensing sentence in both languages: an encounter in war under
legitimate authority; licenses nothing against any living person.
Dropped: Ibn Kathir's and Baghawi's unconfirmed prophetic reports on
awzar, martyr hadiths, Ma'arif's slavery excursus, Hajjaj/Kindi story.
- Gate: word budget trimmed; number parity fixed.
