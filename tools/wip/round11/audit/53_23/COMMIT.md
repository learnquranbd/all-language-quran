Card and 8-section bilingual article on in hiya illa asma'un
sammaytumuha antum wa aba'ukum. 53:19-22 as context; 53:24 and
53:27-28 pointers only.

Sources: at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi, al-Baghawi, Ibn Kathir (ar; en abridged
for the 53:19-26 group), al-Muyassar, as-Sa'di, Ma'arif al-Qur'an.
- Hiya: the names (Tabari) vs the idols (Qurtubi awthan, Baghawi
  asnam, Muyassar); Ibn Jarir via Ibn Kathir (en) on al-Lat/al-'Uzza
  derived from divine names.
- Sultan kept as a disagreement: proof (Ibn Kathir, Qurtubi, Baghawi,
  Muyassar, Sa'di) vs permission, lam yubih wa la adhina (Tabari).
  Sa'di's rule: what has no sultan is not taken as religion.
- Iltifat you -> they (Qurtubi, Baghawi); Qurtubi's tattabi'una reading
  (Isa ibn Umar, Ayyub, Ibn as-Samayfa'; Ibn Mas'ud, Ibn Abbas).
- Zann/hawa per source: Tabari (not yaqin; no wahy), Ibn Kathir (good
  opinion of fathers; leadership), Sa'di, Qurtubi, Baghawi, Muyassar.
- Ma'arif's zann types (baseless vs recognised zanniyyat), via 53:28.
- Huda: Prophet/Book (Tabari, Qurtubi, Baghawi) vs messengers (Ibn
  Kathir); Ibn Zayd and Muyassar "fa-ma ntafa'u bihi"; Sa'di -> 53:24.
Licensing paragraph in both languages; no present-day mapping.
Hadith: none attached; Ibn Kathir's reports belong to 53:19-20, 53:24.
Asbab: none.
Dropped: 7:71, 12:40, 10:66, 6:116 (not tafsir-cited); Ibn Zayd on
min sultan (truncated in source); idol-site history.
