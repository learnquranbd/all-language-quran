Card and 8-section bilingual article for 69:35: fa-laysa lahu al-yawma
hahuna hamim. 69:30-34 as context; 69:36 named only where a source
ties it.

Sources: Tabari, Qurtubi, Sa'di, Ibn Kathir (ar), Baghawi, Muyassar,
Ma'arif (grouped 69:35-37). All named. Ibn Kathir (en) skipped: wrong
group (69:25-34).
- al-yawm as the Day of Resurrection (Tabari, Sa'di, Muyassar, Ma'arif);
  hahuna as the abode of the Hereafter (Tabari); Qurtubi's grammar
  (khabar is lahu, not hahuna).
- hamim: relative (Ibn Zayd via Tabari, Muyassar, Baghawi, Qurtubi, IK)
  vs sincere or bosom friend (Ma'arif) vs relative or friend (Sa'di);
  what he would do kept per source (defend, rescue, feel tender,
  intercede). Qurtubi's etymology from hot water.
- Qurtubi's qila (taqdim wa-ta'khir, hamim as hot water) reported as
  an unendorsed reading.
- 34:23 and 40:18 only as Sa'di quotes them (verified); IK's "wa-la
  shafi'un yuta'" noted as 40:18's wording.
- No causal link from 69:34 asserted; Muyassar's "hadha al-kafir"
  reported as the only pointer back.
Hadith: none attached by any fetched tafsir; said so. IK's narrations
here concern ghislin and are left to 69:36.
Asbab: none given. Licensing sentence in both languages; nobody is
called friendless in the Hereafter.
Dropped: 88:6 via Qurtubi and all ghislin material (69:36 ground);
26:100-101 and 70:10 (not cited).
- 34:23 and 40:18: as-Sa'di quotes both verses without numbers; the numbers are the drafter's identification, stated so in the article.
