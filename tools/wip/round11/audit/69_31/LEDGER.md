Notes on attribution:
- No disagreement exists among the fetched glosses; the article says so and presents the five verbs (enter, enter to suffer heat, submerge, turn over on embers, make burn) as each source's wording, not as rival positions.
- "thumma": no source discusses whether it marks time (after the binding) or rank/severity. The article raises the question as the reader's and states the sources do not take it up; no ruling given.
- Word order (al-jahima before sallûhu): stated in own voice as visible in the Arabic; no meaning assigned (no source gives one). Only reported: Tabari keeps the place first, the other five put the verb first.
- Own-voice grammar: sallûhu is a plural imperative; doubled form (II) of root s-l-y, glossed by form I yasla (Tabari, Qurtubi); hu as object pronoun on each command in 69:30-31; the -iyah "my" nouns of 69:25-29 (visible in the text).
- "Hellfire": the app's own English translation (data/translations/en.json), described as "the English translation shown with the verse", not named.
- Speaker = Allah: IK (en) "At this Allah says" / "He will command".

## Licensing statement
§8.1 (EN and BN): the verse describes a command on the Day of Judgement to the keepers about one already judged; licenses nothing against any living person or community; no right to identify anyone as "the man of this verse"; no sorting into saved and lost. Card reflection and lesson carry the same point.
