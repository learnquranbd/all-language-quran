Card and 9-section bilingual article for 69:31: thumma al-jahima
sallûhu, the second step of the command after seize and shackle (69:30).
Left-hand record left to 69:25; chain left to 69:32.

Sources: Tabari, Qurtubi, Baghawi, Muyassar (grouped 69:30-34),
Ibn Kathir (ar), Sa'di, Ibn Kathir (en, grouped 69:25-34), Ma'arif
(69:30 only).
- Sallûhu by its own root: Tabari awriduhu li-yasla fiha, Qurtubi
  ij'aluhu yasla al-jahim.
- Other verbs kept as each source's wording, not as rivals: Baghawi
  adkhiluhu, Muyassar adkhiluhu li-yuqasiya harraha, IK ighmuruhu
  fiha, Sa'di qallibuhu 'ala jamriha wa-lahabiha.
- Addressees: Muyassar khazanat jahannam, IK guardians of Hell;
  Ma'arif's caution that the verse does not name them kept, its
  unnamed narratives reported as its statement only.
- Al-Jahim: Tabari nar jahannam; Muyassar harr; Sa'di jamr, lahab.
- Fronting of al-jahim and the force of thumma: no source comments,
  so no meaning given; sequence reported as Muyassar and IK give it.
- Grounds from 69:33-34 as Muyassar and IK tie them.
Licensing paragraph in both languages: a command on that Day about
one already judged; no licence against any living person or
community, no sorting of people.
Hadith: none attached to 69:31; IK's narrations sit under 69:32-34.
Asbab: none. Parallels: none cited by a source.
Dropped: Jahim etymology, meaning of word order, time/rank thumma,
Hell descriptions, chain material.
