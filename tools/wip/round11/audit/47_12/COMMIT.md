Card and 8-section bilingual article for 47:12: inna Allaha yudkhilu
alladhina amanu wa 'amilu s-salihati jannatin tajri min tahtiha l-anhar,
walladhina kafaru yatamatta'una wa ya'kuluna kama ta'kulu l-an'am,
wa-n-naru mathwan lahum. Read after 47:11 (mawla / no mawla); 47:15's
rivers only pointed to, 47:13-14 not developed.

Sources: Tabari, Qurtubi, Sa'di, Ibn Kathir (ar; en for 47:12 lines),
Baghawi, Muyassar. Ma'arif fetched but silent on 47:12, not named.
- Times: Ibn Kathir's yawm al-qiyama / fi dunyahum / yawma jaza'ihim.
- Sa'di: 47:12 unfolds 47:11; wukilu ila anfusihim, lost muru'a.
- Gardens as takrima for faith (Tabari, Muyassar); Sa'di's orchards.
- Yatamatta'un: Tabari's hutam, riyash, zina, faniya darisa; the
  unattributed qila (yatazawwad / yatazayyan / yatamatta') in Qurtubi
  and Baghawi, reported as unattributed.
- Likeness: Tabari (no thought of the return, no lesson from the
  proofs), Muyassar, Ibn Kathir (khadman wa qadman, single concern),
  Qurtubi/Baghawi (bellies, heedless of tomorrow), Sa'di (no 'aql).
  All six use hamm/himma; differences of emphasis, no ranking.
- Mathwa: Tabari/Muyassar maskan wa ma'wa, Qurtubi maqam wa manzil,
  Sa'di manzil mu'add, Ibn Kathir's time.
Hadith: Bukhari 5393 (Nafi' from Ibn 'Umar), attached by Ibn Kathir
("thabata fi s-sahih"), quoted whole, marked general; translator's
parenthetical glosses identified. 5394-5396 seen, not used.
Asbab: none. Licensing sentence in both languages.
Dropped: 7:179, 25:44, 15:3, 3:196-197 (no tafsir cites them), Uhud
mawla exchange (no collection named), m-t-' root note (from memory),
Ibn Kathir en's "(with greed)" flagged as translator's addition.
