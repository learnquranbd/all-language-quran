# SOURCES ledger — 37:68

## Tafsir fetched (per PREP.md) and disposition

- **14 — Ibn Kathir (ar)**, 810 chars, covers 37:68. USED: the Qatada/55:44 cross-
  reference and Ibn Kathir's own grading ("تفسير حسن قوي", a good, strong
  interpretation) — §4; the Ibn Mas'ud variant "ثم إن مقيلهم لإلى الجحيم"
  (via as-Suddi) and the full oath about midday — §5–6. Arabic clauses rested
  on: "وقوله (ثم إن مرجعهم لإلى الجحيم)... هكذا تلا قتادة هذه الآية عند هذه
  الآية، وهو تفسير حسن قوي"; "وقال السدي في قراءة عبد الله: ثم إن مقيلهم لإلى
  الجحيم"; "وكان عبد الله يقول: والذي نفسي بيده لا ينتصف النهار يوم القيامة
  حتى يقيل أهل الجنة في الجنة، وأهل النار في النار. ثم قرأ: (أصحاب الجنة
  يومئذ خير مستقرا وأحسن مقيلا)".
  **Dropped**: Ibn Kathir's closing grammatical remark on this passage, "قلت:
  على هذا التفسير تكون 'ثم' عاطفة لخبر على خبر" (I say: on this interpretation,
  "thumma" is a conjunction joining report to report) — genuine and attributable,
  but cut for budget and to avoid duplicating the Abu 'Ubaydah point already
  carried in §2. Noted here rather than silently used or silently lost.
- **15 — at-Tabari**, 847 chars, covers 37:68. USED: Qatada's citation of 55:44
  — §4; Ibn Mas'ud's variant "ثم إن منقلبهم لإلى الجحيم" (via as-Suddi) — §5;
  the midday oath and 25:24 — §6; Ibn Zayd's minority gloss "موتهم" via the
  chain Yunus–Ibn Wahb–Ibn Zayd, with the printed edition's own footnote
  suggesting it may be a copyist's substitution for "مقرهم أو مآلهم" — §7.
- **16 — al-Muyassar**, 270 chars, covers 37:64–68 (grouped). Fetched, read,
  not separately named: it only paraphrases the whole zaqqum-to-hamim-to-
  Jahim sequence in one sentence with no distinct attribution to add.
- **90 — al-Qurtubi**, 821 chars, covers 37:68. USED: the anonymous "qila"
  framing of the sequence question — §3; Maqatil's view that al-hamim is
  outside Jahannam, tied to 55:43–55:44 — §3–4; al-Qushayri's view that
  al-hamim is a location within Jahannam at its edge — §3; Abu 'Ubaydah's
  grammatical point that "thumma" may carry the sense of plain "wa" — §2;
  Ibn Mas'ud's reading "منقلبهم" (matching at-Tabari's wording, not Ibn
  Kathir's/al-Baghawi's) — §5. Arabic rested on: "قيل: إن هذا يدل على أنهم
  كانوا حين أكلوا الزقوم في عذاب غير النار ثم يردون إليها"; "وقال مقاتل:
  الحميم خارج الجحيم، فهم يوردون الحميم لشربه ثم يردون إلى الجحيم، لقوله
  تعالى: هذه جهنم التي يكذب بها المجرمون. يطوفون بينها وبين حميم آن"؛ "وقرأ
  ابن مسعود: ثم إن منقلبهم لإلى الجحيم"؛ "قال أبو عبيدة: يجوز أن تكون 'ثم'
  بمعنى الواو"؛ "القشيري: ولعل الحميم في موضع من جهنم على طرف منها".
  **Correction to the brief**: the watch-points named "32:20" for the "this is
  the Hellfire which the criminals used to deny" clause. The Arabic al-Qurtubi
  actually quotes ("هذه جهنم التي يكذب بها المجرمون") was checked against
  data/quran-json/55.json and data/translations/en.json and matches **55:43**,
  immediately before the 55:44 Qatada/Qurtubi both cite — not 32:20 (which
  reads differently: "ذوقوا عذاب النار الذي كنتم به تكذبون"). The article
  uses 55:43, verified, not the brief's 32:20.
- **91 — as-Sa'di**, 142 chars, covers 37:68. USED: the three-synonym gloss
  of marji' (مآلهم ومقرهم ومأواهم) and the purpose clause (ليذوقوا من عذابه
  الشديد، وحره العظيم، ما ليس عليه مزيد من الشقاء) — §1.
- **94 — al-Baghawi**, 258 chars, covers 37:68. USED: the hamim-outside-
  Jahannam picture (echoing Maqatil's, though al-Baghawi does not name him)
  and Ibn Mas'ud's reading "ثم إن مقيلهم لإلى الجحيم" (matching Ibn Kathir's
  wording, not at-Tabari's/al-Qurtubi's) — §5.
- **168 — Ma'arif al-Qur'an (en)**, 1131 chars, covers 37:65–74 (grouped).
  Fetched, read; the only 37:68-relevant content is a one-line gloss,
  superseded by as-Sa'di's fuller three-synonym treatment. Not separately
  named.
- **169 — Ibn Kathir (en, abridged)**, 6046 chars, covers 37:62–70 (grouped).
  USED to confirm/translate the Arabic Ibn Kathir (14) passage above
  (English rendering of the same "حسن قوي" judgement and the same Ibn
  Mas'ud oath, citing 25:24 as "the best abode... fairest place of repose").
  **Dropped**: the extended Ibn Abi Hatim / Sa'id b. Jubayr description of
  the Fire's people eating zaqqum and drinking hamim (skin falling, flesh
  baked, iron rods) — this is 37:66–67 context the brief said not to rebuild,
  and it carries no distinct claim about 37:68 itself.

## Hadith

No hadith was fetched or used. None of the six tafsir texts gathered for
37:68 attach a Prophetic hadith to this verse — the material on this ayah is
tafsir exegesis plus Companion/Successor reports about qira'at and meaning
(as-Suddi, Ibn Zayd, Ibn Mas'ud, Qatada), not a hadith of the Prophet ﷺ.
Per CLAUDE-LOG.md's stock-hadith watch (Bukhari 660 over-used as a fallback
~11×), no stock hadith was reached for here either — correctly, since the
brief itself flagged none was expected.

## Standing-rule note

The article's final section (§8, "No License Against the Living") states
plainly, in both languages, that the wrongdoers this verse describes are a
specific group named in 37:63, that the verse's fate-description is Allah's
judgment in the Hereafter, and that it licenses nothing against any living
person or community today.
