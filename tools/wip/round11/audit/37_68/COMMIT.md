Card + 8-section bilingual article (1,731 EN words) on 37:68, "Then indeed,
their return will be to the Hellfire," closing the zaqqum/hamim scene that
runs from 37:63 (shipped) through 37:67.

Sources (all 8 fetched via PREP.md, none re-fetched): as-Sa'di (marji'
glossed three ways — ma'aluhum, muqarruhum, ma'wahum — with the purpose
clause "to taste its severe punishment and tremendous heat"), al-Qurtubi
(Abu 'Ubaydah's point that "thumma" may carry the sense of plain "wa";
Maqatil's view that al-hamim sits outside Jahannam, people led out to drink
it and returned; al-Qushayri's rival view that it is simply a place within
Jahannam, at its edge), at-Tabari and Ibn Kathir ar/en (Qatada's citation of
55:44 beside this verse, graded by Ibn Kathir "a good, strong
interpretation"; Ibn Mas'ud's oath that midday on Judgment Day won't come
until Paradise's and the Fire's people already rest in their places, citing
25:24; Ibn Zayd's minority gloss that marji' means "their death," flagged by
the printed edition's own footnote as possibly a copyist's error), al-Baghawi
(matches Ibn Kathir's wording for Ibn Mas'ud's variant reading).

Maqatil vs. al-Qushayri on where al-hamim sits kept as a live, unresolved
disagreement. Ibn Mas'ud's qira'a is transmitted as two different words
across the four tafsirs — منقلبهم (at-Tabari/al-Qurtubi) and مقيلهم
(Ibn Kathir/al-Baghawi) — both named rather than collapsed into one.

Corrected the brief's cited cross-reference: "this is the Hellfire which the
criminals used to deny" is 55:43, verified against data/quran-json and
data/translations/en.json, not 32:20. 25:24's wording verified likewise.

No hadith attaches to this verse in any source fetched; the ledger says so
rather than reaching for the stock Bukhari 660 fallback. No asbab found.

Licensing sentence carried in both languages (§8): the wrongdoers this verse
describes are the group named in 37:63; the verse is Allah's judgment in the
Hereafter, not a label for any living person or community.

Dropped: Ibn Kathir's own grammatical remark on "thumma" (redundant with Abu
'Ubaydah's point); al-Muyassar/Ma'arif read but not separately named; the
extended 37:66-67 suffering narration, not rebuilt. 37:69-74 kept as future
ground.
