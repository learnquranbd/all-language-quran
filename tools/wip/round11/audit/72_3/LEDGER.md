## Hadith
- Sahih al-Bukhari 844 (Darussalam), https://quranx.com/hadith/Bukhari/DarusSalam/Hadith-844 (saved hadith_Bukhari_844.txt). First ten words (EN page): "Narrated Warrad: (the clerk of Al-Mughira bin Shu`ba) Once Al-Mughira dictated". Arabic ends "وَقَالَ الْحَسَنُ الْجَدُّ غِنًى". Grading: in al-Bukhari's Sahih; no separate grade claimed. Translation is ours; the page's English renders the jadd clause "no struggler's effort can benefit against You" (stated in the article). Marked as not attached to the verse by any tafsir read; al-Qurtubi cites the phrase only as usage.
- No hadith on the verse itself; the 72:1 listening-incident hadiths Ibn Kathir refers to ("We have already presented the Hadiths") were not fetched or used.
