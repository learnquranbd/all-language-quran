Card and article on the jinn's declaration that their Lord's jadd is exalted
and that He has taken neither consort nor child; 8 sections, 1,739 EN words.

Sources: at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi, al-Baghawi, Ibn Kathir (ar + abridged en),
as-Sa'di, Muyassar, Ma'arif, all fetched on 72:3.

- jadd glosses kept attributed and unranked except where a source ranks:
  command/power (Ibn Abbas via Ali b. Abi Talha, Qatada, as-Suddi, Ibn Zayd),
  majesty (Ikrima, Mujahid, Qatada), mention (Mujahid; Abu ad-Darda', Ibn
  Jurayj per Ibn Kathir), favours (Ibn Abbas via ad-Dahhak; al-Qurazi),
  dominion (Abu Ubayda, al-Akhfash), wealth (al-Hasan; Anas, Ikrima per
  al-Qurtubi). at-Tabari's own ranking (greatness, power, authority; jadd as
  hazz/bakht) reported as his.
- Grandfather reading: at-Tabari's rejection, al-Qurtubi's report (Muhammad b.
  Ali, Ja'far as-Sadiq, ar-Rabi': ignorant jinn speech, not held to account),
  al-Qushayri's caution, Ibn Kathir's "good chain, but I do not understand it".
- Anna/inna across the surah: al-Qurtubi's fatha readers and his kasra
  preference (Abu Ubayda, Abu Hatim); al-Baghawi's kasra preference;
  at-Tabari's split preference (fatha for revelation, kasra for jinn speech)
  and his differing account of Asim. Rankings kept per source.
- Hadith: Bukhari 844 (al-Mughira, dhikr after obligatory prayer, "wa la
  yanfa'u dha l-jaddi minka l-jadd", with al-Hasan's "al-jadd is wealth"),
  confirmed on quranx; marked as usage, not attached to the verse.
- Licensing sentence: the verse names no human community and licenses nothing
  against any living person or people.
- Dropped: non-standard jadd readings, poetry, al-Farra's grammar, the 72:1
  listening hadiths (not fetched). Asbab not used.
