## Attributions, each on its Arabic clause
- at-Tabari (15): "fayadaʿ amākinahā min al-arḍ idhā nasafahā" → He leaves the places of the
  mountains once scattered. qāʿ = "arḍan malsāʾ"; ṣafṣaf = "mustawiyan lā nabāta fīhi wa-lā
  nashz wa-lā irtifāʿ". Ibn ʿAbbās: "mustawiyan lā nabāta fīhi". Mujahid: "mustawiyan".
  Kufan linguists: "al-qāʿ mustanqaʿ al-māʾ, wa-l-ṣafṣaf alladhī lā nabāta fīhi".
  Kaʿb/ʿAbd al-Malik anecdote: "innamā al-ṣakhra jabal min al-jibāl" + the verse cited.
- al-Qurtubi (90): qāʿ = "al-arḍ al-malsāʾ bilā nabāt wa-lā bināʾ" (Ibn al-Aʿrābī);
  al-Jawhari: "al-mustawī min al-arḍ"; al-Farrāʾ: "al-qāʿ mustanqaʿ al-māʾ, wa-l-ṣafṣaf
  al-qarʿāʾ"; al-Kalbi: "alladhī lā nabāta fīhi"; Mujahid: "ka-annahu ʿalā ṣaffin wāḥidin
  fī istiwāʾih"; summary "wa-l-maʿnā wāḥid… al-qāʿ al-munkashif, al-ṣafṣaf al-mustawī
  al-amlas".
- as-Sa'di (91, on 105–107): mountains "takūnu kal-ʿihn wa-kal-raml, thumma yadukkuhā
  fa-yajʿaluhā habāʾan munbathhan… wa-yajʿalu al-arḍ qāʿan ṣafṣafan"; ʿiwaj absence =
  "tamām istiwāʾihā"; amt = "awdiya wa-amākin munkhafiḍa aw murtafiʿa"; "tabruzu al-arḍ
  wa-tattasiʿu lil-khalāʾiq wa-yamudduhā Allāhu madd al-adīm". Stopped before "yasmaʿuhum
  al-dāʿī" (that is 20:108 — future ground, not preempted).
- Ibn Kathir (14 ar / 169 en): "bisāṭan wāḥidan"; qāʿ = "al-mustawī min al-arḍ"; ṣafṣaf =
  taʾkīd, "wa-qīla alladhī lā nabāta fīhi. wa-l-awwal awlā, wa-in kāna al-ākhar murādan
  bi-l-lāzim". 20:107: "lā tarā fīhā wādiyan wa-lā rābiyatan…"; Salaf named: Ibn ʿAbbās,
  ʿIkrimah, Mujahid, al-Hasan al-Baṣrī, ad-Ḍaḥḥāk, Qatādah.
- al-Baghawi (94): "fayadaʿ amākin al-jibāl"; qāʿ = "mā inbasaṭa min al-arḍ"; ṣafṣaf =
  "al-amlas".
- al-Muyassar (16, 106–107): earth left "munbasiṭatan mustawiyatan malsāʾ lā nabāta fīhā,
  lā yarā… maylan wa-lā irtifāʿan wa-lā inkhifāḍan".

## Disagreement kept (not resolved into one voice)
ṣafṣaf = "level" (Ibn ʿAbbās, Mujahid, via at-Tabari) vs "no vegetation" (Kufan
linguists, al-Kalbi, al-Farrāʾ's qarʿāʾ). Presented as a difference in §5, then Ibn
Kathir's own reconciliation (level preferred, bareness entailed) reported as his, not
imposed as verdict.

## Hadith
None. No tafsir consulted attaches a marfūʿ hadith to 20:106. The Salaf material
(Ibn ʿAbbās, Mujahid, ʿIkrimah, al-Hasan, ad-Ḍaḥḥāk, Qatādah) is athar — word-glosses,
not narrations to the Prophet. Stated plainly in §7. hadith.js NOT run (nothing to
attach; the PREP example number was not pursued to avoid filling the slot with an
unattached narration).
