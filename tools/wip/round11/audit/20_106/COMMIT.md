Card and deep bilingual article on "fayadharuhā qāʿan ṣafṣafan" — the
middle line of the 20:105–107 answer about the mountains on the Day.

Sources: at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi, as-Sa'di, Ibn Kathir (ar + en abridged),
al-Baghawi, al-Muyassar. Lexical core built on the two nouns: qāʿ = a wide
open, uncovered expanse (Ibn al-Aʿrābī, al-Jawhari, al-Baghawi; al-Farrāʾ's
minority "where water gathers"), ṣafṣaf = utterly level and bare (al-amlas;
Mujahid's ṣaff etymology; al-Farrāʾ's qarʿāʾ). as-Sa'di supplies the stages —
mountains to wool, to sand, to scattered dust — and the earth stretched like
leather to widen the Gathering. Kept at-Tabari's Kaʿb/ʿAbd al-Malik report
that even the Rock is "a mountain among mountains."

Disagreement kept: ṣafṣaf as "level" (Ibn ʿAbbās, Mujahid) vs "no vegetation"
(Kufan linguists), then Ibn Kathir's own reconciliation reported as his.

Hadith: none. No tafsir attaches a marfūʿ report to this verse; the Salaf
material is athar, and §7 says so plainly rather than filling the slot.

Ma'arif al-Qur'an dropped (its grouped text covers the Horn, 20:102, not 106).
20:108 (the Caller) left untouched as future ground. Not a condemned-group
verse, so no licensing sentence.
