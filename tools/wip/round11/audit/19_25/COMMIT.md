Maryam is told to shake the palm; ripe dates fall — means and reliance.

Card + deep bilingual article on 19:25, the command to Maryam (AS) under the
palm to shake its trunk for fresh dates while a stream runs beneath her.

Sources: at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi, al-Baghawi, Ibn Kathir (ar + en abridged),
as-Saʿdi, al-Muyassar and Maʿarif al-Qurʾan, all fetched on 19:25.

What the sources gave:
- huzzi = shake/move it (Tabari via Ibn Zayd; Baghawi); the baʾ in bi-jidhʿ read
  as an added, emphatic particle (Tabari, Qurtubi, Baghawi).
- the trunk's state kept as a real disagreement: dry/withered (Ibn ʿAbbas, Abu
  Nahik), fruit-bearing ʿajwa (Mujahid), ṣarfana (Thawri from Abu Dawud), a cut
  trunk revived (Suddi); Ibn Kathir's middle reading — a tree out of its season
  (Wahb); Tabari's tarjih that the readings converge.
- the article's core: Qurtubi's masʾala that provision is decreed yet tied to the
  servant's effort, and that earning is sunnat Allah that does not dent tawakkul
  (against the ignorant ascetics); the 3:37 contrast (heart-free vs heart-full).
- janiyyan: taken fresh (Tabari), not dried/hardened/long from the hand — the
  sound view of Abu ʿAmr b. al-ʿAlaʾ (Qurtubi), at its peak (Baghawi), fresh and
  beneficial (Saʿdi), picked that hour (Muyassar); ruṭab vs dried tamr.
- childbirth note as athar of ʿAmr b. Maymun (Tabari, Ibn Kathir) and ar-Rabiʿ b.
  Khuthaym (Qurtubi, Baghawi), plus Qurtubi's taḥnik link; water-then-food order
  (Maʿarif); Ṭalḥa b. Sulayman's two benefits, body and heart (Qurtubi).

Hadith: none. No source attaches a sound marfuʿ hadith to the verse; the article
says so in one sentence. taḥnik mentioned only as Qurtubi's linkage, no number.

19:24 (caller from below: Jibril or ʿIsa) noted as disputed and left unresolved,
per the brief. Dropped: Ibn Masʿud's «برنيا» reading (Qurtubi: not authentic),
Malik's naqsh fiqh point, the full qiraʾat list — all logged in the ledger.
