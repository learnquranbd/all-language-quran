## Attributions and the Arabic clause each rests on
- huzzī = "move it / shake it": Tabari (Ibn Zayd: «قال: حركيها»); Baghawi («حركي … تقول العرب: هزه وهز به»).
- the bāʾ in bi-jidhʿ is an added/emphatic particle: Tabari («وأدخلت الباء … دخولها وخروجها بمعنى»); Qurtubi («الباء في قوله: بجذع زائدة مؤكدة»); Baghawi («هزه وهز به … أمدد الحبل وأمدد به»).
- trunk was dry/withered: Ibn ʿAbbas via Tabari («كان جذعًا يابسًا»), Abu Nahik («كانت نخلة يابسة»); Qurtubi «كان جذعا نخرا».
- fruit-bearing / ʿajwa: Mujahid via Tabari, Qurtubi, Ibn Kathir ar («قال مجاهد: كانت عجوة»).
- ṣarfāna variety: ath-Thawri from Abu Dawud Nufayʿ al-Aʿma, via Ibn Kathir ar («كانت صرفانة»).
- cut trunk that became a palm when shaken: as-Suddi via Tabari («كان جذعا منها مقطوعا فهزّته فإذا هو نخلة»).
- outwardly a tree but out of its fruiting season → the favour of food/drink: Ibn Kathir ar (Wahb: «لم تكن في إبان ثمرها … امتن عليها بذلك»).
- Tabari's tarjīḥ: the readings converge (trunk vs whole palm) — Tabari («فقد صار معناه ومعنى من قال … واحدا»).
- shaking the dry trunk = to see a further sign, reviving of dead wood: Qurtubi masʾala 1 («أمرها بهز الجذع اليابس لترى آية أخرى في إحياء موات الجذع»).
- provision is decreed yet Allah ties it to the servant's effort (sign would have come without shaking): Qurtubi masʾala 2 («الرزق وإن كان محتوما فإن الله … وكل ابن آدم إلى سعي … وكانت الآية تكون بألا تهز»).
- earning provision is sunnat Allah and does not detract from tawakkul, against the ignorant ascetics: Qurtubi masʾala 3 («سنة الله … لا يقدح في التوكل، خلافا لما تقوله جهال المتزهدة»).
- 3:37 contrast (provision came without earning before; heart-free vs heart-attached): Qurtubi masʾala 3 («كلما دخل عليها زكريا المحراب وجد عندها رزقا … لما كان قلبها فارغا … فلما ولدت عيسى … وكلها إلى كسبها»).
- janī = taken fresh/moist (mafʿūl → faʿīl): Tabari («المجني: المأخوذ طريا»).
- janī = not dried, not hardened, not long from the picker's hand — "the sound view": Abu ʿAmr ibn al-ʿAlaʾ via Qurtubi («لم يذو … لم يجف ولم ييبس ولم يبعد عن يدي مجتنيه؛ وهذا هو الصحيح»).
- janī = reached its utmost, time of picking come: Baghawi («بلغ الغاية وجاء أوان اجتنائه»); Qurtubi («طابت وصلحت للاجتناء»).
- fresh, delicious, beneficial: as-Saʿdi («طريا لذيذا نافعا»).
- soft, freshly picked at that hour: al-Muyassar («رطبًا غَضًّا جُنِيَ من ساعته»).
- vivid revival: fronds, spadix, green→red→ripe in the blink of an eye, no bruising: Ibn ʿAbbas via Qurtubi («كان جذعا نخرا فلما هزت … كل ذلك في طرفة عين … لا ينشدخ منه شيء»).
- the reciters differed (trunk vs palm as subject of the dropping); grammarians' readings: Qurtubi (nine readings via az-Zamakhshari); Tabari (three close readings converge).
- in winter, out of season: Wahb ibn Munabbih via Tabari («يتساقط عليها وذلك في الشتاء»).
- nothing better for a woman in childbed than dates (fresh and dried): ʿAmr ibn Maymun via Tabari and Ibn Kathir en («ما من شيء خير للنفساء من التمر والرطب»).
- nothing better for the woman in childbirth than fresh dates; had Allah known better He would have fed Maryam it; nor for the sick than honey: ar-Rabiʿ ibn Khuthaym via Qurtubi and Baghawi («ما للنفساء عندي خير من الرطب … ولو علم الله شيئا … لأطعمه مريم»).
- dates as later custom for the mother + taḥnīk link: Qurtubi («التمر عادة للنفساء … وكذلك التحنيك»).
- order of gifts: water (stream) named first, then food (dates): Maʿārif al-Qurʾān («water was mentioned first and then date as something to eat»).
- Allah blessed her with food and drink in need: Ibn Kathir ar/en («امتن عليها … جعل عندها طعاما وشرابا» / "blessed her by giving her food and drink").
- two benefits in the stream + the dates (sustenance; consolation of the breast), both miracles: Ṭalḥa ibn Sulayman via Qurtubi («فائدتين … الأكل والشرب … سلوة الصدر لكونهما معجزتين»).

## Hadith
- None used. No tafsir gathered attaches a sound marfūʿ Prophetic hadith directly to 19:25. The
  supporting statements (ʿAmr ibn Maymun, ar-Rabiʿ ibn Khuthaym) are āthār of Companions/Successors,
  reported by the mufassirūn, not Prophetic narrations; the article says so explicitly. No hadith
  number fetched. taḥnīk (a separate, well-attested sunnah) is mentioned only as Qurtubi's linkage,
  not cited as a hadith on this verse.

## Asbab
- Not used. 19:25 is narrative (the Maryam sequence); no asbāb an-nuzūl claimed. Placement given
  instead: it follows 19:23-24 (the pains, the palm, the voice from below) and precedes 19:26.

## 19:24 dispute — deliberately NOT resolved
- Whether the caller "from below" was Jibrīl or the newborn ʿĪsā is disputed (Ibn Kathir en: Ibn
  ʿAbbas, Saʿid b. Jubayr, aḍ-Ḍaḥḥāk, ʿAmr b. Maymun, as-Suddi, Qatada → Jibrīl; Mujahid, al-Ḥasan,
  and Ibn Zayd/Ibn Jarir's preference → ʿĪsā). Left open per brief; only noted as existing.

## Dropped (could not confirm or off-verse)
- Ibn Masʿūd's reading «رطبا جنيا برنيا»: Qurtubi says «ولا يصح» (not authentic) — dropped.
- Malik's fiqh point on nقش (forcing unripe dates) via Ibn Wahb (Qurtubi): real but a tangential
  legal ruling, not reflection material — dropped.
- The full nine qirāʾāt list (Qurtubi via Zamakhshari) and Tabari's poetry shawāhid — mentioned only
  as "the reciters differed / readings converge," not enumerated.
- as-Suddi's "a river ran for her in the miḥrāb": belongs to the 19:24 stream, not the palm — left out.
