Key widened from 3:171 to 3:169-171: do not think those killed in Allah's way
dead; alive with their Lord, provided for, rejoicing, given good news of those
behind them, and the reward of the believers never lost. New card and a
1,750-word, 9-section article. 3:172 cited as text only; 3:173 shipped; 2:154
cited plainly (it forbids the word, 3:169 the thought).

Sources: at-Tabari (with Qatada, Ibn Ishaq, as-Suddi), Ibn Kathir (ar, en abr.,
with Ibn Zayd), al-Qurtubi, al-Baghawi, as-Sa'di, al-Muyassar and Ma'arif on
3:169, 3:170 and 3:171.
- Nature of the life kept as al-Qurtubi's list of views (real; souls returned
  in graves; Mujahid's fragrance; figurative/remembrance; green birds, his
  preference), al-Baghawi's readings, al-Muyassar's barzakh life, and Ma'arif:
  its reality is known only to the Creator, and there is no need to know.
- 'Inda as nearness of honour (al-Qurtubi), rank and closeness (as-Sa'di).
Kept as differences, no position: occasion (Uhud, Bi'r Ma'una, Badr, the
bereaved families, or all together, per al-Qurtubi); the good news concerns
brothers yet to be martyred (at-Tabari, Qatada) or all believers (az-Zajjaj,
Ibn Furak in al-Qurtubi); anna vs al-Kisa'i's inna, at-Tabari preferring anna.
Hadith: Abu Dawud 2520 (summarised, al-Qurtubi: sound chain); Tirmidhi 3010
(Jabir's father, hasan gharib from this route); Muslim 1887 (green birds);
Bukhari 2803 via al-Baghawi ("Allah knows best who is wounded in His way");
Bukhari 2829 (five kinds of shahid) and 1244 (Jabir's weeping not forbidden),
both marked as not tied to the verse.
Register: consolation; §8 states the verses license nothing against anyone
living and make no one a martyr by his own claim.
Dropped: al-Qurtubi's unsourced "five honours" report, Mujahid's "swords are
keys of Paradise", undecaying bodies, Ahmad-only hadiths (Ka'b's bird, river
Bariq), al-Qurtubi's fiqh of washing and debt.
