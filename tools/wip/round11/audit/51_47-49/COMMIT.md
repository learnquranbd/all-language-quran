Replaces the 778-word, 6-section article with a 1,778-word, 8-section one,
from at-Tabari, Ibn Kathir (ar and en), al-Qurtubi, as-Sa'di, al-Baghawi,
the Muyassar and Ma'arif al-Qur'an on 51:47-49. Slots checked. The shipped
card is kept.
Placement after Ad, Thamud and Nuh's people via al-Qurtubi (both are signs);
pointers to 51:20-21 and 51:22.
Bi-aydin: at-Tabari's six chains (Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, Mansur,
Ibn Zayd, Sufyan), all "with strength"; every fetched tafsir agrees.
Musi'un kept as a spread of readings, none chosen: able (Ibn Abbas),
equal to it (al-Hasan), of ample means (al-Qutabi), capacity to create
(at-Tabari, with 2:236), made rich / rich (ad-Dahhak, two forms), widened
its expanses (Ibn Kathir, Muyassar, as-Sa'di, Ibn Zayd), space between
sky and earth, ample provision (Ibn Abbas, al-Hasan by rain, as-Sa'di);
al-Jawhari's "rich and able" as al-Qurtubi's summary. No cosmology claim.
Farsh and mahidun: al-Qurtubi's usage and plural of majesty, Ibn Abbas
via al-Baghawi, as-Sa'di on a bed useful in every respect.
Zawjayn kept as a difference: opposites (Mujahid, preferred by at-Tabari
with his fire-and-snow argument), sun and moon (al-Hasan), male and
female (Ibn Zayd with 21:90; as-Sa'di for animals). Remembrance: the
single Creator (Ibn Kathir, al-Baghawi, al-Qurtubi with 42:11), power and
resurrection, favour. 51:50-51 via the abridged English Ibn Kathir.
Hadith: none attached by any fetched tafsir; said in one sentence.
Dropped from the shipped article: the 2:22, 20:53 and 78:6 links and the
yad-plural remark (no source cites them). 36:36 kept as one pointer.
