Card plus an 8-section bilingual article on Musa's two signs at
the valley: the hand drawn white from the collar and the gesture
against fear, as two proofs to Pharaoh.

Sources (all fetched for 28:32):
- at-Tabari, Ibn Kathir, al-Baghawi, al-Muyassar: baydaa min ghayri
  suu = white WITHOUT baras/leukoderma; stated plainly in both
  languages that this is radiance (lamp, a piece of the moon,
  sunlight, snow), not disease or blemish. Guard against the
  misreading, same as shipped 27:12 but told the al-Qasas way.
- at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi, as-Sa'di on janah (hand / forearm / upper
  arm, per Ibn Abbas and Mujahid) and al-rahb (fear from the
  serpent, per Mujahid, Qatada, Ibn Zayd). Heart of the article is
  the fear-and-reassurance dimension: God steadies Musa's panic.
- Qira'at of al-rahb (rahab / ruhb) kept as a difference of sound,
  not sense, per at-Tabari and al-Qurtubi.
- fa-dhanika burhanan: staff and hand as two decisive proofs
  (Tabari burhan=bayan; Ibn Kathir; as-Sa'di), to Pharaoh and his
  mala; fasiqin = disbelievers / outside God's obedience.

Disagreements kept open: the reading that al-rahb means "sleeve"
(Himyar dialect) with az-Zamakhshari's flat rejection; al-Farra's
"staff"; al-Qushayri's "gird for the message".

Hadith: Sahih al-Bukhari 335 (Jabir), nusirtu bi'l-ru'b, used as a
general narration explicitly NOT revealed on this verse; the
article states no sound marfu' hadith is narrated specifically on
28:32 in the tafsirs fetched.

Carries the "licenses nothing against any living person or
community" sentence on Pharaoh's court in both languages. Future
ground (28:33-34) not preempted.
