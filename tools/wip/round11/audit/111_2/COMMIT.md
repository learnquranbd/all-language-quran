Card and 8-section bilingual article on ma aghna 'anhu maluhu wa ma
kasab, six words; 111:1 and 111:3-5 named as context only.

Sources: at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi, al-Baghawi, Ibn Kathir (ar + en
abridged), as-Sa'di, Muyassar, Ma'arif. Slot check clean: every
Arabic slot discusses 111:2.
- First ma: negation or question (Qurtubi, both allowed); question
  form (Tabari); ma yughni, then qila question (Baghawi).
- Second ma: relative or masdariyya (Qurtubi); al-A'mash's wa ma
  iktasab from Ibn Mas'ud (Qurtubi).
- ma kasab: children (Ibn 'Abbas and others, list incl. 'Ata' by name
  only, Ibn Kathir; Tabari + Mujahid; Baghawi qila; Muyassar); rank,
  jah (Qurtubi); trade profits or children (Ma'arif); unspecified
  (Sa'di). Kept as a range, none chosen.
- Time: this world (Ma'arif), future (Muyassar), past (Sa'di).
Occasion: ransom with wealth and children, Ibn Mas'ud (Ibn Kathir
"dhukira", Baghawi) and Ibn 'Abbas (Qurtubi, Ma'arif); no chain or
grading in any.
Hadith: Abu Dawud 3528 quoted whole (no grading on page; general,
not attached to the verse). Bukhari 4770 too long to quote; pointed
to 26:214 only.
Licensing sentence in both languages: the verse concerns that man
alone, no ground for any statement about descendants, relatives or
any family or clan today.
Dropped: "deeds" gloss and "rebuke" label (not in sources), Ma'arif's
account of his death, 111:3-5 material, harsher wording of the Abu
al-Tufayl report, 92:11/26:88/69:28/3:116 (not cited).
