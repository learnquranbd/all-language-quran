Card and 8-section bilingual article on muttaki'ina 'ala sururin
masfufa wa-zawwajnahum bi-hurin 'in, read as the continuation of
52:17-19 after the Fire of 52:14-16. 52:21 a pointer only.

Sources: at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi, as-Sa'di, Ibn Kathir (ar; en abridged
for 52:17-20), al-Baghawi, al-Muyassar (52:19-20). Ma'arif fetched for
the wrong group (52:9-18) and not used.
- Reclining: as-Sa'di (settled sitting; adorned ara'ik); omitted
  namariq per at-Tabari and al-Qurtubi; Ibn Abbas "in al-hijal" via
  Ibn Kathir (en: "howdahs").
- Masfufa: rows / side by side (at-Tabari, al-Baghawi, Ibn al-A'rabi)
  vs facing each other (Ibn Kathir with 37:44, Muyassar); as-Sa'di on
  number, order and gentle company.
- Zawwajnahum kept as a disagreement: Mujahid "ankahnahum" (married)
  vs al-Qurtubi/Yunus ibn Habib "qarannahum" (joined, 37:22), with
  al-Farra's Azd Shanu'a usage; at-Tabari "make a pair" (males of the
  muttaqin); Ibn Kathir names companions and wives. No position taken.
- Hur/'in: at-Tabari's definitions, Muyassar, as-Sa'di (form and
  character); hur as women of Paradise vs believing women of the world
  and women's reward: not in the fetched texts, stated as such.
Hadith: none. Ibn Kathir's Ibn Abi Hatim (al-Haytham ibn Malik) and
Thabit "balaghana" reports unconfirmable, dropped.
Asbab: none used.
Dropped: al-Qurtubi's chainless reports on the couches' gold, size and
lowering; as-Sa'di's longing sentence (sobriety); 44:54, 55:72, 56:22
(not cited by a fetched tafsir).
