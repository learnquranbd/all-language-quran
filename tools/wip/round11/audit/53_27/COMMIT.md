Card and 8-section bilingual article for 53:27: inna alladhina la
yu'minuna bil-akhirati la-yusammuna al-mala'ikata tasmiyata al-untha.

Sources: Tabari (with Mujahid via Ibn Abi Najih), Muyassar, Qurtubi,
Baghawi, Sa'di, Ibn Kathir (ar; en checked), Ma'arif (53:28 only).
- Who: deniers of the resurrection (Tabari), kuffar al-'Arab who
  neither affirm the Hereafter nor work for it (Muyassar).
- Sa'di makes the denial the cause of the naming; Tabari and
  Muyassar use it as identification only. Kept without position.
- tasmiyat al-untha: Tabari/Mujahid plural inath; Qurtubi ka-,
  Baghawi bi-; Muyassar i'tiqad jahlan; Ibn Kathir inkar. Same report.
- Qurtubi: angels and idols both called daughters; tied to 53:19-21.
- Root s-m-y only at 53:23 and 53:27 in the surah (own reading).
- Sa'di's two failures: no tanzih of Allah, no honour to the angels;
  second clause kept to the angels, not stretched to women.
- Ibn Kathir cites 43:19 ('ibad ar-Rahman, "they will be questioned").
Framed as a rebuke of an invented claim, not about women (53:45 shows
al-untha used of Allah's creation); angels' nature left alone.
Licensing sentence in both languages.
Hadith: none attached; Ibn Kathir's zann narration sits under 53:28
and concerns suspicion, noted, not quoted. Asbab: none.
Dropped: 37:150/37:153/17:40/52:39 by number (not tafsir-cited here),
intercession-hope link to 53:26, naming Quraysh.
