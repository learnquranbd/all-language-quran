## Disagreements / different readings kept
- Who proposes the increase: the creditor's "pay or increase" (Ibn Kathir, al-Baghawi, as-Sa'di,
  Ibn Zayd) vs the debtor's "defer and I will add" (at-Tabari's own description); and credit sales
  (Mujahid via al-Qurtubi) vs loans. Stated side by side, no position.
- The threat in 3:131: "many commentators" = whoever holds riba lawful (al-Qurtubi) vs "the deed
  that strips faith" (al-Qurtubi's qila) vs all sins dragging towards kufr (as-Sa'di), with
  at-Tabari's "enter where they enter by opposing My command". No position taken.
- Obey Allah / the Messenger: fara'id vs sunan, or the ban on riba vs its conveying (al-Qurtubi).
- Why riba sits inside Uhud: al-Qurtubi (2:279 war -> defeat), as-Sa'di (traits of the taqwa
  that brings victory), Ibn Ishaq via at-Tabari (3:132 as reproach for the archers), Ma'arif
  (riba strips taqwa; positive taqwa follows). Stated as the commentators' reasoning, none excluding
  the others; Ibn 'Atiyyah's "no narration" quoted via al-Qurtubi.
