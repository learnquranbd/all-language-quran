Card and 8-section article on an ra'ahu istaghna, the cause the
surah gives for man's transgression in 96:6.

Sources: al-Qurtubi, al-Baghawi, Ibn Kathir (ar and abridged en),
as-Sa'di, Muyassar, Ma'arif al-Qur'an. At-Tabari's 96:7 slot returned
503; his 96:6 comment, which runs on into this verse, is used instead.

Kept as disagreements, attributed, none chosen:
- an as li-an "because" (al-Qurtubi, al-Baghawi) vs idha "when"
  (Ibn Kathir, as-Sa'di, Muyassar).
- istaghna by wealth vs by clan, supporters and helpers (al-Qurtubi's qila).
- the subject as man in general vs Abu Jahl (Muqatil via al-Baghawi);
  Ma'arif holds both.
Al-Farra' via al-Qurtubi on ra'a as a verb of judgment (ra'aytuni,
hasibtuni); the shortened-hamza reading of Mujahid, Humayd and Qunbul.

Hadith: Muslim 2797 (Abu Hurayrah, cited by Ibn Kathir) confirmed on
quranx but described, not quoted: long, and mostly on 96:9 onward.
Dropped: al-Qurtubi's ungraded Ibn Abbas (Abu Salih) report.
Licensing sentence in both languages; no verdict on wealth; final
section labelled as the article's own reading.
Fix: dropped mention of unconfirmed Ibn Abbas/Abu Salih report; dropped 'reciter' aside (general knowledge). At-Tabari 96:7 slot returned 503; his comment attributed only from his 96:6 slot.
