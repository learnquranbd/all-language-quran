Card and 8-section bilingual article for 53:32: alladhina yajtanibuna
kaba'ira al-ithmi wal-fawahisha illa al-lamam ... fa-la tuzakku anfusakum.
53:31 and 53:33 pointers only.

Sources: at-Tabari, Ibn Kathir (ar; en checked), al-Qurtubi, al-Baghawi,
as-Sa'di, al-Muyassar, Ma'arif.
- Defines the muhsinin of 53:31 (Ibn Kathir, al-Qurtubi, Muyassar);
  kaba'ir/fawahish per Tabari, Qurtubi (Muqatil), Sa'di; 4:31 link.
- Exception munqati' vs muttasil (Tabari, Baghawi; Qurtubi on 3:135-136).
- lamam kept as a disagreement, no position: Jahiliyya deeds (Ibn Abbas
  via Ali b. Abi Talha, Ibn Zayd, Zayd b. Aslam, Zayd b. Thabit); short
  of zina (Ibn Mas'ud, Masruq, Sha'bi, Abu Hurayra); a lapse then
  repentance (Ibn Abbas via Ata, Mujahid, al-Hasan, Abu Salih); between
  the two limits (Ibn Abbas via 'Awfi/Hakam, Ibn az-Zubayr, Ikrima,
  Qatada, Dahhak). Tabari's and Ibn Kathir's preference reported.
- Womb/earth per Tabari, Ibn Kathir, Sa'di, Ma'arif; Makhul.
- tazkiya per Tabari, Zayd b. Aslam, Ibn Abbas, Sa'di, Qurtubi; 4:49.
Hadith: Bukhari 6243 (lamam / share of zina), Muslim 2142 (Barra to
Zaynab), Muslim 233 (prayers expiate while major sins avoided), all
quoted whole. Tirmidhi 3284 couplet with his hasan sahih gharib and
Ibn Kathir's doubt about it as the Prophet's words; Mujahid: chanted
in Jahiliyya tawaf.
Asbab: none used (Muqatil's Nabhan story, Ibn Lahi'a report, Kalbi/
Muqatil report: no established chain). Licensing sentence for 53:33.
Dropped: kabir qira'a, sudden-glance view, Abu Bakra praise hadith
(unchecked), dream and mursal reports.
