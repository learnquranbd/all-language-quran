Adds the 20:59 card and bilingual article: Moses names the contest for
the festival day (yawm az-zina) at mid-morning (duha).

Card: the confidence of truth — the politically weaker party asks for the
largest crowd and the brightest hour; fear seeks the quiet corner, truth
seeks witnesses. No scholar names, no hadith, no asbab in the card.

Article, 8 sections, ~1,800 EN words with matched Bengali:
- Muyassar and Ibn Kathir on the plain sense: the festival/New Year day,
  a holiday when all Egypt gathered.
- The disagreement over which day is kept as a disagreement, no position:
  festival day (as-Suddi, Qatada, Ibn Zayd, Mujahid, Muqatil), Ashura
  (Ibn Abbas, Saʿid b. Jubayr), market day (Saʿid b. al-Musayyab),
  Saturday (ad-Dahhak), Nowruz (ath-Thaʿlabi); Ibn Kathir: "no contradiction."
- Wisdom of the public feast: Ibn Kathir (people witness Allah's power and
  the prophets' miracles), as-Saʿdi (largest gathering, things seen as they
  truly are), Ma'arif (all Egyptians assemble; free after their chores).
- Why the forenoon: Ibn Kathir (clearer, not by night), al-Baghawi (open
  daylight, furthest from suspicion), al-Qurtubi (start of day, room left).
- Nothing hidden, nothing "for sale" — the prophetic pattern vs. sorcery's
  need for cover; al-Qurtubi's list of purposes for the packed square; the
  outward spread of the news to town and desert (Ma'arif, al-Qurtubi).

Sources: no marfuʿ hadith attaches to the verse's words; the day-reports are
athar from the Companions and Successors, stated as such. Future ground
(20:60-64) named but not preempted.

Dropped: at-Tabari file returned 20:58's makanan suwan clause, off-verse, so
at-Tabari is not cited; Wahb b. Munabbih's "forty days" (israiliyyat) dropped.
