# SOURCES ledger — 20:59

Verse: قَالَ مَوْعِدُكُمْ يَوْمُ ٱلزِّينَةِ وَأَن يُحْشَرَ ٱلنَّاسُ ضُحًۭى
"[Moses] said, 'Your appointment is on the day of the festival, when the people assemble at mid-morning.'"

## Tafsir fetched (files in this dir) and how used

- **t14 Ibn Kathir (ar)** — USED (named "Ibn Kathir"). Arabic clauses relied on:
  - yawm az-zīna = their festival/Nowruz, free from work, all gather:
    «وهو يوم عيدهم ونوروزهم وتفرغهم من أعمالهم واجتماعهم جميعهم».
  - purpose of the public day: «ليشاهد الناس قدرة الله على ما يشاء، ومعجزات الأنبياء،
    وبطلان معارضة السحر لخوارق العادات النبوية».
  - ḍuḥā = forenoon, more manifest: «ضحوة من النهار ليكون أظهر وأجلى وأبين وأوضح، وهكذا
    شأن الأنبياء، كل أمرهم واضح بين ليس فيه خفاء ولا ترويج؛ ولهذا لم يقل ليلا ولكن نهارا ضحى».
  - opinions on the day: Ibn ʿAbbās = ʿĀshūrāʾ; as-Suddī/Qatāda/Ibn Zayd = their festival;
    Saʿīd b. Jubayr = market day; «ولا منافاة» (no contradiction). All used in §2.
- **t169 Ibn Kathir (en, abridged, covers 20:57–59)** — USED as the English witness for the
  same points (festival/New Year, holiday from work, large gathering; ḍuḥā "most visible, well
  lit, apparent and obvious"; "this is the way of the Prophets… never something hidden, or
  something for sale"). §1, §4, §5 lean on this wording. Grouped file — only the 20:59 portion used.
- **t90 al-Qurtubi (ar)** — USED (named "al-Qurtubi"). Clauses:
  - spread of opinions on the day: festival (Qatāda, as-Suddī); ʿĀshūrāʾ (Ibn ʿAbbās, Saʿīd b.
    Jubayr); market day (Saʿīd b. al-Musayyab); Saturday (ad-Ḍaḥḥāk); Nowruz (ath-Thaʿlabī). §2.
  - ḍuḥā singled out as start of day, room left if it dragged: «وخص الضحى لأنه أول النهار،
    فلو امتد الأمر فيما بينهم كان في النهار متسع». §4.
  - purposes of the packed day: «ليكون علو كلمة الله، وظهور دينه، وكبت الكافر، وزهوق الباطل على
    رءوس الأشهاد… لتقوى رغبة من رغب في الحق… ويشيع في جمع أهل الوبر والمدر». §6, §7.
- **t91 as-Saʿdī (ar)** — USED (named "as-Saʿdi"). Clause: «إنما سأل موسى ذلك، لأن يوم الزينة
  ووقت الضحى فيه يحصل فيه من كثرة الاجتماع، ورؤية الأشياء على حقائقها، ما لا يحصل في غيره». §3.
- **t94 al-Baghawi (ar)** — USED (named "al-Baghawi"). Clauses: day = annual festival, they adorn
  (Mujāhid, Qatāda, Muqātil, as-Suddī): «كان يوم عيد لهم يتزينون فيه ويجتمعون في كل سنة»; ḍuḥā =
  «وقت الضحوة نهارا جهارا ليكون أبعد من الريبة». §2, §4.
- **t168 Maʿārif al-Qurʾān (en)** — USED (named "Ma'arif al-Qur'an"). "wise move… festival day…
  all Egyptians, high and low… assemble"; forenoon "people, having finished their daily chores,
  are free"; "daylight and visibility are at their best"; dispersing crowd "spread the news far
  and wide… known the same day to people living in far flung places." §3, §4, §7.
- **t16 al-Muyassar (ar)** — USED (named "al-Muyassar") for the plain gloss: «موعدكم للاجتماع يوم
  العيد، حين يتزيَّن الناس، ويجتمعون من كل فج وناحية وقت الضحى». §1.

## Fetched but NOT used (with reason)

- **t15 at-Tabari (ar)** — DROPPED for this verse. The fetched text is at-Tabari's commentary on
  the phrase «مكانا سوى» of **20:58** (qirāʾāt of sīwan/suwan; "an even/fair place"), not on the
  words of 20:59 (yawm az-zīna, ḍuḥā). Nothing in it supports a 20:59 attribution, so at-Tabari
  is not named in the article. Flagged so the orchestrator knows the API returned the neighbour's
  clause under the 20:59 request.

## Hadith

- **None used.** No sound (marfūʿ) hadith is attached by the consulted tafsirs to the words of
  this verse. The identifications of "the day of adornment" are reports (āthār) from the
  Companions and Successors (Ibn ʿAbbās, Saʿīd b. Jubayr, Qatāda, as-Suddī, Ibn Zayd, Mujāhid,
  Muqātil, Saʿīd b. al-Musayyab, ad-Ḍaḥḥāk), not Prophetic narrations. Stated plainly in §8.
  (Ibn Kathir's aside «كما ثبت في الصحيح» concerns Pharaoh being destroyed on a like day — that is
  the drowning, far future ground — so it was deliberately not brought in here.)

## Asbāb an-nuzūl

- Not applicable / not used. This is a narrative verse in the Mūsā–Firʿawn sequence; its meaning
  rests on placement, not a revelation occasion. No asbāb claimed.

## Dropped / not asserted

- at-Tabari (see above): the only fetched file that was off-verse.
- Wahb b. Munabbih's report (in t14) that Pharaoh asked for "forty days" — isrāʾīliyyāt and tied
  to 20:58's negotiation, not to 20:59; dropped, no number used.
- No position taken among the competing identifications of the day (kept as a live disagreement
  per the brief). Watch-point 3: not a condemned-group verse — no licensing sentence added.
- Future ground (20:60–64: Pharaoh withdraws and gathers his plan; the magicians assemble and
  conspire) named only as "what the verses that follow tell," not detailed. §8.
