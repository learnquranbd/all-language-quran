# LEDGER 37:53

## SOURCES — tafsir fetched (all via PREP.md's pre-fetched files, none re-fetched)

- t14 Ibn Kathir (ar): `( أئذا متنا وكنا ترابا وعظاما أئنا لمدينون ) قال مجاهد، والسدي: لمحاسبون؟ وقال ابن عباس، ومحمد بن كعب القرظي: لمجزيون بأعمالنا؟` — used: the two-camp gloss (muhasabun / mujaziyyun bi-a'malina), §4.
- t15 at-Tabari: own paraphrase `أإنا لمحاسبون ومجزيُّون بعد مصيرنا عظاما ولحومنا ترابا`; reports Ibn 'Abbas `أئنا لمجازوْن بالعمل، كما تَدِين تُدان`; Qatada `أئنا لمحاسبون`; as-Suddi `محاسبون`; opens with `وهي القراءة الصحيحة عندنا التي لا يجوز خِلافُها لإجماع الحجة من القرّاء عليها`. Used: §2 (reading-consensus note), §4 (Tabari's combined paraphrase), §5 (Ibn 'Abbas's proverb `kama tadinu tudan`).
- t16 al-Muyassar: `أإذا متنا وتمزقنا وصرنا ترابًا وعظامًا، نُبعث ونُحاسب ونُجازى بأعمالنا؟` — read, not separately named (folds the same two readings together; redundant with Tabari/Qurtubi/Baghawi already cited).
- t90 al-Qurtubi: `أي مجزيون محاسبون بعد الموت` — used: §4 (paired gloss).
- t91 as-Sa'di: `أي: مجازون بأعمالنا؟... يقول صاحب الجنة لإخوانه: هذه قصتي، وهذا خبري، أنا وقريني، ما زلت أنا مؤمنا مصدقا، وهو ما زال مكذبا منكرا للبعث، حتى متنا، ثم بعثنا، فوصلت أنا إلى ما ترون، من النعيم، الذي أخبرتنا به الرسل، وهو لا شك، أنه قد وصل إلى العذاب.` — used in full: §6 (the testimony-to-friends framing, quoted and translated).
- t94 al-Baghawi: `مجزيون ومحاسبون وهذا استفهام إنكار` — used: §2 (istifham inkar), §4 (paired gloss).
- t168 Ma'arif al-Qur'an (en): used — the "identity not given in the Qur'an... cannot be said with any degree of certainty" caution (§7); the Tafsir Mazhari names Yahudah/Matrus and the 18:32 linkage (§7, hedged as "some have guessed"); the "advice to avoid bad company" passage, close paraphrase (§8); NOT used: the detailed Suyuti/tabi'in eight-thousand-dinar anecdote (distinct from the Ibn Kathir/Ibn Jarir version) — dropped to avoid conflating two differently-detailed narrations under one set of numbers.
- t169 Ibn Kathir (en, abridged): used — "Both views are correct" (§4); the Ibn Jarir/Furat bin Tha'labah two-partners narration, used in §7 with the specific figure (8000 dinars, house, marriage, two gardens, final refusal) kept to this one version's numbers only; the English rendering "indebted (Madinun)" used in §9 as a translator's-choice observation, not a tafsir attribution.

## Disagreements kept as disagreements

- Lamadinun: Mujahid/as-Suddi "muhasabun" (held to account only) vs Ibn 'Abbas/Muhammad bin Ka'b al-Qurazi "mujaziyyun bi-a'malina" (repaid for deeds) — both named, §4, with Ibn Kathir's explicit "both views are correct" also reported rather than used to erase the distinction.
- The companion's identity: Ma'arif al-Qur'an's own explicit uncertainty is kept front and center in §7 ("cannot be said with any degree of certainty"), with the Tafsir Mazhari naming and the Ibn Jarir/Furat bin Tha'labah narration both presented as commentators' guesses/reports, not settled fact.

## Dropped

- The Suyuti/tabi'in anecdote in Ma'arif al-Qur'an (different dinar figures and purchase sequence from the Ibn Kathir/Ibn Jarir version) — dropped rather than merged with the other narration, to avoid a hybrid of two distinct reports under one set of numbers.
- Any claim connecting the د-ي-ن root of "lamadinun" to the Qur'anic phrase "yawm ad-din" — this is plausible but was not stated in any of the fetched tafsir texts for this verse, so it was left out rather than asserted from memory.
- Any elaboration of 37:54-57 (looking into the Fire, "you almost ruined me") — kept strictly as unbuilt future ground per the brief's watch-point; not even gestured toward in the closing section.
- 36:78 as a cross-reference — not cited; it is not yet shipped and its exact Arabic wording was not checked against this verse's "dust and bones" phrasing, so left out per this session's practice of verifying cross-ref numbers before citing.
