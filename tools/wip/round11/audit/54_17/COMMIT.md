Replaces the 754-word, 6-section article with an 8-section, 1,737-word
source-gated article (shipped card kept).

Sources: at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi, as-Sa'di, Ibn Kathir (ar; en abridged,
grouped 54:9-17), al-Baghawi, Muyassar, Ma'arif, all on 54:17.
Placement after Nuh's story: 54:15's sign (Qatadah's surviving ship vs
Ibn Kathir's ships as a sign, with 36:41-42 and 69:11-12), licensing
sentence in both languages, 'Ad in one line. The refrain's four
stations; al-Qurtubi's reason for the repetition (54:40 has it in full);
Muyassar's urging to recite, learn and teach. at-Tabari's bayyannahu,
fassalnahu, hawwannahu (Mujahid, Ibn Zayd); Ibn Kathir's Mujahid,
as-Suddi and Ibn Abbas; seven ahruf in one line. Ma'arif's limit: easy
for dhikr, not for ijtihad or istinbat; as-Sa'di on the breadth of
dhikr. Al-Qurtubi's ifti'al for muddakir.

Disagreements kept: easy for memorising (al-Qurtubi, Sa'id ibn Jubayr
via al-Baghawi and al-Qurtubi, Ma'arif) vs for understanding and
admonition (at-Tabari, al-Baghawi) vs both (Ibn Kathir, Muyassar,
as-Sa'di, Ma'arif); the muddakir as one admonished (at-Tabari), seeker
helped (Qatadah, Matar, Abu Bakr al-Warraq, Ibn Shawdhab), one who
refrains from sin (al-Qurazi), reader (al-Qurtubi). None chosen.

Hadith: Bukhari 4874 (Ibn Mas'ud's mudhdhakir corrected to muddakir),
attached by Ibn Kathir at 54:15's question, quoted whole. No other.
Dropped: the mudhtakir sound-change account, the "washed by water"
hadith, 44:58/20:2/73:20/47:24 (no fetched tafsir cites them), and the
old article's unsourced points. Old article fix: it gave as-Sa'di the
Muyassar's "understanding and reflection".
