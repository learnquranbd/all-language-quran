- "I was sent to the unlettered ... a Book that water does not wash away" (Muslim, 'Iyad ibn Himar): not attached by any fetched tafsir. Not used.
- Sister verses 44:58, 20:2, 73:20, 47:24: no fetched tafsir cites them. Not used.
- Al-Qurtubi's Torah / 'Uzayr report: in t90 but left out (detailed in the 54:40 article; not needed here).
- From the old short article: (1) "the grammarians describe how the ta became a dal and the dhal merged" — unsourced, dropped; (2) "children who speak no Arabic", "blind men have carried it complete", "fourteen centuries in every language community" — not in the fetched texts (Ma'arif has tender-aged children and huffaz over fourteen hundred years, but not the no-Arabic or blind claims), dropped; only Ma'arif's children point kept; (3) the 44:58 / 19:97 "eased in your tongue vs in the hearer" contrast — 44:58 is cited by no fetched text and Ibn Kathir uses 19:97 as support, not contrast; dropped; (4) muddakir "a participle, so it asks after a kind of person" — unsourced, dropped; (5) "small and constant over large and rare" — unsourced, dropped; (6) the Pharaoh "fifth people without the refrain" point — not repeated.
- Found wrong in the old article: it gave as-Sa'di "meanings eased for understanding and reflection"; as-Sa'di has "للفهم والعلم" (understanding and knowledge); "understanding and reflection" (للفهم والتدبر) is the Muyassar's wording.
