## Disagreements kept (no position)
- Scope: Ibn Abbas (Tabari) "they are the disbelievers"; Ibn Kathir "disbelievers and hypocrites" (Baghawi/Qurtubi faces of both); Ka'b al-Ahbar (Baghawi, Qurtubi) "revealed only about those who stay away from the congregations".
- Glosses of the invitation listed side by side: prostration to Him generally (Tabari's own), obligatory prayer (Ibrahim at-Taymi, Sa'id ibn Jubayr via Tabari), adhan and iqamah / hayya 'ala al-falah (Qurtubi, Baghawi), the legal charge (Qurtubi, wa qila), prayer and worship (Muyassar), prostration with tawhid and worship (Sa'di). Qurtubi's own "the meanings are close" reported as his.
- Ibn Kathir "opposite of what they were" vs as-Sa'di "of the same kind as their deed": shown as two framings of one correspondence.

## Hadith
- Sahih al-Bukhari 4919 (Dar-us-Salam ref; USC-MSA 6/60/441). URL fetched: https://quranx.com/hadith/Bukhari/DarusSalam/Hadith-4919 (saved hadith_Bukhari_4919.txt). Arabic matches the text Ibn Kathir (en) quotes. First ten words used: "Our Lord Allah will lay bare His Shin, and then all". Grading: in al-Bukhari's Sahih; nothing upgraded. Quoted whole in the Darussalam wording; translator's parentheticals "(on the Day of Judgment)" and "(a single vertebra)" omitted and the page's typo "back swill" read as "backs will". Cited because Ibn Kathir's commentary on this passage quotes it and his 68:43 Arabic text paraphrases its ending. The shin clause is quoted only, with an explicit sentence that its meaning belongs to 68:42 and is not taken up.
- Qatada's "ذُكر لنا أن نبيّ الله صلى الله عليه وسلم كان يقول: يؤذن للمؤمنين يوم القيامة في السجود ..." (Tabari) and his "بلغني" version: no Companion named; mentioned in one sentence as left aside, wording not used.
- Qurtubi's "Sahih Muslim, hadith of Abu Sa'id al-Khudri" on white/black faces: reported as his remark only; not quoted, no number (not fetched).
