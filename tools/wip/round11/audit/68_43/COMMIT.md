Card and 8-section bilingual article for 68:43: khashi'atan absaruhum
tarhaquhum dhillah, wa qad kanu yud'awna ila al-sujud wa hum salimun.
68:42 named only as the previous verse; 68:44-47 left to their own verses.

Sources: Qurtubi, Baghawi, Tabari, Ibn Kathir (ar), Muyassar, Sa'di (all
on 68:43); Ibn Kathir (en, grouped 68:42-47, 68:43 portion only); Ma'arif
(grouped 68:35-43).
- Humbled eyes: dhalilah mutawadi'ah, hal (Qurtubi); munkasirah (Muyassar).
- Covering humiliation: from Allah's punishment (Tabari, Muyassar); of
  regret and sorrow (Baghawi); white/black faces (Baghawi, Qurtubi).
- Invitation glosses side by side, none picked: prostration generally
  (Tabari), obligatory prayer (Ibrahim at-Taymi, Sa'id ibn Jubayr), adhan
  and iqamah (Qurtubi, Baghawi), legal charge (Qurtubi), worship (Muyassar,
  Sa'di). Salimun: healthy/able/no ailment/no barrier; aminun (Ibn Abbas).
- Scope difference kept: disbelievers (Ibn Abbas) / disbelievers and
  hypocrites (Ibn Kathir) / those absent from congregations (Ka'b, via
  Baghawi and Qurtubi).
- Opposite (Ibn Kathir) vs same kind (Sa'di); 11:20 via Ibn Abbas,
  verified in quran-json.
Hadith: Bukhari 4919 (Abu Sa'id), cited by Ibn Kathir, quoted whole; shin
clause quoted only, meaning left to 68:42. Qatada's unnamed-source report
noted as left aside.
Licensing paragraph in both languages: no test of anyone's prayer or
sincerity, no living person described as one of these.
Dropped: "five prayers" gloss (not in sources); sifat discussion.
- Bukhari 4919 quoted from the quranx English minus its two bracketed glosses; the article says so (one quranx typo, 'back swill', corrected).
