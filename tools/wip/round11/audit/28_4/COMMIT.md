The opening portrait of Pharaoh's tyranny: self-exaltation, dividing the
people into factions, crushing a targeted group, slaughtering their sons and
keeping their women, and the verdict "of the corrupters."

Card plus a deep bilingual article (8 sections, ~1,770 EN words).

Sources (all from the fetched tafsir for 28:4):
- ʿalā as arrogance/tyranny/transgression: Ibn Kathir, at-Tabari (as-Suddi,
  Qatada), al-Baghawi; al-Qurtubi's deeper reading (Qatada: above worship of
  his Lord, claimed lordship) kept beside Ibn Abbas/as-Suddi's plain arrogance;
  as-Sa'di's "of the people of highness, not of the truly high."
- shiyaʿ as factions/classes set to forced labour (at-Tabari, Mujahid, Ibn
  Kathir, al-Baghawi, al-Qurtubi); Qatada's four fates; as-Sa'di's free hand.
- the sector = Banī Isrāʾīl, weakening = enslavement (at-Tabari/Qatada,
  al-Baghawi, as-Sa'di, Ibn Kathir).
- CRITICAL framing of "keeping the women alive": NOT mercy. Ibn Kathir
  (إهانة واحتقارا, to humiliate and debase), al-Muyassar (glosses the word as
  "enslaved their women"), al-Baghawi (the clause is istiḍʿāf). The article
  states the surviving woman is a captive, counted among the harms.
- the dream/soothsayer background attributed to at-Tabari (as-Suddi) and
  al-Qurtubi, with az-Zajjaj's remark on its folly; not presented as asbab.
- the mufsid verdict unpacked from at-Tabari, as-Sa'di, al-Qurtubi.

Licensing: a prominent section states in both languages that the verse
condemns a tyrant and his regime, not Egyptians or any living people, and
licenses nothing against any living person or community — and is no warrant to
brand any living community "Pharaoh."

Hadith: Sahih al-Bukhari 4686 (Abū Mūsā), "Allah gives respite to the
wrongdoer, but when He seizes him He does not let him escape," then 11:102;
quoted whole, graded only as its inclusion in the Ṣaḥīḥ. Avoided the stock
Bukhari 660 and Bukhari 3443 (26:123).

Future ground (28:5 and 28:6) gestured at lightly as the divine counter-current,
content left for those verses. Nothing dropped for want of confirmation.
