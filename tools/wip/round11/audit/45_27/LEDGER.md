## Reports
- Sufyan ath-Thawri and al-Ma'afiri (Ibn Kathir ar, from Ibn Abi Hatim): reported exactly as given; Ibn Kathir quotes no chain and gives no grading; said so. Marked as a report about later figures, not a hadith. The referent of «فما زالت تعرف» is unstated; said so rather than guessed.

## Hadith
- None. No fetched tafsir attaches a hadith to 45:27; stated in one sentence. hadith.js not run (nothing to confirm).
- Ma'arif's "Do not revile time" belongs to 45:24 in its grouped text; not used.
