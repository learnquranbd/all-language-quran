Card and 8-section bilingual article for 45:27: wa lillahi mulku
as-samawati wal-ard, wa yawma taqumu as-sa'atu yawma'idhin yakhsaru
al-mubtilun. Placed after 45:24-26 in two lines; 45:28 not developed.

Sources: Tabari, Qurtubi, Sa'di, Muyassar, Baghawi, Ibn Kathir (ar +
en 45:27 lines). Ma'arif's group covers only 45:24; not named.
- Mulk: Tabari's sultan turned into an argument against partners;
  Qurtubi khalqan wa mulkan; Muyassar adds 'ubudiyya; Ibn Kathir owner
  and ruler in both worlds; Sa'di breadth and sole management. Unranked.
- Yawma / yawma'idhin: Qurtubi's emphasis-or-badal analysis and his
  "it was said" reading (His is the dominion on that Day), both kept.
- Al-mubtilun: rejecters of revealed proofs (Ibn Kathir, Muyassar);
  speakers of the partner claim and worshippers of other gods (Tabari);
  those who brought falsehood to refute truth, deeds void (Sa'di);
  ashab al-abatil (Baghawi). Kept side by side.
- Yakhsaru: Tabari's ghubn, places in Paradise exchanged for places in
  the Fire; Qurtubi's omitted object, places in Paradise; Sa'di deeds
  voided and reward missed; Baghawi loss made apparent in the Fire.
Report: Sufyan ath-Thawri's question to al-Ma'afiri, via Ibn Abi
Hatim, as Ibn Kathir gives it; no chain or grading in the text, said so.
Hadith: none attached by any tafsir; said in one sentence.
Asbab: none in the sources; not used.
Licensing sentence for the condemned mubtilun in both languages; no
present-day group named.
Dropped: 40:78-79 parallel (no tafsir cites it), Ibn Kathir (en)
45:28-29 material, Ma'arif's 45:24 dahr material.
- Gate: heading changed from one already taken in 40_27 draft; five
  number-parity wordings adjusted.
