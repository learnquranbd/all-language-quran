Card and 8-section bilingual article for 74:15, the man's hope for more
after wealth stretched wide, sons present and a smoothed path (74:12-14
as context). 74:16 "kalla" is used only as the reply. Identity given in
one sentence and deferred to 74:11; the magic verdict left to 74:23.

Sources: Tabari, Muyassar, Qurtubi, Sa'di, Baghawi, Ibn Kathir (en
grouped 74:11-20), Ma'arif (en grouped 74:12-21). All named. Ibn Kathir
(ar) fetch failed (503 on retry); not used.
- azid kept as a disagreement: more of this world (Tabari, Baghawi,
  Muyassar; Qurtubi's first reading) vs Paradise (al-Hasan and others in
  Qurtubi, with al-Walid's "Paradise was created only for me"; Sa'di).
  Qurtubi's two "it was said" readings (left to his heirs; support in his
  disbelief) reported unnamed. No reading set aside.
- thumma as astonishment, not sequence (Qurtubi, with 6:1).
- yatma'u glossed as hoping (Tabari, Baghawi, Muyassar); same verb of
  Ibrahim (AS) at 26:82 and in 7:56, so the fault is placed where the
  sources place it: hope held while disbelieving / rejecting the signs.
- Ma'arif's Ibn 'Abbas (Makkah to Ta'if) and Thawri (ten million dinars,
  some estimate less) reports given as Ma'arif's.
- 'anid: Tabari's camel 'anud; the "grazes apart" detail attributed to
  the edition's footnote citing Lisan al-'Arab, not to Tabari.
Hadith: none; no fetched tafsir attaches a sound hadith (one sentence).
Asbab: not used (owned by 74:11/74:23).
Licensing paragraph in EN and BN: nothing against any living person or
community, and no reading of the verse onto the wealthy today.
Dropped: Wahid ibn al-Wahid title, sons' numbers (165 only), Ma'arif's
digressions on lying and children abroad.
