Replaces the 798-word, 6-section article with a 1,781-word, 9-section
one, from at-Tabari, Ibn Kathir (ar and en), al-Qurtubi, as-Sa'di,
al-Baghawi, al-Muyassar and Ma'arif on 4:142 and 4:143. All slots
checked. The shipped card is kept.
Placement: 4:138-141 politics, then prayer; one line to 2:9 (no reuse of
its article), one bridging line to 4:144-145, the 4:146 exception named.
khadi'uhum: Ibn Kathir (Allah is not deceived; istidraj, forsaking),
at-Tabari (blood protected by the profession, istidraj to the Fire);
the light put out on the Day from as-Suddi, Ibn Jurayj and al-Hasan via
at-Tabari (57:13); requital in al-Qurtubi, al-Baghawi, al-Muyassar
("with the like of their deed"); as-Sa'di's self-deception reading.
kusala: al-Baghawi, at-Tabari (no certainty of return), as-Sa'di (loss
of desire), Ibn Kathir's outward/inward split, Ibn Abbas via Ibn Kathir;
Thanavi's doctrinal-laziness distinction from Ma'arif.
illa qalila kept as a disagreement: at-Tabari (dhikr of show, mirage),
al-Hasan and Ibn Abbas (for other than Allah), Qatada (not accepted),
al-Qurtubi's three readings, Ibn Kathir (no khushu').
mudhabdhabin: at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi (Ibn Jinni, the readings); who
"these and those" are kept as Mujahid (Companions/Jews) against Qatada,
as-Suddi, Ibn Zayd (believers/polytheists); al-Baghawi's legal reading.
Hadith: Bukhari 657, Muslim 622 and 2784, each quoted whole; Ibn Umar's
correction of a variant only as Ibn Kathir cites it from Ahmad.
Licensing sentence in both languages; the lesson turned inward with
as-Sa'di and al-Qurtubi (riya in fard and nafl, 23:1 and 23:2).
Dropped: Ibn Jurayj's naming of Ibn Ubayy (not an established occasion),
Ibn Kathir's unsourced and Abu Ya'la reports, the mawquf/mursal parables.
Corrected from the shipped article: its Muslim ewe hadith added "not
knowing which to follow" (not in Muslim's wording); 4:145 called
"already said"; Ibn Abi Mulaykah, 20:7 and the verb-form claim unsourced.
