Card and deep bilingual article for the creed planted in the middle of
al-Qasas: He is Allah, no deity but He; all praise His in the first life
and the Hereafter; His the ḥukm; to Him you are returned.

Four attributes, one section each, read against the surrounding Qiyamah
scene (28:64-69) rather than run ahead into 28:71-72 or 28:76:
- Tawhid: at-Tabari, Ibn Kathir, al-Qurtubi, al-Muyassar; Ibn Kathir's
  link back to the Lord who "creates and chooses" in 28:68.
- al-Hamd: dominant reading (dunya + Hereafter) on at-Tabari and
  al-Muyassar; the wider "all He does, first and last" reading kept as a
  non-conflicting second on Ibn Kathir. Both land on al-Qurtubi: praise
  His alone. al-Baghawi's picture of the awliya praising in both houses.
- al-Hukm: as-Sa'di's three senses (qadari, dini, jaza'i); Ibn Kathir's
  "none can reverse it"; al-Baghawi with Ibn Abbas. Distinguished from
  28:88's seal — here the affirmation among the living, not the end.
- The return: at-Tabari, al-Muyassar, Ibn Kathir (recompense, nothing
  hidden — tie to 28:69), as-Sa'di.

Hadith: Sahih Muslim 223 (al-hamdu lillah fills the scale), quoted whole,
graded as Muslim's own; links the praise of clause 2 to the weighing at
the return of clause 4.

Dropped: Ma'arif's tafdil/divine-preference discussion (belongs to 28:68,
not these four attributes). No asbab claimed; placement stated instead.
