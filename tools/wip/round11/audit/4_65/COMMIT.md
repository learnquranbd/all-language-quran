Key kept at 4:65 (the three conditions: making him judge, no haraj, full
taslim). 4:59-64 cited for placement, 4:64 in its plain wording only; 4:66 from
the verse text. New card and a 1,733-word, 8-section article.

Sources: at-Tabari, Ibn Kathir (ar; en on 4:64), al-Qurtubi, as-Sa'di,
al-Baghawi, al-Muyassar and Ma'arif on 4:65, all slots on target.
Word study: fa-la as a reply (at-Tabari, al-Baghawi) or emphasis (al-Qurtubi);
shajara and haraj both from tangled trees; az-Zajjaj on taslima; at-Tabari's
grammar putting all three conditions under one hatta. As-Sa'di's islam / iman /
ihsan ladder and his line between rejecting the referral and failing in it,
with the licensing sentence in both languages.
Hadith: al-Bukhari 4585 quoted whole (az-Zubayr and the Ansari at the Harrah),
with Ibn Kathir's note on its mursal form and al-Qurtubi's "thabit sahih";
al-Qurtubi's two-rulings fiqh (settlement first, then the full right). The
Ansari left unnamed and uncharacterised, as al-Qurtubi advises.
Kept as a difference, no position: az-Zubayr's reckoning vs at-Tabari's
preference for the 4:60 pair (Mujahid), with his concession that both can hold.
Ma'arif on the verse after his lifetime, beyond lawsuits, and on unease with a
concession. Application: haraj vs the ache of loss; questions vs resistance.
Dropped: 'Umar report (Ibn Kathir: gharib, mursal, Ibn Lahi'ah), the Damrah
version, the hawa-tabi'an hadith (ungraded), names given to the Ansari,
al-Baghawi's unsourced Miqdad/Thabit story, Ibn al-'Arabi's ruling, school
positions on a judge proposing settlement.
