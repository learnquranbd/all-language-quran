Card and 8-section article (1,737 EN words, 19 paragraphs, 55-110 each).
13:31 is 51 Arabic words, per PREP.md.

Sources: all eight tafsir IDs. At-Tabari's fetched text starts at "wa la
yazal", so he is named only for the qari'a and tahullu clauses. Asbab
(Makkans behind the Ka'ba asking for the mountains moved, springs, the wind,
Qusayy revived) as al-Baghawi and al-Qurtubi give it, and Ibn Kathir's Ibn
Abi Hatim chain to Atiyya al-Awfi from Abu Sa'id, with his "Allah knows
best"; no grading added. Placement from al-Qurtubi (tied to 13:27).

Disagreements kept, both named, no position: jawab law ("it would be this
Qur'an": Qatada via al-Baghawi, al-Qurtubi, Ibn Kathir, as-Sa'di; "they
still would not believe": al-Farra', az-Zajjaj with 6:111, Ma'arif; the
Muyassar joins both); yay'as as "have they not known" (Nakha' via al-Kalbi,
Hawazin, Abu Ubayda) vs despair with knowing implied (al-Farra', Abu
al-Aliya); the yatabayyan reading (Ali and Ibn Abbas per al-Qurtubi) with
al-Anbari's reply; tahullu as the Prophet ﷺ encamped (at-Tabari, Ibn Abbas,
Ikrima, Mujahid, Qatada) vs the calamity (al-Hasan, Ibn Kathir's "apparent");
wa'd as the conquest of Makka vs the Day of Resurrection (al-Hasan).

Hadith: Bukhari 4981 (every Prophet was given miracles...), cited because Ibn
Kathir cites it; marked general, not tied to the occasion.
No-licence statement in both languages (§7); Ma'arif's nearby-disaster lesson
turned to the onlooker's own repentance. One pointer to 10:99 and 11:118.
Dropped: the Dawud/Zabur narration, the drowsy-scribe report, Ta'if/Khaybar
and Arbad details, Ma'arif's miracle comparisons.
