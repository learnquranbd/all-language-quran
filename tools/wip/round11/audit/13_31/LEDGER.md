## Disagreements kept (both named, no position)
- Jawab law: "it would be this Qur'an" (al-Baghawi's first group, Qatada's sense, al-Qurtubi, Ibn Kathir, as-Sa'di)
  vs "they would still not believe" (al-Baghawi's "others", al-Farra', az-Zajjaj, Ma'arif); the Muyassar combines. §3.
- Yay'as: "have they not known" (most, per al-Baghawi; al-Kalbi Nakha'; Hawazin; Abu Ubayda; Ibn Kathir's salaf)
  vs plain despair with knowing implied (al-Farra' via al-Baghawi; al-Qurtubi's "qila"; Abu al-Aliya). §5.
- Reading yatabayyan: Ali and Ibn Abbas (al-Qurtubi), Ibn Abbas (al-Baghawi), "others" (Ibn Kathir); al-Anbari's
  rejection of making it the correct text, via al-Qurtubi. §5.2.
- Tahullu: the Prophet ﷺ with his army (at-Tabari; Ibn Abbas, Ikrima, Mujahid, Qatada) vs the qari'a (al-Hasan;
  Ibn Kathir "apparent"). Wa'd: conquest of Makka vs Day of Resurrection (al-Hasan); as-Sa'di's lasting punishment. §6.

## Dropped

  al-Bukhari recorded) and his "the qur'an here is the Zabur": would need a second hadith fetch; only the naming point
  (qur'an for earlier books) kept.
- Al-Qushayri's report via al-Qurtubi that Ibn Abbas said the scribe wrote yay'as while drowsy: al-Qurtubi himself
  records al-Anbari rejecting the attribution; only the rejection's substance (transmission as in the mushaf) kept.
- Al-Baghawi's Dawud/mountains detail in the asbab and the poetry lines (Imru' al-Qays, Malik ibn Awf, Rabah ibn Adi).
- Al-Qurtubi's Makkan/Madinan alternatives (Ta'if, Khaybar sieges) and Arbad's thunderbolt; Ma'arif's Badr/Uhud,
  moon-splitting, pebbles and Mi'raj comparisons; Ma'arif's framing of nearby disasters as punishment of those
  localities (only its warning-to-onlookers lesson used, with the no-licence statement beside it).
- The guiding-all clause (lahada an-nasa jami'an) not re-developed: one pointer to 10:99 and 11:118 (§5.3).
