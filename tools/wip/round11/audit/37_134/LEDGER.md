# LEDGER 37:134

## Tafsir fetched (all pre-fetched, per PREP.md) and used

- **at-Tabari (t15_134.txt)** — used (§2.1, §3.2). Arabic clause rested on:
  "إذ نجَّينا لوطا وأهله أجمَعينَ من العذاب الذي أحللناه بقومه، فأهلكناهم به." —
  glossed as: saved Lut and his family, all of them, from the punishment sent
  on his people, who were destroyed by that same punishment. Used to support
  "one act, two outcomes" and (in §3.2) that the saving is tied to "those who
  received it."
- **Ibn Kathir, ar (t14_134.txt)** — used (§2.2). Full clause: "فكذبوه فنجاه
  الله تعالى من بين أظهرهم هو وأهله." (they denied him, so Allah saved him
  from among them, he and his family). Quoted transliterated + translated.
- **as-Sa'di (t91_134.txt)** — used (§2.2). Full clause: "فلما لم ينتهوا، نجاه
  اللّه وأهله أجمعين، فسروا ليلا فنجوا." Used for the "night journey" detail
  (fa-saru laylan fa-najaw) as as-Sa'di's own addition.
- **al-Baghawi (t94_134.txt)** — used (§2.3). Full text is just the verse
  itself repeated: "إذ نجيناه وأهله أجمعين" — reported as adding no
  independent content, i.e. silent agreement.
- **al-Muyassar (t16_134.txt), grouped 37:133-135** — used (§6.1). Clause used:
  "إلا عجوزًا هَرِمة، هي زوجته، هلكت مع الذين هلكوا من قومها لكفرها." (an old
  decrepit woman, his wife, who perished with those of her people who
  perished, because of her disbelief). Attributed as a belief-framed account,
  distinct from al-Qurtubi's narrative framing.
- **al-Qurtubi (t90_134.txt)** — used (§4.1, §4.2, §5.1). Clauses used: "سرى
  لوط بأهله كما وصف الله «بقطع من الليل» [هود:81]" (night departure, cross-ref
  11:81); "ثم أمر جبريل عليه السلام فأدخل جناحه تحت مدائنهم فاقتلعها ورفعها...
  ثم جعل عاليها سافلها، وأمطرت عليهم حجارة من سجيل" (Jibril's role, uprooting
  and inverting the cities, stoning); "وأدرك امرأة لوط - وكانت معه - حجر
  فقتلها" (a stone caught up with his wife, who was with him, and killed her).
  **Dropped**: the city-count/population figures ("أربع قرى... خمس فيها
  أربعمائة ألف") — per the brief's watch-point this is al-Qurtubi's own report
  and not load-bearing for the verse; omitted rather than asserted as settled,
  and omitting it also avoided unconfirmed specifics not corroborated
  elsewhere.
- **Ma'arif al-Qur'an, en (t168_134.txt), grouped 37:133-138** — read, not
  used. Its content (admonishment to Makkah's travelers passing Sodom) belongs
  to 37:137-138, future ground; not pulled forward.
- **Ibn Kathir, en abridged (t169_134.txt), grouped 37:133-138** — read, used
  only to corroborate the plain-sense summary already confirmed by the Arabic
  tafsir (denied → saved, household, except the wife); its detail on the Dead
  Sea / destroyed cities belongs to 37:136-138 and was not pulled forward.

## Qur'an cross-references (confirmed against data/translations/en.json +
bn.json, the site's own shipped translation text)

- **11:81** — "except your wife; indeed, she will be struck by that which
  strikes them" — used (§4.1, §6.2) as the Qur'an's own resolution of why the
  wife was excepted, reconciling al-Qurtubi's and al-Muyassar's differing
  emphases rather than picking one as more authoritative.
- **66:10** — "the wife of Noah and the wife of Lot... betrayed them" — used
  (§7.1, §7.2) as Allah's own characterization (ghadarat) of why she was not
  among the saved; offered as one more anchor, not forced.
- **37:135** — "except an old woman among those who remained" — used (§3.1,
  §3.2, §6.1) only to resolve the "all" vs. "exception" tension and to source
  the belief-framed account; not developed beyond that (future ground).

## Asbab an-nuzul

Not used / not applicable — none of the fetched tafsir raises an asbab for

## Licensing guard

Applied in §5.2 and §8.2 (both EN and BN): the verse and the narrative detail
describe a specific people in a specific place, judged for what they did, and
license nothing against any living person or community now or in the past.
Included because the episode (Lut's people) is a historically and currently
sensitive topic per the brief's watch-point, even though no specific
wrongdoing is itemized in the article.

- 37:136-138 (destruction, the ruins travelers pass) and 37:139+ (Yunus) —
