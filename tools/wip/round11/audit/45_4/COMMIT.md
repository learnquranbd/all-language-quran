Card and 8-section bilingual article for 45:4: wa fi khalqikum wa ma
yabuththu min dabbatin ayatun li-qawmin yuqinun. Nine words; the turn
from heavens and earth (45:3) to the self and the creatures; 45:5 and
45:6 in one placing line.

Sources: Tabari, Qurtubi, Baghawi, Sa'di, Ibn Kathir (ar + en),
Muyassar, Ma'arif (45:4 and the endings only from the grouped files).
- Fi khalqikum: Allah's creating of you, O people (Tabari, Muyassar);
  no stages or science added, said so.
- Dabba: in the earth, not of your kind (Tabari, Muyassar) vs heavens
  and earth with angels and jinn (Ibn Kathir) and Sa'di's fihima, kept
  as a difference of scope.
- Ayat as hujaj wa adilla (Tabari, Muyassar); object of yuqinun per
  Tabari, Muyassar, Baghawi, Ibn Kathir en.
- Endings 45:3-5: Ibn Kathir en's ascending grades vs Ma'arif's
  who-benefits reading; both kept, no position.
- Readings ayatun/ayatin: Qurtubi (general reading raf'; Hamza and
  al-Kisa'i kasr; no dispute on 45:3), Baghawi (adds Ya'qub; isti'naf),
  Tabari (by cities; Ubayy claim rejected for lack of a sound
  narration; Humayd ibn Thawr line; both readings correct, no
  preference).
Hadith: none attached by any tafsir; said in one sentence.
Asbab: none in the sources; not used.
Dropped: embryology/biology; Farra' (editor's footnote, not Tabari);
Qurtubi's 45:5 grammar; Ma'arif's Razi reference; 45:13 pointer only.
