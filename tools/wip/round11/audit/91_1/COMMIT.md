Card and 9-section bilingual article for 91:1: wa-sh-shamsi wa duhaha,
two words, the first of the seven oaths of 91:1-7. 91:2 named only as
the next oath; 91:9-10 named only as where the tafsirs place the answer.

Sources: Tabari, Qurtubi, Baghawi, Sa'di, Ibn Kathir (ar + en
abridged), Muyassar, Ma'arif. Slot check: all on 91:1; Muyassar and
IK (en) grouped 91:1-10, used for 91:1 and the answer's placement.
- duha glosses, each attributed: day (Qatada per Tabari/IK/Baghawi,
  al-Farra'), light (Mujahid), splendour (Qatada per Qurtubi, kept
  beside the other transmission), heat (Suddi, Muqatil), spreading
  (Yazidi), all creatures it reveals (Mawardi via Qurtubi), forenoon
  of full light and low heat (Ma'arif), light and its benefit (Sa'di).
- -ha = the sun (Tabari's paraphrase "bi-duha ash-shams").
- Tabari's verdict (sun and its daytime) reported as his; Qurtubi's
  light-never-without-heat; Arab usage; Mubarrad and Abu al-Haytham.
- One oath or two kept as disagreement: Qurtubi "qasam thanin" vs
  Ma'arif's adjectival reading. None chosen.
- Answer: Sa'di, IK (en), Muyassar place it at the soul's success
  and loss; grammar of the link not in the 91:1 texts, left to 91:9.
Hadith: Bukhari 705 (Jabir, Mu'adh, al-A'la/ash-Shams/al-Layl)
quoted whole in quranx's English, cited by Ibn Kathir. Asbab: none.
Cross-refs: 20:119 (cited by Baghawi, Qurtubi); shipped 91:9 by number.
Dropped: 79:29, 10:5, 71:16, 92:1-2, 93:1-2 (not cited for 91:1),
Muslim's number, any astronomy.
