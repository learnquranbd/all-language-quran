Answer of the oath (at-Tabari with Qatadah, al-Qurtubi, al-Baghawi, as-Sa'di).
Ibn Kathir's opposites sworn by opposites, with 78:8 and 51:49. Sa'y as 'amal
('Ikrimah via al-Qurtubi; at-Tabari, al-Muyassar, al-Baghawi), 84:6 (Ma'arif);
shatta as plural of shatit, far apart (al-Qurtubi). Where the difference lies,
kept side by side: belief/obedience (at-Tabari), good/evil (Ibn Kathir),
dunya/akhira (al-Muyassar), al-Qurtubi's guidance pairs plus his two "it is
said" readings (recompense; character). As-Sa'di on amount, energy and the goal
that endures or fades. 92:5-10 in one section: Ma'arif's three marks; al-husna
per Qatadah, Khusayf, Ibn Abbas and ad-Dahhak, Ibn Kathir on 92:9, no position;
92:10 in Ibn Kathir's words with 6:110; Abu Bakr report as a report; licensing
sentence on the stingy denier. 92:7, 92:11, 92:20 one line each.

whole; Muslim 223 (Abu Malik, brought by al-Baghawi and al-Qurtubi), quoted
