Card + 8-section bilingual article (1,741 EN words) for 37:5, "Lord of the
heavens and the earth and what is between them, and Lord of the sunrises."
First shipped ayah of as-Saffat; set after the oaths of 37:1-4 landing on
"your God is One" (Ibn Kathir's muqsam 'alayh; Qatada via Tabari).

Sources: at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi, al-Baghawi, Ibn Kathir (ar + abridged en),
as-Sa'di, al-Muyassar, Ma'arif al-Qur'an, each quoted from the text fetched
for 37:5.
- Rabb as explanation of "One": Tabari's definition and worship conclusion;
  Sa'di's rububiyya-proves-uluhiyya, conceded by the idolaters.
- Grammar aside in one sentence: Tabari's nominative dispute (prefers
  restating wahid); al-Akhfash's accusative via Qurtubi.
- al-mashariq as a named range, no position: Qatada (winter/summer, no
  count); as-Suddi via Tabari 360; Baghawi 360 apertures (unattributed);
  Ma'arif a new point daily; Ibn Kathir stars and planets; Baghawi "every
  place the sun shines on". Singular/dual/plural reconciled per Baghawi
  (73:9, 55:17, 70:40); settings implied (Tabari, Ibn Kathir, Qurtubi 16:81).
- Qurtubi's Ibn 'Abbas narration via 'Ikrima (at-Tamhid, ar-Radd): 365
  apertures, reluctant sun, 70,000 angels, devil burned; ungraded, not called
  a hadith; Umayya in passing. 365 and 360 each reported as its source gives.
- Hadith: Bukhari 3272 (Ibn 'Umar, sun rises between a devil's two horns,
  prayer at sunrise/sunset), quoted whole incl. sub-narrator's doubt, flagged
  as not attached to the verse. Stock Bukhari 660 not used.
- Licensing guard on sun-worship (ancient practice, nothing against any
  living person or community), both languages.
- Fixed: brief's "363" for as-Suddi is 360 in Tabari's text ("ستون وثلاث
  مئة"); "angel dimming the light" not in the clause, rendered cautiously.
- Dropped: Umayya's verses and the tujlad exchange, the setting-prostration
  detail, an-Nahhas by name; 37:6-10 left as future ground.
