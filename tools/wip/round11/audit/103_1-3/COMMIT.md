Replaces the 792-word, 6-section article with a 1,693-word, 7-section deep article.

Sources: at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi, al-Baghawi, Ibn Kathir (ar + en abridged),
as-Sa'di, Muyassar and Ma'arif al-Qur'an, all on 103:1-3 (Ma'arif and Ibn Kathir
en grouped 103:1-3). Covers the range of al-'asr (time at large, the late
afternoon, night and day, the 'Asr prayer, the Prophet's age, "Lord of al-'Asr"),
why time is sworn by (Muyassar's note that a servant may swear only by Allah),
khusr glosses with al-Farra' on 65:9, al-Baghawi's and Ma'arif's capital
image, the four qualities, and as-Sa'di's self-then-others design.

Disagreements kept, no position: al-'asr (at-Tabari includes all; Ibn Kathir calls
the time reading the well-known one); who al-insan is (the disbeliever: Ibn Abbas
via Abu Salih, al-Baghawi's qila; humankind: al-Qurtubi's "sound view", at-Tabari,
Ibn Kathir, as-Sa'di), with the licensing sentence in both languages. Ibrahim's
old-age reading linked to 95:4-6 in one line.

Hadith: Muslim 223 (Abu Malik al-Ash'ari, quoted whole), attached by Ma'arif to
the capital image, confirmed on quranx, marked general. At-Tabarani's report of
Companions reciting al-'Asr before parting and ash-Shafi'i's saying given as
reports via Ibn Kathir; narrator named 'Abdullah ibn Hisn (Ibn Kathir) and
'Ubaidullah ibn Hisn (Ma'arif).

Dropped: al-Qurtubi's ungraded Ubayy narration naming men per clause; 'Ali's
non-canonical reading; the Mushaf-neighbours section and other unsourced lines of
the old article. Defect fixed: the old article stated al-insan means humanity as
settled; the sources disagree.
