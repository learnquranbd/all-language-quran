Replaces the 768-word, 6-section article with a 1,782-word, 8-section
deep article on Zakariyya's call, do not leave me single, and the best of inheritors.

Sources: at-Tabari, Ibn Kathir (ar + en), al-Qurtubi, as-Sa'di,
al-Baghawi, al-Muyassar, Ma'arif al-Qur'an on 21:89; the same seven
also fetched on 21:90 for the answer verse.
- Placement: the three answered calls (21:83-84, 21:87-88, 21:89-90);
  one line each on 19:2-11, 3:38-41 and 21:91. Ibn Kathir: shorter
  than both fuller tellings. As-Sa'di: the verse as an honour.
- Nada: Baghawi's da'a, Ibn Kathir's "in secret", Muyassar's old age;
  as-Sa'di explains the verse by quoting 19:4-6.
- Fardan in five glosses (Tabari's 'aqib, Qurtubi, Baghawi, Ibn Kathir,
  Muyassar); as-Sa'di's fear for the da'wah, Qurtubi's "do not cut this
  virtue from my line".
- Khayr al-warithin: best of those who remain (Qurtubi, Baghawi) and best
  successor (Muyassar, as-Sa'di). The adab: Ibn Kathir's du'a and praise,
  Tabari's "returned the matter to Allah", Ma'arif, as-Sa'di.
- 21:90 in one section. Aslahna dispute kept: fertility (Ibn 'Abbas,
  Mujahid, Sa'id per Ibn Kathir, who prefers it) vs character ('Ata',
  and Ibn 'Abbas per Qurtubi); Tabari and Qurtubi allow both. "They":
  household (Tabari) vs the surah's prophets (Baghawi, Qurtubi).
  Raghaban wa rahaban and khashi'in from Tabari, Ibn Zayd, Qurtubi.
Hadith: none attached to 21:89; the article says so. Qurtubi's Tirmidhi
narration on raising hands (21:90) and Abu Bakr's khutbah not used.
Dropped from the old article: fardan in 19:80/19:95, "khayr al-warithin
only here", translation renderings, details from the other tellings,
the "nothing is owed" gloss (none sourced).
