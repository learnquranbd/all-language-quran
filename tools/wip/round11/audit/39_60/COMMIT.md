Card and 8-section bilingual article for 39:60: wa-yawma al-qiyamati
tara alladhina kadhabu 'ala-llahi wujuhuhum muswaddah, a-laysa fi
jahannama mathwan lil-mutakabbirin.

Sources: Tabari, Qurtubi, Sa'di, Baghawi, Ibn Kathir (ar + en abridged),
Muyassar, Ma'arif (on 39:59 only).
- Who lied about Allah, each attributed: Tabari (the Prophet's people who
  claimed a son and partner and worshipped other gods), Baghawi, Ibn
  Kathir, Muyassar (partner and son); Sa'di widest (consort, false
  prophethood, saying of His law what He did not say).
- Blackened faces: Ibn Kathir (their lying; opens with 3:106's wording,
  verified in 3.json), Qurtubi (Allah's anger closing around them), Sa'di
  (like night, recompense in kind). Tabari's two forms muswaddah/
  muswaaddah. Nothing described beyond the glosses.
- Grammar: Akhfash vs Zamakhshari (via Qurtubi) on tara, no position.
- mathwa/mutakabbirin: Tabari, Muyassar (bala), Baghawi, Ibn Kathir,
  Sa'di (liars and arrogant one people); k-dh-b, k-b-r tie to 39:59.
Hadith: Muslim 91 (Ibn Mas'ud, definition of kibr), whole, marked general
and brought by Qurtubi; Tirmidhi 2492 (proud gathered like specks),
whole in Tirmidhi's wording, graded hasan by Tirmidhi, attached by Ibn
Kathir and Qurtubi.
Licensing sentence in EN and BN; no living group named.
Dropped: Ibn Kathir's ahl al-firqa / ahl as-sunnah gloss (sectarian, not
on this verse's subject); Tabari's Basran grammar note. Asbab: none.
39:61 not pulled forward; 39:56-59 one sentence of context only.
Themes: hereafter, character.
