Replaces the 789-word, 5-section article with a 1,759-word, 9-section
deep article on whoever turns away from My remembrance.

Sources: at-Tabari, Ibn Kathir (ar + en), al-Qurtubi, as-Sa'di,
al-Baghawi, al-Muyassar, Ma'arif al-Qur'an, all on 20:124.
- Dhikri glossed as the reminder Allah sends (Tabari, Muyassar), the
  Qur'an (Baghawi), My Book (Sa'di), My command and revelation (Ibn
  Kathir), religion / proofs / the Messenger (Qurtubi); Ma'arif holds
  Book and Messenger together via 65:10 and 65:11.
- A'rada: Tabari's turning, refusing, not answering; Ibn Kathir's
  tanasahu; Sa'di's two degrees (neglect, denial).
- Danka: Tabari and Qurtubi on the word and Antara's verse; Ibn
  Abbas's ash-shaqa' set against la yashqa in 20:123.
Disagreement kept, no position taken: where the narrow living falls.
This world (Ibn Kathir first; Ibn Abbas via al-Awfi, Dahhak, Ikrimah,
Sa'id ibn Jubayr, Qurtubi with 16:97), the grave (Abu Sa'id, Ibn
Mas'ud, Abu Hurayra, Abu Salih, as-Suddi; preferred by Tabari by
elimination and by Qurtubi; Sa'di with 6:93, 32:21, 40:46), the Fire
(al-Hasan, Qatada, Ibn Zayd with 89:24); Sa'di and the Muyassar on the
combined reading. Second disagreement: blind to proof (Mujahid, Abu
Salih, as-Suddi) or blind of sight (Ibn Abbas in Baghawi, Sa'di with
17:97); Tabari and the Muyassar take both.
Hadith: none quoted; no tafsir attaches a six-collection report. The
marfu' grave reports are given with Ibn Kathir's own gradings (Darraj
report "very munkar", Abu Sa'id mawquf "sounder", one chain "good").
Licensing sentence in both languages; application kept to self-
examination. Dropped from the old article: the 'ard etymology, dhikri
as the servant's own remembrance (not in any source), the masdar note,
13:28 and 20:130 framing; it gave only the worldly reading of danka.
