Card and 8-section article (1,772 EN words, 17 paragraphs); 12:70 is 15
Arabic words per PREP.md. The cup in the brother's bag and the cry
"O caravan, you are thieves".

Sources: at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi, as-Sa'di, al-Baghawi, Ibn Kathir (ar),
al-Muyassar, Ma'arif al-Qur'an, and Ibn Kathir (en, grouped 12:70-72; only
its 12:70 portion used, and the article says so).

- jahhaza from Qatada (at-Tabari), al-Qurtubi and as-Sa'di; the camel loaded
  in the brother's own name from Ibn Ishaq and Ma'arif.
- siqaya and suwa' as one vessel (al-Hasan, Mujahid, al-Baghawi, al-Qurtubi's
  two-headed cup from an-Naqqash on Ibn Abbas); material given briefly, each
  view with its name.
- The central question kept open: al-Qurtubi's four answers (the earlier
  theft of Yusuf, "your state is that of thieves", a stratagem, a question),
  as-Sa'di's "perhaps the caller did not know", and al-Baghawi's three
  "it is said" views, including the one that it was a lapse on his part,
  kept as al-Baghawi's report and not endorsed. No position taken.
- Kept as disagreements: whether Binyamin knew (as-Suddi, Ibn Kathir,
  Muyassar vs al-Qurtubi's narrative) and when the call came (Ma'arif,
  Ibn Ishaq, al-Baghawi vs as-Suddi).
- Plain statement in both languages: a prophet's divinely guided plan in one
  story; licenses no deception, entrapment or false accusation today, and
  rulings belong to qualified scholars. Same for the brothers.
- 12:72, 12:76 and 12:77 pointed to, not taken.
- No hadith attached in any fetched tafsir; no asbab. Said in one sentence.
- Dropped: al-Qurtubi's variant readings of suwa', the al-Abbas vessel report,
  Ibn Kathir en's "golden bowl" heading.
