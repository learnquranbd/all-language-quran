Card and deep bilingual article for 22:18, the prostration verse
(sajdat at-tilawah, marked with the sajdah sign in the mushaf).

Built on the two kinds of sujud, which the sources set out directly:
Maʿarif names them — the submission of creation (Takwin), pre-determined,
involuntary, that no creature escapes, believer or denier, living or dead;
and the voluntary homage of worship that only humans and jinn can withhold.
Ibn Kathir frames the same as "willingly or unwillingly, each in its own
manner," with the believers among "kathir min an-nas" bowing by choice
(so too the Muyassar).

Why the sun, moon and stars are named: Ibn Kathir — because they had been
worshipped besides Allah (41:37), so the verse puts them back among the
subjected servants.

Dispute kept open, three readings side by side, no verdict in our voice:
shadows (Mujahid, Ibn Kathir, cf 16:48); obedience/tasbih (al-Baghawi's
"good position agreeing with Ahl as-Sunnah," 41:11, 2:74, 17:44; Maʿarif
adds real intent in all things); and literal prostration (Abu al-ʿAliya +
al-Qushayri, "sujud haqiqi" needing life and intellect, on a chained report
about the sun). al-Qurtubi's grammar of the rafʿ of "wa-kathir" and Ibn
Abbas's split reading carried at the punishment clause.

Hadith: Sahih Muslim 81a (Abu Hurayrah), quoted whole — Satan weeps at the
son of Adam's sajdah; graded as Muslim's, not upgraded. The al-Hajj
two-sajda reports carried with Tirmidhi's "not strong" and Abu Dawud's
mursal "does not stand"; the practice attested from ʿUmar at al-Jabiya.

Punishment clause read on the wrongdoers who refuse (Ibn Kathir, al-Baghawi),
with the plain both-language line that the verse describes what it describes
and licenses nothing against any living person or community; reckoning left
to Allah and the Day of 22:17. at-Tabari on "yuhin"/"yafʿalu ma yashaʾ"
(21:23), al-Baghawi on saʿada and shaqawa by His will.

as-Saʿdi (t91) not used: fetch failed / wrong group.
Dropped: tree-dream hadith, the Ali mashiʾa report, the eclipse hadith,
at-Tabari's disallowed "mukram" variant — logged in LEDGER.
