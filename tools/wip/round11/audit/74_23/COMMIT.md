Card and 8-section bilingual article for 74:23, thumma adbara
wa-stakbara: the turn. 74:18-22 (thought, measured, looked, frowned)
and 74:24-25 (magic handed down, word of a man) as context only;
identity and wahid left to 74:11, greed for more to 74:15.

Sources: Ibn Kathir (ar), Tabari (entry served under 74:24; 74:18,
74:22), Qurtubi (74:23, 74:18, 74:22), Sa'di, Baghawi, Muyassar (group
74:20-25). Tabari on 74:23 itself returned 503; 168/169 wrong group.
- adbara: tawalla (Sa'di); walla wa-a'rada dhahiban ila ahlihi
  (Qurtubi); raja'a al-qahqara, from the truth (Ibn Kathir).
- istakbara: too great to believe (Qurtubi), proud when invited
  (Baghawi), above admitting it (Muyassar), refusing to yield to the
  Quran (Ibn Kathir); Tabari's two objects, faith and acknowledgment.
- Sequence: Muyassar (schemes ran short, no fault found), Sa'di
  (natija of his effort), Ibn Kathir on 74:18 (what to invent).
Disagreement kept: Qurtubi's bodily reading (went to his family) vs
his qila and Baghawi (turned from faith when invited). Unranked.
Reports of his talk with Quraysh kept as tafsir reports with chain
status: Qatada "za'amu" and Mujahid (Tabari 74:18), Ibn Zayd (Tabari
74:24), ad-Dahhak via "huddithtu" (Tabari 74:22), Qurtubi unchained.
None called hadith; none graded in the fetched texts.
Hadith: none attached; said in one sentence. Asbab: not used.
Named once (al-Walid ibn al-Mughira al-Makhzumi, as the sources give);
licensing sentence in EN and BN.
Dropped: al-'Awfi/Ibn 'Abbas report (169, wrong group), 'Ikrima
version (not in fetched text), Tabari's ahl al-ta'wil line (74:24).
