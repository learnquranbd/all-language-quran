Card and 8-section bilingual article for 44:30: wa-laqad najjayna Bani
Isra'il mina-l-'adhabi-l-muhin. 44:23-29 as the lead-in (44:29 and 44:30
read side by side, pursuers unmourned, pursued rescued); 44:31 named in
one line as the completion (min Fir'awn); 44:32-33 not touched.

Sources: Tabari, Qurtubi, Sa'di, Ibn Kathir (ar), Muyassar, Baghawi.
All six Arabic entries are 77-393 characters; the article stays at the
low end of the band (1,554 EN words) and says plainly what they omit.
Ma'arif's grouped entry (44:29-31) speaks only of 44:29. Ibn Kathir (en)
covers 44:17-26 (wrong group), not used.
- Imtinan: Ibn Kathir (yamtannu, anqadhahum) and Sa'di (imtanna); both
  "kanu fihi", the torment as a condition they lived in.
- muhin = mudhill: Tabari, Muyassar; Ibn Kathir's ihana / idhlal.
- Contents, each attributed: Qatada via Tabari (sons killed, women kept
  alive); Baghawi (+ toil in labour); Muyassar (sons killed, women put to
  service); Qurtubi (the Copts at Pharaoh's command: sons, women in
  service, enslavement, arduous works); Ibn Kathir (forced humiliating
  labour, no killing named).
- Kept as differences, no position: istihya' vs istikhdam of the women;
  the agent named (Pharaoh and his people / the Copts / Pharaoh / none).
- 44:12 and 44:15 (al-'adhab removed by Allah) set beside Tabari's gloss
  of a torment inflicted by people; wording checked in verse-base.json.
- Licensing sentence EN+BN; no link to any present-day people, religion
  or state. 7:141 / 28:4 not cited (no 44:30 source cites them).
Hadith: none attached to 44:30 in any fetched tafsir; said in one sentence.
Asbab: none in the sources; not used.
Dropped: crossing narrative, numbers, duration, geography (no source);
why they were favoured (44:32).
- Review fix: al-Qurtubi's 'the Copts' (al-Qibt) dropped in §4/§5, en+bn, as in 44:17-18 and 44:25; his gloss kept as 'by Pharaoh's command'.
