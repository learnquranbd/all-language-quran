Replaces the 775-word, 6-section article with a 1,767-word, 9-section
one, from at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi, al-Baghawi, Ibn Kathir (ar and en),
al-Muyassar, as-Sa'di and Ma'arif on 3:38. All slots checked. The
shipped card is kept.

Hunalika: time and place (al-Qurtubi, with al-Mufaddal ibn Salama's
hunalika/hunaka distinction); 'inda dhalika (at-Tabari, al-Baghawi);
the closed door of the mihrab (al-Baghawi; 'Ikrima in at-Tabari).
3:37 as one short section: fruit out of season (Ibn Kathir, at-Tabari),
provision with no human cause (at-Tabari), al-Muyassar.
What moved him: at-Tabari's analogy (out-of-season fruit, child from a
barren wife), as-Suddi and Ibn Abbas (via Sa'id ibn Jubayr) in his voice,
Ma'arif (belief already firm; seeing moved him). Age and barrenness are
named only as the commentators' backdrop to hope; the 3:40 question is
left to 3:40.
Hab / min ladunka glosses; dhurriyya as one child (at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi,
al-Baghawi, proof 19:5) beside Ibn Kathir's "a son who will have
offspring", kept as two readings; the feminine tayyiba and at-Tabari's
limit on the rule. Tayyiba glosses named one by one; as-Sa'di's reason.
Sami': at-Tabari (more praising form; Basran view), al-Baghawi
(hearer, "it is said" answerer, 36:25), al-Qurtubi (accepter, sami'a
Allahu li-man hamidah). 14:39 as the only other Sami' ad-du'a, checked
in the text and marked as not drawn by the commentators.
Asking for children as the sunnah of the messengers (al-Qurtubi, Ma'arif,
13:38, 25:74, al-Hasan al-Basri); al-Qurtubi's reply to the Sufis who
called it foolish, and his duty to pray for a child's guidance.
Hadith: Muslim 1631 (three things, a pious child who prays), quoted whole
in Muslim's wording, marked general; al-Qurtubi says it alone suffices.
No asbab. 3:39-41 one bridging line; 19:4 and 19:8 one pointer.
Dropped: Ma'arif's "did not have the courage"; al-Qurtubi's unsourced
Anas report; other narrations (one-hadith rule).
Corrected: "dhurriyya means a continuing line, not a child" (the sources
say one child here); "Sami' is a participle"; tayyiba of speech, food and
life (unsourced); the 3:40 question pulled forward as "a very human sentence".
