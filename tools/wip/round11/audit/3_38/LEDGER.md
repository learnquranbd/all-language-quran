## Hadith
- Muslim 1631. URL: https://quranx.com/hadith/Muslim/Hadith-1631 (saved: hadith_Muslim_1631.txt). First ten words: "When a man dies, his acts come to an end, but". Narrator Abu Huraira. Grading: recorded by Muslim in his Sahih (no higher claim made). Quoted whole in quranx's English, translator's parentheses removed ("(by which people)", "(for the deceased)"). Al-Qurtubi cites it under 3:38 with the wording إذا مات أحدكم; the article quotes Muslim's wording (إذا مات الإنسان), not al-Qurtubi's. Marked as a general narration, not the verse's occasion.
- Not used (one-hadith rule): al-Qurtubi's "رُوي من حديث أنس" (أي رجل مات وترك ذرية طيبة...; passive, no collection, not confirmed); Sa'd on 'Uthman's tabattul (Muslim); 'A'isha النكاح من سنتي (Ibn Majah); Abu Talha's son and the nine children (Bukhari); Anas اللهم أكثر ماله وولده (Bukhari and Muslim per al-Qurtubi; also Ma'arif with "about a hundred children"); Abu Salama du'a; تزوجوا الولود الودود (Abu Dawud).

## Shipped short article (775 words, 6 sections): what changed
- Kept (confirmed): hunalika as time and place (al-Qurtubi, at-Tabari, al-Baghawi); fruit out of season (Ibn Kathir, now also at-Tabari); Ibn Kathir's "righteous offspring"; Sami' ad-du'a only at 3:38 and 14:39 (verified in the text).
- WRONG: "dhurriyyah means offspring in the sense of a line continuing, not simply a child in the house". At-Tabari, al-Qurtubi and al-Baghawi all say it means one child here (proof: waliyyan, 19:5). Ibn Kathir's continuing-line reading is a different claim (a son who will have offspring, with "Allah knows best") and is now attributed to him.
- WRONG/loose: Sami' called "a participle". At-Tabari distinguishes sami' from the participle sami' (سامع) as the more praising form.
- Dropped (unsourced): tayyibah "the same adjective the Quran uses of speech, of food and of a life"; Zakariyya "a servant of the sanctuary with no one to carry the work on" (only at-Tabari's reported "household had died out" is kept, attributed); "he drew the general rule out of the particular case" (replaced by at-Tabari's own analogy and the as-Suddi / Ibn Abbas reports); "He specifies quality and leaves number and sex unmentioned".
- Dropped as future ground or risk: section 5 and 6 of the old article (3:39-41: the angels' call, the 3:40 question as "a very human sentence", kadhalika as "a correction of scale", the sign of silence and dhikr). The 3:40 question was pulled forward; now one bridging line that names it only as "his question".
