Card and 9-section bilingual article for 39:73: wa-siqa alladhina ittaqaw
rabbahum ila al-jannati zumara, the gates and the wa that 39:71 lacks, and
the keepers' salamun 'alaykum tibtum fa-dkhuluha khalidin.

Sources: Ibn Kathir (ar + en abridged), Tabari, Qurtubi, Sa'di, Baghawi,
Muyassar. Ma'arif fetched (39:69 only), not named.
- One verb for both crowds: Qurtubi (disgrace vs mounts to honour), Tabari
  with Ibn Zayd and 19:85 (wafd), Sa'di. Contrast with 39:71-72 in brief.
- Muttaqun (Tabari, Muyassar, Sa'di, Qurtubi); zumar ordered (Ibn Kathir,
  Sa'di). Bridge before entry (Ibn Kathir; Qurtubi via Muqatil).
- The wa, each reading attributed, no position: answer elided (Ibn Kathir:
  mind goes to every hope; Tabari's preferred matruk, "dakhaluha";
  Mubarrad "sa'idu" and Zajjaj via Qurtubi/Baghawi); wa za'ida (Kufans,
  Basrans call it error); wa of state, gates already open in honour
  (Baghawi; Qurtubi via Mahdawi and Nahhas, 38:50); waw ath-thamaniya
  (Qurtubi qila, Abu Bakr b. 'Ayyash) rejected by Ibn Kathir.
Disagreement kept: gates open before arrival (Baghawi, Qurtubi's relayed
view) vs opened after the Prophet's intercession (Sa'di).
- salam: Tabari, Sa'di, Muyassar, Muqatil (Ridwan). tibtum: Tabari,
  Mujahid, Naqqash, Ibn Kathir, Sa'di (hearts, tongues, limbs; fa causal),
  Muyassar (purity from traces of sins), Muqatil (after the bridge).
Hadith: Bukhari 6535 (Abu Sa'id, the bridge) and Bukhari 3257 (Sahl,
eight gates, ar-Rayyan), both quranx-confirmed, quoted whole, al-Bukhari's
Sahih, no separate grading.
Licensing sentence (EN and BN) for the deniers of 39:71-72.
Dropped: Ali's two-springs report (ungraded), Qurtubi's thirteen gates,
hadith as-Sur and Ibn Kathir's unconfirmed narrations. Asbab: none.
39:74-75 not pulled forward; 39:67 not used.
Themes: hereafter.
