Card and 8-section bilingual article for 71:2: qala ya qawmi inni
lakum nadhirun mubin. 71:1 (the command andhir) as the frame; 71:3
pointed to only where as-Sa'di hands over to it; 71:5 used for the
unchanged address qawmi. 71:10 (istighfar) not repeated.

Sources: Tabari, Qurtubi, Ibn Kathir (ar; en grouped 71:1-4), Sa'di,
Baghawi, Muyassar (grouped 71:1-4). All named. Ma'arif wrong group
(70:33-42), not used.
- nadhir: mukhawwif (Qurtubi); warning of Allah's punishment (Tabari);
  if you disobey Him (Muyassar); lifted on repentance (IK en).
- mubin kept as two readings, none picked: clear in warning (IK ar,
  Sa'di, Muyassar) and making the warning clear (Tabari "qad abantu
  lakum indhari"; Qurtubi "muzhir lakum"); in the tongue they know
  (Qurtubi, Baghawi).
- Sa'di's three parts of clarity: what is warned of, what is warned
  against, how deliverance is reached.
Grammar checked against data/irab (elided possessive ya of qawmi,
lakum, mubin form IV active participle). No qira'at in the sources.
Hadith: none attached to 71:2 by any source, said in one sentence.
Bukhari 3340 (Nuh "the first amongst the messengers ... to the people
of the earth") quoted as a general narration, marked not attached.
IK's family-ties narration located at 71:4, not used.
Asbab: none.
Licensing sentence in both languages: the verse licenses nothing
against any living person or community.
Dropped: years of preaching / 29:14 (no source figure), 11:25 and
other parallels (no tafsir cites them), 14:4.
