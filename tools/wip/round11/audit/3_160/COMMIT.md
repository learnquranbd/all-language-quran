Replaces the 625-word, 5-section article with a 1,745-word, 9-section
one, from at-Tabari, Ibn Kathir (ar and en), al-Qurtubi, as-Sa'di,
al-Baghawi, al-Muyassar and Ma'arif on 3:160. All slots checked. The
shipped card is kept.

Placement: one bridging line to 3:159, one line for 3:161; Ma'arif's
Uhud consolation, Ibn Kathir's tie to 3:126.
Nasr glosses named (al-Muyassar, al-Baghawi, al-Qurtubi, at-Tabari's
opponents); fala ghaliba with at-Tabari's and as-Sa'di's gathered creation.
Khidhlan: al-Qurtubi's tark al-'awn, the khadhul doe, Tarafa and the
maqlub view; al-Baghawi's qu'ud 'an an-nusra; yakilukum ila anfusikum.
Badr and Uhud only as al-Baghawi names them; at-Tabari's obedience
condition and Ibn Ishaq's report attributed. Licensing sentence in both
languages: no present group's victory or defeat read as Allah's verdict.
Tawakkul: as-Sa'di's fronting-as-restriction, tawhid/shirk and measure
by faith; at-Tabari's reliance with striving; al-Baghawi's two qila
definitions. Says no fetched text on the verse rules on means.
Hadith (both cited by al-Baghawi): Muslim 218 ('Imran, seventy thousand,
quoted whole), Tirmidhi 2344 ('Umar, the birds, hasan sahih); both marked
general. Ibn 'Abbas (Tirmidhi 2516) confirmed but in no fetched tafsir,
so one sentence says it is not attached.
Dropped: at-Tabari's editorial footnote and warning clause (length),
5:23, own-voice la-of-the-class grammar.
Corrected: a tawakkul definition credited to as-Sa'di that his 3:160
text does not contain; an unsourced khidhlan definition; the own-voice
"never work on the causes" ruling and the lam-of-command line.
