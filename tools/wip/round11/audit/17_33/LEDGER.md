# SOURCES ledger — 17:33

## Tafsir fetched (PREP.md, not re-fetched) and where used

- **15 at-Tabari (ar)** — USED. "illā bi'l-ḥaqq" = kufr after Islam / zinā after
  iḥsān / qawad ("لا تقتل إلا بكفر بعد إسلام، أو زنا بعد إحصان، أو قود نفس"). The heir's
  three choices ("فإن شاء استقاد... وإن شاء عفا عنه، وإن شاء أخذ الدية"); prefers Ibn
  Abbas's reading of sulṭān, citing the fatḥ-Makkah "بخير النظرين". Qirā'āt yā'/tā'
  ("فَلا تُسْرِفْ ... خطاب... النبي والأئمة من بعده"); prefers the walī as referent of
  "innahu kāna manṣūran" ("عُنِي بها الوليّ... هو المنصور"). Jāhiliyya excess: killing
  the noble kin instead of the killer.
- **90 al-Qurtubi (ar)** — USED. sulṭān = "تسليطا إن شاء قتل وإن شاء عفا وإن شاء أخذ
  الدية" (Ibn Abbas, ad-Dahhak, ash-Shafi'i); Malik "السلطان أمر الله"; Ibn Abbas
  "السلطان الحجة"; split Ibn al-Qasim/Malik/Abu Hanifa (al-qatl) vs Ashhab/ash-Shafi'i
  (the choice). Three views on isrāf. Objection + answer on manṣūr ("المعونة تكون بظهور
  الحجة تارة وباستيفائها أخرى"). Says "illā bi'l-ḥaqq" was covered in al-An'ām (→6:151).
  ad-Dahhak: "أول ما نزل من القرآن في شأن القتل، وهي مكية".
- **91 as-Sa'di (ar)** — USED. Every soul ("صغير وكبير وذكر وأنثى وحر وعبد ومسلم وكافر له
  عهد"); exceptions incl. "الباغي في حال بغيه إذا لم يندفع إلا بالقتل"; walī = "أقرب
  عصباته وورثته"; sulṭān = "حجة ظاهرة على القصاص" + tasalluṭ qadarī; conditions "العمد
  العدوان والمكافأة"; right is the walī's, pardon drops qiṣāṣ.
- **94 al-Baghawi (ar)** — USED. sulṭān "قوة وولاية" (Mujāhid) vs ad-Dahhak's choice;
  qirā'a Ḥamza/al-Kisā'ī "فلا تسرف" (tā'); Mujāhid: manṣūr = the slain ("المقتول منصور
  في الدنيا بإيجاب القود... وفي الآخرة بتكفير خطاياه"); Qatāda: the walī.
- **16 al-Muyassar (ar)** — USED. exceptions "بالحق الشرعي كالقصاص أو رجم الزاني المحصن
  أو قتل المرتد"; walī "من وارث أو حاكم"; isrāf "أن يقتل بالواحد اثنين أو جماعة، أو يمثل".
- **14 Ibn Kathir (ar)** + **169 Ibn Kathir (en abridged)** — USED. "سلطة على القاتل...
  بالخيار: قتله قودا / الدية / عفا مجانا"; isrāf = "يمثل به أو يقتص من غير القاتل";
  manṣūr = "الولي منصور على القاتل شرعا وغالبا قدرا".
- **168 Ma'arif (en)** — USED. Injustice not answered by injustice; role-reversal
  (mazlūm→zālim) if the heir exceeds; Jāhiliyya killing two/three for one and
  mutilation (nose, ears); walī real vs ruler as legal walī of all Muslims.

## Hadith

- **Sahih al-Bukhari 6878** (Dar-us-Salam), narrated Abdullah b. Mas'ud.
  URL: https://quranx.com/hadith/Bukhari/DarusSalam/Hadith-6878 (fetched via hadith.js).
  First ten words (EN): "The blood of a Muslim who confesses that none has".
  Grading: recorded by al-Bukhari in his Ṣaḥīḥ (sound); grading not upgraded.
  Directly on the verse — the three cases that make "illā bi'l-ḥaqq" concrete
  (qiṣāṣ for murder / married adulterer / apostate leaving the community). Quoted
  whole from the Bukhari wording; used in §4.

## Asbab / placement

- No specific occasion of revelation confirmed. ad-Dahhak (in at-Tabari and
  al-Qurtubi) says this is the first Qur'anic passage on killing and is Meccan —
  used as placement/period note in §1, not as a sabab.

## Dropped (could not confirm or tangential)

- "The destruction of the world is lighter... than killing a believer" — Ibn Kathir
  gives it as "fi's-Sunan"; Ma'arif via Ibn Mājah (Ḥasan)/al-Baihaqī/Mazharī.
  Grading not independently verified on a fetched page → not quoted.
- Ma'arif's further reports (abetting a killing "with one word"; every sin forgiven
  but kufr/intentional murder) — chains via Ibn Mājah/Iṣbahānī/al-Baihaqī, gradings
  unconfirmed → dropped.
- Ibn Kathir's Ibn Abbas→Mu'āwiya inference of walāya (via aṭ-Ṭabarānī), called "من
  الأمر العجب" — tangential and open to misreading → dropped.
- al-Qurtubi's dispute on whether the walī must be male (Ibn Khuwayz Mindād vs the
  majority admitting all heirs incl. women) → dropped for space.
- Ma'arif's Ḥajjāj b. Yūsuf anecdote → dropped (illustrative, not exegesis of the verse).
