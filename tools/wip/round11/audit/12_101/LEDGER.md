Disagreements kept, no position: (1) the prostration of 12:100 (al-Baghawi's three: bowing / foreheads down as greeting, abrogated / to Allah before Yusuf via Ibn 'Abbas, with his "first is soundest"; as-Sa'di honour; Ma'arif permitted then, haram now). (2) tawaffani musliman: a wish for death (Ibn 'Abbas via as-Suddi and Ibn Jurayj, Qatadah, Mujahid, in at-Tabari; Qatadah also in al-Qurtubi) vs dying in Islam when the term comes (al-Qurtubi's "qawl al-jumhur", as-Sa'di, ad-Dahhak); Ibn Kathir's three possibilities. (3) ta'wil al-ahadith: dreams (at-Tabari, Mujahid, al-Baghawi) vs dreams and more (Muyassar) vs scriptures too (as-Sa'di). (4) min: partitive (al-Qurtubi's main), genus, emphasis.
Never said in own voice that Yusuf (AS) did wrong; §5 reports al-Qurtubi's "far-fetched unless permitted in his law" and Ibn Kathir's "not permitted in our Shari'ah" as theirs.

## Hadith
- **Bukhari 5671** (Dar-us-Salam), https://quranx.com/hadith/Bukhari/DarusSalam/Hadith-5671 (saved hadith_Bukhari_5671.txt). Anas ibn Malik. First ten words (Arabic): «لاَ يَتَمَنَّيَنَّ أَحَدُكُمُ الْمَوْتَ مِنْ ضُرٍّ أَصَابَهُ، فَإِنْ كَانَ لاَ بُدَّ»; EN "None of you should wish for death because of a calamity befalling". Quoted whole from this one wording (§6.1). Grading: in al-Bukhari's Sahih; the page shows no further grade, none added. Attached to 12:101 by Ibn Kathir (Ahmad, plus "rawahu al-Bukhari wa Muslim") and al-Qurtubi ("rawahu Muslim"). Muslim's number not fetched; Muslim named only as the commentators' attribution.
- **Bukhari 4438** (Dar-us-Salam), https://quranx.com/hadith/Bukhari/DarusSalam/Hadith-4438 (saved hadith_Bukhari_4438.txt). 'A'ishah. First ten words (EN): "Abdur-Rahman bin Abu Bakr entered upon the Prophet while I". Used for the siwak, "raised his hand or his finger", «في الرفيق الأعلى» thrice, then died (§7.2); this matches Ibn Kathir's description. In al-Bukhari's Sahih; no other grade claimed.
- Bukhari 4463 also fetched (saved): same «اللهم الرفيق الأعلى» but without the raised finger; not cited.

## Dropped
- Ibn Kathir's other narrations at this verse: Abu Umamah/Sa'd (Ahmad, 'Ali b. Yazid in chain), Abu Hurayrah via Ibn Lahi'ah ("tafarrada bihi Ahmad"), Mu'adh's dream du'a (Ahmad, Tirmidhi), Mahmud b. Labid (Ahmad), 'Ali's "take me to You", al-Bukhari's own prayer, Maryam 19:23, the Dajjal-era grave report: not confirmed on quranx, not used.
- Anas/Yazid ar-Raqashi report of the brothers' forgiveness after twenty years (at-Tabari 19948, Ibn Kathir): Ibn Kathir calls it mawquf with ar-Raqashi and Salih al-Murri "very weak"; at-Tabari's editor calls it "halik". Not used.
- Abu 'Imran al-Jawni (at-Tabari 19949); as-Suddi on Ya'qub's burial and al-'Is; ages, years of absence, children, Nile burial details (al-Baghawi, al-Qurtubi, Ma'arif): Isra'ili/historical, figures conflict; only summarised and set aside in §7.3.
- Ma'arif's Mu'adh and Salman prostration narrations (12:99-100): not confirmed; not used.
- At-Tabari's editor's footnote on Ibn Jurayj (no Qur'anic evidence for "in some of the Qur'an prophets said tawaffani"): editor's, not at-Tabari's; not used.
- Al-Baghawi on 12:100 that the prison was "recompense for a slip": not used (would put fault on a prophet in a quoted aside, and it is 12:100 material).
- Al-Qurtubi's Sahl at-Tustari saying (three who wish for death) and Ibn Kathir's reading of "first" (first to ask death on Islam, like Nuh in 71:28) and his opening line on the favour continuing into the Hereafter: confirmed, cut for length only.
