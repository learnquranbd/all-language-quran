Card and deep bilingual article for the oath answering the resurrection-denier
of 19:66. The scoffer asks "when I am dead, will I be brought forth alive?";
19:67 gives the proof (created from nothing) and 19:68 answers with an oath.

Sources gave: at-Tabari frames the oath as told to the Prophet against those
deniers, gathered "coupled with their allies among the devils"; al-Qurtubi
notes the oath falls after the proof is established and gathers them from the
graves "as He gathers the believers," and judges the wāw of wa'sh-shayāṭīn to
carry the sense of "with" (each denier chained to the devil who misled him,
cf. 37:22). as-Sa'di reads the oath as sworn by His Lordship, He being the most
truthful of speakers; Ibn Kathir as sworn by His Noble Self. al-Baghawi:
"with" the devils, each in a chain. Muyassar and as-Sa'di give jithiyy as
kneeling from the terror, unable to stand.

Disagreements kept: scope of "them" (only the deniers vs. all mankind —
al-Qurtubi, Ma'arif); sense of jithiyy (kneeling / sitting-quʿūd, Ibn Abbas /
standing-qiyām, as-Suddi, Ibn Masʿud / heaps-jamāʿāt, Ibn Abbas, Muqatil);
"ḥawla jahannam" at the rim or within.

Hadith: Sahih al-Bukhari 4974, the qudsi hadith "the son of Adam denies Me,"
which Ibn Kathir attaches to this passage as the refutation of the denier;
grading Sahih, quoted whole from Bukhari. Fetched and confirmed.

Licensing sentence included in both languages: the scene is the Hereafter's
justice on those who denied the raising, not a verdict any reader may pass on a
living person or community.

Dropped: Zamakhshari (named inside al-Qurtubi, not independently fetched — the
grammar reading attributed to al-Qurtubi); al-Qurtubi's disputation reading and
poetic citations, for space. Did not preempt 19:69–72. No asbab established;
verse placement used instead.
