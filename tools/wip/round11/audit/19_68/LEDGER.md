# SOURCES ledger — 19:68

## Tafsir fetched (all in this audit dir; none re-fetched)
- **at-Tabari (15)** — NAMED. Clause: "فوربك يا محمد لنحشرنّ هؤلاء القائلين: أئذا متنا لسوف نخرج أحياء ... مقرنين بأوليائهم من الشياطين". Used for: oath addressed to the Prophet against the deniers of 19:66; gathered "coupled with their allies among the devils." Also "الجثي: جمع الجاثي" and Ibn Abbas → القعود (sitting), tied to 45:28.
- **al-Qurtubi (90)** — NAMED. Clauses: "أقسم بنفسه بعد إقامة الحجة بأنه يحشرهم من قبورهم إلى المعاد كما يحشر المؤمنين" (oath after the proof, gathered from graves like the believers); "الواو ... بمعنى مع أوقع ... مقرونين بالشياطين الذين أغووهم ... في سلسلة" (wāw = "with" more fitting; chained to the devils who misled them); scope question "إذا أريد بالإنسان الكفرة خاصة ... فإن أريد الأناسي على العموم" kept as a question; "يعتلون ... جثاة على ركبهم غير مشاة على أقدامهم" + tie to 45:28; reasons "الاستيفاز والقلق" / "شدة الأمر التي لا يطيقون معها القيام"; heaps reading "جثيا جماعات ... جمع جثوة" (Ibn Abbas, Muqatil); "حول جهنم يجوز أن يكون داخلها"; the general-scope reason the blessed are gathered with the wretched "ليشاهد السعداء الأحوال التي نجاهم الله منها".
- **as-Sa'di (91)** — NAMED. Clauses: "أقسم الله ... وهو أصدق القائلين - بربوبيته، ليحشرن هؤلاء المنكرين للبعث، هم وشياطينهم"; "جاثين على ركبهم من شدة الأهوال ... منتظرين لحكم الكبير المتعال". Used for: oath by His Lordship, most truthful of speakers; kneeling from terror, awaiting the ruling.
- **Ibn Kathir ar (14)** — NAMED (via the en abridged, consistent). Clause: "أقسم الرب ... بنفسه الكريمة، أنه لا بد أن يحشرهم جميعا وشياطينهم"; Ibn Abbas قعود / as-Suddi قياما. Used for: oath by His Noble Self.
- **Ibn Kathir en abridged (169)** — NAMED. Covers 19:66–70 (grouped). Used for: beginning-of-creation-as-proof-of-repetition (from 19:67); "swears by His Noble Self ... gather all of those who worshipped other than Allah and their devils"; Ibn Abbas "sitting" / as-Suddi "standing." Also carries the Sahih hadith (see below).
- **al-Baghawi (94)** — NAMED. Clauses: "والشياطين) مع الشياطين ... يحشر كل كافر مع شيطانه في سلسلة"; jithiyy three readings — Ibn Abbas "جماعات جمع جثوة", al-Hasan & aḍ-Ḍaḥḥāk "جمع جاث ... جاثين على الركب", as-Suddi "قائمين على الركب لضيق المكان."
- **al-Muyassar (16)** — NAMED. Clause: "لنجمعن هؤلاء المنكرين للبعث ... مع الشياطين ... حول جهنم باركين على رُكَبهم; لشدة ما هم فيه من الهول، لا يقدرون على القيام." Used for: kneeling from the horror, unable to stand.
- **Ma'arif al-Qur'an en (168)** — NAMED. Used for: the two scope readings (every infidel with his devil / general reading including believers) and "everyone ... on their knees ... then believers taken across."

## Hadith
- **Sahih al-Bukhari 4974** (hadith qudsi, "son of Adam denies Me"). URL fetched: https://quranx.com/hadith/Bukhari/DarusSalam/Hadith-4974 (via tools/wip/round11/hadith.js; saved to this dir). First ten words (EN): "The son of Adam tells a lie against Me". Grading: **Sahih** (in Sahih al-Bukhari, Kitab al-Tafsir). Attached by Ibn Kathir to this passage ("In the Sahih it is recorded"). Directly on the resurrection-denial of 19:66–67 that the oath of 19:68 answers. Quoted whole, one collection's wording (Bukhari), no hybridising.

## Disagreements kept (no position taken)
- Scope of "them": only the disbelievers vs. all mankind — al-Qurtubi and Ma'arif, kept as a difference.
- Sense of jithiyy: kneeling from terror (Muyassar, as-Sa'di, al-Qurtubi, al-Hasan, aḍ-Ḍaḥḥāk) / sitting = quʿūd (Ibn Abbas via al-ʿAwfī) / standing = qiyām (as-Suddi, Ibn Masʿud) / heaps-groups = jamāʿāt (Ibn Abbas, Muqatil) / standing on knees for lack of room (as-Suddi in al-Baghawi). All reported as reported.
- "ḥawla jahannam": at the rim before entry, or inside surrounding it (al-Qurtubi). Kept.

## Dropped / not asserted
- Zamakhshari is named inside al-Qurtubi's text but I did not fetch him independently, so I attributed the grammatical "wāw = with" reading to al-Qurtubi (who states and endorses it), not to Zamakhshari by name.
- al-Qurtubi's "kneeling for the disputation" (tie to 39:31) and his poetic citations (Ṭarafa, al-Kumayt) dropped for space; not needed.
- Did not develop 19:69–72 content (dragging the worst, "none of you but will come to it," saving the God-fearing) — that is the ground of the following verses; only pointed forward without expounding.
