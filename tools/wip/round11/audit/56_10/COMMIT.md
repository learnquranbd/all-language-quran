Card and 8-section bilingual article (1,690 EN words) on 56:10:
wa-s-sabiquna s-sabiqun. 56:7-9 as frame; 56:11-14 pointed to only.
No shipped articles in surah 56 to overlap.

Sources: at-Tabari, Ibn Kathir (ar), al-Qurtubi, al-Baghawi, as-Sa'di,
al-Muyassar (group 56:10-12, 56:10 clause only), Ma'arif (group
56:10-12, 56:10 part only), Ibn Kathir en (group 56:1-10, 56:7-10 only).
- Placement: third pair (at-Tabari); third category, chiefs of the
  right, fewer, link to 35:32 (Ibn Kathir en).
- Syntax kept as given, no position: subject + tawkid, predicate in
  56:11 (al-Qurtubi, "it is said"); subject + predicate, "ahead to
  obedience = ahead to mercy" (az-Zajjaj via al-Qurtubi); at-Tabari's
  two ways (as-sabiqun al-awwalun; raised by ula'ika al-muqarrabun).
- Ahead here, ahead there: Muyassar, as-Sa'di, Ibn Abbas and ar-Rabi'
  (al-Baghawi), Ibn Kathir with 3:133 and 57:21.
- Identifications unranked and attributed: Prophets, 'Illiyyin, every
  nation, Islam, both qiblas (9:100), five prayers, jihad, mosque,
  repentance (23:61), all Allah calls to, people of the Qur'an.
- Two Ibn Abbas name-lists (Ibn Kathir; al-Qurtubi via al-Mawardi)
  kept side by side, unweighed; explicit non-licence sentence on any
  living group. No sect named.
- Conclusions as stated: at-Tabari (faith, first Emigrants); Ibn
  Kathir (all sound), echoed by Ma'arif.
- Ibn Zayd's three thirds (at-Tabari); Shumait ibn al-'Ajlan (al-Qurtubi).
Hadith: none. The 'A'isha report from Musnad Ahmad could not be
confirmed with a grading; not quoted. 'Abdullah ibn 'Amr athar and
al-Hasan's narration noted as unused. Asbab: none.
Dropped: "glorification" reading of the doubling (no source).
Fixed: Ibn Kathir (ar) cites 57:21 as al-Hadid 22; 57:21 used.
