Card and 8-section bilingual article for 43:70: udkhulu al-jannata antum
wa-azwajukum tuhbarun. 43:67-69 as context (two lines for 43:68-69);
43:71-72 one clause only; 43:73 untouched.

Sources: Tabari, Ibn Kathir (ar; en abridged), Muyassar, Qurtubi, Sa'di,
Baghawi, Ma'arif; plus Tabari and Qurtubi on 30:15, where IK and Qurtubi
refer the word back.
- Udkhulu: "it is said to them" (IK, Muyassar, Qurtubi); Qurtubi's
  alternative joins it to "O My servants who believed"; Tabari's hal
  paraphrase "envied for Allah's honour, glad".
- Azwaj kept as a disagreement: Muslim wives (Qurtubi first), believing
  counterparts (Qurtubi qila, IK nuzara', Muyassar qurana'), hur (Qurtubi
  qila), everyone joined on like deeds incl. wife, child, companion
  (Sa'di); Tabari, Baghawi unglossed. App renderings: "kinds" vs wives.
  No claim on wives' status beyond the texts.
- Tuhbarun: Qurtubi's six (Ibn 'Abbas honour/rank, al-Hasan joy/heart,
  Qatadah bliss/body, Mujahid gladness/eye, Ibn Abi Najih marvel, Yahya b.
  Abi Kathir listening); Tabari's Qatadah x2, Suddi, Ibn Zayd; IK,
  Muyassar, Baghawi, Sa'di pairs. Mujahid variance (Qurtubi vs Tabari
  30:15) reported, not resolved.
- 30:15: Tabari's own reading includes listening; hibrah = surur and
  ghibta; Qurtubi's root notes (Mawardi, Jawhari, Kisa'i, 'Ali b.
  Sulayman's trace, tahbir) and "no contradiction".
Hadith: Bukhari 3244 (no eye has seen), quoted whole, in al-Bukhari's
Sahih; marked general, cited by Qurtubi at 30:15, not attached to 43:70.
No-roll-call sentence in both languages.
Dropped: Taymi report, 'Ali's two-friends report, Awza'i/Tha'labi sama'
narrations, 13:23 / 52:21 / 36:56 (no tafsir on 43:70 cites them).
