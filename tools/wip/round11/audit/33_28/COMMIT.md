The takhyīr: Allah raises the wives' request into a choice between the
world and His own company, and every Mother of the Believers chooses Him.

Sources gave:
- as-Sa'di, Ibn Kathir, al-Qurtubi: the occasion — the wives asked for
  wider nafaqah/clothing; the strain reached a withdrawal; Allah willed to
  ease it for His Messenger and raise the wives' rank.
- Ma'arif al-Qur'an (Jabir in Sahih Muslim; Abu Hayyan): post-Ahzab spoils
  lifted the Muslims' condition; the wives' request was innocent of malice.
- at-Tabari: umattiʿkunna = the mutʿah of divorce (2:236); wa-usarrihkunna
  read through 65:1 — a release shaped by revelation. al-Baghawi concurs.
- al-Qurtubi, al-Muyassar, as-Sa'di: sarāḥ jamīl = a dignified parting with
  no harm, no reviling, nothing withheld of her due.

Disagreement kept: nature of the choice — staying vs divorce (ʿĀʾishah,
Mujāhid, ʿIkrimah) against only dunyā vs ākhira (al-Hasan, Qatāda);
al-Qurtubi's preference for the first attributed to him, not asserted.

Hadith: Sahih al-Bukhari 4785 quoted whole — ʿĀʾishah chose Allah and His
Messenger without consulting her parents, and all the wives followed.
Grading reported as Sahih al-Bukhari; not upgraded.

Reverence held throughout: a clear note that this licenses nothing against
the Prophet's household or against women, and is no template to misuse.

Dropped: the wives-roster and the divorce-counting fiqh (out of scope); the
honey-incident as alternative cause (unconfirmed for this verse). 33:29-34
left as future ground.
