# SOURCES ledger — 33:28

## Tafsir fetched (from PREP; not re-fetched)
- **Ibn Kathir ar (14)** — NAMED. Rests on: "هذا أمر من الله لرسوله ... بأن يخير نساءه بين أن يفارقهن ... وبين الصبر على ما عنده من ضيق الحال، ولهن عند الله في ذلك الثواب الجزيل، فاخترن ... الله ورسوله والدار الآخرة." Also carries the ʿĀʾishah takhyīr hadith (al-Bukhari chain via az-Zuhrī → Abū Salama → ʿĀʾishah) and the Jabir nafaqah narration verbatim — used to confirm the Bukhari wording and the sabab.
- **at-Tabari (15)** — NAMED. umattiʿkunna = the mutʿah Allah obliged on men at divorce, cross-read to 2:236 ("أمتعكن ما أوجب الله على الرجال للنساء من المتعة عند فراقهم إياهنّ بالطلاق"). wa-usarrihkunna read through 65:1 ("وأطلقكنّ على ما أذن الله به ... إِذَا طَلَّقْتُمُ النِّسَاءَ فَطَلِّقُوهُنَّ لِعِدَّتِهِنَّ").
- **al-Muyassar (16)** — NAMED. "wives who had gathered on him asking for an increase in nafaqah"; sarāḥ jamīl = "أفارقكنَّ دون ضرر أو إيذاء" (part without harm or injury).
- **al-Qurtubi (90)** — NAMED. Two readings of the choice: (1) between staying married and release — ʿĀʾishah, Mujāhid, ʿIkrimah, ash-Shaʿbī, Ibn Shihāb, Rabīʿa; (2) only between dunyā and ākhira, kept or released, no ṭalāq in their hands — al-Hasan, Qatāda, ʿAlī. Qurtubi: "القول الأول أصح." sarāḥ jamīl = "طلاقا للسنة من غير ضرار ولا منع واجب لها." ʿĀʾishah "خيرنا ... فاخترناه فلم يعده طلاقا" (Bukhari & Muslim). Reason for "consult parents": he feared her youth might incline her to part, knowing her parents would not advise it.
- **as-Sa'di (91)** — NAMED. Sabab: wives gathered in jealousy and asked for nafaqah/kiswa, persisted, which grieved the Messenger until "آلى منهن شهرًا." Allah's purpose: "أن يسهل الأمر على رسوله، وأن يرفع درجة زوجاته، ويُذْهِبَ عنهن كل أمر ينقص أجرهن." sarāḥ jamīl = "من دون مغاضبة ولا مشاتمة، بل بسعة صدر."
- **al-Baghawi (94)** — NAMED. umattiʿkunna = "متعة الطلاق" (the mutʿah of divorce). Short entry, confirms Tabari/Baghawi agreement.
- **Ma'arif al-Qur'an en (168)** — NAMED (as "Ma'arif al-Qur'an"; not a STEM-gated name). Surah objective = avoid causing pain to the Prophet ﷺ. Sabab via Jabir (Sahih Muslim) + Abū Hayyān (Baḥr al-Muḥīṭ): post-Ahzab spoils of Banū Naḍīr/Qurayẓa improved Muslims' condition; wives jointly asked for wider provision, citing the wives of Caesar/Chosroes; they never meant to pain him. Ruling: desirability (not obligation) of giving the wife the choice to stay content or part honourably with a gift.
- **Ibn Kathir en abridged (169)** — cross-check for the English of the choice-framing and the Bukhari/Jabir narrations; not separately cited beyond Ibn Kathir.

## Hadith
- **Sahih al-Bukhari 4785** — QUOTED WHOLE (§7.2). URL fetched: https://quranx.com/hadith/Bukhari/DarusSalam/Hadith-4785 (saved: audit/33_28/hadith_Bukhari_4785.txt). First ten words: "Allah's Messenger came to me when Allah ordered him to". Refs on page: Dar-us-Salam Hadith 4785; USC-MSA Vol 6, Book 60, Hadith 308. Grading: Sahih al-Bukhari (authentic by the collection's criterion) — not upgraded. Arabic matches Ibn Kathir (14) verbatim (az-Zuhrī → Abū Salama → ʿĀʾishah).
- Noted in-article (not separately quoted): al-Bukhari & Muslim both record ʿĀʾishah "he gave us the choice and we chose him, and he did not count it a divorce" (via al-Aʿmash, per Ibn Kathir); and the "Allah did not send me harsh but a gentle teacher" wording (Muslim, per Ibn Kathir/Qurtubi). Reported as sound, attributed, not re-quoted whole.

## Asbab an-nuzul
CONFIRMED and used: the wives' joint request for wider provision/nafaqah — grounded in the Jabir hadith (Sahih Muslim, in Ma'arif 168 and Ibn Kathir 14) and as-Sa'di/al-Qurtubi. Presented as the occasion, with the elevation framing. al-Qurtubi lists minor variant occasions (jealousy; a gold-ring request) — mentioned only as "the reports differ in detail," none asserted as the sole cause.

## Disagreement kept (no position taken in article voice)
Nature of the takhyīr: (1) choice of staying vs divorce vs (2) choice only of dunyā/ākhira. Both named with their scholars; al-Qurtubi's own preference for (1) attributed explicitly to him.

## Dropped (could not confirm / out of scope)
- al-Qurtubi's long roster of the Prophet's wives (names, dates, numbers) and ʿIkrimah's "nine wives, five of Quraysh" (Ibn Kathir) — DROPPED to avoid numeric/parity noise and to keep reverent focus on the choice, not a catalogue.
- The fiqh of how divorce counts if a wife "chose herself" (Qurtubi masʾala 6–7, Mālik/Abū Ḥanīfa positions) — out of scope for a reflection; not used.
- The honey/Zaynab incident (Ma'arif's cross-ref to 66:1-5) as an alternative cause — mentioned by Ma'arif as possible; DROPPED, kept to the provision cause the verse's own wording supports.
- 33:29-34 material (double reward, special conduct, "stay in your homes," ahl al-bayt/taṭhīr) — deliberately left as future ground per brief.
