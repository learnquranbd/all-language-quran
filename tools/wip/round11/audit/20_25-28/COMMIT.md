article built only from the tafsir texts fetched for 20:25 to 20:28.

Sources: Ibn Kathir on the weight of the errand and his gloss "unless You are
my help I have no strength"; at-Tabari (take in the revelation, boldness to
address Pharaoh; Ibn Zayd "jur'atan li"); al-Qurtubi (light of faith and

Ma'arif; al-Muyassar. Yafqahu: at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi with al-Jawhari on fiqh,
as-Sa'di with 28:34.

Ibn Abbas in al-Qurtubi; al-Baghawi's two versions; Ibn Kathir's date and
ember; Ma'arif) given as the tafsirs' reports, ungraded, beside al-Qurtubi's
qila that it arose in the munajat.

al-Hasan al-Basri, 43:52; al-Qurazi via Ibn Abi Hatim; Ma'arif) vs removed
entirely (al-Qurtubi's qila, 20:36), with al-Qurtubi's own objection reported
as his. 20:29-36 one paragraph. Licensing paragraph on Pharaoh in both languages.

Hadith: none attached to 20:25-28; al-Qurtubi's narrations here concern the
wazir of 20:29, and Ma'arif's unsourced du'a was dropped.
