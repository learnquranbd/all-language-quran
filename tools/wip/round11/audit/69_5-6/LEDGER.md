## Disagreements kept (both names, no position)
- taghiya: cry (Qatada; Tabari's choice; IK, Sa'di, Muyassar, Ma'arif) vs transgression/sins (Mujahid, Ibn Zayd, ar-Rabi', al-Hasan; Baghawi first) vs thunderbolt (al-Kalbi) vs the she-camel's slayer.
- Slayer reading attributed to as-Suddi (Ibn Kathir ar) vs Ibn Zayd (al-Qurtubi); Tabari and IK both give Ibn Zayd as "transgression". Left as found.
- 'atiya: against its keepers (Tabari, Baghawi, Qurtubi first, 'Ali via IK, "many" per Sa'di) vs against 'Ad (Ibn Zayd via Tabari, Qurtubi's qila, Sa'di's "sahih").
- Mikyal report: Ibn 'Abbas's own words (Tabari) vs the Prophet's ﷺ words (Qurtubi, same chain from Sufyan). No grading in either; not presented as a confirmed hadith.
