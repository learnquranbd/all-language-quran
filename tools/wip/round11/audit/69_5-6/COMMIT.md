Card and 8-section bilingual article for 69:5-6: fa-amma Thamudu
fa-uhliku bi-t-taghiya, wa-amma 'Adun fa-uhliku bi-rihin sarsarin
'atiya. 69:4 as the frame; 69:7 named only as the next verse.

Sources: Tabari, Qurtubi, Ibn Kathir (ar; en grouped 69:1-10), Sa'di,
Baghawi, Muyassar (grouped 69:5-8), Ma'arif (69:1-5 and 69:6). All named.
- Taghiya kept as a disagreement: cry (Qatada, Tabari's choice from the
  'Ad parallel, IK, Sa'di, Muyassar, Ma'arif) vs transgression/sins
  (Mujahid, Ibn Zayd with 91:11, al-Rabi', al-Hasan) vs al-Kalbi's
  thunderbolt vs the she-camel's slayer (as-Suddi per IK, Ibn Zayd per
  Qurtubi; attribution left as found). 54:31 via Qurtubi (verified).
- Grammar via Qurtubi (idmar, masdar like al-'aqiba) and Baghawi
  (masdar or na't); root as mujawazat al-hadd with 69:11.
- Sarsar briefly (full voice list left to 41:16 and 54:19); 'atiya kept
  as a disagreement: over its keepers (Tabari, Baghawi, Qurtubi, 'Ali
  via IK) vs over 'Ad (Ibn Zayd, Qurtubi's qila, Sa'di's sahih).
- Mikyal report: Ibn 'Abbas's own words (Tabari) vs marfu' (Qurtubi,
  same chain); ungraded, not presented as a confirmed hadith.
Hadith: Bukhari 1035 (saba/dabur), attached by IK (en); quoted whole,
no upgrade; pointed to 41:16 where it is treated. Muslim fetch timed out.
Asbab: none given. Licensing sentence in both languages; no present-day
group, descendants, site or weather event named.
Dropped: 7:77 and 91:12-14 (not cited), 69:7 husum material, IK (en)
"stored harvest" for khazana (mistranslation; Arabic followed).
- 54:31 pointer is the drafter's own identification (Qurtubi quotes the words without a number); worded so.
