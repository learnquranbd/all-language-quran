Card and 8-section article (1,689 EN words) on 42:42, the verse that
puts the sabil on those who wrong people and overstep on the earth
without right, answering 42:41's "no sabil against them".

Sources fetched for the verse: at-Tabari, Ibn Kathir (ar and en),
as-Sa'di, al-Muyassar, al-Baghawi, al-Qurtubi, Ma'arif (grouped 42:39-44).

Kept as differences: sabil as a lawful path to punish the aggressor
(at-Tabari), as blame and hardship / burden of sin (Ibn Kathir), as the
case for a Shari'ah punishment (as-Sa'di), as being taken to account
(al-Muyassar). Yazlimuna an-nas: those who begin the wrong (Ibn Kathir,
al-Baghawi; most, per al-Qurtubi) vs shirk (Ibn Jurayj). Baghy: lives
and property (most), blood, wealth and honour (as-Sa'di), sins (Muqatil,
al-Baghawi), Quraysh's hope for another religion in Makkah (Abu Malik);
abrogated and for idolaters only (Ibn Zayd) vs general (Qatada).
Al-Qurtubi's fiqh: Ibn al-'Arabi's pairing with 9:91; Sahnun vs
ad-Dawudi on escaping an unjust levy; Ibn al-Musayyib, Ibn Sirin and
Malik on releasing a wrongdoer, with Ibn al-'Arabi's reasons.

Hadith: Muslim 2587 (the two who revile each other: the blame is on
the one who began, so long as the wronged does not overstep), cited by
Ibn Kathir as sahih, confirmed on quranx and quoted whole. Ibn Kathir's
report of al-'Ala' ibn Ziyad's letter (via Ibn Abi Hatim) is marked as
an athar, not a hadith.

Dropped: the Abu al-Yasar hadith al-Qurtubi cites (too long to quote
whole, not fetched); no asbab. Headings avoid 42:40's. The article
states in both languages that the verse licenses nothing against any
living person or community and names no oppressor of today.
