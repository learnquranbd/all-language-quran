The deniers' verdict on their messenger, closing the speech of the disbelieving
notables of 23:33-38: he is only a man who invented a lie about Allah, and we will
not believe him. Framed as the recurring charge hurled at prophet after prophet —
reduce the messenger to "only a man", call the revelation fabrication, and the
summons can be buried.

Sources (all pre-fetched, none from memory):
- at-Tabari: the "invented lie" they name is the messenger's tawḥīd (23:32) and his
  promise of resurrection (23:35); he glosses "huwa" as the messenger and names Salih.
- Ibn Kathir (ar): the lie covers the message, the warning and the news of the maʿād.
- Ibn Kathir (en): they refused a human messenger; the closing lesson — beware of
  disbelieving your messengers.
- al-Baghawi: "we will not believe him" = will not believe in resurrection after death.
- al-Qurtubi: huwa = the messenger; afterā = ikhtalaqa (invented from nothing).
- al-Muyassar: plain paraphrase of the charge.
- Ma'arif (on 23:37): the standing argument of Day-of-Judgment deniers, turned on the
  reader — some who profess faith live as if they too never expected that Day.

Disagreement kept open: the people/prophet — at-Tabari names Salih (Thamud); Ibn Kathir
reports 'Ad or Thamud, leaning Thamud from the Ṣayḥah of 23:41. Article does not settle it.

Hadith: Sahih al-Bukhari 660 (the seven Allah shades) used in §7 as a general narration
on the Day the deniers rejected, explicitly marked NOT attached to the verse — no fetched
tafsir attaches a sound hadith to 23:38, and §7 says so.

Wrongdoers' verse: carries the "licenses nothing against any living person or community"
sentence in both languages, worded fresh against shipped 23:14/23:20/23:27.

Dropped: the §5 irony (their own shirk is the real fabrication about Allah) is kept as the
article's own reflection, grounded in at-Tabari, not attributed to any mufassir as "irony".
as-Sa'di empty; not used. No asbab. No weak report used to fill the hadith slot.
