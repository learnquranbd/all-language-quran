Key 4:152-153 (believe in all the messengers and make no distinction, their rewards,
Forgiving, Merciful; the People of the Scripture ask for a book from the sky; they asked
Musa for greater, "show us Allah openly"; the calf, the pardon). Not widened: 4:150-151
quoted for placement only, so 4:149 and 4:150-151 stay free.
New card and a 1,741-word, 9-section article.

Sources: at-Tabari, Ibn Kathir (ar), al-Qurtubi, as-Sa'di, al-Baghawi, al-Muyassar on
4:152 and 4:153, all slots on target. Ma'arif read, not named.
Centre kept apart from 2:55-56 and 2:135-138: al-Baghawi's "a question of dictating,
not of submitting", as-Sa'di on belief made to hang on a demand and on revelation in
parts (25:32), at-Tabari's comfort to the Prophet and his calf-you-could-see reading,
as-Sa'di's "their rewards, each according to his state", and the mercy that closes both
verses (Ibn Kathir: "if any of them has sins"; al-Baghawi's pardon as a call to repent).
Licensing line early in both languages: describes some people of two past times,
licenses nothing against any Jew, Christian or anyone living; 4:162 cited.
Occasion only as reported: as-Suddi, al-Qurazi, Qatada, Ibn Jurayj (at-Tabari);
Ka'b ibn al-Ashraf and Finhas ibn 'Azura named as al-Baghawi's account.
Kept as differences, no position:
- which book: like the Torah / special to them / letters to named men; at-Tabari's
  choice (kitaban, singular) given as his.
- jahratan: the seeing (at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi) vs the saying (Ibn Abbas in at-Tabari,
  Abu Ubayda in al-Baghawi).
Seeing Allah: no school's case, no sect named.
Hadith: none attached to the verses (said in one sentence). Bukhari 4981 (Abu Hurayra,
"what I was given was revelation") whole, from the Arabic, marked general.
Dropped: Ma'arif's unsourced "Quran explains itself" saying and its garbled Sabbath
line, Ibn Kathir (en) on 4:150-151, the calf-repentance detail, sultan glosses (length).
