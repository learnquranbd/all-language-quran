Card and 8-section bilingual article for 41:16: fa-arsalna 'alayhim rihan
sarsaran fi ayyamin nahisat, the punishment of disgrace in this world and
the more disgracing punishment of the Hereafter. 41:13 and 41:15 one line
each; 41:17 (Thamud) left to its own verse.

Sources: Tabari, Ibn Kathir (ar + en abridged), Muyassar, Qurtubi, Sa'di,
Baghawi, Ma'arif (grouped 41:16-18).
- Sarsar kept as a disagreement: severe (Mujahid, Abu 'Ubayda), cold
  (Qatada, 'Ikrima, Sa'id b. Jubayr, 'Ata', ad-Dahhak), loud (as-Suddi);
  Tabari prefers Mujahid, explained through the sound of a hard wind
  (sarar, ra->sad); Qurtubi's sirr derivation; Ibn as-Sikkit's three
  roots; IK takes all three, punishment of the kind of their strength,
  with 69:6. Sa'di: like crashing thunder.
- Nahisat: consecutive (Ibn 'Abbas, 'Atiyya; IK), ill-omened (Mujahid,
  Qatada, Suddi; Tabari's choice), evil (Ibn Zayd), severe (Dahhak), cold
  (Naqqash), dusty (Ibn 'Isa). Readings nahsat/nahisat (Tabari, Baghawi,
  Qurtubi: Abu Hatim vs Abu 'Ubayd).
- "For them": Ma'arif's statement that no day is unlucky in itself,
  attributed; the 'alayhim qualifier shown in Suddi, Qatada, Muyassar, IK.
- 69:7 seven nights and eight days via IK and Sa'di (checked in
  quran-json); Dahhak's three rainless years via Baghawi and Qurtubi.
- Khizy/akhza and la yunsarun glossed only as fetched.
- Licensing sentence in both languages, incl. storms today and dates.
Hadith: Bukhari 1035 (saba/dabur) quoted whole, marked general and not
attached to the verse; no fetched tafsir cites a hadith. Asbab: none.
Dropped: the Wednesday reports (Qurtubi, Ma'arif), Jabir's rain-and-wind
saying (no source or grading), Dahhak's Makkah delegation, poetry.
