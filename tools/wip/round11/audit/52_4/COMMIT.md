Card and 8-section bilingual article on wa l-bayti l-ma'mur, the fourth
of the six oaths of 52:1-6 (series not retold; 52:7 a pointer only).

Sources: at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi, Ibn Kathir (ar + en abridged),
al-Baghawi, al-Muyassar, as-Sa'di, Ma'arif al-Qur'an.
- Ma'mur: kept alive by its many visitors (Tabari, Baghawi, Qurtubi).
- Identity kept as a disagreement, none chosen: house in the heavens
  opposite the Ka'bah (Tabari "fima dhukira"; 'Ali/ad-Durah, Ibn 'Abbas,
  'Ikrimah, Mujahid, Ibn Zayd; Baghawi, Muyassar, Sa'di, Ibn Kathir,
  Ma'arif) vs the Ka'bah itself (al-Hasan via Qurtubi; Sa'di "qila",
  95:3, 2:125) vs ar-Rabi' b. Anas's raised-House report (22:26).
- Location reports listed, not reconciled: sixth, fourth, seventh,
  nearest heaven, under/facing the Throne; Ibn 'Abbas's fifteen houses.
Hadith: Bukhari 3207 (Bayt al-Ma'mur passage of the Mi'raj hadith,
quoted; attached by Ibn Kathir, Tabari, Qurtubi, Ma'arif); Muslim 162
(Ibrahim reclining against the House) paraphrased, with Ibn Kathir's
"reward of the kind of the deed".
Qatadah's "dhukira lana" report and the 'Ali / Ibn 'Abbas sayings
marked as ungraded; Rawh b. Janah narration only with Ibn Kathir's
rejection; ad-Dahhak's "they claim" report noted with his hedge.
Asbab: none used. Qira'at: none fetched.
Dropped: Ibn Zayd's chainless marfu'; Tabari's Thabit/Anas variants;
52:5-6 reports; surah virtues.
Bukhari 3207: only the Bayt al-Ma'mur detail described (the long Mi'raj hadith not quoted); Muslim 162 paraphrased with attribution; 'sound' wording removed from the article's own voice.
