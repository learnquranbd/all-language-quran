# SOURCES ledger — 27:52

فَتِلْكَ بُيُوتُهُمْ خَاوِيَةًۢ بِمَا ظَلَمُوٓا۟ ۗ إِنَّ فِى ذَٰلِكَ لَءَايَةًۭ لِّقَوْمٍۢ يَعْلَمُونَ
"So those are their houses, desolate because of the wrong they did. Indeed in that is a sign for people who know." (11 words)

## Tafsir fetched (all pre-fetched in PREP.md; none re-fetched)

- **at-Tabari (15)** — NAMED. خاوية = "خالية منهم, ليس فيها منهم أحد, قد أهلكهم الله فأبادهم" (empty of them, no one of them in them, Allah destroyed and annihilated them). بما ظلموا = "بظلمهم أنفسهم بشركهم بالله, وتكذيبهم رسولهم" (wronging themselves by shirk and belying the messenger). Sign = "لعظة لمن يعلم فعلنا بهم... من قومك الذين يكذّبونك" (a lesson/warning for those who know what We did — Muhammad's own deniers). Used in §1, §3, §6.
- **as-Sa'di (91)** — NAMED. خاوية = "قد تهدمت جدرانها على سقوفها وأوحشت من ساكنيها وعطلت من نازليها" (walls collapsed onto roofs, desolate of dwellers). بما ظلموا = "عاقبة ظلمهم وشركهم بالله وبغيهم في الأرض" (consequence of their zulm, shirk, and baghy in the land). Sign = for those who "يعلمون الحقائق ويتدبرون وقائع الله في أوليائه وأعدائه... عاقبة الظلم الدمار والهلاك وأن عاقبة الإيمان والعدل النجاة والفوز" (know realities, ponder Allah's dealings with friends/enemies; end of zulm = ruin, end of faith/justice = deliverance/triumph). Used in §2, §3, §6, §8 (incl. the 27:53 counterpoint, which is internal to Sa'di).
- **al-Qurtubi (90)** — NAMED. Meaning: "خالية عن أهلها خرابا ليس بها ساكن" (empty of its people, in ruin, no dweller). Grammar/qiraat of خاوية: naṣb ʿalā al-ḥāl per al-Farrāʾ & an-Naḥḥās; al-Kisāʾī & Abū ʿUbayda = naṣb ʿalā al-qaṭʿ; ʿĪsā b. ʿUmar, Naṣr b. ʿĀṣim, al-Jaḥdarī = rafʿ as khabar. Used in §2.
- **al-Muyassar (16)** — NAMED. "مساكنهم خالية ليس فيها منهم أحد، أهلكهم الله; بسبب ظلمهم لأنفسهم بالشرك، وتكذيب نبيهم... وهذه سنتنا فيمن يكذب المرسلين" (empty, destroyed for their zulm by shirk and belying the prophet; this is Our sunna with those who belie messengers). Used in §1, §3.
- **al-Baghawi (94)** — NAMED. خاوية = "خالية" (empty); بما ظلموا = "بظلمهم وكفرهم" (zulm and kufr); آية = "لعبرة"; لقوم يعلمون = "قدرتنا" (who know Our power). Used in §2, §3, §6.
- **Ibn Kathir ar (14)** — NAMED. Plot-of-nine narrations: oath (تقاسموا بالله) to attack Salih and family by night and deny it; Mujahid (took oath, destroyed before reaching him, they and their people); Abd al-Rahman b. Abi Hatim report of the cave near Salih's place of prayer and the rock sealing the cave mouth. Used in §4.
- **Ibn Kathir en abridged (169)** — NAMED (grouped 27:48–53). Same plot narrative in English; plus the ifsad detail: 'Ata b. Abi Rabah — the nine "used to break silver coins"; Sa'id ibn al-Musayyib — "Cutting gold and silver (coins) is part of spreading corruption on earth." Used in §4, §5. Also carries "these are their houses in utter ruin, i.e., deserted" confirming the empty-houses sense.
- **Ma'arif al-Qur'an en (168)** — FETCHED, read, NOT named. It comments mainly on 27:49 (the plan; the note on criminals avoiding falsehood; the wali/heir question). Nothing on 27:52 itself not better covered elsewhere; dropped to avoid padding.
