The Thamud aftermath: the empty houses of 27:48-51's plotters held up as a
standing sign. Card plus 8-section bilingual article, source-gated.

Lesson built distinct from the she-camel verses (26:155-157, shipped earlier):
here it is the ruins-as-sermon and the plot-of-nine framing, not the camel.

Sources (all pre-fetched, none from memory):
- at-Tabari: khawiya = empty of them, none left; bi-ma zalamu = wronging
  themselves by shirk and belying the messenger; the sign a warning for
  Muhammad's own deniers who belied him as Thamud belied Salih.
- as-Sa'di: khawiya = walls collapsed onto roofs, desolate; zulm here includes
  baghy in the land; the ponderer learns both halves — zulm ends in ruin,
  faith and justice in deliverance (his own 27:53 counterpoint).
- al-Qurtubi: the qiraat of khawiya (nasb as hal per al-Farra/an-Nahhas;
  al-Kisa'i/Abu 'Ubayda differ; rafa' as khabar by three readers).
- al-Muyassar, al-Baghawi: empty; by zulm and kufr; sign for those who know
  Allah's power / His sunna with those who belie messengers.
- Ibn Kathir (ar + en): the nine's oath to kill Salih by night and deny it,
  God's counter-plot, the rock sealing the cave; the ifsad traced down to
  clipping silver coins ('Ata, Sa'id ibn al-Musayyib).

Hadith: Sahih Muslim 2980 (al-Hijr), quoted whole — "do not enter the
dwellings of those who wronged themselves except weeping," then he hastened
through the valley. Its phrase alladhina zalamu anfusahum echoes bi-ma zalamu.
Chosen over Bukhari 3379/3380, used earlier this wave; grading reported as
Sahih Muslim, not upgraded.

Condemned-people safeguard carried in both languages: the ruins warn the
living and license nothing against any person or community.

Dropped: Ma'arif's 27:49 material (wrong verse); no weak report used.
