Card and 8-section bilingual article for 96:19: kalla la tuti'hu
wa-sjud wa-qtarib, five words, the last verse of al-'Alaq and a verse
of prostration.

Sources: Tabari, Qurtubi, Baghawi, Sa'di, Ibn Kathir (ar), Muyassar,
Ma'arif. Slot check: all sit on 96:19; IK en is the 96:6-15 group, unused.
- Who: Abu Jahl per Tabari, Qurtubi, Baghawi, Muyassar, Sa'di, Ma'arif;
  IK does not name him here. Qatada's report via Tabari kept as his
  report, not an established cause. Sa'di: general for every forbidder.
- kalla: not as AJ says / supposes / is upon / thinks. Kept side by side.
- la tuti'hu: leaving prayer (Tabari, Qurtubi, Muyassar, Baghawi) vs
  persistence and abundance of worship (IK).
- usjud: pray (Qurtubi, Baghawi) vs prostrate (Tabari, Sa'di, Muyassar);
  prayer vs recitation prostration (Qurtubi, Ibn al-'Arabi).
- iqtarib: by obedience, in prostration, by supplication (wa-qila);
  Zayd b. Aslam's split address reported, no side taken.
Fiqh as reported: Ibn al-'Arabi, 'Ali's four 'aza'im (via Ibn Wahb),
Malik's private practice (Ibn Nafi', Mutarrif), Ibn Wahb, Ma'arif. No ruling.
Hadith: Muslim 482 quoted whole; Abu Dawud 875 (al-Baghawi's chain)
closing clause, no grading on page. Muslim 479 named, not clipped.
Muslim 578 / Bukhari 1078 cross-referenced to 84:21, not repeated.
Licensing sentence in both languages; no verdict on anyone's fate.
Dropped: Qurtubi's 'Ata' variant and Mu'adh story; IK en wrong-group reports.
Fix: Abu Dawud 875 quoted whole (page English), not just its closing clause.
