## Fiqh (reported only as sources state; no own-voice ruling)
Ibn al-'Arabi (via Qurtubi): apparent = prayer prostration, but the Sahih report makes it tilawa. 'Ali (via Ibn Wahb chain in Qurtubi): four 'aza'im incl. iqra'. Ibn al-'Arabi: "if sound" entails al-Hajj's second prostration. Ibn Nafi' and Mutarrif: Malik prostrated in his private practice at the close of this surah; Ibn Wahb counted it among 'aza'im. Ma'arif: compulsory for one who recites and listens (names no school). IK: the Prophet ﷺ used to prostrate in inshaqqat and iqra'.
