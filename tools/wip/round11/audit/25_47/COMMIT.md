Replaces the 795-word, 6-section article with a 1,696-word, 8-section one built only
on the eight tafsir files fetched for 25:47.

Sources: at-Tabari ties the verse to the shadow of 25:45-46 and glosses libas as a
shelter and screen like worn clothes; Ibn Kathir (wraps all that exists, with 92:1 and
91:4), al-Baghawi and the Muyassar give the covering; al-Qurtubi reports Ibn al-'Arabi
rejecting prayer unclothed in the dark because "the night is a garment".
Subat: al-Baghawi and Ma'arif take sabt as cutting off; al-Qurtubi lists stretching
out, cutting off (the Sabbath) and staying put, plus al-Khalil's "heavy sleep"; Ibn
Kathir adds rest of body and soul together.
Disagreement kept on nushur: spreading out for livelihood (Mujahid via at-Tabari, Ibn
Kathir with 28:73, Muyassar, as-Sa'di) vs waking and life from nashara al-mayyit
(at-Tabari, citing al-A'sha and 25:3, "sleep is the brother of death", while calling
Mujahid not far from correct; al-Qurtubi likens waking to life after death).
As-Sa'di's argument from absence and Ma'arif's synchronised-sleep blessings close it.
Hadith: Bukhari 6312 (Hudhayfah), the waking du'a ending "wa ilayhi-n-nushur", quoted
whole; attached by at-Tabari (who calls it sound) and al-Qurtubi.
No asbab.
