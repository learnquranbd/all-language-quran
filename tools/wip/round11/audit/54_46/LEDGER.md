  "الذي يحازون به، ويؤخذ منهم الحق بالقسط"; "أعظم وأشق، وأكبر من كل ما يتوهم، أو يدور بالبال".

- 169 Ibn Kathir (en, abridged; grouped 54:41-46): NAMED. "O idolators of the Quraysh"; immunity "that the torment

- 168 Ma'arif (en; grouped 54:43-48): NAMED. "The word adha means more or most calamitous. The word amarr is derived
  from murr which originally means 'bitter'. By extension anything 'difficult' or 'painful'".

## Badr vs Hereafter for 54:45
Tabari, Qurtubi, Muyassar, Baghawi tie the comparison to Badr; Ibn Kathir via the Bukhari report. No fetched
text reads 54:45 as a Hereafter event, so no disagreement exists to report; the article says so in one sentence.
