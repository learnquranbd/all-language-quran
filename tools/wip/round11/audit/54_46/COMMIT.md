Card and 8-section bilingual article for 54:46: bali s-sa'atu
maw'iduhum wa-s-sa'atu adha wa-amarr. 54:47-48 pointers only.

Sources: at-Tabari, Ibn Kathir (ar and en), al-Qurtubi, al-Baghawi,
as-Sa'di, al-Muyassar, Ma'arif.
- bal per Tabari (against denial of the raising); addressees the
  Quraysh of 54:43-45 per Ibn Kathir.
- maw'id: Qiyama (Qurtubi), raising and punishment (Tabari), repayment
  (Muyassar), right taken in full (Sa'di).
- adha/amarr: dahiya and Ibn as-Sikkit (Qurtubi); murr (Ma'arif);
  glosses of Sa'di, Muyassar, Baghawi.
- Comparator is Badr in Tabari, Qurtubi, Muyassar, Baghawi; no fetched
  text reads 54:45 as the Hereafter, so no disagreement to report.
- Sa'di: first and last gathered, struck down or left at ease.
- Shahr ibn Hawshab via Tabari, given literally, not a hadith.
Hadith: Bukhari 4876 ('A'isha: revealed in Makkah while she played)
and 4877 (Ibn 'Abbas: recited 54:45-46 in armour at Badr), quoted
whole; 4993 summarised; 4875 noted. No grading beyond the Sahih.
Asbab: none. Licensing sentence for the Quraysh/Badr passage.
Dropped: 'Umar's report in al-Baghawi (no confirmable number),
cross-refs 53:57-58, 38:11, 8:17 (not cited by a fetched tafsir).
Bukhari 4877 quoted whole across two paragraphs; two quranx English typos shown in brackets ([grievous], [If]). Bukhari 4876 whole.
