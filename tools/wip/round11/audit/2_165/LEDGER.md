## Disagreements kept (no position taken)
- ka-hubbi Allah: as the believers love Allah (at-Tabari, Ibn Zayd in at-Tabari; al-Mubarrad, with az-Zajjaj giving the sense, in al-Qurtubi; al-Baghawi first) vs levelling their own love of Allah with the rivals (Ibn Kaysan and az-Zajjaj in al-Qurtubi, "Abu Ishaq" calling it correct; az-Zajjaj in al-Baghawi; as-Sa'di and al-Muyassar write in this sense). Note: al-Qurtubi lists az-Zajjaj under both.
- Who the andad are: gods/idols (Qatada, Mujahid, ar-Rabi', Ibn Zayd in at-Tabari; Mujahid in al-Qurtubi; al-Baghawi) vs leaders obeyed in sin (as-Suddi in at-Tabari; Ibn 'Abbas and as-Suddi in al-Qurtubi). Muyassar and as-Sa'di's wider lists reported as theirs.
- yara / tara: qurra' as each mufassir gives them (lists not reconciled; Ya'qub is under ta in al-Baghawi). at-Tabari's choice of tara reported as his; Abu 'Ubayd's choice of ya reported via al-Qurtubi.
- Unstated answer of law: Abu 'Ubayd + an-Nahhas vs Muhammad ibn Yazid + al-Akhfash (al-Qurtubi); al-Baghawi's two supplied answers; at-Tabari's; az-Zuhri and Qatada on why it is omitted.
