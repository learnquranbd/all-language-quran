Replaces the 766-word, 6-section article with a 1,763-word, 8-section
bilingual one for 2:165; the shipped card is kept.

Sources: at-Tabari, Ibn Kathir (ar + en grouped 2:165-167), al-Qurtubi,
as-Sa'di, al-Baghawi, al-Muyassar, Ma'arif, all on 2:165. Slots checked.
- Placement after the proofs (al-Qurtubi, as-Sa'di, Muyassar); 2:163 one
  line, 2:164 a bridge only, none of its signs.
- Andad: gods/idols (Qatada, Mujahid, ar-Rabi', Ibn Zayd) vs leaders
  obeyed in sin (as-Suddi; Ibn 'Abbas in al-Qurtubi); al-Qurtubi's pronoun
  note; Muyassar's and as-Sa'di's wider lists, as theirs.
- Ka-hubbi Allah kept as a dispute, no position: the believers' love
  (at-Tabari, Ibn Zayd, al-Mubarrad) vs levelling (Ibn Kaysan, az-Zajjaj,
  "Abu Ishaq" in al-Qurtubi); at-Tabari's objection and slave-sale analogy.
- Ashaddu hubban: al-Baghawi and Qatada with 29:65, Ibn Kathir, as-Sa'di,
  Ma'arif; "loved them first" with 5:54 as al-Qurtubi's/al-Baghawi's qila.
- Yara/tara with the qurra' as each mufassir names them, Ibn 'Amir's
  yurawna; at-Tabari's choice of tara and his reason.
- Unstated answer of law: al-Baghawi, at-Tabari, Abu 'Ubayd/an-Nahhas vs
  Muhammad ibn Yazid/al-Akhfash, az-Zuhri and Qatada, Muyassar, Ibn Kathir.
- Licensing sentence in both languages. 2:166-167 one short section.
Hadith: Muslim 43 (Anas, sweetness of faith) quoted whole, marked as not
attached to the verse. Ibn Kathir's Ibn Mas'ud hadith pointed to 2:22,
not re-quoted. Asbab: none.
Dropped from the old article: the wealth/approval list of rivals put in
the commentators' mouths, 3:31, "source of every blessing", the audit
questions. Corrected: it silently took one side of the ka-hubbi dispute.
