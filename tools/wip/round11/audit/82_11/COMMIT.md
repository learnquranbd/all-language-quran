Card and 8-section bilingual article for 82:11: kiraman katibin, two
Arabic words describing the hafizin of 82:10. 82:10 and 82:12 as
context only; 82:13-16 left for their own entries.

Sources: Tabari, Ibn Kathir (ar), Muyassar, Qurtubi, Sa'di, Baghawi.
Tabari's 82:11 comment sits in the API's 82:12 slot (fetched once).
Ma'arif group covers only 82:6-8; IK en wrong group; neither named.
- Kiraman: noble with Allah (Tabari, Baghawi, Muyassar); Qurtubi
  "ay 'alayya" and 80:16; IK: so do not meet them with foul deeds;
  Sa'di: honour, esteem and respect them.
- Katibin: deeds (Tabari), all deeds (IK), words and deeds (Baghawi),
  plus acts of hearts and limbs (Sa'di), deeds and secrets (Muyassar),
  what you say and mean (Ayyub via Tabari). Spread kept, none chosen.
- Sufyan via Qurtubi on knowing intention (musk / stench), no chain.
- Qurtubi's unnamed disagreement on recorders over disbelievers
  (55:41 vs 82:9-12, 69:25, 84:10) and his right/left objection kept.
- Qurtubi points to 50:18; shipped 50:16 article cross-referenced.
Licensing sentence in both languages.

own chain notes (Hafs layyin; Tammam weakened, accused by Ibn Hibban;
Sallam layyin), wording not quoted. Qurtubi's "ruwiya" reports noted
as unsourced. No asbab.
