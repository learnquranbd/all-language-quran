Replaces the 732-word, 6-section article with a 1,731-word, 8-section deep article, built only on tafsir fetched for 57:22 and 57:23 (shipped card kept).

Sources: at-Tabari, Ibn Kathir (ar; en abridged as cross-check), al-Qurtubi,
as-Sa'di, al-Baghawi, al-Muyassar, Ma'arif al-Qur'an, all fetched for 57:22-23.

Placement: Ma'arif's two causes of heedlessness; al-Qurtubi's reported link to
jihad and guarding wealth; one line each to 57:20, 57:21, 57:24.
Calamity in the earth and in the self: at-Tabari, Qatada, Muqatil, Ibn Hayyan,
Ibn Jurayj, al-Baghawi (illness, loss of children), Muyassar, Ma'arif; Ibn
'Abbas's religion-and-world report; as-Sa'di's good and bad alike.
Fi kitab: umm al-kitab (at-Tabari) and al-lawh al-mahfuz (five others); the
"already settled" reports (Ibn 'Abbas, ad-Dahhak, Ibn Zayd), the Pen, al-Hasan.
Nabra'aha pronoun kept as a disagreement: souls, earth, calamity, all, creation
(Ibn Kathir's preference given as his); Ibn 'Abbas reported both ways.
57:23: Ibn Kathir's purpose line; as-Sa'di; the two readings of atakum with
at-Tabari's judgement. Forbidden joy as farah batar (Muyassar, as-Sa'di), Ibn
Kathir's boasting, al-Qurtubi's limit; the patience/thanks saying given as
'Ikrimah's (Ibn Kathir, al-Baghawi) and as Ibn 'Abbas's (at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi).
Mukhtal fakhur by name; as-Sa'di with 39:49; Muyassar's link to 57:24.

Hadith: Tirmidhi 2156 (decrees fifty thousand years before the heavens), quoted
whole, hasan sahih gharib per the fetched text, attached by Ibn Kathir;
Muslim 2653's Throne addition noted. Asbab: none in the sources.

Dropped: Qadariyya line, leaving-medicine report, ungraded Ibn Mas'ud hadith.
Dropped from the old article: 64:11, 9:51, 2:155-156, the Suhayb and Ibrahim
hadiths, the unsourced means section, and "asa = grief protesting the decree"
(the tafsirs gloss ta'saw simply as grieve).
