Card and 8-section bilingual article for 44:17-18: wa-laqad fatanna
qablahum qawma Fir'awn wa ja'ahum rasulun karim, an addu ilayya
'ibada-llah, inni lakum rasulun amin. 44:13-16 as the lead-in (Sa'di:
the deniers had predecessors); 44:19 and 44:24 named in one line only.

Sources: Tabari, Qurtubi, Sa'di, Ibn Kathir (ar + en), Muyassar, Baghawi.
Ma'arif's grouped entry covers only the smoke; nothing on 44:17-18.
- fatanna: Tabari/Muyassar (tested and tried), Baghawi (balawna),
  Qurtubi (trial = the command to obey, by Musa's sending); Qurtubi's
  unnamed "punished by drowning" with the order reversed, kept beside it.
- rasul karim: Tabari (honoured with Allah; possibly noble among his
  people), Qurtubi (people / character / al-Farra': chosen for prophethood
  and speech), Baghawi, Sa'di. Presented as layers.
- 'ibada-llah kept as a disagreement: object (Mujahid, Qatada, Ibn Zayd;
  Tabari's preference, reported as his) vs vocative (unnamed in Tabari;
  Ibn Abbas in Qurtubi), plus Qurtubi's unnamed "render your hearing".
  The Ibn Abbas report placed differently by Tabari and Qurtubi; both
  reported, not settled.
- rasul amin: Tabari, Baghawi, Muyassar, Ibn Kathir (ma'mun), Qurtubi
  (incl. "trustworthy over what I ask of you"), Sa'di.
- 20:47 (Ibn Kathir, Ibn Zayd) and 26:17 (Tabari) cited only; wording
  checked in verse-base.json. 7:105 not cited by a source; not used.
- Licensing sentence EN+BN. No present-day people named.
Hadith: none attached to 44:17-18 in any fetched tafsir; said in one sentence.
Asbab: none in the sources; not used.
Dropped: "Copts of Egypt" (deliberate); Makkah as addressee (no source
names it); Ibn Kathir (en) material on 44:19-26.
