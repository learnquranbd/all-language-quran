# SOURCES — 37:105

## Tafsir fetched (all pre-fetched in PREP.md; none re-fetched)

- t14 Ibn Kathir (ar): used — §5 "thus We turn hardships... relief and a way out" + cross-ref to
  65:2-3, rest cited verbatim: هكذا نصرف عمن أطاعنا المكاره والشدائد، ونجعل لهم من أمرهم فرجا
  ومخرجا، كقوله تعالى: ومن يتق الله يجعل له مخرجا؛ and §6 (naskh/usul al-fiqh): وقد استدل بهذه
  الآية والقصة جماعة من علماء الأصول على صحة النسخ قبل التمكن من الفعل، خلافا لطائفة من المعتزلة...
  وإنما كان المقصود من شرعه أولا إثابة الخليل على الصبر على ذبح ولده وعزمه على ذلك.
- t15 at-Tabari: used — §1 (gloss of "the vision" = the command of 37:102: الرؤيا التي أريناكها في
  منامك بأمرناك بذبح ابنك) and §7 (widened address: إنا كما جزيناك بطاعتنا يا إبراهيم، كذلك نجزي
  الذين أحسنوا، وأطاعوا أمرنا، وعملوا في رضانا).
- t16 al-Muyassar (grouped 37:104-105): used — §1 (قد فعلتَ ما أُمرت به وصَدَّقْتَ رؤياك) and §7

- t90 al-Qurtubi: used — §7 (نجزيهم بالخلاص من الشدائد في الدنيا والآخرة).
- t91 as-Sa'di: used — §2 (قد فعلت ما أمرت به، فإنك وطَّنت نفسك على ذلك، وفعلت كل سبب، ولم يبق إلا
  إمرار السكين على حلقه) and §8 (المحسنين = المقدمين رضانا على شهوات أنفسهم).
- t94 al-Baghawi: used — §4 (تم الكلام هاهنا؛ ثم ابتدأ... إنا كما عفونا إبراهيم عن ذبح ولده نجزي من

- t168 Ma'arif al-Qur'an (en, grouped 37:105-106): used — §5 ("when a servant of Allah bows down
  before the command of Allah... remains safe... written down in his book of deeds").
- t169 Ibn Kathir (en, abridged, grouped 37:99-108): used — §3 narrative (Ibn Abbas report: the

  NOT used: the entire Isma'il/Ishaq identity debate carried in this file (per brief, that
  question belongs to 37:112 and was deliberately kept out of this article — see note below).

## Hadith

- Sahih Bukhari, Dar-us-Salam Hadith 660 (= USC-MSA Vol 1, Book 11, Hadith 629), fetched via
  quranx.com/hadith/Bukhari/DarusSalam/Hadith-660. Narrator: Abu Huraira. First words: "The
  Prophet (ﷺ) said, 'Allah will give shade, to seven, on the Day when there will be no shade but
  His...'" No grading beyond its presence in Sahih Bukhari; none claimed or upgraded. Used in §8,
  explicitly marked in the article's own prose as a general narration not drawn from this story
  and not mentioning Ibrahim (AS), cited only because it illustrates as-Sa'di's definition of
  al-muhsinin (putting Allah's pleasure ahead of one's own desire).

## Asbab an-nuzul

Not applicable / not used. This is a narrative verse continuing the Ibrahim (AS) sacrifice
story (37:99-111); no tafsir fetched proposed a separate occasion of revelation for this verse,
so no asbab claim is made anywhere in the article.

## Sensitive point handled per brief

The son's name (Isma'il, per the Ibn Abbas report in t169) was deliberately dropped from the
article's narrative in §3, even though the source text names him, because the brief marks the
Isma'il/Ishaq identity question as belonging properly to 37:112 and instructs that it not be
imported into 37:105. The article instead says plainly that this verse and the one before it do
not name the son, and points to 37:112 without taking a position.

## Dropped / not used

- The Ibn Kathir (en) report on the ram's horns being kept and later hung in the Ka'bah
  (Safiyyah bint Shaybah / 'Uthman bin Talhah narration recorded by Imam Ahmad) — dropped. It is
  offered in the source as evidence for the Isma'il identification, which this article avoids;
  using the anecdote without that framing would have been pointless, so it was cut entirely.
- Ibn Ishaq's linguistic argument (from 11:71) for why the sacrificed son must have been Isma'il
  — dropped for the same reason.
- No claim was made anywhere about which son was present; every sentence describing the scene
  uses "the son," "his son," or "the boy."
