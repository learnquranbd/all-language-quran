- **Ma'arif al-Qur'an (168, en)**: who "some of the Jews called the Prophet Uzayr ... the son of God, as did the Christians ... while the mushrikin of Makkah considered the angels to be the daughters of God"; "He does not need any instruments or helpers -- all that He does is to say, 'Be'"; causes "If Allah has chosen to assign certain tasks to certain angels ... or has chosen to employ causes, materials or physical forces ... ## Disagreements kept (both sides, no position)
