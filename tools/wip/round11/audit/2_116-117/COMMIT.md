Card and 9-section bilingual article for 2:116-117: the claim that Allah
took a son, answered by subhanahu, qanitun, Badi' and kun fa-yakun.
Target 2:117 widened to its claim in 2:116; 2:115 and 2:118 left free.

Sources: Tabari, Qurtubi, Ibn Kathir (ar + en abridged), Sa'di, Baghawi,
Muyassar, Ma'arif on 2:116 and 2:117. Slot check: all slots on target.
- Badi' as originating with no prior model (all seven); Tabari's mubdi'
  and its root note; ar-Rabi' and as-Suddi via Tabari.
- 'Isa (AS) made without a father as Badi' and kun (Tabari, approved by
  Ibn Kathir; 3:59 from Ibn Kathir; Qurtubi's amr = 'Isa).
- Kun: once, as He willed (Ibn Kathir: 36:82, 16:40, 54:50); nothing
  resists Him (Sa'di); no instruments or helpers (Ma'arif); neither before
  nor after His command (Tabari). No discussion of the nature of the speech;
  Ma'arif's warning against meddling quoted instead.
Disagreements kept: who made the claim (Tabari: Christians, via 2:114;
Baghawi, Muyassar, Sa'di, Ma'arif: Jews, Christians, Arab polytheists;
Ibn Kathir and Qurtubi in between); qanitun general (Tabari) vs restricted
(Muqatil; Ibn Abbas per Baghawi).
Licensing sentence in both languages. Baghawi's "revealed about" given
as his statement, not as established asbab.
Hadith quoted whole: Bukhari 4482 (placed under the verse by al-Bukhari);
Bukhari 6099, marked general.
Application: trust in His power, kun tied to what He decrees and not to
wishes; means used, heart on Him (Ma'arif).
Dropped: Ibn Kathir's imprecation, the weak qunut narration, the bid'ah
digressions, the kalam footnotes, Ibn Mardawayh's variant.
