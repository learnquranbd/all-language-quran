Assigned 4:124, widened to 4:123-124 (not by your wishes nor the People of the Book's;
whoever does wrong is repaid; whoever does righteous deeds, male or female, while a
believer, is not wronged a naqir). 4:122 and 4:119-120 for placement only; 4:125 ships.
New card and a 1,750-word, 8-section article.

Sources: at-Tabari, Ibn Kathir (ar), al-Qurtubi, as-Sa'di, al-Baghawi, al-Muyassar on
4:123 and 4:124, all slots on target.
Occasion given only as reported: Masruq, Qatadah and as-Suddi (the boast between Muslims
and the People of the Book, answered on both sides), Masruq's "we and you are equal" for
4:124 (at-Tabari, al-Baghawi). Licensing line for Jews, Christians and Muslims.
Kept as differences, no position:
- whom "your wishes" addresses: Muslims and People of the Book (Masruq, Qatadah, as-Suddi)
  vs Quraysh beside Jews and Christians (Mujahid, at-Tabari's choice and reason).
- su': the disbeliever (al-Hasan), shirk (Ibn Abbas via Ali ibn Abi Talhah, Sa'id ibn
  Jubayr) vs every sin (Ubayy, 'A'ishah; at-Tabari; Ibn Kathir "correct").
- naqir on the stone's back (Ibn Kathir, Mujahid) vs its middle ('Atiyyah).
at-Tabari's 4:31 reconciliation, his partitive "min", as-Sa'di on amani, expiation ranks
and repentance; al-Qurtubi on the faith condition and the two readings of yudkhalun.
Hadith: Muslim 2574 (Abu Hurayrah, "even the thorn that pricks him"), whole, from the
Arabic. Tirmidhi 3039 (Abu Bakr's distress) named only with at-Tirmidhi's own grading
(gharib, chain criticised, no sound route), nothing built on it.
Dropped: Ahmad's Abu Bakr ibn Abi Zuhayr narration (unnamed link, not confirmable),
al-Kalbi's ten-for-one report, the Ibn az-Zubayr reports, general expiation hadiths.
