Replaces the 723-word, 6-section article with a 1,780-word, 8-section article
built only from the eight tafsir files fetched for 27:62.

Sources: at-Tabari and al-Muyassar read amman against 27:59's challenge (is
what you associate better, or He who answers...). Al-mudtarr glossed via
Ma'arif (idtirar), al-Baghawi/al-Muyassar (al-makrub), as-Sa'di, and al-Qurtubi's
list: Ibn Abbas, as-Suddi, Dhu an-Nun, Abu Ja'far and Abu 'Uthman an-Naysaburi
(the bankrupt), Sahl (no prior obedience to plead). Al-Qurtubi's reason the
call is answered: ikhlas, from believer or disbeliever (10:22, 29:65); Ibn
Kathir's 17:67 and 16:53. Yakshifu-s-su': ad-durr (Ibn Jurayj in at-Tabari,
al-Baghawi, al-Qurtubi), al-jawr (al-Kalbi), as-Sa'di's three words.
Khulafa' kept as a list with no choice: generations (Ibn Kathir with 6:133,
6:165, 2:30; al-Baghawi; al-Qurtubi), successors of those before (al-Muyassar,
as-Sa'di), your children (an-Naqqash, al-Baghawi qila), the jinn (al-Baghawi
qila), successors of the disbelievers (al-Kalbi). Qira'a ya'/ta' from
al-Baghawi and al-Qurtubi (Abu 'Amr, Hisham, Ya'qub; Abu Hatim's choice).

Hadith: Abu Dawud 5090 (du'a of the distressed, attached by al-Qurtubi via
at-Tayalisi), Abu Dawud 4084 (Jabir ibn Sulaym, attached by Ibn Kathir via
Ahmad), Tirmidhi 1905 (three answered du'as; "sahih" is al-Qurtubi's word,
at-Tirmidhi grades none there). Tawus and Malik ibn Dinar "ask Him yourself".

Dropped from the old article: the 2:173 and 40:60 parallels, the "two scales"
and "retrieval" claims (unsourced). Dropped: al-Ajurri's Abu Dharr report,
the Shihab and Muslim citations, Ibn 'Asakir's stories. Licensing sentence
added where as-Sa'di addresses the mushrikun. Neighbours 27:19, 27:40, 27:52,
27:58 one line each.
