Card plus 8-section bilingual article on the fish swallowing Yunus (AS)
and the single word muleem.

Sources gave:
- at-Tabari: faltaqama = ibtala'a (form from l-q-m); muleem = muktasib
  al-lawm, the one who earned blame whether or not blamed aloud, set
  against maloom; Labid's line. Glosses mudhnib (Mujahid, Ibn Zayd) and
  "fi sun'ihi" (Qatada).
- al-Qurtubi: two readings, ata bima yulamu alayh and al-mu'ib.
- as-Sa'di: the blame named as mughadabatuhu li-rabbih (leaving in anger
  without leave).
- al-Baghawi, al-Muyassar: verb = ibtala'a.
- Ibn Kathir: fish from the Green Sea under command, no flesh cut / no
  bone broken; Yunus thought he died, moved, prayed in the belly, "a
  place of worship no one reached."

Kept open as a disagreement (Ibn Kathir): time in the fish — three days
(Qatada) / a week (Ja'far as-Sadiq) / forty days (Abu Malik) / forenoon
to evening (ash-Sha'bi via Mujahid); wa Allahu a'lam, no pick.

Qur'an's own parallels used: 21:87 (dhun-nun, his cry "inni kuntu mina
az-zalimin") and 68:48-50 (don't be like the companion of the fish, yet
"his Lord chose him"). 37:143 touched only as "the next verse."

Hadith: Sahih al-Bukhari 3395 (Ibn Abbas), "One should not say that I am
better than Yunus bin Matta," quoted whole, grading = in al-Bukhari's
Sahih, as the honour counterweight.

Dropped: the tark-al-awla label (not in any fetched tafsir) — honour and
non-diminishment carried by the Qur'anic parallels and the hadith
instead; Umayya's poetry line; the Mediterranean identification left as
"the Green Sea." Article never calls the prophet's act a sin in its own
voice; every blame-gloss is attributed as a reading of muleem.
