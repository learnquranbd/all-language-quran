Replaces the 711-word, 5-section article with a 1,748-word, 9-section
one, from at-Tabari, Ibn Kathir (ar and en), al-Qurtubi, as-Sa'di,
al-Baghawi, al-Muyassar and Ma'arif on 6:54. All slots checked. The
shipped card is kept.
Setting: as-Sa'di's link from 6:52; Sa'd ibn Abi Waqqas's "we were six"
(Muslim 2413) quoted whole and marked as the occasion of 6:52.
Who is meant: at-Tabari's three views (the poor believers, the askers
about grave sins via Mahan, the advisers per 'Ikrimah and Ibn Zayd)
and his grammatical preference; names from 'Ata' and Ibn 'Abbas;
Ma'arif's "no contradiction".
Salam: Ibn Kathir and the Muyassar (return the greeting) against
'Ikrimah via al-Baghawi and al-Qurtubi (he greeted first); the
Prophet's salam or Allah's (al-Qurtubi, Ma'arif); al-Qurtubi's and
at-Tabari's glosses; Abu al-'Aliyah reciting the verse to visitors.
Rank: 'A'idh ibn 'Amr's report (Muslim 2504) via al-Qurtubi, whole.
Kataba: awjaba, qada, al-Qurtubi's two views; 6:12; "My mercy prevails
over My anger" in al-Bukhari's wording (3194), via Ibn Kathir.
Bi-jahalah: Mujahid, al-Qurtubi, al-Baghawi's three views against the
every-sinner reading (salaf, 'Ikrimah, Mujahid, Muyassar); 4:17, 16:119.
Taba wa aslaha: five commentators; Ma'arif's restitution list.
Readings: annahu/innahu with qurra' per al-Qurtubi, al-Baghawi and
at-Tabari, and at-Tabari's preference. 6:55 one line.
Licensing lines in both languages (Quraysh idolaters; the named men).
Dropped: Ibn Marduyah's marfu', Salman's hundred mercies, Ma'arif's
Abu Talib/'Umar account (no chain), the Mu'adh hadith.
Corrected from the shipped article: "leading men of Quraysh ... before
they would sit" (Muslim says the idolaters), "instructed to speak
first" stated as fact, "nowhere else has He written upon Himself", and
two unsourced own-voice glosses on jahalah and islah.
