## Asbab
Not presented as an established occasion of 6:54. The views on who is meant are given as at-Tabari's three, with names from al-Baghawi ('Ata'), al-Qurtubi (Ibn 'Abbas) and the Mahan reports (at-Tabari). The Sa'd report (Muslim 2413) is marked as the occasion of 6:52. Licensing sentence in both languages for the idolaters of Quraysh (§1.3) and for the named men of Muslim 2504 (§4.2).

## Disagreements kept
- Who greets first: Ibn Kathir and the Muyassar (return their greeting) vs 'Ikrimah via al-Baghawi and al-Qurtubi (the Prophet greeted them first).
- Whose salam: the Prophet's own vs Allah's conveyed (al-Qurtubi, Ma'arif).
- Who is meant: at-Tabari's three views and his preference.
- Bi-jahalah: unknowing / without intent (Mujahid's first line, al-Qurtubi's first gloss) vs every sinner is jahil (some salaf via Ibn Kathir, 'Ikrimah, Mujahid's other line, Muyassar).
- Readings: fath both (Ibn 'Amir, 'Asim; + Ya'qub in al-Baghawi), fath/kasr (Nafi' in al-Qurtubi; people of Medina in al-Baghawi), kasr both (the rest); at-Tabari prefers kasr both.
- Not stated in the article, for the orchestrator: al-Baghawi reports 'Ikrimah saying the verse is about those the Prophet was forbidden to expel, while at-Tabari lists 'Ikrimah under the third view (believers who advised expelling them). The article cites 'Ikrimah via at-Tabari for the third view and via al-Baghawi only for the greeting.
