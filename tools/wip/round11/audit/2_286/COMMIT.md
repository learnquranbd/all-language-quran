Replaces the 913-word, 6-section article with a 1,764-word, 9-section
one, from at-Tabari, Ibn Kathir, al-Qurtubi, al-Baghawi, al-Muyassar
and Ma'arif on 2:286. The shipped card is kept.

Wus' glossed by at-Tabari (with Ibn 'Abbas: 22:78, 2:185, 64:16),
as-Suddi and al-Qurtubi (= taqah) vs Sufyan in al-Baghawi (below taqah).
Kasaba/iktasaba: al-Qurtubi's prepositions and stylistic variation,
with Ibn 'Atiyyah's intensive-form reading credited to him.
Forgetting: at-Tabari's two kinds; error: 'Ata' vs the majority in
al-Baghawi; al-Qurtubi's three classes of legal consequence.
Isr as covenant (at-Tabari and his list) vs transformation, sin without
repentance, the harsh matter; "those before us" as the tafsirs name them,
with a non-licensing line in both languages. Ibn Kathir's two glosses
separate isr from "what we cannot bear"; the early named weights.
'Afw/maghfirah/rahmah: Ibn Kathir vs at-Tabari on where forgiveness
sits. Mawla and the disbelieving people, with a no-warrant line.
Muslim 125 and 126 quoted for the replies only ("Yes", "I have done
so"), closing the narrations quoted under 2:284. Sa'id ibn Jubayr vs
ad-Dahhak on whom the answer was for, kept as a difference.
Bukhari 5009 (Muslim 808) on the two verses at night, with al-Qurtubi's
two readings of "suffice"; Muslim 806 on the two lights, quoted whole.

Dropped: Ibn Majah 2045 (no grading of his own on the page), the Umm
ad-Darda' and "hanifiyyah" reports, al-Qurtubi's unconfirmed virtue
reports. Muslim 173 was confirmed but cut for length.
Corrected from the old article: "rahmah after the debt is settled" and
"Allah could have used a word for maximum effort" (no source), plus the
unsourced fiqh-of-concession and bedtime-habit claims.
