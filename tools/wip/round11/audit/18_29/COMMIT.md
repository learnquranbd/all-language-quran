Card and deep bilingual article for "say: the truth is from your Lord;
whoever wills, let him believe, and whoever wills, let him disbelieve",
the enclosing walls of the Fire, water like al-muhl, and the evil resting place.

Sources (all pre-fetched, none re-fetched):
- Addressees: said to "those whose hearts We made heedless" of 18:28, with
  "I will not drive away the believers for your whims" (Tabari, Baghawi, Qurtubi).
- "Fa-man sha'a ... fa-l-yakfur" reported as threat, not permission, with
  names: Ibn Kathir, Tabari (with Mujahid and Ibn Zayd, 41:40), Baghawi,
  Qurtubi, Sa'di; Sa'di also "not compelled to faith", citing 2:256.
  Ibn Abbas's "whom Allah willed faith for" line (Tabari, Baghawi, 76:30)
  held beside Sa'di's real human will; not a rival position.
- "Zalimin": the disbelievers (Tabari, Ibn Zayd, Ibn Kathir, Baghawi,
  Qurtubi) vs Sa'di's wider "disbelief, sin and disobedience"; kept.
- Suradiq: wall / tent-enclosure / al-Kalbi's neck of fire / smoke (77:30).
- Al-muhl: Tabari's four readings, judged close by Tabari, Ibn Kathir, Qurtubi.
- Murtafaq: Tabari's "place to lean" vs Mujahid's "gathering", with
  Tabari's rejoinder; kept as a disagreement. Paired with 18:31.
- Ma'arif grouped 18:28-30, all on 18:28's background: pointer only.
  2:256 not shipped; 10:99 pointed to instead.

Hadith: no fetched tafsir attaches a hadith graded sound. The muhl narration
(Tirmidhi 2581) and the four-walls narration (2584) reported only with
Tirmidhi's own remark on Rishdin ibn Sa'd; wording not quoted. Qurtubi's
"hasan sahih gharib" for the latter conflicts with the fetched page; not used.

Dropped: "the sea is Jahannam" report; graphic reports (Sa'id ibn Jubayr,
Abu Umama); Sa'di's aside on fighting. Licensing sentence in both languages.
Omitted by choice: as-Sa'di's clause that the verse does not mean abandoning
fighting the disbelievers, left out as off-verse and open to misreading; his
point that none is compelled to faith is kept. (In the ledger.)
