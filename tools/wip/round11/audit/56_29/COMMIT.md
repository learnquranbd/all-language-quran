Card and 8-section bilingual article (1,768 EN words) on 56:29: wa-talhin
mandud. 56:27-28 frame only; 56:30-34 named and left; no shipped pointer.

Sources: at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi, al-Baghawi, Ibn Kathir (ar), as-Sa'di,
Ma'arif; al-Muyassar (group 56:27-34) and Ibn Kathir en (group 56:27-36)
used only for their 56:29 lines.
- talh = banana: Companions/Followers per at-Tabari (Ibn Abbas, Ali via
  al-Kalbi, Mujahid "your bananas", Ata', Qasama, Qatada, Ibn Zayd with
  the Yemen usage); Ibn Kathir via Ibn Abi Hatim (Abu Sa'id, Ibn Abbas,
  Abu Hurayra, al-Hasan, Ikrima, Qasama, Qatada, Abu Hazra); most
  commentators (al-Qurtubi, al-Baghawi); Muyassar; Ma'arif.
- talh = great thorny tree of the Arabs: Abu Ubayda (at-Tabari), with
  al-Farra' (al-Qurtubi, al-Baghawi); Hijaz 'idah tree (Ibn Kathir);
  desert tree with fruit-stacked branches (as-Sa'di); az-Zajjaj:
  thornless in Paradise, like umm ghaylan with fragrant blossom.
- Kept as disagreements: "sweeter than honey" (Ibn Abbas per Ibn Kathir /
  as-Suddi per al-Qurtubi); al-Hasan (banana per Ibn Kathir / shade tree
  per al-Qurtubi, al-Baghawi); Ibn Kathir's "Ibn Jarir gave no other
  view" beside at-Tabari's Abu Ubayda line. No Tabari preference stated.
- Ali's "wa tal'in mandud" with chains as given (at-Tabari, al-Baghawi,
  al-Qurtubi with al-Qushayri and Abu Bakr al-Anbari, Ibn Kathir via a
  shaykh of Hamdan, al-Jawhari); no grading added.
- Mujahid's Wajj attributed, not located. Site EN "[banana]" flagged as
  interpretive; BN follows banana. mandud glosses all attributed.
Hadith: none. Ibn Kathir en's 'Utbah talh-thorn report has no collector
or grading in the fetched text; not quoted. Asbab: none.
Dropped: Wajj's location, acacia/botany, tal' = spathe gloss.
