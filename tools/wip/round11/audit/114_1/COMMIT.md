Card and 8-section bilingual article on qul a'udhu bi-rabbi an-nas, four
words; 114:2 named as the verse that follows, 114:2-6 not treated.

Sources: at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi, al-Baghawi, Ibn Kathir (ar + en
abridged), as-Sa'di, Muyassar, Ma'arif. Slot check clean: every
Arabic slot discusses 114:1.
- qul: to the Prophet ﷺ (Tabari "ya Muhammad", Muyassar "ayyuha
  ar-rasul"); command to al-musta'idh, whoever seeks refuge (Ibn Kathir).
- a'udhu: astajir (Tabari); a'udhu wa a'tasim (Muyassar); yasta'in,
  yasta'idh, ya'tasim, first verb bracketed (Sa'di).
- rabb: Owner and mender of affairs (Qurtubi); nurturer (Ma'arif);
  rububiyya, all created, owned, servants (Ibn Kathir, Sa'di).
- Why people named: venerated / refuge from their evil (Qurtubi, two
  reasons); against venerating people (Tabari, with 114:2); vs Lord of
  the daybreak, 113:1 (Ma'arif from Mazhari/Baidawi). None ranked.
- Three names: rububiyya, mulk, ilahiyya (Ibn Kathir); lordship and
  divinity as ground of refuge (Sa'di).
Makkan (Ibn Kathir en) vs Madinan (Baghawi) kept as a difference.
Hadith: Tirmidhi 2902 ('Uqba, verses without like) quoted whole, at-
Tirmidhi's hasan sahih from the page; Muslim 814 confirmed, not quoted.
Occasion: none in any fetched tafsir for 114:1; not narrated.
Licensing sentence in both languages.
Dropped: Ibn Kathir's qarin, Safiyya, Abu Ya'la and Ahmad reports and
waswas material (114:4-6); 6:112; recitation hadiths not cited.
