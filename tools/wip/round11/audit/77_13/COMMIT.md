Card and 8-section bilingual article for 77:13. This key owns the name
yawm al-fasl and the deferral it answers (77:12); 77:8-11 and the 77:15
refrain appear only as frame; 77:1 and 77:8 are left to their own drafts.

Sources: Tabari, Qurtubi, Ibn Kathir (ar), Sa'di, Baghawi, Muyassar
(grouped 77:8-15), Ma'arif (grouped 77:11-14); frame fetched for 77:11,
77:12 and 77:14. Ibn Kathir en was the wrong group (77:1-10), unused.
- al-fasl: judgement between creatures, taking for the wronged from the
  wrongdoer (Tabari; Ibn 'Abbas in Baghawi; Sa'di "some against others"
  plus each one's own reckoning) and separation by deeds to the Garden
  and the Fire (Qatada in Tabari "and", in Qurtubi "or"). Tabari treats
  the two as one; presented together, wording difference noted.
- Deferral: question of ta'zim (Tabari, Qurtubi, Sa'di, Baghawi); the
  messengers and their matter held until the Hour, read with 14:47 and
  14:48 (Ibn Kathir); time set for deciding between messengers and
  nations (Muyassar, Sa'di).
- uqqitat: gathered (Ibn 'Abbas), deferred (Mujahid), promised
  (Ibrahim), 5:109 (Ibn Zayd); Qurtubi's two views with his preference;
  Tabari's three readings, one meaning; Zamakhshari via Ma'arif.
- Kept: addressee of 77:14, the Prophet (Tabari) vs the human being
  (Muyassar), unranked. 77:38 cited as the surah's own return to the name.
Hadith: none attached in any fetched tafsir; said in one sentence.
Asbab: none in the sources.
Licensing sentence in EN and BN on the deniers and the former peoples.
Dropped: Qurtubi's unsourced "forty years" narration; wayl as a valley
in Jahannam (not in any fetched source); 37:21, 44:40, 78:17 uncited.
