# SOURCES LEDGER: 3:10-11

Assigned 3:10. Widened to 3:10-11 (`prep.js 3:10-11` run; files in this folder).
Why: 3:11 opens with ka-da'bi, a comparison that has no head without 3:10. At-Tabari
paraphrases the two as one sentence, al-Baghawi reports "nazm al-ayah" across both, Ibn
Kathir (en) and Ma'arif group 3:10-11. 3:9 belongs to the shipped 3:8 prayer. 3:12-13
(the warning and the two armies at Badr) left free: Ma'arif treats 3:10-11 only as
the prelude to 3:12, and no tafsir binds Badr into 3:10-11.
The orchestrator's earlier `audit/3_10/` folder (prep for 3:10 alone) is left in place.
Its 3:10 files are the same API texts as the copies here.
