Assigned 3:10, widened to 3:10-11 (wealth and children will not avail those
who disbelieve against Allah; "like the da'b of Pharaoh's people"). 3:9 stays
with the shipped 3:8 prayer; 3:12-13 (the warning and Badr) left free. New card
and a 1,779-word, 9-section article centred on what a person leans on not
standing in for Allah, kept apart from 3:14-15's love of worldly things.

Sources: at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi, Ibn Kathir (ar + en abridged), as-Sa'di,
al-Baghawi, al-Muyassar, Ma'arif on 3:10 and 3:11 (all slots on target).
- Da'b with names: at-Tabari's root (persisting in toil) and Imru' al-Qays;
  ar-Rabi', ad-Dahhak, Ibn 'Abbas, 'Ikrima, Mujahid, Ibn Zayd (40:31), as-Suddi;
  al-Baghawi's 'Ata', al-Kisa'i, Abu 'Ubayda, al-Akhfash, an-Nadr b. Shumayl;
  Ibn Kathir's "the wordings are close".
- Waqud as firewood (at-Tabari, Ibn Kathir with 21:98, as-Sa'di); al-Qurtubi on
  the wuqud reading. Register kept sober.
- As-Sa'di on wealth used to fend off misfortune (34:35, 34:37); "owners of
  wealth and armies" as the link from 3:11 back to 3:10.
- Licensing sentence in both languages (Pharaoh's people, earlier nations, those
  who disbelieve; nothing against the wealthy or anyone's children).
Disagreements kept: min Allah as "from Allah's punishment" (al-Kalbi) or "with
Allah" (Abu 'Ubayda); at-Tabari's addressees vs Ibn Kathir's general reading;
what the kaf attaches to (al-Qurtubi's five views, al-Farra' and an-Nahhas, his
preference for the first) vs at-Tabari's one-sentence paraphrase joining it to
"will not avail"; al-Baghawi's habit-of-denial gloss with the same "it is said".
Hadith: Bukhari 6514 (three follow the dead), marked general, not attached.
The ungraded Ibn Abi Hatim / Ibn al-Mubarak report on 3:10 named as not relied
on, content not given. No asbab.
Note: Ibn Kathir (en) prints 9:55; his Arabic quotes 9:85, which is cited.
Dropped: qira'at of yughni, Ibn 'Arafa/al-Azhari, the Anfal da'b note.
