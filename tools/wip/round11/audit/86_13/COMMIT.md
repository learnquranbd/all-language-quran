Card and 8-section bilingual article for 86:13: innahu la-qawlun
faslun, three words, the answer to the oaths of 86:11-12 (Baghawi:
jawab al-qasam; Qurtubi). 86:14 named only as the verse that follows;
86:15-17 left to their entries.

Sources: Tabari, Ibn Kathir (ar + en abridged), Qurtubi, Sa'di,
Baghawi, Muyassar, Ma'arif. All slots checked, all on 86:13.
- -hu: the Qur'an (Sa'di, Baghawi, Qurtubi, Muyassar, Ma'arif); "this
  statement and this report" (Tabari); the warning of 86:8-9 (Qurtubi,
  wa-qila). None chosen.
- fasl: separates truth from falsehood (Tabari, Qurtubi, Baghawi,
  Muyassar, Ma'arif); haqq (Ibn 'Abbas); hukm (Qatadah per Tabari);
  hukm 'adl (unnamed, Ibn Kathir); haqq wa sidq bayyin wadih (Sa'di);
  haqq wa jidd (Baghawi). Qatadah given as haqq by Ibn Kathir, as hukm
  by Tabari: kept as a conflict.
- raj'/sad' from Ibn Kathir en; r-j-' shared by 86:8 and 86:11 noted
  from the text, not attributed.
- Muyassar's oath ruling one sentence, cross-referenced to 85:1;
  shipped 86:5 cross-referenced by number.
Hadith: al-Harith from 'Ali (Qurtubi, Ma'arif) = Tirmidhi 2906, checked
on quranx; Tirmidhi: gharib, chain majhul, criticism of al-Harith.
~285 words in English, so named not quoted; marked not attached to the
verse. No sound hadith on 86:13.
Asbab: none. Dropped: 31:24 (86:17 ground), 18:1-2, 17:9, 39:23 (not
cited by a fetched tafsir).
Fix: section repeating 85:1's oath-ruling section replaced.
