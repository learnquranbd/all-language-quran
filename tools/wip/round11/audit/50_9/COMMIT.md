Card and 8-section bilingual article for 50:9: wa-nazzalna min as-sama'i
ma'an mubarakan fa-anbatna bihi jannatin wa-habba l-hasid. 50:6-8 as
context; 50:10-11 as the next-verse pointer only, not developed.

Sources: Tabari, Qurtubi, Ibn Kathir (ar; en abridgement), Sa'di,
Baghawi, Muyassar. Ma'arif fetched but its text covers only 50:6.
- Mubarak as glossed: beneficial (IK), many benefits (Muyassar), much
  blessing (Qurtubi), much good with the life of everything in it, the
  rain (Baghawi); Qurtubi: from the sky = from the clouds.
- Jannat: orchards of trees (Tabari, IK, Muyassar; IK en parks/gardens).
- Habb al-hasid: crops kept for grain and storage (IK); wheat and barley
  (Qatada via Tabari, ad-Dahhak via Qurtubi), wheat (Mujahid via Tabari),
  all grains (Tabari, Baghawi), qila every grain reaped, stored, eaten
  (Qurtubi).
- Sa'di's summary: power, wisdom, mercy, oneness; reviving earth as
  proof of reviving the dead, hence 50:11. IK en cites 41:39.
Kept as a disagreement: Basran implied-noun vs Kufan self-annexation
(al-Farra') for habb al-hasid, per Qurtubi; Tabari and Baghawi report
the views, none chosen. Tabari's example verified as 56:95.
Hadith: none attached by any fetched tafsir; stated in one sentence.
No asbab reported.
Dropped: cross-refs 6:99, 80:25-32, 6:141, 22:5 (not in any fetched
tafsir); IK en refs 67:3-4, 51:49, 40:57, 46:33; Sa'di's bracketed
[wal-hubb]; any agronomy or science.
