# SOURCES ledger — 26:66 (ثُمَّ أَغْرَقْنَا ٱلْءَاخَرِينَ — Then We drowned the others)

## Tafsir fetched (all from PREP.md, none re-fetched)

- **at-Tabari (15)** — NAMED. Identification + place/time of the drowning.
  Arabic clause: «ثم أغرقنا فرعون وقومه من القبط في البحر بعد أن أنجينا موسى منه ومن معه».
  Used in §2 (Pharaoh and his people of the Copts, drowned after Moses and his people were saved).

- **al-Qurtubi (90)** — NAMED. Gloss of al-akhirin.
  Arabic clause (whole entry): «يعني فرعون وقومه».
  Used in §2 (the others = Pharaoh and his people).

- **al-Baghawi (94)** — NAMED. Same gloss.
  Arabic clause: «( ثم أغرقنا الآخرين ) فرعون وقومه».
  Used in §2. (Sa'id ibn Jubayr note on the sea's tide NOT used — tangential to this verse; dropped, see below.)

- **as-Sa'di (91)** — NAMED. Completeness of the drowning.
  Arabic clause (whole entry): «ثُمَّ أَغْرَقْنَا الآخَرِينَ لم يتخلف منهم عن الغرق أحد».
  Used in §3 (not one of them held back from the drowning).

- **Ibn Kathir, ar (14)** — NAMED. The two-sided completeness.
  Arabic clause: «أنجينا موسى وبني إسرائيل ومن معهم على دينهم فلم يهلك منهم أحد، وأغرق فرعون وجنوده، فلم يبق منهم رجل إلا هلك».
  Used in §3 (total rescue / total ruin). The long Ibn Abi Hatim → Ibn Mas'ud narration (the sea "speaking", the horseman, 600,000 Copts, no greater blackness seen) is an athar Ibn Kathir relays; NOT used — vivid but not needed and not a sound attached hadith; noted here.

- **Ibn Kathir, en abridged (169)** — NAMED. Group tafsir 26:60–68.
  English used: "the sea divided into twelve paths, one for each of the tribes" (Ibn Abbas); "in it there were windows through which they could see one another, and the water was erected like walls" (as-Suddi); wind made the sea bed solid; "brought Fir'awn and his troops near to the sea"; saved all / drowned all; "this story with its wonders and tales of aid to the believing servants of Allah is definitive proof … of Allah's wisdom."
  Used in §4 (twelve paths, as-Suddi's windows, wind) and §5 (aid to believers = proof of wisdom) and §2 (the pursuing host).

- **al-Muyassar (16)** — NAMED. Sequence of crossing then closing.
  Arabic clause: «فاستمر البحر على انفلاقه حتى عبروا إلى البر، ثم أغرقنا فرعون ومن معه بإطباق البحر عليهم بعد أن دخلوا فيه متبعين موسى وقومه».
  Used in §4 (sea stayed parted until they crossed; then closed over Pharaoh; thumma = order).

- **Ma'arif al-Qur'an, en (168)** — FETCHED, NOT NAMED. Its substance is on 26:62 (the "with me is my Lord" moment; the cave-of-Thawr parallel; 9:40). It carries no reading on the drowning itself. Dropped to avoid diluting the 26:66 spine; noted here.

## Hadith

- **Sahih al-Bukhari 2004** (Ashura). URL fetched: https://quranx.com/hadith/Bukhari/DarusSalam/Hadith-2004
  First ten words (EN used): "The Prophet came to Medina and saw the Jews fasting…"
  Narrator Ibn Abbas. Grading: it is in Sahih al-Bukhari (the collection's own authentic standard); no separate grading upgraded.
  Refs on page: Dar-us-Salam Hadith 2004; USC-MSA Vol. 3, Book 31, Hadith 222. Used whole in §6.
  Directly attached: the day Allah rescued Bani Israel from their enemy = the event of 26:64–66.

## Asbab an-nuzul

- None used. No distinct sabab for this verse; it is narrative sequence within the Musa/Pharaoh account (26:60–68). Placement stated instead (§1, §4).

## Dropped / not used (unconfirmable or off-spine)

- Ibn Kathir's Ibn Mas'ud athar (sea "speaking", horseman, 600,000, blackness) — relayed narration, not a sound attached hadith; not needed. Not written.
- al-Baghawi's Sa'id ibn Jubayr note (sea calm before, then tidal) — off the verse's point; not written.
- Ma'arif al-Qur'an — on 26:62, not on the drowning; not written.
- Nothing from memory. No claim beyond the fetched texts above.

## Watch-points handled

- Spine: same sea = road of rescue and grave for tyrants; one event, two destinies decided by which side of the truth one stood on (§1, §4, §8).
- al-akhirin given tafsir sense briefly; context carries the article (§2).
- Divine judgment on a named tyrant handled soberly; "licenses nothing against any living person or community" carried firmly in both languages (§7).
- Mercy-side noted (tyranny ended, oppressed freed; aid to believers = sign) (§5, §6).
- 26:67 ("most were not believers") and 26:68 ("the Mighty, the Merciful") each touched in one clause only; no narration of 26:67 onward. No shipped-neighbour overlap.
