Replaces the 797-word, 6-section article with a 1,740-word, 9-section
deep article on the Hour that is coming and the raising of those in the graves.

Sources: Ibn Kathir (ar + en), at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi, as-Sa'di,
al-Muyassar on 22:7; al-Baghawi (verse text only) and Ma'arif (22:5
only) fetched, not named.
- Placement: Ibn Kathir's two proofs of 22:5 (man's creation, revived
  earth) and his glosses on 22:6 with 41:39 and 36:82.
- Grammar: al-Qurtubi, joined to dhalika bi-anna in wording, not meaning;
  "wa li-ya'lamu" understood. At-Tabari: "wa li-tuqinu bi-dhalika".
- La rayba fiha: Ibn Kathir, Tabari, Qurtubi, Muyassar (no doubt);
  Tabari's "so do not doubt"; as-Sa'di's "no ground to think it remote".
- Yab'athu: Ibn Kathir (restores, brings into being after nonexistence);
  Tabari (to the standing place of reckoning), Muyassar (reckoning and
  recompense), Qurtubi (reward and punishment), as-Sa'di (deeds good and bad).
- 36:78-80 as Ibn Kathir cites it; Mu'adh ibn Jabal's statement via Ibn
  Abi Hatim, marked as a Companion's words with no grading.
- 22:8-10 in one section with the licensing sentence in both languages.
Hadith: none confirmed. Ibn Kathir's Abu Razin report (Ahmad) checked in
Abu Dawud 4731 and Ibn Majah 180, which carry only the seeing-Allah part;
not quoted.
Dropped from the old article: 15:85, 20:15, 40:59, 22:1-2, 22:55, 22:66,
100:9, the "traveller on the road" image, the "man = person" point (none
sourced). Fixed: the old claim that all five clauses hang on dhalika
bi-anna as causes (al-Qurtubi says otherwise).
