# SOURCES ledger — 35:34

Verse: "And they will say, 'Praise to Allāh, who has removed from us [all] sorrow (al-ḥazan).
Indeed, our Lord is Forgiving (Ghafūr) and Appreciative (Shakūr).'"

## Tafsir fetched (all pre-fetched in audit dir; none re-fetched, none WRONG GROUP)

- **14 Ibn Kathir (ar)** — USED (named).
  - al-ḥazan = fear of what is dreaded: «وهو الخوف من المحذور، أزاحه عنا، وأراحنا مما كنا نتخوفه،
    ونحذره من هموم الدنيا والآخرة» → §2 (grief = fear of everything dreaded; worries of dunyā and ākhira).
  - Ibn ʿAbbās athar on the two names: «قال ابن عباس وغيره: غفر لهم الكثير من السيئات، وشكر لهم اليسير
    من الحسنات» → §6 (forgave much sin, appreciated the little good). Attributed via Ibn Kathir to Ibn ʿAbbās.
- **15 at-Tabari** — USED (named).
  - Qatada: «كانوا في الدنيا يعملون وينصبون وهم في خوف، أو يحزنون» → §2 (worked and wore themselves out in fear/grief).
  - His tarjīḥ that no single kind is meant + the examples: «وخوف دخول النار من الحزن، والجَزَع من الموت من الحزن،
    والجزع من الحاجة إلى المطعم من الحزن… بل أخبر عنهم أنهم عموا جميع أنوع الحزن» → §2.
  - The "second view" (grief of the ẓālim li-nafsih at the standing) he records then sets below the general sense:
    «وقال آخرون: بل عني بذلك الحزن الذي ينال الظالم لنفسه في موقف القيامة … وأولى الأقوال … جميع معاني الحزن» → §3 (disagreement kept).
  - Ghafūr/Shakūr gloss: «لغفور لذنوب عباده الذين تابوا … شكور لهم على طاعتهم» → supports §6.
- **16 al-Muyassar (ar)** — USED (named). Grouped 35:34,35:35.
  - «الحمد لله الذي أذهب عنا كل حَزَن، إن ربنا لغفور؛ حيث غفر لنا الزلات، شكور؛ حيث قبل منا الحسنات وضاعفها»
    → §6 (Ghafūr forgave the slips; Shakūr accepted and multiplied the good deeds).
- **90 al-Qurtubi** — USED (named).
  - ath-Thaʿlabi's preference + Qurtubi calling it al-ṣaḥīḥ, tying al-ḥazan to the three classes of 35:32:
    «قال الثعلبي: وهذا التأويل أشبه بالظاهر … قلت: وهذا هو الصحيح» → §3 (the other side of the disagreement).
  - (Qurtubi's Abu-Darda / Abu Thabit narration on the classes NOT asserted; see Dropped.)
- **91 as-Saʿdi** — USED (named).
  - Absence of every grief: «وهذا يشمل كل حزن، فلا حزن يعرض لهم بسبب نقص في جمالهم، ولا في طعامهم وشرابهم،
    ولا في لذاتهم ولا في أجسادهم، ولا في دوام لبثهم» → §4.
  - Ever-increasing bliss: «فهم في نعيم ما يرون عليه مزيدا، وهو في تزايد أبد الآباد» → §4.
  - The two names' summary: «فبمغفرته نجوا من كل مكروه ومرهوب، وبشكره وفضله حصل لهم كل مرغوب محبوب» → §6.
- **94 al-Baghawi** — USED (named).
  - The catalogue of readings of al-ḥazan: Ibn ʿAbbās «حزن النار»; Qatada «حزن الموت»; Muqatil «حزنوا لأنهم
    كانوا لا يدرون ما يصنع الله بهم»; ʿIkrima «حزن الذنوب والسيئات وخوف رد الطاعات»; al-Qasim «حزن زوال النعم
    وتقليب القلب، وخوف العاقبة»; Saʿid b. Jubayr «هم الخبز في الدنيا»; az-Zajjaj «أذهب الله عن أهل الجنة كل
    الأحزان ما كان منها لمعاش أو لمعاد» → §3. (All these sub-attributions are quoted by al-Baghawi; named as his gathering.)
- **168 Maʿārif al-Qurʾān (en)** — USED (named).
  - "all sorrows stand included"; dunyā as the home of sorrows; the four concerns (world, Resurrection, reckoning,
    Fire); Imām al-Jassās: a believer is never free of concern, "the world is a prison for the believer" → §5.
- **169 Ibn Kathir abridged (en)** — USED (cross-check). Grouped 35:33,34,35.
  - al-ḥazan = "fear of everything that is feared … anxieties of the world and the Hereafter"; Ibn ʿAbbās
    "forgives them for their major sins, and appreciates even the smallest of their good deeds" → §2, §6.
  - On 35:35: "Who, out of His grace, has lodged us … for our good deeds are not equivalent to this" + the
    Sahih hadith "None of you will enter Paradise by virtue of his deeds … unless Allah encompasses me with
    His mercy" → §7.

## Dropped (could not confirm to the gate's standard)

- **Ibn ʿUmar "no waḥsha upon the people of lā ilāha illā Allāh … saying al-ḥamdu lillāh alladhī adhhaba ʿannā
  al-ḥazan."** Carried on the verse by Ibn Kathir (via Ibn Abī Ḥātim and at-Tabarani), al-Baghawi, and Maʿārif
  (via at-Tabarani). NOT in the six books reachable by hadith.js, and its chain runs through ʿAbd al-Raḥmān b.
  Zayd b. Aslam (weak). Dropped from the hadith slot; not asserted anywhere, to avoid upgrading an unverified report.
- **Abu-Darda narration (via al-Aʿmash) that al-ḥazan is specifically the grief of the ẓālim li-nafsih at the
  standing.** Carried by at-Tabari and al-Qurtubi. Used only as the *interpretation* at-Tabari/al-Qurtubi relate
  (§3), attributed to those mufassirūn as a reading — the narration itself is not asserted as a graded hadith.
- Maʿārif's silk/gold hadiths (Hudhayfa, ʿUmar, Abu Saʿid; al-Bukhari & Muslim, via Mazhari) and Ibn Kathir's
  silk/bracelet hadiths belong to 35:33, not 35:34 — not used here.
- The Persian couplet quoted by Maʿārif ("in this world no one is free of sorrow") — rendered as the prose idea
  ("the wise have long called this world the house of sorrows"), not quoted as verse.
