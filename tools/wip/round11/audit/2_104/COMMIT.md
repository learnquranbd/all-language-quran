New card and 1,780-word, 8-section article on 2:104 (ra'ina / unzurna / wa-sma'u),
built only on the tafsir fetched for 2:104. Key kept at 2:104; 2:105 left to the
2:109 envy article.

Sources: at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi, Ibn Kathir (ar), al-Baghawi, as-Sa'di, Muyassar,
Ma'arif al-Qur'an on 2:104 (all slots open on 2:104; none re-fetched).
Occasion given as the tafsirs' report, with names: Ibn Abbas via al-Qurtubi and
al-Baghawi (unattributed) on Sa'd ibn Mu'adh and "do you not say it yourselves?";
as-Suddi (Ibn Kathir, at-Tabari) on Rifa'a ibn Zayd of Banu Qaynuqa', with
at-Tabari's lineage correction. No chain in either; said so in one sentence.
4:46 compared as Ibn Kathir and at-Tabari do. Licensing sentence (Madinah at the
time, nothing against any living person or community) in both languages.
Disagreements kept: the twisting as cause (Ibn Kathir, al-Qurtubi, al-Baghawi,
Muyassar, as-Sa'di, Ma'arif) vs at-Tabari's own view (a coarse word disliked,
like karm / 'abdi; Qatada's account possible but unproven; 'Atiyya rejected);
sadd adh-dhara'i as Malik's and Ahmad's in one report (al-Qurtubi) vs "accepted
by all the jurists" (Ma'arif); insinuation-qadhf, al-Qurtubi vs Abu Hanifah and
ash-Shafi'i; the early glosses and al-Hasan's ra'inan reading (rejected by at-Tabari).
Principles attributed: blocking the means and words that bear only good (as-Sa'di);
wa-sma'u covers Qur'an and Sunnah in word, meaning and response (as-Sa'di).
Hadith: Bukhari 6024 (as-sam 'alaykum, "Allah loves gentleness") and Bukhari 2552
("let none of you say my slave"), quoted whole, both marked general.
Dropped: Abu Dawud 4031 (no grading on the fetched page); al-Qurtubi's
sadd adh-dhara'i hadith list and Ma'arif's Ka'bah example (not fetched);
Khaythama's Torah report; lexical extras (budget).
