Disagreements kept (both sides named, no position):
- Cause of the ban: the twisting by some Jews (Ibn Kathir, al-Qurtubi from Ibn Abbas, al-Baghawi, al-Muyassar, as-Sa'di, Ma'arif) vs at-Tabari's own view (the word itself disliked as coarse, like karm / 'abdi), with his "possible but unproven" on Qatada and his rejection of 'Atiyya.
- Sadd adh-dhara'i: al-Qurtubi (Malik and his followers, Ahmad in one report; Abu Hanifah and ash-Shafi'i differ on the insinuation-qadhf derivation) vs Ma'arif ("accepted by all the jurists"). Stated as "the two descriptions differ".
- Early glosses kept as a range (at-Tabari's list), incl. al-Hasan's ra'inan reading, which at-Tabari rejects.
