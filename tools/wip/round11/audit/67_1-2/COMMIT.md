Replaces the 746-word, 5-section article with a 1,746-word, 8-section deep article, built only on tafsir fetched for 67:1 and 67:2 (shipped card kept).

Sources: at-Tabari, Ibn Kathir (ar; en abridged as cross-check), al-Qurtubi,
as-Sa'di, al-Baghawi, al-Muyassar, Ma'arif al-Qur'an, all fetched for 67:1-2.

Tabaraka glosses, attributed: at-Tabari, Muyassar, as-Sa'di, al-Hasan and the
"dama" view (al-Qurtubi), Ma'arif. Bi-yadihi-l-mulk: dominion glosses by name;
on yad, the Muyassar's affirmation and Ma'arif's mutashabihat note are reported
as they stand, with no position in the article's voice.

Which death and life: Ibn 'Abbas and Qatada (al-Baghawi), at-Tabari, Ibn Kathir
via 2:28, Muqatil (al-Qurtubi). Why death first: al-qahr (with 42:49), the prior
state, and al-Qurtubi's strongest-motive reason, all kept side by side with no
verdict. Qatada's "humbled by death" is marked as carried from Qatada with no
Companion named and no grading. Ibn 'Abbas's ram/mare report is carried by
al-Baghawi and questioned by al-Qurtubi.

Ahsanu 'amala: khayr, not akthar (Ibn Kathir, Ibn 'Ajlan); Fudayl's sincere and
correct (al-Baghawi); as-Sa'di; the Ibn 'Umar report marked ungraded; al-Hasan,
as-Suddi; one line to 18:7. Al-'Aziz al-Ghafur from five commentators.

Hadith: Bukhari 4730 (death as a ram, quoted whole; Muslim 2849's addition
noted; neither names the Sirat, as al-Qurtubi does); Tirmidhi 2891 (hasan) and
Abu Dawud 1400 (thirty verses intercede); Tirmidhi 2890 (al-Mani'a, hasan
gharib); Ibn Kathir's munkar jiddan on the Furat ibn as-Sa'ib story.
Asbab: none in the sources.

Dropped: Sufyan ath-Thawri (not in the sources), Tirmidhi 2892 (no grading,
chain questioned) and other ungraded virtues reports. Dropped from the old article:
"tabaraka used only of Allah", 3:26, 11:7, 39:53, and the unsourced
before-sleep report.
