Replaces the 654-word, 5-section article with a 1,693-word, 9-section deep article, built only on tafsir fetched for 75:36 (shipped card kept).

Sources: at-Tabari, Ibn Kathir (ar), al-Qurtubi, as-Sa'di, al-Baghawi,
al-Muyassar, all fetched for 75:36; Ma'arif al-Qur'an (grouped) for the four
woes of 75:34-35. Ibn Kathir en abridged was the wrong group and not used.

Al-insan held three ways, none chosen: the disbeliever (at-Tabari), the denier
of resurrection (al-Muyassar), ibn Adam generally (al-Qurtubi); licensing
sentence on the denier of 75:31-35. Suda: hamal (Ibn Abbas via at-Tabari),
camels grazing without a herdsman (al-Qurtubi, al-Baghawi), mu'attal
(as-Sa'di), al-Qurtubi's unnamed poet. Readings kept by name: no command or
prohibition (Mujahid, ash-Shafi'i, Ibn Zayd; at-Tabari's own, plus "no worship");
not raised from the grave (as-Suddi in Ibn Kathir, unnamed in al-Qurtubi);
not reckoned, rewarded or punished (al-Muyassar, as-Sa'di). As-Suddi reported
differently by Ibn Kathir and al-Baghawi, kept as a difference. Ibn Kathir's
"covers both states" given as his view; as-Sa'di's "false reckoning, unbefitting
His wisdom". 75:37-40 as the answer via Ibn Kathir and al-Muyassar.

Hadith: none attached by any fetched tafsir; said in one sentence.
Asbab: none for 75:36; Abu Jahl reports left to the 75:31 article.
75:14, 75:20, 75:23, 75:31, 23:115-116, 44:38: one-line pointers.
