Replaces the 799-word, 6-section article with a 1,750-word, 8-section
deep article on Abu Bakr's oath, pardon and the question "would you not love
that Allah forgive you?".

Sources: at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi, Ibn Kathir (ar + en), as-Sa'di,
al-Baghawi, al-Muyassar, Ma'arif al-Qur'an, all on 24:22.
- Occasion: Abu Bakr and Mistah (al-Qurtubi "best known", "sounder");
  variants kept: Ibn 'Abbas and ad-Dahhak (several swore), Mujahid (an orphan
  in his care); al-Qurtubi: the verse reaches the community to the Last Day.
- la ya'tali: swear (aliyya, 2:226) vs fall short (alw, 3:118, an unnamed
  group in al-Qurtubi), no position; az-Zajjaj vs Abu 'Ubayda on an elided la;
  qira'a yata'alla (Abu Ja'far, Zayd ibn Aslam) and at-Tabari's preference.
- fadl glossed three ways; Mistah's three descriptions (Ibn Zayd); aunt's son
  vs aunt's daughter's son; al-Qurtubi: qadhf does not void deeds (39:65).
- 'afw/safh per at-Tabari (resume the giving) and al-Qurtubi ('afa ar-rab').
- The question as measure: Ibn Kathir, as-Sa'di, Muyassar, at-Tabari,
  al-Qurtubi (tamthil wa hujja); reply wording variants; Ibn Kathir's two oaths.
- Oath law: break and expiate (al-Qurtubi, Ma'arif), al-Baji on testimony,
  as-Sa'di on spending on relatives. Ibn al-Mubarak: most hopeful verse.
Hadith: Bukhari 4750 (portion of the long ifk hadith, quoted, attached by
al-Qurtubi/Ibn Kathir/Ma'arif); Bukhari 5997 ("whoever is not merciful...")
as al-Qurtubi's sense-link, marked general. Licensing sentence in both
languages; 24:23-25 in one line.
Dropped from the old article: the month-long rumour, the page-turning safh
etymology, 3:134 / 41:34 / 12:92 and a Muslim hadith (not cited by fetched
tafsir), and editorial "hard case" claims.
