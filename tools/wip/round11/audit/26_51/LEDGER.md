## Named in the article, with the Arabic/English clause each rests on
- **at-Tabari (15)** — ṭamaʿ = rajā': "إنا نطمع: إنا نرجو أن يصفح لنا ربنا عن خطايانا
  التي سلفت منا قبل إيماننا به, فلا يعاقبنا بها" → §2.1 (we hope), §2.2 (not hold to
  account). Ibn Zayd via Tabari: khataya = "السحر والكفر الذي كانوا فيه" → §3.1;
  "first" = "أول من آمن بموسى ... حين رأوها" (first to believe when they saw the
  signs) → §4.1.
- **Ibn Kathir (14 ar)** — khataya = "ما قارفناه من الذنوب، وما أكرهتنا عليه من السحر"
  (sins committed + the magic Pharaoh compelled) → §3.1, §3.3; "first" = "بادرنا قومنا
  من القبط إلى الإيمان" (first of the Copts) → §4.1; "فقتلهم كلهم" (so he killed them
  all) → §7.3.
- **Ibn Kathir (169 en, grouped 49-51)** — threats "resulted only in an increase in
  their faith and submission … the veil of disbelief had been lifted"; "what Musa had
  done could not have been done by any human being unless Allah helped him" → §5.2;
  "So he killed them all" corroborates §7.3.
- **al-Muyassar (16)** — "إنا نرجو أن يغفر لنا ربنا خطايانا من الشرك وغيره؛ لكوننا أول
  المؤمنين في قومك" → §2.1 (we hope), §3.2 (shirk and other), §4.1 (first among your
  people).
- **as-Sa'di (91)** — "خطايانا من الكفر والسحر, وغيرهما … أول المؤمنين بموسى, من هؤلاء
  الجنود" → §3.2; the two possibilities of their fate: "فيحتمل أن فرعون فعل بهم ما
  توعدهم به … ويحتمل أن الله منعه منهم" → §7.3.
- **al-Baghawi (94)** — "أول المؤمنين … من أهل زماننا" (first of the people of our
  time) → §4.2.
- **al-Qurtubi (90)** — "'أن' في موضع نصب، أي لأن كنا. وأجاز الفراء كسرها على أن تكون
  مجازاة"; "معنى أول المؤمنين أي عند ظهور الآية ممن كان في جانب فرعون. الفراء: أول مؤمني
  زماننا. وأنكره الزجاج وقال: قد روي أنه آمن معه ستمائة ألف وسبعون ألفا، وهم الشرذمة
  القليلون … روي ذلك عن ابن مسعود وغيره" → §4.2 (al-Farra' era-reading), §4.3 (grammar
  an=li-an, al-Farra' conditional, az-Zajjaj's objection, ~670,000 believers, the
  small band of 26:54 from Ibn Mas'ud). Prose says "hundreds of thousands" to avoid a
  bare digit; exact figure (600,000 + 70,000) recorded here.
- **Ma'arif al-Qur'an (168 en, grouped 50-58)** — magicians who "involved all their
  lives practicing the sinful sorcery and who believed in the Pharaoh's claim to be a
  god, rather, used to worship him"; devotion "as if they could see the Dooms Day";
  "a miracle of Sayyidna Musa … no less than the miracle of the staff" → §5.1.

## Hadith
- **Sahih Muslim, Hadith 121** — URL https://quranx.com/hadith/Muslim/Hadith-121
  (fetched via hadith.js, saved audit/26_51). First ten words (EN): "It is narrated on
  the authority of Ibn Shamasa". Wording used: 'Amr b. al-'As's condition "أن يُغفر لي"
  and the Prophet's "أما علمت أن الإسلام يهدم ما كان قبله وأن الهجرة تهدم … وأن الحج
  يهدم …" → §6.1-6.2. Grading: Sahih (Muslim's own collection; no separate grade given
  on the page). **Marked in-article as a general narration NOT attached by the
  mufassirun to 26:51** (per §6.1 sentence). Bukhari 7405 (husn al-zann / "I am as My
  slave thinks I am") was fetched as an alternative and NOT used.

## Asbab an-nuzul
- None used. This is a story verse in continuous narrative; no asbab report sought or
  cited. Placement (magicians' reply to Pharaoh's threat, 26:49-51) carries it.

## Dropped / not asserted
- Az-Zajjaj's exact number 670,000 kept out of prose (number-parity), preserved here.
- No claim that a prophet erred; Pharaoh named as the wrongdoer the text names, with an
  explicit note that the verse licenses nothing against any living person (§7.2).
- Their ultimate fate left open (Sa'di's two possibilities + Ibn Kathir's "killed them
  all"), not resolved to one, matching the sources.
