Card and 9-section bilingual article on the deniers' wonder that a
warner came "minhum", and their verdict "shay'un 'ajib".

Sources: at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi, as-Sa'di, al-Baghawi, al-Muyassar,
Ibn Kathir (ar + en abridgement), Ma'arif al-Qur'an.
- bal: at-Tabari's paraphrase (not ignorance of his truthfulness, but
  wonder at a warner from among them); Ibn Kathir's implied oath subject
  (prophethood, resurrection) beside 38:1-2; as-Sa'di's unvalued favour.
- minhum: al-Baghawi (lineage, truthfulness, trustworthiness), as-Sa'di
  (of their kind, so they could learn from him); at-Tabari's no-angel
  reading with 25:7; Ibn Kathir with 10:2.
- al-Qurtubi: an = li-an; pronoun for the disbelievers, or both groups
  then separated by fa-qala al-kafirun; 'ajib/'ujab/'ujjab/u'juba.
- Kept as a disagreement: Qatada (call to one God), "it is said"
  (warning of resurrection), al-Qurtubi preferring the Qur'an's own cause.
- as-Sa'di's sincere-or-feigned analysis; licensing paragraph in both
  languages.
Hadith: Sahih Muslim 873 (Umm Hisham, Qaf on the Friday minbar), about
the surah and marked as not attached to 50:2; no hadith attaches to the
verse itself.
Asbab: none used.
Dropped: al-Baghawi's truncated bal fragment; implicit 22:75 echo;
Abu Waqid and Jabir reports; Qaf letter meaning and 50:3 left to their
own verses.
Muslim 873b translated whole from the Arabic (quranx English has typos), marked not attached to the verse. 38:4 overlap: different angles.
