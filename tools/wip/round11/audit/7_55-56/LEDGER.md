## Disagreements kept
- Tadarru': abasement/lowliness (at-Tabari, al-Baghawi, Ibn Kathir) vs persistence in asking (as-Sa'di) — kept side by side.
- Fasad/islah: shirk and sin after the messengers (at-Tabari, al-Baghawi's four names, as-Sa'di) vs rain and fertility ('Atiyyah) vs both (Ma'arif) vs general (al-Qurtubi's "sounder view") with ad-Dahhak's and al-Qushayri's narrower readings.
- Fear/hope balance: fear in life, hope at death (some scholars via al-Qurtubi; Ma'arif from al-Bahr al-Muhit) vs equal (Ma'arif's own reading) vs by temperament (others in Ma'arif). No position taken.
- Qarib: Ibn Kathir's two reasons, Sa'id ibn Jubayr, at-Tabari (time not kinship), al-Qurtubi's seven (three named), al-Khalil. No position taken.
