Card and 8-section bilingual article on inna l-muttaqina fi jannatin
wa-na'im, read as the reverse of 52:11-16 and completed by 52:18-19
(51:15's angles not retold; 52:20-28 pointer only).

Sources: at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi, Ibn Kathir (ar + en abridged for
52:17-20), al-Muyassar, as-Sa'di; al-Baghawi quotes the verse only;
Ma'arif's grouped text covers 52:9 only, not used.
- Pairing: al-Qurtubi (kuffar then mu'minin), Ibn Kathir (bi-didd),
  as-Sa'di (targhib/tarhib, hearts between khawf and raja').
- Muttaqin: at-Tabari (fara'id and ma'asi), as-Sa'di (li-rabbihim,
  commands and prohibitions).
- Jannat = basatin (at-Tabari, as-Sa'di's garden line); na'im fiha
  (at-Tabari), na'im 'azim (Muyassar), heart/spirit/body (as-Sa'di).
- 52:18: Muyassar's kinds of pleasure; Ibn Kathir's list and rescue
  from the Fire as a bounty in itself; rabbuhum twice.
- 52:19 beside 69:24 (Ibn Kathir): just reward and favour together;
  kuntum ta'malun closes both 52:16 and 52:19.
Licensing sentence for 52:11-16 in both languages.
Hadith: Bukhari 3244 quoted whole, as the source of Ibn Kathir's
unattributed "no eye has seen" phrase, marked general, not tied to
the verse.
Asbab: none.
Dropped: 44:51-52, 51:15, 77:41 (no fetched tafsir cites them);
Ibn Kathir's 52:20 material; any Paradise detail beyond the texts.
Bukhari 3244 dropped: Ibn Kathir names no source for the phrase, so no fetched tafsir attaches it.
