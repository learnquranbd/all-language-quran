Card and 8-section bilingual article for 79:1, wa-n-nazi'ati gharqa
(2 words), the surah's opening oath. 79:2-5 named only as the following
oaths; 79:6 referenced as where the Day is described. No shipped neighbour.

Sources: Tabari, Ibn Kathir (ar), Qurtubi, Baghawi, Sa'di, Muyassar
(grouped 79:1-7), Ma'arif, Ibn Kathir en (heading only).
- an-nazi'at, readings listed, none chosen: angels taking souls (Ibn
  Mas'ud, Ibn Abbas, Masruq, Sa'id b. Jubayr, Abu Salih, Abu ad-Duha,
  as-Suddi in IK; Ali in Qurtubi), death (Mujahid), stars (al-Hasan,
  Qatada, Ibn Kaysan), bows (Ata, Ikrima), raiders who shoot (qila),
  the soul in the chest (as-Suddi), disbelievers' souls (Ibn Abbas via
  Ibn Abi Hatim), wild animals (Yahya b. Sallam in Qurtubi).
- Whose souls: disbelievers (Qurtubi/Ali, Baghawi, Muyassar, Ma'arif)
  vs children of Adam (IK's violent/easy split with 79:2); Sa'di general.
- Kept as disagreement: IK "the first is sound, the majority" vs Tabari
  "not specified, every puller included"; Qurtubi: ighraq runs through all.
- gharqa: ighraq, full draw to the arrowhead (Tabari, Qurtubi, Baghawi,
  Ma'arif); istighraq, ghirqi'; drowning image (IK, Muqatil, Qurtubi qila).
- Soul-taking described only from Ibn Mas'ud (Qurtubi, Baghawi), Muqatil,
  Sa'id b. Jubayr, Ma'arif's unseen-pain note.
- Oath answer: Qurtubi, Muyassar, Ma'arif (deleted), Sa'di's two options;
  Muyassar's note on swearing by other than the Creator.
Hadith: none quoted; no fetched tafsir attaches a marfu' hadith (al-Bara'
narration not cited by any). Asbab: none.
Licensing sentence in both languages. Dropped: description beyond the
fetched text, 79:2-7 content.
