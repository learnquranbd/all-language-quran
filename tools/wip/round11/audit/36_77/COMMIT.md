Card + 8-section bilingual article (1,751 EN words) for 36:77, "does man
not see that We created him from a drop, then at once he is a clear
adversary?"

Sources: at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi, al-Baghawi, Ibn Kathir, as-Sa'di,
al-Muyassar, Ma'arif al-Qur'an, each quoted from the text fetched for 36:77.
- Placement: follows the consolation of 36:76; opens the answer to the
  deniers that runs to the end of Ya-Sin (Ibn Kathir's five names; Sa'di).
- Yara as reasoning from beginning to return (Ibn Kathir, Muyassar, Sa'di).
- Which man: three-way named disagreement, no position: Ubayy b. Khalaf
  (Mujahid, Qatada per Tabari; al-Hasan, Ibn Ishaq, Malik per Qurtubi;
  Baghawi), al-ʿĀṣ b. Wāʾil as-Sahmi (Saʿid b. Jubayr), ʿAbdullah b. Ubayy
  (Ibn ʿAbbas chain). Ibn Kathir's chain notes and his munkar verdict on the
  ʿAbdullah b. Ubayy report reported as his own; his "general, al- for the
  kind" kept. Ma'arif: possibly both.
- Crumbled-bone scene told briefly; the Prophet's reply and the full
  exchange left for 36:78. Qatada's Uhud line reported factually with the
  licensing guard (EN+BN).
- Khaṣīm: Tabari (disputes what his Lord said He would do), Baghawi (jadal
  bil-bāṭil; "how does he not reflect ... and leave the dispute"), Qurtubi,
  Ma'arif. Irony left implicit; 36:78's "forgets his creation" not borrowed.
- Sa'di's "look at the gap between the two states", a fortiori return.
- Hadith: none quoted; no fetched tafsir attaches one to the verse (said
  once). Ibn Majah 2707 (Busr b. Jahhash, no grading on page) mentioned by
  reference to 16:4, not re-quoted. Stock Bukhari 660 not used.
- Distinct from shipped 16:4 (same closing words) and 36:82.
- Dropped: the reply wording, Ibn Kathir's imprecation on Ubayy, Ma'arif's
  al-Baihaqi attribution, the fuller Ahmad wording.
