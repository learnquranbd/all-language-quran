# SOURCES ledger — 29:31

## Tafsir fetched (all pre-fetched in PREP, re-read for this draft)

- **at-Tabari (15)** — NAMED. al-bushrā = "min Allāhi bi-Isḥāq, wa-min warā'i Isḥāqa Yaʿqūb"
  (glad tidings from Allah of Isaac, and beyond Isaac, Jacob). The town: "qaryat Sadūm,
  wa-hiya qaryat qawm Lūṭ." ẓālimīn: "ẓālimī anfusihim bi-maʿṣiyatihim Allāh wa-takdhībihim
  rasūl Allāh." Also carries the Ibn ʿAbbās chain on Ibrāhīm disputing with the angels and
  the quote of "inna Ibrāhīma la-ḥalīmun awwāhun munīb" (11:75) — used in §8 for Ibrāhīm's
  forbearance; the dispute detail itself is left for 29:32 (future ground).
- **al-Muyassar (16)** — NAMED. Same bushrā reading ("bi-Isḥāq, wa-min warā'i Isḥāqa waladahu
  Yaʿqūb"); town = Sadūm; ẓālimīn = "ẓālimī anfusihim bi-maʿṣiyatihim lillāh."
- **al-Baghawi (94)** — NAMED. "bi-Isḥāqa wa-Yaʿqūb"; "yaʿnī qawm Lūṭ, wa-l-qarya Sadūm."
- **as-Sa'di (91)** — NAMED. Angels sent to destroy them, "fa-marrū bi-Ibrāhīma qabl,
  wa-bashsharūhu bi-Isḥāq, wa-min warā'i Isḥāqa Yaʿqūb." Used in §1 for "they passed by
  Ibrāhīm first."
- **al-Qurtubi (90)** — NAMED. "fa-jā'ū Ibrāhīma awwalan mubashshirīn" — they came to Ibrāhīm
  first, bearers of glad tidings. Used in §1 for the "first address" point. (Qurtubi's one
  line frames the errand toward Lūṭ's aid and refers the fuller account to Sūrah Hūd; I did
  not spin a separate dispute from it — the standard bushrā = Isaac reading is carried by the
  other four and by Ibn Kathir.)
- **Ibn Kathir ar (14) + en abridged (169)** — NAMED. Angels came as guests; Ibrāhīm's
  hospitality ("jā'ahum bimā yanbaghī li-ḍ-ḍayf"); they did not eat; "nakirahum wa-awjasa
  minhum khīfa"; they calmed him and gave news of "a righteous son born by his wife Sarah,
  who was present and was astonished." Then, on the destruction, "akhadha yudāfiʿu laʿallahum
  yunẓarūn, laʿalla-llāha an yahdiyahum" — used in §8. Points reader to Sūrah Hūd / al-Ḥijr
  for the fuller scene (used in §2). The 169 text runs on into 29:32-35 (Lūṭ, the overturning,
  the sign) — that material deliberately left untouched (future ground / other drafters).
- **Ma'arif en (168)** — NAMED once, in §7, only to PLACE it: it glosses 29:28-29 (the three
  sins) and cites a report "as Umm Hāni' reports it in a hadith" about pelting/mocking
  travelers in their gatherings. Marked as belonging to 29:29, not to this announcement, and
  not built upon (see Dropped).

## Hadith

- **None used.** No sound marfūʿ hadith is narrated on the announcement at Ibrāhīm's door.
  Per SOURCE-GATE §2 this is stated plainly in §7 rather than filled with a weak report.
  hadith.js was not called because no narration is cited by number or wording.

## Asbāb an-nuzūl

- None. Not applicable to this narrative verse; no occasion-of-revelation claim made.

## Dropped (could not confirm / off-verse)

- **Umm Hāni' report (via Ma'arif, 168)** on the townspeople pelting travelers: off-verse
  (it glosses 29:29, the people's conduct, not 29:31's announcement) and offered by Ma'arif
  without a grading I could confirm. Named in §7 only to place it correctly; not relied on.
- **The Ibrāhīm–angels dispute / the ten households count** (at-Tabari, Ibn ʿAbbās chain):
  confirmed in the fetched Tabari but is the substance of 29:32 (future ground). Only the
  bare "he began to plead" / his ḥilm kept in §8; the bargaining itself left out.
- **Sarah's laugh, the number of guests, the roasted calf** (Hūd/Ḥijr detail): referenced as
  cross-light only (11:69–76, 51:24–30 named in §2), not narrated here as targets.
