Replaces the 715-word, 6-section article with an 8-section, 1,791-word
source-gated article (shipped card kept).

Sources: at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi, as-Sa'di, Ibn Kathir (ar, en abridged),
al-Baghawi, Muyassar, Ma'arif, all on 40:3 itself. Covers the link to
40:1-2 (Ibn Mas'ud on the Ha-Mim surahs; as-Sa'di's mapping of the
descriptions onto the Qur'an's contents), the grammar of the genitive
(at-Tabari; al-Farra', az-Zajjaj, an-Nahhas via al-Qurtubi), ghafir as
satir (al-Baghawi, Ma'arif), the singular dhanb as the act (at-Tabari),
past sin and future repentance (Ibn Kathir), al-Mazhari's distinction via
Ma'arif, the severity's target and at-Tabari's warning, Ibn 'Abbas's
la ilaha illa Allah reading, hope and fear with 15:49-50, ahl al-ishara,
the at-tawl glosses with 4:25 and 14:34, and al-masir with 13:41.

Disagreements kept: grammar (repeated min / attraction / true na't; na't
vs badal vs tense-dependent); at-tawb as masdar vs plural of tawbah;
at-tawl as means, blessings, good, mann or bounty vs power (Ibn Zayd).

Hadith: none; no fetched tafsir attaches a marfu' hadith (stated). Reports
about 'Umar used and marked ungraded: the man who killed (Ibn Kathir,
at-Tabari) and the letter to the Syrian drinker (Ibn Kathir, al-Qurtubi).
40:4 quoted with the licensing sentence in both languages.

Dropped: the Tirmidhi Ha-Mim/Ayat al-Kursi report (not in fetched texts),
Thabit al-Bunani's rider story (length), and the old article's unsourced
claims (sin as a genus, which at-Tabari contradicts; at-tawb as verbal
noun only; 'iqab's root; 40:56 and 40:60 readings; the dying-youth
Tirmidhi report, not cited on this verse).
