## Glosses kept, none chosen
- jamm: much (Mujahid, Qurtubi, Baghawi, Ibn Kathir ar); intense (Ibn 'Abbas via 'Ali, Qatada, Ibn Zayd); much and intense (Tabari, Sa'di);
  love of abundance of wealth (Ibn 'Abbas second chain, Dahhak); fahish per "some" (Ibn Kathir ar); mufrit (Muyassar); excessive (Ma'arif).
  Water imagery: basin (Tabari, Qurtubi, Baghawi), well refilling after drawing (Qurtubi), Zuhayr's jimam (Tabari).
- object loved: wealth / gathering and acquiring it (Tabari) / gathering it and infatuation (Baghawi) / its abundance; lawful and unlawful (Qurtubi only).
- addressee: "O people" (Tabari) vs "the unbelievers" (Ma'arif); Ibn Kathir en "the Servant"; Muyassar continues from "this man". Kept as a difference.
