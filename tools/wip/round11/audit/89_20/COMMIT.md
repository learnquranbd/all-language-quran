Card and 8-section bilingual article for 89:20: wa tuhibbuna al-mala
hubban jamma, four words, the last of the charges after 89:15-16.
89:17-19 named as context only; 89:21 onward left to its entries.

Sources: Tabari, Qurtubi, Baghawi, Sa'di, Ibn Kathir (ar + en
abridged), Muyassar, Ma'arif. All slots open on 89:20.
- jamm: much (Mujahid, Qurtubi, Baghawi); intense (Ibn 'Abbas via
  'Ali, Qatada, Ibn Zayd); both (Tabari, Sa'di); abundance of wealth
  (Ibn 'Abbas second chain, Dahhak); mufrit (Muyassar); excessive
  (Ma'arif). Each attributed, none chosen.
- Water roots: basin (Tabari, Qurtubi, Baghawi), Zuhayr's jimam
  (Tabari), the well refilling after drawing (Qurtubi), Abu Khirash
  line on abundant forgiveness (Qurtubi).
- Ibn Kathir ar "some added fahish" vs en "increases some of them in
  their wickedness": both reported as given.
- Addressee kept as a difference: O people (Tabari) vs the
  unbelievers (Ma'arif). Qurtubi alone: lawful and unlawful.
Ma'arif: sober love of wealth not condemned, only its excess.
Licensing sentence in both languages; no group named.
Sa'di's 87:16-17 and 75:20-21 cross-referenced; shipped 87:14-17
and 75:20 by number. Article's own reading labelled as such.
Hadith: none attached; Ibn Kathir's Abu Dawud orphan narration left
to 89:17. Asbab: none. Dropped: yuhibbuna qira'a (not in fetched
text), valley-of-gold/wolves/old-age hadith, 3:14, 100:8, 102:1-2,
104:2-3 (no fetched tafsir cites them).
