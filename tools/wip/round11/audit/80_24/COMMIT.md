Replaces the 636-word, 5-section article with a 1,743-word, 9-section deep article, built only on tafsir fetched for 80:24 (shipped card kept).

Sources: at-Tabari, Ibn Kathir (ar; en abridged for the 80:17-32 frame),
al-Qurtubi, as-Sa'di, al-Baghawi, al-Muyassar, Ma'arif al-Qur'an, all for 80:24.

Hinge from creation to provision (al-Qurtubi, al-Baghawi). 80:17-23 placement:
Ibn Abbas and Ibn Jurayj on 80:17; 80:23 held two ways, Ibn Jarir (man has not
fulfilled the command) vs Ibn Kathir's own (He has not yet resurrected), no
position. Al-insan as the denier (at-Tabari, Ma'arif) vs man unqualified
(al-Qurtubi, al-Baghawi, as-Sa'di, al-Muyassar); "man" kept as a description,
licensing sentence. Nazar as the heart's looking, tadabbur (al-Qurtubi,
al-Muyassar), tafakkur (as-Sa'di), li-ya'tabir (al-Baghawi), making ready for
the Return (al-Qurtubi). Object of the look: how He arranged it (at-Tabari,
al-Baghawi), his eating and drinking and "a sign for them" (Mujahid via
at-Tabari), the stages it passed (as-Sa'di), its entry and exit (al-Hasan and
Mujahid via al-Qurtubi and al-Baghawi). 80:25-32 in one section via Ibn Kathir
and al-Muyassar: qadb, abb, ghulb glosses by name; Abu Bakr's and Umar's
restraint over abb as reports (Ibn Kathir calls Umar's chain authentic).
Ibn Kathir's imtinan and istidlal. 80:25, 80:33, 80:37, 16:114 one line each.

Hadith: none confirmable on 80:24; said in one sentence. Al-Qurtubi's two
ungraded marfu' reports described, not quoted; Ibn Umar's saying as his report.
Asbab: none.

Dropped from the old article: 86:5 parallel (no source cites it), the
"lexicons" gloss of abb, the eye-look claim for nazar, the "eight things"
count. Defect fixed: 80:23 had been given one reading as the meaning.
