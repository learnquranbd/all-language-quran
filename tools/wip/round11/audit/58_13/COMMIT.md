Card and 8-section bilingual article (1,719 EN words) on 58:13: a-ashfaqtum,
the lifting of the charity-before-consultation rule of 58:12. 58:14 not touched.

Sources: at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi, Ibn Kathir (ar; en group 58:12-13), as-Sa'di,
al-Baghawi, al-Muyassar, Ma'arif (group 58:12-13).
- a-ashfaqtum: fear of poverty (at-Tabari, Muyassar, al-Baghawi); "were you
  miserly" (Ibn Abbas via al-Qurtubi and al-Baghawi); fear of the ruling
  continuing (Ibn Kathir); al-Qurtubi's istifham of taqrir.
- Durations kept apart and attributed: Muqatil ten nights; al-Kalbi one night
  (al-Qurtubi) vs an hour (al-Baghawi); Ibn Abbas and Qatadah an hour; Ali via
  'Abd ar-Razzaq with the narrator's "I reckon he said".
- What lifted it: the next verse ('Ikrimah, al-Hasan); zakah (Ibn Abbas via
  al-'Awfi, al-Qurtubi); the end of 58:12 (Qatadah, Muqatil, in Ibn Kathir).
- Ali reports (Ibn Kathir's qad qila, Mujahid's two routes, Ma'arif) vs
  al-Qurtubi judging what is narrated from Ali weak: kept as a disagreement.
- wa taba Allahu 'alaykum glossed only as the sources do (abrogated, overlooked,
  concession, pardoned, tawbah granted); no charge in the article's voice.
- Prayer, zakah, obedience per at-Tabari, Qatadah, as-Sa'di, al-Qurtubi;
  as-Sa'di's unabrogated courtesy and Ma'arif's achieved purpose.
- Licensing sentence in both languages on those who did not give.
Hadith: Tirmidhi 3300, quoted whole (quranx), graded hasan gharib by
at-Tirmidhi; noted that its wording does not itself say Ali gave.
Asbab: only as the tafsirs report the occasion of 58:12.
Dropped: Mujahid via at-Tabari, Ma'arif's unspecified amount, sadaqah/sadaqat
observation (no source comments), Muyassar/al-Baghawi labels (length).
- Review fix: quranx typo 'barely corn' shown bracketed in the Tirmidhi 3300 quote, not silently corrected.
