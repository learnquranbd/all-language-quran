Glosses kept, none chosen:
- yakhsha: Allah (Baghawi); his Lord (Muyassar); mindful of and fears Allah (Qurtubi);
  fears Allah and His punishment (Tabari); heart fears Allah + knows he will meet Him
  (Ibn Kathir ar/en); fear + knowledge of requital (Sa'di). Al-Mawardi via Qurtubi:
  hope also, but fear more effective.
- sa-yadhdhakkaru: sa-yatta'izu (Ibn Kathir, Baghawi, Muyassar); takes the reminder when
  the Prophet reminds (Tabari); al-muntafi'un, effect = refraining from sins, striving in
  good (Sa'di).
- relation to 87:9's "if": real condition / etiquette (Ibn Kathir en); emphatic, not
  conditional (Ma'arif); remind all, benefit only to the fearer, reward either way
  (al-Qushayri via Qurtubi, wa-qila).
