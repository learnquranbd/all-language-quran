Card and 8-section bilingual article for 87:10: sa-yadhdhakkaru man
yakhsha, three words, answering the four-word command of 87:9 through
the shared root dh-k-r. 87:11 named only as the verse that follows.

Sources: Tabari, Ibn Kathir (ar + en abridged), Qurtubi, Sa'di,
Baghawi, Muyassar, Ma'arif. All slots checked; each discusses 87:10.
- yakhsha: Allah (Baghawi); his Lord (Muyassar); mindful of and fears
  Him (Qurtubi); Allah and His punishment (Tabari); heart fears Allah
  and knows he will meet Him (Ibn Kathir); fear plus knowledge of
  requital (Sa'di). None chosen.
- sa-yadhdhakkaru: will take admonition (Ibn Kathir, Baghawi,
  Muyassar); takes the reminder the Prophet gives (Tabari); those who
  benefit, refraining from sins and striving in good (Sa'di).
- al-Mawardi via Qurtubi: hope also reminded, fear more effective.
- 87:9's "if": remind where it benefits, etiquette of knowledge, with
  two sayings of 'Ali quoted from Ibn Kathir en, no chain or grading
  (Ibn Kathir); emphatic, not conditional (Ma'arif); remind all, reward
  of calling either way (al-Qushayri via Qurtubi). Kept side by side.
- Licensing sentence on al-ashqa in both languages.
Hadith: none attached to 87:10; Ibn Kathir's surah-level and 87:13
reports described generally, not quoted.
Asbab: Qurtubi's ungraded Ibn Umm Maktum report dropped; no al-Wahidi
entry on quranx. Dropped: 35:28 etc. (no tafsir cites them).
Fix: Muyassar uses yakhafu then yakhsha, so "same verb" became "fear of
the same Lord".
