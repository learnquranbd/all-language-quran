Replaces the 777-word, 6-section article with a 1,782-word, 9-section depth
article built only from the tafsir texts fetched for 20:7 and 20:8.

Sources: Ibn Kathir ties 20:7 to the Sender of the Qur'an (25:6). at-Tabari,
al-Muyassar, as-Sa'di and al-Baghawi read in tajhar as loud and quiet being
equal before Him; no fetched text gives asbab or a dhikr-voice ruling.

Disagreement kept on as-sirr wa akhfa, no side taken: what is not yet done or
thought (Ibn Abbas via Ali b. Abi Talha, Sa'id b. Jubayr, Abu Kudayna; Qatada,
ad-Dahhak; Ma'arif, as-Sa'di); told to another vs kept in oneself (al-Hasan in
al-Baghawi, Ibn Abbas in al-Qurtubi); deed vs waswasa (Mujahid), talk of the
self ('Ikrima), resolve vs passing thought (al-Baghawi, qila); akhfa as a verb
(Ibn Zayd per at-Tabari/al-Qurtubi, Zayd b. Aslam per al-Baghawi), with
at-Tabari's grammatical rejection and his own preference reported as his.
One knowledge, one soul (31:28) from the Ali b. Abi Talha report.

20:8: al-Qurtubi's i'rab (Allah as badal of the pronoun in ya'lamu), Ibn
Kathir, as-Sa'di's sequence and his account of the names, at-Tabari on
husna as singular (20:18 parallel). 7:180 and 17:110 one line each.

Hadith: none attached; Ibn Kathir and al-Qurtubi refer the names hadiths
back to al-A'raf. al-Qurtubi's 17:110 report not attributed to Ibn Abbas.

Fixed from the old article: the sirr/akhfa pairings were mispaired and
misattributed to at-Tabari. Dropped (unsourced): 20:44/20:46 payoff, the
mercy/knowledge pairing, the "outward then inward" sweep, own-voice reading
of in tajhar.
