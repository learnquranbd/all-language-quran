and the Muyassar on 15:49, plus the same five Arabic tafsirs fetched on
15:50, read as one sentence. Slots checked, none shifted. Shipped card kept.

Muyassar), firm news with proofs (as-Sa'di).

al-Baghawi, the Muyassar; as-Sa'di on knowledge that moves to tawba.

as-Sa'di (hope must not reach safety and presumption; 89:25-26).

fear, as-Sa'di's sequence, and the I am / My punishment wording noted only.

Shayba door report (Ibn Jarir, at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi via ath-Tha'labi from
Ibn 'Umar, al-Baghawi), al-Mawardi/al-Mahdawi wording; no other grade added.

(Bukhari 3194) named in one line, not reused. Closing: as-Sa'di's two
gazes, al-Qurtubi's middle course, a licensing line, bridge to 15:51.

everyone without qualifier (all three sources tie it to repentance).
