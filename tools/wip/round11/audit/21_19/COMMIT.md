Card plus deep bilingual article on the angels as the model of worship:
nearest to Allah, never too proud to worship, never tiring of it.

Sources gave a single coherent picture, read across eight tafsir:
- Frame from at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi, al-Muyassar, as-Sa'di: the verse is
  the tawhid proof following 21:16-18. "To Him belongs whoever is in the
  heavens and the earth" = ownership and creation (mulkan wa khalqan,
  al-Qurtubi; ʿabidan wa mulkan, al-Baghawi); what is owned cannot be a
  partner or a child. al-Qurtubi ties "those near Him" to the pagans'
  claim that the angels were Allah's daughters.
- la yastakbirun (no arrogance): Ibn Kathir yastankifun + 4:172 parallel;
  al-Qurtubi la ya'nafun + tadhallul; al-Baghawi/as-Suddi la yataʿazzamun;
  al-Muyassar la ya'nafun.
- la yastahsirun (no weariness): the hasir camel etymology (at-Tabari,
  al-Qurtubi, al-Baghawi) with at-Tabari's line of ʿAlqama ibn ʿAbada; the
  salaf's glosses gathered as one meaning (Qatadah, Mujahid, Ibn Zayd,
  Ibn ʿAbbas, Abu Zayd, Ibn al-Aʿrabi).
- as-Sa'di's three reasons they never flag: longing, love, bodily strength.
- Ma'arif: the two human excuses (arrogance, need for rest) the two clauses
  answer; worship refused loses the refuser nothing of Allah's.

No hadith: Bukhari 660 fetched and dropped (sound but unrelated, unattached);
article states in one line that no tafsir attaches a hadith here. No asbab.
21:20 (night-and-day glorification) deliberately not preempted.
Article 8 sections; card themes tawheed + prayer.
