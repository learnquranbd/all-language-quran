Card and 8-section bilingual article for 72:26: 'alimu l-ghaybi fa-la
yuzhiru 'ala ghaybihi ahada. 72:20-25 as the frame (the qul series; 72:26
as the reason for "I do not know" in 72:25, per Ma'arif and Muyassar);
72:27's "except a messenger He approves" read as the stated exception,
72:27-28 guards not developed.

Sources: Tabari, Qurtubi (72:26 and 72:27), Ibn Kathir (ar; en grouped
72:25-28), Sa'di (72:26-27), Baghawi (72:26-27), Muyassar (grouped
72:26-28), Ma'arif. All named.
- Grammar: 'alim as na't of rabbi or with implied huwa (Qurtubi, Baghawi);
  istighraq article and possessive ghaybihi (Ma'arif via Ruh al-Ma'ani).
- Ibn 'Abbas and Qatada via Tabari; Sa'di (hearts and secrets); Ibn
  Kathir's 2:255 parallel; why the exception: miracles, 3:49 (Qurtubi),
  proof of prophethood (Baghawi), what wisdom requires (Sa'di).
- Disagreement kept: the approved messenger is Jibril (Sa'id ibn Jubayr,
  called remote by Qurtubi) vs angelic and human messengers (Ibn Kathir).
- Part, not whole: ma yasha' (Tabari, Qurtubi), ba'd al-ghayb (Muyassar).
- Astrology: Qurtubi on 72:27 (scholars' reasoning, his judgment, the
  ship argument, the 'Ali report flagged as without a chain), attributed;
  licensing sentence in both languages.
Hadith: none attached to 72:26; Bukhari 4627 (keys of the unseen are
five) quoted whole, marked general; 31:34 as recited in it.
Asbab: none.
Dropped: 6:59 (no fetched source), Ibn Kathir's unsourced Hour
narrations, karamat (no source), Qurtubi's blood clause, 72:27-28 guards.
