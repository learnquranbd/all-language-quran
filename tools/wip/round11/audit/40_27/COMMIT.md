Card and 8-section bilingual article for 40:27: wa qala Musa inni 'udhtu
bi-rabbi wa rabbikum min kulli mutakabbirin la yu'minu bi-yawmi al-hisab,
Musa's reply to Fir'awn's threat in 40:26.

Sources: Ibn Kathir (ar + en abridged, grouped 40:23-27), Tabari, Qurtubi,
Sa'di, Muyassar, Baghawi (circumstance only), Ma'arif (frame only).
- Occasion: the threat to kill him (Qurtubi, Baghawi, IK); Sa'di's paired
  ista'ana: Fir'awn leaning on his power, Musa seeking help from his Lord.
- 'udhtu glossed istajartu (Tabari, Muyassar, IK); Sa'di imtana'tu
  bi-rububiyyatihi allati dabbara biha jami' al-umur.
- "wa rabbikum": addressed to Fir'awn and his council (Tabari, Muyassar,
  ayyuha al-qawm); IK ayyuha al-mukhatabun. No text gives a reason for the
  addition; said so. 79:24 contrast labelled the article's reflection.
- "kulli mutakabbir": general, not narrowed to Fir'awn by any text; IK
  "his evil and the evil of those like him", Sa'di "Fir'awn and others
  enter under it". Glosses from IK, Tabari, Qurtubi, Muyassar.
- Tabari's reason for naming disbelief in the Day (no hope of reward, no
  fear of punishment); Sa'di: arrogance + disbelief carry to sharr/fasad,
  linked to Fir'awn's fasad charge in 40:26 as an observation.
- Musa's state reported only through the texts' verbs; no inner state
  asserted. Sa'di's outcome (lutf, means) with a one-line 40:28 pointer.
- 44:20 same wording, cross-ref verified. 40:24 one line.
- Licensing sentence in both languages.
Hadith: Abu Dawud 1537 (Abu Musa, "Allahumma inna naj'aluka fi
nuhurihim..."), quoted whole in Abu Dawud's wording; page shows no grading
by Abu Dawud, none added; marked general, placed here by Ibn Kathir; his
variant wording noted, not blended.
Dropped: Ma'arif's believer names and siddiqin hadith, Sa'di's Abu Talib
parallel (40:28 ground).
Asbab: none.
Themes: trust, prayer.
