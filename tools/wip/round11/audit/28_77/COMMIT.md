Replaces the 723-word, 5-section article with a 1,739-word, 8-section article
built only from the eight tafsir files fetched for 28:77.

Sources: speakers from at-Tabari (Qarun's people; "do not transgress through
your wealth"), Ibn Kathir on 28:76 (the righteous among them) and Ma'arif (the
Muslims). Abtaghi glossed by Ibn Kathir, at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi (the home is
Paradise), al-Baghawi (gratitude) and as-Sa'di (means others lack).
"La tansa nasibak" kept as a genuine dispute, no choice made: deeds sent ahead
(Ibn Abbas two chains, Mujahid, Ibn Zayd in at-Tabari, who adopts it; Ibn
Abbas and the majority in al-Qurtubi; as-Suddi and Ali in al-Baghawi) versus
lawful provision (al-Hasan, Qatada, Ibn Jurayj; Malik; Mansur ibn Zadhan; Ibn
Kathir's list and "rights"; as-Sa'di; al-Muyassar; Ma'arif). Ibn Atiyya's
severity/gentleness contrast, Ibn Umar's joining, Ibn al-Arabi on Qatada and
Malik, al-Hasan's "send ahead the surplus", and the unnamed shroud reading
with al-Qurtubi's two couplets. Ahsin: to creation (Ibn Kathir, Muyassar,
as-Sa'di), in spending (at-Tabari), obedience (al-Qurtubi, al-Baghawi), the
poor (qila). Fasad: transgression, sins, as-Sa'di's three forms.

Hadith: Bukhari 50 (the ihsan exchange of the Jibril narration, attached by
al-Qurtubi). Dropped: al-Baghawi's "five before five" (he labels it mursal),
Ibn al-Arabi's food-habit reports, the shroud's attribution to named early
authorities (unnamed in al-Qurtubi). Qarun's kinship and end in one section,
verse text only; licensing sentence added in both languages.

Dropped from the old article: the 57:20 and 2:201 parallels, the "vehicle"
and "opposite failures" claims, the 28:82 paraphrase (unsourced); "five
clauses" corrected to four.
