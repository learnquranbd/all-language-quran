# SOURCES ledger — 17:71

## Tafsir fetched (from PREP, not re-fetched) and how used

- **at-Tabari (15)** — USED. Lists three groups then states his own preference:
  clause "وأولى هذه الأقوال عندنا بالصواب، قول من قال ... بإمامهم الذي كانوا يقتدون
  به ويأتمُّون به في الدنيا، لأن الأغلب من استعمال العرب الإمام فيما ائتمّ واقتدي به"
  → Tabari PREFERS the "leader followed in the world" reading, on linguistic grounds.
  Also: fatīl = "المنفتل الذي في شقّ بطن النواة" (thread in the cleft of the date-pit);
  right-hand group "قرأه واستبشر ولم يظلم فتيلا".
- **Ibn Kathir ar (14) / en abridged (169)** — USED. Reports the readings (Mujahid/Qatadah
  = nabiyyihim, links 10:47; Ibn Zayd = revealed scripture, "واختاره ابن جرير"; Ibn
  Abbas/Hasan/Dahhak/Abu al-Aliyah = book of deeds). States "وهذا القول هو الأرجح" for
  the book-of-deeds reading, citing 36:12, 18:49, 45:28-29. Right hand read "من فرحته
  وسروره بما فيه من العمل الصالح ... ويحب قراءته", links 69:19. fatīl = "الخيط المستطيل
  في شق النواة". Cites the Bazzar hadith via as-Suddi←father←Abu Hurayrah, "لا يروى إلا
  من هذا الوجه".
  NOTE ON A CONFLICT: Ibn Kathir says Ibn Jarir (Tabari) favored the "revealed scripture"
  reading; Tabari's OWN fetched text says he favored the "leader followed" reading. I
  followed Tabari's own text and attributed to Tabari only what his text states.
- **al-Qurtubi (90)** — USED. Full spread of readings incl. Ali "بإمام عصرهم", Ibn Zayd
  (Torah people by Torah, Qur'an people by Qur'an), madhāhib view (Abu Ubayda). "الكتاب
  يسمى إماما لأنه يرجع إليه". Records Muhammad ibn Ka'b's "bi-ummahātihim" (mothers) with
  three wisdoms, THEN refutes it with the Bukhari/Muslim banner hadith of Ibn Umar ("هذه
  غدرة فلان بن فلان" — called by father's name). Tirmidhi hadith quoted, "قال أبو عيسى
  هذا حديث حسن غريب". fatīl = "الذي في شق النواة".
- **as-Sa'di (91)** — USED. The key synthesis: the right-hand group is who "اتبع إمامه
  الهادي ... واهتدى بكتابه فكثرت حسناته وقلت سيئاته"; reads "قراءة سرور وبهجة"; "ولا
  يظلمون فتيلا مما عملوه من الحسنات" (not wronged a fatīl of their good deeds).
- **al-Baghawi (94)** — USED. Readings (nabiyyihim; revealed book; deeds; imām zamānihim
  to guidance or misguidance, cites 21:73 & 28:41; mothers view). "لا ينقص من حقهم قدر
  فتيل."
- **al-Muyassar (16)** — USED. Whole verse "مبشرًا ومخوفًا"; imām = "الذي كانوا يقتدون
  به في الدنيا"; right hand "فرحين مستبشرين"; fatīl = "الخيط الذي في شَقِّ النواة", reward
  not diminished.
- **Ma'arif al-Qur'an (168)** — USED. imām = book of deeds (Thanavi's translation), cites
  Tirmidhi Hasan Gharib; believer reads in delight and has others read it; joy = iman
  intact + rescue from eternal punishment; only disbelievers get the record in the left
  hand; those in the right are people of faith, pious or sinner.

## Genuine disagreement KEPT (not resolved)
Four readings of "bi-imāmihim" named without forcing one: (1) their prophet — Mujahid,
Qatadah; (2) the record/book of deeds — Ibn Abbas, al-Hasan, Abu al-Aliyah, ad-Dahhak
(Ibn Kathir's arjaḥ); (3) the revealed scripture they lived by — Ibn Zayd; (4) the leader
of their age they followed to guidance or misguidance — Ali, Ibn Abbas via al-Baghawi;
Sa'di and Muyassar lean this way. §3 keeps Tabari (leader) vs Ibn Kathir (book of deeds)
as an explicit standoff.

## Hadith
- **Jami' at-Tirmidhi 3136** — URL fetched: https://quranx.com/hadith/Tirmidhi/Hadith-3136
  (Dar-us-Salam Vol 5, Book 44, Hadith 3136). First ten words of wording used: "One of
  them will be called and given his record". Grading: قال أبو عيسى هذا حديث حسن غريب —
  **Hasan Gharib** (reported as-is, not upgraded). Directly on 17:71 (النبي ﷺ في قول
  الله يوم ندعو كل أناس بإمامهم). Chain: as-Suddi (Ismail b. Abd al-Rahman) ← his father
  ← Abu Hurayrah — same isnād Ibn Kathir gives via al-Bazzar. USED (§4).
- **Bukhari/Muslim "banner for every traitor" (Ibn Umar)** — cited only as al-Qurtubi's
  counter-evidence against the "mothers" reading (people called by father's name). Not
  presented as the verse's hadith; paraphrased from Qurtubi, not quoted whole. Sahih
  (muttafaq 'alayh, per Qurtubi "خرجه مسلم والبخاري").
