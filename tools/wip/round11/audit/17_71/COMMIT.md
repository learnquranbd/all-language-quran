Adds the 17:71 card and a deep bilingual article: the Day every
people is summoned with its imām, the record read in the right
hand in joy, and perfect justice down to a thread's worth.

Sources (all fetched for this verse):
- Four readings of "bi-imāmihim" kept as a genuine dispute, not
  forced: their prophet (Mujahid, Qatadah, +10:47); the record of
  deeds (Ibn Abbas, al-Hasan, Abu al-Aliyah, ad-Dahhak — Ibn
  Kathir's arjaḥ, on 36:12 and the verse's own next clause); the
  revealed scripture they lived by (Ibn Zayd); the leader of their
  age (Ali, Ibn Abbas via al-Baghawi, +21:73 & 28:41).
- Explicit standoff kept: at-Tabari prefers "leader followed" on
  linguistic grounds; Ibn Kathir prefers "book of deeds". Both from
  their own texts (did not repeat Ibn Kathir's contrary claim about
  Ibn Jarir, which Tabari's text refutes).
- Muhammad ibn Ka'b's "mothers" reading shown as the outlier the
  tradition checks, with Qurtubi's Bukhari/Muslim banner hadith.
- Right hand read "in joy": Ibn Kathir, as-Sa'di (ties both halves
  — the follower of the true guide is the one who reads in joy),
  Ma'arif, al-Muyassar; linked to 69:19.
- fatīl = the thin thread in the cleft of the date-stone (Tabari,
  Qurtubi, Ibn Kathir, Muyassar); for the right hand it means not a
  fraction of their good is lost (Sa'di, Baghawi, Muyassar).

Hadith: Jami' at-Tirmidhi 3136, Abu Hurayrah, directly on the
verse; graded Hasan Gharib (reported, not upgraded); same isnād
Ibn Kathir gives via al-Bazzar.

Scope held to the right-hand group (the verse's own scope); the
left hand named only as the unstated contrast. Distinct from the
shipped 17:13 article — no "read your book / deeds on your neck"
angle; this one is summons-by-imām + joy + justice. Licenses
nothing against any living person (§7).
