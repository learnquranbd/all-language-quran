Replaces the 791-word, 6-section article with a 1,776-word, 8-section
source-gated article (shipped card kept).

Sources: at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi, as-Sa'di, Ibn Kathir (ar, en abridged),
al-Baghawi, Muyassar, Ma'arif, all on 42:36, 42:37 and 42:38. Covers the
glosses of "whatever you have been given", khayr wa abqa (at-Tabari,
as-Sa'di), tawakkul as the instrument of every deed (as-Sa'di), the list
grammatically joined to "for those who believe" (al-Qurtubi, at-Tabari),
the kaba'ir/kabir readings and the fawahish glosses, forgiving when angry
as a settled nature (Ibn Kathir, as-Sa'di, with 41:34-35 and 3:134), the
response, prayer and spending, and the Prophet's consulting on war but not
on rulings (al-Qurtubi), with 3:159 and 'Umar's six (Ibn Kathir).

Disagreements kept: who is praised for shura (Ansar before Islam per
an-Naqqash and Ibn Zayd; Ansar at Abu Ayyub's house per ad-Dahhak;
believers in general per Ibn Kathir, al-Baghawi, as-Sa'di; unity of word
per al-Hasan); fawahish as zina vs hadd crimes; how the two words relate.
Address of 42:36: idolaters (al-Qurtubi) vs people at large (at-Tabari).

Asbab: al-Qurtubi's Abu Bakr / 'Umar reports, given as his ungraded
reports. Hadith: Bukhari 3560 (never took revenge for himself), via Ibn
Kathir, quoted whole. Tirmidhi 2266 noted as gharib per at-Tirmidhi, not
relied on. No sound hadith on the shura phrase (stated). Ma'arif's
"obligatory on the state" ascribed to Ma'arif, noting the Ibn Kathir text
here lacks it.

Dropped: old article's 87:17, "surah's name", Makkan-consequence claim,
3:134 ladder and own-voice checklist; corrected its "though revelation
guided him" to al-Qurtubi's war-not-rulings line.
