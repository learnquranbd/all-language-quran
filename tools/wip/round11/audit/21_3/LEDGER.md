# SOURCES ledger — 21:3

## Tafsir fetched (all in audit/21_3/, none WRONG GROUP)
- t15 at-Tabari (ar) — NAMED. Clauses rested on:
  - `لاهية قلوبهم غافلة ... لا يتدبرون حكمه ولا يتفكرون فيما أودعه الله من الحجج` → §1.3, §2.2 (heedless hearts, no pondering of ruling or proofs).
  - `وأسرّ هؤلاء الناس ... النجوى بينهم` + `وقال الذين ظلموا فوصفهم بالظلم بفعلهم وقيلهم ... الإعراض عن ذكر الله والتكذيب برسوله` → §3.1 (named zalimun for deed and speech).
  - `إنسان مثلكم في صوركم وخلقكم ... يعنون بذلك محمدا` → §4.1 (like you in form and making).
  - Ibn Zayd: `زعموا أنه ساحر وأن ما جاء به سحر` → §5.1.
- t90 al-Qurtubi (ar) — NAMED. Clauses:
  - `ساهية قلوبهم ... من قول العرب لهيت عن ذكر الشيء إذا تركته وسلوت عنه` → §2.1.
  - `الذين ظلموا أي الذين أشركوا` → §3.1.
  - Abu Ubayda: `أسروا هنا من الأضداد ... أخفوا ... أو أظهروه` → §3.2.
  - `يأكل الطعام ويمشي في الأسواق كما تفعلون` → §4.2.
  - `السحر في اللغة كل مموه لا حقيقة له ولا صحة` → §5.3.
  - `وأنتم تبصرون ... مثل كنتم تعقلون لأن العقل البصر`; three readings + `ومعنى الكلام التوبيخ` → §6.1.
  - `فأطلع الله نبيه على ما تناجوا به` → §3.2.
- t91 as-Sa'di (ar) — NAMED. Clauses:
  - `قلوبهم غافلة ... بمطالبها الدنيوية وأبدانهم لاعبة` + the contrast `تقبل قلوبهم على أمر الله ونهيه ... ويجعلون القيامة والحساب` → §2.2, §2.3.
  - two readings of `اقترب للناس حسابهم` (last ummah / death is one's reckoning) → §1.2.
  - `بشر مثلكم فما الذي فضله ... يريد أن يتفضل عليكم ويرأس فيكم فلا تطيعوه` → §4.3.
  - `وأنه ساحر ... فانفروا عنه ونفروا الناس` → §5.1.
  - `وهم يعلمون أنه رسول الله حقا بما شاهدوا من الآيات الباهرة ... الشقاء والظلم والعناد` → §6.2.
  - `والله ... قد أحاط علما بما تناجوا به وسيجازيهم عليه` → §7.2.
- t94 al-Baghawi (ar) — NAMED. Clauses:
  - `لاهية ساهية غافلة ... معرضة عن ذكر الله` → §2.1.
  - `الذين ظلموا أي أشركوا` → §3.1.
  - `أنكروا إرسال البشر وطلبوا إرسال الملائكة` → §4.2 (demanded angels).
- t14 Ibn Kathir (ar) — NAMED. Clauses:
  - `يستبعدون كونه نبيا لأنه بشر مثلهم فكيف اختص بالوحي دونهم` → §4.1.
  - `أفتتبعونه فتكونون كمن أتى السحر وهو يعلم أنه سحر` → §6.1.
- t16 al-Muyassar (ar) — read, NOT NAMED in article (its content — heedless of the Qur'an; Quraysh's wrongdoers on a hidden matter; Qur'an called magic — is fully covered by the named mufassirun; Muyassar names "the wrongdoers of Quraysh," which I treated as placement, not an isnad-backed asbab). No unique claim rests on it.
- t168 Ma'arif al-Qur'an (en, grouped 21:3-4) — NAMED. Used the bind: unbelievers could not deny the Qur'an's charm/eloquence/power, so labelled it magic to keep people from the Prophet ﷺ; discussed secretly lest Muslims unveil the fallacy → §3.2, §5.2.
- t169 Ibn Kathir abridged (en, grouped 21:1-6) — NAMED for the opening framing (warning of the Hour, people heedless, not preparing) → §1.1; and the reading of the two whispered charges consistent with the Arabic Ibn Kathir → §4.1, §6.1.

## Hadith
- Checked Bukhari 660 (suggested in PREP) — fetched https://quranx.com/hadith/Bukhari/DarusSalam/Hadith-660 : the "seven whom Allah will shade" hadith (Abu Huraira). NOT related to 21:3. DROPPED.
- Surah-virtue narration in t169: al-Bukhari from Ibn Mas'ud, Surat al-Anbiya among the earliest/most beautiful surahs ("my treasure"). SOUND (Bukhari) but a virtue of the whole surah, not the verse. Mentioned once in §1.3 explicitly marked as a surah virtue, not a comment on 21:3.
- No sound hadith attaches to the words/meaning of 21:3 itself. Stated in §1.3.

## Asbab an-nuzul
- None established/confirmed on a fetched page. Muyassar and the mufassirun place the whisper with Quraysh's deniers as the sura's Makkan setting; used the placement, not an asbab claim. No asbab asserted.

## Disagreements kept
- Meaning of `أسروا`: hidden vs. made public (Abu Ubayda's addad note in Qurtubi) — kept as a genuine ambiguity in §3.2 without picking a side; Ma'arif leans hidden, noted as its lean.
- `اقترب حسابهم`: last-ummah nearness vs. death-as-reckoning (as-Sa'di) — both kept in §1.2.

## Watch-points honoured
- Licensing (§7): zalimun = the Makkan deniers, condemned for heedlessness + mockery + hidden obstinacy; stated in both languages that the verse licenses nothing against any living person/community and no verdict on a heart is ours (as-Sa'di: the account is Allah's). 
- 21:7 sibling: did NOT give the mortal-messenger answer. §4.3 says only that "the Qur'an takes up this premise in the verses that follow" without stating the answer; did not preempt 21:4-6.
- Two charges + contradiction (mortal / magic; claim to clear sight vs. calling truth magic) carried in §5.3 and §6.

## Dropped / not written
- Grammatical i'rab discussion (five wajh in Qurtubi/Tabari on `الذين ظلموا`) — omitted as register noise, not dropped for lack of confirmation.
- Qurtubi's aside `وما علموا أن الله لا يجوز أن يرسل إليهم إلا بشرا` (a brief refutation) — deliberately not used, to avoid preempting 21:7's answer.
