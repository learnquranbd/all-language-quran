Assigned 3:21, widened to 3:21-22 (the charge and its verdict: deeds void,
no helpers). 3:20 and 3:23-25 left free. New card and a 1,767-word,
8-section article on killing prophets "without right", the honour of those
who command justice, and how we treat whoever brings an unwelcome truth.

Sources: at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi, Ibn Kathir (ar + en abridged), as-Sa'di,
al-Baghawi, al-Muyassar, Ma'arif on 3:21 and 3:22 (all slots on target).
- Licensing sentence in both languages: past deeds, nothing against any
  living person or community, 6:164; approval is chosen, not inherited.
- Bi-ghayri haqq as Ibn Kathir gives it (no cause, no crime, only the call
  to truth), with al-Muyassar, Ma'arif, and as-Sa'di on the prophets' right.
- Those who command justice: al-Mubarrad, Ma'qil, Qatada; as-Sa'di's
  "kindness and sincere counsel"; al-Qurtubi's "succession of prophethood".
Disagreements kept: yaqtuluna vs yuqatiluna (at-Tabari's preference,
al-Baghawi's Hamza); Ibn 'Abd al-Barr's consensus bound by ability vs
Ibn al-'Arabi's going ahead under threat, with al-Qurtubi's note on both.
Hadith: Muslim 91 (pride, quoted by Ibn Kathir on the verse); Muslim 49
(hand, tongue, heart, via al-Qurtubi); Tirmidhi 2174 (a word of justice
before a tyrant, hasan gharib), marked as not attached to the verse.
No asbab.
Dropped: all day-count figures (Abu 'Ubaydah report described without
them, its unknown narrator noted from at-Tabari's editor; Ibn Mas'ud's
counts ungraded); as-Sa'di's line naming a community and the curse and
humiliation lines in at-Tabari and Ibn Kathir; al-Qurtubi's weak or
unsourced narrations.
