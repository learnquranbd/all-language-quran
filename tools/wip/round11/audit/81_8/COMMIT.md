Card and 8-section bilingual article for 81:8, wa-idha al-maw'udatu
su'ilat (3 Arabic words). 81:9 named only as the next verse (the content
of the question); 81:10-14 left for their own slots.

Sources: at-Tabari, Ibn Kathir (ar), al-Qurtubi, al-Baghawi, as-Sa'di on
81:8; Muyassar (grouped 81:1-10); Ma'arif (81:8-10); Ibn Kathir en (81:1-10).
- al-Maw'uda glossed side by side: madfuna hayya (Tabari), maqtula and
  girl buried alive (Qurtubi), tifla (Muyassar), Ibn Abbas "the buried".
  Root kept as a difference: Qurtubi links to 2:255; note in Baghawi says
  wa'd, not awd.
- Motives as each states them: dislike of daughters (Ibn Kathir), shame and
  need (Baghawi), poverty only (Sa'di), shame (Ma'arif), angels-as-daughters
  claim, want, captivity (Qurtubi, 16:59). Qurtubi's refusers and Sa'sa'a.
- su'ilat (majority, Tabari's preference by consensus of readers) vs
  sa'alat (Abu ad-Duha, Ibn Abbas, Ubayy's codex); qutthilat noted.
- Meaning of the asking kept unresolved: rebuke (Qurtubi, al-Hasan, 5:116),
  threat (Ibn Kathir), comfort and shaming (Muyassar), tulibat (33:15),
  girl or killers (Ma'arif). Ma'arif's "no one to bring her case".
- Children-of-idolaters point reported as Qurtubi's; Ibn Kathir's
  disagreeing narrations noted, not weighed.
Hadith: Muslim 1442b (Judama bint Wahb, 'azl as hidden burial; verse
words as Ubaidullah's addition), quoted whole via hadith.js; no grading
beyond Muslim's Sahih. Ma'arif's note on other 'azl hadith reported.
Asbab: none given; not used. Licensing sentence in both languages.
Dropped: Qays ibn 'Asim (unconfirmable), fin-nar/fil-janna narrations,
Ikrima marfu', pit description, generalising lines about Arabs, Ma'arif's
abortion ruling, 16:58/6:151/17:31/43:17 (not cited by fetched tafsirs).
Fix: Muslim 1442b grading line reworded (no 'his judgement'); page's mistranslation '(to the suckling women)' noted from al-Qurtubi's Arabic.
