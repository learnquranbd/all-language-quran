Card + 8-section bilingual article on the gardens and grapevines.

Sources fetched for 78:32: at-Tabari, Ibn Kathir (ar + en), al-Qurtubi,
as-Sa'di, al-Baghawi, Muyassar, Ma'arif al-Qur'an; all named, plus the
in-repo Ahsanul Bayaan.
- Hada'iq restates mafaz: at-Tabari (tarjama wa bayan, mafaz a masdar),
  al-Qurtubi (hadha tafsir al-fawz), Ahsanul Bayaan (badal).
- Mafaz kept as a disagreement: Ibn 'Abbas and ad-Dahhak (a place of
  recreation) vs Mujahid and Qatadah (success, saved from the Fire);
  Ibn Kathir's preference for the first reported as his.
- Hadiqa walled: at-Tabari (no wall, no hadiqa) and al-Qurtubi
  (muhawwat, ahdaqa = ahata); Ibn Kathir, as-Sa'di, Muyassar gloss
  without the wall.
- A'nab = kurum a'nab, vines elided (al-Qurtubi, at-Tabari); grapes
  singled out for nobility and abundance (as-Sa'di); trees of Paradise
  and their fruits (al-Baghawi).
Counts from the data: grape noun in 11 verses, only 78:32 in the
Hereafter; palm beside it in 9 of the other 10; hada'iq in 27:60, 78:32,
80:30.

Hadith: none; no fetched tafsir attaches one, stated in the article.
No asbab. 78:21-30 contrast kept to one sentence with the no-verdict line;
78:33-36 left for their own pages; 36:34 palm-and-vine point referred to,
not repeated.
Dropped: muttaqin root gloss and diptote note (not in a fetched text).
