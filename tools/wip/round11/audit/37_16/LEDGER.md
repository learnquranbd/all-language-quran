## Attributions and the clause each rests on
- al-Qurtubi restatement + kind of question: «أي أنبعث إذا متنا ؟ . فهو استفهام إنكار منهم وسخرية»
- al-Baghawi: entry is only the verse text «" أإذا متنا وكنا ترابا وعظاما أإنا لمبعوثون "»; article says he quotes it and adds nothing.
- Ibn Kathir (ar) two verbs: «يستبعدون ذلك ويكذبون به»
- Ibn Kathir (en): "They thought that this was unlikely to happen, and they did not believe it."
- Ibn Kathir (en) heading + Ibn Mas'ud + why-question (group 37:11-19): "The Certainty of Life after Death"; "Ibn Mas`ud said that they admitted that these things were harder to create than they were. If this is the case, then why do they deny the resurrection, when they see things that are greater than that which they deny"
- Ibn Kathir (en) Qatadah on 37:12: "Qatadah said, \"Muhammad was astounded by the mockery of the misguided ones among the sons of Adam.\""
- as-Sa'di wonder + analogy + manner: «ومن العجب أيضا، قياسهم قدرة رب الأرض والسماوات، على قدرة الآدمي الناقص من جميع الوجوه، فقالوا استبعادا وإنكارا»
- al-Muyassar (group 37:15-17): «أإذا متنا وصِرْنا ترابًا وعظامًا بالية أإنا لمبعوثون من قبورنا أحياء»
- Ma'arif (group 37:15-18): ayah of 37:14 = a seen miracle, not rational argument nor Qur'anic verses; "declaring an evidence and argument to be open magic does not make any sense"; "The verses of the Qur'an were not seen. Instead, they were heard."
