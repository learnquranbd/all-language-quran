Card + 8-section bilingual article (1,679 EN words) for 37:16, "when we
have died and become dust and bones, are we indeed to be resurrected?" The
deniers' own voice after 37:12-15; set against 37:11's question, pointed
at rather than rebuilt.

Sources: al-Qurtubi, al-Baghawi, Ibn Kathir (ar + abridged en, group
37:11-19), as-Sa'di, al-Muyassar (group 37:15-17), Ma'arif al-Qur'an
(group 37:15-18), each quoted from the text fetched for 37:16.
- Qurtubi: istifham inkar wa sukhriya, restated as a-nub'athu idha mitna.
- Ibn Kathir: yastab'iduna ... wa yukadhdhibuna; Sa'di: istib'adan wa
  inkaran. Read as two steps, deeming far then denying; 50:3 raj'un ba'id.
- Sa'di: min al-'ajab; qiyas of the Lord's power on deficient human power.
- Muyassar: bones baliya, raised from graves alive.
- Ma'arif: the sign of 37:14 a seen miracle, hence "magic" in 37:15.
- Ibn Kathir: Ibn Mas'ud's report and the why-question; Qatadah on 37:12.
- Grammar in one sentence: the double interrogative a-idha / a-inna.
- Recurrence: 23:82, 56:47, 17:49, 37:53; 23:81. Replies not quoted.
- Hadith: none; no fetched tafsir attaches one (said once). Bukhari 660
  not used. Asbab: none.
- at-Tabari: fetch failed, not used.
- Dropped: "qiyas fasid" as Sa'di's wording (not in the fetched text).
- Licensing guard EN+BN. 37:17-18 left as future ground.
