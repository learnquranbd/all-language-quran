# SOURCES ledger — 29:39

## Tafsir fetched (all from PREP.md, not re-fetched)

- **Ibn Kathir (ar, 14)** — NAMED. Identifies the three: Qārūn = owner of abundant
  wealth and keys of heavy treasures (وقارون صاحب الأموال الجزيلة ومفاتيح الكنوز الثقيلة);
  Pharaoh = king of Egypt in Moses' time; Hāmān = his minister; the two Copts who
  disbelieved in Allah and His Messenger (ووزيره هامان القبطيان الكافران بالله ورسوله).
- **Ibn Kathir (en abridged, 169)** — NAMED. Same identification of the three; and on
  29:40 the differentiated ends used in §6: Qārūn "caused the earth to swallow," Pharaoh
  + Hāmān + troops "drowned... in a single morning, not one of them escaped," each
  "punished for his sins," "It was not Allah Who wronged them... they wronged themselves."
- **at-Tabari (15)** — NAMED. Moses came to جميعهم (all of them) with the bayyināt;
  istikbār = عن التصديق من الآيات وعن اتباع موسى (against affirming the signs and following
  Moses); mā kānū sābiqīn = وما كانوا سابقينا بأنفسهم فيفوتوننا بل كنا مقتدرين عليهم (they
  could not outrun Us and escape; We were fully able over them). Used in §3, §4, §6.
- **al-Qurtubi (90)** — NAMED. The grammar dispute (al-Kisāʾī): carried on ʿĀd/Thamūd
  (29:38), or on fa-ṣaddahum ʿani-s-sabīl, or an elided "ahlaknā" (وقيل أي وأهلكنا هؤلاء);
  sābiqīn = فائتين (escaping), with a second reading سابقين في الكفر answered by بل قد سبقهم
  للكفر قرون كثيرة. Used in §2, §6.
- **as-Sa'di (91)** — NAMED. istikbār in two directions: على عباد الله فأذلوهم (over God's
  servants, humiliating them) and على الحق فردوه (against the truth, rejecting it); فلم يقدروا
  على النجاء حين نزلت بهم العقوبة (no escape when the punishment fell); mā kānū sābiqīn =
  الله ولا فائتين بل سلموا واستسلموا (not outrunning Allah nor escaping; they surrendered and
  submitted). Used in §4, §6.
- **al-Baghawi (94)** — NAMED. Elided verb أهلكنا هؤلاء; bayyināt = بالدلالات; sābiqīn =
  فائتين من عذابنا (escaping Our punishment). Used in §2, §6.
- **al-Muyassar (16)** — NAMED. Supplies وأهلكنا (and We destroyed); جاءهم جميعًا موسى;
  تعاظموا... واستكبروا; ولم يكونوا ليفوتوننا بل كنا مقتدرين عليهم. Used in §2, §6.
- **Ma'arif al-Qur'an (en, 168)** — NAMED. On 29:38 mustabṣirīn = "people of insight";
  "not fools or insane... very clever having insight, but their intelligence... confined
  to mundane considerations"; links 30:7 ("know what is apparent of the worldly life, but
  of the Hereafter they are negligent"); notes the three stories "just passed in Surah
  Al-Qasas." Used in §1, §5.

## Hadith

- **Sahih Muslim 91 (91a)** — USED in §7. URL fetched:
  https://quranx.com/hadith/Muslim/Hadith-91 (saved to this dir).
  First ten words (EN used): "He who has in his heart the weight of a mustard seed of
  pride shall not enter Paradise." + definition "Pride is disdaining the truth ... and
  contempt for the people." Narrator: ʿAbdullah ibn Masʿūd. Grading: ṣaḥīḥ (in Sahih
  Muslim); not upgraded. Marked in the article as a general teaching on kibr, NOT tied to
  the verse. Chosen distinct from 28:76, which used the Bukhari garment-dragging (isbāl)
  hadith (Abu Hurayra) — verified via loadTadabburArticles()['28:76'].

## Disputes kept (no position taken)

- Syntax of "wa-Qārūna wa-Firʿawna wa-Hāmāna": elided "We destroyed" (Baghawi, Muyassar)
  vs. coordinated with ʿĀd/Thamūd of 29:38 vs. coordinated with "ṣaddahum" (Qurtubi via
  al-Kisāʾī). Kept open in §2.
- Reading of sābiqīn: outrunning/escaping the punishment (Tabari, Baghawi, Muyassar, Saʿdi)
  vs. preceding others in disbelief (Qurtubi, who himself answers it). Kept in §6.
