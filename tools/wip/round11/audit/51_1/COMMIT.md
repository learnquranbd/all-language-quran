Card and 9-section bilingual article for 51:1, the surah's opening
oath: wa-dh-dhariyati dharwa. 51:2-4 named only as the next verses;
51:5 named as the oath's answer, not developed.

Sources: Tabari, Ibn Kathir (ar; en abridged), Muyassar, Qurtubi,
Sa'di, Baghawi, Ma'arif.
- Gloss: winds that scatter dust (Tabari, Baghawi, dharat/adhrat),
  winds that stir up dust (Muyassar), Qurtubi's two masdars; Sa'di:
  dharwan by softness and gentleness, strength and force.
- 'Ali at the Kufa minbar, answered "the wind" (IK, "thabata min
  ghayri wajh"); Tabari's twelve reports with varying settings, plus
  Ibn 'Abbas and Mujahid; ahl at-ta'wil agree.
- Qurtubi: "sal tafaqquhan wa la tas'al ta'annutan"; Tabari ties Ibn
  al-Kawwa' to Sabigh's question to 'Umar; Qurtubi's Sabigh report.
  Both ungraded and marked so. Licensing sentence in both languages.
- Oath: Qurtubi (oath affirms honour vs qila: "by the Lord of the
  dhariyat"), kept unranked; Sa'di (mighty created things, benefit,
  proof, why deny?); Muyassar's one-sentence oath and answer.
- Qurtubi's unattributed qila, women who bear children, recorded
  as recorded and not adopted.
Hadith: none attached by any tafsir; said in one sentence. Ma'arif's
note that IK held the marfu' version weak is reported, not quoted.
No asbab.
Dropped: qira'at (idgham) not fetched; marfu' chain; 51:2-4 detail.
