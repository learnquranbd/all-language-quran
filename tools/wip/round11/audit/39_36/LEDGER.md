## Hadith
- Jami' at-Tirmidhi 2349. URL: https://quranx.com/hadith/Tirmidhi/Hadith-2349 (saved: hadith_Tirmidhi_2349.txt). Same chain as Ibn Kathir's (Hayway ibn Shurayh, Abu Hani al-Khawlani, 'Amr ibn Malik al-Janbi, Fadala ibn 'Ubayd). First ten words used (quranx EN): "Glad tidings to whoever is guided to Islam, his livelihood was". Grading on the page: «هذا حديث حسن صحيح» (hasan sahih). Ibn Kathir reports at-Tirmidhi as saying "sahih"; the article gives both and quotes Tirmidhi's own wording (tuba ...), not Ibn Abi Hatim's (aflaha ...). Marked as not about the verse's occasion. An-Nasa'i's copy not fetched; named only as Ibn Kathir names it.

## Asbab
- No asbab an-nuzul established in the fetched texts. Ma'arif says the verse came "in the background of" the idol-curse threat (attributed to Ma'arif only). Qatada's Khalid/al-'Uzza report used as Qatada's illustration of the clause (at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi), explicitly not as an occasion of revelation. Al-Wahidi not fetched.
