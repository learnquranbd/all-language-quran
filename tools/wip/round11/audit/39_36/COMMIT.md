Replaces the 735-word, 5-section article with a 1,751-word, 8-section deep article built only from the eight tafsir files fetched for 39:36. The shipped card is kept.

Sources: at-Tabari, Ibn Kathir (ar, en abridged), al-Qurtubi, as-Sa'di,
al-Baghawi, al-Muyassar, Ma'arif al-Qur'an.
- Question answered "bala": Ibn Zayd via at-Tabari; al-Muyassar (religion and
  world, harm pushed away). Placement after 39:33-35 from the text.
- Kafin without its ya, restored as kafi in pause by some Arabs (al-Qurtubi);
  sufficiency for whoever worships and relies (Ibn Kathir); tied to fulfilled
  servanthood, the Prophet first (as-Sa'di).
- Readings 'ibadah/'abdah: at-Tabari (Madinan/Kufan/Basran split), al-Qurtubi
  (Hamza, al-Kisa'i), al-Baghawi (Abu Ja'far, Hamza, al-Kisa'i; 40:5).
  Disagreement kept: at-Tabari holds both sound; Abu 'Ubayda (via al-Qurtubi)
  chose the singular for the following "yukhawwifunaka". Generic 'abd (103:2)
  and al-Jurjani's reward/punishment sense (al-Qurtubi); Ma'arif's two camps.
- The threat: at-Tabari, as-Suddi, Ibn Zayd, Ibn Kathir; the threateners'
  words (al-Qurtubi, al-Baghawi); Companions threatened (Ma'arif); numbers
  and strength, 54:44 (al-Qurtubi). Licensing sentence in both languages.
- Qatada's report of Khalid at al-'Uzza (at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi), marked as an
  illustration, not an occasion of revelation; Yaqut's note on Suqam.
- Sister verses only as cited: 6:81 (al-Qurtubi), 11:54-56 (Ibn Kathir en at
  39:38). 39:37 and 39:38 via Ibn Kathir en; 39:23 ending noted ("taqaddam").
Hadith: Tirmidhi 2349 (Fadala ibn 'Ubayd), attached by Ibn Kathir (ar),
quoted whole, graded hasan sahih by at-Tirmidhi (Ibn Kathir reports "sahih").
Dropped from the old article: the added-ba grammar claim, the yukhawwifuna
morphology claim, 65:3, the "answered two verses later" framing, and the Ibn
Abbas narration it placed under 39:36 (Ibn Kathir has it at 39:38; wording
not confirmed in a collection).
