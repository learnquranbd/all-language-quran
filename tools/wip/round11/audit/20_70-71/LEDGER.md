# SOURCES ledger — 20:70-71

Two-verse entry. 20:70 (magicians prostrate, "Lord of Aaron and Moses") and
20:71 (Pharaoh's rebuke, conspiracy charge, cross-amputation + crucifixion threat)
covered in balance. Future ground 20:72-73 (the magicians' reply "we will never
prefer you… decree what you will") deliberately NOT preempted.

## Tafsir fetched (files in this dir; none re-fetched)

Verse 70: t15 at-Tabari, t90 al-Qurtubi, t91 as-Sa'di, t14 Ibn Kathir (ar),
t16 al-Muyassar, t94 al-Baghawi, t168 Ma'arif (en), t169 Ibn Kathir (en, abr).
Verse 71: same eight IDs. t169_71 also groups 20:72-73 — only its 20:71 content used.

- al-Qurtubi (t90_71): rebuke "أي تعديتم وفعلتم ما لم آمركم به"; "رئيسكم في التعليم …

  يتعلموا من موسى، بل … قبل قدوم موسى وولادته" → §5.1 (rebuke) and §6.2 (deliberate lie

  أو الله … قلبا للحقائق وترهيبا" → §5.2, §6.3, §7.2 (the muharib-form note, tied to

- al-Baghawi (t94_71): "لرئيسكم ومعلمكم"; "أينا أشد عذابا؛ أنا … أو رب موسى", "أبقى: أدوم"

## Disagreement / nuance kept

Referent of "كبيركم الذي علمكم السحر": al-Baghawi and al-Muyassar gloss it as MUSA being
called their chief/teacher; al-Qurtubi/Ibn Kathir/as-Sa'di frame the same clause as the
false collusion charge. All agree the "he" = Musa and that the charge is a known lie, so
it is presented as a shared reading, not a dispute. No genuine opposed positions to hold.

## Hadith

No rigorously graded marfu' hadith is attached to 20:70 or 20:71 in any tafsir fetched.
Stated plainly in §3.3. The material is athar from the Companions/Tabi'un (Ibn Abbas,
Ubayd b. Umayr, Sa'id b. Jubayr, 'Ikrimah, al-Qasim b. Abi Bazza), all labelled as early
reports, not sound hadith. No hadith.js call made; none needed.

## Asbab

None used. These verses are narrative within a continuous Musa/Pharaoh sequence; placement
(the contest's aftermath) carries the meaning, per SOURCE-GATE §2. No asbab claimed.

## Dropped (could not confirm / avoided)

- The classic reason Aaron is NAMED FIRST (rhyme/fasila, or to foreclose "rabb Musa" being
  misread as Pharaoh) is NOT in any fetched file. §4 makes the foreclosing observation as a
  plain reading of the verse's own wording only, attributing nothing to a named mufassir.
- at-Tabari on 20:69/70 discusses the qira'at of "كيد ساحر" and "يُقتل الساحر حيث وُجد";
  not used — it belongs to 20:69 and risks preempting nothing but adds no value here.
- Number of magicians (Ibn Abbas: seventy; other reports differ) in t169_70 — dropped to
  avoid asserting a disputed count.

## Licensing (required, 20:71)

§7.2 states in both languages that the punishment is the condemned tyrant Pharaoh's threat,
in no way an endorsement or an Islamic prescription, describing what the text describes and
licensing nothing against any living person or community. No verdict on anyone living.
