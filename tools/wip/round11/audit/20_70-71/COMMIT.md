Two-verse entry covering 20:70 (the magicians fall in prostration,
"We have believed in the Lord of Aaron and Moses") and 20:71 (Pharaoh's
rebuke, the conspiracy charge, and the cross-amputation and crucifixion
threat), held in balance. Card plus an 8-section bilingual article.

From the sources: al-Qurtubi's 300-camel load and the staff returning to a
staff; al-Muyassar and as-Sa'di on the proof standing and the sign becoming
mercy for believers and an argument against the obstinate; Ma'arif and Ibn
Kathir (en) on the magicians as experts who knew at once it was no trick.
The prostration read from the passive verb; Sa'id b. Jubayr's report of
their places in Paradise shown while prostrate (via Ibn Kathir), and Ma'arif's
glimpse of the Garden and the Fire, both marked as early athar. The "Lord of
Aaron and Moses" wording treated as fixing faith past every man (al-Qurtubi's
grammar note), with the Aaron-first observation made only from the verse text.

On 20:71: al-Qurtubi, Ibn Kathir and as-Sa'di on the rebuke and the known-false
conspiracy charge (as-Sa'di's refutation: Moses came from Madyan alone and met
no magician); the al-Baghawi/al-Muyassar reading of "which of us" as Pharaoh or
the Lord of Moses, and as-Sa'di on Pharaoh claiming to outdo his Creator.

Licensing: the threatened punishment is stated in both languages to be the
condemned tyrant's, no endorsement and no Islamic prescription, licensing
nothing against any living person; no verdict on anyone living.

Hadith: no sound marfu' hadith attaches to either verse in the tafsir consulted;
said plainly in the article. Dropped: the disputed count of magicians and the
unattested reason for Aaron being named first. Future ground 20:72-73 not
preempted. All gates clean.
