Card and 8-section bilingual article for 48:8: inna arsalnaka shahidan
wa mubashshiran wa nadhira. Placed after the 48:7 refrain, before the
48:9 purpose clause and the 48:10 pledge.

Sources: Tabari, Ibn Kathir (ar; en for the 48:10 placement), Muyassar,
Qurtubi, Sa'di, Ma'arif; Baghawi gives the verse text only.
- Address: no vocative here, unlike 33:45 (IK's pointer to al-Ahzab);
  Tabari "ya Muhammad", Muyassar, Sa'di supply it.
- Shahid kept as differing readings, no pick: over the creation (IK);
  over his nation by their answer (Tabari) or by the conveying
  (Muyassar, Qatada via Qurtubi); deeds / mubayyin / the Day, joined
  by Qurtubi (Sa'id ibn Jubayr pointer to an-Nisa); Sa'di's three
  (deeds, true and false sayings, Allah's oneness); Ma'arif with 4:41,
  own-generation (Qurtubi as cited) vs whole community.
- Qurtubi's hal muqaddara with Sibawayh's falcon example.
- Mubashshir/nadhir per source: by people (IK), by response (Tabari),
  obey/disobey (Muyassar, Qatada), Sa'di's worldly/religious/next-life
  reward and his point that both require naming the deeds.
- 48:9 only as a pointer: Muyassar's run-on gloss, IK/Ibn 'Abbas on
  tu'azziruhu, Ma'arif's note that the pronouns are disputed.
Hadith: none attached to 48:8 by any fetched tafsir; said in one line.
Asbab: none used; Hudaybiyyah only via IK on 48:10 and Ma'arif.
Dropped: Ma'arif's unreferenced "angels present the deeds" report,
48:9 lexical notes, 48:10 pledge numbers and hadiths, 2:143.
