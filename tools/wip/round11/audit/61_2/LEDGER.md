## Asbab: genuine dispute, all kept as reports, none chosen
(1) wish for the most beloved deed, then dislike when jihad was prescribed (Ibn 'Abbas via at-Tabari; Mujahid with Ibn Rawaha); variant with no falling short (Ibn 'Abbas, 'Awfi chain); Uhud variants (al-Baghawi "the exegetes", Muqatil ibn Hayyan via Ibn Kathir, al-Kalbi and Muhammad ibn Ka'b via al-Qurtubi/al-Baghawi). (2) boasting of battle deeds not done (at-Tabari's 2nd view, Qatada; Qatada & ad-Dahhak via al-Baghawi; Mihran & ad-Dahhak via al-Qurtubi); Suhayb's false-claimant story (al-Qurtubi). (3) hypocrites (Ibn Zayd via al-Baghawi, al-Qurtubi). Licensing sentence in both languages. The Companions' falling short is stated only in the sources' words, with Ma'arif's genuine-intention framing beside it.

## Hadith
- USED: Bukhari 33 (DarusSalam), https://quranx.com/hadith/Bukhari/DarusSalam/Hadith-33, Abu Hurayrah, «آيَةُ الْمُنَافِقِ ثَلاَثٌ إِذَا حَدَّثَ كَذَبَ، وَإِذَا وَعَدَ أَخْلَفَ» ("The signs of a hypocrite are three: whenever he speaks..."). Quoted whole (matn rendered from the Arabic, no numbering). Grading: in al-Bukhari's Sahih; attached to the verse by Ibn Kathir.
- USED: Tirmidhi 3309, https://quranx.com/hadith/Tirmidhi/Hadith-3309, Ibn Salam, "A group of us Companions of the Messenger of Allah sat talking...". Quoted through "recited it to us"; the remaining lines (each narrator reciting to the next) summarised in one sentence. At-Tirmidhi gives no grade on the page; his note «وَقَدْ خُولِفَ مُحَمَّدُ بْنُ كَثِيرٍ فِي إِسْنَادِ هَذَا الْحَدِيثِ عَنِ الأَوْزَاعِيِّ» is reported. Ibn al-'Arabi's «لا بأس به» (via al-Qurtubi) reported as his.
- CHECKED, NOT USED: Bukhari 34 (four traits; its wording has «إِذَا عَاهَدَ غَدَرَ», not breaking a promise, so Ibn Kathir's point is reported as his without quoting). Muslim 1050 (Abu Musa, forgotten musabbiha surah quoted by al-Qurtubi; confirmed, left out as length and outside the verse's meaning). Abu Dawud 4991 ('Abdullah ibn 'Amir, dates; cited by Ibn Kathir): chain has an unnamed mawla and no grading shown on the page, so dropped.
- DROPPED: Abu Nu'aym's report via Anas of preachers' lips cut with scissors of fire (al-Qurtubi): not in the six collections, grading unconfirmable.
