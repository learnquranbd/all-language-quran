Replaces the 816-word, 6-section article with an 8-section, 1,786-word
source-gated article (shipped card kept).

Sources: at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi, as-Sa'di, Ibn Kathir (ar; en abridged,
grouped 61:1-4), al-Baghawi, Muyassar, Ma'arif (grouped 61:2-3).
Glosses of the question (at-Tabari; al-Qurtubi's denial-and-rebuke, lie
for the past and broken promise for the future; Muyassar); kabura
maqtan (at-Tabari, Ma'arif) and Ma'arif's major-sin / makruh ruling with
If Allah wills. Promise and vow fiqh: Ibn Kathir's salaf view, Ibn
al-'Arabi via al-Qurtubi (Malik and Abu Hanifa vs ash-Shafi'i on
conditional vows), Malik and al-Qurtubi's court vs character, 2:177,
19:54. Sufyan ibn 'Uyayna's reading; 61:4 and 61:14 one line each.
Disagreements kept: the occasion (wish for the dearest deed, with
Ibn Rawaha and the Uhud variants; boasting of battle deeds, with
Suhayb's false claimant; Ibn Zayd's hypocrites), none chosen, with
Ma'arif's genuine-intention framing; whether the verse reaches the
preacher (as-Sa'di, al-Muthanna via al-Qurtubi) or only false claims
(Ma'arif, who says lax preachers should not stop). Licensing sentence
in both languages.

Hadith: Bukhari 33 (Abu Hurayrah, signs of a hypocrite), attached by
Ibn Kathir, quoted whole; Tirmidhi 3309 (Ibn Salam's gathering), with
at-Tirmidhi's note on the disputed chain and no grade claimed.

Dropped: Abu Dawud 4991 (unnamed narrator), Abu Nu'aym's preachers
report, Muslim 1050; from the old article the "joined with lead" gloss,
the maqt-severity claim, the Usamah hadith, 'A'isha reports, 3:104,
2:271, 33:21. Fixed: promise-breaking was attributed to at-Tabari (it
is Ibn Kathir/Muyassar); "no hypocrite is unmasked" contradicted Ibn Zayd.
