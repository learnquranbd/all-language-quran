Replaces the 741-word, 6-section article with a 1,778-word, 9-section article built only on the tafsir fetched for 59:22-24 (shipped card kept).

Sources: at-Tabari, Ibn Kathir (ar; en abridged as cross-check), al-Qurtubi,
as-Sa'di, al-Baghawi, Muyassar, Ma'arif on each verse. Placement via
at-Tabari's link to the splitting mountain of 59:21 and Ma'arif's closing
attributes. Each name glossed only as the tafsirs give it, every gloss named:
ghayb/shahada (Ibn Abbas, Sahl, al-Baghawi); ar-Rahim tied to believers
(at-Tabari, Muyassar) beside Ibn Kathir's both-worlds reading, kept as is;
al-Quddus (Wahb, Mujahid/Qatada, Ibn Jurayj); as-Salam as free of fault vs
keeping creation safe, with Ibn al-'Arabi's three views; al-Mu'min as granting
security (Ibn Abbas) vs confirming (Qatada, Ibn Zayd, ad-Dahhak, as-Sa'di) vs
declaring His oneness (Mujahid, 3:18); al-Muhaymin as at-Tabari's stated
dispute (witness / trustworthy / confirmer) plus al-Baghawi's mu'aymin
derivation and further holders, no position taken; al-Jabbar as mighty /
mending / compelling; al-Mutakabbir; khalq-bar'-taswir order (Ibn Kathir,
al-Qurtubi, al-Baghawi). Jabir ibn Zayd's greatest-name saying attributed.
Hadith: Muslim 2620 (izar and rida'), Bukhari 2736 (ninety-nine names),
Tirmidhi 2922 (Ma'qil ibn Yasar, seventy thousand angels) quoted whole with
at-Tirmidhi's own grading, gharib. 7:180 in one line.
Dropped: al-Qurtubi's ungraded reports on reciting al-Hashr, Ma'arif's
picture-making ruling, odd readings (cut for length).
Defect fixed: the old article gave the kibriya' hadith to Abu Hurayrah alone
in a wording Muslim does not have; also dropped its unsourced tashahhud
hadith, Ibn Mas'ud supplication, 6:59, 3:6, 17:110, 20:8 and its claim about
the enumerated list's soundness.
