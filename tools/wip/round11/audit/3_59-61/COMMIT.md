Assigned 3:59, widened to 3:59-61 (the likeness of 'Isa as Adam, the truth
from your Lord, the mubahalah). 3:56-58 and 3:62-63 left free. New card and
a 1,756-word, 8-section article centred on the argument from Adam and the
mubahalah as the response of certainty, kept apart from 2:116-117's use of 3:59.

Sources: at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi, Ibn Kathir (ar + en abridged), as-Sa'di,
al-Baghawi, al-Muyassar, Ma'arif on 3:59, 3:60 and 3:61 (all slots on target).
- The argument a fortiori: at-Tabari ("no more wondrous"), Ibn Kathir (sonship
  would fit Adam first; four modes of creation, 19:21), al-Muyassar (all agree
  Adam is a servant), as-Sa'di (not even a doubt, let alone a proof).
- Likeness in one respect and qiyas (al-Qurtubi, al-Baghawi); al-Qurtubi on 25:33.
- Fa-la takun min al-mumtarin: address to the Prophet, the ummah meant
  (al-Qurtubi, al-Baghawi); al-Muyassar's reassurance; never doubt in him.
- As-Sa'di's rule: certainty stands whether or not every objection is untied.
- Licensing sentence in both languages; respectful register (the delegation's
  prayer in the mosque, their return on their own religion, from Ibn Ishaq).
Disagreements kept: the referent of "lahu" (al-Baghawi 'Isa; al-Muyassar and
at-Tabari Adam); fa-yakun (al-Qurtubi future-for-past vs at-Tabari fresh
statement); nabtahil as humble supplication (Ibn Abbas, al-Kalbi) or mutual
cursing (Abu 'Ubaydah, al-Kisa'i, at-Tabari).
Hadith: Bukhari 4380 (Hudhayfah; Najran's leaders decline, Abu 'Ubaydah sent),
quoted whole; Tirmidhi 2999 (Sa'd; who was called), hasan gharib sahih, quoted
whole with no commentary. "When I supplicate, say amin" marked ungraded.
Asbab: the Najran delegation, as given in the fetched tafsir and Bukhari 4380.
Dropped: the "ourselves" debate and fiqh-school note; ungraded threat sayings;
jizya details; Ibn Kathir's Ibn Abbas, Jabir and al-Bayhaqi reports.
