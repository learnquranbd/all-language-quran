Replaces the 689-word, 5-section article with a 1,698-word, 8-section deep article, built only on tafsir fetched for 74:38 (shipped card kept).

Sources: at-Tabari (74:38 slot plus his 74:39 and 74:40 slots, where his
reports on this verse are filed), Ibn Kathir (ar; en abridged for 74:38-48),
al-Qurtubi, as-Sa'di, al-Baghawi, al-Muyassar, Ma'arif al-Qur'an.

Rahinah glossed by name: detained (Ibn Kathir, from Ibn Abbas), seized
(Ibn Abbas via at-Tabari), pledged and held, freeing or ruining (al-Qurtubi),
pledged in the Fire (al-Baghawi), confined until rights are paid (Muyassar),
bound to the neck (as-Sa'di); Ma'arif's pawnbroker image and its two readings
of where the detention is. Al-Qurtubi's grammar kept: rahinah is a noun meaning
rahn, not rahin made feminine for nafs, with his own contrast to 52:21.

Early reports via at-Tabari: Qatadah, ad-Dahhak, Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Ibn Zayd.
Companions of the right kept as a disagreement: children (Ali via Zadhan),
angels (Ibn Abbas via Abu Zabyan), with the shared reasoning at-Tabari gives;
sincere believers / right-hand book (Muyassar, Ma'arif). No position taken.
74:40-48 in one section from Ibn Kathir; licensing sentence in both languages.

Hadith: none attached by any fetched tafsir; said in one sentence. Ma'arif's
unnamed tradition mentioned as unnamed, not quoted. Asbab: none.

Fixed from the old article: "rahinah is feminine because nafs is feminine"
(al-Qurtubi says the opposite); "a pledge is not a punishment" (at-Tabari,
al-Baghawi, ad-Dahhak, as-Sa'di place it in the Fire). Dropped: root count and
2:283, 52:21's descendants context, the unsourced two-by-two reading of the
four confessions.
