## Readings kept side by side (no position taken)
- Content of the claim: what you want and desire (IK, Muyassar); good and honour with Allah, within that covenant (Baghawi); entry to Paradise (Qurtubi); your own ruling (Tabari). Presented as differing levels of specificity, not as a dispute.
- Syntax of "inna lakum lama tahkumun": content of the oaths, accusative position (Qurtubi's main, matches Tabari/Baghawi) vs. "qila" view of a fresh clause with "idhan", "the matter is not so" (Qurtubi). Both reported; no preference.
- Qira'at on balighatun: nominative na't (al-'amma) vs accusative hal (al-Hasan al-Basri); Ibn Hurmuz's "ayna" in 68:38 and 68:39. All via Qurtubi only.
