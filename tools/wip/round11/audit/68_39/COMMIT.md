Card and 8-section bilingual article for 68:39: am lakum aymanun
'alayna balighatun ila yawm al-qiyamah, inna lakum lama tahkumun.
Neighbours 68:34-35, 68:37-38, 68:40-42 only as the sources mention them.

Sources: Tabari, Ibn Kathir (ar), Muyassar, Qurtubi, Sa'di, Baghawi,
Ma'arif (grouped 68:35-43). All named. Ibn Kathir (en) skipped: wrong
group (68:29-38).
- aymanun: 'uhud wa mawathiq (IK, Muyassar, Qurtubi, Baghawi); Tabari
  keeps aymanun; Sa'di 'ahd wa yamin. 'alayna: istawthaqtum (Qurtubi,
  Baghawi).
- balighatun: mu'akkada (Qurtubi, Baghawi, IK), al-mu'akkada bi-llah
  (Qurtubi); to the Day: tantahi bikum (Tabari), la yanqati' (Baghawi).
- lama tahkumun: hukmakum (Tabari); what you want and desire (IK,
  Muyassar); good and honour with Allah, within that covenant
  (Baghawi); entry to Paradise (Qurtubi).
- Kasra of inna from the lam (Tabari, Baghawi, Qurtubi); Qurtubi's
  "qila" view (fresh clause, idhan, "not so") kept beside his main one.
- Qira'at via Qurtubi only: al-'amma nominative; al-Hasan accusative
  hal with three attachments and the 2:241 analogy; Ibn Hurmuz "ayna".
- Sa'di's closing verdict; Ma'arif's justice argument on 68:35.
- Licensing sentence in both languages; no sorting list.
Hadith: none attached by any fetched tafsir; said in one sentence.
Asbab: none given.
Dropped: 45:21, 38:28, 2:111 (not cited); addressees' identity (not
named in fetched text); morphology from memory.
