Replaces the 789-word, 6-section article with a 9-section, 1,744-word
source-gated article (shipped card kept).

Sources: at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi, as-Sa'di, Ibn Kathir (ar, en abridged),
al-Baghawi, Muyassar, Ma'arif, all on 47:15 itself; Muyassar (local) on
47:6, 47:16-17. Covers the link to 47:12 (al-Qurtubi) and 47:14 (Ibn
Kathir, with 13:19 and 59:20); mathal glossed as description ('Ikrimah,
at-Tabari, al-Baghawi, as-Sa'di; 'Ali's reading mithal); the unstated
first half (at-Tabari) and three readings of the join kept side by side
(al-Farra', az-Zajjaj via 47:14, Ibn Kaysan); asin (Ibn Abbas, al-Hasan,
Qatadah, ad-Dahhak, 'Ata'), the short/long reading and al-Akhfash; milk
unmilked (at-Tabari, 'Ikrimah) vs unsoured (al-Qurtubi); honey created
flowing (at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi, Ibn Abbas); wine with 37:46-47 and 56:19
(Ibn Kathir) and at-Tabari on the genitive ladhdhatin; fruit with 44:55
and 55:52; forgiveness weighed four ways, with as-Sa'di's loved/feared
pair; the drink of the Fire in one line; 47:16-17, 47:4-6, 47:24, 47:31.

Licensing sentence on the people of the Fire in both languages.

Hadith: at-Tirmidhi 2571 (four seas), attached by al-Qurtubi and Ibn
Kathir, quoted whole, graded hasan sahih by at-Tirmidhi.

Dropped from the old article: the Ibn Abbas "only the names" saying (not
in any fetched text), 3:133, 9:72, 18:29, 14:16-17, the intensive-form
note; "the commentaries call forgiveness greatest" now attributed to the
Muyassar alone; Qatadah restored to the "not foul" group (Ibn Kathir).
Not used: ungraded marfu' fragments, Laqit, Abu Umamah, the Firdaws and
Sayhan hadiths (unconfirmed), "wine without sin" (no source).
