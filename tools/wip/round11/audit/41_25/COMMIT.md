Card and 8-section bilingual article for 41:25: wa qayyadna lahum
quranā', the companions who made fair what was before and behind them.
Placed after the Day-of-witnesses verses 41:20-24; 41:26 not touched.

Sources: Tabari, Ibn Kathir (ar; en abridged as check), Muyassar,
Qurtubi, Sa'di, Baghawi named. Ma'arif not named (its grouped text
covers only 41:22-23).
- qayyadna: Qurtubi's lexicon (ata'ahu lahu; Qushayri; taqyid as
  exchange); Baghawi ba'athna wa wakkalna, Muqatil hayya'na, Zajjaj
  sabbabna; Sa'di qadayna.
- Companions: devils (Tabari with Suddi and Mujahid, Baghawi, Sa'di,
  Naqqash via Qurtubi) vs devils of humans and jinn (Ibn Kathir,
  Muyassar, Qurtubi qila). Qurtubi's two minority qila reported.
- Before/behind: dunya/akhira (Tabari+Suddi, Baghawi, Muyassar, Mujahid);
  Ibn Abbas reversed; Zajjaj done/resolved; Qurtubi qila; Ibn Kathir
  past/future. Qurtubi's unstated "ansawhum"; Sa'di. No pick.
- Will and responsibility: Ibn Kathir, Tabari, Sa'di quoted only, with
  43:36-37 (IK, Sa'di) and 19:83 (Sa'di), verified in quran-json.
- haqqa al-qawl: al-'adhab; fi as ma'a vs fi jumla; khasirin per Tabari
  (maghbun), Qurtubi/Muyassar, Sa'di, Ibn Kathir.
Licensing sentence in both languages (condemned group).
Hadith: none attached by any fetched tafsir; said in one sentence.
Asbab: none given.
Dropped: Ma'arif's 41:22 narrations; IK-en 41:26-29 material; Arabic
of Qurtubi's poetry line (sense only).
Gate: nums check flagged "one another" (EN-only 1); reworded.
