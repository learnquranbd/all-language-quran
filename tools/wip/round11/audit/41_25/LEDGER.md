## Readings kept side by side (no position taken)
- Companions: devils (Tabari, Suddi, Mujahid, Baghawi, Sa'di, Naqqash via Qurtubi) vs devils of humans and jinn (Ibn Kathir, Muyassar; Qurtubi's qila: jinn, devils and humans). Qurtubi's two minority qila (companions in the Fire; mutual human need) reported as such.
- ma bayna aydihim / ma khalfahum: dunya/akhira (Tabari+Suddi, Baghawi, Muyassar, Mujahid via Qurtubi); reversed: denial of akhira / procrastination and enticement to dunya (Ibn Abbas via Qurtubi); done / resolved (Zajjaj via Qurtubi); like prior sins / what is done after them (qila, Qurtubi); past deeds / future self-image (Ibn Kathir). Qurtubi's unstated verb "ansawhum"; Sa'di's "made it far, made them forget". Article says explicitly it does not choose.
- Divine assignment vs responsibility: Ibn Kathir (His will, kawn, power; al-Hakim), Tabari (punishment due by what they committed), Sa'di (assignment because of their i'rad, with 43:36-37). Reported only; article states it adds no theology of its own.
- fi: ma'a (Baghawi, Qurtubi qila) vs fi jumla (Qurtubi qila, Sa'di).
