Card and 8-section bilingual article for 40:51: inna la-nansuru rusulana
wa-lladhina amanu fi l-hayati d-dunya wa-yawma yaqumu l-ashhad, placed
after the keepers' question in 40:50 (Sa'di's link to 40:45-50).

Sources: Tabari, Ibn Kathir (ar + en abridged), Muyassar, Qurtubi, Sa'di,
Baghawi, Ma'arif (grouped 40:51-55).
- Killed messengers: Tabari's objection (Sha'ya, Yahya killed; Ibrahim,
  'Isa escaped) and his two answers reported, each attributed: help by
  dominance (Dawud, Sulayman, Muhammad), by destroying and rescuing (Nuh,
  Musa), by vengeance after death (Sha'ya, Yahya/Bukhtnassar, 'Isa/Rum);
  or plural meaning one, Muhammad. IK's summary (general wording, some
  meant; intisar in presence, absence or after death), Ma'arif following
  it, as-Suddi via Tabari and Qurtubi. Baghawi: Ibn 'Abbas dominance,
  ad-Dahhak proof, vengeance; "one of these ways". Qurtubi: Abu al-'Aliya
  proofs. Sa'di: proof, evidence, victory; Hereafter judgment. No own
  reconciliation.
- Who is meant kept open: Musa and the believer (Qurtubi) / general /
  Muhammad (Tabari).
- Ashhad kept open: angels (Mujahid, Suddi; IK), hafaza (Baghawi), angels
  and prophets (Qatada), + believers (Muyassar), + bodies (Zayd b. Aslam).
  Qurtubi grammar (shahīd/shāhid, Zajjaj, Nahhas, Akhfash/Farra').
- IK's sunnah of Allah and the Prophet's career; Ma'arif on Makkah amnesty.
- Licensing sentence in both languages. 40:44 one line; 40:52 not used.
Hadith: Bukhari 6502 confirmed but not quoted (too long to quote whole;
general, not attached to the verse); said in one sentence. IK's second
narration and Qurtubi's two narrations dropped (no collection/grading).
Asbab: none; placement given.
Dropped: Ma'arif's "Shu'aib" (Arabic has Sha'ya), Baghawi's 70,000,
IK's Namrud/Dajjal details, group labels for the avenged-upon.
