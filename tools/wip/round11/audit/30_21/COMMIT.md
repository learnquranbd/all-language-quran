Replaces the 747-word, 5-section article with a 1,781-word, 8-section article
built only from the eight tafsir files fetched for 30:21. The shipped card is kept.

Sources: the run of six "wa min ayatihi" signs in 30:20-25, this the second
(Ma'arif), read with 30:20 as a pair (Ibn Kathir en, al-Qurtubi's "it is
said" on man from earth needing a sakan, at-Tabari's "another proof").
"Min anfusikum": own kind (Ibn Kathir, Muyassar, al-Qurtubi, as-Sa'di's
two-way fit) and Hawwa from Adam's rib as the tafsirs report it (at-Tabari
via Qatada, Ibn Kathir with 7:189, al-Qurtubi and al-Baghawi as qila).
Ibn Kathir's other-kind thought experiment; Ma'arif on likeness and difference.
"Li-taskunu ilayha": Muyassar, Ibn Kathir with 7:189, as-Sa'di, Ma'arif.
"Mawadda wa rahma" kept as four readings, none chosen: intimacy and child
(Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, al-Hasan via al-Qurtubi); love and tenderness (as-Suddi,
Ibn Abbas via al-Qurtubi, plus the unnamed inclining-of-hearts view); youth
and old age (Ma'arif, citing al-Qurtubi); bonds without kinship (at-Tabari's
in-law ties, al-Baghawi). As-Sa'di's causes of marriage; Ibn Kathir's list
of what holds a marriage. Plural "signs" (Ma'arif, at-Tabari); the reflecting
people (as-Sa'di, al-Baghawi, Muyassar). Ma'arif on rights, taqwa and courts.
Application balanced for both spouses and for unmarried readers.

Hadith: none quoted. Al-Qurtubi's one narration (Muslim 1436, confirmed)
sits in a juristic aside on marital rights; none is attached to the verse's
love and mercy; the article says so in one sentence.

Dropped: 4:1 and 2:187 (no fetched tafsir cites them), the sakan etymology,
and the household section (Aisha's report and "best to their families",
attached by no fetched tafsir). Fixed: the youth/age reading no longer adds
"or hardship", which Ma'arif does not say.
