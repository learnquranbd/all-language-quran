Replaces the 871-word, 6-section article with a 1,770-word, 8-section
one, from at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi, Ibn Kathir, al-Baghawi, as-Sa'di,
al-Muyassar and Ma'arif on 2:222. The shipped card is kept.

Occasion: Muslim 302 (Anas), quoted whole from Muslim's Arabic with his
grading; non-licence line added (Jews; al-Qurtubi's Magians/Christians).
Adha as glossed: at-Tabari, as-Suddi and Qatada (qadhar), Mujahid (the
blood), al-Baghawi, al-Muyassar, al-Qurtubi (troubling the woman herself,
2:264), as-Sa'di (the ban covers the adha alone). Muslim 298 ("your
menstruation is not in your hand") added, marked as a general narration.
Disagreements kept: extent of separation (above the waist-wrap vs all
but intercourse; full separation called isolated by al-Qurtubi); the two
readings yathurna/yattahharna with their qurra' (at-Tabari vs al-Farisi);
cessation vs cessation plus ghusl (majority vs Abu Hanifa and his two
companions vs Mujahid, 'Ikrimah, Tawus with wudu), no position taken;
four glosses of min haythu and at-Tabari's joined reading; mutatahhirin
as water ('Ata') or sins (Mujahid). Fa'tuhunna as permission (at-Tabari,
al-Qurtubi; Ibn Kathir against Ibn Hazm). Centre: al-Qurtubi on why the
repentant are named first (35:32); at-Tabari's masculine-plural point.
One bridging line to 2:223.
Dropped: expiation dispute, istihadah and duration sections, Muslim 300.

Corrected from the old article: "the commentators observe" (only
as-Sa'di). Dropped as unsourced: the mufassirun-on-modesty claim, 2:187,
the verb-form and participle claims, the 9:108 pairing.
