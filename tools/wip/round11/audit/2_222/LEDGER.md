## Disagreements kept (both sides named, no position)
- Extent of separation: above the waist-wrap (Malik, ash-Shafi'i, al-Awza'i, Abu Hanifa) vs all except intercourse (ath-Thawri, Muhammad ibn al-Hasan, Dawud; ash-Shafi'i's sound view; 'A'ishah to Masruq; at-Tabari's preference); full separation as al-Qurtubi's "isolated opinion".
- Readings yathurna / yattahharna with their qurra' (al-Qurtubi); at-Tabari prefers the doubled, Abu 'Ali al-Farisi the light.
- Cessation vs cessation + ghusl: majority (Malik, ash-Shafi'i, at-Tabari, Muhammad ibn Maslamah, people of Madinah; as-Sa'di; Ibn Kathir's agreement) vs Abu Hanifa, Abu Yusuf, Muhammad (ten-day case) vs Mujahid, 'Ikrimah, Tawus (cessation + wudu). Article states it takes none.
- min haythu: place (Ibn 'Abbas, Mujahid, 'Ikrimah) / time of purity (Abu Razin, 'Ikrimah, ad-Dahhak) / the lawful (Ibn al-Hanafiyyah) / not in fasting, ihram, i'tikaf (al-Asamm); at-Tabari's joined reading.
- mutatahhirin: water ('Ata') vs sins (Mujahid) vs both (as-Sa'di).
