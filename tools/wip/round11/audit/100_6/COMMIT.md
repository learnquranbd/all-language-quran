Replaces the 632-word, 6-section article with a 1,752-word, 8-section depth article.

Sources: Ibn Kathir (ar, en), at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi, as-Sa'di, al-Baghawi,
al-Muyassar, Ma'arif al-Qur'an on 100:6; Ibn Kathir, at-Tabari and al-Qurtubi
on 100:1 (copied from the 100:2 audit) for the oaths.

Disagreements kept, none chosen:
- al-'adiyat: horses in jihad (Ibn 'Abbas, Mujahid, 'Ikrimah, 'Ata', Qatadah,
  ad-Dahhak; at-Tabari's choice) vs the camels of hajj ('Ali, Ibrahim, 'Ubayd
  ibn 'Umayr), with the Zamzam report marked as Ibn Kathir's report.
- al-insan: the disbeliever (Ibn 'Abbas via al-Qurtubi) vs man's nature
  (al-Qurtubi, as-Sa'di); licensing sentence in both languages.
- 100:7 shahid: Allah (Qatadah, ath-Thawri) vs man (al-Qurazi; Muyassar).
- kanud: kafur (13 names), al-Hasan's counting gloss, Kindah/Rabi'ah/Kinanah
  dialect glosses, root kanada (to cut), al-Fudayl's kanud/shakur contrast.

Hadith: none. The Abu Umamah "eats alone" report is given as a tafsir gloss
with Ibn Kathir's grading (Ja'far ibn az-Zubayr matruk, weak) and its mawquf form.
Asbab: ad-Dahhak's al-Walid ibn al-Mughira claim not used.

Dropped from the old article: "takes the rain" (not in sources), an unsourced
reason for the disbeliever reading, 14:34 (uncited), the uncollected dawn-raid
practice. The old article also omitted 'Ali's reading.
