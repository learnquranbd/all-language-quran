Adds the Tadabbur card and bilingual article for 20:30, the naming
inside Mūsā's duʿāʾ: "Hārūn akhī" — Aaron, my brother.

The spine is Mūsā ASKING his Lord for a wazīr (20:29) and then naming
the man he means, kept distinct from 19:53's gift-of-a-brother angle
shipped earlier this session. Grammar of the naming from at-Tabari and
al-Qurtubi (Hārūn as badal of wazīr, or object of "appoint"). As-Sa'di
on why from his family (birr to kin) and on azr / bracing the back,
with the reply he cites, 28:35. Al-Baghawi on why Hārūn: elder and more
eloquent (afṣaḥu lisānan) — the strength where Mūsā felt weak (20:27).

Seniority kept as a live disagreement: older by an unspecified amount
(Ibn ʿAbbās/at-Tabari), by 1 or 3 (al-Qurtubi), by 4 (al-Baghawi), 3 or
4 (Ma'arif). No position forced.

Ibn Kathir's point that this petition was for something outside himself,
the ʿĀʾisha/bedouin report ("the most beneficial brother"), and 33:69
(wajīh) carry the adab beat: asking good for another became Mūsā's own
honour.

Hadith: none attached to the verse. Bukhari 660 (seven whom Allah
shades) checked and dropped as thematic-only, not verse-attached. The
Ibn ʿAbbās same-moment-prophethood and ʿĀʾisha narrations related as
tafsir reports (Ath-Thawrī, Ibn Abī Ḥātim), ungraded, no ruling rested.
28:34 not quoted (not in fetched text). No condemned-group licensing
sentence — not that kind of verse.
