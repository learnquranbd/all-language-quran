## Hadith
- Sahih al-Bukhari 6720 (Dar-us-Salam), https://quranx.com/hadith/Bukhari/DarusSalam/Hadith-6720 — attached by Ibn Kathir («ثبت في الصحيحين»). First ten words (own rendering from the Arabic, quoted whole): "Sulayman (AS) said, Tonight I will go round ninety women". Grading: in al-Bukhari's Sahih. The USC English renders «لو استثنى» as "If he had accepted"; the article renders the Arabic ("Had he made the exception"). Saved: hadith_Bukhari_6720.txt.
- Sahih Muslim 1654 (a-f), https://quranx.com/hadith/Muslim/Hadith-1654 — cited only as "Muslim records it too", with sixty/seventy/ninety across versions. Saved.
- Sahih Muslim 684a, https://quranx.com/hadith/Muslim/Hadith-684 — attached by al-Baghawi (his chain Qutayba-Abu 'Awana-Qatada-Anas = Muslim 684b's chain, which lacks the "no expiation" clause; the article quotes 684a whole, Haddab-Hammam-Qatada, with Qatada reciting 20:14). First ten words: "whoever forgets a prayer, let him pray it when he". Grading: in Muslim's Sahih. Marked in the article as cited for the prayer reading, not as the verse's occasion. Saved.
