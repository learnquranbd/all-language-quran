Replaces the 844-word, 6-section article with a 1,756-word, 8-section article
built only from the tafsir fetched for 18:23 and 18:24.

- Placement: after the guesses over the sleepers' number (18:22), before the
  309 years (18:25, Ma'arif) and "Allah knows best" (18:26), one line each.
- Occasion reported as the tafsirs give it: the three questions and "tomorrow"
  (al-Qurtubi, al-Baghawi "some days", at-Tabari hedged, Ibn Kathir, Ma'arif
  via Lubab); framed in their words, ta'dib, irshad, 'itab. Licensing line.
- Why the clause: al-Qurtubi (not asserting the future), Ibn Kathir, as-Sa'di
  with 81:29; the grammar of the ellipsis (Kisa'i/Farra'/Akhfash vs Basrans).
- Hadith: Bukhari 6720, Sulayman and the ninety wives, attached by Ibn Kathir,
  quoted whole from the Arabic; Muslim 1654 noted. Muslim 684a (forgotten
  prayer), attached by al-Baghawi, marked as not the verse's occasion.
- "Wadhkur rabbaka idha nasita" kept as a dispute: late istithna' (Abu
  al-'Aliya, al-Hasan, Ibn 'Abbas, Mujahid), remember Him on any forgetting
  (Ibn Kathir's other aspect, Muyassar, as-Sa'di, al-Qurtubi), the Kufan
  Muhammad's repentance reading vs the majority; 'Ikrima angry/disobey;
  at-Tabari's "left off His remembrance". No position taken.
- Late exception in oaths: a year, two years, the sitting, near time, joined
  only; at-Tabari (blame lifted, expiation stays) endorsed by Ibn Kathir;
  at-Tabarani's "particular to the Prophet" (al-Walid alone); Ibn 'Atiyya
  (not about oaths); Ma'arif (mustahabb; contracts left to the jurists).
- "Li-aqraba min hadha rashada": at-Tabari, Ibn Kathir, al-Baghawi's three
  glosses incl. clearer proofs of prophethood, Muyassar, as-Sa'di.
- Dropped from the old article: 76:30, the 18:39 and 68:17-18 garden links
  (no fetched source), and its own-voice "rebuke"/"correction" framing.
