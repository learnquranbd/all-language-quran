Card and 8-section bilingual article for 92:20: illa ibtigha'a wajhi
rabbihi al-a'la, five words naming the motive left after 92:19.
92:17-19 and 92:21 named as context only.

Sources: Tabari, Qurtubi, Baghawi, Sa'di, Ibn Kathir (ar + en
abridged), Muyassar, Ma'arif. Slot check: Tabari's gloss and asbab sit
in the 92:19 slot, Qurtubi's asbab in 92:19, Baghawi's in 92:18-19;
all fetched and saved.
- illa: lakin, disconnected exception, accusative; nominative allowed,
  Yahya ibn Waththab's reading; maf'ul lahu (Qurtubi, 4:66); lakin
  (Baghawi); exception from the meaning of 92:19, or contrast, an-Nabigha
  (Tabari). Each attributed, none chosen.
- ibtigha' set against repayment: Tabari's grammarian, Qatada, Baghawi.
- wajh: pleasure and nearness (Qurtubi); pleasure added (Baghawi,
  Muyassar); hope of seeing Him in the Hereafter (Ibn Kathir);
  Countenance (Ma'arif). Reported as theirs, unranked, no position.
- al-a'la: Qurtubi only (deserving of highness; al-muta'ali).
Asbab: Abu Bakr and the freeing of Bilal and others, per Tabari,
Qurtubi, Baghawi, Ma'arif; stated that none grades the reports; Ibn
Kathir's point that the wording is general. Framed as release from
bondage; licensing sentence in both languages.
Hadith: none attached to the verse; Ibn Kathir's Two Sahihs hadith
pointed to as Bukhari 1897, not quoted (long).
Shipped 92:4 cross-referenced by number.
Dropped: 76:9, 6:52, 18:28, 30:38 (not cited), the Nistas exchange,
'Urwa ibn Mas'ud anecdote.
