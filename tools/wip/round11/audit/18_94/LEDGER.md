- t16 al-Muyassar (ar) — NAMED: corruption as ruin of crops/offspring «مفسدون في الأرض بإهلاك الحرث والنسل».

- t169 Ibn Kathir (en, abridged) — NAMED: they are progeny of Adam (Two Sahihs); "with kindness, righteousness and good intentions"; "the power and authority Allah has given me is better."
- t168 Ma'arif (en) — NAMED: progeny of Nuh via 37:77 nass; majority of experts/historians = progeny of Yafith; al-Alusi (Ruh al-Ma'ani) refuted equating Tatars / modern Russia or China with Ya'juj-Ma'juj as error against sound hadith; al-Qurtubi only said the Tatars resembled them / may be forerunners; emergence comes only after the descent of Isa (AS).

## Hadith
- Sahih al-Bukhari 3348, Abu Sa'id al-Khudri (Muslim reports its like). URL fetched: https://quranx.com/hadith/Bukhari/DarusSalam/Hadith-3348
  First ten words (EN): "The Prophet said, Allah will say on the Day of..." Grading: Sahih (Bukhari's own collection; not upgraded). Used in §4 ONLY to fix identity (progeny of Adam) and scale ("from you a [lone soul], and from Gog and Magog a thousand"); marked as speaking to identity/numbers, not to the barrier request.
- Emergence / "hole in the barrier" narrations (Zainab bint Jahsh; Abu Hurayrah "they dig every day"; an-Nawwas long hadith) appear in t168 and are sound/variously graded, but concern the RELEASE near the Hour, not this request — deliberately kept out of scope per watch-point 5, and said so in §4.3.
