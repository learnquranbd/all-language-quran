The people between the two mountains ask Dhul-Qarnayn for a barrier
against Gog and Magog and offer to pay for it. Card plus 8-section
bilingual article, source-gated, kept to the barrier request.

Sources: as-Sa'di (corruption = killing/plunder; the ruler not greedy,
his aim islah, thankful for God-given tamkin), al-Muyassar (ruin of
crops and offspring), al-Baghawi (an interpreter spoke for them via
Ibn Mas'ud's reading; descent from Yafith b. Nuh; al-Kalbi on the
spring raids; kharj vs kharaj by Abu Amr), al-Qurtubi (humans spoke;
Yafith lineage; rejects Ka'b's discharge report; corruption views laid
side by side; kharaj as levy/tribute per al-Azhari; courteous address),
Ibn Kathir (kharj = a great reward, Ibn Jurayj<-Ata<-Ibn Abbas; progeny
of Adam), Ma'arif (progeny of Nuh via 37:77; al-Alusi refutes equating
Tatars/Russia/China with them).

Hadith: Sahih al-Bukhari 3348 (Abu Sa'id al-Khudri; Muslim reports its
like), graded sahih, used only to fix their identity as Adam's progeny
and their vast numbers. Emergence / "hole in the barrier" narrations
kept out of scope as they concern the release near the Hour, not this
request; said so.

Disagreements kept without a verdict: the nature of the corruption; the
kharj/kharaj reading.

Dropped: fanciful physical descriptions and the 400,000-per-nation
reports (grades unstated/weak); the "Turks are a remnant" and "21 of 22
tribes" identifications, to avoid tying them to any modern people;
Wahb b. Munabbih's isra'iliyyat travel tale. A dedicated section states
plainly, in both languages, that the verse licenses nothing against any
living nation, ethnicity or community. Dhul-Qarnayn's refusal of
payment and the sadd/radm distinction left to 18:95-96.

Gate: ALL GATES CLEAN. Article 1663 EN words, 8 sections.
