Card and 9-section bilingual article for 55:1, the one-word opening
verse ar-Rahman. 55:2-4 and the 55:13 refrain as pointers only.

Sources: Sa'di, Tabari, Muyassar, Ibn Kathir (ar), Baghawi, Qurtubi,
Ma'arif; no extra fetches.
- Name: Sa'di (breadth of mercy, generality of ihsan, abundance of
  goodness, width of bounty), Ma'arif (All-Merciful), Tabari (by His
  mercy to you He taught you), Ibn Kathir (His favour and mercy).
- Read into 55:2: Tabari, Muyassar, Ibn Kathir.
- As a reply: Baghawi's two qila reports (25:60 "what is ar-Rahman?";
  16:103 "only a human teaches him"); Ma'arif's two reasons.
- Qurtubi: Sa'id ibn Jubayr and Sha'bi on Alif-Lam-Ra, Ha-Mim, Nun;
  Makki (Hasan, 'Urwa, 'Ikrima, 'Ata, Jabir) vs Ibn 'Abbas (except
  55:29) vs Madani (Ibn Mas'ud, Muqatil); 'Urwa's report of Ibn
  Mas'ud reciting at the Maqam, given without chain.
Disagreements kept: joined-sentence vs reply framing (no grammar terms,
none in sources); place of revelation. Neither chosen.
Hadith: Tirmidhi 3291 (Jabir), quoted whole, gharib with Ahmad's and
Bukhari's remarks on Zuhayr; used for Qurtubi's Makki argument.
Licensing sentence in both languages for the Makkan questioners and
for those who struck Ibn Mas'ud.
Dropped: "bride of the Qur'an" (Qurtubi, rowiya) and Ibn 'Umar report
(named as unconfirmed); Qays ibn 'Asim, Nakhla claim, Ibn Mas'ud's
Mufassal order, Qurtubi's verse count. 17:110 and 13:30 not cited by
any fetched tafsir: not used.
