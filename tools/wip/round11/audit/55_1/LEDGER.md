# SOURCES LEDGER 55:1

## Tafsirs read
All eight 55:1 files from PREP.md read in full (t169 grouped 55:1-10: the 55:1-4 part read; 55:5-10 not used). No extra fetches.

- 91 as-Sa'di: NAMED. "افتتحها باسمه "الرَّحْمَنُ" الدال على سعة رحمته، وعموم إحسانه، وجزيل بره، وواسع فضله" ; "ثم ذكر ما يدل على رحمته وأثرها الذي أوصله الله إلى عباده من النعم الدينية والدنيوية [والآخروية" ; "وبعد كل جنس ونوع من نعمه، ينبه الثقلين لشكره، ويقول: فبأي آلاء ربكما تكذبان" (bracketed part is in the fetched text).
- 15 at-Tabari: NAMED. "الرحمن أيها الناس برحمته إياكم علمكم القرآن. فأنعم بذلك عليكم، إذ بصَّركم به ما فيه رضا ربكم، وعرّفكم ما فيه سخطه، لتطيعوه ... فتستوجبوا بذلك جزيل ثوابه، وتنجوا من أليم عقابه" (read into 55:2).
- 16 al-Muyassar (grouped 55:1-2): NAMED. "الرحمن علَّم الإنسان القرآن؛ بتيسير تلاوته وحفظه وفهم معانيه."
- 14 Ibn Kathir (ar): NAMED. "يخبر تعالى عن فضله ورحمته بخلقه : أنه أنزل على عباده القرآن ويسر حفظه وفهمه على من رحمه ، فقال : ( الرحمن علم القرآن خلق الإنسان علمه البيان )" ; Tirmidhi hadith with "هذا حديث غريب" and Ahmad's reservation on Syrian narration from Zuhayr ; Ibn 'Umar report via Ibn Jarir (and al-Bazzar) mentioned only as unconfirmed. Al-Hasan/ad-Dahhak on al-bayan belongs to 55:4: not used.
- 169 Ibn Kathir (en abridged): used only to cross-check the Arabic (same content); not separately named.
- 94 al-Baghawi: NAMED. "مكية، " الرحمن " ، قيل: نزلت حين قالوا: وما الرحمن؟ وقيل: هو جواب لأهل مكة حين قالوا: إنما يعلمه بشر." Both reports kept as qila, unranked. Keys added by me after verification: 25:60 (وما الرحمن), 16:103 (إنما يعلمه بشر).
- 90 al-Qurtubi: NAMED.
  - Makki/Madani: "مكية كلها في قول الحسن وعروة بن الزبير وعكرمة وعطاء وجابر . وقال ابن عباس : إلا آية منها هي قوله تعالى : يسأله من في السماوات والأرض" (= 55:29, verified) ; "وقال ابن مسعود ومقاتل : هي مدنية كلها . والقول الأول أصح"
  - 'Urwa report: "أول من جهر بالقرآن بمكة بعد النبي صلى الله عليه وسلم ابن مسعود ... فقام عند المقام فقال : ( بسم الله الرحمن الرحيم ) الرحمن علم القرآن ثم تمادى رافعا بها صوته ... ثم ضربوه حتى أثروا في وجهه" (given by Qurtubi without chain or grading; article says so).
  - Tirmidhi hadith + "وفي هذا دليل على أنها مكية".
  - Sa'id ibn Jubayr and ash-Sha'bi: "( الرحمن ) فاتحة ثلاث سور إذا جمعن كن اسما من أسماء الله تعالى ( الر ) ، و ( حم ) و ( ن )". Reported, not endorsed.
- 168 Ma'arif (en): NAMED. "The All-Merciful"; two reasons for opening with the name: "presumably that the infidels of Makkah were unaware of this name ... 'What is Ar-Rahman?' [25:60] This name has been selected here to let them know it" and "a sheer gift flowing from Allah's beneficence, and not because this or any other act is obligatory on Allah ... nor because He is in need of anyone"; "The greatest of all bounties is the knowledge of the Holy Qur'an"; Companions raised in status; Qurtubi "prefers" Makki; Night of the Jinn "took place in Makkah".

## Watch-points
- Grammar disagreement (subject + predicate in 55:2 vs standalone name/answer): NO fetched text frames it grammatically (no mubtada/khabar or i'rab discussion). Kept only what the texts give: Tabari, Muyassar (and Ibn Kathir) read 55:1 into 55:2; Baghawi's two qila reports frame it as a reply (to "what is ar-Rahman?" 25:60, or to "only a human teaches him" 16:103). Presented side by side, holders named, no choice. Grammatical terminology deliberately not used.
- Meaning of the name: only Sa'di's four phrases, Ma'arif's "All-Merciful", Tabari's "by His mercy to you", Ibn Kathir's "His favour and mercy". No Rahman/Rahim distinction (not in fetched texts); no theology of my own.
- Qira'at: none in any fetched text; not mentioned.
- Overlap: 17:110 ("Allah or ar-Rahman", already uses 25:60), 19:58 ("Why the Name ar-Rahman": verses as mercy), 19:65, 1:1-5 checked; no shared argument repeated. 55:13 already carries the Jabir hadith and the refrain: here the hadith is used for Qurtubi's Makki argument and the refrain is pointed to 55:13. 55:2-4 pointers only.
- Cross-refs: 25:60 (Ma'arif cites; Baghawi quotes words), 16:103 (Baghawi quotes words), 55:29 (Qurtubi quotes words), 55:13 (own module pointer). All verified in data/quran-json. 17:110 and 13:30 NOT cited by any fetched tafsir: not used.
- Condemned group: the Makkans who asked "what is ar-Rahman?"/said "a human teaches him" (§5) and the Quraysh who struck Ibn Mas'ud (§7): licensing sentence in both languages in each.

## Hadith
- Jami' at-Tirmidhi 3291, URL https://quranx.com/hadith/Tirmidhi/Hadith-3291 (via hadith.js, saved here). First ten words used: "The Messenger of Allah ﷺ came out to his Companions, and". Quoted whole from the quranx English (apostrophe tidied; "Which of your Lords favor do you deny" rendered "which of your Lord's favours do you deny"). Grading: at-Tirmidhi "هذا حديث غريب لا نعرفه إلا من حديث الوليد بن مسلم عن زهير بن محمد" + Ahmad's and al-Bukhari's remarks on Syrian narration from Zuhayr (munkar). Reported as given, not upgraded. Attached by Qurtubi, Ibn Kathir, Ma'arif.
