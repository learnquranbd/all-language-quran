Replaces the 670-word, 6-section article with a 1,739-word, 9-section one built only
on the eight tafsir files fetched for 25:77.

Sources: placement after 25:75-76 via as-Sa'di (He pays no heed to other than these) and
the Ibn Kathir (en) heading; al-Qurtubi's "a difficult verse heretics clung to".
Ma ya'ba'u: al-'ab' as load, no weight or worth (al-Qurtubi), Abu 'Ubayda's "what weight,
what measure" (al-Baghawi), yaf'al/yasna' (Mujahid, Ibn Zayd, 'Amr ibn Shu'ayb), ma as a
question carrying denial (az-Zajjaj, al-Farra'), the unsaid answer to lawla.
Du'a'ukum kept as a dispute, no position: worship and obedience (at-Tabari, Ibn Kathir,
al-Baghawi for Ibn 'Abbas and Mujahid, Ma'arif; 51:56); faith (Ibn 'Abbas via 'Ali b. Abi
Talha); His call to you (Ibn ash-Shajari, al-Farra' via al-Qurtubi; al-Baghawi); calling
on other gods (al-Qutabi, al-Farisi, ad-Dahhak via al-Qurtubi; 7:194, 4:147) with
at-Tabari's rejection as his; supplication in hardship (an-Naqqash; 29:65, 6:42);
both senses together (as-Sa'di, Muyassar); al-Walid b. Abi al-Walid's "ask Me".
Addressees: Quraysh (at-Tabari), Makkans (al-Baghawi), all people then Quraysh
(al-Qurtubi); the reading fa-qad kadhdhaba al-kafirun (Ibn az-Zubayr, Ibn 'Abbas,
Ibn Mas'ud) as explanatory. Licensing sentence in both languages.
Lizam: Badr (at-Tabari with Abu Dhu'ayb; holders per at-Tabari, Ibn Kathir, al-Qurtubi's
"majority"; seventy captured, seventy killed) vs Day of Resurrection (al-Hasan; Ibn Kathir:
no conflict), Hereafter, fighting, death, Abu 'Ubayda's two glosses, the denial itself
(Ibn Mas'ud via az-Zahrawi; al-Baghawi); creditor and debtor (as-Sa'di, Muyassar).
Hadith: Bukhari 4767 ("five have passed ... and the lizam"), attached by al-Baghawi,
at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi; quoted whole, marked as Ibn Mas'ud's statement, grading as its
place in al-Bukhari's Sahih. No asbab.
Dropped from the old article: the tie to the surah's opening, "the believer is not
addressed" (wrong per al-Qurtubi), "several authorities" for faith, the inverted weight
on lizam, 40:60, 2:186 and the small-requests section. Also dropped: Wahb's Torah report.
