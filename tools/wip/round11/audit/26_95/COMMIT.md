Tadabbur 26:95 — and the hosts of Iblis, all together — the end of
every false allegiance

Card plus deep bilingual article on wa junudu Iblisa ajma'un, the last
name added to the roll driven into the Fire (26:90-95), where the false
gods, their worshippers and the satanic ranks that led them collapse
into one ruin and then quarrel (26:96-99).

Sources gave, and the article keeps open, a real difference on "junud
Iblis": at-Tabari and al-Baghawi hold it in one word — his offspring
AND the jinn and humans who obeyed him; al-Qurtubi lists the readings
separately (his offspring; all he called to idols; per Qatada, al-Kalbi
and Muqatil the ghawun are the devils; and the idols cast in as iron and
copper to punish others). as-Sa'di sorts the host into three ranks —
caller, responder, imitator — so the mere follower is still enlisted.
ajma'un carried by Ibn Kathir ('an akhirihim, down to the last) and the
Muyassar (not one escaped).

Hadith: Sahih al-Bukhari 660 (the seven under the shade, including two
who love one another for Allah's sake), expressly marked as a general
narration NOT attached by any mufassir to the verse — used for the
theme of where allegiance ends. Ibn Kathir's own parallel to 38:64 and
the plain law of 43:67 anchor the "allies turn enemies" point.

Carries the eschatological safeguard: junud Iblis is a category the
next world sorts out, and the verse licenses nothing against any living
person, sect, nation or community.

Dropped: an as-Sa'di note on 26:91 (Fire brought near while empty) —
he was fetched only on 26:95, so unconfirmable here. Ma'arif (en) was
fetched but sits on 26:88-89 (the sound heart), so not cited.
