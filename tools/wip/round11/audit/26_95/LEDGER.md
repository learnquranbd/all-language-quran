# SOURCES ledger — 26:95 (وَجُنُودُ إِبْلِيسَ أَجْمَعُونَ)

## Tafsir fetched (all from PREP, not re-fetched)

- **at-Tabari (15)** — USED. Arabic clause rested on: "وكبكب فيها مع الأنداد
  والغاوين جنود إبليس أجمعون. وجنوده: كل من كان من تباعه, من ذرّيته كان أو من
  ذرّية آدم." Used for: the implied verb (overturned along with the false rivals
  al-andad and the deviators); and the definition of the hosts as everyone among
  his followers, whether his own offspring OR the offspring of Adam (both groups
  in one word). Sections 1, 2, 3.

- **Ibn Kathir ar (14)** — USED. Whole gloss: "'وجنود إبليس أجمعون' أي ألقوا فيها
  عن آخرهم." Used for: "they too will all be thrown in, down to the last of them"
  ('an akhirihim). Sections 1, 5.

- **al-Muyassar (16)** — USED. Clause (covers 26:94-95): "فجُمِعوا وألقُوا في
  جهنم، هم والذين أضلوهم وأعوان إبليس الذين زيَّنوا لهم الشر، لم يُفْلِت منهم أحد."
  Used for: the aides of Iblis who made evil fair-seeming, and "not one of them
  escaped." Sections 2, 5.

- **al-Qurtubi (90)** — USED. Clause: "وجنود إبليس أجمعون من كان من ذريته. وقيل:
  كل من دعاه إلى عبادة الأصنام فاتبعه. وقال قتادة والكلبي ومقاتل: الغاوون هم
  الشياطين. وقيل: إنما تلقى الأصنام في النار وهي حديد ونحاس ليعذب بها غيرهم."
  Used for: the four readings kept open — (1) his offspring; (2) all he called to
  idol-worship who followed; (3) Qatada/al-Kalbi/Muqatil: al-ghawun = the devils;
  (4) idols cast in as iron and copper to punish others. Section 3.

- **as-Sa'di (91)** — USED. Clause: "وَجُنُودُ إِبْلِيسَ أَجْمَعُونَ من الإنس والجن
  الذين أزَّهم إلى المعاصي أزًّا وتسلط عليهم بشركهم وعدم إيمانهم فصاروا من دعاته
  والساعين في مرضاته وهم ما بين داع لطاعته ومجيب لهم ومقلد لهم على شركهم." Used
  for: from mankind and jinn incited to sin; the three ranks — caller (داعٍ),
  responder (مجيب), imitator (مقلّد). Section 4.

- **al-Baghawi (94)** — USED. Clause: "(وجنود إبليس أجمعون) وهم أتباعه ومن أطاعه
  من الجن والإنس. ويقال: ذريته." Used for: followers / whoever obeyed him from jinn
  and mankind; and it is said, his offspring. Sections 2, 3.

- **Ibn Kathir en, abridged (169)** — USED. Covers 26:90-99. Rested on: "(And all
  of the hosts of Iblis together.) they will all be thrown into it"; and the scene
  of the weak turning on their arrogant leaders (26:96-99); and the explicit
  parallel he draws to 38:64, "the mutual dispute of the people of the Fire."
  Sections 1, 6.

- **Ma'arif en (168)** — FETCHED, NOT NAMED. Covers 26:88-97 but its substance
  is on the "sound heart" of 26:88-89 (kinship not availing the disbelievers). It
  says nothing specific on junud Iblis in 26:95, so it is not cited. Not a defect;
  simply off this verse.

## Cross-references (scripture, not tafsir attributions)

- 26:90, 26:91, 26:92, 26:93, 26:94 — the immediate context (from PREP).
- 26:96, 26:97, 26:98, 26:99 — the quarrel that follows (from PREP + Ibn Kathir en).
- 38:64 — cited BY Ibn Kathir en as his own parallel.
- 43:67 (الأخلاء يومئذ بعضهم لبعض عدو إلا المتقين) — cited as the Qur'an's own plain
  statement of the same law; framed as scripture, not attributed to a mufassir.

## Hadith

- **Sahih al-Bukhari, Hadith 660** (Dar-us-Salam). URL fetched:
  https://quranx.com/hadith/Bukhari/DarusSalam/Hadith-660
  First ten words used: "Allah will give shade, to seven, on the Day when..."
  Grading: in Sahih al-Bukhari (his own sound-collection standard); not upgraded.
  MARKED in the article as a general narration NOT attached by any mufassir to
  26:95 — used only for the shared theme (love/allegiance for Allah's sake vs.
  companionship in falsehood). Section 7.

## Asbab an-nuzul

- None used. This is an eschatological passage (the hosts of Iblis as a category
  disclosed on the Day of Judgment); no occasion of revelation applies, and none
  was claimed. The context is the placement within 26:90-102.
