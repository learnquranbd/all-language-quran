Card and 8-section bilingual article for 50:23: wa qala qarinuhu hadha
ma ladayya 'atid. 50:21-22 used as context only; 50:24-26 not developed;
the 50:16 article's recorders section is not repeated.

Sources: Tabari, Ibn Kathir (ar; en cross-check), Qurtubi, Baghawi,
Muyassar, Sa'di, Ma'arif.
- Kept as disagreements, none picked: the qarin as the angel set over
  him (Qatada; al-Hasan, Qatada, ad-Dahhak via Qurtubi; Baghawi;
  Muyassar's writing witness; IK; Sa'di; Ma'arif's Shahid), the driver
  (Ibn Zayd in Tabari; Mujahid in IK), both (Ibn Jarir per IK, "has
  direction and strength", also Ma'arif), the devil (Mujahid's second
  report, Qurtubi only); qarinuhu again in 50:27 per IK (en).
- ma ladayya: record (Qurtubi, Muyassar, Ma'arif) vs the person
  (Mujahid; Baghawi's qila ma = man) vs both (Sa'di) vs punishment
  (Qurtubi's qila); Tabari's gloss leaves it unnamed.
- 'atid: IK's bila ziyada wa la nuqsan; Ibn Zayd reads it of the person.
- Licensing line included (Sa'di's "this denier who turned away").
Hadith: none. No fetched tafsir attaches one to 50:23; IK's Ahmad
narration sits on 50:24-26 and is not used; the article says so.
Dropped: 43:36, 82:10-12 and 50:17-18 by key (not cited by a fetched
tafsir on this verse), 14:22 (IK on 50:27), lexical glosses from memory.
