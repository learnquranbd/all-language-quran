Card and 8-section bilingual article for 55:62: wa-min dunihima
jannatan. 55:60-61 as framing with one pointer to the 55:60 article;
55:64/66/68/76 named as future targets, comparisons framed only.

Sources: at-Tabari, Ibn Kathir (ar; en abridged grouped 55:62-71 as a
check), al-Qurtubi, al-Baghawi, as-Sa'di, al-Muyassar. Ma'arif (group
55:60-63) speaks only to 55:60; unused.
- min dunihima kept as a disagreement: below in degrees (Ibn Abbas),
  in excellence (Ibn Zayd) vs in front of / before (al-Kisa'i,
  ad-Dahhak: second pair of ruby and emerald, better) and nearer the
  Throne (al-Hakim at-Tirmidhi). Tabari records the dispute with no
  stated preference. Ibn Kathir ranks the first pair higher "by the text
  of the Qur'an" (order of mention); Qurtubi argues it from the paired
  descriptions. Muqatil's four names reported without ranking.
- Occupants: Ibn Jurayj, Abu Musa (Baghawi), Sa'di (silver, companions
  of the right), al-Mawardi's stated possibility, Ibn Abbas (palms vs
  crops); Qurtubi: all four for those who fear, the fearers have ranks.
- Tabari's Ibn Abbas report tying 55:62 to 32:17 given as filed, no
  grading added.
- Site EN "[in excellence]" flagged as an interpretive bracket; site BN
  noted as neutral "besides" but without the dual.
Hadith: Bukhari 4878 (quranx), quoted whole, pointer to 55:46-47; noted
that its wording assigns no pair to a verse. Asbab: none.
Dropped: al-Hasan (not in fetched texts), a Tabari preference (none
stated), al-Halimi and furnishings comparisons (length/future targets).
