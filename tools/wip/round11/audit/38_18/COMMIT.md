Card and deep bilingual article on the mountains made to glorify with
Dawud at al-'ashiyy and al-ishraq, read in its placement: the Prophet,
mocked in 38:16, is told in 38:17 to be patient and remember Dawud.

Sources: as-Sa'di (the subjection flows from Dawud's intense turning),
al-Qurtubi (yusabbihna as hal; proof and miracle; ishraq = the sun's
whitening after rising), at-Tabari with Qatadah and Ibn Zayd, Ibn Kathir
(birds halting in the air at the Zabur), al-Baghawi (al-Kalbi; Ibn Abbas
reads ishraq as duha), al-Muyassar, Ma'arif al-Qur'an (Thanavi on the
taste for dhikr and collective remembrance, framed as his inference).

Disagreements kept open: the mountains' tasbih as real speech (Muqatil,
al-Qurtubi's own "tasbih maqal") vs Ibn Ishaq's echo of a beautiful
voice vs the unnamed "qila" reading of tasbih as a pointing sign; and
where al-'ashiyy begins (at-Tabari: 'Asr; Ma'arif: after Zuhr). Birds:
literal hovering is Ibn Kathir's and the Muyassar's; listening birds are
Ibn Ishaq's.

Hadith: Sahih al-Bukhari 1176 (Umm Hani, eight rak'ahs on the day of the
conquest) and Sahih Muslim 748a (Zayd ibn Arqam, prayer of the awwabin),
both quoted whole, gradings as collected; the verse link is Ibn Abbas's
via at-Tabari and Ibn Kathir, stated as such.

Cross-referenced shipped 21:79 and 17:44 rather than repeating them.
Dropped: the ungraded "this is the prayer of al-ishraq" wording (named
as ungraded), Ka'b al-Ahbar's exchange, al-Qurtubi's further duha
hadiths (not confirmed on quranx). No asbab claimed.
