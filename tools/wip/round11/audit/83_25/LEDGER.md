Glosses reported, none chosen:
- rahiq: wine of Paradise / one of the names of wine (IK ar + en, from six authorities); pure wine (Muyassar); pure good wine, Muqatil white wine (Baghawi); drink without adulteration (Akhfash, Zajjaj via Qurtubi); pure wine ("qila", Qurtubi); safwat al-khamr (al-Sihah via Qurtubi, "the meaning is one"); utmost and finest of wine (Khalil via Qurtubi); aged, white, pure, luminous wine (Muqatil and others via Qurtubi); among the most pleasant and delicious drinks (Sa'di).
- makhtum: sealed and withheld from any hand until the righteous break its seal (Baghawi); mutayyan, clay-sealed (Mujahid via Baghawi and Tabari); vessel made fast (Muyassar); mamzuj, mixed (Ibn Mas'ud via Masruq in Tabari); Ibn Zayd's two-worlds contrast (Tabari); Tabari's preference: end / final draught, literal seal and "mixing" sense both argued against. Disagreement kept with names, no position.

## Article's own observation (orchestrator to accept or cut)
§1 p2 and §8 p1 note the verb is passive ("they are given to drink", giver unnamed). Not from a tafsir; it rests on the verse form and the PREP EN/BN translations. §8 p2 sets 83:14 beside 83:25 explicitly as the reader's comparison, "the surah does not make that comparison itself".
