Card and 8-section bilingual article for 83:25: yusqawna min rahiqin
makhtum, four words after the couches and the radiant faces. 83:22-24
as context; 83:26 named only as the verse that follows; 83:27-28 left.

Sources: Ibn Kathir (ar; en abridged), Muyassar, Qurtubi, Sa'di,
Baghawi, Tabari, Ma'arif. No re-fetch.
- Rahiq: wine of Paradise, one of wine's names (IK, six authorities);
  pure wine (Muyassar); pure good wine, Muqatil white (Baghawi); no
  adulteration (Akhfash, Zajjaj), safwa (al-Sihah), finest (Khalil),
  aged white luminous (Muqatil) via Qurtubi; most pleasant drink (Sa'di).
- Makhtum: sealed from every hand till the righteous break it
  (Baghawi); clay-sealed (Mujahid); vessel made fast (Muyassar);
  Tabari's disagreement: mixed (Ibn Mas'ud), clay (Mujahid), Ibn Zayd's
  two worlds, his own preference for the end/final draught, with his
  reasons. Kept as a disagreement, no position.
Hadith: none sound attached; said so. Tirmidhi 2449 (the thirsty
believer and al-rahiq al-makhtum, cited by IK from Ahmad) quoted
whole with Tirmidhi's grading: gharib, mawquf version more correct.
Nothing built on it as Prophetic. No asbab.
Dropped: non-intoxication and worldly-wine comparison (not fetched),
cross-refs 76:5/76:17/47:15/37:47/56:19 (not cited), musk detail
(83:26), Tasnim (83:27-28), Tabari on rahiq (not in fetched part).
