## Hadith (all fetched with hadith.js; saved in this folder)

- **Muslim 1616** (1616a used): https://quranx.com/hadith/Muslim/Hadith-1616 . First ten words: "I fell ill, and Allah's Messenger ﷺ and Abu Bakr came". Wording quoted whole from the Arabic of 1616a (Sufyan b. 'Uyayna from Ibn al-Munkadir), which ends {يستفتونك قل الله يفتيكم في الكلالة}. Sahih Muslim, no separate grading. Variant noted in text: 1616b (Ibn Jurayj) names 4:11 instead.
- **Bukhari 4605** (DarusSalam): https://quranx.com/hadith/Bukhari/DarusSalam/Hadith-4605 . "The last surah to be revealed was Bara'ah, and the last". Same chain Ibn Kathir quotes (Sulayman b. Harb, Shu'ba, Abu Ishaq). Sahih al-Bukhari. (6744 also fetched: same report, Isra'il's wording; not quoted.)
- **Muslim 1617** (1617a): https://quranx.com/hadith/Muslim/Hadith-1617 . "I leave nothing behind me that matters more to me than". Quoted whole from the Arabic (narrated by Ma'dan b. Abi Talha; quranx English mislabels him "Abu Talha"). Sahih Muslim.
- **Bukhari 5588** (DarusSalam): https://quranx.com/hadith/Bukhari/DarusSalam/Hadith-5588 . "The prohibition of khamr has come down, and it is made". Watch-point condition met: collection and wording confirmed. 'Umar's sermon quoted whole (khamr part included); the trailing Abu Hayyan/ash-Sha'bi exchange about rice drink in Sind is not part of the sermon and is left out. Sahih al-Bukhari. (Muslim 3032 also fetched as a cross-check; same sermon; not quoted.)
