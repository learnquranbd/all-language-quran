## Disagreements kept (both named, no position)
- la tahzan 'alayhim: grief over their wealth / missing out (at-Tabari, al-Baghawi, al-Qurtubi's "qila") vs grief over their disbelief / rejection / fate (Muyassar, al-Qurtubi 1st and 3rd, Ibn Kathir abridged, as-Sa'di).
- Ibn Uyaynah's reading of yataghanna: adopted by al-Qurtubi; Ibn Kathir: sound in itself but not the hadith's intent.
- azwaj: asnaf of the disbelievers (al-Baghawi, Muyassar) vs the rich / likes (Mujahid, al-Qurtubi, Ibn Kathir); at-Tabari combines.
