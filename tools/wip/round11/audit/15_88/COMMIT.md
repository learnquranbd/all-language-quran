Card and bilingual article (9 sections, 1,780 EN words) for "do not
extend your eyes toward what We gave some pairs of them to enjoy, do
not grieve over them, and lower your wing to the believers", read
straight after 15:87's gift of the seven oft-repeated and the Qur'an.

Link to 15:87: Ibn Kathir and as-Sa'di (istaghni), at-Tabari (honour
already hastened, plus the "made great the small" saying), al-Baghawi
("qila" joined to what precedes), Ibn Kathir abridged (grouped 15:87-88).
Ma'arif grouped 15:87-93, silent on 15:88; unused and said so.

Tamuddanna: at-Tabari's idiom (desired, wished, wanted), Muyassar,
as-Sa'di, Ibn Abbas via al-Awfi. Azwaj: asnaf of the disbelievers
(al-Baghawi, Muyassar) vs the rich / likes (Mujahid, al-Qurtubi, Ibn
Kathir). Janah: at-Tabari's rifq, al-Qurtubi's bird-and-chick, sides
and 20:22, as-Sa'di's love and honour, 26:215 and 9:128 via 169.

Disagreements kept: grief over their wealth (at-Tabari, al-Baghawi) vs
over their disbelief or fate (Muyassar, al-Qurtubi, 169, as-Sa'di);
Ibn Uyaynah's yataghanna reading adopted by al-Qurtubi, called sound
but not the hadith's intent by Ibn Kathir.

Hadith: Muslim 2963 ("look at those below you"), attached by
al-Baghawi through the same Waki'/al-A'mash line; Bukhari 7527 on
yataghanna, quoted whole. Both "in his Sahih", no upgrade.

Dropped: Qurtubi's seven caravans and Ibn Kathir's Abu Rafi' report
as asbab; al-Baghawi's Ibn al-Mubarak narration (unconfirmed).
No-licence paragraph in both languages: no contempt for the wealthy
or anyone; al-Qurtubi's no-monasticism caveat. No 20:131 article
shipped to overlap. Reflection marked as reflection.
Omitted by choice: as-Sa'di's clause that no good was hoped from them. His
reading (the grief is over the people) is still given; the clause was left
out so the article carries no note of contempt.
