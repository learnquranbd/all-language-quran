Card and 8-section bilingual article for 90:15: yatiman dha maqrabah,
three words, the first named recipient of 90:14's feeding. 90:11-14
context only; 90:16 named as the next step, dha matraba not treated.

Sources: Tabari, Qurtubi, Baghawi, Sa'di, Ibn Kathir (ar + en
abridged), Muyassar, Ma'arif. Slot check: all eight files sit on 90:15.
- Grammar: Tabari and IK restate it'am as a verb with yatiman as the one
  fed; Muyassar keeps the noun. None chosen.
- yatim: young and fatherless (Tabari); named for weakness, father's
  side for people, mother's for animals (linguists via Qurtubi) vs both
  parents dead (some linguists via Qurtubi, Qays ibn al-Mulawwah line).
  Kept as a disagreement.
- maqrabah = qarabah: IK (Ibn 'Abbas, 'Ikrima, al-Hasan, ad-Dahhak,
  as-Suddi), Tabari (Ibn Zayd), Baghawi ("between you and him"),
  Qurtubi (dhu qarabati / dhu maqrabati). No fetched text reads it as
  physical nearness; the article says so.
- Merit: charity + silat ar-rahim (Muyassar); twofold reward (Ma'arif);
  orphan, poor, kin combined (Sa'di, faqir flagged as his addition);
  kin over non-kin, unguarded orphan over supported one (Qurtubi).
Hadith: Nasa'i 2582 (Salman ibn 'Amir, two rewards) quoted whole, page
shows no grading; Tirmidhi 658 described, his own grading hasan; IK's
"isnad sahih" reported as his judgment of Ahmad's chain. Asbab: none.
Licensing sentence for 90:11 in both languages. Shipped 90:13 and 90:4
cross-referenced by number.
Dropped: Bukhari "guardian of the orphan" (Sahl ibn Sa'd) and 89:17,
93:9, 107:2, 2:177, 4:36 (no fetched tafsir cites them on 90:15).
