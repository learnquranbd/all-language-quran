Card and 8-section bilingual article for 78:14, wa-anzalna mina
al-mu'sirati ma'an thajjajan (5 words). Placed after 78:13's lamp as the
first thing in the list that comes down; 78:15-16 named only as the next
verses. No overlap with 78:3 or 78:9.

Sources: Tabari, Ibn Kathir (ar), Qurtubi, Baghawi, Sa'di, Muyassar,
Ma'arif (grouped 78:14-16). Ibn Kathir en (169) was the wrong group, unused.
- al-mu'sirat kept as three readings, none chosen: winds (Ibn Abbas via
  al-'Awfi and Sa'id b. Jubayr, Mujahid, Qatada, Ibn Zayd, 'Ikrima et al.),
  clouds (Ibn Abbas via 'Ali b. Abi Talha / al-Walibi, Abu al-'Aliya,
  ad-Dahhak, ar-Rabi', Sufyan; al-Farra's mu'sir image), heavens
  (al-Hasan, Qatada; Ubayy and others in Qurtubi/Baghawi); Ibn Kaysan's
  "rain-givers" (Baghawi, 12:49). Name-list mismatch between books noted.
- Tabari's min-vs-bi argument and the reading bi-l-mu'sirat ('Ikrima;
  Ibn Abbas in Qurtubi); Baghawi/al-Azhari min = bi. Tabari, Qurtubi and
  Ibn Kathir prefer clouds (reported as theirs; IK calls heavens gharib);
  an-Nahhas in Qurtubi: all sound, may be one.
- thajjaj: poured (Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatada, ar-Rabi'), successive
  (Sufyan/ath-Thawri), copious (Mujahid in Baghawi), abundant (Ibn Zayd)
  vs Tabari's objection; Qurtubi "the meaning is one"; az-Zajjaj.
- Ma'arif on cloud vs sky; 30:48 used by both sides.
Hadith: none attached to the verse, said in one sentence. Tirmidhi 827
('ajj and thajj), quoted whole, no grading on the fetched page so none
given. Marked as a lexical witness, not attached.
Asbab: none in the sources.
Dropped: Tirmidhi 128 (Hamnah) entirely: phrase-only use of a long narration, and Ahmad's doubt in Abu Dawud 287; any
morphology claim not in the text, 78:15-16 content.
