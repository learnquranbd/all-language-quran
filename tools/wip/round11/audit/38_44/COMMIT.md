Card and 8-section bilingual article for 38:44: the bundle (dighth) that let
Ayyub keep his oath without hurting his wife, closing on "We found him patient,
an excellent servant, ever turning back".

Sources: Tabari, Qurtubi, Ibn Kathir (ar), Baghawi, Sa'di, Muyassar, Ma'arif.
- Dighth: Tabari's definition and Awf ibn al-Khari' verse; the kind kept as a
  spread, each with its collector: bundle / fresh plants / tamarisk (Ibn Abbas,
  three chains in Tabari), palm-stalk ithkal (Ibn Abbas in Qurtubi), fresh sticks
  (Ata'), fresh branches (Dahhak), pasture grass (Ibn Zayd), thumam (Ibn Shihab).
- Count: 99 + stem (Qatadah), over a hundred (Ibn Zayd), as many as sworn
  (Dahhak, Ibn Abbas), a hundred (Ibn Kathir, Sa'di, Baghawi, Qurtubi); single
  strike common to all. Oath: a hundred lashes; Baghawi a hundred whip strokes.
- Cause: Qurtubi's four reports and Ibn Kathir's qila, held as ungraded reports;
  Muyassar, Sa'di, Ma'arif on the wife's righteousness. No position.
- Mercy both ways (Ibn Kathir with 65:2-3, Muyassar, Sa'di); Qurtubi's ta'dib
  ruling reported as his, with his own limit; licensing sentence in both languages.
- Fiqh kept as disagreement: general (Mujahid, Dahhak) vs particular (Qushayri);
  Ata' both ways; Malik vs Shafi'i; Abu Hanifah with Ibn al-Humam's conditions
  (Ma'arif); Ibn Kathir's kaffara objection and Qurtubi's reply. Ma'arif on hiyal.
- Awwab: Tabari, Sa'di; Sufyan's patient/grateful parity with 38:30 (Qurtubi).
Hadith: Abu Dawud 4472 (Abu Umamah, a hundred twigs for the wasted man), attached
by Qurtubi, who notes its chain was criticised; no grading on the page; closing
command quoted verbatim.
Cross-refs: 21:83-84 via shipped 21:87, 38:30. No lapse in the prophet's voice.
Dropped: 38:42 narrations (gold locusts, eighteen years), Ma'arif's oath-breaking
ruling and its hadith, Qurtubi's "she erred". Asbab: none.
Arabic count: 38:44 fourteen words. Themes: patience, mercy.
