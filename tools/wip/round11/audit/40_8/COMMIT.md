Card and 8-section bilingual article for 40:8: rabbana wa-adkhilhum jannati
'adnin allati wa'adtahum, wa man salaha min aba'ihim wa azwajihim wa
dhurriyyatihim, innaka anta al-'Aziz al-Hakim.

Sources: Ibn Kathir (ar + en abridged), Tabari, Qurtubi, Sa'di, Baghawi,
Muyassar, Ma'arif (grouped 40:7-14). All named.
- Continuity: the Throne-bearers' dua of 40:7 (not yet shipped) summarised
  briefly; Tabari's framing; Ma'arif's order of requests.
- jannat 'adn, glosses attributed: gardens of residence (Tabari), everlasting
  (Ma'arif, Ibn Kathir en); palaces of gold for prophets, siddiqun, martyrs,
  just leaders (Qurtubi, yurwa, Ka'b's answer to Umar, not marfu').
- man salaha: accusative on hum of adkhilhum (Qurtubi, Tabari), or on hum of
  wa'adtahum (Tabari's alternative). Condition: faith (Qurtubi, Ma'arif via
  Mazhari), faith and deeds (Muyassar, Sa'di), righteous deeds (Tabari).
- Own faith required; rank raised by grace: Ibn Kathir with 52:21, Tabari
  ("even if they did not do his deeds"), Ma'arif. Qurtubi's ar-Ra'd parallel
  = 13:23. 52:21 and 13:23 verified in data/quran-json.
- Sa'id ibn Jubayr's "I worked for myself and for them" in Tabari's wording
  with chain; Baghawi/Qurtubi/Ibn Kathir variants noted; Mazhari's mawquf/
  marfu' judgement reported as his (via Ma'arif).
- azwaj: Sa'di's wives, husbands, companions; family reunion only.
- al-'Aziz al-Hakim: Tabari, Ibn Kathir, Muyassar, Sa'di (names as grounds).
- Mutarrif ibn ash-Shikhkhir (Ibn Kathir, Ma'arif).
Hadith: Sahih Muslim 2732b (Umm ad-Darda', the angel's "Amin, and for you
the like"), quranx-confirmed, quoted whole, Muslim's Sahih, no separate
grading; marked as not attached to this verse.
Dropped: qurrat al-'ayn idiom gloss (memory), Ma'arif's angel counts and
Shahr ibn Hawshab (40:7 ground). Asbab: none. 40:9 and 40:15 not pulled forward.
Themes: hereafter, mercy.
