## Watch-point answer: are relatives joined through their own righteousness?
All fetched sources keep a condition of the relative's own salah: faith (Qurtubi; Ma'arif via
Mazhari) or faith plus righteous deeds (Muyassar, Sa'di), righteous deeds (Tabari). What is
by grace is the rank: Ibn Kathir (raised to equal degree, 52:21), Tabari ("even if they did not
do his deeds"), Ma'arif (lower-rank relatives with them). No source admits a relative without faith.

## Hadith
- Sahih Muslim 2732b, URL https://quranx.com/hadith/Muslim/Hadith-2732 (saved hadith_Muslim_2732.txt).
  Wording used (2732b, Umm ad-Darda' from her husband): "مَنْ دَعَا لأَخِيهِ بِظَهْرِ الْغَيْبِ قَالَ الْمَلَكُ الْمُوَكَّلُ بِهِ آمِينَ وَلَكَ بِمِثْلٍ"
  First ten words (EN as used): "Whoever supplicates for his brother in his absence, the angel entrusted".
  Grading: inclusion in Muslim's Sahih; no separate grading. Brought by Ibn Kathir (en 169) on the
  40:7-9 passage; marked in the article as not attached to 40:8. Ibn Kathir's own quoted wording
  (إذا دعا المسلم...) not used; only the quranx page's 2732b matn. No honorific given to Umm ad-Darda'
  (identity of which Umm ad-Darda' not confirmed).
