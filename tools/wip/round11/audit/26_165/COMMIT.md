Card and deep bilingual article for Lūṭ's rebuke, a conduct named by a
prophet who came to warn, not to harm.

Sources (all fetched for this verse, source-gated):
- at-Tabari + al-Baghawi (via Muqātil): plain gloss, min al-ʿālamīn read as
  "the males of mankind / banī Ādam."
- as-Saʿdī: the one reading, kept as a genuine difference, that none among the
  worlds preceded them in this fāḥisha; also the shirk beneath it, hearts and
  words like the peoples before, turning from the wives out of isrāf/ʿudwān,
  and his unceasing role as forbidder and warner.
- Ibn Kathir (ar + en abridged): the rebuke paired with a direction, toward the
  wives Allah created for them.
- al-Muyassar: those wives made for enjoyment and procreation; the deed oversteps
  the ḥalāl into the ḥarām (ʿādūn).
- al-Qurtubi: they did it to strangers; cross-reference to Sūrat al-Aʿrāf
  (sūra named only, no ayah number in the source).

Disagreement kept: the two readings of min al-ʿālamīn, no position taken.

Hadith: Ṣaḥīḥ al-Bukhārī 3375, "May Allah forgive Lot: He wanted to have a
powerful support" (Abū Hurayrah RA), quoted whole, graded as in the Ṣaḥīḥ.

A mandatory, prominent section in both languages: the verse condemns the act as
the Qur'an does and licenses nothing against any living person or community;
judgement belongs to Allah and lawful authority alone, never to individuals.

Dropped: Bukhari 660 (stock, tangential) and 3387 (compound) in favour of 3375;
11:80 and the "support = Allah" gloss (not in the fetched tafsir); no asbāb.
Future ground (26:166–173) not preempted.
