# SOURCES ledger — 26:165 (أَتَأْتُونَ ٱلذُّكْرَانَ مِنَ ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ)

Sensitive verse: Lūṭ's rebuke of a conduct. Whole article kept in a sober scholarly
register; the act is condemned as the Qur'an condemns it; no modern clinical/identity
vocabulary, no slur, no caricature, no call to punishment by anyone.

## Tafsir fetched (all from PREP; none re-fetched)

- **t14 Ibn Kathir (ar)** — USED (§3). Clause: «لما نهاهم نبي الله عن ارتكاب الفواحش
  وغشيانهم الذكور وأرشدهم إلى إتيان نسائهم اللاتي خلقهن الله لهم». Rests: prophet forbade
  the fāḥisha / approaching males AND directed them to the wives Allah created for them.
- **t169 Ibn Kathir (en abridged)** — USED (§3, confirms t14 in English): "forbade them
  from committing evil deeds and intercourse with males, and he taught them that they
  should have intercourse with their wives whom Allah had created for them." Covers
  26:165–174 (grouped); the 167–173 material (expulsion threat, detestation, prayer,
  deliverance, destruction) is FUTURE GROUND and was NOT used. 27:56 quoted there was
  NOT used (parallels 26:167, future ground).
- **t15 at-Tabari (ar)** — USED (§1, §2). Clause: «أتنكحون الذكران من بني آدم في أدبارهم».
  Rests: plain gloss; reads min al-ʿālamīn as "the males of the children of Adam."
- **t94 al-Baghawi (ar)** — USED (§2). Clause: «قال مقاتل: يعني جماع الرجال. (من العالمين)
  يعني من بني آدم». Rests: gloss attributed to Muqātil; min al-ʿālamīn = "of banī Ādam."
- **t16 al-Muyassar (ar)** — USED (§3, §4). Clause: «وتتركون ما خلق الله لاستمتاعكم
  وتناسلكم من أزواجكم ... متجاوزون ما أباحه الله لكم من الحلال إلى الحرام». Rests: wives
  made for enjoyment and procreation; the deed oversteps the ḥalāl into the ḥarām (ʿādūn).
- **t90 al-Qurtubi (ar)** — USED (§5). Clause: «كانوا ينكحونهم في أدبارهم وكانوا يفعلون ذلك
  بالغرباء على ما تقدم في الأعراف». Rests: they did it to strangers (al-ghurabāʾ);
  cross-reference to Sūrat al-Aʿrāf. Referred to as "Surat al-A'raf" only — NO ayah
  number cited, because the fetched text gives none (did not write 7:80–81).
- **t91 as-Saʿdī (ar)** — USED (§2, §4, §5, §6). Clauses: «يأتون فاحشة لم يسبقهم إليها أحد
  من العالمين» (first of the worlds to commit it — the min al-ʿālamīn note, taken ONLY
  from here); «تشابهت قلوبهم في الكفر فتشابهت أقوالهم» (hearts/words like those before);
  «مع شركهم» (alongside their shirk); «يرغبون عما خلق لهم من أزواجهم لإسرافهم وعدوانهم»
  (turning from the wives out of isrāf and ʿudwān); «فلم يزل ينهاهم» (never ceased
  forbidding) and «الناهين عنه ... المحذرين» (forbidder/warner — his role).
- **t168 Maʿārif (en)** — NOT USED. Marked WRONG GROUP in PREP (covers 26:149–158).

Two readings of min al-ʿālamīn kept as a genuine difference (§2): at-Tabari + al-Baghawi
(syntactic: "the males of mankind / banī Ādam") vs as-Saʿdī (historical: a fāḥisha none
of the worlds preceded them in). No position adjudicated.

## Hadith (§6)

- **Sahih al-Bukhari 3375.** URL fetched: https://quranx.com/hadith/Bukhari/DarusSalam/Hadith-3375
  First ten words (EN used): "May Allah forgive Lot: He wanted to have a powerful…"
  Arabic: «يَغْفِرُ اللَّهُ لِلُوطٍ إِنْ كَانَ لَيَأْوِي إِلَى رُكْنٍ شَدِيدٍ». Narrator Abū Hurayrah (RA).
  Grading: in Ṣaḥīḥ al-Bukhārī (stated as such; not upgraded). Quoted whole, one wording.
  Chosen for the prophet-standing-alone theme; non-inciting; not a punishment narration.
- Bukhari 3387 (longer compound version with the Yūsuf/prison addition) — CONFIRMED but
  NOT used, to avoid a hybrid; 3375 quotes the Lūṭ line whole on its own.
- Bukhari 660 (the "seven whom Allah shades" hadith, the one PREP suggested) — CONFIRMED
  but DROPPED: stock narration repeated widely, only tangential to this verse.

## Asbāb an-nuzūl

Not used. None established/confirmed for this verse in the fetched sources; the passage's
placement (Lūṭ among the sequence of denying peoples in Sūrat ash-Shuʿarāʾ) carries it.

## Dropped / not asserted

- No ayah number for the Aʿrāf cross-reference (source gave none) — named the sūra only.
- Did not import 11:80 ("or take refuge in a strong support") though it underlies the
  hadith: not present in the fetched tafsir for 26:165.
- Did not assert the commentators' gloss that Lūṭ's "strong support" meant Allah — only
  the bare hadith wording was in hand; kept to a general reflection within its plain sense.
- Did not exegete the future ground (26:166 wives abandoned beyond naming it as the
  sentence's completing half; 26:167 expulsion threat; 26:168 detestation; 26:169 prayer;
  26:170–173 deliverance/destruction). 26:160 (denial of messengers) cited as prior context.
