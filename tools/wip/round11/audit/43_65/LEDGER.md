## Disagreements kept (no position)
- Identity of al-ahzab: Qatada (four brought out by Bani Isra'il, via Tabari) / as-Suddi (Jews and Christians,
  via Tabari; with Mujahid via Qurtubi) / al-Kalbi and Muqatil (Nestorians, Melkites, Jacobites, via Qurtubi) /
  Tabari's own combined preference / Ibn Kathir and Muyassar (describe three statements, name no group) /
  Sa'di (parties banded on denial vs the believers). Article explicitly picks none.
- min baynihim: Qatada "ma baynahum" (Qurtubi); "min" extra (Qurtubi on 19:37); "from among those whom 'Isa
  called" (Tabari); "from among themselves" (IK en).
