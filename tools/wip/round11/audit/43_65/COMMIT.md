Card and 8-section bilingual article for 43:65: fa-khtalafa al-ahzabu
min baynihim, fa-waylun lil-ladhina zalamu min 'adhabi yawmin alim.
Closes the 'Isa (AS) passage; 43:63-64 and 43:66-67 as context only,
43:59 referenced, not re-glossed.

Sources: Tabari, Qurtubi (43:65 and, for the Maryam cross-reference,
19:37), Ibn Kathir (ar; en abridged), Muyassar, Sa'di, Ma'arif (43:63).
- Who the ahzab are, none picked: Qatada via Tabari (the four Bani
  Isra'il brought out, unnamed); as-Suddi via Tabari, with Mujahid via
  Qurtubi (Jews and Christians); al-Kalbi and Muqatil via Qurtubi
  (Nestorians, Melkites, Jacobites, positions only as Qurtubi states
  them); Tabari's combined preference; Sa'di (parties banded on denial).
- Three statements about 'Isa (IK, Muyassar), with their own verdict.
- min baynihim: Qatada's ma baynahum, Qurtubi's extra min (19:37),
  Tabari's "from among those 'Isa called", IK en "from among themselves".
- wayl (Tabari's valley), zalamu = kafaru wa ashraku (Qurtubi), alim
  like laylun na'im (Qurtubi), Day of Resurrection (Tabari, as-Suddi).
- 19:36-37 verified in data/quran-json; endings compared (kafaru /
  mashhad yawmin 'azim vs zalamu / 'adhabi yawmin alim).
Hadith: none attached in any fetched tafsir; said in one sentence.
Asbab: none; placement stated.
Licensing sentence in both languages: nothing against any living
person or community, Jewish, Christian or otherwise.
Dropped: Qurtubi's 19:37 evaluative lines and Ibn 'Abbas's mushrikun
view; Ma'arif's "tampered" line; Baghawi (verse text only).
