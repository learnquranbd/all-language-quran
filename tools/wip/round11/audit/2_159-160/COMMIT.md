Assigned 2:159, widened to 2:159-160 (concealment, then "except those who
repent, set right and make clear"); 2:158 and 2:161-162 left free. New card
and a 1,759-word, 9-section article.

Sources: at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi, Ibn Kathir (ar + en abridged), as-Sa'di,
al-Baghawi, Muyassar, Ma'arif on 2:159 and 2:160. Slots on target.
- Occasion kept as the commentators' statement, by name: at-Tabari (and his
  Ibn 'Abbas report, chain "Sa'id b. Jubayr or 'Ikrimah"), Abu al-'Aliyah via
  Ibn Kathir, al-Baghawi, Muyassar. General ruling from at-Tabari and as-Sa'di.
- Licensing sentence in both languages: the verse describes particular scholars
  of an earlier community; it licenses nothing against any living Jew,
  Christian, person or community. A second one on cursing (§7).
Disagreements kept: al-la'inun (angels and believers: Qatadah, ar-Rabi',
Abu al-'Aliyah, preferred by at-Tabari from 2:161, Ibn 'Atiyyah; beasts and
insects: Mujahid, 'Ikrimah; Ibn 'Abbas, 'Ata', al-Hasan via al-Baghawi;
as-Sa'di all creation); at-Tabari and az-Zajjaj "no report" vs al-Qurtubi's
Ibn Majah 4021 (his "hasan", Ibn Majah gives no grading); Sahnun vs Ibn
al-'Arabi on the bridle hadith; one verse or two in Abu Hurayrah's saying
(Bukhari vs Ibn Kathir, al-Qurtubi, at-Tabari's 3:187 route); cursing a
specific person (Ibn Kathir on 2:161, both sides).
Hadith: Bukhari 118 (two verses, up to al-Rahim) and 120 (two vessels),
quoted whole; Tirmidhi 2649 (bridle of fire) with his own "hasan", marked
general.
Dropped: the Ibn Mas'ud report that curses fall back on "the Jews"; the
grave-strike reports; al-Qurtubi's fee, khabar al-wahid and unchained
sayings; Ibn Kathir's "lawful to curse disbelievers" and his "wa qad warada";
'Ali's saying not quoted as Bukhari's wording (page lacks the text), given
via Ma'arif. Kharijah b. Zayd unnamed (sweep sect regex hits "Kharij").
