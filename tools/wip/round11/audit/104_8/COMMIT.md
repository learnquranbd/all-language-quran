Card and 8-section bilingual article on innaha 'alayhim mu'sadah, three
words; 104:7 and 104:9 named as neighbours only, 104:9 not treated.
Al-Hutamah's name left to the shipped 104:1 entry.

Sources: at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi, al-Baghawi, Ibn Kathir (ar + en
abridged), as-Sa'di, Muyassar, Ma'arif (grouped, 104:7 only). Slot
check clean: every Arabic file glosses 104:8 itself.

Pronouns per at-Tabari: -ha = al-Hutamah of 104:4-7; 'alayhim = the
hammazin and lammazin of 104:1.
Kept side by side, none chosen:
- mutbaqah, closed over (at-Tabari; Ibn 'Abbas via Abu Malik, Atiyyah,
  al-Hasan, ad-Dahhak, Qatadah, Ibn Zayd; al-Qurtubi; Ibn Kathir ar)
- mughlaqah, shut (Ibn 'Abbas by a second chain; as-Sa'di; al-Qurtubi
  as qila, speech of Quraysh, "Mujahid said it")
- both (al-Baghawi); "covering" (Ibn Kathir en)
Language: Ibn Zayd awsada al-bab = aghlaqa; al-Qurtubi asadtu al-bab and
the ar-Ruqayyat line; at-Tabari: read with and without hamzah, both
recited, no reciters named.

Hadith: none. Ibn Kathir's Ibn Marduyah report (Abu Hurayrah, gloss
"mutbaqah") reported with his own note that Ibn Abi Shaybah has it as
Abu Salih's words, not raised; not quoted as hadith. At-Tabari's Sa'id
report (Jibril finds it mutbaqah) marked as not traced to the Prophet.
90:20 cross-referenced by number (Ibn Kathir, al-Qurtubi); its tafsir
not fetched or reported. Dropped: 'u'sada reading (not in text),
gates/columns narrations (104:9), Muyassar's chains (104:9).
Licensing sentence in both languages; no verdict on anyone's fate.
