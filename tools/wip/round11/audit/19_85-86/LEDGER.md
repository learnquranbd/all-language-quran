# SOURCES — 19:85-86 (two-verse entry: 85 the wafd, 86 the wird)

## Tafsir fetched (audit/19_85/*.txt) and where used
- **at-Tabari (15)** — USED. Clause: "يعني بالوفد: الركبان" (wafd = the riders); wafada/wufud
  etymology; carries Ali's narration ("ما يحشر الوفد على أرجلهم... يؤتون بنوق... عليها رحال
  الذهب، وأزمتها الزبرجد... حتى يضربوا أبواب الجنة"), Abu Hurayra ("على الإبل"), Ibn Abbas
  ("ركبانا"), Qatada ("إلى الجنة"), Ibn Jurayj ("على النجائب"), the Amr b. Qays deed-as-mount report.
- **al-Qurtubi (90)** — USED. Ellipsis "إلى جنة الرحمن، ودار كرامته"; wafada = going out to a
  king "في فتح أو أمر خطير"; "من شأن الوفود عند العرب أن يقدموا بالبشارات وينتظرون الجوائز";
  grading note on Amr b. Qays report: "ولا يصح من قبل إسناده. قاله ابن العربي"; reconciles mounts
  with the barefoot-gathering hadith (cites Bukhari & Muslim, Ibn Abbas) — mounts given at the mawqif.
- **as-Sa'di (91)** — USED. "مكرمين مبجلين معظمين"; "مآلهم الرحمن، وقصدهم المنان"; the wafid must
  hold "رجاء وحسن ظن"; they come "راجين منه رحمته"... "مطمئنين... واثقين بفضله."
- **al-Baghawi (94)** — USED. wafd = "جماعات جمع وافد"; Ali: mounts, saddles of gold, "إن هموا بها
  سارت وإن هموا بها طارت" (if they wish they move, if they wish they fly).
- **Ibn Kathir ar (14) / en abridged (169)** — USED. wafd = "القادمون ركبانا" on "نجائب من نور";
  "خير موفود إليه"; 19:86 mujrimin "يساقون عنفا إلى النار"، "وردا: عطاشا" per Ata', Ibn Abbas,
  Mujahid, al-Hasan, Qatada; ties the scene to 19:73; grades the marfu' Ali narration "غريب جدا"
  and holds the mawquf form "أشبه بالصحة."
- **Ma'arif en (168)** — USED. wafd = those "received by rulers and princes with respect and honour";
  "these men will ride on their mounts which they used in their worldly life"; or deeds take the shape of mounts.
- **al-Muyassar (16)** — USED (covers 85-86). "ونسوق الكافرين... إلى النار مشاة عطاشا" (driven to the
  Fire on foot, thirsty) — anchors §1 and §5.

## Hadith
- **Sahih al-Bukhari 6527** (Aisha) — USED in §7, marked GENERAL, not attached to the wafd.
  URL: https://quranx.com/hadith/Bukhari/DarusSalam/Hadith-6527
  First ten words (EN): "The people will be gathered barefooted, naked, and uncircumcised."
  Grading: Sahih (Bukhari's own collection). Fetched via hadith.js; used exactly as al-Qurtubi uses it
  (to place the giving of mounts at the mawqif, after the bare gathering).

## Wafd-on-mounts narrations — reported WITH grading, not as sound hadith
- Ali's she-camels-of-Paradise report and the Amr b. Qays deed-as-mount report are given in §4 as
  tafsir material and immediately graded: Ibn al-Arabi (via al-Qurtubi) = isnad not sound; Ibn Kathir
  = marfu' form "very strange," mawquf nearer the truth. §7 states plainly that NO fully sound hadith
  is quoted whole on the honoured arrival itself. This satisfies the source-gate "say so in one sentence."

## Asbab
- None. No asbab an-nuzul used; verse placement (after 19:83-84, the counting of days; answering the
  boast of 19:73) supplies the context instead.

## Licensing
- §8 carries the standing sentence in both languages: the verse sorts the two parties on the Day and
  "licenses nothing against any living person or community"; names no one now, and gives no reader the
  right to place another in the driven crowd.

## Dropped / not claimed
- The lexical warada→wird "watering-place / thirsty herd" image (§5) is presented as the plain sense of
  the two words read together (nasuqu + wirdan = 'itashan, per Ibn Kathir/Muyassar, matching the shipped
  translation gloss), NOT attributed to a named mufassir's discrete reading.
- Ibn Kathir's very long marfu' Ali Paradise-description hadith (t14) was left out entirely (he flags it
  gharib jiddan).
- 19:87 (intercession / covenant with ar-Rahman) named only as "the next verse will turn to who may
  speak for whom" — not explained; future ground left for its own entry.
