Card and deep bilingual article for 22:53, the sequel to 22:52's
naskh clause: Allah lets what Satan casts become a trial that sorts
diseased and hardened hearts, while firm faith is left unharmed.

Sources (all fetched for this verse, none from memory):
- fitna as a test: at-Tabari and al-Muyassar read it as ikhtibār,
  al-Qurtubi as ḍalāla; as-Sa'di frames two groups Allah does not
  concern Himself with and a third (the believers) for whom the same
  casting is a mercy — ties to 22:54.
- umniyya (22:52): sound readings given — "recitation" (Ma'arif, Ibn
  Kathir from al-Baghawi and most; Abu Hayyan) and "wish/spoke" (Ibn
  Abbas via Ibn Kathir). Whole lesson rests on the sound reading, not
  on any incident.
- fī qulūbihim maraḍ = doubters/hypocrites (Ibn Kathir; Ibn Jurayj)
  vs al-qāsiya = mushrikūn (Ibn Jurayj via Tabari, al-Muyassar);
  disagreement kept: Muqatil b. Hayyan read the hardened as the Jews.
- shiqāq baʿīd across Ibn Kathir, al-Qurtubi, at-Tabari, al-Muyassar.

Gharānīq ("satanic verses"): reported ONLY as unauthenticated. Ibn
Kathir — all chains mursal/munqaṭiʿ, none sahih; al-Qurtubi — the
attributed words are a lie against the Prophet ﷺ; Ma'arif — not proved
by authentic sources, some judged it invented. No claim in any voice
that the Prophet recited idol-praise; the alleged words are not quoted.

Hadith: Sahih Muslim 144 (Hudhayfa), trials presented to hearts, the
white heart no fitna harms and the black overturned heart — the exact
mirror of the verse. Whole wording, one collection, grading not upgraded.

Wrongdoer safeguard carried in both languages: the verse describes a
spiritual condition and licenses nothing against any living person or
community.

Dropped (see LEDGER): the gharānīq incident and its words; ath-Thaʿlabī's
prophet-sahw claim; ʿIyāḍ and ar-Rāzī as attributed rejecters (not
cleanly in fetched text); al-Baghawi's "devil named Abyaḍ" detail.
