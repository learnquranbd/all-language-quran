# SOURCES ledger — 22:53

## Tafsir fetched (all in audit/22_53/, from PREP; none re-fetched)
- t15 at-Tabari — NAMED. fitna = ikhtibār: "فتنة يقول: اختبارا يختبر به الذين في قلوبهم مرض من النفاق"; disease = nifāq/doubt in the Messenger's truthfulness "الشكّ في صدق رسول الله". al-qāsiya = mushrikūn (Ibn Jurayj): "وللذين قست قلوبهم عن الإيمان بالله، فلا تلين ولا ترعوي، وهم المشركون". shiqāq: "وإن مشركي قومك يا محمد لفي خلاف الله في أمره، بعيد من الحق". (NOTE: Tabari's text narrates the gharānīq incident with the alleged words — DROPPED, see below.)
- t90 al-Qurtubi — NAMED. fitna = ḍalāla: "ليجعل ما يلقي الشيطان فتنة أي ضلالة"; maraḍ = "شرك ونفاق"; qāsiya = "فلا تلين لأمر الله"; shiqāq = "الكافرين لفي خلاف، وعصيان، ومشاقة لله ... ولرسوله". Gharānīq rejection (used): "فأما ما يضاف إليه من قولهم: تلك الغرانيق العلا، فكذب على النبي صلى الله عليه وسلم؛ لأن فيه تعظيم الأصنام، ولا يجوز ذلك على الأنبياء."
- t91 as-Sa'di — NAMED. two groups Allah does not care for "لطائفتين ... لا يبالي الله بهم"; sick heart = weakness/incomplete faith, least doubt affects it "ضعف وعدم إيمان تام ... فيؤثر ... أدنى شبهة"; hard heart = coarse, unmoved by rebuke/reminder, turns whisper into a proof and disputes "جعلوه حجة لهم على باطلهم، وجادلوا به وشاقوا الله ورسوله"; shiqāq = "مشاقة لله، ومعاندة للحق". THIRD GROUP = mercy: "وأما الطائفة الثالثة، فإنه يكون رحمة في حقها."
- t14 Ibn Kathir (ar) — NAMED. maraḍ = "شك وشرك وكفر ونفاق"; Ibn Jurayj: sick = munāfiqūn, qāsiya = mushrikūn; Muqatil b. Hayyan: qāsiya = the Jews "هم اليهود"; shiqāq = "ضلال ومخالفة وعناد بعيد ... من الحق والصواب"; naskh = "الإزالة والرفع", Ibn Abbas "فيبطل الله ما ألقى الشيطان". Gharānīq rejection (used): "وهذا أيضا مرسل ... وكلها مرسلات ومنقطعات والله أعلم"; names al-Bayhaqī (Dalāʾil), Ibn Isḥāq (Sīra), Mūsā b. ʿUqba as carriers of mursal versions.
- t16 al-Muyassar — NAMED. ikhtibār "اختبارًا للذين في قلوبهم شك ونفاق، ولقساة القلوب من المشركين الذين لا يؤثِّرُ فيهم زجر"; shiqāq = both groups "في عداوة شديدة لله ورسوله وخلافٍ للحق".
- t94 al-Baghawi — used only for the umniyya reading transmitted via Ibn Kathir/Ma'arif ("tamannā = talā/qara'a"). His long gharānīq narration (incl. alleged words, the "shayṭān named Abyaḍ", the Abyssinia return) DROPPED — see below.
- t168 Ma'arif al-Qur'an (en) — NAMED. tamannā = qara'a, umniyya = recitation, "quite clear and straight-forward ... adopted by many commentators including Abu Hayyan". Gharānīq rejection (used): "an incident which is not proved by authentic sources ... Some scholars have held that it is invented by some heretics and enemies of Islam ... an exercise in futility and absolutely undesirable."
- t169 Ibn Kathir (en abridged) — NAMED. Confirms the ar reading: gharānīq reports "all come through Mursal chains ... I do not think that any of them may be regarded as Sahih"; maraḍ = "doubt, Shirk, disbelief and hypocrisy"; Ibn Jurayj sick=hypocrites, hardened=idolaters; shiqāq = "far away in misguidance, resistance and stubbornness."

## Hadith

First ten words (EN used): "Temptations will be presented to men's hearts as reed mat". Grading: sound by inclusion in Sahih Muslim; not upgraded/altered. Used whole in §4, one collection's wording only.
- Bukhari 660 (seven under the shade) fetched as PREP's suggested candidate but NOT USED — only tangential to the fitna/hearts theme; Muslim 144 is the direct match.

## Asbāb an-nuzūl
- NOT USED as an established sabab. The gharānīq occasion-report attached to 22:52 is exactly the material the muhaddithūn rejected (mursal/munqaṭiʿ per Ibn Kathir; kadhib per al-Qurtubi; unproven per Ma'arif). Verse placement (after 22:52's naskh clause, before 22:54's believers) written instead.

## Dropped (could not confirm / must not assert)
- The gharānīq INCIDENT itself and its alleged idol-praise words, narrated in at-Tabari (t15) and al-Baghawi (t94): DROPPED entirely. Reported only that the scholars did not authenticate it (Ibn Kathir mursal/munqaṭiʿ, al-Qurtubi kadhib, Ma'arif unproven/invented). Never asserted in any voice that the Prophet ﷺ recited praise of the idols; the alleged words are not quoted.
- ath-Thaʿlabī's claim (via al-Qurtubi) that the verse proves prophets may be subject to sahw/nisyān/ghalaṭ from Satan's whispering: DROPPED. Brief rule forbids saying in the article's voice that a prophet erred; al-Qurtubi himself immediately refutes the gharānīq attribution as kadhib. Not used.
- al-Qāḍī ʿIyāḍ (ash-Shifāʾ) and ar-Rāzī named in the orchestrator's watch-points as rejecters: DROPPED as attributions. Ibn Kathir's fetched text only says ʿIyāḍ "addressed" it in ash-Shifāʾ (the following clause is garbled in the fetched copy), so his exact verdict is not cleanly confirmable here; ar-Rāzī is not in any fetched text. Rejection is instead carried by Ibn Kathir, al-Qurtubi and Ma'arif, all confirmed above.
- al-Baghawi's "a devil named Abyaḍ" and the Abyssinia-return detail: DROPPED (part of the unauthenticated narration; not needed).
