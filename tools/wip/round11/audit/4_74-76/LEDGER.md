## Licensing (binding)
§1.2 (short) and §8.1 (full), in EN and BN: the verses concerned the believers of the Prophet's ﷺ time and the oppressed believers held in Makkah. They license nothing against any living person or community, no one may lift them out to justify violence, and armed struggle in Islamic law belongs to legitimate authority and qualified scholars, never to individuals or groups acting on their own. The card says the same. The commands quoted from the tafsir (§1.2) are written in the third person in both languages, not as imperatives in the article's voice. The application (§9) is limited to du'a, advocacy, aid, testimony and everyday choices.
