Assigned 4:74, widened to 4:74-76 (selling this world for the Hereafter; why do
you not fight for the oppressed of Makkah; the weak plot of Shaytan). 4:71-73 used
for placement only; 4:77 left free. New card and a 1,749-word, 9-section article.

Sources: at-Tabari, Ibn Kathir (ar + en), al-Qurtubi, as-Sa'di, al-Baghawi,
al-Muyassar and Ma'arif on 4:74, 4:75 and 4:76, all slots on target.
Licensing early (§1) and in full (§8), both languages: the believers of that time
and the oppressed believers of Makkah; licenses nothing against anyone living;
armed struggle belongs to legitimate authority and scholars, never individuals.
Kept as differences, no position:
- yashruna: sell (at-Tabari, as-Suddi; Ibn Zayd "both ways") vs buy, of the
  hypocrites (al-Baghawi); as-Sa'di's soundest: the call is to the laggards.
- alladhina as the fighters (al-Qurtubi, at-Tabari) vs the enemy who sell their
  religion (Ibn Kathir ar; as-Sa'di's third view).
- wal-mustad'afin joined to Allah (az-Zajjaj, az-Zuhri, in al-Qurtubi) vs to
  sabil (Muhammad ibn Yazid); at-Tabari: "and for the oppressed".
Kayd: hidden routes, too weak for the least truth (as-Sa'di); followers without
hope or fear (at-Tabari); Badr (al-Baghawi); Ma'arif's two conditions. The prayer
answered through 'Attab ibn Asid (al-Baghawi, Ma'arif).
Hadith: Bukhari 4587 and 1357 (Ibn 'Abbas: "my mother and I were among the
oppressed"), each whole, marked as a Companion's words; 3123 (the guarantee),
same route from Malik as al-Baghawi's; 3046 ("free the captive"), marked general,
beside al-Qurtubi's ransom-by-wealth duty. 4588 left to 4:98; 804 not used.
Asbab: none established; placement after 4:71-73 used instead.
Dropped: Ma'arif's Ibn 'Abbas "attack him" (not in the fetched al-Qurtubi),
Abu Jandal "ibn Sahl", al-Qurtubi's mixed 4588/1357 wording, the two-thirds report.
