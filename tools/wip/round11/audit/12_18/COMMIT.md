Replaces the 814-word, 6-section article with a 1,753-word, 8-section one,
from at-Tabari, Ibn Kathir (ar and en), al-Qurtubi, as-Sa'di, al-Baghawi,
the Muyassar and Ma'arif on 12:18. Slots checked, none shifted. The shipped
card is kept.
Placement after 12:16-17; the blood as confirmation of their story
(as-Sa'di, Ibn Kathir en), the Muyassar's "meant to testify, became proof".
Dam kadhib: at-Tabari's two grammar views (Basran, al-Farra'), al-Baghawi,
al-Qurtubi; whose blood (Mujahid, Ibn 'Abbas, as-Suddi, Qatadah) as reports;
the kadib reading of al-Hasan and 'A'ishah.
The untorn shirt only as attributed: Ibn 'Abbas, al-Hasan, ash-Sha'bi,
Qatadah; "they forgot" (Ibn Kathir) beside "Allah paired a counter-sign"
(al-Qurtubi's scholars, Ma'arif); as-Sa'di's indications and the dream.
Story material (speaking wolf) noted as "fi al-qissa"/"ruwiya", unused.
Disagreements kept: 'Amir/al-Mawardi's three signs of one shirt vs
al-Qurtubi's "different shirts"; brothers' later prophethood (al-Baghawi
on 12:6 vs al-Qurtubi and Ma'arif on 12:5), no position taken.
Al-Qurtubi's amarat ruling (qasamah, Ibn al-'Arabi), Ma'arif for judges.
Sawwalat, fa-sabrun jamil (az-Zajjaj, Qutrub, al-Mubarrad on the accusative
reading) and al-musta'an glossed by name; as-Sa'di's line on complaint to
the Creator, one bridge to 12:86. Licensing line in both languages.
Hadith: Hibban's "sabr la shakwa fihi" given as Ibn Kathir's mursal with
the unidentified narrator; Bukhari 4690 ('A'ishah, the ifk) quoted as a
use of the phrase; no sound hadith explains it, the article says so.
Removed from the shipped article: the "first strike" hadith (not cited on
this verse), "the blood itself is the liar", the 15:85/70:5 notes, the
unsourced three-step scheme. Dropped: Ibn Abi Rifa'a on 12:83, Habib ibn
Abi Thabit's report, ath-Thawri's saying (length).
