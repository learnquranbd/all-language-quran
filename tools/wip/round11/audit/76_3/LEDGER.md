## Disagreements kept (no position taken)
- sabil: way of good/evil, religion (Ibn Kathir's majority) vs exit from the womb (Mujahid etc.; Ibn Kathir: gharib)
  vs benefits/harms by nature (al-Qurtubi's qila) vs wretchedness/felicity (Mujahid in at-Tabari and al-Qurtubi).

  al-Farra'; rejected by the Basrans per al-Qurtubi; al-Baghawi's qila).
