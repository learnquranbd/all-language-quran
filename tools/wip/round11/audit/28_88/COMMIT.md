Replaces the 744-word, 6-section article with a 1,741-word, 9-section article
built only from the eight tafsir files fetched for 28:88.

Sources: la tad'u glossed as worship (at-Tabari, "O Muhammad"; al-Qurtubi;
al-Muyassar); as-Sa'di "make your worship sincere"; la ilaha illa hu per
al-Qurtubi (negation and affirmation), Ibn Kathir, at-Tabari, as-Sa'di (love
and worship). Kullu shay'in halik: Muyassar halik wa fan, Ma'arif, Ibn Kathir
(the Everlasting, with 55:26-27), as-Sa'di mudmahill and his argument that
worship of the perishing is batil.
Illa wajhah kept as a disagreement: at-Tabari reports "except Him" and "except
what is intended for His Face" (with the poet's line) and does not choose.
Mujahid is under the first in al-Qurtubi, the second in Ibn Kathir (with
ath-Thawri; Abu al-'Aliyah and Sufyan in al-Qurtubi, al-Baghawi); both kept.
Minor glosses with holders: din (as-Sadiq), jah (Abu 'Ubaydah), mulk (al-
Baghawi, qila). Ibn Kathir's reconciliation (essences vs deeds) and al-
Muyassar's sifah sentence reported as theirs; no position in the article.
Hukm and the return glossed from all six; 28:70 shares the two clauses.

Hadith: Bukhari 3841 (Labid's line, attached by Ibn Kathir via Abu Salamah),
quoted whole with the Umayyah clause. Ibn 'Umar at the ruin (Ibn Abi ad-
Dunya via Ibn Kathir) marked as a Companion's practice, ungraded.

Dropped from the old article: "Bukhari and Muslim" (only Bukhari confirmed),
the Ahl as-Sunnah kalam sentence in the article's voice, 13:22 and 30:38, the
man/shay' contrast, the participle claim, the Labid biography. Placement from
Ibn Kathir on 28:85-87; no Juhfa report. Licensing sentence added. Neighbours
28:56, 28:76, 28:77 one line each.
