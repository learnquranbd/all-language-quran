Card and 8-section article (1,779 EN words, 20 paragraphs, 55-110 each).
12:76 is 33 Arabic words, per PREP.md.

Sources: all eight tafsir IDs; 169 is grouped 12:73-76, stated, and only
its 12:76 portion used. Search order "to remove suspicion" (al-Qurtubi,
al-Baghawi, as-Sa'di), Muyassar's "to make the plan firm", Ibn Kathir's
tawriya. Qatada / Ibn Jurayj istighfar reports with al-Qurtubi's answer
(all by Allah's command); as-Sa'di on istakhrajaha. Kidna glossed only as
the mufassirun give it: sana'na (Ibn Abbas, Ibn Jurayj, as-Suddi,
ad-Dahhak), dabbarna (al-Qutabi), aradna (Ibn al-Anbari), requital of
kayd and "tadbir bil-haqq" (al-Baghawi), "loved kayd" (Ibn Kathir),
yassarna (as-Sa'di, Muyassar). Din al-malik: sultan (Ibn Abbas,
ad-Dahhak) vs hukm/qada' (Qatada, as-Suddi), at-Tabari's reconciliation;
the king's penalty (double fine; beating and double fine; Ma'arif) vs the
brothers' law (Ibn Zayd, Muhammad ibn Ka'b).
Hiyal reported, none issued: al-Qurtubi's permissibility with conditions
and his contrast with Abu Hanifa; zakat consensus; Malik vs Abu Hanifa
kept as a disagreement; al-Muhallab; Ibn al-Arabi's "grave mistake";
Ma'arif's hilah shar'iyyah and consensus on voiding devices. The article
states the verse licenses no stratagem to evade a right or ruling today
and refers rulings to qualified scholars.
Fawqa kulli dhi 'ilm: chain ending at Allah (al-Hasan, Qatada, at-Tabari)
and Ibn Abbas's 'alim = Allah; Ali's "you are right and I was wrong".

No fetched tafsir attaches a sound hadith. Bukhari 6955 (Abu Bakr's zakat
letter, via al-Qurtubi's Kitab al-Hiyal note) paraphrased, marked not attached.
Dropped: at-Tabari's "some called it a lapse" (the same view is already
carried, as al-Baghawi's report, in the 12:70 article); editor's footnote;
Ibn Mas'ud's variant (budget).
