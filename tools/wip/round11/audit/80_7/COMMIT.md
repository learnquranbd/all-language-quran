Card and 8-section bilingual article for 80:7, wa-ma 'alayka alla
yazzakka (4 words), the verse closing 80:5-6 about the self-sufficient man.

Sources: at-Tabari, Ibn Kathir (ar), al-Qurtubi, al-Baghawi, as-Sa'di, all
on 80:7; Muyassar (grouped 80:5-7); Ibn Kathir en (grouped 80:1-10);
Ma'arif (grouped 80:3-12).
- ma kept as a difference: question (Tabari, Muyassar: what is upon you
  if he is not purified of his kufr) vs negation (Ibn Kathir: not held
  answerable; Sa'di: not called to account). Qurtubi and Baghawi: upon
  you is only the conveying. No fetched commentator puts the refusal on
  the Prophet; the article never says he erred.
- yazzakka = purified of kufr and submit (Tabari, Muyassar), guided and
  believe (Qurtubi, Baghawi), zakah attained (IK). Same word closes 80:3
  (one cross-reference only).
- Mustaghni: unnamed Quraysh leader (IK en); Muyassar, Sa'di, Ma'arif on
  his stance. Licensing sentence in both languages.
- Sa'di's principle (known before imagined) and seeker priority; IK en's
  equal warning to all; Ma'arif's teaching priority. Emphases kept, no
  position.
Hadith: Tirmidhi 3331 ('A'ishah), quoted whole, hasan gharib, his mursal
note reported; marked as about the surah's opening, not this verse.
Dropped: leader's name (in no fetched text), Sa'di's la yanbaghi clause,
80:1-4 asbab detail and Ma'arif's yadhdhakkar note (80:3 ground), IK en's
Abu Ya'la/Muwatta' note. 80:8-10 and 80:11-12 named only.
