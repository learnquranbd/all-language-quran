Disagreement kept: ma read as question (Tabari, Muyassar) vs negation (Ibn Kathir ar/en, Sa'di); Qurtubi/Baghawi "only conveying" framing. Article states outcome is the same and no fetched commentator places the man's refusal on the Prophet ﷺ. Emphasis difference kept: Ibn Kathir en (equality in the call) vs Sa'di/Ma'arif (order of attention), no position.

## Hadith
- Jami' at-Tirmidhi 3331 (Dar-us-Salam Vol 6, Book 44). URL: https://quranx.com/hadith/Tirmidhi/Hadith-3331 (saved: hadith_Tirmidhi_3331.txt).
  First ten words used: "'He frowned and turned away' was revealed about Ibn Umm". Quoted whole (English of the page).
  Grading: Abu 'Isa: هذا حديث حسن غريب (hasan gharib) — reported, not upgraded. His note that some narrated it from Hisham b. 'Urwah from his father without 'A'ishah — reported.
  Marked as concerning the surah's opening, not attached to 80:7 in particular.

## Asbab
Used only as given in Ibn Kathir (en) (80:1-10 group) and Tirmidhi 3331: leader unnamed ("one of the great leaders of the Quraysh" / "a revered man from the idolaters"). Not fetched from al-Wahidi.

## Dropped / not used
- Names of the Quraysh leader(s) (e.g. any names in other asbab reports): not in any fetched text for 80:7 or its groups. Article says the text does not name him.
- As-Sa'di's clause فإنه لا ينبغي لك (does not befit you): omitted, to keep the article clear of any fault-language toward the Prophet ﷺ; his positive clause (الأليق الواجب) reported instead.
- Ibn Kathir en's "The Prophet being reprimanded" heading and 80:1-2 asbab detail (frowning): 80:1-2 / 80:3 ground; not used.
- Ma'arif's yazzakka/yadhdhakkar analysis and dhikra note: 80:3-4 ground (80:3 drafted separately); only one cross-reference to 80:3 by number.
- Ibn Kathir en's statement that Abu Ya'la / Ibn Jarir recorded 'A'ishah's report and the Muwatta' note: not confirmed on a fetched page; not used.
- Any morphology of yazzakka (yatazakka assimilation) beyond Tabari's gloss yatatahhar: not from fetched text; not used.
- 80:8-10 and 80:11-16: named by number only (future ground).
