Card and 8-section bilingual article for 44:43: inna shajarata
al-zaqqum. Three words, the subject only; 44:44 (ta'amu al-athim) named
as the predicate the sentence needs; 44:45-50 placed in one line.

Sources: Tabari, Qurtubi, Sa'di, Ibn Kathir (ar), Muyassar, Ma'arif.
Baghawi's entry is the verse text only; Ibn Kathir (en) used to
cross-check the Arabic.
- Placement from Sa'di: after the Day of Decision, the split into two
  parties; 44:51 opens the other side.
- Qurtubi's one-letter note: every shajara paused on with ha except this
  one in al-Dukhan; verse-base shows the open ta here vs 37:62. No
  reason invented.
- The tree as the texts give it: Tabari and Muyassar (root of the Blaze,
  its fruit the food; 37:64 wording checked), Sa'di (worst and most
  hideous of trees), Ma'arif's pointer to 37:64-65. No etymology and no
  earthly plant: none in the sources, said plainly.
- Kept as a difference: al-athim as kufr only (Tabari, Ibn Kathir) vs
  many sins / kufr and ma'asi (Muyassar, Sa'di).
- Abu al-Darda and the learner who said al-yatim (Tabari, two chains;
  Ibn Kathir via Ibn Jarir), ungraded; Ibn Kathir's gloss "no food
  other than it". No ruling on recitation drawn.
- The drop: Mujahid (Ibn Kathir), Ibn Abbas mawquf (Tabari).
- Abu Jahl: Ibn Kathir's "more than one said... included, not specific
  to him", no chain given; licensing sentence EN+BN.
Hadith: Tirmidhi 2585 (Ibn Abbas, after reciting 3:102), quoted whole,
graded hasan sahih by at-Tirmidhi; marked as not attached to 44:43.
Asbab: none in the sources; not used.
Dropped: butter-and-dates mockery (not in 44:43 texts; on 37:63);
Ma'arif's before/after-entry question (rests on 44:47); 44:45-50 detail;
17:60's Bukhari report not repeated.
- Review: Tirmidhi 2585 grading re-verified on the refetched quranx page (Arabic: hadha hadithun hasanun sahih).
