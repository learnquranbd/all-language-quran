## Hadith
- Jami' at-Tirmidhi 2585 (Dar-us-Salam Vol 4, Book 37). URL: https://quranx.com/hadith/Tirmidhi/Hadith-2585 (saved: hadith_Tirmidhi_2585.txt)
  - First ten words of the wording used: "If only a drop of Az-Zaqqum were to drip into"
  - Grading: at-Tirmidhi's own, on the page: «قَالَ أَبُو عِيسَى هَذَا حَدِيثٌ حَسَنٌ صَحِيحٌ» — reported as hasan sahih.
  - Context: spoken after reciting 3:102, not attached to 44:43; the article says so and credits Ibn Kathir («وقد تقدم نحوه مرفوعا») with setting it beside this verse.
- Abu al-Darda report (Tabari): a Companion's teaching anecdote with chains, ungraded by both mufassirs; reported as given, no grading supplied, no ruling on recitation drawn.
