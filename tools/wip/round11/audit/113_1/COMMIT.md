Card and 8-section bilingual article (1,748 EN words) on qul a'udhu
bi-rabbi al-falaq, four Arabic words from data.

Sources: Tabari, Qurtubi, Ibn Kathir (ar/en), Baghawi, Sa'di, Muyassar,
Ma'arif. All slots checked against 113:1; no neighbour fetch needed.

Disagreements kept without a position: al-falaq as daybreak (Ibn Abbas
via al-Awfi, al-Hasan, Sa'id ibn Jubayr, Jabir, Mujahid, Qatada), a place
in Hell (prison, house, pit, valley, tree, a name of Hell, with names),
creation (Ibn Abbas via Ali ibn Abi Talha, ad-Dahhak) and all that
splits (Qurtubi from al-Hasan). Weighing given side by side: Baghawi,
Ibn Kathir's report of Ibn Jarir, Tabari's own closing paragraph,
Qurtubi's derivation. Why "rabb al-falaq": Tabari vs Ma'arif/Mazhari.
Makki/Madani split kept (Qurtubi; Baghawi and IK-ar Madani).

Magic report given only as Qurtubi, Baghawi, Ma'arif and IK-en give it,
with Qurtubi's Sahih boundary; long A'isha narration not quoted and no
number given. No living person or group named; licensing line in both
languages. Ibn Kathir's munkar verdict on the marfu' "pit" report kept.
