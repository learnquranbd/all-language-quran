Replaces the 799-word, 5-section article with a 1,754-word, 8-section one built only
on the eight tafsir files fetched for 25:70 and two hadith confirmed on quranx.

Sources: at-Tabari's definitions of taba, amana and 'amila salihan, Qatada via
al-Baghawi, as-Sa'di's three parts of tawba. 25:68-69 in two attributed lines
(Ma'arif on the doubling, the Muyassar on the abiding).
Kept as a disagreement, no position: yubaddilu as traits exchanged in this life
(Ibn 'Abbas via 'Ali ibn Abi Talha and Ibn Jurayj, al-Hasan, 'Ata', ad-Dahhak; Abu
l-'Aliya and Qatada per Ibn Kathir; al-Baghawi's list) vs the record turned on the
Day (Sa'id ibn al-Musayyib, Makhul, 'Ali ibn al-Husayn; Salman and Sa'id ibn Jubayr
per al-Qurtubi). At-Tabari's preference and argument, Ibn Kathir's remorse
mechanism, as-Sa'di's combination, an-Nahhas, az-Zajjaj and al-Qurtubi's own view
reported as theirs. Sa'id ibn Jubayr sits on both lists depending on the book; said so.
Second disagreement: the killer's repentance (Ibn Kathir: 4:93 read on the
unrepentant, 4:48, the man who killed a hundred) vs al-Qurtubi's noted difference
and ad-Dahhak's Makkan/Madinan dating in at-Tabari.
Hadith: Bukhari 4810 (Ibn 'Abbas, the people of shirk asking for expiation; names
25:68 and 39:53), attached by Ibn Kathir; Muslim 190 (Abu Dharr, the last man out of
the Fire), brought by at-Tabari, Ibn Kathir, al-Qurtubi, al-Baghawi. Both quoted
whole; grading as their place in each Sahih. Asbab: Bukhari 4810 only.
Dropped: Successor reports on the occasion (Wahshi, ad-Dahhak, Ibn Zayd), al-Baghawi's
"two years" report, Ibn Kathir's ungraded and gharib narrations, 4:110 and 9:104.
Defect fixed: the old article merged the two sayings as "two moments of the same
mercy", which at-Tabari explicitly rejects; it also had "this passage came down"
where al-Bukhari names 25:68.
Licensing sentence on the 25:68 sins in both languages.
