## Points from the shipped short article
Kept (confirmed): last verse of the surah (al-Qurtubi, Ma'arif, as-Sa'di); as-Sa'di's three fields of sabr; sabiru implies another party (at-Tabari's mufa'alah); ribat from tethering horses, 8:60 (al-Baghawi, al-Qurtubi, Ma'arif); the Muslim hadith on wudu, steps and waiting (Muslim 251); taqwa as a necessary ingredient of all (Ma'arif); falah as winning the loved and escaping the feared (as-Sa'di).
Dropped (no fetched source for this verse): "the surah walked through the wound of Uhud" and the whole "Memory of Uhud" section, including Bukhari's al-Bara report on the archers and the claim that the verse's last command "answers the surah's central wound"; 3:121; 23:1 and its list of habits; 103:3 (Surah al-'Asr); "ladder of patience"; "taqwa seals the sequence the way intention seals a deed"; "all four commands are plural, addressed to a community"; the closing self-help method paragraph; the pairing with 3:190-195 as "night devotions to daylight endurance".
Wrong or unsupported: the Uhud framing is not in any fetched tafsir on 3:200 (none links the verse to Uhud); "Muslim relates ... perfecting wudu when it is difficult" is fine, but the shipped text presents ribat's "plain sense" as the frontier without noting that Ibn Kathir's own first gloss is perseverance in the place of worship and that al-Qurtubi argues the prayer sense is lexical (al-Khalil).
