Adds the Tadabbur card and bilingual article for the wall's ta'wil, the
reveal that closes the Musa–al-Khidr story. The wall incident ships at
18:77; this beat is the meaning and its lessons, not the incident.

Sources (all fetched on 18:82): at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi, as-Sa'di,
Ibn Kathir (ar + en), al-Baghawi, Ma'arif, al-Muyassar.

What the sources gave:
- Kanz read two ways, both kept: buried wealth/money (Ikrimah, Qatadah,
  at-Tabari's own tarjih, al-Muyassar's gold and silver) and a treasure of
  knowledge / inscribed golden tablet (Sa'id b. Jubayr, Mujahid, Ibn Abbas,
  al-Hasan; marfu' via Uthman). az-Zajjaj bridges; Ibn Kathir reconciles.
- Righteous father safeguards the inheritance; Ibn Abbas: the boys are
  protected by his righteousness, never called righteous themselves. Reach
  disputed — seven forefathers (at-Tabari, al-Baghawi), a weaver; al-Qurtubi's
  rule of seven; Ibn al-Munkadir on household and neighbours. Attributed as a
  dispute, not settled.
- The adab of attribution: aradtu (boat, to self), aradna (boy), arada
  rabbuka (wall, to Allah) — Ma'arif and as-Sa'di, with Ibrahim 26:80 and the
  jinn 72:10 as the model.
- Prophet-vs-wali kept as a live disagreement: Ibn Kathir and al-Tha'labi
  lean prophet (+18:65); as-Sa'di reads wali/ilham (28:7, 16:68); no position.
- rahmatan min rabbika as the story's lesson (as-Sa'di's rida, at-Tabari's
  ta'dib for the Prophet); tasti'/tastati' verb subtlety (Ibn Kathir).

Guardrail: al-Qurtubi's refutation of the antinomian claim that awliya are
freed from the sacred law (zandaqa/kufr), plus Qatadah on permitted-then-
forbidden — al-Khidr licenses nothing for anyone. Stated plainly, both
languages, that the verse condemns no living group.

Dropped: no sound hadith settles the treasure's content; the gold/silver and
tablet reports carried as tafsir-attributions with grading uncertain, not
upgraded (Ibn Kathir's own "even if it is authentic"). Bukhari 660 and the
naming-hadith are off this beat and not cited. Boys'/father's names, the
alive-vs-dead debate and the ashudd-age figures dropped as off-scope.
