Card and 8-section bilingual article for 47:31: wa-la-nabluwannakum
hatta na'lama l-mujahidina minkum wa-s-sabirina wa-nabluwa akhbarakum.
Placed after 47:29-30 (hypocrites), which serve as context only.

Sources: Tabari, Qurtubi, Sa'di, Ibn Kathir (ar; en 47:29-31),
Baghawi, Muyassar, Ma'arif.
- Testing: Ibn Zayd (balwa = ikhtibar), Baghawi and Qurtubi (dealt
  with as a tester), Qurtubi (binding by the laws though outcomes are
  known), Ibn Kathir (commands and prohibitions), Sa'di.
- "Until We know" glosses side by side, none chosen: Ibn Kathir
  (known as having happened; Ibn Abbas: to see), Muyassar (what He
  knew from eternity made manifest), Qurtubi (Ibn Abbas: distinguish,
  Ali: see; 'ilm shahada), Baghawi ('ilm al-wujud), Ma'arif (after
  occurrence), Tabari (until His party and allies know).
- Jihad kept as both: fighting (Tabari, Muyassar, Baghawi, Sa'di)
  and broad (Ibn Kathir, Qurtubi, Ibn Abbas via 2:155).
- Akhbar per Tabari, Muyassar, Qurtubi, Baghawi.
- Qira'at: nun vs ya' ('Asim per Tabari; Abu Bakr from 'Asim per
  Qurtubi, Baghawi) with Baghawi's reasons; Ya'qub's wa-nablu.
- Cross-refs only as cited: 2:155, 2:214 (Ibn Abbas), 29:2-3 (Ibn Zayd).
- Uthman's saying (Ibn Kathir on 47:30); al-Fudayl's prayer (Qurtubi).
Hadith: none attached by any fetched tafsir; stated in one sentence.
Licensing sentence in both languages on the hypocrites: licenses
nothing against any living person; no one may judge another's heart.
Dropped: 3:142 (not cited), unnamed "similar texts", Bara'ah excursus.
