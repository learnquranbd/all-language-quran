Assigned 2:136, widened to 2:135-138 (be Jews or Christians, the creed
taught word for word, the measure of 2:137, sibghat Allah), inside the free
window; 2:134 and 2:139-141 left free. New card and a 1,763-word,
9-section article.

Sources: at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi, Ibn Kathir (ar), as-Sa'di, al-Baghawi,
al-Muyassar, Ma'arif al-Qur'an on 2:135 to 2:138 (all at-Tabari slots on target).
- 2:135: two invitations, and at-Tabari's reading of the reply as common
  ground in Ibrahim's millah. Hanif: at-Tabari's five readings, his verdict
  (istiqamah) with the 3:67 tests; Abu Qilabah via Ibn Kathir (believes in
  all the messengers).
- 2:136: qulu/amanna (as-Sa'di), detail vs general (Ibn Kathir), the gift
  of religion and not kingship (as-Sa'di), no distinction = no believing in
  some and denying others (at-Tabari, al-Farra), 4:150-151.
- 2:253 reconciled only as the sources do: "in belief" (al-Muyassar), some
  superior in some ways (Ma'arif), honour in the naming (as-Sa'di).
- 2:137: tashbih between two believings (at-Tabari, Ibn Abbas's reading
  reported "if sound"); shiqaq as sides (al-Qurtubi).
- 2:138: din / fitrah; the rite attributed to at-Tabari and the Ibn Abbas
  report; Qatadah's "dye of Allah is Islam"; as-Sa'di on dye becoming nature.
Disagreement kept: al-Asbat (al-Baghawi's qila, all sons prophets, vs Ibn
Kathir from al-Bukhari: tribes with prophets among them). No position, and
nothing in the article's voice about Yusuf's brothers.
Hadith: Bukhari 4485 (neither believe nor disbelieve), Muslim 727 (727a
wording, Fajr sunnah), each quoted whole.
Asbab not used (Ibn Ishaq chain, ungraded); placement used instead.
Licence sentence on 2:137 in both languages.
Dropped: 'Uthman's blood report, the named tribes, "does your Lord dye",
Ma'qil marfu', convert's ghusl, Ma'arif's sect polemic.
