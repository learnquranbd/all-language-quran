## Named attributions in the article, each traceable above
- Ibn Kathir: verse consoles the Prophet; "what they used to ridicule" = the punishment they deemed far-fetched; cross-ref 6:34. (14, 169)
- al-Qurtubi: tasliyah/taʿziyah; ḥāqa = aḥāṭa wa-dāra (encompassed/encircled); "be patient as they were patient," then promise of victory; jazāʾ (recompense). (90)
- as-Sa'di: consolation that this was the habit of earlier nations; ḥāqa = nazala; "the means were cut off" (taqaṭṭaʿat ʿanhum al-asbāb); "let these beware." (91)
- at-Tabari: messengers sent before him to their peoples; fa-wajaba wa-nazala; the trial/torment their messengers had warned of; these mockers no different from their forebears. (15)
- al-Baghawi: ḥāqa = nazala; jazāʾ istihzāʾihim (recompense of their mockery). (94)
- al-Muyassar: the punishment that had been the object of their scoffing. (16)

## ḥāqa (watch-point 2) — the two shades both kept, not merged
- Encompassed / encircled: al-Qurtubi (aḥāṭa wa-dāra).
- Befell / descended: al-Baghawi, at-Tabari (nazala / fa-wajaba wa-nazala), as-Sa'di (nazala bihim).
Article §3 presents them as two angles on one event; no scholar's position overstated.

## Hadith
- None cited. PREP's default suggestion (Bukhari 660, the seven whom Allah shades) was
  fetched and read; it is unrelated to 21:41 and was rejected. No tafsir here attaches a
  sound, verse-specific hadith, so the article states plainly (§6 p3) that none is added
  and none should be invented. Correct outcome per SOURCE-GATE §2.

## Asbab an-nuzul
- Not used. No established sabab confirmed for 21:41; the tafsir treat it as consolation
  tied to the preceding mockery of 21:36 (placement, not a naming occasion). Article uses
  the placement (link to 21:36) instead.

## Licensing (watch-point 3)
- Standing sentence included in both languages, §7 ("Not a Charge to Level"): the verse
  describes a class the Qur'an itself condemns, licenses nothing against any living person
  or community, and no verdict is passed today. as-Sa'di's "let these beware" turned inward
  on the reader, not outward.

## Dropped / not asserted
- Ma'arif (168): its Badr material belongs to 21:37; dropped to avoid preempting neighbours.
- Ibn Kathir 169's coverage of 21:42–43 (protection by night/day; their gods cannot help
  themselves): deliberately not used (watch-point 4).
- No named identity of the "messengers before you" invented; commentators leave the roster
  open and so does the article.
