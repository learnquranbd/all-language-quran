Card plus deep bilingual article for 21:41, the consolation to the
Prophet that messengers before him were ridiculed and the mockers were
enveloped by the very punishment they scoffed at.

Sources (all pre-fetched, none re-fetched): Ibn Kathir (ar + en
abridged), at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi, as-Sa'di, al-Baghawi, al-Muyassar.
- Consolation frame (tasliyah): Ibn Kathir, al-Qurtubi, as-Sa'di; as-Sa'di
  links it to the sneer of 21:36.
- ḥāqa kept in both shades, not merged: al-Qurtubi (aḥāṭa wa-dāra,
  encompassed/encircled) and al-Baghawi/at-Tabari/as-Sa'di (nazala,
  befell/descended).
- "what they used to ridicule" = the punishment they deemed far-fetched:
  Ibn Kathir, al-Muyassar, at-Tabari (the doom their messengers warned of).
- jazāʾ (recompense matched to the mockery): al-Baghawi, al-Qurtubi.
- as-Sa'di: the means cut off (taqaṭṭaʿat ʿanhum al-asbāb) and "let these
  beware." al-Qurtubi: be patient as they were patient, then victory.
- Cross-ref 6:34 (Ibn Kathir).

No hadith: PREP's default Bukhari 660 is unrelated and was rejected; no
tafsir attaches a sound verse-specific narration, stated plainly and none
invented.

Asbab: none established; placement (link to 21:36) used instead.

Licensing: standing sentence in both languages — describes a class the
Qur'an itself condemns, licenses nothing against any living person or
community, no verdict today; as-Sa'di's warning turned inward.

Did not preempt 21:42–43 (Ibn Kathir 169's coverage of them left unused).
Ma'arif (168) Badr material belongs to 21:37; dropped.
