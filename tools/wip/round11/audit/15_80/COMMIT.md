Card and bilingual article (8 sections, 1,711 EN words) for "the companions
of al-Hijr denied the messengers", built on the plural al-mursalin where
Thamud were sent Salih (AS) alone.

Sources: at-Tabari (ashab = dwellers, 7:44 parallel; Qatadah "companions
of the valley"); al-Qurtubi (meanings of hijr via al-Azhari; location via
Qatadah and at-Tabari); al-Baghawi and as-Sa'di on location. Plural: Ibn
Kathir (ar, and abridged en grouped 15:80-84), the Muyassar, as-Sa'di
("not his person but the truth he brought") and al-Qurtubi ("one religion
in the usul, no tafriq"). Ash-Shu'ara's five "denied the messengers"
openings (26:105-176) checked in quran-json; 26:177 has no "brother".

Disagreements kept: al-Qurtubi's wa-qila reading (Salih, his followers
and earlier prophets) beside denial-of-one-is-all, with al-Baghawi's bare
"Salih alone"; prayer at al-Hijr, some scholars and Ibn al-Arabi (barred)
against al-Qurtubi and Abu Umar (valid where pure). No position taken.

Hadith: Tabuk narrations (Bukhari 3379/3380) already ship at 11:67 and
7:74; pointer only. Bukhari 335 referenced as general, not attached.
Dropped: at-Tabari's ungraded Abu Righal report; al-Qurtubi's other
fiqh issues; 15:81-84 material; any site/archaeology claim. Ma'arif
(grouped 15:78-86) unused.

Licensing sentence in both languages; reflection on rejecting truth for
its bearer and on selective acceptance, marked as reflection.
