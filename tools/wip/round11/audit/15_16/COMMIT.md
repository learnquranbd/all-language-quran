Card and eight-section bilingual article for 15:16 (1,609 EN words, 19
paragraphs): We placed buruj in the heaven and adorned it for the beholders.

Sources: Tabari, Ibn Kathir ar, Qurtubi, Sa'di, Baghawi, Muyassar named
on the verse; Ma'arif (168, grouped 15:16-17) and Ibn Kathir abridged
(169, grouped 15:16-20) named with their groups stated, 15:16 content only.

"Buruj": readings kept side by side with names and no position taken:
stars (Mujahid, Qatada, al-Hasan), great stars / the seven moving ones
(Abu Salih), stations of sun and moon (Ibn 'Abbas; Tabari's own gloss
joins stations and stars; Baghawi's twelve), palaces with guards ('Atiyya
al-'Awfi; Qurtubi's "a group", closed with Allah knows best), Sa'di's
stars like towers and landmarks. Qurtubi and Baghawi's root zuhur /
tabarruj. "Li-n-nazirin": Tabari wide (whoever looks) vs Qurtubi (those
who reflect and take lesson), Muyassar, Ibn Kathir and Sa'di joining
the two; kept as a difference of emphasis.

Stars' use reported from Muyassar, Qurtubi and Sa'di (roads, times,
plenty and drought, darkness of land and sea). Stated once that no
fetched text takes up astrology, so the article decides nothing on it.
No modern-astronomy claims; Ma'arif's "space" discussion dropped, its
deferral to 25:61 reported. 15:17-18 given a single pointer.

Hadith: none attached to 15:16; Ibn Kathir's Bukhari report on the
eavesdroppers noted as belonging to 15:18. Asbab: none.

Reflection turns on looking up on purpose and beauty as a sign; the
15:15/15:16 eyes contrast flagged as the text's own, not a commentator's.
