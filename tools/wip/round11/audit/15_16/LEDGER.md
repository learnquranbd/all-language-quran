## Disagreements kept (no position taken)
- buruj: stars (Mujahid, Qatada, al-Hasan; Tabari, Ibn Kathir, Qurtubi, Ma'arif) / great stars = the seven moving ones (Abu Salih via Qurtubi) / stations of sun and moon (Ibn 'Abbas via Qurtubi; "some" via Ibn Kathir; Tabari's own gloss joins stations and stars; Baghawi: great stars meaning the twelve stations) / palaces with guards ('Atiyya via Ibn Kathir and Baghawi; "a group" via Qurtubi, who ends «فالله أعلم») / Sa'di: stars like towers and landmarks.
- nazirin: wide (Tabari «لمن نظر إليها وأبصرها»; Baghawi repeats the word) vs those who reflect and take lesson (Qurtubi); Muyassar, Ibn Kathir, Sa'di join looking to contemplation. Presented as difference of emphasis.
