# SOURCES 80:37 — li-kulli imri'in minhum yawma'idhin sha'nun yughnihi (6 Arabic words, per PREP data)

## Tafsir fetched (all from PREP; none re-fetched) and named
- 15 at-Tabari (80:37): NAMED. li-kulli imri' = "من الرجل وأخيه وأمه وأبيه، وسائر من ذُكر"; yawma'idhin = "يوم القيامة إذا جاءت الصاخَّة"; "شأن يغنيه: أمر يغنيه، ويُشغِله عن شأن غيره"; Qatada: "أفضى إلى كلّ إنسان ما يشغله عن الناس"; Ibn Zayd: "شأن قد شغله عن صاحبه"; carries the Anas/A'idh b. Shurayh chain.
- 16 al-Muyassar (grouped 80:34-37): NAMED. "يوم يفرُّ المرء لهول ذلك اليوم ... لكل واحد منهم يومئذٍ أمر يشغله ويمنعه من الانشغال بغيره".
- 90 al-Qurtubi: NAMED. Opens with "في صحيح مسلم عن عائشة ... الأمر أشد من أن ينظر بعضهم إلى بعض"; Tirmidhi/Ibn Abbas "حسن صحيح"; "وقراءة العامة بالغين المعجمة; أي حال يشغله عن الأقرباء"; "وقرأ ابن محيصن وحميد (يعنيه) بفتح الياء، وعين غير معجمة; أي يعنيه أمره"; al-Qutabi "يعنيه: يصرفه ويصده عن قرابته ... اعن عني وجهك ... واعن عن السفيه"; Khufaf's line "سيعنيك حرب بني مالك عن الفحش والجهل في المحفل".
- 91 as-Sa'di: NAMED. "قد شغلته نفسه، واهتم لفكاكها، ولم يكن له التفات إلى غيرها". Following clause (two groups, sa'ada'/ashqiya') NOT used: 80:38-42 ground.
- 94 al-Baghawi: NAMED. "يشغله عن شأن غيره"; carries the Sawda report.
- 14 Ibn Kathir (ar): NAMED. "أي هو في شغل شاغل عن غيره"; IAH/Ibn Abbas "مشاة" + "فقالت زوجته" + "أو قال ما أشغله عن النظر"; "وقد رواه النسائي منفردا به"; Tirmidhi "حسن صحيح"; Nasa'i from Aisha "فكيف بالعورات" with verse, "انفرد به النسائي من هذا الوجه"; Anas/A'idh chain; Baghawi's Sawda report; "هذا حديث غريب من هذا الوجه جدا ... قال أبو حاتم الرازي عائذ بن شريح ضعيف". The "gharib jiddan" sits after both reports and its referent is ambiguous in the fetched text, so the article attributes it to "the hadith ... from this route" after naming both, not to Sawda alone.
- 169 Ibn Kathir en (grouped 80:33-42): NAMED for "enough to make him careless of others" and "preoccupied in his business and distracted from the affairs of others".
- 168 Ma'arif (grouped 80:34-42): NAMED. "Each person will be worried about himself ... heedless of anything around them"; Plain of Gathering; bonds "willing to lay down his life"; "even if one sees the other in front of him, he will turn away". Its lower-to-higher ordering of relations NOT used (80:34-36 ground).
- App EN translation "a matter adequate for him" (PREP) named as "the translation shown with this verse in the app".

## Gloss inventory (watch-point 1), none chosen
- sha'n: amr (Tabari, Muyassar); hal (Qurtubi); shughl shaghil (Ibn Kathir); sha'n that occupied him from his companion (Ibn Zayd via Tabari).
- yughnih: yughnihi wa yushghiluh 'an sha'n ghayrih (Tabari); yashghaluh 'an sha'n ghayrih (Baghawi); yashghaluh wa yamna'uh (Muyassar); ma yashghaluh 'an an-nas (Qatada via Tabari); shaghalathu nafsuh (Sa'di); "adequate" (app EN), "enough to make him careless" (IK en). Stated in article: no fetched Arabic commentator glosses it as "suffices"; that sense comes only from the English renderings.
- Reading ya'nihi (Ibn Muhaysin, Humayd) via Qurtubi, with al-Qutabi's gloss.

## Hadith
1. Jami' at-Tirmidhi 3332 — https://quranx.com/hadith/Tirmidhi/Hadith-3332 (saved hadith_Tirmidhi_3332.txt). First ten words: "Ibn Abbas narrated that: the Prophet said: You will be gathered". Grading by at-Tirmidhi: "هذا حديث حسن صحيح قد روي من غير وجه عن ابن عباس". Quoted whole. Cited by Ibn Kathir and al-Qurtubi on this verse; the Prophet answers with the verse.
2. Sahih Muslim 2859 (quranx ref 2859a) — https://quranx.com/hadith/Muslim/Hadith-2859 (saved hadith_Muslim_2859.txt). First ten words: "'A'isha reported that she heard Allah's Messenger (ﷺ) as saying: The". Grading: in Muslim's Sahih, no separate grading line. Quoted whole (short; not clipped). 2859b (Hatim chain note, no "ghurlan") not quoted. Article notes Muslim's wording does not recite the verse; al-Qurtubi places it here.
- Not quoted (named only, with Ibn Kathir's own assessment): Anas/A'idh b. Shurayh (Tabari, IAH); Sawda (Baghawi). IAH/Ibn Abbas variant and Nasa'i/Aisha variant described from Ibn Kathir's text, not quoted as hadith texts (no hadith.js confirmation of an-Nasa'i number).
- Bukhari version of the 'A'isha narration: not cited by any fetched tafsir, not used.
