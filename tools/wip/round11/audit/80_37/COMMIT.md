Card and 8-section bilingual article for 80:37, li-kulli imri'in minhum
yawma'idhin sha'nun yughnihi (6 Arabic words). 80:33-36 used only as the
commentators' context; 80:38-42 left for their own slots.

Sources: at-Tabari, Ibn Kathir (ar), al-Qurtubi, al-Baghawi, as-Sa'di on
80:37; Muyassar (grouped 80:34-37); Ma'arif (grouped 80:34-42); Ibn
Kathir en (grouped 80:33-42).
- Sha'n glosses kept side by side: amr (Tabari, Muyassar), hal
  (Qurtubi), shughl shaghil (Ibn Kathir), Ibn Zayd via Tabari.
- Yughnih: occupies him away from others (Tabari, Baghawi, Muyassar,
  Qatada via Tabari, Sa'di); "adequate"/"enough" only from the English
  renderings, said plainly, none chosen.
- Reading ya'nihi (Ibn Muhaysin, Humayd) with al-Qutabi's gloss and
  Khufaf's line, via Qurtubi.
Hadith: Tirmidhi 3332 (Ibn Abbas, Prophet answers with the verse; hasan
sahih per at-Tirmidhi) and Muslim 2859 ('A'isha, "too serious to look"),
both quoted whole; Muslim's wording noted as not reciting the verse.
Anas and Sawda routes named only, with Ibn Kathir's gharib remark and
Abu Hatim's weakening of A'idh; ambiguous gharib referent not pinned.
Asbab: not used.
Dropped: 70:10-14, 23:101, 31:33, 82:19 (cited by no fetched text),
intercession hadith (unnumbered, 80:34-36 ground), ordering of relations,
Sa'di's two groups and 80:38-42.
