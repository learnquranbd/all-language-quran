New card and a 1,743-word, 9-section bilingual article for 2:55-56
(the demand to see Allah openly, the thunderbolt, the revival). 2:57 left
out: its manna is already worked in shipped 2:58-59 and 2:61.

Sources: at-Tabari, Ibn Kathir (ar + en abridged), al-Qurtubi, as-Sa'di,
al-Baghawi, al-Muyassar, Ma'arif, all on 2:55 and 2:56. Slots checked.
- Words: at-Tabari's well-clearing image for jahratan, al-Baghawi on
  heart-seeing, at-Tabari's sa'iqa root with his 7:143 swoon point, ba'th.
- Who spoke: the seventy of 7:155 (Ibn Ishaq, as-Suddi, ar-Rabi') vs the
  people at large (Ibn Zayd); at-Tabari's verdict that no report
  establishes any cause.
- What struck and "while you looked on": five glosses, four readings.
- Real death or swoon: as-Sa'di open, at-Tabari death, al-Qurtubi's
  rejection of the figurative reading; as-Suddi's "prophets" rejected.
- Seeing Allah: the condition on belief condemned (as-Sa'di, al-Qurtubi,
  Ibn Furak); this-world limit (Ibn Kathir, Ma'arif); 7:143 only as
  at-Tabari and Ibn Kathir use it; 75:22 and 75:23 as text. No sect named.
- Gratitude: at-Tabari's "kept alive to return to repentance", terms
  completed (ar-Rabi', Qatada, al-Muyassar), al-Mawardi via al-Qurtubi.
Licensing line in both languages. Cross-refs 4:153, 2:118, 25:21.
Hadith: none attached to the verses (said in one line). Bukhari 6312
(Hudhayfa, waking du'a) quoted whole, marked as not tied to the verse.
Asbab: none. Dropped: al-Qurtubi's sect-labelled line, at-Tabari's
Madinah polemic, ar-Razi's report, the jahratan syntax and variant
readings (cut for length).
