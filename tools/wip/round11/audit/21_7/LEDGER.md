# SOURCES ledger — 21:7 (al-Anbiyāʾ)

## Tafsir fetched (all from PREP, none re-fetched)
- **Ibn Kathir (14 ar / 169 en)** — NAMED. Clause: "جميع الرسل الذين تقدموا كانوا رجالا من البشر، لم يكن فيهم أحد من الملائكة" (all prior messengers were human men, no angel among them). En abridged adds cross-refs 12:109, 46:9, 64:6, and on 21:8 "بل كانت لهم أجساد يأكلون الطعام" with 25:20, 21:34 (none granted immortality; they lived then died). Used in §2, §4.
- **at-Tabari (15)** — NAMED. Clause: "وما أرسلنا ... إلا رجالا مثلهم نوحي إليهم ... لا ملائكة"; and on the command, "فاسألوا أهل الكتب من التوراة والإنجيل" via Qatada ("يخبروكم أن الرسل كانوا رجالا يأكلون الطعام، ويمشون في الأسواق"). Also records Ibn Zayd (dhikr = Qur'an) and the Jabir al-Ju'fi report of Ali "نحن أهل الذّكر". Used §2, §5, §6, §7.
- **al-Qurtubi (90)** — NAMED. Clauses: "هذا رد عليهم في قولهم هل هذا إلا بشر مثلكم"; "وسماهم أهل الذكر؛ لأنهم كانوا يذكرون خبر الأنبياء مما لم تعرفه العرب. وكان كفار قريش يراجعون أهل الكتاب"; Sufyan's attribution; and the general taqlid ruling "العامة عليها تقليد علمائها". Used §5, §6.
- **al-Baghawi (94)** — NAMED. Clauses: "إنا لم نرسل الملائكة إلى الأولين إنما أرسلنا رجالا"; "فإنهم لا ينكرون أن الرسل كانوا بشرا وإن أنكروا نبوة محمد ... أقرب"; Ibn Zayd (dhikr=Qur'an). Used §2, §5, §6.
- **as-Sa'di (91)** — NAMED. Clauses: doubt = "هلا كان ملكا ... وهلا كان خالدا"; the ilzām "المقرين بإثبات الرسل قبله - ولو لم يكن إلا إبراهيم ... والمشركون يزعمون أنهم على دينه"; retreat answered by "لو أنزلنا ملكا لقضي الأمر" and "لو كان في الأرض ملائكة ... لنزلنا عليهم من السماء ملكا رسولا"; general reading "عامة في كل مسألة من مسائل الدين". Used §3, §6. (See dropped, below, for the النساء clause.)
- **al-Muyassar (16)** — NAMED. Clause: "إلا رجالا من البشر ... ولم نرسل ملائكة، فاسألوا ... أهل العلم بالكتب المنزلة السابقة". Used §5.
- **Ma'arif al-Qur'an (168 en)** — NAMED. "scholars of Injil and Torah ... they know perfectly well that all prophets were human beings"; "may refer to all Jews and Christians even though they have not believed." Used §5. (Its Qurtubi taqlid ruling noted only in passing in §6.)

## Hadith
- NONE used. No marfū' Prophetic hadith is attached to 21:7 in any tafsir fetched. Stated plainly in article §7. Bukhari 660 (PREP's generic pointer) not fetched — not on this verse; a weak/unrelated fill would violate the gate.
- The only narrations present are tafsir āthār on the *meaning* of ahl adh-dhikr (Qatada, Ibn Zayd) and the report of Ali "we are the people of the Reminder" — the latter via Jabir al-Ju'fi (a chain later critics questioned); reported as a saying, not leaned on. Grading not upgraded.

## Asbab an-nuzul
- NOT USED. No established sabab confirmed on a fetched page. Article anchors the verse by its placement instead: a direct reply to the deniers' sneer in 21:3 (and their sign-demand in 21:5), continued in 21:8-9.

## Disagreement kept (no position taken)
- ahl adh-dhikr = people of Torah/Injil (Tabari/Baghawi/Qurtubi-via-Sufyan/Muyassar/Ma'arif/Sa'di) **vs** ahl al-Qur'an / knowing believers (Ibn Zayd; Ali's reported saying). Left side by side in §6.

## Dropped / consciously omitted
- as-Sa'di's inference from "إلا رجالا" that no woman was a prophet ("دليل على أن النساء ليس منهن نبية، لا مريم ولا غيرها") — confirmable in his text but OMITTED for focus: it is tangential to the mortality-of-prophets spine and would open an unrelated controversy. Recorded here so the omission is deliberate, not an oversight.
- Exact verse numbers for Sa'di's quoted proof-texts (the "لو أنزلنا ملكا" / "لو كان في الأرض ملائكة" passages) left undnamed in prose and described as "the further Qur'anic answer he cites," to avoid asserting a reference not written out in the fetched excerpt.

## Differentiation from shipped 16:43 (near-verbatim verse)
- 16:43 headings run on ask-the-scholars / taqlid / "when the Reminder means Qur'an" / general-knowledge lesson. This 21:7 article takes a **different spine**: the mortality and humanity of prophets as the surah's own answer to 21:3 ("is this but a man like you?"). New opening (the whisper in 21:3), al-Anbiyāʾ context throughout, Sa'di's ilzām and 21:8's eating/dying carried as the argument. The general taqlid reading is acknowledged in one paragraph (§6) then subordinated to the surah's narrow use — so the two articles do not overlap. All 8 headings are distinct from 16:43's in both languages.
