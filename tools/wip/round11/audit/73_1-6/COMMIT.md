Replaces the 854-word, 6-section article with a 1,773-word, 8-section deep article, built only on tafsir fetched for 73:1-6 (shipped card kept).

Sources: at-Tabari, Ibn Kathir (ar; en abridged for 73:1-9), al-Qurtubi,
as-Sa'di, al-Baghawi, al-Muyassar (73:1-4), Ma'arif al-Qur'an.

Al-muzzammil: wrapped in garments, ready for prayer (Qatada) vs wrapped in
prophethood (Ikrima), at-Tabari preferring Qatada; O sleeper (Ibn Abbas,
ad-Dahhak, as-Suddi); al-Qurtubi's three positions and Ibn al-Arabi's
objection. Asbab kept side by side: first revelation (as-Sa'di, Ma'arif,
al-Qurtubi) and the Quraysh taunt (al-Bazzar via Ibn Kathir, with al-Bazzar's
own narrator caveat). As-Suhayli on address by state; as-Sa'di's lesson.
Measure: az-Zajjaj's apposition, at-Tabari's "choice among stations".
Obligation: three views in al-Qurtubi; Ibn Kathir's "on him alone" noted.
Length kept as a difference: a year (A'isha, Ibn Abbas), a year or two
(Qatada), ten years (Sa'id b. Jubayr), eight months (ath-Tha'labi).
Tartil glosses by name; Ibn Mas'ud via al-Baghawi and Ibn Kathir.
Qawlan thaqila: readings by holder (act, commands, promise, hypocrites, descent, scale), al-Qurtubi's night-prayer reading,
Ibn al-Arabi's preference, at-Tabari's "both"; none chosen.
Nashi'ah: whole night / rising / after 'Isha' / after sleep / end / start.
Wat'an and wita'an both sound (at-Tabari); concord vs heavier on the body.

Hadith: Muslim 746 (portion, A'isha on the year of obligation), Bukhari 5046
(Anas), Tirmidhi 2914 (hasan sahih, his grading), Bukhari 2 (the bell).
32:16, 17:79, 73:7, 73:8, 73:20: one-line pointers.

Fixed from the old article: the bell words are the Prophet's, reported by
A'isha. Dropped: the verb-plus-masdar emphasis claim and 51:17-18 (no
