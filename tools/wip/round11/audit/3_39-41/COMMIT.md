Assigned 3:41, widened to 3:39-41 (glad tidings of Yahya in the mihrab;
Zakariyya's "how shall I have a son"; the sign of three days without speech).
3:38 and 3:45 ship; 3:42-44 (the angels to Maryam) left free. New card and a
1,791-word, 9-section article on remembrance when speech is taken, and hope
that age and circumstance do not limit.

Sources: at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi, Ibn Kathir (ar + en abridged), as-Sa'di,
al-Baghawi, al-Muyassar, Ma'arif on 3:39, 3:40 and 3:41 (all slots on target).
- Kalimah = Isa (AS): at-Tabari with his authorities, as-Sa'di, al-Muyassar,
  "most commentators" per al-Qurtubi; Abu 'Ubayda's "a book" (al-Baghawi),
  rejected by at-Tabari. Sayyid glosses; az-Zajjaj's "comprehensive" one.
- Hasur: root of holding back; abstaining from women (at-Tabari's list);
  restraint with capacity, "soundest" (al-Qurtubi; al-Baghawi's reasons);
  incapacity (named by al-Qurtubi) refused by al-Qadi 'Iyad via Ibn Kathir;
  protected from sins (Ibn Kathir, al-Muyassar).
- 3:40 as joy and wonder (al-Muyassar, as-Sa'di, Ibn Kathir); at-Tabari's
  "not doubt, not denial"; the manner only (al-Baghawi, al-Qurtubi).
Disagreements kept: silence as penalty (Qatada, ar-Rabi', at-Tabari, "most"
per al-Qurtubi) vs sign only (an-Nahhas, Ibn Kathir, as-Sa'di, al-Muyassar),
no position; Jibril alone (as-Suddi, al-Baghawi) vs a company of angels
(at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi); the whisper reading of the request (at-Tabari,
al-Baghawi) beside al-Qurtubi's. Nothing in the article's own voice says
Zakariyya (AS) doubted or erred.
Hadith: Bukhari 3746 (Abu Bakra, "this son of mine is a sayyid"), cited by
al-Qurtubi for the word, marked general. Statement that no sound hadith is
attached to the verses. No asbab.
Dropped: ages at the glad tidings, al-Kalbi's "Rabb = Jibril", the
physical-description reports (only Ibn Kathir's verdict kept), 'Ata''s fast
reading, the abrogation and mute-divorce fiqh, unfetched hadith.
Fix: §6.2 rewritten; the as-Suddi/'Ikrima report of a Shaytanic whisper behind the request is noted as left aside, not retold.
