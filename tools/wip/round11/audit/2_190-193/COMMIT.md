Assigned 2:191, widened to 2:190-193 (fight those who fight you, do not
transgress; the Sacred Mosque; fitnah worse than killing; if they cease);
2:189 and 2:194-195 left free. New card and a 1,755-word, 8-section article
that reads 2:191 inside its sequence, its pronoun tied to 2:190's "those who
fight you" (at-Tabari, Muyassar).

Sources: at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi, Ibn Kathir (ar + en abridged), as-Sa'di,
al-Baghawi, Muyassar, Ma'arif on 2:190-193 (slots on target; as-Sa'di's
2:192 slot carries his 2:193 comment).
- Setting (Hudaybiyah, 'umrat al-qada') as the commentators give it: al-Qurtubi,
  al-Baghawi (via al-Kalbi from Abu Salih from Ibn Abbas), Ma'arif; no sound
  hadith attached, said so.
- Licensing sentence in both languages: nothing against any living person or
  community; rules of war belong to legitimate authority and scholars.
Disagreements kept: 2:190 abrogated by 9:36/9:5 (al-Rabi', Ibn Zayd) or muhkam
(Ibn Abbas, Umar ibn Abd al-Aziz, Mujahid; preferred by at-Tabari, an-Nahhas,
Ibn Kathir doubts abrogation); Sacred Mosque clause abrogated (Qatada,
al-Rabi', at-Tabari) or muhkam (Mujahid, Tawus, Abu Hanifa, al-Qurtubi);
fitnah as shirk vs persecution to force a believer out of his faith (at-Tabari,
Mujahid, al-Qurtubi); 2:193 general (al-Qurtubi leans) or tied to "if they
fight you" (Mujahid); Hamza/al-Kisa'i "la taqtuluhum".
Hadith: Bukhari 3015 (women and children), Bukhari 4513 (Ibn 'Umar in the
strife of Ibn az-Zubayr, first report whole; the 'Uthman ibn Salih addition
for fitnah as persecution). Hudhayfah parable via Ibn Kathir from Ahmad, his
"hasan al-isnad", not fetched independently.
Dropped: Buraydah (Muslim), Anas, Abu Bakr's orders to Yazid, the sanctity
and "umirtu" hadiths (not fetched); al-Qurtubi's fiqh categories and jizyah
clauses; first-revealed-verse dispute; Malik via Ashhab; the Nakhlah link.
