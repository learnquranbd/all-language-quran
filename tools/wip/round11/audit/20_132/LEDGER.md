## Disagreement kept (no position)
la nas'aluka rizqan: wealth not asked, bodily work asked (at-Tabari, al-Muyassar) vs not charged with providing for yourself/them/creation (al-Qurtubi, al-Baghawi, ath-Thawri in Ibn Kathir). Ahl: household specifically with ummah by generality (al-Qurtubi) / your people, or those on your religion (al-Baghawi) / wife, children, associates (Ma'arif) — glosses side by side.
