Replaces the 771-word, 6-section article with a 1,775-word, 8-section depth
article built only from the tafsir texts fetched for 20:132.

Sources: placement via 'Urwah reciting 20:131 into 20:132 then calling to
prayer (at-Tabari, Ibn Kathir, al-Qurtubi). Ahl: al-Qurtubi (household in
particular, ummah by generality), al-Baghawi (your people; those on your
religion; 19:55), Ma'arif (wife, children, associates). As-Sa'di: the
command includes teaching what makes prayer sound; istabir with its limits,
pillars and khushu', hard on the soul. Ibn Kathir: rescue them from
punishment, with 66:6. Istabir glossed from at-Tabari, al-Baghawi (29:45
wording), al-Qurtubi; no fetched tafsir comments on the verb form.

Disagreement kept on la nas'aluka rizqan: wealth not asked, bodily work
asked (at-Tabari, al-Muyassar) vs not charged with providing for yourself
and others (al-Qurtubi, al-Baghawi, ath-Thawri in Ibn Kathir). Ibn Kathir
with 65:2-3 and 51:56-58; as-Sa'di's guarantee; Ma'arif's farming image.
'Aqiba from Ibn Kathir, al-Muyassar, at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi, Ibn 'Abbas in
al-Baghawi, as-Sa'di (wording of 7:128 and 28:83).

Reports kept as the commentators' own, unconfirmed in a collection: the
Prophet calling Fatimah and 'Ali each morning (al-Qurtubi, no chain), his
family told to pray in hardship (al-Qurtubi; Thabit via Ibn Abi Hatim in
Ibn Kathir; al-Baghawi's "some musnads"). 'Umar waking his household with
the verse (Zayd ibn Aslam, in at-Tabari and Ibn Kathir).

Hadith: Tirmidhi 2466 quoted whole, graded hasan gharib by at-Tirmidhi;
Ibn Majah 4107 noted for its variant wording only, no grading stated.

Fixed from the old article: it gave Ibn Kathir ath-Thawri's gloss ("We do
not burden you with seeking"). Dropped (unsourced): the istabir-vs-isbir
morphology claim, Abu Dawud's prayer-at-seven report, 20:14 and own-voice
household advice.
