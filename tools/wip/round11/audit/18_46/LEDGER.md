## Hadith
- Muslim 2695, https://quranx.com/hadith/Muslim/Hadith-2695 (saved hadith_Muslim_2695.txt).
  First ten words used: "The uttering of (these words): 'Exalted is Allah (He is". Quoted whole.
  Grading: in Sahih Muslim (collector's own). Attached by al-Baghawi (own chain = Muslim's) and Ma'arif.
  Article states the hadith does not name the verse; the link is the commentators'.
- Abu Sa'id al-Khudri "istakthiru min al-baqiyat as-salihat": NOT confirmed (Ahmad / Nasa'i al-Kubra
  not reachable with hadith.js). Paraphrased only, as the tafsirs' citation; their reported gradings
  ('Abd al-Haqq per al-Qurtubi; al-Hakim per Ma'arif) given as theirs; no grading of our own.
- Not used: "take your shields" (no fetched tafsir attaches it); Ahmad's 'Uthman wudu report,
  Ahmad "bakh bakh li-khams", Ahmad Nu'man ibn Bashir, Tabarani Sa'd ibn Junada, Tirmidhi
  Ibn Mas'ud "ghiras al-janna" (hasan gharib per al-Qurtubi), Ibn Majah reports, Abu Ayyub
  "ghiras al-janna", the daughters narration ("ruwiya", unsourced in al-Qurtubi).
