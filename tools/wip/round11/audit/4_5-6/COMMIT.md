Key widened from 4:6 to 4:5-6: wealth not handed to those who cannot manage it,
provision and a fitting word, testing orphans, handover at maturity, the rich
guardian abstains and the poor one eats fittingly, witnesses, Allah as Reckoner.
New card and a 1,734-word, 9-section article. 4:3 and 4:4 left free; 4:2 and 4:4
cited for placement, 4:7 as text, 4:10 as the sister verse.

Sources: at-Tabari, Ibn Kathir (ar, en abr.), al-Qurtubi, as-Sa'di, al-Baghawi,
al-Muyassar and Ma'arif on 4:5 and 4:6; all slots on target.
Kept as differences, no position: as-sufaha' as women and children, orphans or
women (early reports in at-Tabari) vs anyone who deserves restriction (at-Tabari's
own view with his grammar argument, an-Nahhas, Abu Musa in al-Qurtubi); the age of
puberty (15 vs 17, Dawud); rushd as reason and wealth (at-Tabari) vs religion and
wealth (al-Baghawi), Abu Hanifa's 25; the poor guardian's eating as a loan (Umar,
at-Tabari) vs his due with nothing to repay (al-Qurtubi, Shafi'i followers in Ibn
Kathir); witnessing recommended, obligatory or guidance (al-Qurtubi, al-Baghawi).
Register: readings naming women attributed, at-Tabari's counter-view given, and a
line that the verse licenses no contempt for women as a class.
Hadith: Bukhari 4575 (Aisha's statement on the verse, marked as not the Prophet's
words); Muslim 1826 (Abu Dharr, "do not take charge of an orphan's property"),
attached by Ibn Kathir. Asbab: Thabit ibn Rifa'ah story (al-Baghawi, al-Qurtubi
"qila") not used; placement instead.
Application from Ma'arif (endowments, mosques, schools, public funds): take nothing
with means, only need without; plus inheritance, savings, family business, committee.
Dropped: Abu Umamah report in Ibn Kathir, "asfah as-sufaha'" phrasing, Ma'arif's
4:5 excursus, Amr ibn Shu'ayb and Jabir reports, minor eating views.
