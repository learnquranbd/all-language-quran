Replaces the 838-word, 6-section article with a 1,710-word, 8-section
one, from at-Tabari, Ibn Kathir, al-Qurtubi, as-Sa'di, al-Baghawi,
al-Muyassar and Ma'arif on 2:257. All slots checked. The shipped card is kept.

Placement: 2:255 and 2:256 one line each (no ruling on 2:256); as-Sa'di's
"then He named the cause"; one bridging line to 2:258.
Wali: at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi with al-Khattabi and 47:11, al-Baghawi's
readings and al-Hasan, al-Muyassar; as-Sa'di's two-way wilayah.
Darknesses and light: Qatada, ad-Dahhak, ar-Rabi' (at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi),
at-Tabari's screen image, al-Waqidi's rule with 6:1 (al-Baghawi), Ibn
Kathir, as-Sa'di. Plural/singular from Ibn Kathir only (6:153, 6:1, 16:48).
Taghut: rivals and idols (at-Tabari, al-Muyassar), Shaytan (Qatada),
devils (Ibn Kathir; al-Hasan's tawaghit), Shaytan's party (as-Sa'di),
Muqatil's named men (al-Baghawi); grammar from at-Tabari and al-Baghawi
(4:60, 39:17).
"Out of the light" kept as a disagreement: the 'Isa/Muhammad report from
Mujahid or Miqsam via 'Abdah (at-Tabari's preference, al-Qurtubi) and
al-Baghawi's Jews, against the barring reading (at-Tabari's alternative,
Ibn 'Atiyyah, al-Baghawi, 12:37). No position; licenses nothing against
any living person or community, in both languages.
Hadith: none attached by any fetched tafsir, said once. Ayyub ibn
Khalid's saying (Ibn Kathir via Ibn Abi Hatim) marked as his own, ungraded.
Dropped: soothsayers and 'Umar's gloss (2:256 tafsir, not fetched here);
Ma'arif's line on friendship with disbelievers; 5:16, 14:1, 65:11, 33:43.
Corrected: "the light was available to them through fitrah and 2:256"
(no source; the fetched answers are the report and the barring reading);
"taghut: tyrants, soothsayers, root to overflow bounds" (unsourced).
