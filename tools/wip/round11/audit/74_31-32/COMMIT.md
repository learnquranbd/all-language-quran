Card and 8-section bilingual article for 74:31-32: the keepers of the Fire
are angels, their number nineteen (74:30) set as a test, the People of the
Scripture made certain, the believers increased, the diseased and the
disbelievers asking what Allah meant; none knows His hosts; "hiya" a
reminder; 74:32 kalla and the oath by the moon (74:33-35 as context only).

Sources: Tabari, Qurtubi, Sa'di, Baghawi, Ibn Kathir (ar 74:31; en grouped
74:31-37), Muyassar, Ma'arif (en grouped 74:31-36). All named. Ibn Kathir
(ar) 74:32 fetch failed; not used.
- Quraysh reaction (Abu Jahl; Abu al-Ashaddayn "it has been said" in Ibn
  Kathir; Abu al-Ashadd al-Jumahi via Mujahid in Tabari; Suddi via Baihaqi
  in Ma'arif) given as attributed, ungraded reports, not as asbab.
- Those given the Scripture: Tabari with Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatada,
  Dahhak; Ibn Zayd "that you are the Messenger"; Qurtubi's Ibn Salam vs all.
- Disease kept as a disagreement: Madinan hypocrites to come (majority per
  Qurtubi; kafirun = Jews and Christians) vs Meccan dissent/doubt (al-Husayn
  ibn al-Fadl; kafirun = Arab idolaters), with Tabari, Baghawi, Sa'di, Ibn
  Kathir, Muyassar each placed.
- fitna: Qatada's bala'; Sa'di's two readings (punishment, 51:13; test).
- hiya: Fire/Saqar (Tabari, Qatada, Mujahid, Ibn Kathir, Baghawi, Muyassar),
  the admonition (Sa'di), Qurtubi's four incl. the number; unranked.
- kalla: rebuttal (Tabari, Muyassar) vs joined to oath / "truly" (Farra' and
  another in Qurtubi; Sa'di; Baghawi), with the pause question.
- Ibn Kathir's critique of mapping 19 onto ten intellects and nine souls
  reported; no numerology in the article's voice.
Hadith: Bukhari 3207 (al-Bayt al-Ma'mur, 70,000 angels), attached by Ibn
Kathir; Bukhari's wording quoted.
Licensing paragraph in EN and BN; no claim about any living community or
present-day scripture.
Dropped: Tirmidhi "atat al-sama'" (fetch failed), Ibn Kathir's gharib/munkar
angel reports, Tha'labi's reports in Qurtubi, the wrestling story.
