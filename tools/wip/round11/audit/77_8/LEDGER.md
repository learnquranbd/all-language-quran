Disagreement kept: light gone, stars remain (Tabari, Baghawi, Qurtubi, Ibn Kathir, Muyassar) vs scatter and leave their places (Sa'di); Ma'arif states both possibilities. No position taken.
Parallels 81:2, 82:2: used only because Ibn Kathir (14 and 169) cites them. Root n-th-r shared by انتثرت (82:2) and تتناثر (Sa'di) is a textual observation; article says explicitly neither scholar is shown answering the other.
Textual observations in the article's own voice (from PREP word counts and the Arabic): 77:8-11 each three words, idha + noun + passive verb; 77:8 alone opens with fa.
