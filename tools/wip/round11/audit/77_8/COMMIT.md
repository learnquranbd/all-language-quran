Card and 8-section bilingual article for 77:8, fa-idha an-nujumu
tumisat. Placed as the timing of 77:7 (the oaths' subject, per Ibn
Kathir and Ma'arif; Qurtubi: thumma bayyana waqta wuqu'ihi). 77:9-11
as context only (Ma'arif's count of four, Ibn Kathir, Muyassar); 77:1
and 77:13 not developed.

Sources: Ibn Kathir (ar 14, en 169 grouped 77:1-10), Tabari, Qurtubi,
Sa'di, Baghawi, Muyassar (group 77:8-15), Ma'arif (group 77:7-10).
- tumisat as light gone: Tabari (fa-lam yakun laha nurun wa la daw'),
  Baghawi (muhiya nuruha), Ibn Kathir (dhahaba daw'uha), Muyassar,
  Qurtubi (ka-tamsi al-kitab; tamasa = darasa; the wind effaces tracks).
- Sa'di: tatanatharu wa tazulu 'an amakiniha, no mention of light.
- Ma'arif: destroyed outright, or remaining with light lost; either way
  total darkness.
Disagreement kept: light effaced (five) vs scattered and removed
(Sa'di), Ma'arif open. Unranked.
Parallels 81:2 and 82:2 only because Ibn Kathir cites them; noted that
82:2's intatharat shares n-th-r with Sa'di's gloss, without inferring
dependence.
Hadith: none attached to the verse, said in one sentence. General
surah narration, Bukhari 763 (Umm al-Fadl, last surah heard, Maghrib),
quoted whole and marked not attached. Asbab: none.
Licensing sentence on 77:15's woe in EN and BN. No astronomy claims.
Dropped: Ibn Mas'ud cave-at-Mina narration (not fetched as hadith),
"time for heeding has passed" line (no source).
