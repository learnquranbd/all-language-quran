# SOURCES ledger — 36:34

## Tafsir fetched (all from PREP.md, none re-fetched)

- **at-Tabari (15, ar)** — NAMED. Arabic clause relied on: "وجعلنا في هذه الأرض
  التي أحييناها بعد موتها بساتين من نخيل وأعناب … وأنبعنا فيها من عيون الماء."
  Used for: jannat = basatin (§3); the springs = "we made springs of water well
  up" / anba'na min 'uyun al-ma' (§4).
- **al-Qurtubi (90, ar)** — NAMED. Clause: "جنات أي بساتين. من نخيل وأعناب وخصصهما
  بالذكر لأنهما أعلى الثمار. وفجرنا فيها من العيون أي في البساتين." Used for:
  palm+vine singled out as a'la ath-thamar, the highest of fruits (§2); jannat =
  basatin (§3); springs burst forth fi al-basatin, inside the orchards (§4).
- **as-Sa'di (91, ar)** — NAMED. Clause: "جَنَّاتٍ أي بساتين فيها أشجار كثيرة
  وخصوصا النخيل والأعناب اللذان هما أشرف الأشجار." Used for: gardens hold many
  trees, especially palm and vine = ashraf al-ashjar, noblest of trees (§2).
- **Ibn Kathir (14, ar)** — NAMED. Clause: "أي جعلنا فيها أنهارا سارحة في أمكنة
  يحتاجون إليها." Used for: springs = rivers coursing (anharan sarihatan) to the
  places where needed (§4).
- **al-Muyassar (16, ar)** — NAMED. Clause: "وفجَّرنا فيها من عيون المياه ما
  يسقيها." Used for: springs that give drink / irrigate (ma yasqiha) (§4).
- **al-Baghawi (94, ar)** — NAMED. Clause: "وجعلنا فيها جنات بساتين." Used for:
  the jannat = basatin consensus gloss (§3).
- **Ma'arif al-Qur'an (168, en)** — NAMED. covers 36:33-34. Clause relied on:
  the Ya Sin signs are "on one hand clear proofs of His perfect power, while on
  the other, they serve as mirrors of particular rewards and favors." Used in §1
  (framing) and §8 (sign works two ways).
- **Ibn Kathir abridged (169, en)** — NAMED. covers 36:33-36. Clauses relied on:
  "a sign for them … evidence … of the Creator and His perfect power and ability
  to resurrect the dead" (§8); "springs … means We created therein rivers which
  flow to the places where they are needed" (cross-check §4); on 36:35 "their
  hands made it not … all of that could only come about by the mercy of Allah …
  not by their own efforts and labor and strength. This was the view of Ibn
  `Abbas and Qatadah" (§5); and the kept disagreement — "Ibn Jarir, however,
  understood the word Ma to mean Alladhi … they eat from the fruits … and from
  what their own hands have done … this is the interpretation that he favored …
  fits … the recitation of Ibn Mas`ud" (§6).

## Disagreement kept (both names, no position taken)
§6: the ma of "wa-ma 'amilat-hu aydihim" (36:35). Negation reading (Ibn 'Abbas,
Qatada, carried by Ibn Kathir) = "their hands did not make it"; relative-pronoun
reading (Ibn Jarir at-Tabari, who favoured it; fits Ibn Mas'ud's recitation) =
"and from what their hands worked." Reported as a difference; article takes no
side and notes both leave the growth/life as God's work.

## Hadith (one, quoted whole)
- **Sahih Muslim 2734** (page REF: "Reference Hadith 2734a"). URL fetched:
  https://quranx.com/hadith/Muslim/Hadith-2734
  First ten words of wording used: "Allah is pleased with His servant who says:
  Al-Hamdu" (quranx rendering). In the article paraphrased faithfully as: Allah
  is pleased with the servant who eats a morsel and praises Him, or drinks a
  draught and praises Him. Grading: Sahih — it is in Sahih Muslim (collector's
  own standard); not upgraded. Narrator Anas ibn Malik. Used in §7.
- Bukhari 660 (seven whom Allah shades) was fetched and rejected as off-topic.
