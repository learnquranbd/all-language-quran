## Readings of "the great sign", reported with holders, none chosen
1. Staff and hand together: at-Tabari (own gloss + ahl at-ta'wil), al-Hasan, Mujahid, Qatada (x2), al-Baghawi, al-Muyassar; al-Hasan also in al-Qurtubi.
2. Staff alone: al-Qurtubi qila; ad-Dahhak from Ibn 'Abbas (in al-Qurtubi); Ibn Zayd "the staff and the serpent" (hand not mentioned).
3. White hand alone, gleaming like the sun: al-Qurtubi qila.
4. Splitting of the sea: al-Qurtubi qila.
5. All his signs and miracles: al-Qurtubi qila.
6. Genus of the great sign, not excluding plurality: as-Sa'di.
7. Unspecified "strong proof and clear evidence": Ibn Kathir.
