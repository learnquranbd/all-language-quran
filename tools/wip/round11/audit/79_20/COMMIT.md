Card and 8-section article on fa-arahu al-ayata al-kubra (3 words), the
great sign Musa (AS) showed Fir'awn after the invitation of 79:18-19.

Sources: at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi, as-Sa'di, al-Baghawi, Ibn Kathir (ar) and
the Muyassar (grouped 79:20-22, 79:20 clause only), all fetched for 79:20;
Ibn Kathir en (grouped 79:15-24) for the framing. Ma'arif wrong group.

What the sign was, kept side by side with no ranking: staff and hand
(at-Tabari's own gloss, al-Hasan, Mujahid, Qatada twice, al-Baghawi,
Muyassar); the staff alone (al-Qurtubi qila, ad-Dahhak from Ibn 'Abbas,
Ibn Zayd's "the staff and the serpent"); the white hand alone, the
splitting of the sea, all his signs (al-Qurtubi, each qila); as-Sa'di's
genus reading reconciling the singular with Qatada's "two signs"; Ibn
Kathir names no object. At-Tabari's own chain doubt on al-Hasan reported.

As-Sa'di's quoted wording verified letter for letter at 7:107-108 and
26:32-33. Staff and hand not re-described: cross-referenced to 20:22,
27:12, 28:32 and 26:34.

Hadith: none attached by any fetched tafsir; said in one sentence. No
asbab. Licensing sentence in both languages. 79:21-26 not pulled forward.
