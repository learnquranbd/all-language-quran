Card and 8-section bilingual article (1,739 EN words) on 56:69: a-antum
anzaltumuhu min al-muzni am nahnu al-munzilun. Read as the second step of
56:68's question; the afa-ra'aytum / a-antum ... am nahnu pattern of
56:58-72 noted from the text only.

Sources: all eight fetched tafsirs named (Ibn Kathir ar and en grouped
56:63-72, at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi, as-Sa'di, al-Baghawi, al-Muyassar grouped
56:68-69, Ma'arif grouped 56:63-72).
- al-muzn glosses kept unranked: clouds (Ibn Abbas, Mujahid via Ibn Kathir
  and al-Qurtubi; Qatada, Ibn Zayd via at-Tabari; al-Baghawi); sky and
  clouds (Ibn Abbas via at-Tabari; Ibn Abbas and ath-Thawri via
  al-Qurtubi); white cloud / a fall of rain (Abu Zayd in as-Sihah, via
  al-Qurtubi); clouds and rain (as-Sa'di).
- al-Qurtubi's two poetry lines, and his two questions: thanks by sincere
  worship, and denial of the power of i'ada (tied to 56:57 and 56:62).
- as-Sa'di: rivers on and under the earth, gushing pools, water made sweet.
- 56:70 framed only as the next verse via Ibn Kathir (en): ujaj glossed,
  16:10 and 16:11 cited (verified in quran-json); his 56:74 fresh/salty note.
Hadith: none attached in any fetched note; stated in one sentence.
Asbab: none. Licensing sentence not needed (no denying group addressed).
Dropped: rain meteorology, 25:48-49 / 23:18 / 78:14 / 15:22 (no source
cites them), rain-supplication narrations, rest of al-Qurtubi's second
poem line. Verb/participle contrast kept as own-voice, marked uncited.
Gate: clean on first run.
