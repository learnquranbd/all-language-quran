Card and deep bilingual article for the site of the House and its
first instruction: associate nothing, purify it, for the worshippers.

Sources give a live dispute, kept open in the article's own voice:
- Ibn Kathir (+ "many scholars"): Ibrahim was the FIRST to build the
  House, not built before him; evidence is Abu Dharr's hadith (first
  mosque = al-Masjid al-Haram) and 3:96 "the first House at Bakkah".
- at-Tabari (Qatadah), al-Qurtubi, al-Baghawi, Ma'arif: a foundation
  PRE-EXISTED from Adam's time, effaced/removed in Nuh's Flood, its base
  revealed to Ibrahim. Mechanism reports (told as unseen): the Khajuj
  wind (Suddi via Tabari; Baghawi) sweeping to the old foundation;
  al-Kalbi's cloud with a speaking head "build to my measure".

bawwa'na glossed: watta'na (Tabari/Baghawi), "made/made clear/prepared"
(Ibn Abbas, Zajjaj, Muqatil), "assign a residence" (Ma'arif); lam for
emphasis (Farra' via Qurtubi); "site was not known" (Muyassar).

"Purify My House": from shirk/idols first (Mujahid, Qatadah, Qurtubi's
Jurhum/Amalekite idols, 22:30), widened to sins and physical filth
(as-Sa'di, Qurtubi, Ma'arif's outward-cleanliness view). Both senses kept.

Four classes (ta'ifin/qa'imin/rukka'/sujud) glossed; tawaf the act most
specific to the House (Ibn Kathir), placed first (as-Sa'di); 2:125
parallel quoted (Ibn Kathir) with 'akifin where this verse has qa'imin.
Address dispute (Ibrahim vs the Prophet) kept per Qurtubi's jumhur.

Hadith: Sahih al-Bukhari 3366 (Abu Dharr), quoted whole, sound.
Dropped: 22:27 Hajj-proclamation narrations (un-shipped neighbour);
Kaaba/Adam dimensions asides.
