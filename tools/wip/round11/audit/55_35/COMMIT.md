Card and 8-section bilingual article for 55:35: yursalu 'alaykuma
shuwazun min narin wa-nuhasun fa-la tantasiran. 55:33 as context only;
55:13 pointer once for the dual address; 55:37 and 55:39 untouched.

Sources: at-Tabari, Ibn Kathir (ar; en abridged grouped, 55:35 only),
al-Qurtubi, al-Baghawi, as-Sa'di, al-Muyassar, Ma'arif (grouped).
- shuwaz: smokeless flame (Tabari's own, Ibn 'Abbas, Baghawi's "most
  commentators", Ma'arif, Sa'di); green broken-off flame (Mujahid);
  above fire below smoke (Abu Salih); smoke (ad-Dahhak, Sa'id, Ibn
  'Abbas via Sa'id); torrent of fire (ad-Dahhak); fire and smoke
  together ('Amr, al-Akhfash). Tabarani report via Juwaybir in Ibn
  Kathir given as his, ungraded.
- nuhas: smoke vs brass poured on heads, with names; Tabari prefers
  smoke and his reason is given; Ibn Mas'ud al-muhl, ad-Dahhak oil
  dregs, al-Kisa'i, Muyassar copper, Sa'di smoke-mixed flame.
- Readings: shiwaz (Ibn Kathir, Ibn Abi Ishaq); nuhasin (Ibn Kathir,
  Abu 'Amr; Ibn Muhaysin, Mujahid in Qurtubi) with Baghawi/Mahdawi on
  the genitive; nihas in Qurtubi vs Tabari's "reciters agree on damma".
- Setting: Qurtubi's four framings, Ibn Kathir (turned back while
  fleeing), Sa'di (the standing), Ma'arif (Hell or the challenge),
  Tabari (Day of Resurrection, no explicit link).
- fala tantasiran: Tabari, Baghawi, Sa'di, Ma'arif; mutual help in
  Qurtubi, Muyassar, Ma'arif.
Hadith: none attached; said in one sentence. Asbab: none.
Licensing sentence in both languages.
Dropped: Muqatil's five rivers, Qurtubi's rarer readings itemised,
poet-attribution disputes, Tabari editor footnotes.
