Replaces the 793-word, 5-section article with a 1,751-word, 9-section article
built only from the tafsir fetched for 18:45.

- Placement: follows the two-gardens ruin (18:32-44); at-Tabari reads
  "muqtadira" first as able to lay waste that man's garden. 18:46 one line.
- Audience of "lahum": at-Tabari and al-Qurtubi, the arrogant who asked for
  the poor believers to be sent away; Muyassar, people, especially the
  arrogant; al-Baghawi, your people; as-Sa'di, the Prophet and his heirs.
- Ikhtalata: with the water (at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi) vs the plants with each
  other (al-Qurtubi's "qila"); Ibn Kathir, al-Baghawi, as-Sa'di, Muyassar.
- Al-Qurtubi's sages: five reasons the world is likened to water.
- Hashim: dry (Ibn 'Abbas), broken (ad-Dahhak), the root and the Hashim ibn
  'Abd Manaf story (al-Qurtubi); the cut-off water as an ellipsis.
- Tadhruhu: Abu 'Ubayda, Ibn Qutayba, Ibn Kaysan; Ibn 'Abbas "tudiruhu" in
  al-Qurtubi vs "tuthiruhu" in al-Baghawi, kept as a difference. Qira'a
  "tudhrihi-r-rih" (Talha ibn Musarrif; 'Abd Allah via al-Kisa'i).
- Muqtadir: qadir (al-Baghawi), "this state and that" (Ibn Kathir), bringing
  into being, causing to perish, giving life (al-Qurtubi), at-Tabari's list.
- Sister verses from Ibn Kathir: 10:24 (one line), 39:21, 57:20.
- Hadith: Muslim 1054 (attached by al-Qurtubi) and Muslim 2742 (Ibn Kathir's
  "sweet and green", quoted whole from Muslim), both from Sahih Muslim.
- Closes on as-Sa'di's "suppose you had died" exercise.
- Dropped from the old article: fa/thumma contrast, "asbaha = next morning",
  muqtadir as intensive, the 35:9 resurrection link (none in the sources).
- Fixed: the old article put Muslim's fuller wording in Ibn Kathir's mouth;
  he quotes only "the world is sweet and green".
- Dropped: al-Qurtubi's unsourced "stagnant water" narration.
