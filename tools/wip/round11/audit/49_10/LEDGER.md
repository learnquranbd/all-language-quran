- 169 Ibn Kathir (en, grouped 49:9-10) — named. Bukhari and others on sin not nullifying faith; Hasan ibn 'Ali hadith; Sa'id ibn Jubayr (Aws/Khazraj, palm branches and slippers); as-Suddi ('Imran / Umm Zayd).
- 168 Ma'arif (en, grouped 49:9-10) — named. "several incidents ... totality ... might have been the occasion"; "eliminating the cause of war and mutual dissatisfaction, so that the heart-burnings may come to an end"; "so that the rights of no one are violated"; "not permitted to attribute categorically ... to any of the Companions that he was absolutely wrong ... each of them acted according to his own Ijtihad".

Disagreements kept: who "your two brothers" are (Ibn Kathir: the two factions; at-Tabari: any two fighters; al-Qurtubi: any two quarrelling Muslims / Aws and Khazraj / Abu 'Ali / Abu 'Ubaydah). What Ibn Sirin read: at-Tabari "ikhwanikum" (nun) vs al-Qurtubi "ikhwatikum" (ta, with al-Hasan for ikhwanikum). Closing clause: Ibn Kathir "tahqiq" (confirmation) vs Muyassar "raja'" (hope).
