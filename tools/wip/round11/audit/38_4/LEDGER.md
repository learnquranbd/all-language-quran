## Hadith
- **Jami' at-Tirmidhi 3232** (Dar-us-Salam Vol 5, Book 44, Hadith 3232). URL: https://quranx.com/hadith/Tirmidhi/Hadith-3232 . Chain on page: Mahmud ibn Ghaylan and 'Abd ibn Humayd, Abu Ahmad, Sufyan, al-A'mash, Yahya (ibn 'Abbad), Sa'id ibn Jubayr, Ibn 'Abbas. First ten words used (own translation from the Arabic): "Ibn 'Abbas said: Abu Talib fell ill, and Quraysh came". Quoted whole, split across s5.1 and s5.2. Grading on page (Arabic): «قال أبو عيسى هذا حديث حسن صحيح» = hasan sahih; reported as such. Ibn Kathir (en) gives Tirmidhi's verdict as "Hasan"; article says so and uses the page's grading. Saved: hadith_Tirmidhi_3232.txt.
- Translation note: quranx English adds "enraged" and garbles «يا عم قولوا»; article translates from the Arabic («فقام أبو جهل كي يمنعه» = stood up to keep him from it).
- Attached to the verse by: Ibn Kathir (en) (via Ibn Jarir, with Ahmad/Nasa'i/Tirmidhi) and Ma'arif. Ahmad and an-Nasa'i routes not fetched; named only as Ibn Kathir's attribution.

## Asbab an-nuzul
Used: the Abu Talib occasion, as above. Confirmed on the fetched Tirmidhi page («فنزل فيهم القرآن: ص والقرآن ذي الذكر ... إلى قوله: ... إن هذا إلا اختلاق»), so 38:4 falls inside the passage. Article says the commentators tie the surah's opening to it.

## Framing guard
s7.1 licensing sentence in both languages ("licenses nothing against any living person or community"); slander kept as quoted speech (s3.2, s4.1-4.3, s7.2); no claim about the Prophet's character drawn from the wonder; Ibn Kathir's «قبحهم الله» not repeated. 38:2-3 "pride and dissension" material limited to a cross-reference (s1.1, s1.2 grammar only).
