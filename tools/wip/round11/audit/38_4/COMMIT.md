Card and 8-section bilingual article for 38:4: Quraysh wonder that a warner came from
among themselves, and the disbelievers' charge "a magician, a liar".

Sources: Tabari, Qurtubi, Ibn Kathir (ar/en), Baghawi, Sa'di, Muyassar (38:4-5), Ma'arif (group 38:1-5).
- Wonder: Tabari, polytheists of Quraysh, a warner of their own and no angel from the sky;
  Ibn Kathir, a human like them, with 10:2; Muyassar, a human sent to call and warn;
  Sa'di, "no place for wonder", reasons for gratitude, 'akasu al-qadiyya, ta'ajjub inkar.
- Joining: Baghawi ties "they" to 38:2; Qurtubi's two views (continuation with 38:3 as
  parenthesis / fresh start "of their ignorance") kept side by side, no position.
- Slander: Qurtubi's two glosses of sahir (embellished deceiving speech; "qila" separating
  families) and kadhdhab "in the claim of prophethood"; Muyassar "sahir li-qawmihi".
  Kept as the accusers' meaning, not as description of the Prophet ﷺ.
- Mundhir = the Prophet ﷺ attributed to Tabari, with Qatada and as-Suddi via his chains.
- Ibn Kathir: inherited idol-worship behind the astonishment (read with 38:5); Ma'arif:
  the Prophet ﷺ said no more than that the idols were lifeless and powerless.
Hadith: Jami' at-Tirmidhi 3232 (Ibn 'Abbas, Abu Talib's illness, "one word"), quoted whole
from the Arabic; graded hasan sahih by at-Tirmidhi on the fetched page. Ibn Kathir (en)
reports the verdict as "Hasan"; the page's grading is the one given.
Asbab: used, confirmed on the Tirmidhi page (revelation of 38:1 to 38:7); Ibn Kathir's
Ibn Jarir route carries it to 38:8, stated as a difference.
Framing: licensing sentence in both languages; Ibn Kathir's words of censure not repeated;
38:2-3 kept to a cross-reference.
Dropped: kadhdhab morphology (no fetched text); Ma'arif's other chiefs and variant dialogue;
Ibn Jarir narrative details; 38:6-7 material (Christianity note, Ibn Jarir on 38:6).
Themes: character.
