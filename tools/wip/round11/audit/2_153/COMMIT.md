Replaces the 851-word, 6-section article with a 1,761-word, 8-section
one, from at-Tabari, Ibn Kathir, as-Sa'di, al-Baghawi, al-Muyassar and
Ma'arif on 2:153 (al-Qurtubi's slot only refers back to 2:45). The
shipped card is kept.

Distinct from 2:45-46: the address to the believers, Ibn Kathir's
thanks-then-patience order, at-Tabari's reading tied to the qiblah
change and new rulings (with Ma'arif's matching setting), the unnamed
object of ista'inu (Ma'arif/Mazhari, al-Muyassar, as-Sa'di against
at-Tabari's named tasks), prayer as patience made visible (Ma'arif) and
as-Sa'di's presence-of-heart condition, and the glosses on ma'a.
Disagreement kept: two kinds of sabr (Ibn Kathir, Abd ar-Rahman ibn
Zayd; obedience ranked higher) against three (as-Sa'di, al-Muyassar,
Ma'arif; the abridged English Ibn Kathir adds the third).
Ma'iyyah only as glossed: helper and backer (at-Tabari), help and
support (al-Baghawi), particular vs general withness (al-Muyassar,
as-Sa'di with 57:4), patience as settled trait (as-Sa'di).
Hadith: Muslim 2999 (Suhayb), quoted whole in Muslim's wording, not
Ibn Kathir's variant. Ali ibn al-Husayn's call to the patient given as
his words, not the Prophet's, though Ma'arif labels it a hadith; 39:10
as Ibn Kathir's support. Sa'id ibn Jubayr's definition. No asbab.
Dropped: Abu Dawud 1319 and the nafl-prayer report (used by 2:45-46),
the 2:154 martyr narrations, the abridged edition's 33:44 pointer.

Corrected from the old article: "hastened to prayer" (Abu Dawud says he
prayed); "the commentators note the order sabr then salah" (no source).
Dropped as unsourced: Ya'qub at 12:83 and 12:86, al-Bukhari on Ibrahim's
death, the 1:5 link and the practice list.
