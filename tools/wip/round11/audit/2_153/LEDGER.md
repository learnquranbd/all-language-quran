## Hadith

- Muslim 2999 (Suhayb). URL: https://quranx.com/hadith/Muslim/Hadith-2999 (saved: hadith_Muslim_2999.txt). First ten words of the Arabic used: عَجَبًا لأَمْرِ الْمُؤْمِنِ إِنَّ أَمْرَهُ كُلَّهُ خَيْرٌ وَلَيْسَ ذَاكَ. In Sahih Muslim; Muslim gives no separate grading. Translated whole from Muslim's Arabic. Ibn Kathir cites the hadith without naming a collection and in a different wording (لا يقضي الله له قضاء إلا كان خيرا له); that wording was NOT used, only Muslim's.
- Ali ibn al-Husayn's report (S7) is presented as his words, as Ibn Kathir gives it, explicitly not as a saying of the Prophet ﷺ. Ma'arif calls it "the Hadith"; the article reports that label and corrects it from the Arabic.
- No asbab an-nuzul: none of the eight files gives one. Placement after the qiblah change (at-Tabari, Ma'arif) is written as context, attributed.
