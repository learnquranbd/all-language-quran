Second half of the 30:17-18 tasbih passage: praise across all space
and all the hours of the day.

Sources gave two distinct movements. al-Hamd fi al-samawat wa-l-ard:
at-Tabari reads praise from the whole of creation (angels in heaven,
every creature on earth); al-Baghawi (Ibn Abbas) and Ibn Kathir read
Him as the One praised for all He made. Kept the frame spatial to stay
clear of the shipped 28:70 (hamd across the two worlds).

The four time-words: at-Tabari, al-Baghawi, as-Sa'di, Mujahid and Qatada
map tumsun/tusbihun/ashiyyan/tuzhirun onto Maghrib/Fajr/Asr/Zuhr, with
Isha drawn from 24:58 (the Ibn Abbas / Nafi b. al-Azraq exchange).

Disagreement kept open: whether the five prayers are encoded here, or
the verse is a general command to praise Him always. Stated on Ma'arif's
own authority that salah is not named in the wording; set al-Qurtubi's
iʿtiraḍ reading and Ibn Kathir's dark/light reading beside it, no verdict.
Also carried al-Qurtubi/al-Mawardi (night=tasbih, day=hamd) and the
ordering logic (evening first; Asr before Zuhr as the wusta prayer, 2:238).

Hadith: Abu Dawud 5076 (Ibn Abbas), reciting 30:17-19 morning and evening;
graded jayyid by Ibn Kathir, who carries the same chain. Grading reported,
not upgraded. The Ibrahim "alladhi waffa" note (53:37) and al-Baghawi's
general tasbih-merit reports marked as not fastened to the verse.

Dropped: any sahih claim for the hadith; Ma'arif's "authentic proofs"
wording for the Ibrahim report (chain via Ibn Lahiʿa). No asbab; placement
written instead.
