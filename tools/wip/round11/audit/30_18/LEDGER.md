# SOURCES ledger — 30:18

## Tafsir fetched (all pre-fetched in PREP.md; none re-fetched)

- **at-Tabari (15)** — USED. "وله الحمد في السماوات والأرض ... وله الحمد من جميع خلقه
  دون غيره، في السماوات من سكانها من الملائكة، والأرض من أهلها، من جميع أصناف خلقه
  فيها" → praise from the whole of creation, angels in heaven, people/creatures on
  earth (§2.1). "(وَعَشِيًّا) ... صلاة العصر (وَحِينَ تُظْهِرُونَ) ... وقت الظهر" →
  ʿashiyyan = ʿAsr, tuẓhirūn = Ẓuhr (§4.1). The Nāfiʿ b. al-Azraq / Ibn ʿAbbās exchange,
  mapping the four words to Maghrib/Fajr/ʿAsr/Ẓuhr with ʿIshāʾ from 24:58, and Mujāhid's
  like report (§5.1); Qatāda "أربع صلوات" naming the four (§4.3).
- **al-Qurtubi (90)** — USED. "اعتراض بين الكلام بدءوب الحمد على نعمه وآلائه" → the
  clause as an iʿtirāḍ of unceasing praise (§3.1). "وقيل: معنى وله الحمد أي الصلاة له
  لاختصاصها بقراءة الحمد. والأول أظهر" → the second (salah) view and his preference for
  the first (§3.1). al-Māwardī: night prayer named by tasbīḥ, day by ḥamd, with the
  reason (§3.2). "وبدأ بصلاة المغرب لأن الليل يتقدم النهار. وفى سورة (سبحان) بدأ بصلاة
  الظهر إذ هي أول صلاة صلاها جبريل بالنبي" → Maghrib-first / Isrāʾ begins with Ẓuhr (§6.1).
  al-Jawharī on ʿashiyy = from Maghrib to late-night dark (§4.3).
- **al-Baghawi (94)** — USED. "قال ابن عباس: يحمده أهل السماوات والأرض ويصلون له" →
  inhabitants of heaven/earth praise and pray to Him (§2.2). "(وعشيا) أي صلوا لله عشيا
  يعني صلاة العصر (وحين تظهرون) ... صلاة الظهر" → ʿAsr / Ẓuhr mapping (§4.2). Nāfiʿ b.
  al-Azraq / Ibn ʿAbbās five-prayers report (background to §5.1). General-merit tasbīḥ
  reports (Abū Hurayra "سبحان الله وبحمده ... مائة مرة") cited as not verse-attached (§7.2).
- **Ibn Kathir ar (14) + en abridged (169)** — USED. "هو المحمود على ما خلق في السماوات
  والأرض" → He is praised for what He created in heaven/earth (§2.2). "فالعشاء هو شدة
  الظلام، والإظهار قوة الضياء" / en "ʿAshiyy ... darkness is most intense, Izhar ... the
  brightest point of the day" → the cosmic dark/light reading, no prayer named (§5.2).
  Also carries the Abū Dāwūd recitation hadith with "إسناد جيد ورواه أبو داود" (§7.1),
  and the Imām Ahmad / Muʿādh b. Anas report tying the words to Ibrāhīm's title (§7.2).
- **as-Sa'di (91)** — USED. "فهذه الأوقات الخمسة أوقات الصلوات الخمس ... ويدخل في ذلك
  الواجب ... والمستحب كأذكار الصباح والمساء وأدبار الصلوات" → the five times = the five
  prayers; command reaches obligatory + recommended (§4.2).
- **al-Muyassar (16)** — USED. "وله سبحانه الحمد والثناء في السموات والأرض وفي الليل
  والنهار" → praise/thanks in heavens, earth, night and day (§1.2).
- **Ma'arif al-Qur'an en (168)** — USED. ʿashiyy = ʿAsr, the other = Ẓuhr; evening-before-
  morning (date changes at Maghrib); ʿAsr-before-Ẓuhr (middle/wusṭā prayer, 2:238, people
  busy) (§6.1, §6.2). "the prayer (salah) is not specified as such ... all types of
  worships ... are included. Since prayer is supreme ... included on priority" → the
  not-in-the-wording caveat (§5.2). Ibn ʿAbbās five-prayers answer (§5.1). Ḥasan al-Basrī:
  evening includes Maghrib + ʿIshāʾ (§5.3). Ibrāhīm "alladhī waffā" note (§7.2).

## Asbab an-nuzul

Not used. No asbāb is established for 30:18; it is the second half of the 30:17–18 tasbīḥ
passage. The verse's placement (after the resurrection scene 30:12–16, before the signs
of 30:19 ff.) is written instead (§1.1).

## Dropped / not asserted

- Did NOT grade the Abū Dāwūd hadith as ṣaḥīḥ; the word "sound" was removed from the draft.
  Only Ibn Kathir's "jayyid" is reported.
- Did NOT claim authenticity for the Ibrāhīm "alladhī waffā" report; Ma'arif's phrase
  "authentic proofs" was not echoed, since no collector grade was confirmed and the
  Ibn Kathir chain is weak.
- Did NOT reuse the shipped 28:70 angle (ḥamd across the two worlds, first life and last).
  Section 2 keeps the frame strictly spatial: ḥamd across the heavens and the earth / every
  region of the cosmos. "first and last" in §8.2 means eternally, not two worlds.

## Verse-specific accuracy note

The prayer-times mapping (Ibn ʿAbbās via at-Tabari, al-Baghawi, as-Saʿdi, Ma'arif, Mujāhid,
Qatāda) is given as strong tafsir, and §5.2 states plainly, on Ma'arif's own authority,
that salah is not named in the wording. The alternative (a general command to praise Him
always — al-Qurtubi's iʿtirāḍ reading; Ibn Kathir's dark/light reading) is given beside it
with no adjudication.
