The reward-refrain ("thus do We reward the doers of good") returning for the
fourth time in as-Saffat — after Nuh, after Ibrahim at 37:105, after Musa and
Harun at 37:116, now after Ilyas — given its own center of gravity rather than
rebuilding the 37:105 discussion: what ihsan meant specifically in Ilyas's own
arc, traced through the five verses before it (standing alone against Ba'l
worship at 37:125, flat denial at 37:127, then favorable mention among later
generations at 37:129 and the greeting of peace at 37:130). The capstone names
what those verses had already shown without naming it.

At-Tabari ties the reward to both obedience and deed-quality ("the people of
Our obedience and those who do good in deeds"). Al-Muyassar reads kadhalika
as pointing first to Ilyas's own reward, general rule second. Al-Qurtubi gives
two readings within his own commentary, not a dispute with anyone else:
preserved good mention, or deliverance from hardship in both worlds — kept
side by side. As-Sa'di states outright that Allah praised Ilyas "as He
praised his brothers," naming the surah's prophet-to-prophet refrain directly.
Al-Baghawi's note is the bare verse with no comment; Ibn Kathir (ar and the
grouped en abridgment) both cross-refer to his own earlier comments rather
than re-explain. No disagreement among the five Arabic mufassirun fetched —
stated plainly as a settled convergence rather than a manufactured dispute.

Checked and dropped: Ma'arif al-Qur'an's entry for this verse (grouped
130-132) covers only the Il-Yasin name etymology, not the "general law for
every muhsin" framing that was worth checking for — it is not in the fetched
text, so not attributed to Ma'arif. The closing section's point that
al-muhsinin is an unrestricted plural is instead stated as a grammatical
observation in the article's own voice.

Hadith: Bukhari 660 (the seven shaded on the Day) checked via the standing
stock fallback, quoted in full, and found unrelated — nothing in it names
Ilyas, idolatry, or his specific ordeal. Dropped and said so plainly in both
languages. No tafsir gathered attaches a hadith to this verse. No asbab
an-nuzul applies to a closing reward-formula; none claimed.

Distinguished explicitly from 37:116 (Musa and Harun's reward via the
support/victory arc) and from 37:105 (the Ibrahim-context discussion of this
same refrain, referenced by number, not rebuilt). Forward ground named but
not opened: 37:132 (Ilyas among the believing servants) and 37:133 (Lut's
story beginning).
