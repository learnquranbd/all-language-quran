Card and 8-section bilingual article for 40:67: huwa alladhi khalaqakum
min turab ... wa la'allakum ta'qilun, the stages of a life as evidence
for the "say" of 40:66.

Sources: Tabari, Ibn Kathir (ar + en abridged, grouped 40:66-68),
Muyassar, Qurtubi, Sa'di, Baghawi. Ma'arif (grouped 40:64-71) covers
only 40:64; not named.
- Frame: Tabari reads 40:67 as the description of the Lord the Prophet
  is commanded to submit to; Sa'di "as He created you alone, worship Him
  alone"; IK agent "by His command, management and decree".
- turab = Adam (Tabari, Muyassar, Sa'di "asl"); nutfa = mani (Muyassar),
  Sa'di's later stages; 'alaqa = thick red blood (Muyassar), clot (IK-en).
  Glosses only; article states it makes no embryological claim.
- tiflan = atfalan (Baghawi, Qurtubi), singular for the genus (Qurtubi).
- ashudd: Tabari, Qurtubi, Sa'di, Muyassar glosses; no age named (none
  fetched gives one). shuyukh/shiyukh and shaykhan readings, shaykh past
  forty (Qurtubi).
- Disagreement kept: min qablu = before old age (Tabari, Baghawi, Mujahid
  1) / before ashudd (Sa'di) / before birth, miscarriage (Mujahid 2 via
  Qurtubi, IK, with 22:5 as IK cites it) / unspecified (Muyassar).
- ajal musamma: Tabari, Baghawi, Mujahid, Qurtubi's lam al-'aqiba.
- ta'qilun: Ibn Jurayj resurrection (via IK) vs tawhid/power (Tabari,
  Baghawi, Qurtubi, Sa'di); both kept.
Hadith: Bukhari 3208 (Ibn Mas'ud), quoted whole from quranx, marked as
not attached to the verse by any fetched tafsir; placement in the
Sahih as the grading.
Asbab: none; placement given instead.
Dropped: 23:12-14, 30:54 (not cited by fetched tafsirs); any age for
ashudd; Ma'arif.
