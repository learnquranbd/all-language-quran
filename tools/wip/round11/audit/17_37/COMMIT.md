Replaces the 637-word, 6-section article with a 1,731-word, 8-section one.

Sources: at-Tabari, Ibn Kathir, al-Baghawi, al-Qurtubi, as-Sa'di, the
Muyassar and Ma'arif al-Qur'an, all fetched for 17:37.
- Marah glossed by each: at-Tabari/Qatada (vanity, boasting), Ibn Jurayj
  (do not boast), Ibn Kathir (strutting, swaying, walk of the overbearing),
  al-Baghawi and as-Sa'di (insolence, pride; proud against the truth).
- Al-Qurtubi's two kinds of marah: blamed pride vs praised joy and
  liveliness, with Bukhari 6309 on Allah's joy at repentance and Abu Dawud
  2659 on pride Allah loves (no grading on the page; said so).
- Grammar: marahan not marihan (at-Tabari, al-Baghawi); the marihan
  reading from al-Qurtubi (Ya'qub's report).
- Disagreement kept: takhriq as traverse (at-Tabari, Ibn Kathir, al-Baghawi,
  al-Azhari, an-Nahhas) vs bore into (al-Qurtubi).
- Why the two impossibilities: at-Tabari, al-Baghawi (incl. heels/toes
  reading), al-Qurtubi (a servant hemmed in), as-Sa'di (outcome).
- Hadith: swallowed man, Muslim 2088 (attached by Ibn Kathir), Bukhari
  5789 named; Muslim 91 on kibr (via Ma'arif); Tirmidhi 3637 on the
  Prophet's walk, hasan sahih (via al-Baghawi). Qarun 28:79, 28:81.
- Al-Hasan and Ibn al-Ahtam, from Ibn Kathir (Ibn Abi al-Dunya).
- Licensing line on the swallowed man and Qarun.
Defects fixed from the shipped article: it said the word describes the
walker (the sources say the opposite) and blended Bukhari and Muslim
wordings of the swallowed-man hadith.
Dropped: unsourced 25:63, 31:19, 4:36, 57:23, 28:83 links; unnamed
"man tawada'a" hadith; Saba' story; Tirmidhi 3648 (gharib).
