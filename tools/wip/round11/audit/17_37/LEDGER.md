## Shipped short article (6 sections, 637 words): dropped and wrong
- WRONG: "Arabic puts the word in a form that describes the state of the walker rather than the walking." At-Tabari says the opposite: marahan (verbal noun), not marihan, precisely because it is not a description of the walker but explains "do not walk"; al-Baghawi: "تفسير المشي فلذلك أخرجه على المصدر".
- WRONG-ish: "Marah is not speed and not a style of stride." Ibn Kathir defines it as a stride: "متبخترا متمايلا".
- Hadith of the swallowed man was given as al-Bukhari's with "his manner proud", a phrase from Muslim's variants (يتبختر), not Bukhari 5789: a hybrid. Now Muslim 2088c quoted whole.
- Dropped (unsourced for this verse): 25:63 hawnan and 31:19 contrast; "lan" as emphatic negation for good; mukhtal fakhur in 4:36, 31:18, 57:23; 28:83; "commentators of 25:63 warn against performing humility"; the "Walking Without an Audience" reflection (fasting/prayer counted, bearing not).
- Kept, now sourced: ring 17:22-39 (verse texts + Ma'arif's numbering); Ibn Mas'ud hadith (Muslim 91, via Ma'arif); swallowed man (via Ibn Kathir); down/up reading (now al-Baghawi's heels/toes, attributed).

## Licensing
Section 7 states both reports (the swallowed man, Qarun) describe particular men and license no one to call a living person's misfortune a punishment or to judge another's heart by his gait (EN and BN).
