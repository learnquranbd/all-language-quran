Card and 8-section bilingual article for 39:46-47: qul Allahumma fatira
as-samawati wa-l-ard, the call that answers 39:45's aversion, and the
ransom of the whole earth twice over with ma lam yakunu yahtasibun.

Sources: Ibn Kathir (ar + en abridged), Tabari, Qurtubi, Sa'di, Baghawi,
Muyassar, Ma'arif.
- 39:46: fatir glossed by Tabari/Suddi, Ibn Kathir, Muyassar, Sa'di;
  Qurtubi's grammar (vocative in construct, not na't per Sibawayh); the
  disputed matter per Tabari and Muyassar; Sa'di on muwahhidun vs
  mushrikun both claiming the truth, citing 22:17 and 67:14.
- Rabi' b. Khuthaym reciting it at al-Husayn's killing (Qurtubi, Ma'arif
  with Ruh al-Ma'ani); Sa'id b. Jubayr's saying, marked as not hadith.
- 39:47: zalamu = mushrikun (Ibn Kathir, Tabari, Muyassar), deniers and
  associators (Qurtubi); ransom refused (Ibn Kathir with 3:91, Sa'di with
  26:88-89, Muyassar); Qurtubi points back to Al 'Imran and ar-Ra'd.
Disagreement kept, no position: ma lam yakunu yahtasibun as unforeseen
punishment (Ibn Kathir, Tabari, Muyassar, Muqatil), wrath (Sa'di),
"good" deeds turning bad (Mujahid, Suddi; Baghawi: idol worship as
nearness), deferred repentance / forgiveness without repentance
(Qurtubi qila), riya' (Sufyan ath-Thawri). Ibn al-Munkadir's deathbed fear.
Hadith: Muslim 770 (A'isha, night-prayer opening dua), quranx-confirmed,
quoted whole; Muslim's Sahih, no separate grading. Narrator given as
Abu Salamah per the Arabic chain (quranx English and Ma'arif drop it).
Dropped: Ibn Kathir's Ahmad/Tirmidhi dua narrations; 5:36 and 13:18
(no tafsir cites them by verse). Asbab: none. 39:48-49 not pulled forward.
Licensing sentence (EN and BN) for alladhina zalamu.
Themes: duas, accountability.
