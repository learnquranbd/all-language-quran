Card and 8-section article (1,763 EN words, 19 paragraphs, 55-110 each).
13:3 is 24 Arabic words, per PREP.md.

Sources: all eight tafsir IDs; 169 is grouped 13:3-4, stated, and only its
13:3 part used. Placement from Ibn Kathir and al-Qurtubi (upper world, then
lower). Madd: basataha tulan wa 'ardan (at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi), al-Baghawi,
Ibn Kathir, the Muyassar (readied for livelihood), as-Sa'di (created for the
servants, blessed, made a bed). Rawasi: at-Tabari's arsaytu al-watad,
al-Qurtubi, as-Sa'di (lest it sway, pegs). Rivers: all, plus Ma'arif on snow.
Night: at-Tabari with Qatada, al-Baghawi, the Muyassar, Ibn Kathir (time as
well as place), as-Sa'di with 28:73. Tafakkur: al-Baghawi's definition,
at-Tabari's inference to worship and to the power to revive, as-Sa'di's
nazar i'tibar; tied to 13:2 and 13:5.

Disagreements kept, both named, no position: the earth's shape (al-Qurtubi's
mas'ala against "like a ball" vs Ma'arif, spreading not contrary to roundness);
zawjayn as male and female (al-Farra' via al-Qurtubi, who calls it contrary to
the text, vs Ma'arif, date palm and papaya, unproven for all). The article
says nothing about shape in its own voice and makes no science claims.

Hadith: none attached to 13:3 by any fetched tafsir; said in one sentence.
Dropped: any yughshi/yughashshi reading note (not in fetched texts), the Abu
Qubays report, the poet's name from at-Tabari's editor footnote, IK en's 13:4
material. Parallels shipped at 7:54, 36:36, 2:164 and 45:13 not repeated.
