## Disagreements kept (both named, no position)
- Earth's shape: al-Qurtubi (verse refutes "like a ball"; earth still, at rest, spread) vs Ma'arif (spreading
  not contrary to roundness). §3. Article voice states nothing about the shape; it says it does not choose.
- Zawjayn as male/female: al-Farra' via al-Qurtubi, who rejects it as "خلاف النص", vs Ma'arif (not unlikely,
  date palm, papaya, unproven for all). At-Tabari's reported "four" view and his own "two kinds" also given. §5.
