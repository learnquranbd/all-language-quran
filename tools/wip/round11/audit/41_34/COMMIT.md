Replaces the 717-word, 5-section article with an 8-section, 1,755-word
source-gated article (shipped card kept).

Sources: at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi, as-Sa'di, Ibn Kathir (ar, en abridged),
al-Baghawi, Muyassar, Ma'arif, all on 41:34 itself. Covers the link to
41:33 and the caller (Ma'arif), at-Tabari's reading against 41:30 and
41:26, the hasana/sayyi'a glosses (Muyassar, as-Sa'di with 55:60,
al-Qurtubi's list, al-Baghawi's three pairs), the second la (al-Farra';
at-Tabari's logic and the Basran view on 57:29, 75:1-2), idfa' as
forbearance, pardon and patience (at-Tabari, Muyassar, Ibn 'Abbas via
'Ali b. Abi Talhah), as-Sa'di on relatives, the salam reading ('Ata',
Mujahid), Abu Bakr's reply, the handshake reading (Ibn al-'Arabi via
al-Qurtubi), waliyy hamim as near kin, 41:35-36 with 7:199-200 and
23:96-98 (Ibn Kathir), and 'Ali's word to Qanbar.

Disagreements kept: what hasana/sayyi'a name; la as connective vs extra
for emphasis; occasion Abu Sufyan (Muqatil) vs Abu Jahl (al-Mawardi),
with al-Qurtubi's preference reported; al-Qurtubi's abrogation claim vs
the others, who explain the command as standing. Licensing sentence in
both languages.

Hadith: Bukhari 6263 (Qatadah-Anas on handshaking), quoted whole, cited
by al-Qurtubi for the handshake reading and marked as not about the
verse; no fetched tafsir attaches a Prophetic saying on answering wrong
with good (stated).

Dropped: Ibn 'Abbas "great portion = Paradise" (not in fetched texts),
ad-Dahhak's pair (likely misprint), and the old article's unsourced
claims (hamim as heat; Ibn Kathir's "nature testifies", which he does
not say; 13:22 and 4:86; yulaqqaha morphology).
