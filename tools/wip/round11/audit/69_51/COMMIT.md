Card and 8-section bilingual article for 69:51: wa-innahu la-haqqu
al-yaqin. 69:40-50 as the frame; 69:52 named only as the next verse.

Sources: Tabari, Qurtubi, Ibn Kathir (ar; en grouped 69:44-52), Sa'di,
Baghawi, Muyassar (grouped 69:49-52), Ma'arif (69:44-51, context only;
its text does not reach 69:51). All named.
- Referent kept as a disagreement: the Qur'an (Qurtubi's first reading,
  Tabari with lam yataqawwalhu, IK) vs the regret of 69:50 (Qurtubi's
  qila, hasra as masdar tahassur, so masculine pronoun). No position.
- 69:50 pronoun via IK (en): the rejection (Ibn Jarir, Qatadah) vs the
  Qur'an (IK, "possible"); 26:200-201 and 34:54 as he cites them.
- Grammar: Ibn Abbas via Qurtubi ('ayn al-yaqin, mahd al-yaqin as a
  comparison of wording); not a na't (rajulu al-zarif); Baghawi's
  one-line "annexed to itself because the two words differ".
- Three grades of yaqin taught only as Sa'di's, attributed throughout;
  his "attained by whoever tastes it" carries the reflection.
- 41:44 pointer is the drafter's identification (IK quotes the words
  without a number); verified.
Hadith: none attached by any fetched tafsir; said in one sentence.
Asbab: none given. Licensing sentence on the deniers in both languages.
Dropped: 56:95, 102:5-7 and 69:1 parallels (not cited by any source),
Ahsanul Bayaan's Hour reading (not a gated source).
