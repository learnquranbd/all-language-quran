Card and eight-section bilingual article for 15:22 (1,777 EN words, 22
paragraphs): We sent the winds lawaqih, sent down water and gave it to you
to drink, and you are not its keepers.

Sources: Tabari, Ibn Kathir ar, Qurtubi, Sa'di, Baghawi, Muyassar named on
the verse; Ma'arif (168, grouped 15:22-23) and Ibn Kathir abridged (169,
grouped 15:21-25) named with their groups stated, 15:22 content only.
15:21 (Ibn Mas'ud: no year has more rain, Allah divides it) and 15:23 given
one pointer each.

"Lawaqih": laqih as carrier (she-camel image; Baghawi, Qurtubi) vs
mulqih/malaqih as fertiliser (Abu 'Ubayda); Tabari's own view that they are
both, citing Ibn Mas'ud; Qurtubi's "all of it is sound". What they
fertilise kept side by side with names, no position: clouds (Ibn Mas'ud,
an-Nakha'i, Qatada, ad-Dahhak), trees ('Ubayd b. 'Umayr's four winds, Abu
'Ubayda), both (al-Hasan, Ibn 'Abbas via Ibn Jurayj, Ibn Kathir, Muyassar);
Sa'di's clouds only. Plural winds vs the singular barren wind (Ibn Kathir,
51:41); Hamza's singular reading (Qurtubi, Tabari).
"Asqaynakumuhu": Tabari/Baghawi asqa vs saqa (land and livestock);
Qurtubi records both views. "Khazinin": withholding (Sufyan ath-Thawri,
Tabari) vs storing (Qurtubi, Baghawi, Sa'di, Ibn Kathir "it may mean");
kept as a difference of emphasis. Ma'arif's water-supply, quota and six
blessings reported as its view; its sea/desalination/monsoon explanation
summarised in one attributed clause, no science in the article's voice.

Hadith: none sound attached to 15:22; Tabari's Abu Hurayra "south wind
from Paradise" noted with Ibn Kathir's own "weak chain" and not used.
General narration, marked unattached: Muslim 899b (A'isha, the wind
supplication). Asbab: none.

Trimmed to budget in draft: Tabari's grammarians' paragraph and Ibn
Kathir's sweet-water pointer (56:68-70, 16:10) cut; 56:68-70 kept as a
single reference in the reflection section. No parallel-verse article
shipped (only 2:164; its rain-and-resurrection angle not repeated).
