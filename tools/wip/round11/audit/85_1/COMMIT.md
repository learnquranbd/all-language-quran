Card and 8-section bilingual article for 85:1: wa-s-sama'i dhati
al-buruj, three words, the first of three oaths. 85:2-3 named only;
the Ditch (85:4 on) left to its entries.

Sources: Tabari, Ibn Kathir (ar + en abridged), Qurtubi, Sa'di,
Muyassar, Ma'arif, Baghawi (no gloss; 85:2-3 slots checked). Jawab
material from the Tabari, Qurtubi and Sa'di 85:4 slots, named as such.
- al-buruj: palaces; stars; where the guards are; beautiful creation
  (al-Minhal); sand and water (Sufyan ibn Husayn); stations of sun and
  moon (Tabari's choice, via 4:78; Abu 'Ubayda and Yahya ibn Sallam per
  Qurtubi; Muyassar; Sa'di). Ibn 'Abbas given for palaces by Tabari and
  Qurtubi, for stars by Ibn Kathir and Ma'arif: kept as a conflict.
- Jawab: 85:12 (Qatada; Qurtubi calls it qabih); qutila with lam
  understood (Basran, Farra'); reversed order (Abu Hatim; Ibn al-Anbari:
  error); 85:10; omitted "you will be raised" (Ibn al-Anbari); left
  unstated, fresh statement (Tabari's choice); the oath's own content
  (Sa'di). None chosen. 91:1/91:9 as cited by Tabari and Qurtubi.
- Qurtubi's twelve names reported as his gloss only; no fortune or
  influence derived. Ma'arif's rejection of fixed stars (Mazhari, 36:40).
- Muyassar: only Allah swears by His creation; for a creature it is shirk.
- Shipped 15:16 and 91:9 cross-referenced by number, not re-argued.
- Licensing sentence on 85:4 in both languages.
Hadith: none attached to 85:1; Ibn Kathir's two Musnad Ahmad recitation
reports named as unchecked, not quoted. 85:2-3 hadith left out.
Asbab: none. Dropped: 15:16 root material, all Ditch narratives.
Fix: dropped mention of unchecked Ahmad reports (not confirmable). Oath-answer material taken from the 85:4 slots of at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi, as-Sa'di, said so in the article.
