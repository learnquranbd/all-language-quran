New card and 1,783-word, 9-section article, from at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi,
Ibn Kathir, al-Baghawi, as-Sa'di and Ma'arif on 2:233 (al-Muyassar and
Ibn Kathir (en) as cross-checks). Kept to 2:233 alone.

Placement among the divorce rulings from Ma'arif and al-Qurtubi.
Disagreements kept with names and no position: which mothers are meant
(as-Suddi/ad-Dahhak and at-Tabari: divorced; al-Qurtubi: wives in a
standing marriage; as-Sa'di: both); whether nursing is binding
(al-Baghawi, at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi, Ma'arif); two years as limit when
parents differ (Ibn 'Abbas via 'Ali ibn Abi Talhah, 'Ata', ath-Thawri),
concession (Qatadah, ar-Rabi'), no prohibition after it (Ibn 'Abbas, Ibn
'Umar) or fitted to the pregnancy (Ibn 'Abbas via 'Ikrimah); al-warith
(agnates, all heirs, mahram heirs, the child himself, the surviving
parent, or no-harm only). at-Tabari's own preferences reported as his.
Six-month minimum: the 'Uthman report with Ibn 'Abbas reciting 46:15,
as at-Tabari gives it (#3910, ungraded), and as-Sa'di's deduction.
Tirmidhi 1152 (Umm Salamah, at-Tirmidhi: hasan sahih) on nursing kinship
within two years, as Ibn Kathir attaches it.
La tudarra from Mujahid, Qatadah, al-Hasan, at-Tabari and al-Baghawi,
both parents held to one line. Weaning by consent and consultation
(Mujahid, ath-Thawri, as-Sa'di) and the wet-nurse clause (Ibn Kathir,
al-Qurtubi's three readings of sallamtum).
at-Tabari's text is truncated by the API inside the weaning clause.
Dropped: Ibn Kathir's medical asides on nursing after two years and
colostrum, the Hind hadith, adult nursing, custody masa'il, readings.
