## Range of readings for shughul (kept as a range, no position taken)
niʿma (Mujahid; al-Hasan joined to being spared the Fire) · marital joy (Ibn Masʿud, Ibn ʿAbbas via ʿIkrima, Saʿid b. al-Musayyab — modest one-line mention, no Arabic term, no explicit phrasing, as-Sa'di register) · spared concern for the people of the Fire (al-Hasan, Ismaʿil b. Abi Khalid, al-Kalbi, Saʿid per al-Qurtubi) · samaʿ (Wakiʿ b. al-Jarrah) · visiting (Ibn Kaysan) · guests of Allah («قيل», unattributed, stated as such) · at-Tabari's combined verdict.
Noted inconsistency kept, not resolved: Saʿid b. al-Musayyab is given for the marital reading by at-Tabari but for the "spared" reading by al-Qurtubi; the article reports each as each gives it.

## Dropped
- al-Qurtubi's long «وروي» narration (caller summons the obedient, they ride to the Throne, pass the Sirat, crowd told this verse): no chain, no collection — not usable as hadith; dropped.
- al-Qurtubi's Abu Qilaba report (man "busy with his family"): dropped for register (marital-reading detail).
- Abu Hatim's continuation that the samaʿ report should read as the marital one: not repeated (register; the "perhaps a mistake by the hearer" clause is kept).
- Arabic phrase iftiḍāḍ al-abkār/al-ʿadhārā: not quoted in either language (register).
- Ibn Kathir's full name-list for the marital reading: summarised, not listed.
- Talha b. Musarrif «فاكهين» (accusative ḥāl) and Ibn Masʿud's codex «فاكهين» (Tabari footnote, from al-Farraʾ): not used.
- 36:56-58 (shade, couches, fruit, "Peace") and 36:59: kept as future ground; only "the verses that follow carry the picture further" is said.
