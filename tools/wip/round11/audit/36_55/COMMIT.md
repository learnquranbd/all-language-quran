Card + 8-section bilingual article (1,677 EN words) for 36:55, the
companions of Paradise "this Day, in an occupation, rejoicing".

Sources: at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi, al-Baghawi, Ibn Kathir, as-Sa'di,
al-Muyassar, Ma'arif al-Qur'an, each quoted from the text fetched for 36:55.
- Link to 36:54: Sa'di "recompense of the two parties, beginning with
  Paradise"; Ibn Kathir on leaving the plains for the gardens.
- Shughul kept as a named range: niʿma (Mujahid), spared concern for the
  Fire (al-Hasan, Ismaʿil b. Abi Khalid, al-Kalbi; Saʿid b. al-Musayyab per
  Qurtubi), marital joy (Ibn Masʿud, Ibn ʿAbbas via ʿIkrima, Saʿid per
  Tabari; one modest line, no Arabic term), samaʿ (Wakiʿ), visiting (Ibn
  Kaysan), guests of Allah (qila); Tabari's verdict combines them; Sa'di's
  general wording set as the register.
- Ibn Kathir's "listening to strings" report kept with Abu Hatim's "perhaps
  a mistake by the hearer".
- Qiraʾat as linguistic range: shughl/shughul (both correct per Tabari;
  shaghal rejected); fākihūn/fakihūn (Abu Jaʿfar; Hafs in al-Mutaffifin per
  Baghawi); glosses farihun, masrurun, muʿjabun/ʿajibun, naʿimun; Basran vs
  Kufan grammar, Tabari prefers the Kufan.
- Ma'arif's "no boredom in Paradise" reading of fi shughul.
- Hadith: none; no fetched tafsir attaches a sound one (said once).
  Stock Bukhari 660 not used.
- Dropped: Qurtubi's chainless "caller summons the obedient" narration,
  Abu Qilaba report, iftiḍāḍ phrasing, Talha's fākihīn; 36:56-59 left
  for later.
