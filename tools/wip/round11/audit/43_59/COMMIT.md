Card and 8-section bilingual article for 43:59: in huwa illa 'abdun
an'amna 'alayhi wa-ja'alnahu mathalan li-bani Isra'il. 43:57-58 as
context; 43:60-61 one line only, no descent material.

Sources: Tabari, Ibn Kathir (ar; en abridged for 43:57-58), Muyassar,
Qurtubi, Sa'di, Baghawi, Ma'arif.
- In = ma (Baghawi); ma ... illa restriction in five tafsirs.
- The favour: prophethood (Qurtubi, Baghawi, Muyassar), prophethood and
  messengership (IK), prophethood/wisdom/knowledge/action (Sa'di),
  tawfiq and iman (Tabari); none picked. Qurtubi's "qila": the servant
  is Muhammad ﷺ, first view more apparent.
- Mathal: sign and proof via his mission (Tabari, Qatadah x2) vs sign of
  power, birth without a father (IK, Muyassar, Baghawi, Sa'di, Qurtubi
  with miracles); kept side by side.
- Occasion (43:57-58): Ma'arif's three events (no chains); Ibn Ishaq's
  report flagged chainless; Ibn 'Abbas via al-'Awfi, chain as given,
  ungraded. Sa'di's three answers on 21:98.
- Tabari's line on the Christian claim attributed to him; no polemic
  in the article's voice.
Hadith: Tirmidhi 3253 (Abu Umamah, arguing after guidance), at-Tirmidhi:
hasan sahih; marked as attached to 43:58, not 43:59.
Licensing sentence in both languages (disputants of 43:57 and 43:58).
Dropped: Ibn Mas'ud's "am hadha"; Ibn Ishaq report's Prophetic reply
(chainless); 43:61 descent material.
