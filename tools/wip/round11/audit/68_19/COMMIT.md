Card and 8-section bilingual article for 68:19: fa-tafa 'alayha ta'ifun
min rabbika wa hum na'imun, the night blow on the garden of 68:17-33.
68:20's blackened garden and 68:24's poor left to their own verses.

Sources: at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi, al-Baghawi, as-Sa'di, Ibn Kathir (ar),
al-Muyassar (grouped 68:19-20) and Ma'arif al-Qur'an, all fetched on 68:19.
Ibn Kathir (en) holds no commentary on this verse and is not cited.

Glosses of ta'if kept side by side, none chosen:
- a command of Allah (Ibn Abbas via at-Tabari, two chains, and al-Qurtubi);
  a punishment (Qatada; al-Baghawi; as-Sa'di); Jibril (qila, al-Qurtubi);
  a neck of fire from a valley of Jahannam (Ibn Jurayj); fire from the sky
  (al-Baghawi, al-Muyassar); a heavenly blight (Ibn Kathir); a whirl of
  calamity, fire per "some narratives" (Ma'arif).
- Night-only usage: at-Tabari, al-Farra' (via al-Qurtubi and the editor's
  note), al-Baghawi; the Abu al-Jarrah exception verse reported.
- min rabbika: sent, not chance; Ibn Kathir's hannathahum (oath undone).
- al-Qurtubi's third issue: resolve is taken to account; parallel 22:25,
  cross-reference to 3:135 (not summarised, not fetched).

Hadith: al-Bukhari 31 (two Muslims with their swords), attached by
al-Qurtubi to the point on resolve; core wording quoted whole. No asbab.

Dropped: story names and places (absent from the fetched texts), parallels
18:42 and 2:266 (not cited), al-Qurtubi's blackened-garden clause (68:20),
the al-Farra' qata proverb. Licensing sentence in both languages.
- Bukhari 31: the Prophet's saying and the exchange quoted whole; the narrator's opening scene (al-Ahnaf stopped by Abu Bakra) is omitted and the article says so.
