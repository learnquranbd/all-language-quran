## Dropped
- Story details (place of the garden: Yemen / Sana'a / Abyssinia etc.; number of brothers; their father): no fetched 68:19 text gives them. None named.
- al-Qurtubi's opening clause فجاءوها ليلا فرأوا الجنة مسودة: a fragment of his narrative running on from the earlier verses, and its content (blackened garden) is 68:20 ground. Not used.
- Content of al-Qurtubi's earlier Jibril passage (على ما تقدم ذكره) and his 3:135 discussion: not fetched. Mentioned only as cross-references.
- The Ibn Abbas question in at-Tabari's first chain mentions الطوَفان; unclear wording, not used.
- al-Farra' qata proverb (لو ترك القطا ليلا لنام) from the editor's footnote: its point is hard to render without interpretation. Dropped.
- Parallels 18:42 and 2:266 (garden burnt): no fetched tafsir cites them. Not used.
- Any counter-hadith on intentions not acted upon: not attached by a fetched tafsir. Not used.
- 68:24 (the poor kept out): not touched.
