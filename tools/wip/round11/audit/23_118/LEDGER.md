- t168 Ma'arif al-Qur'an: objectless verbs and general prayer (deliverance from harm and suffering / grant of every wish; protection from bodily harm and legitimate gains), credited to (Mazhari); "the reason why the Holy Prophet was commanded to recite this prayer ... even though he is impeccable, is to impress upon his followers the special merit of this prayer.
