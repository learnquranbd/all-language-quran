Replaces the 791-word, 6-section article with a 1,704-word, 9-section
deep article on the surah's closing du'a, forgive and have mercy.

Sources: at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi, Ibn Kathir (ar + en), as-Sa'di,
al-Muyassar, Ma'arif al-Qur'an, all on 23:118 (t169 grouped 23:117-118).
- Placement: 23:112-116 in brief; al-Qurtubi's "thumma amara"; 23:117
  via Ibn Kathir (reckoning with Allah, no success on the Day).
- Frame 23:1 / 23:117 (Ma'arif), 23:102-103 noted from the text;
  licensing sentence in both languages.
- Who is addressed: Tabari, Muyassar, Sa'di; al-Qurtubi's two readings
  (so the umma follows him / for his umma) kept without a position;
  Ibn Kathir's irshad; Ma'arif (via Qurtubi) on the impeccable Prophet.
- Objectless verbs: Ma'arif (Mazhari), Ibn Kathir "idha utliqa"; objects
  supplied differently by Tabari (me), Sa'di (us), Muyassar (the sins).
- Forgiveness: wipe and conceal (Ibn Kathir), cover (Tabari), pass over
  (Muyassar), rescue from harm (Sa'di). Mercy: accepted repentance
  (Tabari), guidance in words and deeds (Ibn Kathir), every good (Sa'di).
- Khayru-r-rahimin: Tabari/Muyassar, Sa'di's mother comparison;
  23:109-111 echo (phrase only in 23:109 and 23:118, checked in data).
Hadith: none attached. Al-Qurtubi's ath-Tha'labi report (Ibn Lahi'ah)
noted as ungraded and unconfirmed, not built on.
Dropped from the old article: the arhamu-r-rahimin section (7:151, 12:64,
12:92, 21:83), the 23:99-100 link and unsourced reflections; "spoken in
the Fire" for 23:109 corrected.
