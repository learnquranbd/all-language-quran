Card and 9-section bilingual article for 46:21: wa-dhkur akha 'Adin
idh andhara qawmahu bil-Ahqaf wa qad khalati n-nudhuru min bayni
yadayhi wa min khalfihi alla ta'budu illa-llah, inni akhafu 'alaykum
'adhaba yawmin 'azim. Placed after 46:20; 46:22-28 left to their verses.

Sources: Tabari, Qurtubi, Sa'di, Ibn Kathir (ar; en 46:21 lines only),
Baghawi, Muyassar, Ma'arif (46:21 material only).
- Wa-dhkur: warning to Quraysh (Tabari), consolation (Ibn Kathir),
  both in Qurtubi, "with praise" (Sa'di).
- Akha 'Ad: Hud (AS); brother in lineage not religion (Muyassar,
  Qurtubi); tribe + well-wisher (Ma'arif, with 11:50).
- Al-Ahqaf: lexical glosses per source; Tabari's list of places (Sham,
  'Uman-Mahra, 'Uman-Hadramawt, Hisma, ash-Shihr) and his verdict that
  knowing the place fulfils no duty. No place chosen.
- Nudhur kept as a disagreement: before/after in time (Tabari, Farra'
  via Qurtubi, Muyassar) vs towns around 'Ad (Ibn Kathir, 2:66,
  41:13-14). Ibn Mas'ud's wa min ba'dihi reading.
- Alla ta'budu: parenthesis vs Hud's speech (Qurtubi, both); Tabari
  and ad-Dahhak; Sa'di and Ibn Kathir as Hud's words.
- 'Adhab yawmin 'azim: Day of Resurrection (Tabari, Muyassar); Sa'di
  names no day.
Hadith: Ibn Majah 3852 (yarhamuna Allahu wa akha 'Ad), attached by Ibn
Kathir, quoted whole from the Arabic; no grading by Ibn Majah, none
added. A'ishah cloud narrations left for 46:24.
Asbab: none in the sources. Licensing sentence in both languages; no
modern identification of 'Ad.
Dropped: 'Ali's Barhut report, al-Kalbi, extra Shihr/Iram geography,
surah-name origin (not in sources), 7:65 and 26:123 (uncited).
- Gate: three number-parity wordings fixed.
