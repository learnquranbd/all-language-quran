Card and 8-section article (1,657 EN words, 18 paragraphs, 83-110 each).
12:50 is 22 Arabic words, as counted in PREP.md.

Sources: all eight tafsir IDs; 169 is grouped 12:50-53, stated, and only
its 12:50 content used. The refusal to leave until cleared: Ibn Kathir
(honour clean, prison injustice), al-Qurtubi (jailed without crime),
Qatada and Ibn Jurayj on al-'udhr and Ibn Abbas via as-Suddi (all in
at-Tabari), as-Sa'di (patience, sense, judgement), al-Muyassar. Ibn Atiyya
via al-Qurtubi on release as silent pardon; al-Baghawi on the king's eye
of suspicion. Rabbika as the king (as-Sa'di, al-Baghawi, at-Tabari,
Muyassar) and rabbi as Allah (al-Baghawi, at-Tabari, 169). The wife left
unnamed out of adab (al-Baghawi, al-Qurtubi, Ibn Atiyya, Ma'arif).

Disagreement kept: at-Tabari's "it is said" reading of rabbi as Itfir the
Aziz, placed after his own.

Hadith: Bukhari 4694 quoted whole (the Ibn al-Qasim chain al-Qurtubi
places in Kitab at-Tafsir; 3372 noted as the same saying). Attachment
stated per tafsir. Al-Qurtubi's question and answer (hazm vs sabr) and
Ma'arif attributed; nothing in the article's voice on either prophet
falling short. Added before merge: one sentence in both languages leaving
the Lut and Ibrahim clauses to 11:80 and 2:260, since no fetched tafsir
explains them under 12:50 and they were quoted unexplained.

One pointer back to 12:25 and 12:30, one licensing sentence, one pointer
forward to 12:51-53.

Dropped: Ahmad's wording, the Ikrima mursal (Ibn Kathir's label) and a
weak Tabari chain, the Tirmidhi version, Ma'arif's second answer.
