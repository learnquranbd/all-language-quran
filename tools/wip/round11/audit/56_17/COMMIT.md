Card and 8-section bilingual article (1,745 EN words) on 56:17: yatufu
'alayhim wildanun mukhalladun. 56:11-16 as frame, 56:10 pointed to once
for the forerunners as subject (at-Tabari); 56:18 framed only.

Sources: at-Tabari, Ibn Kathir (ar), al-Qurtubi, al-Baghawi, as-Sa'di,
al-Muyassar (group 56:17-19, 56:17 clause only), Ibn Kathir en (group
56:13-22, 56:17 clause only), Ma'arif (group 56:15-17, 56:17 part only).
- yatufu: for service (al-Baghawi, Muyassar), and to meet their needs
  (as-Sa'di); al-Qurtubi: blessing is completed by being surrounded by
  servants. wildan = ghilman; as-Sa'di's hidden pearl (52:24 wording).
- mukhalladun, every gloss kept with names: do not die (Mujahid, chain
  in at-Tabari); one age, no change, no death (at-Tabari); no ageing or
  change (al-Hasan, al-Kalbi, Imru' al-Qays line); no greying (Ibn
  Kathir); al-Farra' and at-Tabari on mukhallad = aged without greying;
  Ibn Kaysan; as-Sa'di; earrings (Sa'id ibn Jubayr, khulda/khuld),
  earrings and bracelets (at-Tabari's "others"), bracelets (al-Farra'),
  girded, mun'amun ('Ikrimah). Only at-Tabari ranks; reported as his.
- Identity kept as a dispute, no position: created without birth
  (al-Qurtubi qila); born in Paradise, "preferred" (Ma'arif); children of
  the Muslims who die young ('Ali, al-Hasan); children of the polytheists
  (Salman); children of the people of this world, no birth in the Garden
  (al-Hasan per al-Baghawi). Discrepancies noted, not reconciled. Article
  states no child's fate.
Hadith: none attaches to 56:17. Salman's saying marked as a Companion's;
the Prophetic report on polytheists' children as servants named as
unconfirmed, not quoted; Ma'arif's general hadith remark unconfirmed.
Asbab: none. Dropped: 76:19 (no source cites it), wildan morphology.
