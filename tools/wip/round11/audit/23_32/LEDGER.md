# SOURCES ledger — 23:32

Verse: فَأَرْسَلْنَا فِيهِمْ رَسُولًۭا مِّنْهُمْ أَنِ ٱعْبُدُوا۟ ٱللَّهَ مَا لَكُم مِّنْ إِلَٰهٍ غَيْرُهُۥ ۖ أَفَلَا تَتَّقُونَ
"We sent among them a messenger from themselves: worship Allah, you have no god but Him; will you not fear Him?"

## Tafsir fetched (all in audit/23_32/), and where used

- **at-Tabari (15)** — USED. Names no messenger; glosses "داعيا لهم" (calling them),
  "اعبدوا الله ... وأطيعوه دون الآلهة والأصنام، فإن العبادة لا تنبغي إلا له" (worship/obey Him
  not the idols, for worship befits none but Him), "ما لكم من معبود يصلح أن تعبدوا سواه",
  "أفلا تخافون عقاب الله بعبادتكم شيئا دونه". Rests §3 (unnamed reading), §5 (clause gloss),
  §6 (fear-of-punishment note).
- **al-Qurtubi (90)** — USED. "يعني هودا؛ لأنه ما كانت أمة أنشئت في إثر قوم نوح إلا عاد" (Hud,
  since only 'Ad arose after Nuh's people); "وقيل: هم قوم ثمود ... يعني صالحا" (2nd view Thamud/
  Salih); evidence "فأخذتهم الصيحة" ~ "وأخذ الذين ظلموا الصيحة"; adds Madyan/Shu'ayb also seized
  by a Cry, "والله أعلم"; on minhum: "من عشيرتهم، يعرفون مولده ومنشأه ليكون سكونهم إلى قوله أكثر".
  Rests §3 (dispute + evidence), §4 (minhum).
- **as-Sa'di (91)** — USED. minhum: "من جنسهم، يعرفون نسبه وحسبه وصدقه، ليكون ذلك أسرع لانقيادهم
  ... وأبعد عن اشمئزازهم"; "فدعا إلى ما دعت إليه الرسل أممهم ... فكلهم اتفقوا على هذه الدعوة، وهي
  أول دعوة"; taqwa: "أفلا تتقون ربكم، فتجتنبوا هذه الأوثان والأصنام". Rests §4 (minhum), §5 (the
  one shared first call), §6 (taqwa of the Lord → shun idols).
- **al-Baghawi (94)** — USED. "يعني هودا وقومه. وقيل: صالحا وقومه. والأول أظهر." Rests §3 (Hud
  more apparent, 2nd view Salih).
- **al-Muyassar (16)** — USED. "هو هود عليه السلام"; "اعبدوا الله وحده ... أفلا تخافون عقابه إذا
  عبدتم غيره". Rests §3 (Hud named plainly), §6 (fear of punishment).
- **Ibn Kathir ar (14)** — USED. "أرسل فيهم رسولا منهم فدعاهم إلى عبادة الله وحده لا شريك له"
  (unnamed). Rests §3 (unnamed reading).
- **Ibn Kathir en abridged (169)** — USED. "It was said this was 'Ad ... Or ... Thamud, because
  Allah says فأخذتهم الصيحة بالحق"; "Allah sent to them a Messenger from among themselves ...
  worship Allah Alone with no partner." Rests §2 (name left open) and §3.
- **Ma'arif en (168)** — USED. Commentators concluded Hud or Salih, to 'Ad and Thamud; flags that
  both are said here to perish by Sayha though other verses tie Sayha to Thamud → some read the
  new generation as Thamud, others take Sayha as general punishment incl. 'Ad. Rests §3.

## Hadith

- **Sahih al-Bukhari, Hadith 660** (Dar-us-Salam). URL fetched:
  https://quranx.com/hadith/Bukhari/DarusSalam/Hadith-660
  First ten words used: "Seven will Allah shade in His shade on the Day..."
  Grading: Sahih (Bukhari's collection; sound by inclusion — not upgraded).
  MARKED IN-ARTICLE as a general narration on taqwa, NOT attached by any mufassir to 23:32.
  Used in §7 for the meaning of "afala tattaqun" (the fifth of the seven: "I fear Allah").

## Asbab an-nuzul

## Disagreement kept (not resolved in the article's voice)

- Identity of "them" / the messenger: Hud→'Ad (al-Qurtubi, al-Baghawi [more apparent],
  al-Muyassar) vs Salih→Thamud (2nd view in al-Qurtubi, al-Baghawi; Ma'arif) vs UNNAMED
  as a type (at-Tabari, Ibn Kathir ar; Ibn Kathir en keeps both open). Left open, as the
  verse withholds the name.
