Card and deep bilingual article for 23:32 — a messenger from
among themselves with the one unchanging call: worship Allah,
no god but Him.

Spine: the call is word-for-word Nuh's in 23:23; as-Sa'di notes
every messenger's first call was this, and all agreed on it.
Built the lesson on the single message and the mercy of a
messenger "minhum" — from their own kind (as-Sa'di, al-Qurtubi:
they knew his lineage/birth, so quicker to follow, less recoil).

Dispute kept open, not resolved: identity of the people and the
messenger — Hud/'Ad (al-Qurtubi, al-Baghawi "more apparent",
al-Muyassar) vs Salih/Thamud (2nd view in al-Qurtubi/al-Baghawi;
Ma'arif's Sayha argument) vs unnamed as a type (at-Tabari, Ibn
Kathir ar; Ibn Kathir en keeps both). Verse withholds the name.

"afala tattaqun" as the taqwa appeal: as-Sa'di reads taqwa of
the Lord that shuns the idols; at-Tabari and al-Muyassar add the
fear-of-punishment gloss. Framed as invitation, not fear alone.

Hadith: Sahih al-Bukhari 660 (the seven shaded; the man who says
"I fear Allah") quoted whole, explicitly marked a general
narration on taqwa NOT attached by any mufassir to this verse.

Licensing caution (both languages): the verse describes a past
people who refused and were destroyed; it licenses nothing
against any living person or community.

Dropped: nothing tafsir-supported cut. No sabab; verse placement
given. Neighbours 23:14/23:20/23:27 are different themes — no
heading or lesson overlap.
