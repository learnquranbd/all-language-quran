## Attribution → the Arabic (or English) clause it rests on
- al-Qurtubi (90): "أي على رجل ليس بعربي اللسان" — a man who is not Arab of tongue. (§2, §3)
- al-Baghawi (94): "جمع الأعجمي، وهو الذي لا يفصح ولا يحسن العربية إن كان عربياً في النسب، والعجمي: منسوب إلى العجم، وإن كان فصيحاً. ومعنى الآية: ولو نزلناه على رجل ليس بعربي اللسان" — the aʿjami/ʿajami distinction (unclear speech vs non-Arab stock); meaning = a man not Arab of tongue. (§2, §5)
- as-Sa'di (91): "الذين لا يفقهون لسانهم, ولا يقدرون على التعبير لهم كما ينبغي" — whose tongue [the listeners] do not grasp, and who cannot express it to them as it ought. (§2)
- at-Tabari (15): "ولو نـزلنا هذا القرآن على بعض البهائم التي لا تنطق" — glosses بعض الأعجمين as "some of the beasts that do not speak"; then the long grammatical note that أعجم at root = the inarticulate, "غير فصيح اللسان", applicable even to an Arab by lineage. (§3, §5)
- Ibn Kathir (14; confirmed in 169): "أنه لو أنزله على رجل من الأعاجم، ممن لا يدري من العربية كلمة، وأنزل عليه هذا الكتاب ببيانه وفصاحته، لا يؤمنون به" — even through a non-Arab who knew no Arabic, carrying all its clarity and eloquence, they would not believe. (§4, §6)
- al-Muyassar (16): "فقرأه على كفار قريش قراءة عربية صحيحة، لكفروا به أيضًا، وانتحلوا لجحودهم عذرًا" — even recited to Quraysh in sound Arabic they would disbelieve and invent an excuse for their denial. (§4)

## Disagreement kept (not resolved)
§3 keeps at-Tabari (word read at its widest — inarticulate creatures / dumb beasts) vs al-Qurtubi, al-Baghawi, as-Sa'di (a non-Arab human). Stated as a difference; noted both readings reach the same conclusion.

## Dropped (could not confirm or off-point for 26:198)
- Ma'arif al-Qur'an (168): its content on this group targets 26:195–196 (Qur'an is only Arabic; a translation is not Qur'an) — off-point for 198; dropped.
- The two hadiths Ma'arif relays — Maqil ibn Yasar in Mustadrak al-Hakim (Surah al-Baqarah from "adh-dhikr al-awwal"), and Ibn Mas'ud via Tabarani/Hakim/Baihaqi (Surah al-Mulk in the Torah): concern 26:196, are secondhand through Ma'arif, gradings unverified; dropped per the no-secondhand / collector's-own-grading rule.
- 26:199–201 content (he recites and they disbelieve; disbelief set in the criminals' hearts; no belief until they see the punishment): future ground per the orchestrator; not expounded. Only the independent parallel 10:96–97, which Ibn Kathir himself adduces on 198, was used.
