Card and deep bilingual article on the two gifts named in the verse:
the Book (the Torah) given to Musa, and his brother Harun appointed
as a wazir at his side. Spine: God equips those He sends with both
revelation and human support; even a prophet was not left to carry
the mission alone, and being a helper to a good cause is a dignity.

Sources (all fetched for this verse):
- al-kitab = the Torah — at-Tabari and al-Qurtubi; at-Tabari's parallel
  with the Furqan given to the Prophet.
- wazir glossed muin wa zahir (helper and backer) — at-Tabari,
  al-Baghawi; Ibn Kathir's triple muwazir/mu'ayyid/nasir, and Harun
  as "another Prophet who helped and supported him."
- al-Muyassar: Harun made a helper, both then sent to Fir'awn.
- al-Qurtubi ties to Ta Ha and cites Musa's du'a "appoint for me a
  wazir from my family" (20:29); the helper as an answered supplication.
- al-Qurtubi's grammar note: dual "Go, both of you" vs the singular
  command; an-Nahhas's objection, al-Qushayri's reconciliation.
- as-Sa'di: the 35-40 run is comfort/warning to the addressees.

Hadith: Sahih al-Bukhari 660 (the seven Allah shades, incl. two who
love each other for Allah's sake) — used in section 4 and explicitly
marked a general narration, NOT attached to the verse; no tafsir
attaches a sound hadith to 25:35 (said in one sentence).

Dropped: the wizr/wazar etymology of wazir (in no fetched tafsir).
Standing rule applied: the destroyed peoples note licenses nothing
against any living person or community.
