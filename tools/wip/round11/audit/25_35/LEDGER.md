# SOURCES — 25:35

## Tafsir fetched (all pre-fetched in this dir; none re-fetched)

- **at-Tabari (15)** — NAMED. al-kitāb = the Torah, on the clause "(مُوسَى الْكِتَابَ) يعني التوراة", with his explicit parallel "كالذي آتيناك من الفرقان" (given to Muhammad ﷺ). wazīr glossed on "وَزِيرًا يعني معينا وظهيرا" (muʿīn wa ẓahīr = helper and backer). Also the addressee = the Prophet ﷺ, warning the mushriks with earlier nations' fate (opening lines).
- **Ibn Kathir ar (14)** — NAMED. wazīr = "نبيا موازرا ومؤيدا وناصرا" (a prophet who is muwāzir / mu'ayyid / nāṣir). Frame: threatens the deniers of Quraysh, begins with Mūsā, then Fir'awn and his troops denied them both ("فكذبهما فرعون وجنوده").
- **Ibn Kathir en abridged (169)** — NAMED. Confirms the English of the above: Hārūn sent "as another Prophet who helped and supported him." Source for the run 25:36-40 read as one warning (Nūḥ drowned, ʿĀd/Thamūd/others destroyed).
- **al-Qurtubi (90)** — NAMED. al-kitāb = the Torah ("يريد التوراة"). Ties to Ṭā Hā and cites Mūsā's du'a "واجعل لي وزيرا من أهلي" (= 20:29) with the sequence "أمر موسى أولا ... قال: اذهبا إلى فرعون". Grammatical knot: dual "اذهبا" vs singular command; an-Nahhās's objection ("هذا مما لا ينبغي أن يجترأ به على كتاب الله"); al-Qushayrī's reconciliation ("إذا كانا مأمورين فكل واحد مأمور").
- **al-Baghawi (94)** — NAMED. wazīr = "معينا وظهيرا" (helper and backer); corroborates at-Tabari.
- **al-Muyassar (16)** — NAMED. Hārūn made "معينًا له" (a helper for him); then both sent to Fir'awn's people who denied → destroyed. (Group-covered 25:35,25:36.)
- **as-Sa'di (91)** — NAMED. Purpose of the story-run: to warn the addressees (35-40 block) lest what befell the past nations befall them; some see the ruins with their own eyes; what stopped belief was that they hoped for no resurrection. Used for the "licenses nothing / warning to the heart" note.
- **Ma'arif en (168)** — NOT NAMED. Only comments on 25:33 (gradual revelation), nothing specific to 25:35. Dropped for this verse.

## Hadith

- **Sahih al-Bukhari 660** (Dar-us-Salam) — fetched https://quranx.com/hadith/Bukhari/DarusSalam/Hadith-660 . First ten words: "The Prophet said, Allah will give shade, to seven, on...". The "seven whom Allah will shade" incl. "two persons who love each other only for Allah's sake." Grading: in Ṣaḥīḥ al-Bukhārī (collection-level; no separate grade given, none upgraded).
  - **Explicitly marked in the article as a GENERAL narration, NOT tafsir of the verse** — no consulted tafsir attaches a sound hadith to 25:35 (stated in one sentence in §6.1).

## Asbab an-nuzul

- None used. No established sabab for 25:35; used the verse's placement (opening of the 25:35-40 comfort/warning run) instead, per al-Qurtubi (Ṭā Hā link) and as-Sa'di.

## Dropped (could not confirm)

- The wizr / wazar etymology of wazīr (bearer of a load / refuge). Asked by the watch-point but NOT present in any fetched tafsir; rendered only the fetched glosses (muʿīn, ẓahīr, muwāzir/mu'ayyid/nāṣir). Etymology omitted rather than asserted from memory.
- No chapter:verse number attached to al-Qurtubi's quoted "go to Fir'awn, he has transgressed" (the fetched text gives no number); paraphrased without a ref. Only confirmed ref used: 20:29.

## Differentiation

- 23:45 (shipped: sending Mūsā & Hārūn with signs/authority) deliberately not reused. This draft's spine is the two gifts — the Book + Hārūn-as-wazīr (helper/support) — and the dignity of being a helper; headings and lesson are distinct.
