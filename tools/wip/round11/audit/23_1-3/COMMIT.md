deep article on the believers' success, khushu' in prayer and turning from laghw.

Sources: at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi, Ibn Kathir (ar + en), as-Sa'di,
al-Baghawi, al-Muyassar, Ma'arif al-Qur'an on 23:1, 23:2, 23:3 (t168/t169 grouped).
- Qad aflaha: qad as emphasis bringing past near present (Baghawi);
  falah glosses (Baghawi, Ibn 'Abbas, Tabari, Ibn Kathir, Sa'di, Muyassar,
  Ma'arif/Qamus); Talha ibn Musarrif's passive reading (Qurtubi).
- 'Umar's "ten verses" report: munkar per Ibn Kathir's quote of at-Tirmidhi,
  sound per Ibn al-'Arabi via al-Qurtubi; kept open. Tirmidhi 3173 checked.
- Khushu' kept as Tabari's disagreement: stillness (Mujahid, az-Zuhri, 'Ali),
  fear (al-Hasan, Ibn 'Abbas), heart ('Ali, an-Nakha'i, Qatadah, al-Hasan);
  Baghawi's wider list and khudu'/khushu' with 20:108; Tabari's "general".
- Gaze-lowering occasion: mursal per Ibn Kathir, not established.
- Obligation vs completion: Qurtubi (obligatory) vs Sa'di (valid, reward
  by the heart), with Ma'arif; 'Ubadah on khushu' lifted first (Tirmidhi 2653,
  hasan gharib).
- Laghw: batil, sins, shirk, no benefit (Tabari, Qurtubi, Baghawi, Ibn Kathir,
  Sa'di, Muyassar); the two Ibn 'Abbas glosses noted; 25:72 one line.
- Bracket of prayer 23:2/23:9 (Ibn Kathir); 23:4-11 in brief; 2:45, 87:14, 91:9.
Hadith: Bukhari 750 and 751 quoted whole, attached by al-Baghawi, marked as
not naming 23:2; Tirmidhi 2616 (hasan sahih) closing lines via as-Sa'di.

salatihim, the unsourced laghw/khushu' link, and an unverified Ibn Majah report.
