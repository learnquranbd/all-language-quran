Replaces the 653-word, 6-section article with a 9-section, 1,753-word
source-gated article (shipped card kept).

Sources: at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi, as-Sa'di, Ibn Kathir (ar, en abridged),
al-Baghawi, Muyassar, Ma'arif, all on 49:11 itself. Covers the placement
(Ma'arif: from the Prophet's rights to rights between individuals; 49:10 and
49:12 one line); sukhriya as contempt (Ibn Kathir, as-Sa'di) and "perhaps
they are better" (at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi); which mockery, kept as a
disagreement (Mujahid: rich of poor; Ibn Zayd: veiled of exposed; at-Tabari:
all of it); qawm and nisa' (al-Qurtubi, al-Baghawi, Ma'arif); the asbab as
the tafsirs give them, ungraded (Thabit ibn Qays, Banu Tamim, Ikrimah ibn
Abi Jahl, Umm Salamah, Safiyyah); lamz glosses and the hamz/lamz
disagreement (Ibn Kathir and as-Sa'di vs at-Tabari via al-Qurtubi, with
104:1, 68:11); anfusakum (at-Tabari, Ibn Kathir with 4:29, as-Sa'di);
tanabuz views and at-Tabari's general verdict; permitted names (as-Sa'di,
al-Qurtubi, Ibn al-Mubarak); bi'sa al-ism (at-Tabari vs Ibn Zayd, al-Baghawi
both, Ibn Kathir, Muyassar); repentance (at-Tabari, as-Sa'di's two kinds).

Hadith: Tirmidhi 3268 (Abu Jubayrah, hasan sahih, quoted whole; al-Qurtubi
gives "hasan"); Abu Dawud 4962 described (no grading); Muslim 2564c and
2586a quoted whole; Muslim 91 and 2564a as marked excerpts; Tirmidhi 2502
(Aisha on Safiyyah) closing words, no grading shown.

Dropped: Abu Dharr, "reproaching a repented sin" and Zamakhshari's
"beloved name" reports (no collection); the shipped Abu Turab hadith
(unsourced) and its homiletic closing section. Fixed: the shipped claim that
lamz is distinct from ghibah (at-Tabari glosses lamz as backbiting and
taunting).
