## Disagreement kept (both names, no position taken)
- §3.3: al-Qurtubi records Ibn Abbas's view that deeds are created as weighable substances; records that Ibn Furak and others
  rejected it. Presented as an unsettled question; the verse does not fix the mechanism.

## Hadith (one collection, quoted whole)
- **Jami' at-Tirmidhi 2639** (the bitāqa) — URL fetched: https://quranx.com/hadith/Tirmidhi/Hadith-2639
  First ten words (EN used): "Indeed Allah will distinguish a man from my Ummah before…"
  Operative wording quoted whole in §4.2: "the scrolls will be light, and the card will be heavy; nothing is heavier than the
  Name of Allah." Grading: **at-Tirmidhi himself said hasan gharib** (قال أبو عيسى هذا حديث حسن غريب) — reported as-is, not upgraded.
  Chosen because it is the paradigmatic mīzān scene and directly shows a light pan outweighed; fits the light-scale verse.

## Weak report deliberately NOT used
- Ibn Kathir (t14) relays via al-Bazzār from Anas a report of an angel over the Balance calling out when a scale is light; Ibn
  Kathir grades its isnād **weak** (إسناده ضعيف, داود بن المحبر متروك). Dropped, not used — the slot is filled by the sound bitāqa.
- al-Qurtubi's bitāqa/salawāt report (via al-Qushayrī) and the Hudhayfa/Jibrīl and Ādam-at-the-Balance reports: grading not
  established on the fetched page; dropped rather than cited.
