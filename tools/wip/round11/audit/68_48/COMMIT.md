Card and 8-section bilingual article for 68:48: fa-isbir li-hukmi
rabbika wa-la takun ka-sahibi al-hut idh nada wa-huwa makzum.
Neighbours 68:46-47 as context; 68:50 as a one-line pointer only.

Sources: Tabari, Ibn Kathir (ar/en), Muyassar, Qurtubi, Sa'di, Baghawi,
Ma'arif. All named.
- hukm: qada' (Qurtubi, Tabari, Baghawi); the message to convey
  (Qurtubi qila); nasr (Ibn Bahr); judgement for him against them (IK);
  respite and delayed victory (Muyassar); qadari/shar'i (Sa'di);
  abrogation by the sword verse (Qurtubi qila, unweighed).
- What "do not be like" forbids, each attributed: haste and anger
  (Qatada via Tabari and Qurtubi); anger, dajar, haste (Qurtubi);
  dajar, haste (Baghawi); leaving angry with his people (IK); lack of
  patience with his people (Muyassar, Sa'di); Tabari's consequence line.
- makzum: maghmum (Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, as-Suddi); makrub (Ata', Abu
  Malik); al-Mawardi heart/breath; mahbus (Ibn Bahr); al-Mubarrad.
- Disagreement kept: the call as the 21:87 tasbih (Qurtubi, Sa'di,
  Baghawi, IK) vs a plea to hasten punishment (Muyassar).
- Parallels only as IK cites them: 21:87-88, 37:143-144. 37:142 and
  21:87 entries pointed to, not repeated. No verdict on Yunus (AS) in
  the article's voice; licensing sentence in both languages.
Hadith: Bukhari 3412 (Abdullah, IK's Sufyan route) and 3416 (Abu
Hurayrah), each quoted whole; Muslim 2376 checked, not quoted.
Asbab: none given.
Dropped: Sa'di's "li-rabbihi" (ambiguous lam); IK's angels report (no
chain); 68:49-52 material.
- Review fix: as-Sa'di's 'mughadiban li-rabbihi' reported in his own words (as 37:142 does), not softened.
