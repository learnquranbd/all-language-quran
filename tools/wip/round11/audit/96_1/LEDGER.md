## Hadith
- Sahih al-Bukhari 3. URL https://quranx.com/hadith/Bukhari/DarusSalam/Hadith-3 (saved: hadith_Bukhari_3.txt). First ten words of the portion used (own translation of the Arabic): "The first of revelation with which the Messenger of Allah". Quoted from the opening through "wrap me, wrap me ... until the fear left him", plus Khadija's reply (§3), stated as part of a longer hadith (continues with Waraqa and the pause). Grading: al-Bukhari's inclusion in his Sahih; no other grading stated. Attached to 96:1 by al-Baghawi (Bukhari's chain), Ibn Kathir (Ahmad's route, "in the two Sahihs"), al-Qurtubi ("في الصحيحين").
- Translated from the Arabic rather than the Darussalam English because the latter inserts "(or what shall I read)?" in the third reply, which is not in the Arabic.
- Muslim 160 fetched (hadith_Muslim_160.txt) for comparison only; not quoted (one collection's wording).
- Bukhari 3's wording stops at 96:3; the article says so and notes Ahmad's route (Ibn Kathir) and al-Baghawi's second route reach 96:5.
