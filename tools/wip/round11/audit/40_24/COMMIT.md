Card and 8-section bilingual article for 40:24: ila Fir'awna wa Hamana wa
Qaruna fa-qalu sahirun kadhdhab, completing 40:23.

Sources: Ibn Kathir (ar + en abridged, grouped 40:23-27), Tabari, Qurtubi,
Sa'di, Muyassar, Ma'arif (grouped 40:24-27). Baghawi fetched (verse text
only), not named.
- Identities only as the texts give them: Fir'awn king of the Copts / of
  Egypt; Haman his minister (wazir: IK ar, Muyassar, Qurtubi, Sa'di;
  "adviser" in IK en); Qarun richest in wealth and trade (IK), owner of
  wealth and treasures (Muyassar, Qurtubi), of Musa's people who
  transgressed with his wealth (Sa'di = wording of 28:76).
- Why the three: Qurtubi's madar at-tadbir (axis of the planning against
  Musa), and Qarun joined because his deed was like theirs. Sa'di (all
  rejected harshly) and Muyassar (denied and grew arrogant) as complements.
- Glosses: Tabari (sahir = the staff seen as a serpent; kadhdhab = lying
  about Allah's sending him); IK ar mumakhriq, mumawwih; IK en adds madman;
  Qurtubi: unable to counter, they carried the miracles over to sorcery.
- 51:52-53 cross-ref from Ibn Kathir, verified in data/quran-json.
- Consolation framing: IK en, Ma'arif. kadhdhab echoed in 40:28.
- 28:76, 29:39, 40:44 (shipped) one-line pointers only; 40:25-26 one line;
  40:27 not pulled forward.
- Licensing sentence in both languages (no living group tied to the three).
Hadith: none attached by any fetched tafsir; stated in one sentence.
Dropped: Ma'arif's siddiqin hadith and the believer's names (40:28/44
ground), IK en's Abu Musa refuge hadith (40:27), Qatadah/7:129 (40:25).
Asbab: none.
Themes: patience, character.
