Card and 8-section bilingual article for 45:32: wa idha qila inna
wa'da Allahi haqqun wa as-sa'atu la rayba fiha, qultum ma nadri ma
as-sa'atu, in nazunnu illa zannan wa ma nahnu bi-mustayqinin. Framed as
the continuation of the 45:31 address on the Day; 45:27 one pointer;
45:33-35 not developed.

Sources: Tabari, Qurtubi, Sa'di, Ibn Kathir (ar + en 45:31-32 lines),
Baghawi, Muyassar. Ma'arif's group text covers only 45:28; not named.
- Frame: Tabari "it will be said to them then"; Sa'di "rebuked also",
  munkirin; Ibn Kathir (en) admonished on 45:31.
- Speaker: the believers (Ibn Kathir) vs unnamed (Tabari, Muyassar).
- Readings: Tabari names the nominative for Medina, Basra and some Kufa,
  the accusative for the general Kufan readers, and accepts both;
  Qurtubi and Baghawi name Hamza for the accusative. Qurtubi's
  nominative-on-position note and his rule against the masdar pronoun.
- Ma nadri: Tabari's takdhib vs Qurtubi's "true or false"; unranked.
- In nazunnu illa zannan: Qurtubi's three taqdirs (al-Mubarrad first),
  unranked; Ibn Kathir marjuh; Baghawi hadsan wa tawahhuman. No
  own-voice grammar.
- Own-voice textual links to 45:24 (yazunnun) and 45:26 (la rayba),
  marked as not drawn by the commentaries.
Hadith: none attached by any tafsir; said in one sentence.
Asbab: none in the sources; not used.
Licensing sentence in both languages; questions and doubts today
explicitly distinguished from the condemned reply.
Dropped: Ma'arif 45:28 material, Ibn Kathir (en) 45:33-37 hadiths.
- Gate: one EN "one" number-parity wording fixed; Qurtubi gloss word
  count corrected (two words, al-ba'thu ka'in).
