# SOURCES ledger — 33:18

## Tafsir fetched (all from PREP, not re-fetched)
- **at-Tabari (15)** — USED. Arabic clauses rested on:
  - muʿawwiqīn: "الذين يعوّقون الناس منكم عن رسول الله... فيصدّونهم عنه، وعن شهود الحرب معه، نفاقا منهم، وتخذيلا عن الإسلام وأهله".
  - halumma: "أي تعالوا إلينا، ودعوا محمدا، فلا تشهدوا معه مشهده، فإنا نخاف عليكم الهلاك بهلاكه".
  - lā yaʾtūna al-baʾs: "لا يشهدون الحرب والقتال إن شهدوا إلا تعذيرا، ودفعا عن أنفسهم المؤمنين".
  - Qatada's "aklat raʾs" morsel report: "ما محمد وأصحابه إلا أكلة رأس... دعوا هذا الرجل فإنه هالك".
  - Ibn Zayd scene (roast meat/bread/nabidh, brother goes to report, finds Jibril already brought the verse): "هذا يوم الأحزاب، انصرف رجل من عند رسول الله... فوجده قد نـزل جبرائيل عليه السلام بخبره".
- **al-Qurtubi (90)** — USED. Clauses:
  - muʿawwiqīn = al-muʿtariḍīn; root "عاقني عن كذا أي صرفني عنه"; "عوّق على التكثير"; "العوق المنع والصرف".
  - halumma linguistic note: "على لغة أهل الحجاز. وغيرهم يقولون هلموا للجماعة، وهلمي للمرأة... الأصل (ها)... ومعنى (هلم) أقبل".
  - "هؤلاء طائفتان" (two groups).
  - three views on the callers: (1) munāfiqūn ("أكلة رأس"); (2) "اليهود من بني قريظة... هلم إلينا... فارقوا محمدا"; (3) Ibn Zayd's two-brother scene (also ascribed to al-Mawardi & ath-Thaʿlabi). Muqatil: "هم عبد الله بن أبي وأصحابه المنافقون".
  - lā yaʾtūna al-baʾs: "خوفا من الموت. وقيل: لا يحضرون القتال إلا رياء وسمعة".
- **as-Sa'di (91)** — USED. "توَّعد... وتهددهم" (threat & warning); muʿawwiqīn "عن الخروج"; halumma "أي ارجعوا" linked to 33:13 "فارجعوا"; "أشد الناس حرصًا على التخلف" (lacking faith/patience, having nifāq/cowardice).
- **al-Baghawi (94)** — USED. muʿawwiqīn = al-muthabbiṭīn; halumma "ارجعوا إلينا، ودعوا محمدا"; Qatada morsel report; Muqatil's fuller account (Jews send to munāfiqūn: "أنتم إخواننا وجيراننا هلموا إلينا"; Ibn Ubayy discourages believers; "فلم يزدد المؤمنون... إلا إيمانا واحتسابا"); baʾs = al-ḥarb; "رياء وسمعة... ولو كان ذلك القليل لله لكان كثيرا".
- **al-Muyassar (16)** — USED. muthabbiṭīn ʿan al-jihād; halumma "تعالوا وانضموا إلينا واتركوا محمدا"; lā yaʾtūna "إلا نادرًا؛ رياء وسمعة وخوف الفضيحة".
- **Ibn Kathir ar (14)** — USED (brief). halumma = "إلى ما نحن فيه من الإقامة في الظلال والثمار" (the shade and fruit).
- **Ma'arif al-Qur'an en (168, grouped 33:13-22)** — USED for placement: two groups of hypocrites outwardly in the jihad; 33:13 "O people of Yathrib".
- **Ibn Kathir en abridged (169, covers 33:18-19)** — USED for the 33:19 continuation: halumma = shade & fruits; ashiḥḥa = no love/mercy (as-Suddi: over booty); rolling eyes = depth of cowards' terror; salaqūkum = sharp tongues (Ibn Abbas: run you down in talk); Qatada: "give us, give us" at booty but cowardly in battle; deeds made worthless.

## Hadith
- **Sahih al-Bukhari 33** — signs of a hypocrite are three (lies / breaks promise / betrays trust). Narr. Abu Huraira.
  - URL fetched: https://quranx.com/hadith/Bukhari/DarusSalam/Hadith-33
  - First ten words (EN): "The Prophet said, The signs of a hypocrite are three"
  - First ten words (AR): "آية المنافق ثلاث إذا حدث كذب وإذا وعد أخلف"
  - Grading: Sahih (Bukhari). **Explicitly marked in the article as a general narration NOT attached to 33:18** — no tafsir fetched attaches a sound verse-specific hadith, and the article states this in one sentence.

## Asbab an-nuzul
- Not written as a formal asbāb. The Ibn Zayd scene (two brothers, Jibril bringing the verse) is reported as a tafsir narration by at-Tabari and al-Qurtubi (attrib. al-Mawardi/ath-Thaʿlabi) and is presented as the third of three disputed views, not as an established sabab. The verse's placement during the Battle of the Trench (al-Ahzab) is given instead.

## Disagreement kept (no position taken)
Who the "those who say come to us" are: (1) the munāfiqūn [Qatada; Muqatil names Abdullah b. Ubayy]; (2) the Jews of Banu Qurayza calling the hypocrites [al-Qurtubi, Baghawi via Muqatil]; (3) Ibn Zayd's single-scene two-brother report. Presented as three recorded views.

## Sensitivity handled
Section 8 states in both languages that the verse describes a particular behaviour at a particular siege, leaves the final verdict to Allah (33:24), and licenses nothing against any named living person, group or community, nor any weaponised charge of hypocrisy; closes as a mirror for the reader's own resolve.

## Dropped (could not confirm / out of scope)
- The exact name of the Companion/brother in Ibn Zayd's scene — not given in the fetched texts; left unnamed.
- "nabidh/drink" rendered as neutral "drink / পানীয়" rather than a claim about its nature.
- No attempt to map muʿawwiqūn onto any later or living group — deliberately excluded per sensitivity.
