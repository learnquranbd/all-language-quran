Card and 9-section bilingual article for 83:7: kalla inna kitaba
al-fujjari la-fi sijjin, six Arabic words answering 83:4-6. 83:8-9 and
83:10-17 left for their own entries; 83:21 cited once (via Qurtubi).

Sources: Tabari, Ibn Kathir (ar; en abridged), Muyassar, Qurtubi,
Sa'di, Baghawi, Ma'arif. No re-fetch.
- Kalla: deterrent, speech complete (Baghawi, Qurtubi's grammarians) /
  haqqan (al-Hasan; Ibn Kathir) / "will you not believe", pause after
  83:6 (Ibn Abbas via Qurtubi). Kept open; 83:14 cross-referenced.
- Kitab: record of deeds (Tabari, Baghawi, Ma'arif, Muqatil) vs
  destination and abode (Ibn Kathir ar, Muyassar). None chosen.
- Al-fujjar: every fajir, kafir, munafiq, fasiq (Sa'di) vs the
  deniers of resurrection (Tabari).
- Sijjin: seventh earth (Tabari's choice), rock (Mujahid, Kalbi,
  Yahya b. Sallam), by Iblis (hadd/khadd/jund across the three texts),
  open pit, loss (Ikrima), parable, fa'il from sijn = narrowness,
  sijjil origin. Qushayri's buried book vs 83:21.
- Ibn Kathir: 95:5-6, 25:13; 83:9 describes the recorded destination,
  not sijjin (al-Qurazi).
Licensing sentence in both languages; no own-voice claim on the unseen.
Hadith: al-Bara' long narration dropped. Abu Dawud 4753 checked: long,
and its wording has no sijjin sentence; not quoted. Abu Hurayra
"open pit" report unconfirmed; said so. No asbab.
Dropped: Anas's reports, Ka'b's black rock, Baghawi's marfu' on
Illiyyun, Ma'arif's Hell-location reports, Abdullah b. Amr/Umar name
clash; 17:71, 69:25, 84:10 (no fetched tafsir cites them).
Fix: spelling-variant claim reduced to 'not rendered; texts as fetched disagree' (possible artefact).
