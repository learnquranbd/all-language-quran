## Disagreements kept (unranked)
- kalla: rebuttal of the denial of raising (Tabari, Muyassar; Ibn Kathir as cause of the
  denial) vs Qurtubi's Ibn 'Abbas report (Abu Jahl not believing the Qur'an's tafsir/bayan,
  tied to 75:19) vs his qila (they neither pray nor give zakat, Makkan disbelievers).
- Addressee: Makkan disbelievers / idolaters (Qurtubi, Baghawi, Muyassar) vs Tabari's
  "servants addressed by this Qur'an who prefer..." with "ayyuha an-nas", and Sa'di's grounding
  in human nature. Presented as difference of scope.
- Readings: ta (Madinah, Kufah; Abu 'Ubayd's choice with his caveat) vs ya (the rest; Abu Hatim).
