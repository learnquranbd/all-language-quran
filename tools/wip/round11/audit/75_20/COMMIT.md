Card and 8-section bilingual article for 75:20, kalla bal tuhibbuna
al-'ajila, read with 75:21 (wa-tadharuna al-akhira) as its other half.
75:16-19 named only as the parenthesis it follows; 75:22-25 left to the
parallel 75:23 draft, sudā (75:36) untouched.

Sources: Tabari (75:20 entry is 75:19 material; his comment taken from
the 75:21 entry), Qurtubi (75:20, 75:21), Baghawi (75:21), Sa'di, Ibn
Kathir (ar 75:20, 75:21; en grouped 75:16-25), Muyassar, Ma'arif (en
grouped 75:19-21). All named.
- kalla kept as a disagreement: rebuttal of the denial of raising
  (Tabari, Muyassar; Ibn Kathir: love of the immediate drives the denial)
  vs Qurtubi's chainless Ibn 'Abbas report (Abu Jahl and the Qur'an's
  bayan, 75:19) vs his qila (no prayer, no zakat).
- Addressee: Makkan disbelievers/idolaters (Qurtubi, Baghawi, Muyassar)
  vs Tabari's "O people" to those addressed by the Qur'an who prefer this
  world, and Sa'di's "man is fond of the immediate". Scope, not ranked.
- Readings tuhibbuna/yuhibbuna (Qurtubi, Baghawi): Madinah and Kufah with
  ta, the rest with ya; Abu 'Ubayd's choice and caveat, Abu Hatim's; ya
  back to 75:13, ta as direct rebuke with 76:27.
- al-'ajila vs al-akhira framed as ithar and tark (Tabari, Sa'di, Ibn
  Kathir's himma), not as condemning ordinary life; 87:16-17 as the
  Qur'an's own ranking.
Hadith: none attached; said in one sentence. Ibn Kathir's Ibn 'Abbas
narration named only as belonging to 75:16-19.
Asbab: none in the fetched texts. Licensing paragraph in EN and BN.
Dropped: any commentator link between li-ta'jala (75:16) and al-'ajila
(none found; root shared noted as text only), Tabari's 75:19 material.
