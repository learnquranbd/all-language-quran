Card and 8-section bilingual article for 74:46, the fourth of the four
admissions of those in Saqar (74:43-46). 74:43-45 kept as context; the
pledge lesson of 74:38 is not repeated.

Sources: Tabari, Qurtubi, Sa'di, Baghawi (ar, 74:46); Tabari, Qurtubi,
Sa'di on 74:47; Muyassar (grouped 74:39-47); Ibn Kathir en (grouped
74:38-47); quranx for Jalalayn, Tanwir al-Miqbas and Maududi. Ibn Kathir
ar failed to fetch; Ma'arif's grouped text has nothing on 74:46.
- yawm al-din glossed as each source gives it: requital, reward and
  punishment (Tabari); Resurrection, requital and judgement (Qurtubi);
  reckoning and requital (Muyassar); Resurrection and Requital
  (Jalalayn); place of requital and manifest just rule (Sa'di).
- takdhib read through its opposite, tasdiq (Tabari, Qurtubi); kana
  negated in 74:43-44, affirmed in 74:45-46 (habitual).
- Sa'di: denial is the effect of plunging into falsehood (74:45 -> 74:46).
- al-yaqin (74:47): death (Tabari, Qurtubi, Sa'di, Ibn Kathir, Muyassar)
  vs death and the Hereafter (Maududi); kept unranked.
Hadith: Bukhari 1243 (Umm al-'Ala', 'Uthman ibn Maz'un), Prophet's reply
quoted whole from the page English, rest described; Ibn Kathir cites it
for yaqin = death. Marked as not attached to 74:46; no fetched tafsir
attaches a hadith to this verse.
Licensing sentence in EN and BN; no judgement on any living person.
Asbab: none; placement given.
Dropped: fiqh of non-believers and the branches of law (no fetched source
raises it); Maududi's 74:43 prayer note (context verse only).
