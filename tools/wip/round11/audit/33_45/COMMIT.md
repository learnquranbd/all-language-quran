Card and deep bilingual article for 33:45 (al-Ahzab), the verse that names
the Prophet's ﷺ mission: shāhid, mubashshir, nadhīr — witness, bringer of
glad tidings, warner. Framed in a surah of trial, where the defined mission
steadies the believers; 33:46 (dāʿī ilā Allāh, sirāj munīr) named to complete
the five attributes but weight kept on the three.

Sources gave:
- shāhid read three ways and kept so: at-Tabari (witness over the ummah by
  conveyance), as-Sa'di (witness over their deeds, good and evil; "a just,
  accepted witness"), Ibn Kathir (witness to Allah's Oneness + against mankind's
  deeds on the Day), anchored in 4:41 and 2:143.
- Disagreement kept as a disagreement: object of shāhid — his own ummah's deeds
  (Tabari/as-Sa'di) vs. witness for the earlier messengers that they conveyed
  (Qatada via Tabari and al-Qurtubi; al-Baghawi plainly). No position taken.
- mubashshir/nadhīr balance: at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi, as-Sa'di (mubashshar = the
  believing muttaqin; mundhar = the wrongdoers), Ibn Kathir.
- 33:46: Ibn Kathir and al-Muyassar on the caller and the lamp (clear as the
  sun; none denies but the stubborn), tied back to 33:43.
- al-Qurtubi on the Prophet's ﷺ many names held in this verse.

Hadith: Sahih al-Bukhari 2125 (ʿAbdullah ibn ʿAmr; the Torah description that
quotes the three words verbatim), quoted whole, graded sahih, not upgraded.
Ibn Abbas report (ʿAli and Muʿadh to Yemen) given as what Ibn Kathir and
al-Qurtubi report.

Dropped: the Abu Saʿid witnessing hadith (kept to one quoted hadith; its sense
carried via Ibn Kathir/as-Sa'di); Wahb ibn Munabbih's Isaiah narration;
precise name-counts softened to "dozens"/"many". Not a condemnation verse — no
licensing sentence needed.
