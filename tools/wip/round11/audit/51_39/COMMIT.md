Card and 8-section bilingual article on fa-tawalla bi-ruknihi wa qala
sahirun aw majnun. 51:38 as context; 51:40 a pointer only.

Sources: at-Tabari, Ibn Kathir (ar; en abridged for 51:38 and 22:9),
al-Qurtubi, as-Sa'di, al-Baghawi, al-Muyassar, Ma'arif al-Qur'an.
- Bi-ruknihi kept open, each holder named, none chosen: his gathered
  forces (Ibn Zayd with 11:80; al-Baghawi; Ma'arif; at-Tabari's own
  gloss); his supporters (Mujahid, in at-Tabari's and Ibn Kathir's
  wordings); his strength (Ibn Abbas and Qatada per al-Qurtubi, vs
  at-Tabari's Ibn Abbas "his people" with the narrator's doubt and
  Qatada "overpowered his people"); his side (al-Akhfash, al-Mu'arrij,
  17:83, 41:51; as-Sa'di); himself (unnamed wa qila). Ibn Kathir calls
  arrogant turning away (22:9) the strong meaning.
- Aw kept as a disagreement: either-or (Ibn Kathir, as-Sa'di) vs aw =
  wa because both were said (Abu 'Ubayda via at-Tabari and al-Baghawi;
  al-Mu'arrij and al-Farra' via al-Qurtubi, with 76:24 and 4:3).
- As-Sa'di: they knew Musa (AS) was truthful (27:14, 17:102).
Licensing paragraph in both languages; no modern mapping.
Hadith: none attached by a fetched tafsir (the saba/dabur narration in
Ibn Kathir's abridged English belongs to 51:41). Asbab: none used.
Dropped: 26:34-37, 40:24, 7:109 and 51:52 (not cited by a fetched
tafsir); "aw" as Pharaoh's own doubt (no source).
