Assigned 2:246, widened to 2:246-248 (the chiefs ask their prophet for a
king; Talut appointed and objected to; the sign of the ark). 2:243-245 left
free; 2:249-251 ships, so the story stops at the ark. New card and a
1,730-word, 9-section article on two things: leadership earned by
knowledge and capacity, and a community that asks for a hard command and
turns from it.

Sources: at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi, Ibn Kathir (ar + en abridged), as-Sa'di,
al-Baghawi, al-Muyassar, Ma'arif on 2:246, 2:247 and 2:248 (all slots on target).
- Licensing sentence in both languages (the chiefs, and those who turned
  away when fighting was prescribed; nothing against any living person).
- Talut's tribe, trade and poverty only as Ibn Kathir, at-Tabari and
  al-Qurtubi report them; as-Sa'di on the "corrupt assumption" behind it.
- Leadership: Ibn Kathir's rule, as-Sa'di's judgement-plus-strength,
  al-Qurtubi's "a root for the qualities of the imam", at-Tabari's
  "kingship is not inherited from fathers".
Disagreements kept: the unnamed prophet (Shamwil: Mujahid, Wahb, at-Tabari,
"the rest" per al-Baghawi; Sham'un: as-Suddi; Yusha': Qatadah, called
far-fetched by Ibn Kathir and weak by Ibn 'Atiyyah; Muqatil, al-Muhasibi;
Ma'arif: Samuel), not settled. Knowledge as war only (al-Kalbi) vs general
(al-Qurtubi); revelation to Talut, left open; body as height vs courage;
who speaks "Allah gives His kingship"; sign asked for (at-Tabari) or
unasked (Ibn 'Atiyyah); sakinah (Qatadah, ar-Rabi', 'Ata' preferred by
at-Tabari); baqiyyah unranked by at-Tabari (no certain report); carried
vs driven on a cart (at-Tabari prefers carried).
Hadith: Muslim 1741 (Abu Hurayrah, "do not wish to meet the enemy"),
joined to the verse by al-Qurtubi, marked general. No asbab.
Dropped: all isra'iliyyat on the sakinah's form and the ark's travels (one
sentence, with Ibn 'Atiyyah's "sound view"), al-Baghawi's slur on the tribe
of Binyamin, at-Tabari's "rebuke to the Jews" reading, Ma'arif's five cities.
