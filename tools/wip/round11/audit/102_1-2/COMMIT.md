Replaces the 757-word, 6-section article with a 1,711-word, 8-section deep article, built only on tafsir fetched for 102:1 and 102:2 (shipped card kept).

Sources: at-Tabari, Ibn Kathir (ar), al-Qurtubi, as-Sa'di, al-Baghawi,
al-Muyassar, Ma'arif al-Qur'an; abridged English Ibn Kathir as cross-check.

alhakum: busied / made forget (al-Qurtubi, Imru' al-Qays), with the object
supplied by at-Tabari, al-Baghawi, Ibn Kathir and as-Sa'di.
takathur as mukathara (al-Qurtubi, as-Sa'di, al-Baghawi); the field kept as a
disagreement: wealth and children (Ibn 'Abbas, al-Hasan), tribes (Qatada),
trade (ad-Dahhak, 'Amr ibn Dinar); at-Tabari leans to wealth from the hadith,
as-Sa'di and al-Qurtubi make it general.
Asbab as conflicting reports: 'Abd Manaf and Sahm counting their dead
(al-Baghawi, al-Qurtubi), Banu Haritha and al-Harith (Ibn Kathir), the Jews
(Qatada); Makkan per all, Madinan per al-Bukhari (al-Qurtubi). Licensing
sentence in both languages.
hatta zurtum, three readings held: death and burial (at-Tabari, Ibn Kathir's
"correct"), counting the dead and a threat (al-Qurtubi's qila). The visit
image: 'Umar ibn 'Abd al-'Aziz to Maymun ibn Mihran and a Bedouin (Ibn Kathir),
al-Qurtubi, as-Sa'di on the barzakh.
Hadith: Muslim 2958, Bukhari 6439, 6440 (Ubayy), 6514; Tirmidhi 3355 ('Ali,
gharib) and 1054 (visiting graves, hasan sahih). No fetched tafsir attaches
a hadith to an-na'im; said in one sentence.
Dropped from the old article: the dates-and-water hadith (no source for 102:8),
57:20, 69:51 and the degrees of certainty, the perfect-tense point.
