## Watch-point correction
- The "visitor must move on" report is from 'Umar ibn 'Abd al-'Aziz (to Maymun ibn Mihran), not 'Ali; plus an unnamed Bedouin ("resurrection, by the Lord of the Ka'ba"). 'Ali's report concerns doubting the punishment of the grave. Written as the text has it.
