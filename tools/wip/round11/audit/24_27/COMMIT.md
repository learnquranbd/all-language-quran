Card and bilingual article on the etiquette of privacy: seeking
leave (isti'dhan) and salam before entering another's home. Built
on the surah's turn from punishing slander to guarding the home;
Ma'arif places isti'dhan next to the adultery/slander penalties.

Sources (fetched, per verse):
- as-Sa'di: home is to a person what a garment is to the body's
  awrah; unannounced entry breeds suspicion.
- Ma'arif: home = sakan (16:80); four wisdoms; the rule binds all
  alike (man/woman, mahram/non-mahram).
- Ibn Kathir + Muqatil b. Hayyan: what Jahiliyya entry replaced.

Disagreements kept open (no position):
- Gloss of tastanisu: isti'dhan (Malik/Ibn Abbas) vs announce
  yourself by tanahnuh (Mujahid, Ibn Zayd, al-Khalil); Tabari's
  synthesis reported as his own.
- Reading: reported Ibn Abbas/Ubayy/A'mash "tasta'dhinu" + the
  scribe-error claim, against al-Qurtubi's rejection defending the
  Uthmanic "tastanisu" (15:9). Both stated; received text noted.
- Order: salam-first (majority) vs leave-first; Mawardi's rule.

Hadith: Sahih al-Bukhari 6241 (permission ordained for the sake of
sight), quoted whole, grading not upgraded; matches as-Sa'di's own
citation. Three-times, don't-face-the-door (Abu Dawud only), and
"Ana ana" carried as commentator-cited, not the verse slot.

Asbab: Adi b. Thabit (the Ansari woman) reported, attributed, not
made decisive. 24:28-29 left as future ground.

Licensing sentence added: casts no suspicion on women as a class,
licenses nothing against any person or community.
No overlap with shipped 24:2 / 24:22 / 24:35 / 24:39.
