Card and 8-section bilingual article on hadha nadhirun mina n-nudhuri
l-ula. 53:50-55 as context; 53:57 a pointer only.

Sources: at-Tabari, Ibn Kathir (ar; en abridged, grouped 53:56-62),
al-Qurtubi, as-Sa'di, al-Baghawi, al-Muyassar, Ma'arif al-Qur'an.
- Who is "this": the Prophet ﷺ (Ibn Kathir, al-Baghawi, Muyassar,
  as-Sa'di; Ibn Jurayj and Muhammad ibn Ka'b per al-Qurtubi; Qatadah
  and Abu Ja'far per at-Tabari) vs the Qur'an (Qatadah per al-Qurtubi);
  Ma'arif holds both open. Qatadah reported both ways; left unreconciled.
- "Of the earlier warners": one of their kind, sent as they were sent
  (Ibn Kathir min jinsihim, with 46:9; Muyassar/Sa'di "not new among
  the messengers"; Tabari's "one of the children of Adam") vs this
  warning is of the warnings in the scrolls of Ibrahim and Musa (Abu
  Malik, in at-Tabari and al-Qurtubi; al-Qurtubi's wa-qila nudhur =
  indhar). At-Tabari's own preference for Abu Malik reported as his;
  the article adopts neither.
- Al-Qurtubi via as-Suddi/Abu Salih: 53:36 to 53:56 all in the scrolls.
- Sa'di's questions; Ibn Jurayj/Ibn Ka'b "obey and succeed, or else".
Licensing sentence in both languages (deniers, 53:50-54 peoples).
Hadith: none attached to 53:56 alone. Ibn Kathir (en) ties "I am the
naked warner" to the warner and 53:57; quoted whole from Bukhari 6482
(Abu Musa), graded as in al-Bukhari's Sahih.
Asbab: none. Pointers: 46:9, 34:46 (both Ibn Kathir-cited), 53:57.
Dropped: Ahmad's small-sins report (not attached, not in hadith.js),
35:24 and 87:18-19 (no fetched tafsir cites them), Abu Ja'far's identity.
Bukhari 6482 quoted whole, linked only via Ibn Kathir's quotation of the phrase 'naked warner' (stated in the article); 53:55 link marked as no source's.
