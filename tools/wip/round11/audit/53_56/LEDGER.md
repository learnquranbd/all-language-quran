## Hadith

- **Sahih al-Bukhari 6482** (Abu Musa). URL: https://quranx.com/hadith/Bukhari/DarusSalam/Hadith-6482 (fetched via hadith.js, saved in this folder). First ten words of the wording used: "My example and the example of the message with which". Quoted whole from the Dar-us-Salam English. Grading: in al-Bukhari's Sahih; nothing added.
  - Attachment: Ibn Kathir (en abridged, tafsir 169) quotes only the phrase «أنا النذير العريان», no collection named, in the grouped commentary on 53:56-62, tying it to the meaning of "warner" and to 53:57. The article says this exactly and states that no fetched tafsir attaches a narration to 53:56 alone. **Orchestrator to decide** whether this attachment is strong enough; if not, section 6 paras 2-3 can be cut to the one-sentence "none attached" statement.
  - Muslim 2283 also fetched (same narration, different wording); not used, to keep one collection's wording.
- Imam Ahmad, Sahl ibn Sa'd, "Beware of small sins" (Ibn Kathir en): mentioned in one sentence as present and left out; not attached to this verse, Ahmad not available in hadith.js; wording not quoted.
