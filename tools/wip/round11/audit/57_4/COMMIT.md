Replaces the 773-word, 6-section article with a 1,782-word, 8-section one
(shipped card kept), from at-Tabari, Ibn Kathir (ar and en), al-Qurtubi,
as-Sa'di, the Muyassar and Ma'arif al-Qur'an on 57:4. Slots checked;
al-Baghawi gives only the verse text.
Placement: 57:1-3 and 57:5-6 (Ibn Kathir en on the dominion and night/day),
57:7 and 57:16 one line each.
Six days: at-Tabari's ansha'a, Ibn Kathir and Muyassar's "and what is between
them", as-Sa'di's Sunday to Friday; Ibn Kathir and al-Qurtubi refer to al-A'raf.
Istawa and ma'iyya only in the tafsirs' words: at-Tabari (rose and was exalted;
witness to you while on His Throne), Muyassar and as-Sa'di (befitting His
majesty; with-ness of knowledge), al-Qurtubi (power, authority, knowledge),
Ibn Kathir (watcher and witness, with 11:5 and 13:10), as-Sa'di with 58:7,
Ma'arif (its nature beyond grasp).
The four movements with each tafsir's list (seed, rain, plants, trees,
animals, snow, decrees, provision, angels, souls, supplications, deeds),
6:59 and the angel with each raindrop from Ibn Kathir.
Disagreement kept: at-Tabari, Muyassar and as-Sa'di hold both clauses as
stated vs al-Qurtubi (outward reading contradictory, ta'wil required, Abu
al-Ma'ali's Yunus remark). No position taken.
Basir: at-Tabari with 45:22, Muyassar, as-Sa'di (deeds and their source).
Hadith: Bukhari 50 (ihsan exchange), as a general narration Ibn Kathir cites.
His two gharib reports named as such, not relied on; Imam Ahmad's two lines.
Dropped: the ijma'-against-hulul remark and the "special" ma'iyya (not in the
fetched texts), 34:2, 9:40, 16:128, 2:255.
Defect fixed: the old article said the commentators were "settled" that the
ma'iyya is knowledge and "not a mixing"; al-Qurtubi argues otherwise and no
fetched text has the phrase. Its deliberateness and 34:2 points had no source.
