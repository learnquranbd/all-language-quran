Card and deep bilingual article on the parting of the sea: the command
to strike, and each parted portion standing like a great mountain.

Spine: deliverance comes after the certain word ("inna maʿiya rabbi",
26:61-62) and after the obedient step (the command to strike) — the road
opens at the dead end, when God's help meets the servant's act.

Sources (all fetched for 26:63):
- al-Qurtubi: the sign is tied to an act Moses performs; the staff of
  itself parts nothing except by the power of God joined to it.
- at-Tabari + Ibn Kathir (Ibn Ishaq): "in it was the authority of God
  He had given him"; the means and the Giver kept distinct (cf. 20:77).
- Twelve paths on the number of the tribes: Ibn Abbas, as-Suddi (Tabari),
  al-Qurtubi, as-Saʿdi, al-Muyassar. as-Suddi's arches/windows and the
  wind drying the sea bed via Ibn Kathir.
- "kaṭ-ṭawd al-ʿaẓīm": mountain-wall reading (Ibn Masʿud, Ibn Abbas,
  Muhammad b. Kaʿb, ad-Dahhak, Qatada) vs ʿAta al-Khurasani's "pass
  between two mountains" — kept as a disagreement.
- Maʿarif al-Qurʾan: the cave of Thawr parallel and the maʿiya/maʿana
  pronoun shift (9:40) for the trust theme.

Hadith: Sahih al-Bukhari 2004 (Ibn Abbas) — the day God rescued Bani
Israel; the Prophet ﷺ fasted ʿAshura, "we have more claim to Moses."
Quoted whole, grading not upgraded.

Also kept: the two merchants who leapt in trusting the command
(al-Qurtubi from Malik); Yushaʿ wading in (al-Baghawi, at-Tabari); the
disagreement over who struck first (as-Suddi: Aaron, vs Moses alone).

Bridged lightly to 26:64-67 without narrating the drowning; one sober
line that the account licenses nothing against any living community.

Dropped (in ledger): the Abu-Khalid kunya reports and the ifkal detail
(sober tone); the horseman plunging in (length); Pharaoh's body ashore
(belongs to 26:64-66).
