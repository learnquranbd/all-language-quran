## Hadith
- Sahih al-Bukhari 2004 (Dar-us-Salam), Ibn Abbas. URL fetched: https://quranx.com/hadith/Bukhari/DarusSalam/Hadith-2004 (saved locally). First ten words of wording used: "The Prophet came to Medina and saw the Jews fasting". Grading: Sahih (in Sahih al-Bukhari). Quoted whole in §8, one collection only. Directly attached to this verse's deliverance (the day God rescued Bani Israel from their enemy).

## Asbab an-nuzul
- Not used. This is a narrative (qasas) verse in the Musa cycle; no occasion-of-revelation is at issue. Placement (deliverance after 26:61-62 certainty) written instead.

## Disagreements kept (both named, no position taken)
- Who struck the sea first: as-Suddi (in at-Tabari) — Aaron struck first and it refused, until Moses came; other chains — Moses alone, at Yushaʿ's / the concealed believer's prompting. → §7.
- Sense of al-tawd: the mountain wall (Ibn Masʿud, Ibn Abbas, ad-Dahhak, Qatada et al.) vs the pass between two mountains (ʿAta al-Khurasani). → §6.

## Dropped (could not confirm / out of scope)
- The kunya reports ("part for me, Abu Khalid, bi-hawlillah"; trembling/ifkal seizing the sea) in at-Tabari and Ibn Kathir — israʾiliyyat-flavoured detail, not load-bearing; dropped to keep the tone sober.
- The concealed believer / horseman plunging into the sea (Ibn Jurayj, Baghawi) — only Yushaʿ's wading kept in §7 as the confirmation example; the rest dropped for length.
- Qurtubi's tail on Pharaoh's body cast ashore — belongs to the drowning (26:64-66), a separate target; not narrated here.
