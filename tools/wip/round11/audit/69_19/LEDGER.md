## Hadith
- USED: Sahih al-Bukhari 2441 (Ibn Umar, najwa). URL https://quranx.com/hadith/Bukhari/DarusSalam/Hadith-2441 (saved hadith_Bukhari_2441.txt). Attached by Ibn Kathir (t14, t169: "in the Sahih"). First ten words used: "Allah brings the believer near, places His cover over him". Translated from al-Bukhari's Arabic, quoted whole from the Prophet's words to the end (split over §6.3 and §6.4); the Safwan b. Muhriz frame is summarised as "he said". Grading: in al-Bukhari's Sahih; no further grading stated. Noted in article: Ibn Kathir's quotation adds بيمينه; al-Bukhari's wording has only فيعطى كتاب حسناته.
- PARAPHRASED, NOT QUOTED, NO NUMBER: Ibn Kathir's 69:24 citation "from the Sahih" (no one's deeds admit him to Paradise, not even the Prophet, except by mercy and grace). Substance checked at Bukhari 6463 (Abu Hurayra: لن ينجي أحدا منكم عمله … ولا أنا إلا أن يتغمدني الله برحمة) and 6464 ('A'isha: لن يدخل أحدكم عمله الجنة), saved here; Ibn Kathir's exact composite wording (اعملوا وسددوا وقاربوا … برحمة منه وفضل) not matched in either, so it is reported only as Ibn Kathir's citation.
