Card and bilingual article (8 sections, 1,668 EN words) for "and the
people of the city came, rejoicing", read in its place after the decree
of 15:66 and before the exchange of 15:68-72.

Sources: at-Tabari and al-Baghawi name the city as Sadum; al-Qurtubi,
the Muyassar and as-Sa'di as the city of Lut. Yastabshirun read two
ways side by side, no position: news passed among them (as-Sa'di,
al-Baghawi: yubashshiru ba'duhum ba'dan) and gladness (Ibn Kathir:
farihin; Muyassar: farihun yastabshirun). Object of the joy kept to the
commentators' own words: greed for al-fahisha (at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi,
al-Baghawi), Qatadah via at-Tabari (al-munkar), the guests' bright faces
(Ibn Kathir), their supposed power over the guests (as-Sa'di).
Sequence against Surah Hud reported from Ibn Kathir only (ar on 15:68,
fetched; en abridged, grouped 15:67-72): the wa does not require order.
Ma'arif file covers 15:48-57 and is set aside as off-verse.

Qur'an links in the article's voice: 11:78 (qawmuhu yuhra'una ilayhi,
one pointer), 11:81 (one pointer), 9:124 and 39:45 (yastabshirun),
15:53-55 (b-sh-r, good news to Ibrahim). 15:63 page pointed to;
15:68-72 pointers only; 15:73-74 untouched.

Hadith: none; no fetched tafsir attaches one to 15:67.

Plain statement in both languages that the verse describes a people as
the text describes them, licenses nothing against any living person or
community, and gives no one leave for contempt.
