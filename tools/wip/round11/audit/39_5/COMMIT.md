Card and 8-section bilingual article for 39:5: creation with truth, the
night wound over the day and the day over the night, the sun and moon
running to a named term, al-'Aziz al-Ghaffar. Read as the answer to the
claims of 39:3-4 and the first step of the argument that closes in 39:6.

Sources: Tabari, Qurtubi, Sa'di, Baghawi, Ibn Kathir (ar + en abridged),
Muyassar, Ma'arif.
- Tabari: yughshi plus yulij; chains for Ibn Abbas (carries), Mujahid
  (yudahwiruhu), Qatada (covers), as-Suddi, Ibn Zayd; sun and moon for the
  count of years; term = the Hour, "until the sun is wound up" (81:1-2).
- Qurtubi: bil-haqq as grounds for sole worship; takwir and the turban;
  ad-Dahhak, Qatada, Ibn Abbas (length exchange); term = the end (82:1-2)
  or the stations, with al-Kalbi; Ghaffar as the one who covers sins.
- Baghawi: al-laff wal-jam'; al-Hasan and al-Kalbi, 9 and 15 hours; 22:61.
- Sa'di: wisdom, command and recompense; night and day never share rule;
  term = the ruin of this abode; forgiveness even for a repentant mushrik.
- Ibn Kathir: succession and pursuit (7:54); term ends at Resurrection.
- Ma'arif: the curtain image; sun and moon move, no scientific verdict.
Disagreements kept, no position: the readings of yukawwir with their
collectors (Ibn Abbas carries two glosses in two collectors); the term as
the world's end vs. each cycle's stations.
Hadith: none attached by any fetched source; said in one sentence.
Asbab: none. Overlap: 7:54's chase and 36:40 limit cross-referenced, not
repeated; 10:5 and 44:38 for bil-haqq. Root k-w-r checked: 39:5 and 81:1
only. Dropped: a fixed gloss for yudahwiruhu, Qurtubi's Ya-Sin back-ref.
Themes: creation, tawheed.
