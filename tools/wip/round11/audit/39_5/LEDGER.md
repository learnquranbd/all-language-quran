## Verse cross-references (checked against data/quran-json)
- Root k-w-r: scanned the whole Qur'an text; only 39:5 (twice) and 81:1 (كُوِّرَتْ). Basis for "only two places".
- 35:13 ends «ذَٰلِكُمُ ٱللَّهُ رَبُّكُمْ لَهُ ٱلْمُلْكُ», same as 39:6. 13:2 and 35:13 have «كُلٌّ يَجْرِى لِأَجَلٍ مُّسَمًّى»; 31:29 has «إِلَىٰٓ أَجَلٍ». 81:1 and 81:2, 82:1 and 82:2 match the Tabari and Qurtubi wordings. 22:61 is yulij (Baghawi's citation). 20:82 is Sa'di's citation.
- Shipped overlap: 7:54 ("Night, Day, and the Chase") already covers the pursuit, 36:40's limit and 13:2's term; this article points to it (§5.3) and does not repeat. 10:5 and 44:38 treat bil-haqq and the denial of idle creation; cross-referenced only (§2.2). Neighbours 39:9, 39:10 and 39:23 have no overlap.

## Disagreements kept, no position
- Sense of yukawwir: covering (Qatada via Tabari, Baghawi, Qurtubi), carrying (Ibn Abbas via Tabari), rolling (Mujahid via Tabari), bringing and taking away (as-Suddi via Tabari; Muyassar), winding over as one leaves (Ibn Zayd via Tabari), laying upon (ad-Dahhak via Qurtubi), lengthening and shortening (Ibn Abbas via Qurtubi; al-Hasan and al-Kalbi via Baghawi), succession and pursuit (Ibn Kathir's summary). Tabari presents his as one sense; Baghawi lists without choosing; Ibn Abbas carries two different glosses in two collectors, each kept with its collector.
- Ajal musamma: end of the world (Tabari, Qurtubi, Ibn Kathir, Sa'di, Muyassar) vs. the stations of each cycle (Tabari "qila"; Qurtubi "qila" with al-Kalbi).
