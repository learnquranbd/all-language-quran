## Hadith

- Bukhari 5978 (Dar-us-Salam), https://quranx.com/hadith/Bukhari/DarusSalam/Hadith-5978,
  saved hadith_Bukhari_5978.txt. First ten words (Arabic matn): أَتَتْنِي أُمِّي رَاغِبَةً فِي عَهْدِ النَّبِيِّ صلى الله عليه وسلم فَسَأَلْتُ. Quoted whole
  (English rendered from the Arabic; "hoping for my favour" follows the quranx English), with
  Ibn 'Uyaynah's line marked as his, a narrator's. Grading: in al-Bukhari's Sahih; nothing added.
- Bukhari 3183, https://quranx.com/hadith/Bukhari/DarusSalam/Hadith-3183, saved. First ten words:
  قَدِمَتْ عَلَىَّ أُمِّي وَهْىَ مُشْرِكَةٌ فِي عَهْدِ قُرَيْشٍ، إِذْ عَاهَدُوا. Used only for the treaty-period detail and
  the answer "نعم، صليها". Same chain as al-Baghawi quotes. In al-Bukhari's Sahih.
- Muslim 1827, https://quranx.com/hadith/Muslim/Hadith-1827, saved. First ten words:
  إِنَّ الْمُقْسِطِينَ عِنْدَ اللَّهِ عَلَى مَنَابِرَ مِنْ نُورٍ عَنْ يَمِينِ الرَّحْمَنِ. Quoted whole from the Arabic,
  marked general and not attached to the verse (Ibn Kathir sets it under the closing clause and
  calls it الحديث الصحيح; his wording عن يمين العرش differs, Muslim's used). Note: the quranx English
  says "'Abdullah b. 'Umar"; the Arabic chain says عبد الله بن عمرو, which the article follows.
- Not quoted: Musnad Ahmad (Ibn al-Zubayr/Qutaylah report) is reported as carried by at-Tabari
  and Ibn Kathir, with no grading in the sources and none given. Muslim's version of the Asma
  report not fetched; the article says only that Ibn Kathir and al-Qurtubi name both collections.
