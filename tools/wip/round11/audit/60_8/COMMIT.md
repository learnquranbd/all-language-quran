Replaces the 769-word, 6-section article with an 8-section, 1,777-word
source-gated article (shipped card kept).

Sources: at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi, as-Sa'di, Ibn Kathir (ar), al-Baghawi,
Muyassar, Ma'arif on 60:8; at-Tabari and as-Sa'di on 60:9 for the
paired-verse section. Covers as-Sa'di's account of why the permission
came (believers feared ties with polytheist relatives were now sinful)
and the rukhsah wording of al-Baghawi and al-Qurtubi; the birr/qist
glosses (Ibn Kathir, at-Tabari, al-Baghawi, Muyassar, as-Sa'di with
31:15, al-Qurtubi's badal); Ibn al-'Arabi's "a portion of wealth, not
justice" against Ma'arif's justice-owed-to-all-plus-courtesy.

Disagreements kept, no position taken: who the verse covers (Mujahid:
believers who did not emigrate; others: non-Makkans who did not; al-
Hasan: allies and covenant-keepers; al-Kalbi, Abu Salih, Ibn 'Abbas:
Khuza'ah; unnamed via al-Qurtubi: women and children; at-Tabari: all
who did not fight, of every religion); abrogated (Ibn Zayd, Qatadah
with 9:5, the truce-cause view) or standing (at-Tabari with his
conditions, al-Qurtubi's "most commentators: muhkam").

Hadith: Bukhari 5978 quoted whole, Ibn 'Uyaynah's revelation line
marked as a narrator's; Bukhari 3183 for the treaty-period detail; the
Ibn al-Zubayr/Qutaylah report as at-Tabari, Ibn Kathir (Ahmad) and
al-Baghawi carry it, ungraded. Muslim 1827 (muqsitun on pulpits of
light) quoted as general, not attached. 60:9 section carries the
licensing sentence in both languages.

Dropped: the shipped article's unsourced birr (19:14, 19:32) and qist-
root (72:14-15) claims, its 5:8 and 5:42 links, and its contemporary
"Where It Bites Now" section.
