Replaces the 785-word, 6-section article with a 1,791-word, 8-section deep article.

Sources: at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi, al-Baghawi, Ibn Kathir (ar + en abridged),
as-Sa'di, Muyassar and Ma'arif al-Qur'an, all on 91:9 (Muyassar/Ibn Kathir en
grouped 91:1-10, Ma'arif 91:9-13). Covers the seven oaths, 91:7-8 (sawwaha on the
fitrah with 30:30; alhamaha glossed by Ibn Abbas, Sa'id ibn Jubayr, Ibn Zayd),
the content of tazkiyah (as-Sa'di's four verbs, Muyassar's purify-and-grow),
91:10 dassaha (Ma'arif with 16:59, Muyassar, Ibn Kathir), and 91:11-15 in one
section with the licensing sentence in both languages.

Disagreements kept, no position: who purifies (the person: Qatadah, Ibn Kathir's
first reading, as-Sa'di, Muyassar; Allah: Ibn Abbas, Ibn Zayd, at-Tabari's own
gloss, al-Baghawi, al-Qurtubi's gloss); the oath's answer (91:9 with implied lam
per az-Zajjaj, implied la-tub'athunna, az-Zamakhshari's la-yudamdimanna with
91:9 as digression, or reordering), all via al-Qurtubi.

Hadith: Muslim 2650 (Prophet cites 91:7-8 on decree, answer quoted whole) and
Muslim 2722 (Zayd ibn Arqam's supplication, quoted whole), both attached by Ibn
Kathir and confirmed on quranx; graded only as in Muslim's Sahih.

Dropped: at-Tabarani's Ibn Abbas report (unconfirmed); an-Nasa'i 5458 and three
unsourced linguistic claims from the old article. Defect fixed: the old article
said the Sunnah "settles" the zakka question; Ibn Kathir cites the prayer after
both readings without choosing.
