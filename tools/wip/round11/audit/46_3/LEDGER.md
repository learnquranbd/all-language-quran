## Disagreements kept unranked
- bil-haqq: Tabari (truth and justice established in creation, no agent) vs Muyassar
  (servants establish it among themselves); Qurtubi's two readings (passing away / recompense).
- ajal musamma: the Day of Resurrection (Qurtubi via Ibn Abbas and others; Baghawi) vs each
  created thing's decreed term (Qurtubi, wa qila); Tabari "a term for all of that known to Him";
  Ibn Kathir / Sa'di / Muyassar stress fixity only.
- 'amma undhiru: relative vs masdariyyah ma (Qurtubi).

## Own-voice observations (not attributed)
- One preposition governs al-haqq and ajalin (genitive visible in verse text; Tabari's
  restatement "وإلا بأجل" quoted as support). Bracketed [for] in the printed translation.
- Musamma as passive participle; mu'ridun as active participle describing a state.
- Juxtaposition of Ibn Kathir's "not 'abath" with his/Qurtubi's "lahun" (article's own).
- Book = His statements, heavens = His actions: framed as "read that way" from Ibn Kathir (en).
