Card and 8-section bilingual article for 46:3: ma khalaqna as-samawati
wal-arda wa ma baynahuma illa bil-haqqi wa ajalin musamma, walladhina
kafaru 'amma undhiru mu'ridun. Placed after 46:1-2 (Book, then sky);
45:22 one pointer for bil-haqq glosses; 46:4 not developed.

Sources: Tabari, Qurtubi, Sa'di, Ibn Kathir (ar + en 46:1-3 lines),
Baghawi, Muyassar. Ma'arif's group text covers 45:28-37; left out.
- One bi governs al-haqq and ajalin (Tabari's "wa illa bi-ajalin").
- Bil-haqq: Ibn Kathir not 'abath/batil; Muyassar and Sa'di la 'abathan
  wa la suda with their purposes; Tabari's justice established in
  creation vs Muyassar's justice the servants establish; Qurtubi's
  lil-zawal wal-fana' and, wa qila, recompense with 53:31.
- Ajal musamma: the Resurrection (Qurtubi, Ibn Abbas and others;
  Baghawi, "pointer to their passing") vs each created thing's term
  (Qurtubi, wa qila); Tabari, Ibn Kathir, Sa'di, Muyassar stress the
  fixing. Unranked.
- 'Amma undhiru: Qurtubi's relative vs masdariyyah ma; content per
  Baghawi, Muyassar, Tabari, Ibn Kathir (Book and Messenger).
- Mu'ridun: lahun (Ibn Kathir, Qurtubi); Tabari's no admonition, no
  reflection; Sa'di's refusal after proof, and the believers' contrast.
Hadith: none attached by any tafsir; said in one sentence.
Asbab: none in the sources; not used.
Licensing sentence in both languages.
Dropped: Ma'arif 45:28-37 material, Ibn Kathir (en) 46:4-6 material.
- Gate: trimmed 1,900 to 1,732 EN words; four number-parity wordings
  and one duplicate heading fixed.
