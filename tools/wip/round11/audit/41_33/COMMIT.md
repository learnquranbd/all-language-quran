Replaces the 640-word, 6-section article with a 1,762-word, 8-section deep article.

Sources: at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi, as-Sa'di, Ibn Kathir (ar + en), al-Baghawi,
Muyassar, Ma'arif, all on 41:33. At-Tabari ties the verse to 41:30 (said
our Lord is Allah, stood firm, then called); al-Qurtubi reads it as a
rebuke to the laghw of 41:26; as-Sa'di: a question of settled negation,
qawl as speech, way and state.

Disagreement kept with holders, no position taken: the Prophet (as-Suddi,
Ibn Zayd, Ibn Sirin, Ibn Abbas, al-Hasan), the mu'adhdhins (Aisha, Ikrima,
Qays, Mujahid, Ibn Umar), every caller (al-Hasan, Qays). Preferences
reported as theirs: Ibn al-'Arabi (via al-Qurtubi) the Prophet, as the
verse is Makkan and the adhan Madinan; Ibn Kathir and al-Qurtubi general.

The three clauses glossed: Ibn Kathir and Qatada on deed matching word;
'amila salihan as prayer between adhan and iqamah (Qays, Abu Umama),
Ikrima, al-Kalbi; Ibn al-'Arabi on why the declaration is spoken;
al-Qurtubi's no-proviso point; Fudayl ibn Rufayda's adhan report.
As-Sa'di's forms of da'wa and ranks of callers (6:132).

Hadith: Muslim 387 (mu'adhdhins' longest necks) and Bukhari 624 (a prayer
between every two adhans), both attached by fetched tafsirs, quoted whole.
Dropped from the old article: 61:2-3, 2:44, 16:125, 12:108 and the
"whoever calls to guidance" hadith (no fetched tafsir attaches them).
Fixed: the old claim that the readings were "not rivals" (at-Tabari
records ikhtilaf) and a misattributed detail in Aisha's report.
