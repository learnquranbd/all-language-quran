Tadabbur 26:170 — So We saved him and his family, all — faith, not nearness, is what delivers

Card plus a bilingual 8-section article on the deliverance of Lūṭ (AS) and
the believing household, built only from tafsir fetched for this verse.

Sources used:
- at-Tabari: najjaynāhu = rescue from the very punishment that fell on qawm Lūṭ.
- Ibn Kathir (ar): ajmaʿīn = kulluhum, "all of them" — emphasis sealing the set.
- al-Muyassar: ahl = his household AND those who answered his call; the exception
  (26:171) is his wife, who "did not share them in faith" and stayed with the perished.
- Ibn Kathir (en): the wife stayed behind and was destroyed; deliverance came by the
  night-departure command (Hūd, al-Hijr), obeyed patiently.

Disagreement kept as a disagreement: al-Qurtubi reads the saved household narrowly
(only his two daughters, per Hūd) beside al-Muyassar's wider believing ahl — no
position taken.

Hadith: Sahih al-Bukhari 4771 (Abū Hurayra), revealed with 26:214 "warn your nearest
kindred" — the Prophet ﷺ tells Quraysh, ʿAbbās, Ṣafiyya and Fāṭima he cannot avail
them against Allah. Sound by Bukhari's own standard; not upgraded. Chosen over the
stock, off-theme Bukhari 660.

Dropped: Maʿārif's secondhand/unnumbered cursing-hadith on 26:166; any 26:171 detail
beyond the counterpoint. Condemned-people caveat carried in both languages: the verse
describes what the text describes and licenses nothing against any living person or
community. Nothing said in the article's own voice that the prophet erred.
