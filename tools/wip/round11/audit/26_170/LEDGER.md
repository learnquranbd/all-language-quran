# SOURCES — 26:170 (فنجيناه وأهله أجمعين)

## Tafsir fetched (from PREP; not re-fetched)
- **15 at-Tabari (ar)** — USED. Clause: «(فَنَجَّيْنَاهُ وَأَهْلَهُ) من عقوبتنا التي عاقبنا بها قوم لوط». Rests §1: the saving is deliverance from the specific punishment inflicted on qawm Lūṭ.
- **14 Ibn Kathir (ar)** — USED. Clause: «"فنجيناه وأهله أجمعين" أي كلهم». Rests §2: ajmaʿīn glossed as kulluhum, "all of them" (emphasis, no exception).
- **16 al-Muyassar (ar, covers 170–171)** — USED. Clause: «فنجيناه وأهل بيته والمستجيبين لدعوته أجمعين إلا عجوزًا من أهله، وهي امرأته، لم تشاركهم في الإيمان، فكانت من الباقين في العذاب والهلاك». Rests §3 (ahl = household + those who answered his call) and §4 (the exception = his wife, who "did not share them in faith," so she remained among the perished). This is the verse's core "faith not proximity" datum.
- **90 al-Qurtubi (ar)** — USED. Clause: «ولم يكن إلا ابنتاه على ما تقدم في "هود"». Rests §3: on the narrow reading his saved household were only his two daughters (per Sūrah Hūd). Kept as the counter-window to al-Muyassar — a genuine difference of scope, no position taken.
- **169 Ibn Kathir (en, abridged, covers 165–174)** — USED. Clauses: "This was his wife, who was a bad old woman. She stayed behind and was destroyed with whoever else was left"; and the cross-reference to Sūrah Hūd / al-Hijr where Allah "commanded him to take his family at night, except for his wife, and not to turn around … So they patiently obeyed." Rests §4 (wife stayed behind, destroyed with the rest — no blame laid on the prophet) and §6 (deliverance via the night-departure command, obeyed patiently). Did NOT use his epithet "a bad old woman" in the article's own voice; kept to "did not believe / stayed behind."
- **91 as-Saʿdi (ar)** — NOT USED. Fetched text is only the verse re-quoted, no commentary.
- **94 al-Baghawi (ar)** — NOT USED. Fetched text is only «قال الله تعالى» + the verse; no reading to attribute.
- **168 Maʿārif (en, covers 166–170)** — NOT USED. Its content is on 26:166 (unnatural act unlawful even with a wife); its hadith mention ("the Prophet ﷺ cast a curse…") is secondhand via Rūḥ al-Maʿānī and unnumbered — would be an upgraded/secondhand citation, so dropped.

## Hadith
- **Sahih al-Bukhari 4771** — USED (§8). URL fetched: https://quranx.com/hadith/Bukhari/DarusSalam/Hadith-4771 (Dar-us-Salam 4771; USC-MSA Vol 6, Bk 60, H 294). First ten words of wording used: "Allah's Messenger got up when the Verse: And warn…". Narrated Abū Hurayra; revealed with 26:214 (وأنذر عشيرتك الأقربين). The Prophet ﷺ names Quraysh, Banū ʿAbd Manāf, ʿAbbās, Ṣafiyya, Fāṭima — "I cannot avail you anything against Allah." Grading: in Ṣaḥīḥ al-Bukhārī, sound by the collector's own standard; not upgraded. Chosen over the PREP-suggested Bukhari 660 ("seven shaded"), which is a stock, off-theme hadith; 4771 is on-theme (kinship/proximity saves no one without faith) and tied to this very surah.

## Asbāb an-nuzūl
- NOT USED for 26:170. No established sabab for this verse; the article uses the verse's placement (answer to the duʿāʾ of 26:169) instead. (26:214's occasion is carried only inside the Bukhari 4771 narration, as the hadith itself reports it.)

## Dropped (could not confirm / out of scope)

- Any detail of 26:171 beyond naming it as the counterpoint (per watch-points — not developed).
