Card and 8-section bilingual article for 43:53: fa-law-la ulqiya
'alayhi aswiratun min dhahab, and the angels muqtarinin. 43:51-52 and
43:54 as context only; 43:47 not drawn on. The 43:52 slur on Musa (AS)
is never repeated; it is answered from the tafsirs.

Sources: Tabari, Ibn Kathir (ar; en abridged for 43:52 and 43:54),
Muyassar, Qurtubi, Sa'di, Baghawi, Ma'arif.
- Demand + condition "if he is truthful": Tabari, Muyassar, Baghawi;
  hal-la and "the Lord of Musa" as agent: Qurtubi.
- Asawira: qulb/aqliba (Tabari, Ibn 'Abbas, Qatada; IK ar); custom of
  the time (Qurtubi); Mujahid's chief's bracelets and gold collar, both
  wordings (Qurtubi, Baghawi); "a chief we must obey" (Baghawi).
- Readings as named: Hafs (+Ya'qub, Baghawi) aswira; Ubayy asawir, Ibn
  Mas'ud asawiir (Qurtubi); cities asawira, al-Hasan aswira (Tabari).
- Muqtarinin: Mujahid, Qatada, Suddi, Ibn 'Abbas, Sa'di, IK, Baghawi,
  Muqatil, al-Kalbi; none chosen.
- Envoys of kings, staff and white hand, angels echoed from Musa's
  words (Qurtubi); outward form vs inner meaning (IK).
- 'Uqda: IK en (coal, 20:36, al-Hasan), 20:27 cited; Ma'arif's two
  readings of the remark.
Disagreement kept: singular of asawira (Abu 'Amr: iswar a dialect of
siwar vs Tabari: iswar = archer, asawira plural of aswira).
Hadith: none attached by any tafsir; IK's 43:55 narration marked as
not attached. No asbab. Licensing sentence in both languages.
Dropped: Ubayy-reading variance between Tabari and Qurtubi; Tabari's
plural analogies; IK en's curses; Ma'arif's underlying sources.
- Fix (review): 20:27 moved out of the sentence attributed to Ibn Kathir (he cites only 20:36); the prayer's location now stands as a separate, verified pointer.
