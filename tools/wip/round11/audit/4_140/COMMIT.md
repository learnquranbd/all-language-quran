Assigned 4:140, kept single (when you hear Allah's verses denied and mocked, do not
sit with them until they turn to other talk, or you would be like them). 4:137-139
and 4:141 quoted from the text only; 4:136-139 and 4:141 left free.
New card and a 1,752-word, 8-section article.

Sources: at-Tabari, Ibn Kathir (ar), al-Qurtubi, as-Sa'di, al-Baghawi, al-Muyassar
and Ma'arif on 4:140, all slots on target.
Centre: the back-reference to 6:68 as Ibn Kathir, al-Baghawi and al-Qurtubi give it
(Makkan command, Madinan restatement); at-Tabari's three readings of nazzala;
al-Qurtubi on hearing the denial; as-Sa'di's faith/reverence pairing.
"Like them": at-Tabari (in disobedience), Ibn Kathir (in sin), Muyassar and as-Sa'di
(content with a sin = its doer), al-Qurtubi (not in every attribute), Ma'arif's two
senses; the strong end (al-Qurtubi, al-Baghawi) reported, not adopted. The article's
own voice: the hinge is rida, the verse speaks to the listener, no takfir.
Duty: object if able, else rise (al-Qurtubi, as-Sa'di, al-Jassas via Ma'arif), with
Ma'arif's condition and al-Hasan al-Basri's saying.
Bid'ah application attributed (at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi, ad-Dahhak/Ibn Abbas with the
chain difference, as-Sa'di, Ma'arif); no group named, none pointed at.
Licensing in both languages for the hypocrites of 4:138, 4:139 and 4:141, and for
Umar ibn Abd al-Aziz punishing a fasting man (a ruler's court).
Kept as differences, no position:
- sitting once the talk turns: al-Baghawi's majority vs al-Hasan; al-Mazhari's
  reconciliation via Ma'arif.
- abrogation: Muqatil (Ibn Kathir) and al-Kalbi (al-Qurtubi) in opposite directions;
  al-Qurtubi: the general body held it in force.
Hadith: Tirmidhi 2801 (Jabir), cited by Ibn Kathir as a parallel, quoted whole, graded
hasan gharib by at-Tirmidhi, Layth's weakening noted. No hadith as occasion.
Abu Wa'il / an-Nakha'i (at-Tabari) marked as a saying, not a hadith.
Dropped: al-Qurtubi's note on whom the hypocrites sat with, at-Tabari's Ibn Abbas
report on unity, as-Sa'di's 57:13, Muslim 49 (fetched, unattached).
