Card and 8-section bilingual article for 42:33: in yasha' yuskini r-riha,
fa-yazlalna rawakida 'ala zahrihi, and li-kulli sabbarin shakur. 42:32
used as the frame; 42:34 (wrecking, pardon) context only; 42:36-38 and
42:40 not drawn on.

Sources: Tabari, Qurtubi, Sa'di, Ibn Kathir (ar; en abridged), Baghawi,
Muyassar, Ma'arif (on 42:30 only).
- Stilled wind: Tabari's paraphrase with Qatada, Suddi, Ibn 'Abbas
  (wuqufan); Qurtubi's Medinan plural ar-riyah and Qatada's yazlilna.
- Rakid: Qurtubi's root survey (water, wind, ship, noon sun, balance);
  Baghawi thawabit; IK "face of the water".
- Measure: IK (ar) wind in the measure they need; IK (en) still / fierce
  / measured wind with the rain analogy; Sa'di on the wind as means;
  42:27 and 42:28 for the surah's own wording.
- Signs: Tabari's admonition, lesson, proof; Qurtubi; Muyassar (running
  and stopping); Sa'di "the one who benefits". Same closing at 14:5,
  31:31, 34:19 (checked in quran-json).
- Kept as a difference: patience in hardship / gratitude in ease (IK,
  Baghawi, Qurtubi) vs patience in obedience (Tabari, Muyassar); Sa'di
  holds both. Intensive form glossed only by Sa'di (kathir as-sabr).
- Qurtubi's Qutrub and 'Awn ibn 'Abd Allah sayings on the pair.
- Boundary via Ma'arif citing Baydawi on 42:30; licensing sentence in
  both languages.
Hadith: none; no fetched tafsir attaches one to 42:33 (stated in text).
Dropped: Sa'di's "fire-driven vessels" remark (modern engineering), IK's
Egypt example, Ma'arif's 42:30 narrations (unconfirmed, other verse).
