## sabbar shakur: each tafsir's gloss (watch-point)
- Ibn Kathir (ar) and (en): sabbar in hardships (الشدائد), shakur in ease (الرخاء).
- al-Baghawi: every believer; the believer's attribute is patience in hardship, gratitude in ease.
- al-Qurtubi: sabbar over affliction (البلوى), shakur for blessings (النعماء); plus Qutrub and 'Awn b. 'Abd Allah sayings on why the pair.
- at-Tabari: ذي صبر على طاعة الله, grateful for His favours and kindnesses (أياديه).
- al-Muyassar: patient upon obedience to Allah, grateful for His favours and bounties (follows Tabari).
- as-Sa'di: كثير الصبر (the only gloss that marks the intensive form); three objects of patience (hardship of obedience, restraining the call to sin, restraining resentment at calamities); shakur in ease, acknowledges, submits, spends the blessing in His pleasure; "this is the one who benefits from the signs".
- Why intensive forms: no fetched tafsir explains the choice beyond Sa'di's كثير. Article says so and stops.
- Kept as a difference, no position: patience in hardship (Ibn Kathir, Baghawi, Qurtubi) vs patience in obedience (Tabari, Muyassar); Sa'di includes both.
