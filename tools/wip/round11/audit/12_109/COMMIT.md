Card and 8-section article (1,674 EN words, 20 paragraphs, 55-110 each).
12:109 is 29 Arabic words, per PREP.md.

Sources: all eight tafsir IDs, each covering 12:109 alone. Answer to "why
no angel" (al-Qurtubi); Ibn Jurayj's 6:91 link and "all said to Quraysh"
(at-Tabari); as-Sa'di's consolation; Ibn Abbas via ad-Dahhak with 25:20,
21:8, 46:9 (Ibn Kathir); Ma'arif on direct wahy and divine choice.
Women prophets kept as a disagreement, no position taken: majority and
"wahy tashri'" (Ibn Kathir), "not women" (at-Tabari), al-Hasan (al-Qurtubi,
al-Baghawi) against the view naming Sarah, the mother of Musa and Maryam
(Ibn Kathir, Ma'arif), al-Qurtubi's rejection of the four-women report,
Ibn Kathir's "honour granted, prophethood does not follow", al-Ash'ari,
siddiqa (5:75). Ahl al-qura: amsar (at-Tabari), Qatada, Muyassar,
as-Sa'di; harsher readings named but not quoted; the article states the
verse licenses no contempt for any community. Travel: athar (Ibn Jurayj),
22:46 (Ibn Kathir), as-Sa'di's warning; no archaeological claim; licenses
nothing against any living person or community. Dar al-akhira: al-Farra'
via at-Tabari vs an-Nahhas and the Basrans (al-Qurtubi), Paradise, 40:51,
11:108. Readings: ta'/ya' (al-Qurtubi), nuhi/yuha (al-Baghawi).

No fetched tafsir attaches a sound hadith. Ibn Majah 4032 (Ibn Umar,
mixing with people and bearing their harm; Ibn Kathir cites the saying
from Ahmad, whose wording is not used), quoted whole in Ibn Majah's
wording, no grading on the page, marked not attached.
Dropped: Dajjal (not in fetched texts), Ibn Kathir's gift narration,
verbatim harsh adjectives on desert-dwellers.
