### Hadith
- Sahih al-Bukhari 4920 (Dar-us-Salam numbering; USC-MSA 6/60/442). URL fetched via hadith.js: https://quranx.com/hadith/Bukhari/DarusSalam/Hadith-4920 . Arabic first words: «صَارَتِ الأَوْثَانُ الَّتِي كَانَتْ فِي قَوْمِ نُوحٍ فِي الْعَرَبِ بَعْدُ». Article's own rendering of the fetched Arabic, first ten words: "the idols that were among the people of Noah came". Quoted whole (matn) from this one collection, split over §3.1-3.2. Grading: inclusion in al-Bukhari's Sahih; page shows no other grading. Stated as Ibn 'Abbas's statement (mawquf), not a saying of the Prophet.
- No fetched tafsir attaches a marfu' hadith to the verse as its explanation; Qurtubi's Sahih Muslim narration ('A'isha, Umm Habibah and Umm Salamah, images in a building in Abyssinia) is mentioned only as general and not attached; not fetched, not quoted (see Dropped).

### Sensitive handling
- Licensing sentence in both languages (§8.1): verse describes a condemned people; licenses nothing against any living person or community; no verdict on any present practice.
- Tribe-specific sentence in both languages (§6.3): the lists say nothing about anyone today carrying these tribal names or living in these places; no blame, mockery or suspicion licensed.
- 71:21-22 named as neighbours only (§1.1); 71:24 named and explicitly not developed (§8.1); 71:25 cited for the end only.

### Dropped
- Muyassar's ruling sentence on the wisdom of prohibiting statues and building domes over graves (practice claim; watch-point: no claims about any living community's practices).
- Qurtubi's Sahih Muslim narration (church in Abyssinia, "worst of creation"): not fetched on its own page; also would bear on a living community; left as one general mention.
- Ibn 'Asakir's report (via Ishaq b. Bishr, Juwaybir, Muqatil) identifying Wadd with Shith / Hibat Allah: identification with a prophet, chain not assessable from the fetched text.
- Tha'labi's report (via Qurtubi) of Noah guarding Adam's body on a mountain in India.
- Layth's distinction wadd (Noah's idol) / wudd (Quraysh's idol, 'Amr b. Wudd), al-Sihah and Ibn Durayd lexical notes, the poetry lines on Wadd and Ya'uq, Abu 'Uthman an-Nahdi's eyewitness of Yaghuth.
- Ibn Kathir (en) comment on 71:24 ("worship continued until our times among Arabs, non-Arabs...") and Ma'arif's note on Noah's supplication: belong to 71:24, not developed.
- Qatadah's Suwa' location «برياط» (uncertain name form).
