Card and 8-section bilingual article for 71:23: wa-qalu la tadharunna
alihatakum wa-la tadharunna Waddan wa-la Suwa'an wa-la Yaghutha
wa-Ya'uqa wa-Nasra. 71:21-22 named as neighbours; 71:24 placed only.

Sources: Tabari, Qurtubi, Sa'di, Ibn Kathir (ar), Baghawi; Muyassar
(71:22-25), Ma'arif (71:23-24), Ibn Kathir en (71:21-24), groups named.
- Speakers: Muyassar's chiefs to the weak; Sa'di's call to shirk;
  Ibn Kathir (en) ties the plot of 71:22 to it, cf. 34:33.
- Qurtubi's two views kept: idols of Noah's people later taken by
  the Arabs (Ibn 'Abbas, "the majority") vs Arabs' idols only, with
  the shift of speakers it implies. General-then-particular, 33:7.
- Origin accounts kept as differences: Muhammad b. Qays (Tabari;
  "between Adam and Noah" in Ibn Kathir's quote), Sa'di, Baghawi;
  'Urwah and Muhammad b. Ka'b (sons of Adam, Qurtubi); Abu Ja'far on
  Wadd (Ibn Kathir); Ibn 'Abbas/Dahhak "idols of Noah's time";
  flood-burial report, attributed.
- Tribe lists side by side: Bukhari, Qatadah (Tabari), Mawardi,
  Mahdawi, Tha'labi (Qurtubi); Waqidi's shapes with "Allah knows best".
- Readings: wuddan (Madinah / Nafi') and waddan (Kufah, Basrah, rest)
  per Tabari, Baghawi, Qurtubi; Tabari: both correct.
Hadith: Bukhari 4920, Ibn 'Abbas's statement, quoted whole from the
Arabic, graded by inclusion in the Sahih; marked as not the Prophet's
words. Qurtubi's Muslim narration mentioned as general, not quoted.
No asbab. Licensing sentence in both languages, plus a line that the
tribe lists say nothing about anyone bearing those names today.
Dropped: Muyassar's domes-on-graves ruling, Wadd = Shith report,
Tha'labi's Adam's-body story, Layth's wadd/wudd split, poetry.

Review fix: removed the one-sentence mention of al-Qurtubi's Sahih Muslim narration on images (EN + BN); it was never fetched or confirmed on quranx.
