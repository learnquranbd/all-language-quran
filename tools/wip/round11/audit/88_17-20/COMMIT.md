Sources: at-Tabari, Ibn Kathir (ar; en abridged for 88:21-22), al-Qurtubi,
as-Sa'di, al-Baghawi, al-Muyassar, Ma'arif al-Qur'an, all for 88:17-20.

The turn from the unseen (88:1 to 88:16) to four visible signs read through
at-Tabari (power over Garden and Fire) with Qatada's report, kept as a
Successor's explanation, not asbab. Addressees: Muyassar, as-Sa'di,
Ma'arif; licensing sentence. Camel first: Ibn Kathir, al-Qurtubi, Muqatil,
al-Kalbi, al-Hasan (via al-Baghawi), al-Mawardi's four uses, Ma'arif;
al-Qushayri's "no particular wisdom to seek"; Qatada/Muqatil on the couches
of 88:13. Al-ibil as clouds kept as a reported reading, no position
(al-Mubarrad, ath-Tha'labi's objection, al-Asma'i from Abu 'Amr, al-Mawardi).
Rufi'at, nusibat, sutihat as glossed (50:6, 21:31 as the tafsirs cite them);
earth's shape only as as-Sa'di's own discussion. Al-Qurtubi's readings
('Ali's first-person khalaqtu..., suttihat). Ibn Kathir's rider and Ibn
'Abbas's challenge via al-Baghawi. 88:21-22 via Ibn Kathir (13:40).

Ibn Kathir; creation exchange quoted verbatim, rest summarised, no separate
grading. Abu Ya'la's narration not used (Ibn Kathir grades it weak).
