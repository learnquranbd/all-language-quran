words; 109:2 named as the verse that follows, 109:2-6 not treated.

Sources: at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi (with Ibn al-Anbari, al-Mawardi),
al-Baghawi, Ibn Kathir (ar + en abridged), as-Sa'di, Muyassar, Ma'arif.
Slot check clean: every Arabic slot discusses 109:1.
- qul: to these mushrikin (Tabari), O Messenger (Muyassar), openly and
  declaring (Sa'di); Ibn al-Anbari on the reading that drops qul.
- al-kafirun: particular persons known never to believe (Tabari); al-
  as ma'hud, specific in general wording (Qurtubi, Mawardi) vs wording
  covers every disbeliever, those faced were Quraysh (Ibn Kathir).
  Kept as a difference, none chosen.
- Design: present/future (Tabari), emphasis or matching the offer
  (Qurtubi, 55:13, 77:15, 78:4-5, 94:5-6), object vs manner (Ibn
  Kathir, 10:41, 28:55); Ma'arif's three views.

Qurtubi (Ibn Ishaq, Abu Salih), Baghawi's six names, Ibn Kathir's
qila; ungraded in all, names as each source gives them; 39:64-66.
