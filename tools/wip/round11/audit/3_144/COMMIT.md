Key 3:144 as given (Muhammad is but a messenger; would you turn back on your
heels?). 3:140 to 3:143 summarised as context; 3:139 and 3:145 ship. New card
and a 1,771-word, 8-section article.

Sources: at-Tabari, Ibn Kathir (ar), al-Qurtubi, as-Sa'di, al-Baghawi and
Ma'arif on 3:144; al-Muyassar and Ibn Kathir (en) read, not named. All slots
checked: every file opens on 3:144.
- Setting: the cry at Uhud and Ibn Qami'ah's claim (Ibn Kathir); Mus'ab's
  death and the thirty who shielded him (al-Baghawi).
- "Only a messenger": at-Tabari, Ibn Kathir (a precedent in them), as-Sa'di
  (their lives never a condition of obeying Allah), al-Qurtubi; 39:30.
Kept as differences: turning on the heels as apostasy (Qatadah via al-Qurtubi,
at-Tabari, Mujahid), literal flight, or the deed of apostates without apostasy
(al-Qurtubi's two "it is said"); as-Sa'di wider; Ma'arif: a passing confusion.
The voices of the rout only as attributed (Qatadah; Ibn Jurayj and ad-Dahhak
place the worst words with the hypocrites; as-Suddi's Anas ibn an-Nadr; the
Ansari via Ibn Abi Najih and al-Bayhaqi; al-Baghawi's returning group). No
Companion named as turning back; 3:155's pardon cited; licensing sentence on
the hypocrites' words in both languages.
The grateful: general (at-Tabari, Ibn Kathir, al-Qurtubi, as-Sa'di) and named
as Abu Bakr and his companions only as attributed ('Ali via at-Tabari, as-Sa'di).
Hadith: Bukhari 4452 quoted whole across one section (Abu Bakr at as-Sunh, his
speech, the narrator's oath, 'Umar's words in his own telling); Bukhari 7219
('Umar's next-morning address, "a light by which you are guided").
Dropped: the at-Tabarani 'Ali report in Ibn Kathir; al-Qurtubi's remarks on
'Umar, 'Uthman and 'Ali that day; reports naming Companions in the rout
(at-Tabari 7946, 7943, 7949; al-Baghawi); the al-Ibana and Ibn Majah 'Umar
reports; budget cuts listed in the LEDGER.
