All eight named.

## Disagreements kept (no position)
- Mathal: at-Tabari/al-Baghawi (likeness = the gods set up as Allah's counterpart); al-Akhfash (no parable as such); al-Qutabi; an-Nahhas (preferred by an-Nahhas himself).
- Seeker/sought: (1) gods/fly: Ibn 'Abbas per at-Tabari and Ibn Kathir, chosen by at-Tabari, "apparent" per Ibn Kathir, followed by Muyassar, as-Sa'di; (2) fly/idol: Ibn 'Abbas per al-Baghawi, unnamed "reverse" in al-Qurtubi; (3) worshipper/idol: as-Suddi and others (Ibn Kathir), ad-Dahhak (al-Baghawi), "some" (at-Tabari), qila (al-Qurtubi), Ma'arif's rendering. The two Ibn 'Abbas reports conflict; stated as such.
