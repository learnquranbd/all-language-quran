Replaces the 785-word, 6-section article with a 1,778-word, 8-section
deep article on the likeness of the fly and the weakness of seeker and sought.

Sources: at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi, Ibn Kathir (ar + en), as-Sa'di,
al-Baghawi, al-Muyassar, Ma'arif al-Qur'an, all on 22:73 (t169 grouped 22:73-74).
- Address and listening: al-Qurtubi's link to 22:71, tad'una/yad'una
  (as-Sulami, Abu al-'Aliya, Ya'qub); as-Sa'di (believers and disbelievers).
- "Duriba mathal" with no story: daraba = ja'ala, mathal = shabah (Tabari,
  Baghawi); al-Akhfash, al-Qutabi, an-Nahhas via al-Qurtubi; Ma'arif.
- Those invoked: idols round the Ka'bah / chiefs / devils (Qurtubi), all
  that is invoked (Sa'di). Why the fly: Qurtubi's four reasons, Ibn Kathir, Sa'di.
- Snatching: Ibn 'Abbas saffron and as-Suddi food (Qurtubi, Baghawi),
  Ibn Zayd jewels (Baghawi), Qurtubi's "bite" view as qila.
- Seeker and sought kept as a three-way dispute, no position: gods/fly
  (Ibn 'Abbas per Tabari and Ibn Kathir), fly/idol (Ibn 'Abbas per Baghawi),
  worshipper/idol (as-Suddi, ad-Dahhak); the conflicting Ibn 'Abbas reports noted.
- 22:74 in two sentences (Tabari, Ibn Kathir).
Hadith: Bukhari 7559 (Abu Hurayrah, "let them create a tiny ant...") quoted
whole as Ibn Kathir's attachment; Muslim 2111 for its image-making setting;
marked as not the occasion of the verse.
Asbab: none given; placement used. Licensing sentence in both languages.
Dropped from the old article: 2:26, 6:91, 39:67 (not cited by fetched
tafsir), the unsourced matlub word-count and "never taken up" claims, and a
hybrid paraphrase of the hadith.
