Replaces the 769-word, 6-section article with a 1,764-word, 8-section deep article, rebuilt from the tafsir files fetched for 32:17. The shipped card is kept.

Placement: "them" = those described in 32:15-16 (at-Tabari); as-Sa'di's turn "as for their reward".
Scope: indefinite nafs in negation covers all creation (as-Sa'di); not even an angel (al-Qurtubi);
Ibn Abbas: no tafsir for it (al-Baghawi), too great to be interpreted (al-Qurtubi); al-Muyassar
and Ibn Kathir describe the frame only.
Readings: ukhfiya / ukhfi "I hide" (at-Tabari; Hamzah and Ya'qub per al-Baghawi, Hamzah per
al-Qurtubi), at-Tabari's verdict that both are well known and close in meaning; nukhfi (Ibn
Mas'ud), yukhfa (al-A'mash), akhfa (Muhammad ibn Ka'b via al-Zajjaj). Plural qurrat a'yun from
Abu Hurayrah (Bukhari 4779, at-Tabari) and Ibn Mas'ud (al-Qurtubi); al-Mahdawi and al-Anbari on it.
Hidden deed, hidden reward: Ibn Kathir (requital of the deed's kind), al-Hasan, as-Sa'di (night
prayer hidden, so reward hidden: hence jaza'an bima kanu ya'malun), al-Baghawi; Ibn Mas'ud's Torah
statement marked as his own words.
Kept as differing readings: al-Qurtubi (the honour is for the highest rank, Muslim's al-Mughira
hadith) and Ibn Sirin (the vision of Allah) beside at-Tabari / as-Sa'di (those of 32:15-16).

Hadith: Bukhari 4779 quoted whole (Abu Hurayrah's own tag to the verse); 4780 described; Muslim
2825 (Sahl: 32:16-17 recited together) and Muslim 189 (al-Mughira) summarised, with at-Tirmidhi's
hasan sahih via Ibn Kathir. Asbab: none.
