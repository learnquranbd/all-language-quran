## Hadith
1. **Sahih al-Bukhari 4779** (Dar-us-Salam; USC-MSA 6/60/302) — https://quranx.com/hadith/Bukhari/DarusSalam/Hadith-4779 — quoted whole in the page's English. First ten words: "Narrated Abu Huraira: Allah's Messenger (ﷺ) said, 'Allah said, 'I have". Grading: in al-Bukhari's Sahih; Ibn Kathir reports at-Tirmidhi's "hasan sahih" for the Sufyan route. Attached to the verse by Ibn Kathir, at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi (Muslim's wording), al-Baghawi (4780 route), as-Sa'di (unsourced wording). Same page carries Abu Hurayrah's plural reading via Abu Mu'awiya (S4). Note: in this route the verse is attached by Abu Hurayrah ("recite if you wish").
2. **Sahih al-Bukhari 4780** — https://quranx.com/hadith/Bukhari/DarusSalam/Hadith-4780 — described, not quoted ("dhukhran, balha ma utli'tum 'alayh"; verse recited in full right after). The reciter's identity (`ثم قرأ`) is left unstated in the article on purpose.
3. **Sahih Muslim 2825** (Sahl ibn Sa'd) — https://quranx.com/hadith/Muslim/Hadith-2825 — paraphrased; Prophet ﷺ recites 32:16-17 together. First words: "Sahl b. Sa'd as-Sa'idi reported: I was in the company". In Muslim's Sahih; cited by Ibn Kathir (Ahmad, Muslim) and al-Qurtubi.
4. **Sahih Muslim 189** (al-Mughira, Musa (AS) and the lowest/highest ranks) — https://quranx.com/hadith/Muslim/Hadith-189 — summarised, flagged "In summary", not quoted. First words: "It is reported on the authority of al-Mughira b.". In Muslim's Sahih; at-Tirmidhi hasan sahih, marfu' sounder than mawquf (via Ibn Kathir). Number not printed in the article (al-Qurtubi's attribution "Sahih Muslim" is what the article states).

## Points from the shipped 769-word article
Kept (now confirmed and attributed): grammar of indefinite nafs in negation (as-Sa'di); Abu Hurayrah's own tag in Bukhari; "Muslim records it as well" (Ibn Kathir; Muslim 2824); 32:18 and the shared tail in 32:19.
Dropped as unsourced: present tense "knows — now"; the "passive when the fact matters more than the actor" claim (replaced by at-Tabari's reasoning); qurra from qarra "cool/settle" and the "may Allah cool your eye" idiom; the 25:74 and 28:9 cross-references; jaza' as "exact settling" and kanu + imperfect as "habit over years"; nuzul as a host's welcome for an arriving guest.
Incomplete/wrong: it said the hadith's tag to the verse "is a Companion's own, not a later commentator's" as if of the hadith generally; that holds for the Bukhari 4779 route, but in 4780 and Muslim 2825 the recitation follows the Prophet's words directly.
