Card and 8-section bilingual article for 82:15: yaslawnaha yawma
ad-din, three words that give 82:14 its day. 82:14 and 82:16 as
context only; 82:17-19 left for their own entries.

Sources: Tabari, Ibn Kathir (ar; en abridged), Muyassar, Qurtubi,
Sa'di, Baghawi, Ma'arif. No re-fetch.
- Fujjar: Muyassar (fell short in the rights of Allah and of His
  servants), IK en (evildoers vs the obedient), Ma'arif (sinners),
  Tabari ("these fujjar"). Licensing sentence in both languages.
- Yaslawna: enter (Baghawi), flame and heat reach them (Qurtubi),
  flame reaches them (Muyassar), severest punishment (Sa'di), enter
  and taste its flame (IK en); Tabari restates without a gloss.
- Yawm ad-din: hisab/jaza'/qiyamah (IK), recompense and reckoning
  (Qurtubi), recompense for deeds (Sa'di), recompense (Muyassar),
  Resurrection (Baghawi), Tabari's requital for deeds and Ibn Abbas:
  one of the names of the Day, magnified and warned of.
- Ma'arif ties 82:13-14 to 82:5; 82:9's ad-din noted from the text.
Glosses kept side by side, none chosen. Hell described only by the
fetched clauses.
Hadith: none attached by any tafsir; said in one sentence. IK's
Banu Hashim narration sits under 82:19 (26:214), left there. No asbab.
Dropped: "disbelievers" as an identification (no fetched text), 82:16
detail, IK on 82:17-19.
