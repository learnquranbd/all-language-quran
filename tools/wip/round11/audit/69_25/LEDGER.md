Notes on attribution:
- Baghawi: the printed text of Ibn as-Sa'ib's line has «[من صدره]» in editor's brackets; the article renders Ibn as-Sa'ib without "from his chest" and gives "from his chest" only for the qila account, where it is unbracketed. The two accounts are kept as two (twisting vs pulling out), no preference stated, as in the source.
- Qurtubi: the fetched text begins mid-narration («وإذا كان...»), so the narrator and isnad are not visible. The article says this explicitly, presents it as something al-Qurtubi carries, not as a hadith, and rests nothing else on it. Omitted from its details: «فيعظم للنار وتزرق عيناه» eyes turning blue (not needed). The gloss «أي يضاعف عليه العذاب. ليس المعنى...» is attributed to "the text", not to a named person, since the following «قال» shows it is an interjection inside the narration and the speaker is unclear.
- Disagreements kept: Baghawi's two accounts of how the left hand is moved. The feeling behind the wish is given in each source's own word (Ibn Kathir nadam, Muyassar nadiman mutahassiran, Sa'di al-hamm wa-l-ghamm wa-l-khizy); the reason (tidings of the Fire, everlasting loss) is as-Sa'di's alone and is marked as such.
- Grammar in own voice (§1, §4): utiya / lam uta passive; ya layta "a particle of wishing" (kept deliberately minimal). Muyassar drops the final ha (kitabi) — visible in its text.
