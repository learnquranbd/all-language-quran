Card and 8-section bilingual article for 69:25: wa-amma man utiya
kitabahu bi-shimalihi, the left-hand record and the wish not to have
been given it. 69:26-29 and 69:19's mirror left to their own entries.

Sources: Tabari, Muyassar (grouped 69:25-29), Sa'di, Ibn Kathir (ar),
Ibn Kathir (en, grouped 69:25-34), Baghawi, Qurtubi; Ma'arif (69:19
ha'umu only) not used.
- Record = kitab a'malihi, yawma'idhin (Tabari, Muyassar); Sa'di's
  kutub a'malihim as-sayyi'a; IK's al-'arasat and al-ashqiya'.
- Left hand: Baghawi's two accounts (Ibn as-Sa'ib: twisted behind the
  back; qila: pulled from the chest to behind the back), kept as two.
- Sa'di's reason for the left hand: tamyiz, khizy, 'ar, fadiha.
- The wish: IK nadam, Muyassar nadiman mutahassiran, Sa'di al-hamm
  wa-l-ghamm wa-l-khizy; Sa'di alone gives the reason (tidings of the
  Fire, everlasting loss).
- Qurtubi's black-book narration: fetched text starts mid-narration,
  narrator not shown; reported as his inclusion, not as hadith,
  with its own note that doubling adds nothing not done.
Licensing paragraph in both languages: describes the Day only, no
licence against any living person or community, no test of anyone's
standing; the reader's own record is the subject.
Hadith: none attached to 69:25; IK's narrations sit under 69:32-34.
Asbab: none. Parallels: none cited by a source, so 84:10 not linked.
Dropped: al-qadiya glosses (69:27), 69:30-34 material, Ma'arif.
