# SOURCES ledger — 26:142

إِذْ قَالَ لَهُمْ أَخُوهُمْ صَٰلِحٌ أَلَا تَتَّقُونَ
"When their brother Ṣāliḥ said to them, 'Will you not fear Allah?'"

## Tafsir fetched (per PREP.md; not re-fetched)

- **al-Qurtubi (90)** — USED (named). Arabic clauses relied on:
  - `أي ابن أبيهم وهي أخوة نسب لا أخوة دين` → "son of their father; a brotherhood of
    lineage, not of faith" (sections 2 "Kin by Blood, Not Creed").
  - `وقيل : هي أخوة المجانسة` → the second reading, brotherhood of kind/stock (section 2).
  - `قال الله تعالى : "وما أرسلنا من قومه" [إبراهيم:4]` → Qurtubi's own cross-reference to
    14:4, read as the principle that a messenger comes in his people's tongue (section 3).
    NOTE: Qurtubi's quoted wording is "من قومه"; I cite 14:4 by its standard sense
    ("in the language of his people") — the verse he points to.
  - `وقيل : هو من قول العرب يا أخا بني تميم ... الزمخشري : ومنه بيت الحماسة` → Arab idiom
    "yā akhā banī Tamīm" = "O one of them", and az-Zamakhshari's line of poetry (section 2).
  - `أَلَا تَتَّقُونَ أي ألا تتقون الله في عبادة الأصنام` → "will you not fear Allah in the
    worship of idols" (section 4).
- **as-Sa'di (91)** — USED (named). Clauses:
  - `في النسب` → brotherhood "in lineage" (section 2).
  - `برفق ولين` → he spoke "with gentleness and softness" (sections 2, 3).
  - `أَلا تَتَّقُونَ الله تعالى, وتدعون الشرك والمعاصي` → "fear Allah and give up shirk and
    acts of disobedience" (section 4).
- **al-Muyassar (16)** — USED (named), 142-portion only. Clause:
  - `ألا تخشون عقاب الله، فتُفرِدونه بالعبادة؟` → "will you not fear the punishment of Allah
    and single Him out in worship" (section 4). The 143–145 portion of this grouped
    entry was NOT used (future targets).
- **Ibn Kathir ar (14)** — USED (named). Clause:
  - `فدعاهم نبيهم صالح إلى الله ... أن يعبدوه وحده لا شريك له، وأن يطيعوه ... فأبوا عليه
    وكذبوه وخالفوه` → called them to worship Allah alone and obey; they refused, denied,
    opposed (section 1). The "no reward" clause (`لا يبتغي ... أجرا`) was NOT built out —
    that is 26:145's ground; mentioned only inside the generic surah-frame list.
- **Ibn Kathir en abridged (169)** — USED (named) for the geography/placement only:
  - "Arabs living in the city of Al-Hijr -- which is between Wadi Al-Qura and Greater
    Syria ... Thamud came after `Ad and before Ibrahim" (section 5).
- **al-Baghawi (94)** — fetched; only the bare verse, no commentary → nothing attributed.
- **at-Tabari (15)** — fetch FAILED / WRONG GROUP per PREP → not used.
- **Ma'arif (168)** — WRONG GROUP (covers 26:128–135) per PREP → not used.

## Hadith

- **Ṣaḥīḥ al-Bukhārī 3380** — USED (section 6).
  - URL fetched: https://quranx.com/hadith/Bukhari/DarusSalam/Hadith-3380
  - First ten words (EN): "When the Prophet passed by (a place called) Al Hijr"
  - Narrator: ʿAbdullāh b. ʿUmar (RA).
  - Grading: ṣaḥīḥ by inclusion in Ṣaḥīḥ al-Bukhārī; no separate per-hadith grade given,
    not upgraded. Attachment to the verse is textual: the Prophet ﷺ said it *at Al-Hijr*,
    which Ibn Kathir (169) identifies as Thamūd's own dwelling-place. Not a general
    narration — it is tied to the site of this people.
  - Also fetched Bukhari 3381 (same matn, "ruined dwellings" wording) as a cross-check;
    3380 chosen because it names Al-Hijr explicitly, fixing the attachment.
  - Banned stock hadith avoided: Bukhari 660 and Bukhari 3443 ("prophets are brothers",
    used at 26:123) — neither used.
