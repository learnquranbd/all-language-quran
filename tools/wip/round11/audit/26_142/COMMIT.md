Ṣāliḥ opens to Thamūd with the surah's one gateway call: will you not
fear Allah — a brother by blood, not by creed.

Card plus deep bilingual article (8 sections, ~1,700 EN words).

Sources (each confirmed on the fetched text for 26:142):
- al-Qurtubi: akhūhum = son of their father, a brotherhood of lineage
  not of faith; a second reading of kind/stock (mujānasa); the Arab
  idiom "yā akhā banī Tamīm"; cross-ref to 14:4 (messenger in his
  people's tongue); a-lā tattaqūn = fear Allah in the worship of idols.
- as-Sa'di: "in lineage"; he spoke bi-rifq wa-līn; fear Allah and give
  up shirk and disobedience.
- al-Muyassar (142-portion only): fear Allah's punishment, single Him
  out in worship.
- Ibn Kathir (ar): called them to Allah alone, to obey; they refused,
  denied, opposed. (en abridged) Thamūd were Arabs of Al-Hijr, between
  Wadi al-Qura and Syria, after ʿĀd and before Ibrāhīm.

Hadith: Ṣaḥīḥ al-Bukhārī 3380 (Ibn ʿUmar) — the Prophet ﷺ at Al-Hijr,
"do not enter the dwellings of those who wronged themselves unless
weeping"; then veiled his face. Ṣaḥīḥ, not upgraded; tied to the verse
by site. Avoided Bukhari 660 and 3443 (used at 26:123).

Carries the "licenses nothing against any living person or community"
sentence in both languages. Kept 143/144/146/155/157 and the no-wage
line (145) for their own verses; recurring surah-frame noted generically.
No disagreement beyond Qurtubi's two readings of "brother", kept as two.
