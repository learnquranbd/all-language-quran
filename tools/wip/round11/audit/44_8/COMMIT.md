Card and 8-section bilingual article for 44:8: la ilaha illa huwa yuhyi
wa yumit, rabbukum wa rabbu aba'ikum al-awwalin. 44:7 as the lead-in
(Muyassar's "if certain, know the Lord is the true God"); 44:9 named in
one line only.

Sources: Tabari, Qurtubi, Sa'di, Ibn Kathir (ar + en), Muyassar (44:7-8
part of a grouped entry). Baghawi restates the verse only; Ma'arif's
grouped entry has nothing on 44:8. Both said plainly.
- La ilaha illa huwa: Tabari (no one fit for worship, so worship none
  else), Muyassar (alone, no partner), Sa'di (none worshipped but His
  Face), Qurtubi (Creator, so no partner that creates nothing).
- Yuhyi wa yumit: Tabari (whom He wills), Qurtubi (life to the dead,
  death to the living), Sa'di (He alone disposes); Sa'di's addition of
  the gathering and recompense, labelled as his alone.
- Rabbukum wa rabbu aba'ikum: Tabari and Qurtubi rabb = malik; Sa'di
  rabb = nurturer of first and last. Presented as complementary.
- Addressees only as Tabari/Muyassar give them ("O people ... your gods
  that neither harm nor benefit"); Qurtubi's warning against denying
  Muhammad. No source names Makkah; said plainly. Licensing sentence
  EN+BN.
- 7:158 parallel from Ibn Kathir (ar and en), wording checked in
  verse-base.json.
Hadith: none attached to 44:8 in any fetched tafsir; said in one sentence.
Asbab: none in the sources; not used.
Dropped: 26:26 and 2:258 parallels (not cited by a source); IK (en)
Ibn Sayyad report (about the surah); an unsourced grammar remark on the
verbs' tense.
