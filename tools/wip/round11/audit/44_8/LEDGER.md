"أيها الناس ... آلهتكم" and Muyassar's "آلهتكم" used as the only identification
(people worshipping gods besides Allah), attributed. Article says plainly that no fetched
text goes further. Licensing sentence present EN+BN (§5.2).

## Own-voice remarks (not attributed)
- §4.2 "Read side by side, the glosses bring out both sides of the one word rabb"
  (Tabari/Qurtubi malik vs Sa'di murabbi): framed as complementary, not as a dispute,
  since neither rejects the other.
- §4.3, §7.2, §8.2: reflective paragraphs drawing on the verse text and the glosses above.
- §7.2 states the phrase is "rabbu aba'ikum" (He is their Lord), not "was": nominal
  sentence, no past-tense verb in the Arabic.
