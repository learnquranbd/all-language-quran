Card and 8-section bilingual article (1,778 EN words) on 67:19: the
turn from the threats of 67:16-18 to a-wa lam yaraw, saffat,
wa-yaqbidna, ma yumsikuhunna illa ar-Rahman, bi-kulli shay'in basir,
and the 16:79 parallel. 67:15 dhalul linked, not repeated; 67:20-22 untouched.

Sources: Ibn Kathir (ar; en group 67:16-19), Muyassar, Qurtubi, Sa'di,
Baghawi, Ma'arif. Tabari's file carried no commentary, so not named.
- Tone: Sa'di 'itab wa hathth; Muyassar's charge of heedlessness; IK en
  frames 67:16-19 as gentleness and respite (35:45).
- Saffat: Baghawi, IK, Muyassar, Qurtubi; an-Nahhas on saff/qabid and
  Abu Khirash's line, via Qurtubi.
- Holding: Qurtubi, Baghawi (in both states), Muyassar; IK's taskhir of
  the air min rahmatihi wa lutfihi; Sa'di on bodies made for flight;
  Qurtubi's earth-for-man / air-for-birds link. Gravity framing is
  Ma'arif's, attributed to it alone.
- Basir: IK (what suits each creature), Sa'di (tadbir by wisdom),
  Muyassar (no tafawut), Ma'arif "watchful".
- 16:79 cited by IK ar and en, verified in quran-json; Allah vs
  ar-Rahman difference stated as text only, no source explains it.
Disagreement kept: yaqbidna as a motion within flight (IK, Qurtubi,
Muyassar) vs folding to stop or descend (Qurtubi's qila, Sa'di).
Licensing sentence in both languages: the deniers of the passage.
Hadith: none attached by any tafsir; said in one sentence. No asbab.
Dropped: Ma'arif's transition to 67:20, any reason for the Name choice,
ornithology, Qurtubi's qawa'im unglossed.
