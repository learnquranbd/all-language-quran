## Differences kept
- Speaker: al-Baghawi names the keepers of the Fire; Ibn Kathir, Muyassar, as-Sa'di use the passive. Presented as compatible, but sourced only to al-Baghawi.
- Abu Jahl/Badr link: al-Baghawi states it as his explanation; al-Qurtubi reports it with `وقيل`. Ibn Kathir uses the same words (jamīʿun muntaṣir) without naming anyone. Stated as three distinct postures.

## Hadith
None. No fetched tafsir attaches a hadith to 37:25. Ibn Kathir's Ibn al-Mubārak / ʿUthmān ibn Zāʾida report is a saying of an early scholar and is labelled in the article as not a hadith. Stock fallback Bukhari 660 deliberately not used (CLAUDE-LOG watch).

## Asbab an-nuzul
Not used. The Abu Jahl/Badr link is presented as the commentators' connection of meaning, explicitly not as an occasion of revelation (no fetched source frames it as one). 54:44 cited by number only, not fetched or quoted beyond the phrase al-Baghawi and al-Qurtubi themselves quote.

## Licensing guard
§5 "A Named Man, No Licence", both languages: the verse describes what the text describes and licenses nothing against any living person or community.
