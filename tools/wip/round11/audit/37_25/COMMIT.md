Card + deep bilingual article (8 sections, 1,663 EN words) on the
keepers' taunt "mā lakum lā tanāṣarūn" as the halted procession of
37:22-24 is questioned.

Sources: Ibn Kathir (ar + abridged en), at-Tabari, al-Muyassar,
al-Qurtubi, as-Sa'di, al-Baghawi, Ma'arif al-Qur'an, each fetched on the
verse. Taqrīʿ/tawbīkh agreed by Ibn Kathir, al-Qurtubi, Muyassar,
al-Baghawi; at-Tabari's one-line address to al-mushrikīn billāh.

Abu Jahl's Badr boast "naḥnu jamīʿun muntaṣir": al-Baghawi states the
link outright (marking it as 54:44, cited by number only); al-Qurtubi
reports it with wa-qīla; Ibn Kathir echoes the wording without naming
anyone. Presented as a connection of meaning, not asbab an-nuzul.
Licensing guard in both languages.

Speaker kept as sourced: al-Baghawi alone names the keepers of the
Fire; the others use the passive. Ma'arif places the halt near the Sirat.

Grammar note (al-Qurtubi): tatanāṣarūn with one tāʾ dropped; al-Bazzī
doubled the tāʾ in waṣl. One short paragraph.

As-Sa'di's three failed claims (ward off, rescue, intercede) and his
"fa-lam yanṭiqū": the silence treated as the verse's core. 37:26-31
left as future ground.

No hadith: none attached by any fetched tafsir; stock Bukhari 660 not
used. Ibn al-Mubārak / ʿUthmān ibn Zāʾida saying labelled as not hadith.
Dropped: al-Bazzī's riwāya identity, surah chronology (unsourced).
