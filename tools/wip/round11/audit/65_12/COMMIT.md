Card and bilingual article (8 sections) on the closing verse of at-Talaq.

Sources: Ibn Kathir (ar, en), at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi, al-Baghawi, as-Sa'di,
al-Muyassar and Ma'arif al-Qur'an, all fetched on 65:12.

Disagreements kept and attributed, with no position taken:
- mithlahunna: seven earths in layers with gaps and inhabitants (al-Qurtubi's
  majority view, which he prefers); stacked with no gaps (ad-Dahhak); spread
  out and divided by seas (al-Kalbi from Ibn Abbas); "in number" (al-Baghawi);
  likeness in what they contain (at-Tabari); Ibn Kathir rejects "seven climes".
- al-amr: revelation (Muqatil) or decree and destiny (the majority, per
  al-Qurtubi), each changing the span of baynahunna; also life/death, wealth
  and poverty, and the wondrous management (Ibn Kaysan, al-Baghawi). As-Sa'di,
  al-Muyassar and Ma'arif's tashri'i/takwini pairing hold the readings together.
- Ma'arif: the Qur'an is silent on the form of the earths. Ibn Abbas's reserve
  is reported via Mujahid. For the Abu ad-Duha report, al-Bayhaqi's verdict
  (sound chain, highly anomalous) is quoted through Ibn Kathir.

Hadith: al-Bukhari 2453 (Aisha, the hand-span of land and seven earths),
quoted whole, and 2454 (Ibn Umar). Muslim 1610 is mentioned via al-Qurtubi.
The article marks it as evidence the commentators bring, not as a narration
about the verse. No asbab.

Dropped: the angels gloss (no source), parallels 41:12 and 67:3 (not cited),
the ungraded cosmology reports (distances, materials, Kursi), al-Qurtubi's
Abu Hurayra wording and the Abu Nu'aym du'a.
