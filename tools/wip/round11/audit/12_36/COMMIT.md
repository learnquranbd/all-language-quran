Card and 8-section article (1,682 EN words, 19 paragraphs, 79-104 each).
12:36 is 27 Arabic words, as counted in PREP.md.

Sources: all eight tafsir IDs, each ungrouped on 12:36. Who the two were
(baker and cupbearer) given as Qatadah's report via at-Tabari and Ibn
Kathir; names and the king from Ibn Ishaq via at-Tabari; the poisoning
plot as as-Suddi's report, with the fuller al-Baghawi/al-Qurtubi story
marked as narration. Khamr as grapes: Ibn Mas'ud's reading a'siru 'inaba
(at-Tabari, Ibn Kathir), Ibn 'Abbas (at-Tabari), the Oman usage from
ad-Dahhak (at-Tabari, Ibn Kathir, al-Qurtubi), al-Baghawi's naming by
outcome and al-Qurtubi's elided word.

Disagreements kept without a position: invented dreams to test him (Ibn
Mas'ud, as-Suddi) against real dreams (Ibn 'Abbas, Mujahid; Ibn Kathir's
"view of most"), plus Abu Mijlaz's split view. Min al-muhsinin: tending
the sick and needy (ad-Dahhak, Qatadah, at-Tabari's preferred view),
"if you interpret it" (Ibn Ishaq), knowledge of interpretation
(al-Baghawi, al-Farra' via al-Qurtubi), as-Sa'di's joining of the two.

No fetched tafsir attaches a prophetic hadith to 12:36. Al-Bukhari 7042
(invented dreams), confirmed on quranx DarusSalam, quoted whole and marked
as general; al-Qurtubi's Tirmidhi citation noted; its hasan sahih grading was removed before merge because
the Tirmidhi 2283 page fetched shows no grading.
The poisoning reports are stated to settle nothing about any prisoner
today. 12:37-40 and 12:41 get one pointer.

Dropped: other names for the two, Wahb's donkey report, the "do not love
me" plea, two further dream hadiths al-Qurtubi cites unfetched.
