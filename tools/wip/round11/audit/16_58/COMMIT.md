Card and bilingual article (8 sections, 1,650 EN words) for "when one of
them is given news of a female, his face darkens and he suppresses grief".
16:57 (daughters ascribed to Allah) and 16:59 (hiding, humiliation or
burial) kept as pointers only.

Sources: at-Tabari (one of those who assigned daughters to Allah; face
dark from dislike; kazim = held grief down; Ibn Abbas "you are content
with them for Me, not for yourselves" and the Jahiliyya practice; Qatada:
practice of the Arab mushrikin, the believer content with Allah's
apportioning, "many a girl is better for her family than a boy", "fed his
dog and buried his daughter"; Ibn Abbas hazin; ad-Dahhak kamid); Ibn
Kathir (gloomy with worry; silent from grief); al-Qurtubi (az-Zajjaj
figurative vs al-Mawardi literal colour "view of the majority"; al-Akhfash
anger; Ali ibn Isa kazama waterskin; pointer to Surah Yusuf -> 12:84);
al-Baghawi (grief and anger held in); as-Sa'di (shame before his kind);
the Muyassar; Ma'arif (two traits censured). Ibn Kathir abridged grouped
16:56-60; said so, used only 53:21-22 and 43:17.

Disagreements kept: literal vs figurative darkening (az-Zajjaj via
al-Qurtubi vs al-Mawardi); grief vs anger in kazim. No position.

Hadith: none attached to 16:58 by any fetched tafsir; said so. Muslim 2631
(Anas: whoever provides for two girls until they come of age), quoted
whole from the Arabic, marked as general and not attached; in Muslim's
Sahih, no further grading on the page. No asbab.
Licensing sentence in both languages (no contempt for any living people);
reflection turns on the reader's own welcome of daughters, no group named.
