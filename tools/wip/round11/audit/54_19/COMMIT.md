Card and 8-section bilingual article for 54:19: inna arsalna 'alayhim
rihan sarsaran fi yawmi nahsin mustamirr. 54:18 framing; 54:20-21
pointers only; 41:16 cited via al-Qurtubi's Fussilat reference, 69:7 via
as-Sa'di's wording; 54:2 echo flagged as reader's observation only.

Sources: at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi, Ibn Kathir (ar; en abridged checked),
al-Baghawi, as-Sa'di, al-Muyassar, Ma'arif al-Qur'an (grouped, framing).
- sarsar: Tabari's cold + blast + shriek (sarir) with kubkibu morphology;
  cold (Ibn 'Abbas, Qatada, ad-Dahhak, Ibn Kathir, Muyassar), severe
  (Sufyan, Ibn Zayd, Sa'di), hard-blowing (Baghawi), loud (Qurtubi qila).
  Fuller list left to 41:16.
- yawmi nahsin as construct (shu'm/sharr: Tabari, Qatada, Ibn Zayd) vs
  "severe" (Ibn 'Abbas, ad-Dahhak) needing yawmin nahisin; Tabari knows
  no such reading here; Qurtubi names Harun al-A'war.
- 'alayhim: the ill fixed on 'Ad (Ibn Kathir via Dahhak/Qatada/Suddi,
  Qurtubi, Sa'di, Muyassar); Ibn 'Abbas "a day they took as ill omen".
- mustamirr kept three ways, no position: continuing into the Fire
  (Ibn Kathir, Tabari/Qatada, Qurtubi), seven nights/eight days (Sa'di),
  bitter (ad-Dahhak, al-Kisa'i), firmly twisted (Qurtubi qila).
- Wednesday reports (az-Zajjaj, Ibn 'Abbas, Baghawi qila) and Qurtubi's
  objection/answer reported attributed only; no rule drawn.
Hadith: none. Qurtubi's Jabir and Masruq reports have no collection or
grading in the text and could not be confirmed; not quoted as hadith.
Asbab: none. Licensing paragraph for 'Ad in both languages; no modern
identification, no cursed days.
Dropped: Baghawi's "about a year" (likely corrupt bi-nuhusih); Ma'arif's
no-unlucky-day point (not in this verse's fetch; it ships at 41:16);
54:20 details.
