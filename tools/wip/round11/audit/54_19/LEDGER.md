# SOURCES ledger 54:19

Verse: إِنَّآ أَرْسَلْنَا عَلَيْهِمْ رِيحًۭا صَرْصَرًۭا فِى يَوْمِ نَحْسٍۢ مُّسْتَمِرٍّۢ (9 words, PREP).

## Hadith
- None used. Al-Qurtubi mentions (a) a hadith of Jabir on the Prophet's du'a answered on Wednesday between Zuhr and 'Asr (he refers to his al-Baqara discussion) and (b) a report "Masruq narrates from the Prophet" ending "يوم الأربعاء يوم نحس مستمر". Neither has a collection, number or grading in the fetched text; hadith.js needs a collection+number and neither could be located in the six collections it covers. Both reported only as al-Qurtubi's argument, explicitly not quoted as hadith, no rule drawn.
- Bukhari 1035 (dabur) not used: not attached by any tafsir fetched for 54:19, and already used at 41:16.
