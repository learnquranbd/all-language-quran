Card and 8-section bilingual article for 76:25: the command, after 76:23
(the Qur'an sent down) and 76:24 (patience, do not obey the sinner or the
ungrateful), to mention the name of your Lord bukratan wa-asilan; 76:26
(prostration and long glorification by night) as context only.

Sources: Ibn Kathir (ar, and en grouped 76:23-31), Tabari, Qurtubi, Sa'di,
Muyassar (grouped 76:24-25), Ma'arif (grouped 76:22-31). Baghawi carried
only the verse text, unnamed. Tabari has no own gloss on 76:25 in the
fetched text; his Ibn Zayd report and 76:26 gloss are used, and said so.
- Times: first and last of the day (Ibn Kathir, Sa'di, Muyassar); Qurtubi
  on the plurals of asil (asa'il, usul) with two lines of verse.
- Which prayers, kept as a disagreement: bukratan = subh/Fajr (Ibn Zayd,
  Qurtubi); asilan = Zuhr (Ibn Zayd) vs Zuhr and 'Asr (Qurtubi). Qurtubi
  carries it on: Maghrib and 'Isha, then night voluntary prayer ("Ibn
  Habib said it"; his view judged good).
- Prayer vs remembrance: prayer (Ibn Zayd, Qurtubi), dhikr and du'a
  (Muyassar), both (Sa'di's list, Ma'arif), times only (Ibn Kathir).
- Night verse, attributed only: Tabari "most of the night" with 73:2-4,
  Ibn 'Abbas "prayer and tasbih"; Ibn Zayd's first-obligation-then-nafila
  via 73:20 and 17:79; Qurtubi's list (abrogated by the five prayers /
  nadb / particular to the Prophet). No ruling in the article's voice.
- Addressee: the Prophet (Ibn Kathir, Ma'arif "remedy for persecution");
  extension only as they give it (Ibn Zayd "and from the people"; Qurtubi
  and Sa'di gloss by prescribed prayers without saying more).
Hadith: none attached in any fetched tafsir; said in one sentence.
Asbab: none in the sources.
Licensing sentence in EN and BN on the sinner and ungrateful of 76:24.
Dropped: 7:205 number (Qurtubi says only "end of al-A'raf"); Baghawi.
