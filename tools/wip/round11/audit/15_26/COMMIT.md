Card and eight-section bilingual article for 15:26 (1,791 EN words, 19
paragraphs): We created man from salsal, from hama' masnun.

Sources: Tabari, Ibn Kathir ar, Qurtubi, Sa'di, Baghawi, Muyassar named on
the verse; Ibn Kathir abridged (169, grouped 15:26-27) named with its group
stated, 15:26 content only; Ma'arif (168, grouped 15:24-28) stated as
covering 15:24 only and not used. 15:27-29 left as future ground.

"Salsal": dry clay that rings when tapped (Qatada, Mujahid, Ibn 'Abbas,
ad-Dahhak; Abu 'Ubayda via Qurtubi, "most commentators") vs putrid
(Mujahid via Ibn Abi Najih, chosen by al-Kisa'i per Baghawi and Qurtubi).
Tabari and Ibn Kathir prefer ringing on 55:14; both views kept by name.
"Hama' masnun": altered/putrid (Ibn 'Abbas, Mujahid, Qatada, ad-Dahhak;
al-Farra'), smooth (Ibn Kathir), poured (Abu 'Ubayda; Ibn 'Abbas "moist"
per Qurtubi and an-Nahhas), formed (Sibawayh; a Basran in Tabari), set
upright (al-Akhfash). No position taken.
Stages turab-tin-hama'-salsal given only as Qurtubi's majority view;
Ibn 'Abbas's tin lazib/salsal/hama' report (Tabari); Baghawi's unnamed
athar. 37:11 and 55:14 as wording, 3:59 noted as not cited by the
fetched commentators. No science claims in the article's voice.

Hadith: Muslim 2996 (A'isha, angels from light, jinn from fire, Adam
from what was described), attached by Ibn Kathir on 15:26-27. Abu Dawud
5116 fetched, drafted, dropped for budget. Asbab: none.

Dropped for budget: Baghawi's insan etymologies. No shipped article on
55:14, 3:59 or 37:11; 32:7 and 6:2 clay points not repeated.
