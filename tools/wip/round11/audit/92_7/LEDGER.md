- Ma'arif (168, en): "yusra literally denotes 'ease and comfort' ... but here it refers to 'Paradise'" · "deeds that will lead them to the comforts of Paradise" · "'easy' or 'difficult' qualify deeds, and not persons ... This probably implies ... predisposed" · "both these things are the results of the use of the God-given choice and free will. Therefore, man is rewarded or punished."

Glosses kept side by side, none chosen. al-yusra: goodness (Ibn 'Abbas via IK), Paradise (Zayd b. Aslam via IK and Qurtubi; Ma'arif), the easy trait = acting by what pleases Allah (Tabari, Baghawi; Tabari adds: so Paradise becomes due in the Hereafter). nuyassiruhu: guide to means of good until easy (Qurtubi), guide + tawfiq + ease his affairs (Muyassar), ease his affair / every good / leaving every evil (Sa'di), make ready (Tabari; Baghawi "in this world"), person made easy = disposition, "probably" (Ma'arif).
