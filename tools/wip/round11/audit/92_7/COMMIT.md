Card and 8-section bilingual article for 92:7: fa-sanuyassiruhu
lil-yusra, two words. 92:5-6 named only as the condition; 92:8-10
named only as the counterpart; 92:11 not touched.

Sources: Tabari, Qurtubi (92:7, plus 92:5 for the occasion), Baghawi,
Sa'di, Ibn Kathir (ar + en abridged), Muyassar, Ma'arif. Slot check:
all on 92:7; Muyassar grouped 92:6-7, Ma'arif 92:7-10, IK (en) 92:1-10.
- al-yusra, none chosen: goodness (Ibn 'Abbas via IK), Paradise (Zayd
  b. Aslam via IK and Qurtubi; Ma'arif), the easy trait of acting as
  Allah is pleased (Tabari, Baghawi).
- nuyassiruhu: guide to means of good (Qurtubi), guide and tawfiq
  (Muyassar), every good and leaving evil made easy (Sa'di), make
  ready (Tabari, Baghawi), the person eased, "probably" (Ma'arif).
- Qadar: IK (en) "preordained decree" and Ma'arif "choice and free
  will" reported as theirs; no position taken.
Hadith: Bukhari 4946 ('Ali, funeral, stick) quoted whole in quranx's
English, cited by Qurtubi and IK. Named only: Tirmidhi 2135 ('Umar,
at-Tirmidhi's own hasan sahih), Muslim 2648 (Jabir/Suraqa), Abu Bakr
via Ahmad (ungraded), Qurtubi's two young men (no source), marked general.
Bukhari 4947-4949 dropped: over the 110-word paragraph limit.
Asbab: Abu Bakr freeing weak Muslims (Qurtubi on 92:5, IK via Ibn
Jarir from 'Amir), ungraded, kept as reported. Bilal dropped (in no
fetched text). Cross-refs: shipped 92:4 by number.
Dropped: 6:110, 87:8, 91:7-10, 9:111 (not cited for 92:7), IK's
defective "some of the salaf" clause.
