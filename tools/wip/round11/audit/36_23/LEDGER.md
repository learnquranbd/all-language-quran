# SOURCES — 36:23

## Tafsir fetched (all from PREP.md, not re-fetched)

- **Ibn Kathir (ar, 14)** — NAMED. Rests on: «أأتخذ من دونه آلهة ؟ استفهام إنكار
  وتوبيخ وتقريع» (rhetorical question of denial/rebuke) and «هذه الآلهة ... لا
  يملكون من الأمر شيئا ... وهذه الأصنام لا تملك دفع ذلك ولا منعه ولا ينقذونني»,
  with his citation of «فلا كاشف له إلا هو [يونس: 107]». Used in §1 (inkār/tawbīkh)
  and §4 (idols possess no command; none removes harm but He; 10:107).
- **at-Tabari (15)** — NAMED. Rests on: «إذ مسني الرحمن بضر وشدة» (durr = harm and
  hardship) and the two-part «لا تغني عني شيئا بكونها إليَّ شفعاء، ولا تقدر على رفع
  ذلك الضر عني» + «ولا يخلصوني من ذلك الضر». Used in §3 (durr) and §4 (the two
  failures: cannot lift / cannot extract).
- **al-Qurtubi (90)** — NAMED. Rests on: «إن يردن الرحمن بضر يعني ما أصابه من
  السقم» (durr = sickness/saqam) and «ولا ينقذون يخلصوني مما أنا فيه من البلاء».
  Used in §3 (durr as illness, kept as a narrower reading) and §4 p3 (lā yunqidhūn
  = rescue from the affliction).
- **as-Sa'di (91)** — NAMED. Rests on: «لأنه لا أحد يشفع عند الله إلا بإذنه، فلا
  تغني شفاعتهم عني شيئا». Used in §5 (intercession only by Allah's leave).
- **al-Baghawi (94)** — NAMED. Rests on: «استفهام بمعنى الإنكار، أي لا أتخذ من
  دونه آلهة» (§1, refusal) and «أي لا شفاعة لها أصلا فتغني» (§5, the idols have no
  intercession at all). Also «بسوء ومكروه» for durr (§3).
- **al-Muyassar (16)** — fetched, read, NOT named (plain paraphrase, same content
  as the above; covers 24–25). No distinct claim rests on it.
- **Ma'arif al-Qur'an (en, 168)** — NAMED. Rests on: al-madinah = a large city not
  a village (supports a great town); and Ibn Ishaq from Ibn Abbas, Ka'b al-Ahbar,
  Wahb ibn Munabbih that the man's name was Habib, a carpenter by the better-known
  account. Used in §2, explicitly marked as historical narration, not a ruling of
  the text.
- **Ibn Kathir (en abridged, 169)** — fetched, read; corroborates the ar Ibn Kathir
  (rhetorical question to rebuke; idols neither harm nor benefit nor save; cites
  6:17). Content folded under the Ibn Kathir attribution in §4; not separately
  named. Its 36:25–26 material (his death, "Enter Paradise") deliberately NOT used,
  per the orchestrator's note that 36:26 is another drafter's target.

## Hadith (article §7)

- **Jami' at-Tirmidhi, no. 2516** (Ibn Abbas). URL fetched:
  https://quranx.com/hadith/Tirmidhi/Hadith-2516 (via tools/wip/round11/hadith.js,
  saved to this dir). First ten words of the wording used: "Be mindful of Allah and
  He will protect you". Grading: **at-Tirmidhi himself said حسن صحيح (hasan sahih)**
  — reported, not upgraded. Marked in §7 as a GENERAL narration: the commentators
  do NOT attach it to 36:23; it states the verse's principle, not a comment on it.

## Asbab an-nuzul

- None used. No distinct, confirmed sabab for 36:23 beyond the narrative placement
  (the believing man of 36:20–25). The identity/name reports (Habib) are given as
  historical narration in §2, not as an asbab, and marked as such.

## Dropped (could not confirm / out of scope)

- Did not name Ibn Ashur / ar-Razi / Ibn al-Qayyim — not in the API and not fetched.
- Did not cover 36:26–27 (the man's death and entry to Paradise) — out of scope by
  the orchestrator's note (another drafter's target); al-Muyassar and Ibn Kathir
  (en) both carry it but it was left unused.
- Did not assert a specific number of messengers (the surah has two then a third,
  36:14) — avoided a count to stay accurate and clear of the verse's own point.

## Sensitivity note

Watch-point honoured: the town is destroyed later (36:29), but 36:23 is a
believer's call to tawhid. §8 p3 states plainly, in both languages, that the verse
licenses nothing against any living person or community.
