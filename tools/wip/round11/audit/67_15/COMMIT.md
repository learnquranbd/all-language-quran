Replaces the 741-word, 5-section article with a 1,751-word, 8-section deep article (shipped card kept).

Sources: at-Tabari (his 67:15 text is filed under slot 67:16, fetched and
saved as t15_16), al-Qurtubi, al-Baghawi, Ibn Kathir (ar; en abridged as a
cross-check), as-Sa'di, Muyassar and Ma'arif al-Qur'an, all on 67:15.

Covers the favour after 67:14 (Ibn Kathir) and the warning beside it in
67:16-18 (Ma'arif, Muyassar), with a licensing sentence for 67:18 in both
languages. Dhalul as compliant and the broken-in mount (al-Qurtubi with his two
qila readings, al-Baghawi, at-Tabari, Muyassar, Ma'arif, Ibn Kathir, as-Sa'di).
Manakib as shoulder and side (Ma'arif; al-Qurtubi; al-Farra' via al-Baghawi).
Fa-mshu as amr ibaha (al-Qurtubi), walking for earning (Ibn Kathir, as-Sa'di,
Muyassar, Ma'arif's "probably"), min rizqihi (at-Tabari, al-Baghawi, al-Hasan
via al-Qurtubi), nushur as the raising (all seven).

Disagreement kept, no position in the article's voice: manakib as mountains
(Ibn 'Abbas, Qatada, ad-Dahhak's hillocks), edges and regions (Ibn 'Abbas by
another chain, al-Kalbi, Muqatil), roads and passes (Mujahid, as-Suddi,
al-Hasan), with at-Tabari's preference for regions and sides attributed to him.
The Bashir ibn Ka'b episode is told with its versions kept apart.

Hadith: Jami' at-Tirmidhi 2344 ('Umar, the birds out hungry and home full),
attached by Ibn Kathir, confirmed on quranx, quoted whole, graded hasan sahih
as at-Tirmidhi graded it, and marked as an illustration, not asbab.

Dropped: Abu al-Jald's earth-size report; from the old article the 62:10 and
67:24 links, the nushur/intashiru root echo, "the jurists" (now al-Qurtubi),
"no single choice" (at-Tabari does choose) and editorial lines.
