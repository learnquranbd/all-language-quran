Part of Musa's answer to Pharaoh's "who is your Lord?" (20:49): the Lord
is known by four acts on the earth itself — the ground made a bed, roads
threaded through it, rain sent down, and diverse paired plants raised by it.

Sources: Ibn Kathir frames the verse as completing Musa's description of
his Lord (mahd = a place of rest to settle, stand, sleep, travel; roads on
the earth's "shoulders", cross-ref 21:31; plants of sour, sweet and every
kind). al-Qurtubi and al-Baghawi on the qira'at mahd/mihad (verbal noun vs
noun for a thing spread, like bisat); al-Baghawi glosses salk as inserting
roads into the ground and cites Ibn Abbas. as-Sa'di: the earth subdued so
you may build and plant, roads that join land to land, rain reviving dead
earth as provision for people and their animals. Ma'arif: azwaj = kinds/
species beyond human reckoning. al-Qurtubi/al-Akhfash: shatta may qualify
the pairs or the plants; shatta = scattered/diverse.

Iltifat kept and attributed: al-Baghawi and al-Qurtubi mark the shift from
"He made/opened/sent down" to "We brought forth"; al-Qurtubi preserves the
second view that all of it remains Musa's speech — kept as a disagreement.

Tawhid: the Maker of all this is the only One worthy of worship (Musa's
proof to Pharaoh). No condemned-group licensing sentence (not that kind of
verse). No hadith: none of the seven tafsirs attaches one to 20:53, stated
in one sentence.

Dropped: al-Baghawi's printed "An-Naba 16" citation for "alam najʿal
al-arda mihada" corrected to its real location 78:6. Did not preempt 20:54.
