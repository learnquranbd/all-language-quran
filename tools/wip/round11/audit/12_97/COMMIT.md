Card and 8-section article (1,647 EN words, 21 paragraphs, 55-110 each).
12:97 is 8 Arabic words, per PREP.md.

Sources: all eight tafsir IDs; 169 is grouped 12:96-98, stated, and only
its 12:97 clause ("with humbleness") used. Al-Qurtubi's ellipsis ("when
they returned from Egypt") and his note that the 12:95 speakers were
grandsons or other kin, not these sons, who were away. Ibn Kathir (ar):
spoke gently. Istaghfir lana glossed by at-Tabari (pardon, cover, no
punishment at the Resurrection; sins against the father and Yusuf) and
the Muyassar (Yusuf and his full brother). Khati'in: mudhnibin
(al-Baghawi), "we have confessed our sins" (at-Tabari), acknowledgment
(as-Sa'di). Why ask the father: al-Qurtubi (the grief caused needs his
release, bi-ihlalihi) and Ma'arif (one who asks another to pray also
prays himself); the article states no other fetched text addresses it.
Al-Qurtubi's ruling reported: the wronged must be told the wrong and its
extent; general release disputed, he holds it does not avail; al-Muhallab.

Hadith: Bukhari 2449 (Abu Hurayrah, settle a wrong today), attached by
al-Qurtubi, confirmed on quranx, quoted whole in al-Bukhari's wording.
12:98 given a pointer only; its timing reports not taken. The article
reads the verse as the brothers' repentance and states it licenses
nothing against any living person or community.

Dropped: khati'/mukhti' distinction (not in fetched texts), as-Sa'di's
"naja'u bi-dhalik" (sense uncertain), IK en's 12:96 and 12:98 reports,
the asbat question (not raised).
