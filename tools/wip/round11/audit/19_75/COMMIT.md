Answers the deniers' boast of 19:73: whichever party is astray, the Merciful
lets the rope out — an extension that deepens the loss, not a favour.

Sources (all fetched for 19:75):
- at-Tabari, al-Baghawi, al-Qurtubi: the imperative "fal-yamdud" is a command in
  form, a statement (khabar) in meaning — He leaves the misguided in their error;
  at-Tabari's word for it is imlaa, glossed by Mujahid.
- al-Qurtubi: the extension lengthens the delusion so the punishment is severer
  (cf. 3:178, "We only extend that they increase in sin"); "utmost in threat".
  His second reading — a duaa the Prophet is told to make — kept as a disagreement,
  no position taken.
- as-Sa'di: respite as a verdict already running — content with error, God
  increases him in love of it as punishment (cf. 61:5, 6:110); at the end the
  knowledge avails nothing, no return to act on it.
- Ibn Kathir: the closing "worse in place, weaker in host" mirrors and overturns
  their 19:73 boast; al-Baghawi/at-Tabari read likewise.
- Ma'arif: context — the deniers rich with retinues, believers poor; wealth
  misread as merit and thought permanent.

Hadith: Sahih al-Bukhari 4686 ("Allah gives respite to the wrongdoer...", recited
over 11:102) used in section 8, marked a GENERAL narration on imlaa, NOT tied to
19:75. PREP's suggested Bukhari 660 was the "seven shaded" hadith — off-verse,
dropped.

Licensing: article and card state plainly, both languages, that the verse
describes the deniers of that day and licenses nothing against any living person
or community; istidraj framed inward, as self-examination.

Dropped: Ibn Kathir's mubahala framing (his alone, pulls to other surahs); the
term "istidraj" is not in the fetched tafsir and is flagged as the tradition's
name, not a source's word. 19:76 (increase for the guided) gestured at only, not
preempted.
