Card and 8-section bilingual article (1,688 EN words) on 57:7: aminu
billahi wa rasulihi wa anfiqu mimma ja'alakum mustakhlafina fihi. Placed
after the dominion block of 57:1-6; 57:8-11 not discussed.

Sources: all eight fetched tafsirs named (Ibn Kathir ar, and en grouped
57:7-11 with only the 57:7 part used; at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi, as-Sa'di,
al-Baghawi, al-Muyassar, Ma'arif note signed Muhammad Taqi Usmani).
- Addressee kept open: disbelievers of Makkah (al-Baghawi); "O people"
  (at-Tabari, al-Muyassar); Ibn Kathir names none but stresses complete and
  persistent faith; as-Sa'di "His servants".
- anfiqu: al-Qurtubi's four readings unranked (sadaqa, Allah's way, zakat,
  other obedience); Ibn Kathir's accountability warning.
- mustakhlafin: deputies (Ma'arif's majority view, al-Qurtubi's ownership
  and permission image, as-Sa'di "to see how they act", Ibn Kathir 'ariya)
  and successors (at-Tabari and ahl at-ta'wil, Mujahid's mu'ammarin,
  al-Hasan and al-Qurtubi's nuwwab/wukala', al-Baghawi on Quraysh, Ma'arif
  "in transit"). Ibn Kathir's heir who obeys or disobeys.
- Reward: Paradise (al-Qurtubi); Lord's pleasure first (as-Sa'di).
Hadith: Muslim 2958 (Mutarrif from his father, "My wealth, my wealth"),
attached by Ibn Kathir, quoted whole; grading = inclusion in Sahih Muslim.
Ibn Kathir's "and what is besides that" addition found in Muslim 2959
(Abu Hurayra), not in 2958's Shu'bah route; both facts stated.
Asbab: none in any fetched text; stated. Licensing sentence not needed.
Dropped: 57:10 conquest distinction and mirath clause; 57:8 material.
Gate: one nums fix (দুটো to দুটি), then clean.
