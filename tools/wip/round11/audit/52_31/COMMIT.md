Card and 8-section bilingual article on qul tarabbasu fa-inni ma'akum
mina l-mutarabbisin, read as the reply to 52:30's "a poet for whom we
await rayb al-manun". 52:29 and 52:32-34 pointers only; 52:48 one clause.

Sources: at-Tabari, Ibn Kathir (ar; en abridged for 52:29-34), Muyassar
(52:30-31), al-Qurtubi, as-Sa'di, al-Baghawi. Ma'arif skipped (wrong group).
- What they awaited: his death (Muyassar nuzul al-mawt; al-Baghawi and
  as-Sa'di intaziru bi l-mawt); Ibn Kathir en: rid of him and his Message.
- Ibn Kathir's Dar an-Nadwa report (Ibn Ishaq < Mujahid < Ibn Abbas;
  Zuhayr, an-Nabigha) given as his report on 52:30, not as asbab.
- Tarabbus = intizar in all six; at-Tabari adds tamahhalu; bikum
  al-'adhab (al-Qurtubi, Muyassar).
- What he awaited kept as a difference: Allah's command (at-Tabari,
  al-Baghawi), outcome and victory in both worlds (Ibn Kathir), outcome
  (Muyassar), Badr by the sword (al-Qurtubi, al-Baghawi), punishment from
  Allah or by our hands (as-Sa'di). No position taken.
Licensing sentence in both languages; no licence to wish death on anyone.
Hadith: none attached by any fetched tafsir; stated in the article.
Asbab: none established.
Dropped: 9:52, 20:135, 11:122 (not cited by a fetched tafsir); rayb
al-manun glosses beyond Muyassar/Ibn Kathir en (52:30 tafsir not fetched).
