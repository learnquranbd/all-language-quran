Replaces the 740-word, 5-section article with a 1,794-word, 9-section deep article, built only on tafsir fetched for 89:27-30 (shipped card kept).

Sources: at-Tabari, Ibn Kathir (ar; en abridged for the 89:21-30 frame),
al-Qurtubi, as-Sa'di, al-Baghawi, al-Muyassar, Ma'arif al-Qur'an.

Frame: al-Qurtubi's link from the man of 89:15-16 to the soul at rest;
89:23-26 as the contrast with a licensing sentence in both languages.
al-mutma'innah glosses held by name: at rest in Allah's promise (at-Tabari,
Ibn Abbas, Qatadah, al-Hasan); certain of its Lord (Mujahid); still and firm
(Ibn Kathir); remembrance (as-Sa'di, al-Muyassar, 13:28 via al-Qurtubi and
al-Baghawi); content with decree, secure from punishment, Ubayy's al-aminah.
Disagreement kept, no position: when the address falls (at death; at the
Resurrection, at-Tabari's choice; both, Ibn Kathir, as-Sa'di; Abu Salih's
split). rabbiki as the body vs return to Allah (Ibn Kathir: "gharib", 6:62,
40:43). fi 'ibadi vs the reading fi 'abdi (Ibn Abbas; Ibn Mas'ud's fi jasadi
'abdi) with at-Tabari's ruling for 'ibadi. 29:9, 27:19, 12:101 as cited.
Asbab ('Uthman, Hamzah, Khubayb, Abu Bakr) as the tafsirs' reports, none
chosen; Ibn Kathir's "mursal hasan" reported as his.
Hadith: an-Nasa'i 1833 (Abu Hurairah, white silk), attached by Ma'arif,
quoted whole from quranx with the English page's missing clause supplied
from the Arabic in brackets; no grading from an-Nasa'i.
Dropped from the old article (unsourced): the three-souls stations scheme,
4:69, the funeral-recitation claim; old "at death and at the Resurrection"
was stated as agreed, now kept as a dispute.
