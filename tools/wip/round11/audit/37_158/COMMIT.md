Card and 8-section bilingual article for 37:158, continuing the rebuke of 37:149-157.

Sources: Tabari, Qurtubi, Ibn Kathir (ar/en), Baghawi, Sa'di, Muyassar, Ma'arif (37:158 part only).
- al-jinna: Qurtubi says most mufassirun take it as the angels (ahl al-ishtiqaq: unseen;
  Mujahid/Ibn Abbas: a clan of angels; Abu Malik: keepers of the gardens); Baghawi, Tabari
  and Muyassar agree. Sa'di and Ma'arif read it through the jinn.
- nasab, every reading named: Ibn Abbas, "Allah and Iblis are brothers" (Tabari, Ibn Kathir,
  Qurtubi; Ma'arif rates it weightier); the angels as daughters with mothers among the
  sarawat/mukhaddarat of the jinn, via Mujahid with the Abu Bakr question (Tabari, Ibn Kathir,
  Qurtubi, Baghawi); Qurtubi's musahara; al-Hasan's partnership in worship (Qurtubi prefers
  it, citing 26:98; Baghawi carries it).
- Source clash kept unresolved: Qatada/Kalbi/Muqatil name the Jews, Mujahid/Suddi/Muqatil
  name Kinana and Khuza'a, Tabari's Mujahid names Quraysh. The licensing sentence is in both
  languages.
- la-muhdarun: Mujahid says the jinna are brought to the reckoning; Suddi/Qatada say the
  claimants are brought to punishment. Tabari and Tha'labi (in Qurtubi) prefer punishment on
  the surah's usage (37:57, 37:127). Ma'arif leaves both open.
Hadith: none attached by any fetched tafsir, so none used. The Abu Bakr exchange is marked as
a tafsir report, not a hadith. No asbab.
Dropped: imprecations in the source wording; a memory-based remark on the verb ja'alu.
Themes: tawheed, accountability.
