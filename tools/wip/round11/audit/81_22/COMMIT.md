Card and 8-section bilingual article for 81:22, wa-ma sahibukum bi-majnun
(3 Arabic words). 81:19-21 (noble messenger, Jibril) and 81:23-25 named only
as context; 81:26 cross-referenced by number.

Sources: at-Tabari, Ibn Kathir (ar), al-Qurtubi, al-Baghawi, as-Sa'di,
Muyassar on 81:22; Ma'arif (grouped 81:19-28); Ibn Kathir en (grouped 81:15-24).
- Companion = Muhammad: Tabari ("O people", chain to Maymun b. Mihran), Ibn
  Kathir (ash-Sha'bi, Maymun, Abu Salih), Qurtubi, Sa'di; Baghawi: said to
  the people of Makkah; Muyassar: "Muhammad, whom you know".
- Majnun: speak from madness and rave (Tabari, closing with 37:37 wording);
  suspected in his speech (Qurtubi); "mad, and says it from himself"
  (Baghawi); enemies seeking to extinguish his message, answered by three
  superlatives (Sa'di); rebuttal of enemies' criticism (Ma'arif).
- Answer to the oath: Qurtubi, Baghawi (Qur'an via Jibril + Muhammad not as
  Makkah says); Ma'arif: 81:19-20 subject of oath.
- Angelic and human messenger paired: Sa'di and Ibn Kathir en.
- Ma'arif's note that some read the noble messenger as the Prophet kept.
- Licensing sentence and a note that the verse speaks of a charge, not of
  people living with mental illness, in both languages.
Hadith: none attached by any fetched tafsir; stated in one sentence.
Asbab: Qurtubi's "wa qila" fainting account (no chain) not used.
Dropped: 37:36, 7:184/34:46/23:70/53:2 (not cited), 53:5-16 (81:20/23
ground), al-Batha'/Hira sighting details, Abu Bakr-Musaylima report,
"emphatic ba" (i'rab data silent).
