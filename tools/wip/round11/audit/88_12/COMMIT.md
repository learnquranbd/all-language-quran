Card and 8-section bilingual article for 88:12: fiha 'aynun jariyah,
three words, the flowing spring in the lofty Garden. 88:10-11 named as
context only; 88:13 named for its shared fiha shape, contents not pulled
forward.

Sources: Tabari, Qurtubi, Sa'di, Ibn Kathir (ar + en abridged),
Muyassar. Baghawi's 88:12 slot holds only the verse (88:11 and 88:13
slots checked: no 88:12 note); Ma'arif's 88:11-13 group is all on 88:11.
- 'ayn: genus, many springs (Ibn Kathir: indefinite in an affirmative
  statement; Qurtubi: 'ayn = 'uyun, cf. Surat al-Insan; Sa'di: ism jins).
  Tabari and Muyassar keep the singular. No spring named by anyone.
- jariya: sariha, flowing freely (Ibn Kathir); in no furrow (Tabari);
  gushing water and delicious drinks, on the ground, no furrow (Qurtubi);
  its waters gush (Muyassar); springs they open and direct at will
  (Sa'di). Each attributed, none chosen.
- 88:9 striving in obedience (Muyassar), Sufyan (Ibn Kathir en).
88:5 'aynin aniyah set beside 88:12 as a wording observation, with the
licensing sentence in both languages.
Hadith: none attached. Ibn Kathir's Ibn Abi Hatim report from Abu
Hurayrah named as ungraded and left aside; wording not quoted.
Asbab: none. Dropped: 47:15, 76:5-6, 55:50, 55:66 (no fetched tafsir
cites them); Tirmidhi/Salsabil/Kawthar narrations; any description of
the springs beyond the fetched glosses.
