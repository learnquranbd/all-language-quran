Card and 8-section bilingual article for 88:1: hal ataka hadithu
al-ghashiya, four words, the surah's opening question. 88:2 named only
as the verse that follows; 85:17 (same formula) cross-referenced by
number, its hal ataka discussion not repeated.

Sources: Tabari, Qurtubi, Baghawi, Sa'di, Ibn Kathir (ar + en
abridged), Muyassar. Ma'arif's 88:1 slot holds only 87:18-19 text and
its 88:2 slot starts at 88:2-3: not named. Other slots open on 88:1.
- hal ataka: qad, per Qutrub via 76:1 (Qurtubi), qad ataka (Baghawi);
  kept a question to the Messenger (Muyassar, Tabari's address); not of
  your knowledge (Qurtubi, qila); not before in this detail (Ibn 'Abbas
  per Qurtubi); question meaning "it has now come" (al-Kalbi). None chosen.
- hadith: its story and report (Tabari); khabar (Muyassar).
- al-ghashiya: Day of Resurrection (Ibn Kathir: Ibn 'Abbas, Qatada,
  Ibn Zayd; Qurtubi: most); the Hour (Qatada, Ibn 'Abbas per Tabari);
  the Fire over the faces (Sa'id per Tabari; Sa'id ibn Jubayr, Muhammad
  ibn Ka'b, Abu Salih from Ibn 'Abbas, 14:50, per Qurtubi); covers
  creation, the second blowing, the people of the Fire (Qurtubi, qila).
  Tabari's own call, keep it general, reported as his.
Hadith: Sahih Muslim 878a (al-A'la and al-Ghashiya on both Eids and
Friday) and 878c (ad-Dahhak's question), quoted whole from quranx, cited
by Ibn Kathir. Ibn Abi Hatim's report via 'Amr ibn Maymun ("yes, it has
come to me") given as Ibn Kathir gives it, ungraded, nothing built on it.
Asbab: none. Dropped: Ma'arif; 20:9/79:15/12:107 (no fetched tafsir
cites them); all 88:2-7 material.
Fix: dropped unconfirmed Ibn Abi Hatim/'Amr ibn Maymun report; section renamed.
