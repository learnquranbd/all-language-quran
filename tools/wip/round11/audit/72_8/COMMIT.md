Card and 8-section bilingual article for 72:8: wa-anna lamasna
as-sama'a fa-wajadnaha muli'at harasan shadidan wa-shuhuba. 72:1-2 and
72:7 as frame, 72:9 and 72:10 as the completion and the open question.

Sources: Tabari, Qurtubi, Ibn Kathir (ar; en grouped 72:8-10), Sa'di,
Baghawi, Muyassar, Ma'arif (grouped 72:8-9). All named. Tabari, Qurtubi
and Ibn Kathir (ar) also fetched on 72:9 for the guarding dispute;
flagged as "on the next verse" wherever used.
- Lamasna glosses kept per source: sought and wanted it (Tabari), sought
  its news as was our habit (Qurtubi), sought to reach it to hear its
  inhabitants (Muyassar), came to it and tested it (Sa'di).
- Haras: keepers (Tabari), angels (Qurtubi, Muyassar, Baghawi); al-Kalbi's
  lowest heaven (Baghawi); Qurtubi's singular shadid and masdar option;
  Ma'arif's sama' = cloud.
- Dispute kept with both sides named (Qurtubi on 72:9): begun at the
  mission (al-Kalbi, 'Atiyya from Ibn 'Abbas via al-Bayhaqi, Ibn 'Umar,
  Ibn Sabur, Nafi' ibn Jubayr, Ubayy, al-Jahiz) vs existed and increased
  (the majority per Qurtubi, Aws ibn Hajar, al-Zuhri's "made harsher and
  stricter", Ibn Qutayba). Qurtubi's own preference reported as his;
  Ibn Kathir/as-Suddi and Ma'arif positions added. Article takes none.
Hadith: Sahih al-Bukhari 4921 (Ibn 'Abbas, devils turned back by flames,
Nakhla, Surat al-Jinn revealed), quoted whole from the quranx English;
grading = inclusion in his Sahih. Tabari's Sa'id ibn Jubayr report beside it.
Asbab: not presented as asbab.
Licensing sentence in both languages; no astronomy claim in own voice.
Dropped: Ma'arif's 'A'ishah cloud narration (unconfirmed), Ma'arif's
meteor science, as-Suddi's Ta'if story, 67:5, 37:6-10, 15:17-18.
