Replaces the 728-word, 6-section article with a 1,740-word, 8-section
article, every attribution checked against the fetched tafsir.

Sources: the 71:10 slots of at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi, as-Sa'di, Ibn Kathir
(ar/en), al-Muyassar and Ma'arif, plus the 71:11 and 71:12 slots of
at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi, as-Sa'di and Ibn Kathir, fetched for the istisqa'
and midrar material.

- 71:10 as Nuh's (AS) report of his own call inside the complaint of 71:5-9;
  li-taghfira lahum (71:7) as the stated aim.
- Istighfar as asking plus turning from shirk (at-Tabari, al-Muyassar,
  as-Sa'di, al-Qurtubi, Ibn Kathir); al-Fudayl's "aqilni" via al-Qurtubi.
- Ghaffar as kathir al-maghfira (as-Sa'di); kana = lam yazal (al-Qurtubi).
- 71:11-12 in one section: jussive answer and two readings of as-sama'
  (al-Qurtubi); midrar glosses; Muqatil's drought report and Qatada's
  "greedy for this world" kept side by side as two explanations.
- 'Umar's (RA) istisqa' with istighfar only (Ibn Kathir; at-Tabari's
  ash-Sha'bi chain, majadih of the sky, 11:52 in one line).
- Ma'arif's two readings of min in 71:4 and the people's-rights condition.
- Licensing sentence on Nuh's people in both languages.

Hadith: none; no fetched tafsir attaches one with collection and grading
(al-Qurtubi's Hudhayfa narration has neither and is not quoted).
Dropped: al-Hasan's answers (not in the fetched text); the shipped
article's udder/army roots, its Bukhari "chief of istighfar" hadith (not
attached), and its "weight, gravity" gloss of waqar, which the source
gives as great majesty.
