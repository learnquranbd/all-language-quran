## Hadith
- Jami' at-Tirmidhi 3328 — URL https://quranx.com/hadith/Tirmidhi/Hadith-3328 (saved: hadith_Tirmidhi_3328.txt).
  Page ref: Dar-us-Salam Vol 6, Book 44, Hadith 3328. Narrator Anas ibn Malik.
  Wording used (article's rendering of the Arabic matn, whole): "Allah, Mighty and Majestic, said: I am worthy to be feared; so whoever fears Me..."
  Arabic first ten words of matn: قَالَ اللَّهُ عَزَّ وَجَلَّ أَنَا أَهْلٌ أَنْ أُتَّقَى فَمَنْ اتَّقَانِي فَلَمْ يَجْعَلْ
  Grading (collector's own, on page): هَذَا حَدِيثٌ حَسَنٌ غَرِيبٌ وَسُهَيْلٌ لَيْسَ بِالْقَوِيِّ فِي الْحَدِيثِ وَقَدْ تَفَرَّدَ بِهَذَا الْحَدِيثِ عَنْ ثَابِتٍ — reported exactly (hasan gharib; Suhayl not strong; alone from Thabit). Not upgraded.
  Note: page English says "Blessed is He and Most High"; Arabic has عز وجل — article follows the Arabic ("Mighty and Majestic").
- Ahmad, Ibn Majah, an-Nasa'i versions: named only as Ibn Kathir reports their transmission (all via Suhayl); not fetched, no wording quoted, no numbers given.
- Ahmad's "recited the verse" detail: from Ibn Kathir's quoted Ahmad chain (t14), described, not quoted as hadith text.
