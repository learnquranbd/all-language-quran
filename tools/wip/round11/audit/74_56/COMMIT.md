Card and 8-section bilingual article for 74:56, the closing verse of
al-Muddaththir: remembering depends on Allah's will; He is ahl al-taqwa
and ahl al-maghfira. 74:49-55 kept as context only.

Sources: Tabari, Qurtubi, Ibn Kathir (ar), Sa'di, Baghawi, Muyassar
(grouped 74:54-56), Ma'arif. All named; 169 was the wrong group, unused.
- yadhkuruna/tadhkuruna: ya' = general readers (Qurtubi), "the others"
  (Baghawi); ta' = Nafi' and Ya'qub (both). Abu 'Ubayd chose ya' (74:53),
  Abu Hatim chose ta' (more general), per Qurtubi; unranked, agreed on
  the light form.
- Will: Tabari (no one is able except as Allah enables), Muqatil via
  Baghawi and Muyassar (wills guidance for them), Ibn Kathir's pairing with
  76:30. Sa'di's reply to the Qadariyya and Jabriyya kept, attributed.
- Ahl al-taqwa: Qatada (Tabari, two chains; Ibn Kathir), Baghawi, Tabari,
  Sa'di, Muyassar, Ma'arif.
- Ahl al-maghfira: forgiveness tied to repentance/fear/faith (Tabari, Ibn
  Kathir, Baghawi, Sa'di, Muyassar) vs Muhammad ibn Nasr via Qurtubi (even
  one who fell short of fear) and Ma'arif (whenever He wishes); kept as a
  difference, Qurtubi does not choose.
Hadith: Tirmidhi 3328 (Anas), matn quoted whole from the Arabic; graded
by at-Tirmidhi hasan gharib, Suhayl not strong, alone from Thabit, reported
exactly. Ahmad/Ibn Majah/Nasa'i named only as Ibn Kathir lists them.
Asbab: none in any fetched text; stated in the article.
Dropped: Baghawi's variant wording of the hadith, Ibn Majah/Nasa'i
numbers (unfetched).
