Card and 8-section bilingual article on wa qawma Nuhin min qablu,
innahum kanu hum azlama wa atgha. 53:50-51 and 53:53-54 pointers only.

Sources: at-Tabari (with Qatada, two chains), al-Qurtubi, al-Baghawi,
Ibn Kathir (ar), as-Sa'di, al-Muyassar (grouped 53:51-54), Ma'arif
(grouped 53:50-52). Ibn Kathir en abridged: wrong group, unused.
- Verb ahlaka carried over from 53:50 (Tabari, Qurtubi, Baghawi);
  "before" = before 'Ad and Thamud (Ibn Kathir: before these).
- Glosses: Tabari azlam = wronging themselves, greater kufr; atgha =
  transgression and rebellion against God. Ibn Kathir one phrase
  (ashaddu tamarrudan); Muyassar two; Baghawi 'utuww.
- More than whom: those destroyed after them (Tabari, Ibn Kathir,
  Muyassar, Sa'di) vs no people at all (Qatada) vs all three nations
  over the Arab polytheists (Qurtubi's qila, with his consolation for
  the Prophet ﷺ). Kept open.
- Reason: length of the call and their insolence (Baghawi), length of
  Nuh's stay (Qurtubi), 950 years of calling (Qatada; the words of
  29:14, stated as such). Father-and-son scene in Qatada and Qurtubi,
  differences noted.
Licensing sentence in both languages.
Hadith: none attached; said so. Asbab: none.
Dropped: their harm to Nuh (AS) as a reason (no fetched source); flood
retelling; 71:x, 54:9-10, 11:36 (not cited by any fetched tafsir).
29:14 named because Qatada's phrase as Tabari quotes it equals that verse (checked); no tafsir cites the number.
