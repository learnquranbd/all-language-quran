Replaces the 673-word, 6-section article with a 1,775-word, 8-section deep article, built only on tafsir fetched for 90:4 (shipped card kept).

Sources: at-Tabari, Ibn Kathir (ar; en abridged for 90:1-10), al-Qurtubi,
as-Sa'di, al-Baghawi, al-Muyassar (90:2-4), Ma'arif al-Qur'an (90:4-7 group).

Oath: answer to the oaths of 90:1-3 (at-Tabari, Qatadah, al-Qurtubi); Muyassar's
Makkah and Adam. kabad occurs only here (verified by search of the text);
al-Qurtubi's root (shidda; milk, liver) and Labid's line (at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi).
kabad, kept as a disagreement: hardship (Ibn Abbas, al-Hasan, Qatadah, Ikrimah,
Sa'id b. Jubayr, Mujahid with 46:15; al-Qurtubi's "our scholars" catalogue and
its proof of a Creator, echoed by Ma'arif; Yaman's weakest creature) and
at-Tabari's choice of it via Labid; upright stature (Ibn Abbas in one report,
Ikrimah, Ibrahim, Ibn Shaddad, Abu Salih, ad-Dahhak; Ibn Kathir with 82:6-7 and
95:4; al-Qurtubi's favour in creation, Ibn Kaysan; al-Baqir's assent). Minor:
Ibn Zayd's sky, Muqatil's strength, al-Hasan's gratitude and patience.
as-Sa'di holds both readings without choosing.
al-insan: son of Adam (al-Qurtubi); the Abu al-Ashaddayn report (al-Kalbi in
al-Qurtubi; Muqatil in al-Baghawi, naming Usayd b. Kaladah) marked as reports,
ungraded, with a licensing sentence in both languages.
90:5-10 from Ibn Kathir (al-Hasan, Qatadah, Mujahid, Ibn Mas'ud, 76:2-3);
90:11-17 in one paragraph from the verse text.
Hadith: none attached to the verse; said in one sentence.
Dropped from the old article (unsourced): the preposition "in, not for" point,
the indefinite-noun claim, "liver at the middle of a person", the 84:6 link.
