## Disagreements kept
- mafatih: keys (al-Qurtubi's preferred reading: the routes to the unseen) vs storehouses (as-Suddi via at-Tabari; al-Baghawi; Muyassar); Ma'arif: same outcome.
- Content of the keys: five (Ibn 'Abbas via at-Tabari; the hadith) vs al-Baghawi's list ('Ata', states and endings, what has not yet been); Muyassar "including" the five.
- al-barr/al-bahr: Mujahid (via al-Baghawi) desert vs towns; or the known land and sea.
- ratb/yabis: Ibn 'Abbas, 'Ata', "living/dead", "everything" (al-Baghawi).
- kitab mubin: Lawh vs Divine knowledge (Ma'arif).
- Why write it: at-Tabari (test of the recording angels; 45:29 per "qila"; or another reason) vs al-Qurtubi (lesson for angels; "qila" magnification).

Grading: in al-Bukhari's Sahih; no further grading claimed.
