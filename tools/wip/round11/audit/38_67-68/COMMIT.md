Card and 8-section bilingual article for 38:67-68: qul huwa naba'un 'azim,
antum 'anhu mu'ridun, read inside the warner frame of 38:65 and 38:70 and
before the account of the exalted assembly at 38:69.

Sources: Tabari, Qurtubi, Sa'di, Baghawi, Ibn Kathir (ar + en abridged),
Muyassar. Ma'arif (grouped 38:65-68) not used; its surah plan is already in
the shipped 38:65 article.
- Tabari: the Qur'an, answering the deniers' "ikhtilaq" of 38:7; Mujahid and
  as-Suddi with chains; Shurayh's courtroom report "is the Qur'an not a naba'?"
- Qurtubi: reckoning, reward and punishment (Qatada's meaning), parallel
  78:1-2; also the Qur'an (Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatada) and "great in benefit".
- Sa'di: resurrection and recompense; turning away "as though no reckoning";
  bridge to 38:69 as proof of truthfulness.
- Baghawi: the Qur'an (Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatada); "qila" al-qiyama, 78:1-2.
- Ibn Kathir: Allah's sending of the Messenger; reports Mujahid, Shurayh and
  as-Suddi for the Qur'an; mu'ridun as ghafilun.
- Muyassar: the Qur'an, great in benefit; heedless, not acting on it.
Disagreement kept, no position: Qur'an vs reckoning/Resurrection vs the
sending; Qatada on both sides in Qurtubi, reported as found.
Hadith: none attached by any fetched source; said in one sentence. The
Shurayh report is marked as an athar, not a hadith, ungraded. Asbab: none.
Overlap: 38:65's warner and names, 38:76 and 38:78 cross-referenced only.
Framing: licensing sentence in both languages (the deniers addressed).
Dropped: the tawhid reading (in no fetched text; article says so), any
detail of Shurayh's case. Arabic counts: four + three words. Themes:
guidance, hereafter.
