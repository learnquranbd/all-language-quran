## The naba' disagreement (kept, no position)
- The Qur'an: at-Tabari (own view + Mujahid, as-Suddi, Shurayh), al-Muyassar, al-Baghawi first (Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatada), al-Qurtubi second (same three names); Ibn Kathir ar reports it from Mujahid, Shurayh, as-Suddi.
- Reckoning / Resurrection: al-Qurtubi first (meaning given to Qatada; parallel 78:1-2), as-Sa'di, al-Baghawi second ("qila", al-qiyama; parallel 78:1-2).
- The sending of the Messenger: Ibn Kathir's own gloss (ar and en abridged).
- Also kept: "great in benefit" (Muyassar; al-Qurtubi "qila").
- Qatada appears on both sides in al-Qurtubi; reported as found, not reconciled (§5.3).

## Mu'ridun glosses
Ibn Kathir ghafilun; at-Tabari munsarifun + not acting + not believing; Muyassar ghafilun munsarifun, not acting; as-Sa'di "as though no reckoning". Stated as differing in measure.

## Hadith
None. No fetched source attaches a hadith of the Prophet to 38:67-68; said in one sentence (§8.1). hadith.js not run (nothing to confirm).
The Shurayh report is an athar in at-Tabari with its chain (Ya'qub b. Ibrahim – Hushaym – Hisham – Ibn Sirin – Shurayh); presented as a report about a judge's use of the verse, explicitly not a hadith, no grading claimed (at-Tabari gives none).

## Licensing sentence
Present in both languages (§8.1): the verses address the Prophet's people who denied him (at-Tabari) and license nothing against any living person or community.
