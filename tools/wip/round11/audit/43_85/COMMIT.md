Card and 8-section bilingual article for 43:85: wa tabaraka alladhi lahu
mulku as-samawati wa-l-ardi wa ma baynahuma wa 'indahu 'ilmu as-sa'ah wa
ilayhi turja'un. 43:81-84 as placement only (43:81 not re-argued);
43:86 named once for intercession; 41:47 a one-line pointer.

Sources: Tabari, Muyassar, Ibn Kathir (ar), Qurtubi, Sa'di, Baghawi.
Ma'arif (grouped) silent on 43:85, said so; IK (en) duplicates IK (ar).
- Tabaraka glossed only as fetched: Qurtubi (tafa'ul from baraka),
  Muyassar and Sa'di (abundance of good, wide attributes, great kingdom),
  IK (freedom from faults settled for Him); framed as two emphases, no
  position.
- Mulk: IK creator/owner/disposer without resistance; Tabari sultan,
  decree executed; IK's naqdan wa ibraman noted as sharing a root with
  43:79, as the article's observation.
- Tabari's partner question and IK's "above a child" attributed; licensing
  sentence in sec 4, EN and BN.
- Hour: Tabari/Muyassar define it; Qurtubi time of its coming; IK in the
  wording of 7:187; Sa'di's hasr from the fronted 'indahu.
- Readings kept as reported, with the difference: Qurtubi gives yurja'un
  to Ibn Kathir, Hamza, al-Kisa'i; Baghawi to Ibn Kathir and al-Kisa'i
  only. Qurtubi's fatha reading (Ibn Muhaysin, Humayd, Ya'qub, Ibn Abi
  Ishaq) reported. Reciter Ibn Kathir distinguished from the mufassir.
- Requital in near-identical words (Tabari, IK, Muyassar); Sa'di's just
  judgment and both-worlds ownership.
Hadith: none attached in any source; said in one sentence. Bukhari 50
and 31:34 left to 41:47.
Asbab: none in the sources; not used.
Dropped: Ahsanul Bayaan note (register check only); no Hour dates/signs.
- Review fix: the reciter Ibn Kathir sentence now rests on al-Qurtubi's own verb qara'a, not on memory (en+bn).
