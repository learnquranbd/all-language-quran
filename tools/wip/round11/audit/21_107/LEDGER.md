## Disagreement kept (no position taken by the article)
- Mercy to believer and disbeliever: Ibn Abbas (at-Tabari x2, Ibn Kathir, al-Qurtubi, al-Baghawi); at-Tabari judges it sounder (reported as his tarjih).
- Mercy to believers only: Ibn Zayd (at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi, al-Baghawi).
- Offered-to-all / received-by-some framing: Ibn Kathir, as-Sa'di, Muyassar; Ibn Kathir also adds Ibn Abbas's reprieve.
- 'alamin widths: all people (Muyassar; Ibn Abbas in al-Qurtubi), those of creation he was sent to (at-Tabari), all creatures incl. jinn (Ma'arif), believers only (Ibn Zayd).
- "Rahmatan" grammar (he is the mercy vs sent because of mercy): fetched texts speak of him as the mercy (Ibn Kathir, as-Sa'di, al-Baghawi); none discusses the grammar; the article says so in one sentence.

## Hadith
- Muslim 2599, https://quranx.com/hadith/Muslim/Hadith-2599 (saved: hadith_Muslim_2599.txt). First ten words: "Abu Huraira reported it was said to Allah's Messenger (ﷺ): Invoke". Quoted whole. Grading: in Sahih Muslim (collector's inclusion); Ibn Kathir: انفرد بإخراجه مسلم. Attached to 21:107 by Ibn Kathir (ar and en).
- "Innama ana rahmatun muhdat": carried by al-Baghawi (no chain), Ma'arif (Ibn Asakir), Ibn Kathir; NOT used as a hadith. Article reports Ibn Kathir's record of al-Harbi and al-Bukhari (mursal via Hafs b. Ghiyath) and the Ibn Umar route through an unnamed man.
