Replaces the 737-word, 5-section article with a 1,677-word, 8-section depth
article built only from the tafsir fetched for 21:107.

Sources: at-Tabari, Ibn Kathir (ar and en), al-Qurtubi, al-Baghawi, as-Sa'di,
al-Muyassar and Ma'arif al-Qur'an, all on 21:107.

Disagreement kept: Ibn Abbas (via Sa'id b. Jubayr, in at-Tabari, Ibn Kathir,
al-Qurtubi, al-Baghawi) makes the mercy reach believer and disbeliever, the
latter spared the swallowing and stoning of earlier nations; Ibn Zayd (in
at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi, al-Baghawi) limits al-'alamin to those who believed,
citing 21:111. At-Tabari's preference for Ibn Abbas is reported as his; the
article decides nothing. Ibn Kathir, as-Sa'di and the Muyassar frame the mercy
as offered to all and received by some (14:28-29, 41:44).

'Alamin glosses: all people (Muyassar), those he was sent to (at-Tabari), all
creatures including jinn (Ma'arif).

Hadith: Muslim 2599 (not sent as an invoker of curse), quoted whole, as
attached by Ibn Kathir. "Rahmatun muhdat" is not used as a hadith; Ibn
Kathir's record of its disputed chain (al-Harbi, al-Bukhari: mursal) is
reported instead.

Dropped: Salman narration (unverified), at-Tabarani's Abu Jahl report, Ma'arif
on jihad as mercy, and the old article's 9:128, 3:159, 34:28, 7:158, Ta'if
and animal hadiths, none attached by a fetched tafsir.
Defect fixed: the old article gave Ibn Abbas's reading as the commentators'
consensus and omitted Ibn Zayd; it also called unchecked hadiths sound.
Licensing sentence added in both languages. Shipped card kept.
