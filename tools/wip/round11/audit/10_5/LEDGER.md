## Disagreements kept
- qaddarahu: moon alone (Ibn Kathir, Muyassar, Ma'arif after weighing) vs both, singular for one (at-Tabari's second wajh, al-Qurtubi's and al-Baghawi's first qila). At-Tabari, al-Qurtubi, al-Baghawi do not choose.
- dhalika: the sun, moon and their stations (at-Tabari) vs the creating and measuring (al-Baghawi).
- diya'/nur: synonyms (lexicographers per Ma'arif) vs strength distinction (az-Zamakhshari, at-Tibi) vs Ibn Kathir's "two kinds" vs al-Qurtubi's definitions; intrinsic/derived reading reported with Ma'arif's rejection.
- Readings: yufassilu (Ibn Kathir the reciter, Abu 'Amr, Hafs, Ya'qub; preferred by Abu 'Ubayd, Abu Hatim) vs nufassilu (the rest); tufassalu from Ibn as-Samayfa' (al-Qurtubi only).
