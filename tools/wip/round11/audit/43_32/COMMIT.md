Sources: at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi, Ibn Kathir (ar, en abridged), al-Muyassar on 43:32;
as-Sa'di and al-Baghawi partly filed under 43:31 (slot shift; fetched as t91_31,
t94_31); as-Sa'di's 43:32 slot is his 43:33 text; Ma'arif used for the licence only.
- Objection of 43:31: two towns (Ibn Kathir); the named men kept as differing reports:
  Qatada, Mujahid, Ibn 'Abbas (al-Baghawi), Ibn 'Abbas via ad-Dahhak (at-Tabari);
  one line each on 43:22, 43:24, 43:30.
- rahmata rabbik = prophethood (al-Muyassar, al-Qurtubi, as-Sa'di); at-Tabari's
  wider honour/favour; Ibn Kathir and as-Sa'di on the wording of 6:124.
- Smaller-to-larger argument: al-Qurtubi, as-Sa'di, al-Baghawi, at-Tabari; Qatada's
  observation on provision and eloquence; ma'ayishahum reading (al-Qurtubi).
- Degrees: lists of Ibn Kathir, al-Muyassar, at-Tabari, al-Baghawi, al-Qurtubi.
- sukhriyya kept as a disagreement with no pick: service (at-Tabari's own, as-Suddi,
  Ibn Zayd), ownership (Qatada, ad-Dahhak; Ibn Kathir folds it into the first),
  mockery (al-Qurtubi, "it was said"); al-Akhfash and the kasra reading of Ibn
  Muhaysin and Mujahid, not tied to the mockery sense.
- Closing mercy: Paradise (at-Tabari, Qatada, as-Suddi, al-Muyassar, al-Baghawi),
  general mercy (Ibn Kathir), al-Qurtubi's four views, as-Sa'di with 10:58.
- 43:33-35 in two lines (Ibn Kathir, as-Sa'di); 43:36 one line.
- Hadith: none attached to 43:32 by any tafsir; one sentence says so.
- Licence sentence in both languages; application on contentment and mutual service.
- Dropped from the old article: "bank balance", nahnu-restriction, ma'ishah from
  'aysh, builder/farmer example. Fixed: the old article said the commentators read
  sukhriyya as employment rather than ownership; Qatada and ad-Dahhak read ownership.
