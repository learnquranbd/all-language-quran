## Glosses named
- la tahinu: la tad'ufu (Mujahid, ar-Rabi', Ibn Ishaq, Ibn Kathir, al-Muyassar); + la tajbunu (al-Baghawi, al-Qurtubi); bodies (as-Sa'di); Qatada's 'ajz/wahn in pursuit; al-Hasan's press on.
- la tahzanu: la ta'saw fa-tajza'u (at-Tabari); la ta'saw (Ibn Ishaq); two objects (al-Qurtubi); hearts (as-Sa'di); past vs future (Ma'arif).
- al-a'lawn: outcome/victory (at-Tabari, Ibn Ishaq, Ibn Kathir, al-Muyassar, al-Baghawi, al-Qurtubi); in faith and hope of help and reward (as-Sa'di); physical height on the mountain (Ibn 'Abbas / Ibn Jurayj reports); derived from al-A'la, echo of 20:68, ummah's virtue (al-Qurtubi, attributed).
- in kuntum mu'minin: condition (at-Tabari, Ibn Ishaq, al-Qurtubi, al-Muyassar, Ma'arif) vs in = idh, causal (al-Baghawi; al-Qurtubi with qila). Kept as a difference, no position.
- Licensing sentence present in 7.3, both languages.

## Disagreements kept
- Occasion: Ibn 'Abbas / Ibn Jurayj (during the fighting, Khalid on the mountain) vs al-Kalbi in al-Baghawi (after Uhud, at the order to pursue, 4:104). Both kept, no position.
- Particle in: conditional vs causal (above).
