Replaces the 946-word, 6-section article with a 1,741-word, 8-section
one, from at-Tabari, Ibn Kathir (ar and en), al-Qurtubi, as-Sa'di,
al-Baghawi, al-Muyassar and Ma'arif on 3:139. All slots checked. The
shipped card is kept.

Placement: 3:137-3:138 before, 3:140 named only as the next verse.
Setting: at-Tabari's ta'ziya for Uhud and az-Zuhri's report (3:139 to 3:154).
la tahinu: la tad'ufu, la tajbunu (al-Baghawi, al-Qurtubi), Qatada, al-Hasan.
la tahzanu: at-Tabari's ta'saw fa-tajza'u, al-Qurtubi's two objects,
as-Sa'di's body/heart split, Ma'arif's future/past.
al-a'lawn: outcome (six sources), as-Sa'di's "in faith", al-Qurtubi's
al-A'la root and 20:68 echo, attributed.
Kept as disagreements: occasion (Ibn 'Abbas/Ibn Jurayj on the mountain
vs al-Kalbi on the order to pursue, 4:104); in as condition vs as idh.
Licensing sentence in both languages; no superiority claim in own voice.
Hadith: Bukhari 3039 (al-Bara'), core Hubal / "Allah is Higher and more
Majestic" exchange quoted, marked as part of a longer hadith and as not
cited by the tafsirs nor the occasion.
Dropped: casualty figures (length), al-Qurtubi's conquests claim,
Ma'arif's "balance tilted", "war goes in turns" (3:140's ground).
Corrected: "not a verb promising a future victory ... present standing"
(most sources read outcome, several supply a future); Muslim/Anas,
Abu Dawud/Thawban, "third year after Hijrah" (not in fetched sources).
