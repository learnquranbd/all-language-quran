Replaces the 671-word, 5-section article with a 1,777-word, 9-section one,
built only from the eight tafsir files fetched for 64:11 (shipped card kept).

Sources: at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi, as-Sa'di, Ibn Kathir (ar; en abridged,
grouped 64:11-13), al-Baghawi, Muyassar, Ma'arif. Ibn Kathir's tie to 57:22.
Bi-idhni-llah glosses: Ibn 'Abbas (command; decree and will), at-Tabari,
al-Baghawi, al-Qurtubi, al-Farra', "by His knowledge" (qila), Muyassar;
as-Sa'di on the scope and the servant's duty. Asbab (the claim that truth
would spare the Muslims) only as al-Qurtubi's qila, read as a possible
setting. Belief as knowing the blow is by His leave (at-Tabari, al-Baghawi,
al-Qurtubi); Ibn Kathir's compensation in the heart.
Yahdi qalbah readings kept side by side, none chosen: Ibn 'Abbas via 'Ali
b. Abi Talhah (certainty), 'Alqamah (content and submits), Sa'id b. Jubayr
and Muqatil b. Hayyan (istirja', 2:156), plus al-Qurtubi's list (patience,
firmness, Sunnah, al-Kalbi, reward). Qira'at from al-Qurtubi: yuhda
(as-Sulami, Qatada), nahdi (Talhah, al-A'raj), yahda' (Ikrimah, Malik b.
Dinar). As-Sa'di: the guided heart at rest, 39:10, the self left to itself,
the general sense with 14:27; Muyassar on guidance rooted in the heart.

Hadith: Muslim 2999 (Suhayb, amazing is the affair of the believer), which
Ibn Kathir attaches as agreed upon in another wording; Muslim's wording
quoted whole. Ahmad's 'Ubada report not used (Ibn Kathir: lam yukharrijuh).

Dropped from the old article: musibah/sawab etymology, permission not as
approval, 57:23 and 9:51, 'Alqamah as Ibn Mas'ud's student, the unsourced
"order of operations" and tawakkul readings.
