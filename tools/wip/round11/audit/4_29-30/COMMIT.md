Assigned 4:29, widened to 4:29-30 (wealth by falsehood, trade by consent, do not
kill yourselves; 4:30's "that" and its threat). 4:26-28 used as verse context only;
4:31-35 left free. New card and a 1,742-word, 9-section article.

Sources: at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi, Ibn Kathir (ar + en), as-Sa'di, al-Baghawi,
al-Muyassar, Ma'arif on 4:29 and 4:30 (all slots on target).
- batil: at-Tabari, as-Suddi, al-Baghawi's list; Ibn Kathir's stratagems; Ibn
  Abbas's returned-garment case; as-Sa'di on squandering one's own wealth.
- Consent: munqati' (Ibn Kathir, al-Qurtubi); both readings (at-Tabari); as-Sa'di's
  conditions (no riba, choice, known and deliverable, word or act); Ma'arif on
  consent under pressure (hoarding, dower, bribe).
- anfusakum: one another (at-Tabari, as-Suddi, 'Ata', al-Hasan, al-Qurtubi's
  consensus), oneself (al-Baghawi, as-Sa'di, Ma'arif), ruin (Abu 'Ubayda with
  2:195, as-Sa'di, al-Qurtubi on risk out of greed).
Kept as differences: eating at others' tables and 24:61 ('Ikrima, al-Hasan)
against at-Tabari's rejection; khiyar al-majlis (Shurayh, ash-Sha'bi, al-Shafi'i,
Ahmad and others against Malik, Abu Hanifa, Abu Yusuf and Muhammad; at-Tabari and
al-Qurtubi prefer the first; the article takes no side); spoken acceptance vs
sale by action (Ibn Kathir); what "that" in 4:30 points to ('Ata', as-Sa'di,
al-Muyassar, at-Tabari's choice from 4:19).
Hadith: Abu Dawud 334 ('Amr ibn al-'As, tayammum on a cold night) quoted whole,
no grading by Abu Dawud; 335's Abu Qays chain and Ibn Kathir's preference for it
stated. Bukhari 2079 (Hakim ibn Hizam) quoted whole. Suicide narrations referred
to, not quoted; no verdict on anyone's end; the section closes on "kana bikum
rahima" and on seeking trusted people and qualified professionals.
Licensing sentence on 4:30's threat in both languages.
Dropped: the mursal "no Muslim may cheat a Muslim"; unconfirmed trader narrations
(Tirmidhi, ad-Daraqutni, al-Isfahani); Ma'arif's modern-finance examples (reader
referred to a scholar); al-Qurtubi's 'urban and ghabn discussions.
