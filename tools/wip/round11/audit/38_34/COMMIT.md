Card and 8-section bilingual article for 38:34: the trial of Sulayman, the body
placed on his chair, and thumma anab, read between the praise of 38:30 and the
promise of 38:40.

Sources: Tabari, Qurtubi, Ibn Kathir (ar), Baghawi, Sa'di, Muyassar, Ma'arif.
- Fatanna: all gloss as ibtila'; Ibn Kathir, Baghawi, Sa'di specify loss of the
  kingdom; Qurtubi "ibtalayna wa-'aqabna" and Zamakhshari's 20+20 years.
- Causes: Sa'di's "khalal" and two of Qurtubi's reports (Ibn Abbas via Ibn Jubayr;
  Ibn al-Musayyab), attributed, noted as ungraded with no chain to the Prophet.
- Jasad, every reading named with its collector, no position: devil (Tabari's own
  view, Ibn Kathir's list, Sa'di; Qurtubi "most", Baghawi "most famous"); half child
  (Muyassar adopts; Baghawi qila; Qurtubi via an-Naqqash); son dead in the clouds
  (Qurtubi, ash-Sha'bi); Asif ibn Barkhiya (Qurtubi, Baghawi); Sulayman ill
  (Qurtubi qila; Ma'arif credits ar-Razi, calls it conjecture). No source makes the
  body an image; the image appears only as a reported cause (Wahb).
- Ring stories: Ibn Kathir's grading kept as given (all Isra'iliyyat; Ibn Abbas
  chain strong but taken from the People of the Book; munkar re his wives);
  Qurtubi's weakening, al-Hasan via Baghawi, Ma'arif's rejection as tafsir.
- Anaba: returned to his kingdom (Tabari, Ibn Kathir, Baghawi) vs returned to
  Allah and repented (Qurtubi, Sa'di, Muyassar); both kept.
Hadith: Bukhari 6639 (Abu Hurayrah, ninety women), quoted whole; placement here
disputed (Muyassar, Baghawi, Qurtubi vs Ma'arif), both kept; wording does not name
the verse.
Cross-refs: 34:12 (ifrit hadith on 38:35, not repeated), 2:102 (wa ma kafara).
Framing: no lapse in the article's voice; licensing sentence in both languages.
Dropped: other reported causes, Jabir ring-inscription report, Ka'b's throne
description (Ibn Kathir: very strange), ring-story detail. Asbab: none.
Arabic counts: 38:34 nine words, 38:35 fifteen. Themes: patience, mercy.
