Card and 8-section bilingual article for 108:1: inna a'taynaka
al-Kawthar, three words. 108:2 and 108:3 named as context only.

Sources: Tabari, Qurtubi, Ibn Kathir (ar + en abridged), Sa'di,
Muyassar, Baghawi, Ma'arif on 108:1. Slot check: all slots discuss 108:1.
- Address: "O Muhammad" (Tabari), "O Prophet" (Muyassar), a favour
  recalled (Sa'di). Reading antaynaka (al-Hasan, Talha; Umm Salama)
  from Qurtubi. No tafsir comments on the tense; none claimed.
- Kawthar as fawʿal from kathra and its Arabic uses (Qurtubi, Baghawi).
- River (Ibn Umar, Ibn Abbas, Aisha, Anas, Mujahid, Abu al-'Aliya) vs
  abundant good (Ibn Abbas via Sa'id, Sa'id, Ikrima, Mujahid, Qatada)
  vs pool ('Ata'); Qurtubi's sixteen sayings. Sa'id's "river is part
  of the good" kept as Sa'id's; Muharib's reply kept as recorded.
- Tabari prefers the river; Ibn Kathir the inclusive good; Qurtubi the
  river and pool; Ma'arif joins them. None chosen.
Hadith quoted whole: Bukhari 4964, 4965 (Aisha's own word), 4966;
Tirmidhi 3361 (hasan sahih, his grading). Muslim 400 too long to quote
whole: named, not quoted, not clipped.
Makki/Madani and the 108:3 occasion reports kept as disagreements;
licensing sentence in both languages. No shipped overlap.
Dropped: tense gloss, Ahmad's variants, Thawban's pool report, the
"fingers in the ears" report, Qurtubi on the four caliphs.
