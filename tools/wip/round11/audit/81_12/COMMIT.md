Card and 8-section bilingual article for 81:12, wa-idha al-jahimu su''irat
(3 Arabic words), the eleventh of the surah's twelve idha clauses. 81:13
named as the next verse and counterpart only; 81:14 named as the answer,
not treated; 81:1 and 81:8 by number only.

Sources: at-Tabari, Ibn Kathir (ar), al-Qurtubi, al-Baghawi, as-Sa'di on
81:12. Muyassar and Ibn Kathir en (grouped 81:1-10) not used; Ma'arif
text stops at 81:11.
- Glosses side by side, none chosen: heated (as-Suddi via Ibn Kathir),
  kindled (Qatada), kindled so heated (Tabari), kindled and set raging,
  heat increased (Qurtubi), blazes as never before (Sa'di).
- Qatada: stoked by the anger of Allah and the sins of the children of
  Adam (Tabari with chain, Ibn Kathir, Qurtubi).
- Readings: doubled = kindled time after time (Tabari, Qurtubi); reader
  lists differ (Tabari Madinah/Kufa; Qurtubi Nafi', Ibn Dhakwan, Ruways;
  Baghawi Madinah, Sham, Hafs from 'Asim). Kept as given.
- For the disbelievers (Qurtubi) / enemies of Allah (Baghawi); licensing
  sentence added. No fetched tafsir glosses al-jahim itself; none supplied.
Hadith: Tirmidhi 2591 via hadith.js (Abu Hurayra, kindled a thousand
years red, white, black; cited by Qurtubi), quoted whole with at-Tirmidhi's
own verdict that the mawquf is more correct; stated as not established
as the Prophet's words. No sound hadith attached; said so.
Asbab: none given; not used.
Dropped: "fire of Adam's children" narrations, 25:12, 67:7-8, 17:97 (not
cited by fetched tafsirs); memory-sourced biographical asides.
