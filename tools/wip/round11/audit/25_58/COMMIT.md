Sources: at-Tabari (lasting life with no death; trust, hand over, submit, be patient
"in the matter of your Lord"; hasbuka: He suffices you as knower of His creation's sins,
counted until the Day of Resurrection); as-Sa'di (complete, absolute life; matters
concerning you and creation); the Muyassar; Ibn Kathir (in all your affairs; 57:3;
make Him your store and refuge; 5:67; join hamd to tasbih; sincerity in worship and
reliance with 73:9, 11:123, 67:29; knowledge from which no atom's weight escapes;
25:59 read as the same Ever-Living Creator); al-Qurtubi (tawakkul as the heart's
dependence, means taken without depending on them).
Disagreement kept on sabbih: worship in gratitude (at-Tabari), prayer (al-Baghawi,
al-Qurtubi's qila), tanzih from the partners (al-Qurtubi, Muyassar), the spoken
subhanallah/alhamdulillah (al-Baghawi's qila); no position.
Hadith: Bukhari 794 ('A'isha), "Subhanaka-llahumma rabbana wa bi-hamdika,
Allahumma-ghfir li", quoted whole; Ibn Kathir attaches its first half. Ibn Kathir's
Salman report ("prostrate to the Living who does not die") given only as his citation
with his grading (mursal hasan), marked unconfirmed in any collection.
No asbab.
