## Qur'an cross-references (verse text, checked in data/quran-json/16.json and data/translations; not attributed to any mufassir)
- 16:44 li-tubayyina lin-nas (same root b-y-n), 16:64 li-tubayyina lahum alladhi-khtalafu fih, 16:92 wa la-yubayyinanna lakum yawm al-qiyamati ma kuntum fihi takhtalifun, 16:35 al-balagh al-mubin. 16:38 and 16:40 used as one pointer each.

## Hadith
None. No fetched tafsir attaches a hadith to 16:39; the article says so in one sentence (section 6, para 2). Qatada's line in at-Tabari is identified as his own comment. No hadith fetched.

## Asbab an-nuzul
Not used; none in the fetched texts.

## Own-text observations (from the wording, not attributed)
- Two purpose clauses joined by wa; 11 Arabic words (PREP count).
- Tense: li-ya'lama (future knowing) vs kanu kadhibin (past lying).
- First clause broad ("them"), second narrowed to alladhina kafaru.

## Disagreements kept, no position
- What they differed about: the resurrection (al-Qurtubi, Muyassar, at-Tabari) vs every matter (Ibn Kathir "min kulli shay'", as-Sa'di great and small questions); plus al-Qurtubi's "wa qila" construction attaching li-yubayyina to 16:36.
- Scope of "liars": in the oath against resurrection (Ibn Kathir, at-Tabari, Muyassar, al-Qurtubi) vs as-Sa'di's wider exposure of their worship. Article says it does not choose.

## Licensing
Section 7 para 1, both languages: describes those who swore against the resurrection; licenses nothing against any living person or community.

## Dropped
- 169's "Day of Calling (between the people of Fire and of Paradise)" bracket gloss (translator's addition; kept only "Day of Calling" from tafsir 14's يوم التناد).
- 169's 16:40 material (54:50, 31:28, "the command once") beyond the one pointer.
- Any eschatological detail beyond Ibn Kathir's driving to the Fire + 52:14-16 and as-Sa'di's list.
- Any identification of who "they" were historically beyond the texts.
