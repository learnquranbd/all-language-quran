Card and bilingual article (8 sections, 1,730 EN words) for "that He may
make clear to them what they differed about, and that those who
disbelieved may know they were liars", read as the purpose of the
resurrection the deniers swore against in 16:38.

Sources: Ibn Kathir (His wisdom in the Return; lahum = people; every
dispute, 53:31; liars in their oaths; driven to the Fire, 52:14-16);
at-Tabari (restored "Allah will surely raise ... to make clear"; deniers
and others who differ; reviving creation after perishing; Qatada: people
in general); al-Qurtubi (li-yubayyina = li-yuzhira; the matter of the
resurrection; "wa qila" link to 16:36 with resurrection, idols, taqlid,
Abu Talib); al-Baghawi (make manifest the truth); as-Sa'di (great and
small questions; regrets, useless gods, fuel for Jahannam; 16:40 pointer);
the Muyassar. Ibn Kathir abridged grouped 16:38-40 and Ma'arif 16:36-40
(its note is on 16:36 only); said so.

Disagreements kept: object of the dispute, the resurrection (al-Qurtubi,
Muyassar, at-Tabari) vs everything (Ibn Kathir, as-Sa'di), plus
al-Qurtubi's alternative construction; the lie as the oath vs as-Sa'di's
wider exposure of their worship. No position taken.

Hadith: none attached to 16:39 by any fetched tafsir; said so. Qatada's
line marked as his own comment. No asbab.

Dropped: 169's bracketed gloss on the Day of Calling; 16:40 material
beyond one pointer; eschatology beyond the fetched texts. Licensing
sentence in both languages.
