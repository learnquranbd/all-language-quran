Replaces the 677-word, 5-section article with a 1,778-word, 8-section
deep article on not created in vain and the true King, Lord of the noble Throne.

Sources: at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi, Ibn Kathir (ar + en), as-Sa'di,
al-Baghawi, al-Muyassar, Ma'arif al-Qur'an, all on 23:115 and 23:116 (t169 grouped 23:112-116).
- Placement: 23:112-114 via Ibn Kathir's English (short lives wasted,
  transient over eternal); addressees kept as a difference: the wretched
  (Tabari) vs "O creation" (Muyassar, Sa'di).
- 'Abathan: play and falsehood (Tabari, Ibn Jurayj, Baghawi), no wisdom
  (Ibn Kathir), untended with no command or recompense (Muyassar, Sa'di);
  cattle comparison and 75:36 (Qurtubi, Baghawi, Ibn Kathir); al-Hakim
  at-Tirmidhi via al-Qurtubi.
- Readings turja'un/tarji'un (Tabari's verdict, Hamza, al-Kisa'i, Ya'qub);
  'abathan as hal (Sibawayh, Qutrub) or masdar (Abu 'Ubaydah).
- 23:116 exaltation kept as a difference: above creating in vain (Ibn Kathir,
  Muyassar, Sa'di) vs above the polytheists' ascriptions (Tabari, Baghawi),
  both in al-Qurtubi; al-Malik al-Haqq (Muyassar, Sa'di).
- Throne only in the tafsirs' words (Ibn Kathir, Muyassar, Baghawi, Sa'di),
  31:10 / 26:7; al-karimu reading and "nowhere else" (Qurtubi); rabb case (Tabari).
- Last sermon of 'Umar ibn 'Abd al-'Aziz via Ibn Kathir / Ibn Abi Hatim.
Hadith: none used; the Ibn Mas'ud ruqyah and Abu Nu'aym reports are named as
ungraded narrations from outside the six collections, nothing built on them.
Asbab: none given; placement used. Licensing sentence in both languages.
23:117-118 in one paragraph.
Dropped from the old article: 21:16, 44:38-39, 38:27, 23:12-14, 23:88,
20:114 (not cited by fetched tafsir), unsourced word-rarity claims; fixed its
claim that the verse puts 'abathan in the questioned people's mouths.
