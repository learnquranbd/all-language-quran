## Disagreements kept
- Addressees of 23:115: the wretched in the scene (at-Tabari) vs "O creation" (Muyassar, as-Sa'di).
- What 23:116 exalts Him above: creating in vain (Ibn Kathir, Muyassar, as-Sa'di) vs the polytheists' ascriptions, partner and daughters (at-Tabari, al-Baghawi); al-Qurtubi gives both.
- turja'un vs tarji'un (at-Tabari: close in meaning, both correct; readers per Qurtubi/Baghawi).
- 'abathan: hal (Sibawayh, Qutrub) vs masdar / maf'ul lahu (Abu 'Ubaydah), via al-Qurtubi.
- al-karimu (Ibn Muhaysin, reported from the reader Ibn Kathir) vs al-karimi, via al-Qurtubi; at-Tabari on rabb nominative.
