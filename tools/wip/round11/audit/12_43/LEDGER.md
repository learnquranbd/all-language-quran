## Dropped
- Any Egyptological/dynastic identification of the king (none in sources beyond the name ar-Rayyan ibn al-Walid, which is kept attributed to Ibn Ishaq via at-Tabari and to al-Qurtubi). No Fir'awn comparison made.
- 'Ali ibn Abi Talib's dream-lore in al-Qurtubi (cows as years of plenty/hardship, fitan, etc.): general, not tied to the verse's wording, and would pre-empt 12:47-49. Named only as set aside.

- al-Qurtubi's grammar note on sab' / siman (al-Farra'): cut for length.
- Ibn Ishaq's "he knew it was a real dream" (at-Tabari #19331): cut for length.
- Ibn Kathir en's 12:44-49 content (see above).
