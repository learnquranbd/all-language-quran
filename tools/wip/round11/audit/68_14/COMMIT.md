Card and 8-section bilingual article for 68:14: an kana dha malin wa
banin, the five words that close the 68:10-13 list. 68:15 placed only
as the next verse; 68:11's names not repeated.

Sources: Tabari, Qurtubi, Baghawi, Ibn Kathir (ar; en grouped 68:8-16),
Muyassar (grouped), Sa'di; Ma'arif (grouped 68:10-15) read, not named.
- Qira'at as each author lists them: question (a-an / aan kana) per
  Tabari (Abu Ja'far, Hamza), Qurtubi (Abu Ja'far, Ibn 'Amir and others
  with one long hamza; al-Mufaddal, Abu Bakr, Hamza with two) and Baghawi
  (adds Ya'qub); the rest read a statement. Mismatch stated, not resolved.
- Question senses: reproach of the man (Tabari's "more evident") or of
  whoever obeys him; Qurtubi's three completions (you obey him / he says
  legends / he disbelieves and is arrogant); Baghawi's bare question.
- Statement: tied to "do not obey" (Tabari) vs maf'ul min ajlih of an
  implied "he disbelieves" (Qurtubi); cause of his transgression and
  arrogance (Muyassar, Sa'di). No reading chosen.
- Ibn Kathir: muqabala, his answer to favours; cites 74:11-30 (74:12-13
  checked: malan, banin). No name drawn from it.
- Names: al-Walid ibn al-Mughira (Muyassar; Sa'di "or someone else",
  hedge kept). Sa'di's general-rule principle. Licensing sentence in both
  languages; wealth and children not a charge, argued from the texts.
Hadith: none attached by any fetched tafsir; stated in one sentence.
Asbab: none used as such.
Dropped: 18:46, 34:35-37, 17:6 (no tafsir cites them), own-voice
grammar of "an", any preference between the readings.
