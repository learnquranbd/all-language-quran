## Asbab an-nuzul

Used: Jabir's report (Bukhari 4528), with the licensing sentence in both languages (3.2). At-Tabari's confirmation of the Jabir/Ibn Abbas occasion cited.
Not used (other occasion reports in the tafsir, not fetched/confirmed individually): Umm Salamah via Hafsah bint 'Abd ar-Rahman (Ahmad, Tirmidhi "hasan" per Ibn Kathir); 'Umar "I turned my saddle" (Tirmidhi: Ibn Kathir quotes "hasan gharib", al-Qurtubi quotes "hasan sahih": the two disagree, a further reason to leave it); Ibn Abbas on the Ansar and Quraysh customs (Abu Dawud via al-Qurtubi); Himyar and Abu Sa'id reports (Ibn Abi Hatim, at-Tahawi, Abu Ya'la); Murrah al-Hamdani's report in at-Tabari.

## Deliberately left out (watch-points)

- The Nafi' / Ibn 'Umar debate and every report on the dispute over the prohibition (al-Qurtubi's and Ibn Kathir's long discussions, the "Kitab as-Sirr" attribution to Malik, Ibn 'Abd al-Hakam on ash-Shafi'i, al-Qurazi's argument, the twelve-Companion count, ad-Darimi, at-Tayalisi, Tawus): one sentence only (4.3), attributed to al-Qurtubi, as-Sa'di and Ibn Kathir. Ibn Kathir's word is "jumhur al-'ulama'" (majority), not ijma'; the article says "majority". Al-Qurtubi's ijma' point is about a different ruling (the ratqa'), so it was not turned into a "consensus" claim.
- Hadith on the prohibition (Khuzaymah, Ibn Abbas "la yanzur Allah", Abu Hurayrah "mal'un"): not fetched, not used; no gradings given.
- Ma'arif's list of positions: not reproduced (register).
- Sa'id b. al-Musayyib's 'azl reading and Ibn Abbas on 'azl (al-Baghawi): left out (register and length).
- "Ayna" reading of anna: not named, because its only reports in at-Tabari are the Nafi'/Ibn 'Umar ones.
- Ibn Kathir's gloss on "the believers" (obedient ones): cut for length.
- Hadith of children sent ahead ("three children", al-Baghawi via Malik; al-Qurtubi): not fetched; the reading is given without the hadith.
- Bukhari 5090 "a woman is married for four" (al-Baghawi under the chastity reading): not fetched, not used.
- Muslim 2860 (see above).
