Key kept at 2:223 alone (2:224-228 left free). New card and a 1,743-word,
8-section article: harth as the ground where children grow, Jabir's occasion,
anna and its one limit, the readings of qaddimu, the du'a, taqwa and the
meeting, and the wife's food, clothing and dignity.

Sources: at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi, Ibn Kathir (ar + en abridged), as-Sa'di,
al-Baghawi, Muyassar, Ma'arif on 2:223. Slots on target.
- Harth: at-Tabari's muzdara' awladikum, Ibn Abbas's manbit/mawdi' al-walad,
  al-Qurtubi's simile with Tha'lab's lines (the growing is Allah's). Stated
  plainly that it is not a word of possession.
- Occasion: Bukhari 4528 (Jabir, the cross-eyed claim), with at-Tabari's
  confirmation and the licensing sentence in both languages.
- Prohibition in one sentence: al-Qurtubi, as-Sa'di, and Ibn Kathir's
  "majority of scholars" (his word is jumhur, not ijma').
Disagreements kept: anna as how (Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Ikrimah, Qatadah,
as-Suddi) / when (ad-Dahhak, Ibn Abbas) / at-Tabari's "from whichever way"
vs al-Qurtubi's "wider than all three" with Sibawayh; qaddimu as general
good (as-Suddi, al-Kalbi; at-Tabari's choice, with his reason) vs Bismillah
('Ata', Ibn Abbas), du'a (Mujahid), a child, chaste marriage, children sent
ahead; as-Sa'di's intention reading folding both.
Hadith: Bukhari 6388 (the du'a; al-Baghawi's own chain), Muslim 1631 (marked
as not about the verse), Abu Dawud 2143 (Mu'awiyah ibn Haydah: feed her,
clothe her, do not revile, do not strike; no grading from Abu Dawud).
Dropped: the Nafi'/Ibn 'Umar debate and all prohibition hadith; other
occasion reports (Umm Salamah, 'Umar's saddle: Tirmidhi grading quoted
differently by Ibn Kathir and al-Qurtubi); Muslim 2860 under mulaquh; 'azl
readings; Bukhari 5090 and the "three children" hadith (not fetched).
Application: marriage as a trust before Allah, kindness, and intention.
