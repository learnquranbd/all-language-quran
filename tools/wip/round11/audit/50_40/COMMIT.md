Card and 8-section bilingual article on wa mina l-layli fa-sabbihhu wa
adbara s-sujud, read as the completion of 50:39's patience-and-tasbih
timetable (52:48 not retold).

Sources: at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi, Ibn Kathir (ar + en abridged for 50:39),
al-Baghawi, al-Muyassar, as-Sa'di, Ma'arif al-Qur'an.
- Night: Ibn Kathir (prayer, with 17:79); al-Baghawi (Maghrib and
  'Isha, Mujahid any hour); at-Tabari (Ibn Zayd al-'atama vs Mujahid the
  whole night, preferring Mujahid); al-Qurtubi's four sayings (Abu
  al-Ahwas, Mujahid, Ibn Abbas, Ibn Zayd) with Ibn al-'Arabi's grounds.
- Adbar as-sujud kept as a disagreement: two rak'as after Maghrib (Umar,
  Ali, al-Hasan, Abu Hurayrah et al.; at-Tabari by ijma', al-Baghawi
  "most commentators") vs tasbih after prayers (Ibn Abbas via Mujahid,
  Abu al-Ahwas; Ibn al-'Arabi strongest) vs nawafil (Ibn Zayd; an-Nahhas
  on the apparent sense) vs witr; Ibn Abbas and Mujahid on both sides.
- Qira'at idbar/adbar per at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi, al-Baghawi; 52:49.
No position taken, no fiqh ruling.
Hadith: Bukhari 843 (poor emigrants, 33 each), Muslim 595 closing line,
Muslim 597 (33/33/33 + tahlil), Abu Dawud 1275 (Ali, two rak'as after
each prayer but Fajr and 'Asr); all attached by a fetched tafsir.
Ibn Abbas marfu' (Rishdin) reported only with Ibn Kathir's weakening.
Asbab: none used.
Dropped: Ibn al-'Arabi's waking-at-night narration (unchecked); Ma'arif's
Bukhari attribution for the 597 wording; al-Baghawi's count-of-ten
version; 50:39-only reports (Anas, Jarir).
