Replaces the 659-word, 5-section article with a 1,769-word, 8-section deep article, built only on tafsir fetched for 99:7-8 (shipped card kept).

Sources: at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi, Ibn Kathir (ar; en abridged for 99:6),
as-Sa'di, al-Baghawi (commentary filed under 99:8), al-Muyassar, Ma'arif.

Frame: 99:6 ashtat and the showing (Ibn Kathir); at-Tabari on the future
ya'mal kept as an urging. Dharrah glossed in the tafsirs' words only: small
ant (Muyassar, al-Baghawi, Sa'id ibn Jubayr), red ant (Ibn Abbas via
'Ikrimah), weightless red worm (at-Tabari, Yazid ibn Harun), dust on a
struck hand (al-Qurtubi, Ibn Abbas); as-Sa'di's a fortiori; 4:40.
Yarah: sees its reward/recompense (at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi, Muyassar) or sees
it in his book (Sa'id ibn Jubayr); yurahu reading (al-Jahdari, as-Sulami,
'Isa ibn 'Umar, Aban 'an 'Asim), al-Qurtubi prefers yarahu by 3:30.
Disagreement kept, no position: Ibn Abbas via at-Tabari/al-Baghawi (believer
shown sins and forgiven, disbeliever's good turned back) vs al-Qurazi (each
sees it in this world), with al-Qurtubi's Ibn Abbas close to al-Qurazi;
at-Tabari's Anas/Abu Bakr report (ungraded) and his own leaning reported.
Generality: Ibn Mas'ud "most decisive verse", scholars' agreement (al-Qurtubi).
Asbab: Sa'id ibn Jubayr / Muqatil on 76:8, marked as reported, not settled.
Hadith: Bukhari 2371 (donkey portion of the horses hadith, also Muslim 987),
Muslim 214 (Ibn Jud'an), Ibn Majah 4243 (no grading of his own), Bukhari
1417 marked general. 'A'isha's grape (Muwatta via al-Qurtubi; al-Baghawi).
Licensing sentence added where the disbeliever is discussed as a group.
Dropped (unconfirmable): Sa'sa'a's "hasbi" (Ahmad unreachable), Ahmad-only
narrations, Abu Sa'id (Ibn Lahi'ah alone). From the old article: sunbeam-mote
gloss, mithqal etymology, 21:47 and Muslim's Abu Dharr hadith (not attached
in fetched wording); old "all-embracing" quote now in Bukhari's own order.
