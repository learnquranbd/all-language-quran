Card and 8-section bilingual article (1,775 EN words) on 61:14, the last
verse of as-Saff: kunu ansar Allah, man ansari ila Allah, the two factions,
fa-ayyadna ... fa-asbahu zahirin. 3:52 linked (Qurtubi's Al Imran pointer).

Sources: Tabari, Qurtubi, Ibn Kathir (ar; en as check), Sa'di, Baghawi,
Muyassar, Ma'arif.
- Readings: ansaran lillah vs ansara Allah, both sound (Tabari); Abu
  'Ubayda's preference (Qurtubi). Who speaks: implied "say, O Muhammad"
  vs direct address (Qurtubi).
- Ansar Allah: of His religion (Muyassar, Abu 'Ubayda); all states, words,
  deeds, selves, wealth (IK); Sa'di's list incl. refuting falsehood,
  learning Book and Sunnah, enjoining good.
- Ila: "with" (Baghawi, Qurtubi + al-dhawd proverb) vs "in what nears to
  Allah" (Qurtubi qila, Muyassar), "in calling to Allah" (IK), Mujahid's
  "follow me to Allah". Hawariyyun: sincere friend (Ma'arif), elect, first
  to believe (Qurtubi/Ibn 'Abbas), white clothes/fishermen, washers, fullers.
- Ansar parallel (IK) and 'Aqaba: 70 vs 72 (Qatada/Ma'mar) vs Qatada's
  "all from Quraysh" twelve; kept unresolved.
- Victory: at Muhammad's ﷺ sending/confirmation (Ibn 'Abbas, Tabari,
  Ibrahim, Muyassar, IK, Ma'arif/Mazhari) vs in Isa's own time (Mujahid)
  vs Muslims now (Qurtubi qila) vs by proof (Zayd ibn 'Ali, Qatada);
  Ma'arif's no-jihad note. No position taken.
Licensing paragraph in both languages: nothing against Christians, Jews or
anyone living. Denying-faction and sect labels (IK, Ibn 'Abbas report) not
reproduced.
Hadith: Tirmidhi 2925 (Jabir, Mawqif), gharib sahih per Tirmidhi; IK's own
wording has no collection, said so. No asbab.
Dropped: Ibn Ishaq's envoys, Qatada's 'Aqaba terms (no chain), Dajjal note.
- Hadith: Tirmidhi 2925 rendered from the Arabic (quranx English wording differs); grading gharib sahih is Tirmidhi's own.
