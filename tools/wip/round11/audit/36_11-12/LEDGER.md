# SOURCES — 36:11-12

## Tafsir fetched (from PREP.md, not re-fetched)

### 36:11
- **as-Sa'di (91)** — NAMED. Arabic clause: "إِنَّمَا تُنْذِرُ } أي: إنما تنفع نذارتك، ويتعظ بنصحك { مَنِ اتَّبَعَ الذِّكْرَ }" and "من اتصف بهذين الأمرين، القصد الحسن في طلب الحق، وخشية اللّه". Rests: innamā restricts where the benefit lands (not the duty); the two marks (sound aim + awe); forgiveness for sins, noble reward for righteous deeds and good intention ("فَبَشِّرْهُ بِمَغْفِرَةٍ } لذنوبه، { وَأَجْرٍ كَرِيمٍ } لأعماله الصالحة، ونيته الحسنة").
- **Ibn Kathir abridged en (169)** — NAMED. Clause: "only the believers will benefit from your warning, those who follow the Reminder, which is the Qur'an"; "even when no one sees him except Allah… he knows that Allah is watching"; reward "vast and great and beautiful"; places 67:12 beside it.
- **Ma'arif en (168)** — NAMED. Clause: God "placed before human beings two alternate ways"; for one who chooses disbelief "the supply of things… that would increase his disbelief never run short." Used for the free-choice framing of the sealed hearts (36:7-10).
- at-Tabari (15), al-Qurtubi (90), al-Baghawi (94), Ibn Kathir ar (14), Muyassar (16) on 36:11 — read, consistent, not separately cited (shorter restatements of the above).

### 36:12
- **as-Sa'di (91)** — NAMED. Clauses: "نبعثهم بعد موتهم لنجازيهم على الأعمال" (resurrection for recompense); "{ وَآثَارَهُمْ } وهي آثار الخير وآثار الشر، التي كانوا هم السبب في إيجادها في حال حياتهم وبعد وفاتهم" (the broad legacy reading — knowledge, teaching, enjoining good, books, prayer/zakat/sadaqa imitated, building a masjid; and likewise evil); imām mubīn = "كتاب هو أم الكتب… وهو اللوح المحفوظ."
- **al-Qurtubi (90)** — NAMED. Clauses: "نحييهم بالبعث للجزاء" preferred over reviving-by-faith (ad-Dahhak/al-Hasan); the broad āthār list "علم علموه، أو كتاب صنفوه… أو بناء بنوه من مسجد… أو سيّئ كوظيفة وظفها بعض الظلام على المسلمين"; footsteps reading "خطاهم إلى المساجد" of Ibn Abbas/Umar/Sa'id b. Jubayr; "قال النحاس: وهذا أولى ما قيل فيه… لأن الأنصار كانت منازلهم بعيدة عن المسجد"; Tirmidhi via Abu Sa'id al-Khudri with grading "هذا حديث حسن غريب من حديث الثوري"; Muslim via Jabir; imām = "الكتاب المقتدى به" / al-Lawh al-Mahfuz (Mujahid, Qatada, Ibn Zayd) / "صحائف الأعمال" (a group).
- **Ma'arif en (168)** — NAMED. "sent ahead" = baggage gone ahead; recording = preservation "so that there remains no probability of error, forgetfulness, increase or decrease"; āthār = fruits/consequences that keep surviving; cites man-sanna (from Ibn Abi Hatim) and the footsteps/Banu Salima harmonisation.
- **Ibn Kathir abridged en (169)** — NAMED. "the deeds they used to do and the legacy they left behind"; Mujahid "their footsteps," Qatada/al-Hasan same; cites man-sanna (Muslim, from Jarir; in full in Ibn Abi Hatim with the Prophet reciting this verse), the three-continuing-deeds hadith (Muslim, Abu Hurayra), and the Banu Salima hadith (Ahmad, also Muslim, from Jabir); imām mubīn = al-Lawh al-Mahfuz (Mujahid, Qatada, Ibn Zayd).
- **al-Baghawi (94)** — NAMED. āthār = "ما سنوا من سنة حسنة أو سيئة" + footsteps; imām mubīn = al-Lawh al-Mahfuz.
- at-Tabari (15), Muyassar (16) on 36:12 — read, consistent, not separately cited.

## Hadith (fetched + saved)

1. **Banu Salima — footsteps recorded. QUOTED WHOLE (the single whole quote).**
   - Collection/number: Sahih Muslim, Reference Hadith 665a (USC-MSA Book 4, Hadith 1407).
   - URL fetched: https://quranx.com/hadith/Muslim/Hadith-665 (saved under audit dir).
   - First ten words (EN used): "There were some plots vacant around the mosque. Banu…"
   - Narrator: Jabir b. Abdullah. Grading: sound by inclusion in Sahih Muslim (Muslim gives no per-hadith grade; not upgraded beyond "in the Sahih").

2. **Three continuing deeds — referenced via Ibn Kathir, confirmed.**
   - Collection/number: Sahih Muslim, Reference Hadith 1631 (USC-MSA Book 13, Hadith 4005).
   - URL fetched: https://quranx.com/hadith/Muslim/Hadith-1631.
   - First ten words: "When a man dies, his acts come to an end…"
   - Narrator: Abu Hurayra. Grading: sound (Sahih Muslim). Cited in article §8.1 as Ibn Kathir reports it from Sahih Muslim (paraphrased, not a competing whole quote).

3. **Man sanna sunnatan hasana — referenced, not quoted whole.**
   - Attributed in-text as Ibn Kathir reports it: recorded by Muslim from Jarir b. Abdullah; in Ibn Abi Hatim's fuller version the Prophet recited 36:12 after it. Confirmed in tafsir 169 (and 168) fetched for this verse. No independent collection number asserted (not fetched); attribution kept to what the tafsir states.

## Asbab an-nuzul
- The Banu Salima occasion is the sabab for the footsteps reading of āthār, confirmed on al-Qurtubi (90) and al-Baghawi (94) and Ma'arif (168). Noted in §7. The Makki/Madani wrinkle (surah Makki, event Madani) is handled as the sources handle it — verse general, recited later at the event (Ma'arif, following Ibn Kathir). Not forced into the card.

## Dropped / not used
- The Abu Jahl "curtain over the eyes" narrations on 36:9-10 (Ma'arif/Ibn Kathir): Ma'arif judges most such reports weak ("The Tafsir of the verse cannot be based on such sources"). Dropped — not needed for 36:11-12.
- The ʿAbdullah b. ʿAmr / Zayd b. Thabit footstep narrations (Ibn Kathir, via Ahmad/Nasa'i/Ibn Majah): supportive but secondary; dropped to keep one whole hadith quote per the gate.
- The reviving-a-dead-heart-by-faith reading of "nuhyi al-mawtā" (ad-Dahhak, al-Hasan): kept only as the minority "some also read" note, since al-Qurtubi marks the resurrection reading as clearer. Not asserted as the primary sense.
- No hadith number asserted for man-sanna (not fetched); kept to the tafsir's own attribution.
