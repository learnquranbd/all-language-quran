Card and 8-section bilingual article for 41:3: kitabun fussilat
ayatuhu qur'anan 'arabiyyan li-qawmin ya'lamun, the first target in
Fussilat. Ha-Mim and 41:2 one line each; 41:4-5 not drawn on.

Sources: Tabari, Ibn Kathir (ar + en abridged, grouped 41:1-5),
Muyassar, Qurtubi, Sa'di, Baghawi, Ma'arif. All named.
- Placement: Makkan per IK-en; Tabari's Basran grammarians: kitabun is
  the predicate of an unstated subject, the tanzil of 41:2 is a Book.
- fussilat: bayyinat (Tabari with Suddi, Baghawi); bayyinat wa fussirat
  (Qurtubi); meanings clear, rulings firm, with 11:1 (IK); full clarity
  (Muyassar); Qatada lawful/unlawful, al-Hasan promise/threat, Sufyan
  reward/punishment (via Qurtubi); every kind on its own, tamyiz
  al-haqa'iq (Sa'di); Qurtubi's variant reading, two meanings.
- qur'anan 'arabiyyan: clear wording, inimitable, 41:42 (IK); al-fusha
  (Sa'di); easy to understand (Muyassar). Grammar in one paragraph: hal
  (IK), bayan falls on it (Baghawi), Basran view only (Tabari, text
  truncated), wa ju'ila 'arabiyyan (Sa'di).
- li-qawmin ya'lamun: knowers of Arabic (Muyassar, Baghawi); the firmly
  grounded scholars (IK); meaning clear as wording (Sa'di); those who
  intend to ponder (Ma'arif). Kept side by side, no position.
- Sa'di's al-jahilun with 2:6; Ma'arif's pointer to 41:4. Licensing
  sentence in both languages.
Hadith: none attached by any fetched tafsir; said in one sentence.
Asbab: none given; Makkan placement instead.
Dropped: surah-name derivation (no fetched text); 12:2, 43:3, 26:195
(not cited); rest of Tabari's grammar dispute and the variant
reading's vowelling/reciters (not in fetched text).
