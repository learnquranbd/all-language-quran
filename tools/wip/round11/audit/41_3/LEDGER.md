## Readings kept side by side (no position taken)
- fussilat: bayyinat (Tabari, Suddi, Baghawi); bayyinat wa fussirat (Qurtubi); meanings clear + rulings firm (IK); full clarity, meanings and rulings (Muyassar); lawful/unlawful + obedience/disobedience (Qatada), promise/threat (al-Hasan), reward/punishment (Sufyan), all via Qurtubi; every kind set apart, tamyiz al-haqa'iq (Sa'di); variant reading's two meanings (Qurtubi).
- li-qawmin ya'lamun: those who know the Arabic tongue (Muyassar, Baghawi); firmly grounded scholars (IK ar/en); so that meaning becomes clear as wording (Sa'di); those who intend to ponder (Ma'arif).
- Grammar of qur'anan: hal (IK); accusative because the bayan falls on it (Baghawi); grammarians differed, Basran view only in fetched portion (Tabari); "wa ju'ila 'arabiyyan" (Sa'di). One paragraph.
