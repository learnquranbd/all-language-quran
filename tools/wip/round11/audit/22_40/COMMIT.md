Card and deep bilingual article for 22:40, the verse that names the
wronged of 22:39: those evicted from their homes for no cause but saying
"our Lord is Allah."

Sources: at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi, as-Sa'di, Ibn Kathir (ar + abridged en)
and Ma'arif al-Qur'an, all on 22:40; al-Baghawi and al-Muyassar read as
corroboration.

- Frames the fighting permission as defensive redress (Ibn al-Arabi in
  al-Qurtubi: ten years of patience before war was allowed) and states
  in both languages that the verse licenses nothing against any living
  person or community.
- The dafʿ principle (God checks one people by another) carried with
  at-Tabari's spread of readings — Ibn Jurayj, Ibn Zayd, Ali via his
  companions, Mujahid — and his own general choice; as-Sa'di's 2:251.
- Four houses identified with the group each mufassir assigns
  (sawami'/monks, biya'/Christians, salawat/Jews, masajid/Muslims),
  Qatadah's Sabian-Magian reading kept as a live disagreement, and Ibn
  Jarir's preferred alignment. Protection of non-Muslim houses presented
  as the mufassirun give it (Ibn Khuwayz Mindad; Ma'arif's scope caveat).
- "supporting Allah" kept as supporting His dīn/cause (al-Qurtubi,
  as-Sa'di, at-Tabari); Qawiyy/Aziz per Ibn Kathir and al-Khattabi.

Hadith: Jami' at-Tirmidhi 3171 (Ibn Abbas; hasan per at-Tirmidhi),
quoted whole, attached to the revelation of the permission (22:39).

Dropped for budget: the Khandaq rajaz, Abu al-Darda's report, the
qira'at detail, and Ibn Kathir's extra cross-refs (37:171-173, 58:21).
