Card and 8-section bilingual article for 92:11: wa-ma yughni 'anhu
maluhu idha taradda, six words, the last line on the man of 92:8-10.
92:10 and 92:12 context only; 92:7 named only; qadar not treated.

Sources: Tabari, Qurtubi, Baghawi, Sa'di, Ibn Kathir (ar), Muyassar,
Ma'arif. Slot check: all seven files sit on 92:11; IK en abridged is
the 92:1-10 group, not used.
- ma: denial (jahd) or rebuking question (tawbikh), Qurtubi, both
  kept; Tabari's paraphrase interrogative; Muyassar, Ma'arif negation.
- taradda: falls into Jahannam (Abu Salih, Qatada, Zayd ibn Aslam;
  Muyassar; Tabari's stated preference on usage, radiya vs taradda)
  vs dies (Mujahid via Tabari's 3 reports, IK, Baghawi; Qurtubi's lead
  gloss; Sa'di "halaka wa mata"). Ma'arif joins grave and Hell.
  Kept as a disagreement, none chosen.
- Link to 92:8-10: Tabari, Baghawi, Sa'di, Muyassar each in own words;
  Tabari "free of need of his Lord" vs Muyassar "of his Lord's reward".
- Sa'di: only righteous deeds accompany; unpaid wealth a wabal
  (bracketed clause flagged).
Hadith: none attached by any fetched tafsir; said in one sentence.
Shipped 92:4 cross-referenced by number, narration not attached here.
Asbab: none in the fetched texts; man not named.
Licensing sentence in both languages; no verdict on anyone's wealth.
Dropped: 111:2, 69:28, 26:88, 92:18, 104:3 (not cited on 92:11).
