Replaces the 659-word, 6-section article with a 1,771-word, 8-section one.

Sources: at-Tabari, Ibn Kathir (ar, en for 17:111), al-Baghawi, al-Qurtubi,
as-Sa'di, the Muyassar and Ma'arif al-Qur'an, all fetched for 17:110.
- Occasion of the first half kept as the commentators' reports, ungraded:
  ya Rahman ya Rahim (at-Tabari's Ibn 'Abbas, Makhul and the Rahman of
  al-Yamama) vs ya Allah ya Rahman (al-Baghawi with Abu Jahl, al-Qurtubi,
  Muyassar, Ma'arif); al-Qurtubi's qila on Bismillah.
- Ayyan ma: at-Tabari's two readings of ma, al-Baghawi, Mujahid; names
  many, Named One (at-Tabari, al-Baghawi, al-Qurtubi; Ibn Kathir 59:22-24);
  7:180 in one line.
- Husna by revelation and tawqif (al-Qurtubi); call by the fitting name
  (as-Sa'di); Bukhari 7392 on ninety-nine names, noting at-Tabari's
  variant adds "all in the Qur'an", not in al-Bukhari's wording.
- Second half: Bukhari 4722 quoted whole; Ibn Ishaq's eavesdropper
  report; ad-Dahhak's "lapsed after the Hijra".
- Disagreement kept on salatika: du'a (Bukhari 4723, 'A'isha; Mujahid,
  Abu 'Iyad, 'Ata', 'Abdullah b. Shaddad), recitation (Ibn 'Abbas, Qatada,
  'Ikrima, al-Hasan), tashahhud ('A'isha via Hafs, Ibn Sirin, Ibn Mas'ud),
  sincerity (al-Hasan, Ibn 'Abbas), day/night (al-Qurtubi), Ibn Zayd;
  at-Tabari's own weighing reported as his.
- Measure: mukhafata (al-Qurtubi, al-Baghawi), Ibn Mas'ud, Qatada, 7:205;
  Abu Bakr and 'Umar via Ibn Sirin, and Tirmidhi 447 (gharib, mostly
  mursal; no mention of the verse) as cited by al-Baghawi.
- Licensing line on the Makkan mockers; 17:111 link from Ibn Kathir.
Dropped: unsourced 25:60, 20:8, 25:67 links and the shipped article's own
claims on ibtigha and sincerity; Mu'adh al-Juhani hadith on 17:111.
