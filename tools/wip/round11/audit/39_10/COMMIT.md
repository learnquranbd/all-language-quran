Replaces the 949-word, 6-section article with a 1,775-word, 9-section article built from
the eight tafsir files fetched for 39:10.

- The address: at-Tabari, al-Baghawi and al-Qurtubi on the taqwa asked for; Ibn Kathir's
  "continue in obedience"; as-Sa'di on calling by the noblest name ("O generous one, give").
- "fi hadhihi ad-dunya" kept as a disagreement: at-Tabari's two attachments (to hasanah =
  health and wellbeing, as-Suddi; to ahsanu = Paradise), al-Baghawi's Muqatil vs as-Suddi,
  al-Qurtubi's two goods and al-Qushayri's ranking with al-Qurtubi's reply; Ibn Kathir and
  the Muyassar combining; as-Sa'di's inward goods with 16:97.
- The spacious earth: as-Sa'di's objection-and-answer, Ma'arif's "excuse" reading; Ibn Abbas
  (leave Makkah), Mujahid in two wordings, 'Ata with 4:97, Sa'id ibn Jubayr; al-Qurtubi's
  Paradise reading (3:133, 39:74) and al-Mawardi's "wide provision" with Sufyan ath-Thawri.
- Ja'far ibn Abi Talib and the Abyssinian emigrants carried only as reports (al-Qurtubi from
  Ibn Abbas, al-Baghawi "it is said"), not as an established occasion of revelation.
- "Without account": Qatada, al-Awza'i, Ibn Jurayj, as-Suddi, al-Qurtubi's three glosses,
  the Muyassar, Ma'arif's "without claim"; 'Ali's saying marked as a Companion's.
- Who the patient are: al-Qurtubi's "those who fast", Malik, al-Baghawi, as-Sa'di's three
  kinds, an-Nahhas on sabir alone vs sabir 'ala.
- Hadith: Sahih Muslim 1151 (al-A'mash wording), attached by al-Qurtubi, quoted whole.
- Dropped: the Qatada-from-Anas "no scales for the people of affliction" report and the
  al-Husayn "tree of affliction" report (no collection named); the shipped article's 39:53
  and 29:56 parallels, "honour" in the worldly good, and lines given to "the scholars" with
  no source.
