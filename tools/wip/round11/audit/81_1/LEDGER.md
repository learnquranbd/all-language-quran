## Hadith
1. Bukhari 3200. URL https://quranx.com/hadith/Bukhari/DarusSalam/Hadith-3200 (saved). Abu Hurayra, chain via Abu Salama b. Abd ar-Rahman (NOT via al-Hasan; al-Hasan appears only in al-Bazzar's version, dropped). Wording used (whole): «الشَّمْسُ وَالْقَمَرُ مُكَوَّرَانِ يَوْمَ الْقِيَامَةِ». Grading: in Sahih al-Bukhari. Note: Ibn Kathir and al-Baghawi quote «يكوران»; the article quotes the Bukhari page's «مكوران». The page's English parenthesis "(deprived of their light)" is the translator's and is not quoted.
2. Tirmidhi 3333. URL https://quranx.com/hadith/Tirmidhi/Hadith-3333 (saved). Ibn Umar. First ten words: «مَنْ سَرَّهُ أَنْ يَنْظُرَ إِلَى يَوْمِ الْقِيَامَةِ كَأَنَّهُ رَأْىُ عَيْنٍ». Quoted whole (all three surah openings). Grading by Tirmidhi: «هَذَا حَدِيثٌ حَسَنٌ غَرِيبٌ». Tirmidhi's note on Hisham b. Yusuf's shorter version also reported.
