Card and 8-section bilingual article for 81:1, idha ash-shamsu kuwwirat
(3 Arabic words), the opening of Surat at-Takwir. 81:2 named only as the
next verse; 81:3-14 and the conditional's answer left for their own slots.

Sources: at-Tabari, Ibn Kathir (ar), al-Qurtubi, al-Baghawi, as-Sa'di on
81:1; Muyassar (grouped 81:1-10); Ma'arif; Ibn Kathir en (grouped 81:1-10).
- Root takwir: turban and clothes bundle (Tabari, repeated by Ibn Kathir),
  kara al-'imamah (Qurtubi), Abu 'Ubayda via Qurtubi, az-Zajjaj via Baghawi.
- Glosses kept side by side, none chosen: darkened, went (Ibn Abbas),
  faded (Mujahid), light went (Qatada, ad-Dahhak, al-Hasan, Muqatil,
  al-Kalbi), thrown (ar-Rabi'), overturned/cast down (Abu Salih), falls to
  earth (Zayd b. Aslam), ghuwwirat (Sa'id b. Jubayr), into the Throne (Ibn
  Abbas via Qurtubi). Tabari's and Ma'arif's reconciliations reported as theirs.
- Elided-verb grammar after idha not raised by any fetched tafsir: omitted.
Hadith: Bukhari 3200 (Abu Hurayra, sun and moon mukawwaran; quoted whole
from the Bukhari page, not the commentators' yukawwaran) and Tirmidhi 3333
(Ibn Umar, "as if seen by the eye", 81:1, 82:1, 84:1; hasan gharib per
at-Tirmidhi), both via hadith.js. Ibn Kathir's own weak verdict on the Abu
Ya'la (ar-Raqashi) addition reported.
Asbab: none given; not used.
Dropped: Ahmad's version (unconfirmable), al-Bazzar's al-Hasan exchange,
Ibn Abi Hatim mursal, Ibn Abbas sea-and-wind report and Ubayy's six signs
(Companion reports, unconfirmable, 81:2-6 ground), Ma'arif's "thrown into
the ocean" paraphrase of Bukhari, Sa'id's Persian remark, 75:8-9, 39:67.
Fix: dropped unsourced 'restricted to sound reports' clause (Bukhari 3200).
