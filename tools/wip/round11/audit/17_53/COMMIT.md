Tadabbur 17:53 — tell My servants to say the word that is best

Card plus deep bilingual article on "say that which is best; Satan
sows discord among them; Satan is a clear enemy."

Sources (all fetched for this verse):
- at-Tabari, al-Muyassar, Ibn Kathir: allatī hiya aḥsan = the best of
  speech to one another; al-Hasan's gloss — answer an insult with
  "may Allah forgive you," not like with like.
- as-Saʿdi: the command reaches every speech that draws near to Allah,
  and the rule to prefer the better of two goods; "whoever masters his
  tongue masters his whole affair"; the remedy — the real enemy is
  Satan, so be gentle and subdue the nafs he enters through.
- al-Baghawi/al-Qurtubi: the reading split kept as a split — speech to
  the mushrikīn in daʿwah (al-Kalbi; 6:108 parallel) vs. speech among
  believers (al-Qurtubi's own choice; verse muḥkam, "be brothers").
- Reported occasions carried as disagreeing, not settled: ʿUmar ibn
  al-Khattab (ath-Thaʿlabi/al-Mawardi/Ibn ʿAtiyya/al-Wahidi) and
  al-Kalbi's "Muslims asked leave to fight."
- The "before jihad was commanded" note carried only as al-Hasan/
  al-Kalbi's reported view, never in our voice.
- yanzagu baynahum: harsh speech as the seam/trap-door Satan works
  (at-Tabari, Ibn Kathir "speech leads to action," Ma'arif).

Hadith: Sahih al-Bukhari 7072, weapon-pointing (Abu Hurayrah),
muttafaq ʿalayh per Ibn Kathir, quoted whole in Bukhari's wording.

Dropped: Ibn Kathir's Banu-Salit narration (majhūl, ungraded, general).
al-Qurtubi's two-gatherings report used only as illustration, marked
ungraded. PREP's Bukhari 660 was the wrong hadith; corrected to 7072.
