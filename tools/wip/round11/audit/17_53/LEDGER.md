## Hadith
- USED: Sahih al-Bukhari 7072 (weapon-pointing). URL fetched: https://quranx.com/hadith/Bukhari/DarusSalam/Hadith-7072 (also USC-MSA Vol 9, Book 88, Hadith 193). First ten words (EN): "None of you should point out towards his Muslim brother". Grading: Sahih al-Bukhari; Ibn Kathir states al-Bukhari and Muslim both recorded it via ʿAbdur-Razzaq (muttafaq ʿalayh). Chain on the fetched page: عبد الرزاق عن معمر عن همام عن أبي هريرة — matches Ibn Kathir's citation exactly.
- NOTE on PREP: PREP.md's suggested "Bukhari 660" is the wrong hadith (the seven under Allah's shade); confirmed by fetching it, then located the correct number (7072) via WebSearch on sunnah.com and confirmed the wording by fetching the quranx page.

No grading given, narrator majhūl, and it is general rather than tied to this verse's "best speech." Dropped per SOURCE-GATE.
