Card and bilingual article (8 sections, 1,752 EN words) for "He created
man from a nutfa, and then he is an open disputer", built on the eight
words that put the drop and the disputer side by side.

Sources: nutfa only as glossed (Ibn Kathir "weak, lowly", abridged en
"insignificant", at-Tabari and the Muyassar ma' mahin, Ma'arif); at-Tabari
(proof against you; al-insan = all people; mubin = makes his dispute plain
by his speech); al-Qurtubi (after the tawhid proof, man's contrariness;
khasim = mukhasim; wonder at man); the timing of the turn in Ibn Kathir,
at-Tabari, as-Sa'di, the Muyassar and Ma'arif. Ibn Kathir abridged is
grouped 16:3-4; only its 16:4 part used.

Disagreement kept: khasim mubin as adversary of his Lord (most) beside
as-Sa'di's two possibilities, the second counting reason and speech as a
favour to thank for. No position taken.

Asbab: Ubayy ibn Khalaf and the decayed bone, as al-Qurtubi ("ruwiya")
and al-Baghawi ("nazalat", but "the correct view: the verse is general")
give it, both tying 36:77; no grading added.

Hadith: Ibn Majah 2707 (Busr ibn Jahhash, the palm and the throat), cited
by Ibn Kathir under the verse; quoted whole, no grading shown or added.
Ibn Kathir's longer Ahmad wording not quoted.

Dropped: any embryology claim (al-Qurtubi's sulb/tara'ib clause); a
grammar label for fa-idha. Licensing sentence in both languages; 23:14,
15:26 and 16:78 themes not repeated.
