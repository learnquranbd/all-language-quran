## Disagreement kept (named, no position)
- khasim mubin: adversary of his Lord (Ibn Kathir, at-Tabari, Muyassar, al-Qurtubi, al-Baghawi, Ma'arif) vs as-Sa'di, who gives both "adversary" and "made rational and articulate, a favour to thank for" as possibilities without choosing. Also mubin glosses: making his dispute plain by speech (at-Tabari), evident dispute / "qila" with falsehood / one who voices his mind (al-Qurtubi).
- Asbab scope: al-Qurtubi "ruwiya" Ubayy; al-Baghawi "nazalat fi Ubayy" but "the correct view: the verse is general". Reported as each gives it, no grading added.

## Hadith
- Sunan Ibn Majah 2707 (Busr ibn Jahhash al-Qurashi). URL https://quranx.com/hadith/IbnMajah/Hadith-2707 (saved hadith_IbnMajah_2707.txt). Wording quoted whole from this page (Darussalam English). First ten words: "the Prophet (ﷺ) spat in his palm then pointed to it". Grading: none shown on the fetched page; none claimed or added (article says so). Attached to 16:4 by Ibn Kathir (ar and abridged en). Ibn Kathir's longer wording (two cloaks, gathered and withheld), which he attributes to Ahmad as well, is NOT quoted; Musnad Ahmad not fetched.
