Tadabbur 20:40 — Allah recounts His favours to Mūsā: the sister,
the return to his mother, the killing survived, the trials, the
years in Madyan, and arriving at a decreed time.

Card: providence read backwards — the hard road as an unread favour;
themes patience + trust. Article: 8 sections, 1,752 EN words.

Sources: at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi, al-Baghawi, as-Saʿdi, Ibn Kathir (ar),
Ma'arif al-Qur'an (en). Sister named Maryam and the wet-nurse offer
(Qurtubi/Baghawi/Tabari via as-Suddi & Ibn Isḥāq, 28:12). qurrat
al-ʿayn cool-vs-hot tears (Qurtubi); "wa lā taḥzana" (Baghawi).

Sensitive — "wa qatalta nafsan": framed only as the tafsir gives it.
The Copt (Ibn ʿAbbās); the strike khaṭaʾ, in error — at-Tabari's
marfūʿ from Ibn ʿUmar and al-Qurtubi citing Ṣaḥīḥ Muslim; Mūsā's
own remorse and Allah's forgiveness (28:15–16). Never called a
murderer in the article's voice. Dedicated section states the verse
passes no verdict on the Copts and licenses nothing against any
living community.

"fatannāka futūnan": kept the disagreement — trial/testing (Ibn
ʿAbbās, Qatāda, aḍ-Ḍaḥḥāk) vs. purification (Mujāhid). The long
ḥadīth al-futūn reported with Ibn Kathir's verdict: largely mawqūf,
little marfūʿ, Isrāʾīliyyāt via Kaʿb — used for its arc only, its
particulars flagged as lore no belief rests on.

Madyan ten years (Qurtubi/Baghawi), Wahb's longer count noted
qualitatively. "jiʾta ʿalā qadar": mawʿid (Mujāhid) / prophethood
at forty (Ibn ʿAbbās, Qatāda) / the fixed decree (Muḥammad b. Kaʿb);
Tabari unifies; Saʿdi's "not by chance."

Dropped: age-12 at the killing, Harun's age gap, the 28-year and
goat anecdote figures. 20:41 material (wastanaʿtuka, Adam–Mūsā
hadith) withheld as next-verse ground. No hadith.js fetch. Gate clean.
