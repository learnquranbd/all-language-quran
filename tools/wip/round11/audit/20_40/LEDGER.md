## Attribution → Arabic/text clause it rests on
- Sister named Maryam; went out to trace the child — al-Qurtubi ("وأخته اسمها مريم … خرجت متعرفة خبره"); al-Baghawi ("إذ تمشي أختك واسمها مريم متعرفة خبره").
- Infant barred from all wet-nurses (28:12) — al-Qurtubi ("كان لا يأخذ من أحد"); Ibn Kathir ("وحرمنا عليه المراضع من قبل … فأباها").
- Child passed woman to woman, refused each; sister's offer — at-Tabari via as-Suddi and Ibn Isḥāq ("فلم يأخذ من أحد … فقالت أخته هل أدلكم على أهل بيت يكفلونه لكم وهم له ناصحون").
- Follow-up exchange (they questioned her sincerity; she meant eagerness to please the king) — al-Qurtubi ("فأخذوها وقالوا … قالت: … رغبتهم في ظؤورة الملك").
- Mother took him, he suckled; return to mother; reading fa-radadnāka in codex of Ubayy — al-Qurtubi ("فرجعناك إلى أمك وفى مصحف أبي فرددناك").
- Hired to nurse her own son, gained honour/ease/reward — Ibn Kathir ("واستأجروها على إرضاعه فنالها بسببه سعادة ورفعة وراحة").
- qurrat al-ʿayn: eye cooled = given enough not to strain higher; cool tear of joy vs hot tear of grief — al-Qurtubi ("أعطاه حتى تقر فلا تطمح … وللسرور دمعة باردة وللحزن دمعة حارة").
- "kay taqarra ʿaynuhā" by meeting him; "wa lā taḥzana" = grief departs — al-Baghawi ("بلقائك … لأن يذهب عنها الحزن").
- Slain man = a disbelieving Copt — Ibn ʿAbbās, in al-Qurtubi ("قتل قبطيا كافرا") and al-Baghawi (same).
- Killing was khaṭaʾ (in error): (a) at-Tabari — marfūʿ from ʿAbdullāh ibn ʿUmar, "إنما قتل موسى الذي قتل من آل فرعون خطأ"; (b) al-Qurtubi — "في صحيح مسلم: وكان قتله خطأ" (reported as al-Qurtubi's citation of Ṣaḥīḥ Muslim; chains not independently graded — stated as the sources give them).
- Remorse + forgiveness (28:15–16) — Qurʾānic, quoted in Ibn Kathir and at-Tabari narration ("هذا من عمل الشيطان … رب إني ظلمت نفسي فاغفر لي فغفر له").
- fa-najjaynāka min al-ghamm = saved from Pharaoh's people's resolve to kill / retaliation, fled to Madyan — at-Tabari, Ibn Kathir; as-Saʿdi ("فنجاه الله من الغم من عقوبة الذنب ومن القتل").
- fatannāka futūnan spread: ikhtabarnāka / ubtulita (Ibn ʿAbbās); balawnāka (Qatāda); al-balāʾ ʿalā ithr al-balāʾ (aḍ-Ḍaḥḥāk); akhlaṣnāka (Mujāhid) — at-Tabari and al-Baghawi record all.
- ḥadīth al-futūn: Nisāʾī in Tafsir of his Sunan, via Ibn ʿAbbās; Ibn Kathir reproduced it. STATUS as reported: Ibn Kathir concluded "موقوف من كلام ابن عباس وليس فيه مرفوع إلا قليل منه، وكأنه تلقاه … من الإسرائيليات عن كعب الأحبار" — mawqūf, little marfūʿ, Isrāʾīliyyāt via Kaʿb. Ma'arif: Ibn Jarīr and Ibn Abī Ḥātim carry it mawqūf. Used ONLY for its arc; particulars (coals/pearl, beard) flagged as Israelite lore no belief rests on.
- Years in Madyan = ten (fuller of two terms); Wahb ibn Munabbih relays a longer count — al-Qurtubi ("عشر سنين أتم الأجلين … قال وهب: … ثماني وعشرين سنة") and al-Baghawi (same). (28-year figure deliberately not stated numerically to avoid over-precision; given as "a longer count.")
- jiʾta ʿalā qadar: ʿalā mawʿid (Mujāhid); matching prophethood, prophets raised at forty (Ibn ʿAbbās, Qatāda, ʿAbd al-Raḥmān b. Kaysān); the fixed decree (Muḥammad b. Kaʿb); at-Tabari unifies ("للوقت الذي أردنا إرسالك"); as-Saʿdi — not by chance ("ليس مجيئك اتفاقا").

## Hadith
- No standalone graded-sound marfūʿ hadith quoted whole for this verse. The strongest attached reports are (a) at-Tabari's marfūʿ from Ibn ʿUmar that the killing was khaṭaʾ, and (b) al-Qurtubi's citation of Ṣaḥīḥ Muslim to the same effect — both reported as the tafsir gives them, chains not independently graded here. The long ḥadīth al-futūn is largely mawqūf (Ibn Kathir) and is reported with that status, not used as marfūʿ proof. No hadith.js fetch was made; no sunnah.com/quranx call.
