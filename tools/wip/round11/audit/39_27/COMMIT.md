Card and 8-section bilingual article for 39:27: wa-laqad darabna
li-n-nasi fi hadha al-Qur'ani min kulli mathal, read as the surah's
pause after 39:24-26 to name its own method and its aim, tadhakkur.

Sources: Tabari, Qurtubi, Sa'di, Baghawi, Ibn Kathir (ar + en abridged),
Muyassar. Ma'arif's grouped text covers only 39:24; not used.
- Ibn Kathir: bayyanna bi-darb al-amthal; the mathal brings the meaning
  near to minds; cites 30:28 (ta'lamunahu min anfusikum) and 29:43.
- Tabari (Muyassar verbatim): examples of past generations, set out for
  the mushrikin as warning; li-yatadhakkaru fa-yanzajiru.
- Qurtubi: every example they need (6:38), and qila: the destruction of
  past nations as example; yatadhakkarun = yatta'izun (also Baghawi).
- Sa'di: all kinds (good and evil people, tawhid and shirk, every
  example that brings realities near); so they know and act.
Disagreements kept, no position: what min kulli mathal covers (four
glosses named by source); the address (Sa'di general vs. Tabari's
mushrikin).
Cross-refs: 30:28, 29:43 (Ibn Kathir), 6:38 (Qurtubi), Arabic verified.
17:89, 18:54, 30:58 not cited: no fetched tafsir cites them.
Hadith: none attached by any fetched source; said in one sentence.
Asbab: none. 39:28 named only as the next verse; 39:29 not pulled
forward. Licensing sentence added (EN and BN) for the past peoples.
Themes: parables, guidance.
