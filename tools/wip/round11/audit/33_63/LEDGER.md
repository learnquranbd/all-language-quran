# SOURCES — 33:63

Verse: يَسْـَٔلُكَ ٱلنَّاسُ عَنِ ٱلسَّاعَةِ ۖ قُلْ إِنَّمَا عِلْمُهَا عِندَ ٱللَّهِ ۚ وَمَا يُدْرِيكَ لَعَلَّ ٱلسَّاعَةَ تَكُونُ قَرِيبًا

## Tafsir fetched (all pre-fetched in audit/33_63/, none re-fetched)

- **at-Tabari (15)** — NAMED. Plain sense of the question + "its knowledge is with Allah": "يَسْأَلُكَ النَّاسُ يا محمد عَنِ السَّاعَةِ متى هي قائمة؟ ... إنما علم الساعة عِنْدَ الله لا يعلم وقت قيامها غيره". Gloss of wa mā yudrīka: "وما أشعرك يا محمد لعل قيام الساعة يكون منك قريبًا. قد قرب وقت قيامها، ودنا حين مجيئها". Rests §1, §3 (innamā / none knows but He), §4 (wa mā ash'araka), §5 (nearness paraphrase).
- **al-Qurtubi (90)** — NAMED. Who asked: "هؤلاء المؤذون لرسول الله ... لما توعدوا بالعذاب سألوا عن الساعة ، استبعادا وتكذيبا ، موهمين أنها لا تكون". Prophethood point: "وليس في إخفاء الله وقتها عني ما يبطل نبوتي ، وليس من شرط النبي أن يعلم الغيب بغير تعليم من الله". Two-fingers hadith: "بعثت أنا والساعة كهاتين ... خرجه أهل الصحيح". Grammar: qarīban not qarība, sā'ah treated as al-yawm, parallel "إن رحمة الله قريب من المحسنين"; alternate layta reading "ليت الساعة تكون قريبا". Hidden purpose: "إنما أخفى وقت الساعة ليكون العبد مستعدا لها في كل وقت". Rests §2, §3, §5, §6, §7.
- **al-Muyassar (16)** — NAMED. Motive of askers: "استبعادًا وتكذيبًا". Rests §2.
- **as-Sa'di (91)** — NAMED. Motives: "استعجالاً لها، وبعضهم، تكذيبًا"; pivot: "ومجرد مجيء الساعة، قرباً وبعدًا، ليس تحته نتيجة ولا فائدة ... هل يستحق العبد العذاب، أو يستحق الثواب؟" and that the following verses describe who deserves punishment. Rests §2, §8.
- **al-Baghawi (94)** — NAMED. Gloss of mā yudrīka: "أي شيء يعلمك أمر الساعة ، ومتى يكون قيامها؟ أي : أنت لا تعرفه". Rests §4.
- **Ibn Kathir (14 ar / 169 en)** — NAMED. Meccan precedent (al-A'raf) vs this Medinan verse, referral of knowledge to Allah; nearness parallels 54:1, 21:1, 16:1. "فاستمر الحال في رد علمها إلى الذي يقيمها ، لكن أخبره أنها قريبة". Rests §3 (Meccan/Medinan), §5 (parallels).
- **Ma'arif al-Qur'an (168 en)** — NAMED. Disbelievers "mockingly ask: When would that Qiyamah come?"; "everyone should be prepared for it as if it were very close"; and after Qiyamah's horrors "the entire worldly life of thousands of years will seem to be very short". Rests §2, §7.

## Hadith

1. **Sahih al-Bukhari 6503** (two fingers) — fetched https://quranx.com/hadith/Bukhari/DarusSalam/Hadith-6503 ; saved. First ten words (EN): "Narrated Sahl: Allah's Messenger said, I have been sent". Wording used, quoted whole: "I have been sent, and the Hour, like these two," with his index and middle fingers stretched out. Grading: Sahih al-Bukhari (sahih by the collector's placement; no separate grade given). al-Qurtubi attaches this hadith directly to the verse ("خرجه أهل الصحيح"). Used §5.
2. **Sahih al-Bukhari 50** (hadith of Jibril) — fetched https://quranx.com/hadith/Bukhari/DarusSalam/Hadith-50 ; saved. First ten words (EN): "Narrated Abu Huraira: One day while the Prophet was sitting". Used only the clause on the Hour: "The answerer has no better knowledge than the questioner" (rendered "the answerer knows no more about it than the questioner"), and the fact that he then recited 31:34. First fetch ETIMEDOUT; retried once, succeeded. Grading: Sahih al-Bukhari (sahih by placement). Used §4. NOTE: this hadith recites 31:34 and lists the five keys / portents — kept OUT of the article to stay distinct from the shipped 31:34; only the "asked knows no more than the asker" line and the bare cross-reference to 31:34 are used.
