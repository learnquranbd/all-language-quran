# SOURCES ledger — 24:58

## Tafsir fetched (all pre-fetched in PREP, covers 24:58 unless noted)

- **at-Tabari (15)** — NAMED. Two readings of "الَّذِينَ مَلَكَتْ أَيْمَانُكُمْ": Ibn Umar
  "هي على الذكور دون الإناث"; Abu Abd al-Rahman "هي في الرجال والنساء ... بالليل والنهار";
  Tabari's own tarjih "وأولى القولين ... عُني به الذكور والإناث؛ لأن الله عمّ ... جميع أملاك أيماننا".
  Two qira'at of "ثلاث عورات": rafʿ (Madina/Basra) vs naṣb (Kufa), "قراءتان متقاربتا المعنى ...
  فبأيتهما قرأ القارئ فمصيب". "طوّافون ... يدخلون ويخرجون ... بغير إذن". Ibn Abbas: rukhsa to
  enter between the three times without leave. Also the three-neglected-verses athar.
- **al-Qurtubi (90)** — NAMED. "هذه الآية خاصة والتي قبلها عامة" (general vs particular).
  The six views on the ruling (mas'ala 2): (1) mansukha — Ibn al-Musayyab, Ibn Jubayr [listed];
  (2) nadb not wajib — Abu Qilaba; (3) means women — al-Sulami [called weakest]; (4) men not
  women — Ibn Umar; (5) was wajib because no locks/doors, returns if condition returns — Ibn
  Abbas via al-Mahdawi; (6) muhkama wajiba on men and women — "قول أكثر أهل العلم ... القاسم
  وجابر بن زيد والشعبي". 'awra glossed "الأوقات التي تقتضي عادة الناس الانكشاف فيها". Umar/Mudlij
  sabab incl. the sujud shukr. NOTE: Qurtubi says "وهي مكية" of the verse — NOT repeated
  (surah an-Nur is Madani; treated as his slip, dropped).
- **as-Sa'di (91)** — NAMED. Reason for the three times (night change of clothes vs short
  midday rest still-dressed: "قيده بقوله وحين تضعون ثيابكم من الظهيرة"). "يحتاج إليهم دائما،
  فيشق الاستئذان منهم في كل وقت" → basis for the hardship point. Closing names read as
  wisdom fitting each ruling to its place.
- **Ibn Kathir (ar, 14)** — NAMED. Relatives vs strangers framing; reasons per time; the
  ṭawwāfūn → CAT hadith link "إنها ليست بنجس؛ إنها من الطوافين عليكم أو الطوافات" (attributes
  to Malik, Ahmad, Ahl al-Sunan). "محكمة ولم تنسخ". Ibn Abbas three-verses athar; the
  satr/curtains athar with his own verdict "هذا إسناد صحيح إلى ابن عباس"; "لآمر جاريتي هذه
  تستأذن علي". al-Suddi and Muqatil (Asma bint Murshida) reports.
- **al-Baghawi (94)** — NAMED. Sabab: Ibn Abbas → Mudlij b. Amr sent to Umar at zuhr; Muqatil
  → Asma bint Marthad. "لم يبلغوا الحلم" = "ليس ... الأطفال الذين لم يظهروا على عورات النساء،
  بل الذين عرفوا أمر النساء ولكن لم يبلغوا" (used in §2.2). Qira'at naṣb (Hamza, Kisa'i, Abu
  Bakr) vs rafʿ. Naskh dispute incl. al-Sha'bi "لا والله ما نُسخت" and Sa'id b. Jubayr.
- **al-Muyassar (16)** — read, corroborating only (three times, tastut, ṭawwāfūn for service);
  not separately named.
- **Ma'arif al-Qur'an (en, 168)** — NAMED. Household isti'dhan vs visitor isti'dhan; adult male
  slave is non-mahram so phrase = slave-girls/minor slaves; the ʿamal/naskh discussion, majority
  hold firm not abrogated (attributed to Qurtubi); Ibn Abbas narrations both ways; the burden
  falls on adults to teach children (the pray-at-seven analogy, §8.1).
- **Ibn Kathir (en abridged, 169)** — read, corroborates reasons per time and the "clear, not
  abrogated, people hardly follow it" point with Abu Dawud/Ibn Abbas and ash-Sha'bi (Musa b.
  Abi Aisha). Used as cross-check; ar Ibn Kathir cited by name.

## Hadith

- **Cat hadith** — Jami' at-Tirmidhi 92. URL fetched:
  https://quranx.com/hadith/Tirmidhi/Hadith-92 (saved Tirmidhi_92 in this dir).
  First ten words (EN): "Humaidah bint Ubaid bin Rifa'ah narrated: Kabshah bint Ka'b".
  Wording used, quoted whole from this collection: "It is not impure, it is only one of those
  that roam around among you" (إنها ليست بنجس إنما هي من الطوافين عليكم أو الطوافات).
  GRADING as the page gives it: Tirmidhi's own remark "وهذا أحسن شيء رُوي في هذا الباب" (the
  soundest report in this chapter) + "وقد جوّد مالك هذا الحديث". Reported as-is; NOT upgraded to
  "sahih". Tie to verse: Ibn Kathir cites this ṭawwāfūn hadith directly under 24:58.

## Asbab an-nuzul

- USED (as the mufassirun give them, two accounts, no verdict, §7.1):
  (a) Ibn Abbas via al-Baghawi/al-Qurtubi — the boy Mudlij of the Ansar sent to Umar at midday;
  (b) Muqatil via al-Baghawi/Ibn Kathir — Asma bint Marthad and her grown servant.
  Presented as two reports without ranking, per the tafsirs. Not driven to a single "occasion".

## Dropped / not used

- Qurtubi's "وهي مكية" — dropped as a slip (an-Nur is Madani).
- al-Suddi's report (companions wishing to be intimate at these hours then wash for prayer) —
  confirmable in Ibn Kathir but dropped to keep the tone sober per the brief; no graphic detail.
- Tafsir Mazhari's makruh-tanzihi ruling on entering one's own house (quoted inside Ma'arif) —
  dropped; it is a secondary fiqh graft, not needed for the lesson.
- Qurtubi's long digression on naming ʿishāʾ vs ʿatama and the fada'il of ʿishāʾ/fajr in
  congregation (mas'ala 7-8) — off-topic for this verse's spine; dropped.
- The variant name "Asma bint Murshida" (Ibn Kathir) vs "bint Marthad" (Baghawi) — used the
  Baghawi spelling "Marthad"; the difference noted here, not in the article.
