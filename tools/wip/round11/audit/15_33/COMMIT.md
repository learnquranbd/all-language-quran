Card and bilingual article (8 sections, 1,750 EN words) for Iblis's reply,
"I am not one to prostrate to a human You created from ringing clay, from
altered black mud", read as the answer to the question in 15:32.

Sources: at-Tabari, al-Baghawi and al-Qurtubi all supply the unspoken half
of the comparison (he is of clay, I am of fire, and fire eats clay);
as-Sa'di names pride against the command and enmity to Adam and his
offspring, and his admiring his own element; the Muyassar glosses the
refusal as "it does not befit me" and names pride and envy. Ibn Kathir
(abridged, grouped 15:28-33) links 7:12 and 17:62. Ma'arif (grouped
15:31-40) explains why the command reached Iblis though addressed to the
angels. Ibn Kathir's Arabic verdict on at-Tabari's Ibn Abbas report
(strange, doubtful, apparently Isra'ili) is given, and nothing built on it.

Hadith: Sahih Muslim 91 (pride is disdaining the truth and contempt for
people), marked as a general narration not attached to the verse; no
fetched tafsir attaches a hadith to 15:33.

Dropped: rebuttals of the fire-over-clay reasoning (none in the texts on
this verse; al-Qurtubi defers to al-A'raf, pointer to 7:12); the angel or
jinn question (not raised by any fetched text); grammar of lam akun
beyond the Muyassar gloss. 15:26's clay readings pointed to, not
repeated; 15:34-35 left to their verses. Plain statement that the verse
licenses nothing against any living person or community.
Fixed before merge: the quranx English of Muslim 91 says "mustard seed" where
the Arabic has mithqal dharra; both languages now follow the Arabic (a speck).
