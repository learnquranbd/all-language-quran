- 168 Ma'arif (en), GROUPED 15:31-15:40 per the file's COVERS line (not 15:31-38), stated as grouped. NAMED (§6): "the command to prostrate was given, along with the angels, to Iblis as well ... as Iblis too was present among the angels ... It was for this reason that Iblis did not respond by saying that he was never asked to prostrate". Its remark on أبى أن يكون مع الساجدين concerns 15:31 and was not used.
