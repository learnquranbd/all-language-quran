## Universality (watch-point)
No fetched tafsir cites 25:1, 7:158 or 34:28 (or any verse) on "man hawlaha"; none cited. Reported only: Sa'di's two-step widening; Tabari/Muyassar "all people", Qurtubi "all creation", IK "all lands east and west", Baghawi "villages of the whole earth"; Ma'arif's range. No position taken.

## Yawm al-jam' gatherings (watch-point)
Fetched texts give only: first and last on one plain (IK, Sa'di); first and last + people of the heavens and of the earths (Baghawi); servants for reckoning and presentation (Tabari). "People and jinn", "souls and bodies", "people and their deeds" are NOT in any fetched text; not used.
