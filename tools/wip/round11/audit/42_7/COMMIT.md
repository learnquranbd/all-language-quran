Card and 8-section bilingual article for 42:7: wa-kadhalika awhayna
ilayka qur'anan 'arabiyyan, umm al-qura wa man hawlaha, yawm al-jam'
la rayba fihi, and fariqun fi-l-jannati wa fariqun fi-s-sa'ir. 42:3 for
placement; 41:3 one line; 42:5 not drawn on; 42:8 left to its own verse.

Sources: Tabari, Ibn Kathir (ar; en abridged as rendering), Muyassar,
Qurtubi, Sa'di, Baghawi, Ma'arif (grouped 42:7-9).
- Arabic Qur'an: Tabari and Qurtubi on the hearers' tongue (14:4 wording);
  IK "clear"; Sa'di "a favour upon the Messenger and upon people".
- Umm al-qura: Makkah in all; Baghawi and Muyassar "its people". Title:
  nobler than all lands (IK, with Tirmidhi 3925, hasan sahih gharib);
  earth spread from beneath it (Qurtubi, qila); origin and foundation
  (Ma'arif).
- Man hawlaha: Arab villages, then all creation (Sa'di) vs all people /
  all creation / east and west / whole earth from the start (Tabari,
  Muyassar, Qurtubi, IK, Baghawi); Ma'arif's range. Kept, no position.
  No universality verse cited, since no fetched tafsir cites one.
- Yawm al-jam': Tabari's two parsings (3:175) vs Qurtubi's bi-; first
  and last on one plain (IK, Sa'di), plus heavens and earths (Baghawi);
  IK's 64:9 and 11:103-105; Baghawi "after the gathering they part".
- Fariqun: raf' as new sentence (Tabari; Qurtubi), al-Kisa'i's nasb;
  parties by belief and following (Tabari, Muyassar, Sa'di).
Hadith: Tirmidhi 2141 (two books), quoted whole, hasan gharib sahih;
clauses explained from Tabari's note and IK (en). Baghawi's ungraded
"bounty / justice" ending reported as his variant.
Licensing sentence in both languages. Cross-refs checked in quran-json.
Dropped: Abu Firas mawquf (Adam's two handfuls) with IK's remark on it,
Ahmad's Abu Nadrah report, Tabari's Musa balagh, own-voice qadar.
