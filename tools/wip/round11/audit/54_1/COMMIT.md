Card and 8-section bilingual article on iqtarabati s-sa'atu wa-nshaqqa
l-qamar. 54:2-3 (the deniers' "continuing magic") a pointer only.

Sources: at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi, as-Sa'di, al-Baghawi, al-Muyassar, Ibn
Kathir (ar; en abridged checked), Ma'arif al-Qur'an.
- Iqtarabat: ifta'alat from qurb, a warning and command to prepare
  (Tabari); = qarubat, like azifat al-azifa (Qurtubi); near relative to
  what has passed (Qurtubi). 16:1, 21:1 via Ibn Kathir; 53:57 via Ma'arif.
- The Makkan request for a sign as told by Tabari, Muyassar, Sa'di
  (Abu Qubays / Qu'ayqi'an), Baghawi (Muqatil: rejoined), Qurtubi.
- Disagreement kept, no position: already split (Qurtubi, Hudhayfa's
  reading "wa-qad inshaqqa", "jumhur"; Ibn Kathir "agreed upon"; Tabari's
  athar) vs awaited (a group, al-Qushayri, al-Mawardi calling it the
  majority view, al-Hasan: after the second blowing). Qurtubi's two qila
  readings and Ibn Kaysan's reordering (al-Farra', 53:8) recorded.
- Quraysh "ask the travellers" (Tabari, Baghawi; ungraded) and Jubayr via
  Ahmad (Ibn Kathir); licensing sentence in both languages.
- Hudhayfa's al-Mada'in sermon (Tabari, Ibn Kathir), marked as his words.
Hadith: Muslim 891 (Eid recitation), Bukhari 4864 (Ibn Mas'ud) and 3868
(Anas), each attached by Baghawi/Ibn Kathir with the same chain; Bukhari
4825 (Ibn Mas'ud, mawquf, "five have passed"). All "in the Sahih".
Dropped: Ma'arif's science/history rebuttal and Malabar story; the weak
"remains of the day" report; two-fingers hadith (already in 33:63);
Tirmidhi grading (unconfirmed); Tabarani "eclipse" report.
Muslim 2800/2803 timed out, not used; two-fingers hadith left out (33:63 already quotes it); Bukhari 4825 marked Ibn Mas'ud's own words.
