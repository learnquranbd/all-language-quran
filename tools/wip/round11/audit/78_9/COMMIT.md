Replaces the 714-word, 6-section article with a 1,679-word, 8-section deep article, built only on tafsir fetched for 78:9 (shipped card kept).

Sources: at-Tabari, Ibn Kathir (ar; en abridged for the 78:1-10 frame),
al-Qurtubi, as-Sa'di, al-Baghawi, al-Muyassar, Ma'arif al-Qur'an, all for 78:9.

Frame: Ibn Kathir's great news = the Day of Judgement and the list 78:6-16 as
proof of power over the Hereafter; licensing sentence on the deniers.
Grammar: al-Qurtubi's ja'alna = sayyarna (two objects); al-Muyassar keeps the
verse inside the question of 78:6. Subat glosses held by name: rest for the
body (at-Tabari, al-Muyassar, al-Qurtubi, al-Baghawi; as-Sa'di adds the cut
in occupations); cutting off movement (Ibn Kathir ar, al-Baghawi's second
view, al-Qurtubi's sabata sha'rahu, Ma'arif on worries); stretching out and
easy pace, with the poet's line (al-Qurtubi). Disagreement kept: Saturday
derivation (at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi) vs Ibn al-Anbari's "rest is not called
subat", no position. Death-likeness with its limit (at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi,
az-Zajjaj via al-Baghawi). Ma'arif on sleep as an equal, free, imposed gift.
25:47 one line via Ibn Kathir's al-Furqan note; 78:3, 78:14, 78:18 one line.

Hadith: none attached by any fetched tafsir; said in one sentence.
Asbab: none.

Dropped from the old article: 30:23 and its "signs" argument, the al-Bara
bedtime du'a (unattached, unverified), the Makkah-concession claim, the
"nine makings" count, the sleep-to-reckoning inference beyond Ibn Kathir.
