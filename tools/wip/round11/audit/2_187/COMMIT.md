Key kept at 2:187 (inside the free window 2:187-188); 2:188 left free. New card
and a 1,750-word, 8-section article centred on ease after hardship and on limits
kept at a distance.

Sources: at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi, Ibn Kathir (ar + en abridged), as-Sa'di,
al-Baghawi, al-Muyassar, Ma'arif al-Qur'an on 2:187. Slots on target. at-Tabari's
API text stops at the wisal discussion, and the 2:188 slot is his own 2:188, so
nothing on i'tikaf or hudud is attributed to him.
- The night rule lifted: Ibn Kathir, as-Sa'di. Libas in at-Tabari's three senses
  (closeness, sakan with 25:47 and 7:189, a screen), al-Qurtubi, Muyassar, and
  Ibn Kathir's link to the concession. Register kept modest; rafath as allusion
  (Ibn 'Abbas via at-Tabari).
- 'Umar named only as the tafsirs report him (at-Tabari 2415, Ibn Kathir), with
  no grading, never framed as sin in the article's voice. Ibn al-'Arabi's zuhd
  line via al-Qurtubi; Ibn Kathir's "rahmah, rukhsah, rifq".
Disagreements kept: taba/'afa as repentance and pardon or as lightening and ease
(al-Qurtubi, 73:20; as-Sa'di joins them); "ma kataba" as children (Ibn Kathir's
list, at-Tabari's preference), the concession (Qatadah) or Laylat al-Qadr (Ibn
'Abbas, Mu'adh via al-Baghawi); fasting for i'tikaf (Malik, Abu Hanifa, Ahmad vs
al-Shafi'i, Ahmad's other view, per al-Qurtubi); touching in i'tikaf (most
scholars vs Malik, per al-Baghawi); "fa-la taqrabuha" as do not overstep (Ibn
Kathir) or keep away from the means as well (as-Sa'di, with 2:229).
Hadith: Bukhari 1915 (Qays ibn Sirma), 4508 (al-Bara', "betray themselves"),
1916 ('Adi ibn Hatim, the two cords), 2026 (i'tikaf in the last ten), all whole.
Dropped: Ibn al-'Arabi's 'Umar-not-Qays occasion argument, the horizon vs streets
dawn dispute, Sahl ibn Sa'd (1917), the Prophet's remark to 'Umar, which mosques.
