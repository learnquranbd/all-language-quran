Card and 8-section bilingual article (1,671 EN words) on 56:35: inna
ansha'nahunna insha'a. 56:34 frame only; 56:36-38 framed and left; one
pointer to 56:22 (shipped v767).

Sources: at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi, al-Baghawi, Ibn Kathir (ar), as-Sa'di,
Ma'arif; al-Muyassar (group 56:35-38) and Ibn Kathir en (group 56:27-36)
used only for their 56:34-35 lines.
- insha' glosses attributed: created and brought into being (at-Tabari,
  Qatada), created and originated/ibda' (al-Qurtubi), new creation
  (al-Baghawi), unlike the worldly making, complete, not perishing
  (Muyassar, as-Sa'di). No source explains the verbal noun; none claimed.
- Antecedent: al-Akhfash (unmentioned) vs Abu Ubayda (hur of 56:22-23),
  both via at-Tabari and Ibn Kathir; Ibn Kathir: furush context implies
  the women (38:31-32 parallel); al-Qurtubi: firash/libas/izar for woman,
  2:187, two reasons for the bare pronoun; Ma'arif: obvious if firash =
  women.
- Referent kept as a disagreement: hur created without birth (al-Qurtubi
  "qila", Abu Ubayda, Ma'arif) vs women of this world restored to youth
  (al-Qurtubi "qila", Ibn Abbas via al-Baghawi, Ibn Kathir's own gloss,
  Muyassar/as-Sa'di wording). Ma'arif holds both (its ranking); al-Qurtubi
  unranked; at-Tabari states no preference.
- Site EN "[the women of Paradise]", "[new]" and BN "ঐ হুরদেরকে" flagged
  as translator's brackets; BN sides with the hur reading.
Hadith: Tirmidhi 3296 (Anas) named with at-Tirmidhi's own grading
(gharib; Musa ibn Ubayda and Yazid ar-Raqashi weakened), not quoted.
Shama'il 239 (old woman, via al-Hasan) quoted whole; no grading on the
page, no Companion named, stated. Umm Salama, Salama ibn Yazid and
al-Bayhaqi/'A'isha named as unconfirmed. Asbab: none.
Dropped: Ibn Kathir's marital/strength narrations and the Sur hadith,
'uruban/atraban glosses (56:37 ground), Ma'arif's "dark-coloured".
