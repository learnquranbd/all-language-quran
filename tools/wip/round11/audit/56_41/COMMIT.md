Card and 8-section bilingual article (1,642 EN words) on 56:41: wa-ashabu
sh-shimali ma ashabu sh-shimal. 56:27 and 56:39-40 frame only; 56:42-47
named and left (56:47 next in wave); no shipped pointer.

Sources: at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi, as-Sa'di, Ibn Kathir (ar); al-Muyassar
(group 56:41-44) and Ibn Kathir en (group 56:41-50) used only for their
56:41 lines plus a frame pointer; al-Baghawi's note is the verse text
only (stated); Ma'arif's group text covers 56:39-40 only (stated).
- Name, kept as a disagreement, unranked: books taken in the left hand
  (al-Qurtubi) vs led along the left from the place of reckoning to the
  Fire (at-Tabari). as-Sa'di: people of the Fire and of ill-omened deeds.
- Question: wonder put to the Prophet, what is theirs and what is
  prepared (at-Tabari, Qatada via Bishr-Yazid-Sa'id); made grave in
  affliction and punishment (al-Qurtubi); what state they are in (Ibn
  Kathir ar/en, answered from 56:42); how evil their state (Muyassar).
- Site EN keeps the question; BN renders an exclamation; noted.
- Licensing sentence in both languages; no living group named.
Hadith: none attached in any fetched note; two-handfuls / Adam's-back
narrations not confirmed on a fetched page; omitted and stated.
Asbab: none.
Dropped: "ill-omened" / Adam's-left-side glosses of the name, the 56:9
mash'ama link and the 56:8-9 parallel (no 56:41 source draws them).
