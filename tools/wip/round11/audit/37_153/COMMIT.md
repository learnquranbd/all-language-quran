Card plus 8-section bilingual article on "has He chosen daughters over
sons?" (a-stafa al-banat 'ala al-banin), the rebuke of Quraysh's claim.

Sources gave:
- at-Tabari: muwabbikhan, to those of Quraysh who said Allah has
  daughters; majority qira'at question hamza; meaning one either way,
  46:20 as the parallel for dropping the question alif.
- al-Qurtubi / al-Baghawi: qira'at al-'amma a-stafa; joined reading
  attributed (Abu Ja'far; Qurtubi adds Shayba, Nafi', Hamza); Abu Hatim's
  objection and Qurtubi's reply (al-Farra'); two wa-qila routes.
- as-Sa'di, al-Muyassar, Ibn Kathir: ikhtara; reason-for-choosing framing;
  17:40 by number.
- Ma'arif (group 37:149-157): ilzami answer, premise used not adopted.
