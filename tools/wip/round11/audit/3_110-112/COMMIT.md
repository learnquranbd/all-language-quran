Assigned 3:112, widened to 3:110-112 (the best community brought out for
people; harm only in words; the humiliation and its named causes). 3:108-109
left free; 3:106-107 and 3:113-115 ship. New card and a 1,746-word,
8-section article; the licensing sentence opens it in both languages, citing
laysu sawa' (3:113 to 3:115).

Sources: at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi, Ibn Kathir (ar + en), as-Sa'di, al-Baghawi,
al-Muyassar, Ma'arif. Slots: at-Tabari runs one verse behind (t15_111 = end
of 3:110, t15_112 = 3:111); his 3:112 fetched from the 3:113 slot (t15_113).
- Kuntum = antum (at-Tabari, 8:26 / 7:86); "best of people for people"
  (Ibn Kathir's list, al-Muyassar); ma'ruf and munkar defined.
- The praise conditional: 'Umar at Hajj via Qatada ("fulfil Allah's
  condition"), al-Qurtubi (praise leaves when changing wrong is abandoned),
  Ibn Kathir (else like 5:79), as-Sa'di (3:104 commanded, 3:110 fulfilled).
- Habl = covenant (Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatada, 'Ikrima, as-Suddi, ar-Rabi',
  ad-Dahhak, 'Ata'; Ibn Zayd's al-'Aqaba usage); no present-day application.
- Causes: at-Tabari on the signs and the killing; second dhalika as root
  (Ibn Kathir, as-Sa'di); at-Tabari and Qatada: a warning to this ummah.
Kept as differences: who kuntum names (Muhajirun / Companions / whole
ummah: at-Tabari prefers al-Hasan, Ibn Kathir general); adha as cut-off
(at-Tabari) or connected (al-Qurtubi); the two ropes (Ibn Kathir dhimma and
aman, al-Baghawi Islam and jizya, Ma'arif all people).
Hadith: Tirmidhi 3001 (Bahz; seventy nations), hasan per at-Tirmidhi,
quoted whole. No sound hadith on 3:112, said in one sentence.
Dropped: Ibn Mas'ud's ungraded "300 prophets a day"; Ma'arif's application
to a present-day state; Khaybar/Sham expansions; commentators' generalisations
about Jews as such; asbab from 'Ikrima and Muqatil (not established).
