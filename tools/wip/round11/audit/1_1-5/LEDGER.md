## Dropped from the shipped article (not confirmed in the files fetched for 1:1-5, or contradicted by them)
- "Carries more names in the tradition than any other surah": not in any fetched file. Replaced with al-Qurtubi's count of twelve.
- "Al-Bukhari and Muslim relate there is no prayer for the one who does not recite the Opening of the Book": Ibn Kathir (t14_5) does cite this from the Sahihs ('Ubadah), but the hadith was not fetched through hadith.js, so it is dropped. Muslim 395's own "deficient" wording is used instead.
- "Recites it at least seventeen times a day": arithmetic, not from a fetched source, and the 1:6 article already carries it.
- "Both positions are held by great authorities; the difference shows in whether an imam recites it aloud": too vague, and incomplete. Per Ibn Kathir, Malik's school does not recite the basmalah in the prayer at all. Now named side by side.
- "Ar-Rahman describes mercy as His very nature, ar-Rahim mercy in action": WRONG framing. No fetched mufassir splits the two names as essence/action. Al-Baghawi's essence/action split concerns two definitions of rahmah, not the two names. The sources give general/specific (at-Tabari, al-Baghawi, al-Muyassar, as-Sa'di) or same-meaning (al-Baghawi's first group).
- "His mercy has already been named twice, and it will be named twice more": the count only works if the basmalah is a verse of the surah, which is the disputed question. Dropped.
- "Hamd is wider than thanks" stated as fact: WRONG as an unqualified claim. At-Tabari holds the two interchangeable (t15_2). Ibn Kathir reports a relation in which each is wider in one respect. Only al-Baghawi says hamd is wider. Now kept as a disagreement.
- "The verse does not command us to praise": WRONG. Al-Baghawi (taqdiruhu qulu al-hamdu lillah), al-Muyassar (fi dimnihi amrun li-'ibadihi) and Ibn Kathir quoting Ibn Jarir all read a command or lesson inside the statement.
- "The definite article makes it total ... whoever utters it": not in the fetched texts in this form. As-Sa'di's «فله الحمد الكامل, بجميع الوجوه» is close but was not used. Dropped.
- Rabb as "the one who raises something stage by stage toward completion": not in the fetched texts. At-Tabari gives master, mender and owner; al-Baghawi gives owner and nurturer. Dropped.
- 'Alamin as "mankind, jinn, angels, and whatever we have no name for": not as given. Replaced with at-Tabari's definition and al-Baghawi's two reports.
- "Sovereignty could terrify; so mercy is set before judgment": own-voice reasoning. Replaced with al-Qurtubi's tarhib/targhib reading, which pairs mercy with Rabb al-'alamin, not with the Day.
- "Placing it in every prayer keeps accountability daily": kept only as al-Muyassar's statement.
- "We do not approach Him as customers"; "the single worshipper prays as part of a community across centuries": own voice, and the plural belongs to the 1:6 article. Dropped. Ibn Kathir's explanations of the plural were not used either.
- "The commentators note the shift": now attributed to al-Qurtubi and Ibn Kathir by name.
- "The fronting means only": kept, but now as as-Sa'di (hasr) and Ibn Kathir (ihtimam wa hasr), with al-Qurtubi's different reason (ihtimam, and not naming the worshipper before the Worshipped).
