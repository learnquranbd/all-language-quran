Disagreements kept: as-Suddi as reported by at-Tabari (wealth / Paradise) against al-Baghawi (lawful provision and righteous deeds / forgiveness and reward), both given (S2). The two reports from Ali (al-Baghawi: righteous wife / Paradise; al-Qurtubi: beautiful wife / hur / bad wife, which al-Qurtubi himself rejects) (S2). Whom "those" in 2:202 covers: both groups (as-Sa'di) against those who asked for both worlds (Ma'arif, abridged Ibn Kathir) (S8). Hereafter hasanah: Paradise alone (at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi "by consensus", al-Muyassar) against wider lists (Ibn Kathir, as-Sa'di, Ma'arif), presented as wider lists (S4).

Statement that the word gathers them all, attributed: at-Tabari (generality, not to be narrowed), al-Qurtubi (indefinite in a prayer, every good in turn; majority view), Ibn Kathir (لا منافاة ... كلها مندرجة), Ma'arif ("inclusive of all that is good"; S3 uses its list only).
