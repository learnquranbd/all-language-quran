Card and bilingual article (8 sections, 1,751 EN words) for the angels'
reply to Lut, "rather, we have come to you with that which they were
doubting", read as the answer to 15:62's "you are people unknown".

Sources: every fetched mufassir names the doubted thing as the
punishment. Ibn Kathir (ar): their punishment, destruction and ruin,
doubted as to its befalling them bi-sahatihim; al-Qurtubi and
al-Baghawi: they doubted it would descend on them; at-Tabari's gloss
"the punishment of the people of Lut" via Mujahid (fetched text is cut
at the start, so only Mujahid named). Difference of emphasis kept, no
position: as-Sa'di (yukadhdhibunaka), al-Baghawi (la yusaddiqunahu)
and the Muyassar (la yusaddiqun) pair the doubt with belying Lut; Ibn
Kathir and al-Qurtubi speak of the doubt alone. Muyassar's la takhaf
reported as its paraphrase. Ibn Kathir abridged named as grouped
15:61-64 (young men with handsome faces; 15:64 tied to 15:8). Ma'arif
file covers 15:48-57 (Jannah) and is set aside as off-verse.

Qur'an links in the article's voice: 29:29 (bring us the punishment if
you are truthful), 54:36 (fa-tamaraw, same root), 15:6-8 (the demand
for angels), 10:94 (al-mumtarin), 2:260.

Hadith: none; no fetched tafsir attaches one to 15:63.

11:81 and 11:82 get one pointer each; 15:64-66 pointers only. Plain
statement in both languages that the verse describes a people as the
text describes them and licenses nothing against any living person or
community.
