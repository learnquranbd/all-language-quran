## Tafsir (all from files already fetched by prep.js; none re-fetched)
- 14 Ibn Kathir (ar) — NAMED. Clause: "يعنون بعذابهم وهلاكهم ودمارهم الذي كانوا يشكون في وقوعه بهم وحلوله بساحتهم" (punishment, destruction, ruin; doubted its befalling them and descending bi-sahatihim).
- 169 Ibn Kathir (en, abridged) — NAMED, grouped 15:61-64 (said so). Used: angels as young men with handsome faces entering his home; "bringing the punishment and destruction that the people doubted they would ever suffer"; on 15:64 wa ataynaka bil-haqq he cites 15:8; "in affirmation of the news ... he would be saved and his people would be destroyed". 15:64 pointer only.
- 90 al-Qurtubi — NAMED. Clause: "أي يشكون أنه نازل بهم , وهو العذاب". On-verse here (verse text quoted in the file). Same line was the off-verse stray in 15:36's file.
- 94 al-Baghawi — NAMED. Clause: "يشكون في أنه نازل بهم ، وهو العذاب ، لأنه كان يوعدهم بالعذاب ولا يصدقونه".
- 91 as-Sa'di — NAMED. Clause: "جئناك بعذابهم الذي كانوا يشكون فيه ويكذبونك حين تعدهم به".
- 16 al-Muyassar — NAMED. Clause: "قالوا: لا تَخَفْ، فإنَّا جئنا بالعذاب الذي كان يشك فيه قومك ولا يُصَدِّقون" (la takhaf reported as the Muyassar's paraphrase, not verse text). Rest of its 15:63-65 paraphrase not used (15:64-65 future ground).
- 15 at-Tabari — NAMED (Mujahid only). Clause: "( بِمَا كَانُوا فِيهِ يَمْتَرُونَ ) قال. بعذاب قوم لوط. حدثنا القاسم ... عن ابن أبي نجيح، عن مجاهد، مثله." Fetched text is truncated at the start; first narrator's name missing, so only Mujahid named; article says so.
- 168 Ma'arif (en) — SET ASIDE, off-verse: file covers 15:48-57 (Jannah: no weariness, no expulsion). Article says so in one sentence.

Reading kept: all agree the thing doubted = the punishment. Difference of emphasis kept without position: Ibn Kathir / al-Qurtubi doubt alone (about its arriving); as-Sa'di / al-Baghawi / Muyassar pair doubt with belying / not believing Lut.
