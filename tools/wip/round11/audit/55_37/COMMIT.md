Card and 8-section bilingual article for 55:37: fa-idha inshaqqati
s-sama'u fa-kanat wardatan ka-d-dihan. 55:33 and 55:35 as framing only;
55:38 refrain with one pointer to 55:13; 55:39 not developed.

Sources: at-Tabari, Ibn Kathir (ar; en abridged as group 55:37-45),
al-Qurtubi, al-Baghawi, as-Sa'di, al-Muyassar. Ma'arif (group 55:35-38)
speaks only to 55:35; noted as unused.
- Day of Resurrection: Tabari, Qurtubi, Ibn Kathir, Sa'di, Muyassar;
  69:16, 25:25, 84:1-2 as cited by Ibn Kathir; Baghawi: gates for angels.
- warda kept as a disagreement: rose (Muyassar, Qurtubi qila), red
  (Sa'id ibn Jubayr, Qatada), ward horse with seasonal coat (Ibn Abbas
  via Qabus, Tabari, Abu Salih, ad-Dahhak, al-Farra', Baghawi),
  colour changes (Ibn Abbas via al-Awfi, al-Hasan).
- dihan: oil and its variants (Mujahid, ad-Dahhak, Ata x2, Muqatil,
  al-Hasan, Zayd ibn Aslam, as-Suddi) vs red hide (ad-Dahhak from Ibn
  Abbas, Abu Ubayd and al-Farra' via Qurtubi, al-Kalbi). Only Tabari
  ranks (oil). Site EN translation follows rose/oil, BN red leather.
- Melting: Ibn Kathir, Muyassar, Sa'di, Ibn Jurayj; three tie it to
  the severity and terror of the Day.
- fa-idha answer: no fetched source discusses it; none supplied.
- Refrain as favour per Ibn Kathir (en), named as group reading.
Hadith: none. Ibn Kathir's Musnad Ahmad narration not confirmable here;
left out unquoted. Asbab: none.
Dropped: al-Mawardi's sky-colour claim (no science), az-Zajjaj on
w-r-d (length), 70:8 for muhl (not cited by a source).
