Card + 8-section bilingual article (1,660 EN words) on 37:55, the believer
looking down and seeing his qarin "in the midst of the Hellfire" after 37:54's
"would you care to look?"

Sources (all fetched for 37:55 via PREP.md, none re-fetched): at-Tabari (the
elided clause before this verse, "they said: yes"; Qatada's report that the
believer "asked his Lord to let him look"; two chains on the companion being
unrecognisable but for Allah's making him known — Khulayd al-'Usari and
Mutarrif ibn 'Abdillah), Ibn Kathir ar/en abridged (the six-name consensus
naming "sawa'" as the middle of Hellfire — Ibn 'Abbas, Sa'id ibn Jubayr,
Khulayd al-'Usari, Qatada, as-Suddi, 'Ata' al-Khurasani; al-Hasan al-Basri's
"as if he were a burning star"; Qatada's report of boiling skulls; Ka'b
al-Ahbar's report of windows in Paradise), al-Qurtubi (Ibn Mas'ud's gloss of
"sawa'" with thorny plants around the Fire's middle; the sawa'/exhaustion
idiom via Abu 'Ubayda; the same "unrecognisable" report via a second chain,
anonymised as "some of the scholars"), as-Sa'di (sawa' as the punishment's
floods, already closed around him), al-Baghawi (the grammar of sawa' as
"equal sides"; Ibn 'Abbas's report of openings in Paradise), al-Muyassar
(confirms the figure seen is "qarinahu").

Sawa' al-jahim kept as the near-unanimous reading it is, stated briefly as
consensus rather than built up as a dispute, per the brief's watch-point.

No hadith attaches to this verse in any source fetched; §7 says so in both
languages rather than letting the Ibn 'Abbas/Ka'b al-Ahbar tafsir reports
read as hadith by omission. No asbab in any fetched source.

Cross-referenced 37:53 and 37:56 by number only, without rebuilding either
verse's own draft, per the brief's instruction.

Licensing sentence carried in both languages in the closing section: the
verse describes one disbeliever's end inside one story, names no community
and no person living today.

Dropped: at-Tabari's shadhdh-recitation digression on "muttali'uni" (a
grammar question, not verse content); the Yahudah/Matrus and two-partners
narrations, already the proper territory of 37:51-53.
