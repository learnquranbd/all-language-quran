# LEDGER 37:55

## SOURCES — tafsir fetched (all via PREP.md's pre-fetched files, none re-fetched)

- t14 Ibn Kathir (ar): `قال ابن عباس، وسعيد بن جبير وخليد العصري وقتادة، والسدي، وعطاء الخراساني [وغيرهم] يعني في وسط الجحيم. وقال الحسن البصري: في وسط الجحيم كأنه شهاب يتقد. وقال قتادة: ذكر لنا أنه اطلع فرأى جماجم القوم تغلي. وذكر لنا أن كعب الأحبار قال: في الجنة كوى إذا أراد أحد من أهلها أن ينظر إلى عدوه في النار اطلع فيها، فازداد شكرا.` — used in full: §3 (the six-name consensus on "wasat al-jahim"), §6 (al-Hasan al-Basri's "ka'annahu shihabun yattaqid", Qatada's "jamajim al-qawm taghli"), §7 (Ka'b al-Ahbar's windows-of-Paradise report, named explicitly as his report, not a hadith).
- t15 at-Tabari: `وفي الكلام متروك استغني بدلالة الكلام عليه من ذكره، وهو فقالوا: نعم` — used §1 (the elided clause). `سأل ربه أن يطلعه` (Qatada, on 37:54's question) — used §2. `لولا أن الله عرفه إياه ما عرفه، لقد تغير حبره وسبره بعده` (Khulayd al-'Usari via Qatada) and `والله لولا أنه عرفه ما عرفه، لقد غيرت النار حبره وسبره` (Mutarrif ibn 'Abdillah via Qatada) — used §5. The shadhdh-recitation discussion (Ibn 'Abbas's reported alternate reading of "muttali'uni") — read, not used: a grammar/recitation-variant digression unrelated to this verse's content watch-points.
- t16 al-Muyassar: `هل أنتم مطلعون لنرى مصير ذلك القرين؟ فاطلع فرأى قرينه في وسط النار` — read for the paraphrase's confirmation that the figure seen is "qarinahu" (his companion), supporting the §2/§8 cross-reference to 37:53; not separately quoted (redundant with Tabari/Ibn Kathir already cited for "wasat").
- t90 al-Qurtubi: `أي في وسط النار والحسك حواليه، قاله ابن مسعود` — used §4 (Ibn Mas'ud's thorns). `تعبت حتى انقطع سوائي أي وسطي` and the Abu 'Ubayda/'Isa ibn 'Umar anecdote `كنت أكتب يا أبا عبيدة حتى ينقطع سوائي` — used §3 (etymology of sawa'). `وعن قتادة قال: قال بعض العلماء: لولا أن الله جل وعز عرفه إياه لما عرفه، لقد تغير حبره وسبره` — used §5 (the "some of the scholars" chain, kept distinct from the Khulayd/Mutarrif chain in Tabari).
- t91 as-Sa'di: `أي: في وسط العذاب وغمراته، والعذاب قد أحاط به` — used §3 (as-Sa'di's gloss of sawa').
- t94 al-Baghawi: `إن في الجنة كوى ينظر أهلها منها إلى النار فاطلع هذا المؤمن` — used §7 (Ibn 'Abbas's report of openings in Paradise, as reported by al-Baghawi). `وإنما سمي وسط الشيء سواء لاستواء الجوانب منه` — used §3 (etymology).
- t168 Ma'arif al-Qur'an (en): read for background on the qarin narrative (the Yahudah/Matrus guess, the two-partners story) — not used in this verse's article; that material belongs to 37:51-53 and was flagged in the brief as already-covered ground, so it was not rebuilt here.
- t169 Ibn Kathir (en, abridged): confirms the same six-name consensus on "in the middle of Hell" and al-Hasan al-Basri's "burning star" in English, and the Furat bin Tha'labah two-partners narration ending with "he looked down and saw him in the midst of Hell" — read as cross-check against t14's Arabic; not separately quoted since t14 and t15 already supply the Arabic.

## Disagreements kept as disagreements

- None found. On "fi sawa'i al-jahim" there is a genuine six-way naming of the same reading (Ibn 'Abbas, Sa'id ibn Jubayr, Khulayd al-'Usari, Qatada, as-Suddi, 'Ata' al-Khurasani, per Ibn Kathir) plus as-Sa'di's independent gloss, all converging on "the middle" — stated in §3 as consensus, per the brief's watch-point, rather than dressed up as a dispute. Al-Hasan al-Basri's "burning star" (§6) and Ibn Mas'ud's "thorns" (§4) are additional images layered onto the same location, not competing locations, and the article says so explicitly in both sections.
- The "changed beyond recognition" report appears through two chains in at-Tabari (Khulayd al-'Usari; Mutarrif ibn 'Abdillah) and once more in al-Qurtubi (anonymised as "some of the scholars," still via Qatada) — reported in §5 as three routes to one claim, not as three different claims.

## Dropped

- At-Tabari's long discussion of the shadhdh (anomalous) recitation attributed to Ibn 'Abbas ("hal antum muttali'uni") — a recitation/grammar question outside this verse's watch-points and not needed to support any claim made.
- Ma'arif al-Qur'an's and Ibn Kathir's English-language retellings of the Yahudah/Matrus guess and the two-partners narration — already the proper territory of 37:51-53; not rebuilt here per the brief's instruction to cross-reference 37:53 by number only.
- Any specific identification of who the "enemy" is in Ka'b al-Ahbar's report, beyond what the text itself gives (an unnamed enemy in the Fire) — nothing more specific was stated in the fetched text, so nothing more specific is claimed.

## Standing-rule check

Section 8 states plainly, in both languages, that the verse describes one disbeliever's end inside one story, names no community and no person living today, and licenses no one to claim to know any living person's or group's fate.
