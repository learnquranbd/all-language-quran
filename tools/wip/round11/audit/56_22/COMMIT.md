Card and 8-section bilingual article (1,737 EN words) on 56:22: wa hurun
'in. 56:15-21 as the list it closes; 56:10 pointed to once for the
forerunners; 56:23 (pearls) and 56:24 (reward) framed only.

Sources: at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi, al-Baghawi, Ibn Kathir (ar), as-Sa'di,
al-Muyassar (group 56:20-24). Ma'arif (group 56:21-26) and Ibn Kathir en
(group 56:13-22) noted as passing over the verse itself.
- Glosses per source: hawra' = pure white, intense black of the eye; 'ayna'
  = wide-eyed in beauty (at-Tabari); kohl, charm, beauty, splendour; large
  beautiful eyes (as-Sa'di); white, large-eyed (al-Baghawi); wide eyes
  (Muyassar).
- Nominative wa hurun 'in: readers of Medina, Mecca, Kufa, some Basrans
  (at-Tabari); the majority, choice of Abu 'Ubayd and Abu Hatim
  (al-Qurtubi); "and with them / for them are" (at-Tabari, al-Akhfash, Ibn
  Kathir, as-Sa'di); joined to thullah 56:13 (al-Qurtubi); "hur 'in go
  round among them" (al-Baghawi).
- Genitive wa hurin 'in: most Kufans, some Medinans (at-Tabari); Hamza,
  al-Kisa'i and others (al-Qurtubi); Abu Ja'far, Hamza, al-Kisa'i
  (al-Baghawi). Attachment kept per name: form only (al-Farra',
  at-Tabari, al-Baghawi, two poetry lines); to bi-akwab by sense
  (al-Zajjaj); to jannat 56:12 (al-Qurtubi); plain join (Qutrub); Ibn
  Kathir's two possibilities with 5:6 and 76:21.
- Accusative (al-Qurtubi only, with readers and Ubayy's codex).
- Al-Kisa'i's objection kept; at-Tabari: both readings correct, unranked.
Hadith: none attaches to 56:22. Asbab: none.
Dropped: origin of the hur (no source raises it), parallels 44:54, 52:20,
55:72, 37:48 (not cited for this verse). Noted: at-Tabari's printed text
vowels the nominative as genitive; article follows his word bi-r-raf'.
