Card and deep bilingual article for the command to Banī Isrāʾīl to
dwell in the land after Pharaoh's drowning, and the promise of the Last.

Sources (all pre-fetched, none re-fetched):
- "uskunū al-arḍ" read three ways, kept as a difference of focus:
  Shām (at-Tabari, al-Muyassar); Shām and Egypt (al-Qurtubi, al-Baghawi);
  the inherited land / Pharaoh's land, easts and wests (Ibn Kathir, citing
  26:59). Ibn Kathir's reading of the line as glad tidings of the conquest
  of Makkah (sura Makkan, pre-Hijrah; parallel to 17:76-77) noted.
- "waʿd al-ākhira" kept as a live disagreement: the Resurrection / al-Qiyāma
  (jumhūr — Tabari, Muyassar, Baghawi, Qurtubi, Sa'di, Ibn Kathir) vs the
  descent of ʿĪsā (AS) from the sky (al-Kalbi, reported by Qurtubi and
  Baghawi). Neither forced.
- "lafīf" given its linguistic sense: intermingled, wound together, none
  knowing another, no one drawing off to his tribe (Tabari, Qurtubi,
  Baghawi), with al-Jawharī and al-Aṣmaʿī; plus the plainer "jamīʿan"
  gloss (Ibn ʿAbbās, Mujāhid, Qatāda, Daḥḥāk). Sa'di: gathered so each
  worker is repaid.

Hadith: no sound report is attached to 17:104 by any fetched tafsir; said
plainly. The sound Ṣafwān b. al-ʿAssāl nine-signs hadith belongs to 17:101,
named as such and not to this verse. Bukhari 660 (the seven Allah shades)
used only as a general, marked narration on the Day of gathering.

Declined: the 17:4-8 "two corruptions" link to waʿd al-ākhira — no fetched
tafsir draws it here, so flagged as unstated rather than supplied.

Sensitivity: Banī Isrāʾīl history kept strictly descriptive and scriptural;
§7 states in both languages that the verse licenses nothing against any
living person or community and passes no political verdict on anyone today.

All self-gates clean: 1778 EN words, 8 sections.
