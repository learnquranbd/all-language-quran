# SOURCES ledger — 17:104

## Tafsir fetched (all pre-fetched in PREP; none re-fetched)
- **at-Tabari (15)** — NAMED. "uskunū al-arḍ" = أرض الشام (land of Shām): `اسكنوا الأرض ) أرض الشام`.
  waʿd al-ākhira = the Hour: `فإذا جاءت الساعة، وهي وعد الآخرة`. lafīf: `مختلطين قد التفّ بعضكم على بعض، لا تتعارفون ... لا ينحاز أحد منكم إلى قبيلته وحيِّه`; etymology `من قولك: لففت الجيوش`. Also the "jamīʿan" gloss (Ibn ʿAbbās, Mujāhid, Qatāda, Daḥḥāk) and "the two come to one meaning".
- **al-Qurtubi (90)** — NAMED. land = أرض الشأم ومصر (Shām and Egypt): `اسكنوا الأرض أي أرض الشأم ومصر`.
  waʿd al-ākhira = al-Qiyāma; AND al-Kalbi: descent of ʿĪsā: `وقال الكلبي ... يعني مجيء عيسى - عليه السلام - من السماء`. lafīf: intermingled from every place, believer with disbeliever, none knowing another; al-Jawharī (`اللفيف ما اجتمع من الناس من قبائل شتى`), al-Aṣmaʿī (`اللفيف جمع وليس له واحد ... مثل الجميع`), like scattered locusts `كالجراد المنتشر`.
- **al-Baghawi (94)** — NAMED. land = أرض مصر والشام (Egypt and Shām): `يعني أرض مصر والشام`.
  waʿd al-ākhira = yawm al-qiyāma; AND al-Kalbi: descent of ʿĪsā: `وقال الكلبي ... يعني مجيء عيسى من السماء`. lafīf = the great mixed gathering, `فيهم المؤمن والكافر والبر والفاجر`.
- **al-Muyassar (16)** — NAMED. land = أرض «الشام» (Shām); waʿd al-ākhira = yawm al-qiyāma, raised from graves to the standing of reckoning: `فإذا جاء يوم القيامة جئنا بكم جميعًا مِن قبوركم إلى موقف الحساب`.
- **as-Sa'di (91)** — NAMED. lafīf = jamīʿan, and the point: `ليجازى كل عامل بعمله` (each worker repaid for his work).
- **Ibn Kathir ar (14)** — NAMED. Reads al-arḍ as the inheritance of the oppressed (mustaḍʿafūn) — easts and wests, Pharaoh's land, crops, treasures; cites 26:59 `وأورثناها بني إسرائيل`. Glad tidings to the Prophet ﷺ of the conquest of Makkah though the sura is Makkan pre-Hijrah; parallel to 17:76-77. lafīf = jamīʿan (Ibn ʿAbbās, Mujāhid, Qatāda, Daḥḥāk): `جميعكم أنتم وعدوكم`.
- **Ibn Kathir en abridged (169)** — NAMED (grouped 17:101-104). Same inheritance/Makkah-conquest reading in English; "all of you, you and your enemies".
- **Ma'arif en (168)** — NAMED (grouped 17:101-108). Used only for the Ṣafwān b. al-ʿAssāl hadith, which it attaches to 17:101 (nine signs), NOT to 17:104.

## Disagreements kept (as disagreements, no position taken)
1. Which land: Shām (Tabari, Muyassar) vs Shām+Egypt (Qurtubi, Baghawi) vs the inherited land / Pharaoh's land (Ibn Kathir). Framed as difference of focus.
2. waʿd al-ākhira: Resurrection/al-Qiyāma (jumhūr: Tabari, Muyassar, Baghawi, Qurtubi, Sa'di, Ibn Kathir) vs descent of ʿĪsā (AS) from the sky (al-Kalbi, reported by Qurtubi and Baghawi). Both left standing.

## Hadith
- **NOT attached to 17:104.** No fetched tafsir attaches a sound hadith to this verse. Stated plainly in §6.
- **Ṣafwān b. al-ʿAssāl** (nine signs) — via Ma'arif, reported Abu Dawud, an-Nasa'i, at-Tirmidhi, Ibn Majah; Ma'arif calls it Ṣaḥīḥ. Belongs to **17:101**, not 17:104. Named in §6.1 only as the neighbouring-verse report, explicitly not on this verse. No number cited (not shown on a fetched hadith page).
- **Bukhari 660** — USED as a general/thematic narration on the Day of gathering, explicitly marked "not a comment on 17:104". URL: https://quranx.com/hadith/Bukhari/DarusSalam/Hadith-660 (fetched via hadith.js). Refs: Dar-us-Salam 660; USC-MSA Vol 1, Book 11, Hadith 629. First ten words (EN): "The Prophet said, Allah will give shade to seven on". Grading: Ṣaḥīḥ (Ṣaḥīḥ al-Bukhārī). Wording taken from Bukhari alone, not hybridised.

## Asbab an-nuzul
- Not used. No occasion-of-revelation report was sought or confirmed; the section work rests on the verse's placement after 17:103 (drowning of Pharaoh), which the tafsir make explicit.

## Dropped / not asserted
- The tie between waʿd al-ākhira here and the "two corruptions" of 17:4-8: NO fetched tafsir on 17:104 makes this link. Explicitly declined and flagged in §4.3 rather than forced (per orchestrator watch-point 2).
- Ibn Abi Razīn's gloss (via Tabari) "min kull qawm" — folded into the general lafīf sense, not separately attributed to avoid over-citing a chain-only report.
- No claim was made about any modern people, state or territory; the standing disclaimer is in §7 (both languages).
