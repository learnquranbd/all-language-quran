Card and deep bilingual article for 19:37 (the factions differed
over ʿĪsā; woe from the scene of a tremendous Day). Follows ʿĪsā's
own tawḥīd in 19:36.

Sources (all fetched for this verse):
- aḥzāb: at-Tabari carries Mujahid (= People of the Book); al-Muyassar,
  as-Sa'di and Ibn Kathir read the sects of Jews and Christians; al-Baghawi
  narrows to the Christians as three parties. Kept as a real disagreement:
  al-Qurtubi's Ibn ʿAbbas reading (the confederate mushriks against the
  Prophet) is reported without deciding between the two.
- Shape of the error: al-Qurtubi's excess/deficiency pair; the three
  Christian positions (Allah / son / third of three) mapped to Yaʿqūbiyya,
  Nasṭūriyya, Malkāniyya; the Jewish slander (sorcerer, son of Yūsuf,
  child of adultery) reported as the historical factions' stated views.
- Qatādah's four-scholars narration (at-Tabari, Ibn Kathir) and Ibn
  Kathir's Constantine-council note carried explicitly as reports, not
  rulings; the true statement (servant, messenger, word to Maryam, spirit)
  from Ibn Kathir via ʿAmr b. Maymūn, Ibn Jurayj, Qatādah.
- mashhad: at-Tabari (Wayl / Day of Resurrection), al-Qurtubi's three
  senses incl. the assembly they gathered in, as-Sa'di on the Day's scale.

Hadith: Sahih al-Bukhari 3435 (ʿUbādah b. aṣ-Ṣāmit), sound; quoted whole
from Bukhari. Fixes the true creed about ʿĪsā and ties it to Paradise/Hell.

Licensing: §8 states in both languages that the verse describes a doctrinal
error of the ancient sects and licenses nothing against any living person
or community; no verdict on anyone today.

Dropped: the two further Ṣaḥīḥayn respite hadiths (kept to one confirmed
hadith); the tafsirs' curse formulae against the Jews (not reproduced).
Asbab: none established; verse placement used instead.
