# SOURCES — 19:37

Verse: فَٱخْتَلَفَ ٱلْأَحْزَابُ مِنۢ بَيْنِهِمْ ۖ فَوَيْلٌۭ لِّلَّذِينَ كَفَرُوا۟ مِن مَّشْهَدِ يَوْمٍ عَظِيمٍ
"Then the factions differed among themselves; so woe to those who disbelieved from the scene of a tremendous Day."

## Tafsir fetched (8) and how used

- **at-Tabari (15)** — USED. "فاختلف المختلفون في عيسى، فصاروا أحزابا متفرّقين من بين قومه" and Mujahid's gloss "قال: أهل الكتاب" → §2.1 (aḥzāb = People of the Book). Qatādah four-scholars narrative "لما رُفع ابن مريم، انتخبت بنو إسرائيل أربعة من فقهائهم…" → §4.1–4.2. "فاقتتل القوم… فأنـزل الله… وَيَقْتُلُونَ الَّذِينَ يَأْمُرُونَ بِالْقِسْطِ" → §4.2 (link to 3:21). Mashhad: "فوادي جهنم الذي يدعى ويلا… من شهودهم يومًا عظيما شأنه، وذلك يوم القيامة"; Qatādah "شهدوا هولا إذا عظيما" → §7.1.
- **al-Qurtubi (90)** — USED. "اختلفت الفرق من أهل الكتاب في أمر عيسى… فأفرطت النصارى وغلت، وفرطت اليهود وقصرت" → §3.1 (two extremes) and party names Nasṭūriyya/Malkāniyya/Yaʿqūbiyya → §2.1/§3.2. Ibn ʿAbbas reading "المراد من الأحزاب الذين تحزبوا على النبي… من المشركين" → §2.2 (kept as a genuine difference). Three senses of mashhad (maṣdar/shuhūd; the place creation witnesses like maḥshar; the great assembly they gathered in for counsel and agreed on kufr "إن الله ثالث ثلاثة") → §7.2.
- **as-Sa'di (91)** — USED. "الأحزاب، أي: فرق الضلال، من اليهود والنصارى… فمن غال فيه وجاف" → §2.1/§3.1. positions (Allah / son / third of three / walad baghī like the Jews) → §3.2–3.3. mashhad yawm al-qiyāma witnessed by "الأولون والآخرون، أهل السماوات وأهل الأرض، الخالق والمخلوق… الزلازل والأهوال… يتبين ما كانوا يخفون ويبدون" → §7.3.
- **Ibn Kathir ar (14)** — USED. "اختلفت أقوال أهل الكتاب في عيسى بعد بيان أمره ووضوح حاله" → §1.1. positions incl. "جمهور اليهود… على أنه ولد زنية، وقالوا كلامه هذا سحر" → §3.3; "هو عبد الله ورسوله… وهذا هو قول الحق الذي أرشد الله إليه المؤمنين… عن عمرو بن ميمون وابن جريج وقتادة" → §5.1. Qatādah four-scholars → §4.1–4.2. Constantine council "أن قسطنطين جمعهم… ألفين ومائة وسبعين أسقفا… لم يجتمع على مقالة واحدة أكثر من ثلاثمائة وثمانية" → §4.3. ʿUbādah hadith cited here → §6.
- **Ibn Kathir en abridged (169)** — USED (grouped 34–37); parallel English of the above, incl. "opinions of the People of the Book differed… after the explanation of his affair"; the list of positions; "This latter view is the statement of truth." Confirms §1, §3, §5.
- **al-Muyassar (16)** — USED. "فاختلفت الفِرَق من أهل الكتاب… فمنهم غال فيه وهم النصارى… ومنهم جافٍ عنه وهم اليهود، قالوا ساحر، وقالوا ابن يوسف النجار… يوم عظيم الهول، وهو يوم القيامة" → §2.1, §3.2–3.3, §7.1. "تعالى الله عما يقولون" → §3.2.
- **al-Baghawi (94)** — USED. "النصارى سموا أحزابا لأنهم تحزبوا ثلاث فرق… النسطورية والملكانية واليعقوبية… يوم القيامة" → §2.1 (Christians as three parties), §7.1.
- **Ma'arif al-Qur'an en (168)** — USED (grouped 34–38). "Christians exalted him… son of Allah; the Jews… called him the illegitimate son of Yusuf the carpenter" → §3.1. Reading of qawl al-ḥaqq (Qurtubi) that ʿĪsā himself is "the word of truth," like kalimat Allah, born by Allah's word without an apparent cause → §5.2.

No file marked WRONG GROUP. Ibn Ashur / ar-Razi / Ibn al-Qayyim not consulted (not in API); not cited.

## Hadith

- **Sahih al-Bukhari 3435**, ʿUbādah b. aṣ-Ṣāmit (RA). URL fetched: https://quranx.com/hadith/Bukhari/DarusSalam/Hadith-3435 (also USC-MSA Vol 4, Book 55, Hadith 644).
  First ten words (EN used): "whoever testifies that there is no god but Allah alone…"
  Arabic opening: "مَنْ شَهِدَ أَنْ لاَ إِلَهَ إِلاَّ اللَّهُ وَحْدَهُ لاَ شَرِيكَ لَهُ…"
  Grading: sound — it is in Ṣaḥīḥ al-Bukhārī (Ibn Kathir notes it as agreed-upon, muttafaq ʿalayh). Reported grading not upgraded. Directly on the verse: it fixes the true statement about ʿĪsā's nature that the factions strayed from, and ties it to the reality of Paradise/Hell (the "tremendous Day"). Quoted from one collection (Bukhari), whole; not hybridised. Used in §6.

## Asbab an-nuzul
Not used. No established sabab for 19:37; the article gives the verse's placement instead (it follows ʿĪsā's own tawḥīd in 19:36). Qatādah's four-scholars report and Ibn Kathir's Constantine note are carried as *reports on the historical differing*, explicitly marked in §4.3 as Qatādah's own telling ("it was mentioned to us"), not a marfūʿ hadith and not a ruling.

## Dropped / not used (could not or should not confirm)
- Ibn Kathir's second Ṣaḥīḥayn hadiths — "Allah gives respite to the wrongdoer until He seizes him" and "No one is more patient with harm heard than Allah" — NOT quoted. They are relevant to the respite-until-Judgment theme but I fetched and quoted only the ʿUbādah hadith (one collection's wording, whole). Mentioned here as available but deliberately not added, to keep a single confirmed hadith.
- The curse formulae in the Arabic tafsirs ("عليهم لعائن الله" on the Jews) — deliberately NOT reproduced; watch-point 3 / §8 licensing. Positions attributed only as the historical factions' stated views.
- Specific council identification/date beyond "a great council under Constantine, ~thousands of bishops, ~300-odd agreeing" — kept at the level Ibn Kathir gives; no extra historical detail added from memory.
