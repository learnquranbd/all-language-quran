## Brief watch-point discrepancies (corrected against the text)
- Abu Talib: al-Qurtubi's wording is plural («قولوا ... أبوا وأنفوا») and addressed at the deathbed with Quraysh gathered; the refusal is the gathering's. The brief's "he (Abu Talib) refused" is NOT in the text and was not written. No verdict on anyone's fate; no chain given by al-Qurtubi; not presented as hadith.
- Hudaybiya: al-Qurtubi gives no chain, only «ذكر هذا الخبر البيهقي»; written as "al-Bayhaqi mentioned this report", not "via al-Bayhaqi's chain".
- Abu al-'Ala' scene: it is a Successor's own statement (maqtu'), not attributed to the Prophet; written so. Not run through hadith.js against Bukhari/Muslim vision-of-Allah narrations: any match would be a different text, and blending would be a hybrid.

## Dropped / softened by choice
- Abu al-'Ala' scene: the names of the first two groups (Jews with 'Uzayr, Christians with the Messiah) deliberately not named; written as "groups ... asked what they used to worship, and directed to the left". Licensing guard and "not a description of any present-day community" in EN and BN.
- al-Qurtubi's attribution «والذي قبله القشيري» (ambiguous referent) not used.
- 37:36-41 content (mad poet, "came with the truth", punishment, chosen servants) kept out; only 37:22 cited for the gathering with what they worshipped.
