Card and 8-section bilingual article for 52:29: fa-dhakkir fa-ma anta
bi-ni'mati rabbika bi-kahinin wa la majnun. 52:30-34 a pointer only.

Sources: Tabari, Ibn Kathir (ar; en abridged), Muyassar, Qurtubi,
Sa'di, Baghawi. Ma'arif's grouped text covers 52:21 only; not used.
- Addressees: Tabari (his people and others), Qurtubi, Baghawi (people
  of Makkah), Muyassar, Sa'di (Muslims and disbelievers; proof and
  guidance); remind with the Qur'an / what was sent down / ni'am Allah.
- Bi-ni'mati rabbik side by side: Tabari, Muyassar (prophethood,
  rajahat al-'aql), Qurtubi (risala), Baghawi (rahma, 'isma), Sa'di
  (minhu wa lutfihi), IK (bi-hamdi llah).
- Kahin: IK and Sa'di (ra'iy of the jinn; Sa'di's hundred lies),
  Qurtubi/Baghawi (min ghayri wahy), Muyassar. Majnun: IK (mass),
  Tabari (ra'iy for the majnun too), Muyassar, Sa'di (akmal an-nas
  'aqlan); Tabari's "Allah does not forsake you".
- Command before denial tied by Sa'di's wa-li-hadha.
Disagreement kept: oath or not in bi-ni'mati rabbika (Qurtubi's two
qila views, no preference).
Speakers: IK (Quraysh), Qurtubi ('Uqba ibn Abi Mu'ayt, Shayba ibn
Rabi'a), Baghawi's nazalat line reported as his, unchained, not asbab.
Hadith: none attached by any tafsir; said in one sentence.
Licensing sentence for the accusers in both languages.
Dropped: 69:41-42, 81:22-25, 51:52, 68:2 (no fetched tafsir cites
them); IK's Dar an-Nadwa report (52:30); Ma'arif's 52:21 hadiths.
