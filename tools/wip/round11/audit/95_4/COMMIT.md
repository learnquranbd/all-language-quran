Replaces the 662-word, 6-section article with a 1,746-word, 8-section deep article.

Sources: at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi, as-Sa'di, al-Baghawi, Ibn Kathir (ar + en abridged,
grouped 95:1-8), al-Muyassar (grouped 95:2-6) and Ma'arif al-Qur'an, all on 95:4.
Covers 95:4 as the answer of the oath (at-Tabari, Qatadah; al-muqsam 'alayh per Ibn
Kathir, al-Baghawi, as-Sa'di); the oaths in one section (fig/olive as fruits: Mujahid,
'Ikrimah, Muyassar; as the mosques of Nuh and Jerusalem: Ibn 'Abbas via al-'Awfi, Ka'b,
Qatadah, Ibn Zayd; three places of three messengers from "some of the imams"); at-Tabari's
three groups on ahsani taqwim (best form / youth and strength / upright posture) and his
grammatical preference; the later glosses; al-Qurtubi's head-to-feet inventory and the
small-world line, extended by Ma'arif; 95:6 and the 103:1-3 parallel (Ibn Kathir); 95:7-8 with the licensing
sentence in both languages.

Disagreements kept, no position: asfala safilin as the Fire (Mujahid, Abu al-'Aliyah,
al-Hasan, Ibn Zayd, Muyassar, Ibn Kathir's choice) or decrepit old age (Ibn 'Abbas,
'Ikrimah, preferred by Ibn Jarir per Ibn Kathir); who al-insan is (al-Qurtubi's two
views, the disbeliever with two "qila" names, or Adam and his descendants).

Hadith: Bukhari 769 (al-Bara' heard the Prophet recite at-Tin in 'Isha), quoted whole,
graded only as in al-Bukhari's Sahih, marked as about the surah, not the verse. No
fetched tafsir attaches a reply hadith to 95:8; the article says so in one sentence.

Dropped: the Adam-in-His-image narration (unconfirmed, contested reading); the old-age
reading's account of the exception (not in any fetched text); Damascus for the fig; al-Qurtubi's divorce-oath anecdote (cut on review);
four homiletic claims from the old article. Defect fixed: the old article had Ibn Kathir
say justice cannot leave 95:4 and 95:5 unresolved; he says only that Allah wrongs no one
and gives the wronged their due at the Judgement.
