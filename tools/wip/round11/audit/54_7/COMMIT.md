Card and 8-section bilingual article for 54:7: khushsha'an absaruhum
yakhrujuna mina l-ajdathi ka-annahum jaradun muntashir.
54:6 and 54:8 pointers only; no retelling of 54:1.

Sources: at-Tabari, Ibn Kathir (ar; en abridged), al-Qurtubi, al-Baghawi,
as-Sa'di, al-Muyassar, Ma'arif (54:3 only, said so).
- Hal of khushsha'an and the pause at 'anhum (al-Qurtubi).
- khushu' of eyes: abased, no injury (Tabari, Qatada), lowered gaze
  (Qurtubi), at sight of punishment (Baghawi), terror in hearts (Sa'di).
- Why the eyes: trace of might and abasement shows there (Tabari,
  Qurtubi); Qurtubi's 79:9 and 42:45, keys verified.
- Readings khushsha'an / khashi'an with readers named by Tabari,
  Baghawi, Qurtubi; the agreement rule and al-Iyadi's line.
- ajdath = graves, sing. jadath; locusts as speed to the reckoning
  (Tabari, Ibn Kathir, Muyassar) and as sheer number (Sa'di).
- Disagreement kept, no position: Qurtubi reads moths (101:4) and
  locusts as two stages, locusts having a direction; Baghawi reads
  the locusts as bewildered and directionless, 101:4 a parallel.
- Ibn Kathir's 74:9 and 74:10 on the hard day.
Hadith: none attached by any fetched tafsir; stated in one sentence.
Asbab: none; placement used. Licensing sentence for the deniers.
Dropped: 70:43, 36:51, 7:187 (not cited by a fetched tafsir), Tabari
editor's footnotes, Qurtubi's nominal-clause reading.
