Card and 8-section bilingual article for 38:37: the devils subjected to Sulayman (AS),
every builder and diver, the second object of 38:36's "We subjected".

Sources: Tabari, Qurtubi, Ibn Kathir (ar/en, group 38:34-40), Baghawi, Sa'di, Muyassar, Ma'arif (group 38:35-37); quranx 38.37 for Tanwir and Maududi.
- Grammar: restored verb wa-sakhkharna (Tabari, Qurtubi, Baghawi); kulla banna' as badal (Qurtubi);
  "subjected to no one before him" (Qurtubi) and "no one after him" (Sa'di).
- Builders: Ibn Kathir, tasks beyond human strength; Tabari/Qatada and Baghawi keep builders to
  maharib and tamathil, Tabari gives basins and cauldrons to others; 34:13 list cross-referenced.
- Divers: ornaments (Tabari, Qatada), pearls and jewels (Ibn Kathir, Sa'di); Qurtubi and Baghawi,
  Sulayman first for whom pearls were brought up; Qurtubi's an-Nabigha lines on Tadmur.
- Who they were: those naming their kind say jinn (Ma'arif, Maududi, Tanwir); no fetched source
  reads them as humans, so no such disagreement is invented.
- 38:38 fetters: marada (Tabari, Muyassar), rebellion or bad work (Ibn Kathir), disobedience
  (Sa'di), not necessarily iron (Maududi), kept side by side.
- Why the gift: answered prayer (Sa'di) vs exchange for the trial by one of them on the throne
  (Tabari), with Maududi's rejection of that story as Talmudic; al-Hasan via Ibn Kathir on the wind.
- min ba'di: "other than me" (some, Thanavi) vs "even after me" (most), per Ma'arif.
Hadith: Sahih Muslim 542 (Abu ad-Darda, Iblis with a flame of fire, "the supplication of my
brother Sulaiman"), confirmed on quranx; the Prophet's reply quoted whole; graded by inclusion
in Muslim's Sahih; attached by Ibn Kathir to 38:35. Bukhari ifrit report left to 34:12.
Asbab: none in the fetched sources; not used.
Framing: licensing sentence in both languages (38:38's bound wrongdoers).
Dropped: "Two Sahihs" servant-messenger report (unfetched); intensive-form morphology;
Ma'arif on seeking office; Maududi on 38:39; al-Hasan's 38:33 detail.
Themes: gratitude.
