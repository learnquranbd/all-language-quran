## Watch-point 1 (devils vs disobedient humans/jinn): NOT FOUND
No fetched source (8 API tafsirs + quranx page: Tanwir, Jalalayn, Ibn Kathir en, Maududi) reads ash-shayatin here as humans. Article states that no fetched commentary does, and that those naming their kind call them jinn. The real differences kept instead: (a) answered prayer (Sa'di) vs exchange for the trial (Tabari), with Maududi's rejection of the devil-on-the-throne story; (b) division of labour, Ibn Kathir (basins/cauldrons under builders) vs Tabari (under "others who hewed"); (c) why some were fettered in 38:38: Tabari/Muyassar marada, Ibn Kathir two causes, Sa'di disobedience, Maududi mischief/not necessarily iron; (d) min ba'di, two readings per Ma'arif.
