Card and 8-section bilingual article for 68:11: hammazin mashsha'in
bi-namim, read as the continuation of 68:10 inside the 68:8-15 portrait.
68:12-13 placed only; 68:4 not repeated.

Sources: Tabari, Ibn Kathir (ar; en grouped 68:8-16), Muyassar (grouped
68:10-15), Qurtubi, Sa'di, Baghawi, Ma'arif (grouped 68:10-15).
- Hammaz: backbiter who eats people's flesh (Tabari, Ibn 'Abbas, Qatadah,
  Baghawi, Muyassar) vs hand not tongue (Ibn Zayd with 104:1); Tabari's
  hamz/ghamz statement; Hasan's jab in the gathering (Qurtubi, Baghawi);
  face vs absence (Abu al-'Aliya, 'Ata', Hasan) vs Muqatil's reverse vs
  "the same"; Sa'di's habit of faulting and mockery. All kept, no choice.
- Mashsha' bi-namim: carrying talk between people (Tabari, Qatadah),
  "walks with lies" (Ibn 'Abbas); intent to corrupt (Sa'di, Muyassar,
  Qurtubi, Baghawi); Ibn Kathir's fasad dhat al-bayn and al-haliqa;
  Qurtubi's verb forms and al-Farra'.
- Names as the texts give them: al-Akhnas ibn Shariq (Tabari from
  Kalbi); al-Walid ibn al-Mughira (Muyassar; Ma'arif citing Ibn Jarir
  from Ibn 'Abbas). None chosen. Licensing sentence in both languages,
  plus a paragraph against turning the verse into a labelling list.
Hadith: Bukhari 6052 (two graves, attached by Ibn Kathir) quoted whole;
Bukhari 6055 for "and yet it is great", labelled separate; Muslim 105a
(attached by Qurtubi) quoted whole; Bukhari 6056 for qattat.
Asbab: none used as such.
Dropped: Ahmad's Asma' bint Yazid report (unconfirmable), 49:11-12 and
24:19 (no tafsir cites them), any own-voice morphology.
