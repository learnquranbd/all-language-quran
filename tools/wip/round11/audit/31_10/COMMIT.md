Signs of creation open the tawhid case the sura caps at 31:11: a
pillarless sky, mountains that steady the ground, creatures scattered
everywhere, and water that raises every noble kind of plant.

Sources (all pre-fetched, none re-fetched):
- at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi, Ibn Kathir (ar + en abridged), as-Sa'di,
  al-Muyassar, Ma'arif al-Qur'an. al-Baghawi carried no usable
  commentary (file was verse text only).

Disagreement kept open, both sides named, no position taken:
"bi-ghayri 'amadin tarawnaha" — (a) no pillars at all, you can see
there are none (al-Hasan, Qatada; echoed by as-Sa'di, al-Muyassar)
vs (b) pillars you cannot see (Ibn 'Abbas, 'Ikrimah, Mujahid). The
grammar for both is from al-Qurtubi and Ma'arif.

rawasi kept to the Qur'anic anchoring image (mountains lest the earth
sway), with at-Tabari's "an = la" note and al-Qurtubi's Kufan reading;
no modern-geology claim. zawj karim covered as kind/pair (Ibn Kathir
en "in pairs") and karim as good/noble (at-Tabari hasan an-nibta,
Ibn 'Abbas "every beautiful color"). ash-Sha'bi's "people as produce"
reading given as a tafsir reading, not a verdict on anyone living.
13:2 noted as the parallel pillarless-sky proof.

Hadith: none. Suggested Bukhari 660 (seven under the shade) is
unrelated to the verse and attached by no mufassir; dropped. Article
states in one sentence that no consulted mufassir attaches a hadith.

Card: themes creation + tawheed.
