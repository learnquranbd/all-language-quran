# SOURCES — 23:20

## Tafsir fetched (all from PREP.md, not re-fetched)
- Ibn Kathir ar (14), Ibn Kathir en abridged (169), at-Tabari (15), al-Qurtubi (90),
  as-Sa'di (91), al-Baghawi (94), al-Muyassar (16), Ma'arif al-Qur'an en (168).

## Named in the article, with the Arabic/English clause each rests on
- **The tree is the olive** — all sources. Ibn Kathir (14): «يعني: الزيتونة». as-Sa'di:
  «وهي شجرة الزيتون». Muyassar: «شجرة الزيتون». at-Tabari/al-Baghawi/Qurtubi likewise.
- **Placement, chain of favours** — Ibn Kathir en (169) reads 23:18–23:22 as one recital
  of blessings (rain in measure → gardens → olive oil+relish → cattle milk/meat/carriage).
- **Why the olive is singled out** — as-Sa'di: «خصت بالذكر، لأن مكانها خاص في أرض الشام،
  ولمنافعها». al-Qurtubi: «أفردها بالذكر لعظيم منافعها في أرض الشام والحجاز... وقلة تعاهدها
  بالسقي والحفر».
- **Muqatil: first olive grew from the Ṭūr; olive said to be first tree after the Flood** —
  al-Qurtubi §6: «وقال مقاتل: خص الطور بالزيتون لأن أول الزيتون نبت منها. وقيل: إن الزيتون
  أول شجرة نبتت في الدنيا بعد الطوفان». al-Baghawi echoes it verbatim. Marked as "something
  said" in both, no ruling built on it — reported as such.
- **Ṭūr = mountain; called Ṭūr when it bears trees, else jabal; wa-Allāhu aʿlam** —
  Ibn Kathir (14): «والطور: هو الجبل. وقال بعضهم: إنما يسمى طورا إذا كان فيه شجر، فإن عري
  عنها سمي جبلا لا طورا، والله أعلم».
- **Mountain of Mūsā in Shām, olives around it; Ibn Abbas; Ibn Zayd's ridge Egypt→Ayla** —
  Ibn Kathir (14): «وطور سيناء: هو طور سينين، وهو الجبل الذي كلم الله عليه موسى بن عمران...
  وما حوله من الجبال التي فيها شجر الزيتون». at-Tabari (Ibn Abbas): «هو جبل بالشام مبارك» /
  «الجبل الذي نودي منه موسى». Ibn Zayd (at-Tabari & al-Qurtubi): «جبل... ممدود، هو بين مصر
  وبين أيلة».
- **Ṭūr Sīnāʾ = Ṭūr Sīnīn; link to 95:2** — Ibn Kathir (14): «هو طور سينين». al-Baghawi:
  «واختلفوا في معناه وفي "سينين" في قوله تعالى: "وطور سينين" (التين - 2)». The 95:1 mention
  (fig+olive) is that sūrah's own opening, stated as placement, not as a tafsir claim that
  the two olives are identical.
- **Disagreement over "Sīnāʾ" (kept open)** — Mujahid = مبارك (at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi,
  al-Baghawi); Qatadah = حسن; ad-Dahhak = حسن in Nabatean (at-Tabari, al-Baghawi); Muqatil =
  any fruit-bearing mountain (al-Qurtubi, al-Baghawi); jumhūr = proper name like Uhud
  (al-Qurtubi: «وقال الجمهور: هو اسم الجبل؛ كما تقول جبل أحد»). at-Tabari's tarjīḥ: proper
  name annexed to Ṭūr, «كما قيل: جبلا طيئ»; keeps Ibn Abbas's "mountain of Mūsā, blessed"
  but denies Sīnāʾ itself means blessed. Reported as an unresolved difference.
- **tanbutu/tunbitu reading; bāʾ zāʾida debate; meaning converges** — at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi,
  al-Baghawi all record the split. Mujahid: «بثمره». Ibn Abbas: «هو الزيت يوكل، ويدهن به».
- **Uses of the oil (massage / lamp-light / food dressing)** — Ma'arif (168): "massage the
  body, in lamps to provide light and in food as a curry or dressing". Muyassar: «يعصر منها
  الزيت، فيدَّهن ويؤتدم به».
- **ṣibgh — dye vs relish, both readings** — al-Qurtubi §3: «وأصل الصبغ ما يلون به الثوب،
  وشبه الإدام به لأن الخبز يلون بالصبغ إذا غمس فيه». idām reading: Ibn Kathir «أي: أدم، قاله
  قتادة»; at-Tabari/Ibn Zayd «يصطبغ بالزيت الذين يأكلونه... يأتدمون به»; as-Sa'di «يجعل إداما
  للآكلين». Muqatil (al-Qurtubi, al-Baghawi): «الأدم الزيتون، والدهن الزيت».

## Hadith
- **Featured (sound):** Ṣaḥīḥ Muslim 2052 (Jābir b. ʿAbdillāh). URL fetched:
  https://quranx.com/hadith/Muslim/Hadith-2052 . First ten words (EN): "Jabir b. 'Abdullah
  reported that Allah's Apostle (ﷺ) asked his family". Wording used: «نِعْمَ الأُدُمُ الْخَلُّ».
  In Ṣaḥīḥ Muslim (sound). Flagged in the article as a general statement about idām, not
  revealed about this verse (al-Qurtubi §4 cites it in the idām discussion).
- **Verse-specific narration, reported with its weakness, NOT leaned on:** Jāmiʿ at-Tirmidhi
  1851 (also Ibn Mājah), from ʿUmar: «كُلُوا الزَّيْتَ وَادَّهِنُوا بِهِ فَإِنَّهُ مِنْ
  شَجَرَةٍ مُبَارَكَةٍ». URL fetched: https://quranx.com/hadith/Tirmidhi/Hadith-1851 . First
  ten words (EN): "Narrated 'Umar bin Al-Khattab: That the Messenger of Allah". Grading:
  at-Tirmidhi's own note — known only through ʿAbd ar-Razzāq from Maʿmar, who was muḍṭarib
  (inconsistent) in it, sometimes ʿUmar-marfūʿ, sometimes mursal. Cited by Ibn Kathir (14)
  and al-Qurtubi §6, who both relay Tirmidhi's iḍṭirāb note. Grading not upgraded.

## Asbāb an-nuzūl
- None. No asbāb established for this verse in the fetched sources; the article uses the
  verse's placement in the 23:17–22 signs-of-provision passage instead.

## Dropped / not used
- al-Qurtubi's long fiqh of idām (§§4–5: whether solids like meat, cheese, dates count as
  idām; oaths; the vinegar-in-Ṣaḥīḥ chain of nine Companions; the barley-bread-and-date
  Abu Dawud report; "iʾtadimū wa law bi'l-māʾ"; "sayyid idām ... al-laḥm"). Dropped to keep
  the section on the verse's own point; only the sound vinegar wording was used.
- Ibn Kathir's Abu Usayd chain and the ʿUmar/ʿĀshūrāʾ olive-oil anecdote (Ṭabarānī) —
  dropped; the Tirmidhi/ʿUmar wording already carries the same content with its grading.
- The many qirāʾāt sub-variants (tunbitu bi-ḍamm, tunbitu bi-rafʿ al-tāʾ, Ibn Masʿūd's
  tukhriju bi'l-duhn, bi'l-dihān, aṣbāgh, matāʿan) — noted only as "the reciters read it two
  ways" plus the bāʾ debate; the finer list dropped as needless detail.
- No claim made from memory; nothing attributed beyond the fetched clauses above.
