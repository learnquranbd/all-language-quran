Card and 8-section bilingual article for 42:52: wa-kadhalika awhayna
ilayka ruhan min amrina, ma kunta tadri ma-l-kitabu wa la-l-iman,
ja'alnahu nuran, and wa-innaka la-tahdi ila siratin mustaqim. 42:51
kept as frame (link attributed); 42:53 only as the path explained.

Sources: Tabari, Ibn Kathir (ar; en abridged for the 42:51-53 grouping),
Muyassar, Qurtubi (with Qushayri, 'Iyad, Nahhas), Sa'di, Baghawi, Ma'arif.
- Wa-kadhalika: "as to the messengers before" (Tabari, Baghawi, Qurtubi,
  Sa'di, Muyassar); Ma'arif: complement of 42:51.
- Ruh: six glosses kept with names, none picked (Baghawi, Qurtubi);
  Tabari's two camps; IK and Sa'di: the Qur'an. Why "spirit": Sa'di,
  Qurtubi; Qushayri on 17:85; Malik ibn Dinar's "spring of hearts".
- What he did not know: each tafsir's gloss (detail of the law, earlier
  scriptures, laws and landmarks of faith, Ibn Khuzayma's salah + 2:143,
  Qurtubi's four "close" views, the ummi reading + 29:48).
- Faith before the mission: Baghawi (ahl al-usul, din Ibrahim), Ma'arif
  (consensus), Qurtubi (Qushayri's caveat, 'Iyad, his own verdict);
  muta'abbid views set aside by the imams. No own-voice claim of error.
- Ja'alnahu: pronoun (Tabari; Ibn 'Abbas/Dahhak iman vs Suddi Qur'an vs
  wahy) kept as a difference. Nur: Tabari, Sa'di, IK with 41:44.
- La-tahdi: guiding as calling and making clear (Tabari, Baghawi,
  Qurtubi, Muyassar, Sa'di); Qatada + 13:7; Ubayy's la-tad'u and
  Nahhas's verdict. 28:56 not used: no fetched tafsir draws it.
Hadith: none attached to the verse, said in one sentence. Qurtubi's
rejected 'Uthman ibn Abi Shayba report noted only as rejected (Ahmad,
Daraqutni). No asbab.
Dropped: Qurtubi's sira and child-prophet digression, al-Husayn ibn
al-Fadl and Mawardi readings, "man nasha'" = chosen for prophethood.
Cross-refs checked in quran-json.
