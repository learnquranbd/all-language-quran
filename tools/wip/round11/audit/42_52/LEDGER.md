- Ma kunta tadri: each tafsir's gloss given (Tabari, IK, Muyassar, Baghawi, Sa'di, Ma'arif, Ibn Khuzayma, Qurtubi's list). Article voice never says he lacked faith or erred; §5 reports Baghawi, Ma'arif, Qushayri, 'Iyad, Qurtubi that he was a believer before revelation.
- 28:56: NOT cited. No fetched tafsir draws the contrast (verified 28:56 in data, but not used).

- No sira detail used (Qurtubi's Amina, Bahira, 'Arafa, "bughhidat ilayya al-awthan" all dropped).
