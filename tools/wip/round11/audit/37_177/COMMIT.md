Card and 8-section bilingual article for 37:177, the answer to 37:176's "do they hasten
Our punishment?": the punishment descends in their courtyard, evil is the warned's morning.

Sources: Tabari, Qurtubi, Ibn Kathir (ar/en), Baghawi, Sa'di, Muyassar (37:176-177), Ma'arif (group 37:171-179).
- sahah: Tabari, fina' dar al-rajul and the Arabs' "it came down in so-and-so's courtyard";
  as-Suddi bi-darihim (Tabari, Qurtubi, Ibn Kathir); Qurtubi, sahah/sahsah = wide forecourt;
  al-Farra' nazala bi-sahatihim = nazala bihim (Qurtubi) / courtyard stands for the people
  (Baghawi); Muqatil bi-hadratihim; Sa'di near them; Ibn Kathir bi-mahallatihim. Sources
  converge; said so, no disagreement invented.
- Morning: Qurtubi's idmar (fa-sa'a as-sabah sabahuhum) and his reason (the punishment
  used to come then), az-Zajjaj (by killing); Sa'di sabah ash-sharr wal-isti'sal; Ibn Kathir
  their day of destruction; Tabari, warned by Our messenger and did not believe.
- Ma'arif's idiom note and Mazhari's postponing-till-morning remark, attributed, no ruling.
Hadith: Sahih al-Bukhari 2945 (Malik/Humayd/Anas, Khaybar morning), quoted whole, no grading
added; Muslim 1365 confirmed for the closing sentence; Bukhari 371 confirms the Ibn 'Ulayya
route. Ibn Kathir's "in the Sahihayn" reported as his attribution and checked separately;
his Ahmad route (Abu Talha) named, his judgement on it not used as a grading.
Framing: one military episode, not a ruling on war, night/dawn raids or any people; "Jews
of Khaybar" only in Baghawi's/Bukhari's wording as history; licensing sentence in both
languages. Qurtubi's "yubayyinu ma'na" read as illustration; no revelation date claimed.
37:178-179 repetition noted lightly (Baghawi ta'kid, Ibn Kathir en). No asbab.
Dropped: Tabari as source for the morning reason (not in his text); Makkan/Khaybar chronology
(no fetched text); Ma'arif's variant hadith wording; Bukhari book names.
Themes: repentance.
