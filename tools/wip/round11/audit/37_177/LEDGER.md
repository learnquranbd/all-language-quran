## Hadith
- **Sahih al-Bukhari 2945** (Dar-us-Salam; USC-MSA Vol 4, Book 52, Hadith 195). URL: https://quranx.com/hadith/Bukhari/DarusSalam/Hadith-2945 . Chain on page: 'Abdullah ibn Maslama, Malik, Humayd, Anas. First ten words of the wording used (own translation from the Arabic): "Anas (RA) narrated that the Prophet ﷺ went out to Khaybar". Quoted whole. Grading: none shown on the page beyond its place in Sahih al-Bukhari; none added. Saved: hadith_Bukhari_2945.txt.
- Also fetched, not quoted: Bukhari 371 (Isma'il ibn 'Ulayya / 'Abd al-'Aziz ibn Suhayb route, confirms that route in Bukhari; long Safiyya narration) and Bukhari 4197 (Malik/Humayd via 'Abdullah ibn Yusuf, no "Allahu akbar"). Saved files.
- **Sahih Muslim 1365** (quranx reference "Hadith 1365a-i"). URL: https://quranx.com/hadith/Muslim/Hadith-1365 . Arabic «إِنَّا إِذَا نَزَلْنَا بِسَاحَةِ قَوْمٍ فَسَاءَ صَبَاحُ الْمُنْذَرِينَ» confirmed on the page in several sub-narrations. Cited by number for the closing sentence only; wording not quoted. Saved: hadith_Muslim_1365.txt.
- Ibn Kathir's "thabata fi as-Sahihayn" reported as his attribution, then confirmed separately (Bukhari 2945/371, Muslim 1365).
- Ahmad route (Rawh, Sa'id ibn Abi 'Aruba, Qatada, Anas, Abu Talha): named as a route Ibn Kathir adds; his own judgement on it («وهو صحيح على شرط الشيخين») NOT quoted as a grading; article says he "closes it with a judgement of his own" and rests nothing on it.

## Asbab an-nuzul
Not used. None of the fetched texts says the verse was revealed about Khaybar or gives a revelation date; article says the commentators bring the report to illustrate the wording and says nothing on chronology (s6.2).

## Framing guard
s7.1 (one military episode, no ruling on war / night or dawn raids / any people), s7.2 ("the Jews of Khaybar" in al-Baghawi's wording and "the Jews" in al-Bukhari's, as historical report only; licenses nothing against any living person or community), s7.3 (verse not a tool to wish punishment on others). Ma'arif/Mazhari timing note kept as an attributed comment on idiom, explicitly no ruling (s4.2).

## Dropped / corrected
- Watch-point listed at-Tabari as describing the punishment arriving at morning: his fetched text glosses sabah but gives no reason for it. Reason attributed to al-Qurtubi only (s4.1 says so).
- Revelation-order claim (verse Makkan, Khaybar later): not in any fetched text; dropped.
- Ma'arif's "attacked the fort of Khaibar" and its variant hadith wording: not used (Bukhari quoted instead).
- Bukhari book names (Prayer / Jihad): not shown by name on the fetched pages; not stated.
- 37:178 repetition kept light (s8.1); no 37:173-175 ground rebuilt.
