Replaces the 769-word, 6-section article with an 8-section, 1,782-word
source-gated article (shipped card kept).

Sources: at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi, as-Sa'di, Ibn Kathir (ar, en abridged),
al-Baghawi, Muyassar on 51:55 and 51:56; Ma'arif on 51:56. Covers the
placement after 51:52-54 (Ibn Kathir en, Muyassar: keep calling while
turning from them; at-Tabari and Mujahid "wa'izhum"); what to remind with
(al-Qurtubi: admonition, Qatada's Qur'an, punishment and days of Allah);
whom it addresses, kept as a difference (al-Baghawi: Muqatil the Makkan
disbelievers, al-Kalbi the believers); as-Sa'di's two kinds of reminding,
87:9-11 and his salty-ground simile, with the licensing sentence.
51:56 kept as a genuine dispute with holders named and none picked: the
command to worship ('Ali, via al-Baghawi and al-Qurtubi, 9:31, al-Zajjaj);
affirming worship willingly or unwillingly (Ibn 'Abbas via 'Ali ibn Abi
Talha, at-Tabari's choice); to know Me (Mujahid; Ibn Jurayj in Ibn
Kathir; 43:87); 'Ikrimah, al-Kalbi, as-Suddi (31:25); believers only
(ad-Dahhak, al-Kalbi, Sufyan; the "min al-mu'minin" reading given to
Ibn 'Abbas by al-Baghawi, to 'Abd Allah by al-Qurtubi; al-Qushayri, 7:179,
49:14); Zayd ibn Aslam's felicity and wretchedness. At-Tabari's answer on
how the deniers fit (humbled to His decree, opposing only in action) and
Ma'arif's Thanawi and Mazhari answers. 51:57-58 in two lines (Ibn Kathir,
as-Sa'di: not out of need).

Hadith: Tirmidhi 2466 (hadith qudsi, attached by Ibn Kathir), quoted
whole in at-Tirmidhi's wording, graded hasan gharib by him.

Dropped: the Companions'-grief report on 51:54 (not in fetched files),
the shipped article's misattributed as-Sa'di provision claim, its
unattached Bukhari intentions hadith, 2:21 and 36:60-61, and its homily.
