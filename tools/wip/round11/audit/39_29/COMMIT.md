Card and 8-section bilingual article for 39:29: daraba Allahu mathalan
rajulan fihi shuraka'u mutashakisun wa-rajulan salaman li-rajul, the
man owned by quarrelling partners against the man wholly one master's.

Sources: Tabari, Qurtubi, Sa'di, Baghawi, Ibn Kathir (ar + en abridged),
Muyassar. Ma'arif's grouped text covers only 39:24; not used.
- Mushrik vs muwahhid: Sa'di names it outright (mathal li-sh-shirk
  wa-t-tawhid); Ibn Kathir mushrik vs mukhlis, citing Ibn Abbas and
  Mujahid; Baghawi, Tabari, Muyassar the same pair.
- Slave reading of rajul given as the commentators' framing (Sa'di
  'abdan, Muyassar, Ibn Kathir); the article says the image is borrowed
  for a point about worship, not offered as a model.
- mutashakisun: Farra', Mubarrad, Jawhari (via Qurtubi); Baghawi, Tabari.
- Qira'at, brief: saliman (Makkah, Basrah; Ibn Abbas) vs salaman; Tabari:
  both correct, close in meaning; silman from Sa'id ibn Jubayr, Ikrimah.
- al-hamdu lillah: Ibn Kathir, for establishing the proof; Sa'di, for
  distinguishing truth and guiding; Tabari, Baghawi, Muyassar: praise to
  Allah alone apart from every other object of worship.
- aktharuhum la ya'lamun: Tabari, Ibn Kathir, Qurtubi, Muyassar, Baghawi
  (al-akthar means all).
Disagreements kept, no position: what the partners stand for (Mujahid
false god, Qatadah devils, Suddi idols; Tabari joins both); saliman vs
salaman as reconciliation (Tabari, Abu Ubayd vs an-Nahhas).
Cross-refs: 39:27 (one clause), 23:50 (Tabari's analogy).
Hadith: none attached by any fetched source; said in one sentence.
Asbab: none. 39:30-31 not pulled forward. Licensing sentence added (EN
and BN) for the mushrikun. Themes: tawheed, parables.
