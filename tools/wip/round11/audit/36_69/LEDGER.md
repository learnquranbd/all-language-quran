## Disagreements kept (no position)
- Abu Bakr AND ʿUmar (al-Baghawi) vs Abu Bakr OR ʿUmar (Ibn Kathir, al-Qurtubi).
- Tarafa half-line: inverted (Qatada's reports in Tabari/Ibn Kathir/Baghawi; al-Qurtubi) vs correct order (Ahmad via ash-Shaʿbi, an-Nasaʾi al-Yawm wa'l-Layla, Tirmidhi/Baghawi via al-Miqdam). Not resolved.
- Whether the verse faults poetry: Malik (yes, "among its faults") vs Ibn al-ʿArabi (no); Ibn Kathir on lawful poetry.
- Referent of «إن هو»: Muhammad ﷺ (at-Tabari) vs the Qur'an (al-Baghawi) vs "what We taught him" (Ibn Kathir) vs "what he recites" (al-Qurtubi).

Grading: at-Tirmidhi «حسن صحيح». Ibn Kathir attaches it to this verse. Quoted whole (Arabic rendered; quranx English is loose).
