Card + 8-section bilingual article (1,795 EN words) for 36:69, "We did
not teach him poetry, nor is it befitting for him; it is only a reminder
and a clear Qur'an."

Sources: at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi, al-Baghawi, Ibn Kathir, as-Sa'di,
al-Muyassar, Ma'arif al-Qur'an, each quoted from the text fetched for 36:69.
- Setting: al-Kalbi via al-Baghawi (Meccan "poet" charge, verse as
  refutation); al-Qurtubi's Quraysh-at-the-season deliberation. The charge
  is reported only to be refuted.
- Ma yanbaghi lahu: Tabari/Muyassar, Ibn Kathir (not in his nature),
  Baghawi (not made easy), az-Zajjaj via Qurtubi; as-Sa'di's rashid/muhtadi
  vs ghawun contrast, 26:224 named only for the contrast.
- Qurtubi: the absence as a sign against doubt; Ibn al-'Arabi and
  al-Ma'mun: not a fault in poetry or writing (29:48).
- Tafsir-narrated, ungraded: al-Hasan's "Islam and grey hair" (Abu Bakr
  AND 'Umar per Baghawi vs Abu Bakr OR 'Umar per Ibn Kathir, Qurtubi); al-'Abbas b.
  Mirdas (Bayhaqi via Ibn Kathir); Qatada's Tarafa reports.
- Tarafa half-line kept as a genuine variation: inverted (Qatada; Qurtubi)
  vs correct order (Ahmad via ash-Sha'bi, Nasa'i al-Yawm wa'l-Layla,
  al-Miqdam in Tirmidhi and Baghawi). Not resolved.
- Hadith: Tirmidhi 2848 (al-Miqdam, hasan sahih); Muslim 2473 (Unays tests
  the words against the measures of poetry), marked as brought by Qurtubi,
  not narrated on the verse. Stock Bukhari 660 not used.
- Meter by chance (Trench, Hunayn; 3:92, 61:13; "one stitch is not a
  tailor"); Malik vs Ibn al-'Arabi on faulting poetry kept as a disagreement.
- In huwa: Muhammad (Tabari) / the Qur'an (Baghawi) / what We taught him
  (Ibn Kathir) / what he recites (Qurtubi), kept unresolved.
- Dropped: Abu Dawud "or composed poetry of myself" (grading unconfirmed),
  Shaddad b. Aws (gharib), al-Khalil's unchained remark, munkar Bayhaqi line.
- 36:63-68 and 36:70 left to their own entries.
