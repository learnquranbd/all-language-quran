## Hadith

- **Sahih al-Bukhari 3208** (stages of creation), Ibn Mas'ud. URL fetched: https://quranx.com/hadith/Bukhari/DarusSalam/Hadith-3208 . First ten words of wording used: "The creation of each one of you is gathered in". Grading: Sahih (al-Bukhari). Quoted the portion the mufassirun attach to the verse, ending at "the soul is breathed into him" — matching Ibn Kathir's own citation, which stops at "ثم ينفخ فيه الروح"; the qadar tail (cubit/Paradise) was omitted as not bearing on the embryology argument. No hybridizing of variants.

## Second narration (du'a) — carried on tafsir citation, NOT independently fetched

- The Prophet's ﷺ prayer seeking refuge from miserliness, cowardice and being returned to arzal al-'umur. Cited by al-Qurtubi and Ma'arif al-Qur'an, both attributing it to an-Nasa'i on the authority of Sa'd. I did not fetch a collection page for it, so no number and no grade are asserted; it is presented in the article strictly as "al-Qurtubi and Ma'arif note / cite from an-Nasa'i," i.e. an attribution to the mufassirun, not a graded hadith slot.
