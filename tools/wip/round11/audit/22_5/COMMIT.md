Card and deep bilingual article for 22:5, the sustained a-fortiori
proof of resurrection: He who made you through the embryonic stages,
and who revives dead earth, can raise you again.

Sources (all fetched for this verse, none from memory):
- at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi, Ibn Kathir, as-Sa'di, al-Baghawi, al-Muyassar,
  Ma'arif al-Qur'an — turab (Adam / the line's root), nutfah, 'alaqah
  (thick clotted blood), mudghah (a lump the size of a mouthful).
- "mukhallaqah wa ghayr mukhallaqah" kept as a live disagreement:
  at-Tabari's preferred (full-shaped vs the miscarried lump before
  shaping/ensoulment), al-Farra' and Qatadah (complete/incomplete),
  Mujahid (the siqt, shaped or not), Ibn Zaid (head-hands-legs vs
  nothing), Ma'arif's alt (sound-limbed vs deformed), with Ibn
  al-'Arabi's reconciliation by ishtiqaq. No modern-embryology claims.
- li-nubayyina lakum (al-Baghawi) and the second proof, the dead earth
  quickened (Ibn Kathir names it a dalil akhar; as-Sa'di the second
  rational proof); Qurtubi's grammar point that ihtazzat/rabat belong
  to the earth; arzal al-'umur given its due (Ibn Kathir, as-Sa'di's
  "two weaknesses" arc).

Hadith: Sahih al-Bukhari 3208 (Ibn Mas'ud, stages of creation), quoted
whole to "the soul is breathed into him," exactly as Ibn Kathir cites
it here; graded Sahih, not upgraded.

Kept off the page: Ibn Kathir's Anas age-report (he grades it very
gharib / severely munkar) and its Ma'arif parallel — dropped as weak;
Qurtubi's fiqh masa'il on miscarriage/'idda/diyya — not reproduced, per
the no medical/legal-directive rule (respectful of 22:2). The arzal
du'a is carried only as al-Qurtubi's and Ma'arif's citation from
an-Nasa'i (Sa'd), with no number or grade asserted.

Steered to the evidence angle (embryo + dead earth); did not re-argue
the certainty-of-the-Hour that shipped 22:7 already owns. No asbab
claimed; verse framed as the ihtijaj against the disputer of 22:3.
