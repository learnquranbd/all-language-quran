Card and bilingual article (8 sections, 1,761 EN words) for "and horses,
mules and donkeys for you to ride and as adornment, and He creates what
you do not know", built on the verse's silence about eating and its open
last clause.

Sources: at-Tabari (grammar of zinah and the implied verb; Ibn Abbas and
al-Hakam; the other view, his preference and the mirror argument; the
Hereafter reading), al-Qurtubi (khayl from ikhtiyal; Malik via Ibn
al-Qasim; his own view for permission, the Meccan-surah and 40:79
arguments; the majority's "of creation"), Ibn Kathir (Abu Hanifa and the
pairing argument; "fihi kalam"; majority list), al-Baghawi (both lists,
"isnaduhu da'if", Qatada), as-Sa'di, the Muyassar, Ma'arif (makruh via
al-Jassas; beauty vs pride via Bayan al-Qur'an; railways, attributed).

Disagreement kept: horse meat, both sides with names and evidence, no
position; the sources' own disagreement over Malik stated; rulings
referred to qualified scholars. Five readings of the last clause kept;
the modern-vehicle reading only as Ma'arif's.

Hadith: Abu Dawud 3790 (Khalid) quoted whole with Abu Dawud's own
"abrogated" remark; Bukhari 4219 (Jabir, Khaybar) and 5510 (Asma') quoted
whole; no gradings added.

Dropped: at-Tabari's 'Ata'-Jabir report and Ibn Kathir's longer Ahmad
wording (not confirmable); al-Qurtubi's ungraded unseen-world reports
(named as left aside); the Ibn Majah horses hadith; Isra'iliyyat on
horses. 16:5-7 not treated; no asbab in any source.
