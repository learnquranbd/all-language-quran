Card and deep bilingual article for 29:20, the imperative to travel the
land and see how Allah began creation, as proof of the final creation.

Sources gave a near-unanimous reading of an-nashʾa al-ākhira as the
resurrection: at-Tabari (Qatada, Ibn Abbas: life after death / an-nushūr),
Ibn Kathir ar (Day of Resurrection), as-Sa'di (the origination with no
death or sleep). The core argument from first creation to repeat is in
at-Tabari, al-Muyassar and al-Baghawi; Ma'arif adds that repeating is
easier than originating.

al-Qurtubi and al-Baghawi give the two-directions looking: the variety of
living kinds AND the dwellings/ruins of destroyed generations. as-Sa'di
adds continuous creation (humans, animals, plants, clouds, winds) and
sleep as the daily lesser death and waking. A textual note kept: badaʾa
(past) vs yunshiʾu (future) tenses; the qiraʾa an-nashʾa/an-nashāʾa
(Meccan reciter Ibn Kathir and Abu ʿAmr) from al-Qurtubi and al-Baghawi.

No disagreement to keep as a disagreement; the resurrection reading was
effectively unanimous.

Hadith: Sahih al-Bukhari 4935 (recreation from the tailbone), marked as a
general sound narration on resurrection NOT tied by the mufassirūn to this
verse. Collector's grading sahih, not upgraded. Muslim 2955 confirmed but
not used.

Dropped: any modern-science prediction reading (section 7 names and
refuses it). No sabab for 29:20; placement used instead.
