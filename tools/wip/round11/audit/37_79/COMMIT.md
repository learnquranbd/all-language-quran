Card + 8-section bilingual article (1,627 EN words) on 37:79, "Peace upon
Noah among the worlds," continuing straight from 37:78's "left for him
among later generations" into this standalone declaration.

Sources (6 tafsirs fetched via PREP.md, none re-fetched): at-Tabari
(majority reading "salamun" nominative, a fresh sentence; the Kufan
grammarians' alternative parsing as continuing "taraknā"; his own "amanah
... an yadhkurahu ahad bi-su'"), al-Muyassar and al-Qurtubi (the same
"security from being mentioned with evil" gloss, independently worded),
Ibn Kathir ar/en (ties the peace to 37:78's "beautiful remembrance,"
"greeted with peace by every group and nation"), as-Sa'di (37:75-79 as one
answered prayer, closing on his "sunnah with the muhsinun" — praise spread
in proportion to excellence in worship and in dealing with people, anchors
the turn into 37:80).

Two disagreements kept open, both named, neither resolved: Ibn Mas'ud's
variant reading "salaman" (accusative, via al-Kisa'i through al-Qurtubi)
against the mainstream nominative reading; and al-Qurtubi's own two
readings of "al-akhirin" in 37:78 — the ummah of Muhammad ﷺ specifically,
or every prophet after Noah, tied to 42:13 (verified against
data/quran-json/42.json and data/translations/en.json) — cross-validated,
not merged, with Mujahid's separate gloss "an honourable mention by all the
Prophets" (Ibn Kathir en abridged).

Confirmed against the mushaf (data/quran-json/37.json), not memory: the
surah's "salamun 'ala" refrain recurs at 37:109, 120, 130 and 181; none of
those four carries "fi al-'alamin," only Noah's does. No source explains
why, so the article states the fact and leaves the reason unconfirmed.

No hadith attaches to this verse in any source fetched. Dropped, per
instruction: Sa'id ibn al-Musayyab's non-Prophetic scorpion-recitation
claim and the separate genuine hadith on "a'udhu bikalimatillah" that
follows it in al-Qurtubi's passage, both unrelated to this verse. Also
dropped: al-Baghawi's two glosses (redundant) and Ma'arif al-Qur'an's
interfaith-reception line (true but out of scope). 37:80-85 kept as future
ground, touched only to show where the passage leads.
