# LEDGER 37:79

## SOURCES (tafsir)

Fetched (already present in audit dir per PREP.md; all read in full, all ≤900 chars):
- t14 Ibn Kathir (ar) — used. Clause: "لما أبقى عليه من الذكر الجميل والثناء الحسن أنه يسلم عليه في جميع الطوائف والأمم" (because of the beautiful remembrance and good praise left for him, he is saluted with peace among every group and nation). Used in §3 ("Greeted by Every Nation").
- t15 at-Tabari — used. Clauses: "أمنة من الله لنوح في العالمين أن يذكره أحد بسوء" (security from Allah for Noah among the worlds, that no one would ever mention him with evil); the Kufan grammarians' alternative parsing of salamun as continuing taraknā; and his remark "ولو كان: تركنا عليه سلاما، كان صوابا" (had it read "We left for him a peace" that too would be correct) — used in §1 and §2.
- t16 al-Muyassar — used. Clause: "أمان لنوح وسلامة له من أن يُذْكر بسوء في الآخِرين، بل تُثني عليه الأجيال من بعده" — used in §2.
- t90 al-Qurtubi — used. Clauses: "سلامة له من أن يذكر بسوء في الآخرين" (§2); Ibn Mas'ud's reading "سلاما" accusative governed by "تركنا", reported on al-Kisa'i's authority (§1); the two readings of "في الآخرين" — specifically the ummah of Muhammad ﷺ, or among all the prophets since none came after him except commanded to follow his way (§4). NOT USED: Sa'id ibn al-Musayyab's scorpion-protection claim (via Abu 'Umar's at-Tamhid) and the separate Muwatta/Khawla bint Hakim/Abu Hurayrah "a'udhu bikalimatillah" hadith that follows it in the same passage — dropped per instruction; neither is about this verse's content, and the first has no Prophetic attribution.
- t91 as-Sa'di — used. Clause: "وهذه سنته تعالى في المحسنين، أن ينشر لهم من الثناء على حسب إحسانهم" (this is His sunnah with the muhsinun, that He spreads praise for them in proportion to their excellence), tied to "محسن في عبادة الخالق، محسن إلى الخلق" (excellent in worship of the Creator, excellent toward creation) — used in §7, anchoring the turn into 37:80.
- t94 al-Baghawi — read, not used in the end (its two glosses — "peace from Us" and "prayed for until the Day of Judgment" — duplicate ground already covered more fully by Tabari/Muyassar/Qurtubi; omitted to keep the section non-redundant, not because it was unconfirmable).
- t168 Ma'arif al-Qur'an (en, grouped 37:78-82) — read; not directly quoted (its content — universal reverence for Noah across faiths — is covered via Ibn Kathir's en/ar pairing instead, to keep one line of attribution per claim).
- t169 Ibn Kathir (en, abridged, grouped 37:75-82) — used. On 37:79 itself: "he is greeted with peace by all groups and nations" (§3, paired with t14's Arabic). On 37:78 (context for "al-akhirin"): Ibn Abbas "remembered in a good way," Mujahid "an honourable mention by all the Prophets," Qatada/As-Suddi "praised constantly by others," Ad-Dahhak "Salam and praise" — used in §3 and §4 (Mujahid's gloss cross-validates al-Qurtubi's second reading on "al-akhirin").

## Disagreements kept as disagreements
1. Grammar of "salamun" (§1): mainstream reading nominative, a fresh sentence (at-Tabari, majority); Ibn Mas'ud's variant reading "salaman" accusative, continuing "taraknā" (al-Qurtubi, on al-Kisa'i's authority) — named as a reading variant, no position taken.
2. Reference of "fi al-akhirin" in 37:78 (§4): specifically the ummah of Muhammad ﷺ, or among all the prophets tied to 42:13 (both from al-Qurtubi, unresolved in his own text) — both named, cross-validated (not merged) with Mujahid's separate gloss "an honourable mention by all the Prophets" (Ibn Kathir en abridged). No position taken.
