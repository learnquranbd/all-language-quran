Card and 8-section bilingual article for 40:40: man 'amila sayyi'atan
fa-la yujza illa mithlaha ... yurzaquna fiha bi-ghayri hisab, the
believer of Fir'awn's family continuing his plea (40:38-44).

Sources: Tabari, Ibn Kathir (ar + en abridged, grouped 40:38-40),
Muyassar, Qurtubi, Sa'di, Baghawi. Ma'arif (grouped 40:36-43) covers
only the tower of 40:36; not named.
- Frame: placement between ya qawmi (40:39) and wa ya qawmi (40:41);
  IK-en heads 40:38-40 "More of what the Believer ... said" but
  introduces 40:40 with "Allah says". Both reported, not resolved.
  Shipped 40:31 and 40:44 one line each; 40:41-43 not drawn on.
- mithlaha: IK "wahidatan mithlaha"; Tabari punishment for it; Qurtubi
  al-'adhab; Muyassar equal to the sin; Sa'di what harms and grieves.
- Disagreement kept: sayyi'a = shirk (Qatada via Tabari; Qurtubi) vs any
  disobedience (Tabari's own gloss; Muyassar) vs shirk/fusuq/'isyan (Sa'di).
- dhakar aw untha: Tabari "rajul aw imra'a", Muyassar; no elaboration
  beyond the text. Salih: Sa'di heart/limbs/tongue; Ibn 'Abbas via
  Qurtubi la ilaha illa Allah; Qatada via Tabari khayr.
- wa huwa mu'min: Tabari, Muyassar (tawhid), Qurtubi (heart, prophets).
- bi-ghayri hisab: Qatada via Tabari no measuring vessel or scale;
  IK not measured as recompense, no end; Sa'di no limit, no count,
  beyond their deeds; Muqatil via Baghawi no taba'a (no reckoning).
- Contrast justice/grace attributed to IK and Sa'di.
- Qurtubi's qira'a note yudkhaluna/yadkhuluna (reciter Ibn Kathir flagged).
- Licensing sentence in both languages.
Hadith: none attached by any fetched tafsir; said in one sentence.
Asbab: none; placement given instead.
Dropped: 3:195, 4:124, 16:97, 6:160 (no fetched tafsir cites them);
"beyond expectation" gloss (not in fetched texts).
