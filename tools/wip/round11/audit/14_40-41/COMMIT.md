from at-Tabari, Ibn Kathir (ar and en), al-Qurtubi, al-Baghawi, as-Sa'di
and the Muyassar on 14:40 and 14:41, and Ma'arif on 14:37 (its grouped text
is truncated before 14:40). Slots checked, none shifted. Shipped card kept.

the children at 14:37; Ibn Kathir (en) on 14:39 as thanks for an answered du'a.

(firm upon Islam and its rulings) kept as his.

not all, so no reason is supplied. 2:124 noted as an echo only.

al-Qurtubi, al-Baghawi); the Muyassar keeps both.

worship" wording of al-Qurtubi and al-Baghawi traced to Tirmidhi 3371,
graded gharib (Ibn Lahi'a only) and not relied on. Abu Dawud 1479 unused
(no grading on the saved page). No asbab.

own "more apparent" reported as his.

as-Sa'di, al-Baghawi), the mother's Islam (al-Qushayri), conditional
prayer, Adam and Hawwa, and the singular and "two sons" readings, all
unranked. Licensing line in both languages.
