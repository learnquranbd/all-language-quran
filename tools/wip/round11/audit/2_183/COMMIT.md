Replaces the 717-word, 5-section article with a 1,764-word, 9-section
one, from at-Tabari, Ibn Kathir, al-Qurtubi, as-Sa'di, al-Baghawi,
al-Muyassar and Ma'arif on 2:183. All slots checked. The shipped card is kept.

Kutiba after qisas and wasiyya (al-Qurtubi); obligation as a favour
(as-Sa'di). Sawm as holding back: horses, wind, noon (at-Tabari,
al-Qurtubi, al-Baghawi); legal definitions attributed.
"Those before you" kept as a disagreement: Christians (al-Sha'bi,
as-Suddi, al-Baghawi's group), People of the Book (Mujahid, Ibn 'Abbas),
all nations (Qatada, al-Hasan; al-Qurtubi's Mujahid). Likeness: time and
amount / manner / the obligation itself; at-Tabari's preference (time)
beside Ma'arif's caution. Thirty-to-fifty report with the editor's note
on al-Sha'bi's chain; an-Nahhas's Daghfal report ungraded; Ibn Umar
marfu' via Ibn Abi Hatim ungraded. Stages: three days a month, Ashura,
Mu'adh's two-step abrogation; fidya deferred to 2:184.
La'allakum: hope (al-Qurtubi), narrow purpose (at-Tabari, as-Suddi),
three senses (al-Qurtubi), al-Baghawi, al-Muyassar; as-Sa'di's five.
Hadith: Bukhari 5066 (young men, wija'), 1894 (fasting is a shield,
first saying), 1903 (false speech), each quoted whole. No occasion of
revelation in any fetched tafsir; said so.
Dropped: "second year after the hijrah", "Islam built on five", the
2:186 placement section, 2:184-185 concessions, al-Baghawi's king story.
Corrected: "the reports settle nothing" (at-Tabari weighs them);
"agreed-upon" shield hadith (only Bukhari confirmed); taqwa as a barrier
against harm (al-Muyassar: against sins); "routes of appetite" (sources:
paths of Shaytan).
