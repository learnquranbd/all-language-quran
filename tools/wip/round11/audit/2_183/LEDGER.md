## Shipped short article: points dropped and points found wrong
- "Made obligatory in Madinah, in the second year after the hijrah": in none of the fetched files. Dropped.
- "The differing reports the commentators relate ... settle nothing": wrong as a summary. The files give a structured two-part dispute (who; in what the likeness lies) and at-Tabari states a reasoned preference. Replaced with the dispute itself.
- "Taqwa is ... placing something between yourself and what harms you": al-Muyassar says between you and sins (المعاصي), through obedience and worship. Now quoted as his.
- "The commentators add ... fasting narrows the routes of appetite": the sources say it narrows the paths/channels of Shaytan (Ibn Kathir, as-Sa'di); weakening desire is al-Qurtubi's figurative reading. Now attributed separately.
- "In the agreed-upon hadith, fasting is a shield ... say: I am fasting": only al-Bukhari 1894 was confirmed; "agreed-upon" dropped.
- 2:184/2:185 content (concessions for the sick and traveller, "Allah intends ease", Ramadan chosen as the month of revelation): future ground; reduced to one bridging line.
- Whole 2:186 section ("commentators have long noticed the placement ... the missing 'say'"): not in any 2:183 file; 2:186 has its own article. Dropped.
- Homiletic own-voice lines ("muscle", "eleven months", "fasting empties; du'a fills", "diet with rituals", "the Lawgiver built the exemptions"): dropped.
- Kept, now sourced: the kutiba link to 2:178 and 2:180 (al-Qurtubi); "whoever does not give up false speech" (Bukhari 1903 via al-Qurtubi); the shared-road comfort (Ibn Kathir, as-Sa'di, Ma'arif).
