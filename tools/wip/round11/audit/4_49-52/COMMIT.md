Assigned 4:52, widened to 4:49-52 (those who claim purity for themselves; those
given a portion of the Book who believe in jibt and taghut and call the
idolaters better guided; Allah has cursed them, and no helper). 4:54-55 ships;
4:53 left free; 4:47-48 cited for placement. New card and a 1,782-word,
9-section article.

Sources: at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi, Ibn Kathir (ar + en), as-Sa'di, al-Baghawi,
al-Muyassar, Ma'arif on 4:49-52. Slot defect: al-Qurtubi's 4:52 slot carries
his 2:88 passage (confirmed by fetch); used only as his gloss of la'n at 2:88.
Licensing early and plain in both languages: particular people of that time,
the curse on that deed and its doers then, nothing licensed against any Jew or
anyone living, no leave to curse anyone today; repeated with Ma'arif's rule
(no la'nah on a named person unless known to have died in disbelief).
Kept as differences, no position: the claim of purity as self-acquittal
(at-Tabari) vs praise of one another (Ibn Mas'ud, al-Qurtubi); jibt and taghut
as sorcery/Shaytan ('Umar), two idols (Ikrimah), idols and their spokesmen,
or two named men (Ibn Abbas, ad-Dahhak, Mujahid), with at-Tabari's inclusive
reading, Malik's, and as-Sa'di's.
Asbab: the Makkah reports (Ka'b; or a group incl. Huyayy) given as reports,
ungraded in every fetched text, with at-Tabari's own conclusion that the names
stay open. Ibn Kathir on "no helper" with 33:25.
Hadith: none attached to 4:51-52 (stated). Muslim 2142 (Barrah renamed Zaynab)
and Bukhari 2662 (cut your companion's neck) on 4:49; Tirmidhi 1977 (hasan
gharib, marked general) on cursing; Muslim 2722 du'a, marked general.
Dropped: Ibn Abbas fathers/sons discrepancy, Sa'id ibn Jubayr's conflicting
attributions, Abu Dawud 4905 (ungraded, narrator error noted), the jibt hadith
(not fetched), Ma'arif's 33:61 and wider curse rulings, generalising headings.
