Card and 8-section article on wa-arsala 'alayhim tayran ababil, four
words, the third verse of al-Fil; 105:1-2 and 105:4-5 named as context
only, the stones left to 105:4.

Sources: at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi, al-Baghawi, as-Sa'di, Ibn Kathir (ar),
Muyassar, Ma'arif. Slot check clean; Ibn Kathir en is the 104:1-105:1
group, unused. Sender: at-Tabari "rabbuka", as-Sa'di and IK "Allah".

Kept as disagreements, attributed, none chosen:
- ababil: firaq (Ibn Mas'ud, Tabari) vs scattered from every side (Ibn
  Mas'ud per Qurtubi); successive, many, troops, herds of camels;
  an-Nahhas: the glosses agree, great groups, from ibil.
- singular: none (Abu 'Ubayda, al-Farra', al-Akhfash) vs ibalah /
  ibbalah (ar-Ru'asi) vs ibbawl (al-Kisa'i) vs ibbil (al-Mubarrad).
- origin: sea ('Ikrima, 'Ubayd b. 'Umayr, Qatada, IK) vs sky (Sa'id b.
  Jubayr in Qurtubi); Ma'arif's pigeon size kept as Ma'arif's.
- form: white/black/green; Ibn 'Abbas, 'Ikrima, 'Ubayd b. 'Umayr (=
  Muhammad b. Ka'b in Qurtubi), Sa'id b. Jubayr, ar-Rabi'; swallows
  ('A'isha, IK) vs Ma'arif on the word.
All reported as Companion/Successor reports with Tabari's chains; no
gradings in the texts, none added.

Hadith: none; no fetched tafsir attaches a sound one (stated).
Dropped: Qurtubi's marfu' report via Juwaybir (named, not quoted), any
account of the event or army, Qatada's crushing clause (105:4).
Licensing sentence in both languages.
