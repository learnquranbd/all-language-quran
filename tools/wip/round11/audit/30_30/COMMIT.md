The fitrah verse: set your face to the religion as a hanif, the fitrah of
Allah upon which He created mankind — no changing of Allah's creation — that
is the upright religion, but most do not know.

Card plus an 8-section bilingual article (1,795 EN words), source-gated.

Sources (all pre-fetched, confirmed before citing):
- at-Tabari: aqim = saddid wajhaka; fitrah = sina'at Allah (accusative masdar);
  lā tabdīl = "no altering the religion of Allah, should not be done"; dīn
  qayyim = straight, no crookedness; Muʿādh to ʿUmar (ikhlās/fitrah, ṣalāh,
  ṭāʿah) as the pillars that save.
- Ibn Kathir: the hanīfiyyah/millat Ibrāhīm; created upon knowing Him (covenant
  7:172); both readings of lā tabdīl — statement-with-force-of-command ("a good,
  sound meaning") and plain statement (all equal in the fitrah); hadith qudsi
  "I created My servants inclined to truth, then the devils turned them away."
- as-Saʿdi: the face follows the heart; the fitrah = the inborn love of and
  leaning toward the truth; most never recognize the upright religion.
- al-Qurtubi: ḥanīf = inclining from all distorted/abrogated religions; three
  senses of fitrah (Islam / capacity to know the Lord / al-badāʾa, which Ahmad
  held then left); castration reading via ʿIkrimah, Ibn ʿAbbās, ʿUmar.
- Maʿarif al-Qurʾan: the two interpretations (born Muslim vs born able) and the
  four reasons it presses for "capacity" (Torapushti, Shah Waliullah).

Hadith: Sahih Muslim 2658 quoted whole (the newborn on fitrah; the jamʿāʾ/jadʿāʾ
simile) — in Ṣaḥīḥ Muslim, muttafaq ʿalayh; grading not upgraded. Bukhari 1358
fetched to confirm al-Bukhārī also records it; Muslim's wording used instead of
the Bukhari 1385 wording already shipped at 26:26 / 26:75.

Disputes kept open, no position taken: fitrah = Islam vs capacity vs al-badāʾa;
lā tabdīl as prohibition vs statement; "creation" = religion vs the animal's
body (castration). Not a sensitive verse; no licensing sentence needed.

Dropped/trimmed: children-of-the-mushrikīn fate controversy; al-Warrāq's
"fitrah = poverty"; the authority roster for "khalq = dīn" and the minor "dīn =
reckoning" reading (confirmed, cut for length — see ledger).
