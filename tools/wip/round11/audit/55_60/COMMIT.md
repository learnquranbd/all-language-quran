Replaces the 826-word, 6-section article with an 8-section, 1,774-word
source-gated article (shipped card kept).

Sources: at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi, as-Sa'di, Ibn Kathir (ar, en abridged),
al-Baghawi, Muyassar, Ma'arif on 55:60. Covers the placement between the
two pairs of gardens (55:61 refrain, 55:62 in one line each); al-Qurtubi's
four senses of hal (76:1, 7:44, 5:91, 16:35) with this verse as denial,
and the ma-glosses of al-Baghawi and Ibn Kathir; good here repaid there
(Ibn Kathir with 10:26, al-Baghawi, Muyassar, Ma'arif, Qatada, Ibn Zayd);
at-Tabari tying the ihsan to fear of the standing (55:46) and the reward
to 55:46-58; as-Sa'di's worship and benefit to servants, high gardens for
the muqarrabun. The tawhid readings ('Ikrimah, Ibn 'Abbas) against Ibn
al-Munkadir's "blessed with Islam" and al-Sadiq, kept as a difference on
whose the first ihsan is; Ibn al-Hanafiyyah and al-Hasan's "musajjalah
for righteous and sinner" kept as a difference on scope. Ibn Kathir's
point that the reward is grace, hence the refrain.

Hadith: none from the collections is attached to the verse. The Anas
report ("do you know what your Lord said?") quoted as al-Baghawi carries
it with his chain (and Ibn Kathir from him), plus al-Qurtubi's chainless
Ibn 'Abbas version; no grading in the sources, none given.

Dropped: the shipped article's unattached Jibril and "ihsan in
everything" hadiths, its unsourced root and husn etymology claims, and
its human-repayment homily (no tafsir reads the verse that way).
