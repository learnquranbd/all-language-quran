Card and 8-section bilingual article (1,752 EN words) on wa-man ya'shu 'an
dhikri r-Rahman, nuqayyid lahu shaytanan fa-huwa lahu qarin.

Sources: at-Tabari, Ibn Kathir (ar; en for 43:38), al-Qurtubi, al-Baghawi,
as-Sa'di, al-Muyassar, Ma'arif al-Qur'an. As-Sa'di's 43:36 comment sits in
the API's 43:35 entry (offset); fetched once and saved as t91_35.txt.

- ya'shu: at-Tabari's root (looking without steadiness, sight dimmed as if
  filmed over), Ibn Kathir's "dimness of insight", al-Khalil via al-Qurtubi
  and al-Baghawi; 'ashawtu ila vs 'ashawtu 'an (al-Baghawi; Abu l-Haytham
  and al-Azhari via al-Qurtubi).
- Disagreement kept: an-Nahhas and al-'Utbi reject 'asha 'an = turn away;
  al-Qurtubi sides with Abu l-Haytham and al-Azhari.
- Reading ya'sha (Ibn 'Abbas, 'Ikrima per al-Qurtubi; Ibn 'Abbas per
  al-Baghawi) = becomes blind; at-Tabari ties Ibn Zayd's gloss to it.
  Al-Jawhari's night-blindness and khabt 'ashwa' notes reported as lexicon.
- Reading yuqayyid with ya' as each tafsir names its readers.
- Dhikr: the Qur'an (Muyassar, as-Sa'di, al-Qurazi, Ma'arif) vs general
  remembrance of Allah (at-Tabari, Ibn Kathir); al-Qurtubi links to 43:5.
- Nuqayyid as recompense in the commentators' wording; Ibn Kathir's
  parallels 4:115, 61:5, 41:25. No own theology; points to the 41:25
  article for assignment vs responsibility.
- Qarin: here / Hereafter / both (al-Qurtubi, al-Qushayri); pronoun
  direction (at-Tabari); human or jinn and Bayan ul-Qur'an (Ma'arif).
- 43:37-38 context: as-Sa'di's "no excuse"; Ibn Kathir on the complaint.
- No hadith: none attached by any fetched tafsir; al-Qurtubi's unsourced
  khabar via al-Mahdawi dropped; Muslim 2814 not cited, not used.
- Non-licence statement in both languages.
