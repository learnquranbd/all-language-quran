Replaces the 800-word, 6-section article with a 1,774-word, 8-section
one, sourced from at-Tabari, Ibn Kathir, al-Qurtubi, as-Sa'di, al-Baghawi,
al-Muyassar and Ma'arif on 2:2. The shipped card is kept.

dhalika: the near reading (at-Tabari's majority with his "just finished"
reason, Ibn Kathir's list, Ma'mar in al-Bukhari) beside the far one
(al-Qurtubi's ten opinions, al-Farra' via al-Baghawi, Ma'arif's link to
1:6). Torah/Injil reading reported by at-Tabari, rejected by Ibn Kathir.
la rayba: three senses of rayb (al-Qurtubi); statement vs prohibition in
meaning (Ibn Kathir, al-Baghawi with 2:197, al-Qurtubi); as-Sa'di's rule
that praise by negation implies certainty. Pause: Ibn Kathir prefers
stopping after fih; al-Qurtubi's parse fits the other division.
muttaqin vs 2:185: as-Sa'di's two guidances, al-Qurtubi's dalala/tawfiq
and honour, at-Tabari's sharper "would not have singled them out",
Ma'arif's degrees. Taqwa glosses via at-Tabari; the thorny-path report
has Ubayy for al-Qurtubi and Ibn Kathir, Ka'b al-Ahbar for al-Baghawi.
Hadith: Tirmidhi 2451 (Atiyya as-Sa'di), graded hasan gharib by
at-Tirmidhi. No asbab.

Dropped from the old article as unsourced: far demonstrative as
"elevation", the embracing-pause rule, the sun-and-shutters analogy given
to as-Sa'di, 79:45, 47:17 and 3:8. Its gloss of rayb as "unease, something
hidden" is replaced by al-Qurtubi's doubt-and-fear wording.
