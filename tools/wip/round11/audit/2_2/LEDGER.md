## Disagreements kept (named, no position taken)
- dhalika: near meaning (Tabari's majority, Ibn Kathir's list, Ma'mar) vs far meaning (al-Qurtubi's ten opinions, al-Farra' via al-Baghawi, Ma'arif's Fatiha link).
- Torah/Injil reading: at-Tabari reports it with only a grammatical remark; Ibn Kathir rejects it outright.
- la rayba: statement (all) vs report-meaning-prohibition (reported anonymously by Ibn Kathir, al-Baghawi, al-Qurtubi; none argues against it).
- Pause: Ibn Kathir prefers stopping after fih (reasons given); at-Tabari's reading matches; al-Qurtubi's parse (fihi = predicate of hudan) fits the other division. Al-Qurtubi does not discuss the pause itself, and the article says only that his grammar fits it.
- Guidance for the muttaqin: as-Sa'di/al-Qurtubi (guidance for all creation in itself; honour) vs at-Tabari (if it were light for others He would not have singled them out); Ma'arif's degrees.
- Thorny-path report: the one asked is Ubayy (al-Qurtubi; Ibn Kathir with وقد قيل) vs Ka'b al-Ahbar (al-Baghawi). Wording quoted is al-Qurtubi's; Ibn Kathir has شمرت واجتهدت. No chain in any of the three; no grading given or invented.
