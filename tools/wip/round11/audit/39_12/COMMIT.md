Card and 8-section bilingual article for 39:12: wa-umirtu li-an akuna
awwala al-muslimin, read as the second half of the declaration begun in
39:11 (sincere worship), not as a new argument.

Sources: Tabari, Qurtubi, Sa'di, Baghawi, Ibn Kathir (ar + en abridged),
Muyassar; quranx 39.12 for Tanwir al-Miqbas, Jalalayn, Maududi.
- Tabari: first of you to submit; islam as tawhid, sincere worship and
  disowning every other god (Muyassar repeats the three clauses).
- Qurtubi: of this Ummah, and so it was (left his forefathers' religion,
  cast off and broke the idols); the lam as extra (al-Jurjani) or of
  purpose with an elided "worship".
- Baghawi, Jalalayn, as-Suddi (in Ibn Kathir), Muyassar: of this Ummah;
  Tanwir: first of those who accept Islam, unqualified.
- Sa'di: the caller must be first to obey; islam in outward deeds, ikhlas
  in outward and inward, binding on whoever claims to follow him.
- Maududi: practise first what you preach.
Disagreements kept, no position: the lam (zaida vs. purpose); firstness
as order in time (Qurtubi, Tabari) vs. precedence of example (Sa'di,
Maududi). No fetched text reads awwal as rank; not attributed.
Cross-refs: 6:14 (same command without the lam), 6:163, 27:91, 10:72
(Nuh), 2:131 (Ibrahim); no reason for "of this Ummah" put in the
commentators' mouths.
Hadith: none attached by any fetched source; said in one sentence.
Asbab: none. Ma'arif's grouped text covers only 39:10; not used.
Not pulled forward: 39:13-16. No condemned-group address, no licensing
sentence needed. Themes: tawheed, character.
