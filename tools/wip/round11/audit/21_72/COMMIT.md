Card and deep bilingual article on the gift of Ishaq and Yaqub as
nafila — the extra grace beyond what was asked — coming after the fire,
the foiled plot and the deliverance of 21:69-71, and "all of them We
made righteous."

Angle held distinct from the already-shipped 19:49: here the frame is
the surplus (nafila) and "made righteous" (salihin), not prophethood.

Sources (all pre-fetched, none re-fetched): at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi,
as-Sa'di, Ibn Kathir (ar + en abridged), al-Baghawi, al-Muyassar,
Ma'arif al-Qur'an.

Disagreement kept open, not resolved:
- nafila = Yaqub the grandson / walad al-walad (Ibn Abbas, Qatada,
  al-Hakam b. Uyayna; al-Baghawi adds Ubayy b. Kab, Abu Zayd);
- nafila = the gift itself, both sons (Ata, Mujahid = atiyya;
  al-Hasan, ad-Dahhak = fadl).
at-Tabari refuses to choose: no burhan decides, "say what Allah said."

The prayer of 37:100 exceeded (Abd ar-Rahman b. Zayd; al-Qurtubi):
one child asked, Ishaq given, Yaqub added. 11:71 cross-cited by Ibn
Kathir and as-Sa'di. "Made righteous" = obedience and avoiding the
forbidden (at-Tabari), Allah's and His servants' rights (as-Sa'di);
al-Qurtubi's note that the righteousness itself is Allah's making.

No hadith: none of the tafsir attaches a sound verse-specific hadith
to 21:72; said plainly in one sentence. No asbab (placement used).
Dropped: as-Sa'di's extension to Ismail and the Prophet (SAW), as
beyond this verse's named descendants.
