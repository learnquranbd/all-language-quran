Card plus 8-section bilingual article on 20:42, "Go, you and your
brother, with My signs, and do not slacken in My remembrance."

Sources (all pre-fetched, confirmed on the verse before citing):
- Signs (bi-āyātī): Ibn Kathir (arguments/proofs/miracles), at-Tabari
  (evidences/arguments), al-Muyassar (attest divinity, power, truth of
  the message); the nine signs per Ibn ʿAbbās via al-Qurtubi and
  al-Baghawi; hand and staff among them per as-Sa'di. The definite
  article stressed: they are Allah's signs, not the messenger's.
- "do not slacken" (taniyā): glosses lā tubṭi'ā / lā taḍʿufā /
  lā tafturā / lā tuqaṣṣirā (Ibn ʿAbbās, Mujāhid, as-Suddī, Muḥammad
  b. Kaʿb via Ibn Kathir and al-Baghawi); al-Qurtubi's philology of
  wanā with al-ʿAjjāj and Ṭarafa, and Ibn Masʿūd's variant
  "lā tahinā fī dhikrī" widening dhikr to praise, glorification and
  conveying the message.
- Remembrance as strength: Ibn Kathir — dhikr as ʿawn/quwwa and an
  authority that breaks Pharaoh, remembered in the confrontation
  itself. as-Sa'di — mudāwama, tied to the brothers' own promise
  (20:33-34), remembrance easing every burden.

Kept distinct from 20:14 (just shipped): §4 contrasts prayer-for-
remembrance there with steadfast remembrance-in-mission here.

Hadith: none. Ibn Kathir's "munājiz qirnahu" narration is ungraded and
unconfirmable here, so it is dropped and the article says so in one
sentence. No asbab (used the verse's placement — the answered request
for Hārūn). Future ground (20:43-44, gentle speech to Pharaoh) left
untouched. Not a condemned-group verse; no licensing sentence.
Themes: remembrance, patience.
