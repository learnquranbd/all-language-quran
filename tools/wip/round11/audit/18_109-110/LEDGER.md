## Hadith
- Sahih Muslim 2985, https://quranx.com/hadith/Muslim/Hadith-2985 (saved hadith_Muslim_2985.txt). Attached by Ma'arif ("Narrated by Muslim"); al-Baghawi gives a close variant with his own chain from Abu Hurayrah. Wording used: قال الله تبارك وتعالى أنا أغنى الشركاء عن الشرك من عمل عملا أشرك فيه معي غيري تركته وشركه. First ten EN words: "Allah, Blessed and Exalted, said: I am the most free". Grading: in Sahih Muslim; no separate grading.
- Sahih al-Bukhari 6499 (Dar-us-Salam), https://quranx.com/hadith/Bukhari/DarusSalam/Hadith-6499. Attached by al-Baghawi with chain Abu Nu'aym - Sufyan - Salama b. Kuhayl - Jundub, the same as Bukhari's second chain. Wording: من سمع سمع الله به، ومن يرائي يرائي الله به. First ten EN words: "whoever seeks a hearing, Allah will make him heard; whoever". In Sahih al-Bukhari.
- Sahih Muslim 2642, https://quranx.com/hadith/Muslim/Hadith-2642. Attached by Ma'arif (Abu Dharr, "Sahih of Muslim"). Wording: أرأيت الرجل يعمل العمل من الخير ويحمده الناس عليه قال تلك عاجل بشرى المؤمن. First ten EN words: "it was said to the Messenger of Allah ﷺ, what". In Sahih Muslim.
- Ahmad, Mahmud b. Labid (lesser shirk = riya'): cited by Ibn Kathir (t14_110, t169_110) and Ma'arif from the Musnad; Ahmad not reachable, so given only as their citation, not quoted as confirmed.
