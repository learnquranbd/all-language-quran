Card and 8-section bilingual article for 43:24: awa-law ji'tukum bi-ahda
and inna bima ursiltum bihi kafirun. 43:23 used as the frame; 43:22 (the
forefathers' claim, drafted separately) referred to in a line; 43:25
(retribution) context only.

Sources: Tabari, Qurtubi, Sa'di, Ibn Kathir (ar; en abridged), Baghawi,
Muyassar. Ma'arif's group text covers 43:18 only; not named.
- Readings: Baghawi names Ibn 'Amir and Hafs for qala (report), the rest
  qul (command); Abu Ja'far ji'nakum, the rest ji'tukum. Tabari's note on
  ji'tukum / ji'nakum and his preference; Qurtubi's four forms, unnamed.
  Mushaf text qala confirmed.
- Kept as a difference: speaker is every messenger (Sa'di) vs the Prophet
  commanded to say it (Tabari, Ibn Kathir, Qurtubi, Baghawi via
  az-Zajjaj) vs both (Muyassar).
- Ahda: Tabari, Muyassar, Qurtubi (arshad), Baghawi (dinin aswab), Sa'di.
  No claim made about what the comparative concedes; none of the sources
  addresses it, and the article says so.
- Refusal: Tabari jahidun munkirun; Qurtubi on the plural ursiltum and
  his paraphrase of their firmness; Muyassar 'inad; Ibn Kathir on intent
  and arrogance; Sa'di on following hawa.
- Same refusal at 34:34, 41:14, 14:9; awa-law at 2:170, 5:104, 31:21;
  surah sequence 43:26, 43:29-30 (checked in quran-json).
- Licensing sentence in both languages; no living group named.
Hadith: none; no fetched tafsir attaches one to 43:24 (stated in text).
Asbab: none.
Dropped: any concession claim on ahda; a reading-to-speaker rule;
Qurtubi's al-Baqara taqlid discussion (not fetched).
