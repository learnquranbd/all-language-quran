## Watch-points
- qala / qul, ji'tukum / ji'nakum: reported only as named. Baghawi: Ibn 'Amir and Hafs qala (report), the others qul (command); Abu Ja'far ji'nakum, others ji'tukum. Tabari: readers of the cities except Abu Ja'far ji'tukum, Abu Ja'far ji'nakum; prefers ji'tukum (ijma' al-hujja); both cited with qul. Qurtubi: four forms, no readers named (article says so). Mushaf text qala confirmed from PREP.
- Speaker, kept as a difference with no position: each messenger / warner of 43:23 (Sa'di); the Prophet ﷺ commanded (Tabari, Ibn Kathir ar+en, Qurtubi, Baghawi via az-Zajjaj); both (Muyassar). Tabari's "answered as earlier nations answered their prophets" reported as his.
- bi-ahda: glosses reported (Tabari, Muyassar, Qurtubi arshad, Baghawi aswab, Sa'di li-ajl al-huda). No fetched tafsir says the comparative does or does not concede guidance to the fathers' way; the article states that none stops on it and makes no claim either way.
- Licensing sentence present in both languages (§8.1); no living group named.
