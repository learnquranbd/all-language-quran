## Word meanings (watch-point: from fetched tafsir only)
- farid: at-Tabari (aged, worn out; faradat = grew old; poet's old grudge); al-Baghawi (aged, no longer calves).
- bikr: at-Tabari (female not yet covered; here young, not calved).
- 'awan: at-Tabari (middle-aged, calved once and again; harb 'awan); Ibn Kathir via ad-Dahhak from Ibn Abbas (half-way, strongest and finest).
- safra' faqi': at-Tabari (pure colour; fuqu'/nusu'); Ibn Kathir via al-Awfi from Ibn Abbas (intensely yellow, almost white).
- la shiyata fiha: at-Tabari (no colour unlike her hide's colour). His washy/washi etymology cut for length.

## "Any cow would have sufficed" (watch-point)
Used only as given: Ibn Abbas (via Sa'id ibn Jubayr), a Companion's statement, chain graded
sound by Ibn Kathir, with his list of others who said the same; at-Tabari's own reasoning on
2:70; al-Baghawi in his own voice. The marfu' versions are NOT presented as the Prophet's words:
Abu Hurayrah's via Ibn Abi Hatim (Ibn Kathir: gharib, at best Abu Hurayrah's words);
Ibn Jurayj's and Qatadah's in at-Tabari (footnote: mursal); al-Baghawi's (no chain). The article
says no tafsir here attaches a sound hadith to these words.
