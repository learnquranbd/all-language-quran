Card and 8-section bilingual article for 2:67-71: the cow, and the
questions that turned "slaughter a cow" into one cow no herd carried.
Target 2:68 widened to the questioning sequence 2:67-71; 2:72-73 used
as context only; 2:65-66 and 2:74 (shipped) untouched.

Sources: Tabari, Qurtubi, Ibn Kathir (ar + en abridged), Sa'di, Baghawi,
Muyassar, Ma'arif on 2:67 to 2:71. Slot check: all slots on target.
- farid, bikr, 'awan, safra' faqi', la dhalul, la shiyata fiha from
  Tabari, Baghawi, Sa'di and Ibn Kathir only.
- Tabari's narrowing: any cow, then any cow of that age, then colour;
  the narrowing itself the penalty.
- "Had they taken the most ordinary cow": Ibn Abbas via Sa'id ibn Jubayr,
  chain sound per Ibn Kathir; given as a Companion's words. The marfu'
  versions graded as given: Abu Hurayrah's gharib, at best his own words
  (Ibn Kathir); Ibn Jurayj's and Qatadah's mursal (Tabari's footnote);
  Baghawi's without a chain. No sound hadith attached to the verse.
Disagreements kept: musallama (Mujahid vs Ibn Abbas, Qatadah, Abu
al-Aliyah); safra' as black (al-Hasan) vs Tabari and Ibn Kathir; al-ana
ji'ta bil-haqq (Qatadah vs Ibn Zayd); wa ma kadu yaf'alun (Tabari: cost
and fear of exposure; Ibn Kathir: did not want to); a command due at once
(most jurists vs Ibn Khuwayz Mandad, via Qurtubi).
Hadith quoted whole: Bukhari 7288, placed under 2:70 by Tabari.
Licensing sentence in both languages; application turned to our own
questions to scholars (Qurtubi on 2:71, 16:43) and 2:108.
Dropped: the owner, orphan, angel and price stories (one sentence with
Ibn Kathir's verdict on isra'iliyyat), yellow sandals, the Torah's red
cow, salam in livestock, Ma'arif's unsourced "the Hadith says".
