# SOURCES ledger — 26:128 (أَتَبْنُونَ بِكُلِّ رِيعٍ ءَايَةً تَعْبَثُونَ)

## Tafsir fetched (from PREP.md, not re-fetched)
All read from tools/wip/round11/audit/26_128/.

- **at-Tabari (15)** — USED. رِيع glossed as "كل مكان مشرف من الأرض مرتفع, أو طريق أو واد" with two vowellings (ريع/رَيع); Ibn ʿAbbas "شرف"; Mujahid "فج" / "الثنية الصغيرة" / "شرف ومنظر" / "بين جبلين"; Qatadah + ad-Dahhak "بكل طريق"; Ibn ʿAbbas (2nd chain) "بكل طريق"; ʿIkrimah "فج وواد". آية = "بنيانا, علما" / Ibn ʿAbbas "علم" / Mujahid "بنيان" and "بنيان الحمام". تعبثون = "تلعبون" (Ibn ʿAbbas, ad-Dahhak).
- **al-Qurtubi (90)** — USED. رِيع = elevated ground (Ibn ʿAbbas & others); Qatadah/ad-Dahhak/al-Kalbi/Muqatil/as-Suddi "الطريق"; ʿUmarah "الجبل"; Mujahid "الفج بين الجبلين" / "الثنية الصغيرة" / "المنظرة"; Ibn al-Aʿrabi "الصومعة/البرج/التل العالي". آية = علامة. تعبثون = تلعبون, OR "تعبثون بمن يمر في الطريق" (mock passers-by from the heights); al-Kalbi via al-Mawardi: "عبث العشارين بأموال من يمر بهم" (tax-takers). Dovecote reading via Mujahid. Navigation reading: ʿIkrimah + Muqatil "كانوا يهتدون بالنجوم إذا سافروا فبنوا على الطريق أمثالا طوالا ليهتدوا بها".
- **as-Saʿdi (91)** — USED. رِيع = "مدخل بين الجبال"; آية = علامة; تعبثون = "عبثا لغير فائدة تعود بمصالح دينكم ودنياكم".
- **al-Baghawi (94)** — USED. Ibn ʿAbbas (al-Walibi) "بكل شرف"; ad-Dahhak/Muqatil/al-Kalbi "بكل طريق" (al-ʿAwfi from Ibn ʿAbbas too); Mujahid "الفج بين الجبلين" / "المنظرة"; Abu ʿUbaydah "المكان المرتفع". آية = علامة. تعبثون = mock passers-by; Saʿid b. Jubayr + Mujahid: dovecote towers (بروج الحمام), "تعبثون = تلعبون, وهم كانوا يلعبون بالحمام".
- **al-Muyassar (16)** — USED. "بكل مكان مرتفع بناء عاليًا تشرفون منه فتسخرون من المارة... وذلك عبث وإسراف لا يعود عليكم بفائدة في الدين أو الدنيا".
- **Ma'arif al-Qur'an (168, en)** — USED. Mujahid (via Ibn Jarir): passage between two hills; Ibn ʿAbbas + majority: a high place. آية literal = sign/symbol, here = a high palace. تعبثون/ʿabath = of no value or benefit; built high palaces of no benefit, for ego/pride. masaniʿ (26:129): Qatadah = water-tank, Mujahid = strong palace. al-Bukhari: laʿalla used as simile; Ibn ʿAbbas rendered it كأنكم تخلدون. "Construction without need is contemptible"; cites building-has-no-merit narrations (Anas).
- **Ibn Kathir (14, ar) & (169, en abridged)** — USED. رِيع (in sum) = "المكان المرتفع عند جواد الطرق المشهورة"; آية = "معلما بناء مشهورا" (huge/dazzling/sturdy landmark); تعبثون = "عبثا لا للاحتياج... لمجرد اللعب واللهو وإظهار القوة"; the Prophet denounced it as waste of time and bodies, "لا يجدي في الدنيا ولا في الآخرة". (169 confirms ʿĀd destroyed, lived after Nūh, physically strong/violent.)

Disagreement kept as disagreement (no position taken): رِيع = elevated ground/hill (Ibn ʿAbbas, majority, Abu ʿUbaydah) vs. road (Qatadah, ad-Dahhak, al-Kalbi, Muqatil, as-Suddi) vs. mountain pass between two peaks (Mujahid, as-Saʿdi). an-Nahhas: the word genuinely covers both in Arabic. Also the three readings of the "amusement": mocking travellers; dovecote towers; navigation markers.

## Hadith
- **Jami' at-Tirmidhi 2483** — USED. URL fetched via hadith.js: https://quranx.com/hadith/Tirmidhi/Hadith-2483
  First ten words (EN of the operative saying): "A man is rewarded for all that he spends..." (full report via Harithah b. Mudarrib visiting Khabbab; " ... except for the dust — or he said — in the building").
  Arabic operative clause: "يُؤْجَرُ الرَّجُلُ فِي نَفَقَتِهِ كُلِّهَا إِلاَّ التُّرَابَ أَوْ قَالَ فِي الْبِنَاءِ".
  Grading: Abu ʿIsa (at-Tirmidhi) said "هَذَا حَدِيثٌ حَسَنٌ صَحِيحٌ" — reported as hasan sahih, not upgraded.
  Attachment: Ma'arif al-Qur'an adduces building-beyond-need narrations (via Anas) for this verse; the confirmable sound wording of that meaning is Tirmidhi 2483, quoted here from one collection, not hybridised. Framed narrowly (surplus beyond need, not shelter).
  Not a stock hadith used nearby (Bukhari 660 / Bukhari 3443 both avoided per orchestrator note).

## Asbāb an-nuzūl
- None. No tafsir fetched gives a sabab. The verse's placement was used instead: part of Hūd's address to ʿĀd, following the denial and his credentials (26:123–127) and preceding the palaces/tyranny (26:129–130).
