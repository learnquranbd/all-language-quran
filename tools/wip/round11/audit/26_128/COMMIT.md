Hūd's five-word question to ʿĀd: do you build on every height a
monument, idly? Card plus deep bilingual article, source-gated.

Sources gave three live senses of رِيع, kept as a disagreement with
no position: elevated ground/hill (Ibn ʿAbbas, majority, Abu ʿUbaydah),
a road (Qatadah, ad-Dahhak, al-Kalbi, Muqatil, as-Suddi), and a mountain
pass between two peaks (Mujahid, as-Saʿdi); an-Nahhas notes the word
genuinely carries both. آية read as a conspicuous landmark/marker
(at-Tabari, Ibn ʿAbbas, Mujahid; Ma'arif: here a high palace; Ibn Kathir:
a dazzling landmark at a crossroads). تعبثون = in vanity/play (Ibn
ʿAbbas, ad-Dahhak; as-Saʿdi, Ma'arif, Ibn Kathir).

Also kept the three readings of the "amusement": mocking travellers from
the heights (al-Qurtubi, al-Baghawi, al-Muyassar; al-Kalbi's tax-taker
variant via al-Mawardi), dovecote towers (Mujahid; Saʿid b. Jubayr), and
navigation markers (ʿIkrimah, Muqatil).

Article frames the rebuke as against ostentation and the craving for
permanence, NOT architecture — anchored in Ibn Kathir and Ma'arif, with a
light touch on 26:129 (as-if-immortal) and 26:134 (gardens named as
blessing). Carries the "licenses nothing against any living person or
community" clause in both languages, extended explicitly to builders.

Hadith: Tirmidhi 2483 (Harithah b. Mudarrib ← Khabbab), "a man is
rewarded for all his spending except... the building"; Abu ʿIsa grades it
hasan sahih (not upgraded). Attached via Ma'arif's building-beyond-need
narrations; framed narrowly to surplus, not shelter.

Dropped: the loose Anas wordings (collection/grading not fetched) in
favour of the confirmable Tirmidhi one; Ibn al-Aʿrabi's "hermitage" and
ʿUmarah's "mountain" senses of رِيع for space. No sabab established;
placement in the Hūd–ʿĀd address used instead. 26:130 and 26:133 left
untouched (26:133 is the next target).
