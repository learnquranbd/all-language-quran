Card and 8-section bilingual article for 90:13: fakku raqabatin, two
words, the first answer to 90:12's question about the steep pass.
90:11-12 context only; 90:14-16 named as the verses that follow.

Sources: Tabari, Qurtubi, Baghawi, Sa'di, Ibn Kathir (ar + en
abridged), Muyassar, Ma'arif. Slot check: Tabari's gloss sits in the
90:12 slot and Baghawi's 90:13 text in the 90:14 slot; both fetched.
- fakk/raqaba: untying the fetter, the enslaved as a captive bound by
  the neck (Qurtubi); from captivity, "it was said" from slavery
  (Qurtubi); manumission or help with the kitaba, a fortiori the Muslim
  captive (Sa'di); from riqq and asr al-'ubuda (Tabari); i'taq and
  itlaq (Baghawi); believing neck (Muyassar). Each attributed.
- Readings: fakka raqabatan aw at'ama vs fakku raqabatin aw it'amun,
  readers as Tabari and Baghawi list them; Tabari: both correct, verb
  reading better in Arabic, 101:10-11 comparison; IK: meanings close.
- Ranking kept as disagreement: Qatada "most costly" (Tabari); Asbagh
  vs Ibn al-'Arabi (Qurtubi); Abu Hanifa vs his two companions, Sha'bi.
- Inward reading kept beside the outward: Mawardi (via Qurtubi), 'Ikrima
  (via Baghawi). None chosen.
Hadith: Bukhari 2517 (Abu Hurayra, limb for limb, 'Ali ibn al-Husayn)
quoted whole; Bukhari 2518 pointed to by number, not quoted (long).
Al-Bara', 'Amr ibn 'Abasa, Wathila, 'Uqba: named, not quoted (Ahmad,
no collection page). Asbab: none.
Framed as release from bondage; licensing sentence in both languages.
Shipped 9:60 and 90:4 cross-referenced by number.
Dropped: 2:177, 4:92, 58:3, 5:89 (not cited), Hassan's verse.
Fix: dropped Bukhari 2518 pointer (no fetched tafsir cites it) and the 'Ibn Kathir the reader' aside (general knowledge).
