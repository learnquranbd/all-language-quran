Card and 8-section bilingual article for 50:29: ma yubaddalu l-qawlu
ladayya wa ma ana bi-zallamin li-l-'abid. 50:23-28 (the record, the
command, the companion's disowning, "do not dispute before Me") used as
context; 50:30 not developed.

Sources: Tabari, Ibn Kathir (ar; en for context), Muyassar, Qurtubi,
Sa'di, Baghawi, Ma'arif.
- "The word" kept as a disagreement, none picked: Mujahid's "I have
  decreed what I decree" (Tabari, IK, Ma'arif); la-amla'anna jahannam
  (Tabari, Baghawi with 32:13, Qurtubi qila); 6:160 (Qurtubi qila);
  "no lie is told before Me" (al-Farra' via Qurtubi; al-Kalbi and
  al-Farra' via Baghawi, with the al-qawl / qawli argument); Sa'di's
  "what Allah said cannot fail".
- Not unjust: no one punished for another's sin (Tabari, IK, Muyassar),
  only after the proof is established (IK, Muyassar, Ma'arif); not
  punishing one with no crime (Ibn Abbas via Qurtubi; Baghawi); nothing
  added or taken (Sa'di). Why zallam: not in the sources, said so.
- 14:22 via IK for the blame-shifting; licensing paragraph in both
  languages for the disputers.
Hadith: none attached to 50:29 by a fetched tafsir; said so. IK's Ahmad
narration (on 50:24-26) left aside; Muslim 2577 not attached, dropped.
No asbab. Dropped: 4:40, 41:46, 10:44, 11:119, 2:40 (not cited);
Qurtubi's back-reference (not found in 22:10 / 3:182 fetches).
