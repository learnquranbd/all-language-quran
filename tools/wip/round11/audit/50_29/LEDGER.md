# SOURCES ledger — 50:29

Tafsir fetched (all from PREP, local files in this folder): 14 Ibn Kathir (ar), 15 at-Tabari,
16 Muyassar, 90 al-Qurtubi, 91 as-Sa'di, 94 al-Baghawi, 168 Ma'arif (en, grouped 50:29-31),

Extra fetches (one each, both negative): al-Qurtubi on 22:10 (t90_22_10.txt) and on 3:182

وغيرها"). Neither contains a gloss of ظلام; nothing from them is used.

## Attributions and the clause each rests on

"the word" (ma yubaddalu l-qawlu ladayya), kept as a disagreement, none picked:
- Mujahid, decree already made: Tabari (two isnads, Ibn Abi Najih and al-Qasim ibn Abi Bazza)
  "قد قضيت ما أنا قاض"; Ibn Kathir ar "قال مجاهد : يعني قد قضيت ما أنا قاض"; IK en "I have made
  My decision, according to the explanation of Mujahid".
- Ma'arif: "I have made my decision which will certainly be implemented: it will never be changed."
- Tabari's own: "ما يغير القول الذي قلته لكم فى الدنيا, وهو قوله لأملأن جهنم من الجنة والناس أجمعين
  ولا قضائي الذي قضيته فيهم فيها". Addressees: "مخبرا عن قيله للمشركين وقرنائهم من الجنّ يوم القيامة,
  إذ تبرأ بعضهم من بعض".
- Baghawi: "لا تبديل لقولي ، وهو قوله : لأملأن جهنم ... ( السجدة - 13 )" → 32:13 verified locally
  (data/quran-json/32.json: حَقَّ ٱلْقَوْلُ مِنِّى لَأَمْلَأَنَّ جَهَنَّمَ ...).
- Qurtubi, both under qila: "قيل هو قوله : من جاء بالحسنة فله عشر أمثالها ..." → 6:160 verified
  locally; "وقيل هو قوله : لأملأن جهنم ...".
- al-Farra' via Qurtubi: "ما يكذب عندي أي : ما يزاد في القول ولا ينقص لعلمي بالغيب".
- Baghawi: "وقال قوم : ... لا يكذب عندي ، ولا يغير القول عن وجهه لأني أعلم الغيب . وهذا قول الكلبي ،
  واختيار الفراء ، لأنه قال : ما يبدل القول لدي ولم يقل ما يبدل قولي".
- Sa'di: "لا يمكن أن يخلف ما قاله الله وأخبر به، لأنه لا أصدق من الله قيلاً، ولا أصدق حديثًا".

"wa ma ana bi-zallamin li-l-'abid":
- Tabari: "ولا أنا بمعاقب أحدا من خلقي بجرم غيره, ولا حامل على أحد منهم ذنب غيره فمعذّبه به".
- Ibn Kathir ar / Muyassar: "لست أعذب أحدا بذنب أحد ، ولكن لا أعذب أحدا إلا بذنبه بعد قيام الحجة عليه"
  (Muyassar near-identical); IK en and Ma'arif same in English; Ma'arif adds "absolutely fair
  and just decision".
- Qurtubi: "ما أنا بمعذب من لم يجرم ; قاله ابن عباس".
- Baghawi: "فأعاقبهم بغير جرم".
- Sa'di: "بل أجزيهم بما عملوا من خير وشر، فلا يزاد في سيئاتهم، ولا ينقص من حسناتهم".
- Why the intensive form zallam: NOT in any fetched text. The article notes only that the word
  is zallam not zalim and that these commentaries do not take up the question. No answer
  supplied, no kalam on qadar/justice.

Context (IK en, t169): companion of 50:27 = the devil assigned to every man "according to
Abdullah bin Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah and several others"; the man's line "This devil has misguided
me away from the Remembrance after it came to me"; "he himself was misguided, accepting falsehood
and stubborn to the truth"; 50:28 = "sufficient proof by the words of the Messengers ... Divine
Books ... evidences, signs and proofs"; 50:23 record "prepared and completed without addition or
deletion"; 14:22 quoted by IK (verified locally).

Cross-refs used: 32:13 (Baghawi), 6:160 (Qurtubi), 14:22 (IK en). Context verses 50:23-50:28.
Not used (no fetched tafsir cites them): 4:40, 41:46, 10:44, 11:119, 2:40. 11:119 carries the same
la-amla'anna sentence but Baghawi labels it as-Sajdah 13, so only 32:13 is cited.
50:30 not developed (future ground).

## Hadith
None used. No fetched tafsir attaches a hadith to 50:29. IK en (t169) cites Imam Ahmad, Abu Sa'id
al-Khudri, "A neck from the Fire will appear and will speak..." on 50:24-26, not on 50:29, and in
Musnad Ahmad (outside hadith.js collections); mentioned in the article only as not attached and
left aside. The hadith qudsi "O My servants, I have forbidden injustice for Myself" (Muslim 2577)
is not attached by any fetched tafsir: dropped, not mentioned.

## Asbab an-nuzul
None reported in any fetched text; article says so. Not looked up elsewhere.

## Licensing
§7 para 1, both languages: the verse describes a Day-of-Resurrection hearing (condemned of
50:24-26 and their companions) and licenses nothing against any living person or community.
