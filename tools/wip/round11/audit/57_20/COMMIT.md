Replaces the 941-word, 6-section article with a 1,747-word, 8-section deep article, built only on tafsir fetched for 57:20 (shipped card kept).

Sources: at-Tabari, Ibn Kathir (ar; en abridged as cross-check), al-Qurtubi,
as-Sa'di, al-Baghawi, al-Muyassar, Ma'arif al-Qur'an, all fetched for 57:20.

The five words glossed by name: bodies play, hearts diverted (Muyassar,
as-Sa'di); Qatada, Mujahid and two unnamed views via al-Qurtubi; zinah, tafakhur
and takathur from at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi and as-Sa'di. Life stages kept as
readings: Ma'arif's sequence, al-Qurtubi's later-scholars comparison, Ibn
Kathir's stages applied to the parable (with 30:54); the verse numbers none.

Al-kuffar kept as a disagreement: cultivators (al-Baghawi, Muyassar, al-Qurtubi)
vs disbelievers (as-Sa'di; al-Qurtubi calls it good); Ibn Kathir joins them.
Licensing sentence in both languages. Pause at shadid: al-Qurtubi vs al-Farra'.
Two outcomes from at-Tabari (Qatada), al-Baghawi (Muqatil), Ibn Kathir, as-Sa'di;
one punishment against two mercies only as Ma'arif comments it.

Hadith: Muslim 2865 (humility sentence inside a long sermon, attached by
al-Qurtubi); Bukhari 6488 (Paradise nearer than the sandal strap, attached by
Ibn Kathir). Whip-space hadith mentioned with a pointer to 3:185. Asbab: none.

Dropped: the 'Ali/'Ammar report, Muslim's four Jahiliyyah matters, Ma'arif's
sports traditions, a garbled Sa'id ibn Jubayr line, Ibn 'Abbas (not in the
sources). Fixed from the old article: "annama restrictive" (the sources call the
ma a connective), kuffar given as farmers only, ridwan "greater than the garden"
(unsourced), the spending-argument placement and the unsourced practical tips.
