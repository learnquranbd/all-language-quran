Card and 9-section bilingual article for 73:20, the surah's 78-word
closing verse. Centre is the lightening (recite what is easy); the
night-prayer lesson of 73:1-6 and 73:8 is not repeated.

Sources: Tabari, Qurtubi, Ibn Kathir (ar; en grouped 73:19-20), Sa'di,
Baghawi, Muyassar, Ma'arif. All named except 169 (cross-check only).
- nisfahu/thuluthahu: genitive = Medina and Basra (Tabari), general
  readers, chosen by Abu 'Ubayd and Abu Hatim (Qurtubi); accusative =
  some Meccans and Kufans (Tabari), Mecca and Kufa (Baghawi), Ibn Kathir
  the reader and the Kufans, al-Farra' "closer to correct" (Qurtubi).
  Tabari: both sound. No reading set aside.
- lan tuhsuhu: not knowing the measure (Qurtubi, preferred; Sa'di;
  Muqatil) vs not bearing it (Tabari, al-Hasan, Sa'id, Sufyan).
- Recite = prayer (Ibn Kathir; Ibn al-'Arabi) vs recitation itself
  (Qurtubi's preference); Tabari joins both. Amounts kept as reports;
  al-Hasan's "fifty" (Tabari) vs "five" (Ibn Kathir) left open.
- Fiqh: Hanafi one verse vs Malik/Shafi'i al-Fatiha, as Ibn Kathir and
  Qurtubi report; article rules on none and refers readers to their school.
- Abrogation timing kept as three views: a year (Qatada; 'A'isha), a
  year or eight months (Ma'arif), Medinan date (view in Qurtubi, Ibn
  'Abbas). Ibn Kathir's Meccan reading of the fighters clause kept too.
- Prophet's obligation: Qushayri's "well-known" (stayed binding) and
  Ma'arif's "overwhelming majority" (lifted) both reported unranked;
  al-Hasan's small remaining duty and al-Shafi'i's full abrogation named.
Hadith: Muslim 746 ('A'isha to Sa'd ibn Hisham), her night-prayer answer
quoted verbatim from the page English, excerpt of a long narration;
Bukhari 46 (Talha, man of Najd), prayer exchange only, as Ibn Kathir
attaches it. No gradings beyond the Sahihs themselves.
Asbab: none; the twelve-month hold is given as timing.
Dropped: "ten years"/"sixteen months" (in no fetched text), unfetched
hadiths in the tafsirs (Fatiha, witr, Tabarani, Tayalisi, Abu Ya'la).
