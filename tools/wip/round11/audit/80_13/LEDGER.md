### Identity of the scrolls: kept as parallel readings, none chosen
- Preserved Tablet: at-Tabari, al-Baghawi (first reading), Ma'arif.
- Books of the prophets (qila): al-Qurtubi, al-Baghawi; both cite 87:18-19.
- The surah / Qur'an in honoured scrolls, no location named: Ibn Kathir, Muyassar.
- Scrolls in the hands of angel scribes: Muyassar only, via its 80:15 clause; flagged as the next verses' ground.
- Ma'arif's "angels copy their scriptures from it" reported as its second reason for the plural.
- Watch-point gloss "the Qur'an's own written sheets" as a distinct named reading: no fetched text states it in those terms; covered only as Ibn Kathir/Muyassar (Qur'an is in honoured scrolls).

### Mukarramah glosses (all attributed)
mu'azzamah muwaqqarah (Ibn Kathir; Muyassar + 'aliyat al-qadr); greatness and raised worth (Sa'di); 'inda Allah (al-Suddi via Qurtubi); honoured in religion for knowledge and wisdom (Qurtubi's report of at-Tabari); brought down by kiram al-hafazah / descends from the Tablet (qila, Qurtubi); from a Karim, honour of book from honour of owner (qila, Qurtubi).
- Discrepancy flagged in article: the fetched at-Tabari on 80:13 gives the Preserved Tablet reading, not "honoured in religion"; that line is kept as Qurtubi's report of Tabari.
- كرام الحفظة rendered "noble guardians" (not "guardian angels") to stay with the text's wording.
