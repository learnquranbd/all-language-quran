Card and 8-section bilingual article for 80:13, fi suhufin mukarramah
(3 Arabic words), the verse that places the reminder of 80:11-12.

Sources: at-Tabari, Ibn Kathir (ar), al-Qurtubi, al-Baghawi, as-Sa'di on
80:13; Muyassar (grouped 80:9-16); Ma'arif (grouped 80:13-14).
- Scrolls kept as parallel readings, none chosen: Preserved Tablet
  (Tabari, Baghawi, Ma'arif); books of the prophets, qila, citing
  87:18 and 87:19 (Qurtubi, Baghawi); the surah or Qur'an in honoured
  scrolls, no place named (Ibn Kathir, Muyassar).
- Plural suhuf: Ma'arif's two reasons (all scriptures written in the
  Tablet, or angels copy from it), left unranked as Ma'arif leaves them.
- Mukarramah: mu'azzamah muwaqqarah (Ibn Kathir, Muyassar + high worth),
  'inda Allah (as-Suddi via Qurtubi), honoured in religion (Qurtubi's
  report of Tabari, flagged: fetched Tabari gives the Tablet), brought
  down by noble guardians, from a Karim (qila, Qurtubi).
- Unseen kept to the fetched clauses only.
Hadith: none attached by any fetched tafsir; said in one sentence.
Asbab: not used; opening scene via Muyassar's paraphrase only.
Dropped: 56:77-80 and 85:21-22 (cited by no fetched text), the skilled
reciter hadith (not cited here), mutahharah ruling and marfu'ah glosses
(80:14 ground), safarah detail (80:15-16 ground).
