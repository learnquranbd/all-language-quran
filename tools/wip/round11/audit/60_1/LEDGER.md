## Hadith
- Sahih al-Bukhari 4890 (Dar-us-Salam; USC-MSA vol. 6, bk 60, no. 412), Kitab at-Tafsir.
  URL: https://quranx.com/hadith/Bukhari/DarusSalam/Hadith-4890 (saved: hadith_Bukhari_4890.txt via hadith.js).
  First ten words (Arabic matn): «بَعَثَنِي رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم أَنَا وَالزُّبَيْرَ وَالْمِقْدَادَ فَقَالَ انْطَلِقُوا حَتَّى».
  Grading: in al-Bukhari's Sahih; no separate grading given or claimed.
  Quoted whole (matn from «بعثني» to «فقد غفرت لكم»), rendered from the Arabic. The trailing notes (Amr's «ونزلت فيه», «لا أدري الآية في الحديث أو قول عمرو», Sufyan's answer via Ali ibn al-Madini) are reported, not quoted as matn.
  DEFECT in the quranx English: "I am an Ansari man" for «إِنِّي كُنْتُ امْرَأً مِنْ قُرَيْشٍ وَلَمْ أَكُنْ مِنْ أَنْفُسِهِمْ». Not used; article renders the Arabic ("a man among Quraysh but not of them by descent").
- 4890 has Umar say only «دعني ... فأضرب عنقه», without «المنافق». The "hypocrite" wording is reported as what the versions quoted by Qurtubi (Muslim), Baghawi (Bukhari chain via Qutayba) and Ibn Kathir (Ahmad) contain. The article never calls Hatib a hypocrite in its own voice.
- at-Tabari's «فلا تقولوا لحاطب إلا خيرا» report: given as at-Tabari's report from Ali, no grading claimed.

## Asbab
Used: the Hatib letter, as the fetched tafsirs give it (all eight agree on Hatib; Tabari says reports from a group of Companions and others). Bukhari's link of the verse to the event is reported with the narrators' own reservation (whether the verse line is in the hadith or Amr's statement) and Ibn Kathir's note of the Maghazi wording. Woman's identity kept as a difference (Muzaynah / Sarah, Tabari's Ibn Ishaq report; Sarah per Qurtubi, Baghawi).

## Dropped / not used
- Qurtubi's fiqh on the penalty for a Muslim spy (Malik, Ibn al-Qasim, Ashhab: imam's ijtihad; Abd al-Malik: killed if habitual), the Furat ibn Hayyan report, al-Awza'i/Asbagh on a non-Muslim spy: legal tangent, sensitive, outside the verse's tadabbur.
- Qurtubi's letter text ("a army like the night ..."; «ذكره بعض المفسرين»): unsourced.
- Timing variant: Hudaybiyah (Qatada in Tabari; «وقيل» in Qurtubi) vs the Conquest (Ibn Kathir, Ma'arif). Not used; article follows Ibn Kathir's framing only and does not assert a date.
- Number/names of riders beyond Bukhari's (Ammar, Talha, Umar, Abu Marthad variants): not used.
- Woman's "four excluded from the amnesty" (Qurtubi «وروي»): unsourced, dropped.
- Ibn Kathir's 5:51, 5:57, 4:144, 3:28 and 22:40 citations; Sa'di's millat Ibrahim line (60:4 is future ground); Ma'arif's "an infidel ... can never be a friend of a Muslim" generalisation: not used.
- Sa'di's continuation «فخرج العبد من الإيمان» (general case): not used; article reports only that support and alliance follow affection.
- 60:2-3 content (Ibn Kathir en group; Qatada's 60:2 note): untouched. 60:8-9: one-sentence pointer only.
