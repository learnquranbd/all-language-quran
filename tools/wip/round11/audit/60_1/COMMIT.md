Card and 8-section bilingual article (1,754 EN words) on 60:1: the Hatib
letter, ya ayyuha alladhina amanu kept over the rebuke, 'aduwwi wa
'aduwwakum named by deeds, tulquna/tusirruna bi-l-mawadda, a'lamu bima
akhfaytum, the late conditional, dalla sawa' al-sabil. 60:2-3 untouched.

Sources: at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi, as-Sa'di, Ibn Kathir (ar; en group
60:1-3 for the 60:1 part), al-Baghawi, Ma'arif, Muyassar.
- Occasion as the tafsirs give it (IK; Tabari's athar from Companions);
  the woman kept as a difference: Muzaynah or Sarah (Tabari via Ibn
  Ishaq), Sarah (Qurtubi, Baghawi).
- Prophet's response reported: question first, "he has told you the
  truth", refusal with Badr; IK and Sa'di on the accepted excuse;
  Tabari's "say nothing of Hatib but good" as his report.
- Kept side by side: Qurtubi's mu'ataba as a sign of Hatib's standing vs
  Ma'arif calling his calculation inappropriate and illusory.
- Bil-mawadda: ba redundant (Tabari), Zajjaj's news-and-secret gloss
  (Baghawi, Qurtubi), Muyassar joins both, Sa'di's haste, Muqatil's
  nasiha. Conditional: postponed but prior (Tabari, Baghawi), ellipsis
  (Qurtubi), Sa'di's test of intent, Ma'arif on hijra.
Explicit paragraph in both languages: the verse licenses nothing against
any living person or community; "My enemies and your enemies" is no
instruction to treat any present-day people as enemies. 60:8-9 pointer only.
Hadith: Bukhari 4890 quoted whole from the Arabic, with the narrators'
own doubt on whether the verse line is in the hadith; "hypocrite" only as
the wording other versions carry, never in the article's voice.
- Defect noted: quranx English of 4890 has "an Ansari man" for «امرأ من
  قريش ولم أكن من أنفسهم»; not used.
Dropped: Qurtubi's spy-penalty fiqh, the unsourced letter text and
amnesty report, Hudaybiyah timing variant, 60:2-4 matter.
- Bukhari 4890 rendered from the Arabic (quranx English says 'Ansari man'; Arabic: a man from Quraysh not of them); article says so.
