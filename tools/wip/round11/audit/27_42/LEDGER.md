# SOURCES ledger — 27:42

## Tafsir fetched (all pre-fetched in PREP, none re-fetched)

- **Ibn Kathir (ar, 14)** — USED. Clause on كأنه هو: «فكان فيها ثبات وعقل، ولها لب ودهاء وحزم، فلم تقدم على أنه هو لبعد مسافته عنها، ولا أنه غيره... فقالت كأنه هو... وهذا غاية في الذكاء والحزم». And «قال مجاهد: سليمان يقوله» (speaker of وأوتينا العلم = Sulaymān).
- **Ibn Kathir (en abridged, 169)** — USED (grouped 27:41-44; read only the 27:42 portion). Confirms the altered-throne test and the attributes (wise, steadfast, intelligent, strong-willed; "the ultimate in intelligence and strong resolve"); the disguise reports of Ibn ʿAbbās, Mujāhid, Qatādah; "Mujahid said: This was spoken by Sulayman." 27:43/27:44 material in this file deliberately NOT used (future ground).
- **at-Tabari (15)** — USED. «شبهته» / Qatādah «شبهته وكانت قد تركته خلفها»; speaker clause «مخبرا عن قيل سليمان... أي هذه المرأة، بالله وبقدرته على ما يشاء، وكنا مسلمين لله من قبلها»; «عن مجاهد: سليمان يقوله». (Ibn Zayd's «شكت» noted but not quoted in article.)
- **al-Muyassar (16)** — USED. «فظهر لسليمان أنها أصابت في جوابها، وقد علمت قدرة الله وصحة نبوة سليمان»; «وأوتينا العلم بالله وبقدرته من قبلها».
- **al-Qurtubi (90)** — USED. «شبهته به لأنها خلفته تحت الأغلاق، فلم تقر بذلك ولم تنكر، فعلم سليمان كمال عقلها»; «قال عكرمة: كانت حكيمة»; Muqātil «عرفته ولكن شببت عليهم كما شبهوا عليها»; the four speaker-readings of وأوتينا العلم (Bilqīs / Sulaymān / knowledge of her Islam / قوم سليمان) and «والله أعلم».
- **as-Sa'di (91)** — USED. «وهذا من ذكائها وفطنتها لم تقل هو... ولم تنف أنه هو، لأنها عرفته، فأتت بلفظ محتمل للأمرين صادق على الحالين»; the two possibilities for the speaker (Sulaymān giving thanks / the queen's confession about Sulaymān's kingdom and power).
- **al-Baghawi (94)** — USED. Muqātil «عرفته لكنها شبهت عليهم»; ʿIkrima «كانت حكيمة لم تقل نعم خوفا من أن تكذب، ولم تقل لا خوفا من التكذيب»; the «قيل اشتبه عليها أمر العرش لأنها تركته في بيت خلف... أبواب مغلقة» (reported as qīl, used as qīl); the speaker-readings (prophethood-knowledge / Mujāhid's Sulaymān reading / knowledge of her Islam).
- **Ma'arif al-Qur'an (en, 168)** — NOT USED. Grouped file is almost entirely 27:40 (identity of "the one with knowledge of the book," Āṣif ibn Barkhiyā, miracle vs karāmah). That is 27:40's shipped ground; drawing on it here would preempt. No 27:42-specific content beyond what the other sources carry.

## Disagreement kept (no side taken)
Speaker/sense of وأوتينا العلم من قبلها وكنا مسلمين — §5 presents:
1. **Sulaymān (and his people)** — Mujāhid via at-Tabari and Ibn Kathir; knowledge of Allah and His power, submitted before her.
2. **Bilqīs** — al-Qurtubi's reported reading and as-Saʿdi's second possibility; knowledge of Sulaymān's prophethood/kingdom before the sign, submitting to him.
3. **Sulaymān's people** / **knowledge of her coming in submission** — al-Qurtubi's further readings; closed with wallāhu aʿlam.
No position asserted; the shared point (knowledge precedes submission) is stated without resolving the speaker.

## Hadith
- **Sahih al-Bukhari 71** (§6). URL fetched: https://quranx.com/hadith/Bukhari/DarusSalam/Hadith-71 (Dar-us-Salam ref 71; USC-MSA Vol.1 Bk.3 H.71). First ten words (EN): "I heard Allah's Messenger saying, If Allah wants to do". Arabic quoted: «من يرد الله به خيرا يفقهه في الدين، وإنما أنا قاسم والله يعطي». Grading: ṣaḥīḥ by inclusion in Ṣaḥīḥ al-Bukhari (no separate collector grade shown; not upgraded). Used for the "knowledge/understanding as a grant from Allah" point tying to the passive أوتينا. Checked not to be reused in the surah-27 tadabbur shard (only appears in the surah-20 shard, not a nearby verse).

## Asbab
None. Narrative verse in the Sulaymān–Bilqīs sequence; no sabab needed or claimed.

## Dropped (could not use / deliberately withheld)

- al-Baghawi's "seven locked doors" number — kept only as his qīl ("it is said") and without the count, since it is a narrative detail that cuts against the recognised-it reading.
- 27:43 (what she worshipped held her back) and 27:44 (glass floor, final declaration) — present in the grouped Ibn Kathir file; withheld as future ground. Her final submission is left for 27:44.
- Āṣif ibn Barkhiyā / miracle-vs-karāmah (Ma'arif, 168) — withheld as 27:40's ground.
