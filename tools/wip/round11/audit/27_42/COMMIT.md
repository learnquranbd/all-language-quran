Bilqis is shown her disguised throne and asked "Is your throne like
this?" Her answer كأنه هو, "as though it were it," neither affirms nor
denies a thing she can see has been altered.

Card and deep bilingual article on her careful, honest word and the
knowledge that precedes submission.

Sources (all pre-fetched, none re-fetched):
- Ibn Kathir (ar 14, en 169): her ثبات وعقل ولب ودهاء وحزم; the answer
  is "غاية في الذكاء والحزم"; she would not rush to yes (throne far) or
  no (she saw its marks).
- at-Tabari, al-Muyassar: شبهته; knowledge of Allah and His power.
- al-Qurtubi, as-Sa'di: a wording true in both states (محتمل للأمرين
  صادق على الحالين); Sulayman knew كمال عقلها; ʿIkrima "كانت حكيمة".
- al-Baghawi (ʿIkrima): she said neither نعم (fear of lying) nor لا
  (fear of being disbelieved); Muqatil: she clouded it as they clouded
  the throne for her.

Disagreement kept, no side taken: speaker of "وأوتينا العلم من قبلها
وكنا مسلمين" — Sulayman and his people (Mujahid via Tabari and Ibn
Kathir) vs Bilqis (Qurtubi's reading, as-Sa'di's second possibility) vs
Sulayman's people (Qurtubi), closed with wallahu aʿlam.

Hadith: Sahih al-Bukhari 71 — "when Allah wills good for someone He
gives him understanding of the religion... I am only a distributor and
Allah gives" — understanding as a grant, matching the passive أوتينا.

Dropped: Ibn Zayd's شكت shade; al-Baghawi's "seven doors" count (kept
only as his qīl, without the number). Withheld as future ground: 27:43
(what held her back) and 27:44 (glass floor and her final submission);
Asif ibn Barkhiya / karamah (27:40's ground, Ma'arif not used).
No asbab. No condemned-people licensing sentence (not that kind of verse).
