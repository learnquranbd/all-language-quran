Card and 8-section bilingual article on maraja l-bahrayni yaltaqiyan.
55:20 (barzakh) and 55:22 (pearl and coral) pointers only.

Sources: at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi, Ibn Kathir (ar; en abridged checked),
al-Baghawi, as-Sa'di, al-Muyassar, Ma'arif al-Qur'an.
- Maraja: arsala wa-khalla, from maraja dabbatahu (Tabari, Ibn Abbas);
  khalla/arsala/ahmala, root ihmal as in the pasture, also khalata, and
  al-Akhfash's amraja (Qurtubi); let loose (Ma'arif); mixed (Muyassar).
- Yaltaqiyan: fresh pours into salt and blends (Sa'di); meet every year
  (Qurtubi; Tabari's Ibn Abbas report); ends meet (Qurtubi qila); Ibn
  Zayd's "kept them from meeting" via Ibn Kathir.
- Which two seas, kept as a disagreement, no position: sky and earth
  (Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Sa'id b. Jubayr, Ibn Abza, 'Atiyya); Persia and
  Rum (al-Hasan, Qatada); salt sea and fresh rivers (Ibn Jurayj, Ibn
  Kathir, Baghawi, Sa'di, Muyassar, Ma'arif); Qurtubi's east/west and
  pearl-and-coral qila views.
- Tabari's preference (sky/earth, reasoned from 55:22) and Ibn Kathir's
  reply ("the wording does not support it"), both reported; no
  pearl-formation claim in the article's own voice.
- 25:53 via Ibn Kathir; 35:12 and 18:60 not cited by any source, unused.
Hadith: none attached by any fetched tafsir; said so in one sentence.
Asbab: none. Du'a marked as composed from the verse, not narrated.
Dropped: Ma'arif's observational passage (55:20 ground), Ibn Abi Hatim
oyster report (55:22), any modern geography or science claim.
