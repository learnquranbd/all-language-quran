Card and 8-section article (1,784 EN words, 22 paragraphs).
12:90 is 23 Arabic words, per PREP.md.

Sources: all eight tafsir IDs; 169 is grouped 12:89-92, stated, and only
its 12:90 content used (revealed "by Allah's command", 94:5-6).
Recognition: the Qur'an does not say how (as-Sa'di); the covering lifted
(Ibn Ishaq via at-Tabari and al-Baghawi), the smile and the crown with
its mark (Ibn 'Abbas via al-Baghawi and al-Qurtubi), Ya'qub's letter
(al-Qurtubi) all reported as ungraded and none asserted.
Readings kept as a disagreement: the statement form innaka is tied to
Ibn Muhaysin by at-Tabari and Ibn Kathir, to Ibn Kathir the reader by
al-Qurtubi and al-Baghawi (with Abu Ja'far); a-wa-anta from Ubayy;
at-Tabari chooses the question by consensus. Yattaqi with the ya' kept:
al-Qurtubi, naming Ibn Kathir the reader, with his two grammatical
accounts. Qad manna: reunion (Ibn Kathir, Muyassar, al-Baghawi, at-Tabari
"after you separated us"), rescue and rule (al-Qurtubi), faith, taqwa and
establishment by reason of patience (as-Sa'di), Ma'arif's two stages.
Taqwa and sabr defined (at-Tabari et al.); Mujahid's prison and
Ibn 'Abbas's celibacy readings (al-Baghawi). 12:91-92 given a pointer only.

No fetched tafsir attaches a hadith; Muslim 2999 (Suhayb, thanks and
patience), confirmed on quranx, whole matn rendered from the Arabic,
marked as not attached. The article states the verse licenses nothing
against any living person or community.

Dropped: Qunbul and "of Makka" (not in fetched texts), al-Baghawi's
"asked on a guess" view (length), a lexical note on manna (memory).
