## Hadith
- Bukhari 4991 (Dar-us-Salam), https://quranx.com/hadith/Bukhari/DarusSalam/Hadith-4991, saved `hadith_Bukhari_4991.txt`. First ten words (EN, quoted whole): "Gabriel recited the Qur'an to me in one way. Then". Grading: in Sahih al-Bukhari; Ibn Kathir gives none. Attached by Ibn Kathir (ar) as the seven-ahruf hadith, an instance of easing recitation.
- Bukhari 4992, https://quranx.com/hadith/Bukhari/DarusSalam/Hadith-4992, saved `hadith_Bukhari_4992.txt`. Its closing words "إن هذا القرآن أنزل على سبعة أحرف" are exactly Ibn Kathir's wording. Described (Umar hears Hisham b. Hakim recite al-Furqan; both told "it was revealed this way"), not quoted, because it is too long to quote whole in one paragraph.
- Dropped: the "reciter with the noble scribes" and "best of you who learn the Qur'an" narrations. No fetched tafsir attaches them to 54:40; the article says no other hadith is attached.
