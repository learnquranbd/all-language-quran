Card and 9-section bilingual article for 54:40, the fourth and last
"wa-laqad yassarna l-Qur'ana li-dh-dhikr, fa-hal min muddakir". Distinct
from shipped 54:17: placement after Lut (54:36 tamaraw bin-nudhur) and
before Pharaoh (54:41 and 54:42), then 54:43.

Sources: al-Qurtubi, Ibn Kathir (ar; en abridged for 54:36), at-Tabari,
al-Muyassar. al-Baghawi gives the verse text only; as-Sa'di fetch failed
and Ma'arif was the wrong group, neither used.
- Repetition "for alerting and making understood"; qila: every story a
  reminder, hal calls on the understanding as proof (Qurtubi).
- yassarna: eased for memorising, help for who wants it; or "made ready",
  from yassara naqatahu/farasahu (Qurtubi).
- Ease of wording and meaning (Ibn Kathir, Muyassar) vs memorising and
  recitation (Qurtubi, Mujahid, as-Suddi): kept, no position. Ibn Abbas
  via ad-Dahhak; 38:29 and 19:97 via Ibn Kathir.
- Sa'id b. Jubayr: only the Qur'an read whole from memory; ifti'al as
  remembrance becoming nature (Qurtubi).
- muddakir glosses: reciter (Qurtubi), restrained from sins (al-Qurazi),
  admonished and turned (Tabari), seeker of knowledge (Abu Bakr
  al-Warraq, Ibn Shawdhab).
Hadith: Bukhari 4991 (Ibn Abbas, seven ahruf) quoted whole, "in the
Sahih"; 4992 described, not quoted. Attached by Ibn Kathir.
Asbab: none. Licensing sentence for the people of Lut in both languages.
Dropped: "noble scribes" and "best of you" hadith (not attached), 44:58
and 73:20 (not cited), the idgham history and Matar al-Warraq (in 54:17),
the 'Uzayr report.
