Card plus nine-section bilingual article (1,712 EN words) on the residents of
the first pair of gardens, reclining on couches lined with istabraq, with the
fruit near.

Sources (tafsir 14, 15, 16, 90, 91, 94; 168 and 169 grouped, cross-check only):
- muttaki'in as hal from the meaning of 55:46 (at-Tabari; al-Qurtubi hal)
- ittika' as lying down, or sitting cross-legged under "it is said" (Ibn Kathir);
  settled ease like kings on thrones (as-Sa'di)
- bitana under the zihara, side next to the ground (al-Qurtubi, al-Baghawi via
  az-Zajjaj); istabraq thick brocade (Ikrimah, ad-Dahhak, Qatadah via Ibn Kathir;
  at-Tabari), gold-worked (Abu Imran al-Jawni)
- "these are the linings, what of the outer sides?" given to Ibn Mas'ud with
  chain (Ibn Kathir, at-Tabari; one Tabari chain stops at Hubayra), to Ibn
  Mas'ud and Abu Hurayra (al-Qurtubi, al-Baghawi); Ibn Kathir's "tanbih
  bil-adna 'ala al-a'la"
- outer sides: light / solid light / mercy / unknown (Malik ibn Dinar, Sufyan or
  Sharik, al-Qasim, ash-Shami via Ibn Abi Hatim; al-Hasan; Sa'id ibn Jubayr);
  Sa'id ibn Jubayr's 32:17 answer; Ibn Abbas on the hidden outer sides
- jana dan: no climbing, gathered sitting (at-Tabari); standing, sitting, lying
  (Ibn Abbas in al-Qurtubi, as-Sa'di, al-Baghawi); Qatadah: neither distance nor
  thorn; 69:23 and 76:14 (Ibn Kathir)

Disagreement kept: bata'in as the outer faces (al-Hasan, al-Farra', from Qatadah;
at-Tabari's linguists) against Ibn Qutayba and others (two equal faces only).

Hadith: Bukhari 4779 (under 32:17), quoted whole, marked general and not
attached to this verse. No sound hadith is attached to 55:54 in the fetched tafsir.

Dropped: Qatadah's "it was mentioned to us" report on replaced fruit (Tabari) and
al-Qurtubi's chainless "outer sides are glittering light" report. 55:56-58 and
55:62 are framed only.
