## Hadith
- Jami' at-Tirmidhi 3331 — URL https://quranx.com/hadith/Tirmidhi/Hadith-3331 (via hadith.js, saved in this folder).
  First ten words (EN used, quoted whole): "Aishah narrated: "He frowned and turned away" was revealed about Ibn"
  Grading: at-Tirmidhi «هذا حديث حسن غريب» (hasan gharib) — reported as is. His note that some narrate it from Hisham ibn Urwa from his father without Aishah is also reported.
  Attachment: the report is about 'Abasa's opening (80:1); article says 80:3 belongs to it as a continuation of the same address.

## Asbab an-nuzul
