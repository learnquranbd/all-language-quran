Card and 8-section bilingual article for 80:3, wa ma yudrika la'allahu
yazzakka (4 Arabic words). 80:1-2 used only as the commentators' context.

Sources: at-Tabari, Ibn Kathir (ar), al-Qurtubi, as-Sa'di, al-Baghawi on
80:3; Muyassar (grouped 80:3-4, 80:3 clause only); Ma'arif (grouped
80:3-12, 80:3 paragraph and its teaching principle); Ibn Kathir en
(grouped 80:1-10) for the setting and framing.
- Yazzakka glosses kept side by side: purity from sins (Tabari; Baghawi
  via deeds and what he learns from you), purity in the soul (Ibn Kathir),
  base traits left for beautiful ones (Sa'di), purified by his question
  (Muyassar), stage of the righteous (Ma'arif).
- Tension kept: Ibn Zayd's "yuslim" (Tabari with chain, Baghawi) vs Ibn
  Umm Maktum as an early, genuine believer (Ibn Kathir en, Ma'arif).
- La'alla: renderings named; whose hope it voices is not addressed by any
  fetched text, and the article says so.
- Framing attributed only: Qurtubi's 'itab with 6:52 and 18:28; Ibn
  Kathir en heading; Ibn Kathir's equal-warning command; Ma'arif's
  priority-of-teaching principle. No own-voice verdict on the Prophet ﷺ.
Hadith: Tirmidhi 3331 (Aishah) summarised, full quote lives in 80:7;
graded hasan gharib by at-Tirmidhi, with his note on the variant.
Asbab: Ibn Kathir en's unnamed, chainless "more than one of the scholars"
account, attributed as such.
Dropped: Ibn Kathir en's claim about Tirmidhi omitting Aishah (contradicts
the fetched page), the Muwatta' remark, scene names; third-person and
tark al-awla framings (absent from fetched texts); 80:4 dhikra discussion.
