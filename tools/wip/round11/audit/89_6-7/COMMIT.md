Card and 8-section bilingual article for 89:6-7: alam tara kayfa
fa'ala rabbuka bi-'Ad, Irama dhati al-'imad, nine words (6 + 3).
89:5 named as context; 89:8-14 not pulled forward.

Sources: Tabari, Qurtubi, Baghawi, Sa'di, Ibn Kathir (ar + en abridged),
Muyassar, Ma'arif. Ibn Kathir's and Qurtubi's Iram-as-city discussion
sits in the 89:8 slot: fetched and used for 89:7 matters only.
- Iram: ancestor (Ibn Ishaq; Ibn Abbas via 'Ata'; al-Kalbi), tribe
  (Qatadah, Tabari's preference, Ibn Kathir approving, Sa'di, Muyassar),
  nation/ancient/strong (Mujahid), Damascus (al-Maqburi, Ibn al-Musayyib,
  'Ikrimah, Ibn al-'Arabi) vs Alexandria (al-Qurazi), "the destroyed"
  (Ibn Abbas, ad-Dahhak). None chosen.
- Grammar: badal / 'atf bayan; tanwin and idafa readings per Qurtubi;
  Tabari's tanwin argument vs Qurtubi's al-Hasan idafa reading kept.
- dhat al-'imad: tent-poles (Tabari's choice, Ibn Kathir agrees), height
  (Ibn Abbas, Abu 'Ubaydah, Ma'arif), buildings (Ibn Zayd, Muyassar),
  strength (ad-Dahhak, Sa'di).
Hadith: Bukhari 3326 (Adam sixty cubits), quoted whole, marked general,
cited because Qurtubi's Ibn al-'Arabi uses it against the height reports.
Dropped: Miqdam marfu' (unnamed link), Abu Hurayrah stone door (mawquf),
Shaddad city and Ibn Qilabah tales (no grading; Ibn Kathir: Israelite
fables, chain unsound): named only with his verdict, not retold.
Asbab: none. Cross-refs: 69:7, 69:8, 53:50, 7:69, 41:15.
Licensing sentence in both languages; no living group or place named.
