Card and deep bilingual article for 18:2, the completion of 18:1: the
Book made straight (qayyiman), to warn and to give glad tidings.

Sources (all fetched for this verse; none from memory):
- qayyiman as the positive twin of 18:1's negated ʿiwaj: mustaqim
  (Ibn Kathir, at-Tabari, al-Baghawi, al-Muyassar), al-Qurtubi's
  grammar (nasb ʿala al-hal) and ad-Dahhak's "straight in wisdom".
- Two further readings kept as a disagreement: qayyim as guarding/
  confirming the earlier scriptures (al-Farra, in Qurtubi & Baghawi),
  and qayyim as caretaker who keeps others upright (Maʿarif/Mazhari).
- Warning half: baʾs = hastened + deferred punishment (Ibn Kathir,
  at-Tabari, as-Saʿdi); elided object of yunzir (at-Tabari, cf 3:175);
  min ladunhu = from Allah, none punishes as He does (Ibn Kathir);
  warning as mercy (as-Saʿdi, cf 39:16).
- Glad-tidings half: faith joined to righteous deeds (at-Tabari, Ibn
  Kathir; as-Saʿdi: ikhlas + mutabaʿa); ajr hasan = Paradise (all),
  as-Saʿdi on hasan meaning a reward with nothing to spoil it.

Hadith: no sound report expounds 18:2 itself; stated as such. The
sahih virtue of the surah's opening ten (Muslim 809a, Abu ad-Dardaʾ,
protection from the Dajjal) included but marked as a collective merit
of the opening, not a comment on this verse.

Not preempted: 18:3 (abiding forever), 18:4-5 ("Allah has a son"),
the People of the Cave, and the surah-level asbab — all reserved.

Dropped (in ledger): "uncreated" reading, qiraʾat variant, the
non-Muslim recitation reports.
