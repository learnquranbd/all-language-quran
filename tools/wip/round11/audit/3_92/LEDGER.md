## Watch-points
- Abu Talhah's outcome stated correctly (§5.1): the garden went to his relatives and cousins on the Prophet's ﷺ direction; the reward he hoped for (birrha wa dhukhraha 'inda Allah) is with Allah. The shipped defect ("the gift went straight to his own family") is not repeated.
- al-birr glosses with names (§2): Paradise (al-Baghawi: Ibn 'Abbas, Ibn Mas'ud, Mujahid; al-Qurtubi: Ibn Mas'ud, Ibn 'Abbas, 'Ata', Mujahid, 'Amr ibn Maymun, as-Suddi; at-Tabari/Ibn Kathir: 'Amr ibn Maymun; al-Muyassar); birr of the Lord (at-Tabari, Qatada); reward of birr (al-Qurtubi's taqdir); taqwa (Muqatil ibn Hayyan, al-Baghawi); obedience (unnamed in al-Baghawi; 'Atiyyah al-'Awfi in al-Qurtubi); good (unnamed, al-Baghawi); righteous deeds (unnamed, al-Qurtubi); becoming abrar (al-Hasan, al-Baghawi); honour of the religion and taqwa ('Ata', both); as-Sa'di's joining; Ma'arif's root sense.
- mimma tuhibbun (§3): zakat (ad-Dahhak from Ibn 'Abbas, al-Baghawi); abrogated by the zakat verse (Mujahid and al-Kalbi, both); al-Hasan reported two ways (obligatory zakat in al-Qurtubi; every spending for Allah's face, even a date, in al-Baghawi) — kept as a difference between the two reporters, no position; al-Qurtubi's unnamed "comprehensive" reading; Ma'arif's "general, both". No position taken.
- wa ma tunfiqu ... 'alim (§7): at-Tabari (ma = mahma), Qatada, al-Baghawi, al-Qurtubi, al-Muyassar, as-Sa'di, Ma'arif — each named.
- Zayd ibn Harithah (§6.1): at-Tabari (through 'Amr ibn Dinar and through Ayyub) and al-Qurtubi (from Asad ibn Musa), attributed, no grading added in the article. (For the orchestrator: at-Tabari's editor, footnotes 6-7, calls both routes mursal; not stated in the article per the watch-point.)
- Ibn 'Umar (§6.2): the slave girl (Ibn Kathir from al-Bazzar; al-Baghawi) and Nafi' (al-Qurtubi, with Safiyyah's "I think he interpreted"), attributed, no grading added.
