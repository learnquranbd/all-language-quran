Replaces the 726-word, 5-section article with a 1,763-word, 8-section
one, from at-Tabari, Ibn Kathir (ar and en), al-Qurtubi, as-Sa'di,
al-Baghawi and al-Muyassar on 5:32, with Ma'arif on 5:27 for the
setting. All slots checked; Ma'arif's grouped text stops inside 5:27.
The shipped card is kept.
Min ajli: jarr and jinayah (at-Tabari, az-Zajjaj, ar-Rummani), the two
attachments (to 5:31 or to katabna) and Abu Ja'far's reading.
Why Bani Isra'il: al-Qurtubi's first-written-threat account; the
disagreement kept: al-Hasan (for us as for them) against the unnamed
view that it was particular to them. Licensing lines in both languages.
Exceptions as glossed by at-Tabari, the Muyassar, al-Baghawi,
al-Qurtubi (three traits), Qatadah and as-Sa'di; no ruling in own
voice; 5:33 one line, no penalties.
The likeness: Ibn Abbas (prophet or just leader; any sacred soul),
as-Suddi (in the eyes of the slain), Mujahid with 4:93, Ibn Zayd
(qisas), al-Qurtubi's unnamed readings, Sa'id ibn Jubayr and Ibn
Kathir's "most evident", as-Sa'di, at-Tabari's preference and reason.
Ihya: al-Qurtubi's figurative note, at-Tabari with 2:258, rescue from
drowning, burning and collapse (Mujahid), pardon (Ibn Zayd, al-Hasan).
Close: at-Tabari (oath, them = Bani Isra'il) against as-Sa'di (many of
the people); Ibn Kathir's Madinah example with 2:84 and 2:85.
Uthman and Abu Hurayrah report via Ibn Kathir, ungraded.
Hadith: none quoted; Ibn Kathir's one report (Ahmad, Hamzah) could not
be confirmed and is named as not quoted.
Dropped: the misguidance gloss (not found), Ibn Atiyya's analogy.
Corrected from the shipped article: the unsourced "first judged is
bloodshed" hadith, 17:33 and 6:151, the feeding/healing gloss, the
courts-and-vigilante paragraph, and "the mufassirun are explicit".
