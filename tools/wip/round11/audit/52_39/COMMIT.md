Card and 8-section bilingual article for 52:39, the eleventh of the
fifteen am-questions of at-Tur: am lahu al-banatu wa lakumu al-banun.

Sources: Tabari, Muyassar, Baghawi, Qurtubi, Sa'di, Ibn Kathir (ar; en
checked), Ma'arif (silent on this verse).
- Address: Tabari (polytheists of Quraysh; "a-li-rabbikum ayyuha
  al-qawm"), Muyassar; the only 2nd-person-plural turn in the chain.
- Baghawi's twin 37:149 (lahum vs lakum); the pair occurs only there.
- Tabari's qisma diza; Muyassar's iftira' wa kadhib.
- Rebuke (Baghawi inkar, Qurtubi tawbikh) vs threat (Ibn Kathir),
  kept as differing weight.
- Sa'di's two forbidden things: a child at all, and the lesser share;
  the ranking shown as the claimants' own (Ibn Kathir, Qurtubi).
- Qurtubi: such a mind will deny the resurrection; tied to 52:7, 52:45.
Claim framed as a theological invention, not about daughters; angels'
nature left alone. Licensing sentence in both languages.
Hadith: none attached; Jubayr report (52:35-37) noted, not quoted.
Asbab: none. Pointers: 37:150, 37:153, 16:58, 52:40.
Dropped: Ma'arif's 37:150 material, 17:40/43:19/53:21-22 by number,
grammar of am.
52:39: thin sources; article's own text observations (the 15 'am' questions, pronoun shift) checked in quran-json; 37:150/37:153/16:58 named as other entries, not tafsir-cited.
