# SOURCES ledger, 52:39

## Tafsir fetched (all from PREP; none re-fetched) and named
- 15 at-Tabari: NAMED. Addressees: "يقول تعالى ذكره للمشركين به من قريش"; paraphrase keeping the
  second person: "ألربكم أيها القوم البنات ولكم البنون؟"; verdict: "ذلك إذن قسمة ضيزي". (The phrase is
  the wording of 53:22, verified in data/quran-json/53.json; Tabari gives no verse number, so the
  article says "in words the Qur'an itself uses of the same claim elsewhere" and cites no number.)
- 16 al-Muyassar: NAMED. "ألِلهِ سبحانه البنات ولكم البنون كما تزعمون افتراء وكذبًا؟" (keeps "you";
  "fabrication and lie").
- 94 al-Baghawi: NAMED. "هذا إنكار عليهم حين جعلوا لله ما يكرهون"; cites
  "فاستفتهم ألربك البنات ولهم البنون (الصافات - 149)". 37:149 verified in quran-json; the
  they/you contrast (lahum vs lakum) is the article's own observation from the two texts.
- 90 al-Qurtubi: NAMED. "سفه أحلامهم توبيخا لهم وتقريعا"; "أتضيفون إلى الله البنات مع أنفتكم منهن";
  "ومن كان عقله هكذا فلا يستبعد منه إنكار البعث". Link of ahlam to 52:32 stated explicitly as
  the article's own observation ("does not draw the line ... himself").
- 91 as-Sa'di: NAMED. "{ أَمْ لَهُ الْبَنَاتُ } كما زعمتم { وَلَكُمُ الْبَنُونَ } فتجمعون بين المحذورين؟
  جعلكم له الولد، واختياركم له أنقص الصنفين؟ فهل بعد هذا التنقص لرب العالمين غاية أو دونه نهاية؟"
  ("lesser of the two kinds" reported literally; the article then states, from Ibn Kathir and
  al-Qurtubi, that the ranking was the claimants' own.)
- 14 Ibn Kathir (ar): NAMED. "منكرا عليهم فيما نسبوه إليه من البنات ، وجعلهم الملائكة إناثا ،
  واختيارهم لأنفسهم الذكور على الإناث ، بحيث إذا بشر أحدهم بالأنثى ظل وجهه مسودا وهو كظيم";
  "وقد جعلوا الملائكة بنات الله ، وعبدوهم مع الله"; "وهذا تهديد شديد ووعيد أكيد". (His darkened-face
  clause is the wording of 16:58, verified; he gives no number; article says "words borrowed from
  the Qur'an's own picture of such a man". 16:58 is named only as a module pointer.)
- 169 Ibn Kathir (en, abridged): NAMED. Stairway gloss on 52:38: "a stairway to heaven (to the place
  where the angels are)"; on 52:39: "Allah sends a strong warning and stern admonition to them".
- 168 Ma'arif (en): fetched, grouped 52:38-43; its notes concern 52:38 and 52:44 only. NAMED only
  to say it has nothing particular on this verse.

## Emphases kept (not a genuine disagreement; stated as differing weight, no position)

## Own-voice textual observations (verified in data/quran-json)
- am opens 15 questions in 52:30-43 (counted: 30,32×2,33,35×2,36,37×2,38,39,40,41,42,43);
  52:39 is the 11th; 52:39 and 52:41 are the only 5-word am-verses in the chain.
- Only 52:39 among the am-questions addresses the claimants in the 2nd person plural (lakum);
  52:40 addresses the Prophet ﷺ (tas'aluhum).
- al-banatu / al-banun together in that form: only 37:149 and 52:39 (al-banun also 18:46 alone;
  37:153 has al-banati 'ala al-banin).
- 52:7, 52:32, 52:38, 52:43, 52:45 quoted from quran-json.
