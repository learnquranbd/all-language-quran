Card and 8-section bilingual article for 58:1: qad sami'a Allahu qawla
allati tujadiluka fi zawjiha wa-tashtaki ila Allah. 58:2-4 (the zihar
rulings) one pointer sentence only.

Sources: Tabari, Ibn Kathir (ar, en), Muyassar, Qurtubi, Sa'di, Baghawi,
Ma'arif.
- Her name and lineage kept as a disagreement: Tabari's five forms;
  Qurtubi's bint Hakim / Jamila with Khawla "more correct"; an-Nahhas
  reconciles lineages; Sa'di names no one.
- Tujadiluka (Baghawi, Ma'arif, Sa'di); Ibn Mas'ud's tuhawiruka (Tabari,
  Qurtubi). Tashtaki (Tabari, Muyassar; Qurtubi shaka = ishtaka).
- Tahawurakuma as both sides of the exchange (Tabari, Baghawi, Muyassar,
  Sa'di). Sami' basir: Tabari, Baghawi, Muyassar, Sa'di (hint of relief),
  Qurtubi on attributes of the Essence and al-Hakim; all attributed.
- Reports flagged as given: Baghawi chainless; 'Umar via Abu Yazid with
  Ibn Kathir's own "munqati'" note; Qurtubi's 'Umar version chainless.
Hadith: Ibn Majah 2063 ('A'ishah, Khawlah's words, Jibril came down),
quoted whole, no grading by the collector. Ibn Kathir: al-Bukhari cites
the shorter wording mu'allaq in Kitab at-Tawhid; Nasa'i 3460 and Ibn
Majah 188 checked, not quoted.
Licensing sentence in both languages (one household's trouble).
Dropped: Aws's lamam reports, cause-of-quarrel reports, expiation
narratives (58:2-4), the slave-woman identification, 4:128 and 20:46
(no fetched tafsir cites them).
