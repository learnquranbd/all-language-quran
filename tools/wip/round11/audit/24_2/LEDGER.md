# SOURCES ledger — 24:2

## Tafsirs fetched (all in audit/24_2/, none re-fetched)
- t14 Ibn Kathir (ar), t169 Ibn Kathir abridged (en), t15 at-Tabari, t16 al-Muyassar,
  t90 al-Qurtubi, t91 as-Sa'di, t94 al-Baghawi, t168 Ma'arif (en). All cover 24:2.

## Named in the article, with the Arabic clause each attribution rests on
- **al-Qurtubi (t90)** — "authority carries out the hadd, not private persons":
  mas'ala 9 «لا خلاف أن المخاطب بهذا الأمر الإمام ومن ناب منابه ... وقيل الخطاب للمسلمين
  ... ثم الإمام ينوب عنهم». Courts/upright men only + honour of a Muslim grave:
  mas'ala 16 «ينبغي أن يقام بين أيدي الحكام ولا يقيمه إلا فضلاء الناس ... فإن دم المسلم
  وحرمته عظيمة». Definition excludes shubhat nikah / doubt: mas'ala 1 «من غير نكاح ولا
  شبهة نكاح». Whip "between two whips": mas'ala 10 (Zayd b. Aslam, via Malik — Abu Umar
  notes it mursal). This verse abrogates confinement+harm verses "بالاتفاق": mas'ala 1.
  Majority = unmarried; Ishaq b. Rahawayh + al-Hasan combine jald+rajm (Ali w/ Shuraha):
  mas'ala 6. ra'fa readings + دين الله = حكم الله + belief-clause as spur: mas'ala 18-19.
  ta'ifa number views (1→1000 Mujahid/Ibn Abbas; 2 Ata/Ikrima; 3 Zuhri/Qatada; 4
  Malik): mas'ala 20; two purposes (disgrace/deterrence OR du'a for them): mas'ala 21.
- **at-Tabari (t15)** — the two ra'fa readings and his tarjih: «واختلف أهل التأويل ...
  فقال بعضهم هو ترك إقامة حد الله» (Mujahid, Ata, Ibrahim, Ibn Zayd) vs «ولكن أوجعوهما
  ضربا» (al-Hasan, Sa'id b. al-Musayyab); prefers first «لدلالة قول الله بعده في دين
  الله». Abu Mijlaz: not for the sultan to drop it "once raised to him". ta'ifa = one
  and up: «والعرب تسمي الواحد فما زاد طائفة».
- **Ibn Kathir (t169 en)** — natural pity not banned, only the pity that makes the judge
  drop a required punishment (Mujahid: "if the matter is taken to the ruling authority,
  the punishment has to be carried out"); the Companion who pitied the sheep → rewarded;
  'Umar's khutba on ayat ar-rajm; the "judge by the Book of Allah" case (100+exile /
  stoning). Stoning-for-married reported here as the tafsir's discussion, no verdict given.
- **as-Sa'di (t91)** — «فرحمته حقيقة بإقامة حد الله عليه»; ra'fa forbidden whether
  natural or for kinship/friendship; witnessing "يقوى بها العلم ويستقر به الفهم فلا يزاد
  فيه ولا ينقص"; muhsan's hadd is rajm "بالسنة الصحيحة المشهورة" (reported, no verdict).
- **al-Baghawi (t94)** — «والرأفة معنى في القلب لا ينهى عنه لأنه لا يكون باختيار الإنسان»;
  jald "لئلا يبرح ولا يضرب بحيث يبلغ اللحم"; the two ra'fa camps with the same names.
- **Ma'arif (t168 en)** — zina is a hadd fixed by Qur'an+sunna, not judge's discretion;
  ordinary claims need two witnesses, zina needs FOUR upright eyewitnesses "without the
  slightest doubt"; a rejected witness risks the qadhf charge (80 lashes) "so no one will
  dare testify where there's even slightest degree of doubt"; "slightest doubt → hadd
  remitted"; the an-Nisa gradation (4:15-16) and Ibn Abbas on "way forward"; the surah is
  a defence of chastity "even of the eyes".

## Verse refs used (parity checked both languages)

## Hadith (article §7)
- Sahih al-Bukhari — quranx URL fetched:
  https://quranx.com/hadith/Bukhari/DarusSalam/Hadith-660 (Dar-us-Salam 660; USC Vol.1
  Bk.11 H.629). First ten words used: "Seven will Allah shade in His shade on the".
  Grading: in Sahih al-Bukhari → sound (sahih) by the collector; reported as such, not
  upgraded. Chosen deliberately as a NON-VIOLENT anchor: the man who refuses illicit
  intercourse saying "I fear Allah" — the chastity the verse protects.
- The punishment hadiths (the "judge by the Book of Allah" case, Ubada b. Samit
  al-bikr-bil-bikr, 'Umar's ayat ar-rajm) are present in Ibn Kathir/Ma'arif and are
  reported ONLY as those tafsirs' discussion of the married/stoning question, with names
  and no verdict in the article's own voice. Not fetched on quranx, so not quoted as the
  §7 hadith.

## Asbab

## Dropped / not asserted
- Stoning of the married: NOT stated in the article's own voice; carried only as the
  commentators' reported discussion (names, no verdict), per the brief.
- al-Qurtubi's reasons for naming the woman first (mas'ala 5) and the several general
  du'a-narrations in his mas'ala 22 (Hudhayfa "six traits of zina", etc.): dropped —
  not needed and the latter are general admonitions not tied by a fetched isnad here.
- Exile ("taghrib for a year"): mentioned by Ibn Kathir/Muyassar/Baghawi as sunnah with
  a juristic dispute; left out of the article to avoid dwelling; the §3 focus stays on
  the Qur'anic hundred lashes.
