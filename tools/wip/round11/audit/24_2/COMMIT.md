Card plus 8-section bilingual article on the hadd of a hundred lashes for
zina between unmarried persons — framed strictly as classical penal law
administered by a legitimate authority (imam/qadi), licensing nothing to
any private person, family or community: no vigilantism, no honour violence.

Sources (all fetched, none from memory):
- al-Qurtubi: the addressee is the ruling authority and his deputy, "no
  disagreement" (mas'ala 9); the hadd is carried out only before judges by
  upright men the ruler picks, the blood and honour of a Muslim being grave
  (16); doubt / shubhat nikah bars it (1); whip "between two whips" (10).
- Ma'arif: the near-impossible evidentiary bar — four upright eyewitnesses
  with no doubt, vs two in ordinary claims; the rejected witness risks the
  qadhf charge of eighty lashes (24:4), so accusation is made almost
  impossible; slightest doubt remits the hadd; the surah defends chastity.
- at-Tabari + Ibn Kathir + al-Baghawi + as-Sa'di on "let no pity seize you":
  it forbids a judge suspending a PROVEN sentence once the case reaches the
  authority, not tenderness of heart (Baghawi: pity is involuntary, not
  forbidden; Ibn Kathir: the sheep-pity Companion was rewarded); mercy
  pervades the religion. Both at-Tabari readings kept with names.
- ta'ifa to witness: number views 1→1000 / 2 / 3 / 4 (Mujahid, Ata, Zuhri,
  Malik); two purposes, deterrence or du'a for the two (Qurtubi).

Married/stoning: carried only as the commentators' reported discussion
(as-Sa'di, Ibn Kathir, 'Umar's khutba, Ubada b. Samit; Qurtubi's jumhur vs
Ishaq/al-Hasan), names but no verdict in the article's own voice.

Hadith: Sahih al-Bukhari (quranx 660), the seven Allah shades — the man who
fears Allah and refuses; sound per the collector, chosen as a non-violent
anchor for the chastity lesson.

Dropped: exile/taghrib, the "woman named first" reasons, Qurtubi's general
zina-admonition narrations, asbab (none confirmed — placement given instead).
