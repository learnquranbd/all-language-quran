Card + 8-section bilingual article on the Horn and the coming in crowds.

Sources fetched for 78:18: at-Tabari, Ibn Kathir (ar + en), al-Qurtubi,
al-Baghawi, as-Sa'di, Muyassar, Ma'arif al-Qur'an; all named.
- Link to 78:17: at-Tabari says the "day" renders the Day of Decision;
  al-Qurtubi reads the second yawm as a substitute for the first.
- Sur: at-Tabari "a horn, in our view" vs Qatada "al-sur is al-khalq",
  kept as a disagreement without a position.
- Afwaja glosses, each attributed: zumar (Mujahid, at-Tabari, al-Baghawi,
  al-Qurtubi second); umam each with its imam (al-Qurtubi first,
  Muyassar); each nation with its messenger (at-Tabari, quoted by Ibn
  Kathir, 17:71); by deeds and character ("some scholars", Ma'arif).

Hadith: Bukhari 4935 (forty between the two blasts), placed by al-Bukhari
under this verse per Ibn Kathir, quoted whole. Tirmidhi 2430 (the sur is
a horn), quoted whole; graded hasan in the Arabic, hasan sahih in the
page's English, the lower kept.

Dropped: al-Qurtubi's Mu'adh report (ten kinds, «وروي», no collection,
no grading); Ma'arif's Abu Dharr narration (Nasa'i 2086, ungraded),
mentioned only as ungraded. No asbab.
Cross-refs by number only: 39:68, 39:73, 36:51, 20:102, 17:71, 11:104.
