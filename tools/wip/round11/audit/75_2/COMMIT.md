Card and 8-section bilingual article for 75:2, wa la uqsimu
bin-nafsil-lawwama: the second oath, whether it is an oath at all, what
the la does, the meanings of lawwama, and the oath's target in 75:3-6
(75:14 named as context only).

Sources: Tabari, Qurtubi, Sa'di, Baghawi, Ibn Kathir (ar 75:2; en grouped
75:1-10), Muyassar (grouped 75:2-4), Ma'arif (en grouped 75:2-3). All named.
- Oath or not kept as a disagreement: both oaths (Qatada; Ibn Abbas and Sa'id
  ibn Jubayr per the IK abridgement; Tha'labi in Qurtubi; Tabari; Baghawi)
  vs the Day only, "I do not swear by the soul" (al-Hasan via Qatada in
  Tabari; Qurtubi, incl. the reciter Ibn Kathir's reading of 75:1).
- la: connective (Baghawi), emphasis "la wallahi" (Abu Bakr ibn Ayyash),
  rebuttal of the deniers (Farra' in Baghawi; Tabari's preference with his
  speech argument), emphasis of a negation (IK abridgement); unranked.
- lawwama: believer's self-reproach (al-Hasan's "what did I mean by my
  words", Qurtubi's Ibn Abbas/Mujahid/Hasan, Muyassar, Ma'arif) vs every
  soul on the Day (al-Hasan via Juwaybir in IK ar; al-Farra'; Sa'di) vs the
  disbeliever's regret / blameworthy soul (Muqatil, Qatada "fajira", Ibn
  Abbas "madhmuma"); Qurtubi's link between the dispraise reading and
  denying the oath. Tabari's "close in meaning" verdict reported as his.
- Three-stage nafs scheme given only as Ma'arif's report of Sufi usage.
Hadith: none attached by any source; said so. No asbab.
Licensing sentence in EN and BN on the fajira/kafir readings.
Dropped: Qurtubi's unattributed Adam (AS) report, Alqama's burial remark,
Baghawi's truncated opening reading fragment.
