## Hadith
- Sahih al-Bukhari 3584 (Jabir b. 'Abdullah), https://quranx.com/hadith/Bukhari/DarusSalam/Hadith-3584, saved as hadith_Bukhari_3584.txt. First ten words: "The Prophet (ﷺ) used to stand by a tree or a". Grading: in al-Bukhari's Sahih; Ibn Kathir calls the report mutawatir. Ibn Kathir names no collection; 3584 chosen because its wording ("تئن أنين الصبي الذي يسكن... على ما كانت تسمع من الذكر") matches his. Marked in the article as a comparison, not an occasion. Quoted whole in the Darussalam English; the translator's bracketed glosses "(ﷺ)" after "Messenger", "(for delivering the sermon)", "(the pulpit)", "(missing)" omitted. Also checked 3583 and 3585 (not used).
- Al-Hasan al-Basri's remark: attributed only to Ibn Kathir's "in some narrations"; no collection or grading claimed.
