Card and 8-section bilingual article for 85:10: inna alladhina fatanu
al-mu'minina wa-l-mu'minat thumma lam yatubu, 14 Arabic words (8 + 6).
85:4-9 named by number, story not retold; 85:11 named only.

Sources: Tabari, Ibn Kathir (ar; en abridged), Muyassar, Qurtubi,
Sa'di, Baghawi, Ma'arif. Slot check clean; no re-fetch.
- Fatanu: burned (Ibn 'Abbas, Qatadah, Dahhak, Ibn Abza; Qurtubi,
  Muyassar), tried by torment and burning (Tabari), tormented (Mujahid
  in Tabari's chain; IK lists him under burned, difference kept),
  tormented and burned (Baghawi), persecuted (Ma'arif). Qurtubi's
  coin-in-furnace usage; Baghawi's parallel 51:13.
- Scope: Qurtubi, Baghawi, Ma'arif tie it to the Ditch; Tabari, Sa'di,
  Muyassar silent; none generalises. Ma'arif: clause invites repentance.
- Lam yatubu: desist and regret (IK); from kufr and the deed (Tabari);
  Sa'di "offered them repentance"; Ma'arif restrictive phrase.
- Al-Hasan's remark in three variants (IK, Sa'di, Ma'arif), each as given.
- Two punishments: Hell hereafter / burning in this world (Tabari,
  al-Rabi'; Qurtubi view 1; Baghawi via al-Rabi' ibn Anas and al-Kalbi);
  added hereafter punishment, al-Hariq a name of Jahannam, cold then heat
  (Qurtubi wa-qila); emphasis or worldly (Ma'arif); like for like (IK).
  None chosen.
Licensing sentence in both languages; no living group or conflict named.
Hadith: none attached to 85:10; Muslim's boy-and-king left for 85:4.
Dropped: Dhu Nuwas and Ditch narratives (85:4-9 material), 9:126 and
16:110 (not cited), any gloss on naming men and women (not in sources).
