# SOURCES LEDGER: 70:40

## Tafsir fetched (all from PREP.md list; none re-fetched)
IDs read: 15 at-Tabari, 90 al-Qurtubi, 91 as-Sa'di, 14 Ibn Kathir (ar), 169 Ibn Kathir (en, grouped 70:36-44; whole file read), 94 al-Baghawi, 16 Muyassar, 168 Ma'arif (en, grouped 70:33-42).
Named in the article: all eight. Ma'arif named only to say its fetched text ends the surah commentary at 70:33 and says nothing on 70:40.

### Attributions and the clause each rests on
- Context 70:36-39 (rebuke; broke away group by group; 70:39 "despised semen"; repeating creation easier, "something that they themselves confess to"): t169 "Allah rebukes the disbelievers who, in the time of the Prophet, saw him and the guidance..." / "from despised semen" / "repeating the process is something easier than performing it the first time. This is something that they themselves confess to."
- la = sila, means aqsimu (al-Qurtubi): t90 «" فلا أقسم " أي أقسم . و " لا " صلة».
- la signals negation / rebuttal (Ibn Kathir): t14 «وتقدير الكلام : ليس الأمر كما يزعمون أن لا معاد ولا حساب ، ولا بعث ولا نشور ، بل كل ذلك واقع وكائن لا محالة . ولهذا أتى ب " لا " في ابتداء القسم ليدل على أن المقسم عليه نفي ، وهو مضمون الكلام ، وهو الرد على زعمهم الفاسد في نفي يوم القيامة»; t169 "He is swearing by a denial of their claim". Kept beside Qurtubi as a difference, no position.
- Tabari, easts/wests of the earth: t15 «فلا أقسم بربّ مشارق الأرض ومغاربها».
- Qurtubi, sun's risings/settings; "already discussed": t90 «هي مشارق الشمس ومغاربها . وقد مضى الكلام فيها».
- Qurtubi variant reading (singular) by Abu Haywa, Ibn Muhaysin, Humayd: t90 «وقرأ أبو حيوة وابن محيصن وحميد " برب المشرق والمغرب " ... على التوحيد».
- Baghawi, every day of the year: t94 «يعني مشرق كل يوم من أيام السنة ومغربه»; adds nothing on «إنا لقادرون».
- Muyassar, swore by Himself; sun, moon, other kawakib; signs pointing to resurrection; qudra tamma: t16 «أقسم تعالى بنفسه، وهو رب المشارق والمغارب للشمس والقمر وسائر الكواكب؛ لما فيها من الآيات الباهرة الدالة على البعث، إنا لقادرون قدرة تامة».
- Sa'di, oath by the risings/settings of sun, moon, stars; signs on resurrection: t91 «هذا إقسام منه تعالى بالمشارق والمغارب، للشمس والقمر والكواكب، لما فيها من الآيات الباهرات على البعث».
- Ibn Kathir, Lord who created heavens/earth, made east and west, subjected stars: t14 «أي : الذي خلق السماوات والأرض ، وجعل مشرقا ومغربا ، وسخر الكواكب تبدو من مشارقها وتغيب في مغاربها».
- Ibn Kathir, greater power already witnessed; 40:57, 46:33, 36:81-82: t14 «وقد شاهدوا من عظيم قدرة الله تعالى ما هو أبلغ من إقامة القيامة ، وهو خلق السماوات والأرض ، وتسخير ما فيهما من المخلوقات من الحيوانات والجمادات ، وسائر صنوف الموجودات» and the three citations, then «وقال هاهنا : ( فلا أقسم برب المشارق والمغارب إنا لقادرون )».
- 70:41 readings (future ground, used only because IK ties it to this oath): t169 "on the Day of Judgement We will bring them back (to life) in bodies that are better than these bodies" with 75:3-4 and 56:60-61; "Ibn Jarir preferred the meaning to be: `a nation who will obey Us and not disobey Us.'" with 47:38; "However, the first interpretation is more obvious since the other Ayat support that, and Allah the Most High knows best." Tabari's own 70:41 text NOT fetched; attributed as Ibn Kathir's report of Ibn Jarir. Both kept; IK's preference reported as his; article takes none.

Parallels verified in data/quran-json: 40:57, 46:33, 36:81, 36:82, 75:3, 75:4, 56:60, 56:61, 47:38, 70:38, 70:39, 70:41 (all cited by fetched tafsirs or the context).

## Hadith
None. No fetched tafsir attaches a hadith to 70:40 (stated in one sentence). The Jabir ibn Samura narration in t169 ("ما لي أراكم عزين") is attached to 70:37's 'izin; mentioned by narrator only, marked as not attached; no wording or number quoted. hadith.js not run.

## Asbab
None given by any fetched source; not used. Verse read in its placement after 70:36-39.

## Sensitivity
Licensing sentence in both languages (§6): the verses describe people of the Prophet's time; licenses nothing against any living person or community; no labelling, no "replaced group" claim, no sorting into saved/lost.
