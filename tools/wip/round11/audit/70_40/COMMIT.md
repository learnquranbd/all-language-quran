Card and 8-section bilingual article for 70:40: fa-la uqsimu bi-rabbi
al-mashariqi wa-l-magharibi inna la-qadirun. 70:36-39 as the frame;
70:41 named only as Ibn Kathir ties it to the oath.

Sources: Tabari, Qurtubi, Ibn Kathir (ar; en grouped 70:36-44), Sa'di,
Baghawi, Muyassar, Ma'arif (grouped; nothing on this verse). All named.
- The added la kept as a disagreement: Qurtubi, sila meaning aqsimu;
  Ibn Kathir, it marks the oath as a rebuttal of their denial of the
  return. No position.
- The plural in each source's words: Tabari, the earth's easts and
  wests; Qurtubi, the sun's; Baghawi, every day of the year; Muyassar
  and Sa'di, sun, moon and stars; Ibn Kathir, the stars rising and
  setting. Qurtubi's singular reading (Abu Haywa, Ibn Muhaysin, Humayd).
- Why the horizons: Muyassar and Sa'di, signs pointing to resurrection;
  Ibn Kathir, the greater creation already witnessed (40:57, 46:33,
  36:81-82).
- inna la-qadirun with 70:41: Ibn Kathir's better bodies on the Day
  (75:3-4, 56:60-61) beside Ibn Jarir's obedient nation (47:38), with
  IK's stated preference reported as his; article takes no side.
Hadith: none attached by any fetched tafsir (Jabir ibn Samura belongs
to 70:37). Asbab: none. Licensing sentence in both languages.
Dropped: summer/winter risings and horizons glosses (not in sources),
comparison with 55:17, 73:9, 37:5, 26:28 (not cited), Qurtubi's earlier
discussion (not fetched), emphasis grammar (not in sources).
