Replaces the 854-word, 6-section article with a 1,767-word, 9-section
one, sourced from at-Tabari, Ibn Kathir, al-Qurtubi, as-Sa'di, al-Baghawi,
al-Muyassar and Ma'arif on 2:8, 2:9 and 2:10. The shipped card is kept.

Placement: Mujahid's 4/2/13 count (al-Qurtubi, at-Tabari) and al-Qurtubi's
reason for the order. Occasion only as the tafsirs give it: at-Tabari's
consensus, Ibn Abbas on the Aws and Khazraj hypocrites, Ibn Kathir's Ibn
Ubayy history, al-Baghawi's three names. The verse names nobody, and a
licensing paragraph says so in both languages, with at-Tabari's rule of
outward rulings (via al-Qurtubi).
yukhadi'una Allah: al-Qurtubi's three readings (al-Hasan: the Messenger),
al-Baghawi with 33:57, Ma'arif, Ibn Kathir with 58:18. At-Tabari refuses
"they deceived the believers". Readings of the second verb: Nafi', Ibn
Kathir and Abu Amr against Asim, Hamza, al-Kisa'i and Ibn Amir. At-Tabari
prefers yakhda'una; Ibn Kathir says both mean the same.
marad glosses: doubt, hypocrisy, riya', each with its holders. The increase
comes through new ordinances (at-Tabari, 9:124-125), in kind (Ibn Kathir,
47:17), and as sin after sin (as-Sa'di). Statement or supplication is kept
as al-Qurtubi's unnamed "it is said" pair; the others read a statement.
yakdhibun/yukadhdhibun: Kufa against Madinah, Hijaz and Basra (at-Tabari,
who argues for yakdhibun from 63:1); Ibn Kathir keeps both.
Hadith: Bukhari 33 (signs of the hypocrite), via as-Sa'di, marked as general.
No asbab page; no sound hadith attached to the verses.

Left out as labelling risk: the "most were Jews" lines and al-Qurtubi's
licence to supplicate against hypocrites. Dropped from the old article:
the unsourced emphatic-particle claim, its kana-habit grammar, and its
misattribution of Ibn Zayd's "religion, not body" to the early
commentators. Its missing yukadhdhibun reading is now added.
