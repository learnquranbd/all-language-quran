## Dropped or not used (fetched but left out, with reason)
- al-Baghawi «وأكثرهم من اليهود», and at-Tabari's and Ibn Kathir's lines tying the hypocrites to the Jewish rabbis / People of the Book: left out deliberately. They would turn a portrait into a label on a living community.
- al-Qurtubi's inference, under the supplication view, «دليل على جواز الدعاء على المنافقين»: left out for the same reason. The two views themselves are kept.
- The fiqh debate in al-Qurtubi and Ibn Kathir on why the Prophet ﷺ did not kill the hypocrites, and on the zindiq: out of scope and a licensing risk. Only at-Tabari's outward-rulings principle and the burial are used.
- The Sahihayn report "lest people say Muhammad kills his companions": cited by Ibn Kathir and al-Qurtubi but not fetched, so its number is unconfirmed. Not used.
- al-Qurtubi's «لا تخادع الله...» narration (no chain or grading in the text) and the Abu Razin report (al-Qurtubi: «سنده ليس بالقوي»): not used.
- Cut for length, all confirmed:
  - at-Tabari's definition of the deception (to escape killing and captivity)
  - the Arab saying in al-Qurtubi
  - Ibn Zayd on "harmed themselves" and on "a disease in religion, not bodies"
  - al-Baghawi's root of marad as weakness
  - as-Sa'di's two diseases
  - Ibn Kathir's points that Makkah had no hypocrisy and that no Emigrant was among them
  - as-Sa'di's post-Badr dating
  - Qatadah, Mujahid and as-Suddi as further holders of the occasion
- Also not used: al-Qurtubi's khad' roots (fasad, concealment), Abu Hatim's gloss of yukadhdhibun, Junayd and the arbab al-ma'ani glosses, Ma'arif on hypocrisy as a physical disease, and al-Baghawi's imala note.
- Avoided: 4:142 and the Bukhari two-faced hadith, both used on the shipped 2:14-16 page.

## Old article (854 words, 6 sections): points dropped or found wrong
- "The commentators draw the obvious lesson from the arithmetic: the concealed disease needed the longest description." No fetched commentator says this. Dropped; the count itself is kept, attributed to Mujahid.
- "an emphatic particle pressed into the denial" (wa ma hum bi-mu'minin). Not in any fetched text. Ibn Kathir's emphasis note is about the hypocrites' own claims (inna/lam in 63:1, and their claim here), not about the denial. Dropped.
- "Ibn Kathir relates their view that this is a disease in the religion rather than the body." This is wrong as attributed: the religion-not-body line is Ibn Zayd's alone (Ibn Kathir and at-Tabari both give it as his). The early glosses are doubt, hypocrisy and riya'. Corrected.
- "kana with the present verb marking continuous, habitual practice" and "the punishment attaches to a habit, not an act". Not in fetched tafsir. Dropped. The habit-of-lying point is now Ma'arif's, attributed.
- The old article gave only "lie" for bima kanu yakdhibun. It omitted the yukadhdhibun reading, which at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi, Ibn Kathir and al-Baghawi all record. Now added.
- "a verb form that pictures deception as an attempted contest". This is close to at-Tabari's view that mufa'ala needs two parties, but the old article stated it unattributed. Replaced by the attributed views.
- Editorial passages (mask grows comfortable; the mirror and audit list; "smaller gaps"). Not sourced. Replaced by the Bukhari 33 self-check and as-Sa'di's rule.
- Kept because confirmed: the 4/2/13 count, the Madinan setting after the believers grew strong, 63:1, recompense in kind, and the continuation into 2:11 to 2:14.
