Replaces the 825-word, 6-section article with an 8-section, 1,797-word
source-gated article (shipped card kept).

Sources: at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi, as-Sa'di, Ibn Kathir (ar; en abridged,
grouped 57:16-17), al-Baghawi, Muyassar, Ma'arif (57:16-18). Placement
after the wall of 57:12-15 via as-Sa'di. A-lam ya'ni glosses (at-Tabari,
Muyassar, as-Sa'di, al-Qurtubi with al-Hasan's alamma); al-Khalil's
definition of 'itab and al-Hasan's "slow, yet most beloved" (al-Qurtubi).
Takhsha'a as soften, submit, obey across five commentators and Ma'arif;
nazala/nazzala qira'a (at-Tabari, al-Baghawi).
Disagreements kept: four years (Muslim) vs thirteen years (Ibn 'Abbas
via Ibn Kathir; Emigrants vs believers in al-Baghawi/al-Qurtubi);
addressees as hypocrites (al-Kalbi, Muqatil, as-Suddi), believers, or
believers in Musa and 'Isa (al-Qurtubi, from 57:19); wa la yakunu as
coordination or prohibition (Ruways's la takunu); al-amad as time since
Musa, ad-dahr (Mujahid), or the wait for the Prophet (Muqatil ibn
Hayyan); fasiqun in five readings, with al-Qurtubi's group that stayed
firm and believed. Licensing sentence in both languages.
Ibn Mas'ud's horn report (at-Tabari) as his own words; 57:17 via Ibn
Kathir; Abu Musa (al-Baghawi, no chain) and al-Fudayl (al-Qurtubi);
5:13, 12:3, 39:23, 57:19 as the tafsirs cite them.

Hadith: Muslim 3027 (Ibn Mas'ud, four years), quoted whole. Shaddad's
"first thing lifted is khushu'" kept as at-Tabari's Qatada report with
both chains, no collection or grading.

Dropped: the joking report, "Allah finds you slow in khushu'", and the
Makkah-hardship/Madinah-ease accounts (unconfirmable); from the old
article the comfort framing, 2:74 and unsourced reflections. Fixed its
claim that "nothing dramatic happened" to those given the Book (Ibn
Kathir names alteration of the Book as the cause).
