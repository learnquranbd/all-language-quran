Replaces the 646-word, 6-section article with a 1,755-word, 9-section deep article built only from the eight tafsir files fetched for 42:25. The shipped card is kept.

Sources: at-Tabari, Ibn Kathir (ar, en abridged), al-Qurtubi, as-Sa'di,
al-Baghawi, al-Muyassar, Ma'arif al-Qur'an.
- Placement: 42:23-24 in two lines; al-Qurtubi's unchained Ibn 'Abbas report
  (the suspicious repent, then 42:25) with his "the verse is general"; 42:26
  as two readings (as-Suddi/Ibn Jarir: He answers; as-Sa'di: they respond).
- Tawba as return to tawhid (at-Tabari, Muyassar), root sense (Ma'arif), two
  definitions (al-Baghawi, Sahl ibn 'Abd Allah).
- 'Ibadihi: Ibn 'Abbas "His awliya'" vs al-Qurtubi "general"; 9:104 on
  al-Qurtubi's back-reference to Bara'a. Ibn Mas'ud's answer by this verse
  (at-Tabari, Ibn Kathir).
- Conditions: as-Sa'di (quit, regret, resolve, for His face); Ma'arif (rights
  of others, partial repentance).
- Ya'fu kept as differing glosses: sins repented of (at-Tabari, al-Baghawi),
  shirk before Islam (al-Qurtubi), past vs future (Ibn Kathir); as-Sa'di's
  erasure of trace and penalty; Ibn Kathir's four verbs with 4:110.
- Qira'a taf'alun/yaf'alun: at-Tabari (both correct, prefers ya), al-Qurtubi
  (Abu 'Ubayd, Abu Hatim), al-Baghawi.
- Knowledge clause as three readings: pardon with full knowledge (Ibn Kathir),
  warning (at-Tabari, Muyassar), sincerity known only to Him (as-Sa'di).
Hadith: Muslim 2747 (Anas, the lost mount), attached by Ibn Kathir and
al-Baghawi, quoted whole; Ibn Mas'ud and Abu Hurayra versions only mentioned.
Dropped from the old article: the imperfect-tense, 'an-colouring and iltifat
claims (no fetched source; the ya reading has no shift of person), 39:53,
66:8 and the daily-account advice.
