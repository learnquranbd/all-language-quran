Replaces the 689-word, 6-section article with a 1,758-word, 8-section deep article, built only on tafsir fetched for 110:3 (shipped card kept).

Sources: at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi, Ibn Kathir (ar; en abridged for the 110:1-3
group), al-Baghawi, as-Sa'di, al-Muyassar, Ma'arif al-Qur'an; all slots open
on 110:3, none re-fetched.
- sabbih bi-hamdi: at-Tabari (praise and thanks for the promise kept; you will
  then join Him); al-Qurtubi's two readings, pray vs tanzih, his "the first is
  more apparent"; as-Sa'di's thanks with 14:7 and the continuing help.
- al-fath: Ibn Kathir's conquest of Makkah "single view" (tribes waiting;
  Bukhari 4302 referenced) kept beside at-Tabari's chain where the elders
  answer "cities and palaces".
- Why istighfar: al-Qurtubi's question with "ma'sum", his answers (humility
  before the favour, attachment, worship for its own sake, alert to the ummah,
  for the ummah), al-Hasan (al-Baghawi) and as-Sa'di on sealing; none chosen.
- tawwab: at-Tabari, al-Muyassar, al-Qurtubi.
- Timing kept as reports: an-Nasa'i "last surah" (via Ibn Kathir), 'Ata' b.
  Yasar (Madinah after the conquest), al-Qurtubi/Ibn 'Umar (Mina, Farewell
  Hajj, then 5:3); two years / sixty / eighty days, not settled.
Hadith quoted whole: al-Bukhari 4970 ('Umar and the elders of Badr), 4968
and 4967 ('A'isha, bowing and prostration), Muslim 484 ('A'isha, "a sign has
been set for me"); no grading beyond the Sahih collections.
Dropped: Ahmad reports (unreachable), Umm Salama (gharib), Abu Hurayra and
'Ikrima (no chain), Tirmidhi's grading as relayed. From the old article: the
tasbih/hamd definition, 2:199, the kana gloss, the unsourced habit.
