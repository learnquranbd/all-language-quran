# SOURCES ledger — 23:78

## Tafsir fetched (all pre-fetched in audit/23_78/, on 23:78 unless noted)

- **Ibn Kathir (ar, 14)** — USED. Arabic clause relied on: "جعل لهم السمع والأبصار
  والأفئدة، وهي العقول والفهوم، التي يدركون بها الأشياء، ويعتبرون بما في الكون من
  الآيات الدالة على وحدانية الله" (afʾidah = intellects/understandings; ear+eye
  perceive things and take lessons from the signs pointing to Allah's oneness).
  Also "وما أقل شكركم" and the cross-ref to 12:103 "وما أكثر الناس... بمؤمنين".
- **Ibn Kathir (en abridged, 169)** — USED (grouped 23:76–83). Clause: "He has given
  them hearing, sight and understanding through which they come to know things and
  draw lessons from them, the signs which attest to the Oneness of Allah"; "Little
  thanks you give... how little you thank." Cross-ref 12:103 confirmed here too.
- **at-Tabari (15)** — USED. Clause: "أحدث لكم أيها المكذّبون بالبعث بعد الممات
  السمعَ... والأبصَارَ... والأفئدة التي تفقهون بها، فكيف يتعذّر على من أنشأ ذلك
  ابتداء إعادته بعد عدمه" (address to resurrection-deniers; the One who originated
  the faculties can restore the dead). Also "تشكرون... خير الله... قليلا".
- **al-Muyassar (16)** — USED. Clause: "أنشأ لكم السمع لإدراك المسموعات، والأبصار
  لإدراك المرئيات، والأفئدة لتفقهوا بها... فشكركم لهذه النعم المتوالية عليكم قليل لا
  يُذْكَر" (little, not worth mentioning).
- **al-Qurtubi (90)** — USED. Clauses: "عرفهم كثرة نعمه وكمال قدرته"; "قليلا ما
  تشكرون أي ما تشكرون إلا شكرا قليلا. وقيل: أي لا تشكرون البتة" (the two readings:
  only a little / reported view: not at all — kept as a difference).
- **as-Sa'di (91)** — USED. Clauses: "بمننه على عباده الداعية لهم إلى شكره، والقيام
  بحقه"; sight "لتنتفعوا... في مصالحكم"; "الأفئدة أي: العقول... وتتميزون بها عن
  البهائم"; the counterfactual "فلو عدمتم السمع والأبصار والعقول... ماذا تكون
  حالكم؟"; "أفلا تشكرون... فتقومون بتوحيده وطاعته؟... ولكنكم قليل شكركم مع توالي
  النعم".
- **al-Baghawi (94)** — USED. Clause: "أنشأ لكم... لتسمعوا وتبصروا وتعقلوا... لم
  تشكروا هذه النعم" (to hear, see, reason; did not thank).
- **Ma'arif (en, 168)** — read; NOT cited. The grouped text (23:76–85) discusses only
  the 23:76 Abu Sufyan/Makkah-famine occasion (Mazhari); nothing on 23:78 itself.

## Hadith
- **Sahih al-Bukhari, Hadith 660** (Dar-us-Salam). URL fetched:
  quranx.com/hadith/Bukhari/DarusSalam/Hadith-660 (also USC-MSA Vol.1, Bk.11, H.629).
  First ten words used: "Allah will give shade to seven on the Day". Grading: Sahih
  al-Bukhari — placement in the Sahih is its grading; not upgraded.
  **Explicitly marked GENERAL / NOT attached to 23:78** by any fetched tafsir; cited
  only as a portrait of the faculties (heart→mosque, eyes→tears, youth in worship)
  spent on their purpose. No fetched tafsir attaches any hadith to 23:78 — stated in
  the article (section 7, para 1).

## Asbab an-nuzul
- NOT used for 23:78. No asbab established for this verse in the fetched sources; the

## Dropped / not asserted
- Any connection to 16:78, 32:9, 67:23 — DROPPED. No fetched tafsir on 23:78 cites
  them, so per the brief they are not claimed.
- Any tafsir *remark* on the order (hearing before sight) — none of the fetched
  commentators comment on the sequence, so the order is noted only as a plain
  observation of the verse's own wording, attributed to no mufassir.
- The fuʾād-as-emotion sense — dropped; the fetched commentators are unanimous that
  afʾidah here = intellect/reason (ʿuqūl / tafqahūn / taʿqilū).
