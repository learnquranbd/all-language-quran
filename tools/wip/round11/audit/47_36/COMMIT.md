Card and 8-section bilingual article for 47:36: innama l-hayatu
d-dunya la'ibun wa lahw, wa in tu'minu wa tattaqu yu'tikum ujurakum
wa la yas'alkum amwalakum. Placed after 47:35; 47:37-38 left to them.

Sources: Tabari, Ibn Kathir (ar; en for bracket and Qatadah on 47:37),
Muyassar, Qurtubi, Sa'di, Baghawi, Ma'arif.
- Setting: Tabari and Ma'arif read it as the 47:35 call continuing;
  Ibn Kathir tahqir/tahwin, Sa'di tazhid.
- La'ib/lahw: Sa'di bodies/hearts; Baghawi batil wa ghurur; Muyassar
  la'ib wa ghurur; Qurtubi points back to al-An'am (6:32).
- Exception "except what was for Allah": Ibn Kathir, Tabari; Sa'di at
  the term's end.
- Taqwa per Sa'di, Tabari, Muyassar; Baghawi al-fawahish.
- Ujur: Tabari's day of poverty, Baghawi, Muyassar, Sa'di, Ma'arif.
- La yas'alkum amwalakum kept as a disagreement: not all (Ibn
  'Uyaynah via Qurtubi and Baghawi, Muyassar, Ma'arif); no need of it
  (Ibn Kathir, Qurtubi qila, Baghawi, Ma'arif/Thanawi, 51:57);
  tawhid instead (Tabari); His own wealth (Qurtubi qila); Muhammad ﷺ
  asks no wage (Qurtubi, Baghawi, 25:57); no harmful burden (Sa'di).
  No pick. Ibn Kathir en bracket "(to sacrifice all of)" flagged as
  the translator's.
Hadith: none attached by any fetched tafsir; said so.
No condemned group in the verse; no licensing sentence.
Dropped: 29:64, 57:20 (uncited); Ma'arif's produce and livestock
rates; 47:37-38 development.
- Gate: one heading over six words and an EN-only "One" fixed.
