# SOURCES ledger — 26:186

Verse: وَمَآ أَنتَ إِلَّا بَشَرٌۭ مِّثْلُنَا وَإِن نَّظُنُّكَ لَمِنَ ٱلْكَٰذِبِينَ
al-Aykah to Shuʿayb (AS): "You are but a man like ourselves, and indeed we think you among the liars."

## Tafsir fetched (all in audit/26_186/, from PREP; none re-fetched)

- **t15 at-Tabari** — USED. basharun mithlunā glossed تأكل وتشرب ("you eat and
  drink [like us]"). naẓunnuka glossed: وما نحسبك فيما تخبرنا وتدعونا إليه إلا ممن
  يكذب فيما يقول ("we only reckon you, in what you tell us and call us to, among
  those who lie in what they say") — the vocabulary of naḥsabu, a hunch. Used in
  §1, §4.
- **t16 al-Muyassar** — USED. وما أنت إلا واحد مثلنا في البشرية، فكيف تختص دوننا
  بالرسالة؟ ("how are you singled out, rather than us, with the message?"); وإن
  أكبر ظننا أنك من الكاذبين فيما تدَّعيه من الرسالة ("our greatest supposition is
  that you lie in what you claim of messengership"). Used in §2, §4. (File also
  covers 26:187 — the sky-fragment demand was NOT used; future ground.)
- **t91 as-Saʿdi** — USED, the spine of the article. فليس فيك فضيلة اختصصت بها
  علينا ("no merit in you by which you were singled out above us"); this shubha
  raised by "those before them and after them… because of their agreement upon
  kufr and the likeness of their hearts"; the messengers' answer quoted —
  إِنْ نَحْنُ إِلَّا بَشَرٌ مِثْلُكُمْ وَلَكِنَّ اللَّهَ يَمُنُّ عَلَى مَنْ يَشَاءُ
  (14:11); نظنك = جراءة منهم وظلم وقول زور قد انطووا على خلافه ("audacity,
  injustice, false speech, which they had folded over its opposite"); every
  messenger given signs by which people يتيقنون his truth and trust; **خصوصا
  شعيبا… الذي يسمى خطيب الأنبياء** ("the orator of the prophets") for debating his
  people بالتي هي أحسن; his people تيقنوا صدقه; and their report of suspicion is
  itself كذب منهم ("a lie from them"). Used in §2, §3, §5, §6, §7 (closing tie).
- **t90 al-Qurtubi** — USED. ما نظنك إلا من الكاذبين في أنك رسول الله ("we suspect
  you a liar only in [the claim] that you are Allah's messenger") — pins the
  alleged lie to the office. Used in §4 as the narrow reading.
- **t14 Ibn Kathir (ar)** — USED. تتعمد الكذب فيما تقوله، لا أن الله أرسلك إلينا
  ("you deliberately lie in what you say — not that Allah sent you to us"). Used
  in §4 as the wider reading that also denies the Sender.
- **t169 Ibn Kathir (en, abridged)** — USED only for the 26:186 clause, which
  matches t14 ("we think you are deliberately lying… and Allah has not sent you").
  The rest of this grouped file (26:187 kisaf / 26:189 Day of Shadow, the
  seven-days punishment, the Ibn ʿAbbās report via Ibn Jarīr) was DELIBERATELY NOT
  used — it is the future ground the brief forbids preempting.
- **t94 al-Baghawi** — NOT USED. File contains only the verse text, no comment, so
  nothing is attributed to al-Baghawi.
- **t168 Maʿarif al-Qurʾan (en)** — NOT USED for 26:186. Despite the grouped
  header, its text is entirely about 26:183 (short measure / the labourer, the
  ʿUmar–Muwaṭṭaʾ ṭaffafta narration). That content belongs to the shipped 26:183;
  not reused here.

## Hadith

- **Sahih al-Bukhari 6066**, Abu Hurayra (RA). Fetched:
  https://quranx.com/hadith/Bukhari/DarusSalam/Hadith-6066
  (Dar-us-Salam 6066; USC-MSA Vol 8, Book 73, Hadith 92).
  First ten words (EN used): "Beware of suspicion, for suspicion is the worst of
  false…" (rendered "the most false of speech"; Arabic فإن الظن أكذب الحديث).
  Grading: in Sahih al-Bukhari → ṣaḥīḥ by the collector's placement; not upgraded.
  **General narration, expressly marked in §7 (both languages) as NOT revealed
  about Shuʿayb or al-Aykah** and not to be tied to them; applied thematically —
  ẓann is akdhab al-ḥadīth, the exact instrument al-Aykah used to brand a truthful
  man. Not among the nearby stock hadith the brief bars (Bukhari 660; 3443 at
  26:123; 4770/4771 at 26:214).

## Disagreement kept (not resolved)

- What the alleged lie fastens onto: al-Qurtubi (and al-Muyassar) narrow it to the
  claim of messengership/risāla; at-Tabari spreads it over all Shuʿayb told and
  called them to; Ibn Kathir widens it a second way, to deny that Allah sent him.
  Presented in §4 as complementary shades resting on one base (ẓann), with no
  position taken.

## Condemned-people rule

- §1/§8 state in both languages that the verse records what one condemned people
  said and that the story of al-Aykah licenses nothing against any living person
  or community — it is a mirror for the reader.
