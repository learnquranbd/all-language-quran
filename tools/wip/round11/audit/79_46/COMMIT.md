Replaces the 706-word, 5-section article with a 1,742-word, 8-section deep article, built only on tafsir fetched for 79:46 (shipped card kept).

Sources: at-Tabari, Ibn Kathir (ar), al-Qurtubi, al-Baghawi, al-Muyassar
(79:43-46 group), Ma'arif al-Qur'an (79:40-46 group). As-Sa'di fetch failed;
Ibn Kathir (en) was the wrong group; neither used.

Frame: 79:42-45 only as al-Muyassar (asked mockingly; no knowledge of it with
the Messenger; warn whoever fears it) and Ma'arif (the date is Allah's
prerogative) give it; licensing sentence on the deniers in both languages.
Glosses held by name: Ibn Abbas via ad-Dahhak on 'ashiyyah and duha limits
(Ibn Kathir, al-Muyassar), and his "one day" (al-Qurtubi); al-Farra' on the
forenoon annexed to the evening, the Banu 'Uqayl line (at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi,
al-Baghawi); at-Tabari's "end of a day or its beginning"; Qatada's "the time
of the world in the eyes of those who beheld the hereafter"; hawl as the
cause (at-Tabari, al-Muyassar, al-Qurtubi).
Disagreement kept: stay in the world (most) vs in the graves (al-Qurtubi,
al-Baghawi, both unnamed qila), no position.
46:35 via al-Qurtubi and al-Baghawi; 79:29 and 79:40 one line each.

Hadith: none attached by any fetched tafsir; said in one sentence.
Asbab: none.

Dropped from the old article: 10:45, 30:55, 20:103-104, 23:112-114 (no
fetched tafsir cites them), the budgeting and waiting reflections, the claim
that neither word can mean a day (against Ibn Abbas's "one day" in
al-Qurtubi). Old BN used Latin digits and hyphen ranges.
