## Disagreement kept (no position taken by the article)
- Accusative of shayban: verbal noun (some Basrans per at-Tabari; al-Akhfash per al-Qurtubi) vs specification/tamyiz (another grammarian per at-Tabari; az-Zajjaj per al-Qurtubi). An-Nahhas's preference for al-Akhfash reported as his; at-Tabari chooses neither.
- bi-du'a'ika: my calling You (at-Tabari, Ibn Jurayj, Ibn Kathir, al-Qurtubi, as-Sa'di, Muyassar, al-Baghawi's main gloss) vs Your calling me to faith (al-Baghawi's qila, unpreferred).
