## "Then where is the Fire?" (watch-point)
Every version and grading is as the fetched text gives it, none added: Heraclius' letter (at-Tabari 7831, Ibn Kathir from Ahmad, also al-Qurtubi) — no grading in Ibn Kathir's tafsir; at-Tabari editor's footnote judges Muslim ibn Khalid weak. 'Umar and the Jews / men of Najran (at-Tabari 7832-7835) — no grading stated, none given. Ibn 'Abbas (7836) — footnote: chain to him sahih, mawquf. Abu Hurayrah marfu' (al-Bazzar via Ibn Kathir) — footnote: al-Hakim "sahih on the conditions of the two shaykhs", adh-Dhahabi agreed (said of al-Hakim's route, so the article says "his version"). Ibn Kathir's preference ("وهذا أظهر") reported. His second meaning (day on one side of the world, Garden above, Fire below) not used: cosmology kept out of the article.

## Points from the shipped article
- Kept (confirmed): maghfirah before Jannah (Ma'arif); 57:21; Uhud setting (only as at-Tabari/Ibn Ishaq on 3:132); first mark is spending (al-Baghawi); kazm = filling/tying a waterskin (at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi); "not the one who wrestles" (Bukhari 6114); the Sahl ibn Mu'adh hadith; ability as part of kazm (at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi); three rising stages (now attributed to Ma'arif, Ibn Kathir, as-Sa'di, with at-Tabari/al-Muyassar's different reading kept); 41:34 dropped (Ma'arif cites it, cut for length); bridge to 3:135.
- Dropped (no source): "the width is given so that no one fears the Garden filling up"; "a race for limited seats breeds rivalry"; "Money is ... the entry-level test of sincerity"; "Allah loves ... stated as a fact rather than a promise"; "read as a ladder, the verse is merciful to beginners"; "buy ten seconds — the interval kazm actually occupies"; "the commentators note" (unnamed).
- Wrong in the shipped text: (1) "given his choice of reward" — at-Tirmidhi (and Abu Dawud) say he is given his choice of the hur. (2) "the strong man is ... agreed upon in al-Bukhari and Muslim" was unconfirmed in shipped form; now Bukhari 6114 confirmed and the two-Sahih statement attributed to Ibn Kathir. (3) "Sari'u — race, hurry" as the verse's word: the majority reading is wa-sari'u; sari'u without waw is the reading of Nafi' and Ibn 'Amir (al-Qurtubi, al-Baghawi). (4) "the commentators note that maghfirah precedes Jannah because the soul must be cleared" — only Ma'arif says it, tentatively ("May be"), and gives a different reason (deeds cannot pay the price of Paradise).
