Replaces the 739-word, 5-section article with a 1,760-word, 8-section
one, from at-Tabari, Ibn Kathir, al-Qurtubi, as-Sa'di, al-Baghawi,
al-Muyassar and Ma'arif. The shipped card is kept.

Slots: at-Tabari's 3:133 sat under 3:134 and his 3:134 under 3:135
(fetched as t15_135); t15_133 is his 3:132, used only as such.
Context: Fire prepared / Garden prepared (Ibn Kathir); Ibn Ishaq's Uhud
note on 3:132 via at-Tabari; the without-waw reading of Nafi' and Ibn 'Amir.
Width: Ibn 'Abbas's joined garments (majority, per al-Qurtubi), width
implies length (al-Baghawi, az-Zuhri, as-Sa'di), dome (Ibn Kathir),
figure of speech (al-Qurtubi, al-Baghawi), 'ard as price (Abu Muslim via
Ma'arif). "Where is the Fire?": Heraclius, 'Umar, Ibn 'Abbas and Abu
Hurayrah versions, each with only the grading the fetched text states.
sarra'/darra', kazm, ghayz vs ghadab, 'an an-nas: glosses named.
Disagreement kept: muhsinin as the listed deeds themselves (at-Tabari,
Ibn Ishaq, al-Muyassar) or a higher stage (as-Sa'di, Ibn Kathir, Ma'arif's
three stages). Maymun ibn Mihran's slave girl (al-Qurtubi) and 'Ali ibn
al-Husayn (Ma'arif, from al-Bayhaqi), attributed.
Hadith: Bukhari 6114 (bis-sura'ah) and Tirmidhi 2021 (hasan gharib),
each quoted whole. One bridging line to 3:135-136.
Dropped: weak or unfetched hadith (at-Tabari 7842, Ahmad, al-Hakim,
"la taghdab"), cosmology and the created-now debate.
Corrected: "given his choice of reward" (the hadith says the hur);
"Sari'u" as the verse's word (minority reading); unsourced claims on the
Garden filling up and on ten seconds of kazm.
