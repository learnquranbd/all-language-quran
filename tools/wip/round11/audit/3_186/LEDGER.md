## Hadith

- Bukhari 4566, https://quranx.com/hadith/Bukhari/DarusSalam/Hadith-4566 (saved as
  hadith_Bukhari_4566.txt). EN first ten words: "Narrated Usama bin Zaid: Allah's Messenger (ﷺ)
  rode a donkey, equipped". AR: "أَنَّ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم رَكِبَ عَلَى حِمَارٍ عَلَى قَطِيفَةٍ".
  Grading: in al-Bukhari's Sahih; no other grading given or added. Quoted (Darussalam English,
  one sentence whole, "(ﷺ)" written as ﷺ): "So Allah's Messenger ﷺ
  excused him, for the Prophet ﷺ and his companions used to forgive the pagans and the people of
  Scripture as Allah had ordered them, and they used to put up with their mischief with
  patience." The rest is paraphrased and the article says it is a long hadith. Its placement at
  the tafsir of this verse is stated on Ibn Kathir's authority (t14). Al-Qurtubi's "fi
  as-Sahihayn" not repeated (Muslim number not fetched).

al-Baghawi, al-Qurtubi's note that it is in the siyar), al-Baghawi's Muhayyisah/Huwayyisah

this verse), Ibn Abi Hatim's abridged Usamah report, al-Qurtubi's remaining grammar
(no hamz on the waw; singular and dual forms) and his "this verse was revealed" after the Ibn
Ubayy pardon, Ma'arif's applied paragraph, as-Sa'di's 41:35 citation.

From the shipped 832-word, 5-section article:
- Wrong: "the aftermath of Uhud" framing; Ibn Kathir (t14/t169) places the address on arrival in
  Madinah before Badr. Dropped.
- Wrong: "Al-Wahidi and Ibn Kathir relate that the verse came down with ... Ka'b ibn al-Ashraf
  in view": al-Wahidi was not fetched, and Ibn Kathir (ar and en) does not mention Ka'b. The
  report is az-Zuhri's, carried by at-Tabari, al-Baghawi and al-Qurtubi. Corrected.
- Wrong: as-Sa'di "reads the pairing as the religion stated in short: bearing what is disliked,
  and staying inside what Allah permits": not in his text. He says taqwa within patience is
  intending Allah's face and not passing the lawful limit of patience. Replaced.
- Unsourced, dropped: adha as "harm that wounds without destroying" and "guaranteed not to be
  fatal" (3:111 kept as a bare cross-reference); 'azm as "the settled decision that cuts
  hesitation off" (replaced by the fetched glosses); "much of the insulting will come from
  people ... entrusted with a Book and hid it" (reads 3:187 back); "patience can curdle / taqwa
  keeps the silence honest"; "the commentators take anfus to cover body, health, family and
  life itself" (replaced by named glosses).
- Kept and now sourced: the emphatic lam and nun as oath (al-Baghawi); 31:17 and 42:43; the
  Usamah hadith (now with number and grading).
