  - `أَيَحْسَبُ هؤلاء الأحزاب الذين فرقوا دينهم زبرا` → the "they" = the factions of 23:53 (§2).
  - `و"ما" ... نصب؛ لأنها بمعنى الذي` → *mā* = relative *alladhī* (§1).

  - Mujahid via Tabari: `نُمِدُّهُمْ) قال: نعطيهم` → gloss "We give them" (background, not separately cited).

- **as-Saʿdi (91)** — USED. `أيظنون أن زيادتنا إياهم بالأموال والأولاد، دليل على أنهم من أهل الخير والسعادة ... ليس الأمر كذلك` → the "proof/dalīl" false-inference (§4). `نملي لهم ونمهلهم ونمدهم بالنعم، ليزدادوا إثما ... حتى إذا فرحوا بما أوتوا أخذناهم بغتة` → reprieve → sudden seizure, his quotation of 6:44 (§6).
- **Ibn Kathir (ar, 14)** and **Ibn Kathir (en, 169)** — USED (cross-checked ar↔en). Clauses:

  - `بل إنما نفعل بهم ذلك استدراجا وإنظارا وإملاء` → istidrāj + inẓār + imlāʾ (§3).

- **al-Muyassar (16, grouped 55-56)** — USED. `إنما نعجل لهم الخير فتنة لهم واستدراجًا، ولكنهم لا يُحِسُّون بذلك` → fitna + istidrāj; "do not sense it" (§1, §3, §8).

## Hadith (article §7)
- **Ibn Masʿūd (RA), recorded by Imam Ahmad (Musnad)**, as **cited by Ibn Kathir under this verse** (present verbatim in both t14_55.txt and t169_55.txt).
  - Wording used (whole, the verse-bearing portion): "Allah gives the things of this world to those whom He loves and those whom He does not love, but He gives religious commitment only to those whom He loves; so whoever Allah gives religious commitment, He has loved him."

  - Source: Musnad Ahmad, via Ibn Kathir's tafsir on 23:55 (t14_55.txt / t169_55.txt). No standalone hadith page fetched.
  - **Number:** none cited (not shown on a fetched standalone page) → collection only.
  - **Grading:** Ibn Kathir gives **none** here; none claimed or upgraded. Reported in-article as cited by Ibn Kathir, from Musnad Ahmad, ungraded — not one of the six books.
