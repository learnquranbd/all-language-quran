Read 23:55 with 23:56, since the question (do they think wealth and
children are Us hastening good to them?) is only answered in the next
verse: "Rather, they do not perceive." Card and lesson stay anchored on
23:55; 23:56 glossed as its completion.

Spine is istidrāj. The mufassirūn converge: what the deceived read as a
sign of God's approval is a drawing-on toward reckoning, not honour.
- at-Tabari: the "they" are the factions of 23:53; mā = relative alladhī;
  the extending is only imlāʾ and istidrāj.
- al-Qurtubi: address turned to the Prophet; not thawāb but istidrāj/imlāʾ.
- as-Saʿdi: they think increase is a proof (dalīl) they are people of
  good and happiness — it is not; reprieve runs until "when they rejoiced
  in what they were given, We seized them suddenly" (his citation of 6:44).
- Ibn Kathir: al-maghrūrūn; not for honour or nearness; strings 34:35,
  9:55, 3:178, 68:44-45, 74:11-16, 34:37, and Qatāda: judge people by
  faith and deeds, not wealth and children.
- al-Muyassar: fitna and istidrāj; "they do not sense it."

Hadith: Ibn Masʿūd via Musnad Ahmad, as Ibn Kathir cites it under this
verse — Allah gives the dunya to those He loves and does not love, but
gives the dīn only to those He loves. Quoted whole (verse-bearing
clause); no number on a standalone page, and Ibn Kathir gives no grading,
so none claimed.

Stated plainly in both languages that the verse condemns a false belief
about prosperity, not wealth or the wealthy, and licenses nothing against
any living person or community.

Dropped: Maʿarif (its fetched text covers only 23:53); al-Baghawi's bare
gloss; the off-topic remainder of the hadith. No asbāb (none established).
