Replaces the 759-word, 6-section article with a 1,768-word, 8-section
one, from at-Tabari, Ibn Kathir, al-Qurtubi, as-Sa'di, al-Baghawi,
al-Muyassar and Ma'arif on 2:281. All slots checked. The shipped card is kept.

Context: 2:278-2:280 as the verses say them, with no ruling; 2:275 and
2:284 one line each; 2:282 as a single bridging line. The seal on the
rulings (as-Sa'di), admonition (Ibn Kathir), completing faith (al-Muyassar).
Yawman as object, not adverb; the Day is Resurrection (majority) or death
(some), with Ibn 'Atiyya preferring the first (al-Qurtubi).
Readings: tarji'una (Abu 'Amr, Basra) and turja'una, with al-Qurtubi's
parallels 88:25, 6:62, 18:36; al-Hasan's ya reading and Ibn Jinni.
Full payment: at-Tabari (no isti'tab, nothing small or great left), as-Sa'di,
al-Qurtubi (kasb, against the Jabriyya; "wa hum" follows the meaning of kull).
Not wronged: at-Tabari (like for evil, ten for good), al-Muyassar, as-Sa'di.
The z-l-m echo with 2:279 is marked as textual, not drawn by the sources.
Last verse revealed, kept as a disagreement, no position: 2:281 (Ibn Abbas
by several routes, Sa'id ibn Jubayr, 'Atiyya/Abu Sa'id, as-Suddi, Ibn
'Umar); the verse of riba (ash-Sha'bi from Ibn Abbas); the debt verse (Ibn
al-Musayyib, mursal per the footnote); 9:128 (Ubayy, Ibn Abbas, Qatada);
4:176 (al-Bara'). Al-Qurtubi's preference and Ibn Hajar's reconciliation
(via the at-Tabari footnote) are attributed.
Time lived afterwards, all attributed, none chosen: nine nights, seven,
21 days, 31 days, three nights, three hours. The 280-verses placement
reports are included.
Hadith: Bukhari 4544 (Ibn Abbas, riba) and 4654 (al-Bara', kalala),
quoted whole. No sound marfu' hadith attaches to the verse.
Dropped: 'Umar's report and "81 days" (not in the sources); 5:3.
Corrected: "turja'una is passive" (Abu 'Amr reads it active); the riba
verse pinned to 2:278; own-voice harmonisation of the last-verse reports;
"2:282 the longest verse", 29:57, the wafa' root, the kasabat/iktasabat
contrast and 6:160 (all unsourced).
