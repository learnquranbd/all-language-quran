Card + 8-section bilingual article (1,542 EN words) on 37:45, the first
item of the cup circulated among the reclining believers: "yutafu
'alayhim bika'sin min ma'in."

Sources (all fetched for 37:45): at-Tabari (yutafu: servants circle with
flowing wine visible to their eyes, not sunken; Dahhak/Suddi "every ka's
in the Qur'an is wine"; his own rule that an empty vessel is not a ka's),
al-Qurtubi (same ka's rule; an-Nahhas on qadah/ma'ida named only when
filled; Ibn Kaysan's za'ina/howdah analogy; az-Zajjaj on min ma'in as
wine flowing like springs on the earth's surface), al-Baghawi (parallel
ka's rule; min ma'in as visibly flowing), as-Sa'di (the youths prepared
for service, "these are the cups of wine" — flagged in-article as his
own elaboration, not the bare ayah's wording), Ibn Kathir ar + abridged
en (min ma'in: flowing rivers, not fearing cutting off or running dry;
the en parallel to 56:17-19 used for one cross-reference only).

Kept narrow per brief: Ibn Kathir's and al-Muyassar's "white / delicious
/ no headache / no intoxication" material dropped — that glosses
37:46-47's own words, left as future ground. Ma'arif al-Qur'an fetched
but not used; its grouped text is entirely about 37:44's thrones, not
37:45.

Hadith: none attached by any fetched tafsir, and none flagged essential;
said once in the ledger. No stock Bukhari 660.

Defect guarded: §8 states plainly that a Paradise wine-reward scene
licenses nothing about wine in this life, which the Qur'an forbids
elsewhere — general knowledge in the article's own voice, not sourced to
one mufassir. No asbab; none established.
