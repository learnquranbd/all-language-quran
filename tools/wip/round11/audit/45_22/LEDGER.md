Watch-point 1 (wa li-tujza): no fetched text says what the "wa" joins. Qurtubi/Muyassar gloss li as li-kay only. Article says so and supplies no grammar theory (§4.2). Tabari's gloss worded with li- (للعدل) is reported as his paraphrase wording, with no rule drawn (§2.1).
