Card and 8-section bilingual article for 45:22: wa khalaqa Allahu
as-samawati wal-arda bil-haqq wa li-tujza kullu nafsin bima kasabat wa
hum la yuzlamun. Placed as the answer to 45:21, which serves only as
setting; 45:23 not developed.

Sources: Tabari, Qurtubi, Sa'di, Muyassar, Ibn Kathir (ar + en 45:22
line), Ma'arif (45:22 parts of its 45:21-22 group). Baghawi: verse
quoted, no comment, said so.
- Bil-haqq: justice (Ibn Kathir, Tabari, Muyassar), wisdom (Muyassar,
  Sa'di), truth (Tabari, Muyassar, Qurtubi's "true matter"); kept side
  by side, unranked.
- Tabari: not created for zulm and jawr; distinguishing wrongdoer and
  doer of good "in the near term and the later".
- Li-tujza: Qurtubi and Muyassar gloss wa li-kay, in the Hereafter.
  No text explains what the wa joins; said so, no grammar theory added.
- Kasabat: Tabari's three exclusions (good not cut short, no one
  punished for another's crime, no one honoured with another's good).
- La yuzlamun: Tabari and Muyassar "in the recompense of their deeds";
  Ma'arif "to wipe out wrong and injustice". No own-voice theology.
- Ma'arif: world as domain of deeds and test, not of requital; set
  beside Tabari as two emphases. Sa'di: the question at recompense is
  gratitude.
Hadith: none attached by any tafsir; said in one sentence.
Asbab: none in the sources; not used.
Licensing sentence for the 45:21 evildoers in both languages.
Dropped: Tamim ad-Dari report (45:21, at-Tabarani), 59:20 parallel and
Ma'arif's 45:21 argument, all 45:23 material, creation-in-truth
cross-refs (none cited by any tafsir on 45:22).
- Review: §6 EN heading changed from a transliteration to 'None of Them Wronged'.
