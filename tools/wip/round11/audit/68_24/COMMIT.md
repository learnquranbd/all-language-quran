Card and 8-section bilingual article for 68:24: an la yadkhulannaha
al-yawma 'alaykum miskin, the content of 68:23's whisper. 68:19 and
68:25 to 68:33 left to their own verses.

Sources: Ibn Kathir (ar), Tabari, Muyassar (grouped 68:23-24), Qurtubi,
Sa'di, Baghawi, Ma'arif (grouped 68:21-24); 68:24 files 62-395 chars, so
the same mufassirun were fetched once at 68:17 for the story, each use
attributed "at 68:17". Ibn Kathir (en) carried verse text only; not named.
- Prohibition aimed at the speakers: la tumakkinu (IK, Muyassar);
  miskin indefinite = ahadan min al-masakin (Muyassar); IK's faqir.
- Who the poor were: father told them to attend (Qurtubi 68:24);
  Ibn Abbas via al-Kalbi (Baghawi, Qurtubi version; on/off the mat
  difference kept); as-Suddi via Asbat (Qurtubi); Ma'arif.
- After the father: Qatada (Tabari); heirs' reasons differ: little
  wealth, many dependants (Ibn Abbas) vs "why give these poor" (Suddi).
- Place and time only as Baghawi and Qurtubi give them, attributed.
- Whisper: Sa'di's greed and fear of being overheard; Ma'arif; IK's
  "give none in charity".
- Qurtubi's 6:141 note (verified in quran-json); zakat question not ruled.
- Test the parable mirrors: Prophet's sending (IK), wealth as
  leading-on (Sa'di), drought/hunger (Baghawi, Qurtubi), famine (Ma'arif).
Licensing paragraph in both languages: no verdict on the wealthy, heirs
or any living person or community.
Hadith: none attached with a source; Qurtubi's unsourced night-reaping
report named as such and not relied on.
Dropped: Ethiopia (Ma'arif citing IK, not in IK text), "father a fool"
narratives, Jibril/at-Ta'if (68:19 ground), Tabari on hard (68:25).
- 68:24's own tafsir files were thin; the story details are from each tafsir's 68:17 text (same story), marked 'at 68:17' in both languages.
