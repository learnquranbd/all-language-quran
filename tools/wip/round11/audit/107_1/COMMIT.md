Replaces the 747-word, 6-section article with a 1,645-word, 7-section deep article (shipped card kept).

Sources: Ibn Kathir (ar; en abridged grouped 107:1-7), at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi,
al-Baghawi, as-Sa'di, Muyassar and Ma'arif al-Qur'an, all on 107:1.
Covers the address (O Muhammad, Ibn Kathir and at-Tabari; unnamed in the Muyassar),
al-Qurtubi's unsaid "is he right or wrong", the glosses of ad-din, 'Abdullah's
reading without bi, at-Tabari's "so he does not obey Him" and as-Sa'di's two sets
of rights, Ma'arif's preamble reading, 107:2-3 with 89:17-18, and 107:4-7 in one
section.

Disagreements kept, no position: ad-din as Return/Resurrection and Recompense,
Recompense and Reckoning, Allah's reward and punishment, the judgment of Allah
(Ibn Abbas) or the Reckoning (Ibn Jurayj); whom it came down about (al-'As ibn
Wa'il, a hypocrite, al-Walid ibn al-Mughira, Abu Jahl, 'Amr ibn 'A'idh, Abu Sufyan
and the orphan), as reports; Makkan or Madinan (al-Qurtubi); the five readings of
sahun (Ibn Kathir); al-ma'un as lent household things (Ibn Mas'ud) or zakah
(Ma'arif, for 'Ali, Ibn 'Umar, al-Hasan, Qatada, ad-Dahhak).

Hadith: Muslim 622 (Anas, "that is the prayer of the hypocrite", Muslim's single
wording, quoted whole), attached by Ibn Kathir to 107:5; none on 107:1 itself.
Licensing sentence in both languages for the named men and for the hypocrites.

Dropped: Ahmad's "man samma'a" (unreachable); sahun as no hope of reward and
Sa'd's "delaying it" (not in the fetched texts). Defect fixed: the old article
cited 69:33-34 as a third parallel; no tafsir on the verse cites it.
