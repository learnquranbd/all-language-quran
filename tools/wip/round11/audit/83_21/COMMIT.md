Card and 8-section bilingual article on yashhaduhu al-muqarrabun, the
two words that close the description of the record of the righteous
(83:18 to 83:20).

Sources: at-Tabari, Ibn Kathir (ar and abridged en), al-Qurtubi,
as-Sa'di, al-Baghawi, the Muyassar (grouped 83:18-21) and Ma'arif
al-Qur'an. At-Tabari's 83:21 comment is filed by the API under 83:20;
the prep file for 83:21 held his 83:22 text, so 83:20 was fetched.

Glosses kept side by side, none chosen:
- verb: bear witness (IK en), witness and attend (al-Baghawi), look
  upon (Muyassar), attend/be present/observe (Ma'arif).
- -hu: the record (at-Tabari, as-Sa'di, al-Baghawi, Muyassar), the deeds
  and their writing (al-Qurtubi), 'illiyyin (Ma'arif's second reading).
- al-muqarrabun: angels (Qatada, Ibn Zayd); near angels of every heaven
  (at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi, Muyassar); all the people of heaven vs the near
  ones of every heaven (Ibn 'Abbas, as at-Tabari and Ibn Kathir give
  him); angels in 'Illiyyin (al-Baghawi); angels plus souls of prophets,
  siddiqun and martyrs (as-Sa'di); righteous souls (Ma'arif alt.);
  Israfil (Wahb and Ibn Ishaq via al-Qurtubi, marked as their statement).

Hadith: Ma'arif's Muslim narration on martyrs' souls confirmed (1887) but
long and on another verse, so not quoted; no hadith attached to 83:21.
Asbab: none. Dropped: Ma'arif's abode-of-souls discussion, Ka'b on
'Illiyyin, 4:69 and 56:88-89 (not cited by any fetched tafsir).
