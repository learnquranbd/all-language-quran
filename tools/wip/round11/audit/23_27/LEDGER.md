## Named in the article + the Arabic/English clause each rests on
- **Ibn Kathir** (t14/t169): fa-awhayna → command to build; "min kull zawjayn
  ithnayn = dhakar wa unthā min kull ṣinf min al-ḥayawānāt wa-n-nabāt wa-th-thimār";
  "illā man sabaqa ʿalayhi al-qawl … humu alladhīna lam yu'minū bihi min ahlihi,
  ka-bnihi wa zawjatihi"; la tukhāṭibnī → "lā ta'khudhka ra'fa bi-qawmika … fa-innī
  qad qaḍaytu annahum mughraqūn"; "wa qad taqaddamat al-qiṣṣa … fī sūrat Hūd"; en
  quotes 54:10 (fa-daʿā rabbahu annī maghlūb) and 11:41 (irkabū fīhā bismillāh).
- **at-Tabari** (t15): bi-aʿyuninā = "bi-mar'an minnā wa manẓar"; wa-waḥyinā =
  "bi-taʿlīminā iyyāka ṣanʿatahā"; on tannūr: "wa qad dhakarnā fīmā maḍā ikhtilāf
  al-mukhtalifīn fī ṣifat fawr at-tannūr … aghnā ʿan iʿādatihi" (records the
  dispute, defers the position); fasluk = "adkhil … wa-ḥmil"; Ibn Abbas isnad:
  the excepted son "huwa Yām alladhī ghariqa"; la tukhāṭibnī = "lā tas'alnī fī
  alladhīna kafarū … an unjiyahum … ḥatamtu ʿalayhim an ughriqa jamīʿahum".
- **al-Muyassar** (t16): bi-aʿyuninā = "bi-mar'an minnā … wa anta fī ḥifẓinā wa
  kalā'atinā"; tannūr = "al-makān alladhī yukhbazu fīhi", water "nabaʿa bi-quwwa …
  ʿalāma ʿalā majī' al-ʿadhāb"; excepted "ka-zawjatika wa-bnika"; sifāt note:
  "wa fī hādhihi al-āya ithbāt ṣifat al-ʿayn lillāh … dūna tashbīh wa lā takyīf"
  (reported as the mufassir states it, no kalām).
- **al-Qurtubi** (t90): fa-awhaynā = "arsalnā ilayhi rusulan min as-samā'"; fasluk =
  adkhil; qirā'a "qara'a Ḥafṣ 'min kullin' bi-t-tanwīn, al-bāqūn bi-l-iḍāfa";
  al-Hasan: "lam yaḥmil Nūḥ … illā mā yalidu wa yabīḍu, fa-ammā al-baqq wa-dh-dhubāb
  wa-d-dūd fa-lam yaḥmil … innamā kharaja min aṭ-ṭīn".
- **as-Sa'di** (t91): ship = "sababan wa wasīlatan li-n-najāt qabla wuqūʿ asbābihi";
  bi-aʿyuninā = "fī ḥifẓinā wa kalā'atinā bi-ḥaythu narāka wa nasmaʿuka"; tannūr =
  "fārat al-arḍ wa tafajjarat ʿuyūnan ḥattā maḥall an-nār"; zawjayn = "min kull jins …
  dhakaran wa unthā tabqā māddat an-nasl"; excepted "ka-bnihi"; la tukhāṭibnī = "lā
  tadʿunī an unjiyahum … al-qaḍā' wa-l-qadar qad ḥatam annahum mughraqūn".
- **al-Baghawi** (t94): fasluk = adkhil; excepted = "man sabaqa ʿalayhi al-ḥukm
  bi-l-halāk".
- **Ma'arif** (t168): tannūr = oven for flat round bread; also "the whole earth";
  "a specific oven in the Kufah mosque"; "others believe … somewhere in Syria";
  boiling-water sign traced to Mazhari.

## Dispute kept open (watch-point 2)
al-tannūr: literal baking oven (al-Muyassar, Ma'arif) vs. the earth's surface /
whole earth bursting into springs (as-Sa'di, Ma'arif) vs. a specific place —
Kufah mosque or Syria (Ma'arif). at-Tabari notes the disagreement and defers.
Named the sides, took no position.

## Excepted family member (watch-point 3)
Son and wife, per Ibn Kathir, al-Muyassar (both), at-Tabari (son = Yām, drowned),
as-Sa'di (son), al-Baghawi (decree preceded). Fuller account referenced to Surah
Hud with 11:41, because Ibn Kathir does so (t169). Prophet's fault NEVER asserted;
the son's choice stated as the son's. Wrongdoer-judgement "licenses nothing
against any living person or community" sentence carried in both languages (§7.2).

## Hadith
No fetched tafsir attaches a sound, connected hadith to 23:27 — stated plainly in
§8.1. One thematically related narration used, explicitly marked as NOT tied to the
verse by any mufassir:
- Sahih al-Bukhari 660. URL: https://quranx.com/hadith/Bukhari/DarusSalam/Hadith-660
  First ten words (EN used): "The Prophet said, Allah will give shade to seven".
  Grading: in Sahih al-Bukhari (ṣaḥīḥ; not upgraded). Used only the "man who
  remembers Allah in seclusion until his eyes overflow" strand, for the bi-aʿyunina
  theme. Count "seven" dropped from prose to keep number-parity clean; the shade
  hadith's substance kept.
