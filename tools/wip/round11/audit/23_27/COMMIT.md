Nuh's plea in 23:26 is answered not with instant victory but with a task:
build the ship. Card and deep bilingual article added.

Sources (all from PREP, none re-fetched):
- bi-aʿyunina = under God's watching care: at-Tabari (bi-mar'an minna),
  al-Muyassar and as-Sa'di (fi hifzina wa kala'atina, He sees and hears you).
  al-Muyassar's sifat note reported as he states it, no kalam.
- wa-wahyina = God taught the very making of the ark (at-Tabari, al-Muyassar).
- fasluk = adkhil (at-Tabari, al-Baghawi, al-Qurtubi); pairs of every kind
  (Ibn Kathir; as-Sa'di on preserving the breeding stock); al-Qurtubi's Hafs
  qira'a and al-Hasan's view on what was carried.

Disagreement kept open: al-tannur — literal baking oven (al-Muyassar, Ma'arif)
vs. earth's surface bursting into springs (as-Sa'di, Ma'arif) vs. a specific
place, Kufah mosque or Syria (Ma'arif). at-Tabari notes the dispute and defers.
No position taken.

Excepted family member = son and wife (Ibn Kathir, al-Muyassar; at-Tabari names
the son Yam; as-Sa'di; al-Baghawi). Fuller account referenced to Surah Hud and
11:41 because Ibn Kathir does. Prophet's fault never asserted; the son's choice
stated as the son's. Wrongdoer-judgement line carried in both languages: the
verse licenses nothing against any living person or community.

Hadith: no fetched tafsir attaches a sound hadith to 23:27 — said plainly.
Bukhari 660 (sahih) used only as a thematically related narration, explicitly
marked as not tied to the verse, for the bi-aʿyunina thread.

Dropped: at-Tabari's poetry shawahid and his deferred tannur position; the
11:42-46 dialogue beyond the Surah Hud reference. Logged in LEDGER.
