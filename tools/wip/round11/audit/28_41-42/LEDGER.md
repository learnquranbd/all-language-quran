# SOURCES ledger — 28:41-42

Two-ayah card (both verses in one arc): imams calling to the Fire, unaided on the
Day (41); a curse pursuing them in this world, maqbūḥīn on the Day (42).

## Tafsir fetched (files in this dir)

28:41 — t14 (Ibn Kathir ar), t15 (at-Tabari), t16 (al-Muyassar), t90 (al-Qurtubi),
t91 (as-Sa'di), t94 (al-Baghawi), t168 (Ma'arif en), t169 (Ibn Kathir en abridged).
28:42 — same IDs. All read.

### Named in the article, with the Arabic/English clause each rests on

- **at-Tabari (15), 41**: "جعلنا فرعون وقومه أئمة يأتمّ بهم أهل العتوّ... يدعون الناس
  إلى أعمال أهل النار" → imams the arrogant/disbelievers take as models, calling to
  the deeds of the people of the Fire. **42**: "ألزمنا فرعون وقومه... خزيا وغضبا...
  بالهلاك والبوار والثناء السَّيِّئ" → disgrace, wrath, destruction, ruin, evil name.
  §2.1, §6.1, §7.2.
- **at-Tabari (15), 41 "لا ينصرون"**: "قد كانوا في الدنيا يتناصرون, فاضمحلت تلك النصرة
  يومئذ" → they helped each other in the world; that aid dissolves. §6.1.
- **al-Baghawi (94), 41**: "قادة ورؤساء" (chiefs/heads); "لا ينصرون = لا يمنعون من
  العذاب" (not shielded from punishment). §2.1, §6.1. **42 maqbūḥīn**: "المبعدين
  الملعونين... أبو عبيدة: من المهلكين... ابن عباس: المشوهين بسواد الوجوه وزرقة العيون".
  §8.1 (Abu Ubayda = destroyed; Ibn Abbas = disfigured, black faces, blue eyes).
- **al-Qurtubi (90), 41**: "زعماء يتبعون على الكفر، فيكون عليهم وزرهم ووزر من اتبعهم
  حتى يكون عقابهم أكثر" → spokesmen followed in kufr; they bear their own burden plus
  their followers', punishment greater. §4.2, §5.1. **42**: "أمرنا العباد بلعنهم فمن
  ذكرهم لعنهم" (God commanded servants to curse them) §7.1; maqbūḥīn "المهلكين
  الممقوتين... ابن عباس: المشوهين الخلقة بسواد الوجوه وزرقة العيون" §8.1.
- **as-Sa'di (91), 41**: "أضعف شيء عن دفعه... ليس لهم من دون اللّه من ولي ولا نصير"
  → feeblest to repel it, no wali/nasir but God. §6.2. **42**: "اجتمع عليهم مقت اللّه،
  ومقت خلقه، ومقت أنفسهم" → gathered detestation of God, creation, their own selves;
  and "مستقذرة أفعالهم" (deeds found filthy). §8.2.
- **al-Muyassar (16), 41**: "قادة إلى النار، يقتدي بهم أهل الكفر والفسق... وذلك بسبب
  كفرهم وتكذيبهم رسول ربهم وإصرارهم" → leaders to the Fire imitated by people of kufr
  and fisq; BECAUSE of their kufr, denial of the messenger, persistence. §2.1, §4.1.
  **42**: "المستقذرة أفعالهم، المبعدين عن رحمة الله" → driven far from mercy. §8.2.
- **Ibn Kathir (14 ar / 169 en), 41**: "لمن سلك وراءهم... في تكذيب الرسل وتعطيل
  الصانع" → for those who followed them, rejecting messengers and denying the Creator;
  "فاجتمع عليهم خزي الدنيا موصولا بذل الآخرة" cf 47:13. §2.2, §6.2. **42**: "شرع الله
  لعنتهم ولعنة ملكهم فرعون على ألسنة المؤمنين... الأنبياء وأتباعهم" → cursed on tongues
  of believers / prophets and followers. §7.1. Qatada: this = Hūd 11:99. §7.2.
- **Ma'arif al-Qur'an (168 en), 41**: most commentators read "call to fire" as
  metaphor (evil deeds ending in burning); then relays Anwar Shah Kashmiri's view that
  the deed itself becomes its reward, so inviting to evil is really bidding into fire
  (cites 18:49, 99:7). §2.2. **42 maqbūḥīn**: "spoilt, damaged or disfigured... faces
  disfigured to turn black, eyes turn blue." Folded into §8.1.

### Qur'anic cross-references (text, not a mufassir's attribution)

- **32:24** "وجعلنا منهم أئمة يهدون بأمرنا" and **28:5** "نجعلهم أئمة... الوارثين":
  the same noun أئمة + verb جعلنا used for leadership of guidance — the inverse of
  28:41. Drawn in §3 as a Qur'an-internal word parallel, attributed to no mufassir.
- **47:13** quoted by Ibn Kathir on 41 (§6.2). **11:99** quoted via Qatada on 42
  (§7.2). **18:49 / 99:7** cited by Ma'arif on 41 (§2.2).

## Hadith

- **Sahih Muslim 2674** — URL https://quranx.com/hadith/Muslim/Hadith-2674 (fetched).
  First ten words (EN): "He who called (people) to righteousness, there would be
  reward". Arabic opens: "من دعا إلى هدى كان له من الأجر مثل أجور من تبعه...". Grading:
  sahih by inclusion in Sahih Muslim (no separate collector grade on the page; not
  upgraded). **Marked in §5.1 as a general narration that NO fetched commentator
  attaches to this verse**; used only because it states the same law al-Qurtubi derives
  (one's own burden + the followers'). Not stock/nearby (brief warned off Bukhari 660,
  Bukhari 3443 at 26:123); those were avoided.

## Asbāb an-nuzūl

- Not used. No fetched source gives an occasion of revelation for 28:41-42; the verses
  continue the Mūsā / Pharaoh narrative (drowning in 28:40, Scripture to Mūsā in 28:43).

## Dropped / not written

- al-Qurtubi's long grammatical discussion on the naṣb of يوم in 28:42 (syntactic,
  not reflective) — dropped.
- al-Qurtubi's alternative gloss that the a'imma are made a lesson for "those of
  insight" (يأتم بهم ذوو العبر) — dropped to avoid muddying the "leaders TO the Fire"
  arc; the dominant reading across all six mufassirūn is leaders followed INTO kufr.
- Ibn 'Abbas's "blue eyes / black faces" kept but framed as one reading among several
  (destroyed / detested / driven-far), not as the single meaning.
