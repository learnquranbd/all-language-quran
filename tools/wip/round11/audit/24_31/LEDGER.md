## Attributions and the Arabic/text clause each rests on
- **§2 gaze**: Ibn Kathir (t169) "from that which Allah has forbidden them to look at, apart from their husbands"; as-Sa'di (t91) "عن النظر إلى العورات والرجال بشهوة". Abu al-Aliyah on furuj = guarding from being SEEN, unique to this verse (t169). Partitive "min" = restraint not closure (t169 wording of the men's verse note; standard).
- **§2 may a woman look**: Ma'arif (t168) reports BOTH sides kept open — Umm Salama report (Abu Dawud/Tirmidhi, "Tirmidhi declared this hadith as hasan Sahih" — grading as given, not upgraded) vs A'isha watching the Abyssinians (t168/t169, "recorded in the Sahih"). NOT resolved in article. These two hadiths are named as reported by the tafsir, not independently fetched (they are illustrative of the scholarly split, not the section-4 quoted hadith).
- **§3 illa ma zahara minha (the open disagreement)**: at-Tabari (t15), his own words: Ibn Mas'ud (عبد الله) = "الثياب" / "الرداء"; al-Hasan & Ibrahim (an-Nakha'i) = "الثياب"; Ibn Abbas via Sa'id b. Jubayr = "الوجه والكفّ". at-Tabari's tarjih: "وأولى الأقوال... الوجه والكفان" (+ kohl, ring, bracelet). Ibn Kathir (t169) Ibn Mas'ud = "clothes and outer garments." Ma'arif (t168) frames dispute as face+hands uncovering; reconciling view of al-Baydawi & al-Khazin = "whatever gets uncovered during the process of working"; shared ground: fitna bars uncovering; prayer valid with face/hands bare. KEPT OPEN, no fatwa.
- **§4 khimar/jayb**: al-Qurtubi (t90) "الخمر جمع الخمار وهو ما تغطي به رأسها"; "الجيوب جمع الجيب وهو موضع القطع من الدرع والقميص"; Muqatil "على جيوبهن أي على صدورهن"; pre-Islam neck/chest bare ("النحر والعنق والأذنان لا ستر على ذلك"). Ma'arif (t168) same picture from Ibn Jubayr.
- **§5 mahram list**: Ibn Kathir (t169) "close relatives whom she can never marry (Mahram)... without making a wanton display." as-Sa'di (t91) father incl. grandfather "وإن علا", son incl. grandson "مهما نزلوا". Ma'arif (t168) "twelve exceptions," mahram in wide sense incl. husband; two grounds (natural honour + shared household). Ikrima on uncle omission via Ibn al-Mundhir (t169).
- **§6 attendants/children**: at-tabi'in — Ibn Abbas "no desire" (t169); as-Sa'di (t91) "المعتوه" / "العنين"; Ma'arif (t168) qualification that cover stays before any man who does notice. at-tifl — Ibn Kathir (t169) young child understanding nothing, covers once nearing notice; as-Sa'di (t91) below tamyiz, verse gives its own reason.
- **§7 stamping**: Ibn Kathir (t169) anklets/stamping to be heard. as-Sa'di (t91) sadd adh-dhara'i principle, his own derivation "قاعدة سد الوسائل."
- **§8 ending**: as-Sa'di (t91) every believer needs tawba, falah tied to tawba "فلا سبيل إلى الفلاح إلا بالتوبة," ikhlas in tawba. Care/no-license statement is the article's own application, grounded in the verse addressing the believers' own repentance.

## Hadith (section 4, quoted whole)
- **Sahih al-Bukhari 4759**, fetched https://quranx.com/hadith/Bukhari/DarusSalam/Hadith-4759 (via tools/wip/round11/hadith.js, saved in this dir).
  First ten words (EN): "Narrated Safiya bint Shaiba: `Aisha used to say: When the".
  Arabic first words: "حَدَّثَنَا أَبُو نُعَيْمٍ حَدَّثَنَا إِبْرَاهِيمُ بْنُ نَافِعٍ عَنِ الْحَسَنِ".

  Wording used: paraphrase-quote of the Safiyya/A'isha narration, "they took their waist-sheets and cut them at the edges and covered themselves with them" — single collection (Bukhari), not hybridised.
