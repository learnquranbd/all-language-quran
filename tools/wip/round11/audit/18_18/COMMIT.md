Card and deep bilingual article for the sleepers who looked awake, were
turned right and left, their dog at the threshold, and the awe that
guarded them.

Sources (all pre-fetched, none re-fetched): at-Tabari, Ibn Kathir (ar/en),
al-Qurtubi, al-Baghawi, as-Sa'di, al-Muyassar, Ma'arif al-Qur'an.
- Awake-looking: open eyes lest they spoil (Qurtubi's mufassirun, Sa'di,
  Baghawi, Ibn Kathir's "some scholars"); Qurtubi's second view, frequent
  turning. Turning: Ibn Abbas, lest the earth consume them; frequencies
  kept as differing reports; Sa'di on causes tied to effects.
- al-wasid: Tabari's three views and his preference (the door, 104:8).
- Dog's names/colours shown as reports only, with Ibn Kathir's verdict
  (rajm bil-ghayb, pointer to 18:22) and Qurtubi's "any colour you name".
- Dog-or-man views (Tabari, Qurtubi, Baghawi's lion) with Ibn Atiyyah's
  rebuttal. Honour of company: Ibn Kathir; Jawhari via Ibn Atiyyah in
  Qurtubi.
- The terror kept as a disagreement: awe Allah placed (Tabari, Ibn Kathir,
  Sa'di) vs the place, the open eyes, hair and nails (Baghawi's list;
  Qurtubi rejects hair/nails); Ma'arif: cause unfixed. Addressee kept
  open: the Prophet (Tabari, Baghawi) vs any onlooker (Muyassar, Sa'di,
  Ma'arif). Mu'awiyah report flagged ungraded; no cave location given.

Hadith: Bukhari 3225 (angels and a dog), attached by Ibn Kathir; Bukhari
3688 (with those you love), brought by Qurtubi, marked as not about the
cave; Bukhari 822 (forearms like a dog), cited via Ibn Atiyyah.

Dropped: Ibn Kathir's unconfirmed "hasan" hadith, Qurtubi's dog-keeping
fiqh, the qira'at of la-mulli'ta (length), paradise/ear-folding reports.
