Card and 9-section bilingual article for 46:35, the closing verse of
al-Ahqaf: fa-sbir kama sabara ulu-l-'azm min ar-rusul ... balagh,
fa-hal yuhlaku illa-l-qawmu-l-fasiqun.

Sources: Tabari, Ibn Kathir (ar; en checked), Muyassar, Qurtubi, Sa'di,
Baghawi, Ma'arif.
- 'Azm: hazm (Ibn 'Abbas), jidd and sabr (Dahhak), intensity (Ibn
  Jubayr), tested and made more earnest (Tabari's qila), Sa'di.
- All messengers vs some: min for kind (Ibn Zayd, Ibn 'Abbas, 'Ali ibn
  Mahdi, IK's possibility, Ma'arif) vs the subset lists: five, Abu
  al-'Aliyah, Suddi, Muqatil, A'raf/Shu'ara' six, al-Hasan's four,
  al-Kalbi, An'am eighteen, Ibn Jurayj, Yunus exception, twelve. None
  picked; 42:13 article pointed to, not repeated.
- La tasta'jil: object = punishment; Baghawi's hedged "ka'annahu" on the
  wish; Muqatil (du'a against them); Sa'di's reversal.
- Hour of a day: world (Tabari, Sa'di, Muyassar) vs world and barzakh
  (Baghawi, IK); Yahya vs an-Naqqash. Refs 23:112-113, 79:46, 10:45.
- Balagh: Tabari's two senses (via IK too), Sa'di's two, Qurtubi
  (al-Hasan vs Ibn 'Isa), Baghawi (= tabligh).
- Fasiqun glosses; Zajjaj/Qurtubi: strongest verse of hope.
Hadith: Muslim 131 (Ibn 'Abbas, intentions; 131b adds "none perishes
except he who is doomed"), attached by Tabari via Qatadah.
Licensing sentence in both languages.
Dropped: 'A'ishah report via Ibn Abi Hatim (unconfirmed); Muqatil's
Uhud occasion; al-Hasan's Dawud story; reasons given for the Yunus view;
abrogation claim; shadhdh readings.
Fix: Muslim 131 quranx English typo 'But it he intended' shown as '[if]' (Arabic wa-in); nums gate flag on 'seven hundred' is a false positive.
