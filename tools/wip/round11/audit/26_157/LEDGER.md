# SOURCES — 26:157 (فعقروها فأصبحوا نادمين)

## Tafsir fetched (all from PREP.md, not re-fetched)

- **at-Tabari (15)** — USED. Arabic clause: "فعقروا الناقة التي قال لهم صالح: لا
  تمسوها بسوء, فأصبحوا نادمين على عقرها, فلم ينفعهم ندمهم." Rests: the deed was
  defiance of Salih's command (§2); regret over the hamstringing that did not
  benefit them (§3).
- **al-Qurtubi (90)** — USED. "فأصبحوا نادمين أي على عقرها لما أيقنوا بالعذاب.
  وذلك أنه أنظرهم ثلاثا فظهرت عليهم العلامة في كل يوم، وندموا ولم ينفعهم الندم عند
  معاينة العذاب. وقيل: لم ينفعهم الندم لأنهم لم يتوبوا، بل طلبوا صالحا عليه السلام
  ليقتلوه... وقيل: كانت ندامتهم على ترك الولد... وهو بعيد." Rests: three days'
  respite + sign each day and regret useless at the sight of punishment (§3);
  the regret-is-not-tawba reading, and sought to kill Salih (§4); the rejected
  "regret over the young camel" reading, judged بعيد (§5).
- **al-Muyassar (16)** — USED. "فنحروا الناقة، فأصبحوا متحسرين على ما فعلوا لمَّا
  أيقنوا بالعذاب، فلم ينفعهم ندمهم." Rests: naḥarū gloss (slaughtered outright),
  §2; remorse once certain of punishment, did not benefit, §3.
- **al-Baghawi (94)** — USED. "على عقرها حين رأوا العذاب." Rests: regret came
  when they saw the punishment, §3.
- **Ibn Kathir (ar 14 / en 169)** — USED for the collective framing only.
  en 169: "the time for their destruction drew near, they conspired to kill
  her." Rests §6 (they conspired = a collective deed). NOTE: the earthquake /
  Sayhah detail (14 and 169) is the ʿadhāb of 26:158 — deliberately NOT used
  here, reserved for that verse per the watch-point.
- **as-Sa'di (91)** — NOT USED. Fetched text is only the verse re-quoted, no
  commentary; nothing attributable.
- **Ma'arif al-Qur'an (en 168)** — NOT USED for 26:157. Though the API grouped
  it over 149–158, the fetched text comments only on 26:149 (hewing houses with
  pride); it says nothing about the hamstringing or the regret, so nothing is
  attributed to it here.

## Hadith

- Jami` at-Tirmidhi, Hadith 3537 (Ibn ʿUmar). URL fetched:
  https://quranx.com/hadith/Tirmidhi/Hadith-3537
  First ten words of wording used: "Indeed Allah accepts the repentance of a
  slave as long". Arabic: "إن الله يقبل توبة العبد ما لم يغرغر".
  Grading: at-Tirmidhi's own — **hasan gharib** ("قال أبو عيسى هذا حديث حسن
  غريب"); reported verbatim, not upgraded.
  MARKED in the article (§7) as a GENERAL narration on repentance's cutoff, NOT
  attached to 26:157.
- Avoided the banned stock hadith per watch-point: Bukhari 660; Bukhari 3443
  (26:123); al-Ḥijr Bukhari 3379/3380 (used at 26:142, 26:155). The "most
  wretched / hamstringer" hadith (ashqāhā, cf. 91:12) was NOT used: no fetched
  tafsir gives the lone-doer / "most wretched" detail, and the watch-point gates
  naming Qudār ibn Sālif on the tafsir, which is silent.

## Asbab an-nuzul
- Not used. This is narrative sequence within the Thamud passage, not an
  occasion-of-revelation; placement (after the sign of 26:155 and the warning of
  26:156, before the punishment of 26:158) is given instead.

## Dropped (could not confirm, or gated)
- Qudār ibn Sālif as the named doer, and "the most wretched of them" (91:12):
  dropped — not in any tafsir fetched for this verse. Collective framing (§6)
  built only on the plural verb عقروها + Ibn Kathir's "conspired."
- The three days' change of complexion (faces yellow/red/black) sometimes told
  with al-Qurtubi's "العلامة في كل يوم": dropped the colour detail; the fetched
  text says only that a sign appeared each day, so §3 says exactly that.
- Earthquake + Sayhah (Ibn Kathir): held back for 26:158, not dropped as false.
