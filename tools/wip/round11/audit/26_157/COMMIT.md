Thamud hamstring the she-camel and are left regretful — but the remorse
comes too late to count. The card and article build the one distinct lesson:
sorrow that arrives only when the consequence is in sight is not repentance.

Sources (all pre-fetched, none re-fetched):
- at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi, al-Muyassar, al-Baghawi converge on fa-lam yanfaʿhum
  nadamuhum — their regret did not benefit them, felt only when certain of the
  punishment.
- al-Qurtubi carries the key distinction (regret ≠ tawba: they did not repent,
  and sought to kill Salih) and a second reading he rejects as baʿīd (regret
  over sparing the camel's young) — kept as a weighed disagreement, not flattened.
- al-Muyassar's naḥarū gloss used for the verb; at-Tabari grounds the deed as
  defiance of the 26:156 command.
- Ibn Kathir's "they conspired" + the plural verb carry the collective-complicity
  section; no tafsir names Qudār / "the most wretched," so the doer is not named.

Hadith: Tirmidhi 3537 (Ibn ʿUmar), repentance accepted until the soul reaches
the throat — his own grading hasan gharib, reported not upgraded, and marked a
general narration not attached to the verse.

Dropped: Qudār ibn Sālif and 91:12's "most wretched" (ungated by the tafsir);
the three-days complexion-change colour detail. The earthquake/Sayhah ʿadhāb is
held for 26:158, not used here. Carries the "licenses nothing against any living
person or community" sentence in both languages.
