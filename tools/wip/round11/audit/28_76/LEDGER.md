# SOURCES ledger — 28:76 (Qārūn)

## Tafsir fetched (all pre-fetched in PREP, re-read here; none WRONG GROUP)
- **14 Ibn Kathir (ar)** — USED. Kinship list (cousin): "قال ابن عباس... كان ابن عمه. وهكذا قال إبراهيم النخعي... وقتادة ومالك بن دينار وابن جريج... أنه كان ابن عم موسى". Ibn Ishaq (uncle): "وزعم محمد بن إسحاق... أن قارون كان عم موسى". Keys measure: "ليثقل حملها الفئام من الناس لكثرتها". al-fariḥīn gloss: Ibn Abbas "يعني المرحين"; Mujahid "الأشرين البطرين الذين لا يشكرون الله". Who counselled: "وعظه... صالح قومه... على سبيل النصح والإرشاد: لا تفرح".
- **15 at-Tabari (ar)** — USED. Records both kinship views, most scholars = cousin ("وأكثر أهل العلم في ذلك على ما قاله ابن جُرَيج"), closes "والله أعلم" (general Tabari idiom). Qatādah's line that baghy destroyed him: "فأهلكه البغي" (also in Ibn Kathir via Qatādah).
- **16 al-Muyassar (ar)** — USED. baghy = overstepping bound in pride/domineering: "فتجاوز حدَّه في الكِبْر والتجبر عليهم".
- **90 al-Qurtubi (ar)** — USED. Ibn Bahr reading of baghy: "نسبته ما آتاه الله من الكنوز إلى نفسه بعلمه وحيلته؛ قاله ابن بحر". mafātiḥ two senses + 6:59 cross-ref: "هي جمع مفتح... وقيل: مفاتحه خزائنه كما قال وعنده مفاتح الغيب". 'uṣba numbers "على أحد عشر قولا" (3 up to 70). Garment-span baghy (Shahr b. Hawshab) + the hadith "وفي الحديث لا ينظر الله إلى من جر إزاره بطرا". Grammar: Qārūn non-declinable (foreign + definite). Yahyā b. Sallām/Ibn al-Musayyab: Pharaoh's agent over Bani Israil.
- **91 as-Sa'di (ar)** — USED. qawm = Bani Israil favoured above the worlds: "من بني إسرائيل الذين فُضِّلوا على العالمين". Keys-if-so-treasuries force: "حتى أن مفاتح خزائن أمواله لتثقل الجماعة القوية... فما ظنك بالخزائن؟". faraḥ warning: "لا تفرح بهذه الدنيا... وتلهيك عن الآخرة".
- **94 al-Baghawi (ar)** — USED. mafātiḥ = keys (Qatādah, Mujāhid, jamāʿa) vs storerooms: "هي جمع مفتح... هذا قول قتادة ومجاهد وجماعة، وقيل مفاتحه خزائنه". Qatādah baghy = excess of wealth "بغى عليهم بكثرة المال"; ad-Dahhāk = shirk "بغى عليهم بالشرك". Garment-span (Shahr) + Ibn Umar hadith variant "جر ثوبه خيلاء".
- **168 Ma'arif (en)** — USED. Thematic link to 28:60 "whatever you are given is the enjoyment of worldly life"; Qārūn non-Arabic/Hebrew; kunūz = plural of kanz (buried treasure); two baghy readings (cruelty [Yahyā b. Sallām, Saʿīd b. al-Musayyab, Pharaoh's vigilance post] / arrogance); faraḥ permitted vs blamed with 57:23, 30:4, 10:58.
- **169 Ibn Kathir (en abridged, covers 76+77)** — USED for cross-check of kinship + al-fariḥīn gloss (Ibn Abbas "rejoice and gloat"; Mujahid "insolent and reckless... do not thank Allah"). Did not draw on its 28:77 portion (future ground).

## Hadith
- **Sahih al-Bukhari 5788** (Abu Hurayra). URL fetched: https://quranx.com/hadith/Bukhari/DarusSalam/Hadith-5788 (saved to audit dir). First ten words (EN): "Allah will not look, on the Day of Resurrection, at". AR: "لاَ يَنْظُرُ اللَّهُ يَوْمَ الْقِيَامَةِ إِلَى مَنْ جَرَّ إِزَارَهُ بَطَرًا". Grading: in Ṣaḥīḥ al-Bukhārī (sahih; not upgraded). Wording matches al-Qurtubi's exact citation ("جر إزاره بطرا") on this verse; al-Baghawi cites the Ibn Umar "khuyalāʾ" variant — I quoted ONE collection whole (Bukhari/Abu Hurayra), not a hybrid.

## Asbab

## Dropped (could not confirm / isrāʾīliyyāt — left out, per watch-points)
- **Exact kinship (cousin vs uncle)** — reports conflict (Ibn Abbas etc. = cousin; Ibn Ishaq = uncle). Kept as an open disagreement; asserted only "min qawmi Mūsā". Flagged as unconfirmed isrāʾīliyyāt detail.
- **Number of al-ʿuṣba** — al-Qurtubi lists ~11 opinions (3 to 70). Did NOT fix a number; reported only that the range exists and the number was never revealed.
- **mafātiḥ = keys vs chests** — given as a lexical point with both readings, not as a story.
- **al-Qurtubi's graphic isrāʾīliyyāt** (Qārūn bribing a woman to accuse Musa of zinā; the sixty-mule leather-key detail from the Injīl; Qārūn meeting Yūnus in the deep; the daily sinking) — DROPPED. Unverifiable isrāʾīliyyāt, and the accusation tale touches a prophet; not written.
- **Qārūn swallowed by the earth / "I was given it for knowledge"** — DROPPED from this article: that is 28:77–82, future ground.
