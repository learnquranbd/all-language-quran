Qārūn: "min qawmi Mūsā" — one of the chosen nation who turned
on his own. Card plus 8-section bilingual article (1658 EN words).

What the sources gave:
- as-Sa'di: qawm = Bani Israil, favoured above the worlds; the
  sting is that the wrongdoer rose from inside the community.
- baghy read across the mufassirūn, kept as a spread not a verdict:
  al-Muyassar (pride/domineering), al-Baghawi (Qatādah: excess of
  wealth; ad-Dahhāk: shirk), al-Qurtubi (Ibn Bahr: crediting the
  treasure to his own skill), Ma'arif (cruelty: Pharaoh's vigilance
  post over Bani Israil, per Yahyā b. Sallām / Ibn al-Musayyab).
- kunūz/mafātiḥ: Ma'arif (kanz = buried treasure); mafātiḥ given
  both readings — keys (Qatādah, Mujāhid, al-Baghawi) vs storerooms
  (6:59) — as a lexical point, not a story.
- faraḥ: Ma'arif's distinction, blamed here and 57:23, praised 30:4
  and 10:58; al-fariḥīn = gloating / ungrateful (Ibn Abbas, Mujahid).

Disagreements kept: Qārūn's kinship to Musa (cousin, majority per
Ibn Kathir/at-Tabari; uncle per Ibn Ishaq) left open as unconfirmed
isrāʾīliyyāt. Number of al-ʿuṣba NOT fixed (al-Qurtubi's ~11 views).

Hadith: Sahih al-Bukhari 5788 (Abu Hurayra), "Allah will not look...
at one who drags his garment out of pride" — the report al-Baghawi
and al-Qurtubi attach to Qārūn's garment-span arrogance; one
collection quoted whole, graded sahih (Bukhari), not upgraded.

Balance stated in both languages: the verse condemns baghy and faraḥ,
not wealth; no warrant to despise the rich as a class; it describes
what the text describes and licenses nothing against the living.

Dropped: al-Qurtubi's graphic isrāʾīliyyāt (the false-accusation
tale, sixty-mule keys, Qārūn and Yūnus); 28:77–82 left as future
ground. Full ledger in audit/28_76/LEDGER.md.
