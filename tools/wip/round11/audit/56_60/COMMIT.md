Replaces the 688-word, 5-section article with an 8-section, 1,774-word
source-gated article (shipped card kept).

Sources: at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi, Ibn Kathir (ar; en abridged, grouped
56:57-62), al-Baghawi, Muyassar (56:60-61), Ma'arif (56:57-62); as-Sa'di
fetch failed. Placement via al-Qurtubi's ihtijaj (who causes death can
create and raise) and Ma'arif's series of arguments from 56:57.
Qaddarna: hastened for some, delayed for others (at-Tabari, Mujahid),
Muqatil's old age vs childhood (al-Baghawi), Ibn Kathir's sarrafnahu;
ad-Dahhak's "equal for heaven and earth" via four texts; al-Qurtubi's
qadayna / katabna. Qira'a qadarna (Mujahid, Humayd, Ibn Muhaysin, Ibn
Kathir the Makkan reader) with al-Baghawi's "two dialects".
Masbuqin kept as three readings, no position: none escapes the term
(at-Tabari), not overcome in replacing you (al-Qurtubi, al-Baghawi,
Ma'arif), not unable (Ibn Kathir, Muyassar). Nubaddila amthalakum:
replacement by others (al-Baghawi, Ma'arif) vs change of shape at the
Resurrection (Ibn Kathir en, Muyassar). Fi ma la ta'lamun: shapes,
states, dust or metamorphosis (Ma'arif), with the licensing sentence.
56:62 with 36:78-79 (Ibn Kathir en); 3:185, 21:35, 56:52, 56:69, 56:77
in one line each.

Hadith: none attached by the tafsirs; the article says so.

Dropped: from the old article the nahnu-fronting claim, qadar as
"measuring a quantity", the race image, 35:11 and 54:49 (no fetched
source); unnamed continuation readings now attributed.
