Disputes kept, no position taken:
- qaddarna: timing allotted per person (at-Tabari, Mujahid, Muqatil via al-Baghawi, Ibn Kathir's sarrafnahu, Ma'arif) vs equal for heaven and earth (ad-Dahhak via Ibn Kathir ar/en, al-Qurtubi, al-Baghawi); al-Qurtubi's qadayna / katabna and "the meaning is close" reported as his.
- masbuqin: none escapes the term (at-Tabari) / not overcome by anyone in replacing you (al-Qurtubi, al-Baghawi, Ma'arif) / not unable (Ibn Kathir ar/en, al-Muyassar; al-Baghawi's 'ajizin too). No preference stated.
- nubaddila amthalakum: replace you with others like you (al-Baghawi, Ma'arif) vs change your own shapes / creation on the Day of Resurrection (Ibn Kathir en, al-Muyassar). No ruling.
- fi ma la ta'lamun: shapes and forms (Ibn Kathir en); attributes and states (al-Muyassar); dust, or animal metamorphosis as in past nations (Ma'arif).
- Qira'a: qadarna (Mujahid, Humayd, Ibn Muhaysin, Ibn Kathir the Makkan reader per al-Qurtubi; Ibn Kathir only per al-Baghawi, "two dialects") vs qaddarna (the rest). Received text in data/quran-json/56.json has the shadda.
