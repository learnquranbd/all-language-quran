Card and 8-section bilingual article for 75:23, ila rabbiha nazira, read
with 75:22 (nadira) and with 75:24-25 (the scowling faces) as context only.
75:20-21 named as the link it follows; the 75:20 article is not repeated.

Sources: Tabari (75:23 entry is his 75:22 comment; the 75:23 dispute and
his preference come from the by_ayah 75:24 entry, saved as t15_24.txt),
Qurtubi, Ibn Kathir (ar and en grouped 75:16-25), Baghawi, Sa'di,
Muyassar (grouped 75:22-23), Ma'arif (grouped 75:22-25).
- nadira: Tabari's "hasana jamila min an-na'im"; al-Hasan, Mujahid,
  Ibn Zayd glosses; al-Hasan's line tying nadira to nazira (Tabari,
  Baghawi, Ibn Kathir); Sa'di "beauty upon their beauty".
- Kept as a disagreement, both sides named as the texts name them:
  looking at their Lord (al-Hasan, Ikrima, Atiyya al-Awfi; Qurtubi's
  "jumhur"; Ibn 'Abbas in Baghawi; Sa'di, Muyassar, Ma'arif) vs waiting
  for reward/provision/command (Mujahid by several chains, Abu Salih;
  Qurtubi adds Ibn Umar, Ikrima, Mawardi, "known only from Mujahid").
- Tabari's own preference quoted (al-Hasan and Ikrima). Qurtubi "very
  weak", Tha'labi and Azhari on nazara ila; Qushayri on ila/ala'; Ibn
  Kathir "has gone far afield", 83:15 and ash-Shafi'i. Article voice neutral.
- Ma'arif's naming of the Mu'tazilites and Kharijites kept to one sentence.
Hadith: Bukhari 554 (Jarir, full moon), quoted whole; marked as reciting
50:39, linked to 75:23 by Ibn Kathir and Qurtubi. Tirmidhi 3330 (Ibn Umar,
recites 75:22-23), graded gharib by at-Tirmidhi, mawquf route noted.
Licensing sentence in EN and BN on the darkened faces of 75:24-25.
Asbab: none in the sources.
Dropped: Suhayb, Abu Musa, Abu Sa'id/Abu Hurayrah, Abu Razin and Jabir
reports (numbers not confirmed); any sound-play claim beyond the texts.
Defect noted: Ibn Kathir (en) names "Jabir" for the moon hadith; the
Arabic has Jarir, followed here.
