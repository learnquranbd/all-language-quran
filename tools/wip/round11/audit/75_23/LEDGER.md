## Hadith
- Bukhari 554 (Jarir) — https://quranx.com/hadith/Bukhari/DarusSalam/Hadith-554 — quoted whole (quranx English, incl. Isma'il's closing remark). First ten words: "Narrated Qais: Jarir said, We were with the Prophet (ﷺ) and". Grading: in Sahih al-Bukhari; nothing added. Marked in text: the hadith recites 50:39, not 75:23; the link to 75:23 is Ibn Kathir's and al-Qurtubi's.
- Also checked: Bukhari 7435 ("You will definitely see your Lord with your own eyes", the wording Ibn Kathir cites) and 7434 (Jarir variant). Not quoted.
- Tirmidhi 3330 (Ibn Umar) — https://quranx.com/hadith/Tirmidhi/Hadith-3330 — saying quoted whole; the recitation rendered by reference (quranx English garbles it: "They shall be looked at their Lord"). First ten words: "Indeed the least of the people of Paradise in rank". Grading: at-Tirmidhi "هذا حديث غريب", and notes it was narrated from Ibn Umar mawquf (Ibn Abjar; al-Ashja'i/Sufyan). Reported as gharib, not upgraded.
