## Disagreements / differences kept
- Object of "law kanu ya'lamun": preference between the two abodes (Ibn Kathir, al-Muyassar, as-Sa'di) and knowledge of the world's passing (al-Baghawi) / that the Hereafter is so (al-Qurtubi), vs at-Tabari's: they would desist from denial and shirk. Both kept, no position.
- Scope: at-Tabari ties "this life" to the mushrikun; as-Sa'di reads it as a general report with zuhd/longing as its purpose. Both kept.
- hiyy: Abu Ubayda (singular, = hayat) vs an unnamed other (plural on fu'ul), both via al-Qurtubi.
