## Disagreement kept (no position taken)
"la ta'jal bil-qur'an": (1) reciting with Jibril before he finishes: Ibn Abbas in al-Qurtubi; al-Baghawi, as-Sa'di, al-Muyassar, Ibn Kathir. (2) teaching/reciting it to the Companions, or dictating it, before its meanings are explained: at-Tabari's own, with Mujahid, Ibn Jurayj, Ibn Abbas (another report), Qatada; al-Baghawi from Mujahid and Qatada. (3) asking for it to be sent down before its time: al-Qurtubi, qila. (4) delivering it to people before the explanation of its interpretation: al-Qurtubi, qila. Also yuqda = completion of delivery vs of explanation; the qira'a نقضي (Ibn Mas'ud and others in al-Qurtubi; Ya'qub in al-Baghawi).
The watch-point's fourth form ("do not hasten to ask for the revelation of something whose explanation is to come") is not worded that way in any fetched text; the nearest is al-Qurtubi's al-Hasan report (below), so it is not presented as a separate reading.
