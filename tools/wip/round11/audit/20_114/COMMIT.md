Replaces the 893-word, 6-section article with a 1,766-word, 8-section depth
article built only from the tafsir texts fetched for 20:114.

Sources: al-Malik al-Haqq glossed from at-Tabari, al-Muyassar (king who
subdued every king and tyrant), al-Qurtubi (dhu al-haqq; above children and
rivals), al-Baghawi, as-Sa'di (kingship His attribute; creaturely kingship
deficient and passing) and the abridged Ibn Kathir (everything from Him true;
no punishment before warning). Placement via as-Sa'di's link to 20:113.

Disagreement kept, no side taken, on "la ta'jal bil-qur'an": reciting with
Jibril (Ibn Abbas in al-Qurtubi; al-Baghawi, as-Sa'di, al-Muyassar, Ibn
Kathir) vs teaching it before its meanings are explained (at-Tabari with
Mujahid, Ibn Jurayj, Ibn Abbas, Qatada; al-Baghawi) vs al-Qurtubi's two qila
(asking for its sending down; delivering it before its interpretation).
Yuqda as delivery vs explanation; the naqdiya reading (Ibn Mas'ud in
al-Qurtubi, Ya'qub in al-Baghawi). 75:16-19 as the tafsirs cite it.

Zidni 'ilma: at-Tabari, Ibn Kathir with Ibn 'Uyaynah, al-Baghawi with Ibn
Mas'ud's du'a, Ma'arif, as-Sa'di (the means to knowledge; adab of the
listener and the one asked). Al-Hasan's report in al-Qurtubi noted as
ungraded and not built on.

Hadith: Bukhari 5 cited as a narration to the effect Ibn Kathir gives;
Bukhari 4982 (Anas) quoted, marked general; Ibn Majah 251 quoted whole, no
grading; Tirmidhi 3599 graded hasan gharib on the fetched page, which Ibn
Kathir quotes as gharib; both reported.
