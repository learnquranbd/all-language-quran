Card and 8-section bilingual article for 72:12: wa-anna zanannā an lan
nu'jiza Allaha fi al-ard wa-lan nu'jizahu haraban. Jinn as speakers;
72:1-2 and 72:8-11, 72:13 as frame only, neighbours not developed.

Sources: Tabari, Qurtubi, Ibn Kathir (ar; en grouped 72:11-17), Sa'di,
Baghawi, Muyassar. All named. Ma'arif (grouped 72:10-12) speaks to 72:10
only; not used.
- Zann = knowledge/certainty: Qurtubi ('ilm wa-yaqin, contrasted with the
  zann of 72:5 and 72:7), Tabari ('alimna), Baghawi ('alimna wa-ayqanna),
  Muyassar (ayqanna), Ibn Kathir (na'lamu), Sa'di (became clear to us now).
  Qurtubi: reached by istidlal and reflection on Allah's signs.
- Fi al-ard vs haraban kept as differing emphases, no position:
  Tabari/Baghawi (if He intends harm / if He seeks us), Muyassar
  (wherever we are / fleeing to the sky), Ibn Kathir (even going deep
  into flight), Sa'di (every means of escape), Qurtubi (haraban as hal,
  haribin; "by flight or otherwise").
- Tabari's "wherever they were" as the verse's summary.
Grammar checked against data/irab. Quran parallels in the article's own
voice: 2:46, 69:20 (zann as certainty), 42:31, 29:22, 8:59 (mu'jizin),
55:33, 9:118 (Sa'di's la malja'a echoed, not attributed to him).
Licensing sentence: the verse is the jinn's statement about themselves
and licenses no claim about any person, human or jinn.
Hadith: none attached to 72:12. Asbab: none.
Dropped: Ibn Kathir's al-A'mash anecdote and tara'iq qidada (72:11),
Muqatil's report (72:16), any guess at which signs.
