## Fi al-ard vs haraban (watch-point), as kept in §5
Tabari / Baghawi: condition per half (if He intends harm / a matter; if He seeks us).
Muyassar: wherever we are vs fleeing to the sky. Ibn Kathir: second half as
intensification ("even if we went deep into flight"). Sa'di: every means of escape.
Qurtubi: haraban = hal (haribin), both halves folded into "by flight or otherwise".
Presented as differing emphases, no position taken.

## Grammar
