Card and 8-section bilingual article for 54:27: inna mursilu n-naqati
fitnatan lahum fa-rtaqibhum wa-stabir. 54:23-26 framing; 54:28-29 and
54:31 pointers only; 26:155 pointer (cited by Ibn Kathir abridged).

Sources: at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi, al-Baghawi, as-Sa'di, al-Muyassar, Ibn
Kathir (ar; en abridged checked), Ma'arif al-Qur'an (grouped, framing).
- mursilu glossed ba'ithu / mukhriju (Tabari, Muyassar, Qurtubi,
  Baghawi); the requested sign from the hadbah / rock (Tabari, Baghawi's
  ta'annut, Ibn Kathir). Qurtubi's two-rak'ah emergence report kept as
  "fa-ruwiya", no chain or grading; fuller story left to 26:155.
- fitna = ikhtibar / ibtila' / mihna / imtihan in all six; Tabari's
  two-sided question; Qurtubi: maf'ul lahu. "naqat Allah" as Tabari's
  phrase only. Sa'di: the greatest blessing was also the test.
- Disagreements kept, no position: object of irtaqib (conduct: Tabari,
  Qurtubi, Baghawi; punishment: Muyassar; Ibn Kathir open; Sa'di both)
  and of istabir (watching, harm, calling, outcome promised).
- ta -> ta' in istabir (Tabari; Qurtubi: itbaq with sad), no meaning
  drawn from it.
Hadith: none attached by any fetched tafsir; stated in one sentence.
Asbab: none. Licensing sentence for Thamud in both languages; no modern
geography; nothing about Salih (AS) erring.
Dropped: "intensified patience" from the verb form; the water rota,
milk and young camel (26:155 / 54:28); hamstringer's name and Mujahid
(54:28-29); Ma'arif's "first destroyed" aside.
