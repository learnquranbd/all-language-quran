## Disagreements kept (no position taken)
- Object of irtaqib: people's conduct (Tabari, Qurtubi, Baghawi) vs the punishment coming (Muyassar) vs open "what their affair comes to" (Ibn Kathir); as-Sa'di holds both with "aw".
- Object of istabir: patience in watching (Tabari; Baghawi first) / on harm (Qurtubi; Baghawi's qila) / in calling (Sa'di) / calling + harm (Muyassar) / with them, outcome is yours (Ibn Kathir).
