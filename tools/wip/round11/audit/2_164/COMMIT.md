Replaces the 868-word, 6-section article with a 1,779-word, 9-section
one, from at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi, Ibn Kathir, as-Sa'di, al-Baghawi and
al-Muyassar on 2:164. The shipped card is kept.

Occasion as reports only: at-Tabari's 'Ata' (how can one god suffice),
Abu ad-Duha (let him bring a sign), Sa'id ibn Jubayr and as-Suddi (as-Safa
turned to gold), Ibn Kathir's Ibn Mardawayh/Ibn Abi Hatim line; at-Tabari's
verdict that no report settles it and the meaning is the same either way.
No grading in any source; non-licence line added. One bridging line to
2:163, one line to 2:22, one to 2:165 (as-Sa'di: all love is His).
Each sign as glossed: khalq, plural heavens/singular earth (al-Qurtubi,
al-Baghawi), ikhtilaf as succession from khuluf (at-Tabari, 25:62),
ships laden yet not sinking, as-Sa'di's questions, the sea-travel ruling,
the earth's life and death, tasrif and sahab.
Disagreements kept: dabbah excluding birds (at-Tabari) or all animals
(al-Qurtubi, 11:6); tasrif as His turning vs the winds' own shifting
(at-Tabari against a grammarian); musakhkhar as sent vs held still
(al-Qurtubi prefers sent); min as-sama' as clouds or the sky (al-Baghawi);
two readings of ikhtilaf (al-Qurtubi).
Reasoning per at-Tabari (mawadi' al-hujaj; the addressees affirmed a
Creator but associated idols), al-Muyassar, al-Baghawi, as-Sa'di.
Hadith: Muslim 899 ('A'ishah, wind and cloud on his face, "Mercy"),
cited by al-Qurtubi for musakhkhar, quoted whole, marked as not about
the revelation. No graded hadith on the revelation itself.
Dropped: al-Qurtubi's unsourced "woe to whoever reads it and does not
reflect", Ka'b, Nuh's bird-shaped ship, Ma'arif's fluid/ice science.

Corrected from the old article: "the mufassirun read the list" as proof
of resurrection (only as-Sa'di). Dropped as unsourced: two-books claim,
30:21-24, 35:9, 21:22, the stars-guide-ships line and the practice list.
