## Disagreements kept (none chosen)
- naqata: accusative of warning, "beware" (al-Qurtubi, at-Tabari, Ibn Kathir, as-Sa'di, al-Baghawi) vs implied "leave" (al-Qurtubi's wa-qila; al-Baghawi citing az-Zajjaj).
- What to beware: any harm (Ibn Kathir, Muyassar) vs hamstringing (as-Sa'di, al-Baghawi, al-Qurtubi).
- suqyaha: her drinking / her day at the water (at-Tabari + Qatada, Ibn Kathir, Muyassar, al-Baghawi, al-Qurtubi) vs her milk given them to drink (as-Sa'di).
