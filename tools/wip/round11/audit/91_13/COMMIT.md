Card and 8-section bilingual article for 91:13: fa-qala lahum rasulu
Allahi naqata Allahi wa-suqyaha, seven words, Salih's warning to Thamud.
91:11-12 and 91:14-15 named as frame only, not treated.

Sources: Tabari, Qurtubi, Baghawi, Sa'di, Ibn Kathir (ar + en
abridged), Muyassar, Ma'arif. Slot check: all eight files sit on 91:13.
- Speaker: all name Salih (AS); Sa'di: he spoke muhadhdhiran.
- naqata: accusative of warning, "beware" (Qurtubi's al-asad al-asad,
  Tabari, IK, Sa'di, Baghawi) vs implied "leave" (Qurtubi's wa-qila
  with 7:73; Baghawi citing az-Zajjaj). None chosen.
- Beware of what: any harm (IK, Muyassar) vs hamstringing (Sa'di,
  Baghawi, Qurtubi). Kept side by side.
- suqyaha: her day at the water (Tabari + Qatada "qism Allah", IK,
  Muyassar, Baghawi, Qurtubi; "that weighed hard on them") vs her milk
  given them to drink (Sa'di). Kept as a disagreement.
- Placement: Ma'arif reads Thamud as the example of 91:10's failing group.
Hadith: none attached to 91:13. Dropped Bukhari 4942 (the 'Abdullah ibn Zam'a
sermon): Ibn Kathir cites it under 91:12, not this verse. Asbab: none.
Licensing sentence in both languages; Salih kept as the messenger only.
Shipped 91:9, 26:155 and 54:27 cross-referenced by number.
Dropped: 11:64, 26:156, 54:28 (not cited by number on 91:13); IK's
name for the hamstringer, 54:29 and Qatada's allegiance report (91:12/14).
