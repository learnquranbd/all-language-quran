Card and 8-section bilingual article for 75:14: bal after the informing of
75:13; basira and its feminine ending; who the witness is; 75:15 ma'adhir as
context only; Muslim 2968; seeing others' faults; admission against oneself.

Sources: Tabari (the 75:14 text came from the by_ayah 75:15 fetch; the 75:14
fetch is his 75:13), Qurtubi, Ibn Kathir (ar), Baghawi, Sa'di, Muyassar
(grouped 75:14-15), Ma'arif (grouped 75:14-18). Ibn Kathir en wrong group.
- Ending -a: intensive (Abu 'Ubayd in Qurtubi; Baghawi; Tabari unnamed);
  "you are a proof against yourself" (Akhfash; Basrans in Tabari); limbs
  (al-Qutabi in Qurtubi; Baghawi); 'ayn basira (al-Farra' verse).
- Kept as a disagreement: limbs as witnesses (Tabari's lead; Ibn 'Abbas via
  'Ali b. Abi Talha; 'Ikrimah, Muqatil, al-Kalbi; 24:24 in Qurtubi) vs man
  himself (Ibn 'Abbas via al-'Awfi, Qatada, Ibn Zayd with 17:14; Ibn Kathir;
  Sa'di "witness and reckoner"; Muyassar). Recording angels (Suddi, Dahhak).
- 75:15: excuses (Mujahid and others; Qurtubi's preference, 40:52, 6:23) vs
  curtains in Yemeni speech (Dahhak, Zajjaj); Ibn 'Abbas via Mawardi.
- Qatada and al-Hasan on seeing others' faults; the Injil saying given as
  "it used to be said", not as hadith.
- Ibn al-'Arabi via Qurtubi: admission against oneself accepted (9:102);
  an-Nakha'i on excuses.
Hadith: Muslim 2968 (Abu Hurayrah), attached by Qurtubi; closing part only,
quranx English. Licensing sentence in EN and BN on the hypocrite.
Asbab: none in the sources.
Dropped: 36:65 and 41:20 (no fetched tafsir cites them); Qurtubi's fiqh of
iqrar, 3:81, 58:18 and the Unays hadith. 75:2 left to its own draft.
