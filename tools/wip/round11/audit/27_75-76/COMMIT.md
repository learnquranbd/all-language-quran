Two-ayah card and bilingual article for 27:75-76, read as one arc:
nothing hidden in heaven or earth but is in a clear Record (75), and
from that all-encompassing knowledge the Qur'an settles for the
Children of Israel most of what they disputed (76).

Sources (confirmed per verse, Arabic clauses in LEDGER):
- 75: ghā'ibah = everything concealed/absent from a creature's eye
  (al-Baghawi, as-Sa'di; Ibn Abbas via at-Tabari and Ibn Kathir).
  kitāb mubīn = al-Lawḥ al-Maḥfūẓ / Umm al-Kitāb, holding all that was
  and will be; mubīn = exhaustive and exact, not public (at-Tabari,
  al-Qurtubi, al-Baghawi, al-Muyassar, as-Sa'di).
- 76: akthar = most, "of the matter of religion" (al-Baghawi); the
  truth in most disputed things (at-Tabari, al-Muyassar); haymanah over
  earlier Books, removing ishkāl (as-Sa'di); altered Torah/Gospel and
  dropped rulings (al-Qurtubi). ʿĪsā (AS) as the chief example: two
  opposite errors answered by the just middle word (Ibn Kathir, with
  19:34; at-Tabari).

Disagreement kept as disagreement (al-Qurtubi on al-ghā'ibah): al-Hasan
(the Hour) vs an-Naqqāsh (hidden punishment) vs Ibn Shajarah (general),
no verdict laid over them.

Bridge 75->76 taken from Ma'arif al-Qur'an: authority to overrule a
dispute belongs to the one superior in knowledge.

Hadith: Sahih Muslim 2653 (decrees written 50,000 years before creation,
Throne over the water), quoted whole, marked as a general narration not
attached by the mufassirūn to the verse. Bukhari 3435 confirmed but not
used.

Carries the non-license sentence in both languages: clarifies doctrine,
licenses nothing against Jews, Christians, or any living community.
27:77-78 deferred as future ground, not expounded.
