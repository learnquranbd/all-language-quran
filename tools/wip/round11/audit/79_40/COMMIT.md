Card and 8-section article on wa-amma man khafa maqama rabbihi wa naha
an-nafsa 'ani-l-hawa, the answering half of 79:37-39 (9 Arabic words).

Sources: at-Tabari, Ibn Kathir, al-Qurtubi, as-Sa'di, al-Baghawi, the
Muyassar and Ma'arif al-Qur'an, all fetched for 79:40. Ibn Kathir abridged
(en) used only for his 79:37-38 glosses.

Glosses kept side by side for maqam rabbih: the servant's standing at the
reckoning (al-Qurtubi, ar-Rabi', at-Tabari's questioning, Ibn Kathir's
standing and judgement, Muyassar, Ma'arif); a standing that is Allah's
(Qatada; as-Sa'di's al-qiyam 'alayhi and just requital); fear at the
moment of sin in this world (Mujahid, Muqatil, al-Kalbi). No ranking.
naha an-nafs and al-hawa: each source's qualifier named (what Allah
dislikes, forbidden things it craves, al-ahwa' al-fasida, [evil] desire).
Ma'arif's three levels from Tafsir Mazhari summarised; 15:42 per Ma'arif.
55:46 cited as al-Qurtubi's nazir. Sahl and Ibn Mas'ud sayings marked as
al-Qurtubi's chainless citations.

Hadith: none attached to the verse by any fetched tafsir. Bukhari 52
(Ma'arif, general) fetched but too long to quote whole; dropped, not
clipped. The "desire follows what I brought" report not confirmed; dropped.
Asbab: al-Qurtubi's reports conflict (Mus'ab, Abu Bakr, al-Kalbi's general
case); none relied on, verse read by placement.
Licensing sentence added for the transgressors of 79:37-39. 79:41 named
only as the verse that follows.
