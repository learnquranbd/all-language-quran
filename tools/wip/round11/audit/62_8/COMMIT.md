Replaces the 799-word, 6-section article with a 1,763-word, 8-section one,
built only from the eight tafsir files fetched for 62:8 (shipped card kept).

Sources: at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi, as-Sa'di, Ibn Kathir (ar; en abridged,
grouped 62:5-8), Muyassar, Ma'arif; al-Baghawi only repeats the verse.
Context 62:6-7 via Ibn Kathir's reading of the challenge (invoke death on
the misguided party), with 2:94-96, 3:61 and 19:75 as his parallels.
Licensing sentence in both languages. Fleeing as hating and refusing the
wish (at-Tabari, Ma'arif), as-Sa'di's "written for them", Muyassar's la
mafarra. Az-Zajjaj on the fa of fa-innahu (condition sense of alladhi)
and al-Qurtubi's pause reading, with Zuhayr and Tarafa. Mulaqikum glosses
(at-Tabari, Muyassar, Ma'arif) and 4:78 (Ibn Kathir). Ma'arif on avoiding
causes of death (leaning wall, fire; plague a separate issue, 2:243-244).
Ghayb and shahada (at-Tabari), Qatada's "Allah humbled the son of Adam by
death", the informing and recompense (at-Tabari, as-Sa'di, Muyassar).

Hadith: none quoted. Ibn Kathir's Ahmad report on the Jews and the wish
for death not confirmed: Bukhari 4958 and Tirmidhi 3348 carry only the
Abu Jahl sentence. The Tabarani fox report named as his citation, not as
hadith.

Dropped from the old article: "the challenge was rhetorical" (Ibn Kathir
reads it as real), firar as battle flight, 51:50, 3:185, naba' and
habitual-kana grammar, Medinan status, the 62:11 hinge reading.
