## Who 2:4 describes (watch-point): both views kept, with names, no position taken
- Same people as 2:3, every believer: Mujahid, Abu al-Aliyah, ar-Rabi' ibn Anas, Qatadah (via Ibn Kathir); Ibn Kathir's own preference; al-Qurtubi's second "qila".
- Both verses about the believers of the People of the Book: Ibn Kathir's view 2 (unnamed).
- 2:3 Arab believers, 2:4 believers of the People of the Book: as-Suddi from Ibn Abbas, Ibn Mas'ud and Companions; at-Tabari's choice (per Ibn Kathir; at-Tabari's 2:4 report 290 and his 2:5 discussion agree); al-Baghawi; al-Qurtubi's first "qila". Ibn Kathir's criticism that at-Tabari argued only from symmetry is reported as his.
- 2:5 "those": at-Tabari's three views and his justice-based preference for both groups.

## Earlier scriptures (watch-point)
Only what the sources say: as-Sa'di (scope, no distinction); Ibn Kathir (detail vs outline, with Bukhari 4485); Ma'arif (true as revealed, excepting changes and distortions; abrogated, act on the Qur'an alone); al-Qurtubi's two answers. Licensing sentence in both languages (S6 P2), since the article reports at-Tabari's implied rebuke of the People of the Book of that time; 29:46 quoted from Ibn Kathir.

## Iman vs yaqin (watch-point)
From the text only: Ma'arif (denial vs doubt and hesitation; oral affirmation not enough), as-Sa'di's definition, al-Qurtubi's definition, al-Baghawi's "qila" (knowledge by inference; Allah not called muqin).
