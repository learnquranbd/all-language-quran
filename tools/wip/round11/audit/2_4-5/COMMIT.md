Key widened from 2:4 to 2:4-5 (2:5 closes the description; 2:3 left free).
New card and an 8-section, 1,765-word bilingual article.

Sources: at-Tabari, Ibn Kathir (ar + en abridged), al-Qurtubi, as-Sa'di,
al-Baghawi, al-Muyassar, Ma'arif, on 2:4 and 2:5. Slots checked.
- What was sent down: the Qur'an (al-Baghawi, al-Qurtubi), plus the Sunnah
  (al-Muyassar, as-Sa'di with 4:113); Ibn Abbas: no distinction between messengers.
- Who 2:4 describes, kept as a disagreement: all believers (Mujahid, Abu
  al-Aliyah, ar-Rabi', Qatadah; Ibn Kathir's preference) vs believers of the
  People of the Book (as-Suddi from Ibn Abbas and Ibn Mas'ud; at-Tabari's
  choice; al-Baghawi); al-Qurtubi gives both. al-Qurtubi's i'rab ties 2:4 to
  2:5; at-Tabari's justice argument for "those" meaning both groups.
- Earlier books: as-Sa'di; Ibn Kathir's detail vs outline; Ma'arif (true as
  revealed, excepting distortions; abrogated); al-Qurtubi's two answers.
- At-Tabari's implied rebuke of that time, with the licensing line in both
  languages and 29:46 from Ibn Kathir.
- Akhirah and yaqin: at-Tabari on the name, Ibn Abbas on its contents,
  al-Muyassar on why it is named apart; yu'minun vs yuqinun from Ma'arif,
  definitions from as-Sa'di, al-Qurtubi, al-Baghawi.
- 2:5: Ibn Kathir, at-Tabari, as-Sa'di on 'ala vs fi (34:24); falah from
  al-Qurtubi, al-Baghawi, Ibn Abbas, as-Sa'di.
Hadith: Bukhari 4485 (do not affirm or deny) and Bukhari 97 (two rewards),
both quoted whole, marked as not naming the verse. Asbab: none used.
Dropped: al-Qurtubi's Abu Dharr 104-books report and Ibn Kathir's Ibn Abi
Hatim narration on 2:1-5 (no grading given); al-Qurtubi's unattributed
Ibn Salam nuzul clause.
