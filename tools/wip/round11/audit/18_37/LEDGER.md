# SOURCES ledger — 18:37

Verse: 18:37, the believing companion's rebuke to the arrogant owner of the two
gardens (parable of the two men, 18:32-44).

## Tafsir fetched (files in this dir) and how used

- **t15 at-Tabari (ar)** — NAMED. Speaker is the companion "less than him in
  wealth and children" (أقل منه مالا وولدا), yuḥāwiruhu = "yukhāṭibuhu wa
  yukallimuhu" (addressing/speaking to him). Creation stages: min turāb = "خلق
  أباك آدم من تراب"; nuṭfa = "أنشأك من نطفة الرجل والمرأة" (both parents);
  sawwāka rajulan = "عدلك بشرا سويا رجلا ذكرا لا أنثى". Ties akfarta to denial of
  being remade "خلقا جديدا بعد ما تصير رفاتا" (resurrection). Used in §2.1, §3.2,
  §4.1, §5.1, §6.1, §7.1.
- **t14 Ibn Kathir (ar)** — NAMED. "واعظا له وزاجرا عما هو فيه من الكفر بالله
  والاغترار"; akfarta = "إنكار وتعظيم لما وقع فيه من جحود ربه"; turāb = creation
  of Adam from clay, offspring "من سلالة من ماء مهين"; the self-evident knowledge
  that one "كان معدوما ثم وجد" and owes existence to the Creator. Used §3.1, §3.2,
  §4.1, §4.2, §5.1.
- **t16 al-Muyassar (ar)** — NAMED. "واعظًا له"; nuṭfa = "نطفة الأبوين"; sawwāka
  = "بشرًا معتدل القامة والخَلْق"; resurrection argument: "القادر على ابتداء
  الخلق، قادر على إعادتهم." Used §3.2, §5.1, §7.1.
- **t90 al-Qurtubi (ar)** — NAMED. Name of the believer disputed: "يهوذا أو
  تمليخا; على الخلاف في اسمه" (kept AS a disagreement, no position). Argument:
  what he acknowledges "أبدع من الإعادة" (more wondrous than the resurrection);
  sawwāka = "معتدل القامة والخلق، صحيح الأعضاء ذكرا". Used §2.2, §6.1, §7.1.
- **t91 as-Sa'di (ar)** — NAMED. "ناصحا له"; stages "نقلك من طور إلى طور حتى
  سواك رجلا"; "لم تحصل لك الدنيا بحولك وقوتك، بل بفضل الله"; blends ingratitude +
  resurrection denial ("تجحد نعمته، وتزعم أنه لا يبعثك"). Used §3.2, §3.3, §6.2,
  §6.3.
- **t94 al-Baghawi (ar)** — NAMED. "صاحبه المسلم"; turāb = "خلق أصلك من تراب";
  sawwāka = "عدلك بشرا سويا ذكرا". Used §4.1, §6.1.
- **t168 Ma'arif al-Qur'an (en)** — NAMED (grouped 18:34-39). The two-men
  argument: wealth taken as proof of divine pleasure; refutation that worldly
  wealth "is given by Allah... even to the disbelievers and sinful persons,
  rather to the snakes, scorpions and beasts"; "Allah's approval... depends on
  one's faith and deeds alone and not on the amount of wealth." Used §1.3, §5.2,
  §8.1.
- **t169 Ibn Kathir abridged (en)** — NAMED (grouped 18:37-41). "denunciation";
  Adam from dust, offspring "from despised liquid." Used §3.1, §4.1. The two
  hadith it carries (Abu Musa, treasure of Paradise "lā ḥawla wa lā quwwata illā
  billāh") sit on the 18:39 phrase, NOT 18:37 — not used, noted in §8.3.

## Hadith

None used. No sound hadith attaches directly to 18:37. The only narrations in
the fetched tafsir — the Abu Musa "treasure of Paradise / lā ḥawla wa lā quwwata
illā billāh" (t169, Ibn Kathir cites it "in the Sahih") and the Anas "mā shāʾ
Allāh lā quwwata illā billāh" report from Shuʿab al-Īmān (t168, Ma'arif) — both
gloss the phrase of **18:39**, not this verse. Stated as such in §8.3 and left to
their own place per the no-preempt watch-point. No number fetched, none quoted.

## Asbab an-nuzul

None. 18:37 is inside a coined parable (mathal), not an occasion of revelation;
no asbab claimed. Placement (the believer's reply within 18:32-44) used instead,
per SOURCE-GATE §2.

## Dropped / not asserted

- The Abu Musa and Anas narrations above — dropped from the hadith slot because
  they belong to 18:39; disclosed in §8.3 rather than silently used.
- The believer's name (Yahūdha / Tamlīkhā) — kept explicitly as a disputed point
  (al-Qurtubi), no side taken.
- No claim that any prophet erred; the negative figure is the arrogant garden-
  owner, treated as a parable type. §8.2 states plainly, both languages, that the
  verse licenses nothing against any living person, rich man, neighbour or
  community.
- Nothing asserted from memory; every attribution above rests on an Arabic/English
  clause in the fetched files.
