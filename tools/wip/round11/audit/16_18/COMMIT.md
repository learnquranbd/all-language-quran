Replaces the 844-word, 6-section article with a 1,684-word, 9-section one,
from Ibn Kathir (ar, en), at-Tabari, the Muyassar, as-Sa'di, al-Baghawi,
al-Qurtubi and Ma'arif on 16:18. The shipped card is kept.
Built on what is the verse's own, not the 14:34 twin: the roll of gifts in
16:3-16:16 that it sums (with Ibn Kathir's notes on rivers, landmarks and
stars), the turn from 16:17 (Ibn Kathir, as-Sa'di, Ma'arif on the group),
and the ending inna-llaha la-ghafurun rahim.
Count: Muyassar's hasr and plural paraphrase; at-Tabari reads la tuhsuha as
being unable to render thanks; as-Sa'di's bare count, then breaths, glances,
the unknown and the harm averted. Al-Qurtubi only points back to 14:34.
Ending: what is forgiven is taqsir in thanks (at-Tabari, al-Baghawi,
Muyassar); Ibn Kathir's three conditions; as-Sa'di's little thanks accepted;
Rahim as favours not cut off for shortfall and sins (al-Baghawi) and
punishment not hastened (Muyassar). 16:7 and 16:14 read from the text.
Kept as found: at-Tabari ties the pardon to tawbah and inabah; the others
state it without naming that condition.
No hadith: no fetched tafsir attaches one. No asbab.
Dropped from the shipped article as unsourced for 16:18: the ihsa-as-
encompass and singular-ni'mah notes, the body examples, "two endings as two
sides", the thanks-loop, 16:53, the feet-swelling hadith, three organs of
shukr. Fixed: thanks-shortfall wording misattributed to as-Sa'di.
