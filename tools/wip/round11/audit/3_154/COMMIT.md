Key 3:154 alone: the drowsiness sent down as security after the grief at Uhud,
the party kept awake by their own selves, "the matter belongs wholly to Allah",
and the test and sifting of hearts. New card and a 1,750-word, 9-section article.
3:155-3:158 stays free; 3:155 is cited once, for the pardon.

Sources: at-Tabari, Ibn Kathir (ar), al-Qurtubi, al-Baghawi, as-Sa'di,
al-Muyassar and Ma'arif on 3:154; all slots on target.
Kept as differences, no position: amn vs amanah; the sleepless party as the
hypocrites (at-Tabari's reports, al-Baghawi, al-Qurtubi) vs hypocrisy or weak
faith (as-Sa'di); "the matter" as victory (al-Baghawi, as-Sa'di) vs the going
out (al-Qurtubi, Muyassar); li-yabtaliya addressed to the hypocrites (at-Tabari)
vs all present (Ibn Kathir, as-Sa'di); tamhis as cleansing vs disclosing; the
sleep in the ranks (Abu Talhah) vs after the Meccans left (as-Suddi).
Qadar explained as the tafsir does (Ibn Kathir's binding decree, as-Sa'di on
means and decree), no theologians' debate.
Hadith: Bukhari 4562 (Abu Talhah's sword) and Tirmidhi 3007 (hasan sahih),
each quoted whole; Muslim 2664 ("if" opens the work of Shaytan) quoted whole
and marked as not attached to the verse.
Names: Mu'attib ibn Qushayr and Abdullah ibn Ubayy only as attributed reports,
with the licensing sentence; no Companion named as turning back.
Asbab: none; the az-Zubayr quote stops before "about that Allah sent down".
Dropped: al-Hasan's report (matruk narrator per at-Tabari's editor), Qatadah's
addition, Ibn Mas'ud and Abd ar-Rahman ibn Awf reports, the kullahu readings
and 48:12 (length).
Fix: the man named in the reports (al-Qurtubi; az-Zubayr via Ibn Kathir) left unnamed, since his standing is disputed.
