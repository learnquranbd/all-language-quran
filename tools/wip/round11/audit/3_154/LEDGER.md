## Disagreements kept (no position taken)
- amn and amanah: same meaning vs the "it is said" distinction (al-Qurtubi, al-Baghawi).
- Who the sleepless party were: the hypocrites (at-Tabari with Qatadah, ar-Rabi', Ibn Ishaq, Ibn Zayd; al-Baghawi; al-Qurtubi) vs hypocrisy or weak faith (as-Sa'di).
- "The matter" in hal lana min al-amr: victory (al-Baghawi, as-Sa'di) vs the going-out (al-Qurtubi's first view, al-Muyassar).
- Addressee of li-yabtaliya: the hypocrites (at-Tabari) vs everyone present (Ibn Kathir, as-Sa'di).
- Tamhis: cleansing (as-Sa'di, al-Qurtubi) vs bringing out and making manifest (al-Baghawi).
- When the sleep came: in the ranks (Abu Talhah) vs after the Meccans left (as-Suddi in at-Tabari), set side by side.

## Dropped
- al-Hasan's report (at-Tabari 8097, "not everyone who fights is killed"): at-Tabari's editor marks Bahr as-Saqqa' in the chain matruk.
- Qatadah's addition about the hypocrites (al-Bayhaqi in Ibn Kathir; Tirmidhi 3008, not fetched with --save, seen while finding 3007): Ibn Kathir judges it Qatadah's own words; not used.
- Confirmed but cut for length: Ibn Mas'ud "drowsiness in battle is security, in prayer from Shaytan" (at-Tabari 8083, Ibn Kathir); Abd ar-Rahman ibn Awf "sleep was cast on us at Uhud" (at-Tabari 8079); Ibn Kathir's 48:12 parallel; the kullahu/kulluhu readings (Abu Amr, Ya'qub; at-Tabari prefers the accusative); al-Qurtubi's "it is said" that kutiba al-qatl means fighting was prescribed; at-Tabari's rule that "that Allah may test" means His friends come to know; al-Qurtubi's other readings of li-yabtaliya; al-Baghawi's "it is said" that the sleep was meant to tell the hypocrites apart, and his "it is said" that they thought Muhammad ﷺ had been killed; al-Qurtubi's note that ta'ifah can mean one person; Ibn Ishaq's (at-Tabari 8089) "they hoped for no good end".
- 3:155 material (Ma'arif on Zajjaj, the Companions' standing, Hatib, Ibn Umar on Uthman; Ibn Kathir's Ahmad report on Abd ar-Rahman ibn Awf and Uthman): outside the key.
