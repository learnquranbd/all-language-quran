## Hadith
- Tirmidhi 406. URL https://quranx.com/hadith/Tirmidhi/Hadith-406 (saved hadith_Tirmidhi_406.txt). First ten words of the Prophet's wording used: "There is no man who commits a sin, then stands". Grading: at-Tirmidhi "حديث علي حديث حسن لا نعرفه إلا من هذا الوجه". In this wording he recites 3:135, not 4:110; the article says so.
- Checked, not quoted: Abu Dawud 1521 (DarusSalam; recites 3:135; no grading shown), Tirmidhi 3006 (no grade, raf'/waqf note).
- Ahmad's version (both verses recited) is cited only as Ibn Kathir gives it, with his note "وذكرنا ما في سنده من مقال". Not quoted as a hadith text.
