Card and 8-section bilingual article (1,759 EN words) on 59:2: huwa
alladhi akhraja, li-awwal al-hashr, the two surmises, the unreckoned
direction and the terror, yukhribuna buyutahum, fa'tabiru. 59:3-5 untouched.

Sources: at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi, as-Sa'di, Ibn Kathir (ar; en group
59:1-5 for the 59:2 part), al-Baghawi, Ma'arif (group 59:2-4), Muyassar.
- Group: Banu an-Nadir (Muyassar, Tabari; IK from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid,
  Zuhri; Qurtubi via Ibn Abbas "Surat an-Nadir"); al-Hasan's Banu
  Qurayzah kept as the dissent Qurtubi records via Tha'labi.
- Background only as reported: IK's treaty and breach, IK's maghazi
  summary via Ibn Ishaq/Yazid b. Ruman; Tabari's terms; Qurtubi's
  "ruwiya" version. Siege length kept as a difference: 21 nights
  (Qurtubi) vs 6 days (IK).
- Li-awwal al-hashr unranked: first gathering to Sham (Tabari, Zuhri,
  Ibn Abbas via IK); first of several (Kalbi, Murra via Baghawi; Sa'di);
  fire at the end of time (Qatada); first/middle/last (Ibn al-Arabi);
  Muslims' mustering (Ma'arif). Al-Hasan's mursal marked chainless.
- Yukhribun: Abu Amr's tashdid vs takhfif (Tabari prefers takhfif;
  Baghawi: one meaning); timber, lintels, pegs (Zuhri, Yazid, Ibn Zayd);
  inside/outside (Qatada); rebuilding breaches (Ibn Abbas); covenant-
  breaking as majaz (Zuhri, Ibn al-Arabi in Qurtubi).
- I'tibar: hearts' sight (Tabari), eyes (Qurtubi), 'umum al-lafz and
  qiyas of like to like (Sa'di). No qiyas dispute in any fetched text.
Explicit paragraph in both languages: the verse licenses nothing against
any living person or community, including People of the Scripture today.
Hadith: Bukhari 335 (nusirtu bi-l-ru'b), marked general, not attached.
Abu Dawud 3004 (cited by IK) not used: no grading on the fetched page.
Dropped: fort names, Qatada-fire "in the Sahih", fiqh asides, 59:3-6 matter.
- Review fix: Bukhari 335 described, not partly quoted (rule: quote whole or describe).
