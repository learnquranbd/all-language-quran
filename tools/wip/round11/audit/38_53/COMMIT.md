Card and 9-section bilingual article for 38:53: "this is what you are promised for the
Day of Reckoning", the five-word seal on the Garden of 38:49-52.

Sources: Tabari (with as-Suddi), Qurtubi, Ibn Kathir (ar/en, group 38:49-54), Baghawi,
Sa'di, Muyassar, Ma'arif (group 38:52-61); quranx 38.53 for Tanwir, Jalalayn, Maududi.
- Referent of hadha: description (Ibn Kathir), bliss (Muyassar), recompense (Qurtubi,
  Sa'di), honour promised in this world (Tabari); shift third to second person (Jalalayn).
- Where the sentence is spoken: Tanwir "Allah will say to them" vs Baghawi "say to the
  believers"; kept side by side.
- Readings: ta (general) vs ya as report (Qurtubi: Ibn Kathir, Ibn Muhaysin, Abu Amr,
  Ya'qub, as-Sulami, Abu Ubayd, Abu Hatim; Baghawi's shorter list, Qaf parallel 50:32).
- li-yawm: "on the Day" (Qurtubi with al-A'sha, Baghawi) vs "in this world, for the Day"
  (as-Suddi via Tabari).
- Neighbours via grouped sources: opened gates (Ibn Kathir; Maududi's three senses, 39:73),
  atrab (Ibn Kathir one sense, Ma'arif and Maududi two).
- Six hadha in 38:49-59 (PREP counts); 38:54 via Muyassar; 11:108 and 16:96-97
  cross-referenced, not repeated.
Watch-point: no fetched source disputes the Garden as afterlife vs reckoning; the real
differences above are kept instead of an invented one.
Hadith: none attached by any fetched source; said so in one sentence.
Asbab: none in the fetched sources; not used.
Framing: licensing sentence in both languages (38:55-56 transgressors).
Dropped: 56:18 cups detail; Ma'arif's spouse-age advice; any biography of the reciter.
Themes: hereafter, accountability.
