## Watch-point 1 (afterlife vs reckoning): NOT FOUND as phrased
No fetched source disputes that the Garden is the afterlife: Ibn Kathir (after resurrection), Tabari (fi l-akhira), Muyassar (yawm al-qiyama) all place it there. Real differences kept instead, both sides named, no position:
(a) where the sentence is spoken: Tanwir "Allah will say to them" (in the Garden) vs al-Baghawi "say to the believers" (told now);
(b) the li- of li-yawm: "fi yawm al-hisab" (Qurtubi, Baghawi) vs as-Suddi/Tabari "in this world, for the Day";
(c) ta vs ya reading (Qurtubi's and Baghawi's lists differ in length: Baghawi omits Ibn Muhaysin and Ya'qub; reported as given).
Note: the gates and fruit are 38:50-51, not 38:53; handled as the referent of hadha (§7).
