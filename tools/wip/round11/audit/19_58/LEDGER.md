# SOURCES ledger — 19:58 (sajdah verse)

## Tafsir fetched (all pre-fetched in audit/19_58; none re-fetched)
- **14 Ibn Kathir (ar)** — NAMED. Group mapping via as-Suddi & Ibn Jarir: "فالذي عنى به من ذرية آدم: إدريس ... من حملنا مع نوح: إبراهيم ... من ذرية إبراهيم: إسحاق ويعقوب وإسماعيل ... من ذرية إسرائيل: موسى، وهارون، وزكريا ويحيى وعيسى ابن مريم". Idris = grandfather of Nuh, "هذا هو الأظهر أن إدريس في عمود نسب نوح". Competing view kept: "وقد قيل: إنه من أنبياء بني إسرائيل" from Isra' greeting ("الأخ الصالح" not "الولد الصالح"). Genus-of-prophets point: "بل جنس الأنبياء". kharru: "سجدوا لربهم خضوعا واستكانة، وحمدا وشكرا". Consensus on sajda: "أجمع العلماء على شرعية السجود هاهنا، اقتداء بهم". Umar athar: "قرأ عمر ... سورة مريم، فسجد وقال: هذا السجود، فأين البكي؟" (via al-A'mash > Ibrahim; Ibn Kathir notes "أبي معمر" dropped in one riwaya). Also cites Sahih Bukhari / Mujahid–Ibn Abbas on Sad-sajda + "فبهداهم اقتده".
- **15 at-Tabari** — NAMED. Same 4-group split; his last group "موسى وهارون وزكريا وعيسى وأمه مريم" (names Maryam, omits Yahya). Reason for splitting nasab though Adam gathers all: "لأن فيهم من ليس من ولد من كان مع نوح ... وهو إدريس، وإدريس جدّ نوح". Umar athar (same chain, isnad given: ابن بشار > عبد الرحمن > سفيان > الأعمش > إبراهيم).
- **90 al-Qurtubi** — NAMED. 4 masa'il. (1) Adam's seed = Idris alone, carried-with-Nuh = Ibrahim alone, Ibrahim's = Isma'il/Ishaq/Ya'qub, Israel's = Musa/Harun/Zakariyya/Yahya/Isa; "فكان لإدريس ونوح شرف القرب من آدم، ولإبراهيم شرف القرب من نوح". (2) effect on hearts: al-Hasan "في الصلاة"; al-Asamm = "الكتب المتضمنة لتوحيده وحججه"; Ibn Abbas = "القرآن خاصة". (3) Abu Bakr ar-Razi argued obligation of sajda on listener+reciter; al-Kiya: "هذا بعيد ... وليس فيه دلالة على وجوب ذلك عند آية مخصوصة". (4) du'a for this sajda: "اللهم اجعلني من عبادك المنعم عليهم، المهديين الساجدين لك، الباكين عند تلاوة آياتك".
- **91 as-Sa'di** — NAMED. anama = "نعمة لا تلحق، ومنة لا تسبق"; Fatiha link ("صراط الذين أنعمت عليهم"). Verses carried "الإخبار بالغيوب ... واليوم الآخر، والوعد والوعيد"; kharru = "خضعوا ... وأثرت في قلوبهم ... البكاء والإنابة، والسجود"; contrast "لم يكونوا من الذين ... خروا عليها صما وعميانا". ar-Rahman: "آياته من رحمته بعباده ... هداهم بها إلى الحق، وبصرهم من العمى، وأنقذهم من الضلالة".
- **94 al-Baghawi** — NAMED. Adam's seed = "إدريس ونوحا"; Ibrahim among those carried "لأنه ولد من سام بن نوح"; Ibrahim's = Isma'il/Ishaq/Ya'qub; Israel's = Musa/Harun/Zakariyya/Yahya/Isa. sujjadan = pl. of sajid, bukiyyan = pl. of baki.
- **16 al-Muyassar** — NAMED. Phrases third/fourth line as "من ذرية إبراهيم، ومن ذرية يعقوب" (Israel=Ya'qub); kharru "خضوعًا واستكانة، وبكوا من خشيته".
- **168 Ma'arif (en)** — NAMED. Same mapping (Idris; Ibrahim; Isma'il/Ishaq/Ya'qub; Musa/Harun/Zakariyya/Yahya/Isa). Bayan ul-Qur'an purpose: guards against exalting prophets like Jews with Uzair / Christians with Isa. Weeping was prophets' practice; Qurtubi's du'a quoted.
- **169 Ibn Kathir (en, abridged)** — cross-check for 14; Bukhari/Mujahid–Ibn Abbas report on Sad-sajda + "follow their guidance" (referring to Dawud).

## Hadith
- **Sahih al-Bukhari (Umar athar is NOT this)** — the verse-attached narration used is the **athar of Umar ibn al-Khattab** (mawquf), reported by Ibn Jarir & Ibn Abi Hatim via al-A'mash > Ibrahim an-Nakha'i. Presented explicitly as Umar's saying, not a Prophetic hadith. No grading upgraded.
- **Sahih al-Bukhari, Mujahid–Ibn Abbas** (Sad-sajda / 6:90) — cited as reported by Ibn Kathir from Sahih al-Bukhari; sound; used for the sajda/"follow their guidance" theme, attributed to Ibn Kathir.
- **Sahih al-Bukhari, Hadith 660** (fetched: quranx.com/hadith/Bukhari/DarusSalam/Hadith-660; first words "Narrated Abu Huraira: The Prophet said, Allah will give shade to seven..."; the tears clause "a person who remembers Allah in seclusion and his eyes are then flooded with tears"). SOUND (Sahih al-Bukhari) but **NOT tafsir-attached to 19:58** — used only as a marked general narration echoing bukiyyan; number omitted in prose (collection named), category count "seven" not stated to keep num-parity clean.

## Asbab an-nuzul
- None. Not an asbab verse; it is the summation/sajda close of the prophet-sequence. Not used.

## Dropped / not asserted
- The Idris-in-Bani-Israil view is kept as a live disagreement (not resolved in the article's voice); Ibn Kathir's own lean (Adam's line) is reported as his, not asserted as fact.
- at-Tabari's inclusion of Maryam (and omission of Yahya) in the last group noted but not turned into a claim about who is "really" in the group.
- Did NOT preempt 19:59-60 (successors who neglected prayer) — out of scope per brief.
- No claim that any prophet erred.
