Tadabbur 19:58 — recited to, they fell prostrate and weeping

Sajdah verse: the summation that gathers the surah's prophets and names
the mark of the favoured — not their stations but their response to
revelation.

Sources: the four-group mapping (Adam's seed = Idris; carried with Nuh =
Ibrahim; Ibrahim's seed = Isma'il, Ishaq, Ya'qub; Israel's seed = Musa,
Harun, Zakariyya, Yahya, Isa) via as-Suddi & Ibn Jarir in Ibn Kathir and
at-Tabari; al-Baghawi and al-Qurtubi add Nuh to Adam's line and anchor
Ibrahim through Sam b. Nuh; al-Muyassar reads Israel as Ya'qub.

Disagreements kept: Idris's branch — Nuh's ancestral line (Ibn Kathir's
lean, "al-azhar") vs. a prophet of Bani Israil (from the Isra' greeting);
left open. On the ruling: consensus that the sajda is legislated (Ibn
Kathir) vs. Abu Bakr ar-Razi's claim of obligation, rejected by al-Kiya.

Heart (as-Sa'di, Ibn Kathir): the verses of ar-Rahman carried news of the
unseen, the Last Day, promise and threat; the prophets fell in humility
and tears, not deaf and blind. ar-Rahman's name marks the verses as mercy.

Hadith: no marfu' report is attached to the wording; the verse-attached
narration is Umar's athar (Surah Maryam, "this is the prostration — where
is the weeping?", Ibn Jarir & Ibn Abi Hatim), marked mawquf. Ibn Kathir's
Bukhari/Ibn Abbas report (Sad-sajda, follow their guidance) used for the
sajda theme. Bukhari 660's tears-clause cited only as a general narration,
marked not tafsir-attached.

Dropped: nothing asserted from memory; no preempting of 19:59-60; no claim
of prophetic error. Qurtubi's sajda-du'a included from the fetched text.
