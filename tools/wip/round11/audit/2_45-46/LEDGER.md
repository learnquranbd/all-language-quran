## Who is addressed (watch-point)
- at-Tabari: the rabbis (ahbar) of the People of the Book, in his own summary of the verse.
- Ibn Kathir: in the context of the warning to the Children of Israel, but general, for them and others; he opens with "His servants".
- al-Muyassar and as-Sa'di: "in all your affairs", no named addressee.
- Licensing sentence included in both languages (S1 P3), since the article reports at-Tabari's description of the rabbis.

## Disagreements kept (named, no position taken)
- sabr: restraint for obedience / from sin (at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi's lexical root) vs fasting (Mujahid via al-Baghawi, al-Qurtubi, Ibn Kathir; unnamed in at-Tabari); Umar's two kinds; as-Sa'di's three; al-Baghawi's "and = upon" reading.
- salah: prescribed prayer vs supplication (al-Qurtubi's "a group").
- innaha: prayer (at-Tabari, Mujahid) vs the counsel (Ibn Kathir's possibility) vs al-Baghawi's list (each, ellipsis, al-Mu'arrij, patience inside prayer, al-Husayn b. al-Fadl) vs al-Qurtubi's further views (answering Muhammad, the Ka'bah). At-Tabari's rejection of "answering Muhammad" reported as his.
- zann: certainty (majority per al-Qurtubi; at-Tabari; early reports) vs the dissent with "sins" implied (al-Qurtubi; called forced by Ibn Atiyya).
- liqa': seeing (al-Baghawi) / coming to Him (al-Baghawi "qila") / meeting His recompense (al-Qurtubi).
- raji'un: Resurrection (Abu al-Aliya, at-Tabari's preference with 2:28) vs by death (others); Ibn Kathir's "affairs return to His will".
- Khushu' and validity of prayer: reported only as Ma'arif reports al-Ghazali; no position.
