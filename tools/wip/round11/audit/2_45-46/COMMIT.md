Replaces the 755-word, 5-section article with a 1,701-word, 8-section
bilingual one for 2:45-46; the shipped card is kept.

Sources: at-Tabari, Ibn Kathir (ar + en abridged), al-Qurtubi, as-Sa'di,
al-Baghawi, al-Muyassar, Ma'arif, all on 2:45 and 2:46. Slots checked.
- Addressees: at-Tabari's rabbis of the People of the Book (covenant,
  leadership, the world) beside Ibn Kathir's "general, for them and
  others"; licensing line in both languages. 2:40 and 2:153 one line each.
- sabr: habs root (al-Qurtubi, at-Tabari), Mujahid's fasting via three
  mufassirs, Umar's two kinds, as-Sa'di's three, al-Baghawi's "and = upon".
- salah: at-Tabari's recitation answer and 20:130, Ibn Kathir with 29:45,
  al-Qurtubi's supplication reading with 8:45.
- innaha: prayer (at-Tabari, Mujahid) vs the counsel (Ibn Kathir) vs
  al-Baghawi's and al-Qurtubi's lists; at-Tabari's rejection kept as his.
- khashi'in glosses, as-Sa'di's definition, al-Qurtubi on counterfeit
  khushu', Ma'arif reporting al-Ghazali on validity.
- zann as certainty (majority, at-Tabari, early reports) with al-Qurtubi's
  dissent; liqa' and the return, at-Tabari's preference via 2:28.
Hadith: Abu Dawud 1319 (Hudhayfa) quoted whole, no grading of his own,
marked as not naming the verse; Ibn Kathir's chain note. Muslim 2968
paraphrased as an excerpt of a longer hadith. Ibn Abbas/Qutham via
at-Tabari and al-Qurtubi. Asbab: none.
Dropped from the old article: the 1:5 root link, the Bilal "give us rest"
citation (not confirmed), own-voice images and advice.
