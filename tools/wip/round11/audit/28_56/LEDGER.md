## Sensitive handling (Abu Talib)
His end is reported only in the hadith's words (s2) and in Ibn Kathir's attributed words (s4). Neither as-Sa'di nor Ma'arif gives a verdict: as-Sa'di frames it as the Prophet's kindness to his uncle, and Ma'arif/Ruh al-Ma'ani advise restraint. The article notes their reticence and adds no verdict of its own. The licensing sentence is in s8.4 in both languages.
