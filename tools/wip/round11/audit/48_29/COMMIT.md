Card and 9-section bilingual article for 48:29: Muhammadun rasulu llah
wa-lladhina ma'ahu ashidda'u 'ala l-kuffari ruhama'u baynahum. Closes
al-Fath; 48:1, 48:10 and 48:18 are not retold.

Sources: Tabari, Ibn Kathir (ar; en cross-check), Muyassar, Qurtubi,
Sa'di, Baghawi, Ma'arif.
- Who "those with him" are: Companions (Qurtubi's preference, Tabari),
  Emigrants and Helpers (Sa'di), all believers (Qurtubi's "qila"),
  Hudaybiyah first, all Companions by generality (Ma'arif).
- Licensing paragraph in both languages: conduct toward those fighting
  them, in its setting; no licence against anyone living; Ma'arif's
  60:8 reading of the firmness.
- Sima kept as a disagreement: the Day (Ibn Abbas/'Awfi, 'Atiyya,
  Muqatil, al-Hasan), bearing and khushu' (Ibn Abbas/Ibn Abi Talha,
  Mujahid with the Mansur exchange), night-prayer pallor (Shimr,
  al-Hasan), dust and ablution (Sa'id ibn Jubayr, 'Ikrima, Malik);
  Tabari's both-times view reported as his.
- Torah/Gospel break: two likenesses (Dahhak, Qatada, Ibn Abbas via
  Qurtubi, Sa'di, Tabari's waw argument) vs one (Mujahid); al-Farra'
  both ways; Ma'arif's three possibilities.
- Malik's saying reported as given by Qurtubi, Baghawi, Ibn Kathir,
  Muyassar, and Ma'arif's narrower reading; applied to no one.
- Minhum: bayan al-jins (IK, Ma'arif), generic or emphatic (Qurtubi),
  the later shoots (Tabari, via Baghawi); partitive only as Ma'arif
  records and rejects it.
Hadith: Muslim 2586a (believers as one body; IK attaches) and Bukhari
3673 (do not revile my Companions; Qurtubi attaches), both quoted whole.
The Ibn Majah "prays much at night" line not used as hadith: IK says
mawquf, Ibn al-'Arabi via Qurtubi says it was inserted by mistake.
Asbab: none; Hudaybiyah only for Ma'arif's treaty-name point.
Dropped: sect names in IK and Ma'arif, Baghawi's Companion-merit
hadiths and clause-to-caliph mapping, Ma'arif's Bible quotations.
Sect names for the Malik ruling and Ma'arif's partitive 'min' holders left unnamed in the article; every ruling attributed to its scholar. Muslim 2586a translated from the Arabic.
