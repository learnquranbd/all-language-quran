Add Tadabbur 26:97 — the deviators' confession in the Fire, sworn too late.

Card plus deep bilingual article (8 sections, 1662 EN words), on
"تالله إن كنا لفي ضلال مبين" — "By Allah, we were in manifest error."

Distinct lesson: truth confessed too late buys nothing. 26:95 (hosts of
Iblis / shared ruin) is already live; this sequel builds a separate point
and does not restate its loyalty lesson.

Sources confirmed before citing:
- at-Tabari: ḍalāl = a going-away from the truth; mubīn = the error makes
  its own departure clear to whoever contemplates and ponders it. This is
  the pivot — the clarity was available all along in the world.
- al-Qurtubi: تالله = they swore by Allah; ḍalāl = loss, ruin, bewilderment.
- as-Sa'di: their misguidance became clear only then, and they conceded
  Allah's justice in punishing them — a confession that ratifies the verdict.
- al-Muyassar: confessing their mistake; plain error with no concealment.
- Ibn Kathir (en abridged): the weak blame their leaders, then themselves.
- Hadith: Sahih al-Bukhari 6538 (Anas) — the too-late ransom; what was
  asked was easier, tawḥīd. Not Bukhari 660 (over-repeated nearby).

Equating (26:98) and the mujrimūn (26:99) left as future ground; only a
single light touch on 98 to name the error as shirk.

Licenses-nothing stated plainly in both languages: the verse describes the
condemned confessing their own shirk, and licenses nothing against any
living person or community; the judgment falls on an act, not a people.

Dropped: the tāʾ-oath "surprise/wonder" grammatical nuance — unconfirmable
from the fetched tafsirs, not asserted from memory. Asbab: none established;
verse placement given instead.
