# SOURCES ledger — 26:97 (تالله إن كنا لفي ضلال مبين)

## Tafsir fetched (from PREP, re-read this session; not re-fetched)

- **t14 Ibn Kathir (ar)** — fetched, NOT named. Its only gloss on 26:97 runs
  straight into the 26:98 إذ clause ("we made your command obeyed as the Lord
  of the worlds is obeyed, and worshipped you alongside Him"). That is 26:98
  equating material, which the watch-point reserves as future ground, so I did
  not build on it. (The English abridged Ibn Kathir t169 is what I cite as
  "Ibn Kathir" below.)
- **t15 at-Tabari (ar)** — NAMED. Arabic clause relied on:
  "تالله لقد كنا في ذهاب عن الحقّ … يبين ذهابنا ذلك عنه عن نفسه، لمن تأمله
  وتدبره، أنه ضلال وباطل." Rests two attributions: (1) §3 ḍalāl = a
  going-away / departure from the truth; (2) §4 mubīn = the error makes its own
  departure clear, by itself, to whoever contemplates (taʾammala) and ponders
  (tadabbara) it, that it is error and falsehood. This is the article's pivot.
- **t16 al-Muyassar (ar, grouped 97-99)** — NAMED ("the Muyassar"). Clause:
  "قالوا معترفين بخطئهم، وهم يتنازعون في جهنم مع مَن أضلوهم، تالله إننا كنا في
  الدنيا في ضلال واضح لا خفاء فيه؛ إذ نسويكم برب العالمين المستحق للعبادة وحده."
  Rests: §5 muʿtarifīn bi-khaṭaʾihim = confessing their mistake; ḍalāl wāḍiḥ lā
  khafāʾa fīh = plain error with no concealment in it; §6 the equated One is
  "the Lord of the worlds, alone deserving of worship." I did NOT draw on the
  grouped 98/99 tail (equating / the mujrimūn) beyond the single light touch on
  98 the article already makes — 99 untouched.
- **t90 al-Qurtubi (ar)** — NAMED. Clause: "تالله حلفوا بالله … في خسار وتبار
  وحيرة عن الحق بينة، إذا اتخذنا مع الله آلهة فعبدناها كما يعبد." Rests: §2 تالله
  = they swore by Allah (ḥalafū billāh); §3 ḍalāl = loss (khasār), ruin (tabār)
  and bewilderment (ḥayra) away from the truth, manifest; and the cause clause
  "when we took gods alongside Allah and worshipped them as [He] is worshipped."
- **t91 as-Sa'di (ar)** — NAMED. Clause: "… تالله إن كنا لفي ضلال مبين … فتبين
  لهم حينئذ ضلالهم وأقروا بعدل الله في عقوبتهم وأنها في محلها …" Rests: §5 the
  confession is addressed to the idols they had served; their misguidance became
  clear to them only then (ḥīnaʾidhin); and they conceded Allah's justice in
  punishing them, that the penalty fell where it belonged. (Sa'di's further note
  that they equated only in worship, not in creation, is 98 ground and was left
  for that verse.)
- **t94 al-Baghawi (ar)** — fetched, NOT named. The file is the bare verse text
  with no commentary; nothing to attribute.
- **t168 Ma'arif (en, grouped 88-97)** — fetched, NOT named. Its entire text is
  commentary on 26:88-89 (the sound heart, wealth and children). It carries
  nothing specific to 26:97, so it is not cited.
- **t169 Ibn Kathir (en abridged, grouped 90-99)** — NAMED ("Ibn Kathir"). Used
  for §1 only: the weak followers say to their arrogant leaders "we were
  following you; can you avail us anything from the Fire?" then "they will
  realize that themselves are to blame" and swear 26:97. The abridged text's
  own gloss of 26:98/99 (obeyed/worshipped you; "nobody called us except the
  evildoers") was NOT used — reserved as future ground per the watch-point.

## Hadith (one, quoted whole)

- **Sahih al-Bukhari 6538** — used in §7. URL fetched:
  https://quranx.com/hadith/Bukhari/DarusSalam/Hadith-6538 (saved to this audit
  dir via hadith.js). First ten words of the wording used: "A disbeliever will
  be brought on the Day of Resurrection". Narrator: Anas ibn Malik (رضي الله
  عنه). Grading: reported as being in Ṣaḥīḥ al-Bukhārī — inclusion under
  Bukhari's own conditions for a sound chain; no grading upgraded or invented.
  Fit: the too-late ransom; what was asked was easier — tawḥīd (join none in
  worship with Allah). Mirrors 26:97's error (shirk) confessed when correction
  buys nothing. Deliberately NOT Bukhari 660 (seven shaded), which the
  watch-point flags as over-repeated nearby.

## Asbab an-nuzul

- Not used. None of the fetched pages carries an established sabab for 26:97;
  the verse is narrative sequence (the scene of the Fire in Sūrat ash-Shuʿarāʾ),
  not an occasion-of-revelation report. The article states its placement instead
  (§1: out of 26:91-96, the dispute).

## Dropped — wanted to say but could not confirm

- The grammatical claim that the tāʾ-oath (تالله, vs wāw/bāʾ) carries a nuance
  of wonder/surprise/regret. Flagged in the watch-point, but none of the
  fetched tafsirs comments on the particle choice, and I would not assert it
  from memory. Dropped. The article renders تالله only as what IS confirmed:
  a solemn oath, "they swore by Allah" (al-Qurtubi), treated as a sworn
  confession — its force carried by the fact of the oath, not by an
  unconfirmed grammatical gloss.
- Sa'di's point that the equating was "in worship, not in creation," and his
  reading of the four faculties (ʿibāda, maḥabba, khawf, rajāʾ): touched only
  lightly (§8 names devotion/fear/hope as a reflection prompt, not as an
  attributed 26:98 exposition), since the equating belongs to 26:98.
