Card and eight-section bilingual article for 15:4 (1,664 EN words, 19
paragraphs): no town destroyed but that it had a known writing.

Sources: Tabari, Baghawi, Qurtubi, Sa'di, Muyassar, Ibn Kathir ar named
on the verse; Ibn Kathir abridged (169, grouped 15:4-5) named with its
group stated; Ma'arif (168, grouped 15:3-7) named only to say its comment
is on 15:3 and is not used.

"Kitabun ma'lum": every fetched text glosses it as ajal. Tabari's "a
known period" (mudda) and Sa'di/Muyassar's "decreed for its destruction"
kept as a difference of emphasis, not a dispute; Tabari joins them.
Qurtubi places the writing in al-Lawh al-Mahfuz; Baghawi: struck in
advance, neither earlier nor later. Qarya read as ahl qarya (Tabari,
Baghawi); Sa'di's "deserving of the punishment". Ibn Kathir's two
conditions, proof established and term ended, set beside 17:15 and 28:59.
Tabari's application to Makkah and 169's warning to Makkah reported;
no fetched text carries it further. 15:5 gets a single pointer.

Hadith: none attached to 15:4 in any fetched tafsir; said once.

The article states in both languages that the verse describes Allah's
decree over past towns and licenses nothing against any living person
or community, and names no modern event. Reflection turns on the
reader's own unread term and what respite is for.

Dropped: grammar note on the waw (no fetched text discusses it);
Ma'arif's 15:3 narrations; the 15:5 clause beyond a pointer. Avoided the
cross-refs and ajal debate already shipped at 10:49 and 13:38.
