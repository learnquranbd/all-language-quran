## Characterisations in the article's own voice (checked against the texts above)
- "Every commentator fetched glosses kitab as ajal": Tabari, Baghawi, Qurtubi (ajal); Sa'di, Muyassar (muqaddar / ajal muqaddar li-ihlakiha); Ibn Kathir (ajal). True of all seven Arabic/EN texts on 15:4.
- Tabari "mudda" (length) vs Sa'di/Muyassar "li-ihlakiha" (end): presented as a difference of emphasis, explicitly NOT a dispute.
- "In every text fetched, the application stops at the Makkah of the revelation": Tabari and 169 apply to Makkah; Muyassar frames as reply to the Makkans' demand; others make no application. None extends further.
- 15:1 / 15:4 "kitab" echo: stated as an observation on the text, explicitly flagged as not drawn out by any commentator fetched.
- The ma...illa (negation + exception) structure: plain description of the sentence, no attribution.

## Safety
§6 states in both languages that the verse describes Allah's decree over towns already destroyed as the text and commentators describe it, licenses nothing against any living person or community, and gives no right to call any earthquake, flood, war or ruined city a punishment. No modern event, war or city is named. The reflection (card and §8) turns on the reader's own term.
