Replaces the 891-word, 6-section article with a 1,769-word, 8-section
one, from at-Tabari, Ibn Kathir, al-Qurtubi, as-Sa'di, al-Baghawi,
al-Muyassar and Ma'arif on 2:216. All slots checked. The shipped card is kept.

Kutiba as furida, as in 2:183 (at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi); kurh named before
any argument (al-Muyassar, Ibn Kathir). Setting: Makkan restraint, then
post-hijrah Madinah and 22:39 (as-Sa'di, al-Qurtubi); one line to 2:217.
Who was bound, kept as a disagreement: Companions only ('Ata; al-Awza'i
in al-Qurtubi; ath-Thawri in al-Baghawi), communal (at-Tabari's
preference, al-Baghawi's majority, Ma'arif with 9:122), individual
(Sa'id ibn al-Musayyib via al-Mawardi), az-Zuhri's middle (Ibn Kathir,
al-Baghawi). No position; no ruling for today; licenses nothing against
any living person or community, in both languages.
Kurh/karh (at-Tabari, Mu'adh ibn Muslim); aversion of nature, not of the
command (al-Qurtubi, al-Baghawi); 'Ikrimah's disliked-then-loved;
abrogation by 2:285 reported with at-Tabari's rejection.
'Asa: al-Asamm, "binding except 66:5", Abu 'Ubaydah (al-Qurtubi);
as-Sa'di's deeds-vs-worldly-circumstances line. General principle:
Ibn Kathir, al-Hasan and ad-Darir's lines, al-Qurtubi's surgery
likeness, Ma'arif; as-Sa'di's walk with His decrees.
Hadith: Muslim 1742 (do not wish to meet the enemy, ask for well-being),
marked not attached to the verse; Bukhari 3004 (parents) as Ma'arif's
evidence. No asbab in any fetched tafsir.
Dropped: at-Tabari's Ibn 'Abbas "be content with the decree" report
(editor: chain obscure, text munkar); Muslim 1910 and Ibn Kathir's
"Sahih" narrations; Umm Salamah and istikhara (no tafsir cites them).
Corrected: "'asa only marks possibility" (al-Qurtubi: from Allah binding).
