## Disagreements kept (no position taken)
- Who was bound: (a) the Companions only / then, 'Ata (at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi with al-Awza'i, al-Baghawi with ath-Thawri, "voluntary"); (b) communal duty, at-Tabari's stated preference + "generality of scholars", al-Baghawi "majority", Ma'arif; (c) individual and permanent, Sa'id ibn al-Musayyib via al-Mawardi (al-Qurtubi); (d) az-Zuhri's on-everyone-but-stay-unless-needed (Ibn Kathir; al-Baghawi with al-Awza'i). Noted al-Awza'i appears on different sides in different books.
- 'asa: al-Asamm (= qad) vs "binding from Allah except 66:5" vs Abu 'Ubaydah (ijab), all via al-Qurtubi; as-Sa'di's deeds-vs-worldly-circumstances line.
- Abrogation by 2:285 (Ibn 'Abbas via 'Ikrimah) reported with at-Tabari's rejection.
- Article-voice sentence (§3.4, EN and BN): sets positions side by side, takes none, gives no ruling for today, licenses nothing against any living person or community.
