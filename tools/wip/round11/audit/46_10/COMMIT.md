Card and 8-section bilingual article for 46:10: qul ara'aytum in kana
min 'indi Allahi wa kafartum bihi wa shahida shahidun min bani Isra'il
'ala mithlihi fa-amana wa-stakbartum. Placed after 46:7-9 (magic,
invented, nothing new among the messengers); 46:11-12 not developed.

Sources: Tabari, Qurtubi, Sa'di, Ibn Kathir (ar; en 46:10 lines only),
Baghawi, Muyassar, Ma'arif.
- Ara'aytum: Baghawi's "tell me", Qurtubi's word for asking.
- Omitted answer: Baghawi a-laysa qad zalamtum and al-Hasan's fa-man
  adallu minkum; Qurtubi's az-Zajjaj, a-fa-ta'manun, an-Naqqash's
  reordering; Muyassar and Sa'di's "greatest wrong" question.
- 'Ala mithlihi: Torah (both camps in Tabari, Masruq's pair), earlier
  scriptures (Ibn Kathir), mithl as connective (al-Jurjani, Baghawi).
- Witness kept as a disagreement: Ibn Salam (Tabari's chains, Ibn
  Kathir's and Qurtubi's lists) vs Musa / believers of Bani Isra'il
  (Masruq via ash-Sha'bi: Makkan surah, Madinan convert). Tabari:
  Masruq closer to the wording, yet glosses with Ibn Salam on the
  Companions' reports; Ibn Kathir says Ibn Jarir chose Masruq's view,
  reported side by side. Ibn Kathir's generic witness; Qurtubi's
  Madinan verse in a Makkan surah; Ma'arif. No position taken.
Hadith: Bukhari 3812 (Sa'd) quoted whole from the Arabic, with the
narrator's doubt (Malik or the hadith) the quranx English omits.
Tirmidhi 3256 hasan gharib / 3803 gharib, mentioned with his grades.
Asbab: only as the tafsirs carry it; Tabari's narratives ungraded.
Licensing sentence in both languages (no verdict on any present-day
people, religion or state).
Dropped: Anas's three questions (shipped at 2:97-98), Ma'arif's
"prophecy" line and Jews/Christians framing, Ibn Kathir 46:11-14.
- Gate: one number-parity wording fixed; Qushayri clause reattributed.
