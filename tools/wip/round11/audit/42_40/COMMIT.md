Replaces the 748-word, 5-section article with a 1,717-word, 9-section depth article.

Sources: at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi, al-Baghawi, Ibn Kathir (ar and en), al-Muyassar,
as-Sa'di, Ma'arif al-Qur'an, all on 42:40 (the two English files grouped with 42:39-44).
- Placement: al-Qurtubi's two kinds of believer, the forgivers of 42:37 and the
  self-defenders of 42:39, with this verse setting the limit; Ma'arif on yantasirun.
- Why the recompense is called sayyi'a: al-Baghawi (resemblance in form), al-Qurtubi
  (it stands opposite and grieves likewise), at-Tabari (the deed of su'; 6:160).
- The cap of likeness: al-Muyassar "without addition", as-Sa'di's rank of justice,
  Ibn Kathir with 2:194, 16:126, 5:45; Ma'arif's no-sin condition.
- Kept as disagreements: wounds only (Muqatil, Hisham ibn Hujayr) vs words too
  (Mujahid, as-Suddi); Ibn Zayd's idolaters-and-abrogated reading vs at-Tabari's plain
  sense; az-zalimin as the one who begins (Ibn Kathir, Ibn 'Abbas, Sa'id ibn Jubayr) vs
  the one who exceeds in retaliation (Ibn 'Isa), with as-Sa'di holding both.
- Pardon and repair: at-Tabari, Ibn 'Abbas, al-Muyassar; as-Sa'di's islah condition;
  an-Nakha'i via Ma'arif. Reward: as-Sa'di's "recompense of the kind of the deed".
- Hadith: Muslim 2588 (charity does not decrease wealth...), attached by Ibn Kathir,
  quoted whole. Al-Hasan's and 'Ali ibn al-Husayn's Day-of-Judgment reports given as
  their own words, ungraded. Licensing sentence on az-zalimin in both languages.
- 42:41-43 closed with Ibn Kathir's glosses, Sa'id ibn Jubayr, and Thanawi via Ma'arif.
- Dropped from the old article: Ahsanul Bayaan attribution, the 40:40 parallel, the
  'afa etymology and 'ala/'inda contrast (unsourced). Fixed: the old article gave only
  the "who begins" reading of az-zalimin.
