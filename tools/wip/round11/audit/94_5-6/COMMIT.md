Replaces the 799-word, 6-section article with a 1,747-word, 8-section deep article, built only on tafsir fetched for 94:5-6 (shipped card kept).

Sources: at-Tabari, Ibn Kathir (ar; en abridged for 94:1-4 and 94:7-8),
al-Qurtubi, as-Sa'di, al-Baghawi (commentary filed under 94:6), al-Muyassar,
Ma'arif al-Qur'an.

Frame: 94:1-4 via Ibn Kathir (6:125, Mujahid and Qatadah on 94:4); the fa
as link (al-Muyassar; al-Jurjani via al-Baghawi and al-Qurtubi). ma'a read
as accompaniment (as-Sa'di, 65:7 as his parallel) beside Mujahid's "ease
follows hardship" and Ma'arif's "in the wake of".
Disagreements kept, no position: the repetition as emphasis (al-Farra' via
al-Qurtubi, Ibn Kathir, al-Baghawi, 102:3-4) vs one hardship, two eases
(Tha'lab, "the commentators" via al-Baghawi, as-Sa'di, Ma'arif); al-Jurjani's
horseman-and-sword objection and his worldly/Hereafter eases; al-Qurtubi's
expulsion/conquest pairing; al- of generality (as-Sa'di) vs prior knowledge
(Ma'arif). "Lan yaghliba 'usrun yusrayn" reported as carried: al-Hasan mursal
(Ibn Kathir's word), Qatadah "mentioned to us", Ibn 'Abbas (no chain),
'Umar's letter with 3:200, Ibn Mas'ud's hole (unnamed link, at-Tabari).
Asbab: none; Ibn Kathir's own weakening of the Anas report stated.
Hadith: as-Sa'di's "relief with distress" not quoted; Tirmidhi 2516 checked
and lacks the clause, Ahmad unreachable. 94:7-8 via Ibn Kathir and Mujahid.
Dropped from the old article (unsourced): ma'a as settled, 3:159 tawakkul
link, the boycott, Muslim's "affair of the believer", the supplication and
advice list; old "commentators treat the repeat as emphasis" was one view
of two, and the saying was not chainless, now reported as the tafsirs give it.
94:2 wizr gloss (48:2) left out.
