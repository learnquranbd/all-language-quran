Card and 8-section bilingual article for 43:3: the oath of 43:1-2 and
its answer, mubin, inna ja'alnahu, qur'anan 'arabiyyan and la'allakum
ta'qilun. 41:3 not repeated; 43:4-8 as context only.

Sources: Tabari, Ibn Kathir (ar; en abridged for 43:4-5), Muyassar,
Qurtubi, Sa'di, Baghawi, Ma'arif; 43:2 fetched for five of them to
attribute the oath link.
- Oath: Tabari, Ma'arif (oath as argument, Qur'an its own proof), Sa'di
  (by the Qur'an upon the Qur'an; 43:3 the muqsam 'alayh), Qurtubi
  (second oath, answer inna ja'alnahu; qila: al-kitab = all books).
- Mubin: IK, Qurtubi, Baghawi, Sa'di (left unrestricted), Tabari,
  Suddi, Qatada; Ma'arif's counsel vs ijtihad line with 54:17.
- Ja'alnahu: anzalnahu (Tabari, IK, Muyassar, Suddi), sammaynahu wa
  wasafnahu (Qurtubi, 5:103), qulnahu (Mujahid), bayyannahu (Zajjaj,
  Thawri), Baghawi's list with 43:19, 15:91, 9:19. Unranked. No
  createdness debate: no fetched text raises it.
- Why Arabic: Tabari (first hearers Arabs), Thawri (each prophet's
  people's tongue), Muqatil (tongue of heaven, reported, not adopted),
  IK and Sa'di on eloquence marked as their judgements.
- Ta'qilun: Sa'di, IK (26:195), Muyassar, Tabari, Qurtubi.
Disagreements kept: oath's answer (inna ja'alnahu vs Ha-Mim, Ibn
al-Anbari's pause); addressees (Ibn 'Isa: Arabs only vs Ibn Zayd:
reflect, general).
Hadith: none attached by any tafsir; said in one sentence. No asbab.
Licensing sentence for 43:6-8 in both languages.
Dropped: Mi'raj-revelation view, 43:4 detail (Lawh, 56:77-80, 80:11-16).
