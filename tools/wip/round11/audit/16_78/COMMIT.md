Replaces the 683-word, 6-section article with a 1,709-word, 8-section one,
from at-Tabari, Ibn Kathir (ar and en), al-Qurtubi, al-Baghawi, as-Sa'di,
the Muyassar and Ma'arif on 16:78. Slots checked, none shifted. Shipped
card kept.
Placement: 16:73-77 summarised, Ibn Kathir (en) on 16:77; 16:79 one line.
La ta'lamuna shay'an: al-Qurtubi's three sayings (covenant, decree, own
benefit); Muyassar, as-Sa'di, at-Tabari's "Allah taught you"; Ma'arif on
crying and suckling, kept as its own account. Qira'at of ummahatikum
(al-Qurtubi, al-Baghawi).
The stop at shay'an, faculties made before birth (at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi,
al-Baghawi), beside Ibn Kathir's and as-Sa'di's gradual growth; no side.
Each faculty's use: Ibn Kathir, at-Tabari's social reading, al-Qurtubi's
command/traces/knowledge of Him, as-Sa'di's three doors.
Order: only Ma'arif gives a reason (hearing first, "perhaps"); speech
folded into hearing (al-Qurtubi's qila, Ma'arif). Nothing asserted about
infants in the article's voice.
Al-af'ida: hearts (at-Tabari, Muyassar, Ma'arif) and reason (Ibn Kathir),
seat in the heart or, qila, the brain.
La'allakum tashkurun: al-Qurtubi's two readings, al-Baghawi, as-Sa'di's
purpose and warning, at-Tabari's thanks not shared with rivals, the
Muyassar; 67:23-24 from Ibn Kathir.
Hadith: Bukhari 6502 (the wali hadith), attached by Ibn Kathir, quoted
whole in al-Bukhari's wording, which differs from his quotation. No asbab.
Dropped from the shipped article as unsourced: the singular/plural note,
23:78, 32:9, 46:26 and the fixed-order claim, 16:70, and its wrong claim
that the commentators disagree on the reason for the order.
