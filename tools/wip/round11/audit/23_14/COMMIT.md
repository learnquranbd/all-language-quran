Replaces the 791-word, 6-section article with a 1,763-word, 9-section
deep article on the stages from nutfa to another creation and the best of creators.

Sources: at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi, Ibn Kathir (ar + en), as-Sa'di,
al-Baghawi, al-Muyassar, Ma'arif al-Qur'an, all on 23:14 (t169 grouped 23:12-16).
- Placement: 23:12 Adam (Ibn Jarir, Qatada via Ibn Kathir), 23:13 womb;
  Ma'arif on thumma/fa marking the pace of the stages.
- Stage glosses only as the tafsirs give them (Ibn Kathir, 'Ikrima,
  Muyassar, Sa'di, Baghawi); forty-day timings attributed. No embryology.
- Qira'a 'azman/'izaman: Ibn 'Amir and Abu Bakr singular (Baghawi);
  'Abdullah's 'azman wa-'asaban (Tabari); Ibn 'Abbas "bone of the spine".
- Khalqan akhar kept as a dispute: spirit (Ibn 'Abbas, 'Ikrima, Sha'bi,
  Mujahid, Abu l-'Aliya, Dahhak, Ibn Zayd; 'Ali four months), post-birth
  unfolding (Ibn 'Abbas via al-'Awfi; Qatada hair; Dahhak teeth and hair),
  full youth (Mujahid), male or female (al-Hasan); Tabari picks spirit,
  Qurtubi general, Ibn Kathir no contradiction. Article picks none.
- Ahsan al-khaliqin: Mujahid "best of makers" vs Ibn Jurayj ('Isa, 3:49);
  Tabari prefers Mujahid with Zuhayr's line; Qurtubi, Baghawi, Ma'arif (29:17).
Hadith: Bukhari 3208 (Ibn Mas'ud, forty days) quoted whole as Ibn Kathir's
attachment; Muslim 2643 cited; Ahmad/Bukhari order difference noted.
Asbab: 'Umar's agreement (Ibn Kathir, Qurtubi; ungraded in both), the
Zayd/Mu'adh report with Ibn Kathir's rejection, Ibn Abi Sarh and 6:93
(Qurtubi), all reported as theirs. 23:15-16 via Ibn Kathir and 29:20.
Dropped from the old article: 36:78-79, 30:27, 50:15, 40:64, 37:125 (not
cited by fetched tafsir), and its claim that khalaqa is used of a creature
only for 'Isa (Ma'arif cites 29:17; Tabari and Qurtubi: any maker is khaliq).
