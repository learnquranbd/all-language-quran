Replaces the 792-word, 5-section article with a 1,778-word, 8-section deep article built only on the tafsir fetched for 66:6 (shipped card kept).

Sources: at-Tabari, Ibn Kathir (ar; en abridged, grouped 66:6-8), al-Qurtubi,
as-Sa'di, al-Muyassar, al-Baghawi, Ma'arif, all fetched for 66:6.
- Qu anfusakum wa ahlikum: Ali "teach them and discipline them" (at-Tabari,
  Ibn Kathir); at-Tabari's "teach one another"; Ibn Abbas in two wordings
  (at-Tabari/Ibn Kathir vs al-Qurtubi); Mujahid's taqwa; Qatada's command,
  forbid, help, restrain; ad-Dahhak and Muqatil's duty to teach.
- Disagreement kept: ad-Dahhak (let the families guard themselves) vs Ali,
  Qatada, Mujahid (deeds for oneself, counsel for family), with Ibn al-Arabi's
  grammatical verdict reported via al-Qurtubi.
- Children inside anfusakum (some scholars, 24:61); al-Kiya on 20:132, 26:214;
  household circle from Ma'arif and as-Sa'di.
- Stones: idols (Ibn Kathir "it is said", 21:98; as-Sa'di) vs sulphur (Ibn
  Mas'ud, Mujahid, al-Baqir, as-Suddi; at-Tabari); 2:24 via al-Qurtubi.
- Ghilaz shidad glosses (at-Tabari, Ibn Kathir, al-Qurtubi); the two final
  clauses split by al-Qurtubi; as-Sa'di's praise of the angels.
Hadith: Bukhari 893 (Ibn Umar, all of you are guardians), quoted whole;
Abu Dawud 494 (Sabra, command a boy to pray at seven), no grading on the
page; Ibn Kathir quotes at-Tirmidhi as hasan, Tirmidhi 407 reads hasan sahih.
Licensing sentence added for the Fire's people (both languages).
Dropped: 'Umar's question (no chain), Ibn Kathir's mursal gharib report,
Ikrima's and al-Baghawi's numbers, al-Qurtubi's ungraded hadiths, 19:55.
Defect fixed: the old article called the duty "teaching, not controlling",
which no fetched text says; it also cited 19:55, which no tafsir here attaches.
