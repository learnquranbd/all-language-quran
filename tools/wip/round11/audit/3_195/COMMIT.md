Key kept at 3:195 (their Lord's answer to the du'a of 3:191-194: no worker's
deed lost, male or female; the emigrants' reward). 3:190-191 and 3:200 ship.
New card and a 1,752-word, 9-section article.

Sources: at-Tabari, Ibn Kathir (ar + en), al-Qurtubi, as-Sa'di, al-Baghawi,
al-Muyassar on 3:195; Ma'arif grouped 3:195-199 (all slots on target).
- istajaba = ajaba with the fa' of sequence and 2:186 (Ibn Kathir); al-Hasan's
  "Rabbana, Rabbana" (al-Qurtubi); du'a of worship and of asking (as-Sa'di).
- la udi'u = la uhbitu (al-Baghawi); paid in full by the deed's measure
  (Ibn Kathir); "min" emphatic vs explanatory kept as al-Qurtubi gives it.
- ba'dukum min ba'd: support, milla, din and one ruling for all (at-Tabari);
  al-Kalbi; Adam and Hawwa' ("it is said"); ad-Dahhak with 9:71; one din
  (al-Qurtubi); equal in reward (Ibn Kathir, as-Sa'di, al-Muyassar).
- qatalu/qutilu readings: al-Baghawi's attributions, at-Tabari's two accepted.
- takfir as erasing / covering in the Hereafter / covering in both worlds.
Asbab: Umm Salamah on women in the hijrah, Tirmidhi 3023 quoted whole; the
page shows no grading by at-Tirmidhi (stated), al-Hakim's "sahih on
al-Bukhari's condition" quoted via Ibn Kathir; at-Tabari's three chains.
Hadith: Muslim 1885 (Abu Qatadah, all sins wiped except debt), quoted whole,
with Ma'arif's point that rights of people are not covered.
Licensing: in both languages for the fighting and for 3:196-197.
Dropped: Ja'far as-Sadiq's five-Rabbana saying, the "last verse revealed"
report, at-Tabari 8370, the editor's chain notes, Shaddad ibn Aws.
Flag: data/nuzul 3:195 entry says Tirmidhi 3023 is "hasan sahih"; unconfirmed.
