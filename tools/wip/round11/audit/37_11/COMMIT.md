Card + 8-section bilingual article (1,658 EN words) for 37:11, "ask them:
are they harder to create, or those We have created? We created them from
clinging clay."

Sources: at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi, al-Baghawi, Ibn Kathir, as-Sa'di,
al-Muyassar, Ma'arif al-Qur'an, each quoted from the text fetched for 37:11.
- Placement after 37:5-10 (Sa'di); addressees the Meccans (Qurtubi,
  Baghawi), deniers of resurrection (Tabari); question of taqrir (Baghawi).
- "Those We created" as a named range: Mujahid heavens/earth/mountains
  (Tabari; Qurtubi adds seas), Tabari's own angels/devils/heavens/earth,
  Ibn Kathir, ad-Dahhak tying it to 37:5, Sa'id b. Jubayr angels.
  Cross-refs only: 40:57 (Qatada; Baghawi), 79:27 (Baghawi; Qurtubi).
- Minority "former nations" (Baghawi qila; Qurtubi), with the licensing
  guard EN+BN.
- Qira'a: Ibn Mas'ud / ad-Dahhak "man 'adadna" (Tabari, Ibn Kathir), one
  paragraph, no theological weight.
- Lazib: Tabari (lasiq; ba/mim; Qays latib), Baghawi majority, Ibn Kathir's
  glosses, Muyassar. Kept unresolved: muntin (Mujahid, ad-Dahhak per Baghawi
  and Qurtubi) vs Ibn Kathir's report of the same names; khalis (as-Suddi,
  al-Kalbi); Sa'di qawi shadid with 15:26 named only.
- Adam or every human (Muyassar vs Ma'arif's food chain).
- Sa'di's two-step a fortiori argument; Ibn Kathir; Ma'arif.
- Hadith: none; no fetched tafsir attaches one (said once). Bukhari 660 not used.
- Asbab: Qurtubi's Abu al-Ashadd report not used (single source).
- Fixed vs brief: the "as-Suddi = heavens and earth" attribution is not in
  Tabari; the 37:5 link is ad-Dahhak's and is attributed to him.
- 37:12-17 left as future ground.
