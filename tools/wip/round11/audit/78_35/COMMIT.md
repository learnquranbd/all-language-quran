Card and 8-section bilingual article for 78:35, la yasma'una fiha
laghwan wa la kidhdhaba (6 words). 78:32-34 named only as the list
before it; 78:36 not touched.

Sources: Tabari, Qurtubi, Baghawi, Ibn Kathir (ar), Sa'di, Muyassar
(grouped 78:31-35), Ma'arif (grouped 78:30-35, the turn only), Ibn
Kathir en (renderings).
- laghw, two lines kept side by side: falsehood in speech (Tabari,
  Muyassar, Baghawi, Qurtubi "what is discarded and thrown aside") vs
  speech without benefit (Ibn Kathir, Sa'di). Qatada: batil and ithm.
- kidhdhab, three readings, unranked: mutual calling-liar (Tabari
  mukadhaba, Muyassar, Baghawi takdhib), hearing lies (Qurtubi, Ibn
  Kathir "ithm kadhib"), sin (Sa'di).
- Qira'at: al-Kisa'i's light kidhaban; Tabari rejects it on the readers'
  consensus (al-A'sha cited); Qurtubi and Baghawi report it without
  judgement, with their derivations; Qurtubi's 78:28 doubling view.
- Cross-refs: 56:25-26 (Sa'di; five shared words, salam exception),
  52:23 (Ibn Kathir; dar as-salam, salim min an-naqs). 19:62 dropped,
  no fetched tafsir cites it.
Hadith: Bukhari 934 (Abu Hurayra, Friday "be quiet" = laghawta), cited
by Qurtubi for the word; marked as not about Paradise and not attached
to the verse. No tafsir cites a hadith on speech in Paradise; said so.
Asbab: none. Licensing sentence in both languages (78:28 deniers).
Dropped: qira'at status claims, rasm argument, al-A'sha's line itself.
