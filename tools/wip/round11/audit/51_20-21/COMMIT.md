Replaces the 722-word, 5-section article with an 8-section, 1,741-word
source-gated article (shipped card kept).

Sources: at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi, as-Sa'di, Ibn Kathir (ar, en abridged),
al-Baghawi, Muyassar, Ma'arif, all on 51:20 and 51:21 (en Ibn Kathir
grouped 51:15-23). Placement after 51:15-19 (Ibn Kathir) and the turn
to resurrection evidence (al-Qurtubi; Ma'arif with 51:8); earth signs
as Ibn Kathir's inventory, as-Sa'di's land list, al-Baghawi's "when
they travel", Ma'arif on why the earth is named; 'ibar wa 'izat
(at-Tabari, Qatadah by two chains) and al-Qurtubi's three marks;
lil-muqinin as those who benefit (al-Qurtubi, Muyassar); the implied
"signs" in 51:21 (al-Qurtubi's qila, al-Baghawi); the self in Ibn Zayd
(30:20), Qatadah, as-Suddi, al-Hasan, 'Ata from Ibn Abbas, al-Qurtubi's
small world, Ma'arif's universe in miniature with 23:14; a-fa-la
tubsirun in at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi (sight of the heart), Muyassar,
Muqatil, Ma'arif, as-Sa'di. 51:22, 51:47-49, 51:56 in one line each.

Disagreements kept: sabil al-khala' wal-bawl (Ibn az-Zubayr, Mujahid,
as-Sa'ib ibn Sharik) vs the levelled joints for worship (Ibn Zayd),
with at-Tabari's wider preference; Ma'arif (deniers addressed) vs
Tafsir Mazhari (the God-fearing described, with 3:191).

Hadith: none attached by any fetched tafsir; the article says so.

Dropped from the old article: the soul/provision/guidance list (no
source), the self-knowledge observation, 80:24, 41:53 and 30:8
parallels (not cited), and its own speculative lines. Four humours and
Ma'arif's embryology detail not used.
