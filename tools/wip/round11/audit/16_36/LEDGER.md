## Hadith
None. No fetched tafsir attaches a hadith to 16:36. Article says so in one sentence (section 7, para 3). No hadith fetched.

## Asbab an-nuzul
Not used. None reported in the fetched texts; article says none is attached.

## Own-text observations (from the verse wording, not attributed)
- The first clause names Allah as guide; the second names no agent (haqqat ... ad-dalala). Stated as a feature of the wording only, with no inference drawn beyond "the commentators fill it differently".

## Disagreements kept, no position
- Scope of taghut: at-Tabari = Satan; al-Baghawi / al-Qurtubi / Muyassar = every object of worship besides Allah (with lists).
- Framing of "haqqat 'alayhi ad-dalala": from the people's refusal (as-Sa'di, at-Tabari, Muyassar) vs by prior decree until death on disbelief (al-Qurtubi, al-Baghawi).

## Sectarian label
"Qadariyya" appears once, attributed to al-Qurtubi as his own term, stated as his.

## Dropped / not written
- Any identification of messengers with particular peoples, regions or figures in other religions (beyond Ma'arif's hedged "may have been", which names nobody).
- Linguistic claims about ijtanibu / taghut root not in fetched text.
- Ibn Kathir's "خلق النار وأهلها من الشياطين والكفرة" clause: not needed.
- 16:37 material (eagerness of the Prophet ﷺ) and further divine-will discussion: pointed to 11:118 and 10:99 instead.
- Travel-and-see expansion: pointed to 12:109.
