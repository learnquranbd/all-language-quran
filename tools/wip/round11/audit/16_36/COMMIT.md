Card and bilingual article (8 sections, 1,739 EN words) for "We sent to
every nation a messenger: worship Allah and avoid the taghut", read as the
answer to the "had Allah willed" excuse of 16:35.

Sources: Ibn Kathir (every generation and group; Nuh first to Muhammad ﷺ;
21:25, 43:45; legislative vs universal will, no argument for them; 47:10,
67:18); at-Tabari (as We sent among you; taghut = Satan; guided vs those
who swerved; addressed to Quraysh, traces of the destroyed); al-Qurtubi
(taghut = every object of worship: Satan, soothsayer, idol, caller to
misguidance; travel mu'tabirin); al-Baghawi (every object of worship;
prior decree); as-Sa'di (proof over all nations, one call; split by
response; bodies and hearts); the Muyassar (devils, idols, the dead, any
wali; al-mu'anid). Ma'arif grouped 16:36-40 (India/Pakistan question,
35:24 vs 28:46, hedged, names nobody) and Ibn Kathir abridged 16:35-37;
said so, only 16:36 content used.

Disagreements kept: scope of taghut (Satan vs every object of worship);
haqqat read from the people's refusal (as-Sa'di, at-Tabari, Muyassar) vs
by prior decree (al-Qurtubi, al-Baghawi). "Qadariyya" reported as
al-Qurtubi's own term.

Hadith: none attached to 16:36 by any fetched tafsir; said so. No asbab.

Dropped: any identification of messengers with peoples or figures;
further divine-will discussion (pointers to 11:118, 10:99); travel-and-see
expansion (pointer to 12:109). Licensing sentence in both languages.
