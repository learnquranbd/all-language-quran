Card and 8-section bilingual article for 38:65: qul innama ana mundhir and
la ilaha illa Allah al-Wahid al-Qahhar, read after the quarrel of the people of
the Fire (38:64) and into 38:66-70.

Sources: Tabari, Qurtubi, Ibn Kathir (ar + en abridged), Baghawi, Sa'di,
Muyassar, Ma'arif (grouped 38:65-68).
- Addressees: Tabari (polytheists of your people, "O company of Quraysh"),
  Baghawi (polytheists of Makkah), Muyassar (your people), Ibn Kathir (deniers),
  Sa'di (when they demand what is not in your hand).
- Mundhir: mukhawwif (Qurtubi, Baghawi); Muyassar's cause; Tabari's warning
  "ahead of severe punishment" with the call to hasten repentance.
- Ilah: ma'bud (Qurtubi), rightful worship (Sa'di, Muyassar), Tabari's two
  rights and his contrast with what neither harms nor benefits.
- Al-Wahid / al-Qahhar glosses from Qurtubi, Tabari, Muyassar, Ibn Kathir.
Disagreements kept, no position: innama as rebuttal (Ibn Kathir, "not as you
claim") vs limit of the role (Sa'di); the names as proof (Sa'di, qahr
inseparable from oneness) vs description (Ibn Kathir, Muyassar) vs portrait into
38:66 (Tabari) vs weight on prophethood (Ma'arif).
Hadith: none attached by any fetched source; said in one sentence. Asbab: none.
Cross-refs: 38:4 (shipped v634) as pointer only, not repeated; 38:66, 38:70.
Framing: licensing sentence in both languages.
Dropped: unsourced root gloss for al-Qahhar, Ma'arif "tawhid among the
arguments", a Tabari/Ma'arif alignment inference, verse ref for Sa'di's phrase.
Arabic count: 38:65 eleven words. Theme: tawheed.
