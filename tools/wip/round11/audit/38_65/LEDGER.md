## Disagreement kept (emphasis, not meaning)
- As-Sa'di: al-Wahid al-Qahhar = decisive proof of divinity (qahr inseparable from oneness).
- Ibn Kathir, Muyassar: description of who He is; no proof framing in fetched text.
- At-Tabari: portrait running into 38:66 ending in contrast with what neither harms nor benefits.
- Ma'arif: verse's weight on confirming prophethood, the surah's main purpose.
- Innama: Ibn Kathir (rebuttal: "not as you claim") vs as-Sa'di (limit of the role). No position taken.
