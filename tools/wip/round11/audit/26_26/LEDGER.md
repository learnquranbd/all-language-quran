# SOURCES — 26:26 (قَالَ رَبُّكُمْ وَرَبُّ ءَابَآئِكُمُ ٱلْأَوَّلِينَ)

## Tafsir fetched (from PREP.md, not re-fetched)
All eight files under audit/26_26/ read. Named in the article:

- **Ibn Kathir (ar, t14 / en, t169)** — used, §1.2, §2.1, §8.2.
  - Gloss (t14): "ربكم ورب آبائكم الأولين) أي: خالقكم وخالق آبائكم الأولين، الذي كانوا قبل فرعون وزمانه" → "your Creator and the Creator of your forefathers, those before Pharaoh and his time." (§2.1)
  - Context (t169): Pharaoh denied the Creator outright — "he used to say... مَا عَلِمْتُ لَكُمْ مِّنْ إِلَـهٍ غَيْرِى (28:28)... They used to deny the Creator." The 26:23 question was denial, not inquiry into God's nature. (§1.2)
  - On 26:27 (t169): "when Fir`awn was defeated in debate, he resorted to the use of his force and power." → §8.2 (Pharaoh beaten, falls back on force/derision).
- **at-Tabari (t15)** — used, §2.2. "فقال لهم الذي دعوته إليه وإلى عبادته (رَبُّكُمْ) الذي خلقكم (وَرَبُّ آبَائِكُمُ الأوَّلِينَ)" → the verse as the completion of Mūsā's invitation; possessive "your Lord who created you."
- **al-Qurtubi (t90)** — used, §3.1, §3.2. "فزاد موسى في البيان... فجاء بدليل يفهمونه... لأنهم يعلمون أنه قد كان لهم آباء وأنهم قد فنوا وأنه لا بد لهم من مغير، وأنهم قد كانوا بعد أن لم يكونوا، وأنهم لا بد لهم من مكوّن." → Mūsā "increased the exposition," argument from origination (ḥudūth): they had fathers, those perished, so there must be a Changer/Maker; came to be after non-being → a Maker. Also notes Pharaoh replied "على جهة الاستخفاف" (mockery) — carried into §8.
- **al-Muyassar (t16)** — used, §4.1. "الرب الذي أدعوكم إليه هو الذي خلقكم وخلق آباءكم الأولين، فكيف تعبدون مَن هو مخلوق مثلكم، وله آباء قد فنوا كآبائكم؟" → the rhetorical turn: how worship one created like you, whose fathers perished like yours?
- **as-Sa'di (t91)** — used, §5.1. "رَبُّكُمْ وَرَبُّ آبَائِكُمُ الأَوَّلِينَ تعجبتم أم لا، استكبرتم، أم أذعنتم." → the truth stands whether you marvel or not, are arrogant or submit.
- **Ma'arif al-Qur'an (en, t168)** — used, §6.1. On this exchange: prophetic dialectic (munazarah) is not the win-at-all-costs game; Mūsā admitted his fault in the Egyptian's death (26:20) and turned Pharaoh's favour-boast into a charge about the enslaved (26:22), arguing for truth not standing. (Note: t168 covers 26:17–26 grouped; the munazarah discussion is on the whole exchange, applied here to the escalation, not claimed as a comment on 26:26's words alone.)
- **al-Baghawi (t94)** — fetched, NOT named: file is only the verse text (31 chars), no reading to attribute.

## Hadith
- **Sahih al-Bukhari, Hadith 1385** (fitrah). URL: https://quranx.com/hadith/Bukhari/DarusSalam/Hadith-1385 (fetched via hadith.js, saved). First ten words: "Every child is born with a true faith of Islam". Narrator: Abu Huraira (رضي الله عنه). Grading: in Sahih al-Bukhari (ṣaḥīḥ; not upgraded — it is Bukhari's own collection).
  - **Explicitly marked in §7.1 as NOT attached to 26:26 by any tafsir fetched** — offered as a thematic parallel (fitrah / the forefathers who turn a soul from its innate Lord). This satisfies the rule "a general narration must be marked as not attached to the verse."
