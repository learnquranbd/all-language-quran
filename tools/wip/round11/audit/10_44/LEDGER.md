## Hadith
- Sahih Muslim 2577 (USED). https://quranx.com/hadith/Muslim/Hadith-2577 (saved in this folder by hadith.js). Attached to 10:44 by Ibn Kathir (ar and en), "رواه مسلم بطوله". First ten words of the wording used: "O My servants, I have forbidden oppression for Myself and". Quoted as its opening line and closing line with an ellipsis, and marked in the article as part of a long hadith; one middle line ("all of you are astray except those I have guided...") quoted as from the same hadith. Second chain (2577c/d, Qatada > Abu Qilabah > Abu Asma'): "unlawful for Me and for My servants" paraphrased. Abu Idris al-Khawlani kneeling: Sa'id's note on the same page. Grading: in Muslim's Sahih; no further grading given or added.
