Card and 9-section bilingual article on wa tasiru l-jibalu sayra,
read as the second half of a pair with 52:9 (tamuru s-sama'u mawra),
both tied to waqi' of 52:7. 52:11-12 a pointer only, with the
licensing sentence on the deniers.

Sources: at-Tabari, Ibn Kathir (ar; en abridged for 52:1-10), al-Baghawi,
al-Muyassar (52:7-10), al-Qurtubi, as-Sa'di; Ma'arif for mawr only.
Extra fetches: 52:9 (Tabari, Qurtubi, Ibn Kathir, Baghawi, Sa'di) and
18:47 (Qurtubi, Sa'di), the latter licensed by al-Qurtubi's al-Kahf
pointer.
- Sayr: to dust (Tabari, Baghawi, Ibn Kathir) vs drift like cloud
  (Muyassar, Sa'di); al-Qurtubi keeps Muqatil (levelled with the earth,
  chainless) and an unattributed cloud view with 27:88 as its bayan.
- 18:47 readings via al-Qurtubi: nusayyiru / tusayyaru (81:3) /
  tasiru (52:10 as support); his cloud-then-dust sequence with 56:5-6.
- As-Sa'di's stages (52:10, 18:47) and his question on the weak human.
- Mawr (52:9): Ibn Abbas (moving / splitting, two chains), Mujahid,
  Qatadah, ad-Dahhak, all ungraded in at-Tabari; Baghawi, Ma'arif,
  Muyassar; al-A'sha's cloud line; Ibn Zayd's "no knowledge of it".
Hadith: none attached to the verse by any fetched tafsir; stated.
Asbab: none.
Dropped: 78:20, 20:105-107, 77:10, 101:5 (not cited by a fetched
tafsir); 'Umar report (52:7-8, ungraded); Ibn Bahr's falak view; any
physics or geology.
