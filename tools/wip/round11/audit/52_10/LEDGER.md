## Disagreements kept (no position taken)
- 52:10 sayr: Muqatil (levelled with the earth) vs "wa qila" (move like clouds, 27:88), both in al-Qurtubi; Tabari/Baghawi/Ibn Kathir go straight to dust; Muyassar/Sa'di give the cloud motion. Qurtubi's 18:47 note presents cloud-then-dust as stages, reported as his framing there.
- 52:9 mawr: moving (Ibn 'Abbas via one chain, Qatadah), revolving (Mujahid), revolving + surging (ad-Dahhak, Tabari's choice per Ibn Kathir), splitting (Ibn 'Abbas via another chain); Ibn Zayd: no knowledge of it.

## Gradings / ungraded marks
- All Companion/Tabi'un reports on 52:9 are given by at-Tabari with chains he does not grade: stated as ungraded in §7.
- Muqatil on 52:10: chainless in al-Qurtubi; stated. "Wa qila" view: unattributed; stated.
