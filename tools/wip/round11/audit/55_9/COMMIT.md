Card and 8-section bilingual article on the command to keep the
weight upright with qist and not make the balance deficient, read
inside its setting: the balance set down beside the raised sky (55:7).

Sources: at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi, as-Sa'di, al-Baghawi, Ibn Kathir (ar
and en), the Muyassar and Ma'arif on 55:9; the same six fetched on 55:7
for what al-mizan means there.

Disagreements kept, none picked: al-mizan in 55:7 as justice
(Mujahid, Qatadah, as-Suddi; Tabari, Baghawi, Muyassar, Ibn Kathir with
57:25), the Qur'an (al-Husayn ibn al-Fadl), the actual scale (al-Hasan,
Qatadah, ad-Dahhak), judgement, or the Hereafter balance; aqimu as
setting the tongue of the scale upright (Abu ad-Darda, 'Ata) or as
keeping the practice up like prayer (qila); the second half as worldly
weighing or the scale of deeds; the addressees as those who weigh for
others, every son of Adam (Qatadah), or the mawali (Ibn Abbas).

Also: Ibn 'Uyaynah's hand-and-heart line, Mujahid on qist, Ibn Zayd on
takhsir, the takhsaru variant, and Abdullah's khafada reading.

Cross-refs only where a fetched tafsir cites them: 26:182, 11:84, 57:25.
No hadith attached to the verse; Ibn Abbas's words marked as a
Companion's counsel, ungraded. No asbab.

Dropped: "Sufyan" for Ibn 'Uyaynah, any claim about 55:8 commentary
(not fetched), any trade or fiqh ruling of our own.
