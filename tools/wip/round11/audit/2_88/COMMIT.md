New card and a 1,738-word, 9-section article on "our hearts are wrapped",
written from the fetched tafsir only.

Sources: at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi, Ibn Kathir, al-Baghawi, as-Sa'di, Muyassar,
Ma'arif al-Qur'an on 2:88 (all slots open on 2:88; no neighbour-slot issue).
As-Sa'di names the sentence an excuse and a lie; at-Tabari reads bal as
Allah's own denial of the claim.
Disagreements kept: ghulf (reading of the cities; covered: Ibn Abbas,
Mujahid, Qatadah, Abu al-'Aliyah, as-Suddi, Ibn Zayd) vs ghuluf (Ibn Abbas,
al-A'raj, Ibn Muhaysin per al-Qurtubi; vessels full of knowledge: 'Atiyyah,
Ibn Abbas via ad-Dahhak, al-Kalbi), with at-Tabari rejecting ghuluf as
anomalous; fa-qalilan as few believe (Qatadah), little faith (Ma'mar,
at-Tabari's choice on grammar) or none at all (al-Waqidi, unnamed in
at-Tabari).
The curse given with its cause, bi-kufrihim, as following their disbelief
(at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi's "sin repaid with a greater").
Licensing sentence on the speakers (both languages).
Hadith: no sound hadith attached to the verse, stated. Hudhayfa's
four-hearts saying via at-Tabari 1497, given as mawquf with a broken chain
per the edition's note. Bukhari 71 quoted whole as a general principle.
Sister verses as cited: 41:5 (Qatadah, Ibn Zayd), 4:155 (Ibn Kathir);
2:91 and 61:5 as Quran text. Du'a: 20:114 plus a labelled composed plea.
Dropped: ar-Razi (bracketed in Ibn Kathir, not fetched); Ahmad's marfu'
four-hearts version (footnote only); Ibn Kathir's "Ansar" reading report.
