Assigned 2:180, widened to 2:180-182 (the bequest, the sin of altering it,
setting right a testator's janaf or ithm); 2:178-179 qisas left out. New card
and a 1,750-word, 8-section article.

Sources: at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi, Ibn Kathir (ar + en abridged), as-Sa'di,
al-Baghawi, Muyassar, Ma'arif on 2:180, 2:181 and 2:182. Slots on target.
Disagreements kept, no winner declared: abrogation in three positions with
names (wholly: Ibn Zayd, Ibn 'Umar, Mujahid, Ibn 'Abbas via Ibn Sirin, held by
Ibn Kathir; for heirs only: Qatada, Tawus, al-Hasan, ar-Rabi', Ibn 'Abbas via
'Ali b. Abi Talha, ash-Shafi'i per al-Qurtubi; not abrogated: ad-Dahhak,
Tawus, al-Hasan per al-Qurtubi, chosen by at-Tabari; as-Sa'di reconciling);
haqqan as obligation (at-Tabari, as-Sa'di) vs prudence (al-Qurtubi); the amount of khayr
(figures attributed: 'Ali, 'A'ishah, Ibn 'Abbas 60 dinars, Tawus 80, Qatada,
Aban an-Nakha'i; az-Zuhri's "little or much" chosen by at-Tabari); khafa as
fear (at-Tabari) or knew (al-Baghawi); mending at the bedside (Mujahid,
at-Tabari) or after death (Ibn 'Abbas, Qatada, ar-Rabi', Ibn Kathir).
Hadith: Tirmidhi 2120 "no bequest for an heir" with his own "hasan sahih";
Bukhari 2742 (Sa'd, "a third, and a third is much") and 2738 (will written
within two nights). No asbab; al-Qurtubi's qisas link given as placement.
Dropped: Ma'arif's "mutawatir" and 150,000 Companions; ad-Daraqutni's
heir-consent wordings; al-Qurtubi's "named only here" count (2:240 also has
wasiyya); Tirmidhi 2117, Bukhari 2743 and 1419 (fetched, cut for length);
weak reports Ibn Kathir himself flags. 'Amr b. Kharijah unnamed (sweep regex).
Application: write a will while well, fairness to kin with no share, keep
the dead's wishes; legal detail referred to a scholar.
