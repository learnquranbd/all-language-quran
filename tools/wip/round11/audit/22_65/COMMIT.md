Spine: taskhīr (subjection) framed as mercy. The verse tallies gifts already
in hand — earth, animals, crops, minerals, ships on the sea, the sky held from
falling — and seals them with raʾūf raḥīm, so every subjected thing is offered
as evidence of God's kindness, calling to gratitude and worship.

Sources (all pre-fetched, none re-fetched):
- at-Tabari: earth/beasts subjected to man's needs; bi-amrih = by His power;
  an taqaʿa = "that it not fall"; ending joined to His raʾfa/raḥma as favour.
- as-Sa'di: catalogue (animals ride/carry/eat, trees/fruit, minerals; ships
  carry goods, sea-ornaments); if not for His mercy the sky would fall (cites
  35:41); He is more merciful than one's own parents.
- Ibn Kathir (ar): sweep animal/inanimate/crop/fruit (cites 45:13); raging sea,
  gentle sailing, trade; kind "even with their wrongdoing" (cites 13:6).
- al-Qurtubi: another favour; holding = creating stillness state after state;
  "except by His leave" = leave for it to fall, i.e. the appointed Day.
- al-Muyassar: subjection summary; ships by His power/command; sky guarded.
- Ma'arif (en): taskhīr = "to serve"; command retained by Allah, benefit to man
  (river-chaos argument).

Reading-difference kept as a difference (not resolved in article's voice):
al-fulka accusative (general reciters, tied to "what is on the earth") vs
al-Aʿraj's al-fulku nominative; Tabari prefers accusative by consensus.

Hadith: Sahih al-Bukhari 5999 (ʿUmar; captive-woman nursing) quoted whole,
graded sound, not upgraded — matches as-Sa'di's "more merciful than parents".

Watch-points honoured: mercy-frame kept the verse's own; no modern-cosmology
claim about the sky (tafsir's own limit stated); "except by His leave" noted as
the Day it will fall; steered clear of 22:46 (heart's sight) and 22:73 (fly)
themes and headings. No asbab (general sign-verse); placement described instead.

Dropped: al-Baghawi (nothing unique beyond named four); Ibn Kathir en (169)
used only to cross-check the Arabic.
