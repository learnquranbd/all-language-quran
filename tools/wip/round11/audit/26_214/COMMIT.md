Card and deep bilingual article for 26:214, the home verse of the
aṣ-Ṣafā episode: "And warn your nearest kindred."

Sources (all confirmed before citing):
- at-Tabari, al-Muyassar: warn the kin closest in relation, of a
  punishment that falls for disbelief; he began with Banū ʿAbd
  al-Muṭṭalib.
- as-Saʿdi: placed after 26:213 — perfect self, then perfect others,
  kin first; the specification confirms the universal command, not
  cancels it (with Ibn Kathir, cross-refs 42:7, 36:6).
- Ma'arif al-Qur'an: why daʿwah starts at home (kin know you, a false
  claim is exposed, a sincere nucleus carries the message); 66:6.
- al-Qurtubi: lineage-nearness avails nothing with distance in deeds;
  and the same hadith permits keeping ties with, guiding and advising
  a disbelieving relative ("a kinship-tie I shall keep moist").
- Ibn Kathir: nothing saves them but faith in their Lord.

Hadith: Ṣaḥīḥ al-Bukhārī (Abū Hurayra, DS 4771) quoted whole as the
primary — he names his aunt Ṣafiyya and daughter Fāṭima, "ask of my
wealth … I cannot avail you anything against Allah"; the Ṣafā-climb
narration (Ibn ʿAbbas, Bukhārī DS 4770) described as companion. Both
confirmed on quranx; no grading upgraded.

Asbāb: the Ṣafā gathering, grounded in the tafsir and the two Ṣaḥīḥ
narrations, not from memory. Dropped the weak ʿAlī-feast/successor
narration (Ibn Kathir: chain "matrūk kadhdhāb"), the Abū al-Dardāʾ
report, and the "wa-rahṭaka" variant reading. Kept 26:215–217 ground
(gentleness to followers, disavowal, trust) untouched.

Themes: character, accountability.
