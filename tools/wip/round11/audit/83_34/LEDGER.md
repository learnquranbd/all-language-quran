## Glosses kept side by side (none chosen)
- al-yawm: Day of Resurrection (IK, Tabari, Qurtubi, Sa'di, Muyassar; IK-en "Day of Judgement"); the Hereafter (Baghawi "في الآخرة"); a moment within it, when they see them in the floods of punishment (Sa'di "حين يرونهم").
- How/where: gates of the Fire opened and shut, repeatedly (Abu Salih via Baghawi; via Qurtubi with chain, believers "على الأرائك", mockery ascribed to Allah per 2:15); openings (kuwa) between Paradise and Fire (Ka'b via Qatada in Qurtubi; Ka'b unchained in Baghawi), with 37:55.
- Nature: in return/counterpart (IK ar), retribution (IK en), "as they laughed in the world" (Qurtubi), recompense of the same kind as the deed, believers at rest (Sa'di), rendered as mockery "يسخر" (Muyassar).
