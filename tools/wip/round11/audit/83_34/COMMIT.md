Card and 8-section bilingual article for 83:34: fa-l-yawma alladhina
amanu mina al-kuffari yadhakun, the reversal of 83:29-33. Six words;
83:29 (eight) ends on the same yadhakun, the two parties change places.

Sources: Tabari, Ibn Kathir (ar + en abridged, grouped 83:29-36),
Muyassar, Qurtubi, Sa'di, Baghawi, Ma'arif. All named.
- al-yawm: Day of Resurrection (IK, Tabari, Qurtubi, Sa'di, Muyassar);
  the Hereafter (Baghawi); the moment they see them in the floods of
  punishment (Sa'di). Kept side by side, none chosen.
- Nature: in return for their laughter (IK), as they laughed in the
  world (Qurtubi), recompense of the deed's kind with believers at rest
  (Sa'di), rendered as mockery (Muyassar).
- How/where: Abu Salih's gates opened and shut, repeatedly (Baghawi;
  Qurtubi with chain al-Kalbi < Abu Salih, under 2:15, 'ala al-ara'ik);
  Ka'b's openings between Paradise and Fire with 37:55 (Qurtubi via
  Qatada; Baghawi unchained). Reported as theirs, no grading given.
- Parallel 23:108-111 (IK-en; Qurtubi "end of al-Mu'minun"); 83:33 per
  IK-en. Shipped 23:111 and 37:55 cross-referenced, not re-argued.
- Licensing sentence in both languages; no living group named.
Hadith: none attached by any fetched tafsir; said in one sentence.
Asbab: none for 83:34; 'Ali report not in fetched text, not used.
Dropped: Ma'arif's present-day application (names a living group);
6:10, 11:38 (not cited); 83:35-36 glosses (future ground).
