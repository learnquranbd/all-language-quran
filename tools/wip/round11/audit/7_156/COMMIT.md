Replaces the 799-word, 5-section article with a 1,758-word, 8-section
one, from at-Tabari, Ibn Kathir (ar and en), al-Qurtubi, as-Sa'di,
al-Baghawi, al-Muyassar and Ma'arif on 7:156. All slots checked. The
shipped card is kept.

Setting: the last part of Musa's plea from 7:155 (as-Sa'di, Ma'arif),
the cause of the tremor only as Ibn Kathir (en) gives it.
uktub glossed awjib/athbit (Ibn Kathir, al-Baghawi); hasanah per
at-Tabari, Ibn Jurayj, al-Qurtubi, al-Baghawi, as-Sa'di, Muyassar.
hudna = tubna (at-Tabari's list, Ibn Kathir: so in the language too);
'Ali's report on the name al-Yahud with Ibn Kathir's weakness note.
'adhabi: the tremor itself (at-Tabari, Muyassar), al-Qurtubi's two
views, Ibn Kathir's reply to "Your trial", Ma'arif with Kashmiri, 6:147.
Kept as disagreements: at-Tabari's three readings of "all things"
(general wording / specific meaning, Ibn 'Abbas; this world vs the next,
al-Hasan and Qatadah, with 'Atiyyah's lamp image from al-Baghawi;
mercy as repentance, Ibn Zayd); whether Musa's request was granted
(Ibn 'Abbas in at-Tabari vs Ma'arif and Ruh al-Ma'ani).
The Iblis and Jews-and-Christians reports as early commentators'
sayings, al-Hudhali's weakness noted; licensing lines in both languages.
Hadith: Muslim 2753 (Salman, hundred mercies), cited by Ibn Kathir,
quoted whole. Bukhari 3194 left to 6:54 (one line). One bridge to 7:157.
Dropped: Jundub's bedouin hadith, the Ahmad routes, at-Tabarani's
Hudhayfah report (gharib per Ibn Kathir).
Corrected from the shipped article: the hundred-mercies wording given to
Muslim from Abu Hurayrah, unconfirmed (the tafsir has Muslim from Salman); the
unsourced perfect-tense and will-vs-no-will contrasts.
