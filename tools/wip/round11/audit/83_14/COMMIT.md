Replaces the 762-word, 6-section article with a 1,764-word, 8-section deep article, built only on tafsir fetched for 83:14 (shipped card kept).

Sources: at-Tabari, Ibn Kathir (ar; en abridged for the 83:7-16 frame),
al-Qurtubi, as-Sa'di, al-Baghawi, al-Muyassar, Ma'arif al-Qur'an, all for 83:14.

Frame: the charge of 83:13 (Ibn Kathir en on 83:12-13, with 16:24 and 25:5;
Muyassar's "falsehoods of the ancients"). Kalla held three ways, no position:
rebuff of the charge (al-Qurtubi, at-Tabari, Ibn Kathir, Muyassar, Ma'arif),
"truly" (al-Hasan via al-Qurtubi), "they will not believe" (Muqatil via
al-Baghawi). Hafs's stop on bal given in al-Qurtubi's words only.
Rayn as overpowering (al-Baghawi, at-Tabari, wine and Abu Zubayd's line;
Abu 'Ubayda, Abu 'Ubayd, 'Umar, Abu Zayd via al-Qurtubi), al-Zajjaj's rust
and thin cloud, the imala readings. Ibn 'Abbas reported as "sealed"
(at-Tabari, al-Baghawi) vs "covered" (al-Qurtubi's preferred report), kept.
Abu Mu'adh's rayn < tab' < iqfal; Ibn Kathir's rayn/ghaym/ghayn.
Tabi'un: al-Hasan, Qatada, Mujahid (palm image, 2:81), Ibn Zayd, 'Ata';
Hudhayfah and Bakr ibn 'Abdullah via al-Qurtubi.
83:15 disagreement kept: veiled from His honour (Qatada, Ibn Abi Mulayka) vs
from seeing Him (al-Hasan); at-Tabari's both; Muyassar and ash-Shafi'i (via
Ibn Kathir) on the believers' seeing. 83:16-17 one line. Licensing sentence
on the deniers; as-Sa'di's contrast; 83:4, 83:7, 83:18-21, 83:25, 83:34 one line.

Hadith: Tirmidhi 3334 (Abu Hurayrah, black spot), quoted whole, graded hasan
sahih by at-Tirmidhi; Ibn Majah 4244 noted for its "believer" wording.
Asbab: none.

Dropped from the old article: the "saktah observed by Hafs" claim (al-Qurtubi
says the stop is to make the lam distinct, "not for the sakt"), the hapax
claim, the kana+imperfect grammar note, 18:57 and the 2:7/47:24 comparison,
Muslim's Hudhayfah reed-mat hadith (unattached), unsourced reflections.
