SOURCE: Ma'arif (en) (tafsir 168) on 24:45
COVERS: 24:43,24:44,24:45,24:46,24:47,24:48,24:49

مِنَ السَّمَاءِ مِن جِبَالٍ فِيهَا From the sky mountains (of clouds) having hail in them - 43. Here the word سَمَاء is purported for clouds, and Jibal جِبَالٍ (mountains) for big masses of clouds, while hails are called Barad.
