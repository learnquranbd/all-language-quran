Card and deep bilingual article on the creatures created from water.

Spine: from one origin (water) Allah brings the whole diversity of
animate life in different modes of movement — a sign of His power and
free will, "yakhluqu-llahu ma yasha'". Built on wonder and tawhid, not
biology.

Sources (all confirmed against fetched Arabic):
- Qira'at khalaqa / khaliq (Tabari, Baghawi, Qurtubi); both established,
  Qurtubi refuses to rank them, notes the khalaqa-specific/khaliq-general
  nuance.
- "min ma'" kept as a genuine dispute: nutfah, each creature from its
  fluid (Tabari, Baghawi, mufassirun per Qurtubi, an-Naqqash) vs water as
  origin of all life (jumhur per Qurtubi; as-Sa'di, citing 21:30 and the
  two tracks). Neither position pushed.
- Belly/two-legs/four classes with each mufassir's examples; the
  more-than-four note (Baghawi; Ubayy's mushaf per Qurtubi, marked not
  established by ijma'; an-Naqqash vs Ibn 'Atiyya).
- Taghlih of "man" for beasts (Tabari's Q&A, Baghawi's rule, Qurtubi's
  diversity-to-Maker argument).
- Power/will close via Ibn Kathir and as-Sa'di (rain parable, 13:4).

No sound marfu' hadith attaches; article states this plainly. Bukhari 660
(the PREP suggestion) is the "seven under the shade" hadith and is
unrelated — dropped. Badr "nahnu min ma'" used only as al-Qurtubi's
language witness, marked as not a ruling on the verse.

No modern-science/evolution claims (watch-point). jinn/angels-excluded
boundary kept; Baghawi's water-cosmogony aside dropped.
