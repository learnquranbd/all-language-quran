# SOURCES ledger — 24:45

## Tafsir fetched (all pre-fetched in PREP.md, covers 24:45 unless noted)
- **at-Tabari (15)** — USED. Qirāʾāt (Kufa/Asim split, both mashhūr, "fa-bi-ayyatihimā qaraʾa fa-muṣīb"); "min māʾ" = "min nuṭfah"; belly=ḥayyāt, two legs=ṭayr, four=bahāʾim; the taghlīb Q&A ("man lil-nās… fa-kannā ʿan jamīʿihim kināyatahu ʿan banī Ādam thumma fassarahum bi-man"); "yakhluqu-llāhu mā yashāʾ… qadīr".
- **al-Qurtubi (90)** — USED. dābbah def ("kull mā dabba… dabba yadibbu"); qirāʾāt + refusal to rank ("lā yanbaghī… iḥdā al-qirāʾatayn aṣaḥḥ"); khalaqa-specific vs khāliq-general nuance; "min māʾ = min nuṭfah" (an-Naqqāsh: "amniyat al-dhukūr") vs jumhūr an-nazara (origin of all life, as Adam min al-māʾ wal-ṭīn) + Badr "naḥnu min māʾ" adduced as language witness; jinn/angels excluded (angels from nūr, jinn from nār "fī al-ṣaḥīḥ"); Ubayy's muṣḥaf "yamshī ʿalā akthar" (crab/khashāsh) "laysa qurʾānan lam yuthbithu ijmāʿ"; an-Naqqāsh reason for stopping at four; Ibn ʿAṭiyya counter ("laysat bāṭilan… muḥtāj ilayhā"); taghlīb (ghallaba man yaʿqil, al-mukhāṭab al-mutaʿabbid); diversity → "thubūt al-Ṣāniʿ" + 13:4 parallel.
- **as-Saʿdī (91)** — USED. "māddatuhā kulluhā al-māʾ", cites **21:30** "wa jaʿalnā min al-māʾi kulla shayʾin ḥayy"; two tracks — reproducing animals from nuṭfah-fluid, earth-generated (ḥasharāt) from "al-ruṭūbāt al-māʾiyya", never from non-water; "al-māddatu wāḥida wal-khilqatu mukhtalifa" → "nufūdh mashīʾat Allāh wa ʿumūm qudratih"; rain parable, one earth/one mother, offspring differ, cites **13:4**.
- **Ibn Kathir ar (14) + en abridged (169)** — USED. "qudratahu al-tāmma wa sulṭānahu al-ʿaẓīm… ikhtilāf ashkālihā wa alwānihā wa ḥarakātihā wa sakanātihā min māʾin wāḥid"; belly=ḥayya, two legs=insān wal-ṭayr, four=anʿām; "yakhluqu mā yashāʾ bi-qudratih… mā shāʾa kāna wa mā lam yashaʾ lam yakun".
- **al-Baghawi (94)** — USED. qirāʾāt (Hamza/Kisāʾī khāliq); "min māʾ = min nuṭfah", "kull ḥayawān yushāhad… lā yadkhulu al-malāʾika wal-jinn liʾannā lā nushāhiduhum"; the water-origin variant (water→wind/fire/clay); belly=ḥayyāt/ḥītān/dīdān, two legs=banī Ādam wal-ṭayr, four=bahāʾim wal-sibāʿ; did not mention >4-legged (ḥasharāt) "liʾannahā fī al-ṣūra kal-latī tamshī ʿalā al-arbaʿ"; taghlīb rule ("tujʿalu al-ghalaba li-man yaʿqil").
- **al-Muyassar (16)** — USED (light). "al-māʾ aṣlu khalqih"; belly = "yamshī zaḥfan ʿalā baṭnih kal-ḥayyāt".
- **Maʿārif en (168)** — NOT USED. Fetched text covers only 24:43 (samāʾ=clouds, jibāl=cloud-masses, barad=hail); nothing on 24:45. Grouped file, off-verse for the target.
