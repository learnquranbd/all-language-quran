Card and 8-section article on anna sababna al-ma'a sabba, the first
answer to 80:24's "let man look at his food" (4 Arabic words).

Sources: at-Tabari, Ibn Kathir (ar and abridged en), al-Qurtubi,
as-Sa'di, al-Baghawi, the Muyassar and Ma'arif al-Qur'an, all fetched
for 80:25.

Qira'at kept as reported: inna (kasra, fresh start; Medina and Basra per
at-Tabari, the general reading per al-Qurtubi) and anna (fatha; Kufa,
plus Ruways from Ya'qub per al-Qurtubi), with at-Tabari's verdict that
both are correct. Al-Qurtubi's badal reading (no good pause at ta'amihi),
the "because We poured" sense, and al-Husayn ibn 'Ali's anna-as-how
reading (full pause at ta'amihi; also "from where", with al-Kumayt).
Water glossed by each source: from the sky (Ibn Kathir), al-ghayth sent
down and poured (at-Tabari), al-ghayth and al-amtar (al-Qurtubi), al-matar
(al-Baghawi), rain in abundance (as-Sa'di), from the clouds (Ma'arif).
No disagreement on the water itself; noted as such.
Ibn Kathir (en) on 80:24: a call to reflect on Allah's favour and an
evidence for resurrection from life out of lifeless earth; tied to 80:22.

Hadith: none attached to the verse by any fetched tafsir; said so.
Asbab: none reported. No tafsir-cited cross-refs; 78:14, 15:22, 25:48,
50:9 and 56:69 pointed to by number only. 80:26 named as the next verse;
80:27-32 not drawn on. No scientific claims.
Fix: dropped unattributed 'verbal noun' grammar claim; now says same root.
