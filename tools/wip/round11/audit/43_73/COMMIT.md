Card and 8-section bilingual article for 43:73: lakum fiha fakihatun
kathiratun minha ta'kulun. 43:71-72 as context (gold vessels of food and
drink, the inherited Garden); 43:74 one line plus the licenses-nothing
statement; 43:70 glosses not reused.

Sources: Tabari, Muyassar, Ibn Kathir (ar; en abridged), Qurtubi, Sa'di,
Baghawi; Ma'arif fetched but silent on 43:73.
- Fakiha: Qurtubi's definition and Ibn 'Abbas "all fruits, fresh and
  dried"; Qurtubi's siwa (besides food and drink).
- Kathira as kinds: Tabari and Muyassar min kulli naw', IK min jami'
  al-anwa'; Sa'di's cross-reference to 55:52 (zawjan left to that verse).
- Minha ta'kulun: four glosses reported by name, none ranked (IK choice
  and will, Tabari desire, Sa'di selection, Qurtubi/Muyassar wording).
  Partitive "never exhausted" reading is in no fetched text: raised as an
  unattributed question only.
- Why fruit after food and drink: IK's li-tatimma an-ni'ma wa-l-ghibta,
  the only reason given; nothing added.
- 43:72 balance from IK (en): deeds the cause, entry by mercy, ranks by
  deeds.
Hadith: none. Baghawi's "fruit replaced as it is picked" narration is
al-Bazzar's, outside the six collections; unconfirmed, not quoted, and
the article says so. Bukhari 3244 (43:70) not reused.
Asbab: none in the sources; not used.
Fixed in drafting: dropped a from-memory definition of ghibta and a
"two kinds of each" reading of 55:52.
