minha ta'kulun: glosses reported by name (IK choice+will; Tabari desire; Sa'di selection + fruits' appeal; Qurtubi/Muyassar keep the wording). None ranked.
Partitive-min reading ("a part of it; plenty never exhausted"): NOT in any fetched text. Raised only as a reader's question, explicitly unattributed; the article states only the word order (kathira before minha).
Why fruit after food and drink: Ibn Kathir only (ar + en); Qurtubi's «سوى» noted as separating fruit from food/drink. No other reason supplied.
Al-ghibtah: not defined from memory; given as the abridged English's "joy".
