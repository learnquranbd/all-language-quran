## Disagreement kept (named, no position)
- munkira: active repudiation (al-Baghawi jahida, Muyassar jahida wahdaniyyatih, Ibn Kathir, as-Sa'di) vs reception (at-Tabari mustankira; al-Qurtubi "does not accept admonition, dhikr does not benefit"). Presented as difference of emphasis.
- mustakbirun: against worship (Ibn Kathir, as-Sa'di) vs against accepting the truth (al-Qurtubi), both (Muyassar), object unstated (al-Baghawi); at-Tabari alone adds following the forebears' shirk. No position taken.
