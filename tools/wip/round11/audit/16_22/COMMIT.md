Card and bilingual article (8 sections, 1,775 EN words) for "Your god is
one God; those who do not believe in the Hereafter, their hearts deny,
and they are arrogant", read as the conclusion of 16:17-21.

Sources: al-Qurtubi (after shirk shown impossible, the One worshipped is
one; munkira = does not accept admonition; mustakbirun = too great to
accept the truth); at-Tabari (ilah = ma'bud, single Him out; Hereafter =
promise, threat and return; munkira = mustankira; pride as following the
forebears' shirk; Qatada); the Muyassar (hearts deny His oneness because
they do not fear His punishment, the only stated cause); as-Sa'di (the
believing heart's awe and love; denial of tawhid; pride against worship);
al-Baghawi (jahida, muta'azzimun); Ibn Kathir with 38:5, 39:45, 40:60.
Ma'arif grouped 16:17-23 and Ibn Kathir abridged 16:22-23, said so;
only their 16:22 content used.

Disagreements kept: munkira as repudiation vs as a heart the reminder
no longer reaches; mustakbirun as pride against worship vs against the
truth, with at-Tabari's inherited-shirk reading as his alone.

Hadith: none attached to 16:22 by any fetched tafsir; said so. Muslim 91
on kibr pointed to under 15:33, not repeated; 16:23 left as future ground.

Dropped: al-Qurtubi's unexplained Qadariyya remark; the 16:21/16:22
resurrection echo (no commentator draws it); grammar labels. Licensing
sentence in both languages.
