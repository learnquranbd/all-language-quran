Tadabbur 27:67 — the deniers' doubled sneer at resurrection, and the
proof already standing in front of them.

Spine: istibʿād al-baʿth. The Makkan polytheists (named by al-Qurtubi and
al-Baghawi) ask with a doubled interrogative (a-idhā... a-innā) whether dust
can be raised. as-Sa'di diagnoses the move in one line — they measured the
power of the One perfect in power by their own weak measure — and that is the
article's spine: denial that shrinks God to the limits of human experience.

Sources: Ibn Kathir (ar), at-Tabari, al-Muyassar on the istibʿād after the body
becomes bones and dust; al-Qurtubi and al-Baghawi on the qira'at of the double
interrogative, with al-Qurtubi's point that whichever reading is taken, the
sense is inkār. The answer is drawn from the passage itself: 27:64 names God as
the One who begins creation then repeats it, and Ibn Kathir (en) reads the
stretch as resurrection "and its refutation" — the refutation being creation
itself, already in view.

Hadith: the qudsī report in Sahih al-Bukhari 4974 (Abu Hurayrah, RA) — "the
first creation was not easier for Me than new creation" — answers the verse
almost word for word. Sound by al-Bukhari's collection; not upgraded; avoids the
stock hadith used nearby.

Framing: carries the "licenses nothing against any living person or community"
sentence in both languages — the target is a posture, not a people. 27:68
referenced lightly, not preempted.

Dropped: al-Qurtubi's extended grammatical dispute (named grammarians) and his
parallel verse 34:7 — out of scope / risk of misattribution. No weak report used.

Card: hereafter + creation themes. Article: 8 sections, 1792 EN words, bilingual.
