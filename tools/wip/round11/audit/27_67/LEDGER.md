# SOURCES ledger — 27:67

Verse: وقال الذين كفروا أئذا كنا ترابا وآباؤنا أئنا لمخرجون — "And those who
disbelieve say: When we and our forefathers are dust, will we really be brought
out?" Spine: istibʿād al-baʿth, the deniers reckoning resurrection absurd.

## Asbab an-nuzul

None established or confirmed on a fetched page. Not used. Wrote the verse's
placement instead: it closes the "a-ilāhun maʿa Allāh" passage (27:60–64) and the
resurrection statements of 27:65–66, and its speech continues into 27:68
(referenced lightly, not preempted, per the watch-point).

## Framing

§6 carries the required sentence in both languages: the verse quotes the Makkan
polytheists (so named by al-Qurtubi and al-Baghawi) but describes only what the
text describes and licenses nothing against any living person or community; the
target is a posture (refusing what one cannot personally perform), not a people.
