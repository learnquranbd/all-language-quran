# SOURCES ledger — 24:12

## Tafsir fetched (all from PREP.md, not re-fetched) and where used

- **Ibn Kathir (ar 14 / en 169)** — USED. "هذا تأديب من الله للمؤمنين في قضية عائشة" → §1 (reproach/disciplining). "قاسوا ذلك الكلام على أنفسهم، فإن كان لا يليق بهم فأم المؤمنين أولى بالبراءة" → §4 (measure-against-yourself test). "لولا بمعنى هلا" → §2. "هذا ما يتعلق بالباطن، (وقالوا) أي بألسنتهم" → §5 (inner heart vs tongue). Abu Ayyub / Umm Ayyub narration + "ويقال إنما قالها أبي بن كعب" → §7.
- **at-Tabari (15)** — USED. "وهذا عتاب من الله... أهل الإيمان" → §1 (reproof). "لأن أهل الإسلام كلهم بمنـزلة نفس واحدة... أهل ملة واحدة" → §3. Ibn Zayd: "المؤمنون بنون لها، محرّما عليها" (ʿĀʾishah a mother, believers as sons) → §4 (rendered as sanctity/reverence). Mujahid on parallel verses (4:29, 24:61) → §3. al-Hasan: "لا ينبغي أن يتكلم به إلا من أقام عليه أربعة من الشهود" → §5 (link to 24:13). Abu Ayyub narration → §7.
- **al-Qurtubi (90)** — USED. "لولا بمعنى هلا" → §2. "يقيس فضلاء المؤمنين... فإن كان ذلك يبعد فيهم فذلك في عائشة، وصفوان أبعد" → §4. an-Nahhas: "بأنفسهم بإخوانهم" → §3. "فأوجب الله على المسلمين... أن ينكروا عليه ويكذبوه. وتواعد من ترك ذلك ومن نقله" → §8. Principle: "درجة الإيمان... ولبسة العفاف... لا يزيلها عنه خبر محتمل وإن شاع، إذا كان أصله فاسدا أو مجهولا" → §8.
- **as-Sa'di (91)** — USED. "ظن المؤمنون بعضهم ببعض خيرا، وهو السلامة مما رموا به، وأن ما معهم من الإيمان المعلوم، يدفع" → §2. "فهذا من الظن الواجب... أن يبرئه بلسانه، ويكذب القائل لذلك" → §2 (obligatory opinion). NOTE: as-Sa'di's text imports "سبحانك" (wording of 24:16) into his gloss; I did NOT attribute "subhanaka" to 24:12 — dropped, see below.
- **al-Baghawi (94)** — USED. "لولا هلا" → §2. "بأنفسهم بإخوانهم"; al-Hasan "المؤمنون كنفس واحدة"; parallels 4:29, 24:61 → §3. "هذا إفك مبين أي كذب بين" → §5.
- **al-Muyassar (16)** — USED. "هلا ظن المؤمنون والمؤمنات بعضهم ببعض خيرًا... السلامة مما رموا به... هذا كذب ظاهر على عائشة" → §2 and §5.
- **Ma'arif al-Qur'an (168)** — USED. بأنفسهم = disgracing a Muslim disgraces yourself, "one body"; parallels 49:11, 4:29, 24:61 → §3. Person-shift "ظن المؤمنون instead of ظننتم" + "not fit to be called Mu'mins to the extent of this act" → §6. Ruling (Mazhari): good opinion obligatory unless proven under law; rejecting an unproven accusation obligatory as it is backbiting → §5.

## Hadith (fetched pages, saved under audit/24_12)

- **Sahih al-Bukhari 6064** — QUOTED WHOLE in §7. URL: https://quranx.com/hadith/Bukhari/DarusSalam/Hadith-6064 . First ten words: "Beware of suspicion, for suspicion is the worst of false". Grading: Sahih al-Bukhari (sound; not upgraded). Abu Hurayra (RA). Arabic: "إياكم والظن، فإن الظن أكذب الحديث...".
- **Sahih al-Bukhari 4141** — the ifk hadith (ʿĀʾishah RA), referenced factually in §1 and §7 (vindication only; no salacious detail). URL: https://quranx.com/hadith/Bukhari/DarusSalam/Hadith-4141 . First ten words: "Narrated `Aisha: Whenever Allah's Messenger intended to go on". Grading: Sahih al-Bukhari. Confirms "Allah revealed the ten Verses" declaring her innocence.
- **Sahih Muslim 2770** — parallel ifk narration, referenced in §7 ("al-Bukhari and Muslim"). URL: https://quranx.com/hadith/Muslim/Hadith-2770 . First ten words: "Sa'id b. Musayyib, 'Urwa b. Zubair, 'Alqama b. Waqqas and". Grading: Sahih Muslim. Confirms "Allah exonerated her of this charge".

## Asbab an-nuzul

- Not needed as a separate asbab source. The occasion (the ifk against ʿĀʾishah RA) is established from the surah itself (24:11) and from the sound ifk hadith (Bukhari 4141 / Muslim 2770). The mufassirūn (Ibn Kathir, at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi) additionally report the Abu Ayyub / Umm Ayyub exchange as an occasion for this specific verse; given as such in §7 ("some early authorities held"), with Ibn Kathir's variant that Ubayy ibn Kaʿb (RA) said the like.

## Dropped (could not or would not confirm)

- as-Sa'di's "سبحانك" gloss: dropped, because "subhanaka" is the wording of 24:16, not 24:12; not attributed to this verse.
- The blunt literal form of Ibn Zayd's statement: not quoted verbatim; rendered decently as the mother/sons sanctity point (same content, keeps the ifk decent).
- No "above the seven heavens" hadith wording was quoted; the phrase is used once in §1 as the reverent description of a vindication that came by revelation (anchored to Bukhari 4141 / Muslim 2770: "Allah revealed the ten Verses").
