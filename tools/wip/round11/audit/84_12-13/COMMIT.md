Card and 8-section bilingual article for 84:12-13: wa-yasla sa'ira /
innahu kana fi ahlihi masrura. Two words and five; closes the scene of
84:10-11 (six, three). ahlihi masrura repeats the end of 84:9.

Sources: Tabari, Ibn Kathir (ar), Muyassar, Qurtubi, Sa'di, Baghawi,
Ma'arif. IK-en wrong group (84:1-10), not used. 84:10-11 slots fetched
for 14/15/90/91/94 to name the man of 84:10; all slots checked on-verse.
- Who: the disbeliever in Allah (Muyassar); "whoever of you" with the
  right hand at the neck, left behind the back (Tabari, Mujahid); IK,
  Baghawi, Sa'di on the posture; Qurtubi's unchained Ibn Abbas report
  (al-Aswad b. Abd al-Asad), then "general". Licensing sentence in both.
- yasla: yusalla / yasla (Tabari: both correct), third reading yusla
  (Qurtubi), reader lists per Qurtubi and Baghawi.
- sa'ir: a fire (IK), the Fire and its heat (Qurtubi, Muyassar),
  surrounding him (Sa'di), punishment of Hell (Ma'arif). Nothing added.
- masrur, none chosen: disobedience (Tabari), whims (Baghawi);
  heedless of outcomes (IK, Muyassar), resurrection never in mind
  (Sa'di), oblivious of the Hereafter (Ma'arif); in the world (Qatada,
  Qurtubi). Ibn Zayd's contrast with 52:26-27 (Qurtubi; Ma'arif).
  Article states none of them faults family joy itself.
Hadith: Bukhari 103 (A'ishah, "whoever is called to account"), cited
by Ma'arif on 84:7-12, quoted whole; not attached to 84:12-13 directly.
Shipped 84:6 cross-referenced, not re-argued.
Dropped: "sa'ir is a name of Hell" (not in fetched text); 69:25 (not
cited); 84:14-15 glosses (future ground, named only).
Fix: dropped unchained personal name (al-Qurtubi's report); Bukhari 103 paraphrase replaced by cross-reference to 84:6 (quoted there in full).
