Card plus an 8-section bilingual article (1,602 EN words) on the
forefathers' reply: "we found our fathers upon an umma, and in their
footsteps we are guided".

Sources: Ibn Kathir (ar and abridged en), at-Tabari, al-Muyassar,
al-Qurtubi (on 43:22, and on 2:170, where his 43:22 note says the matter
was treated in full), as-Sa'di, al-Baghawi. Ma'arif fetched but its text
is on 43:18 only; not used.

- Bal read with 43:20-21: Tabari (no book commanded idol worship) and
  Ibn Kathir (no support but taqlid of the fathers); 30:35 parallel.
- Umma glosses reported by name: religion (Ibn Kathir, with 21:92; the
  abridged en cites 23:52), din and milla (Tabari, Sa'di, Baghawi), way
  and school (Umar b. Abd al-Aziz), qibla (al-Farra'), uprightness
  (al-Akhfash), imam (Mujahid in Baghawi). Mujahid's three reports kept
  apart. No fetched tafsir glosses it as community.
- Umma/imma reading: Tabari's two senses of imma and his verdict.
- muhtadun vs muqtadun (43:23): Qurtubi says the meaning is one; no
  contrast drawn by any tafsir or by the article.
- Taqlid: Qurtubi at 2:170 reported with its sides (blamed in falsehood,
  a refuge in truth; disputed in creed, sound in furu'; Ibn Atiyya's
  consensus claim vs the disagreement noted by Ibn al-Arabi and Ibn
  Dirbas; Yusuf (AS) at 12:38). No verdict in the article's own voice.
- Licensing sentence in both languages; no modern group named.

Hadith: none; no fetched tafsir attaches one.
Asbab: Muqatil's named list (via Qurtubi) not established; names left
out, placement given via Qatada in Tabari.
Dropped: Qurtubi's mutraf gloss and all 43:24 material (separate target),
Qurtubi's off-verse kalam polemic at 2:170.
