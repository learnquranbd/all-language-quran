Card and 8-section bilingual article (1,789 EN words) on the messengers'
proofs, the Book and the balance, iron, and help given unseen.

Sources: Ibn Kathir (ar/en), at-Tabari, al-Qurtubi, as-Sa'di, al-Baghawi,
Muyassar, Ma'arif al-Qur'an; all eight named, each clause in the ledger.
- bayyinat, kitab, mizan glossed per source; mizan as justice (Mujahid and
  Qatadah via Ibn Kathir, Tabari, Baghawi, Sa'di) vs the actual scale
  (Ibn Zayd via Tabari and Qurtubi; Muqatil via Baghawi; Qushayri's
  "sent down the Book and set up the balance", 55:7).
- Disagreement kept on anzalna al-hadid: Ibn Abbas report of smith's tools
  coming down with Adam (Tabari, Ibn Kathir, Qurtubi) vs created (al-Hasan in
  Qurtubi; Ma'arif) vs brought from mines (ahl al-ma'ani) vs provision (Qutrub).
- li-ya'lama Allah reported only as glossed: manifest knowledge (Muyassar),
  "may see" (Qurtubi, Baghawi), party of Allah (Tabari), legally and outwardly
  (Ma'arif via Ruh al-Ma'ani).
- Ma'arif's "iron last, as a last resort" and Sa'di's Book/iron pairing.
Hadith: none used. Ibn Kathir's Ahmad/Abu Dawud narration checked: Abu Dawud
4031 carries only the imitation clause; the sword wording was not confirmable.
The "four blessings from the sky" report (Qurtubi, Baghawi) was unconfirmed.
Dropped: Tuesday reports, the balance sent to Nuh, fuller Adam-tools lists.
Licensing line added (§7.1) since Ibn Kathir reads the force against resisters.
