Card and 8-section bilingual article for 51:15: inna l-muttaqina fi
jannatin wa-'uyun. 51:12-14 used as context only, with the licensing line.
Same five words as 15:45; that page's orchards gloss, four rivers and
'iyun reading are not retold.

Sources: Tabari, Ibn Kathir (ar; en for 51:16 pointer), Muyassar,
Qurtubi, Sa'di, Baghawi (verse text only, noted as no comment), Ma'arif.
- Placement: Qurtubi (end of kuffar, then of mu'minin), IK (yawma
  ma'adihim, unlike the ashqiya' in torment, burning and chains), Ma'arif.
- Sa'di frames the verse as reward then the deeds that reached it; the
  deed list (51:16-19) given as a pointer only.
- Muttaqin glosses side by side: Tabari (obedience and avoidance fi
  d-dunya), IK, Muyassar, Qurtubi (al-mu'minin), Sa'di (taqwa shi'ar,
  obedience dithar).
- Gardens: basatin (Tabari, Qurtubi), 'azima (Muyassar), Sa'di's fruits
  with and without worldly counterpart, quoted as his.
- Springs: water (Tabari), running (Muyassar), at the limit of delight
  (Qurtubi), the gardens and servants drink, yufajjirunaha (Sa'di); kept
  as differences of detail.
Hadith: none attached to 51:15 by any fetched tafsir; IK's narrations sit
under 51:17 and are left there.
Dropped: hadith qudsi echoed by Sa'di's wording (not cited by him), 76:6,
cross-refs 44:51-52, 52:17, 77:41 (not cited), 51:17-19 detail, Ma'arif's
kharrasun note.
