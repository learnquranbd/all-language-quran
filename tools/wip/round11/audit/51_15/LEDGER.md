Glosses of muttaqin kept side by side, no position taken: Tabari (obedience + avoiding
sins, in this world), Ibn Kathir (muttaqun for Allah), Muyassar (those mindful of Allah),
Qurtubi (al-mu'minin, by contrast with the kuffar), Sa'di (shi'ar/dithar). Springs: kept
as differences of detail (water / running / delight / who drinks), not as a disagreement.

Lexical glosses stated in the article's own voice, not attributed to a source (auditor
please confirm): shi'ar = garment worn next to the body, dithar = garment worn over it
(§3.2); 'ayn = both spring and eye (§5.1); the verse has no verb (nominal sentence after
inna) (§6.1). Observation that Sa'di's note uses 'uyun in both senses rests on his own
words العيون (eyes) and عيون سارحة (springs).
